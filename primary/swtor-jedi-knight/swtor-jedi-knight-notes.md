## 0. Chapter summary
- **Prologue: Tython (00:00–01:32).**
  - A Padawan (the future Hero of Tython, "HoT") fights the Flesh Raiders.
  - Orgus Din takes HoT as his Padawan.
  - Din's fallen former Padawan, Bengel Morr, reprograms the Flesh Raiders, poisons victims through Twi'lek pawns and seeks the Forge.
  - HoT defeats Morr at the Forge and builds a lightsaber there.
  - Satele Shan knights HoT. Kira Carsen (stowaway, ex-Child of the Emperor) becomes his Padawan on Coruscant.
- **Chapter 1: Darth Angral's Desolator.**
  - **Coruscant:** Tarnis, Angral's son, steals the Radiation Generator and dies to HoT. Darth Angral swears vengeance.
  - **Ord Mantell:** Lord Ferav.
  - **Taris:** Rora Seake, Watcher One and Lord Ravage's men. HoT spares Watcher One.
  - **Nar Shaddaa:** Sadic and Kira's Valis subplot. Valis is a fellow Child of the Emperor, killed by HoT and Kira.
  - **Tatooine:** Praven and the Shock Drum. HoT spares Praven and recovers Kiwiiks.
  - **Alderaan:** Nefarid's Death Mark orbital laser.
  - **Then:**
    - Angral executes Orgus Din on the HoloNet.
    - Uphrades is destroyed by the Desolator.
    - On the *Oppressor*, Angral dies after the Emperor seizes Kira and lightning-kills him through her.
    - Kira breaks free of the Emperor.
- **Chapter 2: the Emperor's fortress.**
  - Din's Force ghost sends HoT to Tatooine.
  - Tol Braga's strike team forms: Sedoru on Balmorra, Sajar on Quesh, Narezz on Hoth. Doc and Rusk join the crew.
  - Lord Scourge meets HoT on Quesh.
  - Braga's plan is to take the Emperor alive and redeem him. HoT foresees failure.
  - The team infiltrates the fortress, and HoT and Kira defeat Scourge.
  - The Emperor fells the whole team with lightning and dominates them.
  - HoT serves him for some time, a montage with no dialogue. Then Din's ghost and Kira free him.
  - HoT frees Kira from the Tormentor. Scourge defects ("300 years" vision).
- **Chapter 3: the Emperor's ritual.**
  - **Belsavis:** Executor Krannus's death cult.
  - **Space station:** Leeha Narezz, dominated, is freed.
  - **Voss:** Fulminiss, Valen-Da, Tala-Reh and the Dark Heart (Selmakor).
  - **Valiant:** Warren Sedoru is defeated and kept alive.
  - **Corellia:** HoT is named Supreme Commander of the Jedi forces. Braga is defeated aboard the *Eclipse* and sent to Tython.
  - **Dromund Kaas Dark Temple:** HoT defeats the Emperor. His body releases a dark explosion and the temple collapses.
  - **Victory:** Cross of Glory ceremony.
- **Epilogue: Emperor's Orphans.**
  - Shuri, a Child of the Emperor, still hears his voice on Voss.
  - Kira talks her into letting her siblings take her.
  - Romance close.

## 6. Character analyses
### Hero of Tython (ANALYSIS)
- **Narrative stature.** The story escalates its praise of him step by step:
  - Din: strongest «in decades».
  - Braga: greatest Jedi «in generations».
  - Kira: «the greatest Jedi warrior in the galaxy».
  - Scourge: «the Jedi's finest» and one of «a few beings» ever to break the Emperor's domination.
  - Praven: «You are simply stronger than me».
- **On-screen peak:**
  - beats Scourge (with Kira)
  - beats a dominated Braga (with a companion)
  - beats the Emperor's host body in direct combat (with T7), blocking his lightning and cutting through mindforms
- **Ceiling:**
  - Lost to the Emperor's opening lightning storm alongside four other Jedi, then was dominated.
  - Braga says even the weakened Emperor outclasses HoT («you are no match for him»), and HoT wins anyway.
  - The Emperor survives as a spirit («he is not gone»).
- **Force style:** mostly saber and physical combat. The Force feats are mind tricks, the saber build and blocking lightning, not raw telekinesis.

### Vitiate / the Emperor (ANALYSIS)
- Shown or told here:
  - possession across space (Kira)
  - instant domination of five Jedi, including a Council master
  - lightning storms
  - mindforms
  - a death-blast that collapses a temple
  - Nathema (told)
- Self-description: «patience of stone and the will of stars».
- Loses with his host body only. The story goes out of its way to say the Hero has not killed him.
- Scourge calls him «more powerful than all the Jedi and Sith combined» *after* the ritual (future tense).
- **Caveat:** these are statements about his potential, not feats performed.

### Darth Angral (ANALYSIS)
- On screen:
  - executes Din (a beaten opponent)
  - uses lightning in gameplay
  - loses to HoT and Kira, then is killed as a "disappointing" tool by the Emperor
- Strategic threat (Desolator) far exceeds his personal duel showing.

### Tol Braga (ANALYSIS)
- The Council's lead war-master: 3-day duel with Dark Council-level Sajar.
- Holds the Emperor's lightning for a moment.
- As a fallen Jedi, his telekinesis outclasses most enemies (slam, terminal throw). He still loses to HoT plus a companion.

### Kira Carsen (ANALYSIS)
- Strong sensing.
- Breaking the Emperor's possession is her signature feat (frame-confirmed).
- Possessed Kira's feats (lightning-kill on Angral, throwing HoT) are the Emperor's power acting through her, not hers.

### Lord Scourge (ANALYSIS)
- Over 300 years old and immortal (lore).
- Loses to HoT and Kira.
- Mostly useful as a witness: he scales the Emperor and HoT, and repeats the Revan/Exile failure.

### Orgus Din, Praven, Kiwiiks, Sedoru, Narezz, Sajar, Fulminiss (brief)
- **Din:** precise telekinesis; becomes a Force ghost.
- **Praven:** honorable duelist; AoE ring; outclassed by HoT; returns as a Jedi on Corellia, «stronger than I've ever been».
- **Kiwiiks:** endured a planet-cracking weapon with a trance, at lasting cost.
- **Sedoru:** Force winds and deck-shake (wiki); proud of «more than 50 Sith».
- **Narezz:** freed by defeat.
- **Sajar:** Dark Council level; telekinetic corpse-hurl.
- **Fulminiss:** a sorcerer whose feat is ritual madness, not dueling.

## 7. Power-scaling takeaways (ANALYSIS)
1. **HoT is above Scourge and above a fallen Braga** in 1v1 or 2v1 terms. Scourge was beaten with Kira; Braga with one companion.
2. **HoT can block the Emperor's lightning** twice on screen, plus once in the fortress. In the fortress the result was a team defeat; in the temple he won after the Emperor was weakened. Braga and Satele both call the Emperor weakened after Corellia.
3. **The Emperor's host body is beatable**, but the Emperor himself is not killed. Use this fight as a lower bound on what HoT can do against a weakened Vitiate, not a full win.
4. **Mental resistance is HoT's real "tier-marker"**: «Nobody controls me… My mind is my own.» He was dominated once and broke free with help. Scourge says only a few beings ever have.
5. **Angral < HoT + Kira.** The Emperor discards Angral at will.
6. **Gameplay feats** (lightning, AoE, shockwaves) are low weight. All cutscene feats are listed separately in §4.
