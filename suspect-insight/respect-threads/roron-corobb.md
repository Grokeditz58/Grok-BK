### Force Accolades:

- (quoted passage)
  > "The peaceful Ithorians species have been a source of many great Jedi Knights of old, since their natural attunement to nature has given them a predilection for Force-sensitivity. Roron Corobb was one of the most well regarded Ithorian Jedi, having trained as Yarael Poof's Padawan before ascending to the rank of Master himself. Under the crafty Poof's instruction, Corobb developed potent mental skills."

  > "Roron Corobb is one of the most well respected Ithorian Jedi, with his potent powers of persuasion and command."

### Combat Abilities:

- Roron Corobb and his Jedi allies held their own against General Grievous and his MagnaGuards. Master Corobb defeated Grievous' guards with such proficiency he was praised by Foul Moudama , Palpatine, and Shaak Ti alike before ultimately succumbing to the General due to him using all four of his lightsabers at once.
  > "[Untranslatable praise by Foul Moudama.]
  > "Yes, brilliant job. Master Yoda would be - ..."
  > ―Foul Moudama and Palpatine (Star Wars: Clone Wars)
  > "Jedi Knights Foul Moudama, Shaak Ti, and the Ithorian, Roron Corobb, make a desperate attempt to keep General Grievous from kidnapping Chancellor Palpatine. Their thrilling chase is filled with incredible battles, amazing acrobatics and lots of close calls. In the end, Foul Moudama falls in battle to General Grievous' lightsabers, but his spirit will live on."
  > ―Hasbro: Foul Moudama Action Figure
  > "The tenacious cyborg general kept hounding the Jedi, eventually confronting them in a duel within the bunker. Grievous used his lightsaber skills and multiple arms to defeat Moudama, Corobb and Ti."

  > "Moudama and Corobb fought well, but Grievous was too strong. He took their lightsabers and the Chancellor and left in his ship. The General wanted the Jedi to know what he'd done. That's the only reason I'm alive."
  > ―Shaak Ti (Star Wars: Reversal of Fortune)

### Force Bellow **(Alter)**:

- Roron Corobb has a unique "Species Ability" that allows him to repulse enemies with a torrent of sonic energy from his powerful four throats in an attack as powerful as any telekinetic burst.
  > "His Ithorian physiology gifted him with multiple mouths and voiceboxes. His powerfully deep voice, when teamed with his mental abilities, gave him remarkable powers of persuasion and command. With extreme exertion, Corobb could couple his powerful multiple-throated shout with a burst of Force telekinesis for a unique ability to repulse enemies with a torrent of sonic energy."
  > ―Star Wars Databanks: Roron Corobb
  > "An Ithorian Jedi Master trained by Yarael Poof, he had two throats which generated a deep guttural language. Combined with his mental skills, his voice could be as powerful as any Force blast."
  > ―The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia
  > "Corobb can combine his powerful multiple-throated shout with a burst of Force telekiensis to release sonic energy."
  > ―Star Wars: Clone Wars Trading Cards

### Force Speed **(Control)**:

- Roron Corobb can move across extremely fast trains, and is capable of contending against opponents that move at a speed near lightspeed.
  > "The electrodrivers that powered Grievous's limbs could move them faster than the human eye can see; when he swung his arm, it and his fist and the lightsaber within it would literally vanish: wiped from existence by sheer mind-numbing speed, an imitation quantum event. No human being could move remotely as fast as Grievous, not even Obi-Wan - but he didn't have to."
  > ―Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith
  > "Three MagnaGuards, each with a double-ended weapon that generated an energy field impervious to lightsabers, each with reflexes that operated near lightspeed, each with hypersophisticated heuristic combat algorithms that enabled it to learn from experience and adapt its tactics instantly to any situation, were certainly beyond Obi-Wan's ability to defeat, but it was not Obi-Wan who would defeat them; Obi-Wan wasn't even fighting. He was only a vessel, emptied of self. The Force, shaped by his skill and guided by his clarity of mind, fought through him."
  > ―Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith
