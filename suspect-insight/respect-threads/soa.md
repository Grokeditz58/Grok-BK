### Force Accolades:

- The massive galactic war between the Galactic Republic and Sith Empire was insignificant next to the might of Soa alone.
  > "You met an incomprehensible power; you may have saved the whole galaxy."
  > ―Shol Bestros (Star Wars: The Old Republic)
  > “A terrible threat has been uncovered on the ice world of Belsavis; a danger so grave that it could make the conflict between the Republic and the Empire irrelevant. Groups of heroes must band together to venture into the ancient and deadly Eternity Vault—where an enemy older than both the Republic and the Empire has been contained… until now.”
  > ―Star Wars: The Old Republic Official Website
  > “The legends say it took an army to stop the Infernal One before the Rakata and the other ancients locked him away with his weapons. If we don’t do anything, though, the war against the Republic may be nothing next to what’ll come.”
  > ―Threnoldt (Star Wars: The Old Republic)
  > "The Rakata civilization was powerful beyond measure; the threats they sought to contain in this prison transcended anything the Republic is currently prepared to face."
  > ―Star Wars: The Old Republic Encyclopedia

- Soa was a galactic-level threat that warrants the attention of the greatest champions of both the Galactic Republic and Sith Empire.
  > "The Rakata known as the Infernal One is a being of terrifying power, threatening both Jedi and Sith alike."
  > ―Star Wars: The Old Republic Encyclopedia
  > “A prison discovered buried in the ice on Belsavis is thought to house an ancient and deadly evil that could threaten the galaxy. To counter this threat, General Threnoldt dispatches Imperial forces to investigate the prison and confront the dangers within.”
  > ―Star Wars: The Old Republic Official Website
  > “Buried deep in the icy mountains of Belsavis, a prison housing an ancient and deadly entity has been uncovered. Both the Republic and the Empire understand the threat that the prison poses, and both sides now send their most powerful heroes to confront and eliminate this powerful foe once and for all.”
  > ―Star Wars: The Old Republic Official Website

- Soa was so powerful that the ancient Rakata and other ancient species were forced to lock him away due to the danger he posed.
  > ‘We uncovered a new section of Belsavis prisoner during a bombing run. It’d been sealed for twenty thousand years, no exit to the surface. Its alien builders, the Rakata, called it the “Eternity Vault.” It’s where they kept the prisoners and technology they could never afford to release.”
  > ―Threnoldt (Star Wars: The Old Republic)
  > “The vault contained the greatest enemies of the ancient Rakata, and was said to be permanently sealed under thousands of tonnes of rock.”
  > ―Shol Bestros (Star Wars: The Old Republic)

### Combat Abilities:

- Soa was an ancient Rakata warlord that conquered a thousand planets - including Korriban.
  > “A sickness of the Force. We uncovered Rakata a carving that shed light on our foe. They speak of a Rakata warlord imprisoned here. “The Infernal One,” who enslaved a thousand worlds; who “drowned the sea of stars in blood.”
  > ―Shol Bestros (Star Wars: The Old Republic)
  > “We've been translating the Rakata inscriptions. Most are indecipherable, but a few refer to a prisoner called "the Infernal One." We've seen the title before, in the Dead Corals of Rugosa and the Ziost Abyss. We thought it a myth—the Rakata warlord who bled and enslaved a thousand planets."
  > ―Threnoldt (Star Wars: The Old Republic)
  > "The great evil sitting at the center of the Eternity Vault is a Force-using Rakata warlord who once brought Korriban to its knees. Now the despot known as "the Infernal One" intends to conquer a galaxy that has forgotten him."
  > ―Star Wars: The Old Republic Encyclopedia

- It required armies of ancients, Rakata and Sith alike, to ultimately overcome and subdue Soa. His strength, however, was still so great that even when captured they could not kill him and were instead forced to lock him away in a vault instead.
  > "The Rakata kept most of their prisoners alive on purpose. The Eternity Vault was for the ones they couldn't kill."
  > ―Shol Bestros (Star Wars: The Old Republic)
  > “The legends say it took an army to stop the Infernal One before the Rakata and the other ancients locked him away with his weapons.”
  > ―Threnoldt (Star Wars: The Old Republic)

- Ultimately, Soa was awakened after twenty thousand years and was defeated by a joint strike force of of "the galaxy's most powerful champions," consisting of the Hero of Tython, Barsen'thor, Voidhound, Meteor, the **Emperor's Wrath II **, Darth Nox, Cipher Nine, and Scree after pressing the team in an extremely difficult fight. It was suggested that the group needed as many allies as possible to complete the mission and that they, obviously, could not do it alone.
  > "Operations... are large-scale multi-group missions where... Republic or Imperial allies... confront the most dangerous threats in the galaxy."
  > ―Star Wars: The Old Republic Official Website
  > “Gather your team, as many allies as you can find, and stop the Infernal One. I fear for our galaxy if he escapes.”
  > ―Shol Bestros (Star Wars: The Old Republic)

### Sith Alchemy** (Alter)**:

- The content in the next section reflects the power(s) of Sith Alchemy, also known as **Force Alchemy.**

- Soa was allegedly the greatest alchemist of the ancient Rakata, creating a abomination named Gharj. Gharj was a massive, near-immortal beast that was both a destroyer and god of civilizations. It has been speculated that the ancient Sith Lords, such as Karness Muur and Exar Kun, attempted to replicate Gharj, but were unable to and rather instead created the inferior terentatek.
  > "Hear it howl, for it was bred to scream, blood and fibers woven into a devouring beast. It was Gharj whose claws scored the canals of Seylott; Gharj who consumed the natives of Alzoc, and was worshiped for it. Gharj whom the alchemists of a younger age sought to emulate, but all would fail. Look upon Gharj, and die."
  > ―Soa (Star Wars: The Old Republic)
  - [image: data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAAAAACH5BAEKAAEALAAAAAABAAEAAAICTAEAOw==]

- Soa created the Annihilation Droid XRR-3 with the Force, warping the machine to where it was said to have destroyed armies of Rakata warriors. The droid, nicknaimed the Silentior, had twenty four distinct weapons that could be utilized in its arsenal.
  > "My finest creation, machine of machines, metal enemies welded together and allied toward destruction. Silentior lived to obliterate the Rakata who opposed me, who did not realize I had risen above them. It decimated armies, and it lives for destruction still."
  > ―Soa (Star Wars: The Old Republic)

### Telepathy** (Alter)**:

- The content in the next section reflects the umbrella title of Telepathy, also known as **Force Fear**, **Mind Trick**, **Mind Control**, **Dominate Mind**, **Force Illusions**, **Force Persuasion**, **Force Confusion**, **Mindform**, **Affect Mind**, and **Alter Mind**.

- Mind Traps are extremely dangerous Rakata devices that can trap a conscious of one into it. It proved to be so successful that even the likes of Revan  himself could not escape one without the aid of another Rakata trapped inside of it. Soa had such influence over these devices that he could instantly create and draw individuals attacking him into nearby Mind Traps, therefore trapping them for theoretically eternity (unless the device is destroyed - in which they were).
  > "Soa uses this ability to create a Mind Trap which seals a player in a psychic prison."
  > ―Star Wars: The Old Republic
  > "The ancient Rakata built mind traps as the ultimate prison. These strange devices leave the victim’s body intact, but draw the psyche into an otherworldly “white room”–a virtual environment created by the mind trap’s power. While inside the white room, the victim does not hunger or age, and his thoughts are not impaired–but he can have no contact with the outside world until another being accesses the device. It is entirely possible for the victim’s body to die and the victim’s psyche to suffer immortality inside a white, featureless void."
  > ―Mind Trap Codex Entry (Star Wars: The Old Republic)
