- Now here's a character from the olden days, back when Marvel had the SW comic rights. Even still though, some interesting characters came about such as Valance here, so let's see what this guy is capable of!

### Link: Imperial Stormtrooper

- ^ To relevant link for the character's background.

### ======

### Sourcebook/Guide Quotes

- (quoted passage)
  > It was while hunting in the Nembus sector that Fud first ran afoul of young stormtrooper commander Beilert Valance. Valance was a cold-blooded warrior, even by the Empire’s standards, known for getting results at all costs. The cunning strategist was at the forefront of the Empire’s efforts to purge the Nembus sector of undesirables — Fud included. Slavers, pirates, and mercs alike didn’t stand a chance against Valance’s well-coordinated and absolute tactics.
  > - Taken from The Not-So Magnficicent 7

- Valance described as a cunning strategist, slavers, pirates and mercs didn't stand a chance against his tactics. A Stormtrooper Commander, he was a cold-blooded warrior, getting results at all costs.

### Comic Scans

This was early on in the GCW, so it probably doesn't apply later on as much, but it's still noteworthy. He fought during the Clone Wars as a grunt, soon enough he rose through the ranks during the Jedi purge and became one of the fiercest Stormtroopers the Empire produced, this before becoming a cyborg.
