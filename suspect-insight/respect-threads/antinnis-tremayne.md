### High Inquisitor Antinnis Tremayne

### Sourcebook Quotes

- (quoted passage)
  > A former Jedi Knight, High Inquisitor Antinnis Tremayne followed a similar path to Darth Vader's, being seduced by the dark side and serving the Emperor hunting down and destroying other Jedi.
  > - Taken from Dark Side Sourcebook

- He was a former Jedi Knight  before becoming an Inquisitor under the Empire.
  - [image: https://comicvine1.cbsistatic.com/uploads/original/12/127594/5634010-d.png]

- He can absorb/dissipate energy, accelerate healing, control pain, hibernation trance, reduce injury, resist stun, projective TP, among other things as shown and what he learned as a Jedi Knight of course.
  > Imperial Intelligence calls so rarely on the Inquisitors that Tremayne has a great deal of free time with which to search for Force Users. He conducts most of these searches in the Outer Rim Territories, where isolated villages produce enough Force adepts, village shamans, dark side devotees and alien students of the Force to keep Tremayne busy for several lifetimes.
  > - Taken from the Dark Side Sourcebook

- He searches for Force Users, such as Force Adepts and Dark Side Devotees and others. Taking note, that regular Dark Jedi and Devotees are pretty powerful themselves as shown.
