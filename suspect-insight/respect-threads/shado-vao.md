- Lightsaber Skill

- As of Star Wars Legacy: Claws of the Dragon, Shado had supposedly not yet found his equal in skill with a lightsaber blade. While this may not be totally accurate, accounting for Shado's contemporaries who he has fought with or against - Cade Skywalker, Kol Skywalker, Darth Talon  and Wolf Sazen - this is still a considerable accolade.
  > "Kol Skywalker's former padawan, Shado has honed his martial skills and has yet to find his equal with a lightsaber."

- --Star Wars: Legacy #0.5

- As young padawans, Shado Vao and Cade Skywalker cut through a group of Sith during the massacre at Ossus.
  - [image: https://comicvine.gamespot.com/a/uploads/scale_small/11111/111114474/4571433-killing%20sith.jpg]

- For years after the Massacre at Ossus, Shado Vao and Wolf Sazen were ambushed by Sith Assassins and repeatedly repelled and defeated them, even when outnumbered.
  > Years later, he joins Master Sazen in a search for Sazen's former padawan, and Shado's childhood friend, Cade Skywalker. Ensuing adventures and skirmishes with the Sith give Shado Vao many opportunities to display his prowess with the double-bladed lightsaber.
