- Edge of the Empire

- According to Sith record, Vodal was the greatest alchemist:
  > "One thousand years ago the planet Athiss held the sanctum of Vodal Kressh, greatest of the Sith alchemists."
  - Source (as named in the post's quote header): Darth Malgus, Star Wars the Old Republic wrote:

- When Vodal died, the Sith Emperor ordered the entire planet a forbidden zone:
  > "When he died, the Emperor declared Athiss forbidden."
  - Source (as named in the post's quote header): Darth Malgus, Star Wars the Old Republic wrote:

- Grand Master of the Jedi Order Satele Shan, ponders Vodal must've been a serious threat for the Emperor to take such action against an entire world:
  > "Kressh must've been a considerable threat if the Emperor's solution was to leave the planet."
  - Source (as named in the post's quote header): Satele Shan, Star Wars the Old Republic wrote:
  > A millennium ago the planet Athiss was a Sith sanctuary that held the sanctuary of Vodal Kressh, a powerful Sith Lord and among the greatest alchemists the Empire had ever known. Vodal was a powerful and ambitious Sith, but he was also utterly mad. So much so that after his death the Emperor ordered Kressh's sanctum sealed, and the planet declared a forbidden zone. Now something stirs on the planet's surface once again.
  - Source (as named in the post's quote header): Holonet, swtor.com wrote:

- A young Master Chamma visited Athiss a few centuries later, by then the planet was covered with an oppressive cloud. Worse, Chamma fought a spiritual Sith entity whilst there. The trauma of this experience lasted a century:
  > But the Jedi Archives tell a grim story. The Jedi Master Chamma visited Athiss early in his career and clashed with a dark side entity there. The duel, and the oppressive feeling of evil on Athiss’s surface, drove Chamma close to the dark side of the Force. Shaken by the experience, he went into a self-imposed exile; it was almost a century before he came to terms with what he had seen and felt on Athiss, and was able to return to the order. The many ruins on Athiss have never been properly studied. They resemble the architecture of the ancient Sith Empire, but their purpose is mysterious. A few records of Athiss survive in the libraries of the Citadel, but contemporary Sith scholars are forbidden from studying them, on the personal order of the Emperor.
  - Source (as named in the post's quote header): Athiss Codex Entry wrote:

- Due to a Republic survey team, Vodal's sanctum was breached and his spirit awakened which caused a powerful disturbance in the Force felt by the Jedi Council:
  > "They awakened something whilst uncovering ancient Sith artifacts. The dark-side grows strong within those Sith ruins and now the Jedi Council senses a profound disturbance in the Force there."
  - Source (as named in the post's quote header): Satele Shan, Star Wars the Old Republic wrote:

- Satele states that Vodal is a threat unlike anything Act 1 Barsen'thor has yet faced, which would include Lord Vivicar:
  > "There is a powerful dark-side threat that must be stopped, this mission is unlike anything you've previously encountered."
  - Source (as named in the post's quote header): Satele Shan, Star Wars the Old Republic wrote:

- Malgus says Vodal's spirit endangers the entire Sith Empire:
  > "The Sith Order's past has resurfaced and we are all in danger."
  - Source (as named in the post's quote header): Darth Malgus, Star Wars the Old Republic wrote:
  > A Republic archaeological team discovered the planet while on a survey mission, and began to excavate the ancient Sith ruins. During their study of the ruins the strike team has discovered something powerful, feeding on the Dark Side power that emanates from the ruins. Now both the Republic and the Empire are scrambling together strike teams to make their way to the planet, confront the threat and make sure that it never leaves the abandoned world.
  - Source (as named in the post's quote header): Holonet, swtor.com wrote:

- The Prophet encountered within states that the strike team's power is nothing next to Vodal's own power:
  > "This was his place of power, you are nothing here!"
  - Source (as named in the post's quote header): The Prophet of Vodal, Star Wars the Old Republic wrote:

- The defeat of Vodal's spirit ends the disturbance in the Force:
  > "The Council no longer feels any disturbance in the Force coming from that world."
  - Source (as named in the post's quote header): Satele Shan, Star Wars the Old Republic wrote:
  > "My augurs felt the final destruction of Vodal's spirit."
  - Source (as named in the post's quote header): Darth Malgus, Star Wars the Old Republic wrote:
