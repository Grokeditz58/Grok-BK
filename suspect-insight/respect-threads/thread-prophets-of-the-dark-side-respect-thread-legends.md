### High Prophet Merili

### Sourcebook/Guide Quotes

- (quoted passage)
  > She's of medium height with an attractive figure that's usually hidden beneath tattered robes. Her robes cover the reptile-scale armor indigenous to her homeworld of Dathomir. A poisonous dagger hides within a sheath under her upper right arm and her walking staff is disguised as a power weapon.
  > - Taken from Polyhedron 103

- Carries a poisonous dagger and a powered weapon disguised as a walking staff.
  > She only resorts to physical battle if her life is endangered. She prefers to let her Force wraiths and controlled Wookiees to fight for her. Merili is one of the witches of Dathomir, who impressed Kadann with her connection to the Dark Side.
  > - Taken from Polyhedron 103

- She is able to control Wookiees and Force Wraiths to fight for her, also being a Nightsister of Dathomir, impressing Supreme Prophet Kadann.
  > Instead she serves as the keeper of the wookiee homeworld Kashyyyk, a position granted to her by Emperor Palpatine. Her desire to remain on Kashyyyk is the only thing protecting the galaxy from her terrible powers and insane ambitions.
  > - Taken from Polyhedron 103

- Keeper of Kashyyyk, which is the only thing that keeps her from using her terrible powers that she has upon the galaxy.
  > With 5 Star Destroyers and nine garrisons under her command. Merili holds Kashyyyk and its star system in an iron grip. Her powers and the horrific monsters she calls forth from the Dark side keeps the wookiees in line. She also has three adult rancors who serve as her guards and constant companions.
  > - Taken from Polyhedron 103

- She holds Kashyyyk and its star system with her powers of the Force and Imperial resources, able to call horrific monsters from the dark side and having 3 full grown rancors as guards.
  > Created by the power of the Dark Side, Force Wraiths are semi-corporeal, nonsentiant shadowy things that obey the commands of their creator. Both Merili and Jedgar know the secret for calling forth Force Wraiths. Merili's Force Wraiths are huge, standing nearly 4 meters tall, they resemble dark, nightmare versions of wookiees that are fearsome and devastating in combat.
  > - Taken from Polyhedron 104

- Merili's Force Wraiths stand near 4 meters tall, resembling wookiees and are fearsome/devastating combatants.
  > Cronal spent many years with them on Dromund Kaas, perfecting his aptitude for Darksight and passing it on to the raven-haired Nightsister and prophetess Merili, whom he considered his greatest pupil. Inculcated by Cronal with such mania, Merili amalgamated this skill with learnings in Aing-Tii flow-walking, Dathomirian Heartshadow and her own dark side deconstructions of spacetime — superseding her guru’s own proficiency notwithstanding her being driven supertemporally insane.
  > - Taken from The Imperial Warlords Despoilers of the Empire

- Was trained by Cronal in the use of Darksight, combining it with Flow Walking.

  > - Taken from Polyhedron 103

- Though her power doesn't match Kadann's or Jedgar's, she's considered the most dangerous Prophet because she's insane.

### High Prophet Jedgar

### Sourcebook/Guide Quotes

- (quoted passage)
  > Tall, bald, and bearded, Jedgar was the true High Prophet of the Dark Side. As a youth studying at the Jedi Temple on Coruscant, he was troubled by dark dreams that had a frightening habit of coming to pass. He had nightmares of the Clone Wars and the Jedi Purge years before either event occurred. Though filled with a talent for seeing the future, Jedgar frightened the other students and could find no Jedi Knight to accept him as an apprentice. He was assigned to the Agri-Corps on his thirteenth birthday, but refused to accept such a menial occupation. Jedgar ran away and followed his dreams to the dark side.
  > - Taken from the Dark Side Sourcebook

- He used to be a Jedi in training, having a talent for seeing things that were to come including the Clone Wars and Jedi Purge, he frightened other students and no one would take him, so he fled and followed to the dark side.
  > Years later, Jedgar's visions led him to Kadann, a Jedi Knight who also left the Order behind. "I foresaw this meeting," Kadann told the young man. "So did I," replied Jedgar.
  > - Taken from the Dark Side Sourcebook

- Jedgar foresaw his meeting with Kadann through his visions.
  - [image: https://comicvine1.cbsistatic.com/uploads/original/12/127594/5368510-s.png]

- Knowing Affect Mind, Battlemind, Drain Energy, Grip, Illusions, TK, Enhance his Abilities, Empathy, Fear, Grip, See Force, having a lightsaber and dark side talisman.
  > Jedgar relishes in his Dark Side abilities and uses them often. His favorite tactic is to create armies of Force Wraiths to send against his enemies, though he isn't opposed to drawing his lightsaber when the chance arises.
  > - Taken from Polyhedron 103

- He uses his powers often. His favorite ability is creating armies of Force Wraiths against his enemies, but he also isn't afraid to use his lightsaber.
  > Created by the power of the Dark Side, Force Wraiths are semi-corporeal, nonsentiant shadowy things that obey the commands of their creator. Both Merili and Jedgar know the secret for calling forth Force Wraiths. Jedgar's are smaller, insectoid things, roughly two meters tall. He uses them as spies, and even warriors when he needs combat fodder.
  > - Taken from Polyhedron 104

- Able to create Force Wraiths like Merili, though his are smaller, using them as spies but he can use them as warriors.
  > Warkin is a huge, unkept Wookiee. The mad Wookiee willingly serves the Dark Side and High Prophet Jedgar, Warkin uses his claws in combat. As a young Wookiee, Warkin found his great strength(impressive even among wookiees) often gave him advantages and power over others. The mad wookiee is not Force Sensitive, but his personality and actions were such that the Dark Side 'noticed' and offered him even more power.
  > - Taken from Polyhedron 104

- Jedgar has a mad Wookiee under his command, this Wookiee having great strength even among his own species, though not Force Sensitive, the Dark Side grants him power.

### Supreme Prophet Kadann

### Sourcebook/Guide Quotes

- (quoted passage)
  > The true Supreme Prophet of the Dark Side, Kadann was a black-bearded dwarf who wore dark robes tha appeared to be cut from the fabric of space itself, with stars gleaming in its inky depths. During the time of the Republic, Kadann was a Jedi Knight. More of a thinker and philosopher than a man of action, Kadann strove to understand the ancient prophecies that concerned the balance of the Force.
  > To these ends, Kadann struggled to reconcile balance with adhering to only the light side of the Force. He came to believe that dark and light in equal measures were needed to truly master and understand the Force.
  > - Taken from the Dark Side Sourcebook

- Former Jedi Knight, striving to understand the balance of the Force, coming to believe both sides were needed mastery to truly understand it.
  > In time, he turned away from the Jedi Code and started to experiment with dark side powers. While his intentions may have been good, Kadann still managed to stray onto the path of corruption. He traveled the galaxy, exploring his theories and meditating on the dark side.
  > - Taken from the Dark Side Sourcebook

- Was eventually corrupted, traveling the galaxy, exploring theories and meditating on the dark side.
  > When Kadann looked deep into the dark side prior to the Battle of Endor, he saw a myriad of possibilities. One of the strongest was the return of balance to the Force and the end of the Empire.
  > - Taken from the Dark Side Sourcebook

- He saw the end of the Empire being the strongest of possibilities at the Battle of Endor.
  - [image: https://comicvine1.cbsistatic.com/uploads/original/12/127594/5368556-hty.png]

- Has a lightsaber and dark side talisman. Able to heal himself and others, TK, See Force, TP, TK, Grip, Proficent with a lightsaber and blasters and simple weapons. Force Mastery, Dissipate Energy and Force Mind.
  > The most ambitious of the Prophets, Jedgar wants power. Unlike Kadann, he wants power now and isn't content to wait for it. He hates the fact Kadann is much more powerful than he is, but he's too afraid to openly oppose the Supreme Prophet.
  > - Taken from Polyhedron 103

- Jedgar is the most ambitious, he wants power and doesn't want to wait, but he's too afraid to oppose Kadann who is much more powerful than he is.
  > Kadann isn't a villain to be fought, but rather outmaneuvered. He rarely even puts on a display of power and never stoops to using Force Lighting the way the Emperor did. He could destroy those who oppose him in an instant, but usually simply leaves if violence is used against him.
  > - Taken from Polyhedron 103

- He isn't one to be fought, can also use Force Lighting and could destroy those who oppose him in an instant, but usually just leaves if there's violence against him.
  > The mysterious darksider called Azrakel was an experiment born in the mind of the Emperor and given life by the High Prophet Kadann. In secret and with the raw power of the dark side, Kadann recreated Azrakel. He trained the young man as he had been taught, providing him with the mentoring necessary to harness his power and strengthen his abilities. As Azrakel, the young man grew strong in body and in the Force. He became Kadann's secret weapon, his tool for performing covert acts that increased his own power and prestige.
  > - Taken from the Dark Side Sourcebok

- He trained Azrakel, a mysterious darksider, training him to harness his power and strengthen his abilites, becoming a secret weapon.
