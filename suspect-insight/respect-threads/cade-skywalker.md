- The content in the next section reflects Cade Skywalker's immense skill in the rare art of Dark Transfer. In unison with Shatterpoint, he can use the power to achieve **Force Healing** or kill them via their weak spots.

- Cade Skywalker's healing powers are immensely powerful, and greater than anyone Luke Skywalker has ever known (which includes galactic entities such as Abeloth).
