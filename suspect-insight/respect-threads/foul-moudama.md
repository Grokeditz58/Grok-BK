### Combat Abilities:

- Foul Moudama and his Jedi allies held their own against General Grievous and his MagnaGuards. Master Moudama defended against Grievous and his guards with merely one arm before ultimately succumbing alongside Jedi Master**Roron Corobb ** due to Grievous using all four of his lightsabers at once.
  > "Jedi Knights Foul Moudama, Shaak Ti, and the Ithorian, Roron Corobb, make a desperate attempt to keep General Grievous from kidnapping Chancellor Palpatine. Their thrilling chase is filled with incredible battles, amazing acrobatics and lots of close calls. In the end, Foul Moudama falls in battle to General Grievous' lightsabers, but his spirit will live on."
  > ―Hasbro: Foul Moudama Action Figure
  > "The tenacious cyborg general kept hounding the Jedi, eventually confronting them in a duel within the bunker. Grievous used his lightsaber skills and multiple arms to defeat Moudama, Corobb and Ti."
  > ―Star Wars Databanks: Foul Moudama
  > "Moudama and Corobb fought well, but Grievous was too strong. He took their lightsabers and the Chancellor and left in his ship. The General wanted the Jedi to know what he'd done. That's the only reason I'm alive."
  > ―Shaak Ti (Star Wars: Reversal of Fortune)

He was only a vessel, emptied of self. The Force, shaped by his skill and guided by his clarity of mind, fought through him."

### Species Abilities:

- The content in the next section reflects to species traits that resemble the Sense powers of **Force Sight**.

- Foul Moudama, as a Talz, had four eyes that allowed him to see through blinding light or total darkness with great accuracy.
  > "Their four eyes - two large and two small - are used for seeing at different times of the day. The small eyes are used for daylight vision, as the bright sunlight reflecting off the snow can be almost blinding. The larger eyes are used during moonless nights, hence the ability to see in the dark."
  > ―Star Wars: The New Essential Guide to Alien Species
  > "Their four eyes are said to be a protective evolutionary development; the larger eyes close to protect against the sunlight's glare off the ice. At night, all four eyes allow the Talz exceptional night vision."
  > ―Star Wars Databanks: Talz
  > "Their two sets of distinctive eyes allow them to see at all times - one set works in bright light, the other in total darkness."
  > ―Star Wars: The Force Unleashed Campaign Guide
  > "Their four specialized eyes allow for perfect vision even during the blinding daylight hours."
  > ―Star Wars: Essential Guide to Planets and Moons
  > "He had a keen visual acuity, stemming from his four eyes."
  > ―Star Wars Databanks: Foul Moudama

### Physical Attributes:

- Foul Moudama is so physically powerful that he carried Chancellor Palpatine, who weighed 165 pounds, while combating the legendary Jedi killer General Grievous and his MagnaGuards or jumping across the vast landscape of Coruscant.
  > "The surviving Nemodian pilot was screaming orders for the droids to magnetize, then started howling that the ship was breaking up, and managed to make so much annoying noise that Grievous smashed his skull out of simple irritation. Then he looked around and realized he'd just killed the last of his crew: all the bridge crew he hadn't slain personally had sucked up the bulk of the random blaster ricochets. Grievous shook the pilot's brains off his fist. Disgusting creatures, Neimoidians."
  > ―Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith
  - [image: data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAAAAACH5BAEKAAEALAAAAAABAAEAAAICTAEAOw==]

- Foul Moudama, as a Talz, was enormous in size and build. He towered over two meters tall, and could use his sharp claws to dig through frozen ice.
  > "The Talz are a tall, physically powerful species from Alzoc III, a frigid world blanketed with fields of snow and ice. Made for their environment, Talz are covered from head to foot with thick, white fur, and their extremely large hands sport sharp-clawed talons that are used for digging through frozen ice and snow. Their fur also protects them from the cold, as does an extra layer of blubber lying beneath their hair."
  > ―Star Wars: The New Essential Guide to Alien Species
  > "Alzoc III is a cold, frozen planet but the Talz have adapted to the harsh climate... Although fierce in appearance and immense in build, the Talz are a quiet, gentle species of primitive, non-violent people who are diligent and tireless in their work. But until that time they will use their great strength as slaves to the empire. It is not unlikely that some of those who are exposed to the galaxy will have developed personalities similar to those of the Wookiees, who they so greatly resemble."
  > ―Star Wars: Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races
  > "Large and strong, these white-furred beings were from the planets Alzoc III and Orto Plutonia."
  > ―The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia
  > "Alzoc III is the frozen homeworld of the Talz. Standing nearly as tall as Wookiees, Talz are covered with shaggy white pelts and speak their buzzing language through a flexible proboscis. When the Empire discovered the out-of-the-way ice world, it realized that the Talz-massive but meek-would make excellent slave laborers."
  > ―Star Wars: Essential Guide to Planets and Moons
  > "Despite his large size and muscular build, he was quite agile."
  > ―Star Wars Databanks: Foul Moudama
