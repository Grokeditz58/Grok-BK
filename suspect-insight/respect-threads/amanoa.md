- Battle Meditation

- Battle Meditation is a power which allows the user to boost the morale of any number of individuals, from a small group to entire armies, and also allows the user to sap the will of an opposing force of equal size, making it a favoured battlefield tactic among Force-users. They do this by visualizing the desired result of a conflict within their minds, and make it a reality by projecting it into the minds of those they desire to affect.

- Amanoa saps the will of the Onderonian beast riders during the Onderon civil war, pushing the conflict decisively in the favour of her own forces. That is, until Jedi Master Arca Jeth overpowers her battle meditation with his own.

### -Tales of the Jedi Companion

- Before we close up I want to clear up a quick misconception - when the text talks about Arca Jeth breaking Freedon Nadd's hold on Amanoa, and disrupting his tomb, it doesn't mean that Arca actually did anything to attack Nadd, or visa versa. There was no confrontation between them. All that happened, was when Arca and the Jedi took the city and cornered Amanoa, Nadd took his power away from Amanoa, leaving her completely powerless, and as a result, dead, because she also had nothing to sustain her physical form. Arca himself notes in the comic that he wasn't responsible for Amanoa's death, but rather Nadd's power had left her being.

- Arca then believes that he has defeated Nadd's spirit, claiming "his light is stronger", which makes sense, because it would seem like Nadd is retreating from him now that the conduit for his power (Amanoa) has been defeated. However, as we find out later, Nadd was able to empower another more capable apprentice in the form of King Ommin , who handily overpowers Arca on two occasions. There really isn't any parity between the power of Arca and Nadd, despite how deceiving the text can be.
