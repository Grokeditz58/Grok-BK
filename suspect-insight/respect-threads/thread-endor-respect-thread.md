### Logray: The Medicine Man

- The once great medicine man and Shaman of Bright Tree Village, the powerful Logray was banished for his use of dark rituals and living sacrifices.

- POWER

- The Leaf Queen thinks Logray can save them from planetary winter caused by an artifact known as the Season Scepter, which transformed mortal travelers into gods of the seasons.
  > The Leaf Queen thinks Logray can save them from planetary winter caused by an artifact known as the Season Scepter, which transformed mortal travelers into gods of the seasons.
  > “Logray, come quickly! You must save us from these terrible snows! Please hurry to the Sunking’s palace, the future of Endor is in your hands!”
  > -The Leaf Queen (Ewoks Season 2: The Season Scepter)

- Logray’s power awe his people, despite Shamans being an old and common facet of Ewok culture.

  > Star Wars Fact File 17, EWO2"Respected for his great wisdom and his extensive knowledge of magic and ritual, Logray was highly suspicious of outsiders. As such, he was particularly opposed to the imperial presence on Endor."
