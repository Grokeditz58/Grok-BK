### Sourcebook/Encyclopedia Quotes

- Knows Affect Mind/Empathy, Force Healing, Force Lighting, Alter/Control, proficent with blaster pistols and simple weapons.
  > He was one of the few handful of Imperials who tried to infiltrate Luke Skywalker's Jedi Academy on Yavin 4. Unlike the others, Brakiss had a true talent for The Force.
  > - Taken from Complete Encyclopedia

- Of the handful of Imperial spies sent to infiltrate Luke's academy, Brakiss showed true talent.
