### Respect Coleman Trebor

- **Coleman Trebor** was a male Vurk jedi master serving on the Jedi Council after the death of Jedi master Yarael Poof and before the beginning of the Clone Wars. He was among the 200 Jedi strike team that participated in the Battle of Geonosis to rescue Obi Wan Kenobi, Padme Amidala, and Anakin Skywalker. Spotting an opening on a distracted Count Dooku Coleman took the Sith Lord by suprise and attempted to put an end to the former Jedi but failed to notice the bounty hunter Jango Fett who quickly dealt with the master Jedi rather easily
  - [image: https://i.servimg.com/u/f77/20/26/17/18/dooku10.jpg]

- Trebor's confrontation with the infamous Count Dooku
