### Combat Abilities:

- Calo Nord killed every bounty hunter that came after him to such a degree that they ultimately cancelled his bounty, deeming him too risky to quarrel with. He then went and personally killed the numerous bounty hunter parties that put the bounty on his head, eventually becoming the greatest of all the bounty hunters.
  > "This ghastly string of killings made him a wanted man. Nord, an uneducated, self-taught survivalist, fled to the Outer Rim and systematically eliminated every bounty hunter that tracked him down, coldly observing their methods and adapting their skills like a murderous idiot savant. When the hunters’ guild officially deemed Nord too risky a quarry and cancelled his bounty, Nord tracked down the parties who had posted the reward and killed them too."
  > ―So Uncivilized: Great Gunslingers in Star Wars
  > "He toyed with the bounty hunters who came after him, learning all their tricks and techniques before dispatching them with ruthless efficiency. Eventually the attempts on his life stopped, though not before dozens had failed in their efforts to collect the small fortune resting on his head. Those responsible for placing the bounty on him were the next to feel Calo's wrath."
  > ―Star Wars Databanks: Calo Nord
  > "A bounty was placed on his head, and Nord killed every hunter who came after him. He became a bounty hunter himself, and, in a matter of years, his name was the most feared in the Outer Rim. No one ever disputed the widely held belief that he silenced more people than the Iridian Plague. Rumor had it that Nord was killed on Tatooine, but his legacy cast a wide shadow over the profession of bounty hunting. Centuries later, the Guild continues to have a difficult time convincing civilians that not all hunters are Calo Nord."
  > ―Boba Fett (Star Wars: The Bounty Hunter Code)
  > "Nord was determined not to be captured, and learned everything he could of the tactics used by the county hunters who pursued him before he killed them all in turn. Eventually, he discovered who had placed the bounty on his head, and executed the man as well."
  > ―The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia

- Calo Nord's prowess was so great that no one ever escaped his grasp, resulting in the creation of an urban legend itself. He collected the bounties of numerous criminals, many who feared the 'myth' of his existence.
  > "Many criminals believed that his existence was a tall told told to frighten criminals away from the lives of crime; this stemmed from the fact very few beings ever escaped to testify his prowess."
  > ―The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia
  > "With each bounty he collected, his reputation grew. There are those who claim Calo is nothing but a myth, a story created to frighten would-be criminals. But the victims staring into his unwavering predator’s gaze could testify that he is all too real … if any of them were still alive."
  > ―Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic Prima Guide

- Calo Nord single-handily defeated a rancor, jumping on its back and throwing a frag grenade down in its mouth. His strength shown in this battle was such that the rancor failed to shake him off his neck.
  > "This datapad contains a record of Calo Nord's trophy kills, sentient and otherwise. The last entry is an account of a recent Rancor hunt: What the Rancor lacked in intelligence it made up for in size, strength and ferocity. Conventional blasters were all but useless against its impenetrable hide, and I was forced to resort to more unorthodox methods to bring the beast down. Some well placed mines near a watering hole were the first step, their explosions herding the creature into a dead-end canyone where I lay in wait, perched among the rocks. As soon as the Rancor was below me, I dropped down onto its massive neck. It reared back, bucking and clawing in an effort to dislodge me, but I had the perfect position. Unable to shake me, the monster opened its mouth in an outraged howl, and I was able to flip several frag grenades down its gaping maw. The explosion blew me clear of the shredded corpse, but luckily the head was still intact. If I take it to Taris, I'm sure Davik will mount it in his trophy room along with the other heads I have collected for him. Davik's also expressed interest in hiring me. I may take him up on his offer, even though I still have yet to claim the greatest hunting trophy of all: a pearl from one of the Krayt Dragons of Tatooine."
  > ―Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic

- Calo Nord killed an Rodian assassin and his partners with quick efficiency. He then went on to kill three members of the Black Vulkar swoop gang via a thermal detonator. Neither opponents stood a chance.
  > "While on Taris in the last year of the Jedi Civil War, Nord killed the Rodian assassin Luugro and his partner, as well as three rowdy members of the Black Vulkars swoop gang in Javyar’s Cantina who harassed him about his diminutive height."

### Armor & Arsenal:

- **Calo Nord's Battle Armor: **This armor was based on heavily modified Mandalorian designs. The maker was killed to appease Nord's ego, ensuring his suit would forever be unique. The armor itself proved to be nearly indestructible, allowing him to survive after an enormous chunk of rubble collapsed on top of him during the Sith bombardment of Taris. For more information, check out the "Durability" section.
  > "Mandalorians are among the most feared combatants in the galaxy, and the quality of their gear may be why. Even the lowliest warrior is a threat in such protective armor."
  > ―Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic
  - [image: data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAAAAACH5BAEKAAEALAAAAAABAAEAAAICTAEAOw==]

- **Mandalorian Heavy Blasters: **Calo Nord's weapon of choice were dual Mandalorian heavy blasters. These weapons were among the finest in the galaxy, and had similar capabilities to Cassus Fett's infamous blaster pistol. Calo Nord used them to quickly cut down two armored Rodians on Taris. He wielded them also during his two encounters with Revan.
  > "Mandalorians boast that a shot from one of these pistols can take a starship out of commission."
  > ―Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic
  - [image: data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAAAAACH5BAEKAAEALAAAAAABAAEAAAICTAEAOw==]

- **Grenades: **Calo Nord carries around dozens of grenades in his arsenal. His **frag grenades **were so great that it completely obliterated a rancor from the inside. The explosion itself threw Calo Nord away from the impact. His **thermal detonators **were capable of killing three members of the Black Vulkar swoop gang before they could react, and his **stun grenades** were used to capture hostages alive.
  > "This Republic device contains a baradium compound that produces a small fusion energy explosion of great force. Civilian possession of these items is outlawed almost everywhere."
  > ―Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic
  - [image: data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAAAAACH5BAEKAAEALAAAAAABAAEAAAICTAEAOw==]

- **Verpine Prototype Shield:** An experimental forearm-mounted personal shield generator in use around the Jedi Civil War, it blocked out energy, sonic, cold, heat, and electrical effects. Calo Nord ensured he had many of these at his disposal at all times - especially before a battle. The assassin droid HK-47 later made note that "lightsabers, while powerful, have trouble penetrating most military issue energy shields."
  > "Though manufactured by the Verpine, these forearm shields are based on highly modified Arkanian designs. They are must-have items for the professional soldier."
  > ―Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic

### d20 Character Summary:

- (quoted passage)
  > "Calo Nord is a ruthless bounty hunter, known to work at various times for the Sith, the Republic, and finally for the Exchange. Thought originally to have been a slave, rumors suggest that he killed his original owners, his parents who had sold him, the bounty hunters who came after him, and even the slavers. He learns to become a lethal bounty hunter in his own right. Cold, menacing, and unapproachable, Nord fears no one. Ultimately, it takes a person of equal power to defeat him, as Revan does when the bounty hunter tries to prevent his escape from Taris in the Ebon Hawk."
  > ―Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide
