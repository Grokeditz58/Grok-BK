## Bastila (Cheth, P25/26)
### Rebuttals
- Malgus beat Kao only late, while enraged and with Kao exhausted.
- Satele "could still hold him onehanded while tearing apart a large tree".
- The pillar-shattering was a Force scream driven by rage at Eleena being hurt.
- Insider quotes: "But he felt no pain. He felt only anger. Hate" (wounds may have amped him), and "He was baiting him, Malgus realised. Feigning weakness."
- The buildings dropped on Malgus were already ruined.
- "in an empire of countless sith that still could mean there are dozens if not hundreds in the same category".
- **Growth was modest:** Zallow beat Adraas in a single Force attack, and the first Malgus–Adraas encounter was only "testing… in a pure blade lock".

### Bastila's case
- She was emotionally hindered: "Every time I try to call on all my teachings to calm myself, they fail me."
- She was described as a Jedi exemplar:
  - KOTOR Campaign Guide: "Maybe the most heroic Jedi of her time, Bastila Shan was a Jedi exemplar."
  - Champions of the Force: "A brash, impulsive, and powerful Jedi".
  - SWTOR Codex: Satele is descended from "Bastila Shan, whose battle meditation skills were peerless".
  - KOTOR prima guide: "Even in the dark halls where the Sith Lords gather, young Bastila is whispered to be a J[edi]…"
- The KOTOR era is the Jedi "prime": "Even more so than in the Clone Wars, these are the days of the Jedi in their prime."
- She escaped Darth Bandon plus his boarding party. Bandon was the Shadow Hand, "second only to" Malak, and above Uthar Wynn.
- **She held off Malak in sabers** long enough for Revan and Carth to escape (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLjd0XNsa4A at ~9 min). Yuthara: "Malak is the strongest of us, and the strongest always rules."

### Summary bullets (verbatim excerpts)
- "Malgus is physically stronger. But Bastila has fought stronger opponents before."
- "Bastila tanked Malak’s lightning for days."
- "Bastila held off Malak in a duel (despite him being superior)… All this is just a heavily hindered LS Bastila."

## Neutral assessment
- Bastila's case relies on statements about her era and on the Malak hold-off. Malgus's case is about physicality and growth.
- BoD's opener is lost, so this record is one-sided. The debate was unfinished.
