## Exar Kun (AncientPower, P5 opener ~12.6k)
### Krayt underperforms
- The big growth quote comes from Krayt himself: "No other source corroborates that his power has increased this drastically".
- Krayt was always seeking "to restore his lost power". His body was failing from Vong parasites:
  - "Cade's escape has left the Emperor weakened"
  - Muur calls his body a "failing husk".
- Krayt says he drove his consciousness "deep within myself" to heal, so the rebirth is a restoration, not a true resurrection.

### Muur and Xoxaan vs Krayt
- Ostrander quote, discussed in SI thread t2866 ("ostrander-karness-muur-vong-krayt"): Muur is "perhaps stronger" than "any other [Sith] we've seen thus far".
- Muur believes he can heal and restore Krayt to power and still control him as a host.
- Krayt "knelt at her [Xoxaan's] feet for twenty years".

### The Exiles' standing
- The Jedi Exiles "cow[ed]" Palpatine in 0 ABY on Korriban: "Droga rescued his cowed master".
- Naga Sadow is the product of thousands of years of Exile breeding. "the apprentice [Nadd] became far more powerful than the teacher".
- Kun destroyed Nadd's spirit.
- Ragnos fogged the minds of Kun and Ulic.
- Kun is stated to be more powerful than Ragnos: "Once the most powerful and dangerous of the Dark Lords of the Sith".

### Chain (verbatim)
