## Ezra (BoD, P35)
### Section 1: lightsaber skill
- Blocks several blows from the Grand Inquisitor in S1 ("Second only to Darth Vader, the Grand Inquisitor was himself a Jedi expert", starwars.com, Star Wars 101).
- Holds off the Seventh Sister and Fifth Brother in S2. The Fifth Brother matched Zeb in strength.
- He is one of 4 beings who matched S2 Kanan in sabers, alongside Ahsoka, Maul and Vader: "There’s no such thing as a tie. You lose, you die." (Twilight of the Apprentice).
- Gains an edge over the Seventh Sister on Malachor, though emotionally charged.
- "The Jedi are growing in their power." / "It will be their undoing." (Fifth Brother and Vader, Shroud of Darkness).

### Section 2: Force power
- "remarkable strength with the Force" (starwars.com).
- "I feel his abilities are growing faster than I can teach him." (Kanan to Yoda, Path of the Jedi)
- Untrained, he sends Kallus flying. In S2 he stops Hondo on a cart moving fast enough to blur.
- **S4 finale:** demolishes the Lothal gateway and throws the debris one-handed while trapped and in pain (with a size comparison against Sidious).

### Section 3: lack of formal training is an asset
- His blaster-lightsaber hybrid.
- Using animals (convor, Loth-wolves).
- Dominating creatures with the Force.
