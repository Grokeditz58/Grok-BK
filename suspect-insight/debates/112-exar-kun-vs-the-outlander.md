## Exar Kun (AncientPower, P2)
- Header: "THOUGH UNABLE TO DEFEAT HIM ENTIRELY, THE JEDI WERE ABLE TO IMPRISON EXAR KUN'S SPIRIT IN THE TEMPLES ON YAVIN-FOUR."

### SoR power levels
- Revan's Yavin device: "Capable of killing every living thing on this moon, as fuel for the Emperor's resurrection."
- "Awakened Vitiate isn't strong enough to wipe Yavin IV of all life and must restore his power on Ziost".
- Nathema ritual: "the ritual on Nathema took days, if not weeks. And the Emperor had to trick hundreds of other powerful Sith… 'He's stronger now,' Revan said."

### Kun's ritual
- Per SWTOR material, Kun "use[d] a ritual to absorb whole armies so that his spirit could withstand the Wall of Light". The ritual left the world in "smoldering ruins" with no life "left to give". This puts Kun's ritual in the same category as Tenebrae's ascension.
- Revan to the HoT: "You barely managed to drive me away last time." Revan is "many times more powerful… than the Hero of Tython at this point".

### Ossus
- TOR states that Ossus was directly hit by the Cron supernova. It survived because of the life force of "countless" generations of Jedi.
- Ood Bnar channeled that same force against Kun (original comic scan: servimg 20064972/527).

### Stats chain
- Pre-Yavin Kun ~ Malgus (both "a 10 in Force power"). Kun has superior speed and strength.
- Jedi Under Siege Malgus is "possibly 'a bit' off of prime Arcann's raw power".
- Redeemed Arcann is "majorly above" SoR HoT.
- **Conclusion:** "Exar Kun is with if not above the level of Malgus and Arcann via stats… Exar Kun quite frankly massacres him."

## Neutral assessment
- This is an unrebutted opener. Its spine is TOR-era retcon material on Kun's ritual and Ossus, plus stat parity with Malgus.
