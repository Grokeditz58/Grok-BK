## Valkorion (P8 "Scaling Chains", P11 "Ziost")
- **Thesis:** "The only way to line up TOR and PT/post-PT scaling chains is to put Valk above Sidious."
- **TOR chains (verbatim excerpts):**
  - "Valkorion ragdolls the Outlander who decisively beats Vaylin who ragdolls Arcann who ragdolls several versions of the Outlander all the way to the Act III HoT who then has several scaling chains down ragdolling Scourge all the way to crushing Vivicar who can destroy hundreds of jedi"
  - "Valkorion is much above Vitiate who beats Revan who oneshots Nyriss who utterly dominates Meetra who dominates on DS nexus Traya who oneshots several Jedi council members who dominate Bastila…"
  - "…novel Revan who is above KotOR Revan who is much above SF Malak who is much above Exar Kun who is far, far, far beyond early-TotJ Exar Kun who is far, far beyond Ommin who is far far beyond Arca Jeth who is far far far beyond Queen Amanoa who can TP dominate thousands of beast riders"
- **PT chains are short:** "RotS Sidious matches Yoda who can beat Dooku with difficulty who can comfortably beat Obi Wan who can comfortably beat Ventress".
  - Aligning RotS Sidious with Valkorion gives absurd results: "Dooku being SWTOR Vitiate tier… Ventress dominating Exar Kun", and even "TPM Obi Wan is like above Vivicar".
  - Two problems: "Dooku is too close to Yoda", and "It makes more sense to accept one outlier (Valkorion) than shift the entire TOR era's average Jedi to being oneshotted by the younglings of the PT era". He closes with "come up with a not-stupid tier list if you can".
- **Ziost:**
  - "Ziost is one of the most potent feats ever… Ziost oneshotted billions, and it was a weakened spirit Vitiate".
  - Byss took 20 years with adepts' help. "If Sidious could absorb planets, why wouldn't he? He couldn't."
  - Force storms only "swallow like a few kilometers at once" and draw on hyperspace energies, i.e. a wormhole does the damage. Sidious was taught them by the Ancient Sith (the Droga quote).
  - "Even if they're equal, that means sub-Vitiate ~ DE Sidious."

## Sidious (The Ellimist, P19)
- **On scaling chains:** "You’re fully willing to accept that Valkorion can do this to lesser opponents… so why can’t Sidious?" There is no defined limit to Sidious's growth after RotS: 30+ years of study, a planet of billions drained, DE hyperspace wormholes, and "needing all Jedi in history to take him down".
- **On Dooku vs Yoda:** "every other line of scaling has Dooku way below Sidious".
  - Gillard puts Dooku at 8 and Sidious at 9, and the 8-to-9 gap is far larger than 7-to-8.
  - In the S6 vision, Anakin stomps Dooku and is ragdolled by Sidious.
  - Sidious stomps Maul and Savage, while Dooku struggles with Savage and Ventress.
  - Sidious > Knightfall Vader > "Zonakin" > Dooku.
  - Conclusion: "Yoda has a beyond-peak performance in RotS, or (more likely) he doesn’t really tap into his true power against a former apprentice".
- **TOR Jedi vs armies:** OCW Jedi also take on magnaguards and droidekas.
- **On Ziost:** Vitiate likely used rituals rather than raw power, and he too only did it twice. Sidious can "do this with a thought".
  - "A single ISD’s hyperspace jump consumes more energy than a planetary civilization would in its lifetime, yet Sidious outdoes that by literally tearing a hyperspace wormhole in spacetime."
  - Sidious could teleport away and storm, or shield himself.
  - "There’s zero evidence they [Ancient Sith] could use it to Sidious’s extent".
- **Supremacy quotes:** "most powerful master of evil the galaxy had ever known"; "greatest master of evil ever to use sith power". He scales over Knightfall Vader, "most powerful enemy ever faced by the Jedi".
- **Spirit and legacy:** Sidious survives as a spirit "without an anchor or even losing his powers", whereas Valkorion needs an anchor. "All Jedi ever across 25,000 years waited just to gang up on Sidious". The EoO version of Valkorion is noted as postdating the debate's start.

## Neutral assessment
- This is the classic TOR vs PT-era super-scaling dispute. Valkorion's argument is structural (era-alignment absurdities). Ellimist's is supremacy quotes plus the open-ended growth of DE Sidious.
- Unfinished; no judgment. Related debates: #9 Plagueis vs Valkorion (draw) and #52 Vader vs Revan.
