## Team Plagueis thesis (MP, P5)
### I. The two most powerful Sith in history
- As of TPM Sidious was "the most powerful Sith of all time" (linked source). *The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia*: "In true Sith tradition, Palpatine murdered his Master upon receiving the skill and ability to do so." → Sidious only "just surpassed Plagueis circa TPM, that makes Plagueis the second most powerful Sith in history, scaling him above all previous Sith in power."
- Defends blurbs: "most powerful Sith Lord in history is an IU statement that has perfect validity within Legends continuity, and yes it can be backed up via corroborative evidence." Rejects the assumption that "subjective" blurbs are invalid.
- Plagueis trained Sidious in Banite arts → comparably skilled duelist and Force user.
- Plagueis's etheric body after the Maladian attack: "a nuclear oval of mottled light, a rotating orb of terrifying energy… a vessel sufficiently strong to contain the full power of the dark side." (Q1, *Darth Plagueis*)
### II. Midichlorian manipulation
- **A. Damage mitigation:** when Sidious attacked, Plagueis didn't raise his barrier (despite "the Force gathering in him like a storm") yet mitigated Sidious's lightning; Bane (whom Sidious is "insanely more powerful" than) could turn bodies to ash → Plagueis can survive lightning more potent than anything Vitiate unleashes [MP's comparison].
- **B. Killing:** perceives midichlorians directly (Q2); the Veruna technique — instructing midichlorians to "return to the Force" (Q3: "I have learned how to manipulate midi-chlorians, and I have instructed the limited n[umber]…"). Described as "one of the deadliest" techniques in Star Wars.
### III. Why Plagueis wins
- Plagueis ≈ TPM Sidious > every prior Sith, and can kill instantly via midichlorian manipulation.
