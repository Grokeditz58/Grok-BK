### P37
- Dismisses BoD's feat comparison and his Ahsoka→Maul→Vader chain as "ABC logic".
- Questions whether Star Wars Comics Magazine 04 (2014) is canon (no Disney logo).
- **Total power:** in Darth Vader (2015) #20, Palpatine reflects that Maul was a loss and Dooku "a temporary tool". Vader is "the most powerful Darth Sidious apprentice".
  - Rebels Recon #2.01: "This is Vader and his prime", which Shioz says may not hold for later versions.
- **Force use:** Vader ragdolls an amped Eeth Koth. He survived the explosion of the largest arms factory in the galaxy. He blocked lightning as a Jedi.
- **Physical:** cybernetic strength, and he can take a direct lightsaber strike and stay combat-ready.
- **Lightsaber:** Eeth Koth landed only one blow while "furious and laid out at 110%". Vader beat Karbin carelessly, chatting with Aphra and calling him unworthy (https://www.youtube.com/embed/7WCJtvoGrxc).
- *A Certain Point of View: Time of Death*: Kenobi "did not have a chance".
- "AEYNtK" (Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know) red-saber ranking list.
- Vader knows Dooku's style.
- **Conclusion bullets** include "Vader, long before his prime, defeats amped former high council in sabers and ragdolls him with the Force" and "Vader easily held back Karbin, a more advanced version of Grievous."

## Dooku (BoD, P30, ~8.9k chars)
- **Thesis:** Vader is stronger in TK "goes without question", but Dooku is underrated.
- **TK comparison:**
  - In the TCW "Son of Dathomir"/Ravna arc (UNCLEAR which exactly; it is the Ravna "vampire" story), Dooku shook a citadel while weakened and infected, to the point that Adi Gallia and Obi-Wan only had to nudge the supports.
  - Vader shook his Mustafar fortress in *Vader Immortal*, but he did it atop a dark-side "locus" cave.
- **Skill:**
  - Young Dooku went toe to toe with Yoda in spars.
  - He held off Anakin and Obi-Wan together, landing hits, months before RotS.
  - Yoda: "Fought well you have, my old padawan." (Ambush!) "It’s a pity I wasn’t there myself, my old master." / "A pity indeed, my fallen apprentice."
  - A drugged Dooku held off an invisible Ventress plus Nightsisters Karis and Naa'leth ("the greatest warriors of our coven").
- **Vader–Maul chain:**
  - Rebels Maul and Ahsoka performed as near equals.
  - Filoni: "Maul wasn’t meant to survive the Malachor episode… the Malachor arc, originally, was more about Vader confronting Maul".
  - Star Wars Comics Magazine 04 (2014): "It's a close call. And these two Sith well-matched in terms of fighting skills."
  - TCW suggests Dooku > prime Maul.

## Neutral assessment
- In canon, Vader > Dooku is the mainstream view: the Darth Vader (2015) "temporary tool" line, the Koth and Karbin feats, and RotS.
- BoD's best angle is skill: Dooku vs Anakin and Obi-Wan in TCW, and his parity with Yoda.
- Unfinished; no verdict.
