## Galen (Syndicate, P6, ~22.4k chars)
### Felucia was a light-side nexus that hindered Galen
- TFU novel: "She had taken a world enjoying the normal flows between the light and the dark sides of the Force and twisted it out of balance. There was still darkness on Felucia, but it was stifled, frustrated, weakened. He strained to awaken it."
- Nexus amp analogy: Tenel Ka on Dathomir.
- **Comic "contradiction":** the yellow caption boxes are PROXY's retelling, not narration. Haden Blackman called the novel "the true story" (http://web.archive.org/web/20100414014010/http://starwars.com/vault/books/news20080819.html).
- **Galen fought through an army of Felucians first:**
  - They have light-bending camouflage and lightsaber-resistant bone blades ("The bone swords were resistant to his lightsaber, but his skill with the Force far exceeded theirs").
  - They heal fast and use TK and energy bolts.
  - Shamans are "among the most powerful natural Force user's".
- The TFU Campaign Guide says Shaak didn't unbalance Felucia alone. The native Felucians drove it, and the planet's later shift to the dark side came from Maris Brood.

### The fight, as the novel tells it
1. Shaak's height-advantage opening strike: "The force of it surprised him".
2. She drives him back down the sarlacc.
3. Galen's lightning makes the sarlacc shake, and "Shaak Ti's right foot slipped".
4. Even fighting follows: "blow and counterblow".
5. The exchange:
   - "You can't keep this up forever." / "Neither can you… You are wasting your strength too quickly."
   - "Your strength is prodigious… that is your doing."
6. A sacrificial final gambit: "three blows that each partially found their mark" (shoulder, chest, nearly his eye).

### The comic version
- Shaak pushes Galen into the sarlacc teeth.
- She charges an energy attack for several seconds; it has "no discernible affect".
- Sarlacc tentacles bind him, and his omnidirectional blast fatally injures Ti.

### Galen's feats before Felucia
- **Raxus Prime droid army:**
  - "He began to tire, not from exertion but from the tedium… There might have been thousands of them."
  - He then blasted them all out of the foyer and into the sky, forming "an artificial hurricane", with a groundquake.
- **Hyperdrive:** he powered a downed ship's hyperdrive with lightning, which by his estimate would otherwise have taken hours.
- **Kazdan Paratus's temple:** he could have "knocked the ridiculous toy castle down around Kazdan's ears with one Force push". Juno calls it an exact replica of the Coruscant Temple, built from armour plates and tank walls.

### Accolades
- "top tier" Force user; "photo negative of Luke Skywalker".
- "Under Vader's relentless tutelage, the Apprentice all but perfected the fine art of lightsaber combat" (Complete SW Encyclopedia).
- "Only high-level masters of multiple forms can achieve and control the ultimate discipline known as Form VII." (Insider 62: Fightsaber)

## Shaak Ti

## Neutral assessment
- Syndicate's case is a thorough handicap analysis: nexus hindrance, fatigue from the Felucians, and a sacrificial ending. It is widely used to support Galen > Shaak Ti.
