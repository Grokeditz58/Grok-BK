## Rancisis + Greedo (Cheth)
- **Opener (P6):**
  - Council hierarchy: Rancisis ranks high even within the council. After Mace became Master of the Order he "can be seen as a tertiary master". He was one of five lifetime council members, led Quinlan Vos's trial (with Mace, Plo, Ki-Adi and Tiin present), and Mace calls him the "senior" member on site. *Legacy of the Jedi* passages: Q6, Q7.
  - **Chair scaling:** in ROTS, Kenobi took Rancisis's seat rather than Kcaj ("Obi-Wan sat in the chair that once had belonged to Oppo Rancisis", Q8), so it is "the seat of the third-highest council member".
  - Rancisis is a warrior: his species is a warrior culture, and he beat the planet's Monarch in hand-to-hand combat.
  - **Malacia:** Jedi vs Sith calls it used against "opponents" and a "powerful defense". The encyclopedia says Rancisis used it "in combat"; Fact File 126 calls it a "martial art". It turns equilibrium and strength against the target, so HoC's strong defences "would only take part in overwhelming him more quickly".
  - **Final fight (Siege of Saleucami):** while running galaxy-wide battle meditation, he sensed the assassins and killed all but one in three panels. They were every Anzati Master except one; a single Anzati Master troubled Tholme. Bulq still wouldn't face him directly.

  - Points out inconsistencies in RNG's chains: if AOTC Dooku is multiple stomp gaps above TPM Dooku ~ Yoda, then HoC > Yoda > all Jedi including HoC. The Yoda spar was a training duel.

That gives "GREEDO > Gorm ~ Mace Windu > Jango Fett < AOTC Hero of Coruscant < ROTS Hero of Coruscant", hence "GREEDO = ROTS Hero of Coruscant."
