## Theses
- **Caedus side (DC77):** Vader post-Mustafar is "like Darth Maul or Count Dooku" in power per George Lucas; Caedus performed far better vs. a stronger opponent (Grandmaster Luke) than Maul did vs. Sidious, so Caedus > Maul ≈ Vader. Caedus also has superior Force feats (illusions on Luke, Force barrier vs. cruiser fire, TK). Backup scaling: Jerec rivals Vader → untrained Kyle survives Jerec → Kyle (later) is Vader-level → Caedus beat Kyle + strike team while injured.
- **Vader side (Ethanion):** Dark-side ROTJ Luke ≥ Knightfall (ROTS) Vader because Luke's frenzy mirrors Anakin's fear/rage-driven power-up against Dooku; yet ROTJ Vader "deals with this reasonably well" despite being hindered (internal conflict), implying ROTJ Vader is very strong. Ethanion said he'd address Caedus later and never did.

## Arguments — Caedus side (DC77)
**Post 1 (P7, opener):**
1. **Comparison of power — Maul ≈ Vader.** George Lucas, *The Cult of Darth Vader*: after Mustafar Vader "wasn't as strong as the Emperor – he was like Darth Maul or Count Dooku" (Appendix Q1). DC77 pre-empts misreading: the passage tracks Vader's power across his life, so the comparison is of power levels.
2. **Caedus > Maul.** In *Darth Maul: Shadow Conspiracy* (Q2), Maul is overwhelmed once Sidious gets serious ("Sidious's sabers were a blur… Maul's saber spun out of his hand"). In *LotF: Inferno* (Q3), Jacen parries Luke's surprise attack while drawing his saber, takes only a shallow kidney wound, and lands "a rib-crunching roundhouse" on Luke. Since GM Luke > TCW Sidious (promised substantiation later), Jacen performed better against a stronger foe → Caedus comfortably > Maul ≈ Vader.
3. **Feats — Illusions:** fooled Luke into thinking Mara was dead (*Dark Nest: The Swarm War*, Q4); cloaked Lumiya from Luke, noted as taking "all his strength when deceiving a Jedi Master of Luke's stature" (*LotF: Bloodlines*, Q5); projected an illusion onto Fondorian shield operators without being on Fondor or knowing the location, while suffering battle-meld backlash (*LotF: Revelation*, Q6).
4. **Feats — Force barrier:** shielded himself from ~twenty assault-cruiser cannon bolts (Swarm War, Q7) — DC77 notes a single blast could drive Zekk to his knees on a lightsaber; shielded himself and his vehicle from an explosion that damaged/ripped apart every building in a ~200 m radius (*Bloodlines*, Q8).
5. **Feats — Telekinesis:** "ragdolled" a Y-wing (*LotF: Betrayal*, Q9); held the *Millennium Falcon* together during reentry by projecting TK through Leia, shocking Luke (*Bloodlines*, Q10); slowed and steadied his accelerating StealthX entering a hangar (*Revelation*, Q11); while maimed (shoulder hole, shattered kneecap, crushed ankle, one arm, gut wound) still Force-blasted Jaina away (*Invincible*, Q12).

**Post 2 (P17):**
6. **Rebuttal of the ROTJ-Luke argument.** "Frenzy" = uncontrolled excitement (dictionary), not a power statement; "a frenzy he had never known. Nor had Vader" is not self-inclusive — Vader is the witness, Luke the one in the frenzy. Even granting it, ROTJ Luke "curbstomped" Vader (fight ~40 seconds, Vader backpedaling), so it hurts Vader.
7. **Backup scaling via Kyle Katarn/Jerec.** Jerec "gives him powers that rival Vader's" (Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II game manual, quoted inline). Barely-trained Kyle survives two Jerec blasts (*Dark Forces: Jedi Knight*, quoted inline: "The blast threw the Rebel backward onto the loading ramp… a second, more powerful explosion hurled him back into the cargo ship… The ramp disintegrated."). So experienced Kyle ≈ Vader/Jerec level; Caedus defeated Kyle with a strike team backing him, while still recovering from the Luke duel (stamina gone within ~40 s, "fodder Jedi" giving him headaches, tagged by blaster fire).

## Arguments — Vader side (Ethanion), single post (P11)
1. **Dark-side ROTJ Luke ≥ Knightfall Vader.** ROTJ novelization p.158: "He rushed to his father with a frenzy he had never known. Nor had Vader." (Q13, read as self-inclusive). Compares to ROTS novelization descriptions of Anakin's fear/rage as the source of his power vs. Dooku (Q15, Q16 "But for Anakin, in the fight there is only terror, and rage." p.82, Q18) and ROTJ p.159 "Each stroke of Luke's saber pummeled Vader, like accusations, like screams, like shards of hate." (Q17). Argument: same narrative beat, same requirement (intent + lack of restraint), so same power class.
2. **Vader held up "reasonably well"** while hindered: "Vader felt his intentions increasingly ambiguous in this conflict; the purity of his evil was being compromised." (ROTJ novelization p.157, inline). Contrast with Dooku's helplessness vs. Anakin: "Dooku's decades of combat experience are irrelevant. His mastery of swordplay is useless." (ROTS novelization p.82, inline).
3. Comic page link given: https://comicvine.gamespot.com/images/1300-5639158 (ROTJ comic).
