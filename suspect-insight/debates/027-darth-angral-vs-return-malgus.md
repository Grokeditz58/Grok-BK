## Malgus (Janix, P2)
- **Pedigree:**
  - Malgus's father: "Your instructors tell me they’ve seen few with your potential in the Force." (*The Third Lesson*, Q1).
  - Sixth Scale Figure bio (Q2).
  - A "Vanguard" of the Empire at the start of the Great Galactic War.
- **Palpatine, Book of the Sith (Q3):** "Malgus submitted utterly to the dark side, and doing so made him an exemplary warrior. His battlefield feats have never been duplicated".
- **Kao Cen Darach:**
  - Malgus names the Battle of Korriban and the Sacking of Coruscant as his top feats. Gnost-Dural calls Kao one of Malgus's most distinguished kills.
  - Kao was a Jedi Battlemaster who trained "among the Order's most promising students" and was the foremost champion against the Empire.
  - Malgus defeated Kao and tanked Vindican's lightning. The Wrath holds that killing a Sith Master proves the apprentice surpassed them.
- **Vindican and Kherus:**
  - Vindican is "Darth Ikoral's greatest Apprentice" per Malgus, whose sensing is reliable (Q4–Q7: he senses bombs and thousands of deaths).
  - So Vindican > Lord Kherus, who fought the Act 3 Strike Team in a multi-act flashpoint.
- **Satele:** Return Malgus is "the most dangerous embodiment of the Dark Side she has ever encountered, which includes Darth Angral and Darth Baras".
- **Angral:** "his greatest feat being such that he dies to the Act 1 Hero of Tython with no parity shown at all."
- **Chain:** **"Return!Malgus >> Kao Cen Darach >> Lord Vindican > Lord Kherus ~ Causing Difficulties to an Act 3 Strike team > Dying Pathetically to Act 1 Hero"**.

## Angral (Would be Lord, P5)
- **"Rise of Angral":**
  - "Damn, guess he excelled and rose immensely through the ranks of the Empire, right?"
  - Palpatine's "never duplicated" is IU-limited ("Imagine if Plagueis heard of Revan holding himself together or Vitiate using the same body for 1,000 years"; Dreadmasters' immortality, Q8–Q9).
  - Malgus is biased toward his former master Ikoral and knew little of Ikoral's whereabouts for 70 years (Ikoral codex, Q10).
  - Janix's logic would put Lord Renning above Vivicar, First Son and Act 3 Voice, and Bask Sunn above Unchained Vaylin, because "Game got much easier as time has gone by".
  - Satele also praises the HoT: "We've lost one of our noblest Masters, but in his place stands a Jedi without equal." (Q12) and "But he also forged our greatest weapon against the darkness." (Q13).
- **Growth and power dynamics:**
  - Deceived Malgus has "28 years of war growth". The Sith Empire is a meritocracy, and Sith traditionally turn on superiors they surpass (Book of the Sith Q14–Q15; the Dark Council codex: "Each is among the most powerful Sith in the galaxy", Q21; Lord Skar, Q22).
  - Yet Malgus defers to Angral:
    - "The pace of this battle has cost me favor with the Dark Council and Darth Angral." (Q23)
    - "Now sit, Malgus.' Angral’s tone left no doubt that the words were not a suggestion." (Q25, *Deceived*)
    - "You will, both of you, follow my commands without question or hesitation." (Q28)
    - Angral edges him aside in favour of Adraas (Q30).
  - Conclusion: "I do not see a reasonable explanation to dismiss a power dynamic between Angral and Malgus that does not end up with Angral >> Deceived Malgus."
  - Malgus's growth accelerates after Deceived ("He felt as if he were the Force", Q31), but he never moves against Angral.
- **Bengel Morr:** in the Jedi Knight prologue the future HoT beats Bengel Morr, a survivor of the Temple and the Battle of Coruscant, who is convinced the HoT could "destroy the entire Sith O[rder]". The point is that Morr doesn't frame it as "a Jedi that could defeat MALGUS".
- **Luke parallels:** RotS novel (Q17), *I, Jedi* (Q18), Databank: "With each passing year, Luke grew more powerful in the Force" (Q19).

## Neutral assessment
- Malgus's strongest points: the Satele quote and Palpatine's "never been duplicated". Angral's strongest: Malgus's in-story subordination to Angral even after 28 years of growth (several verbatim *Deceived* quotes), plus how much the HoT is talked up. Janix conceded before rebutting.
