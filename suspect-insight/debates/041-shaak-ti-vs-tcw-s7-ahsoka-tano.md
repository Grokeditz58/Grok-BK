## Shaak Ti (xolthol), "OPENING STANCE"
1. **Ahsoka's upper limit.** S7's Mandalore duel plus a Dave Filoni interview show "Ashoka is hard capped by Darth Maul": she had to work at her best to compete with him. Maul's overconfidence is what let a young TPM Kenobi win as well, so beating Maul doesn't put her in his league.
2. **"Maul far above Maul?"** TPM Maul < TCW Maul, but the quotes give no measure of the gap. Mother Talzin only restored him to his former strength, his growth period was short, and losing half his body cost midichlorians (compared with Vader). "we have absolutely zero proof that TCW Maul isn't in the same league than TPM Maul."
3. **Qui-Gon's league.** Maul had no clear superiority over Qui-Gon and relied on "an external parameter" to beat him.
4. **"The Togruta absolut superiority".** ROTS Obi-Wan cannot beat 3 MagnaGuards (note {1}), while Shaak Ti fought at least 12 MagnaGuards, "four time more", for an extended period. Qui-Gon ≈ TPM Maul, and Obi-Wan held his own against Maul and Savage in TCW.
- **Conclusion chain:** **"Shaak-Ti >>> Qui Gon Jinn ~ Maul > Ashoka."** "This clearly place Ashoka Tano far below Shaak Ti."

## Ahsoka (King Joker)
- **Ahsoka vs Maul, timestamped breakdown:**
  - Throne room: intense exchanges at 1:05–1:32 and 1:45–2:00. Maul elbows her and disarms one saber.
  - Scaffolding: even dueling at 2:33–2:49, resuming at 2:55 with Ahsoka's elbow jab and kick to the face.
  - "It’s exceedingly clear that the two had a competitive duel. Ahsoka ended the first phase on her terms, and Maul never sustained any advantage until the very end when he disarmed Ahsoka of both sabers."
  - Maul's discipline quote: he survived "a thirty-meter fall… teras kasi".
  - Ahsoka regularly outmanoeuvres more experienced opponents.
- **Shaak Ti's MagnaGuard feat, deconstructed:**
  - She is locked with a single MagnaGuard from 5:06 to 5:32.
  - Two MagnaGuards pursue her from 6:07 to 6:55, and she mostly duels one.
  - The *Complete Visual Dictionary*: "Shaak Ti fights at her best in group combat as she is biologically a[dapted]…"
  - She is disarmed after twenty seconds of frantic defence (9:25–9:45).
  - She made headway only after taking a MagnaGuard's electrostaff. The novel says the guards had Dooku's coaching and "programming in the seven classic forms… But they were not invincible".
  - "Ti’s best feat has a large asterisk next to it."
- **Rebuttals:**
  - Maul's overconfidence is a built-in trait. Filoni says "time and again" and states directly that she competed at Maul's level.
  - The only link xolthol drew between Ti and Kenobi, Qui-Gon or Maul is the MagnaGuard fight.
  - The "three MagnaGuards" quote is answered with the full RotS passage: "In the Force, he felt their destruction…"
  - "unless you also want to argue that Shaak Ti is >> General Grievous..."
- **Closing:** "Shaak Ti's best feat is ambiguous at best, and unimpressive at worst. By contrast, Ahsoka's best feat has her fighting competitively with Darth Maul over several minutes".
