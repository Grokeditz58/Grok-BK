- **Method:** compare ESB Vader vs Luke against AotC Dooku vs Anakin.
  - **ESB:** Vader blocks Luke "with ease" and knocks him down (JN). When Luke attacks again he "drives Vader back" (Novel, Script), "forcing Vader to use defensive tactics" (Script), "matching Darth Vader’s fighting prowess blow for blow" (JN). Later he "forces him back" and Vader loses his balance. In the final exchange Vader drives Luke back only "gradually", and Luke "nicks Vader on the shoulder" (Script).
