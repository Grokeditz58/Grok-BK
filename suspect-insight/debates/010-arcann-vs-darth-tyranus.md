1. **KOTOR II masters:** Vrook, Kavar, Zez-Kai Ell "among the most powerful Jedi masters of their era". Quoted: Force-focus technique "Gifted only to the highest of Jedi Masters – and the Sith" (Q1, KOTOR II dialogue); Bastila: "Even the Sith would think twice before attacking Dantooine… several of the most powerful Mas[ters]"; heading quote "You know, your cage won't hold him. He's an elite Jedi Master." → these three are "well above your average Jedi masters".
2. **Traya:** ragdolls all three "with a mere wave of her hand", then destroys all three simultaneously with Force drain (link); on Malachor V's nexus ("corrupts everything that walks upon its surface") she is stronger still. Chain written as: "Malachor Traya>>Post drain Dantooine Traya>Pre Drain Dantooine Traya>>>The collective power of 3 above average Jedi masters."
3. **Meetra Surik:** hindered physically and mentally on Malachor V (*TOR: Revan* "Traversing its surface had been agony"), still fought through an army of Sith and beat Sion repeatedly and Traya.
4. **Darth Nyriss** nearly kills Meetra "in the opening moment of the battle" (Q2) and charges a killing lightning blow (Q3: "Nyriss was too powerful; her command of the dark side was too st[rong]") → Revan Reborn "easily" redirects it ("I am Revan reborn… And before me you are nothing.").
5. **Vitiate:** "the Emperor was infinitely more powerful than Darth Nyriss had ever been" (Q4); Revan only suffers 2nd-degree burns from it. Summary chain: "Revan Reborn>>>Supercharged Nyriss>>>Nyriss>>Meetra~Malachor Traya>> Post-Drain Dantooine Kreia>Pre Drain Dantooine Kreia>>>The co[llective three masters]".
6. **Hero of Tython:** prophesied slayer of Vitiate, "immense potential" (Vitiate: "You harness immense power, but you lack the purity of will to direct it."; Scourge on HoT's children having unprecedented power); most powerful Jedi as of Act 1.
7. **Arcann:** in KOTFE Ch 8, if the Outlander refuses Valkorion's power, "Arcann casually ragdolls" them → Arcann considerably beyond HoT's full potential.
8. **Dooku's stamina:** tired after AOTC Anakin and Kenobi; RotS novel: "Each parry cost Dooku more power than he'd used to throw Kenobi across the room; each block aged him a decade." (Q5) Dooku's usual escape is lightning/Force offense, which won't work against Arcann.
- **Conclusion:** "Arcann has been demonstrated to be considerably beyond the HoT's full potential, which was in turn demonstrated to be considerably [beyond]…" → Arcann wins.
