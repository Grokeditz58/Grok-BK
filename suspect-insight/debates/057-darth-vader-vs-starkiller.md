## Vader (ISV, P5, ~8.8k chars)
- **Thesis:** in TFU, Vader and Galen are "about equal… if a marginal edge could be given… it would have to be Vader" until Galen "understands a better way to kill".
- **Exhibit A:** Vader holds a combat-ready Galen in a Force grip "for around 10 or so seconds", then a TK blast shatters a structure and throws him into a wall.
- **Exhibit B, TFU novel:** "The intensity of Darth Vader's attack redoubled. 'I have no father.' The apprentice fell back under the rain of blows… at least two of Darth Vader's misses had been horribly near".
- **Exhibit C:** Galen counters Vader's choke "with the force of a small explosion, throwing Darth Vader backward across the room". ISV argues Vader was vulnerable mid-attack, citing Jax Pavan's push of a distracted Vader in *The Last Jedi* (Reaves/Bohnhoff): "sent the Dark Lord staggering back, one, two, three steps".
- **TFU II novel:**
  - Vader blocks "almost casual, disinterested" and "used telekinesis to throw the other [lightsaber] off target". ISV: "To actually target Galen's Lightsabers and throw them off course is to break the force barrier."
  - Vader drives Starkiller to the edge of the spire: "Blow after blow rained on him, forcing him back… a stalemate seemed unavoidable." Galen wins only through trickery (pretending to submit).
  - Video: https://youtu.be/cfv-dH40fwY?list=PLtfXEcg8SXCyg0OzYjGjBFmmX_OWeEdYs&t=721
- **Explicitly declined to counter the exhaustion argument:** "I don't feel like countering Galen's 'Exhaustion' Right now".

## Starkiller (HP, P17, ~51k chars; five sections: intro, TFU fight, TFU 2 fight, Galen's own performances, conclusion)
- **Epigraph:** "I am no longer your apprentice! There is nothing left for you to teach me. You ambushed me twice. You haven't beaten me in a fair fight since I was a child."
- **On the timing of Exhibit A:** "Vader grabs Galen at 0:39, and unleashes a TK wave which sends him flying at 0:41. Vader had him in a hold for precisely 2 seconds… multiply the extent of time it lasted for by 5."
  - Galen was surprised: "He thought he was ready-and so the sheer severity of the opening blow took him by surprise."
  - Maul could replicate holds on Obi-Wan yet dueled evenly.
- **On Exhibit B:** it followed Galen's Dun Moch attempt, hence "redoubled", and Vader never hit him ("misses").
- **Galen pressed Vader:** "whirled and danced around his Master's defenses, testing them to their limits"; he blocks "with elegant precision".
- **Emotional growth is permanent** ("I will kill you, to set you free" carries into TFU II).
- **Exhaustion argument:**
  - Galen spent nearly all his power destroying the *Salvation* ("With his mind and all the power of the Force, Starkiller embraced what remained of the frigate beneath him - and blew it into a billion pieces").
  - He is drained by the cloning tower ("He had never felt so exhausted, at every level of his being").
  - He went days without food or sleep in the pit.
- **TK-deflecting the sabers:** Ventress did the same to Dooku (3:37–3:41) yet lost to him. "Given the fact that you conceded to the above on Discord I’d say we have a dead premise".
- **Being pushed back:** Yoda forces Sidious back in RotS ("He drove Palpatine back across the room").
- **The disarm:** Galen let himself be disarmed ("An indefensible position was exactly what he needed").
- **Galen's advantages:**
  - Wii version: Galen gains the "upper hand" (0:12–0:24) and knocks Vader to his knees (3:23–3:38).
  - Cloning Tower, TFU II script: "Starkiller appears to have the upper hand". Novel: lightning "so concentrated, even the new insulation couldn't absorb it" before a vision distracts him.
  - Ending: Galen cuts off Vader's hand and lightning-blasts him (XBOX 17:44–19:33), disarms him by push in the comic, and ragdolls him in the DS version.
- **Environmental:** "the atomisation of half of a 300m long Frigate" and a redirect feat (truncated in the outline).
- **Gillard level 9 quote:** "Level nine, the highest level of lightsaber fighting, is occupied by a small number of capable sword masters, including Yoda and Darth Sidious."
- **Conclusion:** "Vader absolutely does not scale above Starkiller in any way shape or form".

## Neutral assessment
- HP's opener is far more thorough. It fixes ISV's timing error (2 s, not 10 s) and pairs the exhaustion argument with Galen's later wins.
- Vader's best counters would be that both TFU II fights end with Galen winning only by trickery, and the "casual, disinterested" blocks.
- Onlookers leaned Starkiller. There is no official verdict.
