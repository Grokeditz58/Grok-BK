## Plagueis (Vaelias)
### P6, opener (~31.8k): "Midichlorian Manipulation Power Threshold"
- MM is "not so much an ability, as it is simply being powerful enough + a certain application of willpower".
- Plagueis novel: "I used my will, amplified through my body's own midi-chlorians…"; "there were no powers beyond his reach; none he couldn't master through an effort of will."
- Lucas and AotC framing ties the power to "all powerful", "stop people from dying", "unnatural" and "most powerful Jedi ever".
- Lucas: "a hint in the movie that there was a Sith lord who had the power to create life… Is Anakin a product of a super-Sith…"
- **Thesis:** the threshold lies "vastly beyond AOTC Anakin" and Jedi cannot reach it, so anyone at it is "a 'Super-Sith'… beyond anyone we actually see in the movies".
- **Lucas weakened Plagueis on purpose.** Luceno: "GL was the one who said that Plagueis should be a Muun, and that Plagueis should have an 'accident' that forces him to wear a mask". Lucas also dictated that Plagueis be killed drunk and asleep. Vaelias reads these as precautions "in case Plagueis was indeed at the threshold".
- **Novel feats:**
  - Resurrected Venamis by manipulating midi-chlorians "which should have been inert".
  - Induced pregnancies.
  - Prolonged life.
  - Sidious: "his own powers had increased tenfold over the decades, but he couldn't be certain he had learned all of Plagueis's secrets".

### P10, rebuttal (~29k)
- Lucas "is never clear on this… changing his mind". Vaelias counts "2 vs 16 quotes directly from Lucas and the G-Canon Databanks".
- Lucas's "scam" means he doesn't believe anyone can do it, but "Lucas still agrees a threshold exists". It could also refer to Palpatine.
- "arguing Plagueis never accomplished the feat and that it doesn't exist is futile, it would be like trying to argue Luke never actually marries Mara Jade".
- The RotS novel, which Lucas line-edited, also calls Sidious "Master of all Sith" and the culmination of the Banite plan.
