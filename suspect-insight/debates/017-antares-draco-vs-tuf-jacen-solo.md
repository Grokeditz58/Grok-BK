- **R1:** Draco withstands Sith torture; Vong armour < Imperial Knight armour (Vader-model, extra lightsaber resistance); Jacen unfamiliar with cortosis; Fel struck a saber with the gauntlet in a split second; catching blaster bolts ≠ lightsaber deflection; Legacy Sith kill masters to earn rank; Draco fights off a dozen-plus boosted Korriban guards; concedes Jacen is probably better at TK; Jacen > Cade chain is "mental math".
- **R2:** Vong armour has weaknesses vs phrik; Luke did most of the Slayer work, "Jacens the first one to get hit"; tutaminis only shown by specialists (Corran, Nejaa); Wolf Sazen's chosen Jedi; Draco's push from Cade "did next to nothing"; the Cade who struggled with the Vong had atrophied skills after only 2 weeks of training; Cade's Krayt kill: "Cade force pulled a saber, turned, jumped, and impaled Krayt all before he could react… not a cheap shot."
- **R3:** the Traitor revolt involved "a thousand plus slaves tidal waving over your enemies" ("Already, the inflowing tide of slaves had swamped the warriors guarding the hive-island"); One Sith don't rely on swarm tactics; RPG stats place post-Sith-training Cade below Draco.
- **Closing (P14):** "If Jacen hits that gauntlet even once he’s disarmed for the fight"; "I guess Desann is also way above Luke because he force pushed him that one time."

## Common counterarguments
- Pro-Draco: cortosis gauntlet as a disarm win condition (the judge accepted it as a live factor); phrik/Vader-style armour; martial versatility.
- Pro-Jacen: Vong-slayer and slave-revolt feats; abilities beyond Draco's (Art of the Small, electric judgment, TK); Cade scaling.

## Neutral assessment
- RNG's strongest move was building a direct link (Vong ↔ Cade ↔ Draco), which both judges weighted heavily. Geek's strongest: the cortosis gauntlet plus RPG stat placement of Cade; his main error was conceding Jacen's ability advantages early.
