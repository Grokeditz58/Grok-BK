## Cade (ILS, P7, ~18.9k chars)
- **Power floor:** Cade throws a large multi-passenger shuttle into Darth Talon, carrying her "many hundreds of yards", out of the Jedi's sight. ILS (as written): "I don’t think Maul has many, if any, raw power feats which match or exceed this."
- **Cade's state at the time:** he was at his "weakest" per Jan Duursema (CBR: https://www.cbr.com/ostrander-and-duursema-leave-their-legacy-on-star-wars/). He was a death-stick addict, his skills had atrophied below his padawan level, and he was carrying trauma ("Your scars go deep and are more than physical").
- **Growth:**
  - One Sith training and the Embrace of Pain. He went from barely beating Nihl (only after a distraction let him kick Nihl off a cliff) to dominating him ("folds Nihl in half with a kick", takes his arm "faster than he can react").
  - He learns Shatterpoint and can now read Krayt's "red lines".
- **Healing chain (verbatim):** **"Cade’s nexus debuffed light-side healing > Healing Deliah Blue > Failing to heal Blue while nexus amped using full-effort dark transfer > Rakghoul plague w/ shatterpoint + Sith training > Accelerated coral seeds w/out shatterpoint + Sith training > throwing a huge shuttle hundreds of yards out of sight."**
- **Late-series state:**
  - "a one-man Sith wrecking machine". In *War* he is "more sober, focused and driven".
  - He heals his mother via the light side.
  - Talon sees his "new mastery". Cade went from losing to Talon in sparring to "completely destroying her with telekinesis, throttling her into Krayt’s throne room a bruised and battered mess".
- **Tertiary arguments:**
  - Cade's lightning destroyed a Sith Trooper's saber defence and knocked him out.
  - Dark transfer and Shatterpoint let him heal mid-fight and make Maul's weak points "explode" ("I can see the weak points in you like little broken…").
  - Maul's cybernetic legs are vulnerable to lightning and dark transfer, as with the Muur talisman.
- **Summary bullets:**
  - "Cade’s lowest end power feats as a drug addict with the skills of a padawan are as good as Maul’s best."
  - "Cade literally hits harder than a flying shuttle."
  - "Maul’s fighting style plays into Cade’s advantages nicely."
  - "Maul’s cybernetic legs, if compromised, result in an immediate loss".
