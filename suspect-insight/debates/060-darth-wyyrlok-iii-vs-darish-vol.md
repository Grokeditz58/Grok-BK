## Wyyrlok (ILS, P12 opener, ~22.5k chars)
- **Thesis:** "Wyyrlok is extremely powerful, skilled and knowledgeable in all aspects of the dark side, with a penchant for telepathic attacks. His knowledge of the Sith arts even exceeds Krayt himself." Accolades include "Known as the Loremaster of the Sith".
- **Illusions:**
  - With an "iota" of his power, he forced illusions into nearly a dozen Force-sensitive Sith devotees and made them kill each other. Quote: "You have seen only an iota of the power I possess. It is nothing to enter your mind and feed you illusions. If I wished, I could make you see things that would…" He mused that he could make them claw out their own eyes.
  - Legacy Era Campaign Guide: "He is a master of illusions, capable of using the Force to trick his enemies' minds…"
- **Andeddu mental battle:** Wyyrlok rebuked the attack ("You have doubts, Lord Wyyrlok, and doubts can cripple!") and eradicated Andeddu's mind and spirit by attacking his fear of losing his knowledge. He planted the image of Andeddu's books and holocron destroyed in lava.
- **Willpower:** "Darth Wyyrlok believes that it is willpower that gives the Sith their strength". This matches Plagueis and Sidious. After Andeddu, Wyyrlok's will can "carry both of them" (him and Krayt).
- **Memory Walk:**
  - It compels the victim to relive tragedy, humiliation, shame and fear in an amplifying loop, "a greater method of torture than even physical pain". It can make seconds feel like hours and can strip away the victim's psychological defences (passages on Captain Cahissian).
  - Wyyrlok used it on Krayt mid-duel while losing the saber exchange, and brought Krayt "to his knees temporarily".
- **Vol's vulnerability:**
  - Vol "shrank back in horror" at Abeloth; she was a "nightmare"; he was "terrified" at entering her mind.
  - Evicted from her mind, he suffered "the most exquisite agony" and was left weeping and mumbling.
  - Wyyrlok would loop this trauma: "Memory Walk alone wins the fight", or it floors Vol for a kill.

## Vol (MasterCilghal, P20, ~12.8k chars)
- Agrees with ILS's breakdown, including "the notion that willpower directly correlates into force power".
- **Abeloth's appearance:** it was designed to horrify; her illusions made Sith sabers commit suicide. Vol not being paralysed shows his will.
  - Essential Reader's Companion: "keen mind", "able to peer past Abeloth’s illusions", "frustrated with Vol’s tenacity".
- **The agony:** it came from an injured Abeloth's exertion, and he had "put everything he had into the attack".
- **Scaling:**
  - Vol vs Abeloth compared with FotJ Krayt vs Abeloth. Krayt was probably drawing on the Font of Power, "the most potent [nexus] in the galaxy", while Vol performed similarly without a nexus.
  - Krayt was decaying from Vong implants, yet still far above Wyyrlok ("Krayt perfected his combat techniques over many decades and his skills with telekinesis…"). Wyyrlok only betrayed Krayt once he was unconscious.
  - Chain (verbatim): **"Vol>FOTJ Krayt>>Vector Krayt>>Wyyrlok"**.

## ILS rebuttal (P32, ~31k chars)
- Abeloth's constructs were becoming "solid", and Wyyrlok can do the same "with an iota of his power". Clawing out your own eyes is harder to induce than suicide.
- **Vol did not withstand a direct telepathic attack.** Abeloth was only "coyly teasing and caressing him". His attack used a corrupted memory-extraction technique while Abeloth "left herself wide open". "Abeloth was the one under assault, not Vol." "Vol gained no victory. Abeloth nearly killed him".
- **Krayt/Luke vs Abeloth was her true self Beyond Shadows.** At the end she abandons counterattacks ("The counterattack never came"). Vol fought in his own mind and dream, where he is strongest.
- **Nexus mechanics:** the Living Force is abundant in the physical universe (argument continues).
- Asks for any evidence that Vol processed the trauma quickly.

## Neutral assessment
- Wyyrlok's case is a specific win condition: Memory Walk plus illusions exploiting a documented trauma. Vol's is a raw-power scaling through Abeloth and Krayt.
- Cilghal conceded without answering P32.
- **Weaknesses:** Wyyrlok's illusions on Force-sensitive devotees are low-end targets. Vol's chain depends on the debatable Font of Power assumption.
