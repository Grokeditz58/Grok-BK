## An'ya Kuro (DDB)
- **Opener:** her best showing is a duel with Vader around 1 BBY, "a mere five years before suited Vader peaked". She is about even with him on speed, holds a bladelock and lands hits, but Vader catches her by the wrist and calls her nothing compared to him. Vader's speed: he struck down Order 66 survivor Sia-Lan Wezz before she could react → "An’ya keeping up with Vader in terms of physicality is easily more impressive than anything Malgus has as of Return."
- **R1:** Exar-Kun-over-Vader quotes don't show greater power: "When someone is compared to a nun, combative ability is not the first thing that springs to mind"; "fearsome" refers to Kun's lack of morals. Nothing makes the HoT equal to the Emperor's Wrath. Satele's "most dangerous" line refers to Malgus overall, not Return Malgus. Sidious is "the most powerful Sith Lord of all time" per "over a dozen statements". Satele thinks the HoT can beat False Emperor Malgus because he beat Vitiate. Growth sequence: FE Malgus > Deceived > Hope > Return. Chain: **"An'ya Kuro < 1 BBY Vader < RotJ Vader < RotJ Sidious > Vitiate > FE Malgus >> Deceived Malgus >>> Hope Malgus >> Return Malgus"**.
- **R2:** Kun's "unbelievable power" is hype, and the 2005 Fact File "most powerful Sith" is inaccurate. The Voice was being taken over by Sel-Makor, not empowered (Son of Dathomir analogue: Sidious easily beat a Talzin-controlled Dooku). The "Choose Your Side" videos are gameplay-based. A high-potential Force user would grow over 14 years. "That's... not how percentages work. 80% is a proportion. Vader has 8/10 of Palpatine's power." Chain: "Palpatine >> Marek's full potential according to Palpatine > Vader's potential > RotJ Vader > TFU Vader".
- **R3:** "Kun < Malak << Plagueis < TPM Sidious ~ TPM Dooku < RotS Dooku < RotS Anakin < ANH Vader > 1 BBY Vader >> Kuro". Vader is weaker only than the very strongest PT masters. The HoT never beat Sel-Makor himself. Angral was only "a possible contender" for the Dark Council. Vindican, not Malgus, was chosen to lead the attack. Malgus "broke some bones and had to wear a respirator", while Vader was a quadruple amputee.
- **Conclusion:** Lucas's later "80%" statement outweighs his earlier ones. "A PT force user in Null's armor killed a Jedi Master, not Warb Null himself." Vindican's lightning, mostly absorbed by Kao's saber, still staggered Malgus. Malgus beat Kao Cen Darach only when infuriated and with Kao exhausted. "Vader is definitively more powerful than Return Malgus, and by extension, so is Kuro."

## Malgus (Janix)
- **Opener:** Vader-speed parity "means nothing", since Force users' builds don't decide it. Chain: **"Apprentice Malgus >~ Dark Council Members ~ Chapter 3 HoT > Novel Revan >> Amnesiac Revan > Star Forge Malak >>> Normal Malak > Exar Kun > Darth Vader >> 1 BBY Vader > An'ya Kuro"**. Per Lord Scourge, the Chapter 3 Hero of Tython is stronger than Novel Revan, and the Chapter 3 HoT equals the Chapter 3 Emperor's Wrath. Satele writes of the Return cinematic: "There in the hangar, I first encountered the most dangerous embodiment of the dark side I have experienced".
- **R1:** The Kun quote compares Vader to "a frail and defenseless Nun", and "fearsome" relates to power. The 2005 Star Wars Fact File places Kun as most powerful Sith (later retconned for Malak and Sidious, but not for Vader). The HoT's best Chapter 3 feat is defeating the Voice, which the Wrath also does while the Voice is "empowered even further". Developers treat all classes as equally powerful. HK-47 compares the HoT to Malgus, and the HoT's form is weak next to FE Malgus. Force power isn't linear: Starkiller unleashed his full potential on Sidious to no effect.
- **R2:** "Kun's 'unbelievable power'". Warb Null is stronger than some PT masters, who per Lucas are stronger than Vader, and Null is far weaker than Exar Kun. Sel-Makor recognised a huge boost from possessing the Voice. Baras with "The Entity's" power was believed by the Dark Council to be the Voice. "I am arguing that 80% of Palpatine is Fodder… 800,000/1,000,000 is still 200,000 points away from being an equal." Angral came out of retirement and was considered for the Dark Council. Malgus is "heralded as the Empire's hero"; Hope Malgus isn't stronger than Return Malgus. Malgus tanked Vindican's lightning, and Kao Cen Darach is a Jedi Battlemaster.
- **R3:** RotJ Luke equals Vader, and a much stronger Luke lost to Spirit Kun. Plagueis's supremacy is "a marketing blurb, the lowest form of S-Canon per Leland Chee". G-canon Lucas places PT Jedi above Luke and Vader. The Empire's darkness weakened the Jedi, so Vader's feats against them are against "fodder". "8/10 is fodder."
- **Conclusion:** "George Lucas ensured that your ludicrous Anakin < Vader scaling was as easy to tear down as it was."

## What convinced the judges
- DDB failed to expose the weak links in Janix's chain. Both judges noted Kun > Vader as a weak link, and DDB's Vader > RotS Anakin argument "didn't pan out". The 80% argument was contested; Nute wanted Janix to explain Starkiller.

## Neutral assessment
- Both cases rely on long scaling chains. Janix's strongest point is Satele's "most dangerous embodiment" quote tied to the hangar. DDB's strongest are the direct Kuro–Vader duel and the Lucas 80% figure.
