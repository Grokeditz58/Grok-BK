## The key evidence: Jedi Apprentice: The Threat Within spar (as quoted in full by MP)
- "Qui-Gon brought his lightsaber down in a powerful strike - once, twice, three times. Obi-Wan was there to block each blow… Obi-Wan was fighting like a Jedi Knight… He suddenly had the feeling that one day the boy would beat him."
- "With each stroke, Qui-Gon pushed Obi-Wan farther back. It was not terribly difficult. But… the Padawan was somehow in control."
- "Now he was swinging repeatedly and with phenomenal strength, pushing Qui-Gon in a wide arc around the courtyard."
- "Qui-Gon had to concentrate - hard - to keep ahead of his Padawan."
- "Both men were breathless, sweating from the exertion. This had been no lighthearted spar."
- "It was clear that he had not actually won the match, but that he had stated his case firmly." (Obi-Wan was 17.)
- **LOTL's reading (as quoted by MP):**
  1. It was "no lighthearted spar".
  2. Qui-Gon was "panting and sweating hard… 8 years before TPM".
  3. Obi-Wan "state[d] his case firmly" to be treated as an equal.
  - "Obviously his growth rate is going to be higher than Qui Gon's so by the time we get to TPM, he should be near equal to his master".
- **MP's rebuttal:**
  - Masters and apprentices know each other's styles. RotS novel: "Blade-to-blade, they were identical. After thousands of hours in lightsaber sparring, they knew each other better than brothers…" The same applies to Dooku and Qui-Gon.
  - Qui-Gon's victory was "clear".
  - Qui-Gon grew toward TPM (Jedi Quest: The School of Fear: his mastery was on par with great masters "near the end of his life").

## Qui-Gon case (MP, P5, ~24k chars)
- **Epigraph:** "Worse news yet. I have met Qui-Gon, and he is nothing like some of the others Dooku trained." (Darth Plagueis novel)
- **Accolades:**
  - Master of every form ("Lightsabers: A Guide to the Weapons of the Force"; classical techniques).
  - "Qui-Gon was a master of the Jedi arts, and strong in the Force." (Fact File)
  - "generally regarded as one of the best pure swordsmen the Order had ever seen." (Complete SW Encyclopedia)
  - TPM novel: "His edge now, to the extent that he had one, came from his long experience and intuitive…" (truncated).
  - Sidious calls him a "powerful warrior" and "the great Qui-Gon Jinn" (Episode I Journal: Darth Maul).
  - Plagueis feared for Maul when he heard of the encounter.
- **Scaling:**
  - Dooku in "Most Precious Weapon": "To be honest, I expected much more from Qui-Gon's former padawan."
  - In Cestus Deception, Fisto must slow down for Kenobi, and Kenobi admits he is inferior.
  - Chains (verbatim): **"Qui-Gon > RotS Fisto > AotC Fisto >(>) AotC Kenobi."** and **"Qui-Gon > Ki-Ad Mundi >(=) Early CW Asajj Ventress >(>) AotC Fisto >(>) AotC Kenobi."** The Mundi part was later retracted.
- **Maul duel:** Qui-Gon came closer to killing Maul (Maul "stymied", sent "reeling"). Kenobi lasted longer only through rage and Force augmentation. Qui-Gon "can match Darth Maul evenly for over half a minute".

## Neutral assessment
- Kenobi's strongest evidence is the near-even spar at 17, which onlookers found decisive, plus the Prima Guide "lacks only experience" line.
- Qui-Gon's strongest evidence is the swordsman accolades and the Dooku/Fisto scaling. MP himself conceded his spar rebuttal was weak, and onlookers leaned Kenobi.
- There is no judgment.
