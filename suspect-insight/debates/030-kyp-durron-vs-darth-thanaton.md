## Thanaton (Plag): "Supreme Sorcerer"
- **Power accolades:**
  - Q1, SWTOR Encyclopedia: "Darth Thanaton, a supremely powerful Sith and harsh traditionalist… To combat Thanaton's insurmountable strength, the inquisitor learns the ritual of Force-walking".
  - Q2: "utterly destroys the once supreme Sith".
  - The True Sith "have spent centuries building their strength" (Q4).
  - His era directly follows the one in which the Jedi were "in their prime" (Chris Avellone, Q3).
  - Nox, a prodigy descended from a great Sith, "had to go to great lengths to even compete with Thanaton".
- **Kallig and Hord:**
  - Tulak Hord, "Lord of Hate, Master of the Gathering Darkness and Dark Lord of the Sith" (Q12), predates the Great Hyperspace War. Codex entries cover his secrets and the Force Walk (Q6–Q11).
  - Both are called some of the most powerful Sith of their era, which also had XoXaan and Karness Muur (Muur is compared to Krayt, and Ostrander is quoted as Q13).
  - Plag's chain: **"Karness Muur ~ RoTS Palpatine class ~ Vong Krayt ~< Karness Muur ~< Tulak Hord ~< Darth Nox >>> Darth Thanaton"**. [Chain as written.]
- **Horak-Mul:**
  - Horak-Mul was Ludo Kressh's right hand. As Nox's bound spirit he declares his entire era inferior to Nox's servant Khem Val, who "could easily make anyone the Dark Lord of the Sith of Horak-Mul's era" (that era includes Naga Sadow and Marka Ragnos).
  - Khem Val is "Powerful, cunning and ruthless enough to slay even the strongest Force users" (Q14). "This was the first time any Force user had bested Khem Val" (Tulak Hord, Q15). Thanaton is one of the few who claim to have defeated Khem.
  - Chain: **"Naga Sadow < Hoth Khem << Beginning of Act 2 Nox << 2 spirits Nox < Nox + Khem <<< Darth Thanaton."** [As written.]
- **Stats:**
  - The stats hold Naga Sadow well above Kyp.
  - "NJO Jaina is close…"
  - "Random KOTOR Jedi and Sith are written as superior duelists to Kyp" (SW Miniatures).
- **Knowledge:**
  - Lords who kneel to him hold all of Naga Sadow's collection.
  - The Empire has a branch for recovering artifacts, and the Dark Council has access to Vitiate's hidden repository (Q18).
  - This is "Knowledge that Palpatine feared his servants knowing".
  - "Darth Thanaton has had more time to study arcane Sith texts and dark side lore than Luke's entire Jedi Order has existed for".
  - "Darth Thanaton has had decades to master Sith sorcery, which Kyp should struggle to counter."

## Kyp (RNG)
- **Opening:** "First and foremost, I'd like to congratulate both of us for making it to this point… May fortune smile upon the greater man."
- **Counters:**
  - The accolades don't bind the NJO.
  - Nox needed the Force-walking amp only because he was early in his career. By the second fight he "outstripped Thanaton pretty handily… Like... a literal ragdoll gap". So there should be "a lot more '>'" between Nox and Thanaton.
  - "Successor" and "Heir" don't mean more powerful than Hord.
  - Horak-Mul's praise is about Khem's skill as an assassin, and he is arrogant and overhypes his own death. RNG notes "He was ganked by 2 assassins. Not 5", and offers three readings of the contradiction. Horak "feels the need to overhype what it took to kill him by saying 'Not one, but five!' Mans is definitely compensating."
  - Sadow's spirit was weakened. Vitiate considered ancient Sith spirits a threat.
  - The Kyp miniature (*Imperial Entanglements*) is a "super" early Kyp, yet he still has Force 4. "Using this mini for scaling is like calling GM Luke fodder because ESB Luke is sub Vader." Minis are "wonky", for example suited Vader > KFV.
  - Palpatine's fear is "playing his cards close to the chest".
- **Kyp hype:**
  - "When you looked at Kyp Durron, you knew you were seeing an enormously powerful weapon."
  - Sources: *Jedi vs Sith*, *Galaxy Magazine* 13, *Power of the Jedi*, SW Miniatures Universe Preview 3 (a student Kyp at parity with a KOTOR Jedi Guardian), Fact File 115, FOTJ Dramatis Personae.
  - "Durron is a top tier Jedi in an era that has demonstrable supremacy over PT and TOTJ/KOTOR."
- **Luke chain:**
  - RotJ Luke resists Palpatine's influence while beating Vader. RotS Palpatine used a confusion haze on the B-team.
  - DE Luke is "awakening to the legendary powers of the great Masters of old" (DE endnotes and DE Handbook). DE Palpatine is "far, far, far above ROTS Palpatine".
  - DE Luke outduels Yoda-level, which puts him above the RotS titans. Allston confirms NJO Luke > Kyp in raw power.
  - Chain: **"Post NJO Kyp >> NJO Kyp ~<< (same realm) NJO Luke >> Post DE Luke >>> DE Luke >> RoTS Titan class ~ Karness Muur ~ Vong Krayt ~< Karness Muur ~< Tulak Hord ~< Darth Nox >>>>>>>> Darth Thanaton"**.
- **Dovin basal ("You saw this coming"):** "nothing that Thanaton (or anyone in his realm) has done or can do is on the same scale of both power and skill as the dovin basal feat. Moving black holes, even artificial ones is nothing to shak[e a stick at]". See #5 for the quotes (I19–I20).
