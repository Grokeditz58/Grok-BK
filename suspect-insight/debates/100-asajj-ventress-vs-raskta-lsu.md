## Raskta (Primarch, P10)
- Epigraph: **"Two there should be; no more, no less. One to embody power, the other to crave it."**

### Part I: Debunking Banite scaling
- Rejected sources:
  - The 1999 "powers increased with each generation" source predates Bane.
  - "Bane's plan produced more powerful Sith Lords with each generation" is Disney canon.
  - The Official Star Wars Fact File #1 is in-universe, written by New Republic historians.
  - The Sidious "we… growing in power" quote is in-universe.
- **Labyrinth of Evil:** "For two hundred years before the coming of Darth Sidious the power of the dark side had been gaining strength".
- **Luceno on Gravid:** "Gravid was an invention of mine. I needed some way to distance Plagueis from the ancient teachings and powers." Source: theforce.net Facebook Q&A recap.
- "Lucas also stated that the Rule of Two arrangement never worked".
- Stats place PoD Bane above Dooku.

### Chain (verbatim)
**"Full orbalisk armour Bane >>>>>>>>>> 2 orbalisk Bane > Fire ritual Bane >>>>> PoD Bane > Dooku >> Ventress"**

- Raskta drives full-orbalisk Bane onto the defensive. Zannah saw "both of Bane's opponents were still standing; proof they were exceptionally skille[d]".

## Ventress (Durin, P12–P13)
- Opening: "Asajj Ventress curbstomps Rasta Sue."

### PT supremacy
- Lucas and Gillard: the PT is "the heyday, the Golden Age of Jedi" (YouTube embed).
- RotS novelization, Yoda: "the most devastatingly powerful foe the darkness had ever known".
- The Jedi Council "represent the best of the Order" (Clone Wars Character Encyclopedia).

### Chain (verbatim)
"Raskta Lsu < individual members of Bane-era High Council <<<<< at least 5/12 members of the slightly-post-Bane-era High Council… >~ Volfe Karkko << Volfe Karkko after consuming 'soup' from Anzati ~< pre-AotC Quinlan Vos < CW Quinaln Vos << Agen Kolar."

### Rebuttals to Primarch's Banite points
- The 200-year Labyrinth quote is about the dark side itself, an effect put into place by Tenebrous and his master, not the Sith's personal power.
- Plagueis: "his apprentice carried the imperative forward, and eac[h]…" Durin argues the Sith kept growing after Gravid.
- Lucas's "Rule of Two never worked" meant the Sith kill each other, not that apprentices fail to surpass masters.
- The minis stats are unreliable. They rank Sidious below Saesee Tiin and Plo Koon, and rank Grievous above Sidious and Dooku.

### Ventress feats
- Holds off Obi-Wan and Anakin and TKs them toward lava.
- Tears through both their Force walls and grips them.
- "At her peak, she is ragdolling Kenobi merely 5 months pre-RotS".
- Gives Mace a great fight in the early war, then fights him evenly above the Jedi Temple, a light-side nexus.
- Defeats Kit Fisto (*The Cestus Deception*): "He was like fire, Ventress like smoke… Kit was down!"

### Raskta's limits
- Even amped by Worror's battle meditation, "she wasn't able to land a telling blow".
- "A Weapons Master was not skilled at defending against enemy Force attacks." She needed Farfalla's barrier against Bane's push.
- "Orbalisk Bane has been established as below Bane as he was in Dynasty of Evil", which Durin uses to call Bane-era Raskta "a stomp gap below".

## Neutral assessment
- This is a high-quality Banite-scaling clash. Both sides made sourced cases.
- Ventress has more direct PT feats. Raskta's Tython showing against orbalisk Bane is her strongest feat.
- The debate was paused, so there is no result.
