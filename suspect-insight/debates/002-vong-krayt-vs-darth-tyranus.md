## Theses
- **Krayt (Azronger):** chain — RotS Dooku ≈ RotS Obi-Wan (Kenobi stonewalls Dooku; Dooku's TK toss only happened because Kenobi had no active barrier up) → 17 BBY Ben Kenobi ≈ Jedi A'Sharad Hett (Hett pressured Ben, who needed full focus) → Hett then gained decades of Sith training (XoXaan holocron) and Yuuzhan Vong "Embrace of Pain" empowerment to become Krayt, who at Legacy #1 is still near his Fate-of-the-Jedi-era peak despite coral seeds. So **Krayt >>>> Hett ≈ Kenobi ≈ Dooku**. Later posts add: Krayt "RotS Yoda+" pre-decline; Kenobi's Hett-era motivation amps; Muur argument rebutted.
- **Dooku (HP):** chain — Yoda ≈ AOTC Dooku (Dooku held off Yoda ~40 s exhausted, lightning pressured Yoda) → Yoda > all Revans > KOTOR Revan > Star Forge Malak > Karness Muur (Muur was one of the Exiles who used the Star Map; Star Map ≈ Star Forge dark-side tech) > Krayt (Legacy #31 solicitation: "Emperor Krayt might no longer be the most powerful Sith lord in the galaxy"). Plus Dooku's swordmaster accolades and Force feats (ship-throwing, Tenebrous/Bane scaling).

## Arguments — Krayt side (Azronger)
**Opener (P14, ~7,900 chars of own argument; deliberately no rebuttals):**
1. **Dooku vs Obi-Wan (RotS).** The TK "ragdoll" doesn't prove a tier gap: per the RotS junior novelization Kenobi never raised active Force defenses ("He can't fight in two directions at once…" — Dooku "half-turned and raised a hand. A rush of dark power lifted Obi-Wan off his feet…"). The RotS novelization has Kenobi moving "straight upward over Dooku's head so fast it seemed he'd vanished", his "blade weaving through a defensive velocity so bewilderingly fast that Dooku dared not even try a strike", parrying "every one of his thrusts without so much as moving his feet… riposting with flickering strikes and stabs swifter than the tongue of a Garollian ghost viper". → Kenobi ≈ Dooku.
2. **Obi-Wan vs A'Sharad Hett (17 BBY, *Star Wars Republic: The Hunt for Aurra Sing*-era comic / *Life and Legend*-type novelization — Azronger cites "the novelization").** Kenobi "had continued his Jedi exercises on Tatooine" and "had not allowed his reflexes to become dull" (no decline); needed full focus — distracting thoughts "would probably only get him killed"; Hett landed unarmed strikes; Ben blocked but "wasn't doing so with ease"; it was "fortunate" Ben wasn't out of practice. The blue flash = TK push, not TK arm-ripping (arm taken by saber). Desert terrain only a minor Hett advantage. → Hett is Kenobi's rival.
3. **Hett → Krayt.** XoXaan holocron Sith training: "he gained a great deal of strength in the Force"; Yuuzhan Vong experimentation/Embrace of Pain; Krayt at Legacy #1 is weaker than his FotJ: Apocalypse self (coral seeds), but not drastically.
- Conclusion chain: "Emperor Krayt (>)> Darth Krayt >> A Sharad Hett (Sith) >> A'Sharad Hett (Jedi) < Obi-Wan Kenobi = Count Dooku."

**Second post (P51, ~44k chars):** rebuttals labelled "FALLACY COUNTER 1–5":
4. HP strawmanned the premise (it's Kenobi's *power level* parity, not "can put up a fight"). Hasty generalization: one TK hit without active barrier ≠ ragdoll gap.
5. The TCW duel HP used is >5 months before RotS; Kenobi was also tired, so citing Dooku's fatigue is a double standard. Not going for a kill ≠ holding back on defence.
6. **Anakin scaling:** all-out Anakin beats fresh Dooku in ~12 s (movie) and the novelization (Q-series quotes from RotS novel/junior novel: Mustafar exchanges, "This truth: that he, the avatar of light, Supreme Master of the Jedi Order…") — RotS Yoda is distinguished from AOTC Yoda; Mustafar Kenobi ≈ Vader; "Yoda's ability to use the Force is greater than that of any other Jedi" / "Meet Yoda. He is the most powerful Jedi." used to place Yoda above Mustafar Vader.

All he could do was keep a closer eye on Luke."); novel reveals Ben avoided thinking of Luke to hide him from Hett. **Speed feat:** a Nar Shaddaa passage (Kenobi running faster than a bloodwolf at "eighty kilometers per hour", Nar Shaddaa spinning at "one hundred and seventy kilometers per hour", a podracer at "six hundred kilometers per hour"; "No one, not Jedi, not Sith, had ever duplicated such superhuman locomotion") — when motivated to protect Luke, Kenobi "is able to run faster than any Force user in history".
8. **Krayt vs Karness Muur (Legacy #31-33):** Krayt had been ignoring the coral seeds for 7 years; Morne+Muur combined power; Krayt never drew his second saber; Azlyn Rae stabbed Krayt while Morne-Muur blasted him off a cliff, yet he survived; the solicitation only "proposes a possibility".
