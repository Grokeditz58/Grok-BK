### Darth Sidious (RotS, Legends) vs Darth Plagueis (as of the Darth Plagueis novel)
- Verdict: Sidious, mid-high diff. Confidence: moderate.
- Fights: Sidious killed Plagueis, but drunk and asleep (Lucas dictated that per Luceno, debate #101), so it is an ambush, not a fair duel. Plagueis mitigated Sidious's all-out lightning without a barrier (debates #11, #12).
- Statements: Darth Plagueis novel, Sidious's "own powers had increased tenfold over the decades"; RotS novel calls Sidious "Master of all Sith" and the culmination of the Banite plan (#101). Sidious only "surpassed" Plagueis around TPM (#11).
- Narrative/author intent: the whole Banite line is built to peak in Sidious; Lucas deliberately weakened Plagueis (Muun, mask accident, killed asleep).
- Common opponents: Exar Kun (#87 judged win for Sidious, #101 no judgment vs Plagueis); Valkorion (#9 draw for Plagueis).
- Could flip it: a binding source that Plagueis's midi-chlorian manipulation works as a combat instant-kill on a resisting master.
- Date: 2026-10-20.

### Darth Plagueis vs Exar Kun (TOTJ prime)
- Verdict (updated 2026-10-28 12:17 PM CDT): FLIPPED to lean Exar Kun, high diff. Confidence: low. Reason: the Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia gives Kun the stronger accolade (Vol. II p.235: "Once the most powerful and dangerous of the Dark Lords of the Sith, he was responsible for the deaths of millions") while it calls Plagueis only "one of the most powerful Sith Masters" (Vol. III p.154); Plagueis's "no Sith surpassed him" is his own in-novel belief, and Kun's feats (Senate freeze, Vodo, Cinnagar) are on-page combat/mass feats. Plagueis's Venamis resurrection and Sidious-lightning tank keep it high diff. Server pushback on Plagueis top 3 ([member], [member]) noted but not the reason.
- Previous verdict (2026-10-20 to 2026-10-28): lean Plagueis, high diff. Confidence: low-moderate. Reasoning kept below for reference.
- Key reasoning: the Plagueis novel has him aware of no Sith surpassing him, the chain that won the judged #87 (Sidious > Plagueis > Kun); Databank places Star Forge Malak above Kun (#3), capping Kun. Plagueis's resurrection of Venamis and life-prolonging are Force feats Kun never shows.
- Kun's best case: Fact File "most powerful and dangerous Sith Lord of all time"; JA spirit-Kun > Dark Empire Palpatine chain; Lucas's "it's a lie... a scam" on Plagueis's power and the RotS line that it "was never accomplished by Darth Plagueis" (#101).
- Could flip it: treating Lucas's "scam" comment as overriding Luceno's novel, or making the JA Kun > DE Palpatine bind stick.
- Date: 2026-10-20.

- Date: 2026-10-29. Notes: `notes/daily-research/2026-10-29-session.md`.
