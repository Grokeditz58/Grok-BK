# Troy Denning on UnuThul (Raynar Thul) vs Darth Caedus (Jacen Solo) (searched 2026-11-11, CST)

## Bottom line
- **I found no out-of-universe statement by Denning (interview, chat, Q&A, or post) saying or implying that UnuThul is more powerful than Caedus.** I found no fan post that cites one either. The claim the maintainer heard looks unsourced.
- The text Denning wrote himself leans the **other way**. In Abyss, Raynar was *not* too powerful to capture alive, but Jacen *was* too powerful to capture. In Joiner King, Raynar/UnuThul says the Colony is not strong enough to control Jacen. The best pro-UnuThul line is Han's opinion in Swarm War that "Raynar's more powerful than Luke". It compares Raynar to Luke, not to Caedus, and Luke later overpowers Raynar in ch. 25.

## IN-TEXT lines (novels written by Denning), verbatim

### 1. Fate of the Jedi: Abyss (2009), ch. 1 (opening Asylum Block scene, Leia POV narration)
> Years later, Raynar had reappeared as UnuThul, the badly disfigured, insane Joiner who was leading the Killik Colony's expansion into the Chiss territories. Fortunately, Raynar had not proven too powerful to capture alive, and he had been residing in the Asylum Block for more than seven years while Cilghal helped him put his mind back together.

Later in the same scene:
> Leia's relief was bittersweet. As happy as she was to see Raynar leaving his cell, it made her wish that incarceration and rehabilitation had been possible for her son Jacen. But Jacen had been too powerful to capture and too destructive to leave free, and in the end there had been no choice except to hunt him down.

Text source: online copy of Abyss (epdf.tips/star-wars-fate-of-the-jedi-abyss.html). The second passage is also quoted in the SI Caedus vs Kyp debate (https://www.suspectinsightforums.com/t241-ss-the-tyrannical-ten-darth-caedus-dc77-vs-kyp-durron-sitharchaeologist).
Read: an explicit contrast between the two characters, in the strongest direct comparison in Denning's text, and it favors Jacen/Caedus. It is Leia's POV, and "capture" depends on context as well as raw power.

### 2. Dark Nest I: The Joiner King (2005), ch. 31 (pre-Sith Jacen)
> "Jacen convinced Tenel Ka, or you used Jacen to convince her?" Jaina was thinking of how Raynar had nearly forced her to leave just a few moments earlier—and of the irresistible call that had summoned her and the others to the Colony in the first place. "Your touch can be very compelling."
> "Perhaps, but even we are not strong enough to control Jacen," Raynar said. "He has moved beyond our control—or anyone else's. You know that yourself."

Sources: local read notes (notes/materials/051-dark-nest-trilogy/jk-04.md, ch. 31), Wookieepedia's Jacen quote, and the SI Caedus vs Tyranus debate. I did not check it against a page image this turn.
Read: UnuThul, speaking for the whole Colony ("we"), says it can't control Jacen. This is mind-control resistance, and it is 2005 Jacen, before he became Caedus.

### 3. Dark Nest III: The Swarm War (2006), ch. 2 (Jedi council debate)
> "May the Force be with you on that!" Han scoffed. "You'll need it. Raynar's more powerful than Luke, and he doesn't want your help."
> Luke cocked his brow at Han's assessment of his relative strength, but looked more surprised than insulted and did not protest.
> "Corran, think about what you're asking," Leia said. "Exactly how do you suggest we help Raynar? You know how difficult it is to hold a regular Jedi against his will, and Raynar's resources are immensely more vast. ..."

Earlier in the same scene, Leia: "But Raynar's mind was shattered by the Flier's crash, and the Killiks have a very fluid concept of truth."
Source: online copy at https://bookreadfree.com/519802/12765146 (Swarm War p.5); chapter number from the local notes sw-01.md.
Read: the best pro-UnuThul line, but it is Han's opinion, the comparison is to Luke, and Caedus isn't mentioned.

### 4. The Swarm War, ch. 20 (Jacen to Luke, before Tenupe)
> "Good," Jacen said. "Now use what you are feeling. Your anger and your grief can make you more powerful. Use them when you meet Raynar and Lomi Plo, and you will defeat them."
> ...
> Jacen's face grew worried. "But you're one against two, and they'll have the Force potential of the entire Colony to draw on. You'll need all the power you can get!"
> "No," Luke said. "I'll need strength—and that comes from my way of using the Force."

Source: https://bookreadfree.com/519802/12765335 (p.26); chapter from the local notes sw-03.md ("Ch. 20 (cont.)").
Read: this is Jacen's own assessment of UnuThul. What Jacen stresses is that UnuThul draws on "the Force potential of the entire Colony", which fits the "draws power from the Killiks" argument. He does not compare UnuThul to himself.

### 5. The Swarm War, ch. 25 (Luke vs Raynar), for context
> ... he became the very essence of the immovable object.
> "What am I going to do with you, Raynar Thul? ... You learn nothing from your mistakes."
(Fragments from local notes sw-03.md. A fuller quote is on KMC: https://www.killermovies.com/forums/657102-Lukes-feat-in-Dark-Nest, including "Raynar was no common Jedi. He had the Force potential of the Colony to draw on".)

## Verdict
Not found and likely not real as a Denning statement. Denning's own text, at its most explicit (Abyss ch. 1), implies Jacen/Caedus was the harder one to deal with. What Denning stresses about UnuThul is power borrowed from the Colony ("the Force potential of the entire Colony to draw on"), not his own strength or skill.

### (c) Verdict and inferences
- On the page, Denning consistently writes **Luke > Caedus**: the effortless hold in Inferno Ch. 11, "Luke had beaten him" in Ch. 20, and "I win" in Invincible Ch. 5.
- The *closeness* material is real but narrower:
  1. Out of universe: "even Luke COULD have lost to him."
  2. The Inferno duel is close and costly. Luke is pinned, his knee pops, his nose breaks, and he is garroted. Caedus is "permacrete" and fed by pain. Caedus thinks Ben's stab "probably" saved him, and in Invincible he says he "barely managed to survive."
  3. Caedus out-hides Luke (Tempest Ch. 16), and Mara thinks "It'll take both of us."
  4. Luke would need to be "fortunate enough to kill him" (Inferno Ch. 11, Caedus POV).
- INFERENCE: these support "Luke is clearly ahead but not untouchably beyond Caedus." They do NOT support "evenly matched." Caedus's POV after Inferno concedes the loss outright.
