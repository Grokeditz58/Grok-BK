Compiled Nov 11, 2026 (CST) for the maintainer (request pt444u).

**How this was checked**
- **[EPUB]** means checked word for word this session against the licensed ebook text on the box. This covers Bloodlines, Tempest, Exile, Sacrifice, Inferno and Fury. Chapter numbers come from the ebook chapter files and match the printed "chapter N" headings.
- **[NOTES]** means taken from my earlier full-read notes. Those notes say every quote was script-checked against the book at the time, but I did NOT re-check them this session. This covers Betrayal, Revelation and Invincible, whose book text isn't on the box.
- **[FAN-TX]** means it matches a fan forum transcription only and hasn't been checked against a book file.
- "INFERENCE" marks my reading. Everything in quotation marks is verbatim.
- Authors: Betrayal, Exile and Fury are by Aaron Allston. Bloodlines, Sacrifice and Revelation are by Karen Traviss. Tempest, Inferno and Invincible are by Troy Denning.

## (4) MAIN COUNTERPOINTS (what the maintainer will face)
- **Inferno Ch. 20, Caedus's own view** [EPUB]: "Luke had beaten him. Luke had just kept coming despite his injuries. He had inflicted more damage on Caedus than he had suffered himself". Denning's own book has the loser concede.
- **Inferno Ch. 11** [EPUB]: Luke pins Caedus "with no visible effort … with no apparent exertion"; "Had it been Mara's death instead of Omas's that Luke had just heard about, Caedus knew he would already be dead." (Surprise caveat: "Luke had taken him by surprise".)
- **Invincible Ch. 5** [NOTES]: "It's worse," Luke said. "I win." Luke's foresight says he wins. The problem is the darkness that follows, not the outcome.
- **Invincible Ch. 2** [FAN-TX]: "I know I'm the only one who can be sure of stopping him".
- **Invincible Ch. 9** [NOTES]: Luke is "the one swordsman in the galaxy whom Caedus did not dare fight one-armed". (Fury Ch. 11's "excepting possibly Luke" also concedes Luke may be the better swordsman.)
- **Revelation Ch. 12** [NOTES]: Luke rams, de-guns and Force-crushes Caedus's StealthX. "If I wanted to kill you, I could have done it several times over by now."
- **Sacrifice Ch. 7** [EPUB]: "Luke can still take you in a lightsaber fight."
- **Bloodlines Ch. 17** [EPUB]: Jacen is "more powerful than any of them except Luke".
- **Sacrifice Ch. 21, Mara's last words** [EPUB]: "But Luke will crush you".
- **Wookieepedia** (secondary, not book text): "not as powerful as his uncle in a direct contest of strength … unable to break his uncle's Force grip."
- **Rebuttals to expect:**
  - Fury Ch. 2 is Allston's flattering retelling and contradicts Denning's Inferno Ch. 20.
  - "Jacen was Luke's match" (Fury Ch. 36) is one author's framing.
  - Caedus's "best shot wasn't good enough" (Revelation) and "couldn't kill me" (Invincible) are rationalizations the books refute.
  - The stealth wins came with conditions: they were stacked with Lumiya's cloaking, Luke was distracted, and Jacen needed "all his strength".
  - The Betrayal vision is a branch that never happens.
  - Denning himself added that it was Jaina, not Luke, who took Caedus down.

## Bottom line (INFERENCE)
- Strongest "not a certainty" material:
  1. Denning's "even Luke COULD have lost to him".
  2. Betrayal's vision of Jacen killing Luke across timelines.
  3. Fury's "Jacen was Luke's match", from Luke's own view.
  4. Inferno's costly duel ("battered", "permacrete", the garrote, "probably" saved by Ben's stab) and Invincible's "barely managed to survive".
  5. Luke's grief and taint handicaps.
- The mainline texts still lean Luke (Inferno Ch. 11/20, Revelation, Invincible "I win"). The honest framing is "Luke favored, not guaranteed", not "Caedus wins."
