> Among the founders of the early Jedi Order on Tython was a Master known as Rajivari. Though wise and powerful, Rajivari became dissatisfied with the direction the Jedi were taking. He argued with his peers, who thought his teachings drew too close to the dark side. Eventually Rajivari was cast out of the Order. Rajivari did not go into exile quietly. Instead he raised an army of fanatical apprentices and strange creatures, and attempted to destroy the leaders of the Jedi. However, Rajivari's army was defeated at Kaleth and Rajivari himself died in the final battle.
