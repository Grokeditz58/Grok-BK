Note (our notes, around the quote): Welcome everyone to my first ever Respect Thread (  ). I decided to make a thread for a more obscure character but powerful character that I would surmise most people are not familiar with, so lets get to it. Lord Tagriss was a member of the Dread Host who served as the main military force for the Dread Masters. He held the title of Dread Lord (fun fact: he is the only member of the Dread Host with this title) and acted as an agent of Dread Master Bestia who was noted to be "perhaps the most powerful of the Dread Masters". He was given the task of leading a contingent of the Dread Host to steal the Seeds of Rage from the Emperor's Arcanum and planting them on numerous planets across the galaxy in order to drive entire planetary populations mad. He acts as the final boss to the short Seeker Droid questline in a Heroic 4 mission (quests designed for four players) which takes place in-between the events of Makeb and the Dread War storyline which would place it sometime during 3639 BBY. The protagonists first meet Lord Tagriss on the Arcanum and then encounter him again on Illum, where they fight to the death. [image: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/emojione/assets/png/2122.png?v=2.2.7]
