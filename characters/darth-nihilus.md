# Darth Nihilus

Evidence file for Darth Nihilus, the Lord of Hunger, Sith Lord of the Sith Triumvirate after the Jedi Civil War, **Star Wars Legends** continuity.

**How to read this file**

- Quotes are word for word from the source texts, with whitespace and curly quotes normalized. `[…]` marks an omission inside a quote; quotes are extended to the full paragraph.
- The source is the last line inside each code block, so one copy grabs both. Novels carry a chapter number; novellas, short stories and journals have no chapters and are cited by title only.
- **†** after a source means the quote comes from a published source we could not check against the original. Treat those as secondhand.
- Entries about the same fight are split: what he does well is under the feat sections, what goes wrong is under Limitations.
- Sources read for this rebuild: the dialogue transcripts of Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords on StrategyWiki (Kreia, Visas Marr, Ravager Command Deck and Ravager Bridge pages), downloaded for this rebuild; game lines were matched word for word to those transcripts and name the speaker and the person addressed; Darth Plagueis (checked against the text on file); Legacy Book 1 (the repo's notes and panels); the guide and sourcebook quotes collected in the old version of this file (Prima KOTOR II guide, KOTOR Campaign Guide, The Old Republic Encyclopedia, The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia).
- Game quotes are still marked † because the transcripts are fan-made, not the game files. Stage directions from the transcripts are not quoted.
- Forum and debate material from the old file is not carried over, nor are lines the old file misattributed to Nihilus (HK-47 and others speaking about Revan, and a Legacy panel of Darth Nihl). Canon material is not covered.
- Checklist pass 2026-11-19 (Part 6.4): added the KOTOR Campaign Guide account of his hunger and his betrayal of Traya. No d20 or Saga stat block text for Nihilus is quoted in our notes (only a page summary).
- This file has 50 entries and 51 quotes (50 marked †).

## Growth and Evolution

### 1. Visas says Nihilus cannot resist a gathering of Jedi for long, and that with the Jedi vanishing he may grow strong enough to eradicate all life merely with his presence.

```
He cannot deny his hunger for long - and any gathering of Jedi is something he cannot long resist. And now that the Jedi are vanishing, I do not know what will happen - perhaps he will grow strong enough to eradicate all life, merely with his presence.

— Visas Marr to the Exile, Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords (Obsidian Entertainment, 2004) †
```

### 3. The KOTOR Campaign Guide: a Mandalorian Wars survivor discovers that his emptiness hungers; Traya teaches him to harness his life-draining gift "to radical heights", and he saps her own powers in a double-cross, until "Nihilus has become the hunger".

Victim of the Mandalorian Wars, the man who became Nihilus lost everything: his possessions, his loved ones, and the will to live. While illness cannibalizes his body, a vast emptiness devours his humanity. Then, when all seems lost, he discovers that his emptiness hungered. The first time, he fed it unconsciously. Draining another being's life is frightening, nauseating, but for a euphoric moment, the memories, the illness, and the hunger disappeared.

```

## Overall Force Power

### 4. Visas explains that Katarr was not destroyed with machines or weapons: Nihilus is "a wound in the Force, more presence than flesh, and in his wake, life dies."

```
It was not a thing done with machines or weapons. The Force is far more terrible, and it touches more lives than any machine can hope to slay. For every one that feels the Force, strongly, deeply, each one feels and perceives it in their own way. You have strengths, whether you know it or not. And my master has his. His power is great, and it comes from hunger. He is a wound in the Force, more presence than flesh, and in his wake, life dies... sacrificing itself to his hunger. And those who feel the Force strongly are beacons to his hunger. My people, my planet, would have been attacked in time, it was inevitable, yet we could do nothing about it.

— Visas Marr to the Exile, Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords (Obsidian Entertainment, 2004) †
```

## Force Knowledge and Training

### 17. Kreia says his power is subtle, and that what Nihilus does is the pinnacle of what the Force-feeding Sith assassins could achieve in time.

```
Nothing is impossible with the Force. It is an energy that flows through all living things. And like energy, it may be harnessed, channeled, and consumed at times. It may even be a substance that can burn and ignite. Do not think of his power as one would a weapon, or one of your warships of the Republic. It is terrible, but it is still a subtle thing. The sect of assassins that chase you feed on the Force... what he does is simply the pinnacle of what they could achieve, in time. And that is why they - and their techniques - must be wiped out. No one again must experience and learn what her master did.

— Kreia to the Exile, Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords (Obsidian Entertainment, 2004) †
```

## Limitations

### 40. RULED BY THE HUNGER: Kreia says the power cannot truly be controlled and leaves nothing to conquer: "it rules him, not the other way around."

```
Because it is not something that can ever truly be controlled... and it leaves nothing to conquer in its wake. And it rules him, not the other way around. It has its own will, its own instincts.

### 41. DEVOURED BY HIS OWN HUNGER: Kreia says there is no strength in his hunger; it devours him as he devours others, and "He is already dead."

```
Power? Do you think so? You would be wrong. There is no strength in the hunger he possesses... and the will behind his power is a primal thing. And it devours him as he devours others - his mere presence kills all around him, slowly, feeding him. He is already dead, it is simply a question of how many he kills before he falls.

### 42. CANNOT DIRECT IT: Kreia says his power is not something he can direct or focus; Force-rich worlds draw him, and Katarr called to him like a beacon.

```
It is not something he can direct or focus, much like hunger itself. He is more of a hole in the Force than a living thing. Force Sensitives and worlds rich in the Force draw him. The Miraluka world was one such place. That is why where the Jedi gather, Jedi will die. He will feel it, unless they mask their presence - but Katarr called out as a beacon to him, and he could not resist it. And he cares nothing for the Sith or its teachings... or the Jedi. And when the Jedi are dead, he will feed on the galaxy, the Republic, and eventually, consume the Sith as well.
