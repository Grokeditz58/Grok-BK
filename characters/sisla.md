Through the grate, you see the same ominous black liquid you saw outside covering the floor of the warehouse to an unknown depth 10 meters below you. The mezzanine is filled with old crates and boxes in various states of disintegration. The lighting is very dim, with only scattered light fixtures and the occasional beam of light peeking in through a discolored window.
