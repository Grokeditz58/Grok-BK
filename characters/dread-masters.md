> Despite Revan's influence, the Emperor's will could not be stopped, only slowed. Over the next 200 years, the Emperor advanced his plans for a new invasion. However, Revan's plot and near-successful assassination attempt unsettled the Emperor immensely, and as a result, the Empire's ruler delved into further studies of the dark side. In his studies, the Emperor discovered the rare technique of essence transfer to preserve his physical form from harm.
