# Jaina Solo

Evidence file for Jaina Solo, **Star Wars Legends** continuity. Each entry is a short description of what she does or what is said about her, followed by the full quote in a code block with its source. Anti-feats and limitations are in their own section at the end, each backed by quotes.

**How to read this file**

- Quotes are copied word for word from the sources, with whitespace and curly quotes normalized. `[…]` marks an omission inside a quote.

- The source line is the last line inside each code block, so one copy gets the quote and its citation. Novels are cited with the chapter where we could find it.

- **†** after a source means the quote was transcribed from a published source that was not re-checked against our own copy of the text (film dialogue, DVD commentary, guides, comics). Everything else was matched against the text on file.

- This file has 40 entries and 40 quotes.

## Lightsaber Skill and Combat Mastery

### 1. In Invincible, when a mysterious substance rains down in a Verpine tunnel, Jaina has no Force technique better than what Fett and the Verpine are doing, so she activates her lightsaber, squats and lays the blade flat, using it like a broom to burn the stuff away from her.

```
Jaina had no idea what the stuff was—and it was coming too fast to waste time wondering. When she could not think of a Force technique that would be more effective than what Fett and the Verpine were doing, she simply activated her lightsaber and squatted, laying the blade as flat as she could and using it like a broom to keep the stuff burned away from her.

— Invincible (Troy Denning, 2008), ch. 1
```

## Force Powers

### 12. In a loading vault, Jaina rushes into the darkness extending her Force awareness along the wall and ceiling, senses a sniper and spotter hiding on a high balcony, and, without reaching for a blaster or leaping, grabs and pulls them with the Force.

```
Jaina smiled, more in satisfaction than bloodlust, and rushed into the darkness. Extending her Force awareness high along the wall and ceiling of the loading vault, she sensed a human presence. Two presences—sniper and spotter, hiding on an observation balcony high above the battle. She did not reach for a blaster or a glow rod or try to leap up to their hiding place. She just grabbed and pulled, using the Force to jerk them both forward.

— Invincible (Troy Denning, 2008), ch. 1

## Fights and Feats

### 25. When a Yuuzhan Vong warrior flicks a coufee at her throat, Jaina bats the weapon aside with her free hand and releases the dark power inside her; a fork of lightning blasts a hole through his chest and hurls him onto the rubble.

```
In a motion so fast Jaina barely saw it, the warrior sat up and flicked his coufee at her throat. She could have dodged or blocked with her lightsaber, but she did not. Instead, with the fierce energy crackling inside her, she used her free hand to bat the weapon aside, then raised her hand toward her attacker and released the dark power inside. A fork of lightning crackled into existence a few centimeters beyond her glove tips, then blasted a hole through the Yuuzhan Vong’s chest and hurled him onto the rubble pile smoking and motionless.

— Star by Star (Troy Denning, 2011), ch. Forty
```

### 26. In the Caedus duel, Jaina is blasted by a Force blow that sends her flying and her lightsaber free of his stomach, but from the Nickel One fight she has learned to tuck her chin so her head does not snap back as she strikes the corridor wall.

```
The invisible fist of a Force blast slammed Jaina in the chest and sent her flying back, her breath groaning from her lungs and her lightsaber hissing free of Caedus’s stomach. From the fight on Nickel One, she had learned the dangers of letting her head snap back on impact. She tucked her chin, then fought to hold it there as she struck the durasteel wall on the far side of the corridor.

— Invincible (Troy Denning, 2008), ch. 18
```

## Limitations

*These are anti-feats, limits and caveats, each followed by the evidence behind it.*

```
