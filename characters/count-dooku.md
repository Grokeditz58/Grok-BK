# Count Dooku / Darth Tyranus

Evidence file for Count Dooku, **Star Wars Legends** continuity, from his Padawan years (*Legacy of the Jedi*, *Dark Rendezvous*) through his fall, the Clone Wars as Darth Tyranus, and his death aboard the *Invisible Hand*. Each entry is a short description of what he does or what is said about him, then the full quote with its source on the last line inside the same block. Anti-feats and limitations have their own section at the end, each backed by quotes.

**How to read this file**

- Quotes are word for word from the source texts, with whitespace and curly quotes normalized. `[…]` marks an omission inside a quote; quotes are extended to the full paragraph.
- The source is the last line inside each code block, so one copy grabs both. Novels carry a chapter number; novellas, short stories and journals have no chapters and are cited by title only.
- **†** after a source means the quote comes from a published source we could not check against the original. Treat those as secondhand.
- Entries about the same fight are split: what he does well is under the feat sections, what goes wrong is under Limitations.
- Sources read for this rebuild: Revenge of the Sith (novelization and junior novelization), Attack of the Clones (novelization and junior novelization), Yoda: Dark Rendezvous, Legacy of the Jedi, Labyrinth of Evil, Darth Plagueis, The Clone Wars (Traviss), The Clone Wars: Wild Space, The Clone Wars: Gambit: Siege and The Cestus Deception; comic: Star Wars: Jedi – Count Dooku (2003).
- Comic quotes were transcribed from the page scans of the digital issue. The all-caps lettering is set in sentence case, bold emphasis is dropped, and each balloon is labeled with its speaker where the art makes the speaker clear.
- Narration from the novels is under Author and Guide Statements; what characters say or think is under Statements from Other Characters.
- Canon material (including the Clone Wars TV series and Dooku: Jedi Lost) is not covered.

## Force Sense and Precognition

### 1. On Vjun, Dooku tracks the hidden Yoda through the Force, feeling the shock when the Master steps against the dark current "like a distant groundquake," and then only "a faint sensation of heat on Dooku's skin."

```
He was moving carefully, quietly, hiding his presence in the Force; riding on its back like a leaf whirled gently down a stream. But on Vjun, the Force was bent mightily to the dark side, and every now and then the Master opposed its current. It was these moments Dooku was listening for. Once, several minutes ago, the old Jedi had misstepped, putting a foot against the current, and the shock of it had rumbled through the very bedrock beneath Château Malreaux, announcing the Master’s coming like a distant groundquake.

Or maybe it hadn’t been a mistake. Maybe Yoda wanted Dooku to know he was on his way.

Since then the world had been silent. The old Jedi was moving like a water-skeeter over the surface of the Force, with nothing to herald his coming but a faint sensation of heat on Dooku’s skin, as if he were a blind man at sunrise, the dawn invisible to him but for a pale, spreading warmth.

— Yoda: Dark Rendezvous (Sean Stewart, 2004), ch. 10
```
