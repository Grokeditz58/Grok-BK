## Foresight and Leadership

### 1. Augwynne Djo still rules the Singing Mountain Clan.

```
“Does Augwynne Djo still rule here?”

“Yes. My great-grandmother.”

— Shadow Academy (Kevin J. Anderson and Rebecca Moesta, 1995), ch. 11
```

### 2. Augwynne foresaw Luke and Tenel Ka's coming.

```
“Augwynne told us you would come,” she said.

The woman inclined her head slightly. “Augwynne has guessed this much. She waits for you in the war room.”

And welcome great-granddaughter Tenel Ka Chume Ta’ Djo.”

— Shadow Academy (Kevin J. Anderson and Rebecca Moesta, 1995), ch. 11
```

### 3. Augwynne calls the Council of Sisters.

```
“Then we have no other choice,” Augwynne said with finality. “You must present your case before the Council of Sisters.”

— Shadow Academy (Kevin J. Anderson and Rebecca Moesta, 1995), ch. 11
```

### 4. Augwynne Djo's image: reptile skin, ceremonial helm, riding a tamed rancor.

```
She pictured Augwynne Djo's proud form clad in reptile skin, a ceremonial helm on her head, riding a domesticated rancor. The taming of these ferocious beasts was an appropriate symbol of the courage of her rugged people, since the huge beasts were powerful and their sharp claws deadly.

— Lightsabers (Kevin J. Anderson and Rebecca Moesta, 1996), ch. 3
```

## Statements and Beliefs

### 5. Augwynne warns that the new Nightsisters are subtle and allied with the Empire.

```
“They are not Nightsisters as you and I knew them,” she said. “Not wizened crones with discolored skin, who rotted from the nightspells they spoke.” She shook her head. “No, they are a newly formed order of Nightsisters, young and fair, and allied with the Empire.”

“Their evil is subtle. They tame and ride rancors as we do. They dress as warriors, if they choose. They are not even all women … but they are children of darkness. They are dangerous, with new goals. Do not seek them out.”

— Shadow Academy (Kevin J. Anderson and Rebecca Moesta, 1995), ch. 11
```

### 6. Augwynne sends Tenel Ka off: 'Never concede to evil.'

```
“Daughter of my daughter’s daughter,” she said, “go in safety. The thoughts of the Singing Mountain Clan are with you.”

“I am proud of what you do for your friends. You are a true warrior woman of our clan. Always remember our most sacred rule from the Book of Laws: ‘Never concede to evil.”’

— Shadow Academy (Kevin J. Anderson and Rebecca Moesta, 1995), ch. 17
```

### 7. Augwynne Djo taught Tenel Ka to swim: the strongest hunters cannot be stopped by a lake or river.

```
Augwynne Djo, mother of Teneniel Djo, Tenel Ka's mother, had taught her to swim, saying that the strongest hunters and warriors were those who could not be stopped by a mere lake or river. Ta'a Chume, on the other hand, matriarch of the Royal House of Hapes and mother of Tenel Ka's father, Prince Isolder, had taught swimming as a defense against assassins or kidnappers. In fact, her grandmother had once escaped an attempt on her life by jumping from a wavespeeder into a lake and swimming for shore underwater, so that the would-be assassins assumed she had drowned.

— Lightsabers (Kevin J. Anderson and Rebecca Moesta, 1996), ch. 3
```
