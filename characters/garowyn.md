## Background and Ship

### 1. Garowyn's quantum-armored ship, the Shadow Chaser.

```
The ship was not large, probably built to carry a dozen people, but its lines were clean and smooth, almost inviting Tenel Ka to run her hand along its side. No carbon scoring stained the hull; its surface bore no pits, dents, or evidence of the meteorites commonly encountered in space and atmosphere. The overall design seemed vaguely Imperial, but Tenel Ka could not identify it as any type of craft she had ever seen before.

“Quantum armor?”

“The Shadow Chaser is state-of-the-art. At the moment she’s the only one of her kind.”

“Our journey will take two standard days,” Garowyn said, moving past them and out of the cockpit.

No expense was spared for the Shadow Chaser.”

— Shadow Academy (Kevin J. Anderson and Rebecca Moesta, 1995), ch. 19
```

### 2. Garowyn's introduction: petite but powerfully built, a warrior woman of Dathomir.

```
Though petite, the newcomer was powerfully built. Long, light brown hair streaked with bronze fell to her waist, secured with just enough braids and thongs to keep it out of her way, as befitted a warrior woman of Dathomir.

Her features were refined, and her creamy-brown skin was set off to perfection by her tight-fitting red lizard-skin armor. The black knee-length cape she wore open at the front seemed to be her only outward concession to the fact that she was a Nightsister, and Tenel Ka guessed from the haughty set of her mouth and her shrewd eyes that Garowyn did not often make concessions.

— Shadow Academy (Kevin J. Anderson and Rebecca Moesta, 1995), ch. 19
```

## Force Powers

### 3. Garowyn levitates Tenel Ka a meter above the cabin floor.

```
Garowyn gave a derisive chuckle. “Your pride in self-sufficiency is your weakness.” With that, she closed her hazel eyes, flung her head back, and stretched an arm out toward Tenel Ka.

She was floating a meter above the cabin floor. Tenel Ka stifled a gasp of outrage and attempted to use her mind to wrench herself free.

“Yes,” she said in a guttural, triumphant voice, “try to resist me. Use your anger.”

— Shadow Academy (Kevin J. Anderson and Rebecca Moesta, 1995), ch. 19
```

## Limitations

### 7. Garowyn's hold wavers when Tenel Ka goes limp, and Tenel Ka breaks free.

```
Realizing that this was exactly what she had been doing, Tenel Ka went limp.

As she did so, Garowyn lost her grip slightly, and Tenel Ka wobbled in midair. So, she mused, the Nightsister is not as strong as she thinks she is.

Garowyn was still amusing herself with Tenel Ka’s “struggles” when, with a practiced flick of her wrist, Tenel Ka flung out her line; the grappling hook caught securely on its intended target. Before the Nightsister could notice, Tenel Ka went completely limp again. When Garowyn’s grasp wavered again, Tenel Ka jerked on the line and wrenched herself free, falling to the floor and landing painfully on her rear. She looked up to see Garowyn’s petite form towering over her. But instead of an angry rebuke, all she heard from the Nightsister was a short, sharp bark of amazed laughter.

— Shadow Academy (Kevin J. Anderson and Rebecca Moesta, 1995), ch. 19
```

### 8. Luke lifts Garowyn and she cannot break free, then he seals her in an escape pod.

```
“No,” Luke cut in. “I believe I could lift a person.”

She crossed her arms over her chest, and her hazel eyes dared Luke to move her. Suddenly, her eyes grew wide with astonishment and confusion as her feet drifted off the floor and she rose a full meter and a half into the air.

“I can see that it is time to teach you the power of the dark side as well,” she snapped haughtily She closed her eyes and wrenched with all her might.

Tenel Ka sensed that Luke loosened his grip, but only partially. Garowyn still floated above the deck, but he allowed the force of her movement to turn her around and send her into a dizzying spin.

Within moments they had the gyrating, disoriented Nightsister deposited and sealed within the pod. Tenel Ka’s hand hovered above the automatic jettison switch. Luke nodded. With great satisfaction, she triggered the launch.

With a whoosh and a thump, the escape pod containing Garowyn shot out into deep space.

— Shadow Academy (Kevin J. Anderson and Rebecca Moesta, 1995), ch. 19
```
