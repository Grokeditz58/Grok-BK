# Obi-Wan Kenobi

Evidence file for Obi-Wan Kenobi (Ben Kenobi), **Star Wars Legends** continuity, from his apprenticeship under Qui-Gon Jinn through the Clone Wars, his exile on Tatooine and his appearances as a spirit of the Force. Each entry is a short description of what he does or what is said about him, then the full quote with its source on the last line inside the same block. Anti-feats and limitations have their own section at the end, each backed by quotes.

**How to read this file**

- Quotes are word for word from the source texts, with whitespace and curly quotes normalized. `[…]` marks an omission inside a quote; quotes are extended to the full paragraph.
- The source is the last line inside each code block, so one copy grabs both. Novels carry a chapter number; novellas, short stories and journals have no chapters and are cited by title only.
- **†** after a source means the quote comes from a published source we could not check against the original. Treat those as secondhand.
- Entries about the same fight are split: what he does well is under the feat sections, what goes wrong is under Limitations.
- Sources read for this rebuild: the Revenge of the Sith, Attack of the Clones and The Phantom Menace novelizations; Star Wars: From the Adventures of Luke Skywalker; The Empire Strikes Back and Return of the Jedi novelizations; Kenobi (John Jackson Miller); Labyrinth of Evil; Rogue Planet; The Cestus Deception; Wild Space and the two Gambit novels (Karen Miller); Jedi Apprentice 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 9, 12 and 13, and Jedi Apprentice Special Edition: Deceptions.
- Narration and guide text is under Author and Guide Statements; what characters say or think is under Statements from Other Characters.
- Canon material (including the Clone Wars TV series and the Obi-Wan Kenobi series) is not covered.
- This file has 167 entries and 167 quotes (0 marked †).
