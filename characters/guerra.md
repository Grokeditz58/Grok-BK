## Feats and Skills

### 1. Guerra's rubbery arm shoots across two sleeping platforms to grab Obi-Wan's wrist; his slap on the back sends Obi-Wan flying.

```
Guerra’s arm shot out suddenly. It reached across two sleeping platforms and fastened on Obi-Wan’s wrist. “I don’t have all day. The guards will be here with electro-jabbers for both of us if I don’t get you outfitted.”

Guerra chortled and slapped Obi-Wan on the back, sending him flying.

— Jedi Apprentice 2: The Dark Rival (Jude Watson, 1999), ch. 11
```

### 2. Guerra rigs a sling from a spun carbon tarp under the platform and, with his long, flexible arms, catches Obi-Wan's fall and pulls him to safety.

```
But as he raced toward Obi-Wan, a movement caught his eye from a lower level of the platform. Someone had fashioned a kind sling out of a spun carbon tarp. He was tying it to the struts that supported the main platform. As Qui-Gon watched, two long, flexible arms shot out, positioning the sling in midair.

Obi-Wan seemed to grab on to thin air and pull himself to the left, shifting in mid-fall. He bounced onto the middle of the sling. In another second, those long arms shot out and pulled Obi-Wan to safety.

— Jedi Apprentice 2: The Dark Rival (Jude Watson, 1999), ch. 14
```

## Statements and Beliefs

### 3. Guerra: 'I trust no one... Ever. That's why after three years I am still alive'; at the end: 'Not so, Obawan! I trust you.'

```
“I trust no one,” Guerra said softly. “Ever. That’s why after three years I am still alive.”

Guerra smiled weakly. “I . . . trust . . . no one,’ he said softly. But as Obi-Wan and Qui-Gon deflected blaster fire, he managed to limp to the stairs. Guerra turned. “Not so, Obawan! I trust you.”

— Jedi Apprentice 2: The Dark Rival (Jude Watson, 1999), ch. 13, 14
```
