## Feats and Skills

### 1. Dying from a salt injection, Si Treemba still refuses to betray Obi-Wan.

```
“You don’t look so good,” the Hutt sneered. “We gave you enough salt in that saline injection to deplete all the dactyl in your body.” He leaned back again and chortled. “So why don’t you tell us what we want to know? It beats dying. Someone was with you. Who was it? Arconans never travel alone.”

Si Treemba shook his head again. His head lolled back, and his cheek hit the floor.

“What about your courage?” Obi-Wan countered. “Think about it, Si Treemba. You were dying, and you still would not betray me. You just faced down a Hutt!”

— Jedi Apprentice 1: The Rising Force (Dave Wolverton, 1999), ch. 11
```

### 2. On the decompressing bridge, Si Treemba crawls from handhold to handhold and plugs the hull breach with the spherical compass.

```
But before Obi-Wan could move, Si Treemba dropped to the floor. He crawled across the bridge, reaching for handhold after handhold. Obi-Wan could only hang on to the door frame and watch. He couldn’t stop Si Treemba, and he couldn’t help him.

Si Treemba reached for a spherical compass—the round metal object that served as a backup in case the main nav computer was hit or disabled. Fighting the screaming wind, Si Treemba stumbled to the hull and released the compass near the hole. The vacuum sucked it in, and immediately the rushing air quieted.

— Jedi Apprentice 1: The Rising Force (Dave Wolverton, 1999), ch. 13
```

### 3. In the caves the revived Arconans prove 'ferocious and cunning'; no tunneling draigon catches one by surprise.

```
The Offworld miners and the Jedi worked to guard the cave entrances, but draigons dug new entrances through the rock, so that at times they broke through and came at the miners from above or behind. That’s where the Arconans came in handy. By evening, it was evident to every Hutt and Whiphid on that rock that the Arconans were not cowards. They were creatures born to caves and to darkness, and when it came to time to fight in their own element, they proved themselves to be ferocious and cunning.

No draigon that tunneled through a cave’s roof caught an Arconan by surprise. Indeed, the Arconans were so fierce that the Whiphids and Hutts finally retreated and left them to finish the battle.

— Jedi Apprentice 1: The Rising Force (Dave Wolverton, 1999), ch. 23
```

### 4. Salt increases an Arconan's need for dactyl a hundredfold; without dactyl Arconans sicken and die within a day.

```
“Salt increases our need for dactyl a hundredfold,” Si Treemba explained. “It is a very dangerous substance to Arconans.”

The amusement in Clat’Ha's eyes instantly changed to worry. “Some of them are already beginning to fade,” she said quietly. “If they don’t get dactyl by tonight, they’ll start to sicken and die.”

— Jedi Apprentice 1: The Rising Force (Dave Wolverton, 1999), ch. 9, 18
```
