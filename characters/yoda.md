# Yoda

Evidence file for Yoda, **Star Wars Legends** continuity, from his teaching of Dooku (*Legacy of the Jedi*, *Dark Rendezvous*) through the Jedi Council of the prequel era, Geonosis, Vjun, his duel with Darth Sidious, and his last years on Dagobah. Each entry is a short description of what he does or what is said about him, then the full quote with its source on the last line inside the same block. Anti-feats and limitations have their own section at the end, each backed by quotes.

**How to read this file**

- Quotes are word for word from the source texts, with whitespace and curly quotes normalized. `[…]` marks an omission inside a quote; quotes are extended to the full paragraph.
- The source is the last line inside each code block, so one copy grabs both. Novels carry a chapter number; novellas, short stories and journals have no chapters and are cited by title only.
- **†** after a source means the quote comes from a published source we could not check against the original. Treat those as secondhand.
- Entries about the same fight are split: what he does well is under the feat sections, what goes wrong is under Limitations.
- Sources read for this rebuild: Yoda: Dark Rendezvous, Revenge of the Sith (novelization and junior novelization), Attack of the Clones (novelization and junior novelization), The Empire Strikes Back and Return of the Jedi (novelizations), The Phantom Menace (novelization), Legacy of the Jedi, The Clone Wars (Traviss), Labyrinth of Evil, Shatterpoint, Shadow Hunter, The Approaching Storm, Cloak of Deception, Darth Plagueis, Rogue Planet and Force Heretic III: Reunion; comic: Star Wars: Jedi – Yoda (2004).
- Comic quotes were transcribed from the page scans of the digital issue. The all-caps lettering is set in sentence case, bold emphasis is dropped, and each balloon is labeled with its speaker where the art makes the speaker clear.
- Narration from the novels is under Author and Guide Statements; what characters say or think is under Statements from Other Characters.
- Canon material (including the Clone Wars TV series) is not covered.
- This file has 81 entries and 81 quotes (0 marked †).

## Growth and Evolution

### 1. Meditating while he waits, Yoda finds that the contact he had been resisting succeeds at last: it is Qui-Gon Jinn, and Yoda thinks "Still much to learn, there is."

