## Feats and Skills

### 1. Clat'Ha is 'an able warrior' firing into the Togorian boarders, outnumbered twenty to one; clinging to a locker door, she blasts the Togorian captain off Qui-Gon.

```
Behind the fallen pirate, more Togorians rounded a corner and ran toward them. Clat’Ha, in a blind panic, pulled her own blaster and opened fire. One Togorian screamed in pain, its huge fangs gaping and showing blood.

Clat’Ha dropped low, screaming in rage. She was an able warrior, but they were outnumbered twenty to one. Qui-Gon vowed to do his best to keep her alive.

Suddenly the pirate’s head disappeared. The huge Togorian hurtled backward, grabbed by the fury of the wind.

Qui-Gon looked up the hall. Clat’Ha crouched on the floor, desperately clinging to the handle of a locker door with one hand, her heavy blaster in the other.

— Jedi Apprentice 1: The Rising Force (Dave Wolverton, 1999), ch. 13
```

## Statements and Beliefs

### 3. Clat'Ha: 'Then you must fight him... Jemba is no better than a daggerlip'; 'Her fierceness filled the room.'

```
“Then you must fight him,” Clat’Ha urged. “When you are faced with danger, you build walls and hide behind them. That is the Arconan way. But when a daggerlip tears down your walls, you fight. Jemba is no better than a daggerlip. He intends to destroy us. We can defeat him.”

Clat’Ha drew her blaster, and the Offworld miners raised their weapons and shields, prepared to fight. Obi-Wan studied the fiery woman. Her fierceness filled the room. All it needed was a spark to ignite.

— Jedi Apprentice 1: The Rising Force (Dave Wolverton, 1999), ch. 15
```
