# Darth Bane

Evidence file for Darth Bane, the Dark Lord who created the Rule of Two, **Star Wars Legends** continuity. Each entry is a short description of what he does or what is said about him, followed by the full quote in a code block with its source. Anti-feats and limitations are in their own section at the end, each backed by quotes.

**How to read this file**

- Quotes are copied word for word from the sources, with whitespace and curly quotes normalized. `[…]` marks an omission inside a quote.

- The source line is the last line inside each code block, so one copy gets the quote and its citation. Novels are cited with the chapter where we could find it.

- **†** after a source means the quote was transcribed from a published source that was not re-checked against our own copy of the text (film dialogue, DVD commentary, guides, comics). Everything else was matched against the text on file.

- This file has 224 entries and 224 quotes.

"Does Qordis approve?"
"The decision is mine, not his," Kas'im stated. He almost sounded offended. "I haven't held on to this blade for ten years just so Qordis can decide who I give it to."
Bane answered with a respectful bow, fully aware of the great honor that Kas'im had just bestowed upon him.
