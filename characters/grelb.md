## Feats and Skills

### 1. Grelb strangles and beats Obi-Wan unconscious aboard the Monument.

```
The Hutt pushed his huge, grotesque face close to Obi-Wan’s. “Any miner who doesn’t work for Offworld is the enemy,” he roared, shaking Obi-Wan roughly. “You, slug, are an enemy. And we don’t allow the enemy on Off-world turf.”

The Hutt’s fingers were like huge slabs of meat. They tightened around Obi-Wan’s neck, strangling him. Choking, Obi-Wan dropped his bags and grasped the Hutt’s fingers. His lungs burned and the room spun.

The last thing he remembered was the Hutt’s fist coming straight at him.

— Jedi Apprentice 1: The Rising Force (Dave Wolverton, 1999), ch. 6
```

### 2. Grelb has Si Treemba injected with enough salt to deplete his dactyl.

```
“You don’t look so good,” the Hutt sneered. “We gave you enough salt in that saline injection to deplete all the dactyl in your body.” He leaned back again and chortled. “So why don’t you tell us what we want to know? It beats dying. Someone was with you. Who was it? Arconans never travel alone.”

— Jedi Apprentice 1: The Rising Force (Dave Wolverton, 1999), ch. 11
```

## Statements and Beliefs

### 3. Theft, sabotage and murder are Grelb's methods; 'I did not know that the boy was a Jedi... If I had, he'd be dead by now.'

```
After picking himself up off the floor, Grelb said, “You’ve never complained about my methods before!” Theft, sabotage, and murder were Grelb’s methods, but he made sure that Off-world Mining always profited by them.

“I did not know that the boy was a Jedi when I gave him his first beating,” Grelb apologized. “If I had, he’d be dead by now. I promise, next time—”

— Jedi Apprentice 1: The Rising Force (Dave Wolverton, 1999), ch. 11
```

## Limitations

### 4. Obi-Wan rolls past Grelb's fists and burns his flank; Grelb's bulk makes him clumsy.

```
As the Hutt moved toward Obi-Wan, his enormous fists raised, Obi-Wan ducked and rolled in a classic Jedi defense maneuver. As he passed, he delivered a lightsaber blow to the Hutt’s flank. He heard the flesh sizzle.

Grelb roared in fury as he staggered back. His enormous bulk made him clumsy, and he fell onto the table, crushing the Whiphid’s legs even more. They howled in pain and beat against him with their fists.

Hutts were powerful, but they were not exactly light on their feet.

— Jedi Apprentice 1: The Rising Force (Dave Wolverton, 1999), ch. 11
```

### 5. Grelb fires at Obi-Wan, who senses and dodges the bolt; concentrating too hard, Grelb forgets to keep a lookout and a draigon takes him.

```
The Hutt chuckled softly. He saw his chance and squeezed the trigger on his blaster.

The bolt shot out—but to Grelb’s surprise, young Obi-Wan must have sensed it coming, for he dodged to the side. The bolt barely missed him.

Grelb shouted in rage and prepared to fire again. This time, he would not miss. But suddenly, he felt huge teeth rip into his tail.

He had been concentrating too hard. He had forgotten to keep a lookout. A draigon had found him.

He barely had time to cry out before the draigon yanked him from under his rock.

— Jedi Apprentice 1: The Rising Force (Dave Wolverton, 1999), ch. 22
```
