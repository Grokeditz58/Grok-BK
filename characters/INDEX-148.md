30 lines. Checkpoints CP1, CP2a, CP2b, and CP3a opened 20 of 51 crops. Thirty-one remain unchecked. CP1 remains the shieldship, the tight TIE, the TIE crawler, and the Sun Crusher, including the printed Qui Xux and "stole the Sun Crusher from the Empire." CP2a adds the A-9, the A-wing, the Chariot, the CloakShape, the TIE Advanced x1, and the Outrider. The A-9 and A-wing have no named character. CP2b adds the second Death Star, the Death Star prototype, the desert skiff, the E-wing Type B, the Eclipse, and the B-wing/E2. The prototype, E-wing, Eclipse, and B-wing/E2 crops name no person, so they add no character line. The Death Star II crop prints "the Emperor" and does not print Palpatine. The skiff crop does not say who rescued Han Solo or how. The companion crop `egvv1996-p180-scan109-tie-no-shields-casualties.jpg` was not replaced.
