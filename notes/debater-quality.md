# Suspect Insight debaters ranked by argument quality (not win/loss)

Compiled 2026-10-20 from the deep notes in [local path] (sections: sides, Result/judges, Neutral assessment) and debate-index.md. This replaces the records-only ranking in [local path] for questions about *how well* someone argues.

Criteria: how posts are structured; use of scans and verbatim quotes; whether rebuttals engage the other side; whether the scaling or proposals hold up (direct links vs long inference chains); consistency; how judges and spectators described the posts. Forum results are FORUM OPINION. Timeouts and concessions count only when the notes show *why* someone folded.

Coverage limit: Discord-only debates (STUB-DISCORD: #31-36, 38-40, 44-47, 49, 97, 99, 103-106, 115-120, 122, 124-128, 130-131) have no readable arguments, so people are graded only on their forum threads.
