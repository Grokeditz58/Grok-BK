## Introduction: "The Circle Is Now Complete" (p. 2)
- It frames the game as the link: "It is now time for the final chapter, which will link the previous two to the original three."
- It contrasts the game with playing with toys: battles were once re-enacted "with plastic action figures, with all motion and sound effects provided by the owners themselves."

## Anakin and Obi-Wan (pp. 3–5)
- **Anakin's bio** keeps the Palpatine angle: "a rift with the Jedi Order is forming due to their persecution of his friend and mentor, Chancellor Palpatine. His true destiny has become clouded."
- **Obi-Wan's bio:** "no amount of knowledge or experience could have prepared him for the challenge of training the Chosen One"; "Obi-Wan fears for his friend’s future—for his very soul".
- **Different control sets:**
  - Anakin's Force stun becomes **Force lightning**.
  - Obi-Wan's becomes the **Jedi Mind Trick**.
  - Anakin's critical attacks include "Critical Choke Impale" and "Force Impale".
  - Obi-Wan's include "Critical Arm Slice" and "Neck Strike".
- **Playing style:**
  - "As they become more experienced, their differences increase as Anakin turns to the dark side of the Force."
  - Anakin: "Anakin believes that the best defense is an overwhelming offense. The enemy can’t hurt you if he is destroyed."
  - Obi-Wan "is a Jedi Master whose style epitomizes mastery in classical Jedi training."

## Jedi Training (pp. 6–9)
- **One weapon:** "in this game you use a single weapon—the lightsaber."
- **Mechanics:**
  - Force deflect: hold Block and circle the right stick to send fire "180 degrees in front of your character back at the enemy".
  - Saber locks and shunts, the "Trap Shunt", the Universal Launcher and the Air Tech Escape.
- **Skill meter ratings:** Fair; Good (+150%); Impressive (+200%); Masterful (+300%). Under Masterful, "enemies cannot block your attacks."
- **Secrets:** health surge, Force surge and saber crystal.
- **Lore aside:** "A common mistake young Padawans or Jedi in training make is to focus primarily on their lightsaber skills."

## Allies and Enemies (pp. 10–23): bios plus tactics
- **Mace Windu**, "a diplomat skilled in the art of aggressive negotiations". He is convinced "a Sith Lord is manipulating events". Anakin duels him "about halfway through the story missions"; "you will not see a lot of jumping and wide slashing actions."
- **Cin Drallig:**
  - "a swordsman of nearly unparalleled skills, Cin Drallig represents the Jedi Order’s tenets in their purest form."
  - He lives "deep within the Jedi Temple"; Anakin duels him in his final Temple mission.
- **Serra Keto** (a game-original Jedi), Drallig's student:
  - "Serra’s headstrong and defiant ways confounded her Jedi Masters, nearly leading to her expulsion from the Jedi Order on more than one occasion."
  - She fights with dual lightsabers.
- **Yoda:** "Throughout his 900 years"; "Fearing the worst—that the Jedi Order is unraveling and that his own connection to the Force is wavering".
- **Jedi Knights, Padawans, Snipers and Brutes** are game-original Temple defender classes.
  - Snipers have "an almost symbiotic relationship with their own weapons", throwing and steering their sabers over distance.
  - Brutes are "Selected for their imposing physical nature"; "Unprovoked, the Brutes are kind and gentle." They carry double-bladed sabers.
- **Commander Cody** is "one of the first to emerge from an experimental training program designed to encourage more cognitive thinking among the rank-and-file clone troopers". He "appears only in the cutscenes".
- **Clone troopers:**
  - They were "Commissioned by a mysterious member of the Jedi Order".
  - The guide's lore line: "the clone troopers never question their role in the war or think outside of their preprogrammed parameters".
- **Clone Heavy Gunner:** "Opponents are often advised to take cover at the first sight of a heavy gunner, though this strategy’s effectiveness has yet to be proven."
- **Clone Blaze Trooper:** a flamethrower and jetpack unit.
- **Clone Assassin:** "of the same genetic family as the ARC (Advanced Recon Commandos) troopers", "closer in thinking and attitude to their progenitor Jango Fett". "Through intense training in the arts of Tera Kasi", they can "even dodge the grip of Force powers". "If you see one coming, it’s already too late."
- **Dooku:** "A man of elegance and charm, Dooku’s most dangerous weapon is his intoxicating personality." His style "reflects the short slashes and thrusts common to fencing".
- **Grievous:**
  - "A hideous amalgam of cutting-edge Separatist technology and the broken body of a once-proud Kaleesh warrior".
  - "Grievous hunts Jedi for sport and collects his victim’s lightsabers".
  - He was "Grievous who masterminded the assault on Coruscant and the kidnapping of the chancellor".
- **Grievous bodyguards:** they "attach their broken frames onto their enemy’s bodies and dispatch themselves with fiery explosions."
- **Grapple droid:** "designed specifically for the Separatists’ siege on Coruscant. General Grievous himself personally oversaw its modifications."
- **Crab droid:** "has a fatal factory defect—its delicate neural-net sensors can be exposed and destroyed through an access port on the droid’s back. Separatist engineers are aware of this flaw but are unconcerned".
- **Neimoidian guards** are recruited from prisons, "offering candidates amnesty or commuted sentences".
- **Brute guards** "undergo several extreme genetic enhancements" and are "often kept in stasis".
- **Neimoidian snipers** "lie in silent wait—for sometimes days at a time".

## Missions 1–3 (pp. 24–31)
- **M1, "Rescue over Coruscant":**
  - Anakin, Separatist cruiser, 3 secrets.
  - The mission ends with R2 dropping the hangar shield. The tutorial-style walkthrough keeps pointing to the Combat chart.
- **M2, "An Explosive Development":**
  - Obi-Wan, 4 secrets.
  - The briefing: "the Jedi are unaware that their every move is being tracked on the ship’s command bridge by the leader of the droid forces, General Grievous."
  - The "saber plunge" cuts through locked doors.
- **M3, "Peril in the Elevators":**
  - Anakin, 3 secrets: the elevator-roof fight (the film's elevator sequence) and destroyer droids.
  - Standing in the elevator's upper-left corner "for a few seconds" reveals a saber crystal.

## Missions 3 (end)–13 (pp. 32–65)
- **M4, "Settling the Score"** (Anakin, 2 secrets):
  - The briefing calls Dooku "a stately former Jedi named Count Dooku, who is now the Sith Lord known as Darth Tyranus."
  - It is a four-stage duel. Obi-Wan helps in stage 1 until "Count Dooku has immobilized Obi-Wan under a structure in the room."
  - Stage 3: "the Count calls up some reinforcements", super battle droids. The guide's tip: "Use the super battle droids against Count Dooku."
  - Ending: "Anakin finishes the duel—and finishes a Sith Lord. He then frees Chancellor Palpatine".
- **M5, "It's Not Over Yet"** (Anakin, 3 secrets):
  - The briefing: "There is a sinister mood as Palpatine witnesses Anakin’s flirtation with the dark side while getting revenge on Count Dooku."
  - A hull breach sucks super battle droids into space.
  - It ends with a gun-battery sequence: "Use cannon to defeat banking clan ship".
- **M6, "The General's Right Hand"** (Anakin, 2 secrets):
  - A multi-foe "duel" against Grievous's bodyguards on the bridge as the cruiser tilts. They fight on glass, then on the inverted ceiling.
  - Grievous "decides to escape rather than take on two Jedi."
- **M7, "Investigating Utapau"** (Obi-Wan, 3 secrets):
  - The briefing adds film context: Anakin is "further dismayed to learn that Mace Windu suspects Chancellor Palpatine is behind the perpetuation of the Clone Wars."
  - Sequences: heat vents, Force-run past a cannon, manning a Separatist cannon, the first crab droid.
- **M8, "The Cavalry Arrives"** (Obi-Wan, 4 secrets): the bodyguards, disabling cannons that fire on clone gunships, lowering the control-sphere shields, and a second crab droid.
- **M9, "Showdown with Grievous"** (Obi-Wan, 2 secrets):
  - Obi-Wan chases Grievous "on the back of a native boga lizard".
  - Four stages: the control room, an outside area, a stalagmite cavern, and the landing platform.
  - Grievous has a kneel-and-charge attack and a pistol.
  - The guide treats the ending as the war's end: "you defeat this Separatist general and bring the Clone Wars to an end." Completing it unlocks Grievous for multiplayer.
- **M10, "The Dark Side of the Force"** (Anakin vs. Mace, 2 secrets):
  - The briefing has Anakin seeing "his friend on the wrong end of what appears to be a Jedi assassination attempt."
  - The game diverges from the film: Anakin duels Windu across the office, a balcony, a landing platform and back to the office, where Mace "jumps back up into the chancellor’s office to finish off Palpatine".
  - Mace "tries to Force heal".
  - Ending: "you eventually defeat the great Jedi Master and save the chancellor. However, Anakin has made a decisive move toward the dark side of the Force."
- **M11, "The Hunt Begins"** (Anakin/Vader, Jedi Temple, 5 secrets):
  - The briefing: "Seduced by his newfound power, the newly christened Darth Vader is sent to the Jedi Temple on a quest to send the traitorous Jedi to their doom."
  - Objectives: "Exterminate Jedi" and "Set temple beacon". The mission opens in the Jedi library.
  - "Since he has turned to the dark side, Anakin’s Force stun has changed to Force lightning ... a sustained blast can even kill a Padawan."
  - A hangar turret shoots down "Jedi starfighters". Anakin then activates the beacon, "luring Jedi from all over back to the Jedi Temple—where he will destroy them". In the film, Obi-Wan recalibrates this beacon.
- **M12, "The Final Lesson"** (Anakin, 2 secrets):
  - The briefing: "The harder the Jedi fight against him, the more convinced of their corruption and treachery the young Sith Lord becomes".
  - Cin Drallig is "an incredibly skilled swordsman and Jedi Master". "before Cin can act, his most accomplished apprentice, Serra, confronts Vader on her own." The two are "the Temple’s last hope."
  - It is a "double duel". Tactics include throwing Serra into electrical arcs, and "Knock Cin Drallig to the ground, then use Force lightning on him when he’s prone and helpless."
  - Ending: "you defeat Cin Drallig and claim another Jedi on your quest to destroy them all."
- **M13, "Attack of the Clones"** (Obi-Wan, Utapau, 4 secrets):
  - Order 66 arrives as Cody gets "a secret coded message directly from Darth Sidious, implementing “Order 66”—a pre-programmed directive commanding the clones to turn on their Jedi leaders!"
  - Obi-Wan uses the Jedi Mind Trick on clones, fights clone walkers, blaze troopers and heavy gunners, and a gunship fought only with saber throws ("Don’t try to Force deflect the clone gunship’s attacks").
  - He escapes on a clone juggernaut.
