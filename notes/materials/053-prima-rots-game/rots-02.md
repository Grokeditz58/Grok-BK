# 053 · Prima ROTS game guide, Part 2: Missions 14–17, bonus missions, Multiplayer Duelists and Duel Arenas (pp. 66–96)

## Story missions 14–17
- **M14, "Assassination on Mustafar"** (Anakin, 5 secrets):
  - The map heading misspells it "ASSISSINATION". The info box gives the location as "Separatist Cruiser", an error carried over from the template.
  - The briefing: "With no one left to oppose him, Darth Sidious is ready to declare the Clone Wars over and appoint himself emperor of the galaxy." Sidious "dispatches Darth Vader to the volcanic world to end their lives with a final betrayal."
  - Enemies: Neimoidian guards, brutes and scouts on conveyor belts over magma, plus magma guns and magma lifts.
  - The meeting room: "assassinate all of the leaders left behind by Nute Gunray, the head Neimodian leader, as he tries to escape. This is easy since the Neimoidians do not put up any fight at all."
  - New objective "Destroy Nute Gunray in Neimoidian shuttle": "When the shuttle takes enough damage, it crashes into the magma." This is a game-only death for Gunray; in the film he is cut down in the room.
- **M15, "Aftermath in the Temple"** (Obi-Wan with Yoda, 6 secrets):
  - Obi-Wan is "Reunited with Yoda aboard Senator Bail Organa’s personal starship"; Vader "has left behind an army of clones".
  - Yoda asks for help moving rubble. In the library, the pair face the "library security protocol" and clone gunships, which Obi-Wan fights from a turret.
  - The final area "introduces you to the last new enemy: clone assassins". The objective is "Shut down Temple beacon".
- **M16, "A Friendship in Flames"** (Obi-Wan vs. Vader, 0 secrets):
  - "This mission has no secrets or bacta tanks. The only way to restore your health is to Force heal yourself."
  - Six stages: outside, the control room, a more open area, the containers area, the narrow walkway over the magma, and "a very small platform floating above the magma".
  - "Anakin is good about blocking—Obi-Wan taught him well."
  - A meta tip: "you may momentarily forget which Jedi you are controlling ... follow your own lightsaber rather than that of your opponent."
  - Ending: "the duel ends with Obi-Wan as the victor."
- **M17, "Revenge of the Sith"** (Anakin vs. Obi-Wan, 0 secrets): the same six stages from Vader's side.
  - The briefing: "it becomes clear to the young Sith Lord that Palpatine was right: Obi-Wan, too, has been tainted by the Jedi’s corruption and must also be destroyed."
  - This is the game's non-canon "what if" ending: "Use everything you have learned up to this point against Obi-Wan and you will defeat your old master. You will also rewrite history."

## Bonus missions (pp. 78–84)
- **B1, "The General's Protectors":** you play as a Grievous bodyguard on Utapau; "General Grievous’ bodyguards are the deadliest of droids, as the attacking clone army quickly discovers."
  - The droid "walks quite slow", so you slide instead.
  - A twin-bodyguard team attack; "the bodyguard cannot grapple".
  - It ends with a crab droid.
- **B2, "Grievous on the Run":** you play Grievous fleeing "cutting down any clone trooper foolish enough to stand in his way". You cannot Force heal. A blaster replaces Force push, and four sabers drain the Force meter. It ends with a duel against Obi-Wan.
- **B3, "Mustafar Lava Challenge":** Vader platforming; "unlike those missions, here you can actually fall off the platforms and walkways."
- **B4, "Size Matters Not":** Yoda in the Temple against waves of clones, ending with "five at once" clone assassins. "Yoda has a much stronger push than other Jedi ... The power of this push alone eliminates clone troopers."
- **B5, "Episode IV Death Star":** Vader vs. Ben.
  - The briefing: "After many years, Darth Vader and Ben Kenobi come face-to-face once more. By engaging Darth Vader in combat, Ben Kenobi allows Luke Skywalker to escape certain death... but at what cost?"
  - "For over a quarter century, fans have been waiting to take part in the classic duel between Darth Vader and Ben Kenobi."
  - "Darth Vader’s grapple attack is actually a Force Choke."

## Multiplayer Duelists (pp. 85–93)
- There are nine duelists: Anakin, Obi-Wan, Dooku, Grievous, Mace, Serra Keto, Cin Drallig, Darth Vader and Ben Kenobi. "At the game’s beginning, you can duel only with Anakin and Obi-Wan."
- **Specials by duelist:**
  - Anakin: "Fury" moves; he "fights in the duels with his Dark Jedi powers".
  - Obi-Wan: "Focus" moves.
  - Dooku: Force Lightning Explosion, Whirlwind Strike, Electric Toss and Clearing Spin. His style "reflects an older style of Jedi lightsaber dueling similar to fencing."
  - Grievous is "the only non-Jedi duelist". He cannot heal, and "any health lost during a duel can’t be replaced". His specials are Four Arm mode, Fire Blaster and the 3 Variation Charge-Up.
  - Mace: "all of Mace’s special attacks are close range".
  - Serra Keto: Dual Saber Toss, Force Spiral, Kick Flip Launch, Clearing Slam.
  - Cin Drallig: "1000 Slashes", Double Force Blast, Five Point Strike; his specials make him appear "as a blur".
  - Darth Vader "has learned some new tricks since his duel with Obi-Wan on Mustafar". His specials are Force Choke, Fury Slam, Clearing Spin and Backhand Lunge.
  - Ben Kenobi has "Force Submission", which "converts Ben’s health into Force".

## Duel Arenas (pp. 94–97, part)
- The guide says there are 14 arenas. Those read so far:
  - Throne Room Main Chamber and Throne Room Upper Balcony.
  - Utapau Sinkhole Control Room and Utapau Sinkhole Landing Platform.
  - Palpatine's Office and Palpatine's Office Docking Bay.
  - Temple Training Room: it "features the largest number of objects", including shelves of "small spheres that explode when they hit someone".
  - Temple Control Room: helmets and an electrical arc.
  - Temple Outer Terrace: "While there is a battle going on around you, it is just for show".
  - Mustafar Control Room.
- **Final arenas:**
  - Mustafar Balcony.
  - Mustafar Control Arm: "straight, narrow, and long".
  - Mustafar Lava Platform: "downright tiny".
  - Episode IV Death Star: "This is where the world first saw a Jedi duel, and everyone loves a classic."
