# Making of RotS, pp. 101-160. Own-words notes.
- 101-110 (Jul 24-30 2003): Aldeeran corridor; C-3PO memory wipe; Polis Massa; Palpatine's Senate "new beginning" speech, Empire declared (p107); Lucas reshoots ESB Emperor/Vader scene with McDiarmid for continuity, dialogue about Luke being Anakin's son and strong in the Force (p109); Leia to Bail and Breha.
- 111-113 Christopher Lee films Dooku dialogue/duel; fencing doubles Byrne and Rowling.
- 114-119 Mustafar: Anakin turns on Padmé, Force-choke; outburst meant as accidental, he believes killing the Jedi is right and that he'll save her (p118); duel opening.
- 128 Spielberg suggests the Force as how Anakin reacts to Obi-Wan's moves in the Mustafar duel.
- 131 Anakin kills the Separatist council (Nute Gunray, Wat Tambor, Shu Mai).
- 133 Lucas: the dark side is the shortcut; Anakin's fall is down to its temptation.
- 135-139 Anakin's fall at the lava edge; burnt Anakin; rehab table, mask descends; McDiarmid on the Emperor's smug secret.
- 140-142 General's quarters set modeled on ROTJ throne room; Dooku's death; Lucas decides Palpatine isn't fully satisfied with a maimed Anakin (p142).
- 146-149 Obi-Wan vs Grievous and bodyguards, Boga; Cody; Luke to Owen; Padmé's funeral; Tarkin/Vader/Emperor on the Star Destroyer as wrap; shoot finished five days early (p151).
- 153-160 Part III "Digital Shot Production: A Symphony Is Born": Lucas on editing; ILM (John Knoll), Yoda animation; deleted scenes list (Padmé's worry, Obi-Wan/Anakin, Mace with Yoda) (p158); Gregoire's animatics.
