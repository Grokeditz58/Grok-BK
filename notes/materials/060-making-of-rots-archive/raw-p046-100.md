# Making of RotS, pp. 46-100. Own-words notes (small type; minor details may be off).
- 46-51 (Mar-Apr 2003): R2 written into opening; Grievous a droid with a "plastic sac" for organs; Utapau windmill city; Obi-Wan loses sword, uses pistol vs Grievous; Gillard plans fights on sinkhole model; first draft finished Apr 10-11.
- 52 First draft: Grievous an echo of what Anakin becomes (mostly droid, four arms). Mace says Palpatine is "well versed in the ways of the Jedi" but "no Jedi." Lucas: "Everybody is after power." Anakin kneels: "I want the power to stop death." Order 66.
- 53 Draft: Yoda fights Palpatine; Palpatine escapes; Padmé dies; Lucas: Padmé's heart breaks when Anakin says he can rule the universe.
- 55-57 Spielberg visits animatics; Mustafar burning lava fight; Lucas has Anakin/Obi-Wan swap aggressor roles; on Grievous: "If it doesn't look real, people don't buy it."
- 60 Second draft (Jun 13, 115 pp): Anakin kills Dooku; Yoda "The chosen one, he is. Balance to the Force, he will bring."; Anakin helps kill Mace; Yoda fights the Emperor.
- 61 Padmé dies of "a broken heart"; "the Force runs strong in the Skywalker line."
- 62 4th draft (Jun 26, 129 pp): Plagueis story added (could keep others from dying, not himself). Lucas: "The saving grace is that if you watch the other three movies, then you know everything ends happily."
- 65-100 Part II "Principal Photography: The Moviemakers of Oz" (Fox Studios Sydney, from Jun 30 2003): lightsaber boot camp; saga framed by R2 telling the story to the Keeper of the Whills (p72); Lucas to Christensen: Anakin's anger partly rationalization (p79); Jackson on Mace's style: slow, deadly, "mostly not flashy" (p83); Council "senior triumvirate" Yoda, Ki-Adi-Mundi, Mace (p85); Mace + 3 Jedi confront Palpatine, Michael Byrne doubles McDiarmid, McDiarmid does some fighting himself (p88-91); Anakin cuts Mace's hand, "quick and ugly"; lightning added in post (p93); McDiarmid: Palpatine "tender and seductive" (p94); Ch5 "Return of the Emperor": opera scene "the story of Caesar, Napoleon, and Hitler," democracy into tyranny (p95); opera box replaces office for Plagueis scene (p96).
