# The Making of Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith (J. W. Rinzler, 2005), archive.org loan makingofstarwars0000rinz. Pages 0-45. Own-words notes.
- 0-5 cover, frontispiece (Lucas with clones), title, dedication, contents: Part I Preproduction p10, Part II Principal Photography p64, Part III Digital Shot Production p152.
- 6 Foreword Rick McCallum (Oct 2004); 7-8 Intro: in spring 2002 nobody, Lucas included, knew exactly how Anakin's fall would look.
- 10-12 Part I "The Shadow Citadel of Creation"; prologue on digital scripting.
- 13 Ch1 "Seven Planets for Seven Battles": first art meeting Apr 25 2002, Clone War battles on seven planets; Kenobi, Windu, Yoda on different fronts; teen Boba Fett idea.
- 15-17 Lucas works with no script; McQuarrie volcano "Mufasta"; Lucas recalls the volcanic end of the Obi-Wan/Anakin fight; "Expand our universe."
- 18 (Jun 13 2002) what pushes Anakin to the dark side: Lucas "It'll be Padmé." ~1,600 VFX shots.
- 21 Lava duel stays in "close proximity to the lava."
- 29 Anakin "won't be scarred till the very end"; Padmé "madly in love with him."
- 30 Opening: Anakin vs Dooku duel aboard ship; "making a movie where the bad guys win ... isn't going to be the most successful."
- 32-33 New villain (Grievous) "is not a Sith," droid or alien; "Villains are ageless ... Old people are scary."
- 34 "We have to be careful not to re-create Darth Vader."
- 38-39 Ch3 "The Rise and Fall of Darth Vader": 10-year-old Han Solo on Kashyyyk idea; rehab center where Anakin becomes Vader; Boba Fett cut.
- 40 Qui-Gon ghost in Obi-Wan cave scene, "He's able to retain his personality"; first draft 55 pages; story in one word: "Anakin."
- 41 Rough draft (crop p041): Dooku ("Darth Tyranus") fight is Palpatine's test of Anakin; Palpatine: "I have somebody who I think will become a great Sith Lord."; Anakin beheads Dooku; Palpatine has Anakin cut off Mace's sword hand, then blasts him; Order 66.
- 42 Palpatine reveals he manipulated Anakin's conception via the midichlorians: "I have waited all these years for you ... to fulfill your destiny." Named Darth Vader; Padmé dies in childbirth.
- 43-45 Utapau dragon "reeks of rancor"; Grievous could breathe in space; opening-duel storyboards (Dooku lightning; Palpatine tells Anakin to cut off Dooku's hands).
