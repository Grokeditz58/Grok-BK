## Key power-scaling / lore points
1. **Only Mace or Yoda could really compete with the Emperor; Anakin could have beaten him if not so beat-up** (p205). **VERIFY wording before citing.**
2. **Palpatine exaggerates his weakness vs. Mace to manipulate Anakin** (p204 reshoot discussion); Lucas cut Anakin's "Help me, Master" to avoid redemption.
3. **Dooku fight as Palpatine's test of Anakin** (rough draft, p41, crop `crops/p041-palpatine-tests-anakin-dooku.png`): "I have somebody who I think will become a great Sith Lord."
4. **Palpatine manipulated Anakin's conception via the midichlorians** in the rough draft (p42): "I have waited all these years for you..." (draft-only idea).
5. **Dooku's Force use** in the Feb 2004 animatic: levitates, Force-strangles and flings Obi-Wan, kicks Anakin (p171, p189, p210).
6. Mace's style: slow, deadly, "mostly not flashy" (Jackson, p83); Council triumvirate Yoda, Ki-Adi-Mundi, Mace (p85).
7. Grievous "is not a Sith" (p32); later redesigned as a cunning, manipulative coward (p185–187). Anakin's fall: the dark side is the shortcut; simple greed; "Everybody is after power" (p52, p133, p188).
8. Epilogue (p221): Anakin fulfills the prophecy by destroying the Emperor out of compassion for his son.

### Part I: Preproduction (pp. 0–63)

- 13 "Seven Planets for Seven Battles" (Apr 25 2002); teen Boba Fett idea.
- 15–21 Volcano duel concept; Padmé as Anakin's push (p18); ~1,600 VFX shots.
- 29–34 Anakin scarred only at the end; opening Dooku duel; Grievous "not a Sith"; "careful not to re-create Darth Vader."
- 38–40 Rehab-center Vader; Qui-Gon ghost idea; story in one word: "Anakin."
- 41–42 Rough draft: Dooku test, beheading, Mace's hand, Order 66, midichlorian manipulation (key points 3–4).
- 43–45 Utapau; Grievous could breathe in space; Palpatine tells Anakin to cut off Dooku's hands.

- 55–57 Spielberg animatics; Anakin/Obi-Wan swap aggressor roles.
- 60–62 Second draft (Yoda: "The chosen one, he is"); fourth draft adds Plagueis.
