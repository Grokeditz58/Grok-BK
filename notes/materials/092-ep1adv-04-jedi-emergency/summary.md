# EA4 Jedi Emergency: summary

- Full read (l.1–1301). Yoda carries the book alone.

## Top Yoda feats
1. **Destroys a whole 15-Bartokk hive plus the Queen** (16 kills) while protecting an infant. Count: 2 thrown into the ceiling, 1 down the shaft, 1 cut, 2 electrocuted, 2 off the cables, 3 in the aero lab, 1 rammed off the yacht, the Queen, and 3 in the yacht blast.
2. **Force-blocks 2 charging Bartokks and slams them into the ceiling** (Ch. 4).
3. **Saber-throw "with incomprehensible speed" kills one, then he snaps explosive arrows in mid-air** (Ch. 9).
4. Mind compulsion on a dying Bartokk. Prying with the Force: a droid's grip and a carnivorous plant.
5. Destroys 4 of the 6 X10-Ds himself (two with one stroke, one with two swipes, one via the remote's controller). Defuses or disposes of all 3 plasma bombs; the third goes off on the yacht, away from the city.

## Anti-feats
- Age: he hobbles and admits rescues are "for Jedi Knights". Frexton gets away from him once. He walks into the Bartokk trap.
