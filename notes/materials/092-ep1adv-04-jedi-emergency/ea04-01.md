# EA4 Jedi Emergency: notes

## Front matter and Intro (l.1–58)
- Publisher's summary: "A young girl has been found who has the power to become a Jedi… an evil scientist… Bartokk Assassins."
- The Intro recaps #3. Maul "destroyed an entire fifteen-member Bartokk hive" and sent the 25 fighters back to TF space. Note: l.40 says "*Boonda* had contracted the Bartokks", which is a text slip for Groodo.

## Ch. 1 (l.60–119)
- Boonda eats 7 bowls of lizards; he usually has 12. Groodo calls the Bartokks' **Assassins Guild** and demands the Academy be destroyed or his money back.
- A **Bartokk Queen** heads the Guild's Bartokk chapter from a hideout near the Corporate Sector. 30 Bartokks are dead, two hives. She takes a new hive of 15 to Corulag.

## Ch. 2 (l.121–240)
- Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan argue about the ethics of bringing Bama along.
- The Bartokks fly a stolen 50 m **SoroSuub yacht**, fake-registered to Ausec Grogle of Trandosha, with 6 **X10-D** draft droids. The X10-Ds are 3 m tall, remote-controlled with a 400 m control radius, and taken from Sullust. 3 of them carry **plasma bombs**, each "enough firepower to level a small city". Target: the Science Service tower. Plan: "in two hours, the Academy will be nothing but radioactive cinders."

## Ch. 3 (l.243–549)
- Leeper spots that the yacht shouldn't exist yet.
- Lore: the Academy chose Corulag because the Raithall campus was full. The population grew more than 100× in two decades, and plains were terraformed into flight canyons. The spaceport covers 12 km².
- Mace and Yoda are there, and Obi-Wan takes the blame. Mace: if he hadn't gone after the freighter, "twenty-five droid starfighters might have reached Corulag Academy."
- Yoda and Mace explain why they came: the infant **Teela Panjarra**, 6 months old, high midi-chlorians, parents dead in a dig accident. Chief Scientist **Frexton** won't release her and has Senate friends. Yoda: "Lying, Frexton is." He knew her parents.
- The tower is a 200-storey pyramid. X10-Ds jam the lift, a bomb is detected, and the lights go out.

## Ch. 4–5 (l.551–815)
- **Yoda trips 2 X10-Ds with his gimer stick and disables them with one saber stroke** (made to look like an accident). He jams one bomb's trigger with a piece of a child's sculpture.
- **He Force-blocks 2 vibro-axe Bartokks "into an invisible wall" and flings them into the ceiling** (l.636–640).
- Comms are jammed, so Yoda goes alone.
- He cuts a circle in the lift doors. Two Bartokks with **cryogen whips** freeze the security droid, which had already taken the bomb apart. **Yoda ducks and one sails down the shaft to its death. He cuts the other in two, and its own whip shatters it.**
- He rides a child's repulsor scooter up the shaft to Level 58 and picks the lock. **He Force-opens a security droid's grip and knocks it down.** He finds Frexton loading Teela into a **LOCC** carrier.

## Ch. 6 (l.817–916)
- **He throws a glow rod fast enough to knock Frexton's blaster away**; "Frexton never knew what hit him."
- Bartokks ride a window-cleaner drone up the tower and break the window with a Squib battering ram. **Yoda pushes electrical equipment into the puddle and electrocutes 2.**
- He **Force-compels** the dying one: "Six droids… three bombs… destroy the Science Service tower."

## Ch. 7–8 (l.918–1062)
- Cuts the climbing cables and 2 Bartokks fall. Disintegrates bomb #2 in the waste-fusion canister.
- Frexton runs. **Yoda fires the Squib ram, which is bigger than he is, through a thick wall.**
- The carnivorous plant: its mind is too primitive to reach, so **he Force-pries the vines loose.** He forces the lift doors and rides on top of the lift. He cuts through the hatch.
- Level 32: he works a servo-lifter as a decoy and **cuts down an X10-D with two swipes**. Frexton faints. Trap: 3 Bartokks, an X10-D standing on the LOCC.
- Age note: "over eight hundred years old"; "age had taken its toll"; he hobbles.

## Ch. 9 (l.1064–1218)
- **"With incomprehensible speed", a Force-guided saber throw cuts through the controller Bartokk and its remote, then comes back.** He snaps explosive arrows in half and kills 2 crossbow Bartokks (l.1086–1096).
- Bomb #3 has a 5-minute tamper-proof timer. He takes the skiff with Teela and the bomb. **He rams the disruptor-rifle Bartokk off the yacht with the skiff's nose.** He drops the bomb into the yacht's vent.
- **The Bartokk Queen** crushes her vocabulator and attacks. Yoda: "Not locked up with you, am I. Locked up with me, are you." He lets her fall onto his blade, then cuts her apart until only the head is left.
- He escapes in a pod as the yacht and the last 3 Bartokks explode. Teela giggles.

## Ch. 10 (l.1220–1301)
- Frexton is arrested, and the Academy President bans him from labs. Teela goes to the Temple.
- **Retcon:** Adi "saving Qui-Gon's life" was stopping him from eating toxic **Konkeel pie** at the Hewett senators' banquet. Obi-Wan: "Oh."
- Valorum: the TF droid-fighter matter is under investigation, and the TF denies it. Qui-Gon: "keep a very watchful eye on the Neimoidians."
- Groodo swears off Bartokks, and Boonda never wanted to go to the Academy. They hire Bama, unknowingly, to take them back to Esseles.
