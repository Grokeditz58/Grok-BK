## Hand-read: *The Clone Wars* (Karen Traviss) excerpt, ch. 1–2
This will be read in full as 067. Excerpt highlights:
- Dooku and Ziro plot to frame the Jedi for Rotta's kidnapping. Palpatine (as Sidious, in his inner voice): "the ease with which he moved undetected among them as a Sith Lord". Windu "sense[s]" that "there's more going on here". The Hutt hyperspace routes are the strategic prize.
- **Anakin on Christophsis:** he jumps two stories onto an octuptarra droid and kills it. He enters a dissociative battle state:
  > "He was aware of the position of every droid, every clone trooper, but not consciously ... a kind of blindness where he could still somehow see." / "Metal fragments flashed past his face. Some veered away at angles, deflected not by his lightsaber but an **instinctive and unthinking Force push** ... leaping onto the back of a battle droid and **ripping off its head with a Force-assisted stranglehold**." / "He swung his blade through metal bodies as easily as if he were cutting grass." (The Clone Wars excerpt ch. 2)
- He has flashbacks to the Tuskens and the Blood Carver Ke Daiv, and denies his rage: "I'm not dark ... Not rage." Obi-Wan cuts two droids in half in one sweep. Rex feels Anakin's mood "in the Force" (from Anakin's point of view). Ahsoka arrives: "I'm yours, Master Skywalker."
- This continues the theme of Jedi Trial ch. 28: Anakin's Force-frenzy in combat.
