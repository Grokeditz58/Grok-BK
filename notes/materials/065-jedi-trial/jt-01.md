# Jedi Trial: notes part 1

- Read 2026-11-03 CST from the owner's MEGA epub (handle BS9gSZia; Del Rey ebook v3.1_r1, eISBN 978-0-345-46308-1). David Sherman & Dan Cragg, 2004. Citation "JT ch. N" (P = Prologue).

## Prologue and ch. 3: Anakin against Nejaa Halcyon (sparring)
- On the first day they are even. Anakin's "unorthodox backhand" would have cut Halcyon's shoulder: "That was so close I'm not sure whether or not it counts as a touch." After an hour, "When first one, then the other found his way through the other's defense". (JT P)
- On the second day: "In the end, the Jedi Master bowed to the Padawan." / **"Who would have believed that a mere Padawan could best me with a lightsaber?"** (JT ch. 3, Halcyon). Both men improve every day after that.
- Halcyon: Obi-Wan is "very powerful in the Force. I believe he'll become a member of the Jedi Council one day." / "nobody has ever impressed Obi-Wan with their potential as much as you have." Halcyon is in semi-disgrace for losing the *Plooriod Bodkin*.
- "nearly every physically able Jedi was away from Coruscant" (JT P), a sign of how stretched the Order is.

## Ch. 1–2: the Separatist invasion of Praesitlyn (Operation Case White)
- Admiral Pors Tonith (a Muun from the InterGalactic Banking Clan) gives the force numbers:
> "a fleet of one hundred twenty-six ships ... seventy-five of them capital ships, is investing Sluis Van ... I am at this very moment landing a force of fifty thousand battle droids on Praesitlyn in a feint ... I will land the main force, composed of, give or take, a million battle droids ... I have two hundred ships in my invasion fleet." (JT ch. 2)
- The Separatist ECM blocks the center's "pan-galactic distress signal". They have both tactical and strategic surprise through a stealth suite.
- Ventress oversees Tonith for Dooku: "if I were to come out there it wouldn't be to help you, it would be to replace you". Tonith has heard she "could cut down an opponent with lightning speed".
- The center's chief administrator, Reija Momen, orders the equipment destroyed.

## Ch. 4–5: Praesitlyn defense force (non-Force soldiers)
- **Torpil T-19 starfighter**: "could reach a top velocity of twenty thousand kph". It patrols at 650 kph at 20,000 m. Erk H'Arman downs **10 tri-droid fighters in a one-minute dogfight**. He covers about 150 km back to base "in less than sixty seconds" at low level (roughly 9,000 kph or more).
- Recon trooper Odie Subu rides a modified civilian speeder at 250 kph, less than a meter off the ground. Her cut-down "belly gun" has an effective range of 10 m for an ordinary shooter, but "Odie had learned to hit targets out to sixty meters". 74-Z bikes outclass her speeder.
- General Khamar works out the anvil-and-hammer plan and withdraws to a rock redoubt 10 km behind his line. Coruscant had denied him reinforcements ("almost as if the Republic wanted the Separatists to attack Praesitlyn").

## Ch. 6–7: the center falls; Slayke's counterattack
- Tonith is **stronger than he looks**: "With unexpected strength and agility, Tonith lurched forward, grabbed Reija by the hair, and threw her to the floor." He is over 2 m tall and very thin.
- **Zozridor Slayke's** force: CloakShapes, gun tugs, Phoenix Hawk pinnaces, Carrack light cruisers, Corellian corvettes, Dreadnaughts, and 50,000 combined-arms ground troops. They are outnumbered "at least four to one" in space. They face more than a million droids on the ground and about 200 ships in orbit. His plan is to "grab him by the belt" (stay too close for orbital fire). Slayke "despised the Jedi".

## Ch. 8: Palpatine's meeting (lore)
- Palpatine calls Paige-Tarkin, Mas Amedda, Jannie Ha'Nook, Armand Isard and Sate Pestage. The only available relief force is "The garrison force on Centax One, some twenty thousand clones". Tipoca City's next batch is "Two or three months" away. Palpatine pardoned Slayke.
- **Sly Moore**, using a holoshroud, buys Ha'Nook's vote with the promise of an ambassadorship: "The invasion of Praesitlyn is but a ripple in a vast wave of history".

## Ch. 9: the Council's mission; Grudo
- The Council gives Halcyon 20,000 clones; "Supreme Chancellor Palpatine himself recommended you". Yoda: **"Of our Order, Nejaa Halcyon, a true Master you are."** Windu: "this assignment is as much a trial for you as it is for young Anakin."
- Halcyon chooses Anakin as his deputy. Adi Gallia objects: "Anakin has limited experience leading troops."
- Anakin's prosthetic arm is "even better than the original", with a power cell that never needs recharging. Anakin "reached out into the Force to scan the hotel lobby ... there aren't any Force-sensitives in there" (detecting Force-sensitives).
- Grudo, a Rodian career soldier, serves a drink "supposed to incapacitate Gamorreans, Trandoshans, Wookiees" that leaves Anakin and Halcyon unaffected.

## Ch. 10–11: Centax fleet; clone commandos; a Gamorrean's durability
- Halcyon's flagship is the Centax-class heavy frigate *Ranger*. **50 clone commandos** on the cruiser *Teyr* are assigned to Anakin: "with a Jedi commander their potential as an attack force was virtually unlimited."
- Grudo corrects the division structure to a triangular one ("two up, one back").
- A **Gamorrean** recon rider tosses a human soldier aside "like a doll" and takes "Five [bolts], at least" before he goes down. A 74-Z's armor "absorbed the energy and bled it out onto the sand" from a hand blaster.
- **Dooku used the Force on Tonith once in person**: "a sudden shortness of breath as if a great weight had been placed on his chest ... Dooku wasn't near enough to use the Force against him now". Tonith reports a kill ratio of "For every one of them we kill, they destroy five or six of my droids." Dooku is following a hidden plan: "It just wasn't the plan Tonith thought he was following."

## Ch. 12: Halcyon fights Grudo without the Force (non-Force baseline for a Jedi Master)
> "He never drew his lightsaber. When I dropped my knives and weapons belts, so did he, and we fought hand to hand ... Halcyon seemed unwilling to use Jedi tricks ... and fought like an ordinary warrior. So I used his momentum against him. Many times I flipped him ... But Halcyon sprang back up every time ... He was fast enough to get through a few times, and he hit hard enough to bruise—including a couple of bone bruises." (JT ch. 12, Grudo)
  - The fight goes on until both are exhausted, and ends only when Slayke steals the *Plooriod Bodkin*. Slayke: "he, Slayke, uses his brain, which is more powerful than the Force." Halcyon did not dodge Grudo's thrown knives, which "thunked into the ground between his legs" (a deliberate miss).
- Quartermaster Boulanger's planning figures: 10% losses by day 3; for each soldier killed, three are wounded. Slayke's earlier bounty was 45,000 credits.

## Ch. 13: Slayke's defensive works
- The redoubts Izable, Eliey, Kaudine, the main position and Judlie were built "in just two days while under fire" by engineers and hundreds of labor droids. Tonith tried "six times to take us by storm" and twice took Izable. A Separatist mine tunnel runs at a depth of about 100 m. An E-Web has an effective range of 200 m and a maximum range of half a kilometer. The enemy fire control "only reacts to movement".

## Ch. 14: Anakin and Halcyon confess their marriages
- Halcyon has a wife, **Scerra**, and a son, **Valin**, and has written a dozen sealed letters to them. Anakin confesses his marriage to Padmé. He refuses to use the Force to look in on Padmé even though "his considerable Force sensitivity" might allow it.
- Halcyon's view of Anakin: "a great Force sensitivity; he was bright, bright to the point of genius ... would be a Master someday and even sit on the Council." He references Geonosis and Jabiim ("more than a month of hard fighting").
- Anakin commands **10,000 troops** and flies the *Azure Angel II* (a Delta-7).

## Ch. 15: Tonith presses the attack
- Separatist losses are now "upward of one hundred thousand droids". B'wuf: "for every one of them we kill, they knock out hundreds of our droids!" The fleets are locked in orbit, with neither side willing to break contact.
