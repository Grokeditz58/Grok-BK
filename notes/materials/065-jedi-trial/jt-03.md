# Jedi Trial: notes part 3 (ch. 28 to the Epilogue)

- Read 2026-11-03 CST from the owner's MEGA epub (handle BS9gSZia). Citation "JT ch. N" / "JT Epi."

## Ch. 27–28: Anakin's Force surge (his peak feat in the book)
> "Anakin reached deep into the Force. **A sense of invincible power came over him, infusing him completely.** Even during the desperate battle with the enemy tank droids and the attack on the hill he had not experienced the Force as fully as it now flowed into him. In that instant when he achieved total oneness with the Force he knew he could do anything, and it felt good." (JT ch. 28)
> "Anakin moved with the swiftness and brilliance of a burning sun ... he parried the bolts effortlessly, sending some ripping through the walls and roof, others back into the very droids that had fired them. He wasn't defending now, he was attacking ... The droids ... flew apart like cheap dolls as the lightsaber cut through them in a broad swath of destruction." (JT ch. 28)
> "It seemed as if Tonith's entire army was firing at Anakin, but as he ran at full speed over the uneven ground ... **not a single bolt touched him.** The troopers following hugged the ground and crawled ... while their commander stood erect and ran unscathed through the burning trajectories of death." (JT ch. 28)
- He blows the bunker doors with a thermal detonator from 20 m away, then kills three droids at the inner blast wall "In seconds". He arrives "his clothing smoldering from near hits". He navigates "with unerring accuracy toward Pors Tonith's bunker" (Force guidance).
- **Halcyon senses it from his command post**: "the disturbance in the Force he was experiencing was a result of Anakin's tapping into it ... there was something troubling".
- **Dark-side tilt:** "All he knew was the joy of the Force, a greater joy than he'd ever felt before. There was so much power in the Force, and all that power was his—his!" He moves to kill the surrendered technicians first so Tonith "would know his fate, and fear before he died."
- **Qui-Gon's voice intervenes:** "You must use the Force for good, Anakin." / "**The Force is too strong to use for anything but good, Padawan. Remember that, and you can be the greatest Jedi of all.**" Anakin "severed his connection to the Force. He almost staggered from the sudden loss of so much joy and power". Afterwards his face is "white as a sheet, drained ... lines around his mouth like those of an old man" (the cost of the surge). Tonith thinks him "unbalanced and unpredictable".
- Tonith surrenders and orders a cease-fire. He was "a few more minutes" from relief.
- A clone trooper is killed by a bolt that "burned completely through his body armor" (clone armor vs. a battle-droid rifle).

## Ch. 29: the space battle; the flagship kill
- Halcyon leads 100 fighters and "could barely keep up with the young Jedi pilot, and instead of leading the attack formation, in essence he became Anakin's wingmate."
- Anakin passes under a destroyer at almost 3,000 kph, "too fast ... for even a warship's target acquisition system", then **kills it with one proton torpedo into the engines**.
- "now the Force was upon him again". He makes a pass at the **Separatist flagship** (cloaked) at 5,000 kph, takes a blaster-cannon hit ("Azure Angel II's armor deflected most of the destructive force"), and fires a torpedo "up this ship's spout". "The enormous blast from the destruction of the Separatist flagship decisively tilted the odds ... by engulfing many of the enemy's ships". The blast engulfs Anakin too.
- Anakin to Halcyon: "Say hello to the missus for me."

## Ch. 30: survival; Slayke's strength
- Anakin escapes using a **homemade hyperdrive** on the Delta-7: "just before the missile detonated, I engaged the drive and jumped free." Timing: "The same way I always knew what the ground was like before I could see it when I was Podracing ... It must be the Force." The canopy is fused, and two starboard cannons are gone.
- Halcyon cuts into the fused canopy with his lightsaber; **Slayke rips it off by hand** (a strong non-Force human).
- Halcyon: "compared to him, we're just a couple of old, used-up dishrags in this business of war." Odie: "he was a one-man army." Vick: "Nothing could touch him ... he wasted a lot more droids!"
- Halcyon gives the *Plooriod Bodkin* to Slayke.

## Epilogue
- Anakin marries Erk and Odie. Slayke: "First you win a war single-handed ... you're someone who's going to change a lot of things in this galaxy".
- **Council:** Windu: "We shall invest him with his Jedi Knighthood when he returns." Yoda: "**In the Force great disturbances there were.** Sensed them, did you, my old friend?" Windu: "Evidently Anakin called upon it more than once". Yoda senses "something—he was missing".
- **Sidious** "had sensed the disturbance in the Force that was troubling Yoda—it was not the first time recently either ... now convinced he had been right about the boy." Sidious had expected Praesitlyn to be lost: "His minions on Praesitlyn had been defeated, as he'd expected they would be". Praesitlyn was a test of Anakin, run through Dooku's hidden plan, Palpatine's recommendation of Halcyon, and Sly Moore.
- So Anakin's Force surges on Praesitlyn were **felt on Coruscant by Yoda, Windu and Sidious** (galactic-range sensing of his peak).
