# Jedi Trial: notes part 2 (ch. 15–27)

- Read 2026-11-03 CST from the owner's MEGA epub (handle BS9gSZia). Citation "JT ch. N".

## Ch. 15: Slayke's last stand
- Tank droids ("crawlers") deflect artillery bolts. The countermine flips one onto a bunker. Izable, Eliey and Kaudine fall. Slayke: "When help does arrive we'll have worn these nerfs down to the point where a single Jedi Padawan will be able to kick them to pieces" (a soldier's rhetoric).

## Ch. 16: breaking the blockade; Halcyon clears boarding droids
- The jamming platform is a droid control ship, killed by the *Ranger*'s first salvo: **MG1-A proton torpedoes**, "range of three thousand kilometers, a velocity of twenty thousand kph. Unaffected by energy fields". It was fired at a range of 1,000 km with line-of-sight guidance.
- Cloaked skirmisher ships board the *Ranger* and knock out its propulsion. About 100 crew are unaccounted for. Halcyon leads the counter-boarding in a vacuum suit:
> "His lightsaber flashed in a whirl of energy, easily deflecting the bolts from the droids' laser rifles. He slashed into them, one-two-three ... and, as the next three battle droids came through the hatch, disposed of them in turn. **In six seconds he had turned the compartment into a junkyard.**" (JT ch. 16)
> "The compartment was full of battle droids, all of which opened fire at the same time. Halcyon parried the blaster bolts with his lightsaber. Most of the bolts arced back onto the droids that had fired them." (JT ch. 16)
  - Limit: "Halcyon's suit was nearly melted through in places from near hits". Quegh: "if it hadn't been for you, we'd all be dead now." The *Ranger* is scuttled.
- Anakin calms himself with Jedi training ("slowed his heartbeat") and realizes "how little he had used the Force since they'd set out from Coruscant."

## Ch. 17: the landings; Dooku's assessment
- Anakin flies the lead troop lander at 10 m above the ground, approaching from 100 km out (each craft carries 50 clones). "I've been flying like this since I was a boy!"
- **Dooku on the two Jedi:** "Nejaa Halcyon, you will find, is careful and predictable, but **beware the young Jedi—he is volatile.** This is both a danger to you and a possible weakness you can exploit." / "if either is liable to succumb to his emotions, it is Skywalker." (JT ch. 17)
- Tonith: "not even your vaunted General Grievous, the killing machine". Dooku: "Defy me, question me, and you are dead." Tonith withdraws to the plateau and rigs the center for demolition.
- Reija's broadcast: "I beg you, for the sake of my people, Attack! Attack! Attack!"

## Ch. 18: Slayke meets the relief force; Palpatine sits on the broadcast
- **Slayke has about 2,000 soldiers left of 50,000.** He scorns the Jedi and clones: "If there's anything more useless in this galaxy than a Jedi's brain, it's a clone trooper."
- Palpatine keeps the broadcast secret, correctly predicting that the watch officer Jenbean will leak it: "where emotion rules, the wise man will hedge his bets every time."

## Ch. 19: the reinforcement fleet; Halcyon's quick draw
- The Separatist reinforcement fleet has transports with "hundreds of thousands of battle droids folded motionless in their racks" and a crewed flagship, "a monstrous killing machine in its own right". "Weapons systems that could destroy whole fleets".
- **Halcyon's draw speed:**
> "With one smooth motion made so fast nobody—**not even Anakin**—saw it coming, Halcyon drew his lightsaber and activated it." / "They come in handy when you're outnumbered a hundred to one." (JT ch. 19)
- Anakin stops the posturing with "the dark fury in his eyes". Reija's broadcast moves him: "she reminds me of ..." (his mother, ch. 20).
- Slayke places himself under Halcyon's command.

## Ch. 20–21: the battle plan and reconnaissance
- A vertical envelopment is ruled out: dense AA with quad lasers and ion cannons gives "at least thirty-five percent casualties just going in". Attackers suffer "at least three to one" losses against fortified positions. The plan is a central feint plus a flank sweep. Slayke: "The young Jedi is becoming a strategist."
- Grudo: "I've never seen someone as smart in as many ways as you are. You're quick in everything: to learn, to decide, to act."
- A vibroknuckler cuts a groove in rock 25 mm long and 10 mm deep "in only a few seconds" (melting the rock). Grudo silently beheads a droid with a vibroblade.

## Ch. 21 (end): Grudo's rearguard
> "In ten seconds he lay six of them low ... Blaster bolts caromed off the rocks, singeing him; two hit him glancing blows and he staggered, but didn't go down ... Thirty seconds into the fight there were no more droids to shoot at." (JT ch. 21; Grudo, a non-Force-sensitive Rodian, with a blaster and vibroblade)
- Grudo is then shot in the head by Odie (friendly fire) and dies in the aid station; Anakin threatens the doctor. "Grudo would be avenged."
- L'Loxx's recon of the right-flank hill finds it lightly held and the droids showing a lack of maintenance (cited by Anakin in ch. 26: "there's a lack of maintenance among the battle droids").

## Ch. 22: Green Wizard (clone commando CT-19/39)
- He reaches the center buildings undetected, loses a leg to friendly artillery, ties it off and crawls back to the riverbed before dying. "Clone commandos were at their best when faced with obstacles that would be insuperable to any ordinary being."
- An ARC captain questions Anakin's orders. Anakin decides to lead from the front.

## Ch. 23: the first assault
- Tonith anticipates the flank attack and reinforces the hill. Anakin senses the clones' fear: "**minute perturbations in the Force told Anakin that these five were reacting to the impending attack like regular troopers**". The gunner hits a target at 2,100 m. Anakin rides exposed in the cupola. Halcyon calls off the attack after 20 minutes.

## Ch. 24: the FDC droid; artillery figures
- "Laser and ion cannons ... fire line-of-sight". Slayke's self-propelled heavy mortars have a "maximum range of fifty kilometers ... warheads weighing up to one thousand kilos". The droid claims it could arrange "to demolish everything within fifty kilometers".

## Ch. 25: Anakin in the tank battle (physical and Force feats)
> "Without even thinking about it, Anakin reached down with one arm and grabbed the driver by the bottom of his back plate. With the other he **sent a Force push that flung the cupola hatch open** ... hauled him out of his seat, up into the cupola, and over the side." (JT ch. 25; he one-arms an armored clone out of a burning vehicle)
> "Anakin had to hold himself back: the driver was well trained and in good shape, but even so, **he couldn't match a Jedi at top speed**." / "With an easy Force leap, Anakin bounded onto the vehicle's dorsal surface" (JT ch. 25)
- "Laser and blaster bolts sizzled through the air. **Anakin effortlessly batted them out of the way.**" He leads the column with his raised lightsaber as a beacon.
- A huge mine (in the old Izable tunnel) wipes out the lead company. The droids shelter in bunkers proof against 1,000-kilo mortar shells. Tonith has sliced the Republic command net and fakes Halcyon's voice and authentication code, so friendly artillery shells Anakin's transports. At the foot of the hill, Anakin "used his lightsaber to divert several bolts aimed directly at him", while troopers beside him "were cut down in droves". This is a limit: he cannot shield a massed formation.
- **Ventress to Tonith:** "When this is over we shall meet, and I will kill you." Tonith: "Jedi running about with their lightsabers. Pish! It's firepower and tactics that count".

## Ch. 26: losses and Anakin's raid plan
- Anakin loses over 600 killed, wounded or missing (including all the commandos); Halcyon loses over 1,000. Water needs are 8 L per clone per day, 160,000 L a day for the force. Each clone carries 40 kg or more. Daily supply needs are 2,000 metric tons, but only 1,000 tons a day get through.
- **Anakin backs Slayke's proposal to "burn him off the mesa"** (including the hostages). Halcyon refuses: "under no circumstances will we sacrifice the lives of the noncombatants". Halcyon notices "a hardness that hadn't been there before" in Anakin.
- Anakin knows where the hostages are through the Force: "Trust me, Colonel. I'm a Jedi." The raid uses 17 soldiers and two transports.

## Ch. 27: the rescue raid; Reija's death
- Anakin flies 15 m above the ground "as if he could see the terrain before it showed up in front of them" (precognitive piloting).
- **The corridor fight:**
> "he seemed at the center of a cyclone of blue light as his lightsaber whirled and slashed at the droids whose blaster bolts, aimed directly at him at point-blank range, bounced off the high-energy blade ... **In seconds, the droids were smoking piles of junk** ... to those behind him watching it seemed he had merely strolled through the droids" (JT ch. 27; 6–8 droids)
> "it was as if Anakin knew the droid's intention even before it moved ... his movements were so fast that by comparison the droids' defense was in slow motion." (JT ch. 27; six more in the control room)
- Anakin is 3 m ahead of the fastest clone. A sensed counterattack: "he knew what direction it was coming from."
- **Reija Momen** takes a bolt meant for Anakin (the droid's weapon then fails from poor maintenance). Before that, a precognitive flash: "In a microsecond he saw what was coming and knew where it would lead him, but he was powerless to interfere." "Memories of his mother's death flooded him and he felt the rage rise ... he once again became an Avenger."
- The Separatist reinforcement fleet arrives, masked until it was in range ("I've never seen so many ships in one place").
