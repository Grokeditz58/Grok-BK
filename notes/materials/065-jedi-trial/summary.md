# Jedi Trial: summary of strongest findings

Source: David Sherman & Dan Cragg, *Star Wars: Jedi Trial* (Del Rey 2004; ebook v3.1_r1, eISBN 978-0-345-46308-1). Hand-read in full on 2026-11-03: the Prologue, all 30 chapters and the Epilogue, the author bibliographies, the Del Rey Legends intro, and the *The Clone Wars* (Traviss) excerpt (ch. 1–2). The other back-matter excerpts were skimmed. Setting: Praesitlyn, the early Clone Wars (Anakin's Knighthood trial). Citations are "JT ch. N" (P = Prologue, Epi = Epilogue).

1. **Anakin's peak: "total oneness with the Force", bolt-proof and unstoppable, then a near-fall.**
   > "A sense of invincible power came over him, infusing him completely ... In that instant when he achieved total oneness with the Force he knew he could do anything, and it felt good." (JT ch. 28)
   > "he parried the bolts effortlessly, sending some ripping through the walls and roof, others back into the very droids that had fired them ... The droids ... flew apart like cheap dolls" / "It seemed as if Tonith's entire army was firing at Anakin, but as he ran at full speed ... not a single bolt touched him." (JT ch. 28)
   - He moves to execute surrendered technicians ("all that power was his—his!"). **Qui-Gon's voice** stops him: "The Force is too strong to use for anything but good, Padawan. Remember that, and you can be the greatest Jedi of all." Cutting off the Force leaves him staggering and "white as a sheet".
2. **His Force surges were felt across the galaxy, by Yoda, Windu and Sidious.**
   > "In the Force great disturbances there were. Sensed them, did you, my old friend?" (JT Epi, Yoda) / Sidious "had sensed the disturbance in the Force that was troubling Yoda—it was not the first time recently either ... now convinced he had been right about the boy." (JT Epi)
   - Halcyon also senses it from the command post (ch. 28). Sidious had expected the Separatists to lose Praesitlyn; the campaign was a test of Anakin. Dooku had told Tonith: "beware the young Jedi—he is volatile" (ch. 17).
3. **Lightsaber skill: a Padawan beats a Master; the two clear rooms of droids in seconds.**
   > "Who would have believed that a mere Padawan could best me with a lightsaber?" (JT ch. 3, Halcyon, after their second day of sparring; the first day was even, JT P)
   > "In six seconds he had turned the compartment into a junkyard." / "Most of the bolts arced back onto the droids that had fired them." (JT ch. 16, Halcyon in a vacuum suit)
   > "he seemed at the center of a cyclone of blue light ... In seconds, the droids were smoking piles of junk" / "his movements were so fast that by comparison the droids' defense was in slow motion." (JT ch. 27, Anakin against 6–8 droids, then 6 more)
   - Halcyon's draw was "so fast nobody—not even Anakin—saw it coming" (ch. 19). **Limits:** Halcyon's suit "nearly melted through" from near misses (ch. 16). At the foot of the hill Anakin deflected only the bolts aimed at him while troopers on either side "were cut down in droves" (ch. 25).
4. **Starfighter feats: one torpedo kills a destroyer, and the flagship goes up taking ships with it.**
   > "he was moving too fast—almost three thousand kilometers per hour—for even a warship's target acquisition system" (JT ch. 29; one proton torpedo into the engines destroys the cloaked destroyer)
   > "The enormous blast from the destruction of the Separatist flagship decisively tilted the odds toward the Republic forces by engulfing many of the enemy's ships" (JT ch. 29; he made his pass at 5,000 kph)
   - Halcyon "could barely keep up ... in essence he became Anakin's wingmate." Anakin survives with a **homemade hyperdrive** jump timed by the Force ("The same way I always knew what the ground was like ... when I was Podracing", ch. 30).
5. **Physical feats and senses.** One-armed, he hauls an armored clone from a burning transport while "With the other he sent a Force push that flung the cupola hatch open". An armored clone "couldn't match a Jedi at top speed" (ch. 25). He makes an "easy Force leap" onto a moving transport. He senses the clones' fear: "minute perturbations in the Force" (ch. 23). He finds the hostages and Tonith's bunker through the Force (ch. 26–28) and scans a lobby for Force-sensitives (ch. 9).
6. **Scale of the war and non-Force baselines.** Tonith has about 1,000,000 droids, a 200-ship invasion fleet and a 126-ship force at Sluis Van (ch. 2). The Separatists lose over 100,000 droids (ch. 15). Slayke is reduced from 50,000 to about 2,000 troops (ch. 18). The relief force is 20,000 clones. The T-19 starfighter tops out at 20,000 kph, and Erk scores 10 kills in one minute (ch. 4–5). Grudo (a Rodian) drops six droids in ten seconds and shrugs off two glancing bolts (ch. 21). Halcyon without the Force goes even with Grudo hand to hand (ch. 12). Slayke pulls a fused canopy off by hand (ch. 30).

Other data: MG1-A proton torpedoes have a 3,000 km range at 20,000 kph and are "Unaffected by energy fields" (ch. 16). Slayke's heavy mortars reach 50 km with warheads up to 1,000 kg (ch. 24), yet droid bunkers withstand them (ch. 25). A battle-droid bolt "burned completely through" clone armor (ch. 28). A vibroknuckler cuts rock (ch. 21). Dooku once Force-choked Tonith in person (ch. 11). Ventress promises to kill Tonith (ch. 25). Sly Moore uses a holoshroud (ch. 8). Halcyon has a wife, Scerra, and a son, Valin (ch. 14). The Traviss excerpt shows Anakin on Christophsis using an "instinctive and unthinking Force push" against shrapnel and "a Force-assisted stranglehold" to rip a droid's head off.
