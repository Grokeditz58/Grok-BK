# EA5 The Ghostling Children: summary

- Full read (l.1–689). No Jedi appear. This is the Mos Espa kids' book.

## Feats
- **Anakin (about 8):** Force intuition about machines. **Times a mass transmitter detonation by feel** to wipe out a seeker droid and 6 battle droids, collapsing parts of the fortress (Ch. 14). Ion blaster sabotage.
- **Gondry (Byss cyclops):** regenerates in moments and regrows limbs. Ignores arrows, stun bolts and blaze bugs.
- **Djas Puhr:** heat vision through walls and the nose of a tracker.
- Kitster, Dorn and Pala: infiltration, slicing, spycraft. Kitster dodges a cliffborer worm and beats Gardulla with cleanser.

## Lore
- **The ancient cube:** Jedi duel, volcano, moon star map and forbidden-knowledge symbol. It feels evil. Set-up for later books.
- Rakir Banai is Kitster's father. Sebulba's slave trade: Khiss's price list.
- Implanted bomb transmitters. Gardulla's fortress and pleasure garden. Ghostlings and Datar. Lord Tantos.
