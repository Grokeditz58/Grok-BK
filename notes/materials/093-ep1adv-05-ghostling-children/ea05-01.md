# EA5 The Ghostling Children: notes

## Ch. 1 (l.24–74): the raid on Datar
- Datar: three moons, bayah trees, blaze bugs that burst into flame when crushed. Ghostlings live in hanging vine nests. They are "terribly frail": a touch bruises them, and a weak blow breaks bones.
- Raiders working for Sebulba: **Djas Puhr**, a Sakiyan hunter who sees heat signatures through walls and has a keen sense of smell, and **Gondry**, a cyclops from Byss who **regenerates in moments and can regrow limbs**. Ghostling arrows, stun blasters and blaze-bug bombs don't stop him. Seven children are taken.
- **Princess Arawynne**, 7: "Princess no more… Now, slave Arawynne."

## Ch. 2–3 (l.76–184): Mos Espa
- Pala, a Twi'lek, is trained at Madame Vansitt's Charm Academy in espionage, poisons and hand-to-hand fighting. She is sold to **Lord Tantos**, a Firrerean pirate lord, to become his assassin-spy.
- Anakin works for Watto. Kitster tips him off that Sebulba and Gasgano have arrived. The friends: Amee, Wald, Dorn (a Bothan in Jabba's spy training) and Pala.
- **Anakin's Force intuition:** he "could just feel" that Gasgano's pod has nothing new, and spots Sebulba's new Kuat 40-Z stabilizers the same way.
- Slaves carry bomb transmitters under the skin. Pala fakes a womp-rat panic as a distraction. Anakin finds the energy-cage crate full of Ghostlings with no implants yet.

## Ch. 4 (l.186–214)
- Khiss the Dug runs Sebulba's rare-slave order business: a Bothan for 10,000, a Columi for 100,000, Ghostlings for 30,000 wupiupi each. They are smuggled in crates of "spare parts" on his racing tours. Djas Puhr smells "human children… trouble".

## Ch. 5 (l.216–287)
- Jawa rendezvous at Market Rock in the Jundland Wastes. Anakin can't afford a Radon-Ulzer engine.
- **The cube:** an ancient palm-sized cube with art of two Jedi duelling, a volcano, a star map with landing instructions for a certain moon, and "a lamp with a knife blade through it, the symbol of forbidden knowledge". Anakin feels "something… evil" inside. An old Jawa sells it for exactly his last 3 wupiupi: "a small price to pay for knowledge." Set-up for later books.
- Shmi fixes a droid for her sick friend Matta, whose master Dengula might sell her to Kessel. Shmi: "Whenever we do something kind, it makes all of the problems in the universe shrink a little." Anakin has a Force vision of flying Pala and the Ghostlings to freedom.

## Ch. 6–7 (l.289–362)
- The Ghostlings are weak in Tatooine's heavier gravity. Borofir is 3; Conno and Alamar are 5. Arawynne silently sends a call for help.
- **Kitster's secret:** his father is **Rakir Banai**, a pirate and vigilante who frees slaves and has Hutt bounties on him. Kitster was sold at 4 to Gardulla.
- Dorn hacks Gardulla's house computer. The Ghostlings are meant as "lawn ornaments" in her underground pleasure garden, with predators to hunt them. Implants are due in the morning. Game Book break at l.360.

## Ch. 8–13 (l.364–623): the fortress
- They go in Jawa disguise with light patches for eyes, Kitster's sawed vent, a grappling reel and an ion blaster. The fortress sits on an old mine maze.
- Kitster dodges a cliffborer worm. Gardulla, half-asleep in her pool, makes him scrub her back, then grabs him to eat. **He sprays cleanser down her throat.**
- Dorn releases Gardulla's mutant gourmet bugs (ghost spydrs from Kubindi, effrikim worms, mora beetles that honk "Flee!"). They cause chaos, and a beetle chases Gardulla around her pool.
- Anakin trips a Gamorrean. **He ion-blasts the security console.** Pala shuts the door and the guard's own bolt ricochets back onto him (l.531–537).
- Anakin bluffs Gondry away from guard duty in a fake Jawa voice. Dorn cracks the energy cage.
- **The transmitter implants are used as explosives to blow the door.** The children hide among the gnarltrees in the unfinished pleasure garden, looked after by Ho'Din foresters, rather than flying out under the turret guns.

## Ch. 14 (l.625–689)
- **Anakin's Force-timed detonation:** he scatters the transmitters, then sets them all off at once, destroying a seeker droid and **6 battle droids**. Caverns collapse and "the whole mountain fortress seemed to groan" (l.645–648). He reaches for "a part of himself… just beginning to learn to touch".
- A turret fires on a passing sandcrawler, which covers their escape.
- Coda: Gardulla accuses Sebulba of stealing back his own slaves and won't pay. Sebulba sends Djas Puhr to hunt for them. NEXT: *The Hunt for Anakin Skywalker*.
