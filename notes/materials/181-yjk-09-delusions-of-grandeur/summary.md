# Young Jedi Knights 9: Delusions of Grandeur: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-20 CST. Full read of READ l.1–1776, every line in order: front matter, ch. 1–22, and the back matter (Jedi Bounty preview, ads, back cover). Notes are limited to scaling-relevant material (owner's rulings, 2026-11-20). The quotes are in dg-01 and dg-02; this summary is in our own words.
- Authors: Kevin J. Anderson & Rebecca Moesta. Ninth Young Jedi Knights book (Boulevard Books, 1997), the third in the Bornan Thul / Diversity Alliance arc. 22 chapters.

## Plot
- **Ch. 1, 11, 13, 15, 17–18, 20–21 (Yavin 4 and Mechis III):** Raynar goes to Mechis III to look after his uncle Tyko's droid factories, and the friends go with him. IG-88 and a free Tyko are there: Tyko staged his own kidnapping to lure Bornan out. His "harmless" assassin droids were programmed to spare humans only. Jaina reprograms IG-88 so that he can never intentionally kill anyone. Zekk arrives with Bornan's message, and Dengar follows him and bombs the factories. Jacen, Tenel Ka and Raynar beat him on the roof with the Force, and IG-88 disarms him. Zekk sends Dengar's tracer off as a decoy, Raynar redesigns the factory lines, and IG-88 is sent to find and protect Bornan Thul.
- **Ch. 3, 6, 10, 12, 16 (Zekk):** Zekk is paid for the Fonterrat job, then hired by "Master Wary" (Bornan Thul in disguise) to broadcast a message to the Bornaryn fleet. Dengar ambushes him on Ziost, and Zekk escapes by flying on Force instinct. Boba Fett advises him to finish his first job before taking a second bounty. Zekk finds on Kuar that the assassin droids were built on Mechis III, and at Shanko's Hive Bornan warns that Nolaa's goal could mean "the extinction of all humans".
- **Ch. 2, 4, 8, 14, 19, 22 (Lowie):** Raaba returns and draws Lowie into the Diversity Alliance. On Kashyyyk she completes her syren-fiber rite and wins the Wookiees over with a speech. She takes Lowie and Sirra to Ryloth, where Nolaa and Hovrak welcome "a Jedi" and Corrsk nearly attacks them.
- **Ch. 5, 7, 9:** Lusa tells of her years in the Alliance. Aryn gets Bornan's message that humanity's fate is at stake. Nolaa kills a captured Red Guard with the last vial of Derricote's human-only plague and reveals that she is hunting the Emperor's hidden plague depot.

## Strongest findings (by character)
- **Jacen:** Force-jostles Dengar's twin blaster-cannon shots wide and helps push Dengar's ship off a roof (ch. 20). He and Tenel Ka cut the targeting lasers and dodge the bolts with the Force (ch. 13); the lasers were set to miss, a limitation noted in ch. 15. He also has his empathy and senses (ch. 1, 17, 20). Limitation: he doubts he could deflect Dengar's explosive bolts with his lightsaber (ch. 20).
- **Tenel Ka:** she shields Jacen from Dengar with her body and joins the telekinetic shove (ch. 20). She runs with Lusa because no one else can keep up (ch. 11). Statements: "Lowbacca is a good fighter and a talented Jedi" (ch. 22).
- **Zekk:** Force-instinct flying against Dengar twice (ch. 6, 20), Force-chosen hyperspace jump (ch. 6), and his senses read Bornan (ch. 3, 16). Limitation: the "dud" grenade he thought he had deactivated with the Force was a tracer (ch. 21).
- **Raynar:** telekinesis in the rooftop shove (ch. 20), sparring skill (ch. 11) and a "brilliant" factory redesign (ch. 21).
- **Jaina:** reprograms IG-88 (ch. 17), dodges a laser after a Force warning (ch. 13), and has diagnostic skill (ch. 11).
- **Lowbacca:** his fame as a Jedi (Hovrak and Nolaa, ch. 19) and his treetop leaps and the rite-of-passage background (ch. 2).
- **Dengar:** cybernetic reflexes, the illegal ion cannon and Vader-era elite status (ch. 6). He holds a two-armed blaster cannon in each hand, has lightning speed, and escapes from a falling ship (ch. 20). Boba Fett: "Dengar is … not a fool" (ch. 10).
- **IG-88:** "still as capable as the old IG-88" (ch. 15). His precision fire disarms Dengar without killing him, and his bolts ricochet off his torso (ch. 20). "Built to track down people who do not wish to be found" (ch. 21).
- **Boba Fett:** his presence and reputation in the cantina (ch. 10).
- **Nolaa Tarkona:** the plague, with "more destructive power than the Death Star" (ch. 9), and her charisma (ch. 19). **Raaba:** completes her syren-fiber rite and gives her oratory (ch. 14).
