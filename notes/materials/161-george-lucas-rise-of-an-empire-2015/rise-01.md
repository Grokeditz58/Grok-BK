# *George Lucas: Rise of an Empire* (Bluewater Comics, 2015): page-by-page notes

Source: owner's MEGA cbr `cekUnR7Y`, 24 JPEGs. Citations are by cbr page image. Lettering is transcribed in capitals where the balloon/caption is lettered in capitals, with typography locked as printed.

## pp. 1–2 — Cover and credits

- **p. 1, cover.** Title: GEORGE LUCAS / *Rise of an Empire* (the subtitle in red script). Painted/illustrated cover: a large older-Lucas portrait in a hooded, Vader-like black garment; a younger bearded Lucas in a red plaid shirt operating a film camera; a smaller pair of young men (Coppola-like and a blonde in a tan jacket holding what looks like a lightsaber hilt). Bluewater Comics logo, lower left, signed "Chris Fischer." This is cover art, not a story claim that Lucas is Vader.

## pp. 3–9 — Crash cold-open, childhood, school, draft

- **p. 3.** Caption: "MODESTO, SAN JOAQUIN VALLEY. JUNE 12TH, 1962..." Young Lucas in a small white car; "VROOOOOOOOMMMM!!!"; a red car; a blond driver "OH, NO!"; "CRASH!" / "EEEEEEEEEERTT!!" This is a flash-forward cold open; the childhood origin starts on p. 5.
- **p. 4.** Overturned white car against a tree, smoke, Lucas bleeding on the grass. Certified crop **q01**. Caption (quoted as Lucas): “I REALIZED THAT I’D BEEN LIVING MY LIFE SO CLOSE TO THE EDGE FOR SO LONG. THAT’S WHEN I DECIDED TO GO STRAIT, TO BE A BETTER STUDENT, TO TRY TO DO SOMETHING WITH MYSELF.” Typo **STRAIT**.
- **p. 5.** Birth in Modesto to Dorothy Ellinore and George Walton Lucas, Sr. "GEORGE WALTON LUCAS, JR. WAS RAISED ON A WALNUT RANCH IN HIS YOUTH." "AS A YOUNG CHILD, GEORGE DEVELOPED A FEAR FOR WATER AND COULD NOT SWIM." "HIS INABILITY TO SWIM GAVE HIM THE DRIVE TO SUCCEED LATER IN LIFE." (The comic's causal claim; not a quoted Lucas line.) Young George with a film camera and reels.
- **p. 6.** Thomas Downey High School. "FASCINATED WITH FAST MACHINES"; time "HANGING OUT AT GARAGES"; racing "ON THE UNDERGROUND CIRCUIT AT FAIRGROUNDS." "LEADING TO THAT FATEFUL CRASH JUST DAYS BEFORE HE WAS TO GRADUATE FROM HIGH SCHOOL."
- **p. 7.** "THAT FATEFUL COLLISION WITH THE WALNUT TREE HAD CAUSED GEORGE TO *RETHINK* HIS PLANS FOR THE REST OF HIS LIFE..." "THE RACING. THE CRASH. THE NEAR DEATH EXPERIENCE." "LIFE HAD TO CHANGE..." Racing no longer an option; Modesto Junior College; "SOCIAL SCIENCES: PSYCHOLOGY, SOCIOLOGY, ANTHROPOLOGY." "GEORGE GRADUATED FROM MODESTO JUNIOR COLLEGE IN 1964."
- **p. 8.** USC. "IT WAS AT USC WHERE GEORGE WOULD MEET HIS FUTURE WIFE AND COLLABORATOR: MARCIA LOU GRIFFIN." Student films listed: "LOOK AT LIFE, HERBIE, 1:42.08, THE EMPEROR, FILMMAKER, AND 6-18-67." (The list omits *Freiheit* and *anyone lived in a pretty (how) town*, and *filmmaker* / *6.18.67* are usually dated 1968 / 1967 as a professional short rather than a student-film package.) "PASSIONATE WITH CAMERAWORK AND EDITING FILMS." "IN 1967, GEORGE GRADUATED FROM THE UNIVERSITY OF SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA'S SCHOOL OF CINEMA WITH A BACHELORS OF FINE ARTS IN FILM." (Standard bios place the BFA in 1966; Shields's chronology agrees with 1966. The comic's 1967 graduation is a slip.)
- **p. 9.** "IN 1967, GEORGE TRIED JOINING THE UNITED STATES AIR FORCE AS AN OFFICER." "— BUT HIS NUMEROUS SPEEDING TICKETS PREVENTED HIS ENLISTMENT." "HIS PAST HAD RETURNED TO HAUNT HIM." Then: "GEORGE WAS EVENTUALLY DRAFTED BY THE UNITED STATES ARMY..." Uncle Sam poster. "BUT HIS MEDICAL TESTS REVEALED THAT HE HAD DIABETES AND HE WAS RULED EXEMPT." (This two-step Air Force / tickets then Army / diabetes sequence is the comic's version; other bios often tell only the draft-and-diabetes story.)

## pp. 10–15 — Graduate school, Zoetrope, THX, *American Graffiti*

- **p. 10.** Re-enrolled at USC as a graduate student, 1967. "ONE PARTICULAR CLASS HE TOOK TAUGHT HIM THAT FILM WAS MORE THAN LINEAR." "HE TOOK A CLASS TAUGHT BY LESTOR NOVROS." (Misspelling of **Lester Novros**.) "COLOR, LIGHT, MOVEMENT, SPACE, AND TIME IN FILM." Selected with a group of student filmmakers "TO CREATE A DOCUMENTARY ABOUT THE MAKING OF THE FILM MCKENNA'S GOLD." (The film is *Mackenna's Gold*.)
- **p. 11.** "ONE OF HIS DIRECTORIAL DEBUTS WAS THE SHORT FILM THX-1138: 4EB." "MANY OF THE SCENES WERE FILMED AT LAX: LOS ANGELES INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT." "HIS SHORT FILM THX-1138: 4EB WAS AWARDED THE 1967-68 NATIONAL STUDENT FILM FESTIVAL GRAND PRIZE... FIRST PLACE!" Meeting with Francis Ford Coppola; "THEIR RELATIONSHIP DEVELOPED AND GREW. THEN, ON DECEMBER 12TH, 1969 THE TWO FRIENDS FORMED A FILM COMPANY CALLED AMERICAN ZOETROPE."
- **p. 12.** Feature *THX 1138* "STARRED ACTORS ROBERT DUVALL AND DONALD PLEASENCE." (The comic spells it "PLEASENCE.") "THX 1138 WAS NOT GROSSING SLIGHTLY OVER 2.4 MILLION DOLLARS." The sentence is ungrammatical; the intended sense is that the film was *not* a success, grossing slightly over $2.4 million. "IT WOULD BE A LESSON HE SOON WOULD NOT REPEAT."
- **p. 13.** Next film: *American Graffiti*, "PRODUCED… ON THE SLIM BUDGET OF $775,000 IN 1972." Mel's Drive-In. "IN 1973, AMERICAN GRAFFITI BECAME AN IMMEDIATE SUCCESS." Preview audience cheering; "SEVERAL STUDIO EXECUTIVES STILL DID NOT BELIEVE IN THE FILM." "AMERICAN GRAFFITTI" (extra T) "HAS BECOME ONE OF THE MOST PROFITABLE MOVIES OF ALL TIME!" "GROSSING OVER 117 MILLION DOLLARS IN MOVIE TICKETS WORLD WIDE." (Shields gives $145 million; figures differ by source and date.)
- **p. 14.** Cast portraits in circles: "RICHARD DREYFUSS, RON HOWARD, PAUL LE MAT, CHARLES MARTIN SMITH, CINDY WILLIAMS, CANDY CLARK, MACKENZIE PHILLIPS AND HARRISON FORD."
- **p. 15.** *Graffiti* "WON BEST MOTION PICTURE (MUSICAL OR COMEDY) AT THE 31ST GOLDEN GLOBE AWARDS SHOW." "GEORGE YEARNED FOR COMPLETE CREATIVE CONTROL OVER HIS MOVIES. HE STRUGGLED WITH THE INTERFERENCE OFTEN OCCURRING FROM HOLLYWOOD EXECUTIVES." Anecdote: he "FIRED A PROP MANAGER FOR PUTTING AN INCORRECT BOTTLE IN THE SCENE" — he "WANTED AN AUTHENTIC 1950'S COKE BOTTLE AND THE ASSISTANT HAD PLACED A DIFFERENT TYPE." Prestige, fame, and money enough "TO PRODUCE AND DIRECT HIS NEXT PROJECT..." Cut to a man looking at a paper labeled "STAR WARS."

## pp. 16–19 — *Star Wars*, the franchise, the Ranch, flops

- **p. 16.** "STAR WARS TOOK SEVERAL YEARS TO DEVELOP." "GEORGE STRUGGLED FROM 1973 TO 1974 TO WRITE HIS SCRIPT." Notebook titled "The Star Wars." "BELIEVING IN HIS PROJECT THAT HAD STARTED AS AN IDEA DURING HIS YOUTH ORIGINATING FROM THE EARLY FLASH GORDON SERIALS..." "GEORGE INVESTED ONE MILLION DOLLARS OF HIS OWN MONEY INTO THE FILM." "THE BUDGET FOR STAR WARS WAS SET AT 8.5 MILLION DOLLARS." (A bar chart compares THX, *American Graffiti*, and *Star Wars* budgets.) "FILMING BEGAN IN 1976 ON NINE SOUND STAGES IN BOREHAMWOOD, ENGLAND." Crew painting walls and floors; filming "AS SOON AS THE PAINT WAS DRY" (a "WET PAINT" sign). "THE FILM WAS PLAGUED WITH PROBLEMS..." "ROBOTS DIDN'T FUNCTION CORRECTLY, RUNNING INTO WALLS DURING SHOOTING."
- **p. 17.** "LEGENDARY ACTOR SIR ALEC GUINNESS ALMOST QUIT WHEN HE LEARNED SCRIPT CHANGES HAD KILLED OFF HIS CHARACTER HALF-WAY THROUGH THE SCRIPT." "GEORGE HAD TO CONVINCE THE FAMED ACTOR TO STAY." "SPECIAL EFFECTS ACTUALLY DESTROYED SEVERAL SETS DURING PRODUCTION." Then a leap to success: "70MM 6 TRACK AUDIO," "SOLD OUT," "DESPITE NUMEROUS SETBACKS, STAR WARS WAS A HUGE SUCCESS..." "THE FILM BECAME A PHENOMENON." Marquees for *Empire*, *Jedi*, *The Phantom Menace*, *Attack of the Clones*, and *Revenge of the Sith* (the last title is cut off as "REVENGE OF THE SIT"). Closer: "IT SPAWNED FIVE SEQUELS BECOMING ONE OF THE MOST FAMOUS MOVIE FRANCHISES IN HOLLYWOOD HISTORY..." (Counting the two original sequels plus three prequels from *A New Hope*.)
- **p. 18.** "GEORGE'S SUCCESS ALLOWED HIM TO BRANCH OUT AND BUILD SUPPORT COMPANIES FOR HIS MOVIE PRODUCING EMPIRE..." "IN THE LATE 1970'S GEORGE LUCAS PURCHASED LAND NEAR NICASIO, CALIFORNIA, IN MARIN COUNTY." "HE SPENT NEARLY $100 MILLION OF HIS OWN MONEY TO BUILD THE TECHNOLOGICAL WONDER HE WOULD CALL SKYWALKER RANCH." "THE COMPOUND WOULD LATER BECOME A FILMMAKER'S RETREAT." "SKYWALKER SOUND HAS OPERATED ON THE RANCH SINCE 1987." "ITS INNOVATIONS AND CUTTING-EDGE SOUND EDITING HAS BEEN AN INTEGRAL PART OF MANY MOVIES." (Subject-verb agreement as lettered.)
- **p. 19.** "DESPITE HIS SUCCESS, SOME OF THE LEGENDARY DIRECTOR'S FILMS WERE NOT SO SUCCESSFUL..." "THX 1138 WAS COMMERCIALLY UNSUCCESSFUL." "GEORGE HELPED PRODUCE RED TAILS. IT ALSO FAILED TO ACHIEVE FINANCIAL SUCCESS." "*MORE AMERICAN GRAFFITI* FAILED TO LIVE UP TO THE PROSPERITY OF ITS PREDECESSOR." (*Red Tails* is 2012, so the comic is aware of post-2000 work even while later pages still have him "dating" Mellody Hobson.)

## pp. 20–24 — Attributed reflections, marriage, health, family, "retirement"

- **p. 20.** Caption: "GEORGE LUCAS OFTEN REFLECTED ON HIS PROJECTS WITH DEPTH AND INSIGHT..." "THOUGH A WATERSHED FILM IN HIS CAREER, GEORGE'S THOUGHTS FOR STAR WARS HAVE CHANGED OVER TIME..." Two attributed speech balloons (certified crops **q02**, **q03**), presented as Lucas speaking:
  - "STAR WARS WAS REALLY DESIGNED TO MAKE PEOPLE THINK OUTSIDE THE BOX — TO MAKE PEOPLE USE THEIR IMAGINATION AND THINK ANYTHING IS POSSIBLE."
  - "IN THE BEGINNING, I THOUGHT IT WAS GOING TO BE ONE LITTLE MOVIE."
  - "LITTLE DID HE KNOW IT WOULD BECOME ONE OF THE HIGHEST GROSSING MOVIES IN BOX OFFICE HISTORY." The success of the franchise "ALLOWED GEORGE TO WRITE AND PRODUCE OTHER PROJECTS..." *Raiders of the Lost Ark*, *Indiana Jones and the Temple of Doom*, *Indiana Jones and the Last Crusade* (papers). "AND THE CHANCE TO WORK WITH MANY AWARD WINNING ACTORS AS WELL..." Portraits: Sean Connery, Cate Blanchett, John Hurt. (Blanchett is *Kingdom of the Crystal Skull*, 2008 — again a post-2000 tell.)
- **p. 21.** "HIS PERSONAL LIFE HAS HAD CHALLENGES TOO." "HE MARRIED MARCIA LOU GRIFFIN IN 1969." "THEY FORMED A WORKING RELATIONSHIP OUTSIDE THEIR MARRIAGE TO EDIT GEORGE'S FILMS." "MARCIA WORKED LONG HOURS, TIRELESSLY EDITING SEVERAL OF HIS MOVIES." "SHE BELIEVED IN HIS FILMS. EVEN WHEN HE QUESTIONED HIMSELF AND THE STUDIOS THAT TRIED TO CONTROL HIS CREATIVITY." "SOMETIMES THEY ARGUED ON PROJECTS WHILE EDITING SCENES OF MOVIES OR DIFFERING ON THEMATIC DIRECTION." "EVENTUALLY, THEY DIVORCED IN 1983."
- **p. 22.** Health: diabetes "EARLY ON IN LIFE"; insulin vial and syringe. "DURING THE FILMING OF STAR WARS: A NEW HOPE, HE WAS HOSPITALIZED FOR STRESS AND DIAGNOSED WITH HYPERTENSION." "GEORGE HAD BEEN IN A LONG RELATIONSHIP WITH AND ENGAGED TO SINGER LINDA RONSTADT." "TODAY, GEORGE IS DATING MELLODY HOBSON, PRESIDENT OF ARIEL INVESTMENTS." "THEY HAVE BEEN TOGETHER SINCE 2006." "MELLODY IS PRESIDENT OF ARIEL INVESTMENTS, LLC, A CHICAGO INVESTMENT FIRM MANAGING OVER $3 BILLION IN ASSETS." (They married in 2013; the 2015 comic still says "dating," so this passage is stale.)
- **p. 23.** "DURING GEORGE'S MARRIAGE TO MARCIA, THEY HAD ADOPTED THREE CHILDREN." This is incorrect as a group: "AMANDA LUCAS WAS ADOPTED IN 1981." "KATIE LUCAS WAS ADOPTED IN 1988." "AND JETT LUCAS IN 1993." Katie and Jett were adopted after the 1983 divorce.
- **p. 24.** "TODAY, GEORGE HAS FORGED A NEW PATH FOR LIFE AFTER BLOCKBUSTER MOVIES..." Speech balloon (certified crop **q04**), presented as Lucas: "I'M RETIRING. I'M MOVING AWAY FROM THE BUSINESS, FROM THE COMPANY, FROM ALL THIS KIND OF STUFF." Captions: "HE'S AN EXECUTIVE PRODUCER FOR STAR WARS: THE CLONE WARS ANIMATED SHOW." "AND GEORGE IS ALSO PLANNING A LIVE-ACTION STAR WARS TELEVISION SERIES." "HIS PLANS ARE TO MAKE PERSONAL FILMS. SMALL IN SCOPE, ESOTERIC IN SUBJECT AND SCREENED EXCLUSIVELY IN ART HOUSES." Closer: "RETIREMENT SEEMS A GALAXY FAR, FAR AWAY..."

## Notes on use

- Bluewater Orbit comics routinely paraphrase celebrity lives; the four "Lucas" lines above are the comic's wording. Treat them as the comic's attributions, not as sealed interview transcripts.
- No page discusses Darth Vader as a character, Anakin, the Force, the Sith, the Chosen One, power levels, or canon hierarchy. The cover's hooded portrait is visual pastiche only.
- Recurring lettering/fact slips (for readers comparing bios): STRAIT; LESTOR NOVROS; MCKENNA'S GOLD; GRAFFITTI; ungrammatical THX gross line; 1967 USC graduation; three children "during" the marriage; Mellody still "dating" in a 2015 book after a 2013 wedding; "FIVE SEQUELS"; "REVENGE OF THE SIT."
