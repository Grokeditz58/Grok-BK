# The Cestus Deception: summary of strongest findings

Source: Steven Barnes, *Star Wars: The Cestus Deception* (Del Rey 2004; ebook v3.1_r1). Hand-read in full on 2026-11-03: front matter and timeline, all 82 chapters, the Afterword, the bundled novella "The Hive" (7 chapters), dedication, author pages and the Del Rey back matter. Citations are "CD ch. N" and "Hive ch. N".

1. **The JK bio-droid ("Jedi Killer") is precognitive, absorbs blaster fire, and takes a top Jedi's best effort. Its weakness is power.**
   > "This thing attacked on two levels simultaneously, a strategy beyond most living warriors" / "Kit accelerated into a blur of motion complex and rapid enough to baffle even Obi-Wan’s experienced gaze." (CD ch. 2)
   > "They seemed to aim where a person would be in a moment, rather than where he or she was now." / bolts "sang off the outer casing" (CD ch. 54)
   > "It had probably defeated the equivilent of a hundred warriors, but was running out of power!" (CD ch. 56)
   > "It had required two energy weapons and bruising hand-to-tentacle combat to stop the thing." (Obi-Wan vs one JK, CD ch. 63)
   - Its Force-sensitivity comes from a sleeping dashta eel: "Even unconscious, their nervous systems supply the Force sensitivity" (CD ch. 67). Volume production was a bluff because the cloning needs "another year, at least" (CD ch. 74). The eels "went insane" at killing, so JKs "never have functioned on a battlefield" (CD ch. 78).
2. **Kit Fisto is the better pure swordsman, and Obi-Wan admits it.**
   > "Obi-Wan swiftly discovered that Kit was the better swordfighter, astonishingly aggressive and intuitive in comparison with Obi-Wan’s more measured style. But the Nautolan gave himself deliberate disadvantages" (CD ch. 11)
   - Forms are named: Kit uses Form I, Anakin Form V ("strength"), Dooku Form II, and Obi-Wan Form III (CD ch. 11; Form III again in Hive ch. 3).
   - Kit's battle fever: "He flirted with battle fever, the howling demon in his mind trapped behind the bars of discipline ... Form I’s razor edge" (CD ch. 64). He fights unarmed as "a living, martial hurricane ... unlimited by human vertebral restraints" (CD ch. 77).
3. **Ventress is portrayed as better than Kit one-on-one and nearly finishes Obi-Wan on land. Obi-Wan wins in the water.**
   > "He was like fire, Ventress like smoke ... suddenly—Kit was down! ... for the first time Ventress had pierced his guard." (CD ch. 77)
   - She knocks Obi-Wan onto his back ("the confrontation might be over within seconds"). Underwater he uses "Trust the Force" to cut her "low on the ribs on her right side", and in the breath-holding standoff "something broke inside Ventress" (CD ch. 77).
   - Her claims (POV, not shown): "Faced Master Windu and come within a hairbreadth of defeating him" (CD ch. 25).
   - Her Quy'Tek meditations hide her from Obi-Wan: "He almost sensed me" (CD ch. 25). "You can shield your Force." / "From fools, yes." (CD ch. 74)
4. **Force-perception analysis is used as a detective skill on both sides.**
   > "I am not searching for a thing. I need to sense something that is not there. Where computers falter, the Force may prevail." (Obi-Wan, CD ch. 36–39)
   > "I can feel their ripples in the Force." / "If you see ripples in water, do you not know where the stone was dropped?" (Ventress, CD ch. 51)
5. **A clone with Jedi Flow training out-reads a stronger clone: a non-Force "taste" of Jedi perception.**
   > "Forry might as well have sat down and detailed his every intended motion in advance." / "Was that what it felt like to be a Jedi? Was that even a fraction of how it felt?" (CD ch. 75)
   - Jangotat (A-98) is the first clone to break Code ("It has never happened", CD ch. 62). He calls the *Nexu* strike onto himself and the Five Families. Kit: "Jangotat saved millions" (CD ch. 78).
6. **Lore: Yoda saved the X'Ting about 150 years earlier and is their greatest hero.**
   > "loomed the hollow, chewed-stone statue of a smiling Master Yoda." (Hive ch. 7; at least 70 m tall)
   - A lightsaber burns through a vault door "sandwiched with energy-absorbing circuits that slowed, but never stopped him" (Hive ch. 6).

Other data: there are 1.2 million clones at +12 months (CD ch. 2). Clones hold their breath "almost four minutes" (CD ch. 4). The Labyrinth of Evil excerpt in the back matter says "only half the original army of 1.2 million in fighting shape".
