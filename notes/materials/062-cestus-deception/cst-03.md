# The Cestus Deception (Steven Barnes, Del Rey 2004): notes part 3, Chapters 17–40

- Read 2026-11-03 CST from the owner's MEGA epub (handle lD90BRyA). Citation "CD ch. N".

## Ch. 17: recruiting and Sheeka
- Kit's speech to the miners and farmers: "Your ancestors came here in chains." Nate and Forry take their helmets off for effect. Hints that "The Guides" claim JKs cannot be mass-produced, because of the dashta eels ("more than a few hundred").
- At most "fifty thousand X’Ting remained on the entire planet" (once millions).
- **Sheeka Tull knew Jango Fett** "twenty years ago". On the clones: "It’s him, all right". Nate's catechism: "It took a dozen Jedi to kill him."
- Sheeka on Jango: "he wasn’t perfect, but he was a serious chunk." / "He always seemed invulnerable".
- Kamino songs are mnemonics for "explosives, ordnance manuals ... anatomical vulnerabilities for a hundred major species".

## Ch. 18–20: militia
- Nate addresses about 1,500 people. "The Grand Army of the Republic was 100 percent clone, but local militias were often recruited to supplement it." Recruits include Skot OnSon and **Resta Shug Hai**, a low-caste X'Ting widow who is "as strong as a Noghri".
- The first day's course is built to make about half quit. 40% quit; Nate reports "forty-eight good recruits".
- **Kit practising with the lightwhip** in the cave: "a meter-long thread of light blazed briefly, snaked and coiled through the air". He turns "with a loose-limbed adroitness so smooth that he might almost have turned inside out".
- Sheeka challenges Nate to "react to the world as just a human being". Nate has an old hip wound from a broca on Altair-9.
- Sheeka on Nate: "The shadow of the best— ... —best fighter I ever knew."

## Ch. 19: first audience with Regent G'Mai Duris
- Snoil: "Everything is owned by legal fictions". Duris "has public power, but takes orders in private". She descends from the hive queen, "but only tangentially". Snoil cites the Coruscant Accords.

## Ch. 21: Desert Wind and the Guides
- Thak Val Zsing is a former history professor who has led Desert Wind for 30 years. Kit offers clemency.
- **The basket test:** Brother Fate and an old female X'Ting have Kit put his arm into a basket holding a roughly 10 kg serpentine creature. "He tells the truth." Thak yields leadership. Kit: "It did not try to harm me. But I felt … something. A presence I have sensed before." (This is a dashta eel, the Guides' Force-sensitive oracle.)
- Thak: the plagues "killed about ninety percent" of the X'Ting; "the Five Families weren’t heartbroken."

## Ch. 23: Ventress arrives (key feat chapter)
- **Mount Tolmeatek** rises "thirty-two thousand meters from the valley floor" ("untravelable" in X'Ting).
- Ventress: six dagger tattoos on each side of her head. A belt device fries everything in a ~20 m circle around her ship: "If your people approach my ship ... you’ll need new people."
- Gift to Trillot from Dooku: Ploo II windsnakes with the venom sacs removed ("it is said to be a good death").
- **Ventress vs Remlout** (an X'Ting assassin-clan bodyguard trained in Tal-Gun on Xagobah). She reads his training from his pale neck. "Count Dooku told me that if I wished to progress in the arts, it was vital to take every challenge."
  - She dodges a three-kick combination: "Ventress had moved bonelessly ... shifted only a centimeter or less".
  - Then an unseen strike: "with a series of rending pops Remlout’s supercontracted muscles splintered his own shell." "He is not dead."
- "Obi-Wan. ... I know this one. He needs to die." / "I don’t believe in coincidence. Obi-Wan and I are here on the same business."

## Ch. 24
- Duris cites the Gadon III precedent (breakaway Kif miners: intent at sale, not end use, decides). Republic payment failures and the Gabonna ban led to "food and water riots ... Thousands died."
- Obi-Wan loses his temper: "we will not allow your machines to slaughter our troopers. Whether or not this entails the destruction of your civilization depends upon you."

## Ch. 25: the hive ball (Ventress backstory and ranking claims)
- Five Families heads: Debbikin, Lady Por'Ten, Kefka (part Kiffar), Llitishi (Wroonian), and Duris's cousin Caiza Quill (X'Ting).
- Obi-Wan dances the reel. Yoda taught dance: "A warrior who cannot dance? Clumsy in both war and peace he is."
- Obi-Wan senses danger ("like a flux-wire brushing across his spine") but cannot find its source.
> "“He almost sensed me.” For months Count Dooku had taught her the Quy’Tek meditations." (CD ch. 25)
- Backstory: found by Ky Narec on Rattatak; he was murdered and the Council "had abandoned" him. She attacked Dooku, "been defeated and disarmed", and he completed her training.
> "She had clashed with Jedi. Killed many. Faced Master Windu and come within a hairbreadth of defeating him. Faced Skywalker in battles they would both remember. Obi-Wan had escaped her hand twice, but would not again." (CD ch. 25, Ventress POV; her claim, not narration of a fight)

## Ch. 26
- Caiza Quill killed Duris's mate Filian in a formal duel ("going to the sand") five years earlier, and now threatens her.

## Ch. 27
- Forry's trot is a pace "a common man would find exhausting in ten minutes, and a trooper could continue all day long."

## Ch. 28: Trillot and the radiation codicil
- Palpatine: "you may need to take matters to the next level."
- Obi-Wan slips surveillance by going out a window and riding a service chute, then takes Gritt Chippie's taxi to the Night Shade. He greets Trillot in X'Ting. Trillot: "You think like a criminal." / "One of my many failings."
- **Weak point:** beneath the industrial city of **Clandes** is a landline juncture box with direct access to the main terminal. The area is lethally unshielded, "It would kill you in a few minutes … unless you had a class six Baktoid radiation suit" (price "half a million credits").
- Ventress was listening. She tells Trillot to give Obi-Wan the true information.

## Ch. 29: the Cleft Head (stealth and sensing)
- X'Ting stinger duel: "quarter-meter-long stingers". Males are faster once they shed their egg sacs.
- Duris: the Regency is "a sham". "The spider folk were once strong, but I doubt there is a single intact clan left on the surface." A predecessor's 150-year-old datapad records **Yoda** "marooned here while escorting a prisoner, and did great service to the hive" (tie-in to the bundled *The Hive*).
- Duris: "I sense the hand of a master games player ... It is almost as if a stalemate is actually desired."
> "using a portion of her Force energy to shield herself from Obi-Wan’s keen senses. He was one of the most powerful Jedi she had ever met. She believed herself stronger, but was not so certain as she had once been." (CD ch. 29, Ventress POV)
- She plays "with the edge of his perceptions". Obi-Wan feels "A not-presence ... Something withdrawn" and files it for later meditation.

## Ch. 30–31
- Obi-Wan offers to lift the Gabonna blockade and advance funds for 2,000 JK and JL droids. Quill orders Duris to kill herself or "go to the sand".
- **Snoil's key finding:** the land was bought with **synthstones**, which are counterfeit currency. Cestus Cybernetics was a licensed prison subsidiary under Republic contract, so it was held to ambassadorial standards: "A purchase made with counterfeit currency is no purchase at all ... The land beneath every factory on Cestus still belongs to the hive!" (CD ch. 31)

## Ch. 32: Duris vs Quill "on the sand"
- Obi-Wan's advice: fight in the calm of the ritual death meal and "Go as one already dead."
> "There is a secret to the warrior arts ... It is the willingness to trade lives with your enemy. To never fight for anything you would not die for. ... The arena is not a circle of sand. The arena is your heart." (Obi-Wan, CD ch. 32)
- Quill (assassin-clan lineage, "one of the hive’s most lethal fighters") concedes without a blow and loses his council seat.
- Obi-Wan keeps the land claim as "final, emergency leverage" rather than sending it to Palpatine, who would use it to shut Cestus Cybernetics down. Kit on the deception: "If a few … theatrics can save lives, I believe we must go forward."

## Ch. 33–34
- Obi-Wan smells the scent of Ventress (bantha musk) in Trillot's den. He is "off-balance since first arriving on Cestus".
- Trillot plants tracers on Quill and Lady Por'Ten for Ventress.
- Ventress's dreams "infect" Trillot with images of "death and destruction on a horrific scale" (empathic dark-side spillover).
- Ventress first met Obi-Wan on **Ohma-D'un**, a moon of Naboo: "She had sorely underestimated the Jedi’s courage and intelligence."

## Ch. 36–39: the magcar kidnapping (a staged operation)
- Kit, disguised as "**Nemonus** ... Greetings from Count Dooku", drops through the roof of the Five Families' magcar. Clones play Desert Wind.
- When Quill (assassin-clan) draws a hold-out blaster behind him, "without turning, the intruder’s hand flickered". The lightwhip ("Three meters long ... thin as a thread") slices the blaster in half.
- **Obi-Wan's Force analysis:** the computers cannot find the car. "I am not searching for a thing. I need to sense something that is not there. Where computers falter, the Force may prevail." He traces the ripple of compensating cars and points to an unmapped utility corridor.
- **Magcar run (physical feat):** Obi-Wan rides and hops between high-speed magcars and ore cars, clinging to the tunnel ceiling.
> "For an instant, his body propelled by superbly conditioned muscles and a nervous system in tune with the deepest currents of the Force, Obi-Wan’s velocity came within five meters per second of the magcar’s." (CD ch. 38; he then catches the car)
  - Caveat: he had secretly studied the shuttle patterns all night beforehand.
- **Staged duel, Obi-Wan vs "Nemonus" (Kit with the lightwhip):** "The two adversaries were almost perfectly matched ... somersaulting so that they were virtually walking on the ceiling". Obi-Wan is "the first to penetrate the other’s guard".
  - This is choreographed theatre; Kit had said "Be ready for the performance of a lifetime." Use it for display speed only, not as a real ranking.
- Nate watched from the roof: "in comparison, Kamino’s most advanced martial sciences were mere back-alley thuggery."

## Ch. 40
- Sheeka's Jango backstory: she was 18 and he was 25. "He’d only been free maybe two years, since the Mandalorians were wiped out." They met in the Meridian sector while "He’d lost his armor somehow, and was searching for it". They had about a year together before pirates split them.
- Her leverage: she ran medicine to her sister's embargoed sect on Devon Four.
- Clones' dance: "The Jakelian knife-dance is a primary tool for teaching distance, timing, and rhythm".
