# The Cestus Deception (Steven Barnes, Del Rey 2004): notes part 4, Chapters 41–61

- Read 2026-11-03 CST from the owner's MEGA epub (handle lD90BRyA). Citation "CD ch. N".

## Ch. 41–42: Jangotat; Jedi Flow
- Nate on the GAR: "In the aggregate, we have lived a million lives. All of that experience grows within us. We are the GAR, and it is alive." / "Men like me don’t start the wars. We just die in them."
- Sheeka names him **Jangotat**, "Mandalorian for ‘Jango’s brother.’"
- Kit is uneasy with the deception. Asked whether an ordinary man could learn what the Jedi do: "No." / "Some." Kit on Jango: "A man of great strengths. A worthy foe."
- **Jedi Flow**, taught to the four clones as "a game taught to the very youngest Padawan learners":
  - The Force is an ocean for Jedi and a "stream or river" for ordinary people.
  - Bodily trauma is "armor" that "both protects and numbs".
  - The training triad is "breathing, motion, and alignment". "the greatest value is in transitioning between one exercise and the next."

## Ch. 43: the staged duel is exposed
- Trillot sees Ventress's dreams of "Warships rose. / Towers fell."
- "When she chose, Ventress appeared able to shield herself from the most powerful Jedi."
- The Families are signing the Republic terms when Quill plays an unseen observer's holo of the staged fight ("a lightsaber passing a quarter meter broad of the target"). Duris expels Obi-Wan: "What you cannot win by diplomacy you seek to gain by fear. And if not that, fraud."

## Ch. 44–45: escape pods
- Obi-Wan jumps ship in an escape pod. Its repulsor trigger fails, so he burns two holes in the pod with his lightsaber so that "the lightsaber’s energy field might power that circuit more directly". On the second try it works.
- A black hunter-killer drone fires probe droids and destroys the ship, killing the clone pilot **Xutoo** ("killed one of your brothers"). Snoil survives in a pod.

## Ch. 46–51: sabotage war; Ventress's Force-tracking
- Obi-Wan: "This is no longer a charade." Desert Wind and the clones hit refineries and plants with minimal casualties.
- **Kibo geothermal station** (Kibo Lake is a 50-km crater): an accidental trap. Jangotat dives into the volcanic water to draw fire, and Obi-Wan blows a coolant coil to scald the guards.
  - Obi-Wan's quip: Jedi are "Better" with blasters; "We only use lightsabers to make fights more … equitable."
  - Kit sweeps in on a speeder, "deflecting laser blasts and severing blasters at the barrel". There are fatalities on both sides.
- Five Families bunker: inner walls "thick enough to resist even glazion energy torches for a standard day".
- **Ventress's Force analysis** mirrors Obi-Wan's magcar trick. She walks through a holomap of every sabotage report:
> "You of the Family are dead to the Force. But Obi-Wan. Yes … he is alive with it. He and … yes … One other. ... The Nautolan. ... I can feel their ripples in the Force." / "If you see ripples in water, do you not know where the stone was dropped?" (CD ch. 51)
  - Trillot: "Obi-Wan Kenobi had faked a fantastic demonstration ... Could Ventress actually do such an incredible thing? ... did that not imply that she was superior to the Jedi …?" Obi-Wan's version was a real Force read helped by prior study; the "faked" refers to the kidnapping.
- Ventress orders a field test of the JKs (still non-lethal: "They are not lethal until their Gabonna crystals are replaced") together with Dooku's new **infiltration droids**.

## Ch. 52–56: the cave massacre (JK combat data)
- **Plastoid infiltrator droids:** jelly sacs of magnetic parts that ooze through micro-tunnels, melting rock with surface laser nodes. They travel "a hundred kilometers at an average rate of just under ten kilometers per hour", then self-assemble into treaded droids "as tall as three humans".
- Droid production had already dropped "by more than 30 percent" from the sabotage.
- Thak Val Zsing, drunk, sees them emerge and hides instead of raising the alarm.
- Kit plays a Nautolan wind-string instrument. He rates Obi-Wan "one of the ablest warriors of Kit Fisto’s experience".
- **Kit's evasion:** "Form I-style improvisation applied to pure evasion ... Sizzling bolts missed him by bare centimeters". "The Nautolan had begun to dodge even before the beam arced in his direction". He cuts the infiltrator's treads. These are "personal security droids: apparently the tactical chips hadn’t been swapped."
- **JK performance:**
  > "They could not be stopped, reasoned with, blasted, or evaded." / "They seemed to aim where a person would be in a moment, rather than where he or she was now. The infiltration droids had weaknesses, but the JKs seemed to have none at all." (CD ch. 54)
  - Blaster-rifle bolts "sang off the outer casing, doing no damage".
- Snoil saves Obi-Wan with a portable cannon and is killed by a falling stalactite: "A great barrister, from a great line."
- Jangotat shields Sheeka and takes a graze that burns his back.
- **JK vs the cave spider clan:**
> "It’s running out of power! ... It had probably defeated the equivilent of a hundred warriors, but was running out of power!" (CD ch. 56)
  - The spiders slow it with silk. Obi-Wan has his people bring stalactites down on it: "Even then, the JK trembled against the rock."
  - The spider matron bows and Obi-Wan salutes: "No favors. Just respect."
- This is the book's major JK weakness: a finite power supply under sustained combat.

## Ch. 57–58
- Sheeka loses four security pursuit ships in the mines by running a deliberately over-rich, smoky fuel mix. One pursuer crashes.
- Losses: "Sixteen dead or captured" and the transport destroyed. Nate/Jangotat is "missing and presumed dead".
- Obi-Wan recalls Qui-Gon's exercises behind his magcar sensing ("What do you not see, as well as see?"): "There is another player."

## Ch. 59–61: Zantay Hills healers
- Sheeka's home community in the Zantay Hills, led by **Brother (Nicos) Fate**.
- Jangotat's severe blaster burns heal with fungal dressings and a mysterious "squishy, flat weight" on his chest, "about half a meter long" (a dashta eel). He leaves with only "a pale shininess". "better than synthflesh".
- Brother Fate: "Five hundred years of history doesn’t change a million years of evolution." Mithail: "The Guides tell us that—" (hushed).
- Clone humour: "Not every clone trooper joke ... was somehow related to the arts of death. / Just 95 percent of them."
