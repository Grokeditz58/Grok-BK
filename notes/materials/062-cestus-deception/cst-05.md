# The Cestus Deception (Steven Barnes, Del Rey 2004): notes part 5, Chapters 62–73

- Read 2026-11-03 CST from the owner's MEGA epub (handle lD90BRyA). Citation "CD ch. N".

## Ch. 62–63: Clandes; Obi-Wan vs a JK (key feat)
- 48 survivors are organised into cells. Obi-Wan wonders aloud whether Jangotat "broke Code". Seefor: "It has never happened."
- Clandes Industrial is a 1,000 km² complex with 24 underground levels and "60 percent of Cestus’s economy". The target is the aquifer water supply.
- Obi-Wan nearly uses a mind trick on a venal guard, but Resta bribes him first.
- Obi-Wan climbs the purification plant wall "using his sensitivity to balance on footholds where a reptile might have fallen to its death", senses the alarm conduits by feel, and burns through the lock.
- **Obi-Wan vs one JK (a security unit, not upgraded):**
  1. "Speed. He needed speed." He hurls his lit lightsaber at the control panel to split the JK's priorities. Its tentacles grab at the saber; "the beam severing two of its arms".
  2. With the saber tangled in the wiring, he uses Kit's lightwhip, "ensnaring an entire quadrant of arms in a single instant". The JK shocks him ("The hair on Obi-Wan’s head flared away ... threatened to shut down his nervous system and paralyze his diaphragm") and attempts a retinal scan.
  3. The damaged JK shows "a noticeable time lag in response". He closes in, and eyes shut and Force-guided, he cracks the weakened casing with his hand and then elbow strikes "again and again".
> "He looked down at the droid with newfound respect. It had required two energy weapons and bruising hand-to-tentacle combat to stop the thing." (CD ch. 63)
  - The lightwhip's power cell is exhausted, so he discards it.
- Scaling read: a single JK needs Obi-Wan's full tricks. JK weaknesses: split-priority confusion, and lag after damage. (Compare Kit's ch. 1 demo win and the clone's 20-second loss.)

## Ch. 64: Kit's aquifer raid
- Kit passes the 20-second sensor sweeps by Force sensitivity. He rides the hourly dam surge through a channel ("as if he had never left Glee Anselm"), cuts through a grate, and fights out between "icy flow and the boiling heat ray".
- He hand-walks under a catwalk and drops "fifteen meters down to the deck, landing silently".
- **Kit's Form I berserk edge:**
> "His lightsaber seemed to move of its own accord, before the blasts were launched ... He flirted with battle fever, the howling demon in his mind trapped behind the bars of discipline, but guiding him as he slid down Form I’s razor edge." (CD ch. 64)
  - Eight guards are down, some dead. He takes out a pry-bar technician with a nerve-plexus strike: "Sleep ... All life is a dream."
- Clandes water flow is cut by three-quarters. The death toll has passed thirty.

## Ch. 65–66: Ventress before Duris; Queyta
- Duris on Ventress: she moves with "that same unnatural lightness Duris had noted in Kenobi". She is "more lethal than Master Kenobi ... No shame, no fear. No mercy".
- Ventress names "Count Dooku and General Grievous" as allies. She threatens Duris over the synthstone claim.
> "Count Dooku foretold it, and I have seen it. Whatever else happens, Obi-Wan Kenobi and I will meet again. On Queyta I promised Kenobi I would kill him. My Master wants him alive." (CD ch. 66)

## Ch. 67: the dashta eel grotto (Force lore)
- Dashta eels are named for the explorer Kilaphor Dashta. They are "holy to the X’Ting", and the adults are much larger than the young used in JKs.
> "Not nonsentient. Call it a form of sleep. A gift from the Guides—a lifetime of dreams. Even unconscious, their nervous systems supply the Force sensitivity." (Sheeka, CD ch. 67)
- The eels give only their **unfertilized** young, who "never mature", and "for defensive purposes only" (ch. 68). Weaponising them is "an abomination".
- Jangotat has a Force-vision: "I see myself, back to infancy, out to old age", including children he will never have. He weeps for the first time. "Usually the Guides are a healing influence." Jangotat: "I choose everything that led me to this place, and to you." Sheeka: "I love you."
- The "Guides" are the eels. The Zantay Hills "brothers and sisters" meditate with them (Brother Fate's healing order).

## Ch. 69–73
- Thak Val Zsing is shunned for his cowardice.
- Palpatine sends a supercruiser (Baraka's *Nexu*): "you had better resolve the situation before those ships arrive". Obi-Wan briefly suspects the whole affair is a feint to draw the Confederacy out, then dismisses it ("he did not, could not believe this").
- Yoda is "unavailable, in the field supervising an operation".
- **Obi-Wan spars with the clones:** he recognises Jedi Flow in them. Jangotat has "Emotional empathy. Insight." Obi-Wan puts him down in "a Juzzian arm-lock, nerve-pincered at wrist and elbow". Jangotat: "We are greatly inferior to you."
- Obi-Wan: "we sense the Force with our whole bodies" / "The joy in life comes not from surpassing another’s gifts, but in fully manifesting our own."
- Obi-Wan's endgame: buy the class-six Baktoid radiation suit from Trillot (through Fizzik) and shut down Clandes production through the irradiated control station "without causing extreme damage".
- Baraka is diverted to Cestus from the hunt; the big battle is expected in "the Borleias drift".
