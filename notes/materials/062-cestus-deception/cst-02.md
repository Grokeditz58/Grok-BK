# The Cestus Deception (Steven Barnes, Del Rey 2004): notes part 2, Chapters 4–16

## Ch. 4: the Vandor-3 selenome drop (clone feats)
- ARC A-98 "Nate". Clones "Each stood exactly 1.78 meters". "the ARC troopers were the deadliest soldiers ever created."
- Drop capsules: re-entry heat of "two thousand degrees" is absorbed by a "thermoenergetic force screen". The transport *Nexu* carries "twenty thousand troops". The capsules split into undersea sleds.
- Formation doctrine: each trooper protects his six neighbours, and the geometry emerges ("complex behaviors arising from simple instructions").
- The **selenome**: a reef-colony predator of "Billions" of organisms spanning kilometers, with tentacles made of millions of smaller organisms.
- **Clone physicals:** a tentacle squeeze that would "grind his spine to paste" is resisted by holding his breath ("transformed his midsection into durasteel"). "Like any trooper, Nate could hold his breath for almost four minutes."
- Jango maxim: "Put your fear behind you where it belongs. Then blast everything in front of you into splinters."
- Adm. Baraka: "one point seven percent mortality during these drills" / "Excellent." He thinks "Clones are cheaper to grow than to train."

## Ch. 5
- The maximum allowed drill mortality is 2%. Vandor-3 houses a 50,000-trooper temporary barracks city.
- Clone legs can carry "twice his own weight up a thirty-degree incline at a steady march". Standard troopers take about 9 years from infant to fully trained. Rest-mode sleep and memory consolidation are trained.

## Ch. 6: Cestus history (lore)
- The X'Ting are a hive-insect people. Native "large, deadly, and reputedly nonsentient cave spiders". Population "in the millions".
- 300 years ago the X'Ting leased land to Coruscant for a galactic prison. A dozen Cybot Galactica officers jailed after the Etti IV scandal set up droid fabrication with prison labor, which became **Cestus Cybernetics**. Land was bought from the hive cheaply, then "terrible plagues among the X’Ting" gave the company near-total control.
- **Desert Wind:** guerrillas founded 20 years ago from the paroled prisoners' descendants, against the **Five Families**. "crushed in the past year".
- Plan: Obi-Wan and Snoil negotiate. Kit, an ARC and commandos revive Desert Wind as leverage. No trooper body may be captured, because its "genetic signature would be evidence".

## Ch. 7–8
- Nate: one of a thousand-clone cohort, of whom a dozen were ARCs. "Half had been chosen for personal training by Jango Fett himself". On Geonosis he saved five of his cohort by spotting a destroyer droid's "reflux pattern".
- Forry on the JK footage: "Fast ... As a Jedi?" / "Faster ... But speed isn’t everything." / "the machine was faster, but it didn’t make any difference." / "They beat Jango." Sirty (CT-36/732) was the trooper the JK beat in the demo, "about twenty seconds". He is recruited because he is the only trooper who has fought one.
- Kaminoan mentor Glorii Profus: "From water you are born. In fire you die. Your bodies seed the stars."

## Ch. 9: Council
- Yoda: "Once, Jedi had only to appear to quiet a crowd. Now common brawlers we become." Mace cites "the matter of Antar Four, and even the Battle of Jabiim" (an odd placement against the +12/+15 timeline). Mace: "the maintenance of social order requires both myth and reality ... And the myth has been fractured: only the reality remains."
- "Of all the Jedi, it was Master Windu who held status closest to that of Yoda." His meditations "lifted him to a realm few could dream of".

## Ch. 10
- Jedi rest: "every minute spent in this extreme state was worth three minutes of ordinary slumber."
- Jango "had come within a hair of killing him on three separate occasions". Obi-Wan admits a "slight aversion" to clones. He jokes privately about an army cloned from his own tissue.
- Barrister Doolb Snoil (Vippit, Nal Hutta) owes Obi-Wan a life-debt from Rijel-12 and cannot marry until he repays it.

## Ch. 11: Kit vs Obi-Wan sparring (key ranking)
> "Obi-Wan swiftly discovered that Kit was the better swordfighter, astonishingly aggressive and intuitive in comparison with Obi-Wan’s more measured style. But the Nautolan gave himself deliberate disadvantages, hampered himself in terms of balance, limited his speed, emphasized his nondominant side" (CD ch. 11)
- **Forms:** "Kit was a practitioner of Form I ... the most ancient style". "Anakin, used Form V, which concentrated on strength." "Count Dooku had used Form II, an elegant, precise style". "Obi-Wan himself specialized in Form III ... maximized defensive protection."
- Jedi combat theory: the interaction with an opponent "created a web of energy. A Jedi climbed it, surfed it, drew power from it."
- Kit on the JK: "I should have gone closer to the edge ... Released myself into the Force, become more unpredictable." Obi-Wan: Form I "required too much emotional heat for Obi-Wan’s taste ... dangerous ... to your spirit."
- **Lightwhip:** Obi-Wan builds one from an archive diagram (he once saw a bounty hunter use one in the Koornacht Cluster). If covert action is needed, "all suspicion must fall on Count Dooku."

## Ch. 12
- Legal status: the prison "exists as a legal fiction only, a definition expanded to include the entire planet. Cestus markets goods under a corrections license."

## Ch. 13
- The native population is down 90% in 300 years; immigrants number several million. There are about 800 mushroom varieties.
- "the Jedi was as strong as any two of them" (Kit vs clones). Kit "handled the lightwhip as if he had been spawned with it" within hours.
- Aratech 74-Z speeder bikes reach "550 kilometers per hour". Kit has twin hearts. Nomadic X'Ting number "but a few tens of thousands".

## Ch. 14
- ChikatLik ("the center"): a capital in lava tubes and empty plague-hives. Kiffar honor guard. The Grand ChikatLik hotel was the first prison building. Fizzik is a hotel attendant.
- Obi-Wan has begun to "bristle" at the anthem "All Stars Burn as One".

## Ch. 15: cave spiders
- Kit and four clones fight a nest: six large spiders plus "dozens of the smaller ones". Kit's saber swats venom gobs into steam. The "Geonosis Square" formation holds. Nate draws fire on a speeder bike.
- A giant red female "half the size of a bantha" commands. At the end "she lowered her head as if making obeisance" to Kit. (This sets up the spider clans' sentience.)
- Contact **Sheeka Tull** (pilot of the *Spindragon*). Pass phrase: "Alderaan has three moons" / "Demos Four but two". Kit tells her the aim is "sabotage and deception, in case the government needs to be overthrown."

## Ch. 16: Trillot
- X'Ting cycle genders every three years. **Viptiel** (from Nal Hutta) eases the change. Fizzik sells info on the visitors to his brood-brother **Trillot**, a crime and labour fixer who uses a viptiel cocktail to change in "a single painful night".
- "the last century’s plagues had been no more or less than attempted genocide". Native Cestians are now "less than 3 percent". The X'Ting once drove "the spider clans" into the mountains.
- Trillot is said to speak "more than a hundred languages". He has a Zeetsa dealer's nose and tongue removed for cutting mushroom with Xyathone.
