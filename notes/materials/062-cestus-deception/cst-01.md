## Ch. 1
- Obi-Wan and Anakin return from Forscan VI (Separatist infiltration camp at the edge of the Cron drift). They hold off sleep and food for 36 hours with Jedi skills. Anakin "was already famed as one of the galaxy’s most powerful warriors."
- Obi-Wan: "Anakin’s seeming impetuosity was in fact a deep and profound understanding of superior skills."
- Summoned by Palpatine to the T'Chuk arena (seats half a million).

## Ch. 2: the JK-13 demo (key tech/feat chapter)
- **JK-13 vs a class-four destroyer droid (rebuilt from Geonosis):** the JK projects "a series of rotating energy disks that absorbed the blasts with ease" (absorb, not deflect). Its tendrils thin to "almost invisible fineness" and cut it into sections: "stopped in less than … forty-two seconds."
- **JK vs an ARC trooper:** "nonlethal apprehension" mode. Obi-Wan Force-reads the trooper's next dodge, but the JK nets him mid-leap: "The timing was superb. No. More than superb: it had been perfect. ... almost precognitive". It stuns him with an orange shock.
- Street name: "Among smugglers and the lower classes ... some call them ‘Jedi Killers.’"
- **Kit Fisto vs JK-13:**
  - Kit "favored the Form I style of combat, a fierce—".
  - The JK uses the slow tendrils as a feint while camouflaged tendrils burrow under the sand: "This thing attacked on two levels simultaneously, a strategy beyond most living warriors ... performing at multiple levels of tempo and efficiency".
  - "The sand tendrils were within centimeters of their target before Kit sensed them."
  - Kit rides the yank instead of fighting it, "the Nautolan’s sense of timing faster and more precise than conscious thought". At close range "Kit was the predator"; the disks "no longer absorbed the strikes: now they merely deflected them".
  > "Kit accelerated into a blur of motion complex and rapid enough to baffle even Obi-Wan’s experienced gaze." (CD ch. 2)
  - The JK "had anticipated the Nautolan’s responses". Kit afterwards: "it was, as I’ve heard humans say, a ‘close shave.’" (CD ch. 3)
  - Obi-Wan self-assessment: "He allowed himself no doubt that he would have emerged victorious, but ... Kit’s chaotic, unpredictable approach had given the Nautolan an advantage against the machine. Obi-Wan’s own more measured response might well have proven less effective."
  - 1.2 million clone troopers at this point. Obi-Wan: "I’ve met every one of them."

## Ch. 3: Palpatine's briefing
- JK: "a Force-sensitive bio-droid of a type previously considered impossible", 80,000 credits, the most exclusive personal security droid. Its processor is "a life-support unit for a creature of unknown origin". This is the **dashta eel** (fist-sized, eyeless), "the first nonsentient creature ever found with a profound level of … well, of Force sensitivity" (possibly from Cestus's Dashta Mountains).
- "We have tested it with a variety of opponents, and you, Master Fisto, are the first to prevail." Kit: "Life will always have greater Force-harmony than any machine."
- Economics: Cestus subcontracted for Baktoid. The Trade Federation pullout after Naboo caused chaos, then late Republic payments. Then the Republic restricted **Gabonna memory crystals**, "the only memory crystals fast enough to power a class five personal security droid" (most battle droids are class four).
- Dooku offers to buy "thousands ... Perhaps tens of thousands", supply Gabonnas, and supply cloning tech for eels "superior to Kaminoan technology". JKs become lethal "merely by substituting memory crystals".
- Obi-Wan: such devices "might even nullify the slight precognitive advantage enjoyed by Jedi in combat." He thinks Palpatine is "still a novice in the ways of the Force."
- Palpatine: 95% of Cestus's people are lower class, "descended from uncivilized aboriginals and criminals". The planet is barren and must import soil nutrients, food and medicine.
- Mission: contact Regent G'Mai Duris, negotiate, and convince Cestus that Dooku is too dangerous. Resources: "The best of the best."
