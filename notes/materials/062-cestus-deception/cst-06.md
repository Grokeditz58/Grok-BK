# The Cestus Deception (Steven Barnes, Del Rey 2004): notes part 6, Chapters 74–82 and Afterword

- Read 2026-11-03 CST from the owner's MEGA epub (handle lD90BRyA). Citation "CD ch. N".

## Ch. 74: Ventress's trap; the real plot
- Trillot betrays the Jedi in the smugglers' hive cavern. Obi-Wan is caught in a **Xythan force shield** ("absorbs and returns all energy. Feel free to use your lightsaber.").
- Obi-Wan: "You have perfected the Quy’Tek meditations, Adept ... You can shield your Force." / Ventress: "From fools, yes."
- **The JK order was a sham:**
> "volume production will require cloning, and our efforts to clone the dashta tissue will require another year, at least. For the time being, that is a dead end. A bluff." / "Intended to frighten Palpatine and your precious Jedi Council into an overreaction." (CD ch. 74)
- Ventress recalibrated Obi-Wan's Clandes targeting beacons so that the cruiser would "strike a cave fault, destroying the entire industrial complex and its millions".
- Ventress wants single combat and her "twin sabers trembled", but "Count Dooku wants you alive".

## Ch. 75–76: Jangotat breaks Code
- Resta and Thak Val Zsing spot the Five Families' hidden "resort" bunker under Kibo powering up. Seefor: "under the Kamino Accords I am compelled to warn you that your suggestion is not to Code."
- **Jangotat vs Forry** (clone vs clone; Forry "stronger and faster"):
> "Jangotat saw more clearly now ... as if the entire moment were frozen in invisible ice. He saw Forry’s patterned responses, the programmed blows and chops ... Forry might as well have sat down and detailed his every intended motion in advance." / "Was that what it felt like to be a Jedi? Was that even a fraction of how it felt?" (CD ch. 75)
  - Theme: "It’s not what a man fights with. It’s what he fights for."
- Jangotat knocks out Resta with a nerve-cluster hold ("stronger than most human males") so that she will live and sing her hive's song.
- **Jangotat vs a JK:** its camouflaged floor tentacle changes colour. Blaster bolts "glanced harmlessly off the JK’s golden casing"; it is "Too fast!", and a maximum-power rifle overheats without a hit. A collapsed ceiling buries it but it digs out. It retinal-scans him and paralyses him with a shock.
  - Thak Val Zsing redeems himself with a mine strapped to his chest. Afterwards the JK is "undented. The thing was indestructible", but **the dashta inside goes insane** at a killing in its arms.
  - Jangotat wedges the bunker shut, uses the Families' own gear to call the *Nexu* strike onto himself ("Trooper’s calling the load in right on top of himself"), and dies humming the chitlik song: "From water we’re born, in fire we die ... We seed the stars."

## Ch. 77: Obi-Wan and Kit vs Ventress (key duel)
- The orbital strike briefly weakens the shield. Kit lunges through, "his lightsaber absorbing enough of the energy to keep the shield from frying him".
- Ventress sends young X'Ting at them. The Jedi fight unarmed to avoid a massacre while she studies them: "The Jedi’s unarmed tactics would reveal their lightsaber technique".
  - Obi-Wan: "The Force blossomed within him, and time perception distended, slowing reality to a crawl."
  - Kit: "a living, martial hurricane, his body moving in two and three directions at once, joints flexing, unlimited by human vertebral restraints".
- **Kit vs Ventress:**
> "He was like fire, Ventress like smoke ... Single against double lightblades." / "suddenly—Kit was down! Wounded and groggy from a kick in the jaw, for the first time Ventress had pierced his guard. Her left-hand saber sliced his arm" (CD ch. 77)
  - Kit "had barely evaded death from a lightsaber wound to the head".
- **Obi-Wan vs Ventress:**
  - He pins her blades with a somersault leap. She counters with a decapitation slash and a spinning kick that floors him, "fighting from his back ... the confrontation might be over within seconds".
  - Kit, half-conscious in the water, cuts the dock pilings and both fall into the lake.
  - Underwater, Obi-Wan has his rebreather and she does not. He presses her so that she can't sheathe a saber. She drops one to get her rebreather.
  - He baits her with a feigned upper-left weakness, uses "Trust the Force" to read the water pressure, and **cuts her low on the right ribs**.
  - They rip out each other's rebreathers. In the breath-holding standoff Obi-Wan is ready to die ("Like Duris. I’m ready to die here and now"). "Then something broke inside Ventress", and she flees under an ink cloud.
- Yoda's quoted line: "The dark side has clouded the Galaxy. Difficult to see, the future is."
- Scaling read: Ventress (dual sabers) beats Kit cleanly one-on-one and has Obi-Wan on his back on land. Obi-Wan wins in the water with Kit's help and nerve.

## Ch. 78–82
- Kit: "She may have underestimated me."
- Jangotat's last message: "the dashta inside the JK went insane ... Killing thinking people was just beyond them. Even a sleeping Guide was driven crazy." / "They’d never have functioned on a battlefield."
  - The Five Families probably knew this and lied to Dooku.
  - Kit: "Jangotat saved millions."
- Terms: the Five Families are wiped out (Quill, the Por'Tens, Debbikin, half the Llitishi), the hive council takes power, there are no more JKs, and Cestus gets Republic service-droid contracts. Obi-Wan asks for an amnesty and "Let the spiders have their caves."
- Obi-Wan senses Sheeka's pregnancy by Jangotat: "A strong one ... he’ll have a name, not a number." Asked whether he has ever loved: "Yes."
- The dashta eels leave the grotto.
- Ventress kills Trillot ("You strode my dreams") and leaves shaken: "in the water, when she could have followed him down into death, he had remained firm." She plans to volunteer for "the most dangerous assignment General Grievous could devise".
