## "The Hive" (Steven Barnes; bonus novella, 7 chapters, set just before or early in the main novel)
### Ch. 1: the hive council's request
- Duris has just deposed Caiza Quill as head of the hive council. A Vippit barrister brought by Obi-Wan says that the X'Ting land under Cestus Cybernetics was bought with "worthless synthetic gems", so a lawsuit could break the company. Obi-Wan wants it used as "emergency leverage", not handed to Palpatine.
- **History lore:** the X'Ting beat the spider people and united the planet under a queen. They leased land to Coruscant for prisons. Imprisoned Cybot Galactica executives found droid-grade minerals and, with the wardens' connivance, founded Cestus Cybernetics. The **Great Plague** (the X'Ting believe it was deliberate) killed the royals. A royal egg pair was sealed in a Toong'l Security vault, with eggs destroyed "after three unsuccessful attempts".
- **Jedi lore:** "a hundred and fifty years ago a visitor came from the center of the galaxy. A warrior with powers beyond any the X’Ting had ever seen. He called himself a Jedi."
  - Duris: "If you are half the warrior Master Yoda is said to be, you will prevail."
- Guide: First Rank Jesson Di Blinth, an X'Ting warrior with a three-sectioned crystal staff, contemptuous of offworlders. X'Ting store information in scent marks.
### Ch. 2–3: Zeetsa and the cannibals
- The Zeetsa are Lifemilk creatures that copy faces from their neighbours ("It is dreaming ... And you are a part of the dream").
- Obi-Wan cuts a fist-sized hole through a rusted metal door with his lightsaber.
- **Fight:** near-blind cannibal X'Ting diggers with pry bars and spears, in waves.
> "X’Ting diggers fell before him like scythed grain ... Ferociously fast, the cannibal X’Ting attacked in a frightening wave ... trusting in numbers to carry the day. / Against a Jedi, that was not enough." (Hive ch. 3)
  - **Form III is named:** "It was better to stay in the realm of Form III, the lightsaber combat he had practiced for so long ... defense and attack were two sides of the same coin."
  - He uses the Force to hurl an attacker aside.
  - Jesson fights with "almost weightless agility, kicking and punching in all directions with all six limbs. His weapon whirled like a propeller, almost invisibly fast."
### Ch. 4–5: Hall of Heroes, worms
- The statues are 30–60+ m tall. Obi-Wan makes a Force leap to a hidden opening.
- **Worms:** at least 30 m long and "almost as fast as a human could run". Obi-Wan beheads one and cuts others, and he "felt it clearly through the Force" when they scream.
  - Grapnel swing: the stalactite breaks and they crash.
  - Obi-Wan heaves the wounded Jesson onto a rock one-armed: "ignoring the strain, forcing himself to greater effort".
  - He crosses 20 m of line hand over hand. A worm segment latches onto him, and dangling by one hand he calls his saber to the other.
  - Light repels them. Explanation: the security company brought in the worms as guards, which drove the deep X'Ting into the statues and to cannibalism.
### Ch. 6: the vault test
- **Lightsaber vs a vault door:**
> "this door was certainly tougher than the previous one. Regardless, the Jedi weapon blistered the durasteel ... The door was sandwiched with energy-absorbing circuits that slowed, but never stopped him." (Hive ch. 6)
- Obi-Wan can't read the laser array through the Force: "How he wished that Anakin were here! His Padawan learner was an intuitive genius with all things mechanical".
- Three riddles (the Zeetsa, the heroes, the spider people), each judged "Incorrect". The test actually reads Jesson's grief pheromones:
> "The test was not for your mind. It was for your heart." (Hive ch. 6)
- Obi-Wan's warrior speech: "Tread not the path to war seeking to preserve life. That is a fool’s dream ... Sell your life dearly." / "We all have a hive."
### Ch. 7: the twist
- The eggs are recovered alive. The largest statue (at least 70 m), whose head, torso and cane-ladder they climbed through, is of **Master Yoda**:
> "loomed the hollow, chewed-stone statue of a smiling Master Yoda." (Hive ch. 7)
  - Lore: Yoda saved the X'Ting about 150 years earlier and was honoured as their greatest hero. Obi-Wan has never heard of it: "some of the greatest Jedi were notoriously reticent to speak of their deeds."
