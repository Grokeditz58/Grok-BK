# Young Jedi Knights 2: Shadow Academy: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-20 CST. Full read of READ l.1–2588 (every line, in order). Citation "SA ch. N, p.x" (printed page of the Boulevard printing). Detail is in sa-01 to sa-05; every quote below is pasted from those parts.
- Authors: Kevin J. Anderson & Rebecca Moesta. © 1995 Lucasfilm Ltd. Boulevard edition, September 1995, ISBN 1-57297-025-1. 22 chapters (printed p.1–207), then a bundled The Lost Ones blurb and ch. 1 preview (printed p.209–225).
- Era: directly after Heirs of the Force; the twins are fourteen (back cover). Tenel Ka reflects that "It was not yet two decades since Luke and her parents had eradicated the old Nightsisters" (ch. 19). No ABY year is given.

## Plot
- **Ch. 1 (p.1–12), Jacen and Luke's lightsaber; Tenel Ka's nightmare; Lando arrives:** Jacen secretly practices with Luke's lightsaber in Luke's office against an imagined red-bladed opponent; Luke catches him and says he is not ready. Jacen asks Luke to persuade Tenel Ka to come to GemDiver Station; Luke says she has good reasons. Tenel Ka wakes from a Nightsister nightmare: today Ambassador Yfra of Hapes visits, and she hides being heir to the Hapan throne. Jaina and Lowie preview the trip on Jaina's home-built holoprojector. Lando and Lobot arrive on the Lady Luck and take the twins and Lowie.
- **Ch. 2 (p.12–21), GemDiver Station and the Fast Hand:** The Lady Luck reaches GemDiver Station in Yavin's upper atmosphere, guarded by automated defense satellites. Lando shows the kids Corusca gems and the armored automated cargo pods; they board the armored diving bell Fast Hand on its energy tether.
- **Ch. 3 (p.21–31), Descent into Yavin:** The Fast Hand drops from GemDiver Station on the energy tether; the twins feel the tether; Lando explains the Corusca fishermen.
- **Ch. 4 (p.31–42), The attack on GemDiver Station:** On the control bridge Lobot reports the station is jammed; Jaina identifies four Skipray blastboats and an unknown modified assault shuttle with a fanged boring device. The twins and Lowie sense the attackers want them. The blastboats ion-cannon and destroy the defensive satellites.
- **Ch. 5 (p.42–52), Tenel Ka and Ambassador Yfra:** On Yavin 4 Tenel Ka, in full Dathomiri armor, waits on the temple ramparts for Ambassador Yfra and senses something coming.
- **Ch. 6 (p.52–62), Prisoners in hyperspace:** The three wake in an unlocked-door cell aboard a ship in hyperdrive; Em Teedee has been taken. A stormtrooper guards the door.
- **Ch. 7 (p.62–76), Off Chance to Borgo Prime:** Disguised, Luke and Tenel Ka fly Lando's old blockade runner Off Chance to Borgo Prime; they rehearse their cover as Randon traders seeking industrial Corusca gems to open an Old Republic vault on Ossus.
- **Ch. 8 (p.76–83), Docking at the Shadow Academy; Brakiss:** Qorl docks; his arm has been replaced by a droid arm. The station re-cloaks. Brakiss greets them and explains the Second Imperium's plan for Imperial Dark Jedi. Lowie threatens the guards; Jaina demands Em Teedee.
- **Ch. 9 (p.83–89), To Dathomir:** Tenel Ka wakes from another dark dream aboard the Off Chance en route to Dathomir; Luke and she discuss the Nightsisters.
- **Ch. 10 (p.89–100), First lecture at the Shadow Academy:** Jaina whispers she has a plan. Jacen mocks the stormtroopers with a joke. In the lecture hall of uniformed Imperial youths, Jaina probes Lowie and a blond student. Brakiss lectures and his voice sways Jaina.
- **Ch. 11 (p.100–106), Dathomir and the Singing Mountain Clan:** Luke and Tenel Ka land on Dathomir and climb to the Singing Mountain Clan's fortress. Tenel Ka's great-grandmother Augwynne Djo, who foresaw their coming, warns that the new Nightsisters are not like the old ones: subtle, organized, allied with the Empire. Tenel Ka insists they must go after her friends, and Augwynne calls the Council of Sisters.
- **Ch. 12 (p.106–111), Brakiss's office; Jacen's lessons:** Brakiss meditates on images of cosmic catastrophes, plans his report to the hidden new Imperial leader, and recalls his past at Luke's academy and the failed self-confrontation test.
- **Ch. 13 (p.111–120), The lightsaber and the remotes:** In the training chamber Brakiss gives Jacen his own lightsaber; Jacen considers striking him down.
- **Ch. 14 (p.120–130), Lowie and Tamith Kai:** Lowie, ashamed of yielding to anger under Brakiss's teaching, resolves to resist. Tamith Kai takes his syren-fiber belt and tortures him for hours with noise, glare and icy water, demanding he destroy a sonic generator with the Force. Shown holograms of the twins apparently mocking him, he breaks in a blackout fury and destroys it. Em Teedee, now reprogrammed, praises the Empire; Lowie switches him off.
- **Ch. 15 (p.130–137), The Council of Sisters; visitors at dusk:** At the Singing Mountain, Tenel Ka reports to Luke and they wait. At dusk Vonnda Ra arrives on a rancor with her male student Vilas, using a Force-amplified voice to recruit for the Great Canyon settlement and claiming the old Nightsisters were wrongly despised. Vilas climbs the cliff by the Force and calls down a thunderstorm. Luke decides that he and Tenel Ka will pose as candidates.
- **Ch. 16 (p.137–144), Jaina's disguised duel:** Brakiss has been training Jaina with lightsaber holo-monsters too. She bargains to see Jacen and Lowie in exchange for one more session, fighting under a holographic disguise.
- **Ch. 17 (p.144–154), Rancor ride to the Great Canyon:** Tenel Ka rides a rancor with Luke to the Great Canyon; Luke talks of the rancor he once killed. Rancor riders with Imperial blasters stop them, and Tenel Ka commands her way in as a woman of the Misty Falls Clan. In Vonnda Ra's tent Tenel Ka out-performs Vonnda Ra's lamp lift. Vonnda Ra dismisses Luke as a man, until he hurls her stuffed rancor-head chair through the roof by telekinesis; she accepts both for the Imperial shuttle at dawn.
- **Ch. 18 (p.154–166), The remotes and the Force blades:** Brakiss and Tamith Kai set a new test: the twins, blindfolded, with only sticks and the Force, fight off stones and later knives, while Lowie controls four protective remotes from the observation chamber. The twins are struck repeatedly. Lowie instead slices the control computer and crashes the whole training room. Brakiss praises his initiative and plans another raid on Luke's academy.
- **Ch. 19 (p.166–176), Garowyn's shuttle:** Garowyn arrives in the quantum-armored Shadow Chaser and takes Luke and Tenel Ka aboard for the two-day trip. She dismisses Luke and tests Tenel Ka, who sees through Luke's deliberate holding back. Garowyn levitates Tenel Ka; Tenel Ka goes limp, breaks free and hooks her. Luke lifts Garowyn, holds her helpless and seals her in an escape pod. Tenel Ka makes her first joke, and the cloaked Shadow Academy appears.
- **Ch. 20 (p.176–186), Jacen's escape; Jaina's sabotage:** During sleep period Jacen, in pain from the test, cuts open his door's control plate with his Corusca gem, traces the wires with the Force and opens the door. He finds Jaina by their twin bond, and they free Lowie and the protesting Em Teedee. Jaina plugs Em Teedee into a terminal for the passwords; Lowie drops the cloaking device and seals every door, and the alarms go off.
- **Ch. 21 (p.186–200), Escape from the Shadow Academy:** Brakiss, planning a raid on Yavin 4 with Tamith Kai, senses trouble and is locked out of his office. In the docking bay the three meet Luke and Tenel Ka arriving in the Shadow Chaser. Qorl, uneasy about Brakiss's methods, reports the escape. Tamith Kai blasts through her sealed door. Luke faces Brakiss until Jaina seals the door with the ship's lasers. Tenel Ka breaks Tamith Kai's knee with a kick. Qorl secretly opens the space doors and the Shadow Chaser escapes; Brakiss moves the station.
- **Ch. 22 (p.200–207), Home on Yavin 4:** Back on Yavin 4 the twins speak with Han and Leia; Lando will tighten GemDiver security, and Leia lets Luke keep the Shadow Chaser. Em Teedee is purged of Imperial programming. Tenel Ka's grandmother sends Yfra away and admits to watching over her. On the Great Temple, Luke hands Jacen his lightsaber; Jacen gives it back unlit, saying he is not ready, and Luke teaches that no one is completely evil or good.

## Scale and limits notes
- Brakiss never fights: Luke and he only face off (ch. 21), and the Shadow Chaser's lasers seal the door between them. Luke hesitates and does not want to fight him then (ch. 21, p.193; see sa-04).
- Tamith Kai's top feats: a Force leash on three trainees at once, and blasting her sealed door open, then tearing a plate out bare-handed. Her worst anti-feat: Tenel Ka breaks her kneecap with one kick while she charges dark lightning.
- Luke's top feats: he hurls Vonnda Ra's rancor-head chair through the tent roof, and he lifts and holds Garowyn, who cannot break free. On the ship he deliberately holds back during tests.
- The trainees' tests: blindfolded, they deflect stones and knives with the Force and are hurt badly. Lowbacca wins by crashing the training-room computer. Qorl says the stones-and-knives test had already killed half a dozen trainees.
- Qorl's arc: he alerts the station, then secretly opens the space doors.
