## Thesis

Caedus has been overrated ("wank"). On the text, the author places him at "Maul-level", alongside Exar Kun, Malak, Bane, Mace Windu and Cade. "Or if I'm feeling generous", he would accept Dooku's level, alongside Revan, Arcann, Malgus, Tenebrous and Obi-Wan. He rejects Yoda- or ROTS-titan level. Of the 13 Luke-comparison arguments, "Tallying the results, we have one [✓] and 12 [✗]" (FORUM OPINION).

## Arguments (numbered)

**A. Power growth (post 1)**

1. EC's "massive growth leading into Invincible" claim fails, because pain alone doesn't power a Sith; it needs to become rage. Rage-amp comparisons:
   - Malgus in *Deceived*: "He felt as if he were the Force";
   - Anakin vs Dooku (ROTS novel);
   - Vader in *Dark Lord*.
   
   Caedus instead restrains his anger in *Invincible*.
2. Legacy of the Force spans **just one year** (ERC). One year of growth can't lift Caedus to the ROTS titan class. The author compares this with Anakin's AOTC→ROTS arc.

**B. Opponents (post 2)**

3. Caedus's LotF opponents are all far below the titans:
   - Aurra Sing is past her prime; she lost to a 17-year-old A'Sharad Hett and was manhandled by 19 BBY Vader.
   - Mara's telekinetic limits are poor.
   - Jaina is below the NJO Council.
   - Katarn's team is not above Kit Fisto or Plo Koon.
   
   Each pushes Caedus to or near his limits.

**C. Fights (posts 2–8)**

4. **(1) vs Aurra Sing** and **(2) vs Mara Jade Skywalker:** both were hard fights, with a dislocated knee and a tunnel collapse in the Mara fight.
5. **(3) vs Katarn, Horn, Mithric and Hu'lya** (*Fury*): the team matched him as equals: "They're coordinating. Good for them. Bad for me." He only wins by blindsiding Katarn with a speeder. Supplementary material says dueling Katarn "taxed" him.
6. **Teambusting comparison list (i–xli).** Forty-one group feats by others, which the author calls far beyond Caedus. Examples:
   - Kun freezing the Senate;
   - Revan soloing the Korriban academy;
   - Traya one-shotting the Bladeborn;
   - Valkorion freezing Vaylin, Arcann and the Outlander;
   - Tenebrae pinning the 16-person EOO team;
   - Sidious vs Maul and Opress;
   - Vader vs Drallig;
   - Krayt killing four Imperial Knights;
   - Muur blowing everyone away;
   - Cade and Nat beating 11 Sith Troopers.
7. **"Medium distortion" and "protagonist debuff" are rejected.** Vitiate would remain an existential threat in any medium. The film titans show what LotF writers had seen. Caedus is also the series' antagonist, so the writers could have made him dominant.
8. **(4) vs Jaina** (*Invincible*): taking a lightsaber to the gut lets Jaina "wear Caedus down". Comparisons with the wounds of Maul (bisected), Bane (orbalisks), Windu (abdomen, vs Billaba) and Sion show Caedus's endurance is not special.

**D. Cosmic insignificance (posts 9–10)**

9. Titan-class darksiders affect the Force itself:
   - Tenebrae weighs down the Force for centuries;
   - Vaylin's "baleful presence";
   - Plagueis and Sidious's "etheric war";
   - Taalon shifts the balance after the Pool of Knowledge;
   - Krayt makes the dark side "stronger than ever" (Legacy-era sourcebook scans; B092, B093 OCR).
   
   Caedus has no such effect.
10. **The Revelation telepathy feat counts against Caedus.** Feeding on the rage of about a hundred commanders, he becomes overwhelmed. He needs a feedback loop to influence a handful of minds on Fondor. The author says this is a feat "we'd expect to be performed casually by the likes of Sith initiate Exar Kun".

## Common rebuttals / weak points (ANALYSIS — not from the thread)

- **Scope admission.** The author states he had "not read Legacy of the Force" and knows "very little about the post-ROTJ time period outside of Legacy". This invites the counter that context is missing.
- **Teambusting comparisons** across eras and media (comics, games, novels) assume equivalent opponent quality.
- **Dismissing medium distortion and the protagonist debuff** is contested by Caedus advocates (e.g. DC77 and EmperorCaedus in the existing debates).
- **Verdict ✗s** often turn on the author's reading of context (Inferno, Juke). Others (Ellimist in #94) read the same Inferno duel as Caedus landing devastating blows on a full-power Luke.
- **Most images are dead links**, so 123 of 133 visual citations can't be checked.
- **The Akanah ✓** is conceded but then discounted on "intense skepticism", not on textual grounds.
