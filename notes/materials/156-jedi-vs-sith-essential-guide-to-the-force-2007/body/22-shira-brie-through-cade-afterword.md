- Caption (same leaf, lower right, pointing up into the plate): “DARTH VADER WITH IMPERIAL INTELLIGENCE AGENT SHIRA BRIE. (Trevas)”

A scar marks the left side of her face (viewer’s right). Clear tubes run to her neck and arm. Her right hand is raised against the tank wall; her left hangs at her side. At the far left edge, only the dark armored shoulder and arm of Darth Vader are visible, as if he is standing just outside the tank. A silver 2-1B-type medical droid stands at right behind her, one mechanical hand extended, with corrugated hoses rising to the ceiling and a wall console beyond. The plate is printed as a finished figure study; the notes describe the clothing and scarring exactly as drawn.

## S244 — printed **223** expected; folio omitted

- Caption (UL, pointing left to S243): “DARTH KRAYT AND DARTH TALON. (Trevas)”
- Caption (toward LR, pointing right into the same-page art): “SHADO VAO, WOLF SAZEN, AND K'KRUHK. (Trevas)”
- Right-half art (Trevas), full height: a stacked group of three Jedi against a dark ground. At top, the Twi'lek Shado Vao in dark clothing, a lit green lightsaber at the right edge of the frame. In the middle, hooded blue-skinned Wolf Sazen with a lit blue lightsaber. At the bottom, the Whiphid Master K'Kruhk in brown robes. No folio circle found on this leaf.
- **AFTERWORD** (title in red, boxed, with a gold rule). Gold line: “Recorded **130** A.B.Y.” (no *in*). Author line in roman gray: “Author: Ellu Viss, Jedi apprentice.” Drop-cap **I**. The Afterword body is **roman**, not italic — unlike the first-person recordings that make up most of the book. This is the narrative frame’s close: Tionne Solusar’s records (dated around 40 A.B.Y. in Part Six) are here “nearly a century old.” Full text, visually confirmed from column crops:

  I took Tionne Solusar’s records when I left Ossus. Everything she wrote is nearly a century old, but I’m hoping there might be some information I can use. I want to find out if any other Jedi escaped from Darth Krayt. I want to help them, but I’m not sure where to begin.

  My name is Ellu Viss, and the year is 130 A.B.Y. The Sith-Imperial War is over, and right now it looks like the Galactic Federation of Free Alliances is history. Not to mention most of the Jedi. The Sith have taken over, and they’ve started what they’re calling the new Galactic Empire. They’re being led by Darth Krayt, the first Sith Lord since Emperor Palpatine to rule the galaxy.

  Krayt’s not bothering with that “Rule of Two” business. He has a whole bunch of Darths on his side. His main lieutenant is the Chagrian Darth Wyyrlok. There’re also his two Hands, the former Nagai warlord Darth Nihl and the Twi'lek Darth Talon. There may be more. Two days after Coruscant fell to the new Empire, it was Nihl who led the assault on the Jedi praxeum on Ossus.

  A lot of Jedi are dead. Some surrendered to the Sith, and now they’re serving the Empire as Imperial Knights. I don’t think they got Cade Skywalker, though.

  How did all this happen? Before the war started, most of the Jedi were more concerned about who was sabotaging the Yuuzhan Vong terraforming efforts on various worlds. I don’t think anyone suspected the Sith were involved, or that they even still existed. We weren’t ready for any of this.

  I have to figure out what happened, where we went wrong. There are people out there who need my help.

  May the Force be with me.

- Typography, preserved as printed: *There’re* (contraction); *praxeum*; *Twi'lek* with an apostrophe; *Ellu Viss*; *“Rule of Two”* in quotation marks; *new Galactic Empire* (the phrase is lowercase *new* in the body). The closer is a single roman sentence, *May the Force be with me.*
