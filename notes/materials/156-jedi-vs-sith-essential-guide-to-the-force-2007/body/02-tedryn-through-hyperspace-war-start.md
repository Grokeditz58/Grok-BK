- Caption (UL, pointing left to S025): “JEDI LIBRARIAN ODAN-URR AT THE GREAT JEDI LIBRARY ON OSSUS (Trevas)”
- Continues Sith Holocrons: they “implement restrictive mechanisms to detect whether a student is prepared to receive certain knowledge.” The Sith’s “hologrammic simulacra can exert only verbal influence, but allegedly attempted to corrupt otherwise innocent beings and draw “students” down the path to the dark side.” (quotes around *students*).
