## S015 — Introduction, folio **xv**

- Running label: INTRODUCTION. Kicker: **Recorded 40 A.B.Y.**
- Caption (LL, pointing left): “JEDI MASTER TIONNE SOLUSAR STUDIES A JEDI HOLOCRON IN 40 A.B.Y. (Trevas)”
- Opens, centered then two columns: “Jedi. Hear the words of Tionne Solusar. / It is a dire time.”
- Reports the death of Jedi Master Mara Jade Skywalker by an assassin’s toxic dart; Luke Skywalker “confronted and killed the primary suspect, Lumiya, the self-proclaimed Dark Lady of the Sith.” Lumiya’s confirmed movements “precluded her from being the assassin.” Focus shifts to former Jedi Knight Alema Rar, “a possible apprentice of Lumiya.” “All Jedi feel the loss of Mara Jade Skywalker, but we are not united by grief.” Some Jedi “question the fabric of our Order.” “Despite all the battles that we’ve fought and won, it seems our greatest enemy is—and always has been—ourselves.” “it would seem that the destinies of these two groups will almost always be intertwined.” History “at least twice been lost”: destruction of the original Jedi Temple on Ossus, and when Emperor Palpatine “seized the Jedi Archives on Coruscant.” “It was no coincidence that the Sith were responsible for both incidents.” The Yuuzhan Vong invasion of “the library world Obroa-skai” (printed lowercase *s*) is treated by “galactic academia” as the greater devastation. “Because of the possible temptation of the dark side of the Force, the majority of contemporary Jedi have long maintained that ignorance of Sith history and teachings is for the best.” “belief is not the same thing as knowledge.” “we confine ourselves to a darkness of our own design.” Many Jedi “believed the death of Palpatine brought an end to the Sith threat” and were “subsequently astonished by the return of Lumiya.” Column ends mid-sentence: “but it would be negligent of me to hastily divulge answers about the Sith with-”

## S016 — Introduction, folio **xvi**

- Continues: “out providing relevant information about the Jedi” is on xv; xvi begins “proof that ignorance is *not* the solution.” (*not* italic). “there is no evidence to substantiate that it died with Lumiya on the planet Terephon.” “I can only speculate how my own work will be perceived in the future, but I consider it my responsibility to ensure that our legacy *will* survive to enable future generations, and *not* be confiscated or obliterated.” (*will* and *not* italic). “Our histories are not separate but dangerously entwined, like two branches on a tree that threaten to break each other in their respective attempts to embrace sunlight and shadow.” “unless we examine the history of the Jedi vs. the Sith, the Jedi will remain at a disadvantage.” Closes: “It is a very real possibility that Mara Jade Skywalker’s killer is not only still at large, but also a Sith.” Right side: painting of a long-haired bust / figure in a high-windowed hall (the Jedi Archives setting continued on S017).

## S018 — Time Line, folio **xviii**

- Header **TIME LINE**. Introduces Standard Time Parts hinged on the Battle of Yavin (B.B.Y. / A.B.Y.); Historical Council of the Galactic Federation of Free Alliances “officially established and popularized the Battle of Yavin as the zero point.”
- Entries (verbatim sense, dates as printed): c. 100,000 B.B.Y. Dawn of Sith civilization on Korriban; c. 30,000 B.B.Y. Adas defeats the Rakatan invaders and gains Holocron technology; capital relocated to Ziost, Korriban their tombworld; c. 25,200 B.B.Y. Rakatan Infinite Empire implodes; c. 25,000 B.B.Y.
