## Afterword: "The Jedi's War: From the Private Journals of Mace Windu"
- "I still dream of Geonosis. / But my dreams are different, now."
- **Aftermath:** a Republic task force arrives within 48 standard hours; it had been dispatched for the *Halleck*'s distress call, and the landing is unopposed. Using his authority as General of the GAR, Mace **redesignates the Korunnal Highland**: no longer a war zone; the Senate declares it a **police action**. "I have decided to treat the Summertime War as a law enforcement problem. / Which it would have always been, had the financial interests behind the thyssel bark trade not been able to buy off certain Senators and Judicial sector coordinators."
- Jups and guerrillas are being disarmed and atrocities investigated; the militia is reduced to "Keepers of the peace. Not soldiers."
- **Geptun:** inducted into the GAR, with a transfer "to Republic Intelligence. We will need him." The Lorshan massacre threat "turns out to have been a bluff"; the militia ROE prohibit targeting civilians, and most atrocities were by jungle prospectors.
- **Nick:** convalescing on Coruscant. Mace recommends his brevet major rank be confirmed and that he receive the **Medal of Valor**. **Chalk's real name was Liane Trevval**; she gets a posthumous commission to be eligible for the same medal. Her akk Galthra went into the jungle (custom is to put down akks that lose their Force-bonded partner, as they "go insane, and vicious").
- Pelek Baw casualties: "a staggering number. No one on Haruun Kal will ever forget that night."
- **Kar:** his hands were saved; he is detained in the Jedi Temple, "where his power cannot sway his jailers". **He is charged with "crimes against civilization"**, a category revived after "four thousand years: since the days of the Sith Wars". The Terrel murder charge is held in reserve. Depa faces the same charge "If she’s ever declared competent to stand trial."
- **Depa's state:** in a bacta tank, eyes open, unspeaking and unmoving. "When the healers touch her through the Force, all they find is darkness. Vast and featureless. / She is lost in infinite night." Yoda: "To end her life, she tried ... Recover she may. Someday." Mace: "I would almost have preferred to lose the planet, if I could have saved Depa." The scar may have been torture or self-inflicted: "We may never know."
- **Palpatine scene (dramatic irony):** he visits with Yoda and Mace and probes what causes a Jedi to fall: "Master Billaba is not the only Jedi we have lost to the darkness." Mace: "It’s war ... When saving these innocents means that those innocents must die. I’m not sure that any Jedi can survive such choices for long." Yoda: "Fall you did not ... From this, strength you can take." Palpatine: "such a thing could never happen to—say, for example—you ... Could it?" Mace: "I didn’t tell him that it could. That it nearly had."
- **Mace's war doctrine (key quotes):**
> "She was right about her Jedi of the Future. To win this war against the Separatists, we must abandon the very thing that makes us Jedi. Yes, we won on Haruun Kal—because our enemy broke under the club of Kar Vastor’s monstrous ruthlessness. / Jedi are keepers of the peace. We are not soldiers. / If we become soldiers, we will be Jedi no more." (SP Afterword)
> "The Separatists are not the true enemies of the Jedi ... Even the reborn Sith are not our enemy. Not really. / Our enemy is power mistaken for justice. / Our enemy is the desperation that justifies atrocity. / The Jedi’s true enemy is the jungle." (SP Afterword)
> "my mistake on Geonosis wasn’t a mistake at all. / If I had done as Depa said I should have—if I had won the Clone War with a baradium bomb on Geonosis—I would have lost the real war. The Jedi’s war. / Anakin Skywalker may be the shatterpoint of our war against the jungle. / If he is ... it does not matter if every other Jedi in the galaxy dies. / As long as Anakin lives, we have hope." (SP Afterword)
  - Mace confirms Anakin is alive because of his choice at Geonosis ("he is alive today because my Jedi instincts were working just fine").

## "Equipment" (short story by Matthew Stover): "A Personal Account of the Sub-orbital Action at Haruun Kal, as reported by Auxiliary Heavy-Weapons Specialist CT-6/774"
- Narrator CT-6/774 is the starboard bubble-turret gunner on an "RHE LAAT/i" (Rothana). His partner is CT-014/783 ("Eight-Three"); the pilot is Lt. Four-One and the nav is CL-33/890. Their good-luck exchange: "Take care of the equipment" / "And the equipment will take care of us." Kamino "knowledge pumps" drill this in "Even before we’re awake".
- **System lore:** Haruun Kal has a second, younger asteroid belt in orbit around the planet, "remnants of what once had been the planet’s moon". The *Halleck* orbited more than 10,000 km out because of rumored ion cannons. The *Halleck* was stationed in the Ventran system; "Ventran is on the Gevarno Loop, one of half a dozen systems linked by hyperspace lanes that run through Al’har. Haruun Kal is the only habitable planet in the Al’har system." Force: "a regiment of heavy infantry, twenty Jadthu-class landers, an escort of six starfighters. And us: five RHE LAAT/i-s." Mace is called "General of the Grand Army of the Republic and Senior Member of the Jedi Council".
- **LAAT/i specs (lore):** the navigator runs all three antipersonnel turrets, the main missile launcher and two of the four main cannons; the laser cannons "punch holes through medium armor"; mass-driver missile launchers carry HE, HEAP and APF loads, avoiding baradium ("too unstable") in favour of detonite and proton-core warheads. Each bubble turret also controls four short-range air-to-air rockets.
- **The ambush:** droid starfighters, "Probably Geonosian", hid powered down and clamped to drifting asteroids in the moon-belt for weeks: 64, then 91, 105, 128 more, then a second ambush wave of about 32 (half a wing). "The whole system was a trap." Droid brains "have reflexes that operate at the speed of light." CT-6/774 scores at least five hits and two kills before his turret is shot off the ship.
- (Here two DSFs ramming the bridge is what wounded Captain Trent's crew, consistent with ch. 16.) RP (repulsorpack) troopers ram starfighters: "a starfighter that strikes a combat-armored trooper might as well be flying straight into the side of an asteroid"; chaff clouds shred DSFs "at a couple thousand KPH".
- He fires the last shot of the orbital battle: drifting in his detached bubble, steadied by RP troopers, he uses reserve power-cells to kill the DSF chasing his crippled LAAT/i. The survivors dock on an asteroid and live two days on salvaged life-support until the task force arrives. "Because we are equipment, too. / As long as the Republic takes care of us, we’ll take care of it."

## Del Rey Legends back matter
- The "Star Wars—Legends" intro (Del Rey, May 2014) is standard boilerplate, the same as the 050 back matter.
- **Excerpt: *The Cestus Deception* (Steven Barnes), ch. 1–2.** This is new compared with 050, and book 062 is being read in full next, so it is summarized only briefly here.
  - Ch. 1: Obi-Wan and Anakin are recalled from **Forscan VI** (an unaligned colony at the edge of the Cron drift, where they were countering a Separatist infiltration camp) by Palpatine's emergency directive. They flew 36 hours, "using their Jedi skills to hold their needs for sleep and sustenance to a minimum". Anakin is "already famed as one of the galaxy’s most powerful warriors" but not yet a Knight. The war council meets at the T'Chuk chin-bret arena (half a million seats).
  - Ch. 2: technician Lido Shan demonstrates the **JK-13 "Jedi Killer"** security droid. Rotating energy disks *absorb* bolts. It kills a Geonosis-captured destroyer droid "in less than … forty-two seconds" with fibersaw tendrils, then nonlethally nets and shocks an ARC trooper with "almost precognitive" aim. **Kit Fisto** (Form I) duels it: hidden sand-camouflaged tendrils attack "on two levels simultaneously"; "it was deliberately misleading Kit by performing at multiple levels of tempo and efficiency". Kit's unorthodox speed overwhelms it in close range ("a blur of motion complex and rapid enough to baffle even Obi-Wan’s experienced gaze"). The key point: "the JK had anticipated the Nautolan’s responses." Obi-Wan thinks his own "more measured response might well have proven less effective". The excerpt gives a figure of "1.2 million clone troopers" and has Obi-Wan's line "I’ve met every one of them."
- **The Old Republic era intro** is the same boilerplate as 050.
- **Excerpt: *The Old Republic: Deceived* (Paul S. Kemp), ch. 1.** This is new compared with 050.
  - Zeerid Korr ("Z-man"), an ex-Republic marine who flew Havoc Squadron commandos in a gully jumper, now runs guns for The Exchange in *Fatman* to pay for daughter Arra's medical care (debt of 2M+ credits plus advances). He carries a cargo of 300M+ credits in crew-served laser cannons, MPAPPs and grenades. An Ord Mantell drop is ambushed: he knife-hands one man's throat and quick-draws a chest shot; his armored vest "ablated" a pistol bolt ("would not stop a rifle bolt"); then he detonates the dropped grenade crate with *Fatman*'s plasma cannons, killing six.
  - **Darth Malgus** (respirator, ruined lips; Twi'lek companion Eleena, whom he rescued "from the slave pens of Geonosis"; she nursed him after "the Battle of Alderaan ... from that Jedi witch"). Philosophy: "I serve the Force. The Force is conflict. The Empire is conflict. The two are congruent ... survivors of the conflict come to understand the Force more deeply." His first kill as a boy was "to assure himself that he could kill"; he trained at the Sith Academy on Dromund Kaas. The Mandalorian takes down Coruscant's defense grid, and a hijacked drop ship arrives in 90 seconds. At the Temple statues (Odan-Urr, Ooroo, Arca Jeth): "You have been deceived ... Your time is past." To the guards: "I cannot stop ... Not ever."
  - Aryn Leneer (an empath, Padawan of Master Zallow) and Syo on Alderaan during the peace negotiations (Master Dar'nala and Satele Shan inside), facing a Sith sibling pair: "I see you." / "And I you."
