## Ch. 13: "Jedi of the Future"
- Mace tracks his lightsaber on Kar through the Force: "He’s not settling ... Like a rancor staked out in the desert." Kar "probably senses that even though the fight was real, my submission was fake." The bite wound carried "many different kinds of lethal bacteria".
- **Mace's visions of Order 66 and the Fall:**
> "In my dreams here, I have seen the destruction of the Jedi. The death of the Republic. I have seen the Temple in ruins, the Senate smashed, and Coruscant itself shattered by orbital bombardment from immense ships of impossible design. I have seen Coruscant ... become a jungle far more hostile and alien than any on Haruun Kal." (Mace journal, SP ch. 13)
  - Possible NJO/Yuuzhan Vong foreshadowing ("ships of impossible design", Coruscant as jungle).
- Depa screams in her sleep three times a night. "I have discovered that I hate this place." / "I hate what it’s doing to me."
- Depa on staying: "There is fighting to be done. Can a Jedi walk away?" / "War is not about light or dark. It is about winning. Or dying." Mace: "To fight on after the battle is done—Depa, that is not Jedi ... That’s the dark."
- Mace: "only chance—a whim of Jedi anthropologists and the choice of some elders of ghosh Windu—separates their fate from my own. / I could too easily have grown to become Kar Vastor myself."
- The Korun hate is felt "as though the jungle had become a planetwide resonance chamber ... that all Balawai might have a single skull, bent for a Korun mace". Depa: "We used these people for our own purposes ... Should I now throw them away? Abandon them to genocide?" / "They need me, Mace. I am their only hope."
- Depa's prophecy: "Imagine infantry battles in the fields of Alderaan. DOKAWs striking spacescrapers on Coruscant. ... You haven’t seen serious yet."
- **GAR size stat:** "the Grand Army of the Republic numbers 1.2 million clone troopers, just enough to station a single trooper—one lone man—on each planet of the Republic, and have a handful of thousands left over." (Mace journal, SP ch. 13; so roughly 1.2 million Republic member planets is implied.)
- Depa's fever, migraines and "sibylline" pronouncements; Mace can't record her voice on his datapad ("As though our talks are my own hallucination").
- **Kar's backstory:** Kar and Mace "are the last of the Windu" (the ghôsh was destroyed by attrition over 30 years). Mace lived with ghôsh Windu "for those months in my teens ... to learn some of the Korun Force skills". "I am more thoroughly Jedi than I am Korun." At 14 Kar saw his family massacred by jungle prospectors and survived alone in the jungle for over a standard year (his tan pel'trokal). "Pelekotan traded him power for his humanity" (his loss of speech).
- **Akk Guards as dark mirror:** "They rely on instinct; Jedi rely on training. They use anger and aggression as sources of power ... **I use my sword as a shield. They use their shields as swords.**"
- **Vibroshield tech (key lore/scaling):**
> "It also seems to be a superconductor. / This is why my blade could not cut it: the entire shield is always the same temperature throughout. Even the energy of a lightsaber is instantly conducted away. Hold a blade against it long enough and the whole thing will melt, but it cannot be cut. Not by an energy blade." (Mace journal, SP ch. 13)
  - The metal is fungus-proof, never loses its edge, doesn't rust. Mace's theory: **ancient pre-Sith-War starship armor**, "a mirrorlike superconducting alloy", from "the ancient Jedi starship whose crash stranded on this planet his ancestors, and my own" (Korunnai origin: descendants of a Jedi ship crash).
  - Each Akk Guard builds his own weapons, like Jedi lightsaber tradition. Kar taught himself "the Jedi skill of interception" (blaster deflection); Mace suspects it comes from stories he told ghôsh Windu as a teen: "Kar Vastor may be my fault."
- **Kar's Force healing:** he hums and draws nearly a kilo of fever-wasp larvae out of Besh and Chalk through their skin and into a tyruun fire; "a swirl of power very unlike any I’ve felt from a Jedi healer".
- **Major power ranking:**
> "Such power—and such control—and never a day of training. ... And what Jedi could have done this?" (Depa) / "I could not answer; **Vastor has power on the scale of Master Yoda, or young Anakin Skywalker.**" (Mace journal, SP ch. 13)
- **Depa's thesis: "The Jedi will lose."** "The Republic may very well win. But the Jedi will lose." / "The reason you freed the Balawai, Mace, is the same reason that the Jedi will be destroyed." / "to win this war, we must no longer be Jedi" / "Now the Republic needs us to sacrifice our consciences as well. ... Are Jedi traditions more important than the lives of billions?"
  - Loyalist systems still outnumber Separatist ten to one; the Republic has a titanic manufacturing base.
  - How Depa and Kar drove the CIS off Haruun Kal: the CIS used Pelek Baw spaceport to refit droid starfighters picketing the Al'Har system; Depa terrorized its civilian employees with "an unending series of gruesome killings. One or two at a time." Battle droids are vulnerable to the metal-eating fungi. "The target is the enemy’s will to fight."
> "I wanted to show you what winning soldiers will look like. ... That is the Jedi of the future, Mace. Right there." (Depa pointing at Kar Vastor, SP ch. 13)

## Ch. 14: "Final Entry"
- The Lorshan Pass cave mouth and a cliffside meadow LZ for a "Republic Sienar Systems Jadthu-class lander". Six to ten gunship flights patrol below.
- **Mace's injury tally:** "stun-blast bruises through flame burns, cracked ribs, a concussion, and a human bite wound", plus stings, a rash, and a fungal infection. "The nonphysical injuries—to his confidence, his principles, his moral certainties … to his heart" can't be treated.
- **Journal frame lore:** the journal is "a personal log ... For the Temple Archives"; it is encrypted, "only the archive masters at the Temple have the code key", which protects him "from the temptation to go back and edit entries". The Archives hold "Twenty-five thousand years of records". Nick: "so some weird alien Jedi a thousand years from now can pull this out". Archivist bracket annotations ("[Male voice identified as NICK ROSTU, major (bvt), GAR]").
- **Extraction signal:** a coded tight beam bounced off a mountain to the HoloNet satellite, carrying "a Jedi priority override code that hijacks part of the local HoloNet capacity" to reach the *Halleck*.
- **Lorshan base:** "less a military base than an underground refugee camp"; it honeycombs the whole north wall of the pass. Vibro-axes cut stone "almost as easily as wood". It holds thousands of noncombatants; pneumonia and dysentery are rampant; the grassers are dying in the caverns ("the Fourth Pillar holds them in its grip").
- **Depa as totem:** a woman catches Depa's gaze "from the air" and presses it to her breast. "Depa is more than a Jedi to them. Not a goddess—Force-users themselves, they are not easily impressed by Jedi powers. She is, I think, a totem. ... She is their hope."
- **Nick on Separatist lore:** the Balawai joined the CIS because "the seppies promised to respect their sovereignty ... Planetary rights. And the only planetary right the Balawai care about is the right to kill us all." Separatist droid starfighters and support staff at the spaceport gave the militia "an unlimited supply of gunships"; Korunnai were banned from outside Pelek Baw city limits, and street kids and "troublemakers" were sent to a camp. "That’s where Depa found us." / "I’ll die a free man. That’s what Depa is to us."
- **Force-amplification doctrine (key quote):**
> "A Jedi’s connection to the Force amplifies everything about us ... It makes us more of whatever we already are. If we are calm, it gives us serenity. If we are angry, it fills us with the rage of a god. Anger is a trap. You might think of it as a narcotic, not unlike glitterstim." (Mace journal, SP ch. 14)
> "She is dying, Nick. The war is killing her. ... she is drowning in your anger" / "Just as it is too late for Kar Vastor to become a Jedi, it is too late for Depa to become a lor pelek." (SP ch. 14)
- Depa retrieved Mace's lightsaber from Kar as part of her plan; "I can feel Depa, the Depa I know, only in her weapon." The entry ends with Nick: "That’s far enough. Stop! I mean it." / "[The final sound on Master Windu’s Haruun Kal journal is a nonverbal vocalization similar to a large predator’s warning growl.]" (So Kar arrives, carrying the sabers, instead of Depa.)

## Ch. 15: "The Trap" (end of Part Two)
- Mace: "I’ve seen him block blaster bolts. He can do the same with bullets." (Kar intercepts slugs too; confirmed when "bullets clanged off Vastor’s flashing shields".)
- Kar brings both lightsabers: Depa's is given up as "the weapon of a Jedi" she no longer has use for. Mace: "Depa Billaba was another casualty of his failure at Geonosis. / She was just taking longer to die." All he lost was "a fantasy that someday the galaxy would be again at peace."
- **Kar:** "The jungle isn’t killing her. / You are. ... She measures herself by your standards; that your standards are fatally wrong doesn’t make her failure to live up to them any less painful. ... You are her sire, Mace Windu. Do you not understand how much she loves you?"
- **Kar's hostage counter-play:** a hidden man with a captured shoulder-fired **proton torpedo launcher** will destroy the *Halleck*'s lander unless Mace leaves without Depa. "You are not the only one here who can take hostages." Kar: "if I want you dead, I can kill you myself." / Mace: "That ... remains to be seen."
- Mace escalates legally: Depa broke her parole; "Kar Vastor ... you are charged with the murder of Terrel Nakay"; firing on the lander means treason with Depa implicated, "she will be executed along with you." Kar: "Depa is right: Jedi are insane." Mace: "you don’t say this because it’s true ... You say it because you hope it’s true."
- "Goodbye, Mace, Jedi of the Windu." Kar sends a Force-signal to a sniper 1 km below.
- **Shatterpoint precog feat (major):** in a single instant Mace "kenned" Kar's posture, Nick's draw, and the sniper's bolt "Centered on Mace Windu’s heart"; "the shatterpoint he found and struck by instinct was Vastor’s balance at the lip of the cliff." He shoves Kar off the cliff; Nick's bullet only grazes Kar's temple; Mace Force-snatches both sabers out of Kar's grip, ignites them, and has them in hand "with an easy six or seven milliseconds to spare" to deflect the sniper bolt.
- Kar catches the rock face 1 m down and leaps back up behind his shields.
- **Shatterpoint of the Summertime War (major Force-vision feat):**
> "The purple flame of his blade splintered flaws throughout the planet. Stress fractures spidered from his blade to Vastor, to Nick, into the mountain behind, into the pass below, and to space above, racing in outrippling waves that joined him with what was, but also with what had been, and what would be." (SP ch. 15)
  - Retrocognition: Geptun's Ministry of Justice technicians tested his lightsaber (cutting thyssel, lammas, duracrete, transparisteel, shooting it with blasters and bullets) "So that they could measure and record the emission signature of this blade. / So that their satellites would recognize it". That's why it was drained. The gunships withdrew at the notch pass because they had been ordered to: "if he was killed before he reached Depa ... it wouldn’t solve the militia’s Jedi problem." Geptun wanted him to lead them to the hidden Korunnai.
  - Real-time remote sensing: through one fracture he feels a satellite moving at almost 28,000 km/h, "a silicon brain make an electronic connection", and the release of DOKAW durasteel bars.
- **"Stop!"** Force blasts club Nick to the ground and send Kar spinning against the cliff face. "But not each other. Look around you!" Dozens of gunships are converging; "At least five" DOKAWs are inbound "At thirty thousand kilometers per hour, and accelerating." Kar: "This is YOUR fault!" Mace: "Run."

## Ch. 16: "Shockwaves"
- **DOKAW specs (lore):** a De-Orbiting Kinetic Anti-emplacement Weapon of "hardened durasteel spear, ablative shielding, miniature ion drive, and tiny attitude thrusters"; just over 200 kg at launch and about 100 kg at impact, "traveling at well over ten kilometers per second". Cheap backworld weaponry; vulnerable to turbolasers; inaccurate "against stationary targets smaller than a medium-sized town". "In a word: WHAM."
- Mace absorbs the roof impact with bent legs and catches Nick in a Force-hold "a meter short of severe head trauma". He finds the shatterpoint of a stone slab pinning Kar and cracks it in two with one blade stroke. Kar: "When you’re around, big hard things seem to fall on my head." / "You could have killed me. You should have." / "You’re no good to me dead."
- Mace orders the sick and wounded sealed in the heavy-weapon lockup, anticipating gas. The *Halleck* is "a medium cruiser in-system that’s carrying a regiment of the finest soldiers this galaxy has ever seen"; "those twenty landers can evacuate this entire place within hours" (here a regiment; ch. 11 said a battalion).
- **DOKAW shock:** the commtechs are killed with no marks, their skulls "squishy" (concussion transmitted through rock). Mace Force-suspends himself and Nick in the air to ride out the next shock, then Force-floats the wrecked subspace unit to him and Force-unscrews its panel. **He powers the comm unit with his lightsaber's power cell** (the grip has a "secret interior latch").
- *Halleck* comm: codes "oh six one five" / "one nine" / "seven seven". Captain Trent and the bridge crew are wounded; acting captain Commander Urhal reports "heavy DSF attack". Trade Federation droid starfighters had lain dormant "outside the system’s ecliptic plane amid cometary dust", apparently triggered by the lander; the lander was lost with all hands, and six escort starfighters were overwhelmed.
- **Cascade-of-shatterpoints perception (major Force-sense description):**
> "This sense invaded the Force, touching power and letting the power touch it ... feeding upon the Force and feeding the Force in a regenerative spiral ... it was as though this single moment existed in a crystal shell, and if he could strike it in exactly the right way ... a single stroke whose shockwaves would propagate outward to crash through the trap that held not only him and Nick, but Depa and Kar and the Korunnai, the world of Haruun Kal, the Republic, perhaps the galaxy itself: more than a chain of shatterpoints, it was a fountain of shatterpoints. A cascade. / An avalanche." (SP ch. 16)
- Nick's "Stupid, stupid, stupid" triggers the insight: "Some years ago, the Jedi Order contemplated using droid starfighters for antipirate work ... we decided against it ... Because droids are stupid." Orders: send all remaining landers to the original coordinates, then the *Halleck* jumps to Republic space (stranding Mace).
- Mace asks for the second-best shooter: Chalk ("Especially with the heavy stuff"). "We’re not going to evacuate. We’re going to attack. ... We are going to beat them like a rented gong."

## Ch. 17: "Seeker"
- **Weapon inventory Mace selects:** "A tripod-mounted EWHB-10 with an auxiliary fusion-generator pack. Two shoulder-fired torpedo launchers, with four preloaded launch tubes apiece. A rack of twenty-five proton grenades". The lockup holds only "people who’ll live"; the rest go to "the dead room" (tiered ledges of the dying; flesh-eating fungi).
- Besh is dying of fungus growing in Terrel's knife wounds. **Kar's Force healing:** "searching within Besh’s chest for the essential aura of the fungus that was killing him, focusing power upon it to burn it out spore by spore." Chalk: "Did trust you, me. So did Besh."
- Mace recruits Kar: "Does it involve killing Balawai?" / "Probably not more than a thousand. Maybe two."
- The grasser cavern stampede: Kar, the Akk Guards and twelve akks impose order in a flying wedge, slaughtering the badly injured. "It was wasteful. It was brutal. / It was necessary." Five pairs of Korunnai take five proton grenades each down the five grazing passages. The ankkox's tail mace pounds its pen.

Together: "As Without, So Within." Depa's Greater Mark has been carved off and the wound still festers. "But for Depa, the universe was gone. All that remained was the Seeker. / Alone in the void."
- Depa: "I’m so lost, Mace." / "How does a blind man know the stars have gone out?" / "I’m not a Jedi anymore. I quit. I resign." Mace: "I do understand it. I don’t accept it." "Get up!" — "Reflexes burned into her by more than a decade of unquestioning obedience yanked her instinctively to her feet."
- **Trap reveal:** "nearly a thousand clone soldiers of the Republic will reach this position." "This entire system is a trap. It’s been a trap all along. The Separatist pullback was bait". The CIS has "caught a medium cruiser. Not to mention two members of the Jedi Council." The droid starfighters are "faster, more maneuverable, and better-armed than the landers."
- Mace floats her lightsaber between them: "You can make a choice."
- **Korunnai = Jedi descendants; the lesson of Haruun Kal (key lore):**

That’s what the Summertime War is about. The Balawai want to use the jungle ... The Korunnai want to beat it into submission." / "Why do Korunnai fight the jungle?" / "Because they are descended from Jedi …" / "But … you can’t fight the way things are …" / "But we do. Every day. That’s what Jedi are." / "We don’t have to win. We only have to fight." (SP ch. 17)
