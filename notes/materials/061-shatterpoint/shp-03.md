## Part Two title page: "Victory Conditions"

## Ch. 9: "Instinct"
- **Mace's conditions of victory (thesis):**
> "I didn’t come here to stop Depa. I came here to save her. / I will save her. / And may the Force have mercy on any who would try to stop me, for I will have none." (Mace journal, SP ch. 9)
> "I don’t care who wins. Because no one wins. Not in real war. It is only a question of how much each side is willing to lose." (SP ch. 9)
- **The Depa reunion was a Force-vision (pelekotan).** Mace remembers fighting the gunships back to back with her ("Up or down?") or walking calmly through blasterfire with her. Kar: "You fought alone." His Akk Guards watched Mace alone drive off a six-gunship double flight "single-handed". Yet Depa's lightsaber is gone from his vest, and Mace finds it on his right side next to his own (ambiguous).
  - Kar: "Nick Rostu spoke much of your victory at the pass. **Even I might not be equal to such a feat.**" (Kar on the three-gunship fight: a direct comparison.)
- Kar's doctrine: "Pelekotan brings us waking dreams of our desires and our fears. When we desire what we fear and fear what we desire, pelekotan always answers." / "Only pelekotan is real. ... We are pelekotan’s dream." Kar: "Why should pelekotan show me anything? I have no fears."
- **Kar's powers (Mace's catalogue):** drew the fever wasps out of Besh and Chalk; "the jungle itself seems to part for his passage and tangle itself behind"; trained six **Akk Guards** ("lesser echoes of himself") and *designed and built* the vibroshields; telepathic growl-speech. Mace: "he and I are natural enemies." His jungle movement is "half perception; the other half was raw power ... Power flowed into him and out again, but I did not feel him use it any more than I feel my muscles use the sugars". A grasser trembles near him, held "by an invisible force that overpowered its instincts".
- **ULF reality:** "They were barely alive." The raids are for medpacs and supplies "that our Republic cannot or will not provide". The Korun people are packed into a mountain base; "dysentery and pneumonia had killed more Korunnai than had the militia’s gunships". Gunships now fly night raids with random fire to deny sleep.
- Fauna: half a dozen vine cat species, two smaller akk-dog variants plus "the giant savage akk wolves", and jacunas (flightless pack scavenger birds).
- Prisoners: 28 (24 jups and 4 kids), slated for *tan pel'trokal* at sunset: "The night belongs to us."
- Kar on Nick: "He is weak. Cowardly. Afraid of sacrifice." / "They are expendable." Mace: "No one is." / "it makes him a good man ... Better than you." / Kar: "Better at what?"
- **Mace's read on Kar:** "His is the darkness of the jungle, not the darkness of the Sith. He does not live for power ... He is less a man than he is an avatar of the jungle itself. ... He has a savage purity that I might envy, were I not a Jedi". "Black is the presence of every color."
- **Depa on Geonosis (flash-forward, from her howdah on an ankkox):**
> "You were a great peace officer, Mace, but you’re a terrible general. That’s what cost so many lives at Geonosis: we went in like judicials." / "A general ... would have dropped a baradium bomb on that arena." / "Win at the cost of two Jedi, one Senator, and a few thousand of the enemy." / "Instead, a hundred and more Jedi died, and you have a galaxy at war." (Depa, SP ch. 9)
  - Note: Geonosis Jedi deaths given as "a hundred and more". Depa "no longer walks"; she rides in a curtained howdah.
- Mace: "There is no peace. What we thought was the Great Peace of the Republic was only a dream ... In the Galaxy of War, no one sleeps that well."
- **Mace vs Kar, will-contest #2:** Mace holds the grasser reins and they "might as well have been bolted to a cliff". "Dark power clutched at Mace’s will. You will come with me." Mace takes two steps, then stops and reverses it: "I no longer enjoy your company. ... Go now. Do not return to me without Depa." Kar backs down ("I do not wish to fight you ... Depa will be angry"). "I am a Jedi Master, and a Senior Member of the Jedi Council ... a general of the Grand Army of the Republic. I am not to be sent for."
- Kar's speech is heard by each listener in their own idiom (Nick hears "a Pelek Baw curbmonkey").
- Mace orders Nick to lead the prisoners to the steamcrawler track for militia pickup ("I will not be made a liar"): "Then I will beat you into unconsciousness ... and ask someone else."
> "Kar Vastor ... is not the most dangerous man on the Korunnal Highland." / "You only say that because you don’t really know him." / "I say that ... because he doesn’t know me." (SP ch. 9)

## Ch. 10: "A Jedi's Word"
- *Tan pel'trokal* was Depa's policy: "Wasn’t too long ago Kar used to just kill them all." Nick: "You’ve gone bats ... You’re completely insane." Mace: "No. Just the opposite, in fact." (He echoes Depa's "gone sane".)
- A Balawai prisoner calls Mace "the real Jedi" (as opposed to Depa). Mace: "Jedi are not to be knelt to. We are not your masters, but your servants." / "I don’t care about jungle justice. I care about Jedi justice."
- **Mace vs an Akk Guard, unarmed:** "This is the only weapon I need" (taps forehead), then a double head-butt that breaks the guard's nose and puts him down. He throws his own lightsaber at the second guard's feet and grabs his face: "YOU WANT TO DIE? ... DO IT AND DIE!" (calculated rage). He stares down two akks until they tuck tail. "You still haven’t [seen me angry]" / **"When the big dog’s walking ... little dogs step aside."**
- Then he co-opts the guards: he pops the broken nose straight ("It’s no dishonor to lose to a Jedi"); Mace reads Kar's men as trained "like dogs".
- **Nick sworn in as a major** of the Grand Army of the Republic (brevet), with Mace's improvised oath: "...to defend its citizens, resist its enemies, and champion its justice ... by witness of, aid from, and faith in the Force". Nick feels the new Force-bond: "it has a resonance in the Force not unlike the bond of an akk to its human." "So I’m your dog, now?"
- Tactics: Mace fed Kar the steamcrawler-track destination so Kar would try to trap everyone in one place rather than run them down in the jungle; then he cuts a landing zone with lightsabers (gives Nick Depa's saber) and kneels the 28 prisoners in the open; three gunship flights pick them up. "The lie is in what he assumed I meant, not in what I said." / "I am not willing to purchase life with death, unless that death is my own."
- **Message to Geptun:** "Tell him the Jedi Master has handled his Jedi problem ... if he disarms his irregulars and withdraws the militia from the highland, this war is over. ... in less than a week I’ve solved a problem he couldn’t manage in four months. ... if he does not do as I suggest, he’ll be the problem. And I will solve him."
- ULF base: the **Lorshan Pass caverns**, with subspace comm. Mace: "Depa and I will be leaving this planet." "I have a hostage" — "There is one hostage a Jedi may lawfully take" (himself, implied).
- **Kar's Force web:** six Akk Guards and twelve akks, "All eighteen of them ... Force-bonded not with each other, but each with one single other, as though they were spokes on a wheel of which he was the hub. / The anger Mace felt was Kar’s."
- Mace snaps Nick's nose and takes the lightsaber back before Nick can react. "Vastor is a predator, not a HoloNet villain ... If he planned to kill us, we’d already be dead." An ankkox approaches carrying Depa.

## Ch. 11: "Hostage"
- **Ankkox:** Haruun Kal's largest land animal, "twice the size of grassers—more than half again the mass of a full-grown bantha"; dorsal shell nearly 3 m wide and over 4 m long; seals head and legs in against volcanic gas; armored tail mace snaps "faster than the human eye could see", hitting targets up to 8 m away hard enough "to stun an akk dog or shatter a small tree". A juvenile ankkox's tail mace was the traditional Korun herder weapon. Depa rides a looted lammas-wood howdah on its shell; Kar drives it with a hook goad.
- "In the hush of fading day, the Force showed Mace a shatterpoint. / The darkness of the jungle, not of the Sith. / Life without the restraints of civilization." (Kar's shatterpoint is his animal nature.) Mace's plan: provoke a dominance "dogfight" ("Think of Vastor as a wild akk").
- **Reflex block:** Kar's palm slap at Nick "would have taken his head right off before he could even blink—but that massive arm was intercepted by the heel of Mace’s open hand." Mace then backhands Nick himself to keep the pecking order (with a Force-wink through their new bond). "I should have hit you." Kar: "Don’t touch me again."
- Mace "still couldn’t feel her" (Depa) even a meter away; her presence "blended invisibly into the jungle night".
- **Depa in person:** tattered Jedi robes over Korun homespun; a rag over the Greater Mark (or its scar); "The Lesser Mark still glinted gold on the bridge of her nose"; her hand feels hollow-boned "but her grip was strong and warm". She yanks Mace down "with astonishing strength". Eyes "event horizons: infinitely deep, and infinitely dark".
  - "We all have to die, Mace." / "This is home. ... That’s what I brought you here to learn" / "you can’t stop being a Jedi ... you had to play HoloNet hero."
  - **"I am not in command here."** "You are alive right now because Kar doesn’t want to upset me." "Kar likes me."
  - **"There is no ULF!** ... I made it up! ... There is no pattern. No strategy. There is no ULF. There is just this clan, and that family ... Ragged Korun bandits and murderers." (Depa, SP ch. 11)
- Mace's ship: "a ship onstation with a battalion of troopers" (the *Halleck*).
- **Mace arrests Depa:** "you are hereby relieved of command of Republic forces on Haruun Kal ... suspended from the Jedi Council ... you are under arrest." The hostage is himself: "You can come with me, or you can watch me die. Your choice." Depa: "Who but Mace Windu would think to take himself hostage?" Mace: "I was the only one available."
  - Depa: "How many times are you going to make me save your life?" / "I found your shatterpoint; do you think I’d miss his?" / "I think he doesn’t have one."
- Taunting Kar into a duel: "Do you plan to hide behind her forever?" / "He’d quiet down if you got him fixed." Mace throws his vest and **lightsaber** to Nick: **"That’s how much you scare me."** Kar unbuckles the vibroshields. Kar's rage aura goes "from red to black". Kar's restraint is like "an invisible leash" (Depa); "Depa prefers that you live. But she doesn’t mind if you get hurt."
- "Rules?" / "Jungle rules." Kar launches "as a human missile"; Mace leaps to meet him in midair. Nick takes bets: "A hundred credits says the Jedi makes Kar cry like a baby!"

## Ch. 12: "Jungle Rules" (Mace vs Kar Vastor, unarmed: the book's central power-scaling fight)
- **Opening clash:** "a collision ... of two node-channels of the Force: invisible energy crackled, and vivid blue gap-sparks arced from leaf to leaf in the canopy above. For a moment, they hung in the air, supported by power, grappling". Kar bear-hugs Mace and **bites into his neck going for the carotid**. Mace breaks it with knees and stiffened fingers jammed into the collarbone notch and windpipe, then kicks Kar's chin. Kar: "Not many men can break my grip. You won’t do it twice."
- Mace hasn't slept since before the notch-pass fight.
- **Explicit power comparison:**
> "He knew already he could not match Vastor for raw power. With each breath, he stripped away another layer of restraint ... He had to move his inner peace out of the way to let in the joy. The thrill. ... Because Vaapad was more than just a form of lightsaber combat. / It was a state of mind." (SP ch. 12)
> "The ultimate answer for power is skill." (SP ch. 12)
- **Unarmed Vaapad:** a fist into the solar plexus "driven upward ... by the combined power of the Force and nearly fifty years of Jedi combat training. Mace’s hand sank in to the wrist"; a Force-throw into an akk.
> "Standing toe to toe, the top of Mace’s head barely came to the level of Vastor’s chin ... And even hurt, lurching drunkenly, Vastor still could whip his arms in blindingly fast raking slaps ... / But where Vastor’s speed was blinding, Mace’s was invisible. / Not one of those slaps connected." (SP ch. 12)
  - Mace lands six strikes "Before Vastor could even focus his eyes". "An ordinary man would have been unconscious. Vastor seemed to be getting stronger." He nerve-punches both biceps so Kar's arms drop limp: **"This is called Vaapad, Kar. ... How many arms do you see?"**
- **Kar's telekinesis:** he hurls Mace into a meter-thick lammas tree, then into an akk's skull (crown spines gash his abdomen). Mace grounds the Force grip ("Jedi Padawans learn to counter Force kinesis before they even begin lightsaber training"); **Kar animates the jungle**: a gripleaf trailer (fibers "nearly as strong as durasteel cable") seizes Mace's ankle and a brassvine curves toward his neck. Mace refuses to call his lightsaber ("that would be admitting defeat"), yanks an Akk Guard up and uses the guard's vibroshield edge to cut the vine.
- **Using the opponent's Force grip:** Mace adds his own power to Kar's pull, doubling his speed, and spears Kar's gut headfirst "as though he’d been hit by a concussion missile". He garrotes Kar with the dead gripleaf; "not even he was strong enough to break a gripleaf trailer with his bare hands", but "Pure will powered Vastor to his feet ... He didn’t even seem to notice the weight of a large Jedi Master hanging down his back." Kar throws Mace over his head and slams him down. Mace's last Force-driven double-fist punch flips Kar over backward. Kar gets up smiling: "Is that the best you have?" One slap and a choke-hold KO: "How many arms do you see?"
- **Mace submits** on purpose: "You’re the big dog." / "I was wrong to take your prisoners." He asks for his saber back humbly; Kar gives it to an Akk Guard to destroy until Depa intervenes ("To send him out into the jungle without his weapon would be murder"). Kar keeps the saber in his waistband.
- **Mace's injuries:** "two cracked ribs, a severe ankle sprain from the gripleaf trailer, a moderate concussion, and some internal bleeding, not to mention the bite wound on my neck".
- Depa: "Jedi do not always tell the truth." / "You still take a beating as well as any man in the galaxy." Nick believes the loss was a dive; Depa: half wrong. "The part about letting Kar win."
- **Mace's own power ranking of Kar vs himself (major quote):**
> "What I did was pick a fight I knew I couldn’t win." / "Vastor is … very powerful. Half my age and twice my size. Training and experience can compensate only up to a point. And he is naturally ferocious in a way that no Jedi can duplicate." / "I didn’t have to win. All I had to do was fight." (Mace, SP ch. 12)
> "If he and I had gone at it blade to shield, I would be dead now—even if I’d killed him, the guards and the dogs would have torn me to shreds—and Depa, too, if she’d tried to save me; we’d only barely survived being attacked by three akks in the Circus Horrificus." (Mace journal, SP ch. 12)
  - Context: Mace frames the armed loss as Kar *plus* six guards and twelve akks, not Kar alone.
- **The lightsaber as tracker:** "Even with the Force, I can’t pick Kar out from the jungle around us. ... My lightsaber, on the other hand— ... It is a bell collar that Depa managed to buckle onto a singularly ferocious vine cat."
- **Untrained raw power vs Jedi (major lore quote):**
> "It is possible for an untrained Force-user to wield as much power as the greatest of Jedi—look at Kar. But untrained, the instincts he falls back on are those granted him by nature. It is another of the central paradoxes of the Jedi: the ‘instincts’ we use are not instinctive at all. They are the product of training so intense that they replace our natural ones." (Mace, SP ch. 12)
  - "The oldest child ever accepted for training was nine—and there was much debate over that. A debate that has continued ... for more than ten years." (Anakin reference.)
  - "Because peace is an unnatural state. ... Jedi do not fight for peace. That’s only a slogan ... Jedi fight for civilization, because only civilization creates peace." / "A single Jedi who succumbs to his natural drives ... could do damage that is literally unimaginable." Depa: "are we still talking about Kar? Or is this about Dooku?" Mace: "Our only hope, against beings whose instincts control them, is to absolutely and utterly control our own."
