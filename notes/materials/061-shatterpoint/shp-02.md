## Ch. 4: "The Summertime War"
- **Korun Force stealth:** in the jungle Mace "could no longer easily feel any of the Korunnai in the Force. His gaze had a tendency to slip aside from them". They communicate by "Force-pulse"; Chalk Force-calls the grassers.
- Grassers: stand ~6 m "at full stretch", three eyes, six limbs, walk on hind and mid limbs; these are thin and dull-coated (herds scattered by the war).
- **Akk dogs (stat block):** "Triangular fangs ... jaws that could crush durasteel ... scaled hide thick enough to stop a lightsaber. Splay-toed feet with shovel-sized claws ... Muscular armored tails as long as their landspeeder-sized bodies".
- **Mace's Force-call to akks:** he lays his whole nature bare ("Jedi serenity balancing buried temper, devotion to peace tipping the scales against a guilty pleasure in fighting") and three wild akks answer; "A touch of the Force and a turn of both palms, and the three dogs rolled onto their backs".
- **Nar Shaddaa flashback (Circus Horrificus, Gargonn the Hutt):** Mace hunting smugglers who sold attack-trained akks to the Red Iaro terrorists of Lannik; Depa followed. They fought "mutated giant akks":
> "On that day in Nar Shaddaa, she showed me blade work that surpassed my own; she had continued to grow and study and progress in Vaapad as well as the Force." (Mace journal, SP ch. 4)
> "we had again been Mace and Depa, Master and Padawan, pitting the lethal efficiency of Vaapad against the worst the galaxy could throw at us. ... As Jedi Knights, we were unbeatable." (SP ch. 4)
- **DOKAW** (De-Orbiting Kinetic Anti-emplacement Weapon): orbital "missile-sized rods of solid durasteel" kicked down onto fixed coordinates, "a meteorite strike on demand"; why Korunnai have no campfires.
- Raw thyssel bark "potent enough to cause permanent changes in brain chemistry" (Lesh chews it).
- Squad backstories: Besh's fingers cut off and tongue cut out by Balawai prospectors (myth of a lammas "treasure grove"); communicates by an "extraordinarily expressive Force projection". Chalk's ghôsh gave her militia rapists **tan pel’trokal** ("jungle justice": stripped naked 100 km into the jungle). Chalk: "Now I fight so other girls don’t have to fight."
- **Pelekotan** = Koruun word for the Force, "world-power", literally "jungle-mind". Chalk: "You don’t use pelekotan. ... Pelekotan uses you."
- Militia gunships: customized "ancient Sienar Turbostorms" (blastboats for atmospheric close assault, cannons and missiles, a platoon of heavy infantry, travel in threes, each carries its own surgical field generator). Gunners shoot grasser herds "Just for fun."
- **Summertime War:** "Your war began a few months ago. Ours has been going since before I was born." Korun guerrillas vs Balawai militia "for almost thirty years"; grassers eat thyssel bark/portaak; Korun use Force comms "hardly more sophisticated than smoke signals" because of comm-detector satellites. It became a yearly tradition ("late in the spring ... A month or so before autumn brought the rains again, everyone would go home").
> "Some of Depa’s dazzling success was now explained ... she didn’t have to create a guerrilla army. She’d found one ready-made. / Blooded and hungry." (SP ch. 4)
- Nick: "This is our war, Master Windu. ... You guys are just passing through." / "You keep score by body count." / "You have to let it be fun. You have to find the part of yourself that likes it." Mace: "It’s called the dark side."
- Journal: "For the Korunnai, there is no ‘after the war.’" Death hollows (pooled volcanic gas). Nick's jungle lesson: "The jungle doesn’t promise. It exists. ... Fantasizing about it—pretending it’s something it’s not—is fatal." Mace: "I have a feeling that his lesson applies equally well to this war."
- Seven hanging corpses (unknown side): "This place stank of the dark side." Mace felt it "from hundreds of meters away". Nick: "If they’re Balawai ... this is already better than they deserve."
- Bark-drunk Lesh in Mace's tent resists a Force-pushed order; first mention of **Kar Vastor** ("Teach you, will Kar ... Teach you, will the akks"). Mace's precognition: "By nightfall, Lesh would be dead."
- Mace uses the Force to stand on a moving grasser's saddle. Gunships "travel in threes"; one deploys flamer nozzles that turn the pass into "an inferno" in "just over a second".

## Ch. 5: "Blood Fever" (the gunship fight: top feat chapter)
- Opening: Mace "jabbed the grasser in the nerve plexus" so it leaps 5 m between flame streams; snuffs burning fur "within a bubble of vacuum" by Force-pushing air away; uses the Force "to nail himself to the saddle"; Force-picks the right hypo (gas binder) blind.
- **Shatterpoint in combat:** "The fluid situation in the notch pass gelled, then splintered into crystal: grassers and akks and people and gunships became nodes of stress, vectors of intersecting energy joined by flaws and fault lines." "He saw one bare chance."
- Force-yanks Nick out of the saddle "an instant before a missile took their grasser full in the chest", lands on his feet and Force-catches Nick.
- Gunship loadout recited by Mace: "Two Taim and Bak dual KX-Four ball turrets, port and starboard ... Twin fixed-position Krupx MG-Three mini missile tubes fore and aft, a belly-mounted Merr-Sonn Sunfire One Thousand flame projector—" / "What do we have that can punch through that armor?" / "Nothing." / "So what exactly do you think you’re gonna do?" / Mace said, "Win."
- **Gunship 1:** Force-steers a Nytinite grenade into the starboard turbojet intake; the engine shreds. "Any questions?"
- "The Force launched him over the rocks like a torpedo ... blasting through flames too fast to get burned".
- **Akk-bond hijack:** "Galthra’s bond with Chalk was deep and strong, but it was a function of the Force, and Mace was a Jedi Master. Until he released the akk, Galthra’s bond would be with him." He rides Galthra ("three-quarters of a metric ton of armored predator").
- **Missile redirect:** in "the shaved semisecond after launch", Galthra leaps; "one little nudge in the Force—hardly more than an affectionate chuck under the chin—tipped its diamond-shaped warhead up a centimeter or two", so the missile hits the *lead gunship* "right up its nose". Concussion missiles pull "multiple dozens of standard gravities".
- Fires Chalk's Thunderbolt from the hip until it overheats; dodges two missiles because "he could feel their point of aim before they squeezed the triggers".
- **Vaapad on-page debut, dual-wield vs laser cannons:**
> "He stood motionless, empty-handed, waiting for them to open fire. / Waiting to give them a brief tutorial on the art of Vaapad." (SP ch. 5)
> "It was no longer Mace Windu who acted: the Force acted through him. Depa’s lightsaber snapped into his left hand while his own flipped into his right." (SP ch. 5)
> "On Sarapin, a Vaapad was a notoriously dangerous predator ... It attacked with its blindingly fast tentacles. Most had at least seven. It was not uncommon for them to have as many as twelve. The largest ever killed had twenty-one. The thing about a Vaapad was that you never knew how many tentacles it had until it was dead: they moved too fast to count. Almost too fast to see. / So did Mace’s." (SP ch. 5)
> "Ten bolts reached his blades. Two apiece went back at the damaged ships, bursting against their armor and knocking them reeling to break their target lock. The other six hammered the cockpit of the third gunship, blasting a gaping hole in its transparisteel viewport." (SP ch. 5)
  - Then Force-guides a glop grenade through the hole; gunship 3 crashes into the ridge. Two damaged gunships flee. Net: **one man vs three armored gunships: one destroyed, two crippled.** Mace takes only "superficial burns".
- Nick calls him a "walking one-man war machine" ("I’m not sure the word he used was walking"). Mace quoting Yoda: "‘My ally is the Force.’ ... I had them outnumbered."
- **Blood fever / Vaapad's danger (key doctrine quote):**
> "It had been unsatisfying." (SP ch. 5)
> "It is a side effect of the Force immersion of Vaapad. My style grants great power, but at a terrible risk. Blood fever is a disease that can kill anyone it touches. To use Vaapad, you must allow yourself to enjoy the fight. You give yourself to the thrill of battle. The rush of winning. This is why so few students even attempt the style. / Vaapad is a path that leads through the penumbra of the dark side." (Mace journal, SP ch. 5)
> "Here in the jungle, that shadow fringe is unexpectedly shallow. Full night is only a step away." (SP ch. 5)
  - He nearly punches Nick: "I might have just wanted to feel them burn."
- **Fever wasps:** Lesh is in convulsions; larvae eat the brain and hatch "Through his skull. Like an, an, an eggshell"; thyssel chewers go faster. Chalk and Besh are also infected; Nick and Mace are clean. Head shots scatter larvae, so brain and spine must be destroyed. **Mace does it himself with both blades** ("Yes, I do ... Or you'll have to."). Chalk then mercy-shoots her burned grasser.
- Mace's datapad's subspace coil is wrecked: no link to the *Halleck*; "only the two lightsabers remained" of his outside life.
- **Lor pelek** = "jungle master. Shaman. Witch doctor. Wizard ... In some stories, a lor pelek was not a being at all, but was rather pelekotan incarnate: the avatar of the jungle-mind." Nick: Kar Vastor is "very powerful. More than powerful" and might save Besh and Chalk.
- Nick's plan: take a Balawai outpost's bunker and comm. "Out here, civilians are a myth."

## Ch. 6: "Civilians"
- Journal in the bunker: "I dare not meditate. The dark is too deep here. It has a tidal pull: a black hole that I’ve taken up too tight an orbit around, and it’s tearing me in half." Besh and Chalk in thanatizine suspension (heart under 1 beat/min). Mace tries "to learn to feel the action of the metal-eating fungi in the Force".
- Nick: no civilians in the jungle; jups (jungle prospectors) act as militia irregulars, "vastly more dangerous than the gunships ... and armies of the Separatists put together" and "more ruthless than the ULF". "We don’t murder kids. We’re the good guys."
- **Reveal: the massacre in Palpatine's office was staged** by Depa and Kar Vastor as a lure. Nick helped dress the scene and tipped off Republic Intel. "Depa wanted me here." The Balawai corpses were jups; the rest were Korun prisoners they had tortured; "The children were Korunnai." Kar nailed the wafer in with brassvine thorns.
- Mace: "A Jedi might conceivably fall to the dark from the simple desire to smack the snot out of him [Nick]."
- **Jedi doctrine (key quote):**
> "This isn’t about good and evil ... Jedi are not moralists. That’s a common misperception. We are fundamentally pragmatic. The Jedi is altruistic less because to be so is good, than because to be so is safe: to use the Force for personal ends is dangerous. ... Power to do good eventually becomes just power. Naked force. An end in itself. It is a form of madness to which Jedi are peculiarly susceptible." (Mace, SP ch. 6)
- **Depa's condition (via Nick):** nightmares with screams; never seen eating, "wasting away"; untouchable headaches; "when she walks outside in daylight, she binds her eyes, for she cannot bear the light of the sun". Nick: "I’ve never met someone kinder or more caring than Master Billaba."
- The ULF had already wiped out this outpost: jups draped with necklaces of Korun ears; the Force signature ("a standing wave of power") marks it as Kar Vastor's work. Mace buries the dead anyway.
- Mace: "Luck ... does not exist. Luck is only a word we use to describe our blindness to the subtle currents of the Force." / "there are questions to which we can never have answers; we can only be answers." / "I’m a Jedi. I don’t have to explain reality. I just have to deal with it." / "This is not a discussion. It’s a briefing."
- Nick rides Galthra (persuaded through the Force) to fetch Kar. Mace still feels "nothing of her" (Depa) anywhere on the planet.
- **Raw-power self-assessment (scaling-relevant):**
> "Yoda would probably just reach out, lift the steamcrawler from the gully, and casually float it up the mountain ... Mace was much less confident in his own raw power. / But he had other talents." (SP ch. 6)
- **Volcano / landslide feat:** he Force-shoves "a structure of broken rock ten meters below him and five meters in from the face"; "hundreds of tons of dirt and rock poured into the river of lava", damming it and diverting the flow over the precipice. He rides the crest of the landslide, then holds the rubble with "a wide-focus Force grip" while the kids climb out. He also **Force-yanks the tilting steamcrawler back into place**, but that costs him his hold on the slide; he can only hold one at a time.
- Combat principle: "Use what you’re given. How you fight depends on whom you fight. His first opponent had been the volcano itself."
- Balawai children: Terrel (13, broken arm, fires a blaster at "kornos"), Keela (skull fracture), Pell (8), Urno and Nykl (6). Mace fakes a Korun accent to talk Terrel out.
- **Jango Fett reception:** the kids think Mace is a bounty hunter; a boy asks if he's Jango. Terrel: "Some stinkin’ Jedi snuck up behind him and stabbed him in the back" / "No stinkin’ Jedi could’ve took Jango Fett face to face! He was the best." Mace: "Fett was … killed in a fight." He doesn't say he was the one. "Now their heroes are bounty hunters."
- Dejarik "fork". Yoda maxim: "If no mistake have you made, yet losing you are … a different game you should play." Mace's new game: "Nobody Else Dies Today."

## Ch. 7: "Games in the Dark"
- Balawai irregulars (Pek Rankin's group, fleeing the ULF attack on Terrel's outpost) close in with night vision; Mace feels "the stark threat of their weapons’ points of aim". Dark side as "a black migraine". "I am the blade ... I will have to be; there is no other."
- Mace Force-triggers the Thunderbolt for a warning shot: "If I wanted you hurt, we wouldn’t be talking. You’d already be dead." The ULF attacks the column; a jup father opens fire; Mace deflects every bolt "away into the sky" and is recognized: "Jedi …". Terrel: "A stinkin’ Jedi! ... Kill him!"
- **Dual-blade Vaapad wall:** "Depa’s lightsaber went to his left hand, to mirror his own in his right, and together they wove a wall across the mouth of the bunker". Some badly aimed bolts still slip past; he walks *down* into the fire to draw it from the children. Wounds: "hot slashes of blaster grazes along his flanks ... a chunk that had been torn from one thigh by a glancing hit".
> "He was too deep in Vaapad to make a plan, too deep even to think, but he was a Jedi Master: he didn’t have to think. / He knew." (SP ch. 7)
- **Near-kill of Terrel:** a reflex slice cuts through Terrel's knife and the green blade stops "One centimeter above a brown-haired head" (hair crisps).
- **Steamcrawler shatterpoints / dark-side temptation:**
> "Four steamcrawlers divided by one Jedi equals one huge smoking pile of scrap. / The shatterpoints of the ’crawlers were obvious: neither the linked treads nor the traverse gears ... would stand against a single swipe of a lightsaber. In less than a second apiece, he could turn these armored behemoths into nothing more than hollow metal rocks—but he didn’t. / Because that wouldn’t hurt enough." (SP ch. 7)
> "They’d all die. Every single one of them. / It wouldn’t take him a full minute. / And he’d enjoy it." (SP ch. 7): a precognitive plan to kill every jup, which he aborts ("What am I doing?").
- "drawing upon the Force to sustain himself drew in rage as well." He realizes the rage "wasn’t all his": it answers "the howl of the akks. / To the power of this man."
- **Kar Vastor intro:** a dozen akks (immune to flamers and blaster stings) herd the Balawai into a "slaughter pen". Kar: shaven skull "more than two meters above his bare feet", chest "fused together from granite boulders", vine-cat-pelt pants. "In the Force, he burned with power." Forearm weapons: mirror-polished teardrop shields that whine when activated (vibro-edged).
- The outpost massacre days earlier was "the dark mirror image of a religious sanctification ... to prime the jungle for this dark rite."
- **Kar's "Akk Guards"**: six Korunnai "springing as high as Jedi but without Jedi grace"; their twin shields intercept every blaster bolt: "They could only have learned this from a Jedi. / From one particular Jedi. ... Oh, Depa, no …"

## Ch. 8: "Lor Pelek" (Mace vs Kar Vastor I; Depa's entrance)
- **Vibroshields:** teardrop forearm shields; "bit through blasters"; one cuts a man in half; blood "shivered to mist"; they shine "like a vibro-ax". Kar sprints "inhumanly fast" and cuts *up through a steamcrawler's undercarriage* (the same plan Mace had imagined).
- **Mace's armor-disk pile driver:** reversed grips, kneels and cuts a circle in the mid-deck armor; the disk falls "like a free-falling turbolift" and flattens Kar "like a fusion-powered pile driver". Mace Force-commands "No shooting" and blasters drop.
- **Kar's strength:** Mace back-flips "a quarter of a second" before Kar slams the armor disk up into the ceiling; Kar then slices it in half. Kar speaks in beast-growls whose meaning "unfurled ... inside Mace’s mind" (telepathic); calls Mace **dôshalo** ("clan brother"). "Massacres are necessary, dôshalo."
- Kar's Force-rush "tugged at Mace’s will"; Mace resists: "You’ll have to do better than that." He Force-lifts every blaster in the cabin and slices them all in one flourish.
- **Kar's Force-domination is useless against Mace's will and vice versa:** Mace "used it to hammer at the lor pelek’s will", and Kar ignores him.
- Terrel had stabbed the helpless, thanatizine-bound Besh and Chalk with his knife stub. **Kar kills Terrel** with a vibroshield before Mace can reach him.
- **Lightsaber vs vibroshield (key scaling detail):**
> "Mace used the Force to drive the blades; he would cut through the shields, through both of Vastor’s arms ... But the shields did not cut, and they did not give way. / Their singing whine hummed into Mace’s hands" (SP ch. 8)
> "Vastor lifted shields that still shone mirror bright; Mace’s lightsabers had not even scarred their surface." (SP ch. 8)
  - Kar has filed, vine-cat-sharp teeth. Kar: "We have gone to considerable trouble to bring you here, dôshalo. Must I kill you? ... It will hurt Depa to find you dead."
- **Mace's self-shatterpoint:** "Jedi do not revenge. Jedi do not punish. / Jedi defend." Kar reads him: "You hope I will give you an excuse to kill me. You fear to simply act." Mace realizes:
> "He’d been seeking justifiable homicide. / A perfectly Jedi murder." (SP ch. 8)
> "Without knowing it, he’d been seeking an enemy. Someone he could fight. Someone who would stand in for this war." (SP ch. 8)
- Kar on Terrel: "He was man enough to kill ... What he did was not war, but murder. ... in this jungle, have you seen a jail?" Mace: "Jedi do not fear. ... Hope, I left behind on Coruscant."
- A Korun calls Mace "Depa’s sire" (in Koruun the word means master or father). Kar punches out the Korun who tried to kill kneeling prisoners ("Vastor must have a devastating left hook").
- The akks part for Mace instinctively. Prisoners get *tan pel'trokal*. Kar: "I did" (he survived it himself).
- Nick: "When you save someone’s life ... It’s always temporary." Mace to Nick about the children: "Pretend they are your own."
- **Dark-side epistemology:**
> "Mace had read Temple Archive accounts written by Jedi who had brushed the dark and recovered. These accounts often mentioned how the dark side seemed to make everything clear; Mace knew now that this was only a delusion. A lie. / The truth was exactly opposite." (SP ch. 8)
- **Depa's entrance** (under six-gunship strafing): "It is in the darkest night that the light we are shines brightest" (a Mace maxim she quotes back). Her braids hacked short; face skeletal; a fresh burn scar from mouth to chin; the **Greater Mark of Illumination** (Chalactan adept's gold bead on the frontal bone, worn 20 years) hacked off, leaving keloid scar; a rag blindfold, yet "she stood as though she could see him all too well". "Hello, Mace ... You shouldn’t have come." (End of Part One.)
