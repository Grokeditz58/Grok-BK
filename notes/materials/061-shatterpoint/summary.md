# Shatterpoint: summary of strongest findings

Source: Matthew Stover, *Star Wars: Shatterpoint* (Del Rey 2003; ebook v3.1_r2). Hand-read in full on 2026-11-03: Lucas prologue, Introduction "Dangerously Sane", all 23 chapters, the Afterword "The Jedi's War", the bonus story "Equipment", dedication, author pages and the Del Rey back matter. Citations are "SP ch. N", "SP intro", "SP Afterword" and "SP Equipment".

1. **Shatterpoint is defined as a Mace-specific perception, and he has had it since childhood. It kills Jango.**
   > "Put simply: when I look at you through the Force, I can see where you break." (SP intro)
   > "I was six or seven standard years old ... before I realized that other students, full-grown Jedi Knights, even wise Masters, could sense such connections only with difficulty" (SP intro)
   > "Jango Fett ... the deadliest man in the galaxy. Jango can kill me in less than a second. ... The Force hinted a shatterpoint, and I left a headless corpse on the sand." (SP intro)
   > "Situations have shatterpoints, like gems. ... They are always a function of timing. / There is no such thing as a second chance." (SP intro)
   - It scales up to planet- and war-level: "The purple flame of his blade splintered flaws throughout the planet ... into the mountain behind, into the pass below, and to space above" (SP ch. 15). In the climax, Mace makes Depa's own killing stroke cut the jamming console, which ends the battle (SP ch. 23).
2. **Vaapad: Mace created it; Depa is his only student to master it; it is a state of mind that runs on the thrill of combat.**
   > "Few have studied. One student alone to mastery has risen." (Yoda, SP intro) / "I didn’t study Vaapad. ... I created it." / "[Sora Bulq] did not master Vaapad ... Vaapad mastered him." (Mace, SP intro)
   > "To use Vaapad, you must allow yourself to enjoy the fight. You give yourself to the thrill of battle." (Mace journal, SP ch. 5)
   > "Ten bolts reached his blades. Two apiece went back at the damaged ships ... The other six hammered the cockpit of the third gunship" (SP ch. 5, Mace dual-wielding with Depa's saber)
   > "Vaapad was more than just a form of lightsaber combat. / It was a state of mind." / "The ultimate answer for power is skill." (SP ch. 12)
3. **Depa at her peak is at least Mace's equal with the blade, and above him when boosted.**
   > "she showed me blade work that surpassed my own" (Mace journal, SP ch. 4)
   > "Depa’s mastery of Vaapad makes her virtually unbeatable in personal combat" (Mace journal, SP ch. 1)
   > "A quad laser bolt could make a hole. One did. / The next five went through that hole." (SP ch. 18; eyes closed) / "Twenty-four men. / In less than a minute." (SP ch. 19)
   > "she drove him backward as though he were a child. ... Still he did not strike back." (SP ch. 23)
   - Caveats for ch. 23: Mace refuses to counterattack and already has her blade through his gut; she is amplified by pelekotan backflow from the dying Akk Guards ("She was getting stronger"). Mace says he "reached his own shatterpoint. And he was breaking." Her fall ends in catatonia: "She is lost in infinite night." (SP Afterword)
4. **Kar Vastor has raw power on Yoda's or Anakin's scale with zero training. Mace beats him with skill, restraint and a shatterpoint, never toe-to-toe.**
   > "Vastor has power on the scale of Master Yoda, or young Anakin Skywalker." (Mace journal, SP ch. 13)
   > "where Vastor’s speed was blinding, Mace’s was invisible. / Not one of those slaps connected." (SP ch. 12)
   > "Vastor was younger, stronger, faster, and immensely more powerful ... Mace couldn’t win such a battle on his best day" (SP ch. 23)
   - The finish: Mace TKs a loose vibroshield into Kar's edge-to-edge guard. It "sheared through both Vastor’s shields, through both his wrists, and buried itself in the bone of his chest" (SP ch. 23). The superconducting vibroshields cannot be cut by a lightsaber ("Hold a blade against it long enough and the whole thing will melt, but it cannot be cut", SP ch. 13).
   - Kar is charged with "crimes against civilization", a charge unused since the Sith Wars, and held in the Temple "where his power cannot sway his jailers" (SP Afterword).
5. **Jedi doctrine, war and the dark side. This is the book's thesis.**
   > "A Jedi’s connection to the Force amplifies everything about us ... If we are angry, it fills us with the rage of a god." (SP ch. 14)
   > "Mace knew now that this was only a delusion. A lie. / The truth was exactly opposite." (on dark-side clarity, SP ch. 8)
   > "I don’t have to win ... All I have to do is fight." (SP ch. 12, 17, 23)
   > "Our enemy is power mistaken for justice. / Our enemy is the desperation that justifies atrocity. / The Jedi’s true enemy is the jungle." (SP Afterword)
   > "if I had won the Clone War with a baradium bomb on Geonosis—I would have lost the real war. ... Anakin Skywalker may be the shatterpoint of our war against the jungle." (SP Afterword)
6. **Mace's precognition and reaction-speed feats.**
   > "In even the best-trained human shooter there is at least a quarter-second delay between the decision to fire and the squeeze on the trigger. Deep in the Force, Mace could feel their decision even before it was made" (SP ch. 2)
   - He deflects a bolt "with an easy six or seven milliseconds to spare" (SP ch. 15). He remotely ignites his confiscated lightsaber in another man's hand (SP ch. 22). He survives a free-fall at about 300 km/h with a Force-slowed landing (SP ch. 19).
   - Self-assessed limits: "Yoda would probably just reach out, lift the steamcrawler from the gully ... Mace was much less confident in his own raw power." (SP ch. 6)

Other notes:
- **Korunnai lore:** Mace is a full-blooded Korun, and "all Korunnai can touch the Force". They are theorized to descend from shipwrecked Jedi. Akks have "scaled hide thick enough to stop a lightsaber" (SP ch. 4). Mace can hijack an akk's Force-bond: "Mace was a Jedi Master" (SP ch. 5). Nick learns Force-aiming ("Decide what you want to hit", SP ch. 19) and saves Depa with a pistol shot (SP ch. 23).
- **Separatist and Balawai lore:** the Summertime War is a thyssel-bark trade conflict that the Separatists exploited. The whole Al'har system was a trap: DSFs hid "for weeks, powered down, clamped to drifting asteroids" in the moon-belt (SP Equipment). "The Separatist pullback was bait" (SP ch. 17). Geptun signs a surrender ceding Haruun Kal and Al'har (SP ch. 22) and is later transferred to Republic Intelligence (SP Afterword).
- **Palpatine** probes how a Jedi falls: "such a thing could never happen to—say, for example—you ... Could it?" / "I didn’t tell him that it could. That it nearly had." (SP Afterword)
- **Back matter:** it adds two excerpts not in 050, *The Cestus Deception* (the JK-13 "Jedi Killer" vs Kit Fisto) and *TOR: Deceived* (Malgus), plus the bonus story "Equipment" (see shp-06).
