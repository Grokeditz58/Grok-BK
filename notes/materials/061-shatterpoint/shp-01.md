## Introduction: "Dangerously Sane"
### Mace's journal: Geonosis regret and the definition of shatterpoint
> "Jango Fett bristles with weapons. An instinctive killer: the deadliest man in the galaxy. Jango can kill me in less than a second. I know it. ... I can feel the violence Jango radiates: in the Force, a pulsar of death." (SP intro)
> "In my dreams, the purple flare of my blade sizzles the gray hairs of Dooku’s beard, and in the critical semisecond it takes Jango Fett to aim and fire, I twitch that blade and take Dooku with me into death. / And save the galaxy from civil war. / I could have done it." (SP intro)
- Mace felt Dooku's "web of treason" connecting Jango, the Trade Federation, the Geonosians and the Separatists: "Dooku was the shatterpoint."
> "Imagine a Corusca gem ... harder than durasteel. You can strike one with a five-kilo hammer and do no more than dent the hammer’s face. Yet the same crystalline structure ... also gives it shatterpoints: spots where a precise application of carefully measured force—no more than a gentle tap—will break it into pieces." (SP intro)
> "I was six or seven standard years old ... before I realized that other students, full-grown Jedi Knights, even wise Masters, could sense such connections only with difficulty, and only with concentration and practice. The Force shows me strengths and weaknesses, hidden flaws and unexpected uses. It shows me vectors of stress that squeeze or stretch, torque or shear" (SP intro)
> "Put simply: when I look at you through the Force, I can see where you break." (SP intro)
> "I looked at Jango Fett on the sand in the Geonosian arena. A perfect combination of weapons, skills, and the will to use them: an interlocking crystal of killer. The Force hinted a shatterpoint, and I left a headless corpse on the sand. The deadliest man in the galaxy. / Now: Just dead." (SP intro)
> "Situations have shatterpoints, like gems. But those of situations are fluid, ephemeral ... They are always a function of timing. / There is no such thing as a second chance." (SP intro)
- Yoda: "Your friend he was ... Cut him down you could not—not for merely a feeling." Mace: "Strange: Jedi I am, yet I drown in regret for having spared a life." Geonosis was the worst Jedi slaughter "since the Sith War, four thousand years ago." "Jedi have shatterpoints, too."

- Yoda on Depa: "Member of the Jedi Council, she is. Powerful Jedi. Brilliant warrior—" Mace: "She’d better be. ... I trained her."

- ULF = Upland Liberation Front; "‘uplanders’ is a rough translation of Korunnai"; the Korunnai are Mace's "kin". Haruun Kal is the galaxy's "sole source of thyssel bark" plus portaak leaf, jinsol, tyruun, lammas.

## Ch. 1: "The Downward Spiral"
- Mace's cover: Corellian "Merr-Sonn Power 5" blaster on his thigh, lightsaber "disguised as an old-fashioned glow rod"; the datapad is a subspace transmitter to the medium cruiser *Halleck* in the Ventran system ("twenty armed landers and a regiment of clone troopers").
- **Haruun Kal guide entry** (journal): Al'Har I, "above the clouds"; a sea of "heavier-than-air toxic gases" from volcanoes; the Downstorm (thakiz baw’kal) can "scour lowlands free of oxygen breathers within hours". Capital Pelek Baw on the Korunnal Highland; Korunnai live in seminomadic ghôsh; "Balawai (‘downfolk’)" = all offworlders and settled folk. Grandfather's Shoulder = tallest mountain, an active volcano.
- **Mace's origin:** "I was born on Haruun Kal. ... I am a full-blooded Korun." Returned "only once, thirty-five standard years ago" (as a youth, "to train in the Korun Force-bond with the great akks"). Taken at six months from ghôsh Windu, an orphan ("called by the name of my ghôsh").
- **Earliest memory / first Force use:** infant Mace reaching for Yoda's levitated rattle "without using my hands"; then holding it with "all I could summon of the Force" until it broke: Yoda's lesson that "holding too tightly to what we love will destroy it."
- **Depa origin:** Mace "fought and killed the pirates who had murdered her parents" and rescued the infant. "She is one of the youngest Jedi ever to be named to the Council." "She is the daughter I will never have."
- **The Force veil:** "Since shortly before Qui-Gon Jinn and Obi-Wan Kenobi stood in front of the Council to report the rebirth of the Sith, a mysterious veil of darkness has clouded the Force. Close by—in both space and time—the Force is as it has always been ... But when I try to search through the Force for Depa, I find only shadows".
- **War strategy:** Separatist pickets of droid starfighters and "Geonosian Dreadnaughts"; "Even before the Republic was ready to fight, we had lost." Yoda "is the master strategist of the Jedi Council" (limited engagement on multiple fronts, attrition). The clones: "nearly all 1.2 million of them fully engaged". "Jedi have been covertly inserted on hundreds of worlds" to organize Loyalist partisans.
- **Gevarno Loop:** Al'Har is the hub linking Separatist Killisu, Jutrand, Loposi, Gevarno Cluster with Loyalist Opari, Ventran, Ch'manss. "This is a fact: I found the shatterpoint of the Gevarno Loop. / Another fact: Depa volunteered to strike it."
- **Korunnai lore:** theory that they descend from shipwrecked Jedi of the Sith War era (fungi eat metal and silicates); "a curious genetic fact: all Korunnai can touch the Force." Grassers = "great six-limbed behemoths" that clear meadows; akk dogs are "Force-bonded companions". Haruun Kal government is all-Balawai; it joined the CIS "as a cynical dodge to squelch an ongoing Judicial Department investigation into their treatment of the Korun natives", trading spaceport repair/refit for the Al'har droid starfighter fleet.
> "Depa’s mastery of Vaapad makes her virtually unbeatable in personal combat, and her own cultural training—in the elegant philosophicomystical disciplines of the Chalactan Adepts—makes her uniquely resistant to all forms of mental manipulation, from Force-powered suggestion to brainwashing by torture." (Mace journal, SP ch. 1)
- Depa hoped to recruit "a cadre of Force-capable commandos" from the Korunnai for the GAR.
- **Four Pillars:** Honor < Duty < Family < Herd. "If the well-being of the herd requires the sacrifice of your honor, you do it. ... Whatever it takes. / Even your family." Yoda says for Mace "the Jedi have become my Family, and the Republic itself is my Herd."
- **Feat: shower-room fight (naked, unarmed).** "One hundred eighty-eight centimeters of muscle and bone. Absolutely still." Two capital militia cops; Mace senses "a pressure in the Force over his left kidney", catches the stun-baton wrist, kicks the smaller man's face, then uses his head as a club on the big man's nose. Lines: "I can read your mind ... You only have three ideas, and all of them are wrong." / "I’m a prophet ... I can see the future …" / "You ... Bleeding."
- Flor (Phloremirlla Tenk), Republic Intel station boss (23 years in service), runs the Highland Green Washeteria. In a street firefight she loses her left arm to a blaster bolt; **Mace Force-pinches her brachial artery**: "In the Force, Mace could feel her shredded brachial artery; with the Force, he reached inside her shoulder to pinch it shut."
- Flor on slugthrowers: blasters fail after "Day or two in the jungle"; Korun guerrillas use slug rifles.
- Cliffhanger: militia: "You’re no doctor," one said, and shot him.

## Ch. 2: "Capital Crimes"
- Stunned and held in a binder chair; he reflects that the Force gave "no hint" of Flor's death: "What happens to a Jedi when he can no longer trust the Force? / Was this what broke Depa?"
- **Colonel Lorz Geptun**, chief of security for the capital district (paternal grandfather a Korun). He has enough Force-touch to control a spinning lightsaber ("I believe that means you’re It"). Mace could have Force-triggered the saber to kill or cut free, "He let it spin." Geptun stations shooters down the corridor beyond reach.
- Geptun wants the "upcountry Jedi problem" (Depa) dealt with; bribe haggled from ten thousand to three thousand. "You mean: Jedi Master". "trying to match uncompromising stares with Mace Windu—a losing proposition".
- **Journal on Jedi method:**
> "Jedi do not ‘figure things out’ or ‘come up with a plan.’ ... A Jedi must learn to ‘unthink’ a situation, to ‘unact’ ... A Jedi does not decide. A Jedi trusts. / To put it another way: we are not trained to think. We are trained to know. / But at Geonosis, our knowing failed us all." (SP ch. 2)
> "The shadow on the Force turns our instincts against us." (SP ch. 2)
- **Durability:** a street punk's overhand "shattered against the frontal bone of Mace’s shaven skull" (broken knuckles). "You don’t want to fight me."
- **Character flaw (explicit):**
> "His applicable flaw, here: he liked to fight. This, in a Jedi, was especially dangerous. / And Mace was an especially dangerous Jedi." (SP ch. 2)
- **Precognitive reaction feat:**
> "In even the best-trained human shooter there is at least a quarter-second delay between the decision to fire and the squeeze on the trigger. Deep in the Force, Mace could feel their decision even before it was made: an echo from his future." (SP ch. 2)
> "By the time the blaster bolts were a quarter of the way there, Mace had whirled ... By the time the bolts were halfway there, the Force had snapped his lightsaber into his palm. At three-quarters, his blade extended" (SP ch. 2)
- **Weakness:** Geptun drained his power cell, so the bolts "splattered through" the weakening blade; Mace grazed on ribs and shin (stun); cannon blasts batter him: "Shot. Concussed. Cut. Battered. Bladeless."
- Shatterpoint failing him: "No shatterpoint that he could see would get him out of this." "One breath was all it took. If the Force should bring death to him here, he was ready."
- Line: "you’ve forgotten the only piece of equipment that would actually do you any good." / "An ambulance."
- **Lightsaber philosophy:** began building it as a Padawan after dreaming it for three years ("the dreams of a nine-year-old boy", "forty-one years before" → Mace ~50 now). "A lightsaber was not a weapon. ... Mace would no more give a name to his lightsaber than he would to his hand." / "His lightsaber reflected all he knew. All he believed. / All he was."
- **Telekinetic feat:** he Force-fires his dead hilt "like a bullet" into the leader's forehead, then Force-nudges the falling man's blaster to cut down the partner and spin a speeder bike; wall-runs (Force-spring "five meters up", zigzagging up to the roof through fire); Force-slaps an impact grenade away; "An upward thrust with the Force lifted him over the blast stream".
- **Nick Rostu intro** ("Smiley": slender, darker skin, "startling blue eyes"; Force-nudges Mace through the crowd). He throws Mace **Depa's lightsaber** ("Hey, Windu! Happy name-day!"): "through the Force he felt her, felt Depa as though she stood at his side". With the green blade, "The rest of the fight lasted less than five seconds."
- Korunnai with slugthrowers and a "big light-skinned Korun girl with reddish hair" (Chalk) with a Merr-Sonn Thunderbolt; the fleeing speeder crashes when fungus "ate his fly-by-wire".
- Nick: "That’s my credentials." Mace: "I have a feeling ... that you and I aren’t going to be friends."

## Ch. 3: "Jungle to Jungle"
- Depa's hilt is coated in **portaak amber**, a natural fungicide resin the ULF uses on weapons. One of the dead mercenaries had his throat cut after the fight.
- Yoda maxim: "When all choices seem wrong, choose restraint."
- **Grip strength:** Mace's hold on Nick "would not suffer by comparison with a freighter’s docking claw": "This isn’t my bone-crushing grip. This is my don’t-do-something-stupid grip."
- Chalk's hate of Balawai (implied sexual violence): "Six Balawai scum. Over and done. Never another Korun’s home burn ..."
- Mace's oath: "You will do no murder while I am in your company. ... If you try, I will stop you. Failing that … I will avenge your victims."
- Lightsaber tech: "designed to be easily rechargeable"; he can use the Force "to flip a concealed lock on the inside of its hermetically sealed shell" to swap the power cell. Recharged from looted blasterpacks.
- **Nick Rostu**: nidôsh (clan orphan), ghôsh Rostu was "loosely affiliated with ghôsh Windu"; grew up in Pelek Baw; speaks unaccented Basic. Squad: Chalk (Thunderbolt), brothers Lesh (thyssel-stained teeth) and Besh (mute, scarred, missing three fingers).
- Chalk (in Koruun): "What’s with Jedi Rockface? ... He looks the same when he’s cleaning his weapons as he did while he was using them." Nick: "Would you be happier if he was like Depa?" Mace understands Koruun ("rusty ... the Force offered understanding").
- Nick fires Mace's fungus-eaten Power 5 at Mace's head to prove it's dead.
- **Dark-side pull of the Korunnai:** "These radiated darkness ... Their anger and pain beat against him in waves that triggered resonance harmonics in his own heart. Without knowing it, they called to emotions that Mace’s lifetime of Jedi training was supposed to have buried." Mace: "I am in charge." / "Your chances come in two shapes ... Slim, and fat." / "Master Windu. ... I worked very hard to gain that title, and I’ve worked even harder to deserve it."
- **"Galaxy of Peace"** (Depa via Nick): "you’re, like, from the Galaxy of Peace. ... Haruun Kal, though, we’re a whole different place ... It’s like the laws of physics are different."
- **Twist:** the "upcountry team" were the mercs in the alley; **Chalk shot Flor Tenk**. Nick: Tenk was a double agent who'd sell Mace to "a seppie Intel camp in the Gevarno Cluster". "This isn’t a court of law, Master Windu. It’s a war."
> "That’s what war is ... by the time the Whatever-It-Is that’s moving through the trees out there is close enough that you can see for sure what it is—or who it is—you’re already dead. So you make your best guess." / Mace: "I can. That’s part of what being a Jedi is." (SP ch. 3)
- Mace was "born on the north slope of Grandfather’s Shoulder". Nick: "If we get separated, you won’t live long enough to worry about finding us."
