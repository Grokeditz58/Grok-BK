## Ch. 18: "Unconventional Warfare"
- **Order of battle (key lore/stats):**
  - Militia: 15,000 regulars (5,000 down first, then 10,000 more), 35 GAVs (converted steamcrawlers with chemical cannons and slug repeaters), and **73 Sienar Turbostorm close-assault gunships** (top speed barely Mach 1.5 in a dive). Troop shuttles of 500 each stage from Oran Mas, 50 km northwest. Arpitroops use disposable repulsor packs, with sniffers that detect grasser urine at parts per billion.
  - Militia kit (Separatist-supplied, funded by the thyssel bark trade): Merr-Sonn BC7 carbine with rocket-grenade attachment, six frag grenades, Merr-Sonn Devastator vibroknife, Opankro Graylite ceramic-fiber armor; every sixth man a flame projector; each 20-man platoon an MM(X) grenade mortar.
  - Partisans: "roughly four hundred actives, of whom two-thirds were walking wounded, and over two thousand noncombatants", with two Krupx MiniMag proton torpedo launchers and one Merr-Sonn EWHB-10. In conventional fights "the militia had crushed the partisans in every encounter".
- **Battle plan in action:** proton grenades seal four of the five tunnels ("uncountable tons of rock"). The Tisyn-C nerve-gas grenades fired into the fifth are thrown back out (Force-hurled). Then a **grasser stampede** (bulls over 1.5 t, armor-denting jaws; "seven hundred generations" of breeding) and dozens of akk dogs: "The infantry had nothing that could scratch them". The commander's dream turned into "a nightmarish massacre in less than two minutes".
- **The ankkox as mobile artillery:** "Twelve metric tons of ankkox". Kar drives it: "a Korun as tall as a Wookiee, with shoulders like a rancor’s". Its tail mace spins a GAV turret so its shot hits the GAV behind. Akk Guards on the shell fire proton torpedoes, killing all the GAVs and gunships. Chalk on the EWHB "could be seen to fire with her eyes closed", hits gunships on transsonic strafing runs, and intercepts concussion missiles "tens of meters away ... not one got through". She is defended by "a Korun man and a Chalactan woman who handled Jedi energy blades as though they’d been born with them in hand" (Mace and Depa). Two gunships down.
- **Space battle lore:** Jadthu-class landers are modified Incom shuttles holding up to 60 troopers, packed four by five against a cruiser's hull, with no hyperdrive. Their Arkayd Caltrop 5 chaff gun's slivers work as a weapon against shielded, lightly armored droid starfighters. **977 clone troops in 19 landers** plus five Rothana HR LAAT/I gunships; the clones' "reflexes were not much slower" than droid electronic reflexes. Attrition: 16 landers and 3 gunships reach atmosphere, chased by a full wing of 64 droid starfighters; 14 landers reach the highland, chased by 58; then 12 landers (5 heavily damaged) with 40 DSFs behind, while "three more wings" come in to intercept. None of the LAATs survived.
- **Ankkox-tail launch feat:** with three gunships flaming down the mountain, Mace grabs the ankkox tail; at Kar's Force-command it "fired him straight up the side of the mountain as though he’d been shot from a torpedo launcher". He Force-grabs the windscreen strut and reels himself onto it, his boots "cemented by the Force". He **Force-arms and fires both ejection seats** (pilot and navigator), deactivates the Sunfire projector and engages autopilot by gesture, Force-jams then yanks open the turret hatches so the gunners collide and knock themselves out, and ties them with scrap wire. "What are you waiting for? ... Flowers and a box of candy?"
- He Force-flips switches to slave the ball turrets to the nav console: "Twin Taim & Bak quad laser cannons".
- **Depa's Vaapad (shared deflection feat):** Mace flings his own lightsaber to Depa "like a bullet"; he feels her "Slipping into Vaapad" like "a sinking surrender like an exhausted swimmer drowning in a rising tide". He opens both troop doors to incoming fire:
> "Both blades flashed. / The gunships outside bucked under the impact of their own cannonfire. On one, a turbojet engine blasted loose of its mount ... The other gunship took its cannon blasts directly in the cockpit. / The transparisteel windscreen of a Sienar Turbostorm was thick and very durable ... A quad laser bolt could make a hole. One did. / The next five went through that hole." / "Depa opened her eyes. / They smoked with darkness." (SP ch. 18)
  - Scaling: Depa, eyes closed and fighting a migraine, deflects quad-laser cannon fire with two blades and places six consecutive bolts through one hole in the same cockpit.
- Kar uses his shields to protect Chalk from ricochets.

## Ch. 19: "Ship to Ship"
- The stolen Turbostorm climbs to 7,000 m. Sensors show 53 gunships in the engagement zone (later 67, then 69) and 192 more droid starfighters inbound (the DSFs register as "friendly", i.e. on the CIS side).
- **Nick:** first fire-control use; kills one missile with cannon and the other five with four concussion missiles in a chain fireball.
- **Depa's mid-air kill (feat):** Mace kills the engines; Depa "dived straight up into the sky through the cockpit’s open front", falling with "both lightsabers flaming at full extension". "He felt the surge in the Force that drove her directly into its path. / Her blades took it just below the windscreen and drove in to the handgrips, and the rushing airstream ... flipped her over and whipped her up across the cockpit, dragging her blades through the transparisteel to slice free a huge gaping arc." Nick: "Love them easy-openin’ cans!"
- Kar and Chalk bail out with repulsor-packs. Mace flies "an antique blastboat" through starfighter-grade evasions; a turbojet tears off. "I don’t do easy."
- **Force-guided shooting lesson (Mace teaching precog aim):** "The computer ... can’t use the Force." / "Don’t aim ... Decide what you want to hit. Fire where you know it is about to be." Mace sends Nick "a wave of calm" through their bond; Nick fires without looking at his screens and blows a gunship in half.
- Depa's captured gunship picks up Kar and Chalk in mid-air; Chalk kills the pursuer with one burst ("That ... was shooting.").
- **Clone POV, CRC-09/571** (regimental commander; Geonosis battalion commander in the airborne infantry frontal assault on the battle globes; "the disastrous skirmish on Teyr"): a third of the regiment is dead. Clones ejected in vacuum fight DSFs with personal weapons ("Three light repeaters, when turned upon the same starfighter, could break down its shields"; proton-grenade minefields), and ram DSFs with their own bodies via repulsorpacks. "They were fighting for the regiment." He "could no more disobey a lawful order than he could walk through armor plate."
- **The IFF trick ("droids are stupid"):** the Republic landers cease fire, dropping below the droids' threat horizon. Both stolen gunships (transponders read friendly) lock and fire 59 Krupx MG3 mini-missiles (four MG3s per Turbostorm, two tubes each with four-missile magazines, one shot per second: "Sixteen missiles per second"). Only six hit, but the droids reclassify all Turbostorms as hostile: "Two hundred and twenty-seven DSFs peeled off from the landers ... and fell upon the sixty-nine Turbostorms in a tornado of destruction."
- **Free-fall feat:** Mace Force-leaps out of the cockpit with Nick by the belt a second before it explodes, falling without a repulsor-pack at near human terminal velocity ("On Haruun Kal ... slightly less than three hundred kilometers per hour"). He steers by body angle onto Depa's gunship (2 km down, 250 m west, faking a flat spin), and "a considerable exertion of his Force-strength was required to slow them". He catches the dish mount one-handed, holding Nick over a nearly 1 km drop. Nick: "I … forgive you" (for the arm grip in ch. 1).
- **Depa's massacre (Vaapad feat):** the bay holds a full militia platoon. "The effect of opening fire with energy weapons, point-blank upon a master of Vaapad, was mutely testified to by every charred ring around a finger-sized hole in the armor". She stabbed through the durasteel turret hatches, killing the gunners in their seats. "Twenty-four men. / In less than a minute." Kar: "She belongs here."
- Depa: "It’s not that my way is the right way. I know it isn’t ... But it’s the only way ... To win." / "This battle is a masterpiece. Even after everything I have seen you accomplish, I could never have believed something like this". She argues their "only real weapon" is making the Balawai fear the jungle.
- **Mace's turn:** "you have been right all along ... to abandon them now, when their only choice is to suffer genocide, or to commit it? ... It would be active evil: the way of the Sith. ... The Jedi cannot walk away." / "The Summertime War won’t last that long ... About twelve hours. Maybe less."
- The DSFs withdraw on someone's command: "this someone has the control codes for those starfighters." Mace pulls the gunship's control and recognition codes and issues orders to CRC-09/571 by tightbeam data link. Depa nearly crashes and asks "Are you insane?" Mace: "There’s nothing more dangerous than a Jedi who has finally gone sane." / "We are going to take the whole system. All of it. Right now."

## Ch. 20: "Dejarik"
- **Strategic lore:** "The key to the Gevarno Loop was the Al’har system. The key to Al’har was control of the droid starfighter fleet. The fleet was controlled from a secure transmitter below the command bunker of the Pelek Baw spaceport."
- Two landers stay at Lorshan Pass. Ten climb "straight up, within the Downrush Falls" (the Great Downrush is 1 km wide at the capital), hidden from the obsolete satellites by the "soup" and the falls' spray. The decoy: a Turbostorm faking a reactor breach.
- **Pelek Baw spaceport assault:** seven Akk Guards and two Jedi hit the control center while the clones drop from the hovering landers and take the antiship emplacements "without the loss of a single trooper". The CIS-supplied turbolasers and ion cannons are captured intact. "less than seven minutes ... 286 military personnel [captured], of whom thirty-five were seriously wounded. Forty-eight were killed. Sixty-one civilian employees ... detained". Republic losses so far: Nick estimates 40 percent of the landers.
- **Separatist lore:** the "mutiny box" is a Techno Union console with destruct-charge triggers for every gun and strongpoint. "It seemed the Confederacy did not trust that the justice of its cause was sufficient to ensure the loyalty of its troops."
- Kar and the Akk Guards draw on the prisoners' fear and pain, "storing it like living power cells".
- **Mace and the clones/Geonosis:** helmetless clones remind him of Jango; "Empty helmets sitting on the consoles too closely resembled the full one he had left on the arena sand at Geonosis." CRC-09/571: "We always volunteer, sir. All of us. It’s who we are." All the clones are qualified pilots.
- Major Stempel: Geptun carries the DSF control codes on "a big personal datapad in an armored shell". Mace: "I keep forgetting that he’s smarter than I am."
- **Geptun's hologram:** several hundred DSFs over the capital; 15,000 regulars; Mace's "droid-baffling cost me almost fifty gunships"; about 20 gunships hit the Lorshan perimeter; sappers will break in "within the hour", and "two thousand civilians" are at risk. He demands surrender: "Civilians that you, Mace Windu, would give your life to protect. I cannot be bluffed." He says "dejarik"; Mace: "Isn’t that what we’ve been doing?" Through the Force-bond Mace silently cues Nick to trace the call; Nick: "Got him. The Ministry of Justice. Pegged him with his own bloody satellites!"
- Depa: "Your people—" / Mace: "My people are Jedi." He orders the failing Depa to stay ("That’s an order"), and Kar catches and comforts her (the Depa–Kar bond). The gunship will be "Haruun Kal’s largest landspeeder". "I don’t need him. I need that datapad." / "Colonel Geptun is a dangerous man ... I’ll probably have to kill him. ... Or, possibly, offer him a job."

## Ch. 21: "Inferno"
- **Mace's injury list (endurance):** "his concussion ... his cracked ribs, his sprained ankle that had him limping, the infected blaster-burn on his thigh, the spray-bandaged bite wound that Vastor had given him ... all his minor cuts and the bruises"; "Wounds? Right now he could barely feel them. / Because someone he loved was in danger." (Chalk: "Don’t have time to be hurt, me ... Can hurt later, me.")
- **Mace relieves Depa and Kar of duty.** Clone orders come "only from me"; he warns that the Akk Guards "may become violent without warning", and "You are authorized to use lethal force." (He found his reflection in the visor "too dark".)
- **The Kar repudiation (key):** Kar: "No one else could have done what you have done today ... I am proud to be your dôshalo. You are a credit to the Windu." Mace: "You aren’t. ... I am not Mace, Jedi of the Windu. Windu is my name, not my ghôsh. You and I are not dôshallai. The Windu are no more, and what you have done disgraces their memory. My ghôsh ... is the Jedi." (SP ch. 21)
- **Urban gunship run:** over 200 km/h through the warehouse district; they blast through warehouse walls to cross streets, passing a heavy infantry company. Mace reasons the DSFs won't target ground vehicles ("That’s how": a GAV rolls out).
- **Chalk's feat:** quad lasers can't scratch the GAV's armor, so with defocused eyes she hits its armor-piercing shell "nose-first while still inside the barrel".
- **Mace out-reasons Geptun:** the Ministry trace was bait (troops "or possibly only a very large bomb"); Geptun is at "a place with electronics sophisticated enough to fake the origination data of a comm signal": the Washeteria (Tenk's place).
- **Chalk dies.** She hid a GAV slug wound through the chest ("an exit wound Nick could have put his fist into") and kept fighting "Because people she loved were in danger." Mace turns for the med center at once: "General or not, he was still a Jedi."
- **Kar's betrayal and the sack of Pelek Baw:** clone-piloted spaceport ships drew the DSFs onto the city of "More than four hundred thousand people". Over a hundred ships, "from tiny racing yachts to immense freighters", were sent up; "Drawing fire from the starfighters down upon it could put the entire capital to the torch. / No: not could. / Had." Mace feels the city as "a holocaust of flame and darkness". Kar's hologram: "Thank you for giving us the city, dôshalo ... We have decided to redecorate." / "When everyone is dead." / "She doesn’t want to see you. Ever." Mace assumes they killed CRC-09/571. The dark storm over the city "growled with him".
- **Proton-grenade trap:** the "clank" when Kar climbed the nose was a mag-clamped proton grenade with a shaped charge aimed through the cockpit. Mace Force-fires all ejection seats in time ("What time is it?"). Nick and Chalk's body share her seat.
- Street horrors: a Wookiee and a Yuzzem tearing each other apart; a man halved by a falling hull plate. "Pelek Baw had always been a jungle, but only in a metaphoric sense. Vastor had brought the real one. / He was the real one."
- Nick on Chalk: "I always kind of thought she’d be coming with me." He takes her gunbelt (now two-gunned): "They’ve turned on us. All of them. Kar and his Akks. Depa. Even the clones." / "Kar saved her life ... Temporarily." / "It’s always temporary." Mace: "We’re going to surrender."

## Ch. 22: "Surrender"
- Mace and Nick "surrender" to a militia squad, which drives them to the Highland Green Washeteria. Mace cuts open its blast shutters ("No, it isn’t [locked]").
- The ch. 1 big man from the pro-bi showers is now a lieutenant: "How’s your nose?" **Quick-draw:** "The big man went for his sidearm with an impressively swift draw. / Mace’s was faster." Mace: "Shooting at Jedi is a losing proposition." / "Do your duty. Or do what’s right." / "Yours is not the only nose I’ve broken on this planet ... you deserved it more than he did." Lieutenant: "Then maybe I don’t mind the nose so much ... I guess I’m lucky to be alive at all." / Mace: "You should consider a new line of work."
- **Republic Intelligence station lore:** the hatch is hidden in the tile floor of a sulfurous hot-spring bath, and a stair field generator holds the water back in "walls of sizzling energy". Inside is "the very latest electronic surveillance, code-breaking, and communications equipment" (Geptun has taken over the Republic station).
- Geptun has broken: "I never suspected that Jedi could be capable of such … savagery." / Mace: "Neither did I." / "If your concern for children had included Korunnai, we wouldn’t be here right now." / "You are not the only one with unreliable subordinates." The spaceport also holds the signal-jamming controls.
- **Remote saber ignition (TK feat):** the lightsaber the lieutenant holds "ignited in his hand, the blade stabbing downward to drive a smoking hole through his thigh" and flips to Mace's palm. Nick Force-pulls (or Mace pulls for him) both pistols into his hands.
- **Terms:** the militia ceases operations at Lorshan, hands over the DSF control codes, and Geptun, as "the ranking officer of the Confederacy", signs a formal surrender "ceding Haruun Kal, and the Al’har system itself, to the Republic." / "This is extortion!" / "No ... It’s war." / "Surrender is always made under duress ... That’s why they call it surrender." Automatic transmissions are queued: militia stand-down orders, and a HoloNet report to Coruscant asking for an emergency Republic task force.
- **Mace on Geptun:** "I think your cynicism is a fraud ... if you were truly as corrupt and venal as you pretend, you would be in the Senate." Geptun laughs and drives.
- Mace cuts open the spaceport gate. Clones under CC-8/349 report that Depa ordered the spaceport ships used as DSF bait after the false news of Mace's death, and nothing has been heard from the bunker since. The door is 1 m thick with neutronium bolts.
- "A surge of dark power spread across the city like the shock-front of a fusion bomb. / Behind that door was ground zero."
- Nick on slugs vs blasters: "Slugs don’t bounce." / "Off a vibroshield they will." / "Not off a lightsaber."
- **Scaling/lore:** "Master Yoda knew better than to send troopers against a single enemy Force-user on Geonosis; in that bunker may be as many as seven." / Nick: "Eight." (Depa counted.) "There is no one in that room except the dead ... And the people who killed them." Geptun: "I have never been anything remotely resembling a hero." / "People ... change."

## Ch. 23: "The Hard Way" (climax)
- The bunker is full of the dead. "As a weapon, a lightsaber was uniquely tidy. Even, in a sense, merciful ... A vibroshield was not."
- **Kar's pelekotan stealth vs Mace's Force-sense:** an Akk Guard attacks "Before he’d even seen what it was. / He’d never felt it coming." Kar: "Your Force can’t help you here. Here there is only pelekotan. And we are only pelekotan’s dream." / "One-seventh of me. / Your pardon: one-eighth." (Depa counts as one of the eight.) Mace: "A man like you will always be my enemy, no matter whose side you’re on." / "I don’t have to win ... All I have to do is fight." / "We are all Balawai here."
- Dead clones with their helmets off: "Jango Fett’s dead face stared at him four times over ... nothing but his guilt."
- **Depa's ambush:** she had been cutting toward the transceiver to destroy it, then lay faking death. "A lightsaber’s emitter jammed against his belly ... its green fire chewed a tunnel through Mace’s guts and speared out his back." He locks her hand so she can't slash it free and still won't strike her ("not while ... see no hate or rage but only stark agony"). Instead he cuts open the transceiver chamber: "Geptun! NOW!" Then a pommel strike to her temple, and a kick.
- **Mace vs Depa, Vaapad duel (key scaling):** mirrored backflips; "Their blades flickered and whipped and no bolt touched their flesh." / "His blade whirred through the air. / Hers whirred faster. She advanced. ... This constant near-invisible weave of lethal energy is the ready-stance of Vaapad."
> "He was taller than she, with more reach and weight, and vastly more muscle in his upper body, but she drove him backward as though he were a child. Green flame struck through his guard, and only a frantic jerk of his head turned what would have been a brain-burning thrust into a line of char along his cheekbone. / Still he did not strike back. / 'I will not kill you ... Death is not the answer to your pain.'" (SP ch. 23)
  - **Power mechanic:** "as each Akk Guard died, his share of pelekotan backflowed through the bonds Vastor had forged among them. / She was getting stronger." She scorches his wrist, leg and chest.
  - **Mace's own shatterpoint:** "The only way he could survive was to give more of himself to Vaapad. To give all of himself. / To sink into pelekotan’s dream. / He felt it: he had reached his own shatterpoint. And he was breaking."
  - Caveat: Mace is holding back (no counterattacks) with a hole through his gut and pre-existing injuries, and Depa is boosted by the bond backflow. This is not a clean 1v1 ranking.
- **Nick vs the Akk Guards:** his Force-nudged shots set up clone kills (Prouk, and Thaffal, whose nose Mace broke). Iolu's vibroshield misses Nick's throat and sticks in the wall; Nick kills him, but Iolu's other shield had already opened his abdomen. Kar alone "leaped and spun and slaughtered with demonic precision" among the clones; 24 troopers entered in a wedge around Geptun.
- Nick's collapse comes through the bond and Mace's wounds "crashed upon him"; his fighting spirit "was waiting for him in the dark."
- **The resolution (shatterpoint of the jammer):** Geptun's recall fails with "ECM FAULT". Mace "took one last look at the darkness that called to him ... And turned away." He drops his blade and retreats to the live console, then collapses so Depa's killing stroke cuts through **the signal-jamming console** instead: "COMMAND EXECUTED". The DSFs leave at dawn, and the dark-power swirl recedes.
- Depa: "Oh, Mace … I'm so sorry"; she puts her emitter to her own temple: "We shouldn’t have come." **Nick's shot** knocks the blade out of her hand ("Told you I can shoot …") before he passes out.
- **Mace vs Kar, final (key feat and philosophy):** Kar: "More power than Mace had ever felt." / "Come on, then: jungle rules." / "On the contrary ... Jedi rules ... Jedi do not fear ... And we do not attack. As long as you stand in peace, so do I." / "If you fight a Jedi, you’ve already lost."
> "Vastor was younger, stronger, faster, and immensely more powerful, and he wielded weapons that could not be harmed by the Jedi blade. Mace couldn’t win such a battle on his best day, and this day was far from his best" (SP ch. 23)
  - **The kill shot:** Mace TKs Iolu's vibroshield out of the wall and sends it "like a missile toward Vastor’s back". Kar blocks with his shields edge-to-edge instead of back-to-back: "There was a reason why, when Vastor’s shields met ... he always brought them together back-to-back, instead of edge-to-edge." It "sheared through both Vastor’s shields, through both his wrists, and buried itself in the bone of his chest, stopping less than a centimeter short of his heart." (The vibroshields can cut each other: a vibroshield is the counter to a vibroshield.)
  - "You were warned." / "Killing you is not the answer for your crimes ... Kar Vastor, you are under arrest."
- Ending: Geptun: "Did we win?" / Mace, looking at Depa and recalling his victory conditions: "I seem to be ... the last one standing."
