# Revenge of the Sith (Matthew Stover novelization): Power-Scaling Research Notes

- **Book:** *Star Wars: Episode III – Revenge of the Sith*, by Matthew Woodring Stover (Del Rey, 2005)
- **Continuity:** Legends (Del Rey novelization of the film; the most-cited prose source for the ROTS duels)
- **Source file:** `[local path]` (8,890 lines, 113,836 words). Every citation is `L####` (line number in that file).
- **Method:** The text was split into its 21 chapters plus the Introduction and part-epigraphs (`=N=` markers) and read in chunks. Every chapter was read line by line: Ch1–7, 15–17 and 19–20 in the first pass, and Ch8–14, 18 and 21 in a follow-up line-by-line pass (2026-10-19), whose finds are marked "Line-by-line additions" (§1), §2.14, S39–S51 (§3) and §9.0 (pronoun-only lines). Quotes are exact. All guillemet-delimited quotes in this file are script-checked against the source at the end of this file. OCR noise in the source (e.g. «Lighting blasted», «|which», «[Dooku»») is reproduced *verbatim* and marked *(sic)* where it matters.
- **Version tags:** Every feat and statement carries a version tag. Statements count only for the version they describe. The whole novel is **19 BBY**, so tags separate *states*: e.g. "Anakin, Jedi Knight (pre-fall)" vs "Anakin, rage-amped ('gift of fury')" vs "Vader, newly turned (unsuited)"; "Sidious, concealed" vs "Sidious, unmasked"; "Mace, base" vs "Mace, Vaapad 'superconducting loop' + shatterpoint". Amps (rage, Vaapad, shatterpoint perception, Force-exhaustion debuffs, drugged/hurt states) count as separate versions.
- **Scope note:** A sibling worker covers *Darth Plagueis* and *Labyrinth of Evil*. Plagueis appears here only as Palpatine's opera-scene legend (Ch12), noted as a statement, not a feat.

## 1. Chapter-by-chapter plot notes

**Introduction: The Age of Heroes (L29–124).** A second-person HoloNet framing of the Battle of Coruscant. Grievous has kidnapped the Chancellor. Grievous is introduced as «an abomination of nature, a fusion of flesh and droid» (L75). Younglings across the galaxy believe «when Anakin and Obi-Wan get there, those dirty Seppers are going to wish they'd stayed in bed today» (L97). The adults believe «legendary heroes are merely legends» (L109). *Scaling:* only public reputation, not feats.

**Part One epigraph, "Victory" (L125–142).** «The dark is generous.» The thematic thesis is «With each victory of the light, it is the dark that wins.» (L139). This frames every Jedi "win" in the book (Dooku, Grievous) as Sidious's win.

**Ch1 Anakin and Obi-Wan (L143–834).** A space dogfight over Coruscant. Obi-Wan is «Jedi Master. General in the Grand Army of the Republic. Member of the Jedi Council.» (L275). Anakin can «reach into the Force and feel the engagement throughout Coruscant space as though the whole battle were happening inside his head» (L323). Anakin threads his fighter through a hull slit «with centimeters to spare» (L557). Buzz droids hit Obi-Wan's ship. They land in Invisible Hand's hangar. The chapter carries the book's key Anakin hype passage: «The most powerful Jedi of his generation. Perhaps of any generation. The fastest. The strongest.» (L443). The schoolyard debate over whether Obi-Wan's cleverness can beat Anakin's power is set up at L825. *Scaling:* Anakin's battlefield-wide Force sense; piloting feats.

**Ch2 Dooku (L835–1190).** Dooku POV. He waits beside the shackled Palpatine, as «Count Dooku» (L953). He is «numbered among the Lost» (L1007) and «felt every hour of his eighty-three years» (L1069). He suggests recruiting Kenobi instead (L1107). His Master says of Anakin: «He is powerful. Potentially more powerful than even myself.» (L1123). Dooku reflects that his Master's manipulation is a power that «might be considered to dwarf even the power of the dark side itself» (L1141). Dooku believes the duel will be a staged win for him. *Scaling:* Sidious's hype of Anakin's potential. Dooku's age.

**Ch3 The Way of the Sith (L1191–1580).** Anakin and Obi-Wan reach the General's quarters. In Dooku's Force-perception, Palpatine is «an event horizon» and «A black hole of the Force» (L1351–1353). The duel follows; see Feats §2.3 for exchanges. Dooku dominates at first, trapping Anakin with telekinetic furniture. Then the two Jedi trap him: Anakin is «a Djem So stylist, and as fine a one as Dooku had ever seen» (L1485) and Kenobi «had become a master of Soresu» (L1497). Dooku hurls Obi-Wan aside. He begins to flag from Force exhaustion. Then he duels Anakin alone, and Anakin shows «the gift of fury».

**Ch4 Jedi Trap (L1581–1804).** Present-tense passage. Anakin understands that fear can be a weapon. «Dooku is dead already» (L1593). He smashes aside a table fragment that Dooku hurls «with all the power that the dark side can draw from throughout the universe» (L1613). He takes both of Dooku's hands. Palpatine orders «Do it! Now!» (L1657) and Anakin beheads him. Guilt follows (L1725). Palpatine says «Revenge is the foundation of justice» (L1751). Anakin unlocks the shackles through the Force (L1771) and carries the unconscious Obi-Wan out.

**Ch5 Grievous (L1805–2016).** Grievous POV on the bridge. He kills a gunnery officer with one punch (L1835). His body is described (L1853–1859) and his trophy lightsabers taken from Jedi he killed (L1863–1865). He threatens to disembowel Palpatine live on the HoloNet (L1971). The heroes cross a turbolift shaft that has turned into a hallway (L1891). Palpatine whispers: «You have destroyed their political head» (L1915).

**Ch6 Rescue (L2017–2438).** The gravity flips in the turbolift shaft. They trigger the ray-shield trap. Obi-Wan tests it with his lightsaber (L2289, 2313). Destroyer droids and Grievous's MagnaGuards appear: «Grievous's personal bodyguard droids» (L2361). They surrender: «He's got it.» (L2393). The chapter also has a flash to Anakin's Knighting, with the Padawan braid given to Padmé (L2209).

**Ch7 Obi-Wan and Anakin 2 (L2439–2702).** «This is Obi-Wan Kenobi in the light». On the bridge Obi-Wan is so deep in the Force that he, R2 and Anakin act as one. R2's fusion-welder distracts Grievous, and Obi-Wan uses the Force to «reverse the polarity of the electrodrivers in the general's mech» [anical hands, truncated in the excerpt] (L2505). This is his signature trick, reused on Mustafar. Grievous hurls a console (L2521). He thinks «Kenobi, though-The ease with which Kenobi had taken command of the situation was frightening» and «of the two, Skywalker was reportedly the greater warrior» (L2529). He decides survival is the goal (L2531), breaks a viewport, and flees in a pod. Anakin crash-lands the burning half of the ship: «Between his will and the will of the Force, there is no contest» (L2685).

**Part Two epigraph, "Seduction" (L2687–2702).** «The dark's patience is infinite.»

### 2.1 Darth Sidious / Palpatine

1. **[Sidious concealed, 19 BBY] (Ch3, L1351–1353).** Dooku's Force-sight: «Palpatine was an event horizon» / «A black hole of the Force.» → Dooku perceives him only as an absence. → **Concealment/presence hype from a Sith Lord who knows him; the strongest statement for Sidious > Dooku.**
2. **[Sidious concealed] (Ch3, L1573).** Mid-duel, he changes the plan on the spot: «Don't fear what you're feeling, Anakin, use it!» … «Rage is your weapon. Strike now! Strike! Kill him!» → Dooku is betrayed and dies. → **Strategic feat (live manipulation of a Sith apprentice and a Jedi).**
3. **[Sidious concealed] (Ch2, L1141).** His manipulation «might be considered to dwarf even the power of the dark side itself» (Dooku POV). → **Strategic hype, not combat.**
4. **[Sidious concealed] (Ch15, L5739).** He offers apprenticeship openly: «Join me on the path of true power. Be my friend, Anakin. Be my student. My apprentice.» → Anakin declines «Not yet» (L5743). Sidious still wins one chapter later. → **Manipulation feat.**
5. **[Sidious concealed → unmasked] (Ch17, L6593–6595).** «Tiin frowned and cocked his head. His blade dipped. A smear of red-flashing darkness hurtled from behind the desk.» / «Saesee Tiin's head bounced when it hit the floor.» → **Kills Master Saesee Tiin instantly after a telepathy bait. Speed and deception blitz.**
6. **[Sidious] (Ch17, L6605).** «A small, neat hole in the middle of his forehead leaked smoke» → **Kills Agen Kolar instantly.** «Palpatine stood at the doorway» with «a blade the color of fire» (L6611). Covers the room in a blink.
7. **[Sidious] (Ch17, L6649).** «On Palpatine's desk lay the head of Kit Fisto» → **Kills Kit Fisto off-page in the few seconds Anakin is in transit.** The novel does not show the exchanges. Fisto lasts longer than Tiin and Kolar, but not by much.
8. **[Sidious vs Mace, Vaapad] (Ch17, L6701–6703).** «Vaapad made him an open channel, half of a superconducting loop completed by the shadow» … «no wound taken on either side» … «Impasse.» → **A true sword stalemate with Mace at his peak style.** See §2.8 for the full duel.
9. **[Sidious] (Ch17, L6717).** «a dark surge of the Force nearly blew him right out of the gap he had just cut. Only a desperate Force-push of his own altered his path» → **One distraction-window Force blast nearly kills Mace.**
10. **[Sidious] (Ch17, L6727).** Saber cut in half on the ledge (Mace's feat, Sidious's loss). The text frames the shadow as afraid: «the shadow had become a pulsar of fear» (L6719). Then: «Do you think the fear you feel is mine?» (L6745). → **Reading ambiguity:** the "fear" Mace exploited may have been projected, or may have been Anakin's. See §6.
11. **[Sidious] (Ch17, L6753–6755).** His lightning is reflected back by Vaapad: «Palpatine staggered, snarling, but the blistering energy that loured from his hands only intensified.» / «He fed the power with his pain.» → **Tanks his own reflected lightning and escalates.**
12. **[Sidious] (Ch17, L6769–6779).** «he ramped up the lightning bursting from his hands, bending the fountain of Mace's blade back toward the Korun Master's face» → Mace: «This was beyond Vaapad; he had no strength left to fight against his own blade» (L6773); «Anakin, he's too strong for me-» (L6779). → **Wins the lightning-vs-Vaapad power struggle on raw output; Mace is losing before the fake surrender.**
13. **[Sidious] (Ch17, L6783–6785).** He ends the lightning himself and fakes the surrender: «I ... can't. I give up. I ... I am too weak, in the end.» → **A deliberate act; he «was not afraid. Mace could feel it: he wasn't worried at all» (L6759).**

## 4. Character profiles (personality, arc, relationships, abilities and limits, what the book adds)

### Darth Sidious / Palpatine (19 BBY)
- **Personality:** a performer with total self-control. He plays the «tired, frightened old man» (L6589) right up to the kill. He enjoys cruelty as theatre («Happy Empire Day!», L8163; «Please say it's Kenobi», L8171). Stover's thesis line is «The dark is generous» (part epigraphs), and Sidious is that generosity weaponized.
- **Arc:** Concealed Chancellor (Ch1–14) → semi-reveal to Anakin (Ch15, L5739) → unmasked killer (Ch17) → scarred Emperor (Ch19, L7693) → duel with Yoda that leaves him «gasping» (Ch20) → master of the rebuilt Vader (Ch21).
- **Relationships:** Dooku is a tool (Ch3 betrayal; Dooku POV: «Lord Sidious could come up with a new plan more easily than a new apprentice», L1505). Anakin is his true project (S2, S24). Yoda is the real rival, the only one who costs him his stamina on page.
- **Abilities shown:** Force concealment (event horizon), deceptive speed (Tiin and Kolar blitz), sword parity with peak-Vaapad Mace, lightning strong enough to bend Mace's own blade toward his face, endurance lightning-cage contests with Yoda, mass TK (Senate pods), master manipulation.
- **Limits shown:** he is disarmed once (Mace, ledge). He takes reflected lightning (Mace, and Yoda's rocket-blast). He is exhausted after Yoda. He fails to find Yoda's body. He relies on Anakin as an insurance policy in the office (L6759–6765).
- **What the book adds to his scaling:** Stover is the only source that (a) calls the Mace sword phase a literal impasse, (b) puts Mace's admission «too strong for me» *before* the faked surrender, (c) makes the office fight's fear ambiguous («Do you think the fear you feel is mine?»), and (d) shows him drained after Yoda. Net: **Sidious > Mace (power), Sidious ≥ Yoda (endurance/power), Sidious ≫ Dooku (presence).**
- *Line-by-line additions:* Sidious treats Dooku as expendable: «Dooku was not lost, he was sacrificed» (L3509). His pitch to Anakin flatters power more than skill: «You may become more powerful than all of them together.» (L3697), and Anakin is «the most powerful Jedi in the galaxy» (L5033, D). After Mustafar he lifts Vader «with effortless strength» and keeps him alive by will: «Live, Lord Vader. Live, my apprentice.» (L8613–8617).


### Anakin Skywalker / Darth Vader (19 BBY)
- **Personality:** proud and insecure («Such a fragile little ego», L1443). Love for Padmé is his root and his lever. He fears his own fury («Dread, Dooku surmised, of himself.», L1561).
- **Arc:** Hero (Ch1–7) → shut out of Mastery (Ch9–11) → courted (Ch12, 14, 15) → betrays Mace (Ch17) → Vader (Ch17–20) → maimed and suited (Ch20–21).
- **Relationships:** Obi-Wan («complementary halves of a single warrior», L8413); Palpatine (surrogate father, then master); Padmé (choked, L8283); Mace (mutual distrust; Mace saw him as «The shatterpoint»).
- **Abilities:** top-tier raw TK (the table vs Dooku), Djem So power, the rage amp ("gift of fury"), technopathy (unlocking the shackles), battle-scale Force sense, piloting, dark-side strength (Obi-Wan's arms near breaking), Force "roar".
- **Limits:** emotionally exploitable (the taunt at L1567 costs him his edge); arrogance (L8435); the leap at the end, «Half a second too slow» (L8569).
- **Book adds:** the clearest prose evidence for **restrained Anakin ≈ Dooku** and **rage-Anakin > tired Dooku**. Blade parity with Obi-Wan, and Vader ahead in raw power. Dooku attests to his potential (S9, S10) and Sidious too (S2).
- *Line-by-line additions:* Mace has the most balanced Jedi read: «arguably the most powerful Jedi alive, and he is still getting stronger. But he is not stable.» (L2941). His Council outburst is narrated as «the voice of Dooku's destroyer» (L3953). His boasts escalate from «No Jedi in this room can match my power» (L3955) to «the power of any five Masters. Any ten.» (L4053) and then collapse into «I'm one of the most powerful Jedi alive, but it's not enough.» (L5199). His utility includes psychometry («the whole apartment stank of Obi-Wan», L5095) and prophetic dreams that «had never been wrong» (L3529). The Padmé «bug that he could crush» beat (L5153) foreshadows the choke. Ch21's second-person coda states that suited Vader is «so far less now than what you were» (L8867).


### Obi-Wan Kenobi (19 BBY)
- **Personality:** calm, wry, self-effacing («I'm very flattered that you would consider me a master, but really-», L6027). His fatal flaw is attachment to Anakin, which he resolves on page («He let it go», L8531).
- **Arc:** Hero → Utapau victor → Order 66 survivor → Mustafar executioner who cannot finish the job.
- **Abilities:** best-in-Order Soresu (S16–S17); the polarity trick (Grievous, Vader); TK precision; Force-amped strength; mind tricks.
- **Limits:** loses most pure-power exchanges with Vader (TK roar, grip). He is Force-thrown by Dooku twice and knocked out. Grievous throws him twice in melee.
- **Book adds:** the strongest Legends prose for **Obi-Wan > Grievous (low-mid diff with sabers)** and **Obi-Wan = Vader in blades**. Mace rates Obi-Wan the Jedi most likely to beat Grievous.
- *Line-by-line additions:* Mace calls him «our most cunning and insightful Master-and our most tenacious» (L3849). His faith in Anakin is total: «Anakin can do anything. Except betray a friend.» (L4243). The clones fear him: Cody plans heavy weapons from five companies (L7173). On Utapau's aftermath he survives through his mount's sacrifice, TK, a beast-suggestion and a rebreather, ending in «Empty, he found clarity.» (L7545), which sets up «He let it go.» (L8531).


### Count Dooku (19 BBY)
- **Personality:** aristocratic, amused, contemptuous («this is pathetic.»), tired: «It is ... fatiguing, to play the villain for so long, Master.» (L1069).
- **Arc:** Expects a staged win (Ch2) → nearly loses for real (Ch3) → betrayed and beheaded (Ch4).
- **Abilities:** Makashi; directing the Force subtly into the opponents' balance (L1463); strong TK (furniture, the wall-throw, accelerating a fall); self-recharge (L1545).
- **Limits:** **stamina/age** («each block aged him a decade»; «Force exhaustion»). Makashi can't meet Djem So's power.
- **Book adds:** a 2v1 that turns against him, and a solo fight against Anakin decided by Dooku's stamina. Use it to cap Dooku's endurance in long fights.
- *Line-by-line additions:* Palpatine's verdict on Dooku's end is «a trace overconfident» (L2781), and Sidious privately calls him «sacrificed» (L3509). The Council reads Anakin's win as proof that he can «battle a Sith Lord alone» (L3837).


### General Grievous (19 BBY)
- **Personality:** sadistic, contemptuous of Neimoidians, a pragmatic runner («very good at running away», L6065).
- **Abilities:** 12–20 strikes/sec with four arms; combat-algorithm adaptation; staff-immune armor; strength (the console, throws); holdout-blaster draw speed.
- **Limits:** no Force; the gut sac is a soft point; he panics when his blades are cut.
- **Book adds:** concrete numbers (12/sec, 20/sec), plus Mace's report that Grievous adapted to Vaapad «after a single exchange». Obi-Wan's win is clean in the saber phase.
- *Line-by-line additions:* Yoda wants Masters, not Knights, sent against him (L4761). His burst speed «seemed to teleport» (L3449) is shown only against Gunray. He boasts the Utapau base «can stand against a thousand Jedi. Ten thousand.» (L3459, D). Obi-Wan goes in with a read on him: «how he fights now-and for how he runs away» (L4773).


### Mace Windu (19 BBY)
- **Personality:** stern, principled, distrustful of Palpatine and Anakin. Willing to kill a «Sith disease» without trial.
- **Abilities:** Vaapad (its creator and only master); the loop that turns the enemy's darkness into his power; shatterpoint perception; lightning reflection through Vaapad.
- **Limits:** he has to rely on the opponent's darkness. He loses the lightning struggle by his own POV (S21). His shatterpoint tunnel vision kills him (L6813).
- **Book adds:** Stover's text is the canonical source for "Impasse", and it explicitly states that the loop runs on Sidious's own power. That means the office Mace is a matchup-specific peak version.
- *Line-by-line additions:* his shatterpoint gift is defined in Ch8 (L2799), and he had seen Palpatine as a shatterpoint since the election (L2801). He senses that «The dark side of the Force surrounds the Chancellor.» (L3359). Obi-Wan's POV ranks Mace and Kolar «among the greatest bladesbeings the Jedi Order had ever produced» (L4767).


### Yoda (19 BBY)
- **Personality:** severe and sorrowful. He accepts responsibility (S38).
- **Abilities:** lightning redirection; a body-blast counterattack; high-level saber and hand-to-hand; the lightning cage; Force-sprint escape; he survives a 100 m fall.
- **Limits:** endurance against Sidious («reached the limits of his strength»); strategic despair («just-didn't-have it»).

## 6. Verdict implications (diff wording with confidence words)

*Scale used throughout:* **diff** = how hard the winner has to work (low / low-mid / mid / mid-high / high). **Confidence** = how sure the call is (low / low-medium / medium / medium-high / high). No numeric scores are used.

**Mace (Vaapad) vs Sidious, office, no Anakin → Sidious, mid-high diff. Confidence: medium, leaning medium-high (verdicts.md has Sidious, medium).**
- *For Mace:* the sword phase is a true stalemate (S20, «Impasse»). He wins the ledge disarm (L6725). Vaapad reflects lightning (L6751). Obi-Wan's POV rates him «among the greatest bladesbeings the Jedi Order had ever produced» (L4767, S45).
- *For Sidious:* the impasse only breaks through a shatterpoint that **leads to Anakin** (L6711–6713). The disarm comes after Mace's window-leap, and Sidious's surge «nearly blew him right out» (L6717). Mace could feel it: he wasn't worried at all» (L6759). Kolar, whom Obi-Wan ranks alongside Mace (L4767), dies in seconds (L6605).
