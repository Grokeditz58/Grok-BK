# Star Wars: Empire's End (#1–2, Oct–Nov 1995): series summary and scaling notes

- Source: the two single issues, hand-read page by page (notes `ee-01.md`, `ee-02.md`). Only the comic text and art are used. Every claim below points to an issue and page. (ANALYSIS) marks my own inference.
- Citation form: "EE #N p. X". Each issue has one cover file, so p1 = cover, p2 = credits, p3 = crawl, story from p4. EE #2 p28 is a prose epilogue.

## Story in one paragraph
The Emperor fires Umak Leth's Galaxy Gun at will. It destroys the troopship *Pelagia* with 100,000 troops (EE #1 p6–8). Its first shot at the rebel base on Nespis VIII is a dud, but the second destroys the space city (EE #1 p15–17; EE #2 p3). His last clone is "IN ACCELERATED DECAY" with "NO OTHER CLONES AVAILABLE" (EE #1 p18), so he sends Xecr Nist to take Ysanna shamans for Jedi-blood clones (EE #1 p19, p22). The fleet scatters (EE #1 p21). Luke, Kam, Brand and Rayf storm Bast Castle on Vjun, topple Vader's statue and capture Nist (EE #1 p23–27). On Korriban the Sith spirits refuse to heal the clone but show him baby Anakin near Onderon (EE #2 p4–6). Leia's battle meditation staggers him (EE #2 p7), the *Falcon* escapes *Eclipse II*'s tractor beam (p9), and a decoy led by Vima fails (p11). The Emperor gets into the Kira fortress, kills Rayf, downs Brand and Leia, and grabs Anakin. Han shoots the drained clone dead (EE #2 p18–21). Brand holds the Emperor's spirit as it tries to enter Anakin and dies with it (EE #2 p22–23). R2 and Solusar's team send *Eclipse II* into the Galaxy Gun, which fires into Byss and destroys the throneworld (EE #2 p24–27). The Republic is reborn on Onderon (EE #2 p28).

## Fight log (summary; blow-by-blow in the issue notes)
1. **Galaxy Gun vs. *Pelagia*** (EE #1 p6–8): the troopship is destroyed.
2. **Galaxy Gun vs. Nespis VIII** (EE #1 p13–17): the Kuat V-200s fail and the first shot is a dud. The second destroys the city, and the fleet escapes.
3. **Bast Castle** (EE #1 p24–27): four Jedi against Nist's darksiders. The Howlrunner breaches the gate and Kam is downed. Luke's statue topple turns the fight, and Nist is captured. The Ysanna are found in carbonite.
4. **Leia vs. the Emperor across space** (EE #2 p7): the Emperor is staggered.
5. ***Falcon* vs. *Eclipse II*** (EE #2 p8–11): the tractor generator is destroyed. The decoy escapes, but the Emperor isn't fooled.
6. **Assault on *Eclipse II* and Iziz** (EE #2 p13–17): the rebel fighters' diversion works. The Jedi hit the shuttle and find the Emperor gone.
7. **Kira fortress** (EE #2 p16–21): Leia hurts him, then is floored. Brand is downed, Rayf kills T'iaz, and the Emperor kills Rayf. Han shoots the Emperor.
8. **Brand vs. the Emperor's spirit** (EE #2 p22–23): Brand wins at the cost of his life.
9. ***Eclipse II* vs. the Galaxy Gun** (EE #2 p24–27): the ram makes the gun fire into Byss, and Byss is destroyed.

## Character analyses (text-grounded)
- **Emperor:** deadly in output to the very end. In EE #2 alone he finds Anakin with the Oracle Stone (p6), sees through Vima (p11), mind-tricks Lady Kira (p16), and in one fight floors Leia, downs Brand and kills Rayf (p18–20). But every source agrees his body is failing: the physician (EE #1 p18), the Sith spirits (EE #2 p5), his adept (EE #2 p18) and he himself ("THE BATTLE HAS DRAINED ME", EE #2 p20). (ANALYSIS) EE is a race against his own decay. No hero ever overpowers him at full strength.
- **Leia:** the standout Jedi of EE. Battle meditation across space (EE #2 p7) and a direct Force blast that knocks him back (EE #2 p18), both while still a student ("I HOPE SOMEDAY TO BECOME A JEDI MASTER", EE #2 p16).
- **Luke:** leader and planner. His one Force feat is the statue topple (EE #1 p26–27). He never fights the Emperor in EE, and his last act is to offer surrender (EE #2 p20).
- **Empatojayos Brand:** the hero of the finale. His lightning gun "GOT BOBA FETT" (EE #1 p11; the only Fett reference). He survives a lightning bolt (EE #2 p19), then traps and destroys Palpatine's spirit (EE #2 p22–23). The epilogue credits him: "In the person of Empatojayos Brand, the fire of the Jedi... has flared up into brilliance" (EE #2 p28).
- **Rayf Ysanna:** quick blade work. His blade sweeps across a darksider's saber hand at Bast Castle (EE #1 p25; result not captioned) and T'iaz in one strike (EE #2 p19), but dies at once to the Emperor's lightning (EE #2 p20).
- **Kam Solusar:** a tactician (the Howlrunner missile, EE #1 p24–25) and a sensor (EE #2 p13). He leads the *Eclipse II* commando team (EE #2 p17, p24). He is downed at Bast Castle (EE #1 p26).
- **Vima-Da-Boda:** a teacher and illusionist. Her decoy works physically but not on the Emperor's sense (EE #2 p11). She vanishes after the war (EE #2 p28).
- **Xecr Nist:** survived DE II #6. He duels Luke and is captured (EE #1 p26–27). He never wins a shown exchange.
- **Galaxy Gun / Umak Leth:** the strongest weapon in the saga. It destroys a troopship, a space city and a planet (EE #1 p8, p17; EE #2 p26–27). Its only failure is a supplier's timer (EE #1 p15).

## Honest scaling takeaway (text only)
- **EE settles the saga by attrition, not by a duel.** The Emperor's power is never shown to fall short. His last clone does, and the text says so five times (EE #1 p9, p18; EE #2 p5, p18, p20).
- **The kill is split three ways:** Han's blaster for the body (EE #2 p21), Brand's sacrifice for the spirit (EE #2 p22–23), and R2's navcomputer hack for the Empire's superweapon and throneworld (EE #2 p24–27). Luke's Force is not decisive in EE.
- **Top Force feats in EE belong to the Emperor (Oracle Stone location, lightning kill) and Leia (battle meditation, knock-back).** (ANALYSIS) On EE's evidence alone, Leia lands more hits on him than Luke does.
- **Tech scale outranks individual Force users.** The Galaxy Gun outclasses everything except its own timers, and it is beaten by a hijacked ship, not a Jedi.
