- File: 1 cover + p2–28 (28 image files). Page numbers here equal the file number: p1 = cover, p2 = credits, p3 = crawl, p4–27 story, p28 prose epilogue by Tom Veitch.
- Pages read: p1–28, all, hand-read 2026-11-03.
- Speaker note: several group scenes (p13, p17, p20, p22, p24) don't make balloon tails clear. Those lines are attributed by content or left unattributed, and marked. Wavy balloons on p22–23 are the Emperor's disembodied voice.

## Scaling notes from this issue
- **The Emperor ends on top in raw output but beaten by his body.** In one scene he downs Brand, kills Rayf and floors Leia with lightning (p18–20), after T'iaz volunteers to take Rayf first and loses (p19). His adept says the output is "KILLING YOURSELF" (p18), and by p20 he is "DRAINED" enough to fall to one blaster bolt (p21).
- **Leia is the best Force performer of the Jedi side in EE #2.** She hurts him across space with battle meditation (p7) and knocks him back in person (p18). He calls her "FIERCE" and "IMPRESSIVE", and he credits his "INFERIOR CLONE" for the hit (p18).
- **Brand's spirit capture is the series' finishing move.** It works where every fight before it failed: Palpatine "will never return" (p28).
- **Luke doesn't fight in EE #2.** His contributions are the plan (p12–13), sensing (p13–14) and the surrender offer (p20). The Emperor's "DARK APPRENTICE" taunt (p20) goes untested.
- **Weapons scale:** the Galaxy Gun's last shot destroys a planet, Byss, and the caption rates its projectile as able to neutralize "ALL KNOWN SECURITY SHIELDS" (p26). *Eclipse II*'s firepower is captioned as "HUNDREDS OF HEAVY LASER BATTERIES AND ION CANNONS" (p13), but it ends up as a guided ram.
