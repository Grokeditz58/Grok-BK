# Dark Empire trade paperbacks: extra (non-story) material, hand-read

- **Sources:** three TPB scans from the owner's MEGA folder: *Dark Empire I* (2003, third edition, ISBN 1-59307-039-X; Opus-DCP scan, 153 images), *Dark Empire II Second Edition* (Sept. 2006, collects DE II #1–6 + Empire's End #1–2; stefcuk scan, 203 images), *Empire's End* (Sept. 1997 first edition, ISBN 1-56971-306-5; BlurPixel digital, 58 images).
- **Method:** I made thumbnail sheets of every image in each file and compared them with the single-issue notes (`de1-0*.md`, `de2-0*.md`, `ee-0*.md`). I then viewed every non-story page at full resolution and read all of its text. Quotes are verbatim from the page images, and capitals are kept where the page is set in caps. "img NNN" = position in the scan file, because the TPBs print no page numbers on the extras.
- Hand-read 2026-11-03 (CST). Scans were deleted afterwards, and no images are committed.

## Headline: no Tom Veitch endnotes in this Dark Empire I
The well-known Veitch annotations/endnotes section appeared in the earlier (1993/1995) editions of the *Dark Empire* TPB. **It is not in this 2003 third edition.** After the story (p1–144) the only back matter is a Dark Horse "Timeline of Trade Paperbacks and Graphic Novels" ad page (p145) and the scanner's tag. To read the endnotes we need a 1st or 2nd-edition scan.

None. The extras are credits, an acknowledgment and a recap with no Force, clone or tech claims. The recap only sets up the premise:
> "Six years have passed since the Battle of Endor and the death of the Emperor Palpatine, yet the war for control of the galaxy is still being fought between the remnants of the Empire and the New Republic. ### Power-relevant
**TPB crawl (img 006).** It is reworded from the single issue and adds explicit clone/Anakin intent:
> "With the birth of Leia and Han's third child, Anakin Solo, Luke Skywalker feels a surge of hope for the future of the Jedi Knights."
— Empire's End TPB, "Rage of the Emperor" (img 006)

> "But it is a dark time for the Rebel Alliance: deploying Umak Leth's new terror weapon, the Galaxy Gun, Emperor Palpatine has destroyed Pinnacle Moon and its Rebel Command Base and has driven the Alliance into hiding."
— Empire's End TPB, "Rage of the Emperor" (img 006)

> "Vowing to bring the Rebel Alliance to its knees, Palpatine launches hyperspace projectiles against Rebel troopships . . . and against the Rebel Command Base on Nespis VIII."
— Empire's End TPB, "Rage of the Emperor" (img 006)

> "Thanks to a faulty timing mechanism, the Alliance leaders are able to evacuate Nespis VIII seconds before it is annihilated. To further avoid the Galaxy Gun's deadly precision, the Rebel fleet is forced to disperse throughout the galaxy."
— Empire's End TPB, "Rage of the Emperor" (img 006)

> "Since his last clone body is rapidly nearing its end, drained by the volcanic anger he feels toward the Jedi and Rebel Alliance, Palpatine is determined to rebuild his line of clones and turns to young Anakin to do so."
— Empire's End TPB, "Rage of the Emperor" (img 006)

> "It is up to Luke and his small band of Jedi to protect Leia's children, who are the future of the Jedi. In the name of all the Jedi, Luke pledges to hunt down the Emperor and put an end to his dark reign over the galaxy and restore peace for all time to come."
— Empire's End TPB, "Rage of the Emperor" (img 006)

- Differences from EE #2 p3 (ANALYSIS): the single issue says the body's "life span [is] shortened by" his anger. The TPB says "drained by", and it adds the explicit plan to rebuild the clone line through Anakin. It also calls the Galaxy Gun "Umak Leth's" weapon and gives it "deadly precision".

**Back cover (img 057).** It states the clone-body mechanic and the Jedi-bloodline need outright:
> "The Emperor is back — and this time he's out for blood!"
> "Following his battle with Luke Skywalker in *Dark Empire II*, Emperor Palpatine was forced to occupy an inferior clone-body. That body is now aging rapidly, and Palpatine's final, irrevocable end is near unless he can infuse his line of clones with genetic material from a Jedi bloodline. His target: Han and Leia's youngest child, Anakin. His weapon: the unthwarted Galaxy Gun."
> "Armed with their charge to protect Leia's children, Luke and the Jedi vow to end Palpatine's dark reign for good and restore peace to the galaxy."
— Empire's End TPB, back cover (img 057)

- Statement (ANALYSIS): this is the clearest publisher statement that (a) Palpatine moved into an "inferior" clone after the DE II fight with Luke, and (b) the clone line could only be saved with Jedi-bloodline genetics. It fits EE #1 p9's "GENETICALLY UNSTABLE".

**Epilogue (img 055).** This is the same Tom Veitch prose as EE #2 p28, already logged in `ee-02.md`. The key lines again:
> "In the person of Empatojayos Brand, the fire of the Jedi that was nearly extinguished has flared up into brilliance. Emperor Palpatine, who rose to despotic power on a promise of uniting the galaxy, is dead, his last clone body reduced to ashes. Palpatine will never return, and his spirit will wander forever in the shadowy madness of the evil he embraced."
— Empire's End TPB, Epilogue (img 055)

> "It is Leia's hope that Vima-Da-Boda, who remembers the time when the Jedi were unequaled in strength and wisdom, will live to train Han and Leia's children in the ways of the Force."
— Empire's End TPB, Epilogue (img 055)

> "The remnants of the great fighting force forged by the resurrected Emperor and his dark adepts are scattered throughout the galaxy and across the uncharted worlds of the core."
— Empire's End TPB, Epilogue (img 055)

- Pin-up caption (img 056): "Dave Dorman's cover art from issue two of the *Star Wars: Empire's End* comic-book series." The art shows the Emperor with a crackling Force orb in front of Royal Guards. It is an image only, with no claim.

## Roster / character-file impact
The extras give no new feats, duels or rankings. The new statements are publisher or recap framing:
- Palpatine's "inferior clone-body" after DE II and the Jedi-bloodline requirement (EE TPB back cover);
- "Umak Leth's" Galaxy Gun with "deadly precision" (EE TPB crawl);
- the DE I outcome as a near-"disaster" for Luke (DE II TPB recap).

These add to the existing Palpatine and Galaxy Gun entries but do not change any ranking. The roster is unchanged.
