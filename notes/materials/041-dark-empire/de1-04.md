- (ANALYSIS) The text never says who sent the bad signal. In context, with Luke as "Supreme Commander" of Imperial forces (p3) and Lando's "YOU'D ALMOST THINK HE WANTS TO LOSE," it points to Luke sabotaging the Devastators from Byss. This is an inference.

- Fight (blow by blow):
  1. Leia ignites Vima's saber and cuts a Sentinel's arm to free Han (p23).
  2. The Emperor, in a clone body, disarms her with a burst of Force energy that blows the saber out of her hand (p24).
  3. Under the Emperor's will, a floating capsule above him bursts apart. The placement after the "FORCE SUDDENLY GROWS STRONG IN LEIA" caption and his "WELL DONE... YOUR FIRST STEP TOWARD THE DARK SIDE" make it Leia's doing. The art does not show the source plainly.
  4. The Emperor fires Force lightning from his hand into Leia and drops her unconscious ("JUST A LITTLE DISCIPLINE") (p26).
  5. Han breaks the Sentinel's grip and lunges at Luke. Luke stops him with a raised hand, and Han chokes ("GLAACK!", "GLCK"). This is a Force choke, but a restrained one (p27).
- The speaker of "TRAITOR!" / "OUT OF MY WAY!" (p27) is unclear from the balloon tails. Han is the likeliest.
- Statements (Emperor): Luke is "SO STRONG IN THE FORCE," and Leia is "SO VULNERABLE... SO INEXPERIENCED" but "HOLDS THE KEY TO THE FUTURE." The dark side's "AWESOME DEMANDS" literally consumed his body, which explains the clone cycle and caps what his flesh can take.
- Statement (narration): the Emperor's is the "DARK WILL THAT HAS ENSLAVED A GALAXY."
