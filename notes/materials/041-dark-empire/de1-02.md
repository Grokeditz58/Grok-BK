- Key statements: (a) the reborn Emperor says he too has "grown" since ROTJ ("BUT THEN, SO HAVE I!"); (b) "flesh does not easily support this great power," so his clone bodies keep decaying; (c) he "live[s] primarily as energy"; (d) striking him down in anger means he lives again, possibly "as you" (body-possession threat aimed at Luke); (e) the caption endorses his claim that Luke cannot simply kill him. Luke wins only by learning the dark side ("the dark side has spoken... and for once it speaks truth").
- (ANALYSIS) No fight happens. The comic tells us Luke cannot solve the Emperor by force because of the clone/rebirth loop. That is a durability/resurrection condition, not a statement about raw power.
