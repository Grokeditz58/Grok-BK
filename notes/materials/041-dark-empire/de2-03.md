# Star Wars: Dark Empire II #3 (Feb 1995)

- Script Tom Veitch, art Cam Kennedy, lettering Todd Klein, cover Dave Dorman (p2 indicia: "#3 of 6, February 1995").
- File: 2 cover variants (same art) + p2–27. Page numbers: p1 = covers, p2 = credits, p3 = crawl, p4–27 story.
- Pages read: p1 (both covers) and p2–27, all, hand-read 2026-11-03.
- Speaker note: Cam Kennedy colours Luke and Kam inconsistently from page to page, and both are drawn pale-haired. I attribute their lines by content: names used, captions, and replies. Lines I could not pin to one of them are marked.

## Fight log: Sedriss, Vill Goir and Darktroopers vs. Luke, Kam and the Ysanna (p18–25)
> "MOMENTS LATER, SPECIALLY TRAINED STORMTROOPERS, EMPOWERED WITH THE DARK SIDE OF THE FORCE, EMERGE ONTO THE SANDS OF OSSUS..."
— Star Wars: Dark Empire II #3, p. 19

1. The Imperial hunter *Scourge One* lands. Sedriss: "OUR DARKTROOPERS WILL MAKE SHORT WORK OF THESE SAVAGES, GOIR..." / "THEN YOU AND I WILL TAKE SKYWALKER AND SOLUSAR!" (p19). Kam: "THESE ARE THE DARK-SIDE ELITE I TOLD YOU ABOUT!" (p19).
2. "THE DARKSIDERS ATTACK WITH INEXORABLE FEROCITY. THESE TROOPERS ARE THE BEST IN THE IMPERIAL LEGIONS!" (p20). Rayf and Jem fight with concussion bows (p20).
3. "SHIELDING THEMSELVES WITH THE FORCE, LUKE AND KAM WADE INTO THE ENEMY, WIELDING FORCE POWERS AND LIGHTSABERS IN AN ANCIENT BALLET OF DEATH." (p20)
4. "AFTER TWENTY MINUTES OF FIERCE STRUGGLE, THE NEARLY IMPOSSIBLE ODDS ARE BEATEN--THE YSANNA AND THE JEDI HAVE WON!" (p21). Sedriss: "SKYWALKER HAS WON THE SKIRMISH... BUT HE IS WEAKENED." (p21).
5. Sedriss and Goir use Force choking. Kam: "MY THROAT--LIKE AN INVISIBLE HAND!" / "I CAN FEEL THE CARTILAGE SNAPPING!" (p22).
6. Luke: "THAT'S AN OLD SITH TRICK. IT'S DEADLY... BUT THE HOLOCRON SHOWED ME HOW TO BLOCK IT!" / "IS THROAT CRUSHING THE BEST YOU CAN DO, SEDRISS?" (p22).
7. Sedriss: "CLEAVE THEIR FIGHTING ARMS, GOIR! WE'LL TAKE THEM ALIVE!" (p22).
8. Kam vs. Goir: "CAREFUL, VILL GOIR--YOU FORGET THAT YOU SHOWED ME ALL YOUR MOVES WHEN I WAS ON VJUN!" / "WITH THE ONE-POINTED CONCENTRATION OF A TRAINED JEDI SWORDSMAN, SOLUSAR ENDS THE CAREER OF VILL GOIR!" (p22). This happens in one panel.
9. Luke vs. Sedriss: "WHILE SKYWALKER, A SKILLED JEDI MASTER, UTILIZES A POWERFUL MOVEMENT OF THE FORCE TO HURL SEDRISS TO HIS KNEES." Sedriss: "NYAAAA!" His saber flies loose in the art (p22).
10. Sedriss seizes Jem as a shield: "ONE STEP CLOSER AND I WILL ANNIHILATE THIS WOMAN WITH THE DARK SIDE!" (p23). He senses "THE FORCE IS ACTIVE IN HER... SHE'S TRYING TO USE IT AGAINST ME!" (p23).
11. The tree Ood Bnar grabs Sedriss with its roots (p24). Sedriss "HURLS HIS MIND DEEP INTO THE DARK SIDE, CALLING DOWN ENERGY FROM THE PLANET'S CHARGED ATMOSPHERE--" (p25).
12. Ood: "THIS EVIL ONE IS TRYING TO KILL ME! BUT I AM A GREATER MASTER OF THE FORCE THAN HE." (p25). "OOD DRAWS ON THE LIVING ENERGY OF THE PLANET IN WHICH HE HAS SUNK HIS ROOTS..." This produces a huge white blast (p25).
13. Result: "OOD... GONE!" / "DISINTEGRATED... AND SEDRISS WITH HIM!" / "HE DID IT TO SAVE US, LUKE." (p26). Luke and Jem were "FLUNG TO THE GROUND" (p26).
- (ANALYSIS) After a 20-minute battle that left Luke "WEAKENED" (by Sedriss's claim), Luke still blocks a Sith choke, by his own statement, and takes Sedriss off his feet with one Force move. Kam kills Goir quickly. Sedriss, the Emperor's "MILITARY EXECUTOR", is clearly below Luke here. In #1 he killed two Adepts in about three panels (de2-01). The chain therefore runs: Luke above Sedriss, and Sedriss above the Adepts Nefta and Sa-Di. Ood's self-statement ("GREATER MASTER OF THE FORCE THAN HE") is backed by the outcome, but it was a mutual-destruction blast that drew on the planet's energy.
