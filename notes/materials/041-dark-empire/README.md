# Owner Material 041 — Star Wars: Dark Empire (1991–92), Dark Empire II (1994–95), Empire's End (1995), single issues, hand-read

- **Source:** the single issues of *Star Wars: Dark Empire* #1–6, *Dark Empire II* #1–6 and *Empire's End* #1–2 (Dark Horse, Legends), as digital rips ("Gonk-Empire") from the owner's MEGA folder (Comics VO > Dark Horse > Dark Empire). The main single-issue files were used. Alternates and the TPB were not used.
- **Method:** each issue was downloaded on its own and extracted. Every page image was looked at by the model, in order: all cover variants, credits, crawl, story, back matter. Captions, balloons and SFX were all read. Quotes are verbatim as lettered (comic capitals kept) and were checked against the page image. Speakers are named only when the balloon tail or context makes them clear, and unclear ones are marked.
- **Pages:** "p." is the page within the issue. p1 is the cover set (all variants in the file), p2 credits, p3 recap/crawl, story from p4.
- **Citation:** "— Star Wars: Dark Empire #N, p. X" (likewise *Dark Empire II* and *Empire's End*).
- **Labels:** Feat / Statement / Tech / Fight log, plus (ANALYSIS), which marks my reasoning, not the comic. Only the comics themselves are used, with no outside sources.
- **No quote crops:** like the Legacy (2006) pass (material 039), this pass makes no panel or quote crops. Scans are deleted after notes, and no comic images are committed.
- **Files:** `de1-01.md` … `de1-06.md` + `de1-summary.md` (Dark Empire); `de2-01.md` … `de2-06.md` + `de2-summary.md` (Dark Empire II). `ee-01.md`, `ee-02.md` + `ee-summary.md` (Empire's End; one cover file per issue, so p1 = cover, and EE #2 p28 is the prose epilogue).
- Tracker: `primary/dark-empire/PAGEREAD.md`.

## Three-series scaling takeaway (text only)
- **Emperor:** the strongest Force user across the run. He makes a storm that "rends the fabric of space" (DE #1 p26–27). He outduels Luke in a minutes-old clone (DE #5 p26–27) and raises a fleet-killing Force storm (DE #6 p24–25). His final fight kills Rayf and downs Brand and Leia (EE #2 p18–20). He is beaten three times, and each time not by being overpowered:
  - in DE I by the twins and the unborn child's "LIFE ENERGY" turning his own storm on him (DE #6 p25–27);
  - in DE II, off-panel, by the clone sabotage that left him "GENETICALLY UNSTABLE" (EE #1 p9);
  - in EE by clone decay, Han's blaster and Brand's spirit sacrifice (EE #2 p21–23).
- **Luke:** peaks in DE I, where he shatters the Emperor's saber (DE #6 p22–23). In DE II and EE he is mostly the leader and planner: the Sedriss floor in DE II #3 and the statue topple in EE #1 p26–27. He never beats the Emperor one-on-one in EE.
- **Leia:** grows the most across the run. Force-guided gunnery (DE #1), the Tedryn-Sha kill (DE II #6 p16), then battle meditation and a direct knock-back on the Emperor (EE #2 p7, p18).
- **Dark Jedi (Sedriss, Nist, Tedryn-Sha, Mordi, T'iaz):** they lose every duel that resolves on the page, mostly in one panel. Nist survives his DE II #6 fall and is captured in EE #1 p27.
- **Tech beats the Force on scale.** The World Devastators (DE I) and the Galaxy Gun (DE II #5 Pinnacle Moon; EE *Pelagia*, Nespis VIII and Byss) are the biggest destructive feats. Both are ended by R2-D2's code or navcomputer work (DE #6 p10–13; EE #2 p24–26), not by a Jedi.
