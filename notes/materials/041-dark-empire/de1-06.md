## Luke vs. the clone Emperor, rematch (p22–23)
> "THE GREAT DARTH VADER WAS A SICK MAN IN AN IRON MASK!" / "...BUT THE FEEBLE HEART WITHIN WAS FOREVER POSSESSED BY THE IMPOTENT SIDE OF THE FORCE!" / "YOU CAN BE FAR STRONGER THAN HE WAS!" (Emperor)
— Star Wars: Dark Empire #6, p. 22

> "GET UP! I CAN GIVE YOU THE POWER TO BREAK HER!" / "YOU WILL KILL YOUR SISTER IF I ASK IT!" (Emperor) / "NO... I MADE A BIG MISTAKE... I THOUGHT I HAD TO SAVE THE GALAXY ALL BY MYSELF. BUT THE WAY OF THE JEDI IS NOT A SOLITARY PATH..." (Luke)
— Star Wars: Dark Empire #6, p. 22

> "MY ALLY IS THE FORCE. THROUGH THE STRENGTH OF THE FORCE, YOUR SHROUD OF EVIL HAS BEEN LIFTED FROM MY MIND." (Luke)
— Star Wars: Dark Empire #6, p. 22

> "SO BE IT--" / "--THROUGH THE POWER OF THE FORCE YOU WILL DIE!" (Emperor)
— Star Wars: Dark Empire #6, p. 23

- Fight log (blow by blow):
  1. The Emperor's lightning from his hand knocks Luke down and his saber falls to the floor (p22, drawn; lightning shown hitting Luke).
  2. Luke rises, throws off the Emperor's hold ("YOUR SHROUD OF EVIL HAS BEEN LIFTED") and relights his red saber (p22).
  3. The Emperor leaps in with his saber, lightning crackling around him (p23).
  4. Four wordless panels of exchanges follow. A glowing robed figure, apparently Leia, stands watching in panel 3 (p23).
  5. Luke's slash shatters the Emperor's saber hilt in his hand (p23, drawn: fragments fly). Luke then orders: "NOW, YOUR HIGHNESS, WE WILL ESCORT YOU TO THE ALLIANCE BASE..." (p24).
- Scaling note: this reverses #5. This time Luke, freed of the dark side, beats the same clone body in a saber duel (p23). (ANALYSIS) The Emperor had a settled body this time, and Luke had Leia beside him. So the result can be put down to Luke's restored mind and Leia's support. Nothing on the page shows a raw power change.

- **Major feat (Emperor):** Disarmed, from inside his flagship, he raises a Force storm in space over the Pinnacle moon. The caption says it "RENDS THE FABRIC OF SPACE ITSELF" (p24). Leia says it is "DESTROYING THE WHOLE FLEET! THOUSANDS OF WARRIORS ARE DYING!" (p24), and the Emperor says it is "REDUCING YOUR REBEL FLEET TO DUST!" (p25). Ships are drawn being torn apart (p24). This is fleet-scale, raised in moments.
- **Major feat (Luke + Leia + unborn child):** a joint Force push of "LIFE ENERGY" (p25–26) cuts the Emperor off from his power. His own storm turns on him and "DEVOUR[S]" the ten-mile flagship (p26–27). Leia feels her "THIRD CHILD" join in (p27). (ANALYSIS) The text says this works by separation and redirection. It catches him "OFF GUARD" while "ALL HIS HATRED" is in the storm (p26). That is a timing win, not a power-for-power win, and the text never says the twins could outmatch the storm head-on.
- Luke had warned that the Emperor's "DARK BOOKS" say he has "AWAKENED A CHAOS EVEN HE CANNOT CONTROL" (p25). That is in-text foreshadowing for the result.
- Outcome: the clone Emperor is left in the storm with the flagship, and Luke and Leia escape by shuttle (p26–27). The page does not show his body die.
