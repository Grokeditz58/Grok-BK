## Crawl (p3): "Battle on Byss" (printed in mixed case)
> "Unexpectedly, Sedriss confronts a new adversary — the withered old "tree" is in fact an intelligent being — Master Ood Bnar, a 5000-year-old Neti Jedi! Awakening now from a thousand-year sleep, Master Ood engages Sedriss in a great Force-battle. The encounter ends disastrously when Master Ood draws down a massive charge of energy that destroys them both!"

- Statement: Ood Bnar is a "5000-year-old Neti Jedi" who was in "a thousand-year sleep" (p3). The crawl confirms Sedriss's death: "destroys them both" (p3).

> "XECR NIST--YOU WILL REPLACE SEDRISS AS MY MILITARY COMMANDER." / "TEDRYN-SHA--YOU WILL BE SECOND IN COMMAND." / "I NOW VEST YOU BOTH WITH THE FULL RANK OF DARK JEDI." / "LET THIS POWER ENTER YOU AND FILL YOU WITH THE KNOWLEDGE AND THE STRENGTH OF THE DARK SIDE OF THE FORCE THAT IS MINE TO GIVE--!"
— Star Wars: Dark Empire II #4, p. 9

> "WHO CAN FATHOM THIS MYSTERIOUS POWER AND ITS EFFECTS? WIELDED BY A SAVANT LIKE PALPATINE, THE DARK SIDE CAN KILL A MAN--" / "--OR IT CAN BESTOW THE MOST MALEVOLENT OF GIFTS!" / "THE MAGNITUDE OF PALPATINE'S EMPOWERMENT CAUSES A GREAT DISTURBANCE IN THE FORCE--"
— Star Wars: Dark Empire II #4, p. 9

> "--A SHOCK THAT IS FELT ACROSS THE GALAXY, AMONGST THE RUINS OF ANCIENT JEDI CITIES." / "DID YOU FEEL THAT?" (Luke) / "FEEL WHAT?" (Kam)
— Star Wars: Dark Empire II #4, p. 10

- **Feat (Palpatine, power transfer):** he grants two followers "THE FULL RANK OF DARK JEDI" through a transfer of power. Its "MAGNITUDE" is felt "ACROSS THE GALAXY" (p9–10). On Ossus, Luke feels it and Kam does not ("FEEL WHAT?", p10). (ANALYSIS) The text shows Luke's sensing range is galactic for an event of this size, and that Luke senses more sharply than Kam.
- Statement (Emperor): he credits Luke's wins to Palpatine's own teaching: "I TAUGHT HIM TOO WELL!" (p8). This refers back to Luke's time at his side in Dark Empire I. He also admits weakness: "WITHOUT MY DARKSIDE WARRIORS, SKYWALKER MAY WELL TRIUMPH!" (p8).
- Sensing: after the shock, one of them says "SEDRISS WASN'T LYING. EMPEROR PALPATINE IS ALIVE AGAIN. I KNOW IT." / "BUT WHY DIDN'T I SENSE ANYTHING BEFORE NOW?" (p10). The speaker is not clear from the art; by content it is probably Luke.

## The *Falcon*, Fett, and Ganath (p14–21)
- The *Falcon* destroys two bounty-hunter ships (p15). Fett ambushes it: "HIS LASER-BOLTS FIND THEIR MARK!" (p16). Han hides in an interstellar gas cloud, which Fett's scanner "CAN'T SEE INTO" (p17). "FETT CARRIES A SPARE [helmet] ABOARD SLAVE I." (p17).
- On the far side of the cloud is the isolated Ganath system, "CAUGHT IN AN ANTIQUE ERA OF LOW-TECH, SLOW-SPEED SPACE TRAVEL" (p19). The steam-powered battleship *Robida Colossus* tows the damaged *Falcon* in (p18).

> "LOWER YOUR GUNS, MEN. THERE IS NO DANGER HERE--" / "THESE TWO WOMEN ARE JEDI!" (King Empatojayos)
— Star Wars: Dark Empire II #4, p. 20

> "IT HAS BEEN MANY, MANY YEARS SINCE I HAVE SEEN A JEDI KNIGHT... I THOUGHT VADER HAD KILLED US ALL." / "WHAT LITTLE IS LEFT OF ME!... VADER HUNTED ME AND MY SHIP WAS DESTROYED..." / "...MY RUINED BODY ENCASED IN A PRESSURE SUIT, I FLOATED IN EMPTY SPACE... UNTIL I WAS RESCUED BY THE GANATHANS!" / "THEY SAY A JEDI STRONG IN THE FORCE CAN RULE THOSE LESS POWERFUL THAN HIMSELF. THIS I HAVE DONE... I HOPE WITH JUSTICE!"
— Star Wars: Dark Empire II #4, p. 21

> "IT'S TRUE! THE FORCE IS BRIGHT IN YOU." (Leia, to Empatojayos)
— Star Wars: Dark Empire II #4, p. 21

- Feat (Empatojayos, sensing): he identifies Leia and Vima as Jedi on sight (p20). He survived Vader's purge, after which "MY SHIP WAS DESTROYED" (p21). He does not know Vader is dead: "VADER MUST BE A VERY GREAT WARLORD BY NOW" (p21).
