### Fight log: Sedriss vs. Adepts Nefta and Sa-Di (p25–26)
1. Sedriss confronts the two Adepts over the smashed clone tanks: "MY INTUITION WAS RIGHT-- NONE OF YOU ARE TO BE TRUSTED!" (p25).
2. "THE ADEPT NEFTA HAS BEEN USING A LIGHTSABER TO SMASH THE CLONE TANKS--HE IGNITES IT NOW... TO DEFEND HIS LIFE!" (p26, SFX "ZZRRANNN!"). Blades cross once.
3. Sedriss cuts Nefta down in the next panel ("UHHNG!"): "WHAT'S THE MATTER, NEFTA--IS YOUR MAGIC OF NO USE TO YOU AGAINST A DARK-SIDE WARRIOR?" (p26).
4. Sa-Di pleads, and Sedriss replies: "GOOD--THEN YOU'LL APPROVE MY DECISION TO EXECUTE YOU!" "SEDRISS CLEAVES THE ADEPT IN HALF WITH HIS LIGHTSABER" (p26, SFX "SSHHLURRMM!").
- Result: Sedriss kills two Adepts in about three panels. Neither uses any Force power on the page. (ANALYSIS) Sedriss's line frames the Adepts' power as "MAGIC" that is no match for a "DARK-SIDE WARRIOR" up close. These Adepts are clearly shown as lab-workers or schemers, not duelists, so this is a low-end showing for the Adepts, not a ceiling.

> "I SEE AT LEAST ONE OF MY CHARGEMEN HAS BEEN FAITHFUL DURING MY ABSENCE." (Emperor) / "MY MASTER!" (Sedriss)
— Star Wars: Dark Empire II #1, p. 27

> "BRING ME THE WOMAN JEDI BEFORE SHE HAS HER THIRD CHILD... AND SET ALL YOUR FORCES AGAINST LUKE SKYWALKER!" / "AS FOR THE ARMIES OF THE ALLIANCE, I HAVE MORE THAN A FEW SURPRISES IN STORE FOR THEM!" (Emperor)
— Star Wars: Dark Empire II #1, p. 27

- **Major feat (Emperor):** he survives the DE #6 Force storm and returns in a new clone body, captioned "--REBORN ONCE MORE!" (p27). The Adepts' "NETHERWORLD" line (p25) and the hidden tanks that survived (p23) are the in-text mechanism. How he crossed from the storm to the tank is not shown.
