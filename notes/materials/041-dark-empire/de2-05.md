# Star Wars: Dark Empire II #5 (Apr 1995)

- Script Tom Veitch, art Cam Kennedy (same creative team as #4; p2 indicia: "Star Wars: Dark Empire II, #5 of 6, April 1995").
- File: 2 cover variants (Lando, Threepio and Artoo with a Chrysalis beast behind them) + p2–27. Page numbers: p1 = covers, p2 = credits, p3 = crawl, p4–27 story.
- Pages read: p1 (both covers) and p2–27, all, hand-read 2026-11-03.
- This issue has almost no Force combat. It covers the Byss raid (tech vs. alchemical beasts), Fett's defeat, Empatojayos Brand's statements, Jem Ysanna's training, and the Galaxy Gun's first shot.

## Byss: Viper Automadons vs. the Chrysalides (p4–13)
> "THE DROIDS' ADVANCED MOLECULAR SHIELDING ABSORBS THE BLASTS FROM THE CITADEL'S POWERFUL TURBOLASERS..." / "AND TURNS THE ABSORBED ENERGY AGAINST THE IMPERIAL ARMOR!"
— Star Wars: Dark Empire II #5, p. 5

> "THOSE WAR DROIDS ARE INVULNERABLE TO SHORT-RANGE FIRE!" (officer) / "WHY HAVE YOU NOT ENGAGED THE CHRYSALIDES AS I ORDERED?" (Emperor)
— Star Wars: Dark Empire II #5, p. 5

> "THE EMPEROR'S ADEPTS HAVE UNLEASHED GENETIC ABERRATIONS... ALCHEMICALLY BENT ON DESTRUCTION--" / "--MONSTERS BRED FOR WAR!"
— Star Wars: Dark Empire II #5, p. 7

> "BUT THE WAR DROIDS' SHIELDING IS ENERGIZED BY INCOMING FIRE--AND UTTERLY USELESS AGAINST THE SAVAGE BRAWN OF THE MUTANT BEASTS!"
— Star Wars: Dark Empire II #5, p. 8

Fight log (non-Force):
1. Lando's team sends the Viper Automadons across the moat ("ENGAGE MOLECULAR SHIELDS!", p4). Turbolaser fire is absorbed and turned back on the Imperial armor (p5).
2. The droids start pulling down the turbo emplacements, and turbolifts bring up the beasts "FROM SUB-SURFACE BREEDING PENS" (p6).
3. The Chrysalides tear the Vipers apart: "THE VIPERS ARE GETTIN' RIPPED TA SHREDS!" (p8). Lando: "LOOKS LIKE IT'S GONNA BE NOTHING!" (p8).
4. Salla Zend, Shug Ninx and the smugglers (Lo Khan, Liz Bonbarrin, the Yaka Luwingo) break through checkpoints and fly in with illegal ion guns (p9–11). One ion burst drops a beast (p12).
5. A beast grabs the cargo bay as the ship lifts off. Artoo zaps its claw ("RRG--?"), it falls, and "THE DARK-SIDE CREATURE GETS ITS LAST TASTE OF METAL--THE MAIN CITADEL DEFENSE GUNS!" (p13).
- (ANALYSIS) The Chrysalides are a hard counter to the energy-absorbing Automadons because they attack physically, and they can "EAT METAL" (p11). The text calls them "DARK-SIDE CREATURES" (p5, p13), made through the Adepts' alchemy (p7), but they show no Force use. They are brute-strength threats. Ion weapons and the citadel's own guns can kill them (p12–13).

## The Galaxy Gun destroys Pinnacle Moon (p25–27)
> "THERE IT IS--COMIN' OUT OF HYPER-SPACE... IT'S NOT A SHIP! IT'S SOME KINDA PROJECTILE!"
— Star Wars: Dark Empire II #5, p. 25

> "YOUR BEAMS ARE RIGHT ON TARGET... BUT THE THING IS USING FULL SHIELDS!" / "KEEP YOUR FINGERS CROSSED, KAM. ONE MISSILE CAN'T DO A LOT OF DAMAGE." / "BUT THIS IS NO ORDINARY MISSILE!" / "FIRED FROM THE EMPEROR'S NEW GALAXY GUN, THIS WEAPON CARRIES A PARTICLE DISINTEGRATOR THAT INITIATES A MASSIVE NUCLEONIC CHAIN REACTION!"
— Star Wars: Dark Empire II #5, p. 26

> "THE REACTION BUILDS QUICKLY TO CATASTROPHIC LEVELS, UNTIL EVERY MOLECULE OF THE MOON'S ROCKY CORE BURSTS LIKE A MINIATURE EXPLODING SUN!" / "PINNACLE MOON IS NO MORE." / "MON MOTHMA... THE ALLIANCE... ALL DEAD." (Luke) / "THE ALLIANCE IS FINISHED!" (Kam)
— Star Wars: Dark Empire II #5, p. 27

- Feat (tech): one Galaxy Gun projectile destroys a whole moon (p26–27). This confirms the planet-level claim in #4 p8. (ANALYSIS) Luke and Kam assume everyone at the base died, but the moon's population was seen fleeing at p25 ("IT'S LIKE THEY'RE ABANDONING THE MOON!"). The death toll is not settled on the page.
- Sensing gap (ANALYSIS): Luke picks up "WEIRD SIGNALS" from the projectile through ship instruments (p25). There is no Force warning shown. Luke cannot reach Pinnacle Base by comm ("THEY DON'T ANSWER!", p25).

## Scaling notes from this issue
- There is no Force duel, so this issue only adds context: Brand is a Knight-rank Jedi who survived Vader. Jem is a raw but sensitive apprentice who cuts through a remote in anger. The Galaxy Gun has moon-level destructive power in one shot.
