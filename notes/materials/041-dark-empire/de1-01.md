# Star Wars: Dark Empire #1 — "Book 1: The Destiny of a Jedi" (Dec 1991)

- Script Tom Veitch, art Cam Kennedy, lettering Todd Klein, cover Dave Dorman (credits page, p2).
- File: 7 cover variants (01a–01g: standard, gold, platinum, Dark Horse Classics, 99¢ preview, Wizard Ace, digital original) + p2–27. "p." = the issue's own page label (p1 = cover).
- Pages read: p1 (all 7 covers) and p2–27, all, hand-read 2026-11-03.

## Crawl (p3)
> "One such raid, over the raging Imperial City battleground, ended in disaster: the Alliance Star Destroyer Liberator, commanded by Luke Skywalker and Lando Calrissian, crashed on the planet's surface. Only Luke's skillful use of the ship's deflector shields and anti-grav braking system prevented all aboard from being killed."
— Star Wars: Dark Empire #1, p. 3

> "Then, without warning, civil war erupted, as factions within the Imperial Navy battled the former Emperor's ruling circle for control of the Empire."
— Star Wars: Dark Empire #1, p. 3

- Feat (crawl): Luke used the Liberator's shields and anti-grav braking to bring a crashing Star Destroyer down with no deaths. Piloting/tech skill, not a Force feat as written.

Condition: the walker was "crippled" and "gashed by its own fire" before the push.

- Storm feat: the energy storm comes out of a hyperspace wormhole, sweeps across Imperial City's surface, and lifts Luke, R2 and large pieces of wreckage (walker and vehicle parts are drawn) into the air (p26–27). In this issue the source is only "the other, dark beyond darkness" (named in #2 and #3). The caption calls it a response that "rends the fabric of space itself."
- Statement: "two masters of power" puts the narration's framing of Luke and the unseen Emperor on the same tier.
