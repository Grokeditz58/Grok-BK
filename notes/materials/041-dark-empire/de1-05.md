- Statements (Emperor): (1) he senses Luke's sabotage, "HE CAN HIDE NOTHING FROM ME." (2) He claims everything goes as he "FORESAW." (3) He calls himself "MASTER OF ALL THE JEDI." (4) He can "ENTER ANYONE... OVERSHADOW THE SOUL," including an unborn child. This is essence transfer and possession, stated as a general ability. Here it is only claimed, not shown.
- Statement (Bodo Baas): three who tried to conquer the dark side by learning its secrets all "PERISHED." This frames Luke's plan as historically fatal.
- Feat (Leia): she senses the Emperor's lie at once ("NEVER!"), shoves the old-body Emperor to the floor, and escapes with the Holocron. (ANALYSIS) The weak old body ("FRAGILE FLESH," "HELPLESS OLD MAN") is drawn as physically feeble. The Emperor frames the outburst as a planned test of her "SKYWALKER ANGER."

— Star Wars: Dark Empire #5, p. 22

- **Major feat (Luke):** the "Luke" who rides the *Falcon* into hyperspace and briefs the crew is not physically there: "I'M STILL ON BYSS" (p22). The page does not say when the swap happened. He could have been a projection for the whole reunion with Leia and the escape (p14–21) or only after boarding. Either way, the projection fooled Han, Salla, Chewie, C-3PO and Leia, herself Force-sensitive, from boarding until he chose to reveal it, all while the *Falcon* was in hyperspace. Leia calls it "A DARK-SIDE POWER."
- (ANALYSIS) The text never says the projection physically handled R2. The page shows R2 beside the projected Luke, and R2 arrives with the party.

- Fight log (blow by blow):
  1. Luke gets into the sealed clone lab. The Emperor senses him and says Luke is using powers he gave him (p23).
  2. Luke refuses the body-sharing offer. The Emperor wills his old body to die, and it dissolves in a dark burst (p23). This is a voluntary death to trigger the transfer.
  3. Luke cuts open the clone vats one after another and kills every clone but one, which the Emperor has already entered (p24).
  4. Luke throws the newly decanted clone across the room with a Force blast from his hand (p25). This is the only blow Luke lands.
  5. The clone takes a lightsaber from a wall rack. "GATHERING STRENGTH MOMENT BY MOMENT," he presses Luke "TO THE LIMIT," and Luke can only parry, "DRAWING ON ALL HIS RESOURCES" (p26).
  6. The Emperor knocks Luke's saber away and Luke falls to his knees (p27). The Emperor spares him so he can "SUBMIT."
- Scaling notes:
  - A freshly transferred, naked, minutes-old clone Emperor outduels Luke with a saber, despite Luke's "I AM A JEDI MASTER NOW" (p25–27). The captions say the clone is still "GATHERING STRENGTH." (ANALYSIS) This is not the Emperor at full strength in a settled body.
  - The Emperor says many "SO-CALLED 'JEDI MASTERS' FAILED TO VANQUISH" him (p25) and "I AM THE STRONGER" (p26). These are claims, but the fight backs them up.
  - Luke's projection (revealed p22; DE #6 p3 crawl calls it a "doppelganger" made "USING THE SECRETS OF THE DARK SIDE") is his top feat of skill in this issue. The clone fight is his top loss.
