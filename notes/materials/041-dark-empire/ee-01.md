## Scaling notes from this issue
- **The Galaxy Gun is the dominant power in EE #1.** It one-shots a troopship (p8) and a space city (p17). Its projectiles' shields hold against capital-ship cannons, fighter squadrons and the new Kuat V-200 ion cannons (p7, p14). Its only failure is a faulty timer (p15). Nist counts four firings (p8).
- **The Emperor is limited by his body, not his opponents.** His last clone is "IN ACCELERATED DECAY" (p18), anger speeds it up, and an attack takes him mid-threat (p9). His answer is Jedi-blood clones (p19).
- **Nist's tier:** confident but outclassed. He duels Luke (p26) and is captured alive after Luke's statue topple (p27). Together with his fall to Kam's feint in DE II #6 p18, he has not won a shown exchange against a Jedi.
- **Luke:** the one Force feat is the statue topple (p26–27). He also predicts the plan works ("IT'LL WORK", p24) and openly states he will defeat the Emperor (p27).
- **Ysanna:** sensing at long range from the Ossus chamber (p5). Okko is called a Force user by the Empire's own men (p22), but he is captured without a shown fight.
