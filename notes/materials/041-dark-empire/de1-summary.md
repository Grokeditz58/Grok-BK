# Star Wars: Dark Empire (#1–6, Dec 1991–Oct 1992): series summary and scaling notes

- Source: the six single issues, hand-read page by page (notes `de1-01.md` … `de1-06.md`). Only the comic text and art are used. Every claim below points to an issue and page. (ANALYSIS) marks my own reasoning.
- Citation form: "DE #N p. X". p1 = the cover set, p2 = credits, p3 = crawl, story p4–27.

## Story in one paragraph
Some years after Luke's masters "PASSED ON" (DE #1; no exact date is printed), a Force storm on Imperial City carries Luke to Byss (DE #1–2). There he meets a reborn Emperor living in a string of clone bodies, and he kneels to learn the dark side from within (DE #2–4). Leia, guided by Vima-Da-Boda's lightsaber, goes after him. Han is hunted by Boba Fett on Nar Shaddaa (DE #3–4). Luke secretly sabotages the World Devastators and hides their master codes in R2 (DE #4–6). The Emperor moves into a fifteen-year-old clone and beats Luke in a duel (DE #5, DE #6 p3). Leia talks Luke back from the dark side, and Luke beats the clone with a saber. The twins, with Leia's unborn child, turn the Emperor's own Force storm back on him, and it destroys his ten-mile flagship (DE #6 p19–27).

## Fight log (summary; blow-by-blow in the issue notes)
1. **Luke vs. the Imperial ground forces on Coruscant** (DE #1): one-sided. He beats neks, war droids and an AT-AT.
2. **Leia/Han vs. Boba Fett and Dengar on Nar Shaddaa** (DE #4 p5–9): a running shootout. Nobody is hurt, and the heroes escape.
3. **Leia vs. the Emperor (old clone) on Byss** (DE #4 p23–27): Leia cuts a Sentinel. The Emperor disarms her with a Force burst and lightnings her unconscious. A clear loss.
4. **Luke vs. the Emperor (new clone) in the clone lab** (DE #5 p23–27): Luke smashes the vats and lands one Force blast. The clone outduels and disarms him. A loss.
5. **Luke vs. Leia, dark Luke** (DE #6 p20): blades crossed but no strike. Settled by talk.
6. **Luke vs. the Emperor (clone) on the flagship** (DE #6 p22–23): lightning knocks Luke down. Luke recovers and breaks the Emperor's saber. A win for Luke.
7. **Twins vs. the Emperor's Force storm** (DE #6 p24–27): the storm is turned back and the flagship is destroyed. The Emperor is left inside, and his body's death is not shown.

## Character analyses (text-grounded)
- **Palpatine (clone cycle):** He lives "PRIMARILY AS ENERGY," and his clone bodies keep failing under his power (DE #2, #4). His biggest feats are huge storms: Coruscant in DE #1 and the fleet-killing storm in DE #6, both "rend[ing] the fabric of space." He can disarm and drop Leia with ease (DE #4) and beat a dark-leaning Luke with a saber in a minutes-old body (DE #5). His weak spots are all in the text. He can't control the chaos he raises (DE #6 p25–26). With "ALL HIS HATRED" in one effort he can be caught "OFF GUARD." And Luke beat him with a saber once Leia had drawn Luke out of the dark side (DE #6 p23).
- **Luke Skywalker:** A wide toolkit: telekinesis on droid insides, bolt redirection, pushing a walker over, and above all projection (Byss to Da Soocha in #3, the *Falcon* doppelganger in #5). Mon Mothma calls him "probably the last fully realized Jedi" (DE #3). His record against the Emperor is 1–1 in saber duels (#5 loss, #6 win). In the series' own words his dark-side turn was a "BIG MISTAKE" of going it alone (DE #6 p22).
- **Leia Organa Solo:** She develops fast across the series. Senses Luke from Da Soocha V to Byss (#2), cannon guided by the Force (#1), mind-blinding a gunner in flight (#4), and a focus through Vima's saber (#4). She is outclassed when she faces the Emperor alone (#4). She decides the climax by bringing Luke back and joining her power to his. Her unborn "THIRD CHILD" is felt adding to the Force effort (DE #6 p27).
- **Han Solo:** Not Force-sensitive, and one of the "ORDINARY MEN" (DE #6 p13). He handles the shootouts and the R2 code plan (DE #6 p10).
- **Vima-Da-Boda:** A Jedi for 200 years who hid "AMONG THE LOST." Her lightsaber works as a Force focus for Leia (DE #3–4). No combat feats.
- **Boba Fett:** Survived the Sarlacc (DE #4). In the Nar Shaddaa shootout his wrist rocket misses. No standout feats.
- **R2-D2:** Delivers the series-winning tech feat (DE #6).
- **Bodo Baas (Holocron):** Prophetic statements only (DE #6 p15).
- Kam Solusar, Sedriss, the Dark Side Adepts, Xecr Nist, Empatojayos Brand, Jem and Rayf Ysanne and Carnor Jax do **not** appear in Dark Empire I. They come in Dark Empire II and Empire's End.

## Honest scaling takeaway (text only)
- In this series the Emperor's raw scale is above anything the heroes show. He makes storms that tear apart fleets and city districts, and the narration says they "rend the fabric of space." No hero shows a single feat near that.
- He is beaten here by **cooperation plus redirection, not by out-powering him**. Luke cannot beat him alone (#5, and Luke's own "ONE JEDI CANNOT CONQUER THE DARK SIDE ALONE"). The twins and the unborn child cut him off while he was overcommitted, and his storm did the damage (DE #6 p26). Any "Luke + Leia > Palpatine" claim drawn from DE #6 needs those conditions attached.
- One-on-one duels are tied 1–1 between Luke and the clone, each with heavy conditions. The DE #5 clone was fresh and "GATHERING STRENGTH," and Luke was compromised. In DE #6 Luke was restored and Leia was present.
- Leia is the series' fastest climber. She is not a match for the Emperor on her own (DE #4).
- The Emperor's survival is a matter of the clone cycle and energy form, not toughness. Killing a body does not end him (DE #2), and his fate at the end of DE #6 is left open on the page.
