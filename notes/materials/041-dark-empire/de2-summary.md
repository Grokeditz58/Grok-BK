# Star Wars: Dark Empire II (#1–6, Dec 1994–May 1995): series summary and scaling notes

- Source: the six single issues, hand-read page by page (notes `de2-01.md` … `de2-06.md`). Only the comic text and art are used. Every claim below points to an issue and page. (ANALYSIS) marks my own reasoning.
- Citation form: "DE II #N p. X". p1 = the cover set, p2 = credits, p3 = crawl, story p4–27.

## Story in one paragraph
The Emperor is "REBORN ONCE MORE" in a hidden clone after Dark Empire I. His Darkside Executor Sedriss kills two plotting Adepts (DE II #1 p25–27). Luke, with the redeemed Kam Solusar, goes to Ossus. There they meet the Force-using Ysanna (Jem and Rayf) and beat Sedriss, Vill Goir and a Darktrooper squad, and the ancient Neti Jedi Ood Bnar destroys himself and Sedriss (DE II #3). Han, Leia and Chewbacca find Vima-Da-Boda on Nar Shaddaa, survive Boba Fett and two darksiders, and bring down the Star Destroyer *Invincible* (DE II #2). Hiding in the low-tech Ganath system, they meet the Purge survivor Empatojayos Brand (DE II #4–5). The Emperor empowers Xecr Nist and Tedryn-Sha (DE II #4). Lando's Viper Automadon raid on Byss is shredded by alchemical Chrysalides (DE II #5). Fett is lost in a gas cloud (DE II #5 p20). The new Galaxy Gun destroys Pinnacle Moon with one projectile (DE II #5 p26–27). The Emperor's darksiders, with poison droids and new AT-ATs, ambush the Jedi on New Alderaan. Three Dark Jedi die, Jem Ysanna is killed, and the settlement is razed (DE II #6 p13–21). Smugglers and X-wings rescue the survivors, and on Nespis VIII Leia gives birth to Anakin Solo (DE II #6 p22–27). The Emperor and the Galaxy Gun survive the series.

## Fight log (summary; blow-by-blow in the issue notes)
1. **Sedriss vs. Adepts Nefta and Sa-Di** (DE II #1 p25–26): one blade exchange, then two saber kills. A clean Sedriss win.
2. **Zasm Katth and Baddon Fass vs. Boba Fett** (DE II #2 p6–7): telekinetic disarm and hold. Fett shoots back and runs, and the darksiders let him go. No result.
3. **Han, Chewie and Warb vs. Darktrooper Team Two** (DE II #2 p8–9): "QUICK AND ONE-SIDED--ALL THE CASUALTIES ARE IMPERIAL!"
4. **Chewbacca vs. Boba Fett** (DE II #2 p22–24): Chewie is shot, then rips off the helmet and launches Fett into the roof. A Chewbacca win.
5. **Luke vs. Okko** (DE II #3 p16–17): Luke calls the magic "VERY EASY TO BLOCK" and Okko yields.
6. **Luke, Kam and the Ysanna vs. Sedriss, Vill Goir and Darktroopers** (DE II #3 p18–26): the troopers are beaten after 20 minutes. Luke blocks the choke and floors Sedriss. Kam kills Goir in one panel. Sedriss takes Jem hostage. Ood Bnar's planet-energy blast disintegrates Ood and Sedriss. A Jedi win.
7. **Viper Automadons vs. Chrysalides** (DE II #5 p4–13): the Vipers are shredded. The smugglers' ion guns and the citadel's own guns cover the escape. A Rebel loss with an extraction.
8. **Falcon vs. Slave I** (DE II #4 p16–17; #5 p19–20): Fett hits the *Falcon* in #4. In #5 Brand's lightning gun shorts out *Slave I*, and Fett is lost in the cloud.
9. **New Alderaan** (DE II #6 p13–23): scarab-poison ambush on the sleeping Luke. Jem kills Krdys Mordi, Leia kills Tedryn-Sha, Kam drops Xecr Nist with a feint (Nist survives; see EE #1 p8, p27), and Rayf saves the twins. Jem dies of a blaster wound. AT-ATs raze the settlement, and smugglers plus X-wings get the survivors out. Mixed result: the darksider strike team is beaten, but the Rebels lose the base and Jem.

## Character analyses (text-grounded)
- **Palpatine:** Reborn again (#1 p27). His feats here are strategic and institutional, not personal combat. He empowers Dark Jedi with a transfer felt across the galaxy (#4 p9–10), builds the Galaxy Gun (#4–5), and sends a nightmare to Luke at interstellar range (#6 p14). He says outright that without his darksiders "SKYWALKER MAY WELL TRIUMPH" (#4 p8). He never fights in person in DE II.
- **Luke Skywalker:** Captioned "A SKILLED JEDI MASTER" (#3 p22). He blocks a Sith choke, floors Sedriss with one Force move after a 20-minute battle (#3 p22), blocks Okko's magic with ease (#3 p17), and senses the empowerment galaxy-wide (#4 p10). His weak points in the text: tired and asleep, he is ambushed by masked darksiders and poison droids (#6 p13–15, 21), and his Emperor-made hand "HASN'T WORKED RIGHT" (#6 p13).
- **Kam Solusar:** A "JEDI KNIGHT" per the Holocron (#1 p18). He has a one-panel kill on Vill Goir (#3 p22) and one-panel downs the Executor Xecr Nist (#6 p18). Nist survives to EE #1 p8 and is captured at EE #1 p27. He does not sense the galaxy-wide empowerment that Luke feels (#4 p10).
- **Leia Organa Solo:** Sensing feats (Vima's location #2 p20–21; the Pinnacle Moon blast #6 p4). Heavily pregnant, she kills Tedryn-Sha (#6 p16) and drops Gthull (#6 p19).
- **Sedriss:** The Emperor's Executor. Above the Adepts (#1 p26) and clearly below Luke (#3 p22). Destroyed with Ood Bnar (#3 p25–26).
- **Xecr Nist, Tedryn-Sha, Krdys Mordi:** Empowered or elite Dark Jedi who each go down in one panel to Kam, Leia and the trainee Jem respectively (#6 p15–18). Sha and Mordi die. **Correction:** Nist only falls and is alive in Empire's End #1 p8. (ANALYSIS) Low tier against the heroes in this series despite the "LIVING EXTENSION OF HIS POWER" caption.
- **Ood Bnar:** Ancient Neti Jedi ("5000-year-old", #4 p3 crawl). His planet-energy blast is the biggest single Force output by a hero in DE II, but it kills him (#3 p25–26).
- **Empatojayos Brand:** A "JEDI KNIGHT" (#5 p18), Yoda-trained by his own statement (#6 p10), who survived Vader at the cost of his limbs (#5 p3). Multi-arm combat by caption (#6 p18). Sensing on sight (#4 p20).
- **Vima-Da-Boda:** Hides her presence from darksiders and from Leia (#2 p11–13). Heals poison (#6 p16).
- **Jem and Rayf Ysanna:** Trainees. Telekinesis on a huge boulder together (#6 p10). Jem cuts the remote (#5 p22), kills Krdys Mordi (#6 p15) and dies (#6 p19). Rayf: "A NATURAL WITH THE LIGHTSABER" (#5 p21), and a Force-pull rescue (#6 p19).
- **Boba Fett:** Endures a Force hold and shoots back (#2 p6–7). Beaten by an unarmed, wounded Chewbacca (#2 p22–24). Lost in the gas cloud (#5 p20). Fate not shown.
- **Chewbacca, Han, Lando, Salla, Shug:** Tech and vehicle feats: the *Invincible* takedown (#2), the lightning gun (#5–6), smuggler rescues (#5 p9–13; #6 p22–23).

## Honest scaling takeaway (text only)
- **DE II has no Emperor fight.** His scale is shown through the Galaxy Gun (a moon in one shot, #5 p26–27), the galaxy-felt empowerment (#4 p9–10), and a long-range dream attack (#6 p14). Nothing in DE II overrides DE I's storm feats.
- **Heroes vs. Dark Jedi is lopsided.** Every darksider duel that resolves on the page goes to the Jedi side, mostly in one panel: Goir, Sedriss (by Luke's move, then Ood), Mordi, Tedryn-Sha and Nist. The Emperor's own "WITHOUT MY DARKSIDE WARRIORS" (#4 p8) rates them as necessary but not decisive.
- **What hurts the Jedi is not Force power.** It is masked approach, poison, blasters, AT-AT firepower and a superweapon (#5 p26–27; #6 p13–21). Kam's verdict: "ONCE AGAIN, TECHNOLOGY HAS GOT US BEAT" (#6 p26).
- **Hierarchy shown in the text:** Luke above Kam (sensing, #4 p10), and both above the Dark Jedi. Sedriss is above the Adepts. Okko and the Ysanna's "magic" are below the trained users (#3 p17, 22). Ood Bnar is above Sedriss by his own claim and by the outcome, at the cost of his life.
- **Open at the end of DE II:** Fett's fate (#5 p20), the Emperor alive with the Galaxy Gun (#6 p26), and the surviving darksiders (#6 p20, 23).
