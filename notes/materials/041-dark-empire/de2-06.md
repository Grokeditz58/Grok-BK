### Fight log: Tedryn-Sha and Krdys Mordi vs. Luke (asleep), Jem and Leia (p13–16)
1. The two Dark Jedi get into the settlement unnoticed and send poison-bearing scarab droids onto the sleeping Luke (p13–14). Luke "CAN DIMLY SENSE DARK SHAPES" but does not wake (p13).
2. A Palpatine nightmare hits Luke at the same moment (p14). Luke sees through it as "ONLY A DREAM" but the scarabs are already on his face. He screams and is down (p15).
3. Jem and Leia wake at his cries (p15). Jem cuts Krdys Mordi down with one lightsaber stroke (p15, "NNYUUNH--!").
4. Tedryn-Sha demands Leia's surrender and she refuses, firing a blaster (p15). Tedryn fires back at "BOTH OF YOU" and hits Jem: "UUNNH! LEIA! I'M HIT!" (p16).
5. Leia kills Tedryn-Sha in the next panel (p16, "NYAAAH!"). The weapon is drawn as a white slash through his body. (ANALYSIS) It reads as a lightsaber stroke, but no saber hilt is clearly visible; logged as uncertain.
6. Vima pulls the scarabs off Luke and starts a Jedi healing on the poison (p16).
- Result: both Dark Jedi die; Jem is mortally wounded; Luke is out of the fight. (ANALYSIS) Both darksider deaths are quick one-panel kills by Jem and Leia. Tedryn names himself "PALPATINE'S SECOND IN COMMAND" (p15), freshly given "THE FULL RANK OF DARK JEDI" in #4 p9, yet he fights with a blaster, not the Force, and loses to a pregnant Leia in two panels. Leia's "HAS BEATEN HIS PROUDEST WARRIOR" is her own boast. Luke's loss is to a sleep-time ambush plus a dream attack, not to a fight.
- Statement (Vima): Luke is "OUR LAST JEDI MASTER" (p16).

### Fight log: Kam, Rayf and Brand vs. the remaining darksiders (p17–19)
1. The three Jedi meet the darksiders at the door with lightsabers drawn; the darksiders answer with their own sabers (p17). Rayf's inexperience shows: Kam says he "ALMOST TOOK OFF MY HEAD" (p17).
2. Brand, using several prosthetic fighting arms (one with a built-in saber), is said to "DOMINATE SEVERAL ADVERSARIES AT ONCE" (caption, p18).
3. Kam faces Xecr Nist, newly empowered Executor (#4 p9), who claims "I AM MORE JEDI THAN YOU" (p18). Kam drops him with one feint (p18).
4. Gthull grabs the twins during the fight (p18). Rayf pulls them out of his arms with the Force into Winter's (p19). Leia arrives and drops Gthull, "YYUNNH--", with a beam fired from her hand (p19). (ANALYSIS) Drawn as a shot from her hand; she fired a blaster on p15, so it is most likely the blaster, but the gun is not clearly drawn.
5. Jem dies of the blaster wound despite Vima's healing attempt; light rises from her body (p19).
- Result: the darksiders lose Tedryn-Sha and Krdys Mordi, and Nist is put down (Gthull is dropped but not shown dead). **Correction:** Nist survives. He is alive in Empire's End #1 p8 and captured by Luke in EE #1 p27 and the twins stay safe. Caption: "THE REBELS FEEL FORTUNATE TO HAVE GAINED THE UPPER HAND" (p20). The "SURVIVING DARKSIDERS BREAK FOR COVER" (p20) and later chase the escape in Howlrunners (p23). (ANALYSIS) Nist goes down to one feint after the Emperor's personal empowerment; the comic does not translate that empowerment into a fighting edge.
- Feat (Rayf): Force-pulls two children out of an adult's grip at a distance, calling it "EASY AS TOSSIN' ROCKS" (p19).
- Feat (Brand): multiple simultaneous opponents, by caption only (p18). No individual kill by Brand is shown.
- Name note: the darksider at the interrogation is lettered "KVAG GHULL" on p8 and the child-snatcher "GTHULL" on p17–18. The page does not say whether they are the same person; kept as two names.

## Scaling notes from this issue
- **Dark Jedi tier is low against the heroes.** Three named darksiders go down in one panel each: Krdys Mordi killed by a trainee (Jem, p15), Tedryn-Sha (self-styled "SECOND IN COMMAND") killed by Leia (p16), Executor Xecr Nist dropped by one feint from Kam (p18; **correction:** not killed, since he is alive in EE #1 p8 and captured in EE #1 p27). All three were among those the Emperor had just "empowered" or sent as "LIVING EXTENSION[S] OF HIS POWER" (p8–9).
- **What actually hurt the Jedi was stealth, poison and armor, not Force combat.** The darksiders "MASKED THEIR THOUGHTS" (p21), scarab droids poisoned the sleeping Luke (p13–15), a blaster killed Jem (p16, p19), and the AT-ATs razed the settlement (p20–21). Luke contributes nothing in combat this issue.
- **Emperor at range:** a dream-sending that reaches Luke on New Alderaan (p14), while the Emperor is at Bast Castle on Vjun. No other remote Force attack is shown.
- **Trainee telekinesis:** Jem and Rayf set a boulder several times their height (p10). Rayf Force-pulls two children from a grown attacker (p19). Vima: "AGE MATTERS NOT!" (p10).
- **Healing:** Vima stops poison in Luke (p16) but cannot save Jem from a blaster wound (p19).
- **Brand** is caption-rated to "DOMINATE SEVERAL ADVERSARIES AT ONCE" with multi-arm prosthetics (p18) and is Yoda-trained by his own statement (p10).
- **Galaxy Gun:** described by the Emperor as able to "OBLITERATE THEIR HIDDEN REFUGE WITH A SINGLE PROJECTILE" (p9) and by Kam as "VIRTUALLY INVINCIBLE" (p26). The proven feat stays Pinnacle Moon (#5 p26–27).
