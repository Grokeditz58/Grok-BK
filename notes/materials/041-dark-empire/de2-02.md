### Fight log: Zasm Katth and Baddon Fass vs. Boba Fett (p6–7)
1. Fett draws on the two Darksiders ("BACK OFF--", p6).
2. One Darksider raises a hand. Fett's blaster flies from his grip and he is held in a glow, gasping "KAK!" (p6). A trooper asks: "SHALL I KILL HIM, SIRE?" The answer is: "NO... WE NEED HIS UNDERWORLD CONNECTIONS." (p6).
3. Fett, on his knees and still choking ("NUH...UNH... IMPERIAL SCUM... WON'T WORK FOR... YOU."), fires a wrist rocket and blaster (p7). The blast hits the armored trooper beside them (art).
4. "EVEN A DARKSIDER SHOULD KNOW BETTER THAN TO HARASS A FULLY ARMED BOUNTY HUNTER LIKE BOBA FETT!" (caption, p7). Fett runs for *Slave I*. The Darksiders let him go when a comlink reports the *Falcon* is found: "LET FETT RUN. WE'LL DEAL WITH HIM LATER." (p7).
- Result: Fett breaks free and escapes; no Darksider is shown hurt. (ANALYSIS) The text gives a Dark Jedi a one-handed telekinetic disarm and hold on a fully armored Fett. Fett can still act through it, so it is not shown to be fully incapacitating. The disengagement is the Darksiders' choice, so this is not a win for either side.
