# Darksaber (Kevin J. Anderson, Bantam Spectra 1995): notes part 4, ch. 33–44

- Read 2026-11-04 CST from the owner's epub. This part covers FULL.txt l.3734–4949.
- Citation: "DS ch. N (l.x)". Tags: FEAT / ANTI-FEAT / ATROCITY / LORE / SCALING.

## Ch33 (l.3738), HOTH (l.3734)
- Luke and Callista kill wampas; Callista decapitates one (l.3742–3744). Nonak is torn apart (l.3758). Drom Guldi is a precise marksman (l.3760–3766).
- The wampas pound on the door as a distraction while they dig through the ice walls (l.3838–3840). Sinidic, Nodon, Burrk and Drom Guldi are killed (l.3820–3852). Callista fights without the Force (l.3854).
- Luke, "putting all the Force behind his swing," cleaves the one-armed wampa in half (l.3868–3872). FEAT.
- They cross-wire the yacht and make a blind ballistic takeoff with no comm, no navicomputer and 5% life support (l.3884–3898).

## Ch34 (l.3904), NAL HUTTA (l.3900)
- Durga: Hutts are "businessmen, not warriors" (l.3910).
- The shell-creature information broker holds records on about 5 billion beings (l.3918–3928). It reports that the Empire is unified, the warlords executed, construction up tenfold, and women and aliens now serving (l.3936). A major assault is planned (l.3942), and the commander is Daala (l.3952).

## Ch35 (l.3964), CORE SYSTEMS (l.3960)
- Daala's machine (l.3966–3972). The Night Hammer's ready room is as big as a Victory's command deck; it runs on a small crew with massively redundant automation (l.3974–3976).
- Fleet: 112 Victory-class SDs under Cronus, 45 ISDs and the Night Hammer (l.3992–3996). SCALING.
- Targets: Coruscant is too costly, so the Yavin 4 Jedi academy is chosen (l.4008–4018); Tarkin died at Yavin (l.4020).
- The plan: Cronus hits dozens of minor targets, Pellaeon's ISDs attack Yavin 4, and Daala follows in the Night Hammer (l.4026–4028).

## Ch36 (l.4034)
- Kyp and Dorsk at a nexus station in a stolen Imperial shuttle; TIE factories (l.4046). A rally of tens of thousands (l.4066–4070); aliens now serving (l.4074). Pellaeon announces the attack on the Jedi training center (l.4092).
- Kyp's history: Daala captured him and Han and ordered his execution (Cauldron Nebula); he thought she died at the Maw (l.4106).
- Kyp's outburst (l.4110). He severs a trooper's hand and cuts down a second, Dorsk kills a third, and they deflect bolts (l.4122–4130). They escape with the sublight engines hit and make a short jump to Khomm (l.4136–4164).

## Ch37 (l.4170), HOTH ASTEROID BELT (l.4166)
- Madine's A-wings. Friends including Carlist Rieekan convinced Madine to defect (l.4182).
- Flashback: Mon Mothma opposed assassinating Palpatine at Endor until Madine and Ackbar convinced her; the Sullust decoy (l.4188–4200). LORE.
- Korenn dies when debris breaches his A-wing's shields and he hits an asteroid (l.4222–4236).
- Madine sees the cylindrical fortress with its superlaser; the Hutts "extensively modified" the Death Star plans (l.4258–4260).

## Ch38 (l.4266)
- The yacht's hyperdrive is gone and the sublights are at half power (l.4270–4272). Callista resisted the dark side against the wampas (l.4280). Life support is at 10% (l.4282), and the one patched suit goes to Callista (l.4288–4302).
- Luke enters a deep Jedi trance that slows his metabolism to near suspended animation (l.4296, 4306). FEAT. Callista can't even begin a trance (l.4308). ANTI-FEAT. The temptation "Join the dark side!" (l.4312–4316).
- Luke sends a Force call across space to Leia, as he did at Cloud City (l.4324–4328). FEAT (telepathy).

## Ch39 (l.4330)
- Leia hears Luke's call on the diplomatic shuttle (l.4338–4354) and follows it to locate the yacht (l.4392). FEAT (Leia's sensing). Chewie's hug makes "bones crack" (l.4364).
- Leia orders yellow alert while Wedge continues the maneuvers (l.4384–4388).
- The Falcon finds the claw-scratched yacht (l.4396). Luke is frozen in the trance, hours from death, kept alive by "only his will to survive" (l.4410–4418). FEAT/ANTI-FEAT. He revives (l.4422).

## Ch40 (l.4428)
- Lemelisk's austere office on the nearly complete Darksaber (l.4430–4432). He spots a ship while the Taurill are distracted again (l.4434–4440); inspection scooter (l.4444–4448).
- Flashback: Tarkin is governor at Eriadu, with Ackbar as his pilot. Ackbar drops the shields, and 3 Rebel Y-wings rescue him. Tarkin and Lemelisk escape in a pod and are picked up by Motti's ISD (l.4454–4508).
- The Death Star blueprints were stolen and sent to the Toprawa Relay Station, and Vader hunted the Toprawans. Tarkin: "They won't find a flaw" (l.4510–4514). LORE.
- Darksaber problems: the computer cores crash, the engine-shielding sheeting has millions of micro holes, and the front and aft girders don't match up, so a misaligned superlaser could vaporize the Darksaber itself (l.4522–4526). ANTI-FEAT.

## Ch41 (l.4532)
- Madine and Trandia spacewalk to the Darksaber (l.4544–4560). The hull plates are mismatched and loose and "couldn't possibly hold an atmosphere"; they pry one off with a crowbar (l.4562–4566). Dangling wires and dead terminals: "a disaster waiting to happen" (l.4580). ANTI-FEAT.
- The guards are Gamorreans, Weequays, Niktus and Aqualish (l.4584). A mesh alarm trips (l.4592).
- Trandia draws fire, is shot in the arm, and detonates all her charges, killing the guards and herself. The blast barely damages the engine containment wall (l.4598–4606). Madine is captured (l.4618–4630).

## Ch42 (l.4632)
- The Darksaber is "technically complete" through the "mutual annihilation of errors" (l.4636–4638). The Hutt "outreach program" will ransom planets (l.4640).
- Booby-trapped seats: Durga electrocutes the wrong crewman, a Weequay. "Sorry, wrong button" (l.4674–4676). ATROCITY.
- Palpatine executed Lemelisk 7 times: by airlock, in a molten copper vat ("what the smelter used that day"), and with acid mist (l.4686–4690). LORE/ATROCITY.
- Madine triggered his palm transmitter through the Holonet to Ackbar (l.4706). Madine and Sulamar recognize each other: "You!" (l.4718–4720).

## Ch43 (l.4726), KHOMM (l.4722)
- Khomm's complacency (l.4728–4738). Kyp sends a wide-band alert (l.4740). Dorsk 81 confronts Kaell 116 and Dorsk 80 and refuses to stay (l.4752–4798).
- Daala adapts: launch immediately, and Cronus puts Khomm at the top of his list (l.4816–4836).
- Cronus on the 13X destroys the satellites and orders an indiscriminate bombardment with TIE bombers (l.4838–4852). ATROCITY. He does isometric exercises (l.4844).
- Dorsk 82 survives, but the cloning facility is destroyed and Dorsk 80 is dead (l.4860–4872). Two TIEs lost (l.4880).

## Ch44 (l.4892), NAL HUTTA (l.4888)
- The Yavaris has been on yellow alert for a day (l.4896). Madine's distress signal arrives (l.4908).
- Kyp's broadcast: Daala is alive and the target is Yavin 4 (l.4922–4928).
- The fleet splits: Ackbar and the Galactic Voyager go to Yavin 4, and Wedge with the Yavaris and Dodonna goes to the Hoth belt (l.4934–4948).
