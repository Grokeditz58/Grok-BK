## Ch13 (l.1752), HOTH ASTEROID BELT (l.1748)
- Durga's narcotic smoke (l.1756). Lemelisk and Qwi Xux developed the concept and prototype at the Maw (l.1766, 1788).
- Sulamar boasts of the Scourge of Celdaru, the Massacre of Mendicat "without losing a trooper," and "a hundred worlds in my fist" (l.1778). Lemelisk has never heard of him (l.1780).
- Flashback: at Palpatine's touch, the hologram lines bend and cringe (l.1790). LORE/minor FEAT.
- Death Star specs: 100 km diameter, planetary shielding, turbolasers, tractor beams, ion cannons, hyperdrive; the superlaser can crack worlds (l.1792–1800). About 1 million crew, "worth a thousand Star Destroyers" (l.1804). SCALING.
- Exploiters Gamma and Delta come online (l.1810).

## Ch14 (l.1820)
- Lemelisk strips the design down (l.1830).
- Flashback: Tarkin's slave Ackbar pilots a Lambda shuttle to the Death Star construction site at Despayre in the Horuz system (l.1836–1842). The prison labor is expendable (l.1844), and the debris will doom the colony (l.1850). ATROCITY.
- The Darksaber design: the superlaser in an armored cylinder with the focusing eye at one end, firing a straight shot for better energy conversion, plus a living ring for hundreds of Hutts (l.1872–1878). It looks like a lightsaber, hence the "Darksaber Project" (l.1882–1884). Sulamar: they will hold systems hostage (l.1888).

## Ch15 (l.1898), MULAKO CORPORATION PRIMORDIAL WATER QUARRY (l.1894)

- Luke can't sense her moves; she is "a disconcerting blank." The duel runs nearly an hour and ends in a stalemate (l.1940–1948). FEAT (Callista's skill without the Force; Luke presumably holding back).

- Callista comes from the waterworld Chad, raised on a sea ranch with wander-kelp; her family is dead (l.2002–2028). Djinn Altis's ship, the Chu'unthor, was found crashed on Dathomir (l.2032–2036). LORE.

- Luke's backstory: Owen and Beru, Biggs, Camie and Fixer (l.2060–2074).

## Ch18 (l.2194), CORE SYSTEMS (l.2190)
- Pellaeon comes aboard with 1:45 left (l.2222–2226). Harrsk yields at 1 minute (l.2234), and Daala pauses the countdown at 30 seconds (l.2238–2242).
- The stellar-desert rendezvous was found on the Gorgon's trip back (l.2264).
- Daala's backstory: at Carida she was held back as a woman and made a computer clerk and galley overseer. Under a false identity in the Caridan network simulations she developed tactics innovating on Dodonna's, which were studied fleet-wide. Tarkin found her through 2 slicers and made her an admiral (l.2284–2302). LORE.
- Tarkin orbits a lieutenant in an environment suit to die (l.2308). ATROCITY. The Emperor disliked women and aliens; Thrawn was exiled to the Unknown Regions (l.2288–2292). LORE.
- Pellaeon suggests a detente council. Daala gives him a breathmask membrane (l.2320–2326).
