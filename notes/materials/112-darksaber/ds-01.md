## Ch1 (l.242), TATOOINE (l.238)
- Han and Luke travel disguised as Tuskens. Luke is "using the Force to distract anyone who pays too much attention" (l.264). "Never before had Han seen his friend manipulate so many minds at once" (l.266). FEAT: mass mind-muddling of a Tusken band over days.
- Limit: if they all focused, "not even a Jedi Master would be able to keep up the charade" (l.268). Luke hasn't slept in 2 days because he has to keep monitoring their thoughts (l.362–364). FEAT/endurance.
- Tusken lore: the bantha bond and exile (l.286–298), the storyteller's apprentice killed for one wrong word (l.344–352), krayt pearls (l.338), the band that took a stormtrooper regiment (l.342). LORE. Luke has a cyborg hand (l.290).

## Ch2 (l.372)
- Luke uses a sandwhirl plus the Force to distract the band so the two can split off unnoticed (l.392–402). FEAT.
- Jabba's palace: B'omarr monks, Alkhara (l.412–418). The Tuskens see their tracks and attack (l.436–442). ANTI-FEAT (he released control too early).
- Luke Force-moves the heavy door mechanisms and slams the durasteel door down (l.454–456). FEAT. Green lightsaber (l.460).
- B'omarr brain jars (l.472). Maizor, Jabba's rival, is a brain in a spider-walker (l.494–500).
- The Hutts stole Jabba's passwords to the Imperial Information Center on Coruscant to build an "invincible weapon" (l.516–536). LORE (plot hook).

## Ch3 (l.544)
- Rebuilt swoops (l.550). Luke and Callista are linked, but she has lost all her abilities (l.566–572).
- At Obi-Wan's hut Luke calls Ben and gets no answer (l.586–616). He built his own saber after Cloud City (l.588); C'baoth taught perversions (l.590). Luke resolves that he is no longer a student (l.620).

## Ch4 (l.640), HOTH ASTEROID BELT (l.636)
- Durga the Hutt (birthmark, l.648) aboard an Orko SkyMine ship, with "Imperial General" Sulamar (medals, boyish) (l.652). Orko SkyMine is a sham corporation (l.658).
- Mineral Exploiters Alpha and Beta, with turbolaser turrets and a processing maw (l.672), were designed by Bevel Lemelisk, who built both Death Stars (l.676–680). They lock onto a moving pure-metal target (l.686–700).

## Ch5 (l.738)
- The Exploiters had detected each other, and only Lemelisk has the override (l.704–714). Durga electrocutes a Devaronian through a booby-trapped chair (l.720–722). ATROCITY. The Exploiters shred each other in under 10 minutes (l.730–734).
- Lemelisk: paunchy, spiky white hair, forgetful, plays 3-D crystal-lattice puzzles (l.744–746). Sulamar's claimed exploits: the Massacre of Mendicat, the Subjugation of Sinton, the Rout of Rustibar (l.752).
- Flashback: after Yavin, Palpatine executes Lemelisk with piranha beetles in a wire cage that the Emperor lowers "as if... directing it with invisible powers" (l.776–814). On the exhaust port: "I knew I must have forgotten something" (l.786).
- Lemelisk is revived in a clone body, with his memories transferred through an ancient artifact; the technique was stolen from a Jedi (l.816–824). LORE. He has been executed several times (l.838). His goal is the plans in the Imperial Information Center (l.840–846).

## Ch6 (l.852), CORUSCANT (l.848)
- The twins are 3; Anakin is nearly 2 (l.878). Winter's perfect memory (l.890).
- Hutt lore: they come from Varl; Nal Hutta is the "glorious jewel"; Nar Shaddaa is the Smugglers' Moon; clan names are secret (l.906–914). Durga is fatter than Jabba (l.942). The Taurill are 4-armed simian "pets" (l.944, 960).
- Leia Force-probes the room: the Gamorreans and Taurill are faint, and Durga is a "blank wall." Hutts may be genetically shielded, and Luke couldn't read Jabba (l.962–964). ANTI-FEAT/LORE.
- Durga proposes a legalization alliance; kajidic (l.978–988). The Taurill trigger a blaster incident as a diversion (l.992–1004).
- The Taurill are a hive mind with an Overmind (l.1014); Durga killed the xenobiologist who discovered them (l.1016). ATROCITY.
- Three Taurill slice the Imperial Information Center with Jabba's codes and download the plans (l.1020–1032). Leia counts 97 of 98 (l.1042–1044). Invitation to Nal Hutta (l.1048).

## Ch7 (l.1064), YAVIN 4 (l.1060)
- Callista cannot touch the campfire that the trainees shape as a simple exercise (l.1072–1076). LORE.
- Tionne: pearl eyes, silver hair, historian, "not one of Luke's stronger trainees" (l.1084–1086). Ulic Qel-Droma was "blindfolded to the Force" (l.1096–1100). LORE. Callista knew Obi-Wan as a young military commander (l.1122).

## Ch8 (l.1136)
- Graduation by the river: Kyp, Cilghal (organic-looking saber) and Dorsk 81 become Jedi Knights (l.1160–1168). Kyp plans to snoop on the Empire (l.1174–1178).
- Trainees: Tionne, Kam Solusar, Kirana Ti, Streen (l.1182). Under Kun's influence Kyp went to Kun's jungle fortress and saved Dorsk (l.1202). Khomm has no moons and no seasons and lies near the galactic center (l.1206). Dorsk 82 (l.1212).

## Ch9 (l.1222), CORE SYSTEMS (l.1218)
- Daala is working to unify the warlords, with Kratas. Supreme Warlord Harrsk (half-scarred face, droid eye, an Endor veteran) holds 12 ISDs at a red giant (l.1226–1256).
- Daala's past: she brought 4 ISDs out of the Maw, lost 3, hit Calamari, and the Gorgon was scrapped (l.1234–1238). Warlord titles (l.1240).
- High Admiral Teradoc's 73 crimson Victory-class SDs attack (l.1290). Kratas, commanding the Shockwave, kills a 6th Victory and cripples another (l.1302–1304), then the Shockwave is destroyed and Kratas killed (l.1306–1318). 62 Victories survive (l.1320). SCALING.
- Harrsk forces Daala at gunpoint to lead a retaliation (l.1336–1346).

## Ch10 (l.1348)
- Daala on ISD Firestorm, with 8 ships (l.1350–1354). Harrsk, on the Whirlwind (l.1362), orders a full frontal attack (l.1366).
- Teradoc's ring-system fortress: proximity-charge planetoids cripple 1 ISD and destroy 2, leaving five (l.1382–1398). Hollowed-planetoid garrisons (l.1412).
- Daala refuses to engage the Victories and takes personal weapons control (l.1414–1430). She stuns the weapons sergeant and ion-cannons Harrsk's Whirlwind dead in space (l.1442–1444), threatens to execute mutineers (l.1448) and shields the Whirlwind (l.1450). FEAT (tactical/command).
- Broadcast speech (l.1464–1476). She starts a 15-minute self-destruct countdown unless Harrsk ceases hostilities (l.1484–1490).
- Vice Admiral Pellaeon is on the Victory-class 13X (l.1492), disgraced after Thrawn (l.1500), missing the Chimaera (l.1502). He orders a cease-fire (l.1516–1518).
- Teradoc has tripled in girth and hides in a bunker (l.1524–1526). Pellaeon took the Chimaera after Endor (l.1532–1534). "You, sir, are no Grand Admiral Thrawn" (l.1538). He shuttles to Daala (l.1540).
