# Darksaber (Kevin J. Anderson, Bantam Spectra 1995): notes part 6, ch. 54–63, end of novel

- Read 2026-11-04 CST from the owner's epub. This part covers FULL.txt l.5910–6722.
- Citation: "DS ch. N (l.x)". Tags: FEAT / ANTI-FEAT / ATROCITY / LORE / SCALING.

## Ch54 (l.5914), YAVIN 4 (l.5910)
- A single Knight Hammer shot obliterates an acre of old growth (l.5920). FEAT (orbital fire).
- Without the Force, Callista kills a TIE bomber pilot with her lightsaber, first the hand and then the chest (l.5934–5936), and steals the bomber (l.5938–5948). FEAT (skill).
- She fakes a damage report through static and is waved into an evacuated bomber bay full of armed TIE bombers (l.5956–5980).

## Ch55 (l.5982)
- The Falcon, with Kyp and Leia on the guns. A concussion missile kills a Flying Fortress (l.6008–6010). Han's chicken run again (l.6016–6018).
- Kyp wants Daala (l.6030). Han recalls the torture chair on the Gorgon (l.6040). Han hails Daala and draws her fire (l.6052–6054). 4 TIE squadrons; 3PO counts 218 TIEs (l.6062–6068).
- Ackbar's Galactic Voyager and 4 Corellian gunships arrive and wipe out half the TIEs on the first pass (l.6082). FEAT. Ackbar hates Daala for the attack on Calamari (l.6096).
- Cronus arrives with 20 Victories (l.6122) and kills gunships. The Knight Hammer accidentally hits one of Cronus's ships (l.6136).
- A crippled Corellian gunship rams the 13X, killing Cronus (l.6140–6144). 3 Victories are destroyed and 1 gunship is left (l.6148). The Voyager's shields fail (l.6156–6160).

## Ch56 (l.6162)
- Pellaeon's view: the fleet was "hurled across space as if a giant hand had slapped them" and thrown far outside the solar system in seconds (l.6188). Crew injured or dead; hyperdrive damaged, navicomputer wiped; weeks at sublight (l.6164–6196). FEAT CONFIRMATION (Dorsk's push).

## Ch57 (l.6206)
- Callista sets timers on the bombers' missiles, aims them at the bulkhead to the engines, and sets up a chain reaction through the bombers' fuel tanks (l.6228–6250). Eye of Palpatine recap (l.6236).
- The explosions breach containment and rip out the engine core. The 8-km Knight Hammer is "far too immense to be destroyed" but drifts dead in space (l.6266–6278). FEAT (Callista's sabotage, no Force). She heads to the bridge to face Daala (l.6264, 6276).

## Ch58 (l.6280)
- Pellaeon's fleet is found at the system's edge: "Jedi Knights physically hurled them out of the system," days away (l.6290–6298). SCALING.
- The Knight Hammer's rear explodes and the engines are gone. Bomber bays 14 and 17 are the source; the rear third is sealed; hull breaches on decks 293, 181 and 75; toxic and radioactive waste (l.6304–6318).
- Cronus is dead (l.6330). The ship needs "months to refit" (l.6338) and is drifting into Yavin (l.6344–6352).
- New Republic reinforcements: Assault Frigates, Corvettes, 5 more Mon Cal cruisers, Loronar Strike Cruisers and Carrack gunships (l.6356).
- The crew fits into hundreds of evacuation pods (l.6366). Daala orders abandon ship and stays at the helm for show (l.6368–6382).

## Ch59 (l.6384)
- Daala's command-level escape pods (l.6394). Callista is waiting (l.6402–6404).
- Callista embraces the dark side and deflects four kill-setting blaster bolts with her saber (l.6416–6430). FEAT (dark side).
- Daala switches to STUN. The spreading arcs overwhelm the saber block, short-circuit it and drop Callista (l.6432–6434). ANTI-FEAT. Daala leaves her (l.6440).

## Ch60 (l.6442)
- The Jedi guerrilla defense routs the ground assault, and most TIEs are knocked down by Force-hurled projectiles (l.6444). FEAT (collective).
- Luke cuts through a Massassi tree with his saber and Force-topples it onto an AT-ST (l.6456). FEAT.
- Luke resolves that he wants Callista regardless (l.6462–6466). He senses her dark-side flicker, then nothing (l.6474–6484).
- The Knight Hammer plunges into Yavin (l.6486–6488).

## Ch61 (l.6494), HOTH ASTEROID BELT (l.6490)
- Wedge recovers Lemelisk's scooter (l.6504–6526). Qwi confronts Lemelisk: he lied that the weapons were peaceful (l.6540); "incredibly dense" (l.6542).
- Lemelisk recounts Madine's execution (l.6554). His request for his own execution: "just make sure you do it right this time" (l.6558).

## Ch62 (l.6564), YAVIN 4 (l.6560)
- Pellaeon's 17 SDs are at the system's edge, and the Victories have fled to the Core (l.6566–6568).
- Daala, in a command pod, resigns and hands command of all Imperial forces to Pellaeon (l.6584–6602).

## Ch63 (l.6608), CORUSCANT (l.6604)
- Madine's memorial. Leia's speech covers Dorsk 81's sacrifice, the devastation of Khomm, Callista believed dead with Daala, the scuttled Darksaber, and honors for Madine (l.6638–6654).
- Luke Force-lifts R2 out of the X-wing (l.6664). Dorsk is buried on Yavin 4 (l.6670–6674).
- Callista's recorded message: she escaped in one of Daala's command-level pods. Her powers feel closer, but a dark-side wall blocks them, so she leaves on her own odyssey: "there is a lot of time in the universe" (l.6698–6714).
- End of novel (l.6722).
