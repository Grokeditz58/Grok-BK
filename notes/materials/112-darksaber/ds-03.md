# Darksaber (Kevin J. Anderson, Bantam Spectra 1995): notes part 3, ch. 22–32

- Read 2026-11-04 CST from the owner's epub. This part covers FULL.txt l.2628–3733.
- Citation: "DS ch. N (l.x)". Tags: FEAT / ANTI-FEAT / ATROCITY / LORE / SCALING.

## Ch22 (l.2632), NAL HUTTA (l.2628)
- Wedge is on the escort frigate Yavaris, with Qwi Xux as science officer (l.2634–2640). The assault frigate Dodonna (a modified Dreadnaught) and 6 Corellian Corvettes (l.2642–2644). Ackbar's Red Team: the Galactic Voyager plus 3 gunships (l.2648).
- Crix Madine: Supreme Allied Commander for Intelligence, led the Endor ground assault, Imperial defector (l.2662–2664).
- War game: Wedge's net trap beats Ackbar's dagger. "You were too predictable, Admiral" (l.2680–2726). FEAT (tactics). Madine preps commandos (l.2736).

## Ch23 (l.2738)
- Leia's Corellian Corvette arrives, sending notice only half an hour ahead (l.2746). She browbeats a thin Hutt; Durga is "away on business" (l.2748–2760). Leia's aim is to stop Death Star proliferation (l.2776).

## Ch24 (l.2786)
- The Nal Hutta bog and oily rain (l.2788–2792). Korrda is an emaciated Hutt envoy with a wasting disease (l.2808–2818, 2868–2872). The Hutts have lifted the bounty on Han and Leia (l.2826).
- Durga's palace has famous dungeons (l.2836). Leia senses Korrda's honest confusion; the palace oozes echoes of pain that overwhelm her (l.2846–2848). LORE (Leia's empathy).
- Huttlings (l.2858). The bathhouse labyrinth; Durga surfaces in the bog canal (l.2880–2926). Leia sees through his stalling (l.2934).
- Durga: "the Empire may be doing more than you think." He offers his information broker (l.2944–2950).

## Ch25 (l.2956)
- Madine, in disguise, in the Nal Hutta slums; Hutts use worlds up (l.2964).
- A droid moth from Mechis III plants a microscopic tracer "egg" on Durga's yacht, then self-destructs (l.2976–2996). LORE (tech).

## Ch26 (l.3002), DAGOBAH (l.2998)
- Callista wants places that are personal to Luke (l.3012); jeru tea (l.3018).
- Dagobah: Yoda lifted the X-wing here (l.3028) and died after 900 years (l.3050); Luke's training memories (l.3054).
- Callista: "Why do you stay with me?" She is all-or-nothing (l.3068–3082).
- Nightbat swarm: Luke uses the Force and his saber. Callista hacks with brute force and kills three in one stroke (l.3086–3096). Her anger lets her flicker in the Force, and she pushes the swarm away (l.3098–3100). FEAT (dark side, involuntary). "It was the dark side" (l.3114); she refuses to use it again (l.3120). Luke's blade is "yellow-green" here (l.3090).

## Ch27 (l.3126), HOTH ASTEROID BELT (l.3122)
- Sulamar struts like Tarkin (l.3130). The Darksaber is a lattice cylinder (l.3134). Sulamar has promised surplus computer cores (l.3136).
- Thousands of Taurill in 4-armed environment suits work the construction (l.3144–3150).
- Flashback: Tarkin executes a Despayre convict work crew, and he enslaves Wookiees on Kashyyyk with 600 troopers and burns the forest. Wookiees also built the Maw installation (l.3154–3186). ATROCITY.
- Because the Taurill are easily distracted, girders go in wrong, the computer core receptacle is placed on the waste-heat exhaust, and the superlaser anchors are 90 degrees off (l.3194–3198). ANTI-FEAT (construction).

## Ch28 (l.3212), HOTH (l.3208)
- Callista is afraid to try after Dagobah (l.3218). The Battle of Hoth was "nine years ago" (l.3240).
- Jury-rigged blaster-cannon turrets fire. Callista shoves Luke; he Force-flings himself and leaps a shot (l.3266–3270).
- He deflects a blaster-cannon bolt with his saber but reels; only his synthetic hand let him withstand it (l.3272). FEAT/ANTI-FEAT.
- He climbs a turret, severs its barrel, and hacks through the armored plating of a second turret (l.3280–3284). FEAT. Callista draws fire, and a blast hurls her into the air (l.3282).
- Survivors: Burrk (ex-stormtrooper), the Cathar Nodon and Nonak, Baron-Administrator Drom Guldi of Kelrodo-Ai, and his aide Sinidic (l.3302–3312).
- The wampas have learned teamwork; they killed the party and blew up its ship (l.3320–3332). They also wreck the yacht's comm, navicomputer and environment suits (l.3372–3376). The wampa army is led by a one-armed wampa, Luke's old one (l.3386–3388).

## Ch29 (l.3394), NAR SHADDAA (l.3390)
- The Taurill rebuild a section in 2 days (l.3398). Durga sends Lemelisk to inspect Sulamar's computer cores on Nar Shaddaa (l.3412); he doesn't believe the New Republic suspects anything (l.3424). Twi'lek pilot with a scarred head-tail (l.3430).
- Flashback: Vader comes to the first Death Star "to motivate your workers." He Force-chokes two foremen to death with a crunch, and their bodies are wired to the hull (l.3436–3460). FEAT/ATROCITY (Vader).

## Ch30 (l.3476)
- Wedge and Qwi on Nar Shaddaa. Qwi was brainwashed, designed the Death Star with Lemelisk and built the Sun Crusher; Kyp erased her memory (l.3486–3488). Her memories are gone (l.3536–3540).
- Qwi spots Lemelisk entering a warehouse (l.3544–3554).
- Sulamar's cores are antique and obsolete. The Darksaber has "only a thousandth of the systems" of the original Death Star (l.3578–3582). SCALING.
- Durga ends the diplomacy and takes his own ship back (l.3594–3598).

## Ch31 (l.3602)
- Chewie and R2 on Nar Shaddaa in the Falcon; fake poison-field beacons (l.3616). Orko SkyMine is a PR front (l.3628–3632).
- A slicer finds Sulamar selling computer cores to Durga and claims Sulamar is the "big boss" (l.3648–3670). Chewie ties an alien's 5 tentacles in knots (l.3658). FEAT (minor).

## Ch32 (l.3678)
- Madine (here "Supreme Allied Commander for Special Forces") and the commando Trandia track Durga (l.3680–3686).
- Madine's backstory: a rising Imperial star whose fiancée Karreio died in the battle for Coruscant. He was ordered to spread the Candorian Plague on Dentaal, defected and stole the encryption archives (l.3690–3708). ATROCITY/LORE.
- The team is Madine, Trandia and Korenn in 3 A-wings, with emergency transmitters, to sabotage if possible (l.3716–3718). Destination: the Hoth Asteroid Belt (l.3726).
