# Darksaber (Kevin J. Anderson, Bantam Spectra 1995): notes part 5, ch. 45–53

- Read 2026-11-04 CST from the owner's epub. This part covers FULL.txt l.4950–5909.
- Citation: "DS ch. N (l.x)". Tags: FEAT / ANTI-FEAT / ATROCITY / LORE / SCALING.

## Ch45 (l.4954), YAVIN 4 (l.4950)
- Pellaeon has 17 ISDs, flagship Firestorm (l.4956–4958). A jamming satellite net (l.4972); AT-STs, jungle assault vehicles and TIEs (l.4974).
- Cronus's hit-and-run campaign (l.4986). Daala renames the Night Hammer the "Knight Hammer" (l.4992).

## Ch46 (l.4994)
- Kyp and Dorsk arrive barely an hour before Pellaeon (l.4996). Dorsk senses the trainees through the Force at the Temple of the Blueleaf Cluster (l.5004–5006). About 30 trainees, including Kirana Ti, Streen, Kam Solusar and Tionne (l.5010–5014). Streen senses that the fleet is already there (l.5024).
- Ground and air defense:
  - Kirana Ti Force-hurls a Massassi boulder and downs a TIE (l.5060–5062). FEAT.
  - Kam Solusar and the trainees stone 2 TIEs (l.5064). FEAT.
  - Streen whips up hurricane-strength wind and smashes 4 TIEs together (l.5066–5068). FEAT.
  - Kyp pushes 3 dropped concussion missiles back into a bomber's bay (l.5070). FEAT.
  - Dorsk's boulder downs a second bomber (l.5072). FEAT.
- Dorsk 81's instinctive Force wall flattens an AT-ST's cockpit and crushes it backward into a tree (l.5094–5096). FEAT.
- The jamming net blocks comms (l.5110). Kirana Ti carries Gantoris's lightsaber (l.5112).
- Dorsk's plan: pool about 30 Jedi through him at the Great Temple's apex, using the Massassi temples as a Force focus (l.5130–5152). "Size matters not" (l.5140).
- **Dorsk 81 channels them.** The ships are immovable at first, then move slightly (l.5180–5190). On the final push, "Move!", the 17 Imperial-class Star Destroyers are "slammed backward like twigs in a typhoon" and flung beyond the fringes of the Yavin System, their computers fried and propulsion wrecked, still accelerating (l.5202–5210). TOP FEAT/SCALING.
- Dorsk 81 is burned out from inside, his eyes smoldering sockets, and he dies: "They're gone, my friend" (l.5214–5224). ANTI-FEAT (cost).

## Ch47 (l.5226)
- The 8-km Knight Hammer arrives and finds no sign of Pellaeon and no wreckage anywhere in the system (l.5228–5246).
- Daala's anger backstory (l.5250–5254). She holds back thousands of TIEs and orders an orbital turbolaser bombardment of the jungle (l.5256–5270). ATROCITY.

## Ch48 (l.5272)
- Cronus raids the Chardaan Shipyards and loses 2 Victories to concentrated fighters (l.5284–5308). Seeker-detonators (l.5310).
- He burns the cultural museums of Porus Vida (l.5318–5324) and annihilates a surrendered insectoid diplomatic convoy of 9 ships (l.5326–5342). ATROCITY.

## Ch49 (l.5352), HOTH ASTEROID BELT (l.5348)
- Madine exposes Sulamar as a "junior technician, third grade" (l.5364). Durga takes Sulamar's blaster and seats him in the booby-trapped pilot chair (l.5372–5390).
- Wedge's Yavaris and the Dodonna enter the belt (l.5404).
- The truth about Mendicat: it was a scrap mining station that Sulamar misprogrammed into the sun (l.5418). Durga had worked with Xizor (l.5420). LORE.
- Durga shoots Madine dead (l.5436–5450).

## Ch50 (l.5456), YAVIN 4 (l.5452)
- Callista: "The dark side is never the key to the light" (l.5478).
- The Falcon nearly rams the Knight Hammer (l.5500). Daala jams distress signals (l.5534). Luke and Leia take the gun wells (l.5540–5548).
- Han's chicken run splits the TIE formation, and 2 TIEs collide (l.5556–5566). FEAT (piloting). Luke says the Jedi won't evacuate on the Falcon (l.5592).
- Kyp carries Dorsk 81's body; the 17 SDs are gone (l.5610–5612). 3PO accidentally shoots an AT-ST from the gun well (l.5614–5616).

## Ch51 (l.5630)
- Luke's blade is "green-yellow" here (l.5650). Kirana Ti's Gantoris saber is amethyst-white (l.5650).
- Streen's Force wind-ram slaps 2 scout walkers backward against a Massassi tree (l.5670–5674). FEAT.
- Kirana Ti severs an AT-ST's leg at the knee and kills the pilot (l.5678–5682). FEAT.
- Juggernaut specs (l.5690–5694). Kam Solusar, with no lightsaber, Force-bends the Juggernaut's heavy laser barrels onto itself and mind-commands the gunner to fire, and the vehicle destroys itself (l.5696–5708). FEAT.
- R2 drops a hangar door that crushes an AT-ST (l.5720–5722).
- Callista feels patronized ("I'll protect you") (l.5730–5734); dark-side temptation (l.5736).
- An Imperial Flying Fortress (l.5748–5750): Luke and Tionne use the Force to impale it on a tree stump, then drop a many-ton trunk to crush it (l.5758–5760). FEAT.
- Callista vanishes, undetectable in the Force (l.5768).

## Ch52 (l.5778), HOTH ASTEROID BELT (l.5774)
- Wedge has the Yavaris, the Dodonna and 3 Corvettes (l.5788). Lemelisk envies Madine's permanent death (l.5792).
- Durga ignored calls to test the weapon: "What could go wrong?" (l.5806–5808). Low bids: "you get what you pay for" (l.5812).
- Lemelisk flees in the inspection scooter (l.5818–5826).

## Ch53 (l.5828)
- Qwi: the Darksaber is more maneuverable than a Death Star, recharges faster and can fire more often (l.5834). SCALING (design claim, never realized).
- Madine's transmitter was tied to his life monitor, so he is dead (l.5838–5844).
- The Darksaber acts as a battering ram through the rubble (l.5862). New Republic fire peels its plates (l.5892). It has no hull defenses, no turrets and no TIEs (l.5894). ANTI-FEAT.
- Durga orders the superlaser fired at the colliding planetoids, and it gives a "pffffftt!" fizzle of sparks (l.5900–5906). The Darksaber is crushed between two broken planetoids (l.5908). TOP ANTI-FEAT. Durga and Sulamar die.
