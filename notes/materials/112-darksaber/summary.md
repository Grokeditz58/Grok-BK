# Darksaber (Kevin J. Anderson, 1995): summary

Source: owner's epub, FULL.txt l.1–9344, every line hand-read. Cites are line numbers in FULL.txt. Bundled excerpts from other books (l.6844–8944) are excluded from feats.

## Plot
- **Setup (ch1–6, 12):**
  - About 8 years after Endor (l.228). Luke and Han, disguised as Tuskens, learn that the Hutts used Jabba's stolen passwords to raid the Imperial Information Center for an "invincible weapon" (l.516–536).
  - Durga the Hutt's Taurill slice the plans while Leia receives him on Coruscant (l.1020–1032). The stolen files are the Death Star plans (l.1740–1746).
- **The Darksaber (ch4–5, 13–14, 27, 29, 40–42, 52–53):**
  - Durga hires the cloned, often-executed Death Star designer Bevel Lemelisk (l.776–838, 4686–4690) and the fake "General" Sulamar (l.5364, 5418).
  - Lemelisk designs a stripped-down Death Star superlaser in a cylinder (l.1872–1884), built by distractible Taurill (l.3194–3198) from obsolete cores (l.3578–3582).
  - Madine's commando raid fails: Trandia dies and Madine is captured (l.4598–4630). Durga kills Madine (l.5436–5450).
  - Wedge's fleet attacks. The Darksaber's superlaser fizzles ("pffffftt!"), and it is crushed between two planetoids, killing Durga and Sulamar (l.5900–5908). Lemelisk is captured (l.6504–6558).
- **Daala (ch9–10, 18–20, 35, 45, 47, 54–59, 62):**
  - Daala survives the Maw. She ion-cannons Warlord Harrsk's ship and forces a cease-fire with Pellaeon (l.1442–1540). She gasses the 13 warlords (l.2428–2448) and takes the 8-km Super Star Destroyer Night/Knight Hammer (l.2528–2542, 4992).
  - She sends Cronus raiding (Khomm, Chardaan, Porus Vida; l.4838–4872, 5284–5342) and Pellaeon's 17 ISDs against the Yavin 4 academy (l.4026–4028).
  - Callista sabotages the Knight Hammer, which falls into Yavin (l.6228–6278, 6486–6488). Daala stuns Callista (l.6432–6434) and resigns command to Pellaeon (l.6584–6602).
- **Luke and Callista (ch3, 7, 12, 15–16, 26, 28, 33, 38–39, 50–51, 60, 63):**
  - Callista has lost the Force (l.566–572). They fence to a stalemate (l.1940–1948). On Dagobah her anger flickers into the dark side (l.3098–3120).
  - Wampas strand them on Hoth (l.3320–3388, 3838–3898). Luke's trance and Force call to Leia save them (l.4296–4328, 4410–4422).
  - At the end Callista leaves alone on her "odyssey" (l.6698–6714).
- **Yavin 4 defense (ch46, 51, 60):**
  - The trainees down TIEs and walkers (l.5060–5096, 5670–5760, 6444–6456). Dorsk 81 pools about 30 Jedi and hurls the 17 ISDs out of the system, which kills him (l.5130–5224).
  - Ackbar's Galactic Voyager arrives (l.6082), and a gunship rams Cronus's flagship (l.6140–6144).

## Strongest feats
1. **Dorsk 81 (channelling about 30 pooled Jedi through the Great Temple)** pushes 17 Imperial-class Star Destroyers "slammed backward like twigs in a typhoon," flung beyond the fringes of the Yavin System with computers fried and propulsion wrecked (l.5180–5210). Pellaeon confirms they were "thrown far outside the solar system in a matter of seconds," with hyperdrive damaged and the navicomputer wiped (l.6164–6196, 6298). It costs Dorsk his life (l.5214–5224).
2. **Luke** sends a Force call across space to Leia, who follows it to find the drifting yacht (l.4324–4328, 4338–4392). He sustains himself for days in a near-suspended-animation trance on 10% life support, coming within hours of death (l.4296–4306, 4410–4418).
3. **The academy trainees:**
   - Kam Solusar, with no lightsaber, bends a Juggernaut's heavy laser barrels onto itself and mind-commands the gunner to fire (l.5696–5708).
   - Streen's hurricane wind smashes 4 TIEs (l.5066–5068), and his wind-ram slaps 2 walkers into a tree (l.5670–5674).
   - Kyp pushes 3 concussion missiles back into a bomber's bay (l.5070).

Also of note:
- **Luke:**
  - Mass mind-muddling of a Tusken band for days (l.264–268, 362–364).
  - Deflects a blaster-cannon bolt (it staggers him, l.3272), then cuts through turret armor (l.3280–3284).
  - Cleaves the one-armed wampa in half "putting all the Force behind his swing" (l.3868–3872).
  - With Tionne, impales a Flying Fortress, then crushes it with a falling trunk (l.5758–5760). Fells a Massassi tree onto an AT-ST (l.6456).
- **Dorsk 81:** a Force wall crushes an AT-ST (l.5094–5096).
- **Kirana Ti:** her hurled boulder downs a TIE (l.5060), and she severs an AT-ST's leg (l.5678).
- **Callista:**
  - Without the Force, sabotages the 8-km Knight Hammer so it drifts dead and falls into Yavin (l.6228–6278, 6304–6352, 6486–6488).
  - With the dark side, deflects 4 lethal bolts (l.6416–6430) and pushes away a nightbat swarm (l.3098–3100).
- **Ships:**
  - The Knight Hammer obliterates an acre of jungle in one shot (l.5920).
  - The Galactic Voyager wipes out half of 218 TIEs in one pass (l.6068, 6082).
  - Daala's ion-cannon shot disables the Whirlwind (l.1444).
- **Vader** (flashback): Force-chokes two foremen to death (l.3436–3460).

## Anti-feats
- **The Darksaber:**
  - Its superlaser fizzles ("pffffftt!"), and it is crushed between planetoids (l.5900–5908).
  - It has no turrets, no hull defenses and no TIEs (l.5894). Its hull plates "couldn't possibly hold an atmosphere" (l.4562–4580).
  - Its girders are misaligned, so it could vaporize itself (l.4522–4526). It has "only a thousandth of the systems" of a Death Star (l.3578–3582).
- **Callista:** can't nudge a meal chit (l.2040–2050) or enter a trance (l.4308). Daala's spread stun overwhelms her saber block (l.6432–6434).
- **Luke:**
  - Releases his control too early and the Tuskens attack (l.436–442). The blaster-cannon bolt makes him reel (l.3272).
  - Nearly dies in the trance (l.4410–4418). Can't read Hutts; Leia hits a "blank wall" with Durga (l.962–964).
- **Trandia's** suicide charges barely damage the engine containment wall (l.4598–4606).

## Scaling and lore
- **Ship scales:**
  - Death Star: 100 km, about 1 million crew, "worth a thousand Star Destroyers," the superlaser can crack worlds (l.1792–1804).
  - Night Hammer: 8 km, up to 100,000 crew, "worth twenty Imperial Star Destroyers" (l.2528–2538).
  - Daala's fleet: 112 Victories, 45 ISDs and the Night Hammer (l.3992–3996).
- **Lemelisk's resurrections:** clone revival with memory transfer through an artifact stolen from a Jedi (l.816–824). Seven executions (l.4686–4690).
- **Hutts:** they may be genetically shielded from Force probes (l.962–964). Hutt lore (l.906–914). The Taurill are a hive mind with an Overmind (l.1014).
- **Khomm:** genderless clones (l.2132). **Djinn Altis's ship**, the Chu'unthor, crashed on Dathomir (l.2032–2036).
- **Daala's backstory:** Carida, the simulations under a false identity, Tarkin (l.2284–2302).
- **Madine's backstory:** the Candorian Plague (l.3690–3708). Mon Mothma and the Endor assassination debate (l.4188–4200).

## Atrocities
- Daala gasses the 13 warlords (l.2428–2448) and orders an orbital bombardment of Yavin 4 (l.5256–5270).
- Cronus bombards Khomm and destroys its cloning facility (l.4838–4872). He burns the Porus Vida museums and annihilates a surrendered 9-ship convoy (l.5318–5342).
- Durga electrocutes underlings (l.720–722, 4674–4676) and murdered a xenobiologist (l.1016).
- Tarkin's Despayre and Kashyyyk slave labor (l.1844–1850, 3154–3186).
