Character quick bios:
- **Luke Skywalker:**
  - Jedi Master. Muddles a Tusken band's minds for days (l.264–268). Deflects a blaster-cannon bolt (l.3272). Halves the wampa leader (l.3868–3872).
  - Enters a near-suspended-animation trance and sends a Force call to Leia (l.4296–4328). Fells trees onto walkers and fortresses (l.5758–5760, 6456).
  - Loses Callista at the end (l.6698–6714).
- **Callista:** Jedi from Chad, trained by Djinn Altis (l.2002–2036), now in Cray's body and Force-blind (l.566–572). A strong swordswoman without the Force (l.1940–1948, 5934–5936). Her dark-side flickers show at l.3098–3100 and 6416–6430. Sabotages the Knight Hammer (l.6228–6278), is stunned by Daala (l.6432–6434), and leaves on her odyssey (l.6698–6714).
- **Leia Organa Solo:** Chief of State after Mon Mothma (l.2580). Faces Durga on Nal Hutta (l.2738–2950) and follows Luke's call to find him (l.4338–4392). Gives Madine's eulogy (l.6638–6654).
- **Han Solo / Chewbacca:** Han makes TIE chicken runs (l.5556–5566, 6016–6018). Chewie and R2 slice the Nar Shaddaa connection (l.3602–3670).
- **Admiral Daala:** her Carida backstory (l.2284–2302). Disables Harrsk and gasses the 13 warlords (l.1442–1490, 2428–2448). Commands the Knight Hammer (l.2528–2542, 4992), stuns Callista (l.6432–6434) and resigns command to Pellaeon (l.6584–6602).
- **Vice Admiral Gilad Pellaeon:** Thrawn's former captain (l.1500–1538). His 17 ISDs are hurled out of the Yavin system (l.6164–6196). He inherits command of all Imperial forces (l.6584–6602).
- **Durga the Hutt:** birthmarked and fatter than Jabba (l.648, 942). Has Force-opaque skin (l.962–964). Commissions the Darksaber and kills underlings and Madine (l.720–722, 4674–4676, 5436–5450). Dies when it is crushed (l.5908).
- **Bevel Lemelisk:** designer of both Death Stars, cloned after repeated executions (l.676–680, 776–838, 4686–4690). Captured by Wedge, he asks to be executed "right this time" (l.6504–6558).
- **"General" Sulamar:** a fraud, really a "junior technician, third grade" (l.5364, 5418). Dies on the Darksaber.
- **Dorsk 81:** a Khomm clone who becomes a Jedi Knight (l.1160–1168, 2128–2188). Crushes an AT-ST (l.5094–5096). Dies hurling 17 ISDs out of the system (l.5202–5224).
- **Kyp Durron:** Jedi Knight (l.1160). Scouts the Empire and warns of the attack (l.4034–4164, 4740, 4922–4928). Pushes missiles back into a bomber's bay (l.5070).
- **Academy trainees:** Kirana Ti (boulder, AT-ST leg; l.5060, 5678), Streen (wind; l.5066–5068, 5670–5674), Kam Solusar (Juggernaut; l.5696–5708), Tionne (Flying Fortress; l.5758–5760).
- **Crix Madine:** Imperial defector who refused to spread the Candorian Plague (l.3690–3708). Captured on the Darksaber (l.4618–4630) and killed by Durga (l.5436–5450).
- **Colonel Cronus:** Daala's raider. Commits the Khomm, Porus Vida and convoy atrocities (l.4838–4872, 5318–5342). Rammed and killed (l.6140–6144).
- **Others:** Wedge and Qwi Xux (l.2634–2726, 3476–3554, 5828–5908); Ackbar (l.2648, 6082–6096); Mara Jade (l.1552–1640); Trandia and Korenn (l.3716–3718, 4222–4236, 4598–4606); Harrsk, Teradoc, Delvardus and Kratas (l.1226–1540, 2350–2448); Korrda (l.2808–2872); Taurill (l.944–1032).
