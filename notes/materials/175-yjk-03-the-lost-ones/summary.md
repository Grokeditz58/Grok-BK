# Young Jedi Knights 3: The Lost Ones: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-20 CST. Full read of READ l.1–1265 (every line, in order). Citation "LO ch. N"; the e-book has no page numbers and there is no scan. Detail is in lo-01 to lo-04; every quote below is pasted by script from the same READ.txt.
- Authors: Kevin J. Anderson & Rebecca Moesta. Third Young Jedi Knights book, first published 1995. The library epub has no copyright page, so no edition data comes from it. 22 chapters.
- Era: directly after Shadow Academy. The young Jedi Knights are on a break from the Jedi academy, visiting Coruscant. Jaina says they have known Zekk "About five" years (ch. 10). No ABY year is given.
- GAP: the printed opening of ch. 1 is missing from the epub and the mobi (see README).

## Plot
- **Ch. 1 (READ l.9–), Home to Coruscant:** On the Falcon to Coruscant Jacen tells Jaina a joke; Jaina recalls Zekk's history. The twins shoot space debris from the gun wells with Tenel Ka and Lowie. They land at the palace; Leia, Anakin and Threepio greet them; Em Teedee and Threepio reunite.
- **Ch. 2 (READ l.68–), Breakfast at the palace; Zekk; the undercity; the hawkbat nest:** Breakfast at the palace; Threepio's planned classes; Leia's diplomacy. The four meet Zekk at a tourism deck; he leads them down into the undercity, past Lost Ones gang signs, to a crashed Old Republic shuttle and a hawk-bat nest, where Jacen calms the mother and takes one egg.
- **Ch. 3 (READ l.189–), The Lost Ones ambush; the mother hawkbat strikes back:** Heading back with the egg, the five pass through the Lost Ones' headquarters. Zekk taunts Norys, and the gang drops from the ceiling and traps them on a walkway. The mother hawkbat attacks Norys, Lowie charges the blocker, and they escape.
- **Ch. 4 (READ l.239–), Zekk and Peckhum's apartment:** They go to the midlevel apartment Zekk shares with Peckhum.
- **Ch. 5 (READ l.293–), Solo family holiday at the polar ice caps:** Tauntaun riding, turboskiing and a picnic at the pole. Leia explains the Karnak Alpha banquet, and Anakin talks her into inviting Zekk.
- **Ch. 6 (READ l.337–), Zekk at the banquet:** Zekk arrives at the palace in new formal clothes; Threepio fusses over him; the Karnak Alpha banquet leaves him feeling out of place.
- **Ch. 7 (READ l.410–), Raid on the Adamant:** The supply cruiser Adamant, carrying Admiral Ackbar, reaches Coruscant with hyperdrive cores and turbolaser batteries. A Shadow Academy fleet appears, cripples it with ion cannons, and Qorl's assault shuttle cuts into the hull.
- **Ch. 8 (READ l.463–), Zekk alone; Tamith Kai's test:** Humiliated, Zekk wanders the lower levels, brooding on his "freedom". Tamith Kai, with Vilas and Garowyn, corners him to test him for Force potential.
- **Ch. 9 (READ l.499–), Leia and her children:** The twins worry about Zekk. Leia comes back pleased: the ambassador liked the children, and Leia put her family ahead of the talks; Han stays behind to tell the Karnak children a bedtime story.
- **Ch. 10 (READ l.525–), Zekk missing; Leia briefs the kids on the Adamant:** Zekk misses their meeting two days running. The kids go to Leia, who is busy with the Adamant hijacking.
- **Ch. 11 (READ l.580–), Zekk wakes at the Shadow Academy:** Zekk wakes in a doorless cell; Brakiss greets him and starts winning him over.
- **Ch. 12 (READ l.659–), Moon Dash destroyed; Jaina and Lowie map orbital debris:** Cargo shuttle Moon Dash (Captain Narek-Ag, copilot Trebor) leaves Coruscant, hits something invisible "in clear space", and explodes (the cloaked Shadow Academy). Leia worries about orbital debris, and Jaina volunteers herself and Lowie to map it as their study project.
- **Ch. 13 (READ l.717–), Peckhum comes home to an empty apartment:** Peckhum returns from the mirror station, finds Zekk gone and the twins' worried messages, and realizes something is wrong.
- **Ch. 14 (READ l.766–), Zekk's first training with Brakiss:** Zekk wakes in plush quarters, dresses in a new uniform, and starts Brakiss's training.
- **Ch. 15 (READ l.829–), Plans split: the mirror station and the search:** Peckhum must go back up; Lowie suggests he, Jaina and Chewbacca go along to repair the mirror station. Jacen, Tenel Ka, Anakin and Threepio keep searching the undercity.
- **Ch. 16 (READ l.908–), Up to the mirror station:** Peckhum flies Jaina, Lowie and Chewbacca up to the mirror station in the Lightning Rod, and they start repairs.
- **Ch. 17 (READ l.939–), Into Lost Ones territory; Zekk found:** Tenel Ka leads them into Lost Ones territory, and Jacen's Force hunch guides them to a room where Zekk, in a new uniform, is talking to the gang.
- **Ch. 18 (READ l.1008–), Brakiss reports to the Emperor:** Brakiss reports by hologram to the Great Leader, who appears as Emperor Palpatine: the stolen cargo and the new recruiting plan. The station is rigged to explode if the Dark Jedi get out of hand.
- **Ch. 19 (READ l.1036–), Anakin raises the alarm; Leia leads the rescue:** Anakin senses that Jacen is in trouble and wakes Threepio. Leia believes them this time, calls guards, and has Threepio trace Jacen's comlink.
- **Ch. 20 (READ l.1104–), Repairs on the station; Jaina works out the fighters:** The station is patched up. Lowie keeps plotting debris. Jaina realizes the Imperial fighters in the Adamant footage couldn't have come from far away, so the Shadow Academy must be cloaked in orbit nearby.
- **Ch. 21 (READ l.1179–), The message pod:** An object is left behind where the station was: a message pod. The fleet recovers it, and everyone gathers on the landing pad as it is unloaded.
- **Ch. 22 (READ l.1234–), Back on Yavin 4:** Luke brings the students home in the Shadow Chaser. He senses the coming battle, and the young Jedi resolve to train harder.

## Scale notes
- Force use stays small-scale and personal. Jacen's animal empathy (calming a diving hawkbat, reading its memory of gang shooters), TK on a hawkbat egg, Jaina's Force-guided wiring repair, Jacen's and Anakin's danger sense, and Zekk's first flame manipulation.
- Brakiss's flame sculpting is the most elaborate Force display in the book.
- Jacen states the limit of his tracking ability himself: he cannot locate a specific person by the Force (ch. 17).
- The Shadow Academy's cloaking device hides the station in orbit over Coruscant until a focused solar-mirror beam overloads the shields (ch. 20). The station then jumps to lightspeed.
- No lightsaber combat; Jacen wishes he had one (ch. 17).
- The Great Leader appears by hologram as Emperor Palpatine; Brakiss believes he survived. The Shadow Academy is rigged with explosives in case its Dark Jedi get out of control (ch. 18).
