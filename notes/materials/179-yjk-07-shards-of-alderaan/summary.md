# Young Jedi Knights 7: Shards of Alderaan: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-20 CST. Full read of READ l.1–1360, every line in order: front matter, all 21 chapters, the Diversity Alliance preview, the ads and the back cover. Notes are limited to scaling-relevant material (owner's rulings, 2026-11-20). The quotes are in soa-01 and soa-02; this summary is in our own words and cites them by chapter.
- Authors: Kevin J. Anderson & Rebecca Moesta. Seventh Young Jedi Knights book and the start of the Diversity Alliance / Bornan Thul arc. Copyright page: Boulevard edition, January 1997 (ISBN 1-57297-207-6). 21 chapters. The dedication is to Marina Fitch and Mark Budz.
- Era: shortly after Jedi Under Siege. The academy is rebuilding, Zekk recovers, and Boba Fett hunts Raynar's father.

## Plot
- **Ch. 1, 3:** the academy rebuilds; Tenel Ka and Lowie save Raynar when the ronto brings down the wall; Jacen calms the ronto; Luke talks about change; Han reports that Bornan Thul is missing.
- **Ch. 2, 5–6:** Zekk's fever dream and coma; he and Jaina talk at the river; the friends repair the Lightning Rod; Peckhum gives it to Zekk, who leaves.
- **Ch. 4:** Nolaa Tarkona hires Boba Fett; Fett lets Moorlu ionize him and kills him with a hand-cranked torpedo; he sees through Thul's planted wreckage.
- **Ch. 7, 9–10:** Tenel Ka receives the Rock Dragon; Jacen lures a thyrsi out of a transformer, and Tenel Ka and Raynar catch his fall with the Force; Anakin arrives to "puzzle" the temple back together; Han allows a three-day trip to Alderaan.
- **Ch. 8, 12, 15:** Zekk joins the evacuation of Ennth; Shinnan dies in the final quake; Zekk rejects the colonists' endless cycle and leaves to follow the Force.
- **Ch. 11, 13:** in the Alderaan graveyard the friends feel the old disturbance; Jaina lightsaber-cuts a faceted chunk of Alderaan's core metal; a larval space slug.
- **Ch. 14, 16–19:** Fett ambushes the Rock Dragon to disable and capture it; Tenel Ka's decoy canister lets them hide in a crater cave; Fett buries them and slices their computer; Em Teedee slices his back; Fett uses them as bait for Han; they dig out with muscle, the Force and lightsabers, then broadcast a warning; Fett tries to destroy them, and Zekk drives him off.
- **Ch. 20–21:** Han and Chewie tow them to safety; Zekk decides to become a bounty hunter and help the search for Thul; Leia's birthday on Yavin 4.
- **Back matter:** the Diversity Alliance preview (Kuar; Thul's sash warning). Summarized only; it will be quoted in YJK 8.

## Strongest findings (see the part files for the quotes)
- **Boba Fett** (ch. 4, 17, 19): narrated as "the best"; Tarkona and Han say the same. He gambles on letting himself be ionized, then kills Moorlu with one hand-cranked, sight-aimed torpedo. He deduces the planted wreckage, blasts through a rock wall and detects the slicing at once. Anti-feats: caught with his weapons not engaged, and ambushed himself by Zekk, after which he withdraws damaged.
- **Tenel Ka** (ch. 1, 3, 9, 14, 17–18): senses the wall accident early, then somersaults, tackles and leaps clear; her body is trained to endure. Telekinetic catch of Jacen together with Raynar. Tactical reads of Fett and the decoy canisters. Her lightsaber hacks rock wedges like a battle-ax.
- **Jacen** (ch. 1, 9, 18): animal empathy (the ronto, a Force search that finds the thyrsi). Catches rock slabs with the Force. Anti-feat: falls off the scaffold.
- **Jaina** (ch. 6, 11, 13, 16, 18): top-notch mechanic and leader; senses Alderaan's despair and the ship's engine flow; her lightsaber cuts pure core metal. Anti-feat: can't work the Hapan weapons and misses her shots.
- **Zekk** (ch. 5, 8, 12, 15, 20): "I've killed face-to-face"; his Force knack for finding things; days of evacuation work; a premonition about Shinnan that comes too late; he avoids the Force for fear of the dark side; the Force guides him to Jaina's trouble.
- **Lowie** (ch. 1, 14): vine-swing rescue of Raynar; reacts "with his Jedi senses" in the ambush.
- **Raynar** (ch. 1, 9): Force nudges on blocks; helps catch Jacen with the Force. **Anakin** (ch. 10, 21): puzzle and pattern genius.
