# Coruscant Nights III: Patterns of Force: summary (Michael Reaves, Del Rey 2008)

Source: the owner's epub, eISBN 978-0-307-79589-2, read in full (FULL.txt l.1–8143). Cites are "l.x" in FULL.txt. Feats come from the novel only (l.171–5153); bundled excerpts are excluded.

## Plot
- **Setup:**
  - Rhinann wants the bota (a Force amplifier) for himself (l.193–201).
  - Tuden Sal, who betrayed Lorn Pavan two decades earlier, now serves the Whiplash. He proposes that I-Five assassinate Palpatine (l.501–587).
  - Vader orders Probus Tesla to capture I-Five intact and leave Jax unharmed (l.729–759).
- **Kaj:**
  - Jax and Laranth find the 15-year-old Kajin Savaros beating Tesla inside a repulsor-field gap (l.1017–1121).
  - Jax hides him in Ves Volette's studio, inside a cage of Force-blocking light sculptures, and trains him (l.1879–1939, 2565–2597, 3011–3139).
- **The plot:**
  - Dejah, Den and Rhinann maneuver over the bota and the plan (l.2005–2241).
  - The team votes and Dejah flips to yes (l.3189–3197). Den leaves for Sullust (l.3337–3351).
  - Yimmon vouches for Pol Haus (l.3387–3399).
  - I-Five plays Lorn's hidden "Message Mode Ninety-nine" hologram. Jax resolves: "We're going after the Emperor… Because no one else can" (l.3583–3621).
- **Gallery Row:**
  - Tesla, Mas Sirrah and a third Inquisitor ambush Kaj and Laranth (l.3809–3813).
  - Jax maims one Inquisitor and I-Five kills him (l.3911–3925). Kaj atomizes Sirrah (l.3951). Tesla flees, wounded (l.3963–3979).
  - Jax senses I-Five in the Force (l.4075–4103). Vader learns of the droid's "Force signature" and calls the boy "the key" (l.4127–4159).
  - The assassination plan switches to the Senate, with Jax disguised as an Inquisitor handler (l.4281–4309).
- **Betrayal:**
  - Kaj and Laranth are gassed and captured in a Whiplash tunnel (l.4399–4425).
  - Kaj wakes with his memory wiped, told he is an Inquisitorius initiate: "I'm a Sith" (l.4517–4603).
  - Vader displays Laranth in lekku-disrupting bands and accepts a trade: I-Five for the prisoners (l.4711–4745).
- **Exchange:**
  - The control tower is in an abandoned hangar, and the Emperor there is a hologram (l.4791, 4813–4819).
  - Dejah is the mole, allied with Tesla (l.4829–4863).
  - Vader stops I-Five's lasers (l.4879), pins Jax (l.4895) and takes the bota from Laranth and the pyronium from Jax (l.4897–4907).
  - Vader injects the bota "merely [for] an analysis" and becomes an unstable Force storm (l.4939–4969).
  - Dejah, the third Inquisitor and almost Jax die in it (l.4967–4969, 4999).
  - The Whiplash and Den arrive (l.5001). Rhinann drags Vader through the window to his own death (l.5021–5031). Vader survives (l.5049).
- **Aftermath:**
  - I-Five is rebuilt (l.5057). Den returns home to the team (l.5059–5061).
  - Kaj goes to the Togrutan healers and The Silents on Shili (l.5063–5069).
  - Jax and Laranth share a Force-empathic bond (l.5087–5103). Jax suspects Vader is Anakin (l.5119–5125).
  - A Cosmic Force vision shows Jax where he belongs (l.5139–5147), and a new client, Chan Dash, arrives (l.5137, 5153).

## Strongest feats
1. **Vader on bota: an uncontrolled Force storm** (l.4949–4969). He hurls Force energy "in every direction at once… as if the Force struck through him, using the Dark Lord as a puppet" (l.4957). The blasts shatter the tower window, crush and pin I-Five in ferrocrete (l.4959) and kill Dejah "with bone-breaking force" (l.4967) and the third Inquisitor (l.4969). Jax's saber "ricocheted as if it were made of mere metal" off his Force envelope (l.4999). He drags Jax and Kaj with energy lashes (l.5003–5005) and survives the fall with "a cataclysmic burst… The entire building rocked" (l.5049). Jax's own assessment of baseline Vader: "He could have killed us all in a breath, using just the Force he has access to every day" (l.5117).
2. **Kaj atomizes the Inquisitor Mas Sirrah** with "a massive Force-push from every angle… only a fine swirl of ash" (l.3951). Tesla says not even an echo of his signature remained: "never seen the Force used in such a way" (l.3965–3967). In the same fight Kaj throws Tesla a dozen meters (l.3813), holds a Force shield against two Inquisitors (l.3853), survives a docking station destroyed by Force lightning, then levels the façade with a wave of matter (l.3903–3905). Earlier he pulled energy balls out of a repulsor field, which "should have been impossible", and paralyzed Tesla with a mental attack (l.1059–1081).
3. **Jax: blind and close-quarters lightsaber work.**
   - Eyes closed, he deflects a dozen remote shots with a rod, using Shii-Cho (l.3045–3053). He dodges Kaj's rod blast by gauging intention (l.3103–3107).
   - On Gallery Row he senses an Inquisitor above him, cuts up through a duracrete ledge and takes his leg (l.3911–3915). He somersaults off a blade bind (l.3885).
   - In the tower he drives Tesla back "as if his mind wielded [the blade] without the intermediary of his arms" (l.4983–4985).
   - Vader himself is quickest: he reads I-Five's intent "as an emotion" and stops the lasers centimeters from his hand (l.4879–4881). A gesture pins Jax with nerve-fire (l.4895). He reads Tesla's mind (l.4133–4143).

Also of note:
- Tesla steps into an abyss and lets "the Force lower him like an invisible turbolift" (l.931–935). He destroys a docking station with Force lightning (l.3903).
- I-Five shoots off an Inquisitor's arm and then his throat (l.3921–3925).
- Laranth leaps about 10 m (l.1199–1205).
- Jax's spontaneous Cosmic Force vision (l.5139–5147).
- Kaj's involuntary blast drives a rod 15 cm into plasticrete (l.3103–3107). Another dents a wall rated to a metric ton (l.1513, 1533).

## Anti-feats
- **Tesla:**
  - Beaten by an untrained teenager and left scarred despite bacta (l.1121, 2069).
  - Flees Gallery Row with durasteel through his side: "this second defeat" (l.3973–3979).
  - Swatted aside by Vader's random blast (l.4989).
- **Jax:**
  - Missed the taozin-cloaked Inquisitor (l.2697).
  - Was manipulated by Dejah's pheromones and never suspected her until late (l.3257, 4185–4191, 5073–5077).
  - Is pinned helpless by Vader (l.4895) and floored when his blade bounces off Vader's envelope (l.4999).
- **Kaj and Laranth:**
  - Both are drugged and captured by a gas ambush (l.4399–4417).
  - Kaj almost kills Jax by accident (l.3103–3107).
  - After the memory wipe Kaj can barely use the Force (l.4979, 5065).
- **Vader:**
  - The bota "trip" robs him of control; a sedentary Elomin batters his breathing apparatus and pulls him out of the window (l.5015–5031).
  - Shows rare puzzlement over Kaj and I-Five (l.4121–4127).
- **Laranth:** cannot cloak Vader in his storm (l.4973).

## Lore highlights
- **Bota:** Barriss Offee found it amplified Force ability "exponentially" (l.2005). It is evolving into a weed (l.193–201).
  - Vader expected it to "purify and exponentially increase" his connection, sustained by the pyronium. The Sith Holocron held Darth Ramage's instructions (l.4925).
  - Jax bet the 20-year-old molecule had frayed (l.5113).
- **Volette light sculptures:** a lightsaber crystal plus a cohesion field blocks Force sense and telempathy (l.1879–1939). Six are tuned into a cage (l.3011). The sculpture circuitry can power Jax's Ilum crystal (l.2929–2933).
- **Taozin:** wards make Inquisitors Force-"holes" (l.915–917, 967–971, 3793). Kaj reads them as ripples (l.1873–1877).
- **Inquisitorius:**
  - Inquisitors are not Sith but use red blades (l.1173). Rumored to be trained by Vader (l.3879).
  - The order is centered in a temple, with offices in the ISB (l.4617).
  - Tesla beat his Falleen master Kuthara to earn his place (l.871–907).
- **I-Five:**
  - Has a sporadic Force signature tied to strong emotion (l.3969–3971, 4075–4101).
  - His BPK forbids injuring sentients (l.4255).
  - Message Mode Ninety-nine holds Lorn's message (l.3583–3605).
  - I-Five learned the Sith Zabrak (Maul) "was later killed… on Naboo" (l.4237).
- **Imperial research:** bands that disrupt the Force through Twi'lek lekku (l.4721). An EMP emitter on Laranth's lekku (l.4811).
- **Whiplash:**
  - Thi Xon Yimmon is a Cerean with a binary brain (l.3355). Pol Haus was one of the original operatives (l.3387).
  - The UML is their escape route (l.2365–2369).
- **Force patterns:** "as individual as a person's DNA" (Master Piell). The patterns around Vader are "strangely, unbelievably, familiar" to Jax (l.5119–5123).
- **The Force as Jax sees it:** threads of light and darkness (l.361–363). Kaj: the dammed Force "burns" (l.1485). Monarchomechs, a cyborg tyrannicide sect (l.587).

## Atrocities
- Vader wipes Kaj's memory and "the very meaning of what it was to be a Force-sensitive" (l.4517–4601, 5065).
- He tortures Laranth (l.4711, 4721, 4873) and threatens to dissect the team "piece by piece" (l.4873).
- Imperial seizure of Kaj's family farm on M'haeli (l.1253–1259, 3755–3761, 3891).
- Kaj's rage-driven killing of Sirrah. Jax warns him that "Inquisitors are people" (l.4017–4019, 4247).
