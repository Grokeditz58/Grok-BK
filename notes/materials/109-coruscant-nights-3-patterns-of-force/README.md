Continuity: 19 BBY, soon after Street of Shadows; the timeline puts Coruscant Nights between Imperial Commando: 501st and The Last Jedi (l.7857–7867). Tuden Sal of the Whiplash proposes using I-Five to assassinate Palpatine (l.559–587). Jax and Laranth find the untrained 15-year-old adept Kajin Savaros beating the Inquisitor Probus Tesla (l.1017–1121). Jax hides Kaj inside a cage of Ves Volette's Force-blocking light sculptures and trains him (l.1879–1939, 3011). Lorn Pavan's hidden hologram turns Jax toward the plot (l.3583–3621). In the Battle of Gallery Row, Kaj atomizes the Inquisitor Mas Sirrah and I-Five kills a second; Jax learns I-Five has a Force signature (l.3809–3979, 4075–4103). Dejah Duare betrays Kaj and Laranth to Tesla (l.4399–4425, 4857–4863), and Vader wipes Kaj's memory (l.4517–4601). At the exchange, Vader takes the bota and the pyronium and injects the bota. It turns him into an uncontrolled Force storm that kills Dejah (l.4939–4967). Rhinann drags Vader out of the window and is killed (l.5021–5031). Kaj is sent to Shili to heal, Jax suspects Vader is Anakin, and the Force gives Jax a vision of the Cosmic Force (l.5063–5147).

Character quick bios:
- **Jax Pavan:**
  - Jedi Knight trained by Master Piell, now carrying a red Sith blade (l.1177, 1521). Sees the Force as threads (l.361–363).
  - Deflects a dozen remote shots blind (l.3045–3053). Cuts through a duracrete ledge to take an Inquisitor's leg (l.3911–3915). Drives Tesla back in the tower (l.4983–4985).
  - Pinned and nerve-burned by Vader (l.4895). His blade bounces off Vader's Force envelope (l.4999). Has a vision of the Cosmic Force (l.5139–5147).
- **Darth Vader:**
  - Reads Tesla's mind (l.4133–4143). Stops I-Five's lasers in mid-air (l.4879–4881).
  - The bota turns him into a random Force storm (l.4939–4969). Survives a long fall with a "cataclysmic burst" (l.5049).
  - Jax suspects he is Anakin (l.5119–5125).
- **Kajin "Kaj" Savaros:**
  - Untrained Force prodigy from M'haeli, aged 15 (l.1253–1259).
  - Pulls energy balls out of repulsor fields and paralyzes Tesla (l.1059–1081). Atomizes Mas Sirrah (l.3951).
  - Captured through Dejah's betrayal; his memory is wiped and he is sent to Shili (l.4517–4601, 5063–5069).
- **Probus Tesla:** Vader's Inquisitor, the "Bloodfiend", with a synesthetic Force sense (l.771–773, 865–867). Beaten twice by Kaj (l.1121, 3973–3979). Allied with Dejah (l.4853–4855). Escapes at the end (l.5045).
- **Laranth Tarak:** Gray Paladin. Leaps about 10 m (l.1199–1205). Secretly carried the bota for I-Five (l.4887–4889). Forms a Force-empathic bond with Jax (l.5087–5103).
- **I-Five (I-5YQ):** Lorn Pavan's sentient droid. Has a sporadic Force signature (l.3969–3971, 4075–4101). Kills an Inquisitor with his finger lasers (l.3921–3925). Crushed in the tower, then rebuilt with new weapons (l.4959, 5057).
- **Dejah Duare:** Zeltron telempath. Manipulates Jax with pheromones (l.3257, 3513). Is the mole (l.4829–4863). Killed by Vader's storm (l.4967).
- **Haninum Tyk Rhinann:** Elomin and Vader's former adjutant. Covets the bota (l.193–201). Dies taking Vader through the window (l.5021–5031).
- **Den Dhur:** Sullustan. Leaves for Sullust and Eyar Marath (l.4055–4073). Returns with the Whiplash rescue (l.5001, 5059–5061).
- **Pol Haus:** Zabrak prefect and one of the original Whiplash operatives (l.3387). Suspected of being the mole, wrongly (l.4491–4497).
- **Thi Xon Yimmon / Tuden Sal:** Yimmon is the Cerean leader of the Whiplash (l.3355). Sal is the Sakiyan who once betrayed Lorn and is the author of the assassination plan (l.501–567, 4281).
