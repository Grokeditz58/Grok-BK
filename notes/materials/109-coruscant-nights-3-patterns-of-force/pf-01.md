Laranth has been gone several months with Thi Xon Yimmon — "the fighting prowess of a trained soldier and the wisdom of a Jedi Master" (l.835–841). FEAT (Jax): a sudden compulsion in the Force "like a bola" — locates it "Down and to the west" (l.847–853).
## Ch. 5 (l.855–1136)
- Tesla experiences the Force as scent, sight, sound and taste (synesthesia) (l.865–867). LORE: his master Kuthara, a Falleen; the duality lesson; six hours of Shii-Cho as punishment (l.871–895). FEAT/LORE (Tesla): "earned his place in the Inquisitorius by utterly defeating that master" (l.907). Taozin-scale necklace scrambles his emanations; a Jedi "would have to work awfully hard even to get a fix on him" (l.915–917). Nearly falls into a chasm; the wind saves him (l.919–921). FEAT (Tesla): steps into the abyss; "The Force lowered him like an invisible turbolift"; jackknifes through a gap (l.931–935). Senses a kinetic energy field (l.939). Lightsaber the color of a Corellian sunset/Mustafar lava (l.943).
- Jax follows bright threads down into Ploughtekal; they break (l.947–949).
- Jax and Laranth meet (she also had "her aura tweaked") (l.951–959). LORE: Inquisitors carry taozin wards; Laranth saw one "but couldn't sense him" (l.967–971). Sal came via Sil's Place (Amani pubtender) three weeks ago (l.993–997). Second Force yank (l.1013).
- FIGHT Tesla vs Kaj in the repulsor-field interstice (l.1017–1095): FEAT (Kaj): a Force barrier slaps Tesla out of a Force leap "by someone who had remarkable strength in it" (l.1029–1031); breaks Tesla's Force grip and wrenches his feet out (l.1045); swings a 5 m buttress at him (Tesla deflects it skyward) (l.1051–1053); reads Tesla's intent (l.1057). FEAT (Kaj): "reached into the transparent energy fabric of the repulsor field—something that should have been impossible—and literally wrenched out a blazing ball of energy" (l.1059); the first ball knocks Tesla back despite a barrier (l.1061); the second, parried with the saber, blows him 7–8 m up; his Force cocoon is "all that kept him from breaking bones" (l.1067). Tesla: "So could you be [an Inquisitor], with your power" (l.1073). FEAT (Kaj): mental force — "a rending sound in his head… searing pain"; paralyzes Tesla: "Leave me alone" (l.1079–1081). Tesla's Force lightning multiplies off the field walls (l.1083–1087). FEAT (Kaj): reshapes/closes the field interstice, trapping Tesla; the fields blaze and concuss (l.1089–1095, 1121). ANTI-FEAT (Tesla): soundly beaten by an untrained 15-year-old.
- Jax/Laranth witness: Tesla's crimson saber; Kaj (15–16) exits two stories up with a perfect somersault, "closing of a curtain" to seal the flux (l.1105–1121). Jax: "I'm a Jedi Knight… My name is Jax" (l.1135).
