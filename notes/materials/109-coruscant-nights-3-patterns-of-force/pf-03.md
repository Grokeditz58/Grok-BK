- Second Inquisitor (Mas Sirrah) leaps at Laranth with double lightning (l.3929–3931). FEAT (Kaj, top): "a massive Force-push from every angle"; the Inquisitor is "simply gone… only a fine swirl of ash" (l.3951). Tesla escapes (l.3957–3959). ANTI-FEAT (Tesla): flees; durasteel through his side; uses the Force to slow the bleeding; "second defeat" (l.3973–3979).
