## Strongest findings
1. **Chin's account of the backstory (p415).** Rahn was "a wanderer, sort of an orphan". Morgan found the valley "on another planet, which we left" "unnamed". Rahn "gives Morgan the lightsaber to pass on" "to Kyle". Kyle "was an Imperial soldier, and in fact became one because he thought his" "father was killed by Rebels" (p414). See [jk-04](jk-04.md).
2. **Influences (pp413–414):** *Seven Samurai*, Kurosawa, and *The Good, the Bad and the Ugly*. The Valley of the Jedi stands in for that film's legendary gold, and the Dark Jedi are "sort of like the" "evil seven samurai". The story is personal for Chin: "It’s about my own childhood, growing up with my father," "then losing my father." (p416).
3. **Cut and merged levels.** "In the original Jedi Knight game design, Missions 5 and 6 were a single mission." (p182). Missions 18 and 19 were also one level, from designer Doug Shannon, who "left the company" (p412).
4. **Qu Rahn's assessments of each Dark Jedi**, with stats:
   - Sariss: "SARISS IS THE ONE I" … "CAN SAY I FEAR." (p323). Health 1900.
   - Maw: "For every cord of muscle, there is" … "hatred." (p308). Health 900.
   - Boc: "LACKS BOTH TACT AND TEETH" (p390). Health 1500.
   - Jerec: health 2000. He "has learned how to tap into the Force Energy flow at the core of the Valley" (p396).
5. **Easter eggs and designer secrets:**
   - The "House of Max" in Barons Hed, with "Max, the psychotic bunny with a penchant for wanton violence" (pp164, 173).
   - Christopher Ross's untakeable backpack (p103).
   - Derleth's miracle healing station (p249).
   - Cheat codes such as RACCOONKING and DEEZNUTS (p12).
6. **Moral system:**
   - Hitting WeeGee with the saber "propels you … toward the dark side" (p146).
   - The Ugnaught tests (pp230, 244).
   - Chin: "Yeah, the banality of evil." He credits Reed Derleth with the moral choices (p418).
