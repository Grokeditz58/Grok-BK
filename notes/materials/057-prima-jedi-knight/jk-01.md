# 057 / jk-01: Prima *Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II* guide: front matter and Part I (PDF 1–72)

**Source:** *Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II, The Official Strategy Guide*, by Rick Barba. Prima Publishing (Rocklin, CA), ©1997 Lucasfilm, ISBN 0-7615-0922-4. Read from MEGA `0Ksw2bYb`, a 426-page scan, OCR'd on the box and hand-read page by page.
**Citations** are PDF pages. **Printed page = PDF − 13** (PDF 182 is printed "169" and PDF 308 is printed "295"). The opening missions are slightly off from this: PDF 142 carries OCR "128", while the TOC gives 129. The source file is not committed.

## Front matter (PDF 1–12)
- **Credits page (p3):**
  - Editors: Allan Kausch and Cara Evangelista for Lucasfilm, Mollie Boero for LucasArts, Chris Balmain for Prima.
  - LCCN 96-70082.
- **Contents (pp4–6):**
  - Part I: General Strategies and Statistics.
  - Part II: Jedi Walkthrough (Hard Difficulty Mode), p73. The OCR reads "Ditticulty".
  - Part III: Appendix, which holds the Design Team profiles and an interview with Justin Chin.
- **Introduction (p8):** it bills the game as "ONE OF THE MOST ANTICIPATED GAMES IN INDUSTRY HISTORY". The guide was made "WITH THE HANDS-ON HELP OF THE Jepi KNIGHT DESIGN AND TESTING TEAMS". Barba spent "A GIDDY WEEK AT OUR OWN DESK" in a LucasArts space he calls "A BIZARRE, TWISTED ARCHITECTURE OF DARK CUBICLES RIGHT OUT OF NAR SHADDAA" (the OCR's "Jepi" stands for "JEDI").

## Part I: General Strategies (PDF 13–72)
- **Sources (p13):** "Tim Miller and Matthew Azeveda were the spokesmen for the Jedi Knight testing team". p68 gives Miller's title as "assistant lead tester". Barba says the team helped make Jedi Knight "a surefire candidate for game of the year".
- **Moral system:**
  - "Don’t condemn yourself to the dark side with indiscriminate bomb-tossing on the streets of Barons Hed" (p18).
  - "Waste is as evil as Emperor Palpatine" (p19).
- **Enemy statistics:** "LucasArts inner sanctum in San Rafael" (p22). Christopher Ross supplied the stats.
- **Bestiary notes:**
  - The AT-ST is "Nicknamed “chicken,”" (p25).
  - "The Gran death grunt is the punch line of many a galactic joke." (p35).
  - Gamorreans are "about 1.8 meters tall and weigh about 100 kilos" (p41).
  - The mailoc is the "creepiest creature" (p43).
  - The Kell dragon has health 500. The guide's advice: "don’t get cocky, kid" (p44).
- **Civilians (p45):** "If you truly wish to honor the memory of your father and Jedi Master Qu Rahn, you won’t harm the civilians".
- **Droids:**
  - "It's fun to run into a Gonk and listen to it say “Gonk.”" (p48).
  - Protocol droids are "bordering on sentience" (p48).
  - The medical droid is modelled on 2-1B, which the guide spells "Too-Onebee" (p49).
- **Tester voices:**
  - Azeveda: "I’m kind of a purist about combat" (p42).
  - Azeveda: "I never use my fists unless I’m playing the dark side and I’m taking out pedestrians" (p53).
- **Weapons:**
  - The repeater is "more fun than a barrel of Kian’tharian insectoids" (p55).
  - The lightsaber's "focusing jewel" (p57). Players can practise on the wall device in Morgan Katarn's workshop.
- **Force economy (p64):**
  - There are "a total of 31 Force Power stars".
  - Rank rises after missions 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14 and 18.
  - The six Dark Jedi duels (missions 7, 11, 14, 16, 20 and 21) have no secrets.
- **Protection (p66):** LucasArts testers nicknamed it the hamster ball "because of its sparkling sphere effect".
- **Multiplayer, with Tim Miller (pp68–72):**
  - "Keep moving or die." (p69)
  - "A solid double-hit can knock 98 points off their health." (p69)
  - "Don't let anybody get behind you, or you're dead." (p70)
  - Sariss "Learn from her." (p70)
  - Miller camps in the "Nar Shaddaa loading terminal area" (p72).
