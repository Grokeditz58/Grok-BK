## Mission 9: Fuel Station (p226)
- The setup: 8t88 has escaped to Jerec's cargo ship, which "is about to debark from a fuel station for the Valley of the" Jedi (p226).
- **Ugnaught moral test (p230):** "Gosh, he’s cute. Should we kill him?" … "OK, the answer is no, not unless" … "you crave the Force Powers of the dark side."
- The guide asks "If you’re a true Jedi, push the little fellow to the edge of the lift" (p244). It flags "Another moral choice" (p246).
- **Designer secret (p249):** "a nifty secret that level designer Reed Derleth revealed to me". The guide adds: "this is a miracle healing station".

## Mission 10: 8t88's Reward (p258)
- The setup: "You know he can’t get that map out of his head." (p258). Jerec's ship is "the massive, whale-shaped vessel" (p261).

## Mission 11: The Brothers of the Sith, Gorc and Pic (p273)
- **Qu Rahn's assessment (p273):** "It is a mystery why" … "one would call Gore and Pic twins. Even though one is a" "miniature version of the other, they look nothing alike." Gorc is "the power" and Pic "the energy", "one the voice" and the other the body. The OCR consistently reads "Gorc" as "Gore".
- Tester Azeveda on fighting Pic without Seeing: “He’s hard as hell without it.” The guide: "Focus on Gore first. He’s easier to defeat." (The OCR reads Gore for Gorc.) (p274).

## Mission 12: Escape with the Map (p276)
- **Typo:** "you’ve finally gotten a head in this world—8D88’s head, to be precise." (p276). Elsewhere the droid is 8t88.
- The guide on height: "It’s so high up, you’ll need a shot of bacta after you drop back down!" (p281).

## Mission 13: Lost Planet of the Jedi (p290)
- The setup: "EREC HAS HIS HOOKS IN THIS PLACE, ALL RIGHT." The J is a lost drop cap. "Thank goodness you’re a Jedi Knight. Otherwise you" … "might consider wimping out." (p290).
- A sidebar notes that stormtroopers shot from underwater can't hit back. The guide calls them "idiots" (p295).
- The section ends: "What’s at the top? I’ll give you a clue: It’s dark." (p306).

## Mission 14: Maw: The Revenge (p308, printed 295)
- **Qu Rahn's assessment (p308):** "a bitter individual who loathes all and holds loyalty to few." "For every cord of muscle, there is" … "hatred. It is this hate that keeps his aging body strong." (The OCR moves the last line to the top of the page.)
- Maw has health 900 and uses Force Throw and Grip. p310 splits the fight into three stages.

## Mission 15: The Falling Ship (p312)
- The setup: "EREC’S CARGO SHIP IS FALLING, AND YOU’RE TRAPPED INSIDE." The page header misprints or misreads the number as "Mission 19" (p312). The guide notes "(All other enemies won’t attack)" (p312).
- Of the four routes, the secret airshaft route alone earns a Force star: "We go the extra mile." (p319).
- On the shaft: "Walk up the shaft! (Boy, it’s fun.)" (p321).

## Mission 16: Sariss (or Yun) (p323)
- **Branching:** on the dark path, "you face a rematch with Yun, who’s dis-" "appointed in your Force choice" (p323).
- **Qu Rahn's assessment of Sariss:** "SARISS IS THE ONE I" … "CAN SAY I FEAR." She is "A MASTER, A" … "PERFECTIONIST, QUIET AND" reserved (p323).
- Sariss has health 1900. The guide: "If even Qu Rahn fears her, maybe you’d better dredge up some healthy dread yourself." (p324).
- Yun in the rematch has health 1000: "He also doesn’t like your new attitude." (p326).

## Mission 17: Valley Tower Ascent (p328)
- With the other Dark Jedi gone, that leaves only Boc and Jerec. The caps OCR reads "LEAVES ONLY BOC AND" (p328). The goal is to "save Jan".
- The guide on the crash: "Jerec’s little cargo ship mishap—oops!" (p334). A climb is justified with "Why? Because it’s there!" (p342).

## Mission 18: Descent into the Valley (p352)
- The setup: "EREC HAS CRACKED OPEN THE UNDERGROUND BURIAL CHAMBER." "power of the ancient Jedi flows through him." "Things don’t look so good, do they?" (p352).
- On the drill payload: "Nobody knows, except Jerec. But nobody wants to ask him, not even the Jedi Knight design team." (p357).
- The guide's asides: "Too bad the Empire uses such cheap materials in its new installations." (p359). Also: "Somebody should call maintenance." (p367).

## Mission 19: The Valley of the Jedi (p372)
- The guide flags this one: "So savor it." (p372). It also says "battle through a bajillion more stormtroopers" (p372).
- Kyle: “They must have unearthed the entrance to the valley down there.” (p381).
- **Kell dragons:** the step is headed "Kell! Kell! Kell!" and a caption reads "PLEASE DON’T KELL ME." (p383).
- The stone-door puzzle includes a warning: "(It’s tempting, but don’t cut the rope!)" (p386).
- A Force Boost "adds 100 points to current reserve (maximum at this level is 400 points)" (p387).

## Mission 20: Boc (p390)
- **Qu Rahn's assessment:** "I CAN’T ABIDE THIS CREATURE NAMED Boc." … he is "A CRUDE INDIVIDUAL WHO LACKS BOTH TACT AND TEETH. ONLY A DARK JEDI CAN HE BE." (p390). He fights with two lightsabers.
- The guide on Boc: "he’s got that Twi’lek thing around his neck". He "hops about like a demented durkii" (p392).

## Mission 21: Jerec (p394)
- The framing: "INCE THE TIME OF THE CLONE WARS, NO JEDI BATTLE HAS" "LOOMED QUITE SO LARGE AS THIS ONE." The first S is a lost drop cap. "Fortunately, you have interrupted him in midusurpation." (p394).
- **Qu Rahn's assessment:** "HEAVY BROW OVERSHADOWS THE EMPTY" … "RECESSES THAT NORMALLY EMBRACE EYES." This refers to Jerec's blindness (p394).
- **The mechanic (pp396–397):** Jerec "has learned how to tap into the Force Energy flow at the core of the Valley". The player cuts it off with two wall panels behind sliding Jedi statues. If the statues reach the tower, Jerec "chuckles—the full" "power of the ancient" "Jedi now infuses" "him—and you lose." This is a sidebar, broken into lines by the OCR.
