## Mission 1: Nar Shaddaa (p74)
- The mission opens with the joke "Apparently, Obi-Wan never visited Nar Shaddaa." (p74). The objective: "Stop 8t88 before he escapes with your father’s data disk." (p74).
- The Smuggler's Bar is the "seediest joint this side of the Mos Eisley Cantina". The guide adds: "More Gran. I hate these guys. They make dewbacks look cute." (p78).
- The guide's asides:
  - On two doomed guards: "Don’t you feel bad for doomed" side characters sometimes? (p92; the OCR breaks the line at "sub-sidiary").
  - "“Eetaii!” to you too, lizard boy." (p80)

## Mission 2: The Lost Disk (p94)
- The objective: "Find 8t88’s severed arm to retrieve Morgan Katarn’s data disk." (p94).
- **Designer Easter egg (p103):** Christopher "Ross put a backpack there for your viewing pleasure, but you can't take it." (This is a sidebar; the OCR interleaves it with the main text.) Only fly-mode cheaters reach it.
- The super shield is "invulnerable, but only for about 30 seconds". Also: Jan "won’t" … "land the Crow until you’ve eliminated every one of" the six goons (p120).

## Mission 3: Sulon (p122)
- The Grave Tuskens are "the most barbarous scavengers in the galaxy". Also: "an ungainly homemade droid named WeeGee is Kyle’s key to decoding the data disk" (p122).
- On the battery trench: "My God, that’s a live battery trench!" (p137).

## Mission 4: The Jedi's Lightsaber (p142)
- The setting: "The Empire may be reeling since the death of Emperor Palpatine and the destruction of his sec-" "ond Death Star" (p142). The game is set after ROTJ.
- Kyle: "Let’s see what this saber … can do." The guide: "Didn’t you spend your childhood waiting for this moment?" (p146).
- **Moral trap (p146):** hitting WeeGee with the saber "it propels you … toward the dark side" (the OCR interleaves a sidebar).

## Mission 5: Barons Hed (p164)
- Kyle: “The Empire sure knows how to ruin a perfectly good city.” (p164). The guide adds that "back alley sentiment for the Rebellion runs strong" (p164).
- **The House of Max (pp164, 172–175):** the guide calls it "the enigmatic House of Max". The OCR then garbles the phrase calling it the wildest Easter egg in Jedi Knight. The enemy list includes "Possibly Max (that’s right, I said Max)" (p164).
  - The secret comes "according to level designer Reed Derleth" (p172).
  - p173 describes Steve Purcell's "Max, the psychotic bunny with a penchant for wanton violence". The caption reads "HE’s A BUNNY. I’M A DOG. WE’RE DANGEROUS, BUT WE WORK CHEAP."
  - The guide warns: "Whatever you do, don’t attack Max! He’ll go psycho stalker on you" (p174).
- **Rebel storeroom (pp176–177):** the clerk says “I know nothing. Leave me alone. Leave us in peace!” Once the secret is found: "Long live the Rebellion!" (p177).

## Mission 6: Into the Dark Palace (p182, printed 169)
- **Development note:** "In the original Jedi Knight game design, Missions 5 and 6 were a single mission. Imagine that!" (p182).
- The guide promises "some of the fiercest melee … combat in all of Jedi Knight" (p182).
- Probe droids float like malevolent jellyfish (p191; the OCR breaks the line at "malev-olent"). The guide's aside: "Whoever built this palace is pretty paranoid, wouldn’t you say?" (p202).

## Mission 7: Yun (p205)
- **Qu Rahn's assessment:** "YUN, A YOUNG AND WILLING Dark JEDI, THE NEWEST ADDITION TO THE CAUSE. BRASH AND EAGER" … he is ready to prove himself to his master at any cost (p205; the mission is titled "Yun: The Dark Youth").
- Yun has health 500 and uses Blinding and Persuasion. He has "more health hit points than an AT-ST walker. Good luck." (p205).

## Mission 8: Palace Escape (p208)
- The setup: "Morgan Katarn’s map is destroyed," … "but you know its impression is logged somewhere inside 8t88’s thick titanium skull." (p208).
- On Yun's exit: "Yun has just escaped through the ceiling via some sort of Jedi levitation" (p211).
- Kyle: “There’s gotta be a better way to make a living.” (p214).
- **The author admits defeat (p223):** "(I couldn’t do it. I didn’t meet anyone at LucasArts who had, either. But you’re most welcome to try.)"
- The mission ends with "Time to change CDs!" (p224), because the game shipped on two CDs.
