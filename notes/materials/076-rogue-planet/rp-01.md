## Ch. 1: The garbage-pit wing race (l.114–344)
- Anakin sneaks into an illegal wing race in Coruscant's garbage pits (Wicko district). The pit is 2 km wide and 3 km deep; more than 5,000 pits exist; Coruscant makes "a trillion tons of garbage every hour", and canisters are fired into orbit every 5 seconds through accelerator shields.
- Mace Windu clips Obi-Wan with an elbow: "you should have known I was here." Yoda's charge, in flashback: "The chosen one Qui-Gon gave to us all, not proven, full of fear, and yours to save."
- Obi-Wan's hand-wave trick on a stout boy fails. A Blood Carver racer (later named Ke Daiv) is an assassin with a twister knife and a telescoping arm; he gouges Anakin's wrist.
- Obi-Wan buys oversized wings, leaps and falls about 300 m. Anakin falls under 100 m before getting control; he flies by sensing gradient fields.
- Garbage worms are hundreds of metres long and 3–4 m wide, and "could actually eat a defunct hyperdrive core and survive … for a few seconds."
- Nightmare hint: "The dark empty boots that trod the worst of his nightmares."
- Anakin can't yet levitate "more than a few centimeters."
- **Combat:** Obi-Wan lands both feet on the Blood Carver's wings, cuts the wingtips with two saber swipes, and drops him into a silicone lake.
- **Force feat (key):** Anakin asks for "a little boost … up and out". Something "lifted them through the next shield as if they lay in the palm of a giant hand. Obi-Wan Kenobi had never felt so close to such a powerful connection with the Force, not in Qui-Gon, nor Mace Windu. Not even in Yoda." (l.341–342)

## Ch. 2: Sienar and Tarkin (l.345–403)
- Raith Sienar and Commander Wilhuff Tarkin (Republic Outland Regions Security Force) are both in their early 30s, "might have been brothers", and have been friends for 10 years.
- Sienar: "Every ship I make, I equip with weapons." He shows four Advanced Project ships: black, 20 m wide, flat cooling vanes, spherical cockpits, stackable. These are proto-TIEs.
- Tarkin: "Humans are the future, Raith." His job is to track all Jedi on Coruscant and "discourage any increase in their power". He planted a broken spy droid for Anakin to repair, which bugs the Temple.
- **Proto-Death Star:** a hologram sphere with a slice removed. An "implosion core with a plasma about a kilometer in diameter could power an artificial construct the size of a small moon." Sienar is thinking of "one large ball, ninety or a hundred kilometers in diameter." Tarkin: "A small crew could police an entire system with one vessel."

## Ch. 3: The Temple hearing (l.404–520)
- Mace bio: "In physical training, he was among the toughest to best, because his moves could be so unexpected"; "easily worthy of the legendary Masters of past millennia".
- Obi-Wan: "Pain guides, but it does not support." Anakin: "I burn like a sun inside!" and "My own mother didn't know what to do with me."
- **Thracia Cho Leem** (grey hair, ultramarine eyes, Anakin's height, trains female Jedi): "Anakin, your powers, someday, could surpass those of anyone in this room." Her old apprentice Vergere has been silent for a year. The mission is "far beyond the rule of the Republic".

## Ch. 4–6: Droids, Charza Kwinn, the briefing (l.521–592)
- Anakin repairs a compact protocol droid (Tarkin's plant). His own droid, found in the Council chamber, comes back "neatly cut in half, the edges fused".
- Dream: "something very strong, and it did not care whether it was loved or feared."
- Anakin on meditation: "It's like I'm plugging into a supernova."
- **Star Sea Flower:** a modified YT-1150. Anakin rates it "Class 0.8, faster than anything in the Republic or the Trade Federation listings."
- **Charza Kwinn:** male Priapulin, about 4 m long, five notochords, three pairs of eyes, and spines that work as hundreds of fingers. He has served the Jedi for over a century.
- Briefing: Sekotan ships are "rated easily at zero-point-four". **Early Yuuzhan Vong hint:** "an intrusion was reported three years ago in the Gardaji region, from an unknown space-faring species."

## Ch. 7: Sienar's museum (l.593–665)
- A secret museum more than 1 km under Sienar's plant holds 2,012 failed weapons. Exhibits include four 4 m Kol Huro combat droids and a self-replicating asteroid-invasion pod.
- Sienar is designing the fastest, best-armed ship for a customer "whose name it is death to even whisper" (a Sidious hint). He names the moon-ball the **"Expeditionary Battle Planetoid."**
- Sienar owns a dead Sekotan ship: "The ships are useless unless their owners are alive." About 100 have been made, "Out of twenty million spacecraft."

## Ch. 8–11: Food-kin, Ke Daiv, the hulk (l.666–771)
- Charza eats shipmate food-kin ("They aspire to be consumed by the Big One").
- Tarkin: "Friendship is a thing of the past. All now is alliance and advantage." "It could take decades to make humans the supreme race in this galaxy … we have powerful forces behind us. Forces even I shudder to consider."
- Ke Daiv is from a political family on Batorine and is a former assassin: "Assassination, too, is a kind of sculpting."
- Sienar is to command ex-Trade Federation ships with a "new variety of battle droid … no longer centrally controlled".
- Anakin's prank: a protocol droid dressed in Jedi robes fools Obi-Wan for 2 minutes.
- Sienar's Sekotan hulk "cost me a hundred million credits". It has "No cellular structure, dense and varied tissues that can incorporate both metals and … heat-resistant polymers", and "without its owner, it quickly dies."
