# Rogue Planet (Greg Bear, Del Rey 2000): notes part 2, Ch. 12–24

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. This part covers FULL.txt l.772–1249. Citation "RP ch. N" (descriptive chapter map; see README).

## Ch. 12: Zonama Sekot data (l.772–824)
- Size: "Eleven thousand kilometers" across. It is in a triple system in the Gardaji Rift: a red giant and white dwarf pair make a red/purple "pinwheel" in the sky, and Zonama orbits the third star, a yellow sun "several light-months distant."
- Boras (giant trees) are "five or six hundred meters tall." The inhabitants are settlers, not natives.
- Obi-Wan (Qui-Gon's teaching): "The unknown is a place where we can discover who we truly are."

## Ch. 13–14: Arrival and Anakin's "forwarding" (l.825–887)
- Tarkin's mission is given "level-two priority": "We are below the level of awareness of those who will command the galaxy."
- Obi-Wan carries 10 aurodium ingots worth **3 billion credits**. Anakin: "I could buy … a hundred YZ-1000s!"
- From 30,000 km: north pole white, northern hemisphere green, southern hemisphere under "impenetrable silvery cloud."
- **Force:** Anakin "forwards", feeling ahead into the future, and picks up "a severely faded signal from the future, a voice, large and heavy" (Sekot). Obi-Wan "never felt such a strength of connection with any other being—not even Qui-Gon Jinn."

## Ch. 15: Sienar's squadron (l.888–920)
- Flagship *Admiral Korvin* (an old Trade Federation munitions cruiser): 3 landing craft, 100 troops, more than 3,000 droids, sky mines and droid starfighters, plus a probe ship, decoy and repair station.
- Sienar: "Naboo was lost even though the forces deployed by the Trade Federation were hundreds of times larger than this."

## Ch. 16–18: The northern plateau (l.921–1009)
- The landing plateau is about 1,000 km long, and the snow is rainbow-hued. Visitors may stay 60 days at most.
- A woman's sudden "The Magister tells me you are to go south" is a silent relay of orders.
- **Force:** Obi-Wan senses "a possible imbalance in the Force so drastic it overshadowed anything he had ever imagined. Anakin Skywalker was not so much at risk as he was a possible cause of this imbalance."

## Ch. 19–21: Middle Distance (l.1010–1127)
- Anakin: "one big wave, a really big change that's already happened or is coming."
- **Gann** is a Ferroan: over 2 m tall, pale blue skin. Middle Distance was settled "Sixty standard years" ago. Gann: "The planet is named Zonama. The living world that covers it is named Sekot … The power of Sekot is awesome."
- Lamina is a living building material; furniture roots into the floor to feed. Anakin: "It's an immensity, a unity … Not a lot of little voices." He cannot sense Vergere ("She has gone").
- Sheekla Farrs and her husband Shappa will guide the ship design. Ferroans can smell fear.
- **Lore, the Potentium:** "a controversial view of the Force" that the Council judged to be in error. It is tied to a former apprentice who founded his own Jedi-like school for rich students; Qui-Gon and Mace dealt with it.

## Ch. 22: The E-5 (l.1128–1148)
- Sienar keeps an unmarketed verbobrain program that subverts droids, tested on a Baktoid E-5 heavy droid.

## Ch. 23: Seed-partner chamber (l.1149–1187)
- **Force feat:** "The boy had totally damped all extraneous personal vibrations. He had become quiet in the Force in a way Obi-Wan still found exceptionally difficult … and the boy had done this in fractions of a second."
- Obi-Wan recalls Maul: "encountered and defeated, though just barely."
- An avalanche of spikeball seeds buries both of them.

## Ch. 24: Sienar turns Ke Daiv (l.1188–1249)
- Ke Daiv survived the garbage worms. Blood Carvers' afterlife is the "Art Beyond Dying".
- Sienar: "Killing Jedi is a mug's game … They are strong and they have honor." He turns Ke Daiv against Tarkin with a humans-only rumor.
