# Rogue Planet (Greg Bear, Del Rey 2000): notes part 5, Ch. 48–67 and Coda

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. This part covers FULL.txt l.2315–2870. Citation "RP ch. N".

## Ch. 48: The Potentium and Leor Hal (l.2315–2358)
- Potentium belief, about 100 years ago: "the Force could not push one into evil … a benevolent field of life energy." No adherent fell to the dark side.
- The first Magister was **Leor Hal**, "a particularly prominent young Jedi apprentice who had left the Temple voluntarily." He sold ships to "buy huge hyperdrive cores. And to import huge engines" for one purpose: escape.
- The sky mines spread "for thousands of kilometers."

## Ch. 49–50: The real palace (l.2359–2415)
- The palace is buried by a landslide; the mountain was "hit by laser cannon fire, or something even more powerful."
- There are "hundreds of thousands" of sky mines, with "fierce tracking ability and split-second maneuverability."
- Obi-Wan's doubt: "The Force disappointed you, did it not, Obi-Wan? It allowed your Master to die."

## Ch. 51: Anakin's dark side (l.2416–2454)
- Anakin regulates his own blood chemistry in thin air.
- "What will you do, slave boy?" sets off his anger: "the upwelling of hatred and the dark side … a long, deep cave leading down to unimaginable strength."
- **Perception feat:** a lance swing "quick as lightning" becomes "a slow, curiously protracted swing." "His eyes became black as pitch." Anakin: "I can't hold it back any longer!"

## Ch. 52–53: Battle opens (l.2455–2480)
- Sekotan defenders grab starfighters "in tractor fields, and drag them down." The rectangles rise, "the tallest of them stood more than two kilometers above the jungle."
- Obi-Wan: "To bring back a spiritually damaged boy of Anakin's abilities might be worse than finding him dead."

## Ch. 54: Aftermath (l.2481–2503)
- **Kill feat:** with no weapon, Ke Daiv's "eyes had turned white, and the flesh around his head and neck had cracked. He was bleeding profusely." Anakin: "I did it all wrong … He's like me, but he never had the Jedi to help." Ke Daiv dies.

## Ch. 55: Sekot reveals itself (l.2504–2527)
- Anakin "understood the nature of the Force—the many natures of the Force—better than a century of teaching in the Temple." Precognitive navigation.
- He finds the Magister's corpse wearing a Jedi apprentice signet ring. He puts Jabitha to sleep with a touch.
- Sekot appears as Vergere, then as the Magister: "I think my name, to some, is Sekot."

## Ch. 56–57: Sekot speaks; Tarkin takes Anakin (l.2528–2579)
- Tarkin: "If they do not submit, we will destroy them utterly."
- **Sekot:** "my memories go back billions of turnings. Parts of me saw the pinwheel grow in the sky." "They burned me down here. I've never known such pain." "I reached out with the weapons we had made, and I killed … It was Vergere who drew away the Far Outsiders. She did not kill them; she persuaded them."
- Tarkin captures Anakin and the ship using a sky-mine ship as a lander.

## Ch. 58: Obi-Wan's rescue run (l.2580–2605)
- Holding Qui-Gon's saber, he "felt he now had the strength of two."
- **Combat feat:** he deflects two vulture-droid cannon blasts; "their force nearly ripped the lightsaber from his hands." Charza's YT-1150 kills two starfighters.

## Ch. 59–61: Orbit (l.2606–2661)
- Sekot: "You are part of the fleet I may have to destroy soon." "We have never had a drill and tried everything all at once … The engines, the hyperdrive cores."
- Obi-Wan: "He did it without a lightsaber, against a much stronger foe. Why then do I feel that something went badly wrong?"

## Ch. 62: Tarkin and Anakin (l.2662–2709)
- **Force:** Tarkin's hand "burned" on Anakin's shoulder, and "Tarkin felt something twitch in his chest." Anakin: "I was going to do it again!"
- **Combat feat:** Obi-Wan, "at the hub of a dozen spokes of laser fire … deflected each and every beam," "with supreme skill and blinding speed." Nine guards retreat. Anakin leaps 3 m.

## Ch. 63–64: Pod (l.2710–2721)
- Sienar: "I designed them for lightness, not strength." Tarkin: "destroy all that remains on the planet!"

## Ch. 65: The planet leaves (l.2722–2809)
- **Ship feats:** the hull self-heals a rip across the stern. Obi-Wan learns to pilot through the ship link "in a few seconds." The ship climbs, "reaching orbital velocity in seconds."
- Defender craft are "none larger than sixty or seventy meters." "I think Sekot is the pilot. It's controlling all of them."
- **Planet feats:**
  - "Pillars of brilliant light … Starfighters overhead were blown high into the stratosphere."
  - "hyperdrive field guides—and they're over three hundred kilometers high!" The crust strains and magma rises.
  - One failed engine incinerates everything 100 km north.
  - The engines push Zonama Sekot "slowly, majestically, out of its own orbit." Then it jumps to hyperspace: "All the stars around the planet's circumference seemed to suck inward and then bounce back."
  - Tarkin's fleet is scattered; "at least two escorts rammed the Rim Merchant Einem."
- The ship is "faster than anything that had ever flown before."

## Ch. 66–67: Seline (l.2810–2859)
- The ship is dying. Anakin names her **Jabitha**; she dies at the ice outpost Seline. "The boy loses everything he loves, and yet still he is strong."
- Thracia: "Power and even discipline are not sufficient. Self-knowledge is the most difficult of our many journeys."

## Coda (l.2860–2870)
- "No more Sekotan ships are made."
- Tarkin wins back the Supreme Chancellor's favor with plans for a moon-sized battle station and claims sole credit; Sienar does not dispute it.
- Charza later preys only on Imperial ships. Legend of a rogue planet "ruled by either a madman, a madwoman, or a saint."
- Thracia leaves the Order.
- "No balance. No balance yet."
