# Rogue Planet (Greg Bear, Del Rey 2000): notes part 4, Ch. 36–47

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. This part covers FULL.txt l.1775–2314. Citation "RP ch. N".

## Ch. 36–37: Langhesi forgers; Ke Daiv rejected (l.1775–1817)
- Rock pillars hundreds of metres high; the canopy-enclosed space is big enough to form its own clouds and rain. Forger Vagno: "I can easily forge fifteen."
- Ke Daiv's seeds hate him ("Chewing off all his clothes").
- **Kett on the scars:** "could not have been produced by any weapons known to me … may have been made by neutron dissemblers … yet no one in the known galaxy has learned how to harness such weapons." He calls it "a preemptive strike, or a dramatic show of force."

## Ch. 38: The forging (l.1818–1876)
- **Sekot feat:** the tampasi opens a hole of about 100 m in the canopy, and "sun-hot orange bolts of lightning tumbled … The tampasi controls the weather."
- Sekot to Anakin: "There will be heat and death and resurrection. A seed will quicken. Will it burn or shine? Will it think and create or be ruled by fear and destroy?" All 15 seeds survive.

## Ch. 39: Adventure (l.1877–1904)
- Hundreds of much larger forges: "The big ones are being made now."
- Obi-Wan: "Adventure is lack of planning, failure of training." Anakin, quoting Qui-Gon: "adventure is growth."

## Ch. 40: The Jentari (l.1905–1963)
- Parts: **two Haor Chall type-seven Silver-class engines**, an expensive modified hyperdrive core, and organoform circuits.
- The Jentari are giant "composites! Cybernetic organisms!" "Every second, a thousand moves and assemblies." **A ship is built in under 10 minutes along more than 20 km.**

## Ch. 41: Rogue droids (l.1964–1990)
- Sienar's E-5 babbles in unknown languages, and five Xi Char starfighters launch on their own (a hidden Tarkin subcode). One is shot down.

## Ch. 42: The ship; first attack (l.1991–2085)
- Fitch: "The ship's speed lies in part in those cores, but also in how we connect them with the ship's heart—and with you." She needs 48 hours of bonding or "she would rot from the inside out."
- **Specs:** three lobes "like three smooth oval skipping stones". "There were no weapons. She measured about thirty meters across the beam and twenty-five from stem to stern."
- "We have lived in fear since the Far Outsiders!"
- **Combat:** Obi-Wan ignites Qui-Gon's green saber with "full-sensory alertness" against two droid starfighters. Distracted while shielding Anakin, he is blasted several metres; a rock chip to the solar plexus scrambles his control. Ke Daiv: "I could kill you now, Jedi."

## Ch. 43: Anakin taken (l.2086–2135)
- Ke Daiv hits Anakin with a shaping lance ("Slave boy!"). Jabitha is aboard as a hostage.
- **Dark-side hint:** "the primary component of that strength was a red heat of anger. It is the way, boy. Anger and hatred are the fuel."

## Ch. 44: Tarkin's fleet (l.2136–2168)
- Two converted Hoersch-Kessel cargo haulers and ten Corellian light cruisers. Sienar is arrested: "From the beginning, I've been assigned the role of patsy." Palpatine "has taken an interest."

## Ch. 45: The Far Outsiders (l.2169–2214)
- Obi-Wan decides the voice "was not Qui-Gon … He was truly dead."
- **Shappa on the Far Outsiders:** they came 2 years before Vergere. "They, too, seemed to build all their ships and goods from living matter." They wanted "complete control of Zonama Sekot." "Their weapons were powerful." "We drove off the Far Outsiders … We are so much stronger now than we were a year ago." Vergere "left with the Far Outsiders."

## Ch. 46: The Einem (l.2215–2244)
- Tarkin's arsenal: sky-mine delivery ships, droid starfighters, and cruisers that can "lay waste to any inhabited areas". "I have long suspected this planet could create ships and arms for a rebellion." Dozens of craft rise from the jungles.

## Ch. 47: Vergere's message (l.2245–2314)
- Anakin: "I've never been trained in mind tricks, but I know I can do it. Maybe even better than Obi-Wan." The compulsion fails on Ke Daiv.
- **Vergere's message (key Yuuzhan Vong hint):** "I am leaving with the visitors who have provoked a war here and wiped out half of Zonama Sekot." "The Far Outsiders know nothing of the Force. And the Force knows nothing of them. Yet they are not machines, they are definitely alive, and they may pose a great threat to us all." "The heart of a great living creature has started to beat, and a great mind has become aware of itself."
- A lance mark on the hull heals instantly.
