# Rogue Planet: summary of strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. Full text l.1–4251. Citation "RP ch. N" (descriptive chapter map; the epub has no chapter numbers).

1. **Zonama Sekot moves itself into hyperspace.**
   - Engine "pillars of brilliant light" blow starfighters "high into the stratosphere".
   - Its hyperdrive field guides are "over three hundred kilometers high".
   - The crust strains and magma rises; one failed engine incinerates everything 100 km north.
   - The engines push the 11,000 km planet "slowly, majestically, out of its own orbit". Then the stars "seemed to suck inward and then bounce back", and it is gone (RP ch. 65, ch. 12).
2. **Sekot as a planetary mind.**
   - "my memories go back billions of turnings" (RP ch. 57).
   - It pilots every defender craft, each up to 60–70 m (RP ch. 65).
   - It casts an illusion that fools two Jedi at once, something "no single being—not even a Jedi Master" could do (RP ch. 27).
   - It calls down lightning through iron-tipped boras (RP ch. 38).
   - It impersonates the dead Magister (RP ch. 55–57).
3. **Anakin (12) kills Ke Daiv through the dark side, with no weapon.**
   - The lance swing slows to "a slow, curiously protracted swing", and "His eyes became black as pitch" (RP ch. 51).
   - Ke Daiv's "eyes had turned white, and the flesh around his head and neck had cracked" (RP ch. 54).
   - He nearly does it again to Tarkin, whose hand "burned" (RP ch. 62).
   - *The Clone Wars* novelization confirms the kill (back matter l.3040).
4. **Anakin's raw ceiling.**
   - Obi-Wan "had never felt so close to such a powerful connection with the Force, not in Qui-Gon, nor Mace Windu. Not even in Yoda" (RP ch. 1).
   - He goes quiet in the Force "in fractions of a second", which Obi-Wan finds "exceptionally difficult" (RP ch. 23).
   - 12 seed-partners choose him, against a norm of 3–4 (RP ch. 25).
   - Thracia: "your powers, someday, could surpass those of anyone in this room" (RP ch. 3).
5. **Obi-Wan's blaster defense.**
   - "at the hub of a dozen spokes of laser fire … deflected each and every beam" (RP ch. 62).
   - He deflects vulture-droid cannon blasts whose "force nearly ripped the lightsaber from his hands" (RP ch. 58).
   - Anti-feat: he is blown off his feet when distracted (RP ch. 42).
6. **Sekotan ship.**
   - It is unarmed, about 30 × 25 m (RP ch. 42).
   - It reaches "orbital velocity in seconds", self-heals a hull rip, and is "faster than anything that had ever flown before" (RP ch. 65).
   - The Jentari build one in under 10 minutes (RP ch. 40).
7. **Sienar and Tarkin tech.**
   - Proto-TIE Advanced Project ships.
   - A battle planetoid "ninety or a hundred kilometers in diameter" powered by a 1 km plasma implosion core (RP ch. 2), named the "Expeditionary Battle Planetoid" (RP ch. 7).
   - Tarkin claims the moon-sized station as his own (Coda).
8. **Early Yuuzhan Vong hints (Far Outsiders).**
   - An "unknown space-faring species" intrusion (RP ch. 6).
   - Crust slashes "over three kilometers wide … many kilometers deep" from unknown orbital weapons (RP ch. 29), possibly "neutron dissemblers" (RP ch. 37).
   - The southern hemisphere was burned dead (RP ch. 32, 57). The Outsiders build "from living matter" (RP ch. 45).
   - Vergere: "The Far Outsiders know nothing of the Force. And the Force knows nothing of them" (RP ch. 47). Obi-Wan feels "a void" (RP ch. 30).
9. **Unique lore.**
   - The Potentium heresy.
   - First Magister Leor Hal was a former Jedi apprentice (RP ch. 21, 48).
   - The ice outpost Seline (RP ch. 67).
   - Thracia Cho Leem leaves the Order (Coda).
