# Rogue Planet (Greg Bear, Del Rey 2000): notes part 6, back matter (l.2871–4251)

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. Bundled publisher material, not part of the novel. Cited by line.

## Author pages (l.2871–2889)
- About the Author: more than 25 books, 2 Hugos and 5 Nebulas. Also by Greg Bear. Star Wars Legends intro (Del Rey, 2014).

## *The Clone Wars* excerpt, Karen Traviss (l.2890–3098)
- Dooku and Ziro plot the Rotta kidnapping. The Christophsis battle with Rex, Cody, Obi-Wan and Anakin. Ahsoka arrives.
- **Cross-reference (l.3040):** in a frenzy against droids, Anakin remembers "a Blood Carver called Ke Daiv. He'd killed him, too, years ago." This places the RP kill in Anakin's dark-side pattern.
- Anakin uses a Force-assisted hold to rip off a droid's head.

## Era intros and excerpts (l.3099–4051)
- Old Republic intro (l.3099). Excerpt: *Fatal Alliance* (Sean Williams). Shigar Konshi deflects falling spears with the Force.
- Rise of the Empire intro (l.3230). Excerpt: *Yoda: Dark Rendezvous* (Sean Stewart). Jai Maruk is burned by Ventress; flashback to Dooku and Yoda.
- Rebellion intro (l.3355). Excerpt: *Shadows of Mindor* (Stover). Hobbie and Janson against TIE Defenders.
- New Republic intro (l.3468). Excerpt: *X-Wing: Rogue Squadron* (Stackpole). Corran wins the Redemption sim with nine kills, then is bested by an unnamed veteran TIE pilot.
- **New Jedi Order intro (l.3606):** the Yuuzhan Vong are biotech warriors from another galaxy, cut off from the Force. Excerpt: *Vector Prime* (Salvatore). Mara on Nom Anor: "A blank … As if the Force had nothing to do with him." This echoes Vergere's Far Outsiders.
- Legacy intro (l.3825). Excerpts:
  - *LotF: Betrayal* (Allston): Luke dreams "He doesn't exist." Jacen and Ben raid an Adumar missile plant, and Jacen deflects bolts back into the troopers.
  - *FotJ: Outcast* (Allston): Jaina and Jag Fel; Valin Horn sees his parents as impostors.

## Legends timeline (l.4052–4251)
- Rogue Planet is listed under 32 BBY, right after *Episode I* and before *Outbound Flight* and *The Approaching Storm*. That is the timeline's grouping header; the novel's own blurb says three years after TPM.
- The list ends with *Crucible* (45 ABY), and the last line is the asterisk note "*An eBook novella" (l.4251).
