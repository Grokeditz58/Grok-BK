Character quick bios:
- **Anakin Skywalker:** 12, Obi-Wan's Padawan. A gifted pilot and droid tinkerer, angry about his slave past. 12 seed-partners choose him (the norm is 3–4), and he kills Ke Daiv with the dark side.
- **Obi-Wan Kenobi:** Jedi Knight carrying Qui-Gon's green saber. Unsure he can train Anakin, but superb at blaster defense.
- **Vergere:** Thracia's former apprentice. She leaves with the Far Outsiders to study them and to save the planet.
- **Thracia Cho Leem:** Jedi Master and healer who trains female Jedi. She leaves the Order in the Coda.
- **Mace Windu / Yoda:** Council; Yoda appears in flashback only.
- **Sekot:** the planetary mind of Zonama. It woke recently but has memories of "billions of turnings".
- **The Magister:** son of Leor Hal (a Potentium former Jedi apprentice). He died in the Far Outsider war, and Sekot impersonated him.
- **Jabitha:** the Magister's daughter. Anakin names the ship after her.
- **Gann, Shappa and Sheekla Farrs:** Ferroan guide, architect and grower.
- **Charza Kwinn:** Priapulin pilot of the *Star Sea Flower*.
- **Wilhuff Tarkin:** Republic Outland Regions Security commander, a human supremacist.
- **Raith Sienar:** ship designer working on proto-TIEs and the battle planetoid.
- **Ke Daiv:** Blood Carver assassin, killed by Anakin.

Continuity quirks noted in the text:
- The chapters have no numbers or titles in this edition, so the chapter numbers in these notes come from a scene-break map.
- The blurb says three years after TPM, but the bundled timeline groups the book under the 32 BBY header.
- The Old Republic intro recommends *Deceived*, but the excerpt printed is from *Fatal Alliance*.
