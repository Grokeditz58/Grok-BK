# Rogue Planet (Greg Bear, Del Rey 2000): notes part 3, Ch. 25–35

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. This part covers FULL.txt l.1250–1774. Citation "RP ch. N".

## Ch. 25: Fifteen seeds (l.1250–1297)
- Seed-partners choose Obi-Wan 3 times and **Anakin 12 times**. "Three is normal … Your student must be an extraordinary young man!" Anakin "had gone inward to conserve oxygen" while buried.
- Chant: "Great is the Potentium, great the life of Sekot." Three of the seeds had once chosen Vergere.

## Ch. 26: Sienar reprograms his droids (l.1298–1326)
- Captain Kett objects that this breaks Trade Federation policy. The fleet holds position near the yellow sun.

## Ch. 27: The Magister's palace, an illusion (l.1327–1430)
- Obi-Wan: "The Force is never a nursemaid." A huge brown "battle scar", dug to bedrock, is "no more than a few months old".
- The palace sits on an old volcano about 1,000 km south of the equator. Obi-Wan mind-tricks the guards into believing they checked for weapons.
- The Magister was "a promising student" of the Jedi 50 years ago. Obi-Wan's question about Vergere is wiped from his tongue.
- **Sekot feat:** the Magister and his identical daughters "were not real. Yet the illusion had been powerful and almost completely convincing. In Obi-Wan's experience, no single being—not even a Jedi Master—could delude two Jedi at once." He doubts he could use persuasion "on the living tissue, the biosphere, of an entire world." "We've met our match."

## Ch. 28: Shappa and the design (l.1431–1500)
- The seeds molt into four-legged creatures. "The normal complement is three or four."
- Shappa Farrs, a one-armed architect, drafts a ship 30 m long with a wingspan over 90 m. Anakin: "This is ferocious."
- Yoda, recalled: "An artist the Force is … Unpredictable they are, like children."

## Ch. 29: Ke Daiv's recon, the scars (l.1501–1540)
- **Far Outsider weapon scars:** "At least fifteen deep slashes in the crust, over three kilometers wide … clefts many kilometers deep. Perhaps these are the effects of large orbital weapons, though of a power and type unfamiliar to me."
- Rectangular elevations "four hundred kilometers by two hundred" (later revealed as engines). The factory valley is "a thousand kilometers long, three kilometers deep."
- Sienar gives Ke Daiv his private ship and "over six billion credits" to pose as a client. Xi Char vulture droids: 3 landing ships × 10, stealth-cloaked.

## Ch. 30: Jabitha and the bora cycle (l.1541–1598)
- **Yuuzhan Vong hint (Force void):** Obi-Wan feels a change "which Obi-Wan could only describe as a void. Neither Qui-Gon nor any other Jedi Master had ever hinted at such things."
- "There can be only one pilot." Obi-Wan admits he "could never hope to match Anakin's skill in that area."
- Jabitha is the Magister's real daughter; she hasn't seen him in months.
- **Forging:** old boras "call down lightning from the sky … The branches actually have iron tips!" The Magister's father built the first spacecraft from the colony ship's engines.

## Ch. 31: How Sienar got his hulk (l.1599–1649)
- It came from a dying Gensang spice thief and a Zonama sales droid. Kett's past includes transporting "immigrants whose bodies were laced with time-delay biological weapons."

## Ch. 32: Underground river (l.1650–1681)
- "All parts of the planet are connected." Gann: "The south died of a disease just a few months ago, the entire hemisphere."

## Ch. 33–34: Ke Daiv lands; Qui-Gon's voice (l.1682–1722)
- Sienar on the scars: like "snips made along puckered flesh by an expert surgeon".
- Phantom Qui-Gon voice: "He is no more special than any other child, is he?" / "To you, he is." Anakin is "the youngest client ever."

## Ch. 35: Factory valley (l.1723–1774)
- The bora canopy is over 100 m overhead. Anakin feels like two people, with "vague, dark, and separate memories."
- Obi-Wan on midi-chlorians: Ferroans are strong, "But not, I think, as strong as you."
- **Vergere's first message, from the seeds:** "She's left Zonama Sekot, to pursue an even greater mystery … beings from beyond the boundaries, unknown to the Jedi."
