# 171 summary — Rinzler, *The Making of The Empire Strikes Back* (2010)

Full typography-faithful notes on J. W. Rinzler’s making-of of *The Empire Strikes Back*, built from production paperwork, contemporary interviews (including Alan Arnold’s 1978–1979 tapes), and later reminiscences, which are kept distinct.

The book follows the sequel from the November 1977 story conference through the May 20, 1980, Odeon premiere, the first weeks of release, and the 1980–1981 aftermath. Draft identities are kept separate: no father-reveal in the conference/treatment (“The real drama is whether Luke will become Darth Vader or not”); Brackett’s February 17, 1978, first draft, in which Luke’s father (Skywalker) appears as a ghost distinct from Vader and reveals that Luke has a sister; Lucas’s handwritten April 1, 1978, second draft, the first appearance of “I am your father” / “We will rule the galaxy as father and son,” omitted from the typed copy sent to Ladd; Kasdan’s third and fourth drafts; the February 20, 1979, fifth / shooting script; later shooting-script inserts; and the June 21, 1979, carbon-freeze on-set revisions versus script-supervisor continuity. The on-set gantry briefing (Hamill told; Prowse given the dummy line “You don’t know the truth, *Obi-Wan* killed your father”) is distinct from the handwritten reveal, from Hamill’s earlier volcano/clone story, and from Jones’s first-reading reaction, “He’s lying, I wonder how they’re gonna play that lie out.” Carbon-freeze “I love you” / “I know” is recorded in uncollapsed layers, including Ford’s later memory that “the original line was, ‘I love you, too’” and the April 19, 1980, Northpoint decision to keep “I know.” Conflicting production figures are recorded, not collapsed (Williams 107 vs. “l08” minutes; *Executor* ~150,000 vs. 250,000 lights; ILM 600 vs. Edlund ~440 shots; crawl “have” vs. caption “has”; early-cut wampa chest cut vs. later arm cut).
