## Plot (read/73–94) — scan-range label

1. **Split assault (73–77).** Quin engages Morgukai/droids; orders Aayla to Nat + Tholme. Aayla protests wound — Quin: think only the Force. Dual-saber action; Bok returns (“YOU SURVIVED. YOU **ARE** MORGUKAI”). Blast doors; Bok pursues Aayla; Quin holds Tsyr. Cortosis clash **SCHRAKK**; Quin quip: “Two Morgukai against one wounded Jedi? … Would it help if I closed my eyes?”
2. **Ryloth — Lon refuses (78–79).** Ro’s compound. Lon: answer **no**; houses agree — Ro walks Bright Lands; Kh’aris with him. Kh’aris flees firing (“destined for greater things than a walk in the Bright Lands!”). Lon: secure Ro; pursue son; impound ship.
3. **Termination / fake death / rescue (80–83).** Back Kintan: Aayla cuts toward cells. AX/RX: termination signal; syringe. Tholme fights; droid electrocutes — “HEARTBEAT—NIL. JEDI UNIT… DECEASED.” Feign again: “STOPPED… HEARTBEAT. AND BREATHING. OLD… JEDI TRICK.” Aayla arrives; destroys AX/RX (head **VINNNK**). Nat: can’t be Jedi — she’s female. Aayla: female, Twi’lek, **Jedi**, and his **cousin**; galaxy wider than prejudice. Caption: valiant Jedi may pay with life — don’t waste it.
4. **Quin vs Tsyr / Bok (84–85).** Tsyr has **stolen Tholme’s lightsaber** (“prize by right of battle”). Quin Force-pulls it back; dual green Jar’Kai: “Take it from me. If you can.” Later Bok: “YOU TAKE MY FATHER FROM ME! NOW BOK HONORS HIS FATHER BY TAKING **YOUR** LIFE!” Energy-lash downs Quin.
5. **Hangar / Force certainty (86).** Few guards — Kh’aris trusted droids. Aayla senses Quin “sorely hurt”; **knows** he is not meant to die here — never known anything so clearly. Tholme: “It is the Force… Heed it. **Follow it.**” Tholme takes Nat to ship (“I’ll return the boy to Ryloth. Follow when you can.”).
6. **Aayla vs Bok — Knight claim (87–88).** Bok: only apprentice. Aayla: “WRONG.” / “**I AM A JEDI KNIGHT.**” Severs Bok’s hand (**SWIK**); Force-pushes him. Offers mercy — value of life. Bok spits on mercy, jumps (“I GO MY OWN WAY”).
7. **Former Padawan (89).** Aayla Force-heals Quin. She “disobeyed” order but obeyed the Force as taught. Quin: “Well done… my **former** Padawan.” Ship departs Kintan (Tholme’s saber recovered — speaker confirms find).
8. **Villie extracts Kh’aris (90).** Parallel: *Inferno* leaves Ryloth. Villie bluffs Air/Space Control (offers to shove “bad boy” out airlock). Takes Kh’aris to **Ord Mantell**; “better Villie not know” where after.
9. **Bright Lands (91).** Days later. Lon forces Ro into sun-scorched wastes (or House Fenn males all go). Ro curses; notes son escaped. Lon to Nat: ready to go home?
10. **Dooku stronghold (92).** Caption “A HIDDEN STRONGHOLD OF COUNT DOOKU…” Tsyr + Bok kneel — plan failed. Dooku: alive is what matters; Bok’s fall escape ingenious; many plans; failure may bear fruit; **uses for both**. Bok cybernetic hand; honor demands one Jeedai die especially—
11. **Temple ceremony (93–94).** Jedi Temple Coruscant. Mace: Force speaks through actions. Acknowledges Tholme’s former Padawans: **Quinlan Vos — Master** of the Order; **Aayla Secura — Jedi Knight**. Aayla: feels more learner than ever; no final test — learning forever, now by herself. Quin/Tholme: for yourself, not by yourself; Master–Padawan bonds remain; she is ready — journey truly begins. **FIN.**
