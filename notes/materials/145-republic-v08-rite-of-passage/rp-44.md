## Plot (read/51–72)

1. **Dust settles (51).** Caption: ruined Jedi craft; sunset Endless Wastes of **Kintan**. Morgukai: “JEEDAI SHOULD BE DEAD.” Tsyr: both tracks — both Jedi survived. Bok: “A JEEDAI FOR EACH MORGUKAI!” Tsyr: respect Jedi foe; “FACE ONE, AS HAVE I, THEN SAY.”
2. **Destroy Morgukai ship (52–53 spread).** Spinning lightsaber discs (thrown sabers) shred ship/supplies — **VIT VIT / SCHRAKK / BOOM**. Quin: “Satisfactory. Their ship is gone, along with their supplies and means of communicating with their base. Our positions are equal.” Aayla: “I overthrew.” Quin: “You **overthought**.” Thermal detonator → “JUMP!”
3. **Fall / mission priority (54).** Force-break fall. Quin: Morgukai not the mission — rescue Tholme + Nat. Fight only if must. Tsyr: wait where Jedi will be (home-ground).
4. **Dungeon — Tholme + Nat (55–56).** Caption “FORTRESS KH’ARIS, THE DUNGEONS…” AX/RX: “JEDI-UNIT DESIGNATED THOLME SHUT DOWN” / store with Nat. Tholme wakes: droids **immune to Jedi mind manipulations** → he **dropped life signs to minimal** to be placed with Nat. Comforts Nat (Van dead; Nat feels unloved): all linked through the Force; Jedi word to get him home.
5. **Ryloth demands (57–58).** Lon threatens war. Kh’aris (not Ro) holds boy. Terms: end exile; dissolve Lessu council → Kh’aris sole ruler; extend one-ruler model planetwide. Lon concedes. Kh’aris turns on Ro: took blame for Ro’s crimes; exile was to be short; survived on smuggled **ryll**; “Who controls Ryloth controls the ryll”; Ro = past → exile Ro.
6. **Villie → Lon (59).** Message from Jedi: stall; Quin tracking son. Villie self-talk: spice load; maybe friendship.
7. **River of Fire crossing (60–61).** Lava / ground-glass sand. Force-leap stepping stones; Quin moves rock (**WHUMP**). “DO NOT THINK. ACT.”
8. **Morgukai ambush at Fire River (62–68).** Cortosis staff vs blue saber (**FAZT** — does not cut through). Aayla traps between Morgukai + lava; deflects bolts; duels Bok. Quin steadies rock for her leap; wounded by Morgukai **throwing discs** (SCHIK/TCHEK). Tsyr’s blaster hits Bok (“YOUR SHOT WOUNDED ME!”); Tsyr abandons son to lava as Morgukai test: prove yourself or deserve death. Bok screams “FATHER!”
9. **Fortress approach / cave (69–70).** Difficult climb (unexpected). Sandstorm. Shelter. Aayla’s slave-role trauma → hesitation. Quin: question useful, doubt not; she passed preliminary Knight trials — **this is her final test** (Force-sent, unforeseeable). Aayla more **intuitive** with Force than Quin or even Tholme. Joint meditation; prepare for battle.
10. **Enter fortress (71–72).** Drop into red energy shaft. Quin senses Tholme deep within — and something else. Tsyr (cortosis staff crackling; seeker droids): “WELCOME, JEEDAI!” / salute / “NOW… IS TIME FOR YOU TO DIE!” **Issue end.**
