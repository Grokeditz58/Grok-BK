# 049 Obsession: summary of strongest findings

**Plot:** About five months before ROTS, Obi-Wan, supposedly "on leave", hunts Asajj Ventress, whom Anakin believes he killed on Coruscant. The trail runs through the Black Sun boss Xist on Trigalis (#1), an attack by Durge on Drama Korr's yacht (#2), and Anakin killing Durge by Force-launching his escape pod into a star (#3). It ends in the Battle of Boz Pity, where the Intervention crash-lands and Grievous kills Soon Bayts and Adi Gallia (#4–5). Dooku has Ventress rebuilt and then orders her shot when she becomes a liability. Anakin cuts her down, and she "dies" in Obi-Wan's arms. The epilogue reveals she survived by Sith meditation and takes over a medical frigate (#5).

## Strongest findings (verbatim, cited)
1. **Obi-Wan's obsession:** "I know she's alive!" … "Where is... ...Asajj Ventress?" (Ob#1 p18). To the Council: "She's still alive, I can sense it... and I can bring her to justice." (Ob#1 p23)
2. **Anakin's certainty, then fear:** "She was powerful, but I killed her. She won't threaten us again." (Ob#1 p20). After striking her down: "No. I was being selfish. I struck her down out of fear..." / "As long as she lives, I'll always be afraid of losing you, Master." (Ob#5 pp20–21)
3. **Ventress's self-image:** "I am fear. I am the queen of a blood-soaked planet and an architect of genocide. I have helped to crack the galaxy in half with this war and conquered every enemy I have ever faced -- including death." (Ob#5 p5)
4. **Dooku's disposability:** "She has failed me at every turn. Before she becomes a captive of the Republic -- -- finish her." (Ob#5 p17). About Grievous: "He cost too much to let him go to waste." (Ob#5 p16). To Mace: "I am just one cog -- -- in a massive war machine." (Ob#5 p14)
5. **Grievous kills Adi Gallia:** "I was built to kill your kind." / "You'd better hope they gave you deflector shields." / "No. Just extra arms." (Ob#5 p8). Over her body: "You're too late. There's nothing you can do for her." (Ob#5 p9)
6. **Obi-Wan's warning to Anakin:** "Imagine what you would have become if -- -- instead of Qui-Gon, that other Sith had found you on Tatooine!" / "Her fate could have easily been yours, Anakin. Why can't you see that?" (Ob#5 p18). Then: "no matter what evil we face -- we'll do so together, as brothers." (Ob#5 p21)
7. **Ventress survives:** "Sith meditation techniques slowed my functions." / "The Force did the rest." / "Far from the Jedi, from this war, from Count Dooku..." (Ob#5 p24, "Two days after the Battle of Boz Pity")
8. **Her parting intel:** "Watch the center of the galaxy..." / "...it is the key to victory... defend Coruscant from Dooku." (Ob#5 p22). This points ahead to the Battle of Coruscant in ROTS.

## Other points
- Durge's feats and boasts: "I was once buried alive -- for sixty years!" and "if the Sith and the Mandalorians and the Bloodboilers of Kragis couldn't kill me -- then you will not kill me!" (Ob#2 pp21–22). Anakin's grim smile as the pod falls into the star, and Obi-Wan's rebuke "So now it's my fault you can't stay your hand?" (Ob#3 pp14–18).
- Obi-Wan already knows about Anakin and Padmé: "every Padawan on Coruscant knows where to find you. But I don't care what you do on leave." (Ob#2 p7)
- The ARC trooper Alpha (blue armor) jokes with Anakin about clone nicknames ("Odd Ball? You're naming your squad members?" "You started it, kid." Ob#4 p7). He survives a through-wound: "I'll never walk again... ...but my hands work fine." (Ob#5 p10)
- Jedi at Boz Pity: Mace Windu, Kit Fisto, Ki-Adi-Mundi, Plo Koon, Saesee Tiin (Gold Squadron), Adi Gallia, Soon Bayts (Ob#4 p6). Obi-Wan says it "has been years since I stood in the same room as Soon Bayts" and that Adi Gallia was pulled away "from her pirate friends".
- Anakin pilots the Intervention out of hyperspace between the blockade and the planet, guided by the Force ("Anakin, trust me. Reach out with the Force"), then crash-lands it: "Sorry about that landing." (Ob#4 pp8–15)
- Boz Pity is a "graveyard planet": "The people of this system were giants. We think." (Ob#4 p16)
- Obi-Wan's line "In any war, the only decisive battle is the last one." (Ob#5 p23)

## Quirks
- The spelling "Bayts" is used throughout the dialogue (Ob#4 p6, p19).
- One Obi-Wan whisper balloon (Ob#4 p11) is lettered too small to read at scan resolution, so it is not quoted.
- In Ob#5 p12, the balloon tail for "I still think there's a part of her that Dooku hasn't corrupted!" is ambiguous; context points to Obi-Wan.
