This is an unauthorized Scholastic “I AM” series juvenile biography. The copyright page states: “UNAUTHORIZED: This book is not sponsored by or affiliated with George Lucas or any of his representatives.” The Introduction (pp. 4–8) and the rear-cover blurb are first-person Norwich-as-Lucas (“I am George Lucas.”). From Chapter One onward the body is third-person “George.” First-person narration is the author’s literary persona, **not** a certified Lucas interview. Quoted statements marked “he said,” “he once said in an interview,” “George said,” or “As he later explained” are Norwich’s report of Lucas, not independently checked transcripts. Bold vocabulary words are a kids-educational feature, defined in the Glossary (pp. 120–121).
