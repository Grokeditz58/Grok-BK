## Ch. 10: Back to the Beginning (pp. 98–105)

p. 98: chapter-art of a convention (Boba Fett, Leia-cosplay, a stormtrooper, a Jedi).

- **p. 99 typo as printed:** "millions of fan who had seen the original" (missing "s").
- **Terminology tangle to flag:** p. 95 said *Return of the Jedi* "concluded the second trilogy"; p. 97 "start in on the first trilogy"; p. 99 "In that sense, the second trilogy was pretty much a guaranteed success" (here "second trilogy" most naturally means the next trilogy being made, i.e. the prequels — inconsistent with the earlier first/second usage).
- **p. 100, typography locked, attributed "George said." Crop `quotes/q03-p100-sweet-kid-dark-path.jpg`:** "The first trilogy is primarily about how Anakin Skywalker, father of Luke Skywalker, became Darth Vader. "He was a sweet kid, helpful, just like most people imagine themselves to be," George said. "He eventually realizes he's going down the dark path, but he thinks it's justifiable. The idea is to see how a **democracy** becomes a **dictatorship**, and how a good person goes bad—and still, in the end, thinks he's doing the right thing."" Em dash; bold vocabulary words.
- Next paragraph: "the first story is basically the backstory to the second trilogy that fans already knew so well (it's often referred to as the *Star Wars* "prequel trilogy")." Couldn't use the same actors; Ewan McGregor as young Obi-Wan; Hayden Christensen as grown Anakin (doesn't show up until Episode II) because of chemistry with Natalie Portman as Padmé, Luke and Leia's mother.
- Sidebar "From Film to Digital": *Attack of the Clones* "one of the first major films to be shot almost entirely with high-definition digital cameras." George decided to direct the entire first trilogy.
- **p. 103, typography locked:** "I could do other things that up to that point had been impossible," he says. "The success of that rerelease not only told me that I could create these creatures and build better sets and towns than I could before, but that the *Star Wars* audience was still alive—it hadn't completely disappeared after fifteen years."
- *The Phantom Menace* opened May 19, 1999; "more than $64 million on its opening weekend, and would go on to earn more than $1 billion worldwide." "Episode II - *Attack of the Clones*, which opened on May 16, 2002, took in about $650 million worldwide." (The Time Line said May 19, 2002 — flag.) "Episode III - *Revenge of the Sith*, added just under $850 million." "the Force was still very much with George." (This is the Index's p. 105 Force hit.)
- Film Notes p. 104, *The Phantom Menace*: "The first movie in the *Star Wars* series"; Qui-Gon Jinn spelled correctly; Ewan McGregor, Natalie Portman as Padmé Amidala, Liam Neeson, Jake Lloyd as Young Anakin Skywalker. George's job: Executive producer, Writer, and Director.

## 10 Things You Should Know About George Lucas (pp. 112–115)

1. Born May 14, 1944; two older sisters and one younger sister.
2. Original dream was to become a race-car driver, changed by the teenage accident.
3. Film School Generation.
4. Diabetes in his twenties kept him from being drafted.
5. Started ILM.
6. **Typography locked:** "George is extremely interested in mythology. *Star Wars* was influenced by the teachings of Joseph Campbell, who wrote the book *The Hero with a Thousand Faces*."
7. Prefers the editing side of filmmaking.
8. Merchandising and sequel rights for a lower director's fee is one of the most profitable gambles in Hollywood history.
9. George Lucas Educational Foundation publishes Edutopia.
10. "George has three children, all of whom are adopted." (Amanda, Katie, Jett; Mellody Hobson / Everest are not in this August 2013 first printing — record as the book's claim.)

## 10 MORE Things That Are Pretty Cool to Know (pp. 116–119)

1. Billionaire, net worth more than $7 billion; almost always wears a flannel shirt, blue jeans, and beat-up sneakers.
2. Indiana Jones named after George's dog, Indiana; originally Indiana Smith; Spielberg convinced him to change it.
3. "George wrote the entire *Star Wars* screenplay on paper with a No. 2 pencil."
4. "*Star Wars* was originally imagined as one movie, but George realized the story was too big, so he divided it into three parts." (The original trilogy only, in this book.)
5. Skywalker Ranch is on Lucas Valley Road, "though the name of the road is pure coincidence."
6. "George's nickname in high school was Luke."
7. Tom Selleck then Harrison Ford for Indy.
8. Chewbacca's voice: Ben Burtt and the sound engineers "used a mix of bears, walruses, dogs, and lions."
9. Beach vacation during premieres.
10. "*Star Wars: Episode I – The Phantom Menace* has earned more than $1 billion worldwide, making it the most successful of all the *Star Wars* films." (The book's 2013 "to date" claim.)

## Glossary (pp. 120–121)

Archaeology; Avid: very committed; Boundless: unlimited; Collaborate; Compassionate: feeling sympathy for and a desire to help someone who is suffering; Corrupt: immoral; Democracy: a form of government in which the people choose their leaders in elections; Dictatorship: a form of government in which a ruler has complete control over the country; Fantastical: based on fantasy; Glamorize: to make something seem glamorous; Humility; Iconic: very famous and well-known; Kindred: similar in kind; Perseverance; Resemblance; Studious: liking or tending to study very hard.
