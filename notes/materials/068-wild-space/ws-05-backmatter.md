## Excerpt 1 (l.11911): Karen Traviss, *The Clone Wars: No Prisoners* (identified by content)
- It opens on the epigraph by Armand Isard, "Director of Republic Intelligence", about JanFathal. "ATHAR, CAPITAL OF JANFATHAL, OUTER RIM". Hallena (an agent; "Gilad" is her partner) runs a source. Rex is with Ahsoka, and Pellaeon advises Ahsoka on clothing (l.12306–12375). This is book 069's opening chapter, so it is covered in full in the 069 notes.
- Lore: Hallena on Force-users in espionage: "the war had suddenly made her aware of how many beings there were who could sense her feelings or even try to shape her thoughts." (l.12142)

## Era intro: THE OLD REPUBLIC (l.12599) and *The Old Republic: Fatal Alliance* excerpt (ch. 1, "SHIGAR KONSHI")
- Era text: Naga Sadow; Ruusan "decimating the so-called Brotherhood of Darkness"; Darth Bane's "only two Sith at a time" (l.12632–12644).
- Excerpt feats: Shigar Force-pushes a jetpacking Mandalorian "sideways into the building" (l.12885). He deflects two darts (l.12890); then, as rockets tear through a monorail's columns, "Only with the greatest exertion of the Force that Shigar could summon was he able to deflect them [falling metal spears] into the ground around them" (l.12911–12913). He clears smoke "with one out-thrust push" (l.12918). His Master silences him "with a gentle but irresistible telepathic nudge" (l.12695).
