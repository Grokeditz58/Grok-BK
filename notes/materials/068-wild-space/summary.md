# Wild Space (Karen Miller, 2008): summary of strongest findings

Source: the owner's MEGA epub (handle cb0UzLLI), Del Rey ebook v3.1_r1, eISBN 978-0-307-79599-1. Legends. Line refs point to the extracted text kept outside the repo. Detail is in ws-01 to ws-05.

1. **Obi-Wan stops and docks an out-of-control starship with the Force, weeks after near-fatal injuries.**
   > "tightening the Force around its sturdy frame. The ship lost more momentum… Was barely moving" (ch. FOURTEEN, l.6979)
   > "He floated a starship with the Force. A whole starship. And he didn't even break a sweat." (Bail, ch. FIFTEEN, l.7059)
   > a Jedi "who could carry a starship with the power of his mind … and emerge unscathed from a hail of blasterfire" (ch. SIXTEEN, l.7601)

2. **Kenobi–Skywalker parity and the dueling ranking.** Obi-Wan is "renowned as one of the Temple's most accomplished and formidable duelists. They were so attuned to each other now… that in many ways it was like dueling with himself." (ch. FOUR, l.1520). In their Ataru sparring bout, "The training lightsabers moved in a blur too fast to see with ordinary eyes." (l.1623). The bout has no winner.

3. **A Sith holocron overpowers Obi-Wan across a planet.** Zigoola's "modified holocron" (l.8369) makes him crash his ship and choke Bail: "as though an army of Sith had bent their malevolent minds upon him" (ch. SEVENTEEN, l.8104). It strips basic powers: "I might as well be a droid." (ch. NINETEEN, l.9731). While hallucinating he fights with the dark side ("Channeled it without thinking", l.9448) and fells trees, "slashing three saplings with one blow" (l.9360). He destroys "an ancient black glass pyramid… Such a small thing to contain so great and malevolent a power" (ch. TWENTY-ONE, l.10738–10746). He breaks out by burning himself with his own saber so a telepathic crystal can reach Yoda across Wild Space (ch. TWENTY-TWO, l.11044–11080).

4. **Sidious power statements.** "not even he [Yoda] could hide them from the greatest Sith Lord ever known" (ch. EIGHT, l.3517). Sidious uses dark-side foresight, "Searched the shifting matrix of the future… sifted possible from probable" (l.3561), to locate the hidden Sith world. He stings Dooku with the dark side because he "imagined himself an equal" (l.3595).

Other data:
- Lore: "The loss of a limb had been known to affect a Jedi's powers. True, Anakin Skywalker possessed more midi-chlorians than any Jedi in history" (ch. TWO, l.679).
- Record: "the only Jedi in a thousand years to have faced a Sith in mortal combat and lived" (Padmé on Obi-Wan, ch. ELEVEN, l.4940). "You've beaten the Sith three times now." (Bail, ch. TWENTY, l.9822)
- Yoda, per Mace: "You're the best we have at navigating the Force." (ch. EIGHTEEN, l.8857). Yoda himself: "Defeat the dark side alone I cannot." (ch. SEVEN, l.3265)
- Dooku at Geonosis (Obi-Wan's memory): "Deflected each blinding attack with casual, consummate skill. Forced the error—and took Anakin's arm." (ch. SEVENTEEN, l.8211)
- Vokara Che's crystal healing: "a massive surge in the Force… The crystal pulsed, bright as an emerald sun." (ch. EIGHT, l.3843)
- Anakin's battle of Bothawui against Grievous happens off-page. It is known only through Obi-Wan's vision (l.5845) and the after-action call ("You saved Bothawui", l.5900).

New findings from the full line-by-line read (2026-11-03):
- Obi-Wan runs up a wall, across the ceiling and down the other side during the Ataru bout (ch. FOUR, l.1633). The earlier skim missed this.
- The Force-shield under a waterfall, "I had barely turned six the first time I did it" (l.9722), fails on Zigoola. He does it again in the Temple arboretum, "perfectly dry", and restarts the flow with a wave of his hand (ch. TWENTY-THREE, l.11610–11694).
- Obi-Wan's Force-wave twice throws Bail about thirty paces: "This time, the Jedi embraced it" (ch. TWENTY-TWO, l.10870, l.10899).
- Obi-Wan's attachment heresy: "Yoda was wrong about the dangers of attachment… it could also strengthen it… by his love for Qui-Gon, and Anakin." (ch. TWENTY-ONE, l.10706–10714)
- Irredeemability debate: "The Sith are irredeemably evil" vs. Bail's "There's no such thing as irredeemable" (ch. SEVENTEEN, l.8678–8704). Bail later decides "Obi-Wan coldly right" (l.10585).
- Obi-Wan's visions of other fronts: Adi Gallia on Agomar, Ki-Adi-Mundi "defeated almost to death on Barab I", Eeth Koth at Korriban (ch. FOURTEEN, l.6699–6704).
- Anakin's feat: seventy-five traveling double-twist backflips, then a one-arm handstand (ch. TWENTY-THREE, l.11701).
- Sidious, on hearing of the Zigoola outcome: "That's what an aging apprentice was for. Housekeeping." / "This Republic will fall. I have foreseen it." (l.11826, l.11844)
- Back matter: the Rebellion-era excerpt is *Allegiance*, and the *Betrayal* excerpt does include a lightsaber deflection fight (l.15709–15858).
