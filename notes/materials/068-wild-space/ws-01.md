## Ch. FOUR (l.1306): NOW: training Ahsoka; the Kenobi–Skywalker sparring bout
- Anakin sets Ahsoka "fifty repetitions of Niman form, level one" (l.1357).
- Anakin's own view of his post-Geonosis state: "his balance in the Force now irrevocably altered" by the prosthetic (l.1425). He calls the Dooku loss his own fault: "my impatience led to my defeat" (l.1434).
- Anakin's private credo: "Love doesn't weaken us. It makes us stronger." (l.1470) / "Why have power at all if we're forbidden to wield it as strongly as we can?" (l.1502)
- **Ranking statement:** Obi-Wan is "renowned as one of the Temple's most accomplished and formidable duelists. They were so attuned to each other now… that in many ways it was like dueling with himself." (l.1520)
- They "leapt lightly over the edge of the balcony to land cushioned by the Force" (l.1527). Ahsoka Force-jumps up to the observation balcony (l.1533).
- **The bout (Ataru, Form IV):** "Master Obi-Wan and Master Anakin play as they dueled each other in the demanding Ataru Form IV." (l.1581) "The training lightsabers moved in a blur too fast to see with ordinary eyes." (l.1623) The sequence: "Strike—block—parry—evade—bind—counterbind—counter-measure—broken time—body-to-body and double release—Force push—Force pull—feint—backflip—recover … and start again." (l.1630) Anakin "Force-leap[s] over Master Obi-Wan's head" (l.1587). Outcome: no winner given; Obi-Wan swats Anakin's backside (l.1613); "not even Jedi like Obi-Wan and Anakin could keep up this kind of pace and power forever" (l.1638).
- Ahsoka: "I'll never handle a lightsaber as well as they do, even if I train twenty hours a day." (l.1578)
- Lore on crystals and prosthetics: "A Jedi could sense the Force in a lightsaber's crystal, sense the amplification of power… So how did it feel to have that link interrupted by a prosthetic hand?" (l.1599)
- Anakin Force-floats the droidcam to the balcony (l.1654).
