## Highlights

- **Luke Skywalker:** Luke lifts boulders off buried colonists on Eol Sha (JS ch.5). In CotF, his spirit is outside his body; he teaches through "There is no try" and the dark-side test, and names the students "champions of the Force" (ch.44, p.257).
- **Exar Kun (spirit):** his spirit corrupts Gantoris and then Kyp, teaches Kyp the Sith ways, and helps him pull the Sun Crusher out of Yavin's core. He blasts Luke's spirit out of his body. Kyp and the Jedi students together destroy him in CotF.
- **Kyp Durron:** named "the most powerful Jedi Knight of this generation" (CotF ch.31, p.193). With the Sun Crusher he sets off the Cauldron Nebula stars against Daala (DA) and destroys the Carida system (CotF), uses resonance torpedoes, lures the Death Star prototype into a black hole, and survives being crushed into a message cylinder in a Jedi trance (CotF ch.39–40).
- **Gantoris / Streen:** Gantoris builds a lightsaber on his own and burns to death after facing Exar Kun (DA). Streen is a weather adept and hears others' thoughts.
- **Admiral Daala:** she makes a blind hyperspace jump, attacks Calamari, and survives the Maw explosion: "We have been wounded—but we will never be defeated!" (CotF ch.42, p.247).
- **Sun Crusher:** an invulnerable quantum-armour superweapon that sets off supernovas.
