# Young Jedi Knights 1: Heirs of the Force: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-19 CST. Full read of READ l.1–1971 (every line, in order). Citation "HotF ch. N (p.x)" (printed page of the Berkley Jam printing). Detail is in hotf-01 to hotf-05.
- Authors: Kevin J. Anderson & Rebecca Moesta. © 1995 Lucasfilm Ltd. Berkley Jam printing, ISBN 0-425-16949-9 (original 1995 Boulevard edition). 22 chapters, then a bundled Shadow Academy blurb and ch. 1 preview.
- Era: the book says Qorl's TIE "had lain hidden and crippled on the surface of Yavin 4 for more than twenty years" (ch. 18, p.183). Jaina says people have been at the Jedi academy for eleven years (ch. 14, p.154), and the twins are fourteen (back cover). The book gives no ABY year.

## Plot
- **Academy life (ch. 1–5):** A month into training on Yavin 4, Jacen's crystal snake escapes. The twins track it with the Force to Raynar's room, and Jacen calms both snake and boy. Tenel Ka of Dathomir climbs the outside of the Great Temple and rappels into class. Luke tells the students about Brakiss, an Imperial spy he tried and failed to redeem. Next day a six-legged monster bursts into the landing clearing. Luke disarms the supply courier Peckhum with the Force, then faces the beast unarmed until it leaves, and is visibly drained afterward. Han and Chewie bring Chewie's nephew Lowbacca as a new student, with Em Teedee, a miniaturized translator droid. Gifts: an old hyperdrive for Jaina, fronds for Jacen's stump lizard. A food fight with Raynar ends when Luke freezes the whole dining hall in midair.
- **The wreck (ch. 6–10):** Chewie gives Lowie a dismantled T-23 skyhopper, and Jaina helps him rebuild it in hours. From the treetops Lowie spots a glint. Luke leaves on a pressing matter, and Tionne takes over the teaching: the ballad of Vodo-Siosk Baas and Exar Kun, the swimming parable, leaf-lifting. The four friends find a TIE fighter that crashed at the Battle of Yavin. They restore it in secret as a "group exercise". Four of them together lift its second solar panel down from a Massassi tree by telekinesis, and Jaina installs Han's hyperdrive. Jaina deliberately leaves the weapons unrepaired.
- **Captured (ch. 11–16):** The surviving pilot, Qorl, captures them. Tenel Ka escapes into the jungle, and Lowie climbs away under fire to his T-23. Qorl shrugs off the twins' mind trick and TK disarm attempt, and Jaina deflects his aim from Lowie's speeder. Lowie crash-lands on the canopy and treks all night on an injured knee to the academy, where nobody can understand him because Em Teedee has fallen into the jungle. Tenel Ka panics, gets lost and shelters in a cave. Two-headed winged reptiles drive her out, and she thinks about her secret Hapan royal parentage. Qorl tells the twins the Battle of Yavin from the Imperial side and refuses to believe the Empire has fallen ("Surrender is betrayal"). He forces them to finish the repairs. Woolamanders carry off Em Teedee.
- **Escape and pursuit (ch. 17–22):** Lowie uses the old Rebel command center to call the Falcon. Tenel Ka finds Em Teedee, follows his directional sensors back by morning, and boards the Falcon as it launches. Qorl, as an act of mercy, leaves the twins alone in the jungle and attacks the academy, but his cannons are dead. The crystal snake bites his gauntlet. He breaks a partial tractor-beam hold, outmaneuvers the Falcon in the treetops, taunts Han to slow him, and jumps to lightspeed on Jaina's hyperdrive just as Han gets a target lock. The twins climb a Massassi tree with Force help and signal with smoke, and the Falcon picks them up. Luke returns, praises the students, and asks whether they still want to be Jedi Knights. They all say yes.
- **Bundled preview:** the Shadow Academy blurb (Brakiss's dark academy), and Shadow Academy ch. 1, in which Lando's GemDiver Station is attacked by Skipray blastboats and an unnamed dark woman (Tamith Kai, unnamed here) has the twins and Lowie stunned and taken.

## Scale notes
- Telekinesis in this book is mostly small objects, with group lifts and their limits stated outright. The twins together can lift a sleeping boy; Jaina alone holds him "only for a second or two". Four students lift a TIE solar panel with "mental coordination" problems. The big displays are Luke's: freezing a whole food fight in midair, a disarm from range, and facing down the monster, after which he is exhausted.
- Qorl is the main non-Force combatant. He resists a mind trick, a TK disarm and a partial tractor beam, outflies the Falcon near the treetops, and is defeated only by sabotage. Han misses repeatedly.
