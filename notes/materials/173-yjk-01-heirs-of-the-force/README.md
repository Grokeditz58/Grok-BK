Characters (quick reference):
- **Jacen Solo**: animal mind-touch and calming, joint TK with Jaina. Weak at fine TK, and cannot calm Qorl.
- **Jaina Solo**: mechanic. Restores the TIE, installs the hyperdrive, sabotages the weapons with rigged readouts, deflects Qorl's aim by TK. Cannot hold Raynar alone for more than a second or two.
- **Tenel Ka**: Dathomiri warrior training, climbing and grappling hook, prefers her body to the Force. Panics when lost; Force-sense dodging; secretly a Hapan princess.
- **Lowbacca**: Chewbacca's nephew. Computers, T-23 pilot, all-night trek on an injured knee, solo syren-plant rite.
- **Em Teedee**: miniaturized translator droid, "fluent in six forms of communication", directional sensors.
- **Luke Skywalker**: freezes a whole food fight in midair, disarms by TK from range, faces the monster unarmed and is exhausted afterward. Brakiss is his failure.
- **Qorl**: Death Star TIE pilot stranded for 20+ years. Resists mind trick, TK disarm and tractor beam; outflies the Falcon near the treetops; jumps to hyperspace.
- **Tionne**: "not a particularly strong Jedi", a loremaster and singer (ballad of Vodo-Siosk Baas and Exar Kun).
- **Han Solo, Chewbacca, Raynar, Peckhum, Brakiss** (recounted), **Lando and Lobot** (preview), and the **dark woman** of the preview (Tamith Kai, unnamed here).
