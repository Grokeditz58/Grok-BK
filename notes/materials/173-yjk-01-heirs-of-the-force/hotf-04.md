## Ch. 18 (p.179–183; READ l.1484–1525) — Qorl lifts off

Qorl thanks the twins in his way, says their usefulness is at an end, decides to leave them alone in the jungle rather than kill them, and announces he will destroy the "Rebel base" before leaving to rejoin the Empire. The twins plead that it is only a school; Qorl seals himself in and lifts off.

## Ch. 19 (p.185–192; READ l.1526–1594) — Tenel Ka's flight; finds Em Teedee; boards the Falcon
- Plot: Tenel Ka outruns the two-headed flying reptiles, wedging one between trees, dodging by Force-sense, and dives into a stand of Massassi trees where the reptiles turn on a woolamander pack instead. Em Teedee is dropped at her feet. His directional sensors guide her back through the night; she bandages her wounds and jogs/marches to the academy by morning, arrives as the Falcon is launching, has Em Teedee hail it at full volume, and boards as the TIE howls overhead.
- TENEL KA stamina: "breath burned in her lungs, but she did not cry out." (p.184). LORE: "She remembered hearing that such a beast had almost killed Master Skywalker many years ago." (p.185) [two-headed reptile]
- TENEL KA uses terrain: "She pushed through a narrower space between two trees and heard a thump above her as the flying creature got stuck in the opening" (p.185)
- TENEL KA LIMITATION + FORCE SENSE/DANGER-DODGING FEAT: "She sensed, rather than saw, the outstretched claws, and tried to turn aside, but slipped on rotting vegetation and fell hard against a fungus-covered log. She sensed a second pair of claws rip through the air where her stomach had been only moments before." (p.186) / "Why couldn’t she remember her Jedi calming techniques when she needed them? ## Ch. 20 (p.193–200; READ l.1595–1656) — Qorl's attack run on the academy
- Plot: Qorl flies low over the jungle, remembering his training and vengeance creed, misreads the academy (power generator) as a military base, plans a temple-strike/hangar-block/freighter/generator sequence, and dives on the Great Temple — but the weapons are dead (Jaina's sabotage, readouts rigged). The crystal snake strikes his gauntlet; he jettisons it. The Falcon's lasers chase him. The twins, far off, follow events through the Force and set off for home.
- QORL SKILL statement (narration): "He remembered his days as a trainee. He had been an excellent pilot⟨—⟩one of the best in his squadron-soaring through mock battles and enforcing the Emperor’s unbending will." (p.193)
- QORL LIMITATION + FEAT: "With his thickly gloved, badly healed arm, he found it difficult to control the Imperial craft-but he was a fighter pilot. He was a great pilot. He had managed to land his ship, despite grievous engine damage, under heavy enemy fire. He had survived undetected in hostile te⟨rr⟩itory for two decades." (p.193–194)
- QORL mindset: "The Empire is my family. The Rebels wish to destroy the New Order. The Rebels must be eliminated-ELIMINATED!" (p.194); "He, a single soldier, would secure vengeance for the entire Empire." (p.194)
- QORL tactical read: spots the power station → "this base was still used as a military installation" (p.195); does not recognize the Falcon ("some kind of light freighter", p.195); plan: hit hangar doors/Great Temple first, then the freighter, then the power station: "From start to finish, it would take only a few minutes to bring the Rebels to their knees." - QORL ANTI-FEAT (weapons dead — sabotaged by Jaina): "He squeezed the button again and again⟨—⟩and nothing happened! The weapons systems did not respond." (p.197); "Over and over he tried to fire, but the laser cannons were completely dead." (p.197)
- QORL vs the crystal snake: "It lunged and bit down as he reached out with his crippled arm to block it. (p.198) / "He let the TIE fighter fly itself as he reached with his good hand to grab the long body of the serpent just behind its head. - Falcon opens fire: "The Rebel light freighter had taken to the air and was flying after Qorl like a furious bird of prey. And its weapons worked just fine." (p.199)
- TWINS LONG-RANGE FORCE SENSING (limited): "Jacen and Jaina sat beside each other, deep in concentration. They reached out with the Force to see what was going on back at the Jedi academy. Their powers were only sufficient to bring them shadowy images, distant echoes of thoughts … but it was enough." (p.199)
- JAINA FEAT (sabotage): "“He didn’t know I never fixed the weapons systems … but then, he never asked. I managed to jury-rig the readouts so they would look normal,” Jaina said at last. “He can fly, but his ship is defenseless.”" (p.199)
- QORL intent: "He would kill all those who had conspired to blow up the Death Star, who had killed Darth Vader and Grand Moff Tarkin. He, a single soldier, would secure vengeance for the entire Empire." (p.194)
- QORL (does not recognize the Falcon): "Qorl didn’t immediately recognize the make or model of the lone ship below. It was some kind of light freighter, not a Rebel X-wing or any of the familiar battleships he had learned about during his rigorous combat training." (p.195)
- QORL tactics: "It was probable, Oorl realized, that the Rebels kept a large force of more powerful fighters in an underground hangar bay" (p.196)
- QORL ANTI-FEAT (sabotaged weapons): "His eyes swept the diagnostic panels, but all the readings seemed normal." (p.197)
