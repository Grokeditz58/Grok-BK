## Ch. 1 (p.1–14; READ l.84–208) — the crystal snake
- Plot: A month into his stay, Jacen has his menagerie set up in the Great Temple's student quarters. His crystal snake escapes; the twins (Jaina is building a device from parts scavenged from the old Rebel war room) search with the Force, find it in Raynar's room as it strikes, Jacen calms both snake and boy, and the twins jointly levitate the bitten, sleeping Raynar before Jaina loses the hold.
- (Passages here were read but are not quoted: not scaling-relevant; see the Plot bullet.)
- SETTING: Jacen's menagerie — stintarils, mud-nest crabs, mucous salamanders, "iridescent blue piranha-beetles"; "Jacen was proud to have the only specimens in captivity in his menagerie." (p.2)
- LUKE teaching philosophy: "To be a Jedi, Uncle Luke explained, one had to understand many pieces of the whole tapestry of the galaxy, not just a rigid pattern set by other people." (p.3)
- JACEN FORCE (animal affinity): "He had always had a strange and deep affinity for living creatures, much as his sister had a talent for understanding machinery and gadgets. He could coax the animals with his special Force talent, getting them to come right up to him, where he could study them at his leisure." (p.3)
- JACEN calms spiders by whispering: "Calm down. It’s all right.” The spiders stopped their antics and settled down to drink through their long, hollow fangs." (p.4)
- Crystal snake: bite = "a moment of piercing pain, and then the victim fell into a deep sleep" ~an hour, no ill effects (p.5). Jacen "I’ve got a bad feeling about this,” ... unconsciously echoing the words his father so often used." (p.5) Uses "one of his uncle Luke’⟨s⟩ Jedi relaxation techniques to keep himself calm" (p.5).
- JAINA: room full of parts scavenged from "the old Rebel war room"; with Tenel Ka the twins "had somewhat clumsily used their Force powers to haul the heavy machine along the winding corridors" (p.7) — LIMITATION/early TK (group effort, "clumsily").
- JAINA character statement: "I’m not building this because it’s useful,” she said. “I’m doing it to see if I can. Once I know I can do it, I won’t have to waste time anymore wondering whether anything I learn here is useful or not.”" (p.8)
- Jacen: "If something’s wrong, we should be able to sense it. Maybe I can use the Force to track the snake, wherever it might be hiding.”" (p.9); touches doors, "caught no tingling sensation" (p.10).
- RAYNAR intro: noble, "purple, gold, and scarlet cloth, the colors of his noble family’s house"; never in Jedi training clothes (p.10); "spoiled and troublesome Raynar" (p.4).
- JACEN FORCE (animal mind-touch): "He let his mind flow, imagining what a snake’s thoughts might be like. As usual he felt many things at once through the Force, but he focused down, looking for his snake. He sensed a slim, languid line of thought, an easily satisfied mind that right now felt cozy and safe." (p.10–11)
- JACEN FORCE (calming two minds at once): "Jacen quickly sent out a calming message, using Jedi relaxation techniques Luke had taught him. He sent peaceful thoughts, quieting thoughts, that calmed not only the serpent but Raynar as well." (p.11)
- TWINS TK (joint levitation of a sleeping boy): "Closing their eyes, the twins concentrated. They touched the fringes of Raynar’s colorful robes with their fingertips as they imagined how light he could be … that he was merely a feather wafting into the air … that he weighed nothing at all, and they could make him drift upward… . Jacen held his breath, and the still-snoring Jedi student began to rise from the tiled floor." (p.12)
- ANTI-FEAT JAINA (cannot hold the lift alone): "Jacen’s quick movement and release of the Force startled Jaina, and she managed to hold Raynar up for only a second or two. As Jacen worked to calm the serpent, Jaina’s grasp on the floating boy weakened and finally broke." (p.13)
- JACEN: "He sent tingling thoughts into its mind until the serpent fizzed with pleasure." (p.13). Jacen to Raynar: "I can honestly say that all of my pets are completely accounted for.”" (p.14)

## Ch. 2 (p.15–28; READ l.208–305) — Tenel Ka's entrance, Luke's Brakiss lecture
- Plot: The students gather in the grand audience chamber. Tenel Ka arrives by climbing the temple exterior and rappelling in. Luke lectures on learning by action and on failure, telling the story of Brakiss, an Imperial spy he took in as a student and tested with an illusion; Brakiss fled back to the Empire. Luke ends on choices.

She had little interest in the bookish studies, the histories and the meditations; but she was an excellent athlete who preferred action to thinking." (p.18); "impatient, hard-d⟨riv⟩en, and practically humorless" (p.18).
- TENEL KA FEAT (climbs the Great Temple exterior, rappels inside): "She must have climbed the temple from the back,” Jacen said." (p.19); "Tenel Ka lowered herself like a spider on a web, walking precariously down the long smooth surface of the inner wall." (p.19)
- TENEL KA philosophy/limitation: "She could have used her Jedi powers to speed the descent, but Tenel Ka relied on her body whenever possible and used the Force only as a last resort. She thought it showed weakness to depend too heavily on her special powers." (p.19)
- TENEL KA TK: "With a snap from the Force she popped her grappling hook up and away from the stone above and neatly caught it in her hand as it fell." (p.19)
- TENEL KA durability-ish: "Despite her exposed skin, Tenel Ka never seemed bothered by scratches or insect bites, though she made numerous forays into the jungle." (p.20); "Tenel Ka rarely smiled." (p.20). Rancor joke ("The appetizer!") falls flat (p.21).
- LUKE description: "now he carried calm power in his lean form, a thunderstor⟨m⟩ bottled up in a diamond-hard gentleness. Through many trials Luke had somehow emerged bright and strong. - LUKE presence: "Everyone fell silent, as if a warm blanket had fluttered down over them." (p.22); "a soft voice that nevertheless carried the length of the grand audience chamber" (p.22).
- LUKE teaching: "I don⟨'t⟩ believe the training of a true Jedi comes from listening to lectures. I want to teach you how to learn action, how to do things, not just think about them." (p.22)
- LUKE on failure (statement): "I didn’t say that I never fail,” Luke said. “No Jedi ever becomes perfect. Sometimes, though, what we succeed in doing is not exactly what we intended to do. Focus on what you accomplished, rather than on what you merely hoped to do. Or what you failed to do. Yes, recognize what you have lost⟨—⟩but look in a different way to see what you have gained.”" (p.23)
- BRAKISS (Luke statement): "A few years ago I had a brilliant trainee named Brakiss. He was a talented student, a voracious learner. He had a great potential for the Force. He seemed kind and helpful, fascinated by everything I had to teach. He was also a great actor.”" (p.24)
- LUKE FEAT (sensed a spy instantly; caught prior infiltrators): "once it became known that I had founded an academy to teach Jedi Knights, it’s not surprising that the remnants of the Empire would have their own students infiltrate my academy. I managed to catch their first few attempts. They were clumsy and untalented." / "But Brakiss was different. I knew he was an Imperial spy from the moment he stepped off the shuttle and looked around at the jungles on Yavin 4. ⟨I⟩ could sense it in him, a deep shadow barely hidden by his mask of friendliness and enthusiasm. But in Brakiss I also saw a real talent for the Force. Part of him had been corrupted long ago. He had a deep flaw surrounded by a beautiful exterior." (p.24)
- LUKE motive: "Because if there could be good even in the heart of my father, Dar⟨t⟩h Vader, there must also be goodness in some one as fresh and new as Brakiss.”" (p.24–25)
- BRAKISS/LUKE (illusion test): "He stayed here for many months, and I took special interest in teaching him, guiding him, nudging him toward the light side of the Force in every way. He seemed to be turning, softening … but Brakiss was colder and more deceptive than even I had suspected. During one part of his training, I sent him on an illusionary quest that would seem real to him, a test that made him face himself. Brakiss had to look inward-to see his very core in a way that no one else could ever see." (p.25)
- LUKE ANTI-FEAT (failed to turn Brakiss; Brakiss took Jedi teachings to the Empire): "I had hoped the test would heal him, but instead Brakiss lost that battle. Perhaps he was simply not prepared to confront what he saw inside himself. It broke him somehow. He fled from this jungle moon, and I believe he went straight back to the Empire-taking with him everything that I had taught him of the Jedi Way.”" (p.25)
- LUKE presence: "Luke looked at him with narrowed eyes so full of power that the arrogant student flinched and put his hand back down." (p.25)
- LUKE reframing: "I did show Brakiss my compassion. I did let him learn the secrets of the light side, uncorrupted by what he had already been taught. And I did make him look at himself and realize how broken he was. Once I accomplished that much, the task was no longer mine. The final choice belonged to Brakiss himself. And it still does.”" (p.26)
- LUKE Force-touch on audience: "As Luke’s gaze passed over them, Jaina felt an electric thrill, as if an invisible hand had just brushed her." (p.26)
- LUKE: "Here at my Jedi academy I can give you tools to use when facing those choices. But I cannot make the choices for you. You must succeed in your own way.”" (p.26). R2 alarm; "Trouble out on the landing pad!" (p.27)

## Ch. 3 (p.29–38; READ l.305–386) — Luke vs the six-legged monster
- Plot: Tenel Ka leads the twins up the Great Temple's outer face with her grappling hook (the twins use the Force to help). A huge six-legged tusked monster bursts into the landing clearing as the supply courier Peckhum lands the Lightning Rod. Luke walks out unarmed, disarms Peckhum by TK, faces the beast down within a meter until it leaves, and is visibly exhausted afterward. The students unload Peckhum's supplies by Force-lifting.
- Tenel Ka: "We can do it faster my way. Jaina, follow!”" (p.29) Grappling hook throw: "The muscles in her arm rippled. At precisely the right moment she released the hook." / "Jacen and Jaina helped it with the Force, guiding the hook so that it seated properly in the moss-covered sill." (p.29)
- TENEL KA climbing: "Tenel Ka ascended like a lizard up a sunbaked cliff face" (p.30); "Pebbles crumbled under her feet, but she didn’t seem the least bit concerned about falling." / "“Around to the side,” she said, not even panting." (p.30–31)
- JACEN (Force-assisted climb; vanity): "As he climbed, his arms ached. He used the Force when he needed to, raising his body up, catching himself when his feet slipped. He would have preferred to show off his physical prowess, especially with Tenel Ka watching." (p.30)
- PECKHUM intro: supply courier, "the Lightning Rod" (p.31).
- MONSTER description: "As enormous as a shuttlecraft, its huge squarish body was covered with shaggy, matted hair⟨⟩ tangled with primordial moss. It stood on six cylindrical legs that were like the ⟨b⟩oles of ancient trees. Its massive triangular head sat like a Star Destroyer on its shoulders, but instead of eyes inset in its skull, it had a cluster of twelve thick, writhing tentacles, each one glistening with a round, unblinking eye. Curved tusks sprouted from its mouth, long and sharp and wicked enough to tear a hole through a sandcrawler." (p.31)
- Tenel Ka (bravado): "Working together, we can fight it,” she said. “Follow!”" (p.32); bellow "seemed to make the ancient stone blocks tremble" (p.32).
- LUKE (unarmed approach): "Luke Skywalker wore only his Jedi robe. Jacen expected to see him holding his lightsaber, but both of Luke’s hands were empty." (p.33)
- LUKE voice projection: "Luke spoke in an oddly calm and clear voice that was not at all muffled by the distance. “No, here! Look at me,” he said." (p.33)
- LUKE TK DISARM (gesture, at range): "But Luke turned to him and motioned with his hand. “No,” he said. The blaster rifle went flying out of Peckhum’s grip." (p.34)
- LUKE statement: "This creature means no harm,” Luke said, his voice quiet but firm. He never took his eyes off the beast. “It’s just frightened and confused. It doesn’t know where it is, or why we are here.” He drew a deep breath. “There’s no need for killing.”" (p.34)
- JACEN (limit of his beast-taming): "He had used his own talents with the Force to confront many strange animals out in the jungle, but never anything as powerful as this monster, never such a boiling mass of anger and confusion." (p.34)
- LUKE FEAT (faces it down within a meter): "The monster snuffled and waited, motionless, its wicked tusks less than a meter away from Luke Skywalker." (p.35) / "“Go,” Luke told the creature. “There is nothing you need here.” The monster reared up on its hind set of piston legs, its eye tentacles thrashing in a frenzy⟨.⟩ Then it let out another highpitched trumpet before it spun around and crashed off into the thick undergrowth." (p.36)
- LUKE LIMITATION (exhausted afterwards): "Like a snapped string, Luke’s shoulders slumped with exhaustion. He seemed barely able to keep himself from trembling" (p.36)
- LUKE statements: "“I had enough weapons,” Luke said with calm conviction. “I had the Force.”" (p.36); to Jacen: "“You will be able to do anything you want, Jacen,” Luke said. “You have the potential⟨—⟩as ⟨lo⟩n⟨g⟩ as you have the discipline.”" (p.36)
- Peckhum: "you faced that monster with no weapons at all." (p.36)
- TWINS+TENEL KA TK (unloading a supply ship): "It was hard work, demanding more concentration and control of their Jedi lifting abilities than they were used to, but they finished in less than an hour." (p.38) Han and Chewbacca bringing "another Jedi trainee" from Kashyyyk (p.37).
