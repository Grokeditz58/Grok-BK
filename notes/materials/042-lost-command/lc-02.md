# Darth Vader and the Lost Command #2 (Dark Horse, Feb 2011): page-by-page notes
Credits: script Haden Blackman; pencils Rick Leonardi; inks Daniel Green; colors Wes Dzioba; letters Michael Heisler; cover Tsuneo Sanda. ~19 BBY. Citation: LC#2 p. NN = scan file index (p. 01 = cover, p. 02 = credits/crawl, p. 03 = first story page).

- **p. 01 (cover, Sanda):** Vader in a starfighter cockpit. Cover text: "ATTACK POSITIONS!"
- **p. 02 crawl:** > "It is still the early days of the Empire, and Emperor Palpatine's apprentice Darth Vader is still being tested and evaluated by his Master—and others. Alone with his own doubts, Vader wonders if he made the right choice in betraying the Jedi and becoming a Sith." … "Moff Tarkin's son, an admiral for the Imperial fleet, disappeared along with his command months prior." … "Vader and battalions of the 501st Stormtrooper Legion attack and conquer the locals. But then Vader encounters Lady Saro, the system's religious leader . . ." Indicia: #2, February 2011.
- **p. 03:** "Atoa, the Ghost Nebula." A Star Destroyer. In the medical bay, Vader orders Atoan dissection: "Expose the cranial cavity." Shale: "Vader is losing patience, Lady Saro. If you don't tell us where to find Admiral Tarkin soon, I fear you'll be next on the dissection table." Saro: "Lord Vader will see that my offer is reasonable. In time." Vader: "Proceed through the sternum and extract the lungs."
- **p. 04:** "Your species is remarkable." "Captain Shale, did you know Atoans have no hearts?" Saro: "From our feet to our fingertips, we have a *thousand* hearts. They make us faster and stronger than your kind." Vader: "You also bleed to death much more quickly. Now, you will tell me how you learned to speak Basic, or I will seek out each of your hearts and crush them between my fingers." Specimen tanks hold bodies.
- **p. 05:** Saro: "I prayed to the twenty-nine Atoan gods to give me the power to bring peace. Then I captured one of your troopers and swallowed his tongue. Soon I could hear and speak as you do." "He did not survive the ritual." Vader: "You confess to killing an Imperial stormtrooper? I should execute you now." Saro: "I am my people's shaman. Their spiritual protector." Her terms: "I will help you find Garoche Tarkin … and in exchange, you will declare me *queen* of the Ghost Nebula."
- **p. 06:** Vader: "I could *force* you to tell me." Saro: "And I will lie. You will follow false leads until you become so enraged you kill me." "Take her back to deck sixteen." Shale: "The Emperor has made Moffs of lesser *men*…" "We have no leads!"
- **p. 07:** Vader Force-chokes Shale: "This is *my* command!" "And it will be your *last* command if you fail. Moff Tarkin will see to that." "I will meditate on it." Shale: "Take the bodies to the incinerator. They've told us all they can."
- **p. 08–09 (Vader's meditation dream, sepia/burgundy borders):** Padmé as Supreme Chancellor; Anakin: "The galaxy is done with politicians. I told the Senate that we need a Jedi leading us." A son: "*We* believe in you, Mother." Padmé: "Now go. Jinn and I are right behind you." "Members of the Republic Senate, I present your Supreme Chancellor … Padmé Amidala! The galaxy's true guardian of peace and freedom!" "If she brings peace to the *entire* galaxy, she'll make the Jedi Order obsolete." "We can only hope."
  - (ANALYSIS) In Vader's fantasy the son is named **Jinn** (after Qui-Gon), and a Jedi stands beside Anakin, apparently an older Obi-Wan figure.
- **p. 10:** "…without you." "I love you." Unmasked Vader in his chamber: "Nooo!" "Auuugh!" ***WHAM!*** He smashes the console. "Padmé…"
- **p. 11:** in the specimen garden, Saro: "If you've come to kill me, I could not ask for a more beautiful place to die." Vader: "The Empire collects specimens from every conquered planet. Test subjects for our experiments with biological and chemical weapons." "I agree to your terms. Once we find Admiral Tarkin, this system is yours. But you will swear loyalty to the Emperor." Saro: "I'll even build temples in *your* honor should you so desire, Lord Vader."
- **p. 12 (splash):** "…we will find Admiral Tarkin's ship." The wrecked Star Destroyer, with V-wings and an Eta-2-style fighter.
- **p. 13:** Vader: "Stay in formation." Pilot: "How did this happen? Lady Saro claims the Atoans have no warships…" "Look at the damage … those explosions came from *within* the destroyer." Vader: "Sabotage. Follow me. We'll search the vessel."
- **p. 14:** an ambush of organic, gourd-like Atoan fighters. "They were hiding in the wreckage. We didn't sweep the debris field thoroughly enough."
- **p. 15:** Vader: "Move into attack positions." Atoan pilots speak in (mirrored) alien script.
- **p. 16:** Vader: "Attack in groups of three. Use standard distract-and-flank maneuvers." "They aren't nearly as agile as our V-wings. Take the fight closer to the wreckage."
- **p. 17:** Vader: "Fall back. I will take the last one alive." His cannons cripple the Atoan fighter's engine.
- **p. 18:** Vader rams and herds the fighter into the wreck.
- **p. 19:** it crashes into the hangar of the derelict.
- **p. 20:** orange-suited Atoan pilots in EVA, armed, approach Vader's grounded fighter.
- **p. 21:** Vader cuts down a pilot and grabs another. "I only need *one* of you alive."
- **p. 22:** Vader kneels to a hologram of Palpatine: "The pilot revealed everything before he died. We are already en route to our next target." Palpatine: "This mission is vital, Lord Vader -- Garoche Tarkin is easily replaceable, but his father is a powerful and loyal ally. He excels at spreading fear. I will not have him distracted." On the bridge, Saro on a nameless world: "Most of the planet is covered in black oceans … the seas consume all who enter."
- **p. 23:** Vader: "The loss of his son could make Tarkin even stronger, my Master. … I could ensure that Garoche does not survive his rescue, and it would appear that he had been murdered by the insurgents." "Tar pits. The planet is covered in *hot tar*." Shale: "Those tar pits may be combustible … set the whole planet on fire and kill Garoche." Palpatine: "Yes…yes. Hate would then consume the father. He truly would stop at nothing to destroy our enemies. Carry out your plan, Lord Vader." "As you wish, my Master."
- **p. 24:** Shale wants a recon mission; Vader: "No. We invade now." "Commander Voca, ready the walkers and ground troops." "They are merely a distraction." "For what?" "A stealth attack." Next issue: "Quagmire!"
- **p. 25:** "G85" scanner tag. **Not part of the comic; excluded.**
- (ANALYSIS) Power and character notes: Vader Force-chokes his own officer (p. 07). Atoans have "a thousand hearts" that make them "faster and stronger" (p. 04). Vader is a competent squadron tactician and pilot (pp. 13–19). He and Palpatine plot Garoche's death to radicalize Tarkin (pp. 22–23).
