# Star Wars: Darth Vader and the Lost Command #1 (Dark Horse, Jan 2011): hand-read notes

Credits (p. 02): script Haden Blackman; pencils Rick Leonardi; inks Daniel Green; colors Wes Dzioba; letters Michael Heisler; cover Tsuneo Sanda; 25th Anniversary variant cover by Douglas Wheatley. Editor Randy Stradley. Era: Rise of the Empire, "approximately nineteen years before the Battle of Yavin".
Citation convention: "LC#1 p. NN" is the scan page, where p. 01 is the cover, p. 02 the credits/crawl, and p. 03 story page 1.

- **p. 01 cover** (Sanda): "Darth Vader in Command!" Vader with a red saber in front of clone troopers.
- **p. 02 crawl:** "Though Vader has risen to a position of great power, his ascension has not been without sacrifice. He betrayed his Jedi friends, lost his limbs in a battle with his former master, and inadvertently killed the woman he loved. Now the galaxy's ostensive second in command is haunted by thoughts of what he gave up, and of what could have been . . ."
- **p. 03:** a dream or fantasy of Padmé, pregnant, with an unscarred Anakin in a garden. Padmé: "He's strong in the Force. I feel it already." Anakin: "He *is* my son." Padmé: "… the son of *Anakin Skywalker*? The Jedi who saved Mace Windu from Darth Sidious and brought the Sith to justice … and the youngest leader of the Jedi Council …" Anakin: "Seeing you … it's the only thing that keeps me sane."
- **pp. 04–06:** Vader suspended out of his suit in a medical chamber, with surgical droids. "Please keep me sane…" "Beginning reassembly now." "Engaging neural stimulators." *FSSSCHH!* "AAAAGGGGGGGH!" A droid: "Routine maintenance complete. All neural links have been reset. You should be operating at peak efficiency for another --" Vader, gripping his scarred head: "Schedule the next procedure." "Give me my helmet. I am being summoned."
  - (ANALYSIS: canon depiction of Vader's suit needing periodic "routine maintenance" and neural-link resets, a limitation of his early suit.)
- **pp. 07–08:** Vader walks past red-robed Royal Guards into a volcanic throne room. Palpatine: "Lord Vader. Kneel before me." Vader kneels: "What is thy bidding … my master?"
- **p. 09:** "Rise … my apprentice." Moff Tarkin's officer has gone missing in the Atoan system, the "Ghost Nebula": "Largely unexplored, but inhabited." "One of my Star Destroyers entered the system several weeks ago to hunt insurgents and simply vanished." The commander is "Admiral Garoche Tarkin … my *son*" (Wilhuff Tarkin speaking).
- **p. 10:** Tarkin to Vader: "You are not *my* choice for this mission. I know of your failure to protect the AT-AT factory on Otavon XII. A failure caused by your obsession with hunting Jedi." Vader: "The Jedi Order is *still* a threat to us." Tarkin: "The Jedi are irrelevant…" Palpatine: "ENOUGH!" "Lord Vader, I trust you will not be so easily distracted this time?" Captain Shale "will ensure that it succeeds": "I went through the academy with Garoche. We were rivals, but also friends." Vader: "I do not require a shadow." Palpatine: "You and Captain Shale will take two battalions of the 501st Legion. Find Admiral Tarkin and bring him home."
- **p. 11:** "The Atoan system. Four days later." A Venator-class Star Destroyer with escorts and LAAT gunships in the hangar. "Our intelligence on Atoa is limited." "We must be prepared for a war." Vader: "Crush any resistance, but leave the officers alive. For interrogation."
- **p. 12:** gunships and AT-TEs land on a snowy world by a river city. "501st! Move out!" (Vader with clone troopers.)
- **p. 13:** "Report, Commander Voca." "Movement up ahead, Lord Vader." "Scanning a few hundred armed soldiers holding the city … and a scouting party headed our way." Vader: "Cut them down."
- **p. 14:** a battle of AT-TEs and AT-RTs against fur-clad Atoan soldiers.
- **p. 15:** "They're retreating to the watch-tower." Vader: "Good. We will trap them inside." Vader saber-cuts Atoans; his blade deflects green blaster bolts. Atoan speech is lettered in untranslated alien script.
- **p. 16:** a female Atoan sniper is clubbed (*KRACK!*) by Captain Shale's black-armored troopers (shadow troopers style). Shale: "Well, my arrival proved quite timely, didn't it?" "Right then. Let's keep moving."
- **p. 17:** "They have heavy artillery and snipers up there. We're pinned down." "A walker could topple the tower." Vader: "No. I want their leaders alive. We'll storm the doors." Shale: "Wait. There's a way we can reduce our losses. Keep them occupied while I take the high road." Vader: "Go."
- **p. 18:** the snipers aim at Vader in the open. Vader raises both hands: ***FWOOSH!*** He Force-pushes a wall of snow up the tower and blinds the snipers.
- **p. 19:** Shale's troopers rappel up the tower behind the snow cover. "The snipers are blind! Let's go!"
- **p. 20:** Vader deflects; a clone is killed ("Ack--"). Shale's men take the snipers: "Got you!" "Don't die on me quite yet."
- **p. 21:** Vader drives his saber into the tower doors, then makes a hand gesture: ***WHAM!*** The blast door is Force-blown inward. "Commander Voca, secure the tower." "And drag these men to the river."
- **p. 22:** Atoan prisoners kneel by the river. Vader: "One standard month ago, you captured an Imperial officer. Tell me where he is being held, or you all die here." A protocol droid: "Essentially the same response as the others, Lord Vader. Almost impossible to translate, but I believe he's lamenting the death of his people."
- **p. 23:** Vader: "Sweep the area again. Execute anyone old enough to hold a weapon. Then burn the city." "And these prisoners?" "Drown them." Shale: "Vader … someone is out there." Vader: "Hold your fire." White-robed figures arrive by boat; a hooded woman steps onto the water.
- **p. 24:** she stands on the river surface. "Servants of the Empire, we bid you welcome. I am the Lady Saro, High Priestess of Atoa. And I will help you find whatever you seek." "Next issue: Vader vs. the Phantom Squadron!"
- **p. 25:** "G85" scanner-group tag (Darth Maul art). **Not part of the comic; excluded.**
- (ANALYSIS) Power notes for #1: a two-handed Force-push wall of snow up a multi-story tower (p. 18); a gesture blows in a fortified blast door (p. 21); routine saber deflection. Vader orders mass executions (p. 23). His suit needs routine neural-link reset maintenance (pp. 04–06).
