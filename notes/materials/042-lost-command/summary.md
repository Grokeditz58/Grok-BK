# 042 Lost Command: summary of strongest findings

**Plot:** c. 19 BBY. Palpatine sends Vader to the Atoan system in the Ghost Nebula to find Admiral Garoche Tarkin, Moff Tarkin's son. Lady Saro, High Priestess of Atoa, offers to help in exchange for being made queen. Vader's conquest kills her rivals. Captain Shale (Garoche's friend) mutinies. Garoche has gone over to Saro, who is carrying his child. Saro's illusions of Padmé break Vader down. He collapses the cathedral on everyone, survives, and Palpatine lets Wilhuff Tarkin believe natives killed his son.

3. **Two point-blank explosions survived:** the droid bomb (LC#3 pp. 15–16, only the chest panel damaged: "No more attacking from the shadows… Now we take the bridge.") and Shale's suicide belt, taken unmasked: "Boom." (LC#5 pp. 11–12)
4. **Unaided leap from a gunship to a moving city** (LC#3 p. 06). **Breaks energy bonds and a crystal pillar** (LC#5 p. 05). **Brings down the crystal cathedral** (LC#5 p. 21). Force push through a snow wall (LC#1 p. 18). Saber cut through a vault door (LC#3 pp. 13–14). Palm-stopped blaster bolt (LC#4 p. 05).
5. **Combat declarations:** > "I am Darth Vader." "I am a Dark Lord of the Sith." "I am death!" (LC#4 p. 19). > "This is my command!" (Force choke of Shale, LC#2 p. 07)

## Lore / character
- **Saro's power:** > "It is *my* gift to see, and let others see." (LC#5 p. 18). She projects visions strong enough to deceive Vader, and she walks on water (LC#1 p. 24).
- **Vader's confession:** > "Yes. I killed you. I killed *you*. I killed Anakin. I killed the child you carried. And I *hate* myself for all that I have done." (LC#5 p. 16)
- **Palpatine on the Tarkins:** > "Garoche Tarkin is easily replaceable, but his father is a powerful and loyal ally" (LC#2 p. 22); payoff: > "He believes that his son was murdered by natives." "Hatred gnaws at him, making him vicious and resolute. He is now a much more powerful servant." (LC#5 p. 23)
- **Garoche's turn:** > "I came here to conquer this system for my father. I killed *children* in his name. … She *forgave* me." (LC#4 p. 23)
- **Shale's motive:** > "I swore an oath... to be a good *soldier*... Not a *slave*. The Emperor doesn't know the difference." (LC#5 p. 10)
- **TPB intro:** Vader "inadvertently killed the woman he loved" and is "the galaxy's ostensive second in command" (TPB file 007).
