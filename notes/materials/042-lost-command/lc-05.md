# Darth Vader and the Lost Command #5 (Dark Horse, May 2011): page-by-page notes
Credits (p. 02): script Haden Blackman; pencils Rick Leonardi; inks Daniel Green; colors Wes Dzioba; lettering Michael Heisler; cover art Tsuneo Sanda; designer Kat Larson; assistant editor Freddye Lins; editor Randy Stradley; publisher Mike Richardson. Special thanks: Jann Moorhead, David Anderman, Troy Alders, Leland Chee, Sue Rostoni, Carol Roeder at Lucas Licensing. Citation: LC#5 p. NN = scan file index (01 = cover).

- **p. 01 (cover, Sanda):** Vader, arms out, crystals shattering around him. "VADER WILL *NOT* BE CHALLENGED!"
- **p. 02 crawl:** > "Darth Vader was sent to the Atoan system to locate a missing Star Destroyer and its commander, Admiral Garoche Tarkin, the son of the Emperor's most loyal ally." … "Following an encounter with the mysterious Lady Saro, who promised to help locate Tarkin in exchange for being named queen of the Atoan system, Vader embarked on a war of conquest, destroying the native defenses and setting the stage for Saro's reign. But still Tarkin was not found." … "Then came the betrayal by Vader's second in command, Captain Shale, and the stormtroopers loyal to him. Vader and his last squad of trusted troopers from the 501st were ambushed on the Atoan crystal planet, and Vader was captured by Shale, Saro, and the *traitor* Garoche Tarkin . . ."
  - Era sidebar: "The Rise of the Empire (1,000–0 years before the Battle of Yavin)". > "The events in this story take place approximately nineteen years before the Battle of Yavin." Indicia: #5, May 2011; © 2011 Lucasfilm Ltd.
- **p. 03:** Padmé vision before the bound, unmasked Vader: "Oh, Anakin... look at you. You are so... ...so *broken*." Vader: > "I am only what my masters have made me." Padmé: "Self-pity does not suit you, Anakin."
- **p. 04:** Vader: > "Do not speak that name again! Anakin Skywalker is gone! He died with *you*!" Padmé: "Then there's no one for me here..." She walks away into the light. Vader: "No! Wait!" "Come back!"
- **p. 05 (splash):** "Come back!" Vader breaks Saro's blue energy bonds and shatters the crystal pillar he is chained to.
  - (ANALYSIS) While unmasked, burned, and on a breath tube, he breaks the energy restraints and a crystal pillar by rage/Force, set off by the vision.
- **p. 06:** in the throne hall, Garoche gives Saro a necklace: "This necklace will protect you, and always guide you back to me." Saro: "Then I now have all I will ever--" ***[explosion]*** "--need?" Vader bursts through the wall behind them.
- **p. 07:** Vader seizes a guard: "Where is she?!?" Shale: "Garoche! Go!" (Garoche pulls Saro away.) Vader: "Where are you hiding her?!?" Shale, rifle up: > "Vader! Your mission ends here!"
- **p. 08:** Vader hurls the guard and swats the rifle away. "Shale! What have you done with her?" Shale: "I'm only here to rescue Garoche!" Shale grabs Vader's lightsaber from his belt: > "But I'm happy to kill you in the process. And with your own weapon, no less."
  - (ANALYSIS) Vader's "her" means Padmé. He is still chasing the vision.
- **p. 09:** Shale swings the red saber. ***KRAK!*** Vader catches Shale's wrist with his mechanical hand and disarms him; the saber flies. Vader grapples him: > "Where -- is -- my -- *wife*?!"
- **p. 10:** Shale, beaten on the floor: "I... I have... no idea what--" Vader: "Liar! Traitor!" Shale: > "I know you... you think I have betrayed the Empire. But it is the Empire who has betrayed *me*. I swore an oath... to be a good *soldier*... Not a *slave*. The Emperor doesn't know the difference. So now my loyalty is to Garoche alone. My *friend*." Vader: > "Your death will change *nothing*! It will *mean* nothing!" Shale: "Heh. Will yours?" A belt of charges goes ***DEET--***.
- **p. 11:** "Wha--" *DEET--* Shale: > "Boom." ***DEEEEEEEEET--***. Vader throws Shale away from himself.
- **p. 12 (splash):** the suicide-bomb explosion. Vader, unmasked, braces and shields his head with his arm in the blast.
  - (ANALYSIS) His second point-blank explosive in the series (cf. LC#3 pp. 15–16). He survives it unmasked and without his suit's full protection.
- **p. 13:** in the catacombs beneath the cathedral, Garoche: "It's a labyrinth down here." Saro: "Be at ease, Garoche. My two hands helped build this cathedral. The map is in my mind." ***BOOOOM!*** Garoche: "Shale! We have to go back for him!" Saro: > "No. We agreed he would use the bombs only if death descended. And so it has. But for *Shale* alone. *We* can still escape. But only if we go *now*." Garoche: "He may have finally *killed* Vader..." Saro: > "Oh, my love, I know clearly that he did not."
  - (ANALYSIS) Saro ("my two hands helped build this cathedral") implies great age or a long life, consistent with her apparent Force/mystic abilities (water-walking, LC#1 p. 24).
- **p. 14:** Vader, unmasked, his saber recovered, walks the dark corridors. A hooded, glowing Padmé figure: "Padmé... you've come back." "You know that I have *never* left you." Vader: "But you *did* leave..."
- **p. 15:** "...You *died*." "No, I did not die." Her hood falls back to show a withered, corpse-like face: > "You *killed* me. *You*." Vader covers his face, then kneels before her (the figure is young-Padmé again).
- **p. 16:** Vader: > "Yes. I killed you. I killed *you*. I killed Anakin. I killed the child you carried. And I *hate* myself for all that I have done." The figure: "Lord Vader..." The face is part-Padmé, part-withered: > "...I *forgive* you." A tear runs down Vader's scarred cheek.
  - (ANALYSIS) Vader openly confesses killing Padmé and (he believes) their child, and admits self-hatred. It is a key Legends characterization beat for c. 19 BBY. The "forgiveness" mirrors Saro's method with Garoche (LC#4 p. 23: "She *forgave* me").
- **p. 17:** Vader reaches out and his hand passes through the "Padmé" figure. "Padmé?" It fades: "No! What's happening?" "Where have you gone???" A dark-haired, veiled woman behind him: > "I am right here, Lord Vader. Still in your heart, in your head."
- **p. 18:** Saro (with Garoche holding a blaster): "Where I have been all along." Vader: "No, *no*! It was *you*!" Saro: > "It is *my* gift to see, and let others see." Vader: "Bring her back!" Saro: "I cannot!" Vader advances: "Bring her back--"
  - (ANALYSIS) Confirmed: the Padmé visions (LC#4 p. 24; LC#5 pp. 03–04, 14–17) were Saro's illusions/projections. It is an Atoan mental-projection ability strong enough to sway Vader for a long stretch.
- **p. 19:** Vader Force-chokes Garoche, lifting him ("Hurk"), and warns Saro: "--or *he* dies!" Saro, her hand on Vader's arm: > "Please, no more murders tonight, Lord Vader. All your scars... they *can* be just damaged flesh, nothing more. Let *our* survival be *your* repair." She touches his face: > "If not for us, if not for you, then for she... ...who is unborn." Vader: "I--"
- **p. 20:** Vader's fist opens and Garoche drops, gasping ("Hukk--huuuhhh"). Saro: "Then it is as I believed..." Vader: > "No." He seizes Saro by the hair. Garoche: "Wait! What are you do--"
- **p. 21 (splash):** Vader, arms raised, brings the crystal cathedral down on all of them. Garoche holds Saro in the rubble: "No... not yet... we had no time --" Saro: > "Shhh. It's all right. We had time enough." Garoche and Saro are buried, and so is Vader, whose eyes are closed. Fade to black.
  - (ANALYSIS) A self-sacrificial Force collapse of the cathedral/crystal structure. Garoche and Saro (and her unborn child) die. Vader survives being buried.
- **p. 22:** caption: > "You were buried *alive* for days." Vader is back in the medical/suit chamber with droids, his armor being refitted. Palpatine: "How did you survive, Lord Vader?" Vader: > "The dark side gave me all I needed." Palpatine: > "The traitors *destroyed*, an insurgency *crushed*, a system *conquered*. You *have* done well."
  - (ANALYSIS) **Key durability/endurance quote:** buried alive for days after the cathedral collapse, unmasked and wounded, sustained by the dark side.
- **p. 23:** Vader on the suit rack. Vader: "And what of Moff Tarkin?" Palpatine: > "He believes that his son was murdered by natives." > "Hatred gnaws at him, making him vicious and resolute. He is now a much more powerful servant." > "Yes, you have done very well indeed, my apprentice." > "But there is still much left to do." A droid brings the helmet.
  - (ANALYSIS) This pays off the LC#2 pp. 22–23 plan ("Hate would then consume the father"). Wilhuff Tarkin is deceived about Garoche's death.
- **p. 24 (final splash):** Vader, re-helmeted, kneels before Palpatine: > "What is thy bidding... ...my master?" "THE END."
- **p. 25:** "G85" scanner tag. **Excluded.**

## Key takeaways (LC#5)
- (ANALYSIS) Feats: breaks Saro's energy bonds and a crystal pillar (p. 05); disarms Shale with his bare hand while Shale swings a lightsaber (p. 09); survives Shale's point-blank suicide bomb unmasked (p. 12); Force choke (p. 19); brings down a crystal cathedral (p. 21); buried alive "for days" and survives via "the dark side" (p. 22).
- (ANALYSIS) Saro's ability: "It is *my* gift to see, and let others see" (p. 18). It is a Force-like illusion/projection power that fooled Vader across several scenes.
- (ANALYSIS) Character: an explicit confession, "I killed you. I killed Anakin. I killed the child you carried. And I hate myself" (p. 16).
