# Darth Vader and the Lost Command TPB (Dark Horse Books, Nov 2011): extras / front & back matter
Source: digital TPB scan (120 image files). Hand-read in full as a contact-sheet pass: files 012–117 are the five issues' story pages, already read issue by issue in lc-01…lc-05, with no chapter dividers between them. The non-story pages were read individually.

- **File 000:** front cover by Michael Kutsche (Vader in profile against a blue sky).
- **Files 001–002, 004:** endpaper/blank; half-title; title page. Title page credits: script Haden Blackman, pencils Rick Leonardi, inks Dan Green, colors Wes Dzioba, lettering Michael Heisler, front cover art Michael Kutsche.
- **File 003, era page:** > "THE RISE OF THE EMPIRE (1,000–0 years before the Battle of Yavin) … After the seeming final defeat of the Sith, the Republic enters a state of complacency. In the waning years of the Republic, the Senate rife with corruption, the ambitious Senator Palpatine causes himself to be elected Supreme Chancellor. This is the era of the prequel trilogy." > "The events in this story take place approximately nineteen years before the Battle of Yavin."
- **File 005, indicia:** collection designer Tina Alessi; editor Randy Stradley; assistant editor Freddye Lins. > "This volume collects *Star Wars: Darth Vader and the Lost Command* #1–#5." CIP: "back cover art, Tsuneo Sanda". ISBN 978-1-59582-778-4; digital ISBN 978-1-62115-092-3; "First print edition: November 2011". © 2011 Lucasfilm Ltd.
- **File 006:** "Illustration by Tsuneo Sanda": Vader in a starfighter cockpit (the #2 cover art).
- **File 007, TPB-only intro text:** > "The Republic has fallen and been replaced by the Empire. Emperor Palpatine consolidates his power, assisted by former Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker, now known to the galaxy as Darth Vader." > "Though Vader has risen to a position of great power, his ascension has not been without sacrifice. He betrayed his Jedi friends, lost his limbs in a battle with his former Master, and inadvertently killed the woman he loved." > "Now the galaxy's ostensive second in command is haunted by thoughts of what he gave up, and of what could have been . . ."
- **Files 008–011:** the story opens (the #1 dream sequence etc.; see lc-01).
- **File 118:** Imperial-crest end page (no text).
- **File 119, back cover:** > "Darth Vader is tasked with a mission to locate a lost Imperial expeditionary force—one led by the son of Vader's rising nemesis, Moff Tarkin." > "The perils of Vader's journey into the unexplored Ghost Nebula are compounded by traitors among his crew, the presence of the system's religious leader—the beautiful and mysterious Lady Saro—and the fact that Vader himself is still haunted by the death of Anakin Skywalker's beloved Padmé . . ." Blurb (IGN.com): "Within two pages, writer Haden Blackman dives right into the psyche of one of sci-fi's most beloved characters…"
- **Not present:** no sketchbook, cover gallery, or afterword in this edition. The single issues' covers (Sanda) are only in the issue scans.
- (ANALYSIS) The TPB calls Wilhuff Tarkin "Vader's rising nemesis" and calls Vader "the galaxy's ostensive second in command". The intro's word "inadvertently" frames Padmé's death as accidental, which fits Vader's own confession in LC#5 p. 16.
