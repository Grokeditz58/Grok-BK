# Darth Vader and the Lost Command #4 (Dark Horse, Apr 2011): page-by-page notes
Same creative team (Blackman/Leonardi/Green/Dzioba/Heisler). Designer credit: Kat Larson. Citation: LC#4 p. NN = scan file index (01 = cover). 24 story/cover files plus a tag page (25).

- **p. 01 (cover):** "VADER BETRAYED! SOMEBODY IS GOING TO BE SORRY!"
- **p. 02 crawl/indicia (#4, April 2011):** > "Vader woke to find Captain Shale's storm commandos in his quarters . . ."
- **p. 03:** unmasked Vader, saber lit, faces the commandos. > "Stand down -- and you may live long enough to beg for your lives." Commando: "Open fire!"
- **p. 04:** > "Then meet your fate." Commando: "You take orders from a traitor!" … "But this command is mine!" Vader: "This mission is mine!" He deflects the bolts and Force-pushes them.
- **p. 05:** captured commando: > "You'll never find Captain Shale... he's gone to the heart of the system... in search of Admiral Tarkin." Vader: "To make himself the hero." "No...no... he's trying to save the admiral. He thinks you will kill Tarkin." Vader: > "He is right."
  - (ANALYSIS) Vader stops or catches a blaster bolt with his open palm on this page. It is a bare-hand bolt block, not a saber deflection.

- **p. 07:** Vader sabers the commandos. Voca: "Kill them all!" … "It's a mutiny, Lord Vader! They've executed all of the officers and locked down the communications array." Vader: > "None of that matters now, Commander Voca. Gather your men and follow me."
- **p. 08:** "Send your engineers to Shale's hangar and activate the tracking de--" ***BOOOOOM!***

- **p. 13:** the attack shuttle flees the burning Star Destroyer.
- **p. 14:** the shuttle is hit near a red sun and crashes on a planet of crystal spires.
- **p. 15:** ***KER-ACK!*** The hatch is kicked out. "Secure a perimeter! Go!" Vader: "Voca, how many of your squad have survived?" Voca: "Including me? Just nine. And the transport is a total loss." Vader: > "Gather whatever supplies you need. We'll pursue Shale on foot."

- **p. 18:** an insurgent shouts in alien script (untranslated). Splash: Vader leaps into the gunmen, saber raised. Best-panel candidate.
- **p. 19:** ***WHAAM!*** A Force blast scatters the crowd. Vader, surrounded: > "I am Darth Vader." > "I am a Dark Lord of the Sith." > "I am death!" He cuts down the attackers.

## Key takeaways
- (ANALYSIS) Feats: unmasked saber defense against a commando squad (pp. 03–04); a palm-stopped blaster bolt (p. 05); surviving the hangar explosion (p. 12); a leap into an ambush and a Force blast through a crowd (pp. 18–19). He is beaten by an ambush plus massed flamethrowers plus Saro's energy binding (p. 21). He survives burns that Tarkin calls coming "back from the dead" (p. 22).
- (ANALYSIS) Plot: Shale's mutiny is meant to save Garoche Tarkin. Tarkin has gone native under Saro. Saro has been using Vader to kill her rivals. The Padmé apparition (p. 24) is probably Atoan/Saro-induced; the issue doesn't say.
