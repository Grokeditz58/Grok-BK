# Darth Vader and the Lost Command #3 (Dark Horse, Mar 2011): page-by-page notes
Same creative team (Blackman/Leonardi/Green/Dzioba/Heisler; cover Sanda). Citation: LC#3 p. NN = scan file index. **Note:** scan file 04 is a double-page spread (two printed pages), so the file count is 23 + tag for a 22-page story.

- **p. 01 (cover, Sanda):** Vader charging with a red saber among clone troopers and an AT-TE in a dark sea. "DARTH VADER ON THE ATTACK!"
- **p. 02 crawl:** > "Darth Vader is searching for a missing Star Destroyer commanded by Admiral Garoche Tarkin—Moff Tarkin's son. With Captain Shale—a friend of the admiral's—as his second in command…" … "Vader continues to cut a swath through the Atoan system until his troops are poised to assault a planet covered with oceans of boiling tar. Vader is cautioned that an attack could be disastrous, but the Dark Lord has a plan. . . ." Indicia: #3, March 2011.
- **p. 03:** AT-TEs wade through the tar sea. "We're losing traction, sir." "Keep going! Lord Vader won't accept a retreat!" "We've lost one of the rear rotors. We're crippled." "Too late, sir! Brace for impact!" The walker sinks.
- **p. 04 (spread):** ***BOOOOM!*** A walker explodes beside a tar-sea fortress city that is itself a moving ship; speeder bikes. "The Atoans are fleeing! Target the engines! Maybe we'll get lucky…" "Rocket away!" ***THOOM!***
- **p. 05:** a skull-emblazoned LAAT gunship carries Vader and Shale's black troopers. "Another squad gone." Vader: "That will be the last. The Atoans believe they have escaped. They will not be prepared for us." "I hope you're right. We're in range."
- **p. 06:** Vader: "Follow me." Splash: Vader leaps unaided from the gunship toward the city, arms spread. Best-panel candidate.
- **p. 07:** Vader lands on a tower; the troopers zip-line in. Shale: "Regroup on Vader. Go!" Saro: "What do I need to do, Captain Shale?" "Just hang on." She is carried down the line.
- **p. 08:** goggled insurgents on the stairs. Vader Force-chokes one ("Hurk!") while black troopers garrote others; a body is thrown.
- **p. 09:** bodies fall into the burning tar. Trooper: "Lady Saro … the insurgents were carrying this." Saro: "It's an electronic box. For maps." "Good. We need you to decipher the city's schematics." "Lord Vader believes Admiral Tarkin is near."
- **p. 10:** insurgent patrol in steam/fog; ***WHAMMM! KRUNCH!*** A grate attack takes one down.
- **p. 11:** Vader looms out of the steam, and the insurgents are startled ("?" and "!"). His red blade sweeps.
- **p. 12:** "You're certain there is no other way around?" Saro: "The map reveals holding cells beyond this gate. And only *this* gate." "Breaching mines will bring an armed response. Or a total lock-down of our position." "We'll give Lord Vader a few moments more…" Vader: "This way." Bodies litter the tunnel.
- **p. 13:** Vader Force-slams two guards against the vault door, then says "Stand back." His saber melts into the door.
- **p. 14:** he cuts a circle through the vault door. Vader: "Tarkin…" A protocol droid: "Ah, Imperials. Finally." "Where is Admiral Tarkin?" "My master? He was taken away nearly a week ago… but he left this gift, should anyone come for him." It hands over a glowing device.
- **p. 15:** Vader: "Get down!" ***BA-BOOOM!*** The droid's "gift" is a bomb.
- **p. 16:** Shale and Saro are shielded by a trooper (*TUNK!*). Vader's chest panel is damaged and sparking. Vader: "No more attacking from the shadows… Now we take the bridge."
  - (ANALYSIS) Vader survives a point-blank bomb with only damage to his chest control box (p. 15–16).
- **p. 17:** the city bridge; insurgents (alien script). Black troopers rappel down outside the windows and blow in the glass: ***KRA-KOOM!***
- **p. 18:** a firefight on the bridge. A red-haired, goggled insurgent woman is stunned ("Umf!"). Vader: "Lady Saro, tell me the Atoan word for *'hostage.'*"
- **p. 19:** Vader's fighters and walkers land on the green island beside the beached city. Shale: "The captain claims that this entire city has pledged fealty to the Empire -- at Admiral Garoche Tarkin's request." "What? Where's Lady Saro?" Vader: "I sent her back to the ship. My protocol droid can conduct a simple interrogation."
- **p. 20:** droid: "She's shrieking more of the same. She feels these attacks are a betrayal." Vader: "Then another one dies." He Force-hurls a prisoner off the deck into the burning tar ("Aaaaiiii -- … -- iiieeeee--!"). Vader: "Now, tell me the truth!" Droid: "Now she brands Admiral Tarkin a liar." Shale: "This is insane!"
- **p. 21:** Vader Force-throws more prisoners ("Aaaaagh!"), then executes the woman (*PSSSHAK!*). Shale: "I… I thought she was attacking you." Vader: "Sink the city. Bring our forces back to the Star Destroyer. And then we will discuss these charges against Admiral Tarkin. Your *friend*."
- **p. 22 (dream, burgundy borders):** Padmé wakes Anakin: "I just spent five days making sure your son didn't get himself killed hunting Sith on Trandosha… I think I deserve a nap, Padmé." "They are coming for us…" "Wait … who's coming?" "Assassins…"
- **p. 23:** unmasked Vader with a red saber, in his chamber, as armored troopers burst in: "I know who sent you… and he dies next." Next issue: "Vader Betrayed!"
- **p. 24:** "G85" scanner tag. **Excluded.**
- (ANALYSIS) Feats: an unaided leap from a gunship to a moving city (p. 06); a Force choke (p. 08); a Force slam (p. 13); saber cutting through a vault door (pp. 13–14); surviving a bomb blast (pp. 15–16); serial Force-hurling (pp. 20–21). In the dream, the "son" hunts Sith on Trandosha and Anakin is still a Jedi.
