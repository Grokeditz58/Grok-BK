# 054 · Prima Official Game Guide: *Star Wars: The Clone Wars — Lightsaber Duels* / *Jedi Alliance* (flip-book)

- **Status:** DONE, read in full on 2026-11-03.
- **Sources (not committed):**
  - MEGA `AClA1DzI`: a cbz of 115 page scans of the print flip-book. It has the LD half and the JA flip cover only. I OCR'd it and viewed every page.
  - MEGA `0L9w0LaZ`: the Prima eGuide PDF, 178 pp., with a text layer. It holds the LD guide (pp. 1–113) and the complete *Jedi Alliance* DS guide (pp. 114–178).

- **Page mapping:** LD printed page = cbz scan − 2 = eGuide PDF page − 1. JA printed page = eGuide PDF page − 114.

## Files
- `ld-01.md`: the LD print guide, read from the scans with quotes checked against the eGuide text. It covers front matter, Modes, the Way of the Saber, Battlegrounds, all 10 Duelists, the Campaign, the Secret Jedi Scrolls, concept art and the back cover.
- `ld-02-eguide-diff.md`: the page-by-page eGuide vs. print comparison. Only the front matter differs (a digital PM credit, a different ISBN, and the print-only Blu-ray ad). The eGuide also adds the whole JA half.
- `ld-03-jedi-alliance.md`: the *Jedi Alliance* guide, from the eGuide only, read in full. It covers the cast, mechanics, the nine-mission story (Sai Sircu, Ros Lai, the *Devastation*), all 18 dev-trivia sidebars, unlockables and concept art.
