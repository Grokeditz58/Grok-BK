# Prima Lightsaber Duels — part 2: eGuide (`0L9w0LaZ`) vs. the print scan (`AClA1DzI`)

**Method:** I extracted the eGuide's text layer one page at a time (178 PDF pages). Each eGuide page was matched to its best cbz OCR page by word-set similarity, and every eGuide word of 5+ letters that never appears in the cbz OCR was listed for review. Pages with low similarity, or with leftover unmatched words, were checked by eye against the scan. The page-image contact sheets (covers, dedication, concept art, back) were also compared.

## Result
**The Lightsaber Duels content is identical.** eGuide PDF p. N = cbz scan N+1 = printed page N−1 for eGuide pp. 2–113 (Jaccard similarity 0.75–0.95 on text pages).
- Every leftover unmatched word was one of three things:
  - the eGuide's per-page watermark ("Protected by copyright. Unauthorized or unlawful copying or downloading is prohibited.");
  - OCR misses in the scan, such as "Hurikane", "Tyvokka", "Ataru" and "Christophsis" inside dense stat boxes;
  - text the print shows as button icons. The Secret Jedi Scrolls cheat codes are glyphs in print, and the eGuide spells them out (e.g. "ZZZZZ+ZZZZZ+1 Unlock Mustafar", "AAAAA+AAAAA+2 Unlock Raxus Prime") (eGuide p. 109 / print p. 108).
- No wording changes were found in the bios, motivation boxes, walkthroughs or captions. The dedication (eGuide p. 3) is the same: "This book and game are dedicated in memory of Ian Lovell, Lead Animator."
