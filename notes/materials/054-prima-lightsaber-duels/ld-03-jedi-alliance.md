# Prima Jedi Alliance (Nintendo DS) guide — part 3 (eGuide-only half, read in full)

**Source:** *Star Wars: The Clone Wars — Jedi Alliance*, Prima Official Game Guide, by Fernando Bueno, Prima/Random House 2008. eGuide ISBN 9780761561675. This is the second half of the LD/JA flip-book.
**Copy read:** MEGA eGuide `0L9w0LaZ`, PDF pp. 114–178. These pages are not in the cbz scan. I read every page of the text layer in order and viewed the cover (p. 114) and the three concept-art pages (pp. 176–178) as images.
**Page numbers:** "JA p. N" is the printed JA page number, which equals eGuide PDF p. N+114.

## Overview and cast (JA pp. 2–8)
- **Plot blurb (JA p. 2):** "When a cargo of precious lightsaber crystals is mysteriously hijacked, Anakin Skywalker and his new Padawan, Ahsoka Tano, are sent to recover it. ... Anakin, Ahsoka, and eventually Jedi Masters Obi-Wan Kenobi, Mace Windu, Plo Kloon [sic], and Kit Fisto must band together to face a terrifying new enemy." Players pick two of six Jedi per mission across "nine action-packed levels that feature innovative, stylus-based combat".
- **Cast vitals:**
  - Ahsoka: Commander, Togruta, 1.61, Force Power **Environmental**.
  - Anakin: General, 1.88, **Combat**.
  - Kit Fisto: **High General**, Nautolan, 1.96, Environmental.
  - Mace: General, Jedi Master, 1.8 (the LD guide gives 1.88), Combat.
  - Obi-Wan: General, Jedi Master, 1.8, Combat.
  - Plo Koon: "Lifetime member of the Jedi Council", Kel Dor, 1.88, Environmental.
  - The combat/environmental split decides which "Team Up Environment" secret each pair can open.
- **Bios:**
  - Ahsoka: "like her master, she is both highly skilled and overly enthusiastic".
  - Anakin: "His skills are highly respected by the Jedi Council, but some worry about his unpredictable behavior."
  - Kit: "known for his trademark smile that shows he relishes every moment of battle."
  - Mace: "Mace always seems to have the weight of the galaxy on his shoulders. In truth, he is constantly pondering the depths of the Force."
  - Obi-Wan: "His principles and charm make him a natural leader."
  - Plo: "He has a strong sense of justice and is highly skilled in hand-to-hand fighting with his lightsaber."
  - The droids: "R2-D2 is skilled in zapping droids and tapping security consoles, while C-3PO uses his renowned translation abilities and quick thinking to confuse and irritate the opposition." (JA p. 8)

## The Way of the Saber (JA pp. 9–11)
- Stylus controls cover movement, context actions, rapid attacks, stuns and Saber Lock, with the note "Melee enemies are those that can attack you with melee strikes. Droids, for example, are not melee enemies. Nightsisters are." (JA p. 10).
- The rest covers autoblock and the Defense meter, deflection, Force Push and Jedi Assist.
- Team-Up Combat: "Each time you complete a mission using the same team of Jedi, their bond will strengthen and their Force meter will increase by one segment." (JA p. 11)
- Minigames: control panels, door cutting, R2 Security Tap and Jedi Action Sequences.

## Story by mission (walkthroughs read in full; plot beats and lore)
1. **A Long Time Ago — the *Sedawan* (JA pp. 12–17).**
   - Opening: "Jedi Master Luminara watches over the cargo as a group of buzz droids cut into the hull ... Just then, a mysterious figure disembarks the invading ship. It’s a Nightsister...."
   - The ship "was carrying a load of lightsaber crystals from Ilum." At the bridge, "all the escape pods have been jettisoned" and "a woman clad in red and toting a red ’saber enters the bridge". She sets two pairs of Nightsisters on the Jedi and escapes.
2. **Rodia (JA pp. 18–24).**
   - Yoda "is familiar with the woman. She is a Nightsister, and that troubles him deeply." A tracker on her ship was lost on Rodia.
   - The Jedi's A-wing hits the city's dome shield and they free-fall in. Cody "can’t get through the dome shield," so they sabotage the generators: a submarine turret, mines, a filtration tower, and a "megacephalic monster" they kill underwater.
3. **Coruscant — Ziro’s palace (JA pp. 25–30).** The Council needs "the Nightsister Ros Lai". Padmé sends C-3PO and R2-D2, a MagnaGuard captures them, and R2 breaks free from Treadwell droids.
   - C-3PO's translation-trick dialogue to the MagnaGuard is printed: "He wonders if you would be more suited to work as a speed bump." / "He swears you are the sorriest excuse for a droid and would be more useful melted down." (JA p. 27)
   - Ziro is found shackled ("make her stop"). The guide calls her "Asajj Ventress, Dooku’s dome-headed drone." (JA p. 30). Ventress retreats.
   - "a mysterious woman in blue drops in from the vents. It’s Ros Lai!" She helps "as long as you can smuggle her off Coruscant."
4. **Return to Rodia (JA pp. 31–35).** The Jedi chase the Nightsister leader by train and spy her reporting to Sidious "that the stolen crystals are absorbing the Force energy as expected." The boss fight has her hovering and attacked with crystal shards.
5. **Christophsis (JA pp. 36–39).**
   - Padmé's covert probes find "that the Separatists are mining for something on Christophsis". Her transport crashes and Commander Kree's dropship is shot down. Padmé, "her clone comrade, C-3PO, and R2-D2" are freed.
   - Padmé reveals the Separatists are "harvesting crystals from the planet core".
6. **Dathomir (JA pp. 40–43).** "The only place left to go is to the source itself—Dathomir, home of the Nightsisters." Clone hostages are rescued, the Jedi pass booby-trapped tombs, and a Nightsister collapses the ceiling on them: "You’re trapped."
7. **Return to Christophsis (JA pp. 44–47) — the droids-only mission.**
   - Padmé "departs with the injured Commander Rex". R2 and 3PO are mistaken for configuration droids and sabotage battle droids by equipping "the broom", "the drum item" and "a spoon".
   - The pair get coordinates for "Commander Gree" (the guide also writes "Kree" earlier; the inconsistency is the guide's) and find "plans for a new Separatist weapon".
   - Dooku is confronted, and the Jedi tell him "the location of his secret weapon, the Separatist Battle Station, is being sent to the Jedi Council." He escapes with lightning.
8. **Return to Dathomir (JA pp. 48–51).**
   - Yoda reveals "the Nightsisters must be charging the crystals harvested from Dathomir, Christophsis, and Rodia". A rancor chase follows.
   - "Master Luminara held in stasis by the Force" is freed. The Nightsister boss is named: "the Nightsister boss, Sai Sircu".
   - She reports "the crystals are charged and ready for transport to the Devastation starship."
9. **The *Devastation* (JA pp. 52–59).**
   - On the bridge, Sidious is disappointed that the Rodian crystals are missing. "Asajj Ventress suggests that they demonstrate the weapon’s power to Lord Sidious. He likes the suggestion and orders Dooku to set course for Dathomir. ... Sai Sircu takes exception to Sidious’s suggested target and she quietly sneaks away ... then unleashes a Force blast on both Dooku and Ventress!" (JA p. 52)
   - She "then redirects the Devastation toward Coruscant." Yoda, "the only one actually in the Council chamber", sends all six Jedi in pairs.
   - Pair 1 destroys power grids while Ventress fights, and "Sai Sircu arrives and attacks the bald-headed baddy!" Pair 2 destroys Dooku's six targeting consoles ("Avoid direct combat with Dooku—that is for another episode"). Pair 3 duels Sircu among four power crystals.
   - Ending: "Suddenly, in a beam of light, Sai Sircu is joined with the Force... permanently. The Jedi all rush to their starfighters and escape the Devastation just as the large battle cruiser explodes!" (JA p. 59)

## Trivia sidebars (JA, behind-the-scenes; all verbatim)
- "The opening sequence pays homage to the opening shot in Star Wars: Episode 4—A New Hope." (JA p. 12)
- "The Devastation battleship is the sister ship to the Malevolence, as featured in the TV shows." (JA p. 18)
- "The Sedawan is a tuned-up version of the Rebel blockade runner." (JA p. 15)
- "The Sedawan and the Devastation were designed exclusively for the game." (JA p. 26)
- "The Nightsisters were first referenced in the made-for-TV movie Ewoks: The Battle for Endor." (JA p. 17)
- "The planet Dathomir is the home of the Nightsisters. The planet was uniquely redesigned to match the style of the TV Show." (JA p. 29)
- "The game features over 3,500 lines of recorded dialog, which has also been localized into recorded dialog for French, Spanish, and German." (JA p. 33)
- "The voices in the game are from the same actors as the TV show." (JA p. 35)
- "The games cart size is a massive 256 megabytes!" (JA p. 38)
- "Adegan crystals are from the planet Ilum and are the power source of a lightsaber." (JA p. 39)
- "Ilum was featured in the original Clone Wars cartoons." (JA p. 40)
- "The TV show and the Nintendo DS game are produced in the same studio in Singapore." (JA p. 43)
- "Plo Koon is Dave Filoni’s (the director of the TV show) favorite Jedi." (JA p. 45)
- "There are 30 possible playable combinations of Jedi for each mission." (JA p. 47)
- "Nightsisters use a modified version of the lightsaber design. The long swords have an additional two-prong fork at the hilt. The Nightsisters also use a short-sword variant." (JA p. 49)
- "Over 200 concept art pieces were created for the game." (JA p. 51)
- "The game’s original title was Day of the Nightsisters, in reference to LucasArt’s classic Day of the Tentacle." (JA p. 55)
- "The development team is truly multinational with over 15 countries represented." (JA p. 59)

## Unlockables (JA pp. 60–61)
- Costumes come from collecting all Force orbs per mission.
- Extras-screen models come from Ilum crystals, the three Sith holocrons per mission and 5-kill chains. The models include Ziro, Luminara, Sidious, Cody, Nightsister 1 and 2, Padmé, rancor and Sai Sircu models.
- Concept art comes from completion, item-less runs, no-death runs and all-pairings runs. Cheat "tweaks" come from defeating every enemy in a mission; for example, the Immortality tweak: "You’re immune to all but the Power Off button."

## Concept art (JA pp. 62–64, image pages, viewed)
- Christophsis crystal city vistas, the *Devastation*'s red-lit interior and crystal weapon beam, mining/probe droids, and red Nightsister transport ships with orthographic views.
- Nightsister costume turnarounds: blue-clad ranged sister, black-clad saber sister, red-clad sisters. The red-haired blue-robed figure appears to be the Sai Sircu design.
- A Jedi starfighter (Delta-7 style), lava interiors and a crystal cavern.

## Notes
- Sai Sircu, Ros Lai, Commander Kree and the ships *Sedawan* and *Devastation* are game-original. The guide's trivia sidebar confirms the ships "were designed exclusively for the game".
- Recurring guide quirks: clones are called "stormtroopers" (JA pp. 12, 48); "Plo Kloon" (JA p. 2); "Commaner Kree" (JA p. 38).
