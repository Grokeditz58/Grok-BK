# 172 summary — Rinzler, *The Making of Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith* (2005 text)

Full typography-faithful hand-read of the 2005 Del Rey / Ballantine First Edition (MEGA `gWsUDLrI`, catalogued as 2015; the printed book is the 2005 First Edition). Front matter through Index and jacket, print pp. 1–226 (228 CBR images).

Part I covers preproduction and the January 31 rough draft through the June 26, 2003, fourth draft (Mufasta renamed Mustafar; a ten-year-old Han Solo appears on Kashyyyk in the rough draft and is eliminated in the April 13 first draft). Part II is principal photography in Sydney (June 30–September 17, 2003): the Anakin–Palpatine turn is rewritten over a July weekend; on September 1 Lucas changes Obi-Wan’s lava-bank lines to past tense (“I loved you” / “But I could not help you”) and Christensen shoots “Help me, Master” and “I hate you!”; Vader is born in the rehab chamber the same day; the 2003 *Empire* DVD Emperor reshoot with McDiarmid is staged on July 29; principal photography wraps five days early, Vader on a Star Destroyer bridge with Tarkin and the Emperor. Part III is editorial and ILM work through additional photography (August 23–September 3, 2004): Lucas cuts “every scene that doesn’t have anything to do with Anakin”; he tells Spielberg he will not make Anakin’s fall a revenge plot—“Anakin is simply greedy. There is no revenge. The revenge of the Sith is Palpatine… [Vader is] a pawn”; on August 26 he cuts “Help me, Master” because he “didn’t want him to be redeemed”; the epilogue states the prophecy was right and Anakin destroys the Emperor “out of compassion for his son.”
