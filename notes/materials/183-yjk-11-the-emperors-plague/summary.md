# Young Jedi Knights 11: The Emperor's Plague: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-20 CST. Every line of READ l.1–1132 was read in order: front matter and ch. 1–29. The epub has no back matter. Notes are limited to scaling-relevant material (owner's rulings, 2026-11-20). The quotes are in ep-01 and ep-02; this summary is in our own words.
- Authors: Kevin J. Anderson & Rebecca Moesta. Eleventh Young Jedi Knights book (Boulevard Books, 1998). It ends the Bornan Thul / Diversity Alliance arc ('The Fall of the Diversity Alliance'). 29 chapters.

## Plot
- **Ch. 1–3, 9:** The friends recover in bacta on Coruscant and testify to the Senate. Trubor and J'mesk Iman dismiss their account, but Cilghal wins a fact-finding mission to Ryloth. Zekk and Raynar go after Bornan Thul with Zekk's tracer, and Jaina secretly copies the frequency. Aryn decodes Bornan's message, and Leia sends Han with reinforcements.
- **Ch. 4–7, 10, 12:** Bornan blows up his ship (killing the bounty hunter Shakra) and is rescued by Zekk, Raynar and the Rock Dragon, which drives off Fett. Fett had already sliced the storehouse coordinates from Bornan's ship log and sells them to Nolaa. The friends reach the Emperor's plague asteroid first and start planting explosives.
- **Ch. 6, 11, 17, 19, 22:** Nolaa brings her armada and goes down to the depot with Corrsk and Rullak, leaving Raaba in command. Raaba tries to disable rather than destroy the Rock Dragon, and Han's New Republic fleet arrives.
- **Ch. 8, 15, 21 (Ryloth):** Kambrea shows the inspection team a sanitized Ryloth until Sirra punches open a crate of smuggled weapons. In the firefight Luke and Cilghal deflect fire, Lusa's untrained Force surge buries Kambrea under crates, Trubor is shot, and Kur stops the fighting and becomes Ryloth's new leader.
- **Ch. 13–14, 16, 18, 20:** Tenel Ka twists her ankle in a running fight with Rullak's patrol. The trio blow an intersection and take off. Lowie breaks Corrsk's bear hug, cuts off his arm in low gravity, and vents him into space. IG-88, following Jaina's reprogramming to protect Bornan, smashes through the doors for Lowie.
- **Ch. 23–25:** Bornan seals himself into the plague chamber to stop Nolaa. Rullak's wild shooting shatters canisters, killing Rullak and infecting Bornan and Nolaa. Bornan orders IG-88 to save Raynar instead of breaking the chamber open, then dies. Raaba gets Nolaa out.
- **Ch. 26–27:** The Bornaryn fleet helps drive off the armada. Boba Fett, now hired by Tyko Thul, guns down the ambushers in seconds, and Zekk rejects bounty hunting. The trio's combined Force push springboards the Rock Dragon clear. Raaba takes the dying Nolaa into hyperspace, and the depot is destroyed.
- **Ch. 28–29:** Grief and peace for Raynar, and the Cooperative Council is formed. Raynar builds his lightsaber, and Lusa and Zekk become Luke's students. Nolaa dies of the plague and Raaba buries her. Tenel Ka kisses Jacen.

## Strongest findings
- **Lowbacca:** breaks Corrsk's bear hug by biting and boxing his ears, fights him in near-zero gravity, cuts off his arm, and vents him (ch. 14, 18). Hot-wiring and claw strength (ch. 20). Limitations: IG-88 swats him aside twice; he shorts a panel.
- **Tenel Ka:** deflects while running backward; when her ankle turns she drops under a bolt and rolls one-armed; she runs on the injury and tunes out the pain (ch. 13, 16). Tactics and sense of direction.
- **Jacen / Jaina / Tenel Ka:** a combined telekinetic shove springboards the Rock Dragon off the asteroid (ch. 27). Jaina's on-the-run demolition and weak-point analysis (ch. 13, 16).
- **IG-88:** punches through a heavy bulkhead and snaps blast doors off their hinges; his fists dent the plague-chamber wall (ch. 20, 23).
- **Boba Fett:** remote data slicing (ch. 4, 6); whirls 'with such speed that Zekk could barely follow' and kills the ambushers with two pistols (ch. 26).
- **Luke / Cilghal / Lusa:** Luke and Cilghal deflect cross fire with crossed lightsabers, and Luke senses the Bothan's lie (ch. 15, 21). Lusa's untrained Force surge topples tons of crates (ch. 21).
- **Raaba / Sirra:** Raaba's command, reflexes and Wookiee strength, and her constitution beats the plague that kills Nolaa (ch. 22, 25, 29). Sirra punches open a crate and hits a skimmer's fuel module (ch. 15, 21).
- **Zekk:** his danger sense and catch of the falling explosives; his resolve and turn to the light side (ch. 19, 26, 29).
