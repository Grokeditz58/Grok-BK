## Thesis

ILS argues that Ant's series doesn't prove its premises.
- **Wyyrlok fight:** Krayt *absorbed* Wyyrlok's lightning and no-sold his telekinesis, so the fight is a stomp: "Wyyrlok is losing quite badly and Krayt is under no pressure at all".
- **Cade fight:** everything rests on the unproven premise that Krayt used his full power. Krayt's disposition (foresight, confidence, "crazier") and the far bigger power shown in *Vector* show he used "some fraction of his full power". Omega Red gave him no deadline.
- Closing: "Krayt used the power needed for the job."

## Arguments (numbered, ILS + Azronger)

1. **P1 lightning:** deflection vs absorption distinguished; precedents for whole-body absorption; the tutaminis "channel or diffuse" definition; Control/Body classification of tutaminis, Dark Transfer and lightning. Reconstruction:
   - (1) the lightning forms;
   - (2) Krayt, torso enveloped, channels it into the pillars;
   - (3) Wyyrlok throws the rubble;
   - (4) Krayt grabs it;
   - (5) Krayt throws it back and Wyyrlok raises a last-minute bubble.

3. **P3 Talon and full power:** rows 10–12. *Vector* and *Dark Times* are used as a power ceiling. Muur needs only a "suitable host" he can "dominate" (B043, B044); Muur tries Shado and Azlyn (B045/B046); Muurne vs Krayt ends only when Krayt is stabbed in the back (B047/B048).
4. **P4 Omega Red and the disputed panel:** rows 13–17.
5. **P5 conclusion:** rows 18–20, with the Abeloth inconsistency charge.
6. **Azronger addendum (post 2):**
   - (a) Krayt maintained telepathic contact with "millions" of troopers through War, "more of an active than passive effort". Before War his mind was open only to his "closest disciples" (B069 = 013 p105). Cade and Stryfe noted his absence from their heads after Had Abaddon (B070/B071). That might be why Krayt "fell to[o] easily to Cade's blade". (Ant's 018 answers this.)
   - (b) Cade didn't use full potency on Talon. Her warning means "no telling what to expect".
   - (c) Vitiate killed many "too powerful" Jedi and Sith (scans dead), so worry ≠ inferiority.
   - (d) The Top-15 tournament analysis: on p118 the backgrounds change across three panels (ledge, floor, intact pillar; B074), so Krayt walked through three areas batting Cade aside with pure Force. Cade lands no Force hits. Force barriers make ragdolling an equal impossible. *Shadow Conspiracy* quote (Sidious vs Maul).
   - (e) The disputed panel: the blue trail shows a sweeping arc off-panel; Krayt's feet are planted on tiles, so Cade is high up near a wall. Wall-running at an unarmed Krayt would be "immensely impractical" (diagram dead).
   - (f) Saarai scaling: Talon ≈ Stryfe; Saarai dominates Stryfe more than Cade dominates Talon; Saarai is "nascent" next to Wyyrlok. So Cade can't give Krayt a good fight.

## Common rebuttals / weak points (ANALYSIS — not from the thread)

- **P1 overreaches.** Our 010 read of p18:
  - panel 1: "Both fire Force lightning… the streams meet in the middle";
  - panel 2: Krayt "arms flung wide, grinning, inside a web of lightning… no visible damage".
  - So *both* fire first (a clash, against ILS's "Krayt deflected/absorbed" for panel 1), and *then* Krayt takes the lightning on his body (against Ant's "Krayt fires" for panel 2). ILS's "new feat" (absorption) is a fair reading of panel 2 only.
- **ILS's "blue hands" is roughly right** (the glow is violet-blue; our 010 note says purple). But "Krayt shows no glow, so he's throwing like a ball" is undercut by p18 panel 4, where Krayt's open hand halts the rocks in a blue glow: a telekinetic catch.
- **Horns:** "removed the bone, just leaving the artificial sheathing" half-fits B024 vs B025: the long tips are gone. But 020 B013 (same p22 panel, zoomed) shows short grey bone stubs beyond the sheaths. Whether the sheath is "artificial" is not stated on any page.
- **Omega Red:**
  - ILS is right that nothing on-page shows Krayt racing a deadline. Maladi's p108 "PROMISES KEPT. IT IS DONE, MY LORD KRAYT." shows the handover was Krayt's plan, and p116 shows foresight.
  - But ILS's "Krayt has already proven he can bring a corpse back to life" is **wrong on our pages**. p4: "I DIMLY REMEMBER DRIVING MY CONSCIOUSNESS, MY LIFE FORCE, DEEP WITHIN MYSELF…" He preserved himself; he did not revive a corpse.
  - **"This gives me an idea" (p45)** is said as Talon reports Taivas. The next page (p46) is Krayt's "I WANT TO OPEN A PATH FOR SKYWALKER THAT WILL LEAD TO ME… MAKE HIM A GIFT OF YOUR BROKEN IMPERIAL KNIGHT". So "the idea to lure Cade" is a **defensible reading**. But it leads to Taivas, not to Coruscant "at the precise time Roan Fel would be dropping Omega Red".
- **The *Dark Times* narration is not simply "omniscient".** B038 frames it as "CELESTE MORNE CAN ONLY IMAGINE…", and B039 is a *question* ("…OR MERELY TRADE ONE MASTER FOR ANOTHER?"). B040's "THE SPIRIT… IS RIGHT" is declarative, but about Vader's strength.
- **The *Vector* comparison is apples to oranges.** Krayt vs Muurne and a full maelstrom is a different situation. "Bigger art = more power" ignores focus (Ant's 020 point).
- **The Mortis analogy is weak.** Kenobi collapses screaming, and Mortis has special mechanics. It doesn't neutralise Cade's lightning feat on its own.
- **Saarai scaling (Azronger):** Saarai is in War only at p10, so the claimed domination of Stryfe comes from issues we haven't read (likely Legacy #49–50). We can't verify it.
