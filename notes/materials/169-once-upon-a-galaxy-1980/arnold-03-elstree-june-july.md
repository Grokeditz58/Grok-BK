# 169 — Elstree: remaining June and July 1979 days

Covers the remaining June and July Elstree journal days. Thursday, June 21 (carbon-freeze); Thursday, July 19; and Friday, July 27 are in `arnold-01-elstree-june21-july19-27.md`. Citations: Arnold, “EMI Studios, Elstree,” [journal date]. Typography locked. Arnold glosses flagged. Headings locked as printed, including the June 11 / June 21 chronology conflict.

## June (except Thursday, June 21)

Monday, June 4 — **Anthony Daniels**: C-3PO “an irritable, fussy, and typically mid-European diplomat.” Publicists had claimed “C-3PO is entirely mechanical.” Daniels to Lucas (typography locked): “You opened a door for me, but you didn’t tell me that beyond it was another door that you’d slam in my face!” “The visual concept of the film captivates George Lucas. When Ralph McQuarrie shows him a picture he is far more interested in these paintings, in the look and mechanics of them, than in the fact that there are human characters in them, too.” “I care for Threepio. I really do.”

Wednesday, June 21 [heading as printed] — **John Barry** dies suddenly after returning as second-unit director; not the already-locked Thursday, June 21 carbon-freeze tape. Then **“Monday, June 11”** heads the funeral entry but refers to the carbon-freeze as “tomorrow” (Thursday, June 21). **Lock both headings as printed; flag the chronology conflict; do not invent a corrected date.**

Friday, June 15: BBC *Star Wars* radio series; US National Public Radio; twelve episodes in Britain. At lunch, Hamill and Ford “naturally possessive of their roles.” Ford: “Until something like this happens you don’t realize how possessive you’ve become about the character you’re playing.” Hamill to Kurtz: “I want to play my part on radio… I don’t want some other guy playing Luke.” Marilou overdue; Arnold disagrees with withholding the birth announcement.

Monday, June 18: two-thirds through principal photography; about sixty seconds of screen time a day. **Kurtz**: “The material looks good… The filming has been a little slower than we would have liked… Almost every scene has special effects… There’s an element of luck in it all.” “Tension between the actors comes and goes… In Carrie’s case what might seem to be temperament can be explained because she is allergic to a number of things.” “None of us can recapture the bright innocence of the first time.”

Tuesday, June 19 — **Kenny Baker**: “I turned down the role three times. It sounded untheatrical, uncanny… I wondered what the movie was about. None of us knew. Only George Lucas knew, and Gary Kurtz.” Midget / dwarf distinction, typography locked (“deformationmisshapen”).

Wednesday, June 27 — **Harrison Ford** lunch (divorce). Arnold’s gloss, flag as Arnold’s: “I don’t think he will play [Solo] again.”

Friday, June 29: Hamill’s son Nathan Elias is born; Mark sprains a thumb — delays the Luke–Vader laser duel. “the safety of an investment of over $20 million… dependent on the fitness and stability of one young man.” Crew screening as a morale booster (Kurtz); exchange-rate pressure on the budget.

## July (except Thursday, July 19 and Friday, July 27)

Monday, July 2: Luke–Vader confrontation filming resumes; Mark also with Carrie and Billy Dee on Stage 8. Dagobah under construction around the clock; talk of a “hiatus”; root cause “the fire that destroyed Stage 3.”

Tuesday, July 3 — **John Mollo**, typography locked: Lucas “Audiences mustn’t consciously notice the costumes… If they do, you have gone wrong.” (Mollo: they didn’t want anything “designery.”) Luke “country boy… karate-type shirt”; Han “As befits a hustler 器 space, there’s a touch of the dude about him” (garbage: 器 ≈ in); Leia “akin to a nun’s costume”; Kenobi “a cross between a Samurai and a priest.” “sixteen sets of Luke Sy. walker’s combat outfit.” (conversion slip). Another uncertain cluster “槛”.

Thursday, July 5 (Independence Day screening the night before) — Ford has finished UK work and flown back to California. “it is the last we see of him in the film, and it could be the last we see of Han in the saga.” Asked about a third film, Ford would not comment, but: “For at least six months I don’t want to do anything at all.” “Han is full-blooded… identifiable… an urban cowboy looking for love… a sucker for a sacrificial end.” **Kershner**: the freeze “entirely his [Harrison’s]” scene; “a little tension with Carrie.” George Whitear: “quality *Star Wars*.”

Friday, July 6: Mayhew exhausted in yak wool / mohair with Threepio on his back in the carbon-freeze steam; sent home; stand-in on the landing platform, “not the same.” Radio-controlled R2 problems on Stage 2.

Monday, July 9 — exposition, typography locked. **Kershner**: “we had been too explanatory… So we are compressing.” Especially Yoda / Dagobah. “George is doing that right now… took the problem away to his workbench.” Lucas “pops in and out”; “I’ve caught him looking through a camera only twice so far, and he looked at me as guiltily as if he’d stolen a cookie.” Lucas then shot a 15-second bridging sequence (Luke putting on flying gear, talking to a robot), 16 takes; Kershner: “the finest assistant I could hope for.” Carbon-freeze set still dangerous (“Yesterday I got hit on the head with a chunk of metal”).

Tuesday, July 10: Carrie nearly finished; “I come into my own around 10:30 P.M.” Weekend visitors (Penny Marshall, Rob Reiner, Treat Williams, Harvey Keitel).

Wednesday, July 11: Dagobah construction; plasterers down from forty to twenty-eight (*Flash Gordon*). Reynolds: really a fourteen-week job. Yoda’s house “something out of the Brothers Grimm.” “Lucas, by the way, is due to join us again this weekend and will, I am told, use the bog-planet set in connection with another chapter in the saga he is writing.” Call-sheet garbage cluster ೫೦ೋ. Kidnap threat to Carrie via a tip to Debbie Reynolds in Las Vegas — “Today, Carrie has security guards.” (report only; no operational detail.)

Monday, July 16: **Prowse** as self-publicist vs the company exploiting the character, not the actor; gym, book, Harrods, a fitness LP.

Tuesday, July 17 — **Carrie Fisher**, typography locked: “Leia is not a real character. She is more of a caricature, and is somewhat one dimensional… favorite color… white.” “Irvin Kershner directs us as actors more than George did.” “In the first film we had 800 opticals, but in this we have 300.” (Carrie’s figures — flag.) “Empire is more about the people and less about the effects.” “It’s romance in celluloid.”

Wednesday, July 18: Carrie’s farewell; “ಖ್ವpage and tacos” (garbage: ಖ್ವpage ≈ champagne).

Monday, July 23 — **Peter Diamond** choreographed the Vader–Luke duel; tutored Hamill; “Often it takes the courage to say no.”

Wednesday, July 25 — **Bill Welch**: materials up twenty to thirty percent; spend now for “the third film in the saga.” Frank Bruton: “If we can’t give a name to something we call it a ‘greebly’… ‘Greebly is a word George Lucas coined on *Star Wars* for something you can’t otherwise define.’”
