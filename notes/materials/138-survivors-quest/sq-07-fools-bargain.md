## Setup (~17228–early assault)
- Imperial **501st** (Empire of the Hand era) on **Kariek** in driving rain: final push against the Warlord’s fortress.
- Focus unit **Aurek-Seven**: Twister (commander), Dropkick, Watchman, Cloud, Grappler, etc.; local Eickarie ally **Su-mil**.
- Mission problem: Warlord’s remotes/defenses + hostages/prisoners; need Warlord **alive** (species/origin unknown—armor conceals identity).

## Bargain
- Twister/Su-mil strike a deal: Imperials help free Eickarie prisoners; in return Su-mil’s people leave the Warlord alive for Imperial capture rather than immediate honor-vengeance execution.
- Cultural clash: Eickarie “right of vengeance” vs Imperial intelligence need.

## Assault (watchtowers → dungeon → stronghold)
- Third watchtower / fortress approach; prisoner-release operations; dungeon and stronghold breach.
- Flash paste on stronghold door; door shatters; Eickaries surge (“Our world! Our ways!”) ahead of discipline—heavy casualties both sides.
- Interior: Eickarie dead/wounded; last Lakra bodyguards down; **Warlord** pinned under Su-mil—gun aimed not at Warlord but at other Eickaries demanding the kill.
- Su-mil holds the bargain despite wound (blackened side); Watchman kills fortress remotes/defenses from status board.
- Twister reports to Command: stronghold penetrated; Warlord secured thanks to Eickaries.

- Rehab: Su-mil stays hospitalized partly because new InterTribal Council/people resent him—**“fool’s bargain”** that cost vengeance rights.
- Twister: vengeance culture prolonged tribal wars; history will vindicate.
- Why Warlord alive: identity unknown—Lakra, rogue Eickarie, or unknown species. Result: **new species** (not in Imperial files); home system pried; task force with Star Destroyers to make contact.

- Twister offers Su-mil a shot at **501st commission** (nonhuman eligible if world joins Hand)—another “fool’s bargain”; Su-mil accepts immediately; transport waiting.
