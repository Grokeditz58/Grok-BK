## Rebellion intro and *Death Star* excerpt (l.9207–9481)

- The Lambda's stated specs: 20 m, 1,400 g top speed, Class-One hyperdrive, 20 troops, 3 double blaster cannons and 2 double laser cannons (l.9362–9364).
- These are excerpt stats, not *Lockdown*'s.

## Legends novels timeline (l.9483–9682)
- **33 BBY block:** *Darth Maul: Saboteur*\*, *Cloak of Deception*, *Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter*, **Maul: Lockdown** (l.9504–9508).
- So the timeline puts *Lockdown* **after** *Shadow Hunter*, but the plot (Plagueis alive, Palpatine still a senator before the chancellorship) puts it before TPM. This is a known ordering quirk, because Maul dies in TPM at the same 32 BBY marker.
- *Darth Plagueis* is at 67 BBY. *The Approaching Storm* (our 078) is listed under 32 BBY right after *Outbound Flight* (l.9513).
- The list runs to *Crucible* (45 ABY). It ends at l.9682: "*An eBook novella".
