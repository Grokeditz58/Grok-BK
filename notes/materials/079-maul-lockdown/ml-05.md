# Maul: Lockdown: notes part 5, Ch. 51–76 (l.5782–8193)

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. Citation "ML ch. N (l.x)".
- The novel's text ends at l.8193. Back matter is in ml-06.

## Ch. 51: To the Hold (l.5783–6021)
- Maul on Radique's voice: "made him wonder if this man really was a man at all" (l.5796–5800).
- **Blue witch:** masks the bearer from all electronic surveillance in the Hive (l.5819–5825).
- **Lore on Komari Vosa**, per Slipher (l.5851–5871):
  - High priestess of the Gora, with a fortified citadel on **Kohlma**.
  - Controls her people with narcotics and "advanced mind control".
  - "still extremely lethal with a lightsaber".
  - A former Jedi whose "nearly psychopathic levels of aggression" kept her from becoming a Knight.
  - Part of a Jedi task force against the Gora on **Baltizaar**; captured and tortured on Kohlma until she turned to the dark side, killed her captors and took over.
- Maul feels the mission is turning into a test against a Jedi (l.5886–5895).
- "It's been too long since I've killed something… Be careful, or it might be you." (l.5900–5901)
- **He breaks his no-Force order:** a brief **Force choke** on Slipher, who stays conscious and speaks afterward. "Komari Vosa is not the only one with the skills you mentioned." (l.5904–5919)
- Slipher sells him out to the hangar crew and calls him a hostage-taker (l.5941–5948).
- Maul bluffs that the blue witch is a "plasma detonator" (l.5961–5966).
- **Tech:** he hand-slices the CLL load-lifter's cognitive drive using what Trezza taught him on Orsis. The droid goes berserk and topples a 10 m stack of pallets (l.5976–5993).
- **Slipher dies:** the load-lifter's manipulator arm cuts his head off at a 45° angle and its foot crushes the body (l.6007–6016). Last words: "My intellect is vastly superior to yours in every imaginable capacity" (l.6005).
- **This is an indirect Maul kill** via the droid he sabotaged. Count it as a kill caused by a droid, not a direct one.

## Ch. 52: Purge (l.6023–6090)
- Prison barge *Purge*: navigator **Bissley Kloth** (22), captain **Wyatt Styrene** (former smuggler), 46 inmates aboard (l.6026–6040).
- Z-95 Headhunters fire concussion missiles. A ship-to-ship ram boards the barge with a cutting rig (l.6046–6090).

## Ch. 53: Is There a Ghost (l.6092–6206)
- Sadiki blackmails Gaming Commissioner **Dragomir Chlorus** with photos of an affair at the **Outlander Casino and Resort** on Coruscant (l.6141–6151).
- She demands that the casino licence be restored and that IBC contact **Lars Winnick** cover up Slipher's death (l.6157–6168).
- She spots the blue witch's "bluish blur" in a lift camera and hits the alarm (l.6198–6206).

## Ch. 54: Hot Box (l.6208–6287)
- Maul braces against the walls and climbs the lift shaft (l.6221–6230).
- Sadiki drops the lift to slam him into the ceiling (l.6240–6241).
- She offers him a job: "You seem almost supernaturally adept at isolating and exploiting the weaknesses of your opponents" (l.6254–6256). Maul: "It's still a prison." He smashes the speaker with his heel (l.6273–6277).

## Ch. 55: Inbound Flight (l.6289–6402)

  - A Trandoshan hidden in a crate opens fire with a **WESTAR-M5** and kills guard Greer and others (l.6310–6382).
  - Jabba's holo arrives on a repulsor platform pulled by two kell dragons (l.6395–6400).

## Ch. 56: 12 × 18 (l.6404–6451), heat endurance

- Sweat sizzles on the floor. "his own flesh beginning to roast." He pries the 12×18 cm hatch open with the speaker plate before he blacks out (l.6423–6443).
- "Master. I will not fail." "Boy, I'm coming for you." (l.6437, l.6451)

- Jabba speaks Huttese: "Bo shuda…" (l.6463).
- Sadiki's feat:
  - She jams a static pike into the Trandoshan's neck, grabs his WESTAR and shoots kell dragon #1 between the eyes (l.6521–6533).
  - **Dragon #2 bites off her right foot.** Jabba trains the dragons to eat slowly (l.6539–6557).
- **Reconfiguration series 121** drops dragon #2 down a shaft and folds up the hangar (l.6567–6599).

- The ~30 real *Purge* inmates have no heart bombs (l.6622–6632). The load-lifter pulls a black box from Jabba's weapons crate (l.6646–6651).

## Ch. 58: The Dark Backward (l.6656–6764)
- Maul is 100 m up the lift shaft during a catastrophic reconfiguration. He hangs over an abyss above the prison's central drive turbines (l.6658–6694).
- He finds the **Syrox nesting grounds** (larvae in webbing). The worm passes overhead; its hive-mind voices burst into his head (l.6697–6748).
- He crosses 15 m hand over hand on the rungs (l.6719–6720).

## Ch. 59: Syrox (l.6766–6808), lore
- **The Wolf Worm** is from **Monsolar**, where it is worshipped as a god. Pupae live in silken "bag-tents" in the treetops and fall into rivers to infest anyone who drinks the water (l.6770–6786).
- It reached the Hive three years ago inside inmate **Waleed Nagma**, who died seconds into his first fight. The larva absorbed his consciousness (l.6787–6793).
- Its mind is "a prison within the prison" of hundreds. It thinks of itself as "we" (l.6795–6806). It has spawned "countless larvae".

## Ch. 60: Closing Time (l.6810–6920)
- **Backstory:**
  - Artagan and Eogan's mother were Bando Gora.
  - 16 years ago Artagan saved Radique from a Gora ambush, which is why the Gora hate Radique.
  - Radique later offered Artagan protection in return for his **eyes**, and Artagan refused (l.6827–6849).
- Six blind Radique men hack Artagan apart (l.6855–6878).
- **Eogan's feat:** "a blizzard of punches and kicks" that kills all **six** armed blind men barehanded (l.6883–6895).
- Artagan: "The Fifty-Two Fists… I'm so proud of you." He dies (l.6901–6907).

## Ch. 61: Pressure and Time (l.6922–7000)
- The *Purge* inmates bridge the hangar gap with a gangway (l.6933–6941).
- Maul hot-wires the CLL and gets the **package**, a "featureless black shipping crate" (l.6970–6998).

## Ch. 62: Ready Appliance (l.7002–7034)
- The GH-7 grafts an **ortho-prosthesis** onto Sadiki's ankle. 42 hostiles; **36+ guards dead** (l.7006–7028).

## Ch. 63: Sojourn (l.7036–7086), PLAGUEIS TWIST
- **Plagueis on Sojourn** with **11-4D**. He is reanimating the corpse of **Darth Venamis** (Bith) in a vat after "almost twenty uninterrupted hours" (l.7046–7051, 7079–7081).
- **Plagueis secretly supplied Radique's working geological compressor**, routed through Slipher and the load-lifter so that Maul could finish the mission. **He kept it from Sidious** (l.7039, 7065–7086).
- Plagueis finds Sidious's "increasing tendency toward self-reliance to be disturbing" as Palpatine's chancellorship approaches (l.7081–7083).
- **Slipher was Plagueis's agent** (Plagueis: "A humble end for such a promising mind", l.7061).

## Ch. 64: The Tombs (l.7088–7184)
- Jabba's yacht detaches from the *Purge*. Styrene is found with his throat slashed (l.7114–7116).
- **The Bando Gora arrive.** Their skull masks have glowing blue eyes. **Komari Vosa** has white hair and "chemical-yellow eyes" (l.7155–7175).
- "I am Komari Vosa… and this is the Bando Gora. We've come to acquire a weapon." (l.7183–7184)

## Ch. 65: As Below, So Above (l.7186–7220)
- Maul mimics a clawbird caw to call the flock (l.7194–7203).
- **The real Iram Radique** reveals himself in the weapons shop: "Greetings, Jagannath. I'm Iram Radique." (l.7218–7220)

## Ch. 66: Hush (l.7222–7338), Sadiki dies
- Sadiki starts **Terminate** on all ~400 inmates' heart bombs, lowest numbers first, taking about 30 minutes (l.7242–7252).
- Armed with her **KYD-21** (hadrium alloy, 75 shots), she is blinded by the **Wolf Worm** bursting from the wall. The datacenter goes into silent mode, leaving her deaf and mute in effect. It eats her (l.7269–7338).

## Ch. 67: The Man Comes Around (l.7340–7447)
- **Radique:** blue-skinned near-human with black hair and **glowing red eyes**; black robes and gloves (l.7345–7350).
- The **Omega Initiative** is the heart-bomb fail-safe. Radique has "a special dispensation" (l.7381–7385).
- **Force feat: Maul re-forges the synth-crystal with the dark side** inside the compressor, "its very atoms shifting, the lattice tightening". The result is a stable, straight red blade (l.7408–7419).
- The nuke is in the warm, humming crate (l.7431–7442).

## Ch. 68: Malefactors (l.7449–7542), Vosa vs. Jabba's men
- Vosa wields **twin curve-hilted lightsabers**, which she describes as "Form One style of Soresu" (sic; Soresu is Form III). She "moved faster than they could shoot, faster even than the naked eye could see" (l.7463–7471).
- She **bisects a Trandoshan at the waist**. Gora staffs fire green fireballs, and the Gora kill Jabba's entourage (l.7472–7480).
- Jabba: "You fight well for a Jedi whore… what of your beloved Master Dooku?… his former Padawan" (l.7484–7490). **Vosa was Dooku's Padawan.**
- Jabba offers an alliance against **Gardulla Besadii**; Vosa refuses (l.7500–7504).

## Ch. 69: Low Numbers (l.7544–7588)
- Inmates are dying in waves from the Omega charges (l.7549–7553). Maul senses Vosa (l.7576–7581).

## Ch. 70: The Killing Moon (l.7590–7715), Vosa vs. the Syrox
- Vosa remembers **Bogden** ("the burial moon"; Ch. 51 says Kohlma, a continuity quirk) (l.7598–7599). She issues silent telekinetic or telepathic commands to her Gora (l.7617).
- **Syrox stats:** "five meters high and perhaps three times as long" (about 15 m). The Gora's energy balls do nothing (l.7624–7633).
- Vosa's **Jar'Kai** "rising-whirlwind" disembowels it. Its wounds **crust over and heal**. It crashes through the ceiling and **swallows all her Gora** (l.7637–7677).
- Worm: "We are you." Vosa **Force-pushes herself 20 m back and flees** (l.7694–7702).

## Ch. 71: The Sum of Its Parts (l.7717–7839), Radique dies
- The GH-7 has been destroyed. Eogan collapses and is presumed dead from his charge (l.7733–7792).
- **Feat:** Maul kicks Radique into a wall, catches the lightsaber in midair and cuts down a dozen clawbirds in seconds (l.7772–7780).
- Radique shoots Maul in the **right shoulder** with a sleeve pocket blaster (l.7801–7804).
- **Eogan was faking.** His charge had been deactivated earlier. He twists Radique's blaster, breaking both wrists, and **shoots Radique in the head** (l.7811–7831).
- **Eogan kills Radique, not Maul.**

## Ch. 72: Stick It Out (l.7841–7926), MAUL VS. KOMARI VOSA
- "You wear your heritage on your hips." / "Not my heritage. My livelihood." (l.7846–7847)
- Vosa Force-pushes Eogan's blaster away. She is evasive: "Your right arm is slowing you down." (l.7857–7870)
- Maul: "Jar'Kai… Predictable." (l.7880)
- **Maul switches to Juyo, "the Way of the Vornskr — the last of the Seven Forms"**, gripping two-handed despite the bad shoulder (l.7885–7888). Vosa shows fear (l.7889).
- Vosa Force-throws a shelf of surgical tools (l.7890–7893). Maul's blade is "moving almost too fast to see" (l.7901–7903).
- **Maul wins the exchange**, with Vosa down and his blade raised: "Now. Plead for your life." She head-butts his wound (l.7905–7913).
- The Syrox bursts through the floor and grabs his foot (l.7914–7919).
- **Debate note:** Maul has a bad right shoulder, a makeshift synth-saber and a collapsing station, and still beats her in the duel. The fight ends because of the worm.

## Ch. 73: The Wreckers (l.7928–7987), SYROX KILL
- Vosa lands on the worm's head and stabs downward. It pins her and starts to eat her (l.7930–7941).
- Maul's realization: "She was no Jedi. She was no Sith." (l.7946–7947)
- **Kill:** he thrusts the saber into its maw and spins it 360°, carving out its teeth and mandibles from the inside until "the mouthparts themselves" are ribbons. **The Syrox dies** (l.7950–7959). Vosa: "You killed it." (l.7964)
- Kill count: Maul's direct kills reach **7** with the Syrox, and **8** after the Trandoshan in Ch. 74. Slipher is indirect. The clawbirds are not counted.

## Ch. 74: Diastole (l.7989–8076)
- **Force feat (Vosa):** she telekinetically removes the electrostatic charges from both of Maul's hearts in "less than a second when all four chambers are at rest" (l.7993–8010).
- The station is tearing itself apart (l.8030–8046).
- **Feat:** Maul does a "weightless **Ataru**-style spin", dodging a blaster volley, and **decapitates Jabba's Trandoshan** (l.8065–8070).
- They board Jabba's *Star Jewel* with the crate (l.8075–8076).

## Ch. 75: 500 Republica (l.8078–8126)
- Plagueis visits Palpatine at **500 Republica**. Palpatine wears a Dramasian shimmersilk robe (l.8080–8081).
- "There is no Cog Hive Seven any longer… a loose metallic debris field." "According to our initial bioscans, there were no survivors" (l.8096–8105).
- Sidious privately: the Gora "had been there and taken possession of the nuclear device whose procurement had been his ultimate goal" (l.8108–8111).
- Plagueis: "we stand to inherit untold power and glory… Together." (l.8113–8115)

## Ch. 76: Afterimage (l.8128–8193)
- The crate holds "**weaponized uranium**". They travel on the *Star Jewel* from the **Tharin sector through the Sisar Run** to Hutt space (l.8130–8142). The charges were "deactivated and dislodged" (l.8134).
- Maul reasons that Sidious meant him to unleash the dark side at the end, as another test (l.8145–8148).
- Vosa: "Perhaps you should take him with you. As an apprentice." "We're neither of us the people that we once were." (l.8168–8180) The book ends on an unspoken connection.
