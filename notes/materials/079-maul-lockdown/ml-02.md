# Maul: Lockdown: notes part 2, Ch. 12–21 (l.1673–2998)

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. Citation "ML ch. N (l.x)".

## Ch. 12: Face Time (l.1673–1824)
- **Zero** is fed by the kitchen droid on traladon steak with Ramorean caponata, while everyone else gets protein gel (l.1710–1716). He enters and leaves unseen (l.1675–1678, 1824).
- Inmates Miggs and Halleck retell the wampa fight from the holo. Rumor of a "Wolf Worm" deep in the prison (l.1751–1754).
- **Vas Nailhead** is a flesh-eater who devours part of everything he kills. He has a cult following and gets fan mail and proposals; family on Tepasi (l.1768–1772).
- Zero defuses him by naming his sister Delia and the song "Sweet Fronda Fane" (l.1796–1820).

## Ch. 13: Cautionary Tale (l.1826–1936)
- **Healing:** within 12 hours of the wampa fight Maul has "recovered almost completely". His strength "seemed to have intensified in response to the attack" (l.1834–1838).
- Zero's cell is secretly luxurious, with a library, food stores and electronics caches (l.1852–1856).
- **Mission details (Sidious):** "arrange for the purchase of the nuclear device from Radique and its delivery into the hands of the Bando Gora" (l.1866–1870).
- Zero laughs and says Radique "does not exist… a cautionary tale, a myth" (l.1903–1908). Two years earlier another inmate came claiming he had saved Radique's life (l.1891–1899); this foreshadows Artagan.
- Maul asks for a subspace image transmitter. In return Zero wants the gang problem solved, and Maul promises "You'll have no more trouble with gangs." (l.1922–1936)

## Ch. 14: Jar of Flies (l.1938–2150)
- Strabo has been inside 18 months. He took over the Gravity Massive by strangling the former leader (l.1941–1948).
- His second, **Izhsmash** (a Nelvaanian data pirate), gets a fake message "from CO Halcon", the guard captain. Both gangs (13 Massive, 16 Kings) are summoned to the laundry (l.1956–1969, 1999–2000).
- **Maul's trap:** the cryogen vats (liquid nitrogen/helium) vent and the room fills with oxygen-displacing vapor. Both gangs collapse in under 30 seconds, and Strabo and Nailhead claw at the door to save themselves (l.2014–2046).
- Maul, wearing a jury-rigged respirator, climbs the tank, shuts the valves and starts the fans, then blames Halcon (l.2049–2079). The leaders' cowardice turns their own men on them (l.2081–2097).
- **Lore (Radique's system):**
  - Weapon components are smuggled in on supply ships. Each inmate hides one piece, "Nobody knows how they fit together", and the pieces "just disappear" (l.2118–2124).
  - Payment is by **khipus** coded to hidden accounts (l.2126–2127).
  - Just saying Radique's name made Strabo an outcast (l.2101–2112).
- **Feats:**
  - Maul hooks his fingers into Nailhead's nostrils, wrenches his head back, and punches his windpipe.
  - In the same motion, a straight-up kick to Strabo's solar plexus (l.2134–2139). Both gang leaders are down in seconds.
- Maul declares himself leader of both gangs and orders them to leave Zero alone (l.2143–2147).

## Ch. 15: LiMerge (l.2152–2304), Sidious and Plagueis
- The transmitter Zero built is waiting in a morgue chamber; its resolution is impressively high (l.2157–2169). **Sidious appears by holo from the top of LiMerge Tower** (l.2164–2167).
- Sidious, with sarcasm, says the wampa "Proved to be something of a challenge". Maul: "My strength comes from within." Sidious: "Does it indeed? I wonder." (l.2178–2182)
- "Pride and self-regard are not accolades to be earned only once. The power of the Sith must prove itself stronger with every test." (l.2184–2187)
- "Protecting your identity… will be meaningless if you die before you achieve it." (l.2189–2190)
- Goal: "destabilize the Outer Rim, fueling insurgency and separatism" (l.2204–2205).
- **Continuity:** Maul resented being left out of the **Eriadu operation**, where Sidious used security droids to assassinate six Directorate members at the Eriadu Trade Summit and attempted to kill Valorum (l.2210–2217). This ties to *Cloak of Deception*.
- Sidious POV: Maul's heart is "a reactor of pure, distilled rage"; "Cog Hive Seven was an environment that no one survived, yet Maul had already established himself as a dominant presence without relying on the Force." Sidious privately feels "increasing respect" (l.2232–2242).
- **Plagueis (as Hego Damask)** waits inside Sidious's air-taxi. He has **disabled the surveillance** so they can speak "As equals" (l.2247–2253) and wears a transpirator mask (l.2257).
  - Sidious lies to him: "I sent Maul to assassinate him [Radique]" (l.2276). Plagueis knows only the broad outlines of Sidious's Outer Rim work and the "Galactic Civil War" plan (l.2266–2270).
  - Plagueis mentions his "private pursuits on Sojourn" (l.2283). **He arranged for Vesto Slipher's IBC audit, and Slipher reports directly to him** (l.2295–2298).
  - Sidious feels warmth climbing his neck and a "knot tightening in his throat" (l.2274–2275, 2299). Plagueis has him rattled.

## Ch. 16: Nightside (l.2306–2483)
- Nightside is Maintenance Sub-level 3, an unfinished garbage wing. Rumor of a Baragwin shield disruptor once found in the scrap (l.2311–2318).
- **Eogan's training:** he chases rats across girders with his hands tied behind his back (l.2327–2329). He bench-presses and holds a 120 kg debris cube (l.2344–2367).
- **Fifty-Two Fists:** "a blizzard of lightning-fast attacks over a period of less than five seconds… Done properly, it could kill a man three times Eogan's size" (l.2378–2383). Rule: "for every tear, a drop of blood" (l.2393).
- Artagan held the **Blasko Title for three straight seasons** (l.2412).
- Escape plan: Voystock cuts power to the medbay, which forces a 15-minute "black start" during which surveillance is rerouted through the GH-7 surgical droid. The droid is to deactivate their charges (l.2451–2463).

## Ch. 17: Ghost Voltage (l.2485–2557)
- After contact with his Master, Maul feels heightened sensitivity he calls "ghost voltage" (l.2492–2496).
- Izhsmash wrote the cryptoviral code for the attack on the Phage Network. Maul asks him to slice the inmate database (l.2515–2520).
- **Backstory:** **Trezza** taught Maul computer-saboteur skills on Orsis (l.2522–2524).
- A year ago, two cons tried a medbay escape and died there. The killer wasn't the guards but **Radique**, who "never lets anyone leave" anyone who worked for him (l.2537–2550).

## Ch. 18: Black Start (l.2559–2712)
- Chlorus: the **Desilijic Clan (Hutts)** resents the Hive cutting into their revenues and believes Radique is inside. "They've apparently decided to see for themselves." (l.2569–2585)
- Sadiki admits she cut corners: she skipped redundant power, left sublevels unfinished, and has substandard wiring and surveillance, while over-relying on the heart charges. "there had been other compensations. Some of them very lucrative indeed." (l.2641–2663)
- Through the GH-7 feed she sees Voystock helping the Truaxes. The medbay is sealed from inside. Dakarai is missing from the datacenter (l.2697–2709).

## Ch. 19: In My Time of Dying (l.2714–2827)
- Voystock betrays them: "You really think Radique would let you out of here that easily?" (l.2759–2760). He elbows Eogan unconscious.
- Artagan rips the dropbox off Voystock's belt. Voystock shoots Artagan's right leg off below the knee: "Radique's only instruction was not to let you escape… He didn't say anything about not killing you." (l.2775–2796)
- Artagan's dying-bed speech: "I am Artagan Truax… I have killed men in eleven systems… I will not beg for mercy." (l.2809–2818)
- **Kill:** Maul twists **Voystock's head 180 degrees** with one grip on jaw and skull base (l.2820–2824).

## Ch. 20: Open Secrets (l.2829–2883)
- Artagan saved Radique's life "Twenty years ago… On Lakteen Depot. Near the Giju Run." (l.2853). "Eogan knows. Everything." (l.2865)
- Guards storm in and arrest Maul (l.2869–2883).

## Ch. 21: Spindle (l.2885–2997)
- "Touch me with that toy of yours again and you'll find out." (Maul to Smight, l.2892–2894)
- Sadiki says Voystock "will not be missed" (l.2918).
- **Lore/feat:** Maul's EEG from intake shows an **"omicron spindle"**, a cortex pattern the droid has "only seen in certain very proficient telepaths" (l.2936–2938). Sadiki suspects he isn't there to fight.
- "A precision instrument of savagery… You are the one we've been waiting for." (l.2962–2963)
- "You weren't supposed to survive your match with the wampa" (l.2973). Sadiki suspects the Hutts sent him (l.2981).
- Maul spots a hidden **microphone bug** under her desk by tossing her tablet there and reading the reflection (l.2948–2966, 2991–2996). She didn't plant it.

**Running tally after Ch. 21:** 3 kills by Maul (the unidentified champion, the wampa, CO Voystock). The serpent-staff isn't counted. Non-lethal: the Rodian (paralyzed), the 11-AZR guard (broken wrist), Nailhead and Strabo (beaten).
