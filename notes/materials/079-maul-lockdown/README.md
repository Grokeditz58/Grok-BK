Character quick bios:
- **Darth Maul:** undercover as Inmate 11240 "Jagannath" (Zabrak, 1.75 m, 80 kg). He is forbidden the Force and his saberstaff.
  - 8 direct kills: the champion, a wampa, CO Voystock, a varactyl, Rook, a Weequay proxy, the Syrox, and a Trandoshan.
  - He fights Vosa in Juyo, forges a synth-crystal with the dark side, and has been trained on Orsis (where Trezza taught him slicing) and at LiMerge.
- **Darth Sidious / Palpatine:**
  - Runs the mission from a LiMerge Tower holo. He Force-chokes Maul through the holo (ch. 31).
  - Hides his anti-Plagueis purpose, and privately respects Maul "without relying on the Force".
- **Darth Plagueis / Hego Damask:**
  - Muun with a transpirator mask, living on Sojourn with 11-4D and a Kaldani Spires penthouse. He is reanimating Darth Venamis.
  - Ran Slipher as his agent ("Magister"), secretly supplied Radique's compressor, ordered the Hive destroyed, and visits 500 Republica at the end.
- **Komari Vosa:**
  - Bando Gora high priestess, a former Jedi and Dooku's former Padawan. White hair and chemical-yellow eyes.
  - Fights Jar'Kai with twin curved sabers. Fled the Syrox, lost to Maul, then removed his heart bombs with the Force.
- **Iram Radique:**
  - Phantom arms dealer: a blue near-human with glowing red eyes. He uses Weequay proxies, blinded workers and clawbird couriers.
  - Made unstable synth-sabers, and was building a nuke for the Gora. Killed by Eogan.
- **Jabba Desilijic Tiure:**
  - About 600 years old, appears only by holo. Yacht *Star Jewel* (Minstrel-class) with Z-95s and kell dragons.
  - Planted guards and fake prisoners, and offered Vosa an alliance against Gardulla.
- **Sadiki Blirr:**
  - The warden. She runs the gambling empire with the admin droid ThreeDee and the Omega heart-bomb fail-safe.
  - Blackmailed Commissioner Chlorus. Lost a foot to a kell dragon and was eaten by the Syrox.
- **Dakarai Blirr ("Zero"):** operations officer who posed as a Twi'lek gang leader. Killed by Coyle.
- **Eogan and Artagan Truax:**
  - Artagan, ex-Bando Gora, taught his son the Fifty-Two Fists. He is hacked down by Radique's blind men.
  - Eogan kills six of them barehanded, then kills Radique.
- **Coyle:** Chadra-Fan informant who betrays Maul and kills Zero.
- **Vesto Slipher:** IBC field analyst with the "blue witch" cloaking device; Plagueis's agent. Beheaded by the load-lifter.
- **Gang bosses:**
  - Vas Nailhead (Bone Kings), a flesh-eater.
  - Strabo (Noghri, Gravity Massive).
  - Both are eaten by the Syrox.
- **The Syrox ("Wolf Worm"):**
  - Hive-mind parasite from Monsolar, about 5 m by 15 m, that regenerates wounds. It arrived 3 years ago inside the inmate Waleed Nagma.

Continuity quirks noted in the text:
- Vosa's torture moon is called Kohlma by Slipher (ch. 51) but Bogden in Vosa's own memory (ch. 70, l.7598–7599).
- Vosa's style is labelled the "Form One style of Soresu" (ch. 68). Soresu is Form III.
- The *Purge* inmate count is given as 46 (ch. 52) and later as 39. Sadiki expected 32.
- The name of the guard who forged background checks is spelled both Logovik and Lodovik.
- The prison barge *Purge* shares its name with the barge in Schreiber's *Death Troopers*, which is excerpted in the back matter.
- The timeline files the book at 33 BBY after *Shadow Hunter*, even though Maul dies in TPM.
