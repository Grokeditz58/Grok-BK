# Maul: Lockdown: notes part 4, Ch. 34–50 (l.4210–5781)

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. Citation "ML ch. N (l.x)".

## Ch. 34: Hooligans (l.4211–4270)
- Nailhead's appetite is fading; he dreams of "the worm" (l.4219–4227).
- Someone (Sadiki) reconfigures the cells to open a back passage. Strabo and Nailhead lead a joint Kings/Massive band into the walls, armed with sharpened bones (l.4237–4270).

## Ch. 35: Ready Room (l.4272–4341), Jabba appears
- Smight is on glitterstim, which gives "a telepathic boost" (l.4287–4293). He sells spice to other COs (l.4294–4296).
- **Jabba Desilijic Tiure** appears by holo, eight metres of him filling a corner. "if you ever get tired of running that prison, I would be happy to make you one of my slave girls." (l.4328–4336)
- **Reveal:** Smight is one of Jabba's men (l.4329 "his employer").

## Ch. 36: What the Fever Said (l.4343–4412)
- Bando Gora chant: "All hail the skull beneath the hood! Flay the skin and drink the blood!" (l.4380). Artagan: "Iram Radique will never do business with the Gora. Never." (l.4386)
- Maul to Eogan: "Everything here… is a test… you don't have his heart… you'll never be half the man that he is." (l.4394–4398)
- Artagan: Zero "answers to another name" (l.4408–4412).

## Ch. 37: Bestiary (l.4414–4557), massacre of the guards
- Jabba: "I recognize none of these swine… Kill them all and toss their carcasses to swamp slugs." (l.4432–4439)
- Sadiki releases Nailhead, Strabo and the gangs into the ready room: "the only dropbox in the room is strapped to my hip." (l.4447–4480)
- **Logovik** names the Jabba plants: Crete, Galway, Tyson, Olyphant, McCane, Webberly and the rookie (Smight). Logovik himself is one; he forged the background checks (l.4460–4470).
- The guards are slaughtered. Hootkins is impaled on wrist-lashed ribs. McCane's chest is scooped out with a skull. Nailhead and his lieutenant **Massif** rip Olyphant in two. Nailhead caves Logovik's skull with a femur and bites his face (l.4506–4539).
- Smight escapes through the open panel (l.4555–4557).

## Ch. 38: Antidote (l.4559–4637)
- **Speed feat:** "Maul's hand moved faster than the eye could see", grabbing Zero's fork and pinning Zero's sleeve to the table (l.4577–4579).
- Rook was "A decoy". Rook's circle was a **zero** (l.4598–4604).
- Maul claims he dosed the food vat with white metaxas root and offers an antidote. Zero collapses (l.4610–4634).
- Zero: Radique "made a blood oath never to do business with" the Gora (l.4628–4629).
- Maul's offer: "three hundred thousand credits for a proscribed nuclear device" (l.4621–4623).

## Ch. 39: Crawl (l.4639–4730), the Syrox
- **The Syrox is real and sentient** (Smight POV). It speaks telepathically: "a braided skein of a thousand different tongues", the minds of every inmate it devoured, "somehow still living inside it" (l.4677–4693).
- It fills the whole passage: pale, blind-eyed, mouthparts peeling "like the petals of some hideous albino flower", "a dozen rings of teeth", the stink of a mass grave (l.4694–4698).
- Smight runs into the blood-drenched Nailhead and Strabo; then the worm arrives (l.4712–4730).

## Ch. 40: Nobody's Hero (l.4732–4844)
- In the morgue, Maul threatens to kill Eogan unless Artagan types in the **Bando Gora hailing frequency**, and Artagan does (l.4762–4769).
- Maul tells Sidious, "Iram Radique… I found him." Sidious is impatient and leaves the Gora brokering to Maul (l.4780–4797).
- Coyle delivers the case of **300,000 counterfeit credits** (l.4806). Maul drops a Bone King with one fist to the solar plexus without breaking stride (l.4813–4815).
- He spots the Weequay with the clawbird, which eats bantha suet (l.4822–4827). A guard searches the case and finds only bones; Maul had already moved the money into his uniform (l.4841–4844, cf. 4922–4923).

## Ch. 41: The Main Monkey Business (l.4846–4886)
- Sadiki feels "Invulnerable". **Dakarai remotely locked in the next match** and nobody can override it (l.4869–4886).

## Ch. 42: Bleed the Freak, Maul vs. "Radique" the Weequay (l.4888–4997)
- **Opponent: the Weequay** ("Radique") with his clawbird, which has khipus on its feet (l.4907–4912). Maul offers him the 300,000 credits (l.4922–4924).
- **The fight:**
  - The bird claws Maul's face and pecks at his eyes, blinding him with blood (l.4927–4934).
  - The Weequay's pike or dagger strikes his right flank and **numbs his right arm** (l.4939–4951).
  - Left-handed, Maul catches the bird, bites one wing to spread it, uses it as a weapon to rake the Weequay's eyes, and breaks its back (l.4949–4962).
  - "a quick, brutal series of blows to neck and face… almost too fast to register" puts Maul on his knees and then his stomach (l.4967–4974).
- **Backstory:** "his earliest training, the endless, hostile torture that he'd endured on **Mygeeto**" (l.4976–4978). "Never. Give. Up." (l.4980–4986)
- **Kill:** Maul grabs the Weequay's seclusion braids and **drives his skull horns into his throat**, bursting the windpipe (l.4987–4992). "Master, I had no choice." (l.4996)

## Ch. 43: Kaldani Spires (l.4999–5096), Plagueis and Sidious
- **Plagueis's penthouse is atop Kaldani Spires**, looking over Monument Square (l.5003–5005). Sidious had been "monitoring each of his apprentice's fights carefully" (l.5013–5015).
- Plagueis: "His mission was to assassinate Radique without relying on the Force… Ready to be extracted?" (l.5018–5022)
- Sidious hides the true purpose, which is to buy the nuke for the **Bando Gora to "ultimately use against Plagueis"** (l.5059–5062). **This is the book's key Legends plot.**
- Plagueis: "Beings such as ourselves… live with the paradoxical risk of forgetting that there are some elements of the galaxy that we cannot control." (l.5044–5046). "Unless there is something else that you had intended him for" (l.5054). "extremely humbling for its original architects" (l.5073–5074).
- **Plagueis orders: "Contact the Zabrak. Inform him that he is to destroy Cog Hive Seven immediately… you may allow him to think that he will have the opportunity to escape."** (l.5079–5081)
- "There is no one else with whom I know I can be completely and totally candid." (l.5087–5089). The word "Humbling" twists in Sidious "like poison" (l.5092–5093).

## Ch. 44: Shop (l.5098–5244), Radique's arsenal
- Smight saw the worm swallow **Strabo, Nailhead and the others** whole with Y-shaped mouthparts (l.5107–5114). Strabo and Nailhead are dead.
- Radique's lab and armory (l.5141–5213):
  - **J8Q-128 Finbat** shoulder-fired concussion missile: "would annihilate anything in a five-hundred-meter radius" (Smight's belief, l.5184–5193).
  - Proton missile launcher, pulse cannon, Mandalorian assault rifles, flechette launchers, an **LS-150 heavy accelerated charged particle repeater**, breaching grenades, anti-starfighter cluster bombs, freshly made thermal detonators.
  - All of it is assembled and modified by Radique, beyond factory spec.
- **The locked crate** is smaller but heavier, its shell "far more formidable than durasteel", hot and humming. This is the nuke (l.5214–5222).
- Smight's first name is **Augustine** (l.5230). He arms himself with a DT-12 heavy blaster pistol and flechette gauntlets, then is knocked out by an unseen figure (l.5234–5244).

## Ch. 45: Red Saber Dreams (l.5246–5320)
- In a sedated dream in medbay, a black box holds his **saber staff**. A doppelgänger Maul says "You're lost without it!" (l.5259–5285)
- He bisects the double at the waist, then finds himself as the fallen one, looking up at a **Muun: "Plagueis."** Maul doesn't know the name (l.5306–5317). This foreshadows Plagueis, whom Maul has never met.

## Ch. 46: Hangar (l.5322–5397)
- Guard Dawson "Won three hundred credits on that red-skinned rimmer" in the Weequay fight (l.5346–5347).
- **Tech:** CLL-8 binary load-lifters, 3 m tall. Slipher overrides one with "IBC yellow card security variant 377055" to receive a secret package (l.5360–5396).

## Ch. 47: Airborne (l.5399–5453)
- The clawbirds, plural, adopt Maul as their new master: "He was their new master" (l.5420–5434). He follows the flock down to a door: "Welcome to the inner circle." (l.5453)

## Ch. 48: Watch the Throne (l.5455–5557), Jabba arrives
- **Ships:** a customized **Ubrikkian Minstrel-class space yacht, *Star Jewel***, escorted by six **Z-95 Headhunters**, with an optimized **CL-14 hyperdrive motivator** (Hutt space via the Triellus Trade Route). It has a transparisteel dome throne room with chained **kell dragons**, plus turbolasers (l.5457–5469, 5480–5482).
- Jabba is "almost six hundred years" a crime lord. He moved his base to the B'omarr Monastery on Tatooine "just a few short centuries" ago (l.5471–5473, 5516). His pilot and second-in-command is **Scuppa** (l.5480).
- Jabba's bodyguards fight to the death for show: a Gamorrean war criminal against a **dwarf Oskan blood eater** (l.5505–5509).
- Jabba resolves to **destroy Cog Hive Seven** after the guard massacre (l.5528–5535). He ambushes the prison barge **Purge** (l.5544–5550).

## Ch. 49: Cabal (l.5559–5736)
- The weapons shop is surgically clean. **Its workers are inmates whose eyes Radique removed**, "a prerequisite to working down here" (l.5586–5592).
- Slipher is there. Bound Smight is there.
- **"The Weequay that you killed… was a proxy. Radique has a dozen of them in circulation at any time"** (l.5611–5613).
- **Synthetic lightsabers:** red synth-crystals "baked in a geological compressor" (l.5619–5625).
- Coyle and Zero are here. Zero survived the poison; Coyle had warned him (l.5630–5638). This is "Radique's inner circle… The backbone cabal" (l.5640–5641).
- **Synth-saber demo:** Maul offers his own throat. The blade is thin and crackling, "hundreds of semitransparent serpents". It **loops back and bisects Smight's face**, severs his arm, and chops his body to pieces (l.5657–5694).
- Cover story: "When I was a mercenary, I assassinated a Jedi once… I was able to reverse-engineer the components" (l.5705–5707).
- **Radique's voice** is on the intercom. Every blind worker turns its face upward (l.5711–5717).
- Maul strips the hilt in under ten seconds and crushes the crystal: "your geological compressor isn't generating sufficient heat and pressure… All these lightsabers are going to do is kill their users." (l.5719–5726)
- Radique: "Kill him." (l.5735)

## Ch. 50: Behind the Mask (l.5738–5781)
- Maul throws the hilt parts at Zero and tackles him. A punch **knocks off a Twi'lek mask**: **Zero is Dakarai Blirr**, the warden's brother (l.5752–5760).
- Dakarai used the disguise to program the algorithm while serving both Radique and his sister (l.5768–5770). Radique: "Finish him, Coyle." **Coyle shoots Dakarai dead** (l.5771–5776).
- "Mr. Slipher… Take the Zabrak with you." (l.5777–5778)

**Running tally after Ch. 50: 6 kills by Maul** (the champion, the wampa, Voystock, the varactyl, Rook, the Weequay proxy). Kills by others in this stretch: about 9 guards (gangs), Strabo, Nailhead and others (Syrox), Smight (synth-saber), Dakarai (Coyle), Izhsmash (Radique's people).
