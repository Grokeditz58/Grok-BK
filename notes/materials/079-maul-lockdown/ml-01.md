# Maul: Lockdown (Joe Schreiber, Del Rey 2014): notes part 1, front matter and Ch. 1–11

- Read 2026-11-03 CST from the owner's epub (Del Rey ebook v3.1_r1; ISBN 978-0-345-50903-1, eBook ISBN 978-0-345-53566-5; © 2014 Lucasfilm). This part covers FULL.txt l.1–1672.
- Citation: "ML ch. N (l.x)". The edition has 76 numbered chapters. Chapter ranges are line ranges in FULL.txt.
- Big picture: before *The Phantom Menace*. Sidious sends Maul undercover into Cog Hive Seven, a space-station prison where inmates fight to the death for galactic gamblers. His mission is to find the arms dealer Iram Radique and meet him face to face. **He is forbidden to use the Force or reveal himself as a Sith** (l.1567–1569).

## Ch. 1: Cog Hive Seven, first fight (l.136–293)
- **Opponent 1:** an unidentified 2.5 m creature in bone/carapace armor. It has mismatched arms (a mallet fist and a spider-claw) and fights with a **living serpent-staff**. Its first punch knocks out one of Maul's teeth, and it puts the tooth in its own mouth (l.144–158).
- Maul closes half a metre in under a second (l.164). He grabs the creature by the throat, lifts it overhead and crushes its neck tendons until black blood spurts (l.171–183). Then he spins it and slams it to the floor hard enough that the plates reverberate (l.185).
- "he hadn't needed his lightsaber staff or the Force to dispatch this waste of flesh—not that either was really an option" (l.188–189).
- It surprises him twice because **it is "somehow cut off from the Force"**, so Maul's sensory intuition doesn't register it (l.203–206). The serpent's venom blinds him (l.201, 207–209). The staff coils around his neck like a noose (l.216–219).
- **How he wins:** blind, he goes rigid and waits for the snake to overextend, then tears its head off by hand (l.224–234). He takes his tooth back out of the creature's mouth, crams the snake's head in, and staples the lips shut with three of the creature's own piercings bent into hooks; the venomous head bites inside (l.242–251). He finishes it by **driving his horns into its eyes** (l.253–255).
- His vision starts recovering within minutes and he expects a full recovery within hours (l.258–262).
- **Tech:** the cell reconfigures into a funnel and drains the corpse through a hole in the floor (l.270–288). Something "pale and eyeless" may reach up for the bodies (l.287). Then the cell carries him upward (l.293).
- Sidious's voice in his head: "You must end this now." (l.211–215).
- **Kill count:** 1 (the unidentified champion), plus the serpent-staff.

## Ch. 2: Sadiki (l.295–491)
- Warden Sadiki Blirr has an admin droid, 3D-4 ("ThreeDee"). She watches every fight at least twice (l.327).
- The former champion had been on Cog Hive Seven for 6 months. It killed two convicts in transit and then everything it was matched against (l.337–341). It had won six straight (l.426). Maul killed it in under five minutes (Coyle, l.737–738).
- **Maul's cover file (l.361–371):** Inmate 11240, "Jagannath", Zabrak male, 1.75 m, 80 kg, yellow eyes, red skin, mercenary, charged with murder. He was picked up on a sweep of the mining colonies on Subterrel (l.376). Sadiki describes "a crown of ten vestigial horns" (l.343).
- Commissioner Dragomir Chlorus (60+ and looks 20 years younger) warns her that the Hive beats its own odds suspiciously often, and that someone worse than the Gaming Commission or Black Sun is watching (l.441–468). He and Sadiki hint at a past relationship (l.404).
- **Vesto Slipher** (a Muun from the IBC, in Palo fiduciary garb) arrives: "My dear, I am your schedule." (l.491)

## Ch. 3: Hot Mess (l.493–679)
- Maul arrived less than 24 h earlier on a prison barge with 37 other inmates (l.517–525).
- **Feat:** a Rodian with a sharpened whip-band bumps into him. Maul's single elbow strike, delivered without even getting up, **shatters the Rodian's lower spine** and leaves him paralyzed (l.535–541). It was the only time he moved on the whole trip.
- CO Smight (nervous) and CO Voystock (grizzled) process him.
- **Prison tech, the "dropbox":** every inmate gets a subatomic electrostatic detonator implanted in the heart wall; Maul has one in **both his hearts** (l.585–586). A guard types the prison number into a belt "dropbox" and the inmate dies (l.587–588).
- Clarion rules: inmates have 5 minutes to return to their cells for "matching" (l.553–558, 578). Cells are about 3 m domes that turn into spheres and rotate during reconfiguration (l.571, 607–617).
- **Mission (Sidious on Coruscant):** "You are to locate Iram Radique… It will not be as easy as it sounds." (l.640–642). Radique is a reclusive, legendary arms dealer, supposedly an inmate, who works through a shifting "palimpsest" of middlemen (l.643–650). "You will not leave Cog Hive Seven… until you have identified Radique and met with him face-to-face" (l.651).

## Ch. 4: Coyle (l.681–789)
- **Improvised bomb:** Maul seals ammonia cleaner and silica bicarbonate in a pressure cooker and sends it into the 400-degree dishwasher (l.691–695). It goes off as a diversion so he can watch who reacts (l.702–713).
- "Trouble. You want some?" (Maul, l.697).
- **Coyle** is a Chadra-Fan about 3 ft tall who collects bones. He talks in rhetorical questions ("brother") and is the only inmate who didn't flinch at the blast (l.710–729).
- Gangs:
  - **Bone Kings**: about two dozen shaved-head humans with bone piercings, led by Vas Nailhead.
  - **Gravity Massive**: mixed species with the right sleeves cut off and spiral tattoos, led by the Noghri Strabo with a Nelvaanian sidekick (l.747–766).
- **Zero** is a Twi'lek, "Inmate Zero", who has been there from the beginning and deals in the "three I's": Items, Influence, Information (l.768–777). Coyle points Maul to Ventilation Conduit 11-AZR (l.784–787).

## Ch. 5: Knockout Mouse (l.791–988)
- **Artagan Truax** is 55, a pit fighter for three decades, and in for almost a year with his 16-year-old son **Eogan**. He came "by deal with the shadowy hand of the galaxy" (l.835–839).
- **His record here is 4–0 in five matches (the fifth nearly killed him):**
  - The first three were quick kills.
  - A Kaleesh with tusks sharpened to surgical points punctured his lung; he snapped off a tusk and used it.
  - A Klatooinian nearly KO'd him; he took its battle-axe and buried it in its skull (l.840–857).
  - He now has chronic migraines, a tremor and a seizure. He is a "knockout mouse" (l.859–876).
- "Jagannath" is said to mean "The Tooth" (l.805–807).
- **Khipu, a "talking knot":** there are "over six billion different knots in the galaxy". A droid can decode a knotted string into a 21-digit number in 30 seconds. Artagan's khipu holds an orbital Muunilinst account number plus password worth 30,000 credits (l.909–918).
- Voystock demonstrates the dropbox by "accidentally" killing a Vulptereen inmate, who drops instantly (l.948–960). Eogan is Inmate 11033. Artagan has been teaching him the "Fifty-Two Fists" (l.973).
- Artagan admits Eogan is "no killer". He bribes Voystock with the khipu to help them **escape** (l.977–988).

## Ch. 6: The Whole Thing Runs on Blood (l.990–1109)
- Slipher is auditing on behalf of the IBC, which finances the Hive. The Hive "never seems to have a bad fiscal quarter" (l.1010–1014).
- Three Bone Kings impale an inmate with sharpened bone spears and then **debone** him to make new weapons (l.1026–1058).
- Sadiki floats the idea of crew-on-crew tag matches and VIP packages for high rollers to watch in person (l.1037–1069).
- Rattataki saying: "Chee moto, mando sangui": "The whole thing runs on blood." (l.1074–1076). "a crucible from which true superiority emerges" (l.1073).
- Slipher: to make these profits you have to predict winners "almost one hundred percent of the time" (l.1104–1106).

## Ch. 7: Gravity Massive (l.1111–1175)
- Maul suspects Coyle's tip is a setup. He tells Strabo that the Bone Kings want a meeting at 11-AZR, then tells Nailhead ("Vasco Nailhead") that the Massive want one. "They said get ready to bleed." (l.1141–1175)
- Sidious: "You'll go in as a criminal… relying only on your own wit and cunning" (l.1128–1131).

## Ch. 8: Mute Math (l.1177–1288)
- **Tech:** the datacenter is an anechoic chamber. Foam wedges alternate 90° out of phase to simulate "an absolute free-field open space of infinite dimension" (l.1198–1207).
- **Dakarai Blirr**, Sadiki's twin brother, hasn't spoken in ten years: "Math is music" (l.1234–1256). He wrote the matching **algorithm** after upgrading the Ando Overland Podracing course for the Desilijic Clan five years earlier (l.1260–1265). It tracks weight, heart rate and alliances and generates two matches a day (l.1267–1270).

## Ch. 9: Straphanger (l.1290–1436)
- A **clawbird** (from Wayland, about half a metre tall) caws in the duct (l.1318–1336).
- Maul hides above in a maintenance hatch. A CO with a dropbox, working for Zero, thinks Maul came to kill Zero (l.1352–1373). Maul's double rumor brings both gangs crashing into the corridor and they trample the guard (l.1385–1399).
- **Feat:** Maul hangs by his legs, pulls the guard up by his helmet strap, and uses the helmet as a ram to smash the hatch open (l.1401–1403). He breaks the guard's wrist in the hatch to make him talk (l.1411–1413, 1428–1430).
- Intel: no cameras catch Zero, and "Even the guards don't see where or how he gets in and out!" Zero is in Level 8, Cell 22 (l.1418–1426).
- The CO hits the dropbox and **nothing happens** (l.1435–1436).

## Ch. 10: Skin in the Game (l.1438–1519)
- Dakarai overrode the dropbox remotely. A guard's dropbox is overridden only if the inmate is already scheduled to fight (l.1442–1443, 1467–1469).
- **Tech:** the clarion makes about 85% of the station physically reconfigure, with wings and cellblocks shifting position, shown on a live ray-tracing (l.1479–1485). Bookmakers get a 5-minute window to set odds (l.1506–1507).
- The next opponent came from an abandoned spaceport in the Anoat system along the Ison corridor and had been used in illegal fights (l.1488–1491). Slipher: poachers' mortality was worse than the creatures'; "two and a half meters… two hundred kilograms or more" (l.1497–1500).
- "We bet what the algorithm tells us… It's going to be no contest." (l.1513–1519)

## Ch. 11: From the Land of the Ice and Snow, the wampa fight (l.1521–1671)
- Maul wonders whether the dark side saved him from the detonator (l.1536–1549).
- **Backstory:** years of training "on Orsis and then on Coruscant in the LiMerge Building" (l.1542).
- Sidious's order: "You must not ever use the Force, no matter what the circumstances, or all will be lost." (l.1567–1569). Maul catches himself starting to sense with the Force and pulls back (l.1564–1566).
- **Opponent 2: a wampa**, nearly 3 m tall, with one horn snapped and a scarred chest, chained in Nylasteel (l.1581–1589).
  - Its claws rake his back down to the muscle along his spine (l.1597–1600).
  - Maul hits the base of its skull with a full-power elbow that "should have shattered its neck", and it is like hitting stone (l.1610–1615). Its claw slashes his face (l.1621).
  - **A horn head-butt pulverizes its mandible and drives bone into its skull**, which should have been a killing blow, but it keeps coming (l.1629–1636). Maul can't hold its head back by the horn (l.1639–1643).
  - It bites his shoulder and hits the nerve bundles, so his arms go slack. "For the first time the impossible occurred to him—the prospect that he might actually lose." (l.1644–1649)
  - Maul realizes something is "deeply, profoundly wrong" with it (l.1653), meaning it has been tampered with or drugged.
  - **How he wins:** he plunges both arms in up to the elbow beneath its rib cage and **crushes its heart** in his hands (l.1655–1665). Then he collapses from blood loss (l.1666–1671).
- **Kill count after Ch. 11:** 2 match kills (the creature, the wampa). The paralyzed Rodian (non-fatal) and the broken-wrist guard are not kills.
