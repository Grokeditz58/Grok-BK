# Maul: Lockdown: summary of strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. Full hand-read, l.1–9682. Citation "ML ch. N (l.x)". Detail is in ml-01 to ml-06.

1. **Maul's whole run is Force-restricted by order** (ML ch. 11, l.1567–1569)
   - "You must not ever use the Force, no matter what the circumstances." Almost every feat through Ch. 70 is purely physical. He breaks the rule only for a brief choke on Slipher (ch. 51, l.5904–5919), a failed push on the lift hatch (ch. 56, l.6409–6418), the synth-crystal forging (ch. 67) and the end fights.
2. **Physical feats with no Force use**
   - **Champion (ch. 1):** blinded by venom, he tears the head off a living serpent-staff and kills a 6–0 champion that is "cut off from the Force" (l.203–255).
   - **Wampa (ch. 11):** shrugs off claws to the spinal muscle, then crushes the drugged wampa's heart by hand (l.1597–1665).
   - **Varactyl (ch. 27):** rides a 4 m Deathspine varactyl and boils its face on a steam pipe. Its handler says his grip is "brute-force determination… never seen before in any species" (l.3525–3554).
   - **Rook (ch. 30):** tethered underwater, he dissolves Nylasteel ties and a steel floor with girder mold and blood, then snaps Rook's neck (l.3853–3930).
   - **Weequay (ch. 42):** one-armed and blinded, he kills with a horn strike to the throat (l.4987–4992).
   - **Heat (ch. 56):** survives a superheated lift where his flesh roasts, and pries open a 12×18 cm hatch (l.6423–6443).
3. **Maul vs. Komari Vosa** (ML ch. 72, l.7841–7926)
   - Shot through the right shoulder by Radique's sleeve blaster and using a makeshift synth-saber, Maul switches to **Juyo** ("the Way of the Vornskr — the last of the Seven Forms"). Vosa shows fear, Force-throws a shelf of surgical steel, and ends up under his raised blade: "Now. Plead for your life."
   - The Syrox interrupts the kill. Vosa is Dooku's former Padawan (Jabba, l.7484–7490) and dual-wields Jar'Kai, which Maul calls "Predictable" (l.7880).
4. **Maul kills the Syrox Wolf Worm** (ML ch. 73, l.7950–7959)
   - Earlier, Vosa disemboweled it with Jar'Kai, but it **healed** (wounds crusting over), swallowed her entire Gora strike team, and made her **flee by Force-pushing herself 20 m away**, her first retreat ever (ch. 70, l.7637–7702).
   - Maul kills it by thrusting the saber into its mouth and spinning it 360° until "the mouthparts themselves" are ribbons.
   - Syrox stats: about 5 m high and 15 m long, from Monsolar, a hive mind of hundreds of absorbed inmates (ch. 59, ch. 70).
5. **Maul forges a synth-crystal with the dark side** (ML ch. 67, l.7408–7419)
   - Radique's synth-sabers explode and kill their users. Smight dies this way (ch. 49), and Maul diagnoses the compressor fault after stripping a hilt in under 10 s.
   - Maul puts the crystal in a new compressor and reshapes it with the Force, "its very atoms shifting, the lattice tightening", producing a stable red blade. Radique: "Remarkable."
6. **Sidious and Plagueis**
   - **Sidious Force-chokes Maul through a holo link** (ch. 31, l.3968–3978).
   - Sidious's secret purpose is to put the Gora nuke in Vosa's hands, ultimately against Plagueis (ch. 43, l.5059–5062). The Gora get the "weaponized uranium" (ch. 76).
   - **Plagueis:**
     - ran Slipher as his agent ("Magister") and orders Cog Hive Seven destroyed (ch. 43, l.5079–5081);
     - secretly supplied Radique's working compressor (ch. 63);
     - is reanimating **Darth Venamis's** corpse on Sojourn with 11-4D (ch. 63, l.7046–7081);
     - visits 500 Republica at the end; the station is now a debris field, and bioscans say "no survivors" (ch. 75).
7. **Komari Vosa's other feats**
   - Twin curved sabers, deflecting at speeds "faster even than the naked eye could see" (labelled "Form One style of Soresu", sic). She bisects a Trandoshan (ch. 68, l.7463–7480).
   - She telekinetically removes the bombs from both of Maul's hearts, timed to the under-a-second window when all four chambers rest (ch. 74, l.7993–8010).
8. **Other fighters**
   - **Eogan Truax:** kills six armed blind men barehanded with the Fifty-Two Fists (ch. 60), then twists Radique's blaster, breaking both wrists, and **kills Iram Radique** (ch. 71, l.7811–7831).
   - **Radique:** blue near-human with red eyes. He used Weequay proxies ("a dozen… in circulation", l.5611–5613) and clawbird couriers.
   - **Sadiki:** loses a foot to a kell dragon, has it replaced with a prosthetic, triggers the Omega heart bombs on ~400 inmates, and is eaten by the Syrox (ch. 57, 66).
   - **Jabba:** about 600 years old; yacht *Star Jewel* (Minstrel-class) with 6 Z-95s and kell dragons.
9. **Kill ledger:** 8 direct Maul kills, plus Slipher indirectly (his sabotaged load-lifter beheads him) and a dozen-plus clawbirds. The full table is at the end of ml-05.
