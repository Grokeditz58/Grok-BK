# Maul: Lockdown: notes part 3, Ch. 22–33 (l.2999–4209)

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. Citation "ML ch. N (l.x)".

## Ch. 22: Blue Witch (l.2999–3106), Plagueis via Slipher
- **Tech:** Slipher carries EM emitters, ion-pulse shredders and white-noise generators. He planted the bug under Sadiki's desk (l.3001–3007).
- **The "blue witch"** is a finger-sized blue tube in beta from IBC labs on Muunilinst. "It creates a void on the most sophisticated security holo cams… follows you around the room. Works on all electronics, audio and video. Completely undetectable… She runs hot." (l.3013–3020). Damask insisted Slipher take it (l.3023–3025).
- **Hego Damask/Plagueis** appears by holo in a robe and transpirator mask from "an elaborate fortress on a jungle moon" with exotic birdcalls. That is Sojourn, matching ch. 15 (l.3032–3035). Slipher calls him "Magister" (l.3031).
- "I know who his control is," Damask snaps (l.3055).
- **Plagueis's intelligence (l.3073–3087):**
  - 18 months earlier, a **Black Sun vigo** shuttle out of Gateway delivered stolen **Tarascii explosives** from a BlasTech heavy-ordnance satellite.
  - Six months later the same shuttle brought **baradium**.
  - Less than a month ago, **weapons-grade depleted uranium** arrived.
  - Slipher: "You think someone's actually manufacturing thermal detonators here?" Damask: "Or worse."
- Damask: Radique "is real"; Maul's mission may threaten "galactic stability and my own personal safety" (l.3090–3095). "until you have satisfied your assignment, you can consider yourself a permanent resident of the prison." (l.3101–3103)

## Ch. 23: Factory Floor (l.3108–3325)
- **Conditioning:** one-arm bench dips held for a count of 50, ten reps per arm, then an L-sit until every muscle trembles (l.3114–3118).
- **Agility feat:** chasing the clawbird, Maul leaps a waist-high barrier, knocks two prisoners aside without slowing, drops ten metres down a shaft and "hit the ground running", jumps a platform gap, and goes hand over hand along cables (l.3150–3166).
- The factory floor is an unfinished level under the metal shop with poorly calibrated gravity generators (l.3175–3191). It holds Coyle's forest of **bone sculptures**, including a new one at least 8 m tall with skull face and wings that forms a code-like pattern (l.3193–3242).
- Coyle's riddle about **girder mold**: "Slimy to touch, greasy to feel, but mix me with blood and I'll eat through steel." (l.3256–3257). "Anything can be a weapon" (l.3253).
- Zero arrives silently, "as if… borne forth on a current of dark smoke" (l.3264–3265). Saying: "The worm turns… And there are always more bones." (l.3272–3274)
- **The Syrox**, the "Wolf Worm of Cog Hive Seven", lives in the ductwork and grows fat on the blood from the matches. "You may have already encountered its offspring" (l.3281–3286). Coyle: "The nightmare had a nightmare of its own" (l.3278).
- Maul: "I see only weakness. And weakness is its own punishment, just as strength is its own reward." (l.3304–3305)
- Maul wonders if the whole mission is a test by Sidious (l.3311–3316). Zero: "Why does the prisoner chase the bird?… Because it's a rook." (l.3321–3323)

## Ch. 24: Wishlist (l.3327–3407)
- Artagan has vanished from medbay. Eogan knows nothing of Radique. Maul asks why father and son weren't executed after the escape attempt (l.3335–3359).
- Izhsmash delivers the inmate list: **218 names** (l.3373). Maul's own entry is the fake record Sidious supplied (l.3374–3376). He spots "Rook" on it.
- Maul orders Izhsmash to hack the algorithm so his next match is against Rook, within 3 hours, under cover of a diversion Maul will create (l.3391–3404).

## Ch. 25: Skull Game (l.3409–3454)
- Coyle was a **counterfeiter** before prison. Maul commissions **300,000 credits** in fake currency "to buy something" and pays in bones (l.3428–3443).
- Coyle's price is the skull of a **Deathspine varactyl** held in a lower cellblock. "Fifteen meters long" (Coyle). Its sinuses funnel and amplify sound (l.3447–3453).

## Ch. 26: Old Man (l.3456–3494)
- Fevered, Artagan wakes in the dark, questioned by an **Aqualish** and **Zero** about what he told the Zabrak. "Mr. Radique was able to protect you from being terminated… But his protection won't last forever." (l.3477–3486)

## Ch. 27: Varactyl (l.3496–3602)
- Strabo has dropped to "toady", and Izhsmash is de facto captain of the Massive (l.3502–3508).
- **Varactyl kill:** an orange-skinned **Deathspine varactyl, four meters high**, bursts out with Maul riding it; he dropped through a ceiling panel into its cell (l.3525–3532). It rakes his torso with a hind claw, but he holds on with "brute-force determination… never seen before in any species" (l.3534–3537).
  - He grips its crest and drives its armored skull into a steam pipe. The scalding blast boils its face and kills it (l.3546–3554).
  - **Then he snaps the skull completely free of the neck** and carries it overhead down the concourse. Guards step back: "You want to ask him?" (l.3562–3571)
  - It cost him "more strength than he'd expected" and a deep gash in his right flank (l.3575–3578).
- This is the diversion while Izhsmash hacks the algorithm (l.3542–3544, 3558–3560).
- Coyle offers **white metaxas root**, a flavorless poison where "a few granules will kill almost anything". Maul throws it away (l.3589–3596).

## Ch. 28: Submerged (l.3604–3695)
- CO Smight, high on what Maul guesses is glitterstim, zip-ties Maul's ankles to the bench with Nylasteel on the warden's orders (l.3613–3631). "You should be more careful what you put in your body… Spice. It will kill you. If I don't do it first." (l.3634–3636)
- The cell rotates (he hangs upside down by the ankles) and then **floods with ice-cold water**; the temperature drops 20 degrees in two minutes (l.3645–3673). The opponent enters through an underwater hatch (l.3690–3695).

## Ch. 29: Spectators (l.3697–3789)
- Sadiki **rigged this match herself**, overriding Dakarai's algorithm, because "The Zabrak… needs to be broken" (l.3714–3719). The posted odds were for different fighters. Chlorus suspects she was hacked or cheated and **shuts the Hive down** (l.3728–3738).
- Chlorus: the **Desilijic Clan has foot soldiers inside, among the guards** (70–80 guards, forged backgrounds) (l.3751–3785). Sadiki and Chlorus had an affair back when she ran sabacc tables at the Outlander Casino on Coruscant (l.3760–3766).

## Ch. 30: Tank, the Rook fight (l.3791–3930)
- **Opponent 4: Rook, an Aqualish**, fought underwater while Maul is tethered at the ankles.
  - Rook knocks Maul's breath out with a diaphragm hit. Maul gouges his eyes; Rook strikes the back of Maul's skull (l.3799–3806).
  - Sidious's voice: "Your inadequacy is worse than disgraceful. It's nauseating." (l.3814)
- **Feats and ingenuity:**
  - With repeated elbow strikes Maul knocks loose a floor bolt and breathes air through the hole (l.3819–3828).
  - He snaps off one of Rook's tusks, hammers his abdomen and smashes his skull into the wall (l.3830–3834).
  - Worms come up through the air hole (the Syrox's offspring?) (l.3840–3844).
  - **Girder mold:** he grinds it into Rook's scalp wound, where it sizzles; then he mixes it with Rook's blood to dissolve the Nylasteel ties and **eat a hole through the steel floor** (l.3853–3876). Both are sucked into a drainage shaft.
- Rook's intel:
  - "I'm not supposed to be matched, ever. He said I was never going to have to fight." (l.3890)
  - "The birds… collect the different components. Bring them to the shop. If you follow the clawbirds, you'll find Radique." (l.3912–3913)
  - A supply ship docks tomorrow during first watch (l.3915). Rook draws a **circle** as the next link in the chain (l.3922–3923).
- **Kill:** Maul snaps Rook's neck: "You were dead when you were matched with me… It will be quick." (l.3926–3930)

## Ch. 31: Clarity (l.3932–3999), Sidious Force-chokes Maul by holo
- Genpop now avoids Maul as if he carries "a lethal infection" (l.3937–3942).
- Sidious: "Declarations of loyalty are worthless without victory… Or if the time has come for me to find another apprentice more deserving of the honor." (l.3960–3964)
- **Sidious feat:** Maul starts "if I were only allowed to call upon the power of the dark side—" and **an invisible hand clamps his throat through the holoprojector link**, dropping him to his knees (l.3968–3971, 3978).
- "You enjoy untold physical advantages that these inferior combatants could only dream of… Many are watching. You are not the only one inside Cog Hive Seven searching for Radique." (l.3972–3975)
- Maul senses that Sidious himself may be in danger (l.3982–3985), a hint of Plagueis. Ambushed leaving the morgue, he is knocked out by a stun weapon (l.3995–3999).

## Ch. 32: Whiteout (l.4001–4131)
- Maul is strung spread-eagle under blinding lights, which defeat the cameras (l.4003–4010).
- Zero: "You really shouldn't have killed Rook. Mr. Radique was paying handsomely to keep him safe from being matched." (l.4024–4026)
- **Izhsmash is dead**, his jaw broken and his mouth and eye sockets stuffed with circuit board and wire (l.4032–4036).
- **A Weequay with a topknot and the clawbird on his shoulder** (seen in the mess hall on day one) speaks with Zero: "Repercussions, Jagannath." (l.4050–4055)
- A machine starts drawing the cables apart to quarter him (l.4058–4069); his right wrist pops.
- **Artagan's backstory:** he was in the **Bando Gora** ("All hail the skull"). The Gora were going to kill Radique, and Artagan betrayed them to save him. The Gora have hunted him ever since, which is why he brought Eogan to the Hive for Radique's protection (l.4077–4100).
- Sidious memory: "aspects of who we are… can only be revealed to us in the deepest pit of intolerable suffering" (l.4087–4089). Maul again resists using the Force (l.4081–4084).
- **Feat:** Maul **dislocates his own right shoulder** to slip the cable, frees his other limbs, then slams the shoulder back in against the floor (l.4104–4112). He is in darkness on the factory floor.
- Maul concludes **the Weequay is Iram Radique**, and the old man groans assent (l.4118–4124). Maul carries Artagan out over his shoulder (l.4129–4131).

## Ch. 33: Reconfig (l.4133–4209)
- Sadiki defies Chlorus's suspension because betting is already in the millions (l.4157–4162). Dakarai is missing (l.4151).
- Slipher has IBC "yellow-card access" to the security codes (l.4169). Sadiki gathers nine off-duty COs in the ready room, has the temperature raised 5 degrees "to see them sweat", and **reconfigures the prison around the ready room** (l.4138–4200).

**Running tally after Ch. 33: 5 kills** (the unidentified champion, the wampa, CO Voystock, the Deathspine varactyl, Rook). That is 3 sanctioned matches (champion, wampa, Rook) plus 2 outside the ring.
