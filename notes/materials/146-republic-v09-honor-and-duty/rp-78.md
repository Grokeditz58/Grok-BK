### Story
1. **Imperial address (75–79).** Palpatine + Vader address fleet. Vader Force-chokes / throws Capt Jace Dallin for dissent. Jan Dodonna warns Sagoro. Sagoro presented as Saleucami hero now wearing Imperial rank.
2. **Flashback / solitude (80–82).** Rainy Coruscant rented room. Narration: five years since he left; was a “Blue”; Reymet’s trouble; **Sula took Reymet + Lissa**; Sagoro covered their escape → lost Guard career → mercenary → rejoined war via Dodonna.
3. **Purge warning / flight (83–85).** Isaru warns Sagoro he is on a purge list; stormtroopers coming. Sagoro flees in rain (“THANKS, ISARU—” / “—BUT DON’T BE A HERO. LIVE.”). Vader interrogates Isaru: “WHERE IS SAGORO AUTEM?” Isaru: gave warning, doesn’t know where; Vader kills him (saber; damaged blue Guard armor).
4. **Bounty hunters (86–88).** Bothan Ch'ord Sy'fon sells pad (*Scimitar of Kelso*, Nar Shaddaa 0300, Sector 4892…). Betrays. Clone commander presents three hunters to Vader: “THE SHISTAVANEN IS NAMED **SEVERIAN**, THE DUG IS CALLED **TARTUTA**, AND THE HUMAN GOES BY **EVAN HESSLER**.” Vader: “WHY DO YOU WEAR THE MASK, HESSLER?” — “THE RESULT OF AN ‘ACCIDENT,’ LORD VADER.”
5. **Hunt / fight (89–94).** Hunters engage Sagoro in industrial/pit structures. Severian + Tartuta (jetpack / white blades; Sagoro: “HIT THE WRONG TARGET **FIRST**, MURGLAK.”). Hessler hangs back / intervenes.
6. **Reveal / reunion (95–96).** Hessler removes mask = **Reymet**. “DAD -- YOU TAKE HARD-NOSED TO A WHOLE NEW LEVEL.” Reunion; headed to Mom + Lissa (Lissa dating a Corellian). Hyperspace. “WELCOME BACK, DAD.” **END.**
