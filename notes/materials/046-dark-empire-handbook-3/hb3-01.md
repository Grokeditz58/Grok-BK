# Star Wars Handbook 3: Dark Empire (Dark Horse, 1998), notes part 1: pp. 000–026 (complete handbook)

Source: the official handbook pages inside the fan compilation "Dark Empire Trilogy (with Handbook 3 and Star Wars Insider Making ofs Parts 1-3)" (MEGA xC9wHbKZ). Page numbers below are the scan's file numbers (000–026).

- **p. 000 (cover):** Dark Horse "Star Wars Handbook: Dark Empire" cover (Han, Leia, Luke, Chewie, droids, Palpatine clone).
- **p. 001: Boba Fett / Slave II** (illustration by John Nadeau and Jordi Ensign; colors by Dave Nestelle).
  - Recap: Fett's reappearance on Nar Shaddaa, "six years past" after the Sarlacc: "as Fett said, the Sarlacc had found him somewhat indigestible."
  - *Slave II* "slammed into the shield and was significantly damaged" at Byss. Fett broke *Slave I* out of impoundment; dark side adepts Zasm and Fass tried to press him into unpaid service. He "ended any ties he had to the Empire when he attacked the dark side warriors and then escaped".
  - Vima-Da-Boda rescue: "Boba Fett was able to wound Chewie, but the Wookiee's strength and tenacity nearly matched the bounty hunter's." Chewbacca tore off Fett's helmet and set off his rocket pack.
  - Empatojayos Brand's "new lightning gun" severely damaged *Slave I* in the gas cloud.
- **p. 002: Byss** (illustration by Isaac Buckminster Owens; colors by Dave Nestelle):
> "it was an entire world that radiated the dark side … Deep within the galactic core and protected by an impenetrable network of shields and capital ships, the world was a fortress for the Emperor" — p. 002
> "Despite his clone's rapid deterioration, Palpatine seemed invulnerable while on Byss, and his use of Force powers there was more of an afterthought than an exercise. When Leia Organa Solo brandished a lightsaber at him, Palpatine waved his fingers and the ancient weapon shattered. When Leia tried to kill the Emperor by dropping a cooling unit on him, Palpatine shrugged off the crushing impact of a ton of machinery." — p. 002
> "Within Palpatine's lair, and surrounded by the dark side pall that covered the planet, Luke could not stand against the Emperor." — p. 002
  - Ending: *Eclipse II*, reprogrammed by R2-D2, collided with the Galaxy Gun; the weapon misfired at Byss, "and the Rebels watched as the Emperor's throneworld was reduced to cinders."
  - (ANALYSIS: an official statement that Palpatine's Byss feats were casual ("more of an afterthought than an exercise"), plus the "ton of machinery" durability feat.)
- **p. 003: Dark Side Adepts** (illustration by Dusty Abell and Jim Royal; color by Dave Nestelle):
> "While some were merely yes men, cronies barely fit to be footstools in the Imperial Court, others possessed tremendous strength in the Force. Coupled with maniacal lust for power or were merely psychotic, these dark side adepts were some of the most dangerous Force users in the galaxy, short of the Emperor himself." — p. 003
  - **Executor Sedriss:** "Perhaps the strongest disciple of Palpatine after Darth Vader". Orchestrated the first strikes of Operation Shadow Hand. When **Nefta** and **Sa-Di** began destroying the Emperor's remaining clones "in an attempt to take the throne for themselves, Sedriss witnessed, judged, and executed them."
  - **Zasm and Fass:** their Force powers helped them bully Boba Fett. They lost the *Falcon* when the tractor beam "tore into the keel of the Imperial ship" and the SD "plunged into the surface of the Smugglers' Moon".
  - On Ossus, Sedriss and **Vill Goir** met Luke and Kam Solusar:
> "Had it been merely a contest between dark side adepts and Jedi, Sedriss might have been victorious. But the dark lord had not counted on the strength of the native Ysanna, nor the presence of the treelike Jedi Ood Bnar. Dark side Executor Sedriss was consumed in a burst of light side energy." — p. 003
  - **Krdys Mordi and Xecr Nist:** "a traumatic, high-speed induction" to the dark side warriors. They tried to kidnap Jacen and Jaina on New Alderaan, "faced a full assembly of Luke Skywalker's new Jedi, and they were both cut down".
- **p. 004: Empatojayos Brand / Ganath** (Abell and Royal; Nestelle):
  - The gas clouds near Nal Hutta were "intensely radioactive". The Ganathans are trapped in an "antique era of slow-speed space travel and steam engines". Their king is the Jedi Knight Empatojayos Brand, "among the last of the Jedi to be pursued by Darth Vader in the twilight years of the Old Republic". He was given a "repulsorlift-sphere body", "now more machine than man".
  - The Ganathan "antique lightning gun … proved to be as powerful" and sent Fett's ship spiraling.
> "Empatojayos Brand went on to prove that a Jedi does not need a complete body to become a hero." — p. 004
> "When Emperor Palpatine disembodied his spirit from his dying clone and attempted to force his way into the infant Anakin Solo, Brand threw his own damaged body in the way and intercepted the Emperor's vile spirit. Knowing full well that his time had come, Brand was still strong enough to bind the Emperor's spirit within his own body. When he died, the Emperor died with him." — p. 004
- **p. 005: Emperor's Hyperspace Wormhole** (Owens; Nestelle):
> "Vast energy storms that connect wildly disparate spots across the galaxy, hyperspace wormholes are unpredictable and devastating. It was to the Rebel Alliance's detriment that Emperor Palpatine was able to not only control these storms, but to create them." — p. 005
> "a hyperspace wormhole appeared in space and made its way to the surface, indiscriminately destroying friend and enemy vessels alike. Witnesses commented on how the storm seemed single minded, and indeed, it was: the Jedi Luke Skywalker was its target, and even as it tore into the surface of the city-covered planet in a frenzy of random destruction, it made its way toward the spot where Luke … was pinned down" — p. 005
> "In a last-ditch attempt to stab at his enemies, Palpatine evoked another hyperspace wormhole over the Rebels' Pinnacle Moon base. But the combined burst of energy from Luke Skywalker, Leia, and her unborn child sent the Emperor reeling, and his creation, without anybody to control it, went rampant. The wormhole tore into Palpatine's flagship and sent the Emperor reeling into oblivion." — p. 005
  - (ANALYSIS: confirms that Palpatine both creates and steers Force storms across the galaxy, from Byss to Coruscant. Luke and Leia plus the unborn Anakin Solo overpower him.)
- **p. 006: The E-wing** (Nadeau and Ensign; Nestelle): the FreiTek E-wing is "equal to the formidable TIE Interceptor".
  - Makeshift relays let the triple laser cannons "absorb three times their normal power feed", which caused "many terminal overloads".
  - The R7 series was developed because the E-wing's computers were too advanced for R2 units.
  - At Calamari, the World Devastators were "methodically tearing Calamari apart" until R2-D2 used the command codes to set them against each other or shut them down.
- **p. 007: Galaxy Gun** (Owens; Nestelle). Designed by Umak Leth.
> "Previously, Palpatine's doomsday weapons were limited: they had to be present at a chosen site in order to attack it. … Because it was fired from thr [sic] galactic center, and its projectiles traveled through hyperspace, it could strike targets anywhere and everywhere in the galaxy." — p. 007
> "The Galaxy Gun's missile contained a particle disintegrator that sent a nucleonic chain reaction throughout the planet, explosively changing matter to energy." — p. 007 (the Pinnacle Moon, near Da Soocha)
  - A second shot at Nespis VIII "was a dud that crashed harmlessly".
  - *Eclipse II* rammed the Gun, which "misfired into the surface of Byss, destroying the Emperor's throneworld".
- **p. 008: Gen. Lando Calrissian / Gen. Wedge Antilles** (Abell and Royal; Nestelle):
  - The *Liberator* was lost over Coruscant. At Mon Calamari, Lando's tactic was to arrive from hyperspace on top of the blockading fleet.
  - The captured SD *Emancipator* "became a very expensive meal for the unstoppable World Devastator."
  - Wedge's plan had the team ride inside Viper Automadon war droids into Byss. It failed because of the "chrysalide beasts — genetically mutated killing machines":
> "While the war droids could absorb enemy fire and turn it back on the Imperials, the chrysalides merely grabbed the war droids and began eating them." — p. 008
- **p. 009: Imperial Dungeon Ship** (Nadeau and Ensign; Nestelle). Umak Leth's **Universal Energy Cage**:
> "the Emperor assembled the brightest minds in the galaxy to design enclosures that either overpowered a prisoner with energy, or provided no possibility of escape. … In addition to automatic stun charges set off by any attempt to touch the bars, the cage's twin repulsorlifts cradled a captive in a polarity field within the cage. But worst of all, a feedback system within the bars of the cage reflected twicefold any energy directed at them." — p. 009
  - Other cells: "multilock chambers that would present an impossible challenge to even the most advanced psychokinetic"; coffin-like comatose encasements; "continuous disruptor emitters that nullified the senses or destabilized brain waves".
  - The penitentiary levels are patrolled by droids, which are "more resistant to Jedi mind tricks". Duel levels pit prisoners against dark side warriors. Luke was "the sole prisoner" aboard one when called to Byss.
- **p. 010: Vima-Da-Boda** (illustration by Jan Duursema; color by Dave Nestelle):
  - "Directly descended from the legendary Jedi Vima Sunrider", she was "a powerful Jedi during the height of the Republic". After losing her daughter she fell into a despair "so deep that not even her closest friends among the Jedi could sense her"; that same masking hid her from Palpatine. Her experiences "spanned centuries".
  - She sensed Leia's power and saw in her "the spark that would rekindle the Jedi", and gave Leia her lightsaber.
  - On New Alderaan she helped cure Luke after he was poisoned by Imperial scarab droids. She tried to deceive Palpatine into thinking she was Leia, "But the ruse failed, and Vima-Da-Boda was given a glimpse into the black pit of despair and fear that was Emperor Palpatine's mind." She disappeared after the victory.
- **p. 011: Mon Calamari / Battle of Mon Calamari** (Abell and Royal; Nestelle):
  - Palpatine's view: "the Empire did not conquer worlds; rather, it saved them from themselves." The Mon Cal were enslaved; Ackbar's background is given.
  - The World Devastators "scoured the surface for any usable construction materials and funneled them into onboard furnaces where automated assembly lines would use them to construct an endless stream of diabolical weapons."
  - "The *Emancipator* was consumed entirely and the rest of the Rebel fleet counted heavy losses. Even the Rebels' new E-wing fighter wasn't proving a match".
  - Luke had "quietly altered the command codes" and shut them down. "Their insides bulging with molten construction materials and onboard factories that were never supposed to shut down once they had started", they became sitting targets.
- **pp. 012–013: Luke Skywalker** (illustration by Jan Duursema; color by Dave Nestelle):
> "despite the young Jedi Knight's best efforts, Palpatine's grasp on the dark side — and power over the galaxy — remained unshaken." — p. 012
  - At Coruscant an "energy storm" appeared; Luke "allowed himself to be swallowed up by the storm" and was funneled into a dungeon ship to Byss.
> "Knowing that his power could not stand against the Emperor's in a direct confrontation, Luke offered himself to the dark lord, hoping that, by welcoming the power of the dark side, he might find a weakness, and exploit it." — p. 012
  - Leia's presence gave Luke "the power to break his ties". He hid the World Devastator master codes "within the circuits of R2-D2".
> "Luke's presence turned out to be nothing more than an illusion, for Luke had to remain behind and finish one final task: the destruction of Palpatine's clone chambers. … But Palpatine was able to transfer himself at the last moment. Within a new clone body, the Emperor was more powerful than ever. After a brief lightsaber battle, the Emperor subdued Luke and broke his will." — p. 013
> "Telling Palpatine that within the dark side power he had also discovered despair and sadness, Luke struck out at the Emperor's clone. But the Emperor's power was vast, and once again, he unleashed an energy storm, spawned by dark side anger. But the Emperor's will could not stand against brother and sister united by the power of light, and the energy storm turned to consume the Emperor's flagship." — p. 013
  - (ANALYSIS: Luke's projected illusion, meaning he was physically elsewhere while appearing to leave, is an official handbook confirmation. Palpatine is "more powerful than ever" in the new clone body.)
  - Ossus: the Ysanna "had never seen a person with such mastery" of the Force. Rayf and Jem Ysanna became trainees; Ood Bnar "was awakened and helped defeat the dark lords"; Luke and Jem bonded.
  - On New Alderaan Jem was "struck down by one of the dark lords while attempting to protect her new love."
  - Palpatine "continued to launch hyperspace missiles at Rebel emplacements across the galaxy". His minions kidnapped "the best of the Ysanna as genetic stock for a new line of clones". At Vjun, "Darth Vader had built his private retreat, Bast Castle".
  - The final confrontation was on Onderon.
- **p. 014: Nar Shaddaa** (Nadeau and Ensign; Nestelle):
  - The "Smugglers' Moon", "a pale reflection of the glorious city-world Coruscant". The Duros Sector was a Neimoidian enclave in the Trade Federation's heyday.
  - "The bounty on the Solos had quadrupled since the death of Jabba the Hutt." Salla Zend and Shug Ninx appear; the *Starlight Intruder* hauled freight to Byss.
  - "vicious Gank killers". Mako Spince sold out Han. The tractor beam pulled down the docking-control tower, and "the Star Destroyer smashed into the abandoned gantries and warehouses of the moon."
- **p. 015: Ossus / The Ysanna** (Owens; Nestelle):
  - "remnants of the Sith War some four thousand years past". The Ysanna used "projectile weapons enhanced through weak implementation of the Force."
> "But Luke had learned much since his first encounter with the Emperor's dark side energies, and he shrugged off the attack, forcing Sedriss and Goir to attack with their lightsabers. Kam Solusar cut down his former dark side teacher Vill Goir, and Sedriss was thrown to the ground by Luke's powerful use of the Force." — p. 015
> "the tree he was backed up against turned out to be the ancient Jedi Ood Bnar, who had rooted himself in this spot four thousand years earlier in order to become guardian of the planet's Jedi secrets. Ood drew on the planet's living energy to battle Sedriss, and the two were consumed in an explosive flash." — p. 015
  - Beneath Ood's roots was "a cache of ancient lightsabers". The Ysanna were the Emperor's fallback "potential vessels for his dark side power"; Xecr Nist froze Chief Okko and other leaders in carbonite.
- **pp. 016–017: Emperor Palpatine** (illustration by Pop Mhan; color by Dave Nestelle):
> "Long before the Rebel Alliance proved to be troublesome for Palpatine, he had discovered that the corrupting power of the dark side had a detrimental effect on his physical form. Utilizing long-lost magic and the science of cloning, Palpatine created a steady supply of clones of his own body, into which he could throw his being and thus rule the galaxy forever. It was into one of these clones, located on Palpatine's hidden retreat world, Byss, that Palpatine projected himself." — p. 016
> "Faced with the Emperor's limitless power and immortality, Luke succumbed to the dark side." — p. 016
  - Palpatine knew Luke would sabotage him and that Leia would "unwittingly hand over that which Palpatine truly desired: the Force-strong child she carried in her womb."
> "Palpatine's own clone bodies were getting further and further away from the original, and thus they were decaying faster and faster." — pp. 016–017
> "Together, and with a third Force wielder stirring in her womb, Luke and Leia were able to overcome the Emperor with light-side energy, cutting him off from the dark side and the malevolent energies he had spawned. Finally, the Emperor was consumed by his own hyperspace wormhole." — p. 017
  - Two subordinates showed "an alarming lack of loyalty" (Nefta and Sa-Di). The Galaxy Gun missile "completely obliterate[d] the moon".
  - Palpatine "needed fresh genetic stock, strong in the Force, with which he would create a new, endless source of clones."
> "Without a new body he would be resigned to the utter madness beyond death. Palpatine traveled to Korriban, the hidden world-crypt of the Sith Lords and Palpatine's private place of power. Here among the vaults containing the mortal remains of thousands of Sith lords, Palpatine would ask for the Sith Lords' spirits' help in halting the decay of his flesh. Instead, the spirits fed him a vision of the infant Anakin Solo." — p. 017
> "Leia had grown strong in the Force under the tutelage of her brother, and she repulsed Palpatine's attack, weakening his body considerably. But Palpatine's resolve was powerful enough to fight back, and as Luke Skywalker's Jedi trainees filed in to protect the infant Anakin, Palpatine cut them down. In a final bid, Palpatine reached out to grasp Anakin, but was shot down by Anakin's father, Han." — p. 017
  - "the once-great Emperor of a million worlds was no more." — p. 017
  - (ANALYSIS: "Emperor of a million worlds" is a scale statement. The Korriban vaults hold the remains of "thousands of Sith lords".)
- **p. 018: R2-D2 / C-3PO** (Owens; Nestelle):
  - R2 was at Luke's side when Luke was "consumed by the Emperor's hyperspace wormhole during the Battle of Coruscant". Luke "seemingly erased Artoo's memory banks". The World Devastator codes were encoded in Artoo.
  - In the Viper Automadon mission the droids "unwittingly caused the destruction of the primary defense system of the Emperor's Citadel."
  - At Nespis VIII the droids found a spy. "Threepio threw himself between Palpatine and the children". Artoo rerouted the flagship to Byss, "where it collided with the Galaxy Gun and caused the destruction of the entire planet."
- **p. 019: Salla Zend / Shug Ninx** (Duursema; Nestelle):
  - "the Hutts, who had quadrupled the bounties". The *Starlight Intruder* made the "deep-core run" to Byss, and the *Falcon* was hidden in Lo Khan's *Hyperspace Marauder*.
  - An Imperial hunter-killer droid tractor-beamed the *Falcon*. "Shug burrowed into the control computer and took control of the massive droid", using it "to punch through the prison walls"; it then turned on security forces.
- **pp. 020–021: Rebel Alliance** (art on p. 021 by Dusty Abell and Jim Royal: a Coruscant ground battle with an AT-AT, a Lambda shuttle, and stormtroopers vs. Rebel troops; color by Dave Nestelle):
  - The Provisional Council (Mon Mothma, Ackbar, Leia). "During the Emperor's absence, Imperial loyalists were able to maintain a deathlike grip on many galactic systems". Warlords were a diversion "to keep the Rebel Alliance off balance until Palpatine could return".
  - After Thrawn's defeat the "Emperor's Ruling Circle" retook Coruscant, then civil strife broke out and the Rebels retook it. Mon Mothma had failed to realize that the Old Republic's strength came "from a steadfast foundation of justice, provided by the Jedi Knights".
  - The Galaxy Gun destroyed "the base and the entire moon". At Nespis VIII Leia gave birth to Anakin.
  - Nespis: "the first missile was a dud … As the second missile struck its target and exploded with ferocious energy, the Rebel fleet jumped into hyperspace."
  - (ANALYSIS: a minor internal inconsistency with p. 007, which mentions only the dud. Per this page, a second shot hit Nespis VIII.)
  - Afterwards the Alliance officially became the New Republic: Mon Mothma chief of state, Leia "Minister of state".
- **p. 022: The Starlight Intruder** (Pop Mhan; Nestelle): Salla designed the ship "for six years" and had Imperial clearance as a free-agent hauler. The *Falcon* was attached to the *Intruder*'s hull for the run to Byss. The *Intruder* was impounded and scrapped; its modifications went into *Salvager III*.
- **p. 023: World Devastators** (illustration by Andrew Robinson; color by Dave Nestelle):
> "Previously, the Empire drew on thousands of worlds to stock its ranks with the best and brightest, an army spanning the stars." — p. 023
> "Powered by miniature black holes, World Devastators were heavily armored, portable processing plants. A vast mouth at one end swallowed structures, weapons, and any raw material into molecular furnaces within the belly of the mechanical monster." — p. 023
> "it was a builder of nightmares that stole from one planet in order to move to the next." — p. 023
  - They produced "the TIE/D, a robotic fighter capable of outflying and outgunning any Alliance vessel". Attacks "seemed futile against the armored behemoths".
> "Once the kilometers-long vessels were shut down, however, it was a simple matter for Alliance soldiers to take them over and destroy them." — p. 023
  - (ANALYSIS: an official handbook statement of miniature black hole power and kilometers-long size.)
- **pp. 024–025: Leia Organa Solo / The Solo Family** (Andrew Robinson; Nestelle):
> "In her own way, the sister of Luke Skywalker was more powerful than her brother, or even their father, the Jedi Anakin Skywalker who became the dark lord of the Sith, Darth Vader. For it was within Leia to give birth to the children who would one day become the new generation of Jedi" — p. 024
  - (ANALYSIS: framed as legacy ("in her own way"), not raw power. Do not use it as a power-scaling claim.)
  - The twins were foreseen by Luke, Vima, and Palpatine and hidden on New Alderaan under Winter's care. Luke was "becoming introspective, his thoughts dwelling on their father".
> "Luke had apparently gone over to the dark side, his will unable to withstand the Emperor's power. … With both of Vader's children under his control, Palpatine's Empire would be unstoppable. But Leia's will, buoyed by Han's Corellian stubborn streak and her own Skywalker anger, kept her from buckling under Palpatine's dark invitation. Catching the Emperor off guard, Leia threw him to the ground and snatched up a Jedi holocron as she made her escape." — p. 025
  - "Luke turned out to be nothing more than a Jedi illusion." Palpatine "now resided in one of his own clones, much younger and stronger".
> "In his anger, the Emperor loosed another energy storm on the Rebel fleet. But Palpatine's dark side powers could not stand against Luke and Leia's powers together—augmented by the Force-strong child in Leia's womb. Bathed in light-side energies, Palpatine lost control of the energy storm outside." — p. 025
- **p. 026: Jedi Holocron** (Duursema; Nestelle):
> "Relics of enormous age and stunning power … Palpatine … learned much from its limitless store of knowledge." — p. 026
> "despite Palpatine's long years spent with the device, he had failed to extract the deepest pieces of knowledge, locked away within the holocron's core and accessible only by Force-strong individuals steeped in the light side. The Holocron recognized Leia as such and opened up to her as it never had for Palpatine." — p. 026
  - It held the teachings of Bodo Baas, "a powerful Jedi from the Old Republic", who told the story of Ulic Qel-Droma's fall.
- The scan ends at p. 026. No separate credits or back-cover page for the handbook is included in the compilation; it may be omitted.
