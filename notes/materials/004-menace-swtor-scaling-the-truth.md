## Thesis

The author opens: "This blog is in response to Ant'ts post… I can't get onboard with your view of SWTOR." Source material outranks author intent. On that basis:

- the Outlander is the Hero of Tython and is above Arcann, Vaylin and Revan;
- Valkorion grew enormously over 1,000 years and was split (Voice, Children, Zakuul) until KOTFE/EOO;
- Revan never genuinely threatened a whole Valkorion.

Final chain: "Voice << Terror from Beyond << Revan << Arcann <<<< Vaylin <<< Outlander <<< Valkorion".

## Arguments (numbered)

1. **Source material > author intent** (S1).
2. **The Outlander = the Hero of Tython** (S2A), for the four reasons in the table.
3. **Class parity is a game abstraction**, and post-Act-3 growth applies to the HoT (S2B).
4. **Operations and flashpoints are solo-protagonist fights** (S2B), with the Dread Masters/Hargrev and Revan-strike-team examples.
5. **The Outlander > Arcann** (S2C): dark-side route, Arcann concessions, Marr's "new weapons" line (B071).
6. **The Outlander > Vaylin** (S2C): Valkorion's "no more influence" line, and Boyd's own skill concession. Note that Valkorion says of Vaylin, "She is not. She is merely powerful." (B027–B028).
7. **The Outlander > the Six Gods-level threats** (S2C): the "greatest challenge" (Material 005), the "Demon Saviour" (B018) and EOO menace lines.
8. **Ziost is not evidence of weakness** (S3A–B): a weakened Vitiate's cycle; full Valkorion could do it casually.
9. **The narrative trend is underestimation** (S3C).
10. **Mindscapes favour Valkorion** (S3D).
11. **Time-stop is real** (S3E).
12. **Developer opinions are discounted** where they contradict the game (S3F).
13. **Valkorion was split for 1,000 years** (S4); the HoT beat "under half".
14. **Valkorion's growth is "completely inarguable"** (S4).
15. **Revan was never competitive with Vitiate once Vitiate tried** (S5A).
16. **Boyd's "legitimate threat" list is misread** (S5B).
17. **Foundry Revan is not weakened** (S5C).
18. **SoR is incoherent, so Revan beating the Voice ≠ beating Valkorion** (S5D).
19. **The mental war is overstated** (S5E).
20. **Terror from Beyond > Voice-era Vitiate** (S6).
21. **Arcann and Vaylin > Revan** (S7).
22. **Conclusion chain** (S8): "Voice << Terror from Beyond << Revan << Arcann <<<< Vaylin <<< Outlander <<< Valkorion".

## Common rebuttals / weak points (ANALYSIS — not from the thread)

- **The Outlander = HoT is a probabilistic choice.** BioWare and Boyd say there is no canonical class. The four reasons are about narrative prominence, not a canon statement.
- **Marketing superlatives** ("greatest test", "ultimate combat test", "most intense confrontation yet") are attached to almost every SWTOR release. That weakens their value for scaling.
- **"possess planets whilst sipping wine"** is given without a source or link (UNCLEAR).
- **A6** (the Ziost cycle explanation) has no attribution; the author says only that Boyd "agreed with" it.
- **Selective discounting of Boyd.** His statements are dismissed as "useless" where they hurt the argument, yet used where helpful (A15, A19, A32, the "initial" scan, and the skill concession).
- **Split-Valkorion "under 50%"** is an inference from lore about the Voice, the Children and Zakuul. No source gives a percentage.
- **The time-stop argument** depends on reading cutscene staging as literal.
- **Many supporting images are dead Discord links** (15 of 75).
