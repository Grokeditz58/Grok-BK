## Thesis

The p17–22 duel shows Wyyrlok holding up better than "Krayt stomped him" allows:

- he likely matches Krayt's lightning with his own;
- his Force bubble stops Krayt's thrown rocks;
- although a weak duelist ("a diplomat rather than a warrior"; LECG lightsaber +16), he lasts "at least forty feet" of saber combat;
- he still lands a Memory Walk at the end.

Ant asks of each phase, with a shrug: "What are the implications…?" He rejects the idea that Krayt was holding back to humiliate Wyyrlok.

## Arguments (numbered)

1. **Krayt's presence** is "a firestorm of raw emotion and cold rage" (p15). He promises his own "Order 66" and "screams" from the One Sith (p16). He wants blood. Ant attributes "those who stand with the usurper will die" to Krayt.
2. **Opening exchange (p17):** "OF ALL WHO FOLLOWED ME, YOU WERE THE CLOSEST." / "I OFFER NO APOLOGY." / "I WOULD ACCEPT NONE. BEGIN."
3. **Lightning: both men probably fire** (p18 panels 1–2), for three reasons:
   - (A) the identical pose and the same depth of lightning on the arm as Wyyrlok;
   - (B) the drawing does not match how absorption or deflection is drawn elsewhere: no "purple circle", and lightning runs past the palm;
   - (C) an "abrupt 90 degree turn" in one stream is best read as two streams meeting.

   Ant concedes: "Maybe just Wyyrlok. Maybe just Krayt. Maybe both." Krayt's whole torso crackling while two columns break is "no precedent… with tutaminis".
4. **Wyyrlok survives the lightning.** Ant asks for the implications and shrugs; Krayt's face "doesn't care" (B012).
5. **Rocks (p18 panels 3–5).** Wyyrlok hurls the debris. Krayt "grabs them in the air and hurls them back", with a "decent chance" he rips them from Wyyrlok's grasp. Wyyrlok's bubble blocks them; the rocks "bounced off", and Wyyrlok shows no damage on the next page.
6. **Sabers (p19).** Krayt leaps in with two blades, "monstrous" and roaring. Across three panels he is "dominant": Wyyrlok retreats up the stairs and "Krayt slices off his bottom horns".
7. **Duel length.** From B017–B019: at least 8 ft to the stairs, 5 stairs at about 2 ft each, a 12 ft landing and 5 more stairs, "at least forty feet, give or take".
8. **Wyyrlok is a poor duelist:**
   - *Ultimate Visual Guide*: "Wyyrlok is a diplomat rather than a warrior" (B020);
   - LECG lightsaber +16, "among the worst… of any prominent Force user" in Saga Edition, against early Clone Wars Jedi (Fisto +18, Mundi +17, K'Kruhk +16, Plo Koon +20, An'ya Kuro +20, Secura +16, Tiin +19, Vos +16) and Legacy Sith (Maladi +14, Maleval +18, Nihl +21, Talon +18);
   - Str 10, "tied for… physically weakest".
9. **Krayt was not prolonging the fight to humiliate**, for four reasons:
   - (A) no history of doing so;
   - (B) his monologue is about vengeance, not humiliation;
   - (C) there are more flagrant ways to humiliate;
   - (D) humiliating a sorcerer with sabers is "like humiliating Babe Ruth by challenging him to football".
10. **Memory Walk (p20–21).** Wyyrlok lands it. Ant asks whether it was charged during the duel, "Zannah"-style. Wyyrlok concedes Krayt is "strong" but knows his "weaknesses". Krayt: "THIS IS NOT YOUR TRAP -- IT IS MINE". Krayt impales him (p22).
11. **Key takeaways.** Wyyrlok can:
    - block Krayt's lightning with his own;
    - block the rocks with a bubble;
    - last at least 40 feet against a "maybe-trying" Krayt.

## Common rebuttals / weak points (ANALYSIS — not from the thread)

- **"Firestorm of raw emotion and cold rage" is Wyyrlok's sensing of Krayt's presence (a p15 caption).** It is not a description of each attack. Ant attaches it to every phase (lightning, rocks, sabers) to imply each was an all-out effort; the text does not support that.
- **p18 panel 2 cuts against "Wyyrlok matches him".** Krayt stands grinning, arms flung wide, inside a panel-filling web of lightning with no visible damage. Whoever fired, Krayt is not being pressed.
- **p18 panel 4 shows Krayt's open hand halting the rocks in a blue Force glow.** It is a telekinetic catch, which supports Ant's "grabs them in the air". But it also undercuts any idea that the return throw was weak: the rocks bounce off a *bubble*, which is what bubbles are for.
- **Forty feet is an estimate from clipped panels.** The duel is about four drawn beats (p19); the time spent per foot is unknown.
- **Wyyrlok's own words (p10):** "WERE HE THE KRAYT OF OLD, I COULD NOT HAVE DONE IT." The best-informed character rates himself below a healthy Krayt.
- **The LECG has no Force lightning in Wyyrlok's stat block.** Ant uses the LECG for his saber numbers; the same book does not support his lightning parity (see 011, analysis G).

## New knowledge

- **The *Ultimate Visual Guide* (Updated and Expanded) calls Wyyrlok "a diplomat rather than a warrior"** (scan-verified). It is a new out-of-universe source for our Wyyrlok file.
- **LECG Wyyrlok ability scores** (Str 10, Dex 10, Con 12, Int 18, Wis 16, Cha 14) are confirmed from the scan. Our 011 note lacked the Strength value.
- **Horn detail:** by p22 Wyyrlok's lower horns are cut back to their sheaths, a change from his earlier look. This supports Wookieepedia's "hacking apart his horns" more than our 010 note allowed.
- **Proposal worth keeping:** Krayt "rips the rocks from Wyyrlok's grasp" (Ant). ILS agrees with it, and p18 panel 4's open-hand catch supports it.
