# Young Jedi Knights 4: Lightsabers: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-20 CST. Full read of READ l.1–1845 (every line, in order): front matter, all 21 chapters, the Darkest Knight blurb and preview, and the back cover. Citation "LS ch. N, p.X" uses the printed pages of the Boulevard paperback (March 1996). Detail is in ls-01 to ls-05; every quote below is pasted by script from the same READ.txt.
- Authors: Kevin J. Anderson & Rebecca Moesta. Fourth Young Jedi Knights book. Copyright page (scan p6): Boulevard edition, March 1996, © 1996 Lucasfilm Ltd. 21 chapters.
- Era: after The Lost Ones. Luke says the Emperor "has been dead for nineteen years" (ch. 1, p.10). No ABY year is given.
- Big events: Tenel Ka loses her left arm when her flawed lightsaber fails in a sparring duel with Jacen (ch. 5). She reveals herself as the Hapan princess, survives two assassination attempts arranged by Ambassador Yfra, and returns to Yavin 4 with a new turquoise lightsaber. Zekk kills Vilas in a duel to the death and is named the Emperor's "Darkest Knight" (ch. 18).

## Plot
- **Ch. 1 (READ l.132–231), Luke's dawn workout; the assembly; Luke asks the four to build lightsabers:** Luke meditates all night atop the Great Temple, then runs a Force-assisted dawn workout through the jungle canopy while he weighs the Shadow Academy threat. At a general assembly he tells the students he must speed up their training. In the hangar bay he tells Jacen, Jaina, Lowie and Tenel Ka they are ready to build their own lightsabers.
- **Ch. 2 (READ l.232–292), Luke's lightsaber lesson; Jaina grows crystals; Jacen's Corusca gem; Lowie searches the old control room:** In a solarium Luke explains lightsaber components and history, mentions Cilghal and Gantoris, and sends them off. Jaina grows her own crystals in a furnace using the Force. Jacen spends weeks patiently assembling his hilt and chooses his Corusca gem. Lowie hunts through the old Rebel control room with a pessimistic Em Teedee.
- **Ch. 3 (READ l.293–351), Tenel Ka's training regime; the rancor-tooth hilt; the volcano crystals:** Tenel Ka drills at dawn atop the temple with a staff, runs ten kilometers, swims, and resolves that the warrior matters more than the weapon. She carves a rancor tooth into a hilt. Lowie flies her to a volcano, where she pulls hazy crystals from a lava tube. Assembling the weapon, she finds the tooth's hollow too small and every crystal flawed, but presses on.
- **Ch. 4 (READ l.352–452), Remote training with red visors; Jaina duels Lowie:** In a burned-out clearing Luke sets the four against invisible remotes, seen through red visors. Jacen is stung, Jaina and Lowie learn to deflect, and Tenel Ka slices her remote in two by tracking it by ear. Then Jaina duels Lowie and wins the bout with a Force-boosted leap over his sweep.
- **Ch. 5 (READ l.453–518), Tenel Ka duels Jacen; her lightsaber fails and she loses her arm:** Tenel Ka and Jacen spar. She outmaneuvers his attempt to drive her into the boulders and switches hands to show off. In a blade lock her flawed weapon cracks and shorts out, and Jacen's final push carries his blade through her left arm above the elbow. Her lightsaber explodes and she blacks out.
- **Ch. 6 (READ l.519–567), The Denarii Nova; Brakiss moves the starfire; Zekk gets his lightsaber:** The Shadow Academy hides near the Denarii Nova. Brakiss tells Zekk its history, moves the nova's gases with the Force, and gives Zekk an ancient red lightsaber already attuned to the dark side.
- **Ch. 7 (READ l.568–627), The friends brood; Luke on losing a hand; Tenel Ka taken away by a Hapan shuttle:** Jacen and Jaina wander the temple after the accident; Luke had carried Tenel Ka back himself, using the Force to sustain her. Luke will not let them see her yet and talks about losing his own hand. Jacen has a nightmare, wakes to an urgent Force nudge and Lowie's call, and they reach the landing pad as a shuttle leaves with Tenel Ka. Jaina identifies it as a Hapan royal ambassadorial shuttle.
- **Ch. 8 (READ l.628–679), Tenel Ka aboard the Thunder Wraith; Yfra; Ta'a Chume's welcome on Hapes:** Numb and alone on the royal shuttle, Tenel Ka is needled by Ambassador Yfra and blames her own pride. On Hapes, Ta'a Chume greets her alone with a spectacle of servants and performers and tells her that her destiny is to rule.
- **Ch. 9 (READ l.680–726), Tionne's lesson; Jaina's flashback; the tale of Nomi Sunrider:** Tionne leads Jacen, Jaina and Lowie in hilt drills. When the blades are ignited, the colors flood Jaina with memories (Tamith Kai, Brakiss, Tenel Ka's arm, Zekk) and she drops her lightsaber. Tionne tells the story of Nomi Sunrider and hands the weapon back.
- **Ch. 10 (READ l.727–763), The Hapan physicians; Tenel Ka throws off the droid arm with the Force:** Court physicians fit Tenel Ka for a biomechanical arm. She realizes she has let others choose for her. When they clamp on a temporary droid arm, she Force-shoves the doctor and engineer back and flings the arm into a wall.
- **Ch. 11 (READ l.765–808), Skyhopper flight; Luke's decision; the flight to Hapes:** Lowie flies the twins aimlessly in the T-23. Em Teedee realizes they miss Tenel Ka, and they turn back. Luke, rebuffed by Ta'a Chume over the comm, decides that Tenel Ka needs her friends and flies the three of them in the Shadow Chaser. On arrival Jaina recognizes the Hapes Cluster, and Luke reveals that Tenel Ka is more than a simple warrior.
- **Ch. 12 (READ l.809–895), Norys in stormtrooper armor; Qorl's lessons; the Emperor's address:** Norys, now a stormtrooper trainee, revels in his armor. Qorl hurls a spear at him to teach respect for it, gives him a blaster, and runs him through a holographic mynock cave. At the assembly Zekk eyes his rival Vilas, and the Great Leader's hologram, in the form of Emperor Palpatine, tells the Shadow Academy its plans are nearly complete.
- **Ch. 13 (READ l.896–989), Arrival at the Fountain Palace; Luke and Ta'a Chume; Jacen and Tenel Ka reconcile; the garden room; the explosion:** The Shadow Chaser lands at the Fountain Palace. Ta'a Chume tries to turn them away, but Tenel Ka comes out because Luke called her through the Force. Ta'a Chume explains the assassination threat and that she sent Isolder and Teneniel away. Luke leaves the friends for a month. Jacen apologizes, Tenel Ka laughs for the first time and forgives him, and she shows them her Dathomir garden. Then a bomb blast throws her to her knees.
- **Ch. 14 (READ l.990–1055), To Reef Fortress; rooms chosen for each friend; Tenel Ka learns to swim one-armed and wins the race:** Ta'a Chume pilots the Water Dragon yacht to Reef Fortress Island and says she will shuttle back and forth while investigating, leaving Yfra with them. Tenel Ka picks rooms for her friends (a corner room for Lowie, the gadget room for Jaina, the aquarium room for Jacen). At the cove she finds she can still float, tread water and swim, and she beats Jacen in a real race to shore.
- **Ch. 15 (READ l.1056–1141), Tenel Ka defies Ta'a Chume; the Mairan ambassador; her first ruling:** Tenel Ka throws aside the robe and tiara. Jacen persuades her to learn from her grandmother anyway, so she agrees to greet the Mairan ambassador on her own terms, with Lowie and Em Teedee beside her. She defends Lowie from being called a pet, exposes the Mairans' ditanium scheme against the Vergills, and rules against them. Ta'a Chume approves of her judgment.
- **Ch. 16 (READ l.1142–1263), Yfra's wavespeeder outing; the killer seaweed; sabotage:** Stuck on the island with Yfra in charge, the friends are restless. Yfra offers them a reprogrammed wavespeeder for a loop course out to sea. Jacen is unsure whether to suspect her but they go, and Tenel Ka smiles at him. The autopilot takes them into a field of carnivorous seaweed. The engine dies, the weed grabs Jacen, and Tenel Ka (knife), Jacen and Lowie (lightsabers) fight it off while Jaina drives them clear. Lowie and Jaina find the course had no return leg: sabotage.
- **Ch. 17 (READ l.1264–1310), In the grotto; Tenel Ka remembers who she is; suspicion falls on Yfra:** In the cave grotto Jaina and Lowie examine the wavespeeder. Tenel Ka, using a calming technique, envies their two hands, then rethinks. Jacen praises her fighting, and she realizes she had stopped believing in herself. Jaina confirms the sabotage. Tenel Ka rules out her grandmother, and the friends suspect Yfra. Ta'a Chume returns (a conspirator was poisoned under guard) and sends Yfra to the mainland.
- **Ch. 18 (READ l.1311–1418), Zekk vs Vilas, duel to the death in the zero-g arena; the Darkest Knight:** Brakiss leads Zekk to the zero-gravity arena at the station hub, where Zekk must fight Vilas to the death for leadership of the Dark Jedi. After a vicious weightless fight Zekk anticipates Vilas's ambush and cuts him in half. Brakiss, Tamith Kai and the Emperor's hologram congratulate him, and the Emperor names him his Darkest Knight.
- **Ch. 19 (READ l.1419–1497), The Bartokk assault on Reef Fortress:** An explosion wakes Tenel Ka. The perimeter shields and all power are down, and a Bartokk assassin squad is scaling the walls. She rouses the others, takes the ornamental spear from Lowie's room, and they race to Ta'a Chume's tower, cutting down Bartokks on the way. They shut themselves in with the matriarch and kill the three Bartokks inside, while the hive batters the door.
- **Ch. 20 (READ l.1498–1637), The climb down; the wavespeeder chase; the Dragon's Teeth; the seaweed trap:** With no secret passage, Tenel Ka anchors her grappling hook and they climb down the tower: Lowie first, then the twins, then Tenel Ka carrying Ta'a Chume. The Bartokks cut the cord and Lowie catches the matriarch. They flee in a wavespeeder with the Bartokk assault craft after them, use the Dragon's Teeth geyser to throw it, then lure it into the killer seaweed.
- **Ch. 21 (READ l.1638–1691), Back on Yavin 4; the turquoise lightsaber; stronger together:** Tenel Ka's parents return, purge Yfra's conspirators, and back her return to the academy. On Yavin 4 she smashes the tiara with the rancor tooth, builds a new lightsaber with its rainbow gems, and duels Jacen in trust. The four raise their blades together.

## Scale and limits worth noting
- Lightsabers: a blade lock with a flawed weapon overloaded its power source, and Jacen's blade passed straight through the arm (ch. 5). Zekk's blade cuts Vilas in two, and Vilas's blade slices a meteoroid with a mirror-smooth cut (ch. 18). The remotes' low-power lasers sting (ch. 4).
- Force: Brakiss moves the Denarii Nova's gases with the Force but does not know whether he could make a star explode (ch. 6). Luke calls Tenel Ka across the palace through the Force with no transmitter (ch. 13). Jacen cannot reach the seaweed's mind because it has no brain to find (ch. 16). Tenel Ka throws a droid arm with the Force (ch. 10) and slows her own fall from a cut rope (ch. 20).
