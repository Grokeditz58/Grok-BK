# Jedi Apprentice 1: The Rising Force: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-20 CST. Every line of READ l.1–1069 was read in order: ch. 1–24 and the afterword. Notes are limited to scaling-relevant material (owner's rulings, 2026-11-20). The quotes are in rf-01 and rf-02; this summary is in our own words.
- Author: Dave Wolverton. First Jedi Apprentice book (Scholastic, 1999). Obi-Wan is twelve, four weeks from his thirteenth birthday. Cites are RF ch. N, with no page numbers.

## Plot
- **The Temple (ch. 1–5):** Obi-Wan beats Bruck Chun in a blindfolded duel, then outlasts him in a second bout without striking. Bruck hid the news that Qui-Gon is coming and goads Obi-Wan into an unsupervised fight, then reports it. Obi-Wan is posted to the Agricultural Corps on Bandomeer. Yoda, who has foreseen something, gets him one more fight. Obi-Wan wins it by attacking ferociously, but Qui-Gon refuses him because of his anger. Yoda tells Qui-Gon the boy fought like the young Qui-Gon, and Xanatos's name comes up. The Senate is also sending Qui-Gon to Bandomeer.
- **The Monument (ch. 6–12):** An Offworld Hutt, Grelb, beats Obi-Wan unconscious. Clat'Ha of Arcona Mineral Harvest explains the war with Offworld and its chieftain Jemba, blamed for the Varristad dome deaths. Qui-Gon heals Obi-Wan's fever and stops Jemba's lunge with his presence. Against orders, Obi-Wan and the Arconan Si Treemba search for the stolen thermocoms. Si Treemba is captured and poisoned with salt, and Obi-Wan rescues him from Grelb. Qui-Gon says the meddling made things worse.
- **Pirates (ch. 13–14):** Togorian pirates disable the ship. Obi-Wan forces open the bridge with the Force, and Si Treemba plugs the breach. Obi-Wan torpedoes a gunship and a warship and tears the docked cruisers away. Qui-Gon is cut deep by the pirate captain's vibro-ax, but the decompression takes the captain, and Qui-Gon shuts a bulkhead with the Force. Obi-Wan lands the ship on an island ruled by draigons.
- **The dactyl (ch. 15–18):** Jemba steals the Arconans' dactyl and tries to buy their labor with it. Qui-Gon is too weak to sway him. Obi-Wan backs down, and his friendship keeps the Arconans from joining Jemba. Qui-Gon refuses him again, and Obi-Wan lets go of the hope. Qui-Gon works out where the dactyl is hidden and climbs for it alone.
- **The draigons (ch. 19–23):** Under Whiphid fire and in a storm, Qui-Gon climbs the cliff and takes the dactyl. Blaster fire draws a draigon feeding frenzy. He feels Obi-Wan's Force call and leaps onto a draigon, which he steers to the caves. Obi-Wan holds a cave mouth alone. He fights without fear or anger and begins to see attacks before they come. He dodges Grelb's bolt, which kills Jemba, and a draigon takes Grelb. Master and boy fight side by side until the flock leaves. Clat'Ha frees the Offworld workers.
- **Ending (ch. 24, afterword):** Qui-Gon admits he underestimated Obi-Wan but decides to wait and watch. On Bandomeer, a note from Xanatos is waiting for him.

## Strongest findings
- **Obi-Wan (12):** blindfolded Force-guided swordplay and a somersault over Bruck. He Force-pushes Bruck, opens a latched fire door with the Force, and uses Force persuasion on miners. His call to Qui-Gon across the island works, though "he could not control the Force as he wanted". He holds a cave mouth alone against draigons and reads attacks before they come. He senses and dodges a sniper bolt, and deflects a blaster bolt over his back. His proton torpedo shots without a targeting computer destroy two pirate warships.
- **Obi-Wan anti-feats:** knocked out by Grelb after his Force command fails; awkward in his growing body; thinks he could deflect "no more than a few" of thirty blasters; frozen briefly by terror at the draigon swarm; struggles daily with anger.
- **Qui-Gon:** Force presence stops Jemba's charge. He climbs a ledge "no Human should have been able to climb" while wounded and frozen. He leaps from a cave 200 m up onto a draigon and steers it by mind control. He shuts a bulkhead with the Force while bleeding badly, calls his saber to his off hand, and heals his own torn muscles overnight. He deflects blaster fire, cuts down Togorians, and senses Obi-Wan's trouble without a Padawan bond.
- **Qui-Gon anti-feats:** the Togorian captain, four times a man's weight, nearly kills him with the vibro-ax and pins him. Later he is too weak to persuade Jemba and nearly collapses.
- **Yoda:** foresight that Obi-Wan must fight before Qui-Gon, and teachings on rage and on calling another Jedi.
- **New minor files:** Bruck Chun (tough, angry, outfought), Jemba (huge, strong, immune to "puny" Jedi tricks by his own boast, killed by a stray bolt), Grelb (enforcer, beats Obi-Wan, eaten by a draigon), Clat'Ha (able warrior with blasters), Si Treemba (courage; Arconan dactyl and salt weaknesses).
