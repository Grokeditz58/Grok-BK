- Read 2026-11-20 CST, every line in order. The reading text, READ.txt (1,940 lines), was built from the owner's library "not retail" epub of the 1997 Boulevard paperback. That epub is an OCR conversion: paragraphs split by its page breaks were rejoined, chapter openers were identified by hand from the ALL-CAPS first words and the junk heading lines, and OCR misreadings were corrected (see the README). `[p.N]` markers count the epub's own page-break marks in order (the first page of ch. 1 is p.1). They approximate the paperback pages and were not checked against a scan, since the library has no scan of this book. This part covers READ l.1–912.
- Scope (owner's rulings, 2026-11-20): every line was read, but only scaling-relevant material is noted. That means feats of every kind (Force, lightsaber, strength, durability, speed, reflexes, agility, stamina, pain tolerance, marksmanship, hand-to-hand, tech and tactics, senses), skills, statements about characters, narrative portrayal, and anti-feats and limitations. Ordinary non-Force piloting is not noted (the ore-hauler docking in ch. 6, Sirra's take-off in ch. 18, Luke's flying in ch. 22); Force-assisted piloting is (Zekk vs. Dengar, ch. 5). Each chapter keeps a short Plot bullet (our words) so the story can be followed.
- Citation: "JB ch. N, p.X" uses the epub page-break count described above; the citation gives the page where the paragraph starts. Quotes are pasted by script from READ.txt (anchor expansion), never retyped. "…" inside a quote is the book's own ellipsis.
