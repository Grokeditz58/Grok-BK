# EA9 Rescue in the Core: notes

## Front matter
- Gungan dialect note ("try reading them out loud"). Intro: a petition with more than 300 signatures to banish Jar Jar was torn up by Boss Nass, who gave Jar Jar a job instead.

## Ch. 1–2
- Jar Jar is the night janitor at the Otoh Gunga Zoological Research Facility, where every specimen is endangered. Lore on Gungan bubbles: hydrostatic membranes, portal zones, utanode assembly braces, "grown" buildings. The core is the underwater route to Theed.
- He leaves a hatch open. The black-horned hrumphs stampede the control room and burst the aquarium bubbles. Loose: the Great Hohokum (more than 400 years old), **Vink and Nink, the dwarf opee sea killers**, an albino dianoga, fanbacks and daggerts.
- Director Huff Zinga thinks about feeding him to saber-toothed granks. Boss Nass sentences him to a year of probation (banishment if there is another accident) and 6 months at **the Quarry**, a prison grotto at the core's edge, unused for 3 generations, where convicts pounded boulders into sand and back again. "Wesa ganna open it just for yousa." He is spared a pounding only out of respect for his late parents.

## Ch. 3–5
- Commander Wollod pilots a tribubble bongo (15 m, three bubbles, ejectable cockpit). Captain Tarpals has the army transport and Rep Teers the Rep heyblibber. Traffic Control: Goodrow Neb Nob.
- A seaquake hits. Rep Teers and Major Fassa go out of contact. Nass: "Dis pleasure cruise isa now official-ly a rescue mission!" Wollod and Nass are both knocked out.
- **Jar Jar, still in binders, rights the rolling bongo and slips under a ledge before the boulders fall.** He takes the key, launches the distress beacon ("Ipsee, mipsee, monsee… mope!"), and gets loose of a 60 m colo claw fish by reversing into it, which slams its head against the wall. He boosts the hydrostatic field to go over a waterfall, then blows the buoyancy tanks against 12 drain vents. He reaches the Quarry: glowing stalactites, woosha plants.

## Ch. 6–8
- Cambylictus bark as a bandage, for Wollod's head cut.
- **The albino dianoga fight:** he jams sand down its throat, stuns it, and nets it alive.
- Old fortress: a giant skeleton picked clean. **Two veermoks:** one knocks itself out on the wall; the other throws a tooth, the stunner runs empty, and the thrown electropole jolts it down.
- Lift tube, guard post, alarm bell. He breaks the lever panel and tries the levers: the drains seal, the water rises, and a secret tunnel opens. A spydr is shocked with the electropole. The floor drops into a pit with dozens of veermoks. Stepping stones turn out to be a behemoth's spine. **He escapes into Lake Umberbool.**
- He calls Vink and Nink, nets them, and they rip free. **He lures them into a sub pen and seals it by hitting a brace that sets off the emergency seal.**

## Ch. 9–10
- He goes into the core alone. A **sando aqua monster** has grabbed the sub-pen bubble. Jar Jar ejects the cockpit in a panic. The monster lets go, the heyblibber escapes, and the pod bursts in its mouth. **The Great Hohokum carries Jar Jar away on its tusks.**
- Tarpals arrests him. Boss Nass: "Yousa could've leave-ed uss-ens dare… mesa berry proud." Rewards: the festival, no Quarry. He stays on probation.
- **Major Fassa** (Boss Nass's niece, haillu to her knees) swears him **a life debt**. This sets up his entry in the Festival of Warriors. Continuity: he is a life-debt receiver here, before his TPM life debt to Qui-Gon. In TPM he has been banished, so the probation breaks later.
