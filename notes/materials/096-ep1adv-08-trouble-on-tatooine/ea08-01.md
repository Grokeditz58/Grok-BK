# EA8 Trouble on Tatooine: notes

## Ch. 1–3
- Kitster dreams of his father: a big man with dark hair, a white scar from the thumb joint to mid-wrist, and a gold bracelet like a manacle. "You're going to go far."
- Kitster is stunned and chained. The Weequay guards kick him. A slave execution is announced for sunset at the arena: "only five wupiupi".
- Jira says the smuggler ship arrives in under 4 hours and she doesn't have enough money. Anakin: "Get the money… I'll be ready."
- Kitster rubs grease from his cuffs onto his face as a disguise, and **Gardulla does not recognize him** (she owns thousands of slaves). She lists the crimes: bombing her fortress, Podracer exhaust, and cleanser sprayed in her mouth. She offers to spare 2 Ghostlings if they name the boy who got away. Pala says nothing. Gardulla admires Dorn's jammer: "Ingenious." An interrogator droid is used.

## Ch. 4–5
- Anakin sells his junk for glow rods, sandmasks and a hand-held beamdrill. He scouts the arena's sand drains.
- **Pala holds out under the interrogator droid** ("Madame Vansitt can yell louder"). So does Kitster, for about 3 hours. Gardulla: "What can one child do?"
- Dorn in the transport: "dangerous because he had dared to say no". He tells a Gamorrean guard "I defy you" and gets stun-batoned.
- Anakin comes up through the cell drain and **plasma-cuts every lock**. The alarms go off.

## Ch. 6–7
- **The Jawa ion blaster fired down a pipe knocks out 3 capture droids at once.** The masters flood the pipes with sand and the current sweeps everyone into a catch basin about 80×40 m.
- **Anakin bites a rock wart until it runs.** "It tastes awful… You can all take a bite."
- A Ghostling boy, Coniel (about 3), nearly drowns in sand.

## Ch. 8–10
- **Beamdrill used as a flamethrower slices a womp rat.** Anakin marks Xs on the pipes to find his way back.
- Morseerian space pirates (four arms, methane breathers) shoot at his womp-rat growl.
- **Anakin cuts their freight box open and takes about 3,000 wupiupi.** He bluffs with the beamdrill (a methane mask could explode) and they run firing four blasters. "Better get a new lock."
- A slave at the Galactic Food Emporium rips the grate open for him: "You've got twenty minutes."
- Dorn carries Coniel and draws the hunters off. Blaster fire is heard, and he is presumed lost.

## Ch. 11–13
- Anakin outruns Rodian slave hunters in Watto's speeder ("there wasn't a faster speeder on Tatooine") and passes Tuskens on a bantha.
- At Bantha Rock, Jira's glow-rod signal brings in a Corellian freighter. The smuggler is **Rakir Banai, Kitster's father** (same scar and bracelet: "I was a slave once, too"). He says he can handle the transmitters.
- **Anakin turns down freedom** for his mother. He seems to listen to a distant voice: "Yes they do… I hope." That reads as Force hinting.
- Dorn arrives in Jabba's speeder with Coniel. Kitster knocks his father's shot wide. There is life support for one less, and **Kitster gives Dorn his seat without telling his father who he is**.

## Ch. 14
- Sebulba had planned Dorn's death by a flesh-eater and a blindfolded Gamorrean shooting at Ghostlings. The ship escapes, and he shoots Jabba's speeder to pieces.
- About 1,000 left over goes to Anakin and Kitster (the text also says 500).
- Shmi works late covering for her friend Matta, whose master is Dengula. Her ethic: "no one helps anyone else."
- **Podrace:** Anakin "fuses" with the Podracer (Force instinct). The mystery box "opened from the inside" comes back. **Sebulba's vent flash at Metta Drop wrecks him.** He cuts the thrusters by instinct and the cockpit bounces. This is the backstory to his TPM-era crashes.
