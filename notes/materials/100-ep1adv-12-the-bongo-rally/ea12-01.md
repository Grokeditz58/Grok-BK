# EA12 The Bongo Rally: notes

## Ch. 1
- Swagg's log shows he had studied Gungan culture and technology. Jar Jar's probation dates from his destroying the zoo habitat bubbles.
- Tarpals turns down a parade and asks for a Bongo Rally ticket.
- **Rep Been on the ancient law:** no Gungan may pledge a life debt to a Gungan on probation. Fassa pledged after Jar Jar's probation began, so **the life debt is void.** Nass lifts Been in a hug.
- Fassa withdraws the pledge, then kisses Jar Jar's cheek.

## Ch. 2
- Swagg's escape pod has no hyperdrive (15 days to his hideout) and he is wanted in at least 9 systems. His droids carry homing devices: **3-S and 5-S** are in Otoh Gunga, and 4-S is the disposal head. He modified the pod to go underwater and chooses revenge.

## Ch. 3
- Racers Spleed Nukkels and Neb Neb Goodrow wrecked their bongos in a practice crash. Tarpals hands Jar Jar a manta-shaped plasticel ticket for a box and cuts ties: "no botter mesa ever again."
- Nass catches Jar Jar bathing in the sink and hires him as a waiter for the party.

## Ch. 4
- Swagg floods the pod and swims into the sub pen. He breaks the droids' restraints with a prybar and **knocks out Lob Dizz with one blow to the neck**. His plan: weapons, a bongo, and taking out the comm beacon. The rally starts in 40 minutes.

## Ch. 5–6
- Lob Dizz is building new bongos for the racers. Explosions hit her lab. Jar Jar is trapped behind a cabinet and **greases himself with Slather to squeeze through the gap** (a cave explorers' trick).
- The droids drop mines from Nukkels's stolen red tribubble bongo.
- **Nukkels** pulls a mine away in her wake, plays chicken to judge the droids' piloting, and chases them.

## Ch. 7
- The comm beacon is wrecked. They find the ferry pilot knocked out: Swagg has taken the ferry.

## Ch. 8
- Nukkels slips between two mines with a 45° roll and uses a drag chute to stop. The arena: a central bubble for 20,000, 4 habitat bubbles, 3 sub pens each.
- **A droid clubs the pen attendant with one blow.** Nukkels rides a cargo sled after them and crashes.

## Ch. 9
- A droid throws a nyork barrel at Jar Jar, who eats 3 nyorks. **Jar Jar impales a droid on a full-power electropole** (reflex) and it fries.
- The 2nd droid knocks out Brooboo Seep, steals his suit, and sabotages the lift. Jar Jar splices the cable. The course is a ~2 km tube with 20 bongos.

## Ch. 10
- Tarpals is sharing a box with Fassa, who whispers to him and makes him smile. Fassa doubts Jar Jar; **Tarpals: "Jar Jar's onda level."** Jar Jar slips away before Nass sees him.

## Ch. 11
- Tarpals commandeers a bongo and chases the droid in the rally. He reverses up the downward loop and takes the plorkscrew helix.
- **The droid jumps the bongo out through the hydrostatic fields to the lower straightaway** (never done before), and Tarpals copies it. Tarpals rams the reinforced stern and barely dents it. The droid ruptures the tube.
- **Tarpals fakes a dive and passes over the droid to cross the finish line first.** He follows it through the escape tube as its field collapses.
- The droid boards his hull. **Tarpals rolls inverted and scrapes it to ribbons on a barnacle reef.**
- **Swagg's ferry carries ~50 plasma orbs aimed at the dome.** Tarpals jumps between moving subs. His kick makes Swagg fire, and the bolt ricochets into Swagg's chest. **Swagg dies** in the blast, which leaves a small crater on the lake floor. Tarpals swims clear. "Mesa just followed a hunch." He leaves Jar Jar's part out of his report.

## Ch. 12
- At the mansion party Jar Jar is distant with Fassa ("Mesa just una waiter") and kicks the gasser door. Cook Brass Kirs: "Any fool know dat gassers go boom if sumbotty kicken inda door." The bubble field blows and the mansion floods and rolls. Jar Jar tries to save Nass's heyblibber and crashes it into the stairs.

## Ch. 13
- **Jar Jar is banished from Otoh Gunga for life.** Tarpals is his only escort. Jar Jar wishes on a star to go home. The bridge to TPM (he is in exile in the swamp when Qui-Gon finds him).
