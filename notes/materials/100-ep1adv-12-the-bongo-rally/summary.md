# EA12 The Bongo Rally: summary

Full read (1674 lines). Ends the Gungan arc (#10–12) with Swagg dead, Fassa's life debt void, and Jar Jar banished, which sets up TPM.

## Feats
- **Tarpals:** wins the rally in a borrowed bongo while chasing a droid; copies an air jump out of the tube; kills a droid on a reef; boards a moving ferry and kills Swagg with his own ricocheted bolt; prevents ~50 plasma charges hitting the dome over 20,000 people.
- **Spleed Nukkels:** pulls a mine away in her wake; slips between mines; drag-chute stop.
- **Jar Jar:** Slather squeeze; kills a droid on the electropole; lift splice; warns Tarpals.
- **Swagg:** one-blow knockouts (Lob Dizz, the ferry pilot); underwater pod.
- **Droids:** one-blow takedown of the attendant; reckless tube jump; ram-proof hull.

## Anti-feats
- Tarpals barely dents the reinforced bongo stern. Jar Jar's gasser blast floods the mansion.

## Lore and continuity
- **The life-debt law:** a pledge to someone on probation is void. Relevant to Jar Jar's later life debt to Qui-Gon.
- **Jar Jar's banishment is explained** (canon TPM states it).
- Bongo Rally course: ~2 km tube, a three-way fork, a plorkscrew helix, an escape tube.
- Nyorks, Slather, gassers, Brass Kirs.
- Swagg's droid designations: 3-S, 4-S, 5-S. All 14 droids accounted for by the end.
