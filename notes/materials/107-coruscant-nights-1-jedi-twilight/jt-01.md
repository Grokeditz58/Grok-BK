## Ch. 1 (l.209–298)
- Lower-level Coruscant setting (l.211–217). Even Piell: defeated Myk'chur Zug at cost of an eye; survived Geonosis (l.225). Believes he's the only senior Council member to escape the Temple (l.233). Two months in hiding (l.257). Has a "quest" for many lives (l.265).
- FEAT (Piell): senses troopers ~1 km away (l.251); Force-opens a jammed door (l.253); Force-levitates himself up a dead turbolift tube (l.277); cuts out of the tube, Force-carried arc, deflects all bolts of five troopers, "None came close" (l.293). Notes he's far from peak — weeks on the run (l.295).
## Ch. 2 (l.299–368)
- Nick Rostu backstory: gutted by Iolu on Haruun Kal; Force kept him alive (l.303–309). Brevet major, 44th Division "Rostu's Renegades" (l.313). Refused oath, shot two troopers + representative, leapt from a 210th-story window onto a sky lorry, Force told him it was 10 m below (l.325–331). Korunnai Force-touch theory (l.331). Joins the Whiplash after saving a Kitonak (l.337–341). Clones on Muunilinst (l.345).
## Ch. 3 (l.369–430)
- FEAT (Piell): Force leap over five troopers, flawless landing (l.373–377); "sees" through Force after luma flash (l.387).
- ANTI-FEAT (Piell): troopers' luma-grenade + shooting the impact grenade mid-air sucker-punch him; slammed into a pillar, bones snapped, organs burst (l.383–391). Survives briefly only via Force (l.409–411). Passes mission to Nick, naming someone (l.419–429).
## Ch. 4 (l.431–532)
- Jax Pavan as bounty hunter for Rokko the Hutt; Cerean target suicided (l.439–443). LORE (Jax's unique Force-sense): "web-like strands"/threads around beings extending through higher dimensions, showing connections and fates (l.445).
- FEAT (Jax): Force-pulls two blasters from enforcers' hands across 2 m at once (l.465). Can't mind-trick them in bloodlust (l.463).
- Jax knighted three months before the fall (l.485); hid: hair dyed, beard depilated; lightsaber + confounder in greatcoat; holdout blaster, duracris poniard (l.495). Uses Force to cheat sabacc, "suggest" food (l.491). Coruscant population ~1 trillion registered, maybe 3x (l.493). Whiplash <2 months old; Underground Mag-Lev (l.505). Kept Yoda holocron, Dantooine crystal, and a reliquary with a teardrop black metal that glows through the spectrum (l.521). Saw black threads radiating from Anakin, "more than he had ever seen on anyone" (l.525). Felt a searing loss in the Force last night (Piell's death) (l.531).
## Ch. 5 (l.533–602)
- Den Dhur, ex-reporter (Drongar, Phow Ji exposé) is blackballed (l.551–559). I-Five's quest: find Lorn Pavan's son (l.573). FEAT (I-Five): grabs a vibroknife hilt from a Gank and drives it into duracrete; finger lasers, no inhibitors (l.585–591).
## Ch. 6 (l.603–656)
- Kaird of Nediji, Black Sun, rival of Xizor for Vigo under Underlord Dal Perhi (l.613–615). LORE: Falleen pheromones/allelochemicals (l.617). Kaird wants to go home to Nedij (l.633). Midnight Hall in skyhook Sinharan T'sau, 37,730 km tether (l.637). Thula/Squa Tront bota double-cross (l.647).
## Ch. 7 (l.657–712)
- Piell told Nick to find Jax, his ex-Padawan (l.665). Hypertrain 2000 kph; Separatist bombing craters 7 km wide (l.669–671). Nick's Force is "anemic" — mostly akk-dog control (l.689). Five stormtroopers enter the Coruscant Arms (l.707).
## Ch. 8 (l.713–832)
- Rhinann, Elomin aide to Vader, fascinated by the Force (l.715–741). "Do sit down, Rhinann," Darth Vader said (l.743).
- I-Five has a lead: Jax in the Yaam Sector/Blackpit Slums (l.775–779). FEAT (I-Five): disarms two fighters with finger lasers unseen (l.783–785).
- Jax senses dark threads on his door: ≥5 troopers (l.801–805). Suicidal "Let them come… Let them kill you" voice (l.813–821). His Force link "flickered" for an instant (l.829). Blue lightsaber (l.831).
## Ch. 9 (l.833–926)
- Vader knows Piell is dead "So the Force has told me" (l.841). Remaining Jedi are no concern "With one exception… Jax Pavan… He is in the Yaam Sector. I cannot pinpoint his location more specifically without the possibility of alerting him" (l.845). FEAT/LORE (Vader): sector-level sensing of Jax. Brief Force choke on Rhinann (l.849).
- FEAT (Jax): spears the lock, kicks out, kills point man; "the Force was at least three seconds ahead of the moment" and reflects bolts back (l.875–877); squad of five down in seconds with Nick's rear attack (l.879). Force-slows a third-floor drop (l.901); leaps over debris (l.905).
- Nick's threads "grim, durasteel gray" (l.913). Mangler scar (l.917).
## Ch. 10 (l.927–980)

## Ch. 11 (l.981–1092)

LORE: Force can't be measured; midi-chlorians can (l.1061). Den crushes the chip — "Find Jax Pavan. My way" (l.1085–1091).
