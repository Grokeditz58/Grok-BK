## Ch. 23 (l.2041–2202)

Jax switches I-Five off and leaves with Laranth (l.2193–2197).

## Ch. 28 (l.2505–2610)
- Weavers ~65 kph (l.2507). PCBU arms (l.2509). Jax still dodging with the Force; little sleep in three days, "still wasn't at his peak" (l.2515, 2527). I-Five's pressor-field plan: first PCBU spins out and explodes (l.2533–2551).
- FEAT (Laranth): from a moving weaver she shoots incoming slugs and particle beams out of the air — "deflecting them in midshot… she wasn't letting a slug, beam, or blast come anywhere near them" (l.2569–2571). Den pilots through a half-demolished building; a PCBU hits a column (l.2585–2589). FEAT (Laranth): throws a hidden vibroknife into the second PCBU's weapons belt, destroying it; deflects shrapnel "with a wave of her hand"; "I'm never unarmed" (l.2603–2609).
## Ch. 29 (l.2611–2732)
- ANTI-FEAT (Jax): the Force vanishes again — "only a void… he could not access the Force" (l.2617–2621). Charges the PCBU, slashes the repulsor vanes three times from below (l.2627–2633). I-Five lasers the 501-Z's processor (l.2637). Force returns (l.2649).
- Kaird's dart spitter: 15 toxical darts, ten drop a bantha (l.2661). An Ugnaught child spoils his shot (l.2671). FEAT (Xizor): spots the disguise instantly and nearly kills Kaird (l.2671–2673); shoots out the platform under him (l.2697–2699). Kaird: Xizor could bomb Nedij for revenge (l.2689).
- Kaird swings on a live power cable feet-first into Xizor; both shocked (l.2701–2711). Bug-Eyes (10-4TO: glossy black, huge golden insectile eyes, antennae, forearm energy cannon) stuns Kaird on Xizor's order "Again" (l.2713–2731).
## Ch. 30 (l.2733–2814)

I-Five detects Nick lying (skin conductivity, heart rate) (l.2789). ANTI-FEAT (Jax): his Force probe reads Nick "clean" (l.2805) — Nick fooled it (see l.2951).
