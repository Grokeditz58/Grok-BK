## Ch. 43 (l.3781–3872) — Jax vs Xizor
- Green lightwhip, tip breaks the sound barrier; twice the saber's reach; Jax less practiced with whips (l.3783–3793).
- SCALING (Jax's view): "No ordinary humanoid could contend with a Jedi one-on-one and expect to win. Even a true teräs käsi adept… could hope, at best, for a draw" (l.3799).
- Jax diagnoses the blackouts: months of suppressing the Force to hide from Vader became second nature (l.3805–3807).
- FEAT (Xizor): leaps over Jax with a somersault slash — "Jax wouldn't have thought anybody but a Jedi could pull a stunt like that" (l.3813–3815); thumbs the saber off to escape the whip-bind (l.3817). Exchanges (l.3819–3823). ANTI-FEAT (Jax): Xizor stronger than Jax (l.3827).
- FEAT (Jax, no Force): whip shears a charging maintenance droid in two (l.3829). Hit by an astromech's catapult (l.3837).
- FEAT (Jax): in accepting death the Force returns — a reflexive push hurls Xizor three meters into the wall; rises "as if levitated"; smashes the astromech into the wall hard enough to rupture its casing; pulls both weapons to his hands (l.3843–3847). "It had been a matter of the Force choosing him once more as a vessel" (l.3851). FEAT: blows Xizor's pheromones back at him with Force air currents (l.3853).
- FEAT (Xizor): leaps almost as far as a Force-leap; "The prince's mind was powerful, capable of concealing his intentions"; Jax hard-pressed to divine him (l.3859). Grabs Nick's blaster and fires; Jax blocks; Xizor escapes (l.3863–3865).
- FEAT (Jax, sensing): feels Vader arrive — "Someone extremely strong in the Force—stronger than he'd ever encountered before" (l.3869–3871).
