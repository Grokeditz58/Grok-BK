Continuity: 19 BBY, months after Order 66 (timeline l.6784–6790). Even Piell dies passing a mission to Nick Rostu: find his ex-Padawan Jax Pavan and recover the droid 10-4TO ("Bug-Eyes"), which is said to carry vital resistance data. Vader hunts Jax and blackmails Nick by threatening Haruun Kal. I-Five, the droid Jax's father built up, finds Jax with Den Dhur. Black Sun's Xizor and Kaird fight over the droid and over Underlord Perhi. In the Factory District, Jax's Force link keeps cutting out. Nick is impaled, and Jax beats Xizor with Nick's lightwhip. Jax drops his own lightsaber into the reactor and the 12-kiloton blast covers their escape. Rhinann defects with a Sith holocron and reveals the data never existed: the droid was Vader's decoy to flush Jax out. Jax chooses to stay on Coruscant.

Character quick bios:
- **Jax Pavan:**
  - Jedi Knight, knighted three months before the fall (l.485). Works as a bounty hunter in the Blackpit Slums. He senses the Force as "web-like strands" (l.445).
  - Kills a five-trooper squad with the Force "three seconds ahead" (l.875–879). His Force link cuts out four times (l.2617–2621, 2977–2981, 3541–3547, 3715–3733).
  - Beats Xizor with the lightwhip once the Force returns (l.3843–3865). Cuts the shuttle's vanes (l.4029) and stays on Coruscant (l.4165).
  - Parents Lorn and Siena Pavan (l.4202). His pyronium was a gift from Anakin (l.4191).
- **Darth Vader:** senses Jax at sector level (l.845) and invades Nick's mind (l.1913–1917). Nick judges him far stronger than Kar Vastor (l.1887–1891). He engineered the decoy-droid plan (l.4135–4147) and escaped the blast by life pod (l.4101).
- **Even Piell:** Jax's Master. Senses troopers ~1 km away, levitates up a turbolift and deflects all bolts (l.251–293). Killed by a grenade trap (l.383–391). Later appears as a memory lesson: "The Force abets you. It doesn't define you" (l.3573).
- **Nick Rostu:** Korunnai brevet major who fought on Haruun Kal (l.303–331). Vader blackmails him (l.2209–2215). Fools Jax's probe by controlling his autonomics (l.2951). Impaled, but survives (l.3741, 3949–3953).
- **Laranth Tarak:** Gray Paladin, a Twi'lek with dual blasters (l.1203, 1219–1225). Shoots slugs and beams out of the air (l.2569–2571), throws a vibroknife into a PCBU (l.2603–2609) and slags a shuttle cannon (l.4031).
- **I-Five (I-5YQ):** Lorn Pavan's self-aware droid with no inhibitors (l.1847). Wins at sabacc (l.1953), brings down a ceiling (l.2155–2167) and matches Bug-Eyes' beam with his laser (l.3699).
- **Den Dhur:** Sullustan ex-reporter (l.46). Jealous of Jax (l.1839–1851, 2277–2285), but reactivates I-Five to save him (l.3633–3639).
- **Prince Xizor:** Falleen Black Sun Vigo, a teräs käsi master (l.3761). Uses pheromones (l.2995, 3499–3503) and makes a Jedi-like leap (l.3813–3815). Built the Perhi replica droid (l.3285–3313).
- **Kaird of Nedij:** Black Sun assassin who wants to go home (l.633, 1815–1823). Quits Black Sun and funds the group (l.4011, 4171–4175).
- **Haninum Tyk Rhinann:** Vader's Elomin aide (l.50, 715). Steals the Sith holocron and defects (l.3325–3331, 4039–4043, 4111–4147).
- **Dal Perhi:** Black Sun Underlord, rumored to have bested Obi-Wan (l.1445–1447).
