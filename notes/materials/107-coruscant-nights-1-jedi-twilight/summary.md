# Coruscant Nights I: Jedi Twilight: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-04 CST. Full hand-read, l.1–7068. Citation "JT ch. N (l.x)". Detail is in jt-01 to jt-06.
- Author: Michael Reaves (About the Author, l.4216–4218; the books list includes Coruscant Nights I–III, l.4220–4274). This matches the inventory.

## Plot
- **Piell's last mission (ch. 1–3, 7):**
  - Even Piell escapes troopers through a turbolift tube (l.251–293).
  - He is killed by a luma-grenade trap (l.383–391), but first tells Nick Rostu to find Jax Pavan (l.419–429, 665).
- **Jax in hiding (ch. 4, 8–10, 12):**
  - Jax, a bounty hunter in the Blackpit Slums, feels Piell die (l.531).
  - With Nick he wipes out a trooper squad (l.875–879).
  - The mission is to recover the droid 10-4TO ("Bug-Eyes"), said to hold resistance data; its command phrase is "Zu woohama" (l.971–973). Jax is in despair: "Without the Jedi, I am nothing" (l.1135–1187).
- **Vader's hunt (ch. 9, 14, 17–18, 20, 24):**
  - Vader can sense Jax only at sector level (l.845). Rhinann's search narrows it down (l.1253–1291).
  - Smugglers sell Nick to the Palace (l.1669–1679). Vader invades Nick's mind (l.1913–1917) and threatens to scour Clan Rostu's plateau from orbit (l.2209–2215).
- **I-Five and Den (ch. 5, 11, 15, 18, 21, 23, 25):**
  - I-Five is looking for Lorn Pavan's son (l.573) and finds Jax through face-matching on the security grid (l.1383–1419).
  - Sold to Rokko as a sabacc droid (l.1965), he tells Jax: "I am I-Five. Your father sent me" (l.1983).
  - Jax rejects him at first (l.2095), then switches him off (l.2193–2197). Den is tempted to betray Jax (l.2277–2285).
- **Black Sun (ch. 6, 12, 15, 19, 22, 26, 29, 32, 34–35, 37):**
  - Kaird's hypergem trap on Xizor fails (l.1105–1129, 1475).
  - Perhi sends Kaird to kill Xizor (l.1999–2009). Xizor captures Kaird and seizes Bug-Eyes (l.2671–2731).
  - "Perhi" turns out to be a human replica droid Xizor built (l.3285–3313).
- **PCBU chase and the Ugnaught building (ch. 27–30, 33):**
  - Laranth's shooting saves the speeders (l.2569–2609).
  - Jax's Force cuts out twice (l.2617–2621, 2977–2981). Xizor's pheromones let him escape with the droid (l.2995–3011).
- **Feral City (ch. 35–44):**
  - In the Factory District the group meets feral droids (l.3249–3255, 3505–3531). Jax's blade shorts out (l.3515) and he fights without the Force (l.3553–3577).
  - They are all stunned (l.3593). Den reactivates I-Five (l.3633–3639).
  - Nick is impaled (l.3741–3755). **Jax vs Xizor** ends when the Force returns and Jax wins with the lightwhip (l.3783–3865).
  - The data turn out to be gone (l.3901–3905).
- **Vader arrives; the reactor (ch. 44–46):**
  - Jax drops his locked-on saber into the reactor (l.3919–3935). Vader reads the countdown (l.3997).
  - Jax disables the shuttle (l.4029) and Rhinann deserts (l.4039–4043). The 12 kt blast leaves an 80 m crater (l.4071, 4091), and Vader escapes by life pod (l.4101).
  - Rhinann reveals the data were "worthless… The droid was merely a decoy… engineered by Vader" (l.4135–4147). He carries the Sith holocron Lorn once sought (l.4119–4125).
  - Jax stays: "as long as at least one Jedi remains" (l.4165). His last line is "Tell me about my father" (l.4214).

## Strongest feats (power-scaling)
1. **Jax, Force return vs Xizor:** a reflexive push throws Xizor 3 m into a wall. Jax rises "as if levitated," smashes an astromech so its casing ruptures, pulls both weapons to his hands (l.3843–3847) and turns Xizor's pheromones back on him with Force-driven air (l.3853). He wins the whip duel (l.3859–3865).
2. **Jax, squad kill:** "the Force was at least three seconds ahead of the moment." He reflects bolts back, and five troopers go down in seconds with Nick's help (l.875–879).
3. **Vader, sensing and mind invasion:** he senses Jax at sector level, but won't narrow it for fear of alerting him (l.845). He gets "inside his [Nick's] head, a dark shadow," and Nick blacks out (l.1913–1917). In Nick's view, Kar Vastor "wouldn't stand a chance"; Vader's Force is "far more powerful… contained. Pent" (l.1887–1891).
4. **Jax, ~20 m Force leap** to the Lambda shuttle, where he slices its forward repulsor vanes with the whip (l.4023–4029). He also makes a ~10 m leap (l.2999) and a flip (l.3513).
5. **Jax without the Force:** he takes down an 8D8, hamstrings a tread droid and bisects a maintenance droid (l.3553–3577). He stabs Bug-Eyes between its plates with a dagger (l.3725–3727) and shears a droid with the whip (l.3829).
6. **Laranth:** shoots incoming slugs and particle beams out of the air from a moving weaver (l.2569–2571). Destroys a PCBU with a thrown vibroknife (l.2603–2609). Shoots out Bug-Eyes' cannon barrel (l.2985–2987) and slags a shuttle cannon (l.4031).
7. **Xizor:** leaps over Jax, a stunt "anybody but a Jedi" couldn't do (l.3813–3815). Leaps nearly as far as a Force-leap, and his mind hides his intent from Jax (l.3859). Backhands Nick across the chamber (l.3665). Is physically stronger than Jax (l.3827). His pheromones confuse two Jedi (l.2995, 3011).
8. **Even Piell:** senses troopers ~1 km away (l.251), levitates up a turbolift tube (l.277) and deflects every bolt from five troopers (l.293), all while past his peak (l.295).
9. **I-Five:** his finger laser meets a particle beam in midair (l.3699). He brings down a ceramisteel ceiling (l.2155–2167), plays a perfect 23 at sabacc (l.1953) and drives a vibroknife into duracrete (l.585–591).
10. **Vader, tactical:** reads the detonation timing at a glance, "Fourteen minutes" (l.3997). Survives the 12 kt blast (l.4101). His decoy plan fooled Piell, the resistance and Black Sun (l.4135–4147).

## Anti-feats
- **Jax:**
  - Four Force blackouts (l.2617–2621, 2977–2981, 3541–3547, 3715–3733) and a mimed push that fails (l.3731–3735). His own diagnosis is that months of suppressing the Force to hide from Vader made it second nature (l.3805–3807). Nick senses the link failing (l.3229–3239).
  - His mind trick fails on Den (l.2337–2339) and his probe is fooled by Nick (l.2805, 2951). He can't mind-trick enforcers in bloodlust (l.463).
  - The vane droid's discharge shorts out his saber (l.3515). Xizor is physically stronger (l.3827), and an astromech catapult hits him (l.3837).
- **Even Piell:** a luma-grenade and an impact grenade shot mid-air kill him (l.383–391).
- **I-Five:** Bug-Eyes' beam overpowers his laser and hurls him into a wall (l.3705). His ceiling-glaze threat was a bluff (l.2183–2189).
- **Xizor:** Jax wins the duel; Xizor is hit by his own pheromones and flees (l.3853–3865).
- **Vader:** his shuttle is crippled and the blast nearly kills him (l.4029, 4101). Rhinann deserts him (l.4039–4043).

## Scaling statements
- Nick: Jax's power "wasn't nearly as powerful as Vader's," but he "could easily sweep aside any feeble mental defenses" (l.3217).
- Jax: "Even a true teräs käsi adept… could hope, at best, for a draw" against a Jedi (l.3799). Teräs käsi is "particularly effective against Jedi" (l.3761).
- Jax on Vader: "stronger than he'd ever encountered before" (l.3869–3871).
- Falleen are "much stronger than humans, and their reflexes much faster" (l.3657).
- Laranth "better in a fight than any five other warriors he knew" (l.1239). Gray Paladins use minimal Force (l.1219–1225).
- Perhi was rumored to have bested Obi-Wan Kenobi (l.1445–1447).

## Lore
- Jax's Force-sense shows threads of connection and fate (l.445). Black threads radiate from Anakin (l.525).
- The Force can't be measured, though midi-chlorians can (l.1061). A Bothan brain-wave Force tracker has a range of ~20 m (l.1057–1069).
- HRDs vs Kaminoan clones: under three months vs ten years (l.3297–3309).
- The Sith holocron SD41263.1 is the one Lorn tried to buy from Zippa, and only a Force-user can open it (l.3325–3331, 4119–4125).
- A lightsaber melts through a 20 cm blast door (l.3935).
- Coruscant has ~1 trillion registered beings, perhaps 3x that in reality (l.493). 40-story construction droids are at work (l.2015). The Factory District and Cthons (l.3089).
- I-Five's dampers were removed by Lorn; "I am because I think" (l.2867–2881). The Jedi Code closes the novel (l.4195–4198).
- Jax's parents are Lorn and Siena Pavan (l.4202). His pyronium was a gift from Anakin (l.4189–4191).
