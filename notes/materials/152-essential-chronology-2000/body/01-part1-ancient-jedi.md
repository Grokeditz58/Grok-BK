# PART I — TALES OF THE ANCIENT JEDI KNIGHTS — P1–20 (S016–)

## Opening (P1 / S016)
Old Republic history would fill a thousand libraries; some events become legend. Millennia blur details into contradictions/myth; overall conflicts real.
**Pre-Republic:** Corellian system artificially created → evidence of powerful alien architects (also possibly Maw black-hole cluster near Kessel). Coruscant: Taungs vs Battalions of Zhell; Zhell defeated by volcanic eruption; ash plume over Taungs 2 years → Warriors of the Shadow / **Dha Werda Verda** (epic poem); battlefield now under Imperial City skyscrapers.
**Xim the Despot:** most celebrated pre-Republic conqueror; Tion Hegemony; vast armies incl. earliest-known combat automatons (war droids). Empire hundreds of thousands of worlds near Tion. Misjudged Hutt Space / Si’klaata Cluster. Two inconclusive battles on Vontor; Third Battle of Vontor: Xim’s droids vs Hutt conscripts Nikto/Vodran/Klatooinan → Xim vanquished; **Kossak the Hutt** victory.
**~25,000 BBY:** widespread FTL hyperspace → Galactic Republic. Force + Jedi Order as fundamental underpinnings. Earliest Jedi = philosophers (light/dark/living/unifying; Chosen One prophecy). Later active defenders. Training under Masters. Some say order began on **Ossus** (unverified).

## EMERGENCE OF THE SITH

### The Golden Age of the Sith — 5000 B.B.Y. (P2–4 / S017–019)
Great Hyperspace War preview: distant/forgotten Sith Lords vs unsuspecting Republic; high Old Republic period; millennia of impact.
**Sith origins:** First great schism within Jedi (light vs dark); ~century of war; defeated dark Jedi fled beyond known space; found + dominated primitive **Sith** people (treated as gods). With resources + willing slaves forged Sith Empire—golden age of evil, far from Perlemian Trade Route (Republic’s outermost border then). Over millennia lost charts/hyperspace maps → forgot how to find Republic. Republic side: Jedi revolt faded to folktales.
**Republic ~5000 BBY:** expanding; rugged frontiers; hyperspace paths still mapped; travel treacherous.
**Gav & Jori Daragon:** hyperspace mappers; *Starbreaker 12*; Force potential but no Jedi patience; hoped fees from Brotherhood of Navigators. Near-disaster → destitute; ship in Cinnagar repair dock; stole own ship fleeing creditors → random jump into unknown.
**Sith crisis:** After century rule, Dark Lord **Marka Ragnos** died → power vacuum. Funeral on mausoleum world **Korriban**; slave sacrifices; new tomb. Rivals **Naga Sadow** (expand; unproven magic/alchemy) vs **Ludo Kressh** (conservative). Shade of Ragnos: empire’s fate hinges on their conflict.
*Starbreaker 12* arrives mid-confrontation; Daragons captured as spies → **Ziost**. Kressh: precursors to invasion; Sadow: vast field to conquer + external enemy to unify strained factions. Tribunal sentences death; Sadow engineers “escape” with Massassi; plants fake Republic-agent evidence. Galvanizes Empire to strike first. Initiates Gav into Sith sorcery; separates siblings.
**Illustration:** Gav and Jori Daragon (ornate attire).
Jori demands ship (only return course in navicomputer). Kressh raids Sadow’s fortress; Sadow planned it—during chaos sends Jori alone home while trapping Gav. Then unleashes real reserves; crushes Kressh’s fleet/flagship (Kressh presumed dead); crowns himself Dark Lord.
**Illustration caption:** “Naga Sadow’s invasion fleet.”

### The Great Hyperspace War — 5000 B.B.Y. (P5–6 / S020–)
Jori’s ship carries Sadow’s **homing beacon** to Republic heart. Arrives Teta system; arrested fraud/felony/theft; warnings dismissed; sentenced to harsh colony; escapes; reaches **Empress Teta**. Jedi who listen: young **Odan-Urr**, master **Ooroo**, adviser **Memit Nadill** → recall dark Jedi exile legends; convince Teta to prepare. Teta to Coruscant for support—most worlds refuse; few Jedi heed.
Sadow launches surprise raid via *Starbreaker 12* coords into Teta system; war fleets + Jedi vs Sith sorcery. Empress Teta talented commander; Sadow’s sketchy galactic knowledge → unpredictable. Stand at flare-active red giant **Primus Goluud**: Sadow dupes Gav, triggers stellar-core explosion. Jori (no military training but skilled pilot) clashes with brainwashed Gav; he redeems, asks forgiveness; dies in supernova.
Republic rallies: Memit Nadill’s Jedi on Coruscant; Odan-Urr on **Kirrek** (Master Ooroo dies). Sadow retreats → finds Kressh alive; Sith-on-Sith ambush devastates both; arriving Republic fleets finish them.

### Hyperspace War close + Sadow exile (P6–7 / S021–022)
**Illustration (P6):** caption “The defense of Coruscant.” (dense battle art; character IDs in vision descriptors not independently verified for 5000 BBY context—treat as caption-locked only.)
Sadow all-or-nothing diversion: escapes damaged flagship, sacrifices rest; flies between binary **Denarii Nova**, manipulates solar flares to destroy pursuers. Retains one ship + Massassi → jungle moon of gas giant **Yavin**; entombs warship; Massassi as guardians. Suspended animation via Sith tech/sorcery awaiting new dark champion (Ragnos’s foretold golden age).
**Ooroo’s prophecy** (dying Kirrek): trainee Odan-Urr founds vast library, lives to old age among books. Odan-Urr builds grand museum city on **Ossus** from Ooroo’s artifacts + Sith wreckage; dies ~1000 yrs later at start of Sith War—among books as predicted.

## LEGACY OF THE SITH

### The Shadow of Freedon Nadd — 4400 B.B.Y. (P7–8 / S022–023)
Sith Empire crumbled; Republic optimism; expansion/new species. Evil returns as internal cancer.
~6 centuries after Sadow exile: ambitious Jedi **Freedon Nadd** follows rumors to Yavin. Massassi degenerated to primitive savages but remember past when awed by Force; show Nadd Sadow’s resting place. Nadd awakens Sadow; learns Sith sorcery. Sadow’s later fate unknown. Nadd makes himself king on primitive **Onderon** (outside Old Republic).
Onderon: Dxun moon predators → walled city **Iziz**. Nadd dark-side rule; develops destructive tech vs beasts then internal rebels. Banishes criminals/dissenters beyond walls (to be eaten); survivors domesticate beasts → centuries guerrilla **Beast Wars**.
After Nadd’s death: sarcophagus = dark-side focus for descendants; civil war continues. Centuries later Republic/Jedi encounter Onderon → crisis ready to explode.
**Illustration (P8):** “The Beast Wars of Onderon” (beast-riders over city).

### Trials of the Jedi — 4000 B.B.Y. (P8– / S023–)
Republic survival: wise governance + Jedi Knights as warrior society for harmony/justice. (continues next)

### Trials of the Jedi cont. — 4000 B.B.Y. (P9 / S024)
Jedi Master **Arca Jeth** (Arkania) stewardship of Onderon → sends students **Ulic & Cay Qel-Droma** + Twi’lek **Tott Doneeta**. Greeted by **Queen Amanoa**; “beast riders” kidnap citizens; daughter **Galia** abducted. Rescue reveals Galia + warlord **Oron Kira** planned marriage to unify groups. Amanoa refuses peace; taps Freedon Nadd’s power; summons Iziz armies. Cay loses arm → droid prosthetic. Arca arrives; beast riders + light defeat Amanoa’s darkness. Galia & Oron restore peace.
**Parallel — Nomi Sunrider:** wife of newly trained Jedi **Andur**; infant **Vima**; Force-sensitive but reluctant. Traveling to Stenness to deliver Adegan crystals to Master **Thon**; thugs murder Andur; Force vision → takes his lightsaber, slays attackers. Goes to Thon on bleak **Ambria** (armor-plated Tchuukthai); trains without lightsaber vow; pirates force defense; masters **Jedi battle meditation**. Thon brings her to Ossus → Master **Vodo-Siosk Baas**; builds personal lightsaber.
**Illustrations:** archaic lightsaber hand; “Ulic Qel-Droma and Arca Jeth.”

### The Naddist Revolt — 3998 B.B.Y. (P10 / S025)
Onderon unrest: sect revering Freedon Nadd. Arca + students try move Nadd + Amanoa sarcophagi to Dxun; Naddists attack from under Iziz; Arca struck by dark-side energy / captured. Ossus Jedi task force (incl. Nomi) joins Ulic; descend to rescue Arca.
**Satal Keto & cousin Aleema** (heirs, corrupt Empress Teta system): bored aristocrats dabbling in Sith magic with artifacts Odan-Urr recovered post–Hyperspace War; call themselves **Krath**. Steal ancient Sith book from Coruscant museum; come to Onderon seeking translation/teachers amid uprising.

### Naddist close + The Coming Ruin — 3997 B.B.Y. (P11–13 / S026–028)
Ommin delighted by Sith book; gives Satal amulet; Freedon Nadd specter abandons Ommin for Keto aristocrats as rebirth keys. Jedi rescue Arca; Ulic kills Ommin. Satal/Aleema flee to Teta with artifacts; Nadd taunts Arca/Ulic that dark side will claim others. Republic martial law Iziz; Nadd sarcophagus sealed on Dxun behind **Mandalorian iron**.
**Coming Ruin:** Krath coup in Empress Teta system; kill aristocrats; crush seven-world revolt with Sith powers. Word reaches Onderon Jedi.
**Exar Kun (Dantooine):** Vodo’s most talented apprentice; superior vs Crado; Sylvar claws his face; defeats Vodo without restraint; claims finished learning. Studies Hyperspace War/Sith legends; to Onderon as “archaeologist”; Arca refuses arrogant help. Breaks Dxun tomb (Mandalorian iron); Nadd specter shows metal scrolls; prophesies dark future—Kun dismisses then kills mercenary robbers in unthinking violence.
**Korriban:** crypt collapse crushes Kun; Nadd demands dark-side surrender for rescue; empty vow still floods power, heals him naked on clay; shriek reaches Vodo (en route to **Deneba** Jedi convocation). Convocation (Odan-Urr chairs): discuss Krath/Sith threat—interrupted by automated pods raining **Krath war droids**. Master Arca killed by projectile while saving Ulic → Ulic despair → vows infiltrate Krath alone despite Thon/Cay/Nomi warnings; departs for Cinnagar.
**Yavin 4:** Nadd sends Kun to Sadow’s resting place; Massassi capture/sacrifice attempt → Kun calls dark side; obliterates Nadd specter. **Illustrations:** Exar Kun portrait (scarred; Yavin temples); “Reawakening of evil” (Massassi/temple).

### Coming Ruin close (P14 / S029)
Kun Dark Lord; Massassi build temples; finds Sadow battleship. Ulic infiltrates Cinnagar as “fallen Jedi”; saves Aleema from assassin; Satal tortures + injects **Sith poison** (lingering mind effect); inducted as Krath general / Aleema’s pet. Nomi rescue attempt → Ulic orders her execution to keep cover; she escapes via battle meditation; misses his secret message. Later rescue fails; Ulic casts Nomi away. **Kun vs Ulic duel**; both Sith amulets shimmer → shade of **Marka Ragnos** commands alliance for Sith glory return; they clasp hands.

### The Sith War — 3996 B.B.Y. (P15 / S030)
Kun converts weak-willed Jedi at Ossus (Crado idolizes; Sylvar refuses). Steals Naga Sadow holocron from Odan-Urr; **kills Odan-Urr** (fulfills Ooroo prophecy). Lies that Odan-Urr sacrificed knowledge; binds converts with Sith spell on Yavin 4; Crado second-in-command. Ulic: Krath lightning strikes for supplies; all-out Coruscant attack → captured by Nomi/Cay/Doneeta/Vodo/Thon; Aleema escapes. Senate trial; Kun storms in with Massassi; hypnotizes assembly; **kills Master Vodo** with double-bladed lightsaber. Convert assassins murder many Jedi Masters; Crado fails vs Thon.
Kun sends Crado + Aleema to retrieve Sadow flagship weapon (intended victims for incompetence/treachery); they trigger **Cron cluster** starstorm, lose control, die; shock wave toward Ossus.

### The Devastation of Ossus — 3996 B.B.Y. (P16 / S031)
Ossus evacuation; Kun raids; librarian **Ood Bnar** transforms into gigantic tree protecting buried lightsaber cache, drives Kun back. Ulic vs Cay dogfight → Ulic shoots Cay down, kills brother in fury → collapses in horror. Nomi’s Force technique **blinds Ulic to the Force** (strips powers). Ulic offers to lead strike vs Kun on Yavin 4. Farewell to Ood Bnar facing shock wave.
**Illustration:** “The tragedy that turned the tide” (Ulic cradling Cay; Nomi/Doneeta).

### Sith War end — Kun spirit trap (P17 / S032)
Kun chains self at Great Temple focal point; drains Massassi life force → Sith sorcery preserves spirit in temples ~4,000 years (until Luke’s academy students). Jedi orbital bombardment obliterates jungle/temples; Sith War ends.

### The Redemption of Ulic Qel-Droma — 3986 B.B.Y. (P17 / S032)
Ten years later: Force-blind Ulic exiles to fortress on frozen **Rhen Var**. Vima Sunrider (Nomi’s neglected daughter) flees Exis Station convocation seeking training; Ulic teaches honor/duty; she loves him as father. Sylvar + Nomi + scavenger **Hoggon** track them; Sylvar seeks vengeance for Crado but she and Nomi forgive Ulic. Hoggon shoots Ulic in back for “history.” Ulic vanishes into light as luminous being (true Jedi Master state). Vima becomes one of greatest Jedi of her age.
**Illustration:** Vima Sunrider with carved creature bust.

### Repercussions through the Republic — 4000–3000 B.B.Y. (P18 / S033)
**Kanz Disorders:** Provisional Governor Myrial of Argazda dictatorship while Jedi distracted; bombed Lorrd, enslaved population; Lorrdians developed advanced nonverbal communication (still seen); totalitarian ~3 centuries until Jedi topple. Cost ~5 billion lives.
**Hapes Consortium:** Jedi (incl. Arca Jeth) eliminate Lorell Raiders → Hapan women establish queen-mother matriarchy; near-total isolation ~3 millennia until Leia reopens contact.
**Hydian Way:** ~3000 BBY pioneer Freia Kallea discovers major hyperspace route spanning galaxy width; colonization beyond the Slice.

### The New Sith — 2000–1000 B.B.Y. (P18 / S033)
Sith species gone after Golden Age; “Sith” now dark-side cult. ~2000 BBY rogue Jedi founds new Sith order (like Kun); others join; millennium of war vs Republic; destroyed by internal schisms/bloodbath. Survivors → Battle of Ruusan: Lord **Kaan**’s Brotherhood of Darkness (~20,000) vs Lord **Hoth**’s Army of Light; seven battles on Ruusan. Kaan’s thought bomb (Force-energy cauldron) in underground chambers. Hoth marches past severed-head trophies; meeting of Lords in valley.

### Battle of Ruusan illustration (P19 / S034)
Full-page Bill Hughes illustration: **"The Battle of Ruusan"** — armored combatant with lightsaber over fallen warrior in rocky/cavernous setting; debris; secondary figures.

### Ruusan aftermath + Rule of Two + Jedi Valiancy (P20 / S035)
Kaan detonates **thought bomb** → annihilates Army of Light + Brotherhood of Darkness; thousands of spirits trapped in equilibrium in **Valley of the Jedi**. Prophecy: “A Knight shall come, a battle will be fought, and the prisoners go free” — unfulfilled until ~1 year after Endor.
Jedi believe Sith extinct; **Darth Bane** survives; stealth/secrecy philosophy; **Rule of Two** (master + apprentice only) for next thousand years; wait “like monks in a hermitage.”
**Jedi Valiancy 600–400 B.B.Y.:** Fallen Jedi usually banished not executed. **Allya** (fallen Jedi) exiled to Dathomir prison colony ~600 yrs before Palpatine; Force-subjugates prisoners, tames rancors → female “witches” society (Force as magic). ~200 yrs later Jedi training vessel **Chu’unthor** crashes Dathomir; Masters Gra’aton, Vulatan, **Yoda** repelled then Yoda negotiates peace with witches’ leader. **Beldorian the Splendid** (Hutt Jedi) → Nam Chorios crystal-energy dictator. **Ikrit** (Kushiban) finds Exar Kun’s golden sphere on Yavin 4 trapping Massassi spirits; enters centuries-long trance awaiting someone who can free them.
