# Part V — Birth of the New Republic
Source: The Essential Chronology (2000), Kevin J. Anderson & Daniel Wallace; Del Rey. Scan oa8yCByK. Mapping S ≈ P+15.

## PART V open — Birth of the New Republic (P67 / S082)
While Leia/Han/Luke at Bakura, Mon Mothma prepares new galactic government. Eradicating Empire may take decades; Endor = symbolic new era start. Within days: Rebel Alliance → **Alliance of Free Planets** (interim). One month after Endor: Mon Mothma issues **Declaration of a New Republic**. Signatories: Mon Mothma, Leia Organa, Borsk Fey’lya, Admiral Ackbar + officials from Corellia, Kashyyyk, Sullust, Elom → eight form **Provisional Council**; Mothma chief councilor. Several months: avoid large military incursions; consolidate + diplomacy; Wedge Antilles + Rogue Squadron as scouts/negotiators. Empire unfocused without Palpatine; fleet systematically beaten back. Capt. **Gilad Pellaeon** (*Chimaera*) orders Endor fleet retreat/regroup **Annaj** — first Imperial fragmentation signs. Adm. **Harrsk** (Endor task force) refuses orders from “mere captain” Pellaeon → takes fleet to restricted **Deep Core**.

### Imperial Fragmentation — 4–4.5 A.B.Y. (P67 / S082)
Empire “doing a [job of destroying itself]…”; Harrsk first breakaway warlord (Deep Core pocket empire). Without Palpatine/Vader intimidation many subordinates seek own kingdoms; warlordism = primary Empire decline factor.
- Adm. **Teradoc**: miniature empire Deep Core outskirts post-Endor
- Adm. **Gaen Drommel**: SSD *Guardian* subdues home sector
- Grand Moff **Ardus Kaine** (Tarkin successor): **Pentastar Alignment** Outer Rim
- Adm. **Zsinj**: Quelii sector; one of NR’s most formidable foes

### Black Nebula — 4–4.5 A.B.Y. (P68 / S083)
Post-Xizor Black Sun fragmentation. Jeodu **Dequc** tries revive as “Black Nebula”; Mara Jade (Emperor’s Hand) executes him on Svivren just before Endor — later discovers decoy; kills real Dequc months later post-Palpatine. Xizor’s niece **Savan** seeks reunite via **Guri** (human replica droid holding syndicate secrets) on Hurd’s Moon (synaptic rewiring to erase assassin programming). Leia/Han/Lando intervene; shootout; Savan captured; Guri freed (criminal programming purged). Black Sun leaders murder each other; defunct until Coruscant liberation ~3 yrs later.

### Isard’s Ascension — 4.5–5 A.B.Y. (P68–69 / S083–084)
Palpatine’s spirit survived (like Obi-Wan); life essence tortuous journey to Deep Core throneworld **Byss** → young clone body; years reconsolidating in secret. **Sate Pestage** (Grand Vizier) assumes throne; lacks charisma; enemies in Palace. **Ruling Circle** (ex-advisory staff) seeks overthrow Pestage. **Ysanne Isard** (Imp Intelligence head) acts “neutral intermediary” while pitting both against each other for her path to throne. NR threatens wealthy Core world **Brentaal** (near Chandrila). Pestage vows hold; on Isard advice allows incompetent Adm. **Isoto** defend. Despite Baron **Soontir Fel** + 181st, Isoto’s bungling → NR captures Brentaal; Fel taken prisoner.
**Illustration:** “The capture of Brentaal”.

### Isard continued (P69–71 / S084–086)
Pestage blamed for Brentaal loss; realizes Isard betrayed him; secret meet Leia on **Axxila**: offers leave Coruscant undefended for rule of 25 planets; Organa agrees. Isard informs Ruling Circle → arrest warrant; Circle new governing body. Pestage flees **Ciutric**; captured by local governor. NR Rogue Squadron + commandos sent retrieve him (encourage defections) — unsuccessful; Adm. **Krennel** murders Pestage, seizes territory as warlord. Isard ruthlessly exterminates Ruling Circle → assumes throne; holds crumbling Empire >2 years.
~8 months post-Endor: Ackbar push; Isard recalls hundreds of SDs to Coruscant/Core. **Black Sword Command** in **Koornacht Cluster** (Deep Core fringe) ordered depart N’zoth; **Yevetha** dockworkers uprising led by **Nil Spaar** — murder thousands Imperials, seize every SD incl. SSD *Intimidator*. Yevetha cover-up; Isard believes Black Fleet destroyed at Cal-Seti; Koornacht isolated until Black Fleet Crisis ~12 yrs later.
~1 yr post-Endor: Central Committee of Grand Moffs move vs Isard; proclaim **Trioculus** (ex-Kessel slavelord) Imperial leader. Some follow; bulk of fleet incl. Pellaeon/*Chimaera* loyal to Isard. NR via short-lived **SPIN** (Senate Planetary Intelligence Network) moves vs grand moffs. Isard frees Jabba’s father **Zorba** as unwitting agent; Zorba seizes Cloud City from Lando. Trioculus, Zorba, **Prophets of the Dark Side** wipe each other out; conspirator grand moffs executed; Isard more secure.
Tycho Celchu (Rogue) infiltrates Coruscant in Bakura-captured TIE; captured → *Lusankya* prison; escapes; NR suspects sleeper agent.

### General Skywalker — 5–5.5 A.B.Y. (P71 / S086)
NR campaign for **Milagro** (hyperspace junction); 3 months combat AT-ATs; Imperials “slag” surface then flee. NR uses Dreadnaught *New Hope* as orbital HQ; damaged Imp SD arrives for repairs → brawl; Han leads fighters; Mothma coordinates; Luke’s X-wing tactics force surrender. Captured SD renamed ***Crynyd*** (honoring Arvel Crynyd). Luke promoted **General** — loathes command burden; post-Bakura more interested in Force spirituality; Sibwarra encounter + **Kyle Katarn** inspiration (DS plans recovery; Dark Trooper sabotage 5 yrs prior). Warlord cabal led by Dark Jedi **Jerec** seeks **Valley of the Jedi** on Ruusan to overthrow Isard; Katarn single-handedly defeats Jerec; Luke offers Jedi apprenticeship — Katarn declines. Brutal NR Inner Rim campaign; battlefields of **Mindor**.
**Illustration:** Ysanne Isard.

### Mindor close + The Last Grand Admiral? — 6 A.B.Y. (P71–72 / S086–087)
Lord **Shadowspawn** stormtroopers fight to last man; Luke dismayed by loss of life → resigns NR military <6 months after general’s commission.
Palpatine rewarded ambition with titles; best stormtroopers → Royal Guards → Imperial Sovereign Protectors on Byss. **Grand Admiral** = highest Navy rank (max 12); white uniforms + gold epaulets; unparalleled strategists. Had they united post-Endor NR might have died in infancy. Fates: Zaarin (failed pre-Endor coup); many on DS2; **Syn** outfought by Ackbar at Kashyyyk liberation (flagship vaporized); **Grunger** & **Pitta** warlords destroy each other over Corellian sector; **Takel** executed by Trioculus; **Il-Raz** suicides flagship into Denarii Nova; **Batch** assassinated by 2IC who joins Harrsk; **Grant** (“so-called last”) defects to NR ~2 yrs post-Endor → immunity, retires Rathalay. NR unaware of remaining: blue-skinned **Thrawn** (secret promotion after Zaarin treason → Unknown Regions).

### The Battle for Coruscant — 6.5–7 A.B.Y. (P72–73 / S087–088)
~2½ yrs after NR birth Empire still holds majority settled planets + Core. Stepping-stone planets; Wedge recalled from propaganda tour → reformed Rogue Squadron. After two costly attacks capture **Borleias** (Colonies) as forward base. Capital defensive energy shield would negate orbital bombardment — must fall first. Rogues undercover Imperial City sabotage shield generator; also 16 worst criminals freed from Kessel spice mines to foster chaos (opposed by many council; proven correct) — led by **Y’ull Acib** resurrect Black Sun (later plague). Rogues condense water vapor via commandeered 40-story construction droid + remote-control orbiting solar mirror → flash-boil artificial reservoir → monster storm → lightning collapses shields. Ackbar full armada; surprisingly easy — Isard kept only handful of SDs; Ackbar wipes resistance → **Coruscant in NR hands**.
**Illustration:** “Coruscant falls to the New Republic”.

### Krytos Virus continued (P73–75 / S088–090)
Derricote engineered artificial **Krytos virus** (“healthy flesh into bloody soup”); Isard seeded Coruscant water reservoirs before Rebels arrived; tailored to nonhumans (Sullustans, Gamorreans etc.) — humans immune → political wedge. NR appears ineffectual; Mothma spends millions credits bacta/vaccine → near-bankrupt. Tycho Celchu trial: treason + murder of Corran Horn (alleged *Lusankya* brainwashed agent) — innocent; Horn alive (captured by Isard; met Jan Dodonna in *Lusankya*). Horn escape forces Isard flee; SSD ***Lusankya*** rises from under Manarai Mountains district, obliterates homes/businesses, vanishes hyperspace with Isard + many prisoners. Corran Horn return clears Tycho; Luke discovers Horn = grandson of Jedi **Nejaa Halcyon**; Horn refuses Jedi training then. Cure: bacta + *kor* (rare ryll grade) = ***rylca*** vaccine; interracial suspicions remain volatile.
**Illustration:** “The Lusankya escapes its tomb” (P74 / S089).

### The Bacta War — 7.5 A.B.Y. (P75–76 / S090–091)
Isard → Thyferra (bacta planet); coup → head of state. NR won’t depose “elected” leader → Wedge + Rogues resign pursue as civilians. Thyferra defended by *Lusankya* + 2 Imp SDs + 1 VSD. Guerrilla tactics (“Sacorrian grain fly”); base abandoned Yag’Dhul station (Booster Terrik); weapons from Talon Karrde; harass bacta convoys; destroy one capital near Alderaan Graveyard; another captain defects. Bluff: Isard orders *Lusankya* destroy Yag’Dhul station; station fakes 300+ proton torpedo locks → *Lusankya* flees Thyferra → ambushed by 80 real torpedoes (with X-wings/freighters); shields collapse; Booster arrives in captured SD; *Lusankya* surrenders → towed secret shipyard. Dodonna + prisoners moved off weeks prior (delayed rescue). Isard shuttle “destroyed” — engineered cover; will reappear post-Thrawn. Rogues heroes; Booster keeps SD renamed ***Errant Venture*** (movable trading bazaar) despite NRI pushback. Aftermath: NR captures SD *Tyrant* (ex-Vader Death Squadron) → Leia renames ***Rebel Dream*** flagship.

### The Hunt for Zsinj — 7.5–8 A.B.Y. (P76 / S091)
Post-Coruscant Imperial fragmentation ↑; Isard replaced by Moff/adviser coalition; power → independent warlords. **Warlord Zsinj** most powerful (post-Isard defections); arrogant; fights NR + other Imperials; flagship SSD ***Iron Fist***. Gen. Han Solo task force from Mon Cal ***Mon Remonda***; witnesses atrocities; Rogues support; another X-wing group crucial. Wedge reinstated; instead of *Mon Remonda* assembles new pilots — **Wraith Squadron**…

### Hunt for Zsinj continued (P76–78 / S091–093)
Wraith Squadron: commandos/snipers/spies/infiltrators. Capture Zsinj Corellian corvette → pose as crew → infiltrate fleet; learn Ession ambush plan → Battle of Ession NR victory (demolish one Zsinj SD; bomb key manufacturing). Then pose as pirate band → Zsinj hires for Kuat strike; KDY nearly completed new SSD; Zsinj plans steal + pair with *Iron Fist*. NR saboteurs destroy new vessel’s topside shield generator domes; Solo/*Mon Remonda* destroy warship; *Iron Fist* escapes.
**Project Minefield:** Zsinj forced brainwashing of nonhumans (Twi’leks, Gotals, Sullustans…) into assassins (Krytos-like resentment play). Attempts: Twi’lek vs Ackbar; Mothma’s Gotal bodyguard; Twi’lek A-wing pilot *Mon Remonda* near-kills Solo via explosive decompression. NRI cracks pattern → shuts down.
Unprecedented loose NR–Empire alliance vs Zsinj; Adm. **Rogriss** Imperial task force shares intel. Solo lures: fake *Falcon* mockup → later bomb destroys enemy Dreadnaught; Zsinj doesn’t take bait (realizes collaboration). Zsinj survival stratagem: wreckage from Kuat SSD pieced into hodgepodge decoy labeled **IRON FIST**. Solo+Rogriss assault Vahaba asteroid belt damages real *Iron Fist* → jumps Selaggis; disappears into “black cube” = **orbital nightcloak** (light-absorbing satellite cube). Decoy destroyed inside cloak; real *Iron Fist* hyperspace; Solo sees wreckage → believes destroyed → returns Coruscant.

### Hapans / Dathomir continued (P78–80 / S093–095)
Mysterious Hapans arrive in saucer Battle Dragons; **Hapes Consortium** most powerful/wealthy regional federation; sealed behind **Transitory Mists** 3000 yrs by prior Queen Mother. Leia diplomatic visit; extravagant gifts incl. captured Imp SDs. Prince **Isolder** (Chume’da; son of Queen Mother **Ta’a Chume**) presents as marriage suitor; Leia shocked (romance with Han vs political benefits); Mothma urges accept to help end Civil War. Jealous Han wins **Dathomir** in sabacc for 2.4 billion credits (Alderaanian refugee home plan) — but in Zsinj territory. Resigns commission; “kidnaps” Leia + Chewie + C-3PO → *Falcon* to blue-green Dathomir. Zsinj orbital stardock reconstructing *Iron Fist*; *Falcon* attacked → land forest. Intercepted by **Singing Mountain Clan** female warriors (Witches of Dathomir; ride tamed rancors; Force as “magic”); descendants of **Allya** (fallen Jedi exiled ~600 yrs prior). Luke searching Jedi secrets: on Toola finds data cards on crashed *Chu’unthor* + Yoda’s past recovery efforts from Dathomir → teams with Isolder for rescue. Dathomir life energy magnifies Luke’s Force; find *Chu’unthor* wreck; meet **Teneniel Djo**; reunite with Han/Leia.
**Nightsisters**: outcast dark-side witch clan; Imperial slave army; Palpatine previously destroyed all starships at Dathomir Imp prison to prevent escape. Futile attack on Singing Mountain. Zsinj ultimatum to **Gethzerion**: give Han or activate orbital nightcloak (freeze planet days). Activates when attack fails; Gethzerion counteroffer Solo + escape ship. Deal: two transports (one Solo; stripped for Nightsisters). At Imp prison Gethzerion Force-kills guards + Zsinj aide Gen. **Melvar**; Han rescued by *Falcon*; Gethzerion escape shuttle destroyed by Zsinj SDs. Hapes battle fleet engages *Iron Fist* + dozens; *Falcon* knocks out nightcloak.
**Illustration:** Isolder, the Chume’da of Hapes.

### The Death of Zsinj — 8 A.B.Y. (P80 / S095)
During chaos Solo flies *Falcon* straight at *Iron Fist* bridge → pair concussion missiles point-blank → Zsinj vaporized. Battle of Dathomir over. Clan Mother **Augwynne** unites witch clans → petitions NR membership. Luke given box of *Chu’unthor* log recorder discs (invaluable for future Jedi academy). Isolder falls for Teneniel Djo (outrages Ta’a Chume); marries her → heir apparent; daughter **Tenel Ka** (strong Force). Han + Leia agree marry on return to Coruscant…

### Wedding + Picking up the Pieces — 8.5 A.B.Y. (P81–82 / S096–097)
NR Inner Council covers up Han’s kidnapping escapade (no censure). Wedding in Alderaanian consulate building on Coruscant; hundreds friends/dignitaries; billions watch holovid.
Isolder vows join NR; mixed Hapan Royal Court reaction; Ta’a Chume bends for stability; promises Battle Dragons “in due time” — hoped strategic alliance never materializes. Zsinj domain implosion emboldens Imp fleet; Rogriss + others seize liberated territory; abandon NR partnership pretense. Benefit: several new SDs gifted to Leia at Coruscant reception. Ackbar Outer Rim vs Zsinj remnants; encounter High Adm. Teradoc third fleet; heavy losses: most Hapan ships + *Crynyd* destroyed; *Emancipator* + *Liberator* severely damaged → Hast shipyards. Battle of Storinal: *Rebel Dream* shelled by Imp SD *Peremptory* → recaptured by Empire. NR gives back threefold; Rogriss loses majority; NR captures **Kuat** (shipbuilding docks; yards damage delays new construction indefinitely). KDY design team escapes Deep Core aboard half-completed ***Eclipse***. Rogriss retreats; Teradoc scurries home; Ackbar fortifies. **NR now controls three-quarters of settled galaxy**. Both sides grave fleet losses → new warships top priority.
