# Part IV — Rebellion Against the Empire
Source: Essential Chronology (2000). S ≈ P+15. NOT New Chronology 2005.

## THE REBELLION BEGINS

### The Death Star Construction — 3–0 B.B.Y. (P45– / S060–)
After Senate turmoil / Palpatine rise / Jedi slaughter, Emperor seeks weapons as great as Empire to consolidate strength. Work secret due to senator concerns; divert funds, appropriate systems, bury questions in bureaucracy. Enlists Grand Moff Tarkin for isolated facility.
**The Maw** near Kessel: black-hole cluster; island of stability at center (singularities cancel); suicide scouts map route. Tarkin builds top-secret weapons installation: asteroids joined; buildings/vacuum labs; personnel records altered to “died in line of duty.” Researchers: willing **Umak Leth** (driven/partially insane), **Bevel Lemelisk** (ambitious engineer); **Dr. Ohran Keldor**; Mrlssi life-support specialists for huge stations. Captive/kidnapped: **Qwi Xux** (Omwati; only survivor of Tarkin’s rigorous student tests). Administrator **Tol Sivron** (Twi’lek); guarded by four Star Destroyers commanded by…

### Maw / Death Star build continued (P46 / S061)
Four SDs commanded by **Admiral Daala** (Tarkin’s student/lover; highest-ranking Imperial female; protects Maw scientists). Moon-sized battle station + planet-shattering superlaser proposed. **Tarkin Doctrine:** “Rule through fear of force rather than by force itself” → Emperor: rule through fear vs idealistic agencies. Prototype: Wookiee slaves build scaled-down superlaser core in armillary-sphere superstructure. Full-scale under Lemelisk + Vader; orbit penitentiary **Despayre** (Horuz); prison exiles + Wookiee slaves. Tarkin’s personal slave **Ackbar** forced to cooperate. Delays/shortages/sabotage; Vader executes workers/supervisors → back on track. Alliance learns via Ralltiir informant; confirmed by Lord Tion. Assassination attempt on Tarkin fails; Ackbar escapes → joins Rebels. Bail + Mon Mothma set multiple plans to capture blueprints.
**Illustration:** “Tarkin and Lemelisk — architects of terror” (Death Star model).

SDs blockade Toprawa; **Leia** (*Tantive IV*, diplomatic immunity) intercepts data transmission → hyperspace. Toprawa Rebels (**Bria Tharen** / Red Hand Squadron) all killed. ### Dodonna + Capture of Princess Leia — 0 B.B.Y. (P48 / S063)
Mon Mothma instructs **Jan Dodonna** (retired general; Dantooine HQ). Dodonna: first Republic SD captains; resigned over Emperor’s tactics (not treason); Empire’s secret execution order; Mon Mothma warning/recruitment initially refused; Imperial assassins attack him in nightshirt → fights free → staunch Rebel. Tracking device found at Dantooine → evacuate to Massassi ruins Yavin 4. After Corellian Treaty, Mon Mothma issues **Declaration of Rebellion**; Palpatine disbands Imperial Senate; regional governors (Tarkin) rule sectors directly; political settlement impossible.
Leia: young/exuberant; propaganda + coded messages; mercy-mission cover; Vader/Emperor suspect without proof. Perfect agent for Death Star plans; course for Alderaan/Bail. *Devastator* catches *Tantive IV* at Tatooine; Leia hides plans in R2-D2 → find Obi-Wan; droids escape pod; Leia captured. Death Star imprisonment; resists Vader interrogation; Tarkin threatens Alderaan; she knows he isn’t bluffing.

### Alderaan + A New Hope — 0 B.B.Y. (P49 / S064)
Leia reluctantly gives Dantooine location; Tarkin still destroys Alderaan as brutal example (billions); Leia returned for execution.
**Illustration:** “The last hours of Alderaan.”
**A New Hope:** Droids with plans captured by Jawas → sold to moisture farm; Luke finds Leia’s holomessage in R2. R2 leads to reclusive Jedi Obi-Wan; Tusken attack; Kenobi rescues; message delivered. Kenobi agrees take plans to Bail on Alderaan; unspoken incentive: alone knows Luke = Anakin’s son / Vader; Luke Force affinity. Imperials destroy Skywalker home searching for droids; aunt and…

### A New Hope climax (P50 / S065)
Owen/Beru killed; Luke accompanies Kenobi. Hire Solo/Chewie; *Falcon* escapes Imperials/TIEs. Kenobi trains Luke; find Alderaan destroyed; captured by Death Star. Rescue Leia; Kenobi sabotages tractor beam; duel with Vader → sacrifices life so others escape. Yavin 4: plans delivered (Imperial tracer); Dodonna finds thermal exhaust port flaw → proton torpedoes → hypermatter core chain reaction. Battle of Yavin: Rebel fighters vs TIEs (incl. Vader); many die; Luke Force-guided hit destroys Death Star + Tarkin. Enormous Alliance victory.
**Illustration:** “Obi-Wan Kenobi’s sacrifice” (Kenobi vs Vader lightsabers; ethereal Kenobi bust).

### Impact and Consequences — 0–0.5 A.B.Y. (P50–51 / S065–066)
Vader escapes in advanced TIE; sensed Force-strong pilot; private month investigating; Centares Rebel torture reveals destroyer = **Luke Skywalker**, his son.
Palpatine displeased at design flaw; summons **Bevel Lemelisk**; executes horrifically; resurrects via experimental cloning (genius too valuable); forces DS redesign; executes/resurrects him repeatedly (tests cloning for self as dark side destroys body). Second Death Star justified: Alderaan proved concept; only carelessly uncovered exhaust port.
**Prince Xizor** (Black Sun) urges eliminate Bounty Hunters Guild; Palpatine approves despite Vader objections; **Boba Fett** hired → **Bounty Hunter Wars**; Guild fragmented into innumerable splinters.
**Illustration:** “The *Executor* nears completion” (SSD under construction + escorts).

### Rebel Trap — 0–0.5 A.B.Y. (P52 / S067)
Vader employs rogue bounty hunters post–Bounty Hunter Wars. Alliance evacuates exposed Yavin 4; much fleet (Ackbar’s Mon Cal cruisers; government-in-exile refugees) already scattered. Dodonna orchestrates; heavy equipment out → Interdictor flotilla cuts hyperspace; loose blockade (not attack) traps Dodonna/Organa/Skywalker et al. Vader orders hold for *Executor* (Fondor yards; first SSD; personal vengeance sword). High-ranking Imperials deride Vader’s plan.
**Illustration:** “Dark Troopers.”

### Executor intrigue + Hoth found + Dark Troopers + Imperial Counterstrike 0.5–2 A.B.Y. (P53 / S068)
Admirals plot sabotage *Executor*; Admiral **Griff** double-agent ruse; Luke infiltrates Fondor → trap but escapes with real battleship data. Luke follows hypercomet → crash Hoth; exiled Imperial governor = human replica droid; after defeating it suggests Hoth as Alliance base.
Yavin strike ~6 mo post-Yavin: Ackbar feint Vallusk Cluster; *Executor* arrives Yavin 4; Dodonna evacuates but stays → concussion charges destroy TIE bomber squadron; believed dead; actually captured → early inmate secret prison **Lusankya**. Alliance negotiates grain with Imperial Overlord **Ghorin** (Greater Plooriod); grain poisoned → turn tables. **Kyle Katarn** scuttles Dark Trooper project (destroys spacegoing construction site); rescues **Crix Madine** from Imperial prison (Madine defected after ordered release incurable plague); nearly recaptured Corellia → saved by **Rogue Squadron** (cofounded Luke + **Wedge Antilles**); Mon Mothma promotes Madine General. Bail Organa dead → Mon Mothma + **Garm Bel Iblis** top leaders; frequent disagreement; Mothma orders Milvayne attack Bel Iblis deems suicidal → Bel Iblis secedes with loyal forces; fights independently ~9 years.

### Post-Yavin politics + Circarpous Joins the Resistance — 2 A.B.Y. (P54 / S069)
**Borsk Fey’lya** (Bothan) joins Alliance after Yavin (status/power motives). “Ram’s Head” mission destroys four SDs in dry dock. Brief Thila jungle base abandoned; engineers scout incl. Hoth; Mon Mothma keeps options open. Empire launches thousands of probe droids (many false alarms).
**Circarpous IV** (Expansion Region financial hub) covertly funds Alliance over Imperial tariffs. Leia + C-3PO + Luke → rendezvous Circarpous IV; engine malfunction → crash **Circarpous V / Mimban** (illegal Imperial dolovite mine). Captured; Vader at vine-encrusted Temple of Pomojema housing **Kaiburr Crystal** (Force-magnifying). Luke duels Vader (untrained but holds own; crystal edge; Obi-Wan spirit “puppeteer”); severs Vader’s sword arm; Vader falls into crumbling well; Rebels escape. Circarpousians notified of Mimban Imperial presence → business underground opens supply line; funds **KDY Planet Defender ion cannon**. Kaiburr power decreases away from temple; Luke keeps as curiosity/teaching aid → later lightsaber focusing crystal (remarkably strong/efficient).
**Illustration:** “Meeting the Dark Lord” (Luke/Leia vs Vader).

### Home in the Ice — 2–3 A.B.Y. (P55 / S070)
Mon Mothma establishes HQ on Hoth; Gen. **Carlist Rieekan** (Alderaan survivor) commands. Leia in ice tunnels; first hints of romance with Han. Han bounty-hunter run-ins on Ord Mantell; base stays hidden. **Battle of Derra IV:** supply convoy destroyed (Vader-led); flight leader dies → Luke promoted commander. Dark time; Empire still hunts.

## A LIGHT ECLIPSED
### The Battle of Hoth — 3 A.B.Y. (P55– / S070–)
Imperial probot detects faint Hoth transmissions → signals Vader’s flagship; **Death Squadron** deploys. Rieekan intercepts → immediate evacuation. Ion cannon clears escape corridor before first transport; AT-ATs ground assault; snowspeeders buy time; Echo Base falls; heavy losses both sides; Vader strides into ruined command…

### Hoth escape + A New Jedi — 3 A.B.Y. (P56–57 / S071–072)
Leia/Han escape Hoth in *Falcon*; Luke separately. Vader pursues (knows Luke is son; doesn’t suspect Leia yet). Han through asteroid field; hyperdrive damaged; bounty hunters incl. Boba Fett (history with Vader: recovered Icarii prophetess head pre-Yavin). Hyperdrive backup → Bespin / Lando (past Ylesia disagreement). Fett tracks; Vader coerces Lando trap; Han tortured; lure for Luke.
Luke → Dagobah after Obi-Wan vision; meets gnomish Yoda (ex-Jedi Council; Chu’unthor mediator); reluctant training; trust instincts; vision of friends captive → leaves despite Yoda warnings.
Cloud City: Han carbonite → Fett for Jabba; Lando turns assist Alliance; Lando/Leia/Chewie escape *Falcon*; Luke/Vader duel over wind core; Luke loses right hand; Vader reveals fatherhood / join overthrow Emperor; Luke refuses, falls; Force-clings weather vane; *Falcon* rescues → Alliance fleet rendezvous.
Devastating defeat: Hoth lost; Han captured; Luke’s dark heritage. Alliance presses on; rumors of second larger Death Star near Endor; limited victory: Rebel agents destroy experimental superlaser test bed *Tarkin*.
**Illustrations:** “The collapse of Echo Base” (snowtroopers/AT-ST/AT-AT); “Training of a Jedi” (Yoda + Luke levitating rocks).

### Prince Xizor and Black Sun — 3.5 A.B.Y. (P58–59 / S073–074)
Attempts on Luke’s life → Leia turns to Black Sun spy network. **Prince Xizor**: Falleen reptilian; Black Sun head; third most powerful after Palpatine/Vader; seeks eliminate rival Vader; knows Vader–Luke secret; plots kill Luke to make Vader look incompetent (vs Palpatine’s capture-alive plan).
Luke builds new lightsaber on Tatooine; with **Dash Rendar** to Bothawui capture Imperial freighter with DS2 plans; disable freighter; computer core to **Kothlis**; Bothan slicers decrypt; Palpatine allowed capture as Endor trap. Luke captured by bounty hunters Kothlis then escapes Vader encounter; core to Mon Mothma. Leia + Chewie as bounty hunters enter Xizor’s Coruscant fortress; Xizor seductive then imprisons Leia; allows Chewie escape as Luke bait. Rescue; time-delayed thermal detonator; Xizor flees to skyhook *Falleen’s Fist*; castle implodes (gaping Coruscant hole). Xizor navy vs *Falcon*; *Executor* + TIEs ignore Falcon → fire on Xizor’s vessels. Vader ultimatum; Xizor refuses → *Falleen’s Fist* blasted. Black Sun vacuum: **Guri** (human replica droid) drops from sight; lieutenants war.
Meanwhile Grand Admiral **Zaarin** coup: attacks Vader’s fleet Ottega; captures Emperor’s private shuttle Coruscant; defeated; escapes Outer Rim; later eliminated by Grand Admiral **Thrawn**.
**Illustrations:** Prince Xizor portrait; “The capture of the second Death Star plans” (space battle / Outrider-adjacent).

## ALLIANCE TRIUMPHANT
### The Rebellion Regroups — 4 A.B.Y. (P60 / S075)
Complex plan frees Han from Jabba; Jabba dies; Desilijic crime family collapses. Leia/Lando/Han rejoin Rebel fleet. Luke → Dagobah; Yoda (~900) near death; confirms Vader = father; fades into Force; Luke realizes Leia = twin sister (even Vader didn’t know). Mon Mothma addresses troop leaders; Bothan spies delivered decrypted Kothlis core with DS2 location (high Bothan cost); Palpatine personally inspecting → galvanizes Mothma + Admiral Ackbar.
**Illustration:** Admiral Ackbar.

### The Battle of Endor — 4 A.B.Y. (P60–62 / S075–077)
Fleet gathers Sullust. Commando team (Leia, Luke, Gen. Han) → Endor forest moon destroy shield generator. Difficulties; Luke allows capture → Vader takes him to Palpatine on DS2; trap: Imperial battlefleet emerges; shield still up. Luke appeals to father’s heart; Vader unmoved. Emperor manipulates father/son lightsaber duel; Vader falters seeing son’s goodness; learns of daughter Leia; Luke’s rage wounds Vader; Emperor applauds dark steps; Luke surrenders refuses kill father; Emperor Force-lightning; Vader breaks hold → hurls Palpatine into shaft (disintegrated); Vader mortally wounded; Anakin dies → Force; Luke takes black armor, flies away.
Shield generator destroyed; Lando + Wedge into DS superstructure → reactor. Ackbar fleet vs SDs/*Executor*; Arvel Crynyd A-wing suicide → *Executor* bridge → crashes into DS; Calrissian/Antilles warhead → reactor; escape detonation wave. Galaxy celebrates New Order end / Emperor death; Empire far from vanquished; **New Republic can be born**.
**Illustrations:** “Death of the Devastator”; “Heroism on Endor” (Ewok log trap / AT-ST crush).

### The Truce at Bakura — 4 A.B.Y. (P62–63 / S077–078)
Day after Emperor death: Imperial drone → DS wreckage distress: Bakura under alien invasion; half defense force lost. Mon Mothma task force; Obi-Wan spirit urges Luke command. Five Corellian gunships + corvette + *Falcon* → Bakura (Outer Rim / Unknown Regions border).
**Ssi-ruuk**: warm-blooded saurians; **entechment** (human life energy → battle droid circuits). Intent: entech Bakura population. Leia meets Gov. **Wilek Nereus** Salis D’aar with PM **Yeorg Captison** + niece **Gaeriel Captison**; Nereus accepts cease-fire after Emperor death confirmation → first Rebel–Imperial formal truce.
Leia visited by Anakin spirit begging forgiveness; she cannot ignore Death Star torture / Han→Fett / Alderaan; apparition vanishes never returns. Ssi-ruuk flagship *Shriwirr*; Adm. **Ivpikkis**; Force-sensitive human pet **Dev Sibwarra** detects Luke → alerts masters (hope Jedi enables distant entechment)…

### Bakura climax + aftermath (P64–65 / S079–080)
Sibwarra secretly offers Nereus: turn over Luke → Ssi-ruuk leave. Nereus plans eliminate both: infects Luke’s food with **Olabrian trichoid egg pods** (hatch in bronchial tubes aboard *Shriwirr*). Arrests Leia for sedition → Salis D’aar riots; Han rescues; *Falcon* to defend. Luke captured spaceport → *Shriwirr* entechment rig; Sibwarra impressed by Jedi heroism. Sibwarra regains free will → helps Luke escape; Ssi-ruuk crew abandon ship in life pods (fear dying far from homeworlds). Rebel+Imperial united front; Ssi-ruuk armada bombarded → hyperspace Unknown Regions; *Shriwirr* left. Han almost “carom shot” *Falcon* ram Imperial patrol into *Dominant* generator; Leia farewell “Scatter the fire of Rebellion…”; *Dominant* breaks to strike *Shriwirr*; *Falcon* aborts to rescue Luke+Sibwarra; Luke Force-eliminated parasites. Cmdr **Pter Thanas** Imperial leader. Nereus captured by resistance → killed mishap. Captison assumes control → joins Alliance of Free Planets. Thanas oversees Imperial withdrawal then defects → Bakuran home defense; later marries Gaeriel Captison (she becomes PM; commissions warships vs Ssi-ruuk). Sibwarra dies of *Shriwirr* injuries despite Luke’s care; Luke vows find Force-sensitives / restore Jedi.
**Illustration:** “A historic armistice”; “Admiral Ivpikkis of the Ssi-ruuk”.

Mothma shares intel with Empire hoping two-pronged attack; Imperials preoccupied. *Sibwarra* arrives → half-beaten foe; another Unknown Regions force already attacked opposite front…

### Ssi-ruuvi Space close (P66 / S081)
Attackers of Ssi-ruuvi opposite front: recent interpretations suggest **Chiss** (Thrawn’s race). *Sibwarra* + escorts engage Ivpikkis’ fleet toward Ssi-ruuvi homeworld → standstill; negotiations inconclusive. Mon Mothma satisfied Ssi-ruuk cannot remount invasion → vessels fall back assist liberation of **Clak’dor VII**.
