# Part II — The Empire and the New Order
Source: Essential Chronology (2000) Anderson/Wallace. Scan↔print: S ≈ P+15. NOT New Chronology 2005.

## Birth of the Empire — approx. 50–18 B.B.Y. (P21– / S036–)
Opening: dark days of Empire; Palpatine’s thousand-year boast; Galactic Civil War as pivotal era.
**Katana fleet:** 200 slave-rigged Dreadnaughts; hive virus → crew mad; jump to random hyperspace; slave-rig pulls all 199; lost >½ century; erodes Republic confidence.
Republic too large/cumbersome; checks/balances paralysis; stagnation, laziness, complacency, corruption among senators/governors.
**Senator Palpatine of Naboo** (sectorial senator, respected) watches decay; Jedi as greatest obstacle. Jedi intertwined with politics as ambassadors/investigators for Chancellor; HQ near seat of government → image tainted.
Shift begins with taxation dispute: senate resolution **BR-0371** tariffs Outer/Mid Rim hyperspace routes to pay Republic debt. Trade Federation resists; Palpatine’s support may have spurred them; on advice of shadowy **Darth Sidious**, TF blockades Naboo → invades with droid army. Queen Amidala reveals invasion; calls vote of no-confidence in **Finis Valorum** (corruption-scandal weakened); Palpatine elected last Chancellor.
**Anakin Skywalker:** Qui-Gon finds 9-yr-old slave on Tatooine; potent Force vergence; Chosen One prophecy. Council refuses training (age); Windu more concerned re Sith return (Qui-Gon dueled Jedi-like warrior). Mission ends: **Darth Maul** kills Qui-Gon, is himself slain. Council honors dying request over Yoda’s objections; Anakin becomes Padawan under **Obi-Wan Kenobi**. (Parenthetical: Kenobi’s eventual failure → Anakin becomes Darth Vader.)
**Illustrations:** “The invasion of Naboo” (B1 droids + AAT in Theed, P22/S037); Darth Maul portrait; Qui-Gon Jinn with lightsaber (P23/S038).
**Outbound Flight:** Post-Naboo Jedi exploration to breach hyperspace turbulence sphere / seek extragalactic life; Palpatine’s agents intercept/destroy in Unknown Regions; substantial evidence **Thrawn** involved; dashes hopes of contacting nearby galaxies.

### Post-Naboo → Clone Wars gap caveat (P24 / S039)
Outbound Flight destroyed ~2 yrs after Naboo Battle (Thrawn evidence). **Vergere** (Jedi) vanishes after Mace Windu scouting mission to **Zonama Sekot**; year later Obi-Wan + Anakin explore; Vergere may have been captured by unknown aliens.
**Clone Wars** caveat (in-universe historian voice): dark time; planets uninhabitable; many archives destroyed or wiped after Emperor’s ascension; even Obroa-skai lacks accurate details on Clone Wars / Jedi extermination / crowning; historians qualify with caveat needing independent corroboration.
Chaos triggers Dark Jedi insurrection on **Bpfassh**. **Nejaa Halcyon** + others destroy Dark Jedi enclave on **Susevfi**; remaining followers → **Jensaarai** (secret teachings until Luke discovers decades later).
**Illustration:** “Palpatine, the new Emperor” (throne + Royal Guards + kneeling Neimoidian-like figure).

### Empire consolidation (P25 / S040)
Palpatine engineers **Caamas** devastation (peace-through-moral-strength philosophy threat); Bothan saboteurs help sabotage shield generators → firestorms. Senator **Seti Ashgad** (vocal opponent) arrested/exiled to Nam Chorios. **Jedi Purge**: bloody crusade; thousands of Jedi die; **Darth Vader** (ex-Anakin) greatest atrocities. Declares self Emperor to citizenry starved for leadership; initial New Order enthusiasm: strength, order, decisiveness. “The Empire had begun.”
**Eye of Palpatine**: gargantuan asteroid-shaped battlemoon to wipe Jedi enclave on Belsavis; disabled by Jedi saboteurs; refugees flee.
**Ghorman Massacre**: peaceful antitax demo in Sern sector; warship lands on protesters; Palpatine praises captain **Tarkin**, promotes to Moff. Convinces **Bail Organa** Republic ideals dead.

### Dawn of Dissent — approx. 18–0 B.B.Y. (P25– / S040–)
Some senators embrace New Order; Palpatine consolidates; passion for warships/superweapons. Bail + **Mon Mothma** of Chandrila meet secretly re organized rebellion. Coruscant → **Imperial Center**; nonhumans persecuted; antislavery laws repealed; HoloNet restricted (propaganda); **COMPNOR** ideological purity; military expand: Imperial-class SD, AT-AT/AT-ST, stormtroopers “ultimate symbol of Imperial might.”

### Rebel Alliance forms (P26 / S041)
Bail + Mon Mothma continue organizing. Mon Mothma opposes Imperial war machine; accused of treason → underground leadership. **Corellian Treaty** <2 yrs before Yavin: merges three largest revolutionary groups into **Alliance to Restore the Republic**; signed by Bail Organa, Mon Mothma, **Garm Bel Iblis** (Corellia). Mon Mothma heads command; negotiates Mon Calamari capital ships; Incom defectors provide **T-65 X-wing** prototypes/blueprints.
HQ progression: Chrellis planetoid → Briggia → Orion IV → **Dantooine** (ancient Jedi connection; Vodo once trained students there). Palpatine’s **Operation Strike Fear**; Alliance victories (frigate *Priam*; demolish SD *Invincible*).
**Subjugation of Ralltiir:** Core World; Imperial shock troops seize over alleged Rebel High Council sympathizers; torture/interrogation; **Lord Tion** (Tarkin’s sphere) presides. Galvanizing Core atrocity (cf. Ghorman); later overshadowed by Alderaan. “The Rebel Alliance had taken shape.”
