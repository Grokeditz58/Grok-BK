## Afterword (P165 / S180)
**Header:** AFTERWORD.
Preparation chronology long difficult process; teams historians/scholars; by no means comprehensive history galaxy. Nature of time: once completed already out of date. Work continues. Processing droids sifting enormous archives Imperial Information Center search records hidden/altered/damaged Palpatine’s numerous purges. Evidence technicians + data restorers years combing rubble Mount Tantiss [for fre…] info Emperor’s crimes. Archaeologists discovering knowledge ruins Jedi library Ossus; ancient records planets Toola + Dathomir being translated. Many eyewitnesses birth Empire still alive but advanced age — debrief historical record before too late.
Even now historic events in motion. **Borsk Fey’lya** (Rebellion early years; overcome several dark scandals) ascended coveted office chief of state. Master Luke Skywalker’s new Jedi Knights = galaxy’s patrol officers; Skywalker sees need oversee Jedi → seeking approval reestablish **Jedi Council** (unite twelve leading Jedi governing body not seen since Old Republic).
Trouble spots remain. Few inter… **Nom Anor**: professional firebrand / mysterious figure previously involved Carnor Jax’s Interim Council; resurfacing stir trouble planets **Rhommamool** and **Osarian**. Localized controversies; with Imperial Remnant confined pocket Outer Rim, seems “no corners from which a new threat can arise.”
Close: “For only by knowing our history can we understand ourselves—and history never ends.”
