# Part VI — Empire Resurgent
Source: Essential Chronology (2000). Scan oa8yCByK. S ≈ P+15. TOC: Thrawn 83; Return of Isard 89; Resurrection of Palpatine 90.


## PART VI open — Empire Resurgent (P83 / S098)
NR busy with warlords; Imp dominion reduced to ~¼ (mostly Rim outlying). Surviving moffs fight NR + prevent ideological split under warlords. Overconfident NR prognosticators predict imminent end after Endor/Coruscant defeats — but next two years nearly kill NR.

### THE DEPREDATIONS OF GRAND ADMIRAL THRAWN — 9 A.B.Y. (P83–84 / S098–099)
Ailing Empire until Thrawn emerges from Unknown Regions (isolated since pre-Endor). Flagship *Chimaera*; elevates Capt. Pellaeon (Endor retreat orderer) de facto 2IC → helps Pellaeon’s Moff reputation. 6 months reorganize + strategic border raids; impresses Imp leaders → effective control of Empire. Moffs initially see alien political outsider as puppet figurehead; Thrawn popular with fleet + citizenry → precedent: Empire ruled by distinguished military commanders not politicians.
Organizes loyal forces; marginalizes warlords (Krennel; Pentastar/Kaine). Strike library world **Obroa-skai** → Emperor’s secret storehouse **Mount Tantiss** / **Wayland**: (1) functional cloaking device (Project Vorknkx); (2) Spaarti cloning cylinders; (3) mad Jedi **Joruus C’baoth** (clone of Jedi Master Jorus C’baoth). Force-blocking **ysalamiri** protect Thrawn from C’baoth; secures cooperation by promising deliver Luke + Leia (+ unborn twins) as apprentices. C’baoth Force-links fleet ships for supernatural coordination. Twice Noghri death commandos kidnap attempts fail. Cloning complex → thousands clone soldiers; Spaarti to Unknown Regions secret base for special project: **clone of Thrawn himself**. Ysalamiri allow clones in mere days (vs months/years; overcome prior rapid-growth failures). Desperate for ships; plan mole miners drill hulls / board NR ships at **Sluis Van**; steals 51 mole miners from Lando’s **Nomad City** on **Nkllon** (dark-side mobile mining).
**Illustration:** Grand Admiral Thrawn (with ysalamiri).

### Talon Karrde and the Smugglers — 9 A.B.Y. (P84 / S099)
Lando + Han → Myrkr (Karrde base) recruit for NR shipping; unbeknownst Karrde has Luke imprisoned in storage silo. Luke’s X-wing pulled from hyperspace by Interdictor; damaged by Thrawn flagship; stranded; picked up by Karrde freighter; imprisoned (bounty-aware). After Solo/Calrissian arrive Luke escapes; steals Skipray; **Mara Jade** (Karrde 2IC) follows in second Skipray…

### Myrkr / Sluis Van (P84–85 / S099–100)
Luke+Mara chase → collision/crash; through Myrkr forests; thousands ysalamiri strip Luke’s Force (like Ulic Qel-Droma); Mara reveals Emperor’s Hand vow to kill Luke avenge Palpatine; put aside conflict to Hyllyard City; Imp scout patrol finds them; Karrde’s men ambush rescue. Karrde squarely NR side; evacuates Myrkr ahead of Thrawn reprisal. Lando minor wound → medcenter Sluis Van — arrive as Thrawn unleashes fleet; cloaking surprise. Rogues (escort duty) vs TIEs = distraction; mole miners drill NR docked vessels; commandos seize bridges; dozens hijacked capital ships head for hyperspace fringe. Lando master control code reactivates mole miners → drill through controls deny prize; >40 NR warships heavily damaged/out months; Lando persona non grata Fleet Command.
**Illustration:** “The battle of Sluis Van”.

### The Noghri Switch Sides — 9 A.B.Y. (P85–86 / S100–101)
Chewie places Leia under protection on Kashyyyk during Sluis Van. Leia captures Noghri commando **Khabarakh** alive (prevent third attack). Noghri revere Vader as “savior” after starship crash devastated **Honoghr** ecosystem; Empire promised restore for death-commando service — deliberately kept poisoned. Leia (Vader heir) → Honoghr with Chewie+C-3PO on Khabarakh’s ship. Blasted wasteland / catastrophic famine; clans respect blood relative of Vader; discovers Imp decontamination droids prolong famine → reveals → Noghri switch allegiance. Secretly abandon/sabotage Imp assignments. **Rukh** (Thrawn’s personal bodyguard *Chimaera*) waits for appropriate moment.

### The Katana Fleet and the Clone Troopers — 9 A.B.Y. (P86 / S101)
Thrawn manipulates financial records → Ackbar appears treasonous; Fey’lya urges → Ackbar house arrest. Han+Lando investigate **New Cov**; cornered by Imp SD; rescued by 3 vintage Clone Wars Dreadnaughts of Gen. **Garm Bel Iblis** (Alliance founder; quit 9 yrs prior Mon Mothma schism; stubborn pride refuses rejoin). Hit-and-run vs Empire. **Katana fleet** (“Dark Force”): 200 Dreadnaughts lost generations. Bel Iblis contact Capt. Hoffner knows location — Thrawn apprehends first. Karrde also discovers; after Luke+Mara rescue him from *Chimaera*, gives location to NR. Race to barren space; Thrawn arrives moments later…

### Katana / twins / Wayland (P86–87 / S101–102)
Battle for Katana fleet: Luke+Han board flagship *Katana*; Bel Iblis + Karrde reinforcements; Han realizes slave-rigged → steers Dreadnaught into SD *Peremptory*. NR recovers only 15 of 200; dead Imp stormtroopers = clones. Psychological impact; Mothma fears new Clone Wars; prioritize find cloning complex. Ackbar reinstated (scandal = Imp setup). Thrawn devastates Nomad City Nkllon. Leia gives birth twins **Jaina** and **Jacen** Solo on Coruscant. Mara serves NR despite Emperor’s Hand suspicion; knows Mount Tantiss location. Team (Jade, Luke, Solo, Chewie, Lando, droids) → Wayland. Luke+Mara infiltrate Emperor’s auxiliary throne room for self-destruct; meet C’baoth who reveals **Luuke Skywalker** clone (from hand lost Cloud City) wielding Anakin’s original blue lightsaber. Frenzied duel; Mara fulfills “last command” kill Luke by slaying clone instead. C’baoth rage collapses ceiling; Mara skewers him through torso. Calrissian+Chewie sabotage cloning equipment column → overload; Mount Tantiss explodes; team escapes. Loss of artworks/artifacts in Emperor vaults; seems bury evidence Bothan involvement in **Caamas** devastation (Fey’lya relief); data card from rubble decade later → Caamas Document search.
**Illustration:** “The new generation” (Leia with Jacen/Jaina).

### Thrawn’s Fall — 9 A.B.Y. (P87–88 / S102–103)
Thrawn uses cloaking more complexly than Emperor’s Phantom TIE. Ruses: invisibility screen (cloak cruisers under planet shield fire with SD → appear lasers penetrate barriers); Coruscant asteroid siege (22 cloaked asteroids → shields up indefinitely = blockade). Ackbar plans Bilbringi raid for gravfield detector + damage Imp shipbuilding; Thrawn waiting. Rogues fighter attack; Ackbar capital cannonades; Karrde smugglers hit-and-fade defense platforms force Thrawn split forces — still superior tactically…
**Illustration:** “A twisted genius dies” (Thrawn slain in command chair).

### Thrawn’s Fall climax (P88–89 / S103–104)
Empire victory seemed inevitable until chance: **Rukh** on *Chimaera* bridge fatally stabs Thrawn through heart (Honoghr/Leia influence); flees; later caught/executed by stormtrooper squad under Maj. **Grodin Tierce**. Pellaeon takes command → full retreat “live and fight again another day.” Thrawn nearly doubled Empire; held by his authority alone → without him splinters to warlordism; NR begins recapture.

## THE RETURN OF ISARD — 9–10 A.B.Y. (P89 / S104)
NR celebrates; ominous beneath. On Byss Palpatine’s spirit recuperates into new clone; Deep Core star-choked/no hyperspace lanes hide him; contacts warlords/moffs rebuild fleet. Isard fears execution for failures; plots win favor by offering *Lusankya* (in NR dry dock reconstruction). NR sweeps Imp remnants; fleet disappears into hiding. Provisional Council suspects *Lusankya* prisoners in Ciutric Hegemony; priority recover Gen. Jan Dodonna. Rogue raid fails; unexpectedly rescued by Isard’s TIE Defender pilots. Surviving Bacta War Isard meets former enemies: shared goal take down Krennel (betrayed her; allied with pre-Tantiss **Isard clone**). In exchange destroy clone+Krennel → helps Rogues. Rogues pose defecting TIE pilots infiltrate Krennel; NR commandos prepare Dodonna rescue…

### Isard close (P89–90 / S104–105)
Unified assault Ciutric: Rogues + Ackbar force from Bilbringi defenses + commandos. Isard doesn’t participate — treacherously strikes weakened Bilbringi *Lusankya* dry dock; infiltration seizes bridge. NRI Agent **Iella Wessiri** prepared; confrontation in Isard’s former quarters → Wessiri shoots/kills deranged Isard; *Lusankya* recovered. Krennel + Isard clone killed; prisoners rescued (medical/psych). Dodonna refuses treatment; insists meet Mothma; forms “**gray cadre**” (Adar Tallon, Vanden Willard…) → senior military adviser again. Krennel dominion crumbles; Empire loses nearly all Thrawn-won territory; NR fleet advances Rim → Coruscant/Core relatively unprotected — **exactly as Palpatine wanted**.

## THE RESURRECTION OF EMPEROR PALPATINE — 10 A.B.Y. (P90–91 / S105–106)
Six years post-Endor NR precarious without Jedi. Palpatine’s ghastly return — “nightmarish year of horror, calamity, and ruin.” Post-Krennel Imp fleet commanders + ruling council unify; surprise Deep Core attack; NR fleet out of position Rim; systems conquered; merciless Coruscant bombardment; Mothma evacuates capital avoid civilian decimation. Core Worlds Ralltiir, Chandrila, Esseles fall; NR returns guerrilla warfare. Resurrected Palpatine reunites better than Thrawn; Harrsk, Delvardus, Teradoc, Pellaeon swear obedience. Once Coruscant taken → brief bloody **Imperial Mutiny** (ruling council, moffs, fleet, Inquisitorius, COMPNOR, ISB infighting). Wedge promoted general; Han+Lando return active duty. Raid over Coruscant: Alliance SD *Liberator* (Luke, Wedge, Lando) crashes surface; Leia/Han/Chewie/C-3PO rescue; twins hidden New Alderaan. Luke Force-defeats AT-AT single-handed; vows remain devastated Coruscant sensing dark force; Force storm rift sucks Luke+R2 to Byss dungeon ship. Meets cloned reincarnation Palpatine (dozens identical bodies / ancient Sith secrets; Bevel Lemelisk tech tested). Emperor tempts like Anakin; Luke believes use dark knowledge against him from within → **agrees to join**. NR government-in-exile **Pinnacle Base** Da Soocha V (Hutt Space). Palpatine (after Mutiny culled weak) strikes Mon Calamari with huge weapons…
**Illustration:** Emperor Palpatine.

### Dark Empire continued (P91–95 / S106–110)
**World Devastators** (Umak Leth; Byss shipyards): consume everything → recycle → spew war machines. Attack Mon Calamari; *Emancipator* turned scrap crashing into Devastator mouth. Luke Force-vision to pregnant Leia: destiny dark side to save galaxy; stay away — she doesn’t. Luke Emperor’s protégé commanding Imp Mon Cal attack but secretly faulty commands crash/destroy several Devastators. Presents captives to Palpatine; Emperor tempts Leia fight brother; she refuses; reveals clone storehouse. Emperor Force-attacks Leia; Luke no intercede → Han judges by actions. Leia + Han → Nar Shaddaa (Salla Zend, Shug Ninx); meet fallen Jedi crone **Vima-Da-Boda** (gives ancient lightsaber). Via Zend freighter to Byss; captured before Luke. Emperor shows **Jedi Holocron**; wants consciousness into Leia’s unborn; she fights, steals Holocron, escapes. Luke helps divert guards for escape but stays; illusionary double; loads World Devastator override codes into R2 → Alliance completes Mon Cal victory. Luke smashes nearly all clone tanks; weakening Emperor transfers to strong agile **15-year-old** clone; lightsaber duel forces Luke submission. Takes Luke aboard flagship ***Eclipse*** → Pinnacle Base ultimatum: destroy Rebels unless pregnant Leia joins (dark side + unborn as consciousness repository). Leia goes; defies; helps Luke break free; siblings battle Palpatine; Emperor summons Force storm; combined resistance → loses control; storm consumes *Eclipse* + Emperor; siblings flee.
**Illustration:** “World Devastators attack Mon Calamari”; “The resurrected Emperor” (youthful).

### Operation Shadow Hand — 10 A.B.Y. (P94–95 / S109–110)
Backup clone on Byss; while recovers, Executor **Sedriss** + 7 Dark Jedi adepts lead “unstoppable wave assaults.” Balmorra weapons factories: rebuffed by massive new combat droids; uneasy truce to purchase (sellers don’t intend fulfill). Two dark adepts sabotage clone lab seize power; Sedriss kills them. Palpatine reborn in only remaining clone; tasks Sedriss find Luke alive. Deeper betrayal: Royal Guard **Carnor Jax** bribed Byss clonemaster sabotage genetic structure → premature aging/quick death; Jax waits for throne chance.
Luke studies Holocron → finds **Kam Solusar** (Vader Purge survivor) at abandoned Nespis VIII; *lightsider* game; Solusar joins. Ossus (ruined Sith War/Cron): primitive **Ysanna** shamans weak Force; worship enormous twisted tree. Executor Sedriss arrives; ancient Jedi Master **Ood Bnar** (tree-form millennia) grapples Sedriss → both destroyed. Luke finds stockpile ancient lightsabers under Ood remains; trains Ysanna **Jem** + brother **Rayf**. Leia retrieves Vima-Da-Boda Nar Shaddaa; also finds **Empatojayos Brand** (more prosthetic than human; Force still usable).
**Galaxy Gun** (Umak Leth): orbit Byss; hyperspace projectiles destroy any target without warning. First projectile → Pinnacle Base annihilates hidden moon as Luke returns Ossus with Solusar + trainees; fear Mothma/leaders wiped — but core evacuated (Mothma like Rieekan Hoth). Commando teams fail destroy Galaxy Gun; Palpatine regains Inner/Outer Rim. Imp attack on twins’ secret world; Luke poisoned (Vima heals); Jem Ysanna killed. Flee Nespis VIII; Leia gives birth third child **Anakin Solo**. Mon Mothma + leaders…

### Palpatine Vanquished — 11 A.B.Y. (P96–97 / S111–112)
NR regroups derelict floating city; Galaxy Gun attacks → scatter small groups. Final clone failing (rapid aging + dark powers + Jax cellular sabotage); scientists can’t fix tainted genetic material. Palpatine → Korriban; Dark Lord spirits say needs Jedi body → direct to infant Anakin Solo. Flagship ***Eclipse II*** to Onderon; NR attacks ship; Jedi search; Palpatine decoy on flagship while personally seeks Leia. Lando + R2 infiltrate *Eclipse II* sabotage hyperdrive → crashes into Galaxy Gun; both destroyed; final projectile falls into Byss → destroys planet. Dying Palpatine tries transfer into baby Anakin; Luke + Jedi protect; Rayf Ysanna + Empatojayos Brand mortally wounded; Han blasts Palpatine; spirit intercepted by dying Brand who clasps to light holding Emperor presence as both die. Clones gone → **finally and truly defeated**. Imp fleet retreats; abandon Coruscant + Core; Mon Mothma announces NR regained control; capital reestablished Coruscant symbolic continuity. Mothma elected Chief of State + President of Senate; Leia Minister of State.
**Illustration:** “The final death of Palpatine”.

### Jax, Kanos, and the Interim Council — 11 A.B.Y. (P97 / S112)
Empire crumbles faster than post-Endor. Carnor Jax massacres Royal Guards Yinchorr; creates 13-member **Interim Council** (himself senior). Desperate: grants powerful alien leaders membership (Devaronian, Whiphid, Givin, Defel) — too little too late. Many fleets ignore council; Harrsk + warlords retreat Deep Core. Jax/Council territory = narrow band Outer Rim → Colonies. Last of Emperor’s Royal…

### Jax/Kanos close + New Class (P97–98 / S112–113)
Royal Guard **Kir Kanos** learns Jax betrayal; conflict: Yinchorr massacre; Phaeda (Jax SDs try capture Kanos); Gen. Wedge + SSD *Lusankya* (relaunched post-Shadow Hand repairs; secret to many Imperials) liberate Phaeda with Rogues (B-wings + E-wings). Final Yinchorr: Kanos destroys Jax flagship with booby-trapped assault bomber; duel abandoned Royal Guard training compound; kills Jax on elevated platform above bottomless pit; vows avenge Emperor by destroying sham council.
Black Sun tries sabotage council with clones; **Xandel Carivus** disbands org to rule alone as “new emperor” on instructions from shadowy **Nom Anor**. Baron **D’Asta** (pro-Imp business; private fleet) attacks council HQ Ord Cantrell; Kanos executes Carivus on surface.
Imp forces → Deep Core warlord allegiances; worlds like Carida left fortress worlds (NR isolates via trade blockades not invasion). Pellaeon’s *Chimaera* severely damaged/abandoned Duro during Shadow Hand; joins High Adm. Teradoc Deep Core → command large Victory SD flotilla.
NR warship development (**New Class** eight designs) after Palpatine clone assaults exposed secondhand Corellian/captured Imp/modified Mon Cal insufficiency: *Agave* picket; *Warrior* gunship; *Sacheen* light escort (first finished); *Hajen* fleet tender; *Majestic* heavy cruiser; *Defender* assault carrier; *Endurance* fleet carrier; *Nebula* NR Star Destroyer. Years to engineer/manufacture all eight.
