# Part III — Profiles in History
Source: Essential Chronology (2000). S ≈ P+15. NOT New Chronology 2005.
Intro: history as individuals; Palpatine/Mon Mothma as archetypes; scoundrels Han/Lando + youthful Luke/Leia essential; interviewed for early years.

## HAN SOLO (P27– / S042–)
Never knew parents; earliest memory lost/alone on Corellia; **Garris Shrike** takes him into *Trader’s Luck* trading clan (begging/pickpocketing/larceny; beatings). Surrogate mother: female Wookiee cook **Dewlanna** teaches Shyriiwook. Only relative found: cousin **Thrackan Sal-Solo** (cruel bully; later Human League; torments NR in Corellian Insurrection decades later). Expert thief/street fighter; swoop racing for Shrike; alien languages. At 17 forced into Jubilar All-Human Free-for-All; wins vs three larger opponents; Shrike beats him for insubordination.
### Ylesia — 10 B.B.Y. (P27)
At 19 escapes *Trader’s Luck* stowing in robotic cargo freighter → deposited on Ylesia…

### Ylesia continued + The Academy 10–5 B.B.Y. (P28 / S043)
Ylesia: tropical Hutt Space; fake religious retreat; Besadii Hutt spice-processing (glitterstim/ryll slave factories; t’landa Til). Solo pilots spice cargoes; falls for Pilgrim 921 **Bria Tharen**; rescues her; destroys primary glitterstim factory; plunders high priest art; rescues Togorian via rope-ladder. Alias **Vyyk Drago**. Plans sell loot → alter retinas → Imperial Academy. Bank freezes account; Bria leaves him. Forges ID + retinal surgery; applies as true Han Solo. Last night: Garris Shrike (lured by Hutt bounty; only one who knows alias) shot by rival hunter; Solo kills second in bare-knuckle brawl; switches clothes/ID; shoots corpse face → Besadii think Vyyk Drago dead.
**Carida Academy:** talented/rebellious; lands malfunctioning U-33 → nickname “Slick” (Lt. Badure); classmate **Mako Spince** destroys Carida’s mascot moon with gram of antimatter. Graduates top of class (beats Cadet **Soontir Fel** for valedictorian). ~8 months Corellian sector fleet Imperial Navy; rescues Wookiee slave from officer abuse → dishonorable discharge; Wookiee = **Chewbacca** (life debt).
**Illustration:** Bria Tharen portrait.

### The Life of a Smuggler — 5–2 B.B.Y. (P29 / S044)
Solo + Chewie → Hutt Space. Nal Hutta clans: **Besadii** (Aruk + Durga; Ylesia) vs **Desilijic** (Jiliac + nephew Jabba; Tatooine). Nar Shaddaa: Mako Spince intro; meet Shug Ninx, **Salla Zend**, con artist **Xaverri**; Smuggler’s Run, Kessel; learns Kessel Run skirting Maw. Piloting draws **Jabba**; regular Desilijic runs. Besadii discover Vyyk Drago = Han Solo; hire best bounty hunter. **Boba Fett** tracks to Nar Shaddaa; spoiled by **Lando Calrissian** (owns banged-up YT-1300 *Millennium Falcon*); Solo teaches Lando flying basics; Lando → Rafa. Solo works as Xaverri’s magician assistant; buys SoroSuub Starmite named *Bria*. Moff **Sarn Shild** plans blockade Nal Hutta / destroy Nar Shaddaa; Hutts bribe fail on Shild → bribe Admiral **Greelanx** for battle plan → organize defense.
**Illustration:** Chewbacca with bowcaster.

### Battle of Nar Shaddaa + sabacc → Falcon (P30–31 / S045–046)
Localized smuggler defense vs Imperial fleet; Solo unknowingly vs Academy classmate **Soontir Fel** (captain Dreadnaught *Pride of the Senate*); one of admiral’s three Dreadnaughts destroyed → Imperial retreat.
**Illustration:** “The Battle of Nar Shaddaa” (ISD + Y-wing + A-wing-like craft — caption-lock only).
Solo loses *Bria* in mishap; championship sabacc at Yarith Bespin / Cloud City; opponent Lando (back from ThonBoka); Solo wins championship → chooses *Millennium Falcon* from Lando’s used-starship lot.
Chewie marries **Mallatobuck** on Kashyyyk. Solo’s relationship with Salla Zend ends (commitment phobia); flees Hutt Space → Corporate Sector.

### Corporate Sector Blues — 2–1 B.B.Y. (P31 / S046)
CSA semi-independent; “in many ways, worse than the Empire” (profit over ideology). Secret prison **Stars’ End** on Mytus VII (stasis cells for dissidents/smugglers). Solo works for Big Bunji + Ploovo Two-For-One; team incl. labor droid **Bollux** + positronic processor **Blue Max** (in Bollux’s chest). After Chewie captured, pose as entertainers; Blue Max overloads reactor; anticoncussion field funnels blast down → dagger-shaped tower launches into orbit (negligible gravity/no atmosphere). *Falcon* docks to rescue Chewie + prisoners before tower smashes surface. Unconfirmed: CSA salvaged/moved structure → soured later NR–CSA talks.
On arid **Kamar**, accidentally inspire **Cult of Varn** among insectoids (replaced bland documentary *Varn, World of Water* with musical comedy); Kamarians chase “false prophet.” Varn cultists later on Mon Calamari, Bengat, Varn.

### Fiolla / Gallandro / Ammuud (P32 / S047)
Promised 10k for Lur pickup; cargo = slaves → frees them; tracks slavers to Bonadan for pay. Meets **Fiolla of Lorrd** (CSA assistant auditor-general) investigating slaving ring. Ambush → swoop escape (Shrike racing skills). Fiolla + Solo → Ammuud luxury liner; Chewie/droids + CSA territorial manager **Odumin** take *Falcon*. Odumin undercover; agent **Gallandro** (blinding speed-draw). Gallandro challenges clan leader → Solo chosen champion → Gallandro backs down; clan gives slaving records. Rescued from vengeful slavers by CSA *Victory*-class SD. Odumin still intends prosecute Solo/Chewie → Solo takes Odumin + Fiolla hostage to escape.
**Illustration:** “Solo and Fiolla in a hair-raising escape” (swoop through industrial shafts).

### Destitute in the Tion — 1–0 B.B.Y. (P33 / S048)
Flee CSA (destroy-on-sight); squander credits; Tion Hegemony → Grigmin’s Traveling Airshow. Solo job for University of Rudrig; meets old instructor **“Trooper” Badure** hunting *Queen of Ranroon* (Xim-era treasure hauler; vault on Dellalt). Accompanied by Badure + **Skynx** (Ruurian academic). *Falcon* stolen by Badure’s rivals; cross mountains on foot; Solo chin scar from hunting knife (still visible). Captured by **Survivors** cult (inbred *Queen of Ranroon* honor-guard descendants; maintain Xim war-droid army). Escape; locate vault. Bollux/Blue Max rhythmic frequency → Xim war droids lockstep across rickety bridge → collapse. Gallandro forces alliance then betrays; outdraws Solo (shoulder wound); pursues Skynx into lethal no-weapons zone → fried. Survived by infant daughter **Anja** (Anobis; problems for Solo ~25+ yrs later). Treasure = kiirium/mytag crystals (worthless modern). Skynx stays (Dellalt Project; later chroma-wing metamorphosis; offspring **Amisus** leads Unified Ruurian Colonies); Bollux + Blue Max stay with Skynx.

### Return to Ylesia — 0 B.B.Y., months before Yavin (P34 / S049)
Back to Nar Shaddaa. Desilijic poisons Besadii leader **Aruk**; **Durga** takes Besadii with clandestine help from **Prince Xizor**/Black Sun. Durga kills Desilijic leader **Jiliac** in single combat (bodies/tails as bludgeons). **Jabba** rules Desilijic; plots destroy Besadii Ylesia income. Contacts **Bria Tharen** (now undercover Rebel; helped Mon Mothma orchestrate Corellian Treaty). Jabba–Rebel alliance assault Ylesia (Rebels fund via captured spice). Solo + Bria rekindle on Nar Shaddaa; Solo + Lando join for spice share.
**Illustration:** “The Millennium Falcon.”

### Ylesia betrayal + The Last Spice Run — 0 B.B.Y., immediately prior to Yavin (P35 / S050)
Jabba’s assassins kill Ylesian priests; Rebels/smugglers assault colonies; Commander Tharen double-crosses smugglers, takes spice. Solo/Tharen part badly; Lando never wants to see Solo again; comrades think Solo in on betrayal.
Rejected Solo + Chewie take Jabba Kessel Run; tip from **Moruth Doole** → Imperial customs pursuit; *Falcon* closer to Maw than anyone dared; forced dump spice → Jabba angered. Tatooine for credits; **Boba Fett** relays Bria died in Operation Skyhook on Toprawa; Solo agrees tell her family. Enters Mos Eisley cantina → two desert dwellers seeking Alderaan passage; Jabba bounty follows unpaid debt.

## LANDO CALRISSIAN (P35– / S050–)
Reluctant hero; origins a “riddle” (forthcoming but inconsistent). Late twenties high-profile phase. ~4 BBY professional sabacc gambler/con artist; winnings fund fine clothing/cuisine. Wins dilapidated Corellian freighter (*Millennium Falcon*) from Bespin sabacc player who couldn’t cover debt; mixed feelings (can’t fly); hires tutor **Han Solo**.

### Lando early + The Sharu Awaken — 4 B.B.Y. (P36–37 / S051–052)
Best star jockeys in Hutt Space after Solo tutelage; rescues Solo from Boba Fett (obedience drug; orders Fett to rim); Solo teaches piloting free. Still abysmal aviator when leaving Hutt Space (avoid vengeful Fett; new marks).
Oseon (Centrality gambler’s paradise); poor luck → wins ownership of droid in Rafa system. Rents Class Five pilot droid for Rafa IV landing. Rafa: colossal plastic pyramids of ancient **Sharu** (thought vanished; impregnable). **Toka** (“Broken People”) primitive slave labor for human colonists. Collects **Vuffi Raa** (starfish-shaped; five detachable limbs; perky). Arrested on trumped-up charges; forced work for **Rokur Gepta**, Sorcerer of Tund (gray-cloaked; Sith teachings disciple) seeking **Mindharp of Sharu**. Given transdimensional key; search Rafa V; ambushed by Toka; tied to crystalline lifetree; Vuffi rescues. Find Mindharp (changes forms/dimensions). Sharu history: hid underground from unimaginably powerful alien entity; plastic pyramids + crystalline life-orchards drained intelligence; Mindharp = beacon safe to reemerge. Governor steals Mindharp; activates → subharmonic reverse social order; pyramids crumble; new cities; Toka regain intelligence as Sharu. Interdiction field; alien society looks down on lesser sentients; human cities ruined; trade dies; scientists boom; Lando hailed hero. Palpatine posts permanent picket; NR later limits to 500 Obroan Institute researchers.
**Illustration:** “An ancient secret revealed” (pyramid/obelisk awakening; caption-lock only—not Exar Kun era).

### The Battle of Nar Shaddaa — 3 B.B.Y. (P37– / S052–)
Lando + Vuffi flee Rafa in *Falcon* with cargo of life crystals (lifespan rumor; scarce after Sharu return)…

### Nar Shaddaa battle (Lando) + Back to the Oseon — 3 B.B.Y. (P38 / S053)
Life crystals → ~¼ million credits; buys used-starship lot on Nar Shaddaa from dissatisfied Duros; struggles despite Vuffi. Empire invades → joins **Battle of Nar Shaddaa**; Vuffi pilots *Falcon* “like a born maestro”; smugglers win but Lando’s inventory ruined (<1/10 spaceworthy); sells 90% to **Roa** at loss; eyes Yarith Bespin casino.
Regional sabacc entry 10k unaffordable → return Centrality. **Back to the Oseon 3 BBY:** arrive for annual **Flamewind** (3 weeks stellar flares + ionized vapors; brilliant colors; radiation storms). Oseon asteroids = wealthy playground. Forced into drug sting vs **Bohhuah Mutdah** (trillionaire addict; owns Oseon 5792); ferries two agents through Flamewind; agents try arrest → dead in 10 seconds.
**Illustration:** Vuffi Raa portrait.

### Gepta reveal + Fortune Won, Fortune Lost — 3 B.B.Y. (P39 / S054)
Mutdah = **Rokur Gepta** (hatred over Mindharp). Amicable with Palpatine; decommissioned Republic cruiser + Centrality autonomy; can command Imperial naval/TIE bombers. Renatasian-marked starfighters shatter asteroid canopy; *Falcon* escapes as Renatasians hurl towed Dreadnaught engine into asteroid. Target = Vuffi Raa (“Butcher of Renatasia”): 13 yrs pre-Yavin lost human colony rediscovered; Vuffi’s programming paved Imperial takeover as brutal as Caamas (little Senate notice). Lando refuses help kill friend; Flamewind masks escape. Renatasians later team with Gepta.
Mutdah drug bust aftermath: Lando steals drug-payment case → millionaire twenty times over in a day; wealth doesn’t last (audit avoidance; small deposits); 50k to Aargau for sabacc entry; *Falcon* to Dela (Centrality financial hub) for remainder.
**Illustration:** “A gambler and his nemesis” (Lando with blaster facing Rokur Gepta).

### Drea Renthal + To Save the ThonBoka — 3–2.5 B.B.Y. (P40–41 / S055–056)
*Falcon* captured mid-pirate attack by **Drea Renthal** (“pirate queen”; large freebooter fleet; fought Imperials at Nar Shaddaa); Mutdah lockbox sum discovered; charm fails to keep cash; paths cross again later.
Friend **Lehesu** of the **Oswaft** (gigantic vacuum-breathers; Corellian sea ray × Arkanian jellyfish; intense information-dense language; natural hyperspace jumps; ThonBoka nebula / StarCave). Centrality knew of them for generations. Palpatine orders extermination; Imperial blockade ~500 capital ships; modified Carrack cruisers poison interstellar plankton. Lando + Vuffi break blockade; meet elders (~1 km diameter); Oswaft synthesize false targets (Imperial friendly fire) + sonic shouts destroy warships.
**Gepta duel:** arrives battleship; challenges Lando 1v1 zero-g vacuum; threatens EM torpedo irradiate nebula. Recorded by SD *Eminence*; spacesuits/jet packs; Lando shoots Gepta ankle → form withers; Gepta = **Croke** (tiny snail-like Unknown Regions); Palpatine interested in Sorcerers of Tund archaic Sith. Gepta had co-opted/annihilated Sorcerers of Tund via Croke illusion; Tund irradiated wasteland; sect ends.
Thousands of gargantuan 50-km metallic spheres (self-aware droids from Unknown Regions) demand ceasefire to reclaim Vuffi (“child”); built to record galactic experiences; mission complete → departs with them; Imperial genocide abandoned. Mass sightings persist (e.g. Priole Danna festival Lamuir IV, 100k witnesses).
**Illustration:** “The Oswaft versus the Empire” (caption-lock; Oswaft vs Imperial ships—not Yuuzhan Vong).

### Entrepreneurism — 2.5–0 B.B.Y. (P41 / S056)
Oswaft gemstone gift → invest berubium mine Borgo asteroid belt → worthless; fortune lost. Withdraws 50k numbered account awaiting sabacc tournament…

### Sabacc loss + The Respectable One — 0–3 A.B.Y. (P42–43 / S057–058)
Waiting eased by rebuilding Nar Shaddaa lot + romance with Drea Renthal (~weeks). Cloud City sabacc: final vs Han Solo → Solo wins credits + sole *Falcon* ownership. Impoverished Lando borrows 1,500 from Solo; turns into hundreds of thousands via high rollers; Imperial cons (Pesmenben IV berubium-like scam). Second Boba Fett run-in on luxury liner (Fett after Bria Tharen; Lando expendable hostage); saved by Renthal’s pirates who buy freedom. Ylesia battle alongside Solo; Bria spice swindle → Lando blames Han; friendship over when Han asks loan for Jabba.
**Taanab** (~5 mo after Yavin): denta bean cargo; pirate raid; wager → flies freighter vs Norulac freebooters; dumps electrified netting; hero locally; TriNebulon News spreads fame → later Alliance High Command promotes him General before Endor.
Investigates Hologram Fun World (caught in Imperial Project Starscream); owns it ~1 yr after Endor → interest in SkyCenter Galleria. Greatest coup: sabacc at Trest vs **Dominic Raynor** → wins Baron Administrator of Cloud City. Charms citizens; wins Exex board; partners with cyborg **Lobot**; fends Mining Guild; hires commando pilots vs pirates; **EV-9D9** psychotic → dismantles ¼ droid population; Tibanna profits +35%; stable industry for millions. ESB: Solo + Vader + Fett arrive; moral quandary city vs friend → joins Rebellion.

## THE SKYWALKERS (P43– / S058–)
Luke + Leia: ray of hope; influenced modern history more than any other duo. Luke (now Jedi Master) restored Jedi from extinction; Leia second NR Chief of State. Raised separately unaware of origins; father Anakin/Vader; mother a queen; both incredibly Force-strong; Palpatine feared. Obi-Wan hid newborns from Emperor + father as “finely honed weapons.” Luke with **Owen and Beru Lars** on Tatooine…
**Illustration:** “The Skywalker twins” (Leia foreground; Luke Jedi robes behind).

### Skywalker upbringing close (P44 / S059)
Luke: Tatooine (Anakin’s boyhood home); Owen/Beru Lars low-profile isolationists; “Old Ben” watches from Jundland Wastes. Leia: Alderaan; adoptive father **Bail Prestor Organa** (House Organa); raised as princess; confident/poised/quick political thinker; studies Aldera (diplomacy/government/languages); friend **Winter**; Bail takes her to Coruscant etc.; weapons master **Giles Durane** teaches self-defense/marksmanship; youngest ever elected Imperial Senate. Lineage secret until years after Endor; siblings unaware related until ~4 yrs after Death Star rescue. Political fallout of Vader ancestry: some partisan accusations vs Leia; most citizens conciliatory—children not punished for parents; good outweighs shadow legacy.
