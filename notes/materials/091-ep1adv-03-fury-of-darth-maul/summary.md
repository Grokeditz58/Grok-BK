# EA3 The Fury of Darth Maul: summary

- Full read of the scan (OCR l.1–2377). The epub is complete.

## Top Maul feats
1. **Snaps durasteel binders** with no pain (p.43).
2. **Catches 4 armor-piercing arrows in mid-air** and throws them back to kill the snipers (p.45).
3. **Kills the Bartokk hive single-handed.** About 14 of the 15 die: 4 dungeon guards, 3 in the pool room, the axe Bartokk, the fortress blast, 2 on the skiff, the speeder guard flung off, the stowaway, and 3 in the fighter. Severed parts are still a factor.
4. **Deflects 3 blaster bolts back in mid-leap** (p.75–76).
5. **Telekinesis:** a door bolt, saber disarm, hook throw, and a heavy boulder moved 8 m (p.44, 53, 58, 89–90).
6. Pulls a giant spydr off the ceiling by its web. Flings a Bartokk off his speeder one-handed.
- Tech: hacks Bartokk-claw consoles, jams the gravitational sensor, takes over 25 droid fighters.

## Anti-feats

- C-3PX escapes from him, fate unknown.
