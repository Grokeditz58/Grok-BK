# EA3 The Fury of Darth Maul: notes

## Front matter and Introduction (l.1–148)
- Recap of #1–2. Sidious learned of the Bartokk theft from Neimoidian spies and "summoned his dark apprentice".

## Ch. 1 (p.9–14)
- Sidious explains the plan: the starfighters were meant for **taking over Brentaal** to control the Perlemian Trade Route and the Hydian Way. That is now postponed because the Jedi know.
- He gives Maul a data card that **controls the droid fighters**: "Retrieve… if you can, and destroy them if you must." Their client should "live in terror". The Bartokks: "Do with them as you please."
- The *Metron Burner* leaves Esseles for Rhinnal. Trinkatta's arm is regenerating. Obi-Wan wants to chase the freighter instead, but Qui-Gon puts Rhinnal first (Adi once saved his life).

## Ch. 2 (p.17–24)
- **Sith Infiltrator specs:** 26.5 m long, a modified Sienar Star Courier, 6 laser cannons, class 3.0 hyperdrive (later called a Sienar SSDS 11-A), an ion engine with radiator panels, dark eye probes, a speeder bike and a **cloak generator in the prow**.
- **C-3PX:** a TC-type protocol droid with **83 concealed weapons**. Maul modified it himself, and it speaks only when needed.
- On Rhinnal, an EM storm caused the blackout. Adi is stable. Noro Zak is a Baxthrax with leathery wings. The *Radiant VII* is in the hangar. Another storm is due in 3 hours, so they are grounded. Qui-Gon: "That Bartokk freighter isn't going to get anywhere near Corulag."

## Ch. 3 (p.27–39)
- Maul's long-range scan of the Darpa Sector finds 58 ships. The one that withholds ID is heading for Ralltiir. C-3PX briefs him on Bartokks: 15 per hive. Maul: "Failure is not an option."
- Plan: "After I let them catch me." Cloaked approach through a ravine. Speeder climb up the cliff, "used the Force to sense each obstacle". He scales the wall by hand.
- Trap: spotlights, his saber taken, binders. 11 Bartokks, 25 droid fighters in a 5×5 formation, and a leader with a vocabulator. The Bartokks tracked the cloaked ship (**anti-feat for the cloak**). C-3PX lets itself be captured on purpose.
- In the cell, an interrogator droid with 5 laser scalpels. "This entire fortress is going to burn." Game Book break (p.40).

## Ch. 4 (p.43–54)
- **Snaps durasteel binders by pulling his arms apart. "felt no pain"** (p.43).
- Kicks the droid, then dodges "faster than the droid's photoreceptor could follow" so it smashes into the wall. Takes its Bavo Six serum.
- **Force-unlocks the bolt of a reinforced door.** Kills 2 guards with arrows he grabs from their quiver, one stabbed and one thrown.
- Trapdoor: he falls into a cavern. **Catches 4 armor-piercing arrows in mid-air and throws them back through the grate, killing both snipers** (p.45).
- **Draigon-slug:** he spares it and ties it in a knot by its own tail (p.46–47).
- Nine-metre chasm: he is "confident he could jump" it, but goes hand-over-hand across stalactites instead. **He kicks the nine-legged spydr and pulls it off the ceiling by its web** (p.51).
- The Falleen hostage over the toxic pool is a hologram trap. **He Force-pulls his saber from the Bartokk's claw** (p.53).

## Ch. 5 (p.57–66)
- **Kills 2 spear Bartokks:** cuts one in half at the waist, then takes the other's claws and head with one spin. He backflips over the crawling parts and kicks them into the pool.
- **Force-throws the hook to snag the leader**, then injects Bavo Six. Confession: the client is **Groodo the Hutt**, who builds hyperdrives on Esseles. He built the TF prototype and wasn't paid, so he put a sensor tag on it. The target is **Corulag Academy**, revenge for his son being turned down. The leader dies in the pool after a boomerang deflection cuts the chain.
- Munitions room: he sets a baradium thermal detonator for 10 minutes and takes a second one.
- On the freighter he downloads the **crystal gravitational trap** sensor data. A Bartokk with four vibro-axes: he cuts off all four claws in one sweep, and a kick lands it on its own axes.
- He escapes on the speeder at the last second. The fortress blows up and a tower crushes the freighter.

## Ch. 6–7 (p.69–76)
- Speeder chase with the skiff: **a thrown detonator kills 2 Bartokks** and cratered the canyon wall.
- The guard dodges his saber "with dazzling speed" and grabs onto the speeder. **Maul flings it off one-handed.**
- He repairs a hyperdrive motivator fault, then finds a stowaway Bartokk. He vents the bridge, but the Bartokk reseals the hatch. **Maul deflects 3 blaster bolts back in mid-leap** and kills it.
- He beats the fighters to Corulag thanks to his better hyperdrive.

## Ch. 8–9 (p.77–85)
- 25 droid fighters plus the six-winged fighter arrive. The Bartokks detect the cloaked Infiltrator (anti-feat).
- **He jams their gravitational trap sensor**, then takes over all 25 droids with the Sidious card.
- He kills the Bartokk fighter with 6 cannon shots (two for deflector shields, two for particle shields, two for the hull).
- Gives Groodo 30 seconds, then the 25 droids destroy his cruiser. Groodo and his son survive in a pod, which Maul allows. He sends the droids to a Neimoidian base.
- "Are Hutts really afraid of anything?"

## Ch. 10 (p.89–93)
- Groodo and his son **Boonda** land 34 km SW of **Curamelle**, the capital of Corulag. Groodo places a HoloNet call.
- On Ralltiir, **Maul Force-lifts a heavy boulder and drops it 8 m away.** He finds C-3PX's restraining bolt: the droid escaped, but "C-3PX was gone." "He left no footprints."
- Rhinnal: Adi has recovered. The satellite reports 27 ships, with the Bartokk fighter and the cruiser destroyed. Qui-Gon asks: "Who has the droid starfighters now?"
- Yoda by hologram: "Meet you on Corulag, I will. Matters most urgent await us at the Academy." NEXT: *Jedi Emergency*.
- Back cover: "…only one person he can trust… Another Sith Lord." The "A HAMPTA SCAN" end page is a scanner credit, not book content.
