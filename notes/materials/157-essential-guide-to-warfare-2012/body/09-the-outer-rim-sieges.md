**THE JEDI GENERALS.** Throughout their history, the Jedi oscillated between standing apart from the galactic government and being tightly integrated with it. They were wary of ruling, preferring to serve as guardians rather than as military leaders, ministers, or kingmakers. Their interventions had nevertheless shaped the Republic: they prevented the young Republic from annihilating the Tionese; they plotted in secret to overthrow the Pius Dea chancellory; and they served as chancellors ruling large swaths of the galaxy in the centuries before Ruusan. Each time, they surrendered the powers they had assumed and returned to the guardian role.

By 22 BBY, Supreme Chancellor Palpatine asked the Jedi to lead the Republic’s newly revealed clone army. The Judicial Forces were ill prepared to fight a galactic war, and a Jedi apostate—Count Dooku—stood at the head of the Separatist movement. The Jedi accepted, hoping to short-circuit the Separatist threat.

The Jedi—technically already members of the Judicial Department—were appointed officers in the Grand Army of the Republic, the Republic Navy, the Starfighter Corps, and the Special Operations Brigade. Their commands were fluid. Padawans were given the rank of commander; Knights and Masters were referred to as generals.

The wartime bargain caused a rift in the Order. Some Jedi questioned the morality of leading an army of clones bred for war. Padawans and younger Knights succumbed to impatience and anger. Yoda warned: “In this war, a danger there is of losing who we are,” — the page ends cleanly after that quoted sentence (comma inside the closing quotes).

## S107 — printed **96** (folio LL confirmed)

**ARMORY AND SENSOR PROFILE.**

**JEDI CRUISER.** The Venator-class Star Destroyer, popularly known as the Jedi cruiser, was fast enough to run down blockade runners, small enough to land on a planet, and big enough to undertake independent missions. Dorsal flight decks and launch bays could deploy hundreds of fighters. The dual bridge assigned starfighter command to the port tower and standard helm functions to the starboard. Venators were emblazoned in Republic red.

Walex Blissex left Kuat Drive Yards for Rendili StarDrive, where the Victory-class Star Destroyer emerged from the Victor Initiative Project, a collaboration with KDY ordered by the Supreme Chancellor. Victories were smaller than Venators but more heavily armed, with a smaller starfighter complement. They were pressed into early service after Separatist Admiral Dua Ningo broke out of Foerost with his Bulwark Fleet, was chased across the Core Worlds, and was destroyed at the Battle of Anaxes. Late in the war, Venators were supplanted by the hangarless Tector and Imperator classes; the Imperator class was renamed the Imperial, the signature warship of the Empire.

**JEDI STARFIGHTERS.** Shortly before Geonosis, Saesee Tiin supervised a corps of Jedi starfighter pilots and worked with Kuat Systems Engineering to replace the aging Delta-6 fleet. The Delta-7 Aethersprite light interceptor offered state-of-the-art targeting, directed shielding, and a hardwired astromech. Blissex, drawing on the Aurek strikefighter of the ancient Republic (the same design later inspired the A-wing series Blissex created for the Alliance), shaped the type. The Delta-7 lacked hyperspace-calculation capability and was mated with Syliure-31 hyperdrive rings by TransGalMeg Industries. Obi-Wan flew a Jedi Order Delta-7 on the trail of Jango Fett. The Delta-7B adopted Jedi modifications and added a standard astromech port forward of the cockpit.

The Eta-2 Actis interceptor had no shields; armor, sensors, and systems were radically stripped down. It was “an extension of a Jedi pilot’s abilities and reliant on her Force-enhanced senses,” allowing “a Jedi behind the stick to fly almost as fast as she could think.” Kuat Drive Yards’ specialty was warships, not starfighters, and Imperial starfighter contracts went to Sienar Fleet Systems. **AT-TE.** The All Terrain Tactical Enforcer Assault Walker, or AT-TE, emerged from a Rothana Heavy Engineering industrial security vehicle used to defend mining facilities in the wilds beyond the Rim, with its six-legged stance borrowed from Rothana’s arctic horny whelmer (tight-read from `whelmer_p108`; two words, “horny whelmer”). KDY beefed it up. Its principal weapon was a heavy mass-driver projectile cannon, plus six anti-personnel laser cannons. Clawed magnetic grapples and natural grounding made it immune to ion weapons and able to pass through particle shields, unlike repulsorlift vehicles. At the Battle of Bothawui, Anakin deployed AT-TEs on asteroids as a screen against Grievous’s task force. The walker was a mainstay but was vulnerable to mines, and its stubby legs limited gunners’ field of fire. KDY developed long-legged walkers such as the AT-HE, a forerunner of the mighty AT-AT. **THE SECTOR ARMIES.** The Ruusan Reformations decentralized military power, leaving the Judicial Forces as a rapid-response arm and Planetary Security Forces to handle regional sectors. Palpatine invoked ancient Republic law, appointing Governor-Generals to coordinate military action in each sector in consultation with its Senator, while answering to the Chancellor. Printed typography anomaly, tight-read from `risu_p108`: “this system would give risu to a permanent class of regional governors, who assumed direct control of the regional sectors when the Senate was disbanded.” (epub almost certainly “rise”; do not correct).

Governor-Generals improved PSF effectiveness but weren’t an answer to overall strategy. The Republic armed forces were divided into twenty Sector Armies, each charged with military control of a different kind of sector—known as an oversector or priority sector. Contrary to HoloNet impressions, the armies contained both clone and non-clone units—some epic battles were fought without a single clone—and coordinating the two proved difficult. Most non-clone troops (except officers) served in the PSF, in defensive deployments, or with the Judicials.

In the first weeks the Grand Army numbered the equivalent of just two Corps, but soon 3 million troops were ready, and each standard Sector Army numbered nearly 150,000 clone troopers, a number that grew dramatically. Each Sector Army was divided into four subordinate corps, paired with four navy assault lines, each containing two Acclamator-class transports and two frigates. The formation was designed around the so-called 1/4/16/64 Plan: one corps a mobile armored reserve, the others divided into progressively smaller components. In practice one corps remained largely intact as an assault division, while the others were divided into hundreds of fast-moving raiding companies, with only a dozen or so formations operating at battalion or brigade level—most usually drawn from the reserve corps itself. **ARC-170 FIGHTER.** Built by Incom and Subpro, the Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter (printed with a capital *C* in *ReConnaissance*) was designed as a rugged, durable fighter suitable for long-range missions and able to stand on its own without support from carriers and warships.

The ARC-170’s split transverse wings unfolded to expose heat sinks and radiators, aiding the fighter’s shielding and helping it shed excess heat. Its nose was packed with sensors, scanners, and jammers. Armament was provided by two forward-facing laser cannons, two aft cannons, and a sextet of proton torpedoes.

The ARC-170 was a critical part of the Republic Starfighter Corps during the Outer Rim Sieges, battering Separatist warships and ground targets while Jedi Eta-2s and lighter V-wings gave it cover. The ARC-170 proved particularly valuable as a part of the Open Circle Fleet, with the ranks of clone pilots supplemented by non-clone volunteers who’d proved themselves in the Planetary Security Forces and Judicial service. The Empire selected some of those pilots as the progenitors of new clone lines.

The ARC-170 began as a heavier version of Incom/Subpro’s Z-95 Headhunter, beloved by pilots across the galaxy as a reliable, easily modified fighter. During the Clone Wars, the ARC-170s would be modified to produce a pair of heavier bombers, the PTB-625 and the NTB-630. In later years the ARC-170 would be succeeded by the T-65 X-wing, which sacrificed some of the ARC-170’s firepower in favor of regaining the Z-95’s agility.
