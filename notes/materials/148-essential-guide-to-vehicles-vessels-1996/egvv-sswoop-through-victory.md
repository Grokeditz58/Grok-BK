# EGVV 1996 — notes148: S-Swoop → Victory SD (printed 166–189 / scan 102–113)
1996 paraphrase; quotes with crop+locator.

## Printed 166–167 / scan 102 — S-SWOOP (Mobquet Flare-S)
- “Engines with seats”; no rider protection; spin-out prone; >600 km/h. Flare-S hybrid speeder-bike/swoop; reinforced chassis vs fracture; forward vanes + longer stabilizers + central repulsor pod to recover from tumbles. Sealed flight suits for high-gee/altitude.

## Printed 168–169 / scan 103 — TAFANDA BAY (Ithorian Grand Herd Ship)
- Eco-city; Lesser Plooriod commerce; Ithorians live aboard (Ithor jungles sacred). Moss/flowers/trees exterior; dozens of gigantic repulsors. Great Atrium ~1 km; observation decks; botanical domes; jungle ecology rim. Wedge+Qui Xux visit; Momaw Nadon resisted Imperial ag secrets → banished → reinstated after joining Alliance.

## Printed 170–171 / scan 104 — TANK DROID (Arakyd XR-85)
- Automated; ~**32 m** long × >**30 m** tall (2× AT-AT); tracked; water ≤15 m; **70 km/h**. Heavy particle cannon (5 km, level bunkers); 2 forward turbolasers; 4 twin heavy repeating blasters (2F/2R); rear Golan DF.9 AP. Separate gens per weapon; pirated IA R7 brain. Post-Thrawn Coruscant recapture forced NR retreat.

## Printed 172–173 / scan 105 — TIE BOMBER (Sienar)
- Surgical strike; slower/less maneuverable than TIE/ln. Twin pods: starboard pilot/eject/life support; port ordnance. P-s4 twins between. Loadouts: forward 8 concussion OR 4 proton; main bay 8 conc / 4 proton / 8 bombs / 6 orbital mines / 64 thermal dets; 2 forward lasers. Rare hollow-hulled boarding variant. Pre-Emperor-death prototype → Scimitar lineage.

## Printed 174–175 / scan 106 — TIE CRAWLER (Santhe/Sienar Century Tank)
- Post-Thrawn Coruscant; TIE pod between tread housings; single crew. Twin SSct gens; **90 km/h**; field-repairable; scavenged often. 2 forward medium blasters + retractable bottom light turbolaser (any arc). Light armor; **drive/treads easily damaged**. N-s6 fire control; TIE washouts crewed.

## Printed 176–177 / scan 107 — TIE DEFENDER (Sienar prototype)
- Pre-Endor; Zaarin defeat. Tri-wing solar panels. **>300k credits** (~5× TIE/ln); hyperdrive + Novaldex shields; ~**40%** faster sublight than TIE/ln; P-sz9.7 + triple maneuver jets → more agile than Interceptor. Flights of 4; cost + Emperor’s death limited spread; Thrawn elite wings.
- **Quote:** `quotes/egvv1996-p176-scan107-tiedefender-cost.jpg`.

## Printed 178–179 / scan 108 — TIE/D FIGHTER (Sienar TIE/droid)
- Calamari automated; Cybot Ace-6 brain (<10 kg) or remote capital control. Modular TIE/ln+Interceptor parts; rectangular solar for extra drive energy; armor but mass ≈ TIE/ln. **Limitation:** droid brains lack inventiveness/unpredictability — skilled organics superior. Factories on World Devastators (destroyed Calamari).
- **Quote:** `quotes/egvv1996-p178-scan108-tied-droid-limits.jpg`.

## Printed 180–181 / scan 109 — TIE FIGHTER (Sienar TIE/ln)
- Symbol of Imperial Navy space control; spherical pod + solar array wings; small target; incredible maneuverability. Mass numbers from cruisers; planetary/cruiser defense, assaults, bomber escort.
- **Limits (verbatim from crop):** only sublight (weight save); base/cruiser dependent; **two days’ supplies** / combat often refuel after few hours; **no shields, no secondary weapon or drive systems, no onboard life support** (fully sealed flight suits). Empire deems **casualties “inconsequential”** as long as objectives met; some strategists say fragility forces superior pilots. High-gee shock couch + crash webbing + repulsor antigrav; foot yokes + hand yoke — simultaneous flight+fire “very difficult.” Pair fire-linked forward lasers; solar-fed gens/batteries. Common = **TIE/ln** (successor earlier T.I.E./TIE).
- **Quote:** `quotes/egvv1996-p180-scan109-tie-no-shields-casualties.jpg` (+ tight `…-tie-limits-tight.jpg`).

## Printed 182–183 / scan 110 — TIE INTERCEPTOR (Sienar)
- From Vader x1 advancements; faster/more lethal than TIE/ln; ≈ A-wing speed (fastest production). Elongated bent solar panels (smaller profile). Advanced ion-stream + twin-port deflectors; 4 wingtip lasers + advanced targeting. **Little armor, no shields, no life support, no hyperdrive.** By Emperor death ~20% of Imp fighters; Thrawn later began arming with shields.
- **Quote:** `quotes/egvv1996-p182-scan110-tieinterceptor-limits.jpg`.

## Printed 184–185 / scan 111 — TWIN POD CLOUD CAR (Bespin Motors Storm IV)
- Upper-atmo; **1,500 km/h**; ceiling low orbit; closer to starships than airspeeders. Ion main + repulsor maneuver; pressurized pods but **no life support**. Storm IV: Wing Guard; pilot port / gunner starboard; Quadex Kyromaster on boom (open cooling). Easy mods; fixture armor points. **Limitation:** small Incom TX-2 gen struggles with light lasers — swap bulky sensors for Fabritech → room for extra weapons gen.

## Printed 186–187 / scan 112 — V-WING AIRSPEEDER (Slayn & Korpil)
- Verpine (B-wing designers); Mon Calamari defense. **1,000 km/h** ion/repulsor; Chab-Ylwuum scramjet → **1,400** but **impractical combat** (tears apart on turns); ceiling **100 km**. Double laser 2 km — air combat OK, **not enough vs heavily armored**. Minimal pilot protection; **no ejection**. Deployed from speeder transports (4 or 6).
- **Quote:** `quotes/egvv1996-p186-scan112-vwing-scramjet-limits.jpg`.

## Printed 188–189 / scan 113 — VICTORY STAR DESTROYER (Rendili Victory-class)
- Clone Wars–era; 900 m; crew >5,000. 10 quad + 40 double turbolasers; 80 concussion; 10 tractor; 2 TIE squadrons (24); 2,000 troops + drop ships/AT-ATs/juggernauts/Floating Fortresses. Class 1 hyperdrive (faster than ISD). **Deep-space speed weak** — LF9 ions can’t catch modern ships; need Interdictors/gravity wells. One of largest capitals effective in atmosphere. Many sold (CSA bought 250).
