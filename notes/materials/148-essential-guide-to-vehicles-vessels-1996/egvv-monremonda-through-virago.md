## Printed 118–119 / scan 078 — MON REMONDA (Mon Cal MC80B)
- Interim heavy cruiser ~1 yr post-Endor (MC90s needed 4 yrs). Han Solo flagship hunting **Zsinj**; helped destroy SSD *Iron Fist*. ~**1,200 m**; reinforced hull + multiple backup shields (survives fire that wrecks Imperial ships). **48** turbolasers (banks of 12); **20** ion (banks of 4); 4 fighter squadrons (1 A / 2 X / 1 B; later MC80Bs → 8). Almost impossible for humans (Mon Cal physiology/displays). Destroyed by World Devastator *Silencer-7* at Second Battle of Calamari (crew lost after destroying an ISD + supports).

## Printed 120–121 / scan 079 — MT-AT TRANSPORT (Caridan Mountain Terrain Armored Transport)
- Late Imperial walker for sheer inclines; 8 independently articulated clawed legs. Used by Amb. Furgan vs NR on **Anoth**. Central drive pod; platform rotates 180° (pilot front / cargo rear); each leg has rotating double laser. Orbital drop via thermal cocoons (droid brain + thrusters + bistate impact gel). After Carida destroyed by Sun Crusher, only known plant lost (NR suspects plans evacuated). Stat block visible: 19.3 m × 13.9 m; crew 1+1; Move 35 / 100 km/h; Maneuver 2D (1D level); 8× double lasers 4D.

## Printed 122–123 / scan 080 — NEBULON RANGER (Corellia StarDrive Coruscant-class Heavy Courier)
- Great Sith War (~4k yrs pre-Empire): Ulic Qel-Droma, Cay, Tott Doneeta. Right-angle frame; retractable atmo wings; main wing armored (fuel + 6 AlderaanMotors gens); sensor pylon; round airlock. **8 shield gens** but massive drain — raised **only in combat**; vulnerability let Onderon beast-riders down it with **one seeker torpedo**. Pulse cannon; wingtip retractable lasers; rotating laser on 2nd engine array; mandible proton pairs (6 ea); concussion-sphere launchers (8 ea). Modular cargo (or remove for airspeeders); 3 escape pods.

## Printed 124–125 / scan 081 — PRINCE XIZOR'S VIRAGO (MandalMotors StarViper)
- Black Sun personal assault transport; “alive” moving wings/nacelles. Quadex IGT rear + Koensayr N2-f microthrusters per wingtip. Heavy wing armor; 3 Torplex rear shields + 1 forward; 2 Taim & Bak HI-12 double heavy lasers on 180° arms; 2 Borstel proton launchers (3 ea); 4 power gens (1/wing spar). Wings fold for space / expand combat; expanded wings hamper atmo → retract for landing.
