## Printed 190–191 / scan 114 — WILD KARRDE (Modified Corellian Action VI)
- Talon Karrde personal freighter + mobile comms base. Stock Action VI = lumbering 125 m / 90,000 MT; *Wild Karrde* heavily modded. **3 turbolasers** vs capital; extra shielding + reinforced plating → **“as tough as an Imperial Lancer frigate.”** Sensor shroud rivals Imperial spy ships — long-range masked; close-range looks helpless cargo. Sublight ≈ most Imp warships; **Class 1 hyperdrive**; nav computer almost ISD-detailed. Docking bay, airlock, extendable force tube; rear holds → life support (vornskr); forward living/offices; medical + droid bays; hyperradio (military/gov only normally).

## Printed 192–193 / scan 115 — WORLD DEVASTATORS (Custom Planetary Assault Weapon)
- Dark Empire revived Emperor; Umak Leth. Unlike Death Stars: tractor surface into **molecular furnace** → raw materials → droid factories → TIEs / battle cruisers / more Devastators. Hyperdrives + ions; central droid brain holds Imperial vessel plans. **Silencer-7** largest: **3,200 m** × **1,500 m** tall; Mon Calamari assault lead. Crew 25,000; 125 heavy turbolasers, 200 blasters, 80 proton tubes, 15 ions, 15 tractor. Defeat: Palpatine command/control coding → Luke + R2 shut down at Mon Calamari.

## Printed 194–195 / scan 116 — XP-38 LANDSPEEDER (SoroSuub Sport)
- >**300 km/h**; stiff repulsor setting → smooth terrain only unless recalibrated (≤3 hrs rough; most skip reset complexity). 2-person + retractable duraplex windscreen. Autopilot under cowling looks like R2 — **purely cosmetic** (make kids feel like starfighter pilots). 3 rear turbines + twin maneuvering flaps. Post-Luke Tatooine boom; depressed demand for older X-34.

## Printed 196–197 / scan 117 — X-WING FIGHTER (Incom T-65)
- Last Incom design before Imperial seizure; Rebel commandos helped senior designers defect with plans/prototypes. S-foils open “X” combat / closed cruise. 4 Taim & Bak KX9 lasers (simultaneous/sequence/pairs); 2 Krupx MG7 proton launchers ×3 each — DS1 thermal exhaust. Single pilot + IA R2 socket; T-16-like controls. Titanium-alloy hull; Chempat shields; full ejection. Incom GBK-585 hyperdrive; **R2 stores ≤10 jump coordinate sets** (no dedicated nav computer). Engines: Incom 4L4 (alt 4j.4); Novaldex 04-Z; Fabritech ANq 3.6 etc.

Chempat shields; Novaldex; Thiodyne O3-R cryo cells. Alt cannons KX5.

Alliance defensive/training (controls ≈ X/Y). **Han Solo** Mark I vs CSA IRDs at outlaw-tech base. **Mara Jade** modded Z-95 + hyperdrive; nearly destroyed Katana battle; Karrde techs refurbished.
