# EGVV 1996 — notes148: Chariot → Enforcer One (printed 18–49 / scan 028–043)
1996-source paraphrase; quotes only with crop+locator. Two-page body spreads.

## Printed 18–19 / scan 028 — CHARIOT LAV (Uulshos LAVr QH-7 Chariot)
- Imperial Army command speeder; ~12 m; **100 km/h**; max altitude **8 m**. Rear-echelon with artillery/repulsorlift; also scouting.
- Armored canopy + layered plates; rear ECM vs EM bursts; signal scrambler. Single short-range laser (drops from hatch) = last-ditch defense. Crew 3 + command staff; ≥4 comm droids.
- Thrawn upgraded fleet Chariots; personal unit laser → more powerful swivel gun. Used on Myrkr search; **destroyed in Hyllyard City** when Luke toppled a **ten-meter stone arch** onto it.
- **Quote:** `quotes/egvv1996-p018-scan028-chariot-thrawn-hyllyard.jpg`.
- Schematics: long-range comm dish; blast deflection / retractable forward armor; laser mount retracted; 5 rear repulsorlifts; scrambler/encryptor stack.

## Printed 20–21 / scan 029 — CHU'UNTHOR (Republic Fleet Systems)
- Old Republic Jedi mobile academy (Master Yoda + others). **2 km long × 1 km wide × ≥40 m tall**; ~**10,000** Jedi students. Wide corridors/viewports to fight confinement.
- Facilities: hundreds of sparring/workshop/meditation rooms; hospitals; libraries. Crashed Dathomir ~4 centuries before Luke; Nightsisters/rancors cut mission short; Yoda truce with young witch **Rell**; data tapes left → later given to Luke → foundation of Yavin Praxeum.
- Schematics: Port/Starboard Training Wings; Hospital/Command/Instruction/Meditation wings; docking bays; computer core; sensor/comm cluster; bridge; main ion/hyperdrive array (4 nozzles).

## Printed 22–23 / scan 030 — CLOAKSHAPE FIGHTER (Kuat Systems Engineering)
- Older fighter; not frontline (like Z-95); planetary defense / private / bounty / pirate. Atmospheric + short-range space; stock **no hyperdrive**; weak power plants; trades speed for durable hull.
- Highly modifiable: thrusters/weapons/fuel/shields; aftermarket **hyperdrive sled**; rear **maneuvering fin** (so common mistaken for stock). Heavily modded can compete with Alliance **Y-wing**. Nar Shaddaa bounty ship vs Falcon destroyed by Shug Ninx tunnel lasers (had double lasers + concussion launchers; stripped shields for ammo).
- **Quote (full combat/mod block):** `quotes/egvv1996-p022-scan030-cloakshape-combat-mods.jpg`.

## Printed 24–25 / scan 031 — CLOUD CITY (Custom Incom Corp. Repulsorlift City)
- Tibanna mining metropolis **60,000 km** above Bespin core; pop. **6 million**; **16 km** diameter × **17 km** tall incl. unipod; **392** levels + plaza. Upper luxury; middle industry/housing; lower processing + **3,600** repulsorlifts. Central ~1 km wind tunnel. Unipod: **32** tractor beams for spin-sealed Tibanna.
- Handoff: Lando wins Baron Admin via sabacc; Vader/Fett force betrayal; Capt. Treece Ugnaught slave camps; Lando frees Ugnaughts; Empire retakes as Thrawn supply base; post-Thrawn “sleepy mining colony.”
- Schematics: Concourse; admin; landing pads; Tibanna plants; carbonite chambers; weather vanes; tractor/repulsor generators; stalk; unipod.

## Printed 26–27 / scan 032 — COMBAT CLOUD CAR (Ubrikkian Talon I)
- Patrol/combat bridge airspeeder↔starfighter; makers Bespin Motors/Ubrikkian/Aratech/Mekuun/SoroSuub. Max alt ~**100 km**; some ≤**1,500 km/h** (faster than most starships in atmosphere). Talon I: extra plating, weapons, linked ion/repulsor; exposed engine cooling.
- Empire supplied Talon Is to **Noghri** on Honoghr; Thrawn trusted light defense web; Nystao technicians; Luke escorted by two cloud cars.
- Schematics: double blaster nose; airfoils; ion turbine; main fin; reactor; main repulsorlift/ion engine; braking thrusters; landing skid.

## Printed 28–29 / scan 033 — CORAL VANDA (Pantolomin Shipwrights)
- Subocean vacation liner (Tralla/Pantolomin); coral reefs; 8 casino rooms; 16 luxury suites; 4 holographic “adventure rooms”; ≤600 passengers; **unarmed** + security team + dozens of escape pods. Thrawn/Katana hunt: Han+Lando visit; Niles Ferrier tips Thrawn; Solo/Calrissian escape.

## Printed 30–31 / scan 034 — DARTH VADER'S TIE FIGHTER (Sienar TIE Advanced x1 Prototype)
- Vader at Yavin; bridge to Interceptor + TIE/Ad “Avenger.” Custom spaceframe; reinforced durasteel; elongated rear deck; bent solar wings. SFS I-S3a reactor + P-s5.6 twin ions; **shields** + **modest hyperdrive** (policy break); twin heavy blasters; **no life support** (full flight suit); less maneuverable than TIE/ln but far more durable; speed only slightly better (mass + shield draw). Limited production (cost/politics fearing capital-ship budget cuts); Empire prioritized cheaper/faster Interceptor (no shields/hyperdrive).
- **Quote:** `quotes/egvv1996-p030-scan034-vader-tie-x1-limitations.jpg`.

## Printed 32–33 / scan 035 — DASH RENDAR'S OUTRIDER (CEC YT-2400)
- Ebony YT-2400; fast/tough/modifiable. Three stolen KonGar KGDefender military ions; modified SoroSuub Grilltn/Y2TG hyperdrive **Class 0.75** (slower than Falcon 0.50). Dymek double lasers (dorsal+ventral 1D turrets); 2 forward concussion launchers (3 missiles each). Escape pod (6) in cockpit-tube aft.
- Sidebar character quote (verbatim in crop): owes Outrider the best — “brought me home when any other ship would have scattered me across space.”
- **Quote:** `quotes/egvv1996-p032-scan035-outrider-mods-quote.jpg`.

## Printed 34–35 / scan 036 — DEATH STAR (Custom Deep-Space Battle Station)
- Tarkin + Bevel Lemelisk; Vader emissary. **120 km** diameter. Superlaser used on Despayre then Alderaan. ~half volume = reactor/drives/superlaser. Crew ≥~265k soldiers; total personnel nearly **1 million**. Armament: superlaser + **15,000** capital turbolasers + **>700** tractor beams; **7,000** TIEs; 4 strike cruisers; >20k transports; >11k combat vehicles. Capital attacks deemed “insignificant”; Tarkin refused TIEs at Yavin → proton torpedo into unshielded exhaust → core detonation.

## Printed 38–39 / scan 038 — DEATH STAR PROTOTYPE (Maw Installation)
- Opened crop `quotes/egvv1996-p038-scan038-ds-proto-limits.jpg` (CP2b): the prototype superlaser is weaker than the working first Death Star. Its firepower was “only” enough to destroy a planet's core, not vaporize the world. Targeting was never perfected. It was wasteful, and the batteries took hours to recharge. Reduced power cut the destructive ability. It was not built as a practical war station: no maintenance machinery, immense computer core, or hyperdrive; a small command cabin used slave-rigged computers. No named character is in the crop.
- Same spread, outside that crop: Bevel Lemelisk, Tarkin, Tol Sivron, the Maw Installation, Despayre, Kyp Durron, and the Sun Crusher. Those are not facts from the quote image.

## Printed 40–41 / scan 039 — DESERT SKIFF (Ubrikkian Bantha II Cargo Skiff)
- Spread paraphrase, not in the opened crop: utility repulsorlift; cargo above 100 tons; 250 km/h; altitude ceiling 50 m; open deck; rear driver pedestal; labor-droid-simple controls; single repulsor and two steering vanes; magnetic lifters.
- Opened crop `quotes/egvv1996-p040-scan039-desert-skiff-not-combat.jpg` (CP2b): skiffs do not make good combat vehicles. A hand-blaster blast can disable the repulsorlift or smash a steering vane. Jabba's henchmen tumbled into the Sarlacc's mouth when a damaged skiff toppled. Jabba used Ubrikkian Bantha II cargo skiffs as patrol and escort vehicles. They were armor-plated and still unsuited to combat, as Luke Skywalker quickly proved. Jabba had had extendable gangplanks affixed for his Sarlacc feedings. After the rescue of Han Solo, Luke and other Rebel heroes used one of Jabba's skiffs to escape the Great Pit of Carkoon as the sail barge exploded. The crop does not say who rescued Han Solo or how.

## Printed 42–43 / scan 040 — DREADNAUGHT (Rendili StarDrive Heavy Cruiser)
- Old Republic 600 m; slow/poorly armed by modern std. Imperial: 10 turbolaser cannons + 20 quad + 10 batteries; Class 2 hyperdrive; crew **16,000**; often +12 TIE flight deck. Alliance **Assault Frigate** refit: strip superstructure, dorsal fins, automation → crew **<5,000**; no fighters; 15 laser + 20 quad laser + 15 turbolaser batteries. **Katana**/Dark Force: 200 dark-gray slave-rigged Dreadnaughts; crew 2,000; hive virus → lost ~50 yrs; rediscovered Talon Karrde; sought by Thrawn.

## Printed 44–45 / scan 041 — E-WING FIGHTER (FreiTek, Inc.)
- Spread paraphrase, not in the opened crop: NR fighter during Thrawn and the Emperor's rebirth; triple fire-linked lasers; 16 proton torpedoes; about TIE Interceptor speed; tougher hull than an X-wing; needs an R7.
- Opened crop `quotes/egvv1996-p044-scan041-ewing-typeb-overload.jpg` (CP2b): the laser cannons were underpowered because they used synthetically spin-coiled Tibanna gas. Because of the Battle of Calamari, New Republic technicians lacked time for a permanent solution and rigged the cannons to accept three times the standard power feed, risking a terminal overload. Those ships are dubbed E-wing: Type B. No named person is in the crop.

- Opened crop `quotes/egvv1996-p046-scan042-eclipse-superlaser-ram.jpg` (CP2b): the hull and shields were strong enough to ram enemy vessels; ten gravity-well projectors; five hundred heavy laser cannons and 550 turbolasers; fifty TIE interceptor squadrons (six hundred ships) and eight TIE bomber squadrons; a superlaser at two-thirds the power of the first Death Star's main weapon, “merely” enough to crack a planet's crust.
