# EGVV 1996 — notes148: Lancer → Mon Cal Cruiser (printed 102–117 / scan 070–077)
1996 paraphrase; quotes with crop+locator.

## Printed 102–103 / scan 070 — LANCER FRIGATE (KDY Lancer-class)
- **250 m** anti-fighter post–DS1. **20** Corellian AG-2G quad lasers on elevated towers (superior tracking); **single power generator** → gunners balance demands; virtually **no capital-scale weapons**. **Slow** sublight (fighters outrun); armor/shields insufficient vs capital fire; hangar only **2** shuttles + minimal troops. Doctrine: outer perimeter then flank Star Destroyers. Thrawn used in raiding to soften defenses ~5 yrs post-Palpatine.

## Printed 104–105 / scan 071 — LARS FAMILY LANDSPEEDER (SoroSuub V-35 Courier)
- Typical inexpensive family vehicle (vs Luke’s sportier X-34). Seats 3; driver atop before repulsor generator; retractable windscreen; passengers under cowling via folding panels; seats fold → cargo. Cruise ~**100 km/h**. Owen’s homemade sand filters for generator/intakes. Errands to Anchorhead ~2×/month.

## Printed 106–107 / scan 072 — LUKE'S LANDSPEEDER (SoroSuub X-34)
- Bought 2,400 credits (Owen argued; Beru intervened). Open cockpit; retractable duraplex; driver+passenger; limited cargo aft; wheel + pedals; simple scanner. Repulsor hover **1 m**; 3 turbines; top ~**250 km/h**. Constant floater/convertor-coil repairs (left turbine).

## Printed 108–109 / scan 073 — MARAUDER CORVETTE (Republic Sienar Marauder-class)
- **195 m** light cruiser; CSA Picket Fleet (Old Republic reject sold to Corporate Sector). Atmo streamlined **850 km/h**; looks like oversize fighter. Std: 8 double turbolasers (4/station); 3 tractor beams; optional +4 turbolasers with bigger generator. 12 Authority IRD fighters; 2 Espo platoons (80); 2 landing barges. Often pair with Victories. Han/Chewie encounters (outlaw tech base; Stars’ End evacuation).

## Printed 110–111 / scan 074 — MARAUDER STARJACKER (Mod. Byblos E-2 Asteroid Miner; Finhead Stonebone)
- >**100 m** insectoid; one of Stonebone’s two ore-raiders vs Ithullian colossus wasps; later pressed by Bogga/*Enforcer One*. Plasma drills front-mounted as weapons (+3 gens ea + prismatic crystals punch wasp carapace); 4 Corellia StarDrive microthrusters; monomolecular-slicer digging claws; filtration removed → ore + >100 raiders. Tactic: cut hull → vacuum pulls ore/crew into bays.

## Printed 112–113 / scan 075 — MASTER ARCA'S SHIP (*SunGem*; Hoersch-Kessel Delaya-class)
- Jedi mobile training courier. **21** HK ions in banks of 3; atmo ~**950 km/h**. Extendable hull-plating shields (dedicated defense station). Light armament/stealth priority: forward proton launcher (12 high-yield or 24 std) + pair rotating lasers on airfoils (actuator/cells center-fed). Quarters for 8; meditation; lightsaber training; Jedi library; little cargo; 2 escape pods.

## Printed 114–115 / scan 076 — MILLENNIUM FALCON (Mod. CEC YT-1300)
- Looks stock junk to avoid Imperial notice (Han’s plan). Hidden: **Class 0.5** hyperdrive (2× Imperial warship speed); illegal top sensor suite; shielded smuggling compartments. Liberated Myomar deflector system; 2 quad lasers; concussion missiles; 3-min start-up; retractable repeating blaster for ground. Sublight/hyperdrive **finicky** → constant maintenance; **3 droid brains**. Won from Lando via sabacc.
- **Quote:** `quotes/egvv1996-p114-scan076-falcon-class05-finicky.jpg`.

## Printed 116–117 / scan 077 — MON CAL CRUISER (MC80 Star Cruiser)
- NR main battle cruisers; durable/fast; ≈ ISD toughness. Civilian→military after Empire invaded Calamari. **Each unique** (“works of art”) → hard for non–Mon Cal techs to repair. *Home One* (Ackbar; blimp); winged Medical Frigate. **10** squadrons (120 fighters); 29 turbolasers; 36 ion; multiple tractors. Extra shield redundancies → swap damaged arrays mid-combat. Aquatic-anatomy command (hard for humans). Successors: MC80a/B/MC90.
