## Layman’s Guide — technical definitions (1996-source paraphrase)
- **Capital ship:** military starship **≥100 m**; large crews (ISD **>37,000**); turbolasers; armor; deflectors; often fighters. Examples: Tantive IV, Carrack, Lancer, Nebulon-B, ISD, SSD, *Eclipse*.
- **Speeders:** repulsorlift hover; some ion afterburners.
- **Starfighter:** fast, heavily armed space combat.
- **Transport:** nonmilitary cargo/passenger; often weak shields/weapons unless modified (*Falcon*).
- **Hyperdrive:** hyperspace; **mass shadows**; nav computers; cannot change course in hyperspace; **lower class = faster** (Class 3+ common).
- **Sublight:** Hoersch-Kessel ion drive typical; battles at sublight.
- **Repulsorlift:** antigravity; **do not work in space**; can exceed 1,000 km/h in atmosphere.
- **Weapons:** blaster cannons (weaker lasers on vehicles); laser cannons; turbolasers (capital; multi-crew); ion cannons (disable electronics, capture intact); proton/concussion physical warheads; tractor beams (bulky; escape almost impossible once locked).
- **Shields:** particle (physical; must drop to fire missiles/launch craft); energy/ray (lasers; power-hungry; combat-only typically); limited absorption before burnout; vehicle shields << starship.
