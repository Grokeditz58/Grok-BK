# EGVV 1996 — notes148: AT-AT → Carrack (printed 2–17 / scan 020–027)
1996-source paraphrase; quotes only with crop+locator. Body scans are **two-page spreads**.

## Printed 2–3 / scan 020 — AT-AT WALKER (Imperial All Terrain Armored Transport)
- **Role:** Core Imperial ground assault; non-repulsorlift (immune to gravity fluctuations / magnetic fields). Inspired by Old Republic AT-PT.
- **Size/speed:** ~20 m long, >15 m tall; top ground speed **60 km/h** flat.
- **Crew:** Commander, pilot, gunner in movable head; holographic 360° targeting.
- **Weapons:** 2 chin heavy laser cannons; 2 side medium blasters; head rotate 90° L/R, 30° up/down.
- **Capacity:** 5 speeder bikes + ≤40 troops; or 2 AT-STs instead. Kneels to ~3 m to unload ramps.
- **Build:** KDY under Imperial supervision; plants Kuat, Carida, Belderone.
- **Schematics (p.3):** Escape hatches; flexible armored tunnel; sensor array; troop section; drive motor; fuel slug tank; vehicle bay; atmosphere exchangers; filtration; footpad/toe flap.

## Printed 4–5 / scan 021 — AT-PT TRANSPORT (Imperial All Terrain Personal Transport)
- Experimental Old Republic one-man walker; canceled then concepts reused for AT-AT/AT-ST. Rediscovered with *Katana* fleet (Luke/Han vs Thrawn clones).
- **Speed:** 60 km/h; 45° inclines; open/jungle/mountain/urban/shallow water.
- **Config:** Central armored pod; twin blaster cannon + concussion-grenade launcher; legs full-extend or half-crouch.
- **Schematics (p.5):** Comm antenna; emergency flare; viewport; twin blaster; concussion launcher; drive joints; cooling vents; hydraulic adjusters; side entry hatch.

## Printed 6–7 / scan 022 — AT-ST WALKER (Imperial All Terrain Scout Transport)
- Light scout/perimeter; supports infantry & AT-ATs. **8.6 m** tall; top speed **90 km/h**.
- Crew 2 (pilot/gunner); closable viewports; 360° holographic targeting.
- **Weapons:** Chin twin blasters (**2 km** range); port twin light blasters; starboard concussion-grenade launcher; leg **cutting claws**.
- Gyro balance; vulnerable to dense foliage / uneven terrain unbalancing.
- Variant **AT-ST/A** Assault: >10 m; reinforced legs; heavy blaster.
- Access: top hatch; handholds or loading platforms.
- **Schematics (p.7):** Hatch; armor; viewports; weapons; sensor pod; gyro; drive engine; fence-cutting blade on footpad.

## Printed 8–9 / scan 023 — A-9 VIGILANCE (KDY A-9 Vigilance Interceptor)
- Post-Thrawn Imperial interceptor to compete with Sienar TIEs; some NR capture via factory revolt.
- **No shields, no hyperdrive**; compact/fast; shock attacks vs bases/capital ships; faster than TIE Interceptor; ≈ A-wing speed.
- Twin forward lasers (more punch than X-wing); fire-linked or independent kit; servo depress ≤90° for strafing.
- **Combat limitation (verbatim crop):** combat results “consistently disappointing”; no more maneuverable than TIE Interceptor; hull “significantly weaker”; limited utility like NR A-wing; improved A-9 rumored; NR studying design.
- **Quote:** `quotes/egvv1996-p008-scan023-a9-combat-disappointing.jpg`.

## Printed 10–11 / scan 024 — A-WING FIGHTER (Alliance)
- Dodonna + Walex Blissex; fastest at Endor; Arvel Crynyd rams *Executor* bridge.
- **9.6 m**; Novaldex J-77 “Event Horizon” engines; pivoting wing blasters (±60°; some aft-fire mods); optional concussion missiles; sensor-jamming (mainly vs fighters; also jams comms).
- **Combat limitations:** Constant stress → continuous maintenance; lightest hit can cause critical failures; pilot almost totally exposed; **fragile — one hit often destroys**; survive via speed + “foolish chances”; hit-and-fade / “A-wing slash” with slower fighters as bait.
- **Quote:** `quotes/egvv1996-p010-scan024-awing-fragile-one-hit.jpg`.
- Schematics: sensor jamming array; deflector-shield generator; thrust vector / thruster control jets; concussion-missile launchers (front view).

## Printed 12–13 / scan 025 — AMPHIBION (SedriMotors)
- Mon Cal Defense / NR Sea Commandos water assault; troop carrier (2–16 vehicles or ≤20 troops).
- Hover air-cushion (not standard repulsor); quiet; reduced heat/energy; ≤**100 km/h**; lightly armored; **not heavy combat**; open-top → vulnerable from above; rear rotating antipersonnel turret.
- Schematics: micro hover engines; command cabin blast screens; troop bay; armor plates fold for unload; triple rear hover drives.

## Printed 14–15 / scan 026 — B-WING FIGHTER (Slayn & Korpil)
- Ackbar + Verpine; heavy assault vs capital ships. B-wing/E two-seat “not widely accepted.”
- Gyro cockpit often **breaks down** (locks wing assembly; hard turns strain frame). Targeting laser tags enemies (perfect vectors but reveals position).
- Quadex Kyromaster + Vinop O2K reactor + JZ-5g7 convertors; **Class 2 hyperdrive** (“only half as fast” as X-/A-wing); **no full nav computer — only two jump coordinate sets**.
- Weapons: Gyrhil auto-blasters/lasers; ArMek ion; Krupx proton torpedoes; S-foils deploy/close. Alternate configs noted in footnotes.

## Printed 16–17 / scan 027 — CARRACK CRUISER (Damorian Manufacturing Carrack-class light cruiser)
- 350 m; Imperial Navy from early New Order. Std: 10 heavy turbolasers, 20 ion, 5 tractor; or 20 lasers as antistarfighter.
- Compartmentalization → high crew survival; sublight **as fast as X-wing**.
- **Biggest weakness:** insufficient armor around power generators — hit just aft of midline often disables. **No hangar** — external racks for **5 TIEs** only.
- *Dominant* at Bakura (Pter Thanas) vs Ssi-ruuk → later Bakuran Alliance flagship.
