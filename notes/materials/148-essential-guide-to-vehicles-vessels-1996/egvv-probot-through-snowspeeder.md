## Printed 126–127 / scan 082 — PROBOT (Arakyd Viper Probe Droid)
- Vader squadron post-Yavin; thousands to uninhabited systems. One-use hyperdrive pod + droid; braking thrusters (look like meteors). ~**40 km/h** repulsor+thrusters. Blaster but programmed avoid conflict / **self-destruct** if discovered. Extremely intelligent/curious; dense sensor suite (acoustic/EM/motive/seismic/olfactory/optical/zoom/IR/magnetic/radar/sonar). 4 manipulators + 1 high-torque grasp. Instantaneous omnisignal unicode via restricted Imperial HoloNet.
