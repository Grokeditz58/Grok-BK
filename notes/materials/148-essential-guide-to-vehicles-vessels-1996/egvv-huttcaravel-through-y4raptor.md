# EGVV 1996 — notes148: Hutt Caravel → Incom Y-4 Raptor (printed 70–85 / scan 054–061)
1996 paraphrase; quotes with crop+locator.

## Printed 70–71 / scan 054 — HUTT CARAVEL (Ubrikkian Seltiss-2)
- Decadence/style between Nal Hutta–Nar Shaddaa; modest for younger Hutts. Sail + steering vanes + repulsors (atmo); compact rear ions for space; **no hyperdrive**. Looks nonthreatening; 2 hidden lasers behind armor panels; hull plating + shields. Forward luxury cabin (~400k credits) = detachable self-contained escape/transport with own repulsor+ions. Bridge/kitchen/4 servant cabins above; **no escape pods for pilots/chefs**.

## Printed 72–73 / scan 055 — HYPERSPACE MARAUDER (TransGalMed Xiytiar-class Transport)
- Lo Khan + Yaka cyborg Luwingo smuggling freighter; **165 m** (large for smuggler). Long lightly armed then + pair 700-Penetrator lasers; **slow/lumbering** + heavy shields. Unusual computer/comm takes over boarding ships’ computers. Byss: Salla Zend tried hide Falcon inside — spotted by H-Ks; later rescued Lando’s NR assault force.

## Printed 74–75 / scan 056 — I-7 HOWLRUNNER (Incom)
- Imperial fighter (Emperor’s return / Civil War); Falcon encountered squadron on Coruscant rescue (Lando/Luke). Fixed-wing deep space + atmo; faster than old X/Y-wing but **E-wing and TIE Interceptor outrace it**. Twin lasers ≈ Interceptor accuracy but **lack significant power**. **Has dedicated shields** (unlike most Imperial fighters) ≈ X-wing durability; low mass → excellent maneuver. Incom’s first post-nationalization design (after X-wing team defected); compete vs Sienar TIE + KDY A-9. Naval reluctance → mostly planetary bases / smaller capitals (Dreadnaughts, Nebulon-B, old Victories); a few to Emperor’s advisers.

## Printed 76–77 / scan 057 — IG-2000 (Mod. Trilon Aggressor; IG-88)
- ~**20 m**; combat-first (engines/weapons over comfort). Stolen Nebulon-B **Kual Galaxy-15** ion + 3 Quadex cores; sublight ≈ Slave I / Y-wing; 8 bleed-off vents. Extendable combat vanes; **inertial compensators often disabled** (high-G that would kill organics). Weapons: 2 forward lasers, ventral ion, 2 mandible tractor beams. Prisoner hold (8) + stasis tube (only life-supported mid-deck); FX-7 medbay; interrogation; 4 assault drones. Destroyed Tatooine by Fett (tractor + concussion) while IG-88 tried steal carbonite Han; rumors of another IG-88 unsubstantiated.
- **Quote:** `quotes/egvv1996-p076-scan057-ig2000-inertial.jpg`.

## Printed 78–79 / scan 058 — IMPERIAL LANDING CRAFT (Sienar/Cygnus Sentinel-class)
- Lambda-derived; stationary top wing + 2 folding lower; larger cargo + heavier armament. Crew 5. Weapons: 4 retractable lasers/side; 2 concussion launchers (8 ea); retractable ion turret; rotating repeating blasters on landing. Armor ~**+25%** vs Lambda; 4 shield gens; Cygnus HD7 → atmo **1,000 km/h** / sublight ≈ Y-wing; **Class 1** hyperdrive. Capacity: 6 squads (54); or 12 repeating blasters + 6 bikes; combat delivery: 36 bikes or 12 compact assault vehicles. New at Yavin (Vader squadron / Virgillian); post-Endor some sold to NR.

## Printed 80–81 / scan 059 — IMPERIAL STAR DESTROYER (KDY; 1,600 m)
- >100 emplacements: **60** turbolasers + **60** ion. Twin shield domes on command tower. Aft bay ≤150 m ships; forward for assault/walkers/ground (aux TIE). Wing of **72** TIEs (Yavin mix: 4 TIE/ln + 1 Interceptor + 1 Bomber; Endor +1 Interceptor/−1 ln). Support: 8 Lambda, 15 stormtrooper transports, 5 assault gunboats, Skiprays/Gammas variable. Ground: **20 AT-AT + 30 AT-ST + 9,700 troops**; prefab garrison. Imperial II post-Yavin: reinforced hull + more powerful batteries. Engines: 3× KDY Destroyer-I + 4× Cygnus Gemon-4 emergency.

## Printed 82–83 / scan 060 — INCOM T-16 SKYHOPPER
- Transorbital pleasure; Luke/womp rats/Beggar’s Canyon. E-16/x ion + 2 DCJ-45 repulsors; ~**1,200 km/h**; alt ~**300 km**. Triwing; tall dorsal blocks view → holographic HUD. Civilian: none/stun/pneumatic; military/police: plating + lasers; depicted with heavy-duty laser. Pressurized 1+1 cockpit.

## Printed 84–85 / scan 061 — INCOM Y-4 "RAPTOR" TRANSPORT
- Military shuttle pre–Incom seizure; struggled (X-wing association); fame under **Zsinj’s Raptors**. <30 m; **40 troops + 4 AT-STs**; folding assault ramps. Class 2 hyperdrive + backup; nav computer + R2 socket. Forward: 2 fire-linked lasers + recessed concussion (6) — chief gunner; dorsal double laser — 2nd gunner. Swing wings for braking/maneuver; 1 m heat shield + ablative spray. Civilian lighter variant. Han once falsified Falcon as Y-4 to infiltrate Dathomir shipyards.
