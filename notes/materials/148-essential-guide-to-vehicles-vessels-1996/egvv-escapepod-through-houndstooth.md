# EGVV 1996 — notes148: Escape Pod → Hound's Tooth (printed 50–69 / scan 044–053)
1996 paraphrase; quotes with crop+locator.

## Printed 50–51 / scan 044 — ESCAPE POD (CEC Class-6)
- Tantive IV: eight 6-being pods; Imperial law prioritizes organics (droids often left). Explosive separator charges; auto distress beacon (R2 deactivated). Spartan: padded gee-couches; automated pilotless landing; 2 weeks rations/water/shelters/medpacs/breath masks/glow rods/comlinks + civilian hunting blaster. Landing via jets/flotation/parachutes/repulsor depending on model.

## Printed 52–53 / scan 045 — EXPANDED B-WING FIGHTER (Slayn & Korpil B-wing/E2)
- Opened crop `quotes/egvv1996-p052-scan045-bwing-e2-limits.jpg` (CP2b): the B-wing/E is faster and tougher but less maneuverable than the standard B-wing, and as fast as a Y-wing. Standard weapons are two fire-linked laser cannons, three ion cannons, and a proton-torpedo launcher with eight torpedoes. The B-wing/E2 adds a launcher and keeps eight torpedoes. New Republic technicians designed a removable ammo magazine on the “neck,” carrying twelve more proton torpedoes and reducing maneuverability. The slash in B-wing/E and B-wing/E2 is printed. A stray speck above the initial a of ammo is not a diacritic and not a crop clip; the word is ammo. No named person is in the crop.
- Same spread, outside that crop: Ackbar's personal B-wing/E2, the second seat, and the crash. Those are not facts from the quote image.

## Printed 54–55 / scan 046 — EXECUTOR (KDY Super Star Destroyer)
- **8,000 m**; Vader command; Lira Wessex design; Fondor. >1000 weapons; 2 TIE wings (144); 200 support; stormtrooper corps; 38k ground; 3 prefab garrisons + walkers. Ozzel then Piett. Endor: shields down → Arvel Crynyd A-wing into bridge → Death Star gravity pull → impact. 4 in service by Hoth; more building; some now with warlords.

## Printed 56–57 / scan 047 — FLURRY (Mod. SoroSuub Quasar Fire-class Bulk Cruiser)
- Spread paraphrase, not in the opened crop: Virgillian/Sullustan carrier conversion; 340 m; hangar about 50 fighters; repair bays; two heavy turbolasers and two tractor beams; crew 250.
- Opened crop `quotes/egvv1996-p056-scan047-flurry-bakura-destroyed.jpg` (CP3a): for the Bakura mission against the Ssi-ruuk, the *Flurry* carried a prototype battle analysis computer, the creation of General Jan Dodonna. Captain Manchisco got twenty X-wings, three A-wings, and four B-wings. The X-wings included Rogue Squadron under Wedge Antilles. Alliance command knew the B-wings were quite likely to break down after Endor. The Imperial Carrack cruiser *Dominant* destroyed the *Flurry* over Bakura. All hands were lost and were posthumously decorated. The crop prints Captain Manchisco and does not print a first name.
- Same spread, outside that crop: the first name Tessa. That is not a fact from the quote image.
