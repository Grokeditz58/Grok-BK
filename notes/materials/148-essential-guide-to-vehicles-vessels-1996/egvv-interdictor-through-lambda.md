1996 paraphrase; quotes with crop+locator.

## Printed 86–87 / scan 062 — INTERDICTOR CRUISER (Sienar Immobilizer 418)
- **600 m** heavy-cruiser hull; artificial gravity-well gen + **4** projectors simulate stellar mass → prevent hyperspace entry / yank ships out. Armament: **20** quad lasers (short-range capital). Doctrine: battle perimeter. **Biggest disadvantage:** gravity wells take **more than a minute to charge** — quick opponents can jump before redeploy. Thrawn vs Luke (desperation reverse accel comps + proton spoof tractors); attempt capture Karrde/*Wild Karrde* at Myrkr.

## Printed 88–89 / scan 063 — ITHULLIAN ORE HAULER
- Biological: carapaces of dead **Ithullian colossus wasps** (>1 km) converted by Stenness “Nessies” for **mutonium**. Natural hull thick vs blasters; carved interiors for computers/nav/sensors/power. External twin ions on struts; wasp legs = stabilizers/maneuver jets. Low-power defensive blasters + 2 heavy turbolasers in thorax. Head=command; thorax=ore; abdomen=ore/power/drive; fuel pods ~half abdomen. Colossus-wasp poaching (dangerous).

## Printed 90–91 / scan 064 — JABBA'S SAIL BARGE (Ubrikkian Luxury)
- Pleasure over sand/water/ice/grass. Tri-chambered repulsor; **100 km/h** powered / **30 km/h** sail-hover; hover ≤**10 m**. Deck heavy blaster + rail AP blasters; enhanced sensors. Banquet/pantry/Jabba suite/kitchen mid; engines bottom; control front. Destroyed Carkoon: Leia fired laser into deck → chain reaction; Jawas stripped wreck.

## Printed 94–95 / scan 066 — JAWA SANDCRAWLER (Mod. Corellia Mining Digger Crawler)
- Abandoned steam mining vehicles; Jawa clans (hundreds) live aboard. ~**20 m** high; immense turbines + **8** treads. Maze of alcoves/junk/terrified droids; jury-rigged repairs overload-prone. Ionization guns; magnetic suction / front ramp. Defense vs Sand People/krayt; generally peaceful traders (swap meets); desperation threat to roll over moisture farm.

## Printed 96–97 / scan 067 — JUGGERNAUT (KDY HAVw A5)
- Old Republic assault; displaced by AT-AT; “rolling slabs.” **22 m** long × ~**15 m** tall. **10** drive wheels; top **200 km/h** but must slow to **25 km/h** to turn; **dual drive stations** (either end) to reverse instead of U-turn. Capacity **50 troops** or bikes/light speeders (≈/≥ AT-AT) but drive fragile → weeks downtime. Crew 2 + 6 gunners + tower spotter. Turret laser + port/starboard lasers (≈ AT-AT power but range only **2/3**); medium blaster turret (better range/stopping than AT-AT chin); 2 concussion-grenade turrets (10 ea).
- **Quote:** `quotes/egvv1996-p096-scan067-juggernaut-turn-limits.jpg`.

## Printed 98–99 / scan 068 — LADY LUCK (SoroSuub Personal Luxury Yacht 3000; Lando)
- From Orthellin royal mistress; **50 m**; dual pods each with sublight+hyperdrive. Looks unarmed; 1 retractable laser + 2 shields (Lando plans upgrades); sophisticated dual forward sensors; hidden compartments; 3 false transponder IDs; **beckon call** belt datadisk summons droid brain. Amenities: observation deck, jet-stream meditation pool, conform-couches, dining, private suite, 5 visitor cabins, escape pods. Nkllon escape; temp retired; reactivated for Kessel diplomacy after Han vanished.

## Printed 100–101 / scan 069 — LAMBDA SHUTTLE (Sienar Lambda-class)
- Cargo/passenger; stationary top + 2 folding lower wings; retractable landing legs in wings; telescoping ramp. ≤**20** passengers or **80** tons. Has hyperdrive (unlike most Imperial shuttles); reinforced hull + multiple shields. Sienar: 3 double blasters (1 rear) + 2 double lasers; Cygnus military subcontract up to **10** lasers. Crew 4 (+2 optional); solo pilot possible but degraded. Used by Palpatine/Vader/Grand Inquisitor; Emperor’s rumored cloak + HoloNet. Alliance stole *Tydirium* for Endor shield-generator strike.
