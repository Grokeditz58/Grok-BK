1996 paraphrase; quotes with crop+locator.

## Printed 134–135 / scan 086 — REBEL TRANSPORT (Gallofree Yards Medium Transport)
- Echo Base Hoth evacuation. **90 m**; cargo **19,000** metric tons. Open-shell hull; modular pods held by magnetic shield. Crew 7 (incl. 1 gunner) cramped topside pod. Hyperdrive + sublight ions + repulsors for landing. Minimal combat: 4 twin lasers (often stripped for fighters); rely sensors/countermeasures. Cheap but **breakdown-prone** — techs joke as much time in repair as in space.
- **Quote:** `quotes/egvv1996-p134-scan086-rebel-transport-breakdowns.jpg`.

## Printed 136–137 / scan 087 — SCIMITAR ASSAULT BOMBER (Sienar; Thrawn-ordered)
- Newest Imperial bomber; Scimitar wing input. 2-man (pilot+bombardier); forward pod ejectable. Faster than TIE bomber; atmo cruise **850** / dive >**1,000** km/h; **has shields**; reinforced hull. Bomb bay: 16 concussion missiles (9 km; can kill buildings/bunkers/AT-ATs) or proton grenades/thermal dets; linked forward lasers for strafe. Mark II (pre-Thrawn death): cruise 1,150 / dive 1,250.

## Printed 138–139 / scan 088 — SHIELDSHIP (Republic Engineering heat-resistant escort)
- Protect ships to **Nkllon**/Athega sun (std shields insufficient). Lando specs for Nomad City; 12 ships → ≤3 visits/day. Hard to pilot; constant maintenance; **cooling gear replaced every trip**. Umbrella cone ~**800 m** across (honeycombed coolant armor) + **400 m** pylon + drive tug. Slaved nav: 1 hr jump vs 10 hr manual sublight. Thrawn attacks: Nomad drive destroyed; all but 1 shieldship disabled; Bel Iblis evacuation before melt; no more built.
- **Quote:** `quotes/egvv1996-p138-scan088-shieldship-limits.jpg`.

## Printed 140–141 / scan 089 — SHRIWIRR (Ssi-ruuvi Shree-class Battle Cruiser)
- Lead Bakura attack cruiser post-Endor; ~**900 m**. 5 m ceilings (Ssi-ruuk); P’w’eck crawlways. Arms: ~24 heavy turbolaser equiv + 24 ion + 12 concussion + 12 tractor; 500 V-wing droids. **Entechment** priority (capture life-force) — large lab; planned remote human entechment after studying Luke. Bakura fleet: 4 Wurrif light cruisers; 20 F'w'sen pickets; Sh'ner assault carrier (12 D'kee landers); Lwhekk manufacturing; >1,300 battle droids; Adm. Ivpikkis. Captured by Luke (disabled eng control); Alliance refit → *Sibwarra* (“Flute”).
