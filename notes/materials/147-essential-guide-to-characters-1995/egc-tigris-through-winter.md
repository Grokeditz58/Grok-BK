## Printed p.169 / scan p178 — TIGRIS
- Human (Firrerreon) · M · Black and silver striped · Black · still growing · Firrerre · Worldcraft · First: Crystal Star.
- Son of Hethrir + Rillao (both Vader students; Hethrir dark / Rillao light). Hethrir destroyed Firrerre for Empire loyalty; Rillao fled pregnant; later captured; Tigris raised as servant unaware he was Hethrir's son. No Force → not heir. Helped run Worldcraft; secretly fed/comforted kidnapped children. Bonded with Anakin Solo when Hethrir kidnapped Solo kids; learned parentage at Waru temple Crseih; after Waru consumed Hethrir, rescued Anakin; Coruscant with Rillao (scientist/artist/diplomat path; never Force-consumed).

## Printed p.170 / scan p179 — TIONNE

Kun mocked her power; she: "Someday the songs will tell of our great victory over Exar Kun, and I will sing them." Students extinguished Kun. Luke later: "You are the first of the new Jedi Knights. You are the core of what will become a great order to protect the New Republic. You are champions of the Force." +9 yrs Luke left newer students to her (parables/fables/music); youngest students.
