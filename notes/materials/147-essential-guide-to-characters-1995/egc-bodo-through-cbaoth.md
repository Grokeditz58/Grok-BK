## Printed p.9 / scan p018 — BODO BAAS
- Species: Neti (tree-like shapeshifters); sex male; height variable; homeworld Myrkr (text also notes Neti homeworld Ryyk destroyed by supernova; survivors to Myrkr). Affiliation Jedi / Alliance of Free Planets. Weapon lightsaber. First appearance: Dark Empire II #1.
- Illustration: elderly tree-like Neti with long white beard, staff; circular Jedi emblem top-left of art box.
- Top-right sidebar (gray): SPECIES Neti · SEX Male · HAIR COLOR White · EYE COLOR Unknown · HEIGHT Variable · HOMEWORLD Myrkr · POLITICAL AFFILIATION Jedi / Alliance of Free Planets · WEAPON(S) lightsaber · VEHICLE None · FIRST APPEARANCE Dark Empire II #1.
- Bio: thousands of years old; studied under Ood Bnar on Ossus; lived centuries as tree on Myrkr; was Master of Jedi Assembly on Ossus ~400 years before New Republic; recorded Holocron used by Jedi for millennia.
- **Force / Holocron (paraphrase):** Holocron contains knowledge of thousands of Jedi; Bodo later uploaded his own consciousness into it. When Luke Skywalker accessed it during Dark Empire era, Bodo's spirit warned against teaching Leia's children the Force too early and against confronting Palpatine's dark side directly without preparation.
- Caption near art: Holocron / Bodo Baas as guardian spirit.
- Crop: `quotes/egc1995-p009-bodo-holocron.jpg` (Holocron + Palpatine execution / light-side levels stretch).

## Printed p.10–11 / scan p019–p020 — PONDA BABA and DOCTOR CORNELIUS EVAZAN
### Ponda Baba (printed 10)
- Species: Aqualish (Quarren-adjacent? Aqualish); male; tusks; eye color unknown; height ~1.85m; homeworld Ando. Affiliation none / criminal. Weapon blaster pistol. First appearance: A New Hope.
- Illustration: walrus-faced Aqualish in cantina gear; Imperial? no — circular none/criminal.
- Bio: smuggler/mercenary; partner of Evazan; Mos Eisley Cantina confrontation with Luke; Obi-Wan Kenobi severed Baba's arm with lightsaber after Baba threatened Luke.
- Sidebar stats + first appearance ANH.

### Doctor Cornelius Evazan (printed 10–11)
- Human male; scarred face; homeworld Alsakan (or unknown — check: text says he claimed many worlds). Affiliation none. Weapon blaster. First appearance ANH.
- Bio: disgraced surgeon; death sentence on 12 systems; partnered with Ponda Baba; Cantina — "he doesn't like you" / "I have the death sentence…"; Kenobi cut Baba's arm; Evazan fled.
- **Mind-transfer claim (paraphrase + crop):** Evazan researched transferring consciousness to other bodies for immortality; continued underground medical experiments after ANH era.
- Crop: `quotes/egc1995-p011-evazan-mind-transfer.jpg`; `quotes/egc1995-p011-kenobi-cantina.jpg` (Kenobi sabers Cantina).

## Printed p.22 / scan p031 — CALLISTA (Ming)
- Species: Human; female; hair dark; homeworld Chad. Affiliation Jedi. Weapon light sabers / Force. First appearance: Children of the Jedi (Barbara Hambly).
- Illustration: woman in Jedi robes; Jedi emblem.
- **Force essence (strong note + crop):** Callista was a Jedi whose consciousness/essence transferred into the computer core of the Eye of Palpatine dreadnaught after she sacrificed to stop it; later downloaded into a cloned body (of Cray Mingla) but found herself **bereft of Force sensitivity** in the new body — central tragedy of her arc with Luke.

## Printed p.23–26 / scan p032–p035 — LANDO CALRISSIAN
- Species: Human; male; hair black; eyes brown; height ~1.78m; homeworld unknown (Socorro sometimes later — 1995 may say unknown). Affiliation Alliance / New Republic / independent. Weapon blaster. Vehicle Millennium Falcon (formerly), Lady Luck. First appearance: The Empire Strikes Back.
- Illustrations: Cloud City cape portrait with Lobot in bg (p25); Endor commando with DH-17 (p26); earlier sabacc/Bespin art on open pages.
- Bio arc (1995 span):
  - Gambler / sabacc; won Falcon from Han; lost Falcon back; Baron Administrator of Cloud City (Bespin) after winning from Baron Administrator previous.
  - Vader's occupation; deal to betray Han; redemption; helps rescue at Jabba's; General at Endor (leads Fighter attack on Death Star II / *Falcon* with Nien Nunb).
  - Post-Empire: Baron Admin again; Ugnaught unrest; Lobot (cyborg aide with headgear implants) saves Lando with life-jets; SPIN strike team (Leia, Luke, Han, Chewie, Lando, R2, 3PO).
  - Lost Cloud City to Zorba the Hutt in sabacc; Hologram Fun World (Zabian system); Nomad City on Nkllon (Athega) mining hfredium/kammris/dolovite — built from Imperial Dreadnaught hulk + AT-ATs; shieldships vs sun; Figg designs.
  - Thrawn crisis / Katana fleet; with Wedge commanded Liberator & Emancipator; Liberator crashed into Coruscant in Imperial civil war.
  - World Devastators at Mon Calamari; Emancipator destroyed; rescued by Antares Six; Byss infiltration with S-1 Viper Automadons vs chrysalis beasts; smugglers Shug Ninx / Lo Khan rescue; Nespis VIII.
  - Jedi Academy: found Tymmo (con artist, not Force-sensitive; Duchess Mystal of Dargul consort) — million-credit reward.
  - Kessel / Moruth Doole; Sun Crusher; sabacc with Han for Falcon again ↔ Lady Luck trade; Mara Jade on Yavin 4; glitterstim mines reopened with Nien Nunb / Sullustans + droids (no slaves).
  - GemDiver Station (corusca gems, Yavin orbit) ~19 years after Bespin; Lobot aide; Tamith Kai Nightsister attack — Jacen/Jaina/Lowbacca/Em Teedee captured; Lando helps Luke/Tenel Ka rescue; upgrades security.
  - Close: learned responsibility, loyalty, pride; New Republic can count on him.

## Printed p.27 / scan p036 — GAERIEL CAPTISON
- Species: Human; female; hair brown; eyes one green one gray; height unknown; homeworld Bakura. Affiliation Empire (then resigned). Weapons/vehicles unknown. First appearance: The Truce at Bakura.
- Illustration: full-body line art; Imperial emblem top-left of art box.
- Bio: Imperial senator Bakura (Outer Rim; exports repulsorlift components, exotic fruits, candy, liqueur). Parents killed in anti-Empire uprising; raised by aunt/uncle Tiree and Yeorg Captison (Yeorg ceremonial prime minister). Year at Imperial Center postgraduate; returned Salis D'aar before Ssi-ruuk invasion. Skeptical of Alliance aid / Leia's Emperor-death news. Governor Wilek Nereus hid olabrian (trihoic) trichoid larvae in Luke's food to betray him to Ssi-ruuk; Gaeriel warned Luke. After victory: Nereus killed; Pter Thanas defected; Gaeriel resigned Imperial service; aids uncle reviving Bakura; helped draft truce joining Alliance.

## Printed p.28 / scan p037 — JORUUS C'BAOTH (start; continues p.29)
- Name banner: JORUUS C'BAOTH; Imperial logo.
- Stats: SPECIES Human clone · SEX Male · HAIR Gray · EYE Unknown · HEIGHT 1.9m · HOMEWORLD Bortras · AFFILIATION Empire · WEAPON Dark side of the Force · VEHICLE None · FIRST APPEARANCE Heir to the Empire · DEATH Alliance attack on Mount Tantiss.
- Illustration: elderly wild-bearded man, arms in sleeves, Jedi-style robes; heavy hatching.
- Distinguishes original **Jorus C'Baoth** (single u) vs clone **Joruus** (double u).
- Original Jorus: born Bortras (Reithcas sector); Mirnic University age 17; Jedi Training Center Kamparas; private student of a Master; Knight after 2 years; Master after 12 years service. Senate Interspecies Advisory Committee; personal Jedi advisor to Senator Palpatine. Opposed Dark Jedi on Bpfassh; Alderaan delegation → Organa ascendancy; mediated Duinuogwuin–Gotal conflict with Tra's M'ins; ambassador-at-large Xappyh sector.
- Outbound Flight: six Jedi Masters; destroyed by young Thrawn under Palpatine orders (eliminate six Masters).
- Clone Joruus: appears Wayland ~5 years after Empire's fall; Palpatine-created clone; early clones prone to **clone madness** (grown too fast); stationed Mount Tantiss (Spaarti cylinders, cloaking prototype); became "irrevocably insane" / charismatic insanity.
