## Printed p.68–69 / scan p077–p078 — GREEDO

Wuher + C2-R4 process body into pheromonal liqueur for Jabba.

## Printed p.70 / scan p079 — HETHRIR
- Human · Male · Gold/copper/cinnamon striped hair · Black eyes · Tall · Firrerre · Empire · Force-activated lightsaber · Worldcraft · First: Crystal Star · Death: Waru's temple Crseih Research Station. Imperial crest.
- Art: long-haired robes, hands in sleeves.
- Trained with lover Rillao under Vader; Hethrir eager dark side (can dampen others' Force); Rillao healer/light — disappointment. Rillao hid unborn Tigris (no Force) far away; Hethrir imprisoned her in torture device on dead slaver freighter; kept Tigris as servant. Vader: Procurator of Justice — Hethrir abducted freighter of own people then destroyed Firrerre (millions dead). Post-Empire: fled on Emperor-gifted worldcraft; planned Empire Reborn. Sought Force via **Waru** (gold-scaled fleshy mass / false faith healer; drains life to portal into Force). Kidnapped Force kids for dark school / non-Force as slaves; kidnapped Jacen/Jaina/Anakin from Munto Codru for Waru (wanted Anakin). Rillao freed by Leia told Tigris parentage; Tigris helped Anakin escape; Waru died pulling Hethrir into energy field — both destroyed.

Tangrene Ubiqtorate intel center destroyed. Fey'lya funneled money / protected Nest. Name from Corellian ghost legend. Softened on Mothma post-Empire; realized Fey'lya power-hungry not Palpatine-like. Han (met as schoolboy) contacted; Katana six Dreadnaughts incl. *Peregrine*; Alliance salvaged 15, Imperials took more. Leia asked return; Coruscant defense vs Thrawn — took over from Drayson; Stardust Plan (sensor-reflective repolarized dust) vs cloaked asteroids + Karrde timing. Stayed with NR; cloud lifted.

## Printed p.82–83 / scan p091–p092 — MARA JADE
- Human · Female · Red-gold · Green · 1.6m · Unknown · Empire then NR · Lightsaber (blue), blaster · *Jade's Fire*, *Hunter's Luck* · First: Heir to the Empire. Rebel emblem on art.
- Art: full body lightsaber at belt; waist-up goggles-on-forehead.
- Rumors: Palpatine took her as girl after parents killed; trained **Emperor's Hand** — lightsaber, Force, H2H, marksmanship, spy, intrigue; hears Emperor's call galaxy-wide. Secret assassin; public "trinket." Avoided Vader. Tatooine alias **Arica** dancer to kill Luke at Jabba's — missed Carkoon; felt Emperor's disappointment. Endor: last Force command **"You will kill Luke Skywalker."** 4.5 yrs odd jobs; Force faded. Aliases: Karrinna Janisih (Phorliss); Marellis (Caprioril swoop); Celina Marniss (Tropis-on-Vorent hyperdrive). Varonat: Celina meets "Pandis Hart"/Talon Karrde + Tapper Quelev (Morodin safari / Gamgalon); rescued Karrde after Tapper killed; joined crew 5.5 months; Palpatine nightmares. Found Luke's X-wing; Thrawn/C'Baoth arc; planned to kill Luke but became NR asset. Wayland: Luke trained returning Force; killed Luuke; with Luke/Leia slew C'Baoth — compulsion fulfilled, felt free. Luke gave father's Bespin lightsaber; urged NR. Post-Bilbringi Smugglers' Alliance; Karrde retired → Mara runs ops. Brief Yavin Academy visit; Daala war; Kessel liberation + Maw Death Star prototype with Han/Lando; rejected Lando romance initially; *Jade's Fire* helped Leia re: Emperor's Jedi-children wipe plans. Luke: if she completes training, may be among most formidable Jedi.
