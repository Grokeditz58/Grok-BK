## Stats / art
- Human · Male · Black hair · Gray eyes · Tall · Unknown HW · Dark Lords of the Sith · Lightsaber (blue) · *Starstorm One* · First: Dark Apprentice · Death: 7 years after Endor.
- Art: armored robes full body; scarred cheek close-up (Sylvar claw furrows); double-blade action; amulet-grafted arm; Ulic Qel-Droma portrait on mid pages.

## Ancient arc (~4000 yrs before Anakin's fall)
- Tutored Dantooine by Master **Vodo-Siosk Baas**; Holocron Sith history against Master's wishes. Disliked Cathar students Crado/Sylvar ("animals"); Sylvar clawed right cheek in saber drill; nearly killed her; Vodo stick-vs-saber duel — Kun dual-saber rage broke stick.
- Left for Onderon as fake archaeologist → Dxun Freedon Nadd tomb; Nadd spirit → scrolls of Sith resting place; killed Nadd followers in anger.
- **Korriban:** rockslide + Nadd attack left him dying; Vodo's light-side help blocked by blackness; accepted dark side to live (screams rippled Force). Nadd told of Naga Sadow alchemy on Yavin 4 (flesh to dead spirits); Kun claimed he'd research not fall — Nadd laughed "you already have."
- **Yavin 4:** renounced dark briefly → Massassi attack; blood sacrifice Temple of Fire; Sith amulet on Sadow statue grafted to arm → obliterated monster/Massassi including Nadd spirit (dying Nadd warned Aleema & Satal Keto that Kun pretender; real power to them + soon-fallen Ulic).
- Massassi built Sith temples focusing dark energy; black-stone statue private retreat; used Sadow alchemy to make Massassi death-monsters; ship to Empress Teta / Cinnagar.
- Duel Ulic (who'd killed Satal); amulets vision of ancient Sith → **Kun Dark Lord / Ulic Apprentice** + forehead tattoos. *"Together we will bring down the galaxy."*
- Brotherhood of the Sith (distorted Jedi Code); with Krath vs Republic/Jedi. Deneba Great Council; Vodo volunteered to redeem — **Kun slew his Master**. Sith War millions dead. Ulic betrayed Kun's Yavin base; Jedi combined Force leveled temples; Kun drained all Massassi life → spirit trapped in temples when body destroyed.

## Modern arc (Jedi Academy)
- Luke opened academy in Great Temple unaware of Kun spirit. Kun showed Gantoris Eol Sha massacre → Gantoris charged → found incinerated from inside, new lightsaber nearby. Tempted Kyp Durron; helped raise Sun Crusher; dark lightning split Luke spirit from body. Tionne found body; 12 initiates + Leia + twins protected body; planned trap. Streen bait in grand audience chamber; combined will + spirits of Luke and Vodo-Siosk Baas; no Massassi to feed him → **combined Jedi extinguished Kun's spirit**.
