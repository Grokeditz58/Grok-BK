# EGC 1995 — notes147: Emperor's Royal Guards → Gethzerion (printed 52–67)
Source: EGC 1995. printed = scan − 9. Scan p061–p076.
Secondary paraphrase default; verbatim only with crop+locator.

## Printed p.52 / scan p061 — EMPEROR'S ROYAL GUARDS
- Human · Male · Unknown hair/eyes · 1.8m · Unknown HW · Empire · Force pike, heavy blaster pistol · First: ROTJ. Imperial crest.
- Art: full red-robed guard with pike; helmet inset.
- Elite ex-stormtroopers; size/strength/loyalty; armor from Mandalore Death Watch + Thyrsus Sun Guards designs. Number known only to Palpatine/top brass; report only to Emperor. 2m force pikes (lethal); concealed heavy blasters. Rumor: never killed in battle. **Imperial Sovereign Protectors** even more elite — palaces + clone vats on Byss; more ceremonial red armor. Post-death: ranks dissipated/reassigned; some Dark Siders for Emperor clones; possible suicides.

## Printed p.53–54 / scan p062–p063 — DAVIN FELTH
- Human · Male · 1.8m · Empire · Heavy blaster rifle, pistol, concussion-grenade launcher · AT-AT / troop transport · First: ANH.
- Art: stormtrooper walking with rifle; second dynamic firing pose.
- Stormtrooper kit overview: plastisteel over black body glove; "antiblaster cocoon" theory belied by casualties; polarized lenses, filters, comlink; utility belt (rations, batteries, spare comlink); variants seatrooper/snowtrooper/zero-g. Navy trains; Army uses; fanatical devotion.
- Bio: joined at 18; Carida Academy — enthusiasm gone in 30 seconds; roommates Geoff f'Tuhns, Mychael Ologat; excelled → Colonel Veers AT-AT detachment; dry-run destroyed 4 simulated fighters but revealed AT-AT design flaws — Veers questioned (not rewarded). Tatooine sandtrooper unit **1023**: found escape pod + R2 piece; Captain Terrik ordered Jawa slaughter + frame Tuskens; Lars homestead razed (Owen spat on Terrik; Owen+Beru mowed down) — Felth horror. Mos Eisley: Alvien checkpoints; Drax/Zeta door-to-door; cantina as Kenobi/Luke leave; Docking Bay 94 firefight. **Turning point:** as Falcon lifts, Felth kills Captain Terrik instead of Solo. Leaks to Rebellion from inside Empire; no longer just "1023".

## Printed p.55–58 / scan p064–p067 — BOBA FETT
- Human · Male · Unknown · 1.8m · Unknown HW · Freelance BH · Mandalorian armor + weapons · *Slave I* (Firespray) / *Slave II* (Pursuer) · First: **Star Wars Holiday Special**.
- Art: multiple full-body/action poses; skiff firing; armor detail.
- **1995 Legends ID:** formerly **Jaster Mereel**, Journeyman Protector Concord Dawn; exiled for killing corrupt superior; took name Boba Fett + armor. Armor from Mandalorians defeated by Jedi in Clone Wars. Kit: macrobinocular viewplate, IR, comlink, broadband antenna, wrist lasers, flame projector, fiberchord whip/grapple, jet pack + magnetic grapple (20m), concussion grenade launcher. Rival: **Jodo Kast** (fake Fett armor). Bounties e.g. Feldrall Okor 150k; Nivek'Yppiks 500k. Works Jabba/Hutts/Empire. Slow, methodical, unpredictable.
- Pre-Yavin: Droids series Ingo — Fromm gang hire vs Thall Joben; droid BR-17 turned 3PO vs R2; bomb magnet mishap. Holiday Special Panna — "rescued" Luke; with Chewie for sleeping-virus antidote; secretly reported to Vader ("best bounty hunter"). Ota / Snogars / Mole deserter; temp alliance Han/Chewie/Leia then tried capture. Magwit magician → Gorga the Hutt / Bar-Kooda on *Bloodstar* via matter-transmitter hoop.
- Pre-Hoth: Jabba hire vs Solo; first Vader meeting (mutual respect); with Dengar/Bossk/Skorr — Ord Mantell escape. Executor six hunters; tracked Falcon to Bespin; carbonite Solo to Jabba; shot exchange with Luke; destroyed IG-88's *IG-2000*; talked Jabba to raise bounty 100k→250k (carbonite as Vader "art"). Year at court; krayt dragon bounty vs rancor. Carkoon: jetpack hit → Sarlacc; escaped; Dengar nursed with Jabba medical droids.
- Post: *Slave II* Nar Shaddaa; reacquired *Slave I* via dummy corps from Grakouine; Feldrall Okor / Governor Isis thermal detonator fee hike (Boushh mimic). 6 yrs post-Jabba: ambush Han/Leia Nar Shaddaa; chase to Byss — shields clip *Slave II* rudder; Dengar quits. Later: Dark Side Warriors blackmail (stormtrooper-murder past claim) — Fett fights them off; clips Falcon stabilizer; Chewie rips helmet after Fett shoots him; gas-cloud chase — Han disables *Slave I*. Disappeared; reports continue; Solo expects final confrontation.

## Printed p.59 / scan p068 — BORSK FEY'LYA
- Bothan · Male · Cream hair · Violet eyes · 1.4m · Kothlis · Alliance · Blaster · Escort frigate *Qenfis* · First: Heir to the Empire. Rebel emblem.
- Art: arms-crossed Bothan with holster.
- Bothawui species (raised Kothlis colony); fur-ripple communication; power-hungry. Joined Rebellion post-Yavin with faction (~same time Mon Cal); rivalry with Ackbar. Bothans got DS2 location/schedule (many died). Advisor to Mon Mothma. Tried implicate Ackbar treason after Sluis Van (cleared); aide Tav Breil'lya; aided Bel Iblis. Katana fleet: ordered *Qenfis* withdraw trapping Han/Lando/Wedge; Leia+Karrde exposed; arrested Coruscant; pardoned but lost respect. Insisted Mount Tantiss destroyed (offered Karrde 70k). Power diminished; fate unknown.

## Printed p.60–61 / scan p069–p070 — BIB FORTUNA
- Twi'lek · Male · None · Orange eyes · 1.8m · Ryloth · Loyal to Jabba · Hold-out blaster · Personal repulsor skiff · First: ROTJ.
- Art: full robes lekku-wrapped; face close-up; film still; standing drawing.
- Ambitious/obsequious majordomo; "worm heads" slur. Lekku: cognition, communication, sensual. Ryloth twilight world (hot/arid vs cool night); cave cities. Clandestine **ryll** exporter to Corporate Sector → Empire/smugglers/slavers attention; peers ordered death; escaped exile sworn revenge. Jabba glitterstim agent → lieutenant → revenge slave raid on Ryloth (7 cities ruined); took child **Nat Secura** (great house descendant) for future conquest. Outsmarted Bidlo Kwerve for majordomo (Kwerve first rancor snack when presenting rancor gift). Kidnapped dancer **Oola** for Jabba. ROTJ: mind-tricked by Luke; rancor death of Luke attempt fails; Carkoon — Fortuna flees private skiff. **Never seen again.**

## Printed p.64 / scan p073 — YARNA D'AL' GARGAN
- Askajian · Female · Dark brown hair · Askajia · None · First: ROTJ.
- Art: six-breasted corpulent dancer, feathered headgear, arms outstretched.
- Water-storing physiology (swells for dry season). Tribal chieftain's daughter; husband Nauteg; four cublings. Slave raid killed one cub; sold remaining kids to Mos Eisley Imperial prefect Eugene Tolmont; Nauteg fed to rancor for standing up to Jabba. Became dancer; Jabba warty face patches (remind of mother); indentured path to freedom. Mother figure to dancers (hard bond — Jabba kills them); warned Oola; witnessed Oola's death. After Jabba choked by Leia, escaped with stolen goods to buy children back.

## Printed p.65 / scan p074 — GARINDAN ("Long Snoot")
- Kubaz · Male · 1.85m · Kubindi · Highest bidder · Blaster / hold-out · First: ANH.
- Art: hooded full body; ANH comlink still.

## Printed p.66 / scan p075 — GARTOGG
- Gamorrean · Male · None · Blue eyes · 1.8m · Gamorr · Loyal to Jabba · Vibro-ax · First: ROTJ.
- Art: armored vibro-ax full body + ROTJ still.

Leader Ortugg (watched Quarren Tessek; harassed Gartogg). Speak Gamorrese grunts. Murder-sleuthing lower palace kept Gartogg off sail barge → survived Carkoon.

## Printed p.67 / scan p076 — GETHZERION
- Human · Female · Unknown hair · Red-glowing eyes · Dathomir · Nightsisters · Dark side of the Force · First: Courtship of Princess Leia · Death: fleeing Dathomir.
- Art: gaunt frayed-robe witch.
- Nightsisters selective breeding; most need rituals — Gethzerion alone used Force without spells to intimidate. Palpatine allowed her on Dathomir (penal colony) but destroyed orbiting airfield to strand her (and own people); she enslaved colony survivors. Warlord Zsinj (*Iron Fist*) watched Quelii Sector; post-Endor deal: Gethzerion offers Nightsisters + Han/Leia for a ship. Opposed by Teneniel Djo + Luke; Force storms + Zsinj nightcloak. Fled on carrier to Zsinj; Han blew carrier with missiles rather than let Nightsisters loose. Dathomir witches freed to light-side healing/education.
