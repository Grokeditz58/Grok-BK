# Wedge Antilles — EGC 1995 printed pp.5–8 (scan p014–p017)

**Read:** careful image Read of all four pages. Cite: The Essential Guide to Characters (1995), p.N.

## Stat block (p.5)
Human male · hair brown · eyes green · 1.7 m · Corellia · Alliance · blaster pistol · Incom T-65B X-wing · First Appearance *A New Hope*.

## Combat / career (secondary summaries)
- Orphaned when parents killed at Gus Treta fueling depot; freighter → Alliance weapons smuggler; X-wing expert by **age 16**.
- Yavin: Red Two with Luke (Red Five) / Biggs (Red Three); trench run; **hit by Vader’s TIEs → abort**; watches Luke destroy DS1.
- Hoth: Rogue Squadron snowspeeders; with gunner **Wes Janson** **first to successfully trip an AT-AT**; Tallon split escape with Luke/Tarrin.
- Takes Rogue command (commander); Endor **Red Leader**; helps kill communications Star Destroyer; protects medical frigate; **first down reactor shaft**; concussion missiles on **north tower power regulator**; escapes with *Falcon*.
- Post-Endor drone-ship crystal gag; Bakura; stays with Rogues despite promotion offers; Thrawn (Sluis Van, Bilbringi — destroys *Peremptory*); accepts **general**; Dark Empire (*Liberator*/*Emancipator*, World Devastators, Byss Automadons); Force-aura paddles → **confirms no Force affinity**; Qwi Xux bodyguard/romance; Kyp mindwipe of Qwi; Maw Installation raid vs Daala/*Gorgon*; continues High Command with Bel Iblis, Ackbar, Rieekan, Dodonna.

## Verbatim quotes + crops

### Q1 — Yavin trench / Vader hit (p.5)
> Despite evasive maneuvers, Wedge's ship was hit in an attack by Darth Vader and two TIE fighters, forcing him to abort and skim off into space. Then, Biggs's X-wing was destroyed in a blast from Vader's ship. Wedge watched from space as Luke Skywalker completed his run alone. Amazingly, his missiles did find their mark, and the Death Star was destroyed.

- Source: EGC 1995, **p.5** · Asset: `quotes/egc1995-p005-yavin-vader-hit.jpg`  
- Limits: secondary retelling of ANH; Vader combat credited on film primary.

### Q2 — Hoth AT-AT trip (p.6)
> During the battle, many of Rogue Squadron tried their best, but the twelve two-person snowspeeders lost many of their gunners in the battle. Wedge was backed up by his own gunner, Wes Janson. They were the first to successfully trip a walker, causing it to explode.

- Source: EGC 1995, **p.6** · Asset: `quotes/egc1995-p006-hoth-atat.jpg`  
- Combat claim (secondary): first successful AT-AT trip with Janson.

### Q3 — Endor reactor (p.6)
> Both Wedge and the *Falcon* made it to the reactor core. Wedge targeted the power regulator in the north tower, launching concussion missiles, and Lando torpedoed the main reactor. Then the two ships had to retrace their perilous flight through the Death Star superstructure, even as it collapsed and exploded around them. With milliseconds to spare, Wedge and the *Falcon* escaped the shaft into open space. Behind them, the battle station blew up.

- Source: EGC 1995, **p.6** · Asset: `quotes/egc1995-p006-endor-reactor.jpg`  
- Combat claim (secondary): regulator missiles; primary = ROTJ.

### Q4 — No Force affinity (p.8)
> This would aid Luke's search for Jedi students immensely, and in the initial tests, Wedge confirmed what he always suspected: that he had no affinity for the Force.

- Source: EGC 1995, **p.8** · Asset: `quotes/egc1995-p008-no-force.jpg`  
- Statement/limit: Wedge non-Force-sensitive (Jedi Academy-era summary).

### Q5 — Closing (p.8)
> Having survived and triumphed at so many battles, General Wedge Antilles and his Rogue Squadron will likely have many top-flight adventures for years to come.

- Source: EGC 1995, **p.8** · Asset: `quotes/egc1995-p008-wedge-closing.jpg`
