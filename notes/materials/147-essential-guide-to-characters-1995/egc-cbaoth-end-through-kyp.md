## Printed p.29 / scan p038 — JORUUS C'BAOTH (conclusion)
- Continuation from p.28: Luke trained briefly under C'baoth on Jomark (High Castle); realized he was not the teacher sought; left to help Mara rescue Talon Karrde; planned to return to heal C'baoth.
- **Dark-side powers (paraphrase):** bluish dark-Force lightning (like Palpatine); mind-puppetry / complete subjugation; enhance coordination linking people light-years apart.
- Thrawn tension: C'baoth retreated into half-remembered worlds; saw himself as Empire's new leader; once mind-controlled entire *Chimaera* crew to make a point to Thrawn/Pellaeon.
- Mount Tantiss return: killed General Freja Covell by over-forceful mind control (brain tissue shut down). Created **Luuke Skywalker** clone from sample B-2332-54 (Luke's Bespin-severed hand) in Spaarti cylinders; Luuke used Luke's lost Cloud City lightsaber.
- Mara Jade killed Luuke (fulfilling Emperor's destiny decree). C'baoth raged with lightning + ceiling rubble; planned another Mara clone: quote crop area includes *"I have foreseen that Mara Jade will kneel before me… One Mara Jade… or another."*
- Mara charged with Leia's lightsaber; Leia mind-linked to guide her through rock hail; Luke freed leashed vornskrs as distraction; **Mara killed C'baoth with lightsaber thrust**. Death: Alliance attack on Mount Tantiss (sidebar).
- Crops: `quotes/egc1995-p028-cbaoth-clone.jpg`; `quotes/egc1995-p029-cbaoth-death-mara.jpg`.

## Printed p.30–33 / scan p039–p042 — CHEWBACCA
- Stats: Wookiee · Male · Brown hair · Blue eyes · 2.28m · Kashyyyk · Alliance · Bowcaster · *Millennium Falcon* · First: ANH. Rebel emblem.
- Illustrations: full-body bowcaster stance; cockpit smile still; multiple head portraits; Endor-era standing figure.
- Species/culture: ~2m furred; intelligent; dangerous when aroused — arms/legs ripped off, armor crushed with wearer inside. Kashyyyk wroshyr trees / layered ecology; homes high in trees; not anti-technology (repair, piloting, droid programming). Bowcaster archaic crossbow needing tremendous strength; explosive energy quarrels; bandolier holds quarrels/packs.
- Bio: born ~200 years before Yavin; father Attichitcuk ("Itchy"); hologame champion; friend Salporin; left Kashyyyk age 50; explored ~140 years; captured by slavers (killed five barehanded); Empire enslavement (Trandoshan lobbying).
- **Life debt:** Imperial officer Han Solo saved Chewie at hard-labor camp → Han court-martialed; Chewie life debt (honor, not slavery); expanded honor family to Leia + children. Crop: `quotes/egc1995-p031-chewie-life-debt.jpg`.
- Smuggling: Roa's gang → Shug Ninx / Salla Zend / Lando; Falcon from Lando; Nar Shaddaa spacebarn mods. Language: growls; understands Basic; few Wookiees speak it.
- Family: married Mallatobuck (Malla); son Lumpawarrump ("Lumpy"); later nephew Lowbacca to Jedi Academy ~23 years after meeting Luke.
- OT+EU: Docking Bay 94 / Chalmun's; Death Star rescue; Yavin; Crimson Jack; Life Day / Holiday Special-era Imperial harassment note; Hoth carbonite; Jabba; Endor + Ewoks; Kashyyyk slavers Knife/N'Gai + Vargi; Dathomir / Han-Leia marriage; Thrawn (protect pregnant Leia vs Noghri); Mount Tantiss; Dark Empire (Nar Shaddaa, Fett helmet trophy in Falcon); Maw Wookiee slaves; Plett's Well search; Lowbacca to Yavin 4.
- Close: loyalty to Han, Falcon, freedom.

- Art: flight-suit full body; tunic waist-up with rank plaque.

- Guerrilla: AT-ATs vs Eol Sha on Dantooine; Mon Cal shipyards (*Startide* rams *Manticore* per Ackbar); failed Coruscant / Cauldron Nebula (*Basilisk* destroyed by Kyp supernova). Return Maw; Death Star prototype (Sivron) vs Sun Crusher; downloaded weapon plans; headed Core to ally with warlords.

Labria won instruments at sabacc (except Doikk's Fizzz). Witnessed Kenobi/Ponda Baba/Evazan brawl. Eventually recovered instruments and left Tatooine.
