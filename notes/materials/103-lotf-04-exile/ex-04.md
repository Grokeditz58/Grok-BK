## Ch. 18 (l.5166–5537)
- FEAT (Ben): wakes mid-kick and deflects a lunging gray-furred nek (l.5170–5178). Shaker's arc-welder shocks it and loses its arm to a bite (l.5186). Ben's saber cuts halfway through the nek's armored neck and severs its spine (l.5188).

- The voices learn his name: "Protect girl" (l.5216–5224). Ben asks whether they care, or just know what he would listen to (l.5220–5222).
- Venture planning: the Confederation will elect a warlord and then strike shipyards (Wedge and Jag together) (l.5233–5245). Jaina maps Alema's movements; she visited Booster's and Mirax's quarters (l.5251–5269). Leia: "eliminate her ... the easy way ... or the hard way" (l.5271). Han and Wedge monitor the holocams (l.5279). Jag: "it's my mission" (l.5285).
- Alema's calculus over killing Lavint (l.5299).
- Dream: "Ben ship ... Learn ship," with an image of a red, ball-shaped, insectile, aware ship (l.5305–5331).
- Ben snips the tracker's antenna and hides in a ravine at noon (l.5333–5335). Food is running out (l.5339). Mutated birds (l.5343). Neks follow (l.5345).
- Trap: Zekk projects Force charisma as bait (l.5360–5368).
- FEAT (Corran): plucks Alema's poison dart from the air (l.5384). Silver blade vs Alema's blue-black (l.5386).
- FEAT (Alema): throws her lit saber to cut a huge chandelier loose as a distraction, then Force-recalls the saber (l.5396–5400).
- FEAT (Zekk): Force-checks the falling chandelier (l.5402).
- FEAT (Leia): telekinetically reverses Alema's dart back into her mouth; she spits it out unhurt (l.5416–5424).
- Alema cuts through a turbolift floor and drops into the shaft (l.5444–5446).
- FEAT (Jag): his armor stops her dart and he shoots her left shoulder, breaking her clavicle (l.5456–5468).
- FEAT (Alema): Force-hurls Jag into the crate stacks (l.5470). She stops masking the holocams, probably because of the cost (l.5476). She escapes in Lavint's YV-666; a GA override keeps the doors open (l.5484–5490).
- Lavint: "Actually, I hated her" (l.5498). Han gives Lavint the meeting location anyway. It is either leaked, and harmless, or disinformation, "a Dur Gejjen trap" (l.5500–5532).

## Ch. 19 (l.5538–5996)
- Ben has gone three days without food, Kiara one; Shaker drains the batteries (l.5546). Sith citadel below a cliff, big enough for a thousand people; flows of dark side energy (l.5550–5554).
- LORE (the voices' ship): "Desire ... would cause the craft to lift off ... Anger would direct its weaponry ... make contact with his ships" (l.5558).
- Kiara: "You're that way again ... Scary." She is probably Force-sensitive (l.5568–5580). Ben questions Jacen's no-dark-side view: energy "shaped and left here by hundreds of generations of Sith ... was that not the dark side?" (l.5584). He cleanses his thoughts (l.5586–5590).
- Jacen in Niathal's office: "Gilatter" (l.5600).
- Gilatter VIII is the Confederation meeting site; 13 days away (l.5604–5620). Jacen's 4-step plan: StealthX scouts, then units pulled quietly from local fleets, then hide in the gas giant's thin upper atmosphere, then strike on signal (l.5628–5642). Niathal adopts it and will command it herself (l.5644–5646). Jacen: keep his role from Luke to get "wholehearted Jedi involvement" (l.5656–5660).
- Lumiya, "fully healed," joins Jacen; she has lost track of Ben: "He may not have survived" (l.5666–5682).
- Ziost: Shaker's power runs out 100 m from the citadel (l.5686–5698). A nek ambush misses (l.5700–5706). "If one swallows you, I'll jump down his throat" (l.5716).
- Dark intrusion: the thought of killing and eating Kiara. Ben recognizes it is "not his own" (l.5724–5726).
- Dream: a three-ball, fifteen-legged ceiling creature wants "the little piece of meat." Ben sends it out to hunt the neks (l.5740–5766). The voices now say "Eat girl ... Grow strong" (l.5768–5810).
- LORE (Ben's insight): the voices first told him to protect her to get him listening, and then he "had begun to think the way they thought" (l.5828–5830). Thinking and feeling should be "partners" (l.5826). He tells Kiara the story of Anakin's Podrace (l.5836).
- FEAT (Ben), the top feat of his arc: starving and barely focused, he re-arms the tracker on a dummy, then Force-drops a 100 m course of wall stones (some a quarter ton or more) onto the TIE. It wrecks and rolls half a kilometer (l.5784–5868). He takes the pilot's supplies: a Chev in a bronze uniform (Ovvit, l.6185) (l.5872–5874). "It didn't occur to Ben to be sorry" (l.5876).
- Lavint tells Alema the Solos will be at Gilatter VIII; Leia decides to go to see Alema die (l.5881–5899).
- Gilatter: Luke, Mara, Corran, Kyp, Jaina, Zekk and Jag (in Jaina's StealthX) scout and guide the Ninth Fleet into the atmosphere. Niathal's flagship is the *Galactic Voyager*, Ackbar's old ship (l.5903–5909).
- A bronze shuttle circles the crash site and doesn't land (l.5917).
- The voices lead Ben to an 18-niche circular chamber (l.5927). Ben commands with anger: "Come out!" The floor splits and the spherical vessel rises, 10 m across with an "eye" viewport (l.5937–5947).
- LORE (Sith meditation sphere): the interior has no controls, with glowing orange pumice walls (l.5955). Ben's vision: "a red-skinned woman in robes of volcanic hues ... her golden polearm," a Sith pilot (l.5961). A male voice "rich in expectant malice": "Command" (l.5965). It is flown by intent and visualization (l.5987). Launch (l.5989–5995).

## Ch. 20 (l.5997–6552)
- Jacen and Lumiya infiltrate as "Najack Ell" and "Silfinia Ell" of the Ession Freedom Front (l.6001–6031). The Hapes Heritage Council is said to be attending (l.6005). Jacen plans to "distract everyone present—perhaps by killing them all" (l.6009).
- The Confederation fleet includes an Imperial-class Star Destroyer (l.6013). A tractor beam catches Jacen with "no advance warning through the Force" (l.6017).
- Jacen senses a server's signaling lie and threatens to kill her (l.6065–6077).
- REVEAL: hologram of General Turr Phennir, Supreme Commander of the Confederation. The election was a deception, the delegates are "only actors," and a mine grid waits (l.6083–6099).
- Jacen's Force warning "It's a trap! Mines!" reaches Luke and Leia (l.6103–6105, 6141).
- Niathal: continue ahead slow with forward guns sweeping, so the flanking mines can't close (l.6119–6137). The Solos come to save Jacen (l.6147–6157).
- LORE (Alema): memories of her fade from people minutes after she leaves (l.6161).
- FEAT (Jacen): leaps a blaster volley and beheads two guards (l.6167).
- Ben's sphere vs *Boneyard Rendezvous*. Weapons: a bottom-arm laser and a top-arm magnetic accelerator firing head-sized metal balls (l.6195–6205). Ben feels the hull hit as pain (l.6219).
- FEAT (Ben): his first shot glances off. He then out-turns the freighter, sees a TIE ready to launch from an open bay, and Force-steers a ball into the open hold; it punches through the stern and out the starboard side. The freighter falls toward the atmosphere (l.6221–6239).
- The sphere can reach Coruscant in "hours or days" without a navicomputer (l.6247–6257).
- Alema triggers "approach two" on *Duracrud*: a ram run at the dome (l.6266–6268). Lumiya and Alema team up; Lumiya reveals her lightwhip (l.6270–6290).
- FEAT (Luke and Mara): Luke's circle cut severs 5 or 6 blaster barrels and Mara Force-sweeps half a dozen agents (l.6292). Jacen cuts a Bothan in two: "I don't need your help" (l.6296). Han and Leia arrive (l.6298).
- FEAT (Luke): fights Lumiya's lightwhip with saber and shoto (l.6302). Lumiya Force-lifts a severed head to block his shoto (l.6314). Luke telekinetically whips a tablecloth at her, and she cuts it in two (l.6318). He leaps her tendrils; a chair disintegrates (l.6322).
- Han's shot is dodged; Alema catches his bolt on her blade (l.6304, 6312). Leia: "Mine" (l.6310).
- LORE (Lumiya): "I am dead. I have been for decades" (l.6316). "I don't hate ... that was professional. Not personal ... rival schools" (l.6324–6328). She offers her hand: "If I'd wanted to hurt you tonight, I would have killed your nephew instead of letting him flee" (l.6340). Luke senses only peaceful intent and takes her hand (l.6350–6354).
- ANTI-FEAT/ATROCITY (Jacen): flees, leaving his parents behind (l.6344, 6360–6362).
- Alema fights only defensively while waiting for *Duracrud*, then dons a decompression helmet (l.6358, 6374).
- ATROCITY (Alema): *Duracrud* (YV-666) caves in the dome and punches through the station's axis (l.6376–6382). Lumiya escapes with her whip (l.6380).
- FEAT (Leia): with gravity and atmosphere failing, she Force-shoves Han through the door, sacrificing herself (l.6388–6396). Luke saves her with his old grappling line ("farmboy equipment"), with Han hauling too (l.6398–6402).
- Battle of Gilatter VIII: Jacen sees the Alliance's coordination failures (l.6410–6420). The result is a draw (l.6426–6430). Lumiya seizes a transport and lands on the *Anakin Solo* (l.6424).
- Luke weighs Lumiya's gentleness (l.6451). "I can feel Ben" (l.6455).
- Jacen and Lumiya: she tells him to hide his anger in the Force (l.6467–6473). The battle will be cast as a GA success (l.6479).
- LORE (Sith Master's power): beyond battle meditation, "the capacity to sense, to coordinate, just by the power of the mind and the will ... comes with the assumption of the title of Master of the Sith." Lumiya learned it but can never wield it (l.6491–6499). Requirements: "the sacrifice of love ... One you love. One who will leave a void," then a Sith name (l.6503–6511). Jacen won't sacrifice Allana or Tenel Ka (l.6505) and picks his father or mother (l.6513). He doesn't know whether he still loves them (l.6525–6531). Lumiya: "Emotion doesn't weaken you, Jacen. It scares you ... Liar ... You're not quite ready" (l.6533–6543).
- ATROCITY (intent, Jacen): either way, he should kill both parents. "Good-bye, Mom ... Good-bye, Dad" (l.6549–6551). END OF NOVEL (l.6551).
