## Ch. 7 (l.1599–1898)
- Lumiya at star system MZX32905 near Bimmiel. Luke shot her five times: two hits on prosthetics, three in flesh. She heals unnaturally fast through healing trances and Palpatine's alterations (l.1605). Normally she wears a scarf or uses a Force illusion of normal features; now she uses makeup and a Hapan disguise (l.1607–1619). She is about sixty (l.1613). Her asteroid holocomm chamber uses hyperspace transmission; her computer is "Favvio" (l.1623, 1653).
- Lumiya as a "Hapan noblewoman" tells Bothan deputy intelligence chief Tathak K'roylan she can blind GA observers for 10–20 hours so three Bothan fleets can launch (l.1637–1653). She proves it with GAG files on the Bothan assassinations, which she had committed or arranged herself (l.1657–1669). She sends the "Syo package" to Coruscant and Jacen (l.1673).
- FEAT (Lumiya): Force-dulls her pain through a long performance (l.1615, 1667).
- Love Commander gets down by tagging behind a GA recon force and breaking off when Corellian TIEs attack (l.1681–1685). Hides at Elmas private spaceport (l.1677, 1687). News: "fake Jedi" attack on Wedge; a witness: "Jedi aren't so tough ... Jedi hunt" (l.1715).
- Cantina meeting with Denjax Teppler, who uses a look-alike to slip his watchers (l.1731–1735). Teppler: "There's no such thing as treason anymore" (l.1771–1775). His ex-wife died on a diplomatic mission (l.1807). **Dur Gejjen** planned and signed off on killing Tenel Ka; it was assassination, not kidnapping; Wedge didn't know (l.1809–1821).
- FEAT (Leia): Force-senses CorSec massing outside, through walls (l.1823–1825). She cuts a 1.5 m hole in the floor with her saber (l.1841, 1847) and cuts the door hinges (l.1863, 1873).
- FEAT (Leia): mind-tricks a garbage-loader pilot into landing across the alley mouth, at range, without eye contact or voice; it drains her (l.1885–1891). Han and Lando disable it with blaster fire (l.1893). Teppler leaves on foot (l.1897).

## Ch. 8 (l.1899–2230)
- Elmas rental bay: Wedge, Myri, Iella, Corran and Mirax with the *Pulsar Skate* (a Baudo-class yacht) and two X-wings (l.1903–1927). Corran senses Leia (l.1913–1915).
- CorSec raid; TIE crawler (l.1949, 1963). Corran's green-checkered X-wing flips the crawler, then Wedge's X-wing passes (l.1975–1985). Leia nudges the crawler aside (l.1993). Myri is comm officer on the Skate (l.2023).
- FEAT (Wedge and Corran): two X-wings cripple the CEC corvette *Silaban* by concentrating fire on its stern engines (l.2061–2083). Lando's GA pass code gets them out. Wedge: "a gathering of old friends" (l.2089).
- Temple training hall: Ben vs a ball-firing practice droid, with his back to it; he can't sense it through the Force (l.2099).
- FEAT (Ben): Force-flattens a foamsteel ball to jam the droid's hopper (l.2111–2117). He holds his saber against the droid's magnetic pull (l.2121). 94% success ratio (l.2129).
- Seha Dorvald (redhead, undercity accent) slips him a card (l.2133–2179). Text typo "occurred to me" in a third-person passage (l.2163).
- The card's GAG message: Jacen sends Ben to steal the **Amulet of Kalara** from Tendrando Arms on Almania, 215th floor (l.2207–2217).
- LORE (Amulet): while it is active, its wearer "will be invisible in the Force," and so is their Force use (l.2219–2221). Jacen: if someone else has it, "you have to eliminate him before he eliminates you" (l.2225). Ben worries this could be murder (l.2227).

## Ch. 9 (l.2231–2512)
- FEAT (Ben): uses the Force to control friction on his feet as he drops down the laundry chute (l.2241–2245). He figures out the repulsor trap for children (l.2253–2255), drops 5 m silently (l.2265) and goes out through the emergency telescoping access (l.2269–2287). Seha disabled the alarms (l.2273).
- *Errant Venture* in the Kuat system; the "All-Clown Squadron of Fun"; Flag Hangar (l.2309–2327). Originally commissioned as the *Virulence* (l.2323).
- Holocomm: Luke and Mara ask Jacen where Ben is. Jacen technically tells the truth, since Lumiya is monitoring the mission (l.2345). He hides his feelings and "exult[s] in his power to position their emotions" (l.2347, 2357). The cover story: a message about making Luke "move his feet" (l.2361). "...becoming easier ... even with ... Luke Skywalker" (l.2369).
- FEAT/LORE (Jacen): Luke can't detect his deception (l.2347).
- Booster in a massive hoverchair (l.2375). Booster's odds: 37:1 on total conquest of Corellia; with the Bothans in, 14:1 on a negotiated end (l.2397). Wedge says unexplained string-pulling (l.2399). Lando: the Bothan assassinations don't add up (l.2401). Leia: "ghosts" persuading people point to a dark-sider (l.2409). Plan: park the Venture at Corellia to gather intelligence, with approval via reverse psychology on Jacen through Luke (l.2413–2423). Myri and Iella are hired as sabacc dealers (l.2425–2445).
- Zorp House: a Neimoidian finds Lumiya's comm intercept on the 288th floor (l.2451–2475).
- FEAT (Mara): defuses an explosive plus trihexalon booby trap (l.2499–2507). "Oops, sorry, farmboy" (l.2499). Its comms reroute to basement level three (l.2507–2511).

## Ch. 10 (l.2513–2862)
- Lumiya's bedrock-level flat (l.2515–2525). Encrypted datacard in the delivery slot (l.2535–2539). Leia's message asks Luke to help with the Venture approval (l.2543–2551).
- FEAT (Mara): bends backward under a meter-long durasteel dart trap (l.2565–2567). The flat has a private escape tunnel (l.2575–2577).
- Task force office: Jag and Zekk compete at chin-ups; Zekk has a torso scar (l.2585–2591). Jag deduces that Leia is Alema's target (l.2599). Jaina "lucky in war, unlucky in love," Sword of the Jedi; she calls Jag "Colonel" (l.2625–2629).
- Commenor: Lumiya in Hapan guise gives President Fyor Rodan GAG "plans" for conquering Commenor a month after Corellia falls (l.2641–2661).
- FEAT (Lumiya, "elaborate Sith technique"): from her asteroid she finds a mind she has reshaped many times, halfway across the galaxy (l.2685–2691). She puppets a sleeping Quarren nurse (l.2697–2707) and appears to Admiral Matric Klauskin as his dead wife Edela, persuading him to escape the Coruscant veterans' mental hospital (l.2713–2765).
- ANTI-FEAT/cost (Lumiya): it nearly makes her collapse; she spasms (l.2769–2779). LORE: she "deliberately drove [Klauskin] crazy" (l.2779). The GA kept his breakdown quiet; he still holds rank (l.2783).
- Drewwa, moon of Almania: customs sensor tube will catch Ben's lightsaber (l.2793–2799).
- FEAT (Ben): Force-tears a glow-rod pod from the ceiling after a hard effort (l.2809–2815). He then floats his saber across the ceiling past the customs wall and nests it in a far pod (l.2817), retrieving it later (l.2861).
- ANTI-FEAT/dark hint (Ben): thinks of tripping a rude old woman, then uses a Force-backed glare that makes her go gray ("See what I become"). "That's how Jacen would have done it" (l.2829–2859).

## Ch. 11 (l.2863–3146)
- The Crossroutes Business Habitat on Drewwa: 800 m tall with rings every five floors (l.2867). Trang Robotics (l.2869). Mara's intelligence lessons (l.2875–2885).
- Ben's mission credcard is dead: "ACCOUNT NOT FOUND" (l.2905–2911).
- ANTI-FEAT (Ben): his Force suggestion fails on a café owner (l.2917–2921).
- Ben steals for the first time: he Force-flicks a value credcard out of a man's portfolio and pins it to the ground (l.2929–2953).
- Lyster Innovations: a chameleon-cover "corporate culture" reception, with receptionist Gilthor Breen (l.2967–2987). Ben hands over a fake admirer's box of sweets (l.2995–3009).
- FEAT (Ben): projects an illusion that he boarded the turbolift and stays unseen while he creeps back; it drenches him in sweat (l.3021–3027).
- Ben climbs out of the refresher window onto the decorative ring, then down a knotted cable to the 215th floor (l.3031–3043).
- FEAT/ANTI-FEAT (Ben): Force-turns the window crank, slowly ("hard work"). He loses his grip and falls two stories, saving himself with his arms and the Force (l.3049–3067).
- The Amulet is gone. A note: "I will return the Amulet to where it belongs. Be grateful that I spared your lives. — Faskus of Ziost." Ben senses residual Sith glee (l.3081–3087).
- LORE: the Amulet was made on Ziost about 2,000 years ago; dark side energy keeps it from wearing (l.3103). Jacen "didn't believe in dark side energies," so neither does Ben (l.3103). Ziost is the Sith homeworld, a few hours from Almania (l.3107–3109). Ben leaves the fake in the case (l.3115–3117) and Force-unties the cable (l.3119).
- Twist: the "sleeping" protocol droid reports to the pirate freighter *Boneyard Rendezvous* (posing as *High Tide*), Bothan captain Byalfin Dyur: "Red Braid in motion. Tracker activated." "Track the boy" (l.3125–3145). The whole theft is a staged test.

## Ch. 12 (l.3147–3420)
- Bothawui: Klauskin boards the GA frigate *Shamunaar* (Captain Biurk) with "GAG authorization." He says several officers are in Bothan pay and gets a list to "capture or eliminate" them, which will open the observation hole Lumiya promised the Bothans (l.3151–3181).
- Ben steals credcards twice more; "easier, smoother" (l.3185). Ziost is in no public database (l.3187). He gets a jumpsuit and security transponder using Force telekinesis on door latches (l.3195–3199). He targets Hemalian Barkid's unarmed Y-wing (l.3201–3205) and forges a maintenance letter (l.3211–3219).
- Dark line (Ben): he had planned to "cut [the droid's] head off" and wonders whether he would kill the mechanic if the mission needed it (l.3225–3229).
- ATROCITY/ANTI-FEAT (Ben): punches the mechanic in the solar plexus and breaks her jaw with a palm strike. "Jacen would have forgiven me if I'd killed you" (l.3283–3289). He takes the R2 "Shaker" (l.3237, 3299).
- *Errant Venture* in the Corellian exclusion zone: Iella and Myri deal, while Han and Lando play sabacc (Lando slightly ahead) (l.3311–3313). Leia, Wedge and Corran analyze data (l.3317). Syal is aboard "with a boy" (l.3327–3333).
- Analysis: the war itself arose naturally (l.3339–3345). Klauskin was meddled with by "Force ghost manifestations" (l.3351). Toryaz Station led to Thrackan (l.3353).
- LORE (Myri's insight): the plot "scattered the Jedi," breaking up the Solo-Skywalker family as a "resource" (l.3355–3379). Leia's new goal: reunite the family (l.3385–3389). Han: "Except for Jacen" (l.3393).
- Lavint's *Duracrud* (YV-666, "mutant offspring of a bird and a brick") brings Alema into the Venture (l.3395–3419). Alema hides in shadow, "impenetrable by ordinary sight" (l.3405).
