## Ch. 1 (l.165–470)
- Anakin Solo, outside Corellian space. Jacen can't feel the guilt he should for firing on the Falcon; "What sort of father could he be to Allana" (l.169–181).
- Dream: Leia with Mara's old saber (Luke's first, Anakin's last) comes to kill him (l.185–203). Jacen: "you're not a match for any Jedi Knight who's been fighting and training constantly" (l.201). Dream-Leia's Force blast smashes the viewport and she tackles him into vacuum (l.209–223). He wakes; "Was that you?" (Lumiya?) (l.225–227). About ten minutes' sleep in fifteen (l.233).
- FEAT (Jacen, in the dream): on his feet with saber lit before the intruder's blade fully extends, Force-sweeping his chair aside (l.185).
- Coruscant depot: Kyp senses veterans of the Hapes battle (l.245–249). *Jade Shadow* lands (l.253–255). Luke's new cybernetic hand: neural matrix better, "doesn't feel right yet" (l.267–273). Mara: still "the most accomplished lightsaber artist in the galaxy ... just a little less so" (l.275). Kyp's old crush on Jaina (l.279).
- FEAT (Kyp, minor): offers to carry the *Jade Shadow* to the Temple; it vibrates and moves under his Force pressure (l.285).
- Zekk will fully recover from his lightsaber wound (Hapan surgeons) (l.291). Kyp is investigating the "lightsaber accident" (all saber injuries investigated) (l.293–299). Kyp's long gray hair; "seeing a lady" (l.307–311). Omas and Niathal want Luke to make Jacen a Master (l.315–319).
- *Breathe My Jets*: Uran Lavint, smuggler "heir to the tradition of Han Solo," in debt, one creditor hired a bounty hunter (l.323–329); Hutt copilot Blatta (l.333). Thirty-odd-ship run to Corellia (l.337–349) pulled out of hyperspace by an Interdictor in a chevron (l.353–359). A voice claiming to be "the real Captain Lavint" orders the scatter (l.367). An ISD disables her engines (l.375–383). Blatta: "half a year behind in what you owe me" (l.391).
- Anakin Solo: Jacen Force-samples the beaten smugglers (l.395–399). Ebbak, civilian data analyst, silent walker (l.401–403). Lavint was his informer (l.405). Jacen confiscates *Breathe My Jets* and gives her the YV-666 *Duracrud* (l.425–437). Lavint: "What's it like to have once been a hero?" (l.443).
- ATROCITY (Jacen): orders *Duracrud*'s hyperdrive rigged to fail catastrophically after one jump. "She might die." "...she'll be a better person for the experience" (l.447–455).

## Ch. 2 (l.471–650)
- Jacen meets Wedge (l.473–553). Wedge: he didn't know about the Hapes plot — "Because it failed" (l.489–493). Blockade; smuggler convoy captured (l.497). Wedge's terms: rejoin GA with no reparations (l.527). Wes Janson quote: a fanatic "loses his sense of humor" (l.547). Jacen: "I've grown up" (l.549).
- Jacen's self-dialogue in a Vergere-like voice (l.597–641): he knew it was the Falcon (transponder *Longshot*) and was trying to kill them (l.599–613); willing to "sacrifice" Zekk, Ben, Jaina (l.615–617); "it was the right decision"; "You are still learning" (l.623–635). He would do it again (l.641). The animal-loving boy died with Anakin at Myrkr (l.575–581, 645). Vergere taught him to distance himself (l.583–587).
- LORE: Jacen can "sacrifice a lesser love for a greater one" (l.643).

## Ch. 3 (l.651–748)
- Omas's office: Omas asks Luke to make Jacen a Master (l.659–667). Luke senses Omas is fronting for someone (l.669). Luke: Jacen "lacks the emotional maturity" (l.671). Mara: "any eight-year-old with a thermal detonator" (l.679). Niathal: "very receptive to my guidance" (l.689).
- Kyp's fake "taras-chi" story (l.705–715) — really a Kessel bug (l.739).
- FEAT/LORE (Kyp): as a teen he reached "into the gravity well of a gas giant and pull[ed] a spacecraft out of it," something "not many Masters could accomplish"; he says he couldn't now because his purpose would be bad (l.721).
- Kyp supports Luke for Jedi solidarity, but still thinks Jacen should be a Master (l.743). Luke suspects Jacen or Ben leaked the Council talk (l.745–747).

## Ch. 4 (l.749–978)
- Coronet bunker: Wedge, Gejjen, Sadras Koyan (Tralus Chief of State, Centerpoint Party) (l.761), Denjax Teppler (Justice), Rorf Willems, Gavele Lemora (l.765). Wedge says the assault fleet could have held off the blockade (l.769–771). Centerpoint lore: Ben sabotaged it (l.779).
- Gejjen fires Wedge as Supreme Commander and appoints Admiral Genna Delpin (who led the Hapes assault fleet) (l.809–815). Wedge resigns his commission (l.831) and agrees to one last public appearance (l.839–845). He expects to be assassinated (l.855–857).
- Tendrando station, Gyndine: battered Falcon with turrets missing (l.863–865). Lando (cane) (l.869). Han's left arm in a sling (l.877). The Solos recount Jacen's acts: Corellian camps, Ailyn Vel's death under interrogation, firing on the Falcon; the Noghri Cakhmaim and Meewalh incinerated (l.885). Lando bored with no risk (l.907–911).
- Jacen floats a meter up in meditation and finds Lumiya alive (l.919–923). She projects a Force phantom from her asteroid (l.925–931). "Your uncle hurt me" (l.933). Lumiya: test Ben as a possible Sith apprentice (l.945–953). Jacen: "I can sacrifice him if I have to" (l.947); "I must separate Ben from Luke and Mara, and test him" (l.959). "If he fails, he will not long be suitable as your sacrifice" (l.973).
- FEAT (Lumiya): a long-range Force projection from her asteroid used for live conversation (l.925–931).

## Ch. 5 (l.979–1258)
- Temple: Ben (almost 14) sulks. Mara assigns a history of Anakin Skywalker's fall, with resuming Jacen's apprenticeship as the reward (l.1001–1013). Luke felt Jacen channeling the Force strongly — rare, since he "hides from it" (l.1015).
- "Twinsins Thlee" = Jag Fel, Twin Suns Three (l.1019–1049). Exiled from his house and the Chiss (l.1049, 1057). Two years on Tenupe (l.1057).
- LORE (Alema): she can scrub herself from short-term memory (l.1059). Jedi "Force-flash" causes holocam static (l.1067–1071). Jag's gifts: holocam recognition software (l.1065, 1073) and a neck disk that shocks the user each minute after the word "Alema" so short-term memory moves to long-term (l.1077–1083). Cilghal to research a Force version (l.1089).
- Jag walks past Jaina without greeting (l.1109–1119).
- *Love Commander*, SoroSuub 2400 yacht won at sabacc (l.1129–1137); a 50 m yacht with hidden turbolaser, concussion missile and pop-up shields (l.1159–1161). Lando is "Bescat Offdurmin" (l.1147). C-3PO stays to log the Falcon repairs (l.1153). Han's plan: go to Corellia and find out whether Gejjen acted alone in the hit on Tenel Ka (l.1167).
- *Duracrud*: Lavint stranded; transmissions would take eight years to reach the nearest star (l.1177–1185). Alema appears, a stowaway who watched the sabotage and brought replacement parts (l.1205–1233). Deal: help find the Solos and act as her public face (l.1245–1253).

## Ch. 6 (l.1259–1598)
- Wedge's farewell press conference with Delpin (l.1267). Gejjen's bodyguards include a YVH droid (l.1281). Myri: Corellian Intelligence and GAIS both made her offers (l.1285–1295). Retirement dinner in three nights (l.1301).
- Wedge's escape: disguise from Iella in a refresher (Centerpoint jacket, DH-17) (l.1313–1327). Tailed (l.1333). A sniper with a blaster rifle and a fake "Jedi" with an unlit saber attack (l.1345–1353).
- FEAT (Wedge): rolls through speeder traffic returning fire and kills the "Jedi" with hits to the groin and face (l.1355–1359).
- FEAT (Corran, disguised with a red beard): drops four stories from a speeder and kicks the sniper's head back (l.1361–1367). Myri pilots the orange speeder (l.1379–1385).
- Myri: avoiding the main exits let them miss "about three-quarters of the assassins" (l.1405).
- Temple hangar (nine X-wings, one E-wing) (l.1417). Luke funds Jag's Alema task force: "find and neutralize," with capture if possible so she can find "her own path to redemption" (l.1423–1431). Luke runs a parallel Lumiya hunt; she "may be dead now—or maybe not" (l.1439). Jag gets an identicard, a credcard and an X-wing (l.1443). Jaina and Zekk are assigned to him (l.1457). Jag blames Jaina for his exile over the Lowbacca parole; the Chiss don't forgive, and he is "an unperson ... forever" (l.1479–1481).
- *Love Commander* in the exclusion zone: Lando in a white wig and beard, Han dyed yellow (l.1487–1493), Leia made dowdy (l.1501). GA armed shuttle *Spinnerfish* boards them (l.1511–1539).
- FEAT (Leia, mind trick): she gets a GA lieutenant to hand over Intelligence access codes (l.1541–1585). "I still prefer this sort of thing to cutting people's arms off" (l.1589).
