# Legacy of the Force 4: Exile: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-04 CST. Full hand-read, l.1–9826. Citation "EX ch. N (l.x)". Detail is in ex-01 to ex-06.
- Author: Aaron Allston (title page; About the Author, l.6557–6559). This matches the inventory.

## Plot
- **Jacen's thread (ch. 1–2, 4, 9, 13–14, 16, 19–20):**
  - He feels no guilt over firing on the Falcon. Leia attacks him in a dream (l.169–227).
  - He rigs smuggler Uran Lavint's new ship, the *Duracrud*, to fail after one jump (l.447–455).
  - His self-dialogue: he knew it was the Falcon and would do it again; he can "sacrifice a lesser love for a greater one" (l.597–645).
  - Lumiya, alive, has him test Ben as a possible apprentice: "I can sacrifice him if I have to" (l.919–973).
  - He deceives Luke and Mara about Bimmiel and Brisha Syo (l.3708–3742).
  - He plans the Gilatter VIII strike with Niathal (l.5604–5660). The meeting is a Confederation trap led by Turr Phennir (l.6083–6099).
  - He flees and leaves his parents behind (l.6344). Lumiya names the price of becoming a Sith Master, "the sacrifice of love." Jacen decides to kill both parents (l.6491–6551).
- **Ben on Ziost (ch. 8–9, 10–12, 15, 17–20):**
  - Jacen sends him to steal the Amulet of Kalara on Drewwa (l.2207–2227). The theft is a staged test run by the Bothan Dyur and Lumiya (l.3081–3145).
  - He steals a Y-wing and breaks a mechanic's jaw (l.3283–3289). On Ziost he finds the courier Faskus dying with his daughter Kiara.
  - He leaves Kiara to die, then turns back after hearing his birthday message (l.4434–4516). Lumiya orders both killed (l.4887).
  - He survives TIE strafing runs, neks and the Sith voices that urge "Eat girl" (l.5768–5830). He crushes the TIE under a wall (l.5850–5868).
  - He raises a Sith meditation sphere (l.5937–5995) and cripples the *Boneyard Rendezvous* (l.6221–6239).
- **Solos, Wedge and the Venture (ch. 4–9, 12–14, 17–18):**
  - Gejjen fires Wedge (l.809–831), and Corran and Myri save him from assassins (l.1345–1405).
  - Teppler reveals that Gejjen ordered Tenel Ka's assassination (l.1809–1821). Leia's mind tricks get them out (l.1541–1585, 1885–1891).
  - The group gathers on Booster's *Errant Venture* (l.2375–2445). Myri reads the plot as meant to scatter the Solo–Skywalker family (l.3355–3389).
  - In the Second Fleet battle at Centerpoint (ch. 14), Luke and Mara cripple a Nebulon-B (l.3974–3990).
  - Alema infiltrates through Lavint. The ambush on her fails, but Jag breaks her clavicle and she flees in the *Duracrud* (l.5360–5490).
- **Lumiya and Klauskin (ch. 7, 10, 12–13):**
  - Lumiya heals from Luke's five shots (l.1605). Disguised as a Hapan, she plays the Bothans and Commenor (l.1637–1673, 2641–2661).
  - From her asteroid she puppets a nurse to free Admiral Klauskin (l.2685–2781). Klauskin vents the *Shamunaar*'s crew and opens the gap for the Bothan fleets (l.3585–3637).
- **Climax at Gilatter VIII (ch. 20):**
  - Lumiya and Alema fight Luke, Mara, Han and Leia. Lumiya tells Luke the fight is "not personal ... rival schools" and offers her hand; he takes it (l.6316–6354).
  - Alema rams the *Duracrud* into the dome (l.6376–6382). Leia shoves Han to safety, and Luke saves her with a grappling line (l.6388–6402).
  - The battle is a draw (l.6426–6430).

## Top feats
1. **Ben, starving, drops a wall on a TIE (EX ch. 19):**
   - He has gone three days without food (l.5546) and can barely focus.
   - He re-arms Lumiya's tracker on a dummy to lure the TIE's strafing run, then Force-drops a 100 m course of wall stones, some a quarter ton or more, onto the fighter.
   - It crashes and rolls half a kilometer (l.5784–5868). He then takes the pilot's supplies (l.5872–5876).
   - Earlier he Force-lifted a "quarter-ton slab of stone" into a TIE's path (l.4969–4979, 5030).
2. **Ben flies the Sith meditation sphere (ch. 19–20):**
   - He commands the sphere out of the floor with anger (l.5937–5947) and flies it by intent alone (l.5955–5987).
   - Against the *Boneyard Rendezvous* he out-turns the freighter and Force-steers a head-sized magnetic-accelerator ball into its open hold. The ball punches through the stern and out the starboard side, and the freighter falls toward the atmosphere (l.6195–6239).
3. **Lumiya's long-range puppetry (ch. 10):**
   - From her asteroid she finds Klauskin's mind halfway across the galaxy (l.2685–2691).
   - She puppets a sleeping Quarren nurse and appears to him as his dead wife Edela, talking him out of the Coruscant veterans' hospital (l.2697–2765).
   - The cost: she nearly collapses (l.2769–2779). Her years of reshaping his mind later drive him to vent the *Shamunaar* (l.2779, 3585–3637).

## Other feats
- **Leia:**
  - Sends Alema's poison dart back into Alema's mouth (l.5416–5424).
  - Mind-tricks a GA lieutenant into giving up access codes (l.1541–1585).
  - Lands a garbage loader by mind trick at range, without eye contact (l.1885–1891).
  - Force-shoves Han through a door as the dome fails (l.6388–6396).
- **Luke:**
  - Fights Lumiya's lightwhip with saber and shoto and leaps its tendrils (l.6302–6322).
  - His circle cut severs 5 or 6 blaster barrels (l.6292).
  - With Mara, drifts dark in a StealthX and cripples a Nebulon-B with one proton torpedo each (l.3974–3990).
- **Mara:** Force-sweeps half a dozen agents (l.6292). Defuses a trihexalon booby trap (l.2499–2507) and dodges a dart trap (l.2565–2567).
- **Corran:** plucks Alema's dart from the air (l.5384). Drops four stories onto a sniper (l.1361–1367).
- **Jag:** his armor stops Alema's dart and he shoots her, breaking her clavicle (l.5456–5468).
- **Zekk:** Force-checks a falling chandelier (l.5402).
- **Alema:**
  - Throws her saber to cut the chandelier loose, then recalls it (l.5396–5400).
  - Fights through Lando's Wookiee-rated shock cane and wipes his memory (l.3942–3968).
  - Force-hurls Jag into crates (l.5470).
  - Masks herself from holocams across the Venture (l.5476).
- **Jacen:**
  - His Force warning about the mines reaches Luke and Leia (l.6103–6105).
  - Leaps a blaster volley and beheads two guards (l.6167).
  - Deceives Luke and Mara undetected (l.2347, 3708–3742).
- **Ben:**
  - Fights neks in the dark, shooting them through the Force (l.5170–5200).
  - Force-flattens a foamsteel ball (l.2111–2117).
  - Casts a turbolift illusion (l.3021–3027).
  - Lifts an R2 unit (l.4132–4144).
- **Kyp:**
  - Makes the *Jade Shadow* move under his Force pressure (l.285).
  - As a teen, pulled a ship out of a gas giant's gravity well (l.721).
- **Wedge and Corran:** cripple the corvette *Silaban* (l.2061–2083). Two X-wings take on a squadron of twelve Howlrunners (l.3978, 3992–3996).
- **Lando:** his shock cane briefly locks Alema's muscles (l.3942–3950).

## Anti-feats and atrocities
- **Jacen:**
  - Rigs the *Duracrud*'s hyperdrive to fail: "She might die" (l.447–455).
  - Flees and leaves his parents at Gilatter (l.6344, 6360–6362).
  - Resolves to kill both parents (l.6549–6551).
  - He is caught by a tractor beam with no Force warning (l.6017).
- **Ben:**
  - Breaks a mechanic's jaw (l.3283–3289).
  - Abandons Kiara: "Our mission is more important than her life," then turns back (l.4434–4516).
  - His Force suggestion fails on a café owner (l.2917–2921), and he falls two stories (l.3049–3067).
- **Klauskin:** murders Captain Biurk and vents the *Shamunaar*'s crew (l.3585–3637).
- **Lumiya:** orders Ben and Kiara killed (l.4887). Her Klauskin projection nearly makes her collapse (l.2769–2779).
- **Alema:**
  - Her dart hits an Ortolan instead of Mara (l.3784–3790).
  - She rams the *Duracrud* into the station dome (l.6376–6382).
- **Jaina:** doesn't sense Jag enter the room (l.4640).

## Lore
- **Sith Master's power:** the ability to sense and coordinate "just by the power of the mind and the will." It comes with the title; Lumiya learned it but can never wield it. The price is "the sacrifice of love ... One who will leave a void" (l.6491–6511).
- **Amulet of Kalara:** makes its wearer and their Force use invisible in the Force (l.2219–2221). It was made on Ziost about 2,000 years ago (l.3103).
- **Sith meditation sphere:** has no controls; desire lifts it and anger aims its weapons (l.5558, 5955–5987).
- **Lumiya's daughter:** Brisha Syo is an anagram of Shira Brie (l.3451–3455).
- **Lumiya on herself:** "I am dead. I have been for decades" (l.6316).
- **Alema:** her memory-scrubbing ability (l.1059, 6161) and Jag's shock disk countermeasure (l.1077–1083).
