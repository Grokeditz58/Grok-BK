Character quick bios:
- **Jacen Solo:**
  - GAG colonel on the *Anakin Solo* and Lumiya's apprentice. Feels no guilt over firing on the Falcon (l.169–181).
  - Sabotages Lavint's *Duracrud* (l.447–455). Sees himself as able to "sacrifice a lesser love for a greater one" (l.643).
  - Tests Ben through the staged Amulet theft (l.959, 2207–2227) and deceives Luke undetected (l.2347).
  - Plans Gilatter with Niathal (l.5628–5646). Flees and leaves his parents there (l.6344). Resolves to kill them to become a Sith Master (l.6503–6551).
- **Ben Skywalker:**
  - Almost 14 (l.1005, 2237). Jacen sends him to steal the Amulet of Kalara (l.2207). Breaks a mechanic's jaw (l.3283–3289).
  - Abandons the girl Kiara, then returns (l.4434–4516). Resists the Ziost voices (l.5828–5830).
  - Drops a wall on a TIE (l.5850–5868) and raises and flies a Sith meditation sphere (l.5937–6257).
- **Luke Skywalker:**
  - Grand Master with a new cybernetic hand that "doesn't feel right yet" (l.267–273). Refuses to make Jacen a Master (l.671–687).
  - Hunts Lumiya and links Brisha Syo to Shira Brie (l.3451–3463).
  - Fights Lumiya's whip with saber and shoto (l.6302–6322), then takes her offered hand (l.6350–6354).
- **Mara Jade Skywalker:** defuses Lumiya's traps (l.2499–2507, 2565–2567). Her StealthX strike with Luke cripples a Nebulon-B (l.3974–3990).
- **Han and Leia Solo:**
  - Leia is a Jedi Knight. She pulls off mind tricks (l.1541–1585, 1885–1891) and sends Alema's dart back into her mouth (l.5416–5424). She Force-shoves Han to safety at Gilatter (l.6388–6396).
  - Han flies the restored Falcon through a Bothan raid (l.5078–5128).
- **Lumiya:**
  - Dark Lady of the Sith, about sixty. Heals from five shots (l.1605–1613).
  - Manipulates the Bothans, Commenor and Klauskin (l.1637–1673, 2641–2781). Orders Ben and Kiara killed (l.4887).
  - Explains the Sith Master's power and its price (l.6491–6511).
- **Alema Rar:**
  - Twi'lek Dark Jedi who scrubs herself from short-term memory (l.1059, 6161). Stows away on the *Duracrud* (l.1205–1253).
  - Infiltrates the Venture (l.3395–3498) and survives the ambush with a broken clavicle (l.5468).
  - Rams the *Duracrud* into the Gilatter dome (l.6376–6382).
- **Wedge Antilles:** fired as Corellian Supreme Commander (l.809–831). Survives an assassination attempt (l.1345–1359) and joins the Venture group.
- **Corran Horn / Mirax Terrik Horn / Myri and Iella Antilles / Booster Terrik:**
  - Corran rescues Wedge (l.1361–1367) and plucks a dart from the air (l.5384).
  - Myri reads the plot as meant to scatter the family (l.3355–3389).
  - Booster's *Errant Venture* is the group's base (l.2309–2445).
- **Jagged Fel:** exiled from the Chiss. Leads the Alema task force with Jaina and Zekk (l.1019–1083, 1423–1481) and shoots Alema (l.5456–5468).
- **Uran Lavint:** smuggler and Jacen's informer (l.323–455). Alema's cover and dupe (l.1245–1253, 4727–4821).
- **Matric Klauskin:** Commenorian admiral whose mind Lumiya broke. Vents the *Shamunaar*'s crew (l.3585–3637).
- **Kiara / Faskus / Byalfin Dyur / Shaker:**
  - Faskus is a courier, dead on Ziost (l.4358–4426); Kiara is his Force-strong daughter (l.4366).
  - Dyur is the Bothan pirate behind the staged Amulet theft (l.3125–3145).
  - Shaker is the R2 Ben stole (l.3237).
- **Others:** Dur Gejjen, Cal Omas, Cha Niathal, Lando, Kyp Durron, Turr Phennir and Denjax Teppler are supporting cast (Dramatis Personae, l.121–163; Phennir reveal, l.6083–6099).
