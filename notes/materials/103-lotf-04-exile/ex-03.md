## Ch. 13 (l.3421–3753)
- Mara decrypts the card: a bounty hunter's invoice and report: "the Lady's daughter succumbed to injuries inflicted by Grandson 32707" (l.3437–3441). BMX32707 is the Bimmiel system where Syo led Jacen and Ben and Nelani Dinn died (l.3447–3449).
- LORE: Brisha Syo = anagram of Shira Brie, so Lumiya's daughter (l.3451–3455). Luke thinks Ben wounded her during his memory gap and is now Lumiya's revenge target (l.3459–3463). Mara: Lumiya struck only after the daughter died (l.3465). Brainwashing theory for Jacen angers Mara (l.3465–3471). Luke and Shira were "involved emotionally, but not physically" (l.3485). They head to Corellia to confront Jacen (l.3473–3489).
- Alema senses a hidden Jedi on the Venture and decides it must be Leia (l.3496–3498).
- Lavint's "speech about Corellian smugglers": Han, Wedge, Booster and Mirax all grew from the same trunk and are tied to the Jedi, Intelligence and CorSec, so the Solos would be in private areas with Booster (l.3512–3544). Alema feels a rare gratitude for not killing her (l.3546).
- *Shamunaar*: Klauskin arrests the alleged traitors, so the frigate covers the Rimward screen alone (l.3553–3561). He sets up a "simulation" (l.3571).
- ATROCITY (Klauskin): shoots Captain Biurk three times on the auxiliary bridge (l.3585–3593), cuts communications (l.3599–3603) and runs a data card that opens every exterior hatch and vents the crew into space (l.3605–3621). "The eye is closed." He weeps without knowing why (l.3629–3637).
- *Errant Venture*: Luke and Mara reunite with the Solos and Han's distance from Luke closes (l.3644–3648). Jacen is aboard (l.3652–3658). Leia senses an unidentified "watchful" presence (l.3660). Cilghal has developed a Force technique against Alema's memory-wiping (l.3668).
- Maw Casino: Jacen finds Lavint alive and thriving with *Duracrud*. He senses she is withholding something and that his order failed (l.3670–3688).
- Jacen gives Luke and Mara Lumiya's planted Bimmiel story: a false Luke attacked him, and he cut the attacker in two ("A Devaronian") (l.3708–3716). He says Brisha lost an arm and was wounded in the head and chest saving Ben from an "evil Mara" (l.3724). Luke's timing analysis means two enemies (l.3726–3730). Jacen fakes surprise that Brisha was Lumiya's daughter (l.3734). Mara: "Ben cut her to ribbons" (l.3740).
- FEAT (Jacen): keeps up the deception under direct questioning from Luke and Mara (l.3708–3742).
- The Jedi sense a mass emotional surge: the fleet attack has begun (l.3744–3750).

## Ch. 14 (l.3754–4077)
- Alema hides in shadow "deepened by her own abilities" (l.3756). She aims a blowgun dart at Mara (l.3762–3770). Luke stands and looks straight at her (l.3770–3772).
- ANTI-FEAT (Alema): her long-range dart hits an Ortolan instead of Mara (l.3784–3790). She spots Lando (l.3794–3800).
- Dodonna: Admiral Tarla Limpan (Duros) (l.3804). Bothan Assault Cruisers (l.3812) and Commenorians hit the Second Fleet everywhere (l.3836). Limpan orders Centerpoint Station attacked to deny it to Corellia (l.3816).
- FEAT (Corran): leaps up into his cockpit (l.3834). "Ganner" Squadron (for Ganner Rhysode): Wedge 1, Corran 2, Luke 3, Mara 4 (l.3844, 3868–3876). Luke and Mara fly StealthXs (l.3854).
- Jacen's shuttle joins the *Anakin Solo* (Commander Twizzl) (l.3880–3890). Jacen orders the long-range lasers onto Centerpoint instead of the gunships attacking GA ships; Twizzl objects (l.3892–3898).
- Alema vs Lando in a turbolift: she Force-pulls his blaster and cane (l.3914–3938).
- FEAT (Lando): his cane's "Zap-zap" arcs lock her muscles. It was rated for a Wookiee and doesn't knock her out (l.3942–3950).
- FEAT (Alema): fights through the shock and then wipes Lando's memory of the encounter, a "greased worm" in his brain (l.3950, 3956–3968).
- FEAT (Luke and Mara): drift dark in StealthXs, talk only through their Force-bond and wreck the Nebulon-B frigate's stern thrusters with one proton torpedo each (l.3974–3990).
- FEAT (Wedge and Corran): two X-wings against twelve 1-7 Howlrunners down one, then break off the escort (l.3978, 3992–3996). Wedge returns to the Venture: "I wouldn't know whose side to be on" (l.4000). Corran goes with Luke (l.4008).
- *Dodonna* (a Galactic-class battle carrier) is badly damaged (l.4020, 4050). Colonel Fiav Fenn warns against one rally point (triangulation) (l.4040). Centerpoint gets latitudinal and longitudinal trenches (l.4060). The GA retreats to Point Bleak (l.4062–4064).
- Aftermath: Gejjen praises Delpin for doing "what Admiral Antilles could not" (l.4068). The GA extends war to Bothawui and Commenor: the "Corellian Confederation" (l.4072). The *Anakin Solo* is recalled to Coruscant (l.4076).

Ben missing, and the Lumiya/daughter link may explain Lobi's murder (l.4188–4192). Cilghal questions whether Luke and Mara can be dispassionate (l.4198).

- LORE: Kiara is notably Force-strong (l.4366). Faskus is a courier from Almania. The Bothan Dyur gave him the Amulet to leave in a Ziost cave, and a TIE fighter strafed his YT-2400 *Blacktooth* (l.4400–4410). "Guardsman Skywalker will take care of you" (l.4384). Faskus dies (l.4426).
- ANTI-FEAT/dark (Ben): takes all the supplies and leaves Kiara with her father's body: "Our mission is more important than her life" (l.4434–4462). He kicks Shaker over when the droid asks for his mission (l.4464–4470) and thinks of crushing it (l.4474).
- His parents' prerecorded 14th-birthday message plays: "For my fourteenth birthday, I killed a little girl." He cries (l.4484–4502), goes back, and helps bury Faskus under rocks (l.4504–4516). Typos: "flew to its hand" (l.4334), "It's said" for "sad" (l.4432).
