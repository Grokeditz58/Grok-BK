# Legacy of the Force 6: Inferno: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-04 CST. Full hand-read, l.1–8485. Citation "IN ch. N (l.x)". Detail is in in-01 to in-06.
- Author: Troy Denning (About the Author, l.5525–5527; the Round-Robin Interview lists Denning for Tempest, Inferno and Invincible, l.5372). This matches the inventory.

## Plot
- **Prologue and Jacen's cover (Prologue, ch. 1–3):**
  - A week after killing Mara, Caedus flow-walks Tahiri to Anakin's death on Myrkr to bind her to him (l.168–286).
  - Tenel Ka gives him the Hapan Home Fleet on the condition that he makes peace with Luke (l.502–518).
  - At Mara's funeral he keeps his presence hidden; Mara's body returns to the Force when he arrives (l.754–816).
  - Leia cuts through a GAG arrest squad inside the Temple (l.586–638).
- **Ossus hostage crisis (ch. 4, 14):**
  - Major Salle Serpa's GAG battalion holds the Jedi academy (l.1268–1296). Jaina and Jag surrender to protect the students, while Zekk stays hidden (l.1402–1418).
  - After Ben's dream-warning (l.3208–3214), Jaina breaks out, kills two sniper teams and leads a student revolt. Zekk severs Serpa's arm (l.3234–3454).
  - Serpa mutilates Tionne, and children die in the fighting (l.3310–3436).
- **Alema's thread (ch. 5, 16, 20):**
  - She takes Ship from Ossus with the "thought veer" (l.1480–1496), loots Lumiya's asteroid and finds a navigation string (l.1532–1610).
  - On Korriban she finds a cloister of 30+ "One Sith" under White Eyes (l.3900–4094).
  - Lore revealed there: Vergere was Sith, and Lumiya and Vergere planned Jacen (l.4016–4062). Lomi Plo was one of them (l.4038–4044).
  - She returns with Ship and the Holocron of Darth Vectivus (l.5174–5194).
- **Balmorra and Kuat (ch. 6, 8, 10–11):**
  - Jacen's battle meditation falters (l.2014, 2050), and he gives up Balmorra (l.2168–2184).
  - Luke appears in his day cabin without SD-XX seeing him (l.2130–2198).
  - Over Kuat, Luke pins Jacen in his chair, crushes its pedestal and pulls out the Jedi StealthXs (l.2610–2686, 2804–2826).
- **Omas (ch. 6, 9):**
  - Jacen sends Ben to kill Omas, using a doctored recording (l.1738–1754).
  - Ben kills Omas's Guardian droid. When Ben is convinced Jacen is the killer, Omas throws himself onto Ben's blade so Ben can get close to Jacen (l.2220–2386).
- **Kashyyyk (ch. 7, 12, 15–17):**
  - The Solos are jailed by the Wookiees. Leia wins over the Rock Council by force and argument (l.2886–3012). Luke brings the Masters, and the Council votes for "active opposition" (l.3044–3048).
  - Jacen orders the forest burned: "Worlds will tremble" (l.3548–3618).
  - Ben's surprise strike misses Jacen but kills Commander Twizzl, and Jacen drops Ben with lightning (l.3662–3670).
  - In the Jedi strike on the Fifth Fleet, Jacen ambushes in a StealthX (l.4316–4338). Luke apparently dies in Jaina's crossfire (l.4346–4354).
- **Hapes (ch. 18):** Leia fights Tahiri (l.4476–4504). Tenel Ka gives the Solos sanctuary and sends Tahiri back as a POW (l.4542–4574). She turns away from Jacen (l.4620–4634).
- **Climax (ch. 19–20, Epilogue):**
  - Ben is in the Embrace of Pain, cut off from the Force (l.4638–4756).
  - Luke, alive and hidden aboard (l.4758–4760), beats Jacen hand to hand and saber to saber (l.4820–4904). Ben, still bound, stabs Jacen in the back with the vibrodagger (l.4906–4912).
  - Luke stops Ben from killing Jacen, and his sabotage destroys the four long-range batteries (l.4916–4954).
  - Jacen, gravely hurt, uses battle meditation, with Alema's Ship, against the Bothans while the Hapans fire on him (l.5170–5274).
  - At the Dragon Queen parley the Jedi side with the Confederation in all but name (l.5276–5368).

## Top feats
1. **Luke beats Caedus in the Embrace chamber (IN ch. 19–20):**
   - He hides aboard the *Anakin Solo* from everyone but Ben: "Not even Jacen had that kind of control" (l.4758–4760). Jacen searches and feels "no Jedi presence at all" (l.4800–4802).
   - In close combat he stabs Jacen's kidney, burns him to the bone, shatters a bone in his face and breaks a toxic garrote (l.4820–4904). He also sabotages all four long-range batteries (l.4930–4934).
   - Jacen concedes: "Luke had beaten him … inflicted more damage on Caedus than he had suffered himself, and he had even escaped the garrote before Ben struck" (l.4968).
   - Earlier, over Kuat, he Force-hurls Jacen 5 m into his chair and pins him with "no apparent exertion," then crushes the chair's pedestal with three finger-taps (l.2610–2620, 2682–2686).
   - In a StealthX he dodges turbolaser strikes "half a second before they blossomed." Jaina: "he hadn't revealed half of what he could do. Maybe not even a quarter" (l.4258).
2. **Jacen/Caedus at war:**
   - Danger sense lets him dodge Ben's surprise strike; he answers with Force lightning (l.3662–3670).
   - He cuts Ben off from the Force entirely and restores it with a touch (l.4740–4756).
   - In a StealthX ambush he "seemed to anticipate every shot" (l.4316–4332).
   - Injured, with a perforated lung, brain swelling and two back wounds, he feeds on the pain and refuses painkillers (l.4958, 4986, 5012–5018). He then runs battle meditation over the whole theater and Force-presses Admiral Atoko (l.5228–5274).
   - Also the burning of Kashyyyk (l.3548–3618, an ATROCITY).
3. **Ben:**
   - Kills a laminanium Guardian droid (l.2220–2226) and Force-throws stun grenades back (l.2394).
   - Sends Jaina a dream-warning across space (l.3208–3214) and hides his intent from Jacen (l.3120).
   - His surprise strike grazes Jacen and kills Twizzl (l.3662–3664).
   - Bound in the Embrace of Pain, he summons the vibrodagger and drives it between Jacen's shoulder blades (l.4860–4866, 4906–4912).

## Other feats
- **Jaina:**
  - Drugged and asleep, Force-slams a trooper. Force-guides a thrown spoon into an officer's throat (l.3234–3262).
  - Kills two sniper teams, crossing a roof in two Force bounds (l.3366–3382). Force-guided longblaster fire (l.3422–3424).
  - Earlier she bats stun bolts back and Force-hurls an attacker (l.1248–1254).
- **Zekk:** severs Serpa's arm, striking so fast "even Jaina did not see the attack" (l.3448–3454).
- **Leia:**
  - Fights the GAG squad in the Temple: Force-hurls, cuts off arms, Force-guides a grenade (l.586–638).
  - Strips Tarfang of his blaster with the Force and lifts him upside down (l.1904–1906).
  - Rock Council: handspring kick, voice projection, Force-lift (l.2886–2926). Kicks Tarfang off the rock (l.2998–3000).
  - Duels Tahiri "with a speed and ferocity that only a Barabel-trained fighter could achieve" (l.4476–4504).
- **Alema:**
  - Overpowers Ship's will with the "thought veer" (l.1480–1496).
  - Pulls a drum out of a collapsing stack and defuses a baradium bomb with 3 seconds left (l.1532–1548).
  - Crushes a jaw with a Force squeeze (l.3838).
- **White Eyes (Korriban Sith leader):** Force-chokes Alema silent with a raised hand and flings Morto with a glance (l.4072, 4090). Morto pins Alema with strength "so obvious and great that Alema didn't even try to fight" (l.3908–3910).
- **Saba:** projects her eulogy voice through the Force (l.744).
- **Jacen (other):**
  - Foresees a Commenorian flight's destruction and the *Peacebringer*'s loss, and moves his flag just in time (l.1984–2024).
  - Reflex Force-flip 3 m with saber lit (l.2076–2078). Camouflages himself as a "hole in the Force" (l.290–320).
  - Flow-walks Tahiri into the past (l.168–286).

## Anti-feats and atrocities
- **Jacen:**
  - His battle meditation reflects back only confusion, and he fails to anticipate the Commenorian retreat (l.2014, 2050).
  - Force pressure merely irritates Darklighter (l.2044–2046).
  - He can't turn his chair after Luke's pin (l.2784–2788). Without the Jedi he can't press the attack at Kuat (l.2820–2826).
  - Beaten by Luke (l.4968). Without Luke's intervention, Ben's stab would have been fatal (l.4906–4924).
  - Admits: "Even I'm not good enough to project emotions into other Force-users" (l.4766).
- **Atrocities:**
  - Jacen: frames Omas with a doctored recording (l.1742). Burns Kashyyyk (l.3548–3618). Sacrifices about 2,000 crew to lure the Confederation away from Kuat (l.4962). Ordered the murder of thousands of Coruscanti Bothans (l.5354).
  - Serpa: executes his own trooper (l.1294–1296) and mutilates Tionne (l.3310–3394).
  - Alema: "reconfigured" the Roqoo crew (l.1500–1502). Gives Morto flesh-eating bacteria (l.3942–3948).
- **Others:**
  - Night Blades: three lost in the opening salvo, maybe a quarter of the wing (l.4248–4276).
  - Jaina's fire appears to kill Luke (l.4346–4354); he survives (l.5292).
  - Tarfang chokes Leia and slams her head on the stone (l.3008–3010).
  - Luke's earlier Force-trip of the redesigned droid fails (Ben, l.2206–2210).

## Lore
- **Flow-walking:**
  - Flow-walkers are ghosts: shrapnel and flame pass through them, and they stay anchored to their own time (l.192–234).
  - They blur visually and in the Force (l.242). The Force corrects changes (l.180).
  - Jacen can flow-walk only a short way forward, to Kuat (l.1946).
- **Ship:** an ancient meditation sphere used by Jedi and Sith (l.1438). It can take "Control, not command" of fleets that have no spheres of their own (l.5190–5194).
- **Lumiya and the One Sith:**
  - Lumiya's asteroid holds traps, Kaiburr crystals and a Sith's navigation string, "Once" (l.1554–1604).
  - The One Sith on Korriban: thirty-plus lightsabers, White Eyes, "We are only one Sith now" (l.3950–3958).
  - Vergere was Sith, and Lumiya and Vergere's plan created Jacen (l.4016–4062). Lomi Plo was a member (l.4038–4044).
  - The Holocron of Darth Vectivus, a mining middle manager (l.5174–5178).
- **Jacen's Sith creed:**
  - "he had not sacrificed the ability to love—only the ability to be loved in return" (l.2110). Lumiya's battle meditation "was Sith" (l.2106–2108).
  - The Embrace of Pain feeds its pain back to Jacen (l.4672).
- **Korriban:** the miasma conceals; Luke banned Korriban navigation data (l.3786–3794).
- **Kashyyyk:**
  - KashyCorp Owool Interceptors "ride the stripe" in turbolaser static, an "old Jedi trick" (l.3638–3644).
  - Jacen has understood Shyriiwook since age 5 (l.3526).
- **Politics:**
  - Hapes provides nearly a fifth of Alliance combat power (l.4550).
  - Luke: "There is no Alliance. It died with Cal Omas" (l.2712).
- **Round-Robin Interview:**
  - Mara's death was planned at the late-2004 Big Rock Ranch meeting. Traviss suggested Jacen kill someone he loved (l.5378–5386).
  - Denning seeded Jacen's turn in Dark Nest (l.5426).
