## Ch. 5 (l.1420–1611)
- Alema in the academy library: Force suggestion on two GAG guards (l.1422). She senses a projected battle-meld feeling (l.1424). Overhears "hostages" (l.1428–1432). Believes Jacen overreached after Lumiya's death (l.1436).
- LORE: Jedi histories call Ship "an ancient meditation sphere ... used by Jedi and Sith alike," a Force-augmented control vessel that amplifies battle meditation and conceals the commander's location (l.1438). Ship: four wings, veined amber hull that can be opaque or transparent, "Ancient" (l.1468–1470).
- FEAT (Alema): mind-tricks the guards with their own power cells; her "slippery Force presence" robs memory (l.1446–1466). FEAT/LORE: "thought veer" Force technique from the Dark Nest; overpowers Ship's resolve, "forsaking subtlety for sheer power" (l.1480–1496). ATROCITY (Alema): "reconfigured" the Roqoo Depot freighter crew so the Jedi would suspect her rather than Jacen (l.1500–1502). "The Sith will rule again" (l.1506).
- Lumiya's asteroid, Kanz sector (l.1476, 1510–1516). FEAT (Alema): Force-pulls the middle coolant drum out of a collapsing stack, slashes it open, and disarms a baradium bomb with a proton-grenade detonator ("enough ... to shatter the asteroid") at 3 seconds by pulling the right orange wire (l.1532–1548). Lumiya's traps: flechette spray, reversed air lock, poison shower, a Force illusion of Lumiya that transfers damage back to the attacker (l.1554).
- Lumiya's storeroom: cybernetic parts, protein drinks, lightwhip strands, sharpened Kaiburr Crystals, over a million credits (l.1580–1600). A hidden datachip in a cybernetic arm: a hooded male Sith apologizes for "the brevity of your journey" and offers a navigation string — "Once" (l.1590–1604). LORE: Lumiya had hinted there were more than two Sith (l.1606). "What she needed were the Sith themselves" (l.1610).
