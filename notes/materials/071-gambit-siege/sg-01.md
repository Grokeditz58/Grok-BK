## Ch. ONE (l.167): the dying groundcar
- Anakin: "Good thing I'm the Chosen One or we might be in trouble." (l.179) He flies dark, "trusting instead to his instincts and the Force" (l.185–187).
- "Even tired to the bone, Obi-Wan used the Force the way a surgeon used a laser scalpel" (l.257).
- **Joint telekinetic landing:** Obi-Wan (POV; Anakin is flying) "wrapped the Force around them, swaddling the groundcar as once he'd swaddled Bail's starship coming in powerless to a space station dock" (l.314). This is an explicit callback to the 068 starship-catch feat. After the power cell dies: "In perfect, familiar unison they flung themselves into an active trance, imposing the Force upon the unresponsive groundcar and flattening their lethally steep trajectory." (l.340)
- They move the wreck by Force: "The task proved brutally hard… even Anakin's intimidating powers weren't limitless." (l.494–498). They then dismember it with lightsabers and scatter it by Force (l.514).
- Lore on the cost of overuse: "The Force was never meant to be used the way they'd been using it these past few days. These past months. Ever since the war began, in fact." (l.542)

## Ch. TWO (l.770): Dooku
- Dooku's view of the dark side: "Within the true Force, the Force of power and majesty. The Jedi called it the dark side, like frightened children…" (l.837); "They are merely blinded by the power. Too weak to wield it" (l.842).
- **Dooku's foresight limits:** "the Force's eddies were not always reliable. Not even his vast skill and experience could guarantee a clear answer. It was the price he and Sidious paid for stirring the galaxy to war." (l.867–869)
- Dooku reads Durd across the galaxy by hologram ("reading anyone via hologram was a distinct challenge in itself", l.911) and senses a "stench of fear… through the dark side" (l.941). He threatens: "you will feel my fingers closing hard upon the back of your neck." (l.941)
- Durd's droid KD-77 is built so no one else can command it (l.1004–1010).
- **Full line-by-line read additions (2026-11-03):**
  - l.791 "The bridge officer's fear warmed him." l.812-813 Sidious on the Separatists: "when they are no longer useful I shall see them cut down."
  - l.822-823 Dooku reads the starfield "with careless ease". l.827-833 "a weakened Yoda was a very good thing."
  - l.893-896 Dooku pushes "hard through the roiling Force" to Lanteeb.
  - **KEY: Dooku is deceived by a Neimoidian.** l.959 he senses fear but "truth mingled", because Durd believes his own claim; l.977-987 Durd afterwards: "I lied to Count Dooku... And … I think he believed me." Rumor says Dooku "could kill with a look, or the snap of his fingers".
  - l.1033-1036 KD-77 fakes hostage holoimagery. **l.1102-1104 Durd: "Droids only. Jedi can't sense droids. You must exploit their weaknesses. They don't have many"** (cf. l.12918).
