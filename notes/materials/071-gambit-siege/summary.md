# Clone Wars Gambit: Siege: summary of strongest findings

1. **Anakin holds back a theta storm alone**, ch. EIGHT–NINE. "used the Force to hold back the stream of theta particles, and with a shout of rage became one with the storm shield" (l.4749–4766). He holds it for "Probably less than an hour… Even the Chosen One had limits." (l.5107–5109). Obi-Wan thought "surely not even the Chosen One could hold back a theta storm on his own" (l.4805). Afterward: "He'd never exhausted himself like this before." (l.5185–5188)
2. **Explicit presence and power rankings.**
   - Ahsoka on Mace: "His presence in the Force was breathtaking. Standing beside him was like being buffeted by a gale—and he wasn't even trying" (l.9710–9713). Mace makes her more nervous than Yoda.
   - Anakin calls Obi-Wan "one of the greatest ever born" (l.2511–2512).
   - Taria is "a brilliant shadow. One of the best the Temple had ever known" (l.10268–10270).
   - Sidious is "the greatest Sith Lord ever to live" (l.6071).
   - Obi-Wan on Anakin's storm feat: "I'm not certain that Yoda himself could've held this wretched storm back the way you did, for as long as you did" (l.5365–5367).
   - Taria: "one of the best shadows in the Temple"; Yoda: "One of the Temple's best shadows you were. No longer is that true." (l.3492–3498)
3. **Sith foresight lore.** Sidious: the dark side's visions "came to pass without exception" (l.8540–8542), and he caused Yoda's blindness: "he had nurtured the dark side here until the light side's overthrow was almost complete" (l.3566–3568). By contrast, Dooku: "Not even his vast skill and experience could guarantee a clear answer" (l.867–869).
4. **Back-matter *Shatterpoint* excerpt (Vaapad and shatterpoints).**
   - Yoda calls Vaapad "Deadliest of all"; "One student alone to mastery has risen" (l.15067), and Mace says "I created it" (l.15077).
   - Shatterpoint perception: "I can see where you break" (l.14377–14389).
   - Jango was "the deadliest man in the galaxy" (l.14325–14328).
5. **Force-perception limits (full-read additions).**
   - Dooku is deceived by Durd, who believes his own lie (l.959, l.977–987).
   - Durd: "Jedi can't sense droids" (l.1102–1104); in the relief, "The droids she couldn't feel" (l.12918).
   - Sidious can't locate Anakin or Obi-Wan in the dark side: "Merely a shadow on the far edge of sight" (l.3576–3578).
   - Mace and Yoda both admit trouble sensing anything (l.3430–3433).
   - Ahsoka: "Mind-touch over this kind of distance was practically unheard of" (l.9734).
6. **Anakin, Padmé and Palpatine threads (full-read additions).**
   - Obi-Wan: "Anakin, I was jealous." (l.9540). Anakin: "when the Force turns scarlet and bursts through me like hot blood" (l.9563–9565).
   - Sidious: "Show me the son of my heart" (l.8544–8549); "he must be afforded every chance to escape" (l.6472–6474).
   - Obi-Wan and Taria were lovers: "I love her. But I was never in love" (l.11819–11821). Obi-Wan asks "if you crossed that line with Padmé…" (l.11828–11829) and later says "The less time she and Anakin spent together, the better" (l.13199–13202).
   - Taria: "keep your face turned to the light" (l.12263–12269); "be there for him, when I die" (l.12495); "two halves of a whole" (l.12559–12562).
   - Yoda is uneasy about Palpatine (l.3235–3266) and withholds plans from him (l.10243–10247, l.12350–12352). The Council and Palpatine are split by a "wedge" (l.9034–9040).
   - Palpatine: the civilian fleet "sets a precedent that might in the future represent a threat to the security of the Republic" (l.13153–13158).
7. **Healing lore.** Obi-Wan has "no healing crystal" (l.5534–5535), and "Two experienced healers on the war's front line had crippled themselves" doing this (l.5638–5643). Greti "could have been a Jedi—possibly a great one" (l.6868–6872).
