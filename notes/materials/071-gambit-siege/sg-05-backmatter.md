## Era intros and excerpts
- Old Republic (l.13810): *Fatal Alliance* ch. 1 (Shigar Konshi; same as 068/070), l.13879ff.
- **Rise of the Empire (l.14270): *Shatterpoint* (Matthew Stover) opening, "FROM THE PRIVATE JOURNALS OF MACE WINDU" (l.14310ff.). This is significant power-scaling material:**
  - On Jango: "the deadliest man in the galaxy. Jango can kill me in less than a second… in the Force, a pulsar of death." (l.14325–14328); "The Force hinted a shatterpoint, and I left a headless corpse on the sand." (l.14391)
  - Shatterpoint ability: "I can see shatterpoints." (l.14377); "when I look at you through the Force, I can see where you break." (l.14389); "Dooku was the shatterpoint." (l.14359)
  - Mace on the Geonosis losses: "there has not been such a slaughter of Jedi since the Sith War, four thousand years ago." (l.14441–14442)
  - Yoda: "Clouded by darkness are my eyes; the Force shows me only suffering, and destruction" (l.14622). Mace earlier saw "Palpatine was himself a shatterpoint" (l.14610).
  - **Vaapad lore:** Yoda: "the seventh form of lightsaber combat… A powerful form it is. Deadliest of all … But dangerous it is—to its master, as well as its opponent. Few have studied. One student alone to mastery has risen." (l.15062–15067). Mace: "I didn't study Vaapad. I created it." (l.15077). On Sora Bulq: "he did not master Vaapad… Vaapad mastered him." (l.15087). Depa Billaba "is a master of Vaapad" (l.15053), and Yoda calls her a "Powerful Jedi. Brilliant warrior" (l.14547).
