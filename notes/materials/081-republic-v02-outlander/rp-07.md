## Feats / scaling
- **Sharad Hett (as the Outlander):** a renegade Jedi leading **13 Tusken clans** at once, which is "NEVER BEEN HEARD OF BEFORE" (p.10). The Anchorhead raid kills six militiamen and burns the armory (p.9). He stops a killing by cutting the attacker's weapon arm (p.17).
- **Sharad Hett reputation:** titles Howlrunner, Champion of Krmar, Defender of Kamparus, Hound of Worlds, Nemesis of the Kimm. Ki calls him "THE MOST FEARLESS AND DEDICATED KNIGHT OF THIS AGE"; any Jedi under 30 knows his weapon (p.21–22). He has been missing 15 years and was Eeth Koth's Padawan (p.23).
- **Yoda's scaling statement on Ki:** "OF ALL LIVING JEDI, HAVE *YOU ONLY* THE SKILLS TO DEFEAT SHARAD HETT!" (p.24). Strong hype, but its context is *who is free to go*: Ki is the only Council member without personal ties to Hett (p.24). Yoda wasn't ranking Ki above himself.
- **Aurra Sing:** senses Jedi arriving ("ANOTHER WILL BE COMING SOON... CALL IT A GIFT", "FORCE SENSITIVE", p.26) and keeps a case of about six or seven trophy lightsaber hilts (p.26).
- **Tech:** Czerka and Merr-Sonn weapon crates, Jawa "slug-throwers", a Tusken scoped rifle, Aurra's skull-nosed swoop, and Republic surveyor holos.

## Lore
- "Koraght gaghgt takt" means "Blessed be your going out from us". It is a Tusken saying, spoken by Sidrona Diath before the Council about 4,000 years earlier. Diath was born on Tatooine, "A FAILED MINING COLONY" (p.20).
- "Ootman" is Hutt/Jawa for "outlander" (p.10).
- Timeline: this is after *The Phantom Menace*. Qui-Gon has been killed by a Sith lord (p.23).
- Ki was Yoda's Padawan (p.21), and Jabba holds Ki's grudge from vol. 1 (p.25).
