# Outlander TPB — back matter (p.135–143)

- Pages read: **p.135–143**, hand-read 2026-11-03 CST, in order, one image at a time.

## Cover gallery (p.135–140)
- **p.135:** Header "COVER GALLERY", with the caption "Featuring the orininal [sic] comic-book series cover paintings by Ken Kelly." A Tusken war party with gaderffii storms past a sandcrawler over fallen red-robed Jawas. A woman in blue clutches a child, and a bantha stands behind them. Signed Kelly '99. This matches the #7 opening raid.
- **p.136:** Ki-Adi-Mundi in a skiff turret, deflecting blaster fire with a violet saber, against a horned gunman in a red scarf on a sand skiff, with a sandstorm wall behind.
- **p.137:** Ki raises a silver-white saber before a horned krayt dragon. A Tusken with a knife stands in the beast's maw, with another Tusken at the right. This matches the #9–10 krayt fight.
- **p.138:** A goggled, spiked Tusken (Hett or A'Sharad) raising a pinkish-violet saber.
- **p.139:** Ki (silver-white saber) among Tusken raiders, with a gaderffi-wielding rider on a bantha on a dune.
- **p.140:** Aurra Sing (red saber) leaps at Ki (violet saber) on rocky ground under a fiery sky.
- Six paintings for six issues. The gallery does not caption issue numbers; the order above is the file order.

## Covers and house ad (p.141–143)
- **p.141:** Back cover: a duotone (olive) circle crop of the p.140 Aurra-vs-Ki painting on a starfield and red band. There is no text block.
- **p.142:** House ad, "STAR WARS GRAPHIC NOVEL TIMELINE (IN YEARS)", listing eras from Old Republic to Legacy plus Vector. Entries include "Omnibus: Rise of the Sith—33 BSW4", "Episode I: The Phantom Menace—32 BSW4", "Omnibus: Emissaries and Assassins—32 BSW4" and "Legacy—130–137 ASW4". "BSW4 = before Episode IV: A New Hope. ASW4 = after Episode IV: A New Hope." It lists Knight Errant, The Old Republic and Vector, so this page comes from a later (c. 2010–11) printing, not the March 2001 first edition named in the p.4 indicia.
- **p.143:** Front cover in the later-printing trade dress: an olive duotone of the p.138 Tusken-with-saber painting in a circle, "STAR WARS / OUTLANDER", Dark Horse and Lucas Books logos, and the Rise of the Empire era glyph. It is **not** a duplicate of p.1 (different art, different file hash). p.1 is a colour Ken Kelly painting of Aurra and Hett.

## Page-order / file notes
- The scan mixes printings: p.1–4 front matter (first-edition indicia, March 2001) and p.142–143 later-printing back matter and cover. Placing the front cover at the end (p.143) is a scan-order quirk. Nothing is missing.
- Saber colour variance in Kelly's paintings: Ki is violet (p.136, 140) and silver-white (p.137, 139), and the Tusken's blade is pinkish-violet (p.138).
- No letters page, reader names or addresses in this TPB.
