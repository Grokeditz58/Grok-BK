## Feats / scaling
- **Aurra Sing beats Sharad Hett.** This is the arc's headline feat, but it comes with heavy caveats:
  - She first wounds him with a sniper shot from range (p.126).
  - He is fresh from leading a battle and carving through Gardulla's barge.
  - She then matches him blade to blade, Force-hurls a storm of boulders, and impales him (p.128–129).
  - Hett's own scaling quote: "YOU'RE GOOD WITH A LIGHTSABER. SUCH KNOWLEDGE IS NOT ACQUIRED EASILY." She is a former Jedi student of the Dark Woman, and Ki once showed her saber tricks (p.130).
  - She takes his lightsaber for her collection.
- **Aurra outplays the whole board:** she takes pay from both Gardulla and Jabba (p.125–126), and she senses and baits a "little lost Padawan" (p.121).
- **Aurra's limits:** A'Sharad blindsides and disarms her, then chokes her (p.131–132). She escapes only because Ki stops him and she lands a kick. She does not engage Ki.
- **Sharad Hett:** Force-grabs three airhook riders and slams them together (p.120). Leads the "Howlrunner formation" bantha cavalry, a tactic for Jedi Justice cruiser squadrons taught by Oppo Rancisis (p.121). Fights onto the barge and cuts down guards with a gaderffi, then with his saber (p.123–124). Rises after a sniper hit and duels Aurra (p.128).
- **Ki-Adi-Mundi:** reads Aurra's heart through the Force ("THE DARK SIDE IS THERE--COILED--WAITING--BUT IT HAS NOT FULLY CLAIMED YOU", p.131). Senses Aurra's approach ("ANOTHER ENEMY IS NEAR! I FEEL IT!", p.122).
- **A'Sharad:** wounded, he still disarms and throttles Aurra (p.131–132). He inherits Hett's saber and becomes Ki's Padawan (p.134).
- **Limit:** "NOT EVEN OUR FORCE POWERS COULD HAVE HALTED THEIR CHARGE!" (Hett, p.117), so three Force users could not stop a bantha stampede.

## Lore
- Aurra Sing is a former student of the Jedi Master called **the Dark Woman**, and Ki is "my teacher's friend" (p.130). She came to Tatooine for the Boonta (p.113).
- Arms plot: Dreddon of Nal Hutta, the failed Arkanian and Lannik deliveries, Jabba manufacturing the war, and Gardulla's counter-scheme (p.116).
- Hett built his saber "AS MY MASTER EETH KOTH WATCHED" (p.134). Oppo Rancisis taught him the Howlrunner formation (p.121).
- Ending: A'Sharad becomes Ki-Adi-Mundi's Padawan and leaves for Coruscant.
