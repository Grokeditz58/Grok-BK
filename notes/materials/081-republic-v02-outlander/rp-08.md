## Feats / scaling
- **Ki-Adi-Mundi:** fights a skiff full of Jabba's thugs **inside a sandstorm** while masked and goggled. He deflects blaster fire and cuts weapons apart, and senses the betrayal before it is sprung (p.44–47). In his own words he is "NOT YET A MASTER" but sits on the Council, and the Dark Woman calls him possibly her "BEST" potential student (p.28).
- **Aurra Sing:** a mind-compulsion feat. A Trandoshan is forced to beat himself with a pan while begging her to stop (p.32–33). The victim reads it as "JEDI MIND TRICKS", and she denies being a Jedi (p.34). She also senses Ki's arrival in advance ("I FEEL IT", p.35–36).
- **Dark Woman:** Force visions/dreams that predict the danger on Tatooine (p.30).
- **Tech:** a Corellian space cruiser used as a Jedi courier, a Jabba skiff, Aurra's swoop, macrobinoculars, and "TIBANNA GAS" used as cooking fuel (p.35).

## Lore
- The Dark Woman took Ki from his parents and was his "FIRST TEACHER", but Yoda trained him as a Padawan (p.28).
- The Council has 12 members. Ki is on the Council while still a Knight (p.28).
- Jabba's full name is spelled "DESILIJIC" on p.38 and "DESILIJIK" on p.41. This is a lettering inconsistency in the comic.
- A new Mos Espa podrace grandstand is mentioned (p.40).
