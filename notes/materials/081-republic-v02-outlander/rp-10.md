## Feats / scaling
- **A'Sharad Hett (teen Padawan):** draws first blood on an adult krayt dragon with his own self-built red lightsaber, then kills it single-handed with a gaderffi while riding its head (p.75–82). He then cuts the pearl out with his saber (p.83).
- **Sharad Hett:** reads the dragon's behaviour and predicts its lunge ("SHE'S ABOUT TO LUNGE FOR YOUR LEFT", p.71–72). He leads the clans as a "WAR LEADER-- NOT A KING" (p.86). He has trained A'Sharad from birth (p.84).
- **Ki-Adi-Mundi:** dodges the dragon in close quarters while still injured, and uses an open-palm Force gesture that turns the dragon aside (p.73, p.78). He senses nearby danger (p.86). He mostly plays defense here and is rescued.
- **Aurra Sing:** solo saber-kills a swarm of giant rats (p.89–90). She senses a hidden watcher "ALMOST" fully cloaked in the Force (p.88), and tracks by bantha hoof trim (p.88).
- **Anti-feat (Ki):** taken prisoner by the Tuskens, and he admits "I CAN PROMISE YOU NOTHING" (p.86–87).

## Lore
- Krayt dragons track **shadows** and "HAVE TROUBLE DISCERNING TWO-DIMENSIONAL IMAGES" (p.72).
- Tusken krayt hunt: first blood may be drawn by any helper, but the death stroke must be from the **gaderffi**. After the kill comes a rebirth invocation, and the **pearl** is taken from the belly (p.77, p.83). The saying "Whoever has two hands can hold a gaderffi" means a Tusken who loses a hand is left behind by choice (p.87).
- Tuskens consider showing flesh a "VULGARITY" (p.84).
- Hett: "OF COURSE I AM A JEDI. BUT I AM ALSO A TUSKEN!" He has been on Tatooine for "ALMOST 18 YEARS" (p.72). The Council said he disappeared 15 years ago (p.23). **This is a continuity discrepancy in the text.**
- Hett's camp is in **Gafsa Canyon** (p.87). Jabba is suspected of bringing in the cheap weapons through jump beacons (p.86).
