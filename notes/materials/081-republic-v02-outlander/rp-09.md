## Feats / scaling
- **Ki, saber vs. mooks:** offers mercy, then cuts down Hoonta and a group of armed thugs in a sandstorm melee (p.50–51).
- **Ki, Force healing:** speeds up the healing of a broken arm through meditation, but it is "NOT FIXED" (p.58–59).
- **Ki, telekinesis (best feat in the issue):** lifts a whole **buried skiff** out of a dune one-handed, while injured and having just healed himself (p.59). He also Force-pulls his saber out of the sand (p.57).
- **Ki, sensing (limit):** he can "BARELY DETECT" a furtive desert predator "EVEN WITH MY FORCE POWERS" (p.66), then senses the whole swarm clearly (p.67).
- **Aurra Sing:** survives being buried in the storm with her swoop (p.62). She tracks targets by feel ("I CAN FEEL HIM BREATHING", p.65) and with a portable life-sign scanner. Her self-image as a hunter: "NASHTAH" (p.64).
- **Anti-feats:** Ki is betrayed despite sensing it coming, breaks his arm when the skiff crashes, and loses his comlink and medpack (p.54–60).

## Lore
- Aurra's "TEACHER" called her "Nashtah", after a six-legged predator reptile from Dra III (p.64). This links to the Dark Woman's "that child" on p.28.
- The Needles are the centre of the Jundland Wastes, and no outlanders have entered Tusken country "SINCE THE TIME OF THE BANDIT ALKHARA" (p.60).
- Ki's cruiser is crewed by droids (p.59).
