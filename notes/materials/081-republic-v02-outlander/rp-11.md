## Feats / scaling
- **A'Sharad (15):** a mass Force push/hurl that scatters a crowd of adult Tusken warriors and sends one high into the air (p.97). Hett rates him as likely "EVEN STRONGER THAN ME" (p.98).
- **Ki-Adi-Mundi:** a throw/flip into the dung heap, then a kneeling one-handed Force push that scatters about five charging Tuskens (p.94–95). Hett calls it "CLEVER BUT INEFFECTUAL" (p.96). Ki judges Hett's and A'Sharad's hearts through the Force: "I FIND NO EVIL" (p.111).
- **Sharad Hett:** a legendary career ("Howlrunner", "Hound of Worlds", "nemesis of pirates and tamer of tyrants"). He is shown standing over a slain giant reptile beast (p.103). He survived months alone in the Dune Sea and rose to lead the Tuskens, with Force powers as his "edge" (p.104–105).
- **Aurra Sing:** owns a trophy case of about eight lightsaber hilts (p.92), and Hett's son calls her "Ootman"-level game. Her blind-ish dive-and-shoot head shot on a stalking Tusken comes right after she senses him (p.110). From long range she says she could kill all three Force users with three rifle shots (p.109). This is her own boast, not a feat.

## Lore
- Gafsa, the "Waking Place": the Tusken origin canyon (p.91). The Alkhara field report is quoted (p.93).
- "Ootman" = outlander, from Huttese (p.100). Hubba gourd is the Tusken staple (p.99). Tuskens adopt humanoids to keep their blood compatible (p.105). They help only the sick or wounded (p.99). They bury their dead in stone cairns at an ancient site (p.107).
- Ki has an **honor-wife, Mawin** (p.94). Cereans are not used to low humidity (p.94).
- **Hett's backstory:** taken from a homeworld that produced many Jedi. His master was **Eeth Koth**, an orphan from the Smugglers' Moon until age four. He was known as "Howlrunner" and "Hound of Worlds", and was tempted by power and influence (dark-side worry). His homeworld was razed and his family killed, so he flew his cruiser to the Outer Rim and crash-landed on Tatooine (p.101–104). On p.100 he says "I WAS EXILED", but the flashback shows him choosing to leave, effectively a self-exile.
- A'Sharad's mother was K'Sheek. A'Sharad is 15 (p.106). His father has never seen his face.
- This takes place after *The Phantom Menace*: Qui-Gon is dead, Obi-Wan killed the Sith, "always two" (p.111), and there is a hint about Anakin (p.101). Aurra came for the Boonta (p.113, #12).
- Politics: Jabba is accused of starting the war to sell weapons and make Tatooine a smuggling conduit for patrons on Nal Hutta. **Gardulla** is a rival Hutt who hires Aurra (p.108–110).
