# Republic Vol. 2 — Outlander: summary of findings

Sources: Star Wars (1998) #7–#12 (1999), collected in the *Outlander* TPB (Dark Horse, 2001). Hand-read page by page. Citations are "OL p.N" = TPB file page; see the rp-NN.md files for issue mapping (#7 p.5–26, #8 p.27–47, #9 p.48–69, #10 p.70–90, #11 p.91–112, #12 p.113–134).

## Arc in one paragraph
- **#7:** A goggled Tusken warleader with a lightsaber leads 13 clans in raids on Anchorhead and Mochot Steep, but spares a Corellian mother and her baby. Aurra Sing watches. On Coruscant, the Council identifies him as the lost Jedi hero **Sharad Hett**, the "Howlrunner" and Eeth Koth's former Padawan, and sends Ki-Adi-Mundi. Yoda says Ki alone can defeat Hett if he has fallen.
- **#8:** The Dark Woman warns Ki. Aurra toys with a Trandoshan using Force compulsion. Ki confronts Jabba, who sends him into a sandstorm with "his best scum".
- **#9:** Ki kills the traitors, but the skiff crashes and breaks his arm. He Force-heals, lifts the buried skiff and treks into the Jundland, hunted by Aurra ("Nashtah"), until he falls into a krayt dragon's lair. Hett appears.
- **#10:** Hett's son and Padawan **A'Sharad** draws first blood with his self-built red saber and kills the krayt with a gaderffi in his initiation. Ki is taken captive. Aurra massacres a rat swarm.
- **#11:** In the Tusken camp, A'Sharad Force-hurls a mob of warriors. Hett tells his origin: homeworld razed, crash on Tatooine, adopted by the Tuskens. He blames Jabba for the arms war. Gardulla, who has hired Aurra, marches in.
- **#12:** Hett's cavalry is bombed, but he wrecks Gardulla's airhooks and barge. Aurra double-deals Jabba and Gardulla, snipes Hett, then **beats him in a saber duel and kills him**, taking his saber. A'Sharad nearly kills her in rage, and Ki stops him. The dying Hett gives his saber to A'Sharad, who becomes **Ki-Adi-Mundi's Padawan**.

## Top feats
1. **Aurra Sing kills Sharad Hett** (OL p.126–130). Important context:
   - She sniped him first, and he had just fought a battle.
   - Then she matched him saber to saber and Force-hurled a storm of boulders at him.
   - Hett: "YOU'RE GOOD WITH A LIGHTSABER. SUCH KNOWLEDGE IS NOT ACQUIRED EASILY." She learned from the Dark Woman and from Ki's "tricks".
2. **Ki-Adi-Mundi lifts a buried skiff out of a dune** with telekinesis while carrying a freshly broken arm he had just Force-healed (p.58–59).
3. **A'Sharad (15) kills an adult krayt dragon** single-handed in his initiation (p.75–83), and later **Force-hurls a crowd of adult Tusken warriors**, one high into the air (p.97). Hett: "EVEN STRONGER THAN ME, I THINK" (p.98).
4. **Sharad Hett Force-grabs three airhook riders and slams them together** in midair, then carves through Gardulla's barge guards (p.120–124). He led 13 Tusken clans, "NEVER BEEN HEARD OF BEFORE" (p.10).
5. **Aurra's Force compulsion:** she makes a Trandoshan beat himself with a pan (p.32–33). She also saber-kills a giant rat swarm solo (p.89–90) and senses an "almost" fully cloaked watcher (p.88).
6. **Ki's sandstorm skiff fight:** goggled and masked, he cuts through Jabba's thugs and deflects blasters (p.44–51), and senses the betrayal before it is sprung.
7. **Ki's Force pushes:** a kneeling one-handed push scatters about 5 charging Tuskens (p.94–95). Hett calls it "CLEVER BUT INEFFECTUAL".
8. **Hett and Ki can read hearts.** Ki: "I FIND NO EVIL IN YOUR HEART" (p.111) and "THE DARK SIDE IS THERE--COILED--WAITING--BUT IT HAS NOT FULLY CLAIMED YOU!" (to Aurra, p.131).

## Scaling statements
- Yoda on Ki: "OF ALL LIVING JEDI, HAVE *YOU ONLY* THE SKILLS TO *DEFEAT* SHARAD HETT!" (p.24). Ki's reply is "YOU FLATTER ME"; Yoda's is "THE TRUTH ONLY, I SPEAK." Ki never fights Hett; Aurra kills Hett instead.
- Ki on Hett: "THE MOST FEARLESS AND DEDICATED KNIGHT OF THIS AGE" (p.22).
- The Dark Woman on Ki: possibly her "BEST" Padawan; on the Council while "STILL ONLY A KNIGHT" (p.28).

## Anti-feats / limits
- Ki is betrayed by Jabba despite sensing it, crashes, breaks his arm, and loses his comlink and medpack (p.52–60). He "BARELY" detects a furtive predator "EVEN WITH MY FORCE POWERS" (p.66). He is captured by the Tuskens (p.86–87). Against the krayt he is mostly defensive.
- Hett: "NOT EVEN OUR FORCE POWERS COULD HAVE HALTED THEIR CHARGE!" (p.117). He is hit by Aurra's sniper shot without sensing it (p.126).
- Aurra is blindsided, disarmed and choked by the wounded A'Sharad (p.131–132), and flees rather than face Ki.

## Bios
- **Sharad Hett:** Jedi Knight, Eeth Koth's Padawan, and legend ("Howlrunner", "Hound of Worlds", "Champion of Krmar", "Defender of Kamparus", "Nemesis of the Kimm"). He was drawn to power, and after his family was killed he crash-landed on Tatooine and became a Tusken war leader. He built his saber under Koth's eye and dies in #12.
- **A'Sharad Hett:** son of Sharad and the Tusken warrior K'Sheek; 15. Built his own red saber, is a krayt-slayer, and inherits Hett's saber. Becomes Ki's Padawan.
- **Aurra Sing:** bounty hunter and former student of the Dark Woman, who called her "Nashtah". She has Force sense and compulsion, carries a trophy case of saber hilts (about 6–8, growing), uses dual blasters, a sniper rifle and a red saber, and has a skull swoop. Hired by Gardulla, she sells Gardulla out to Jabba.
- **Ki-Adi-Mundi:** a Knight on the Council. The Dark Woman was his first teacher and Yoda trained him. Honor-wife Mawin. Jabba enslaved his daughter (backstory).
- **Gardulla the Hutt:** Jabba's rival. **Jabba:** manufactured the Tusken war to sell Dreddon's junk weapons. **Eeth Koth**, **Yoda**, **Oppo Rancisis** (taught the Howlrunner formation), **Sidrona Diath** (a Tatooine-born Jedi about 4,000 years earlier).

## Lore
- Tusken culture:
  - Gafsa, the "Waking Place" (p.91).
  - Gaderffi rites: "WHOEVER HAS TWO HANDS CAN HOLD A GADERFFII" (p.77, 87).
  - Krayt pearl and rebirth invocation (p.83); exposed flesh is a "vulgarity" (p.84); hubba gourd is the staple food (p.99).
  - They adopt humanoids (p.105) and bury their dead in cairns (p.107).
  - "Ootman" = outlander, from Huttese (p.10, 100). Invocation "KORAGHT GAGHGT TAKT" = "Blessed be your going out from us" (p.18–20).
- Krayt dragons track shadows (p.72).
- Timeline: after *The Phantom Menace*. Qui-Gon is dead, "the Sith have returned", "always two", the boy found on Tatooine (p.101, 111), the Boonta (p.113), and the new Mos Espa grandstand (p.40).

## Page order / file integrity
- 143 of 143 files viewed, with no missing pages and no duplicated story pages.
- Spreads: p.3, p.28, p.71. Reduced-art inset pages in #12 (see README).
- Mixed printings: first-edition indicia (p.4), later-printing ad and cover (p.141–143). The cover at p.143 is out of order.
- Text and colour inconsistencies: Hett's years (15 vs almost 18), "exiled" vs his own choice, Hett's blade yellow (p.22 montage, flashback) vs red (present), Ki's blade violet vs silver-white on the Kelly covers, DESILIJIC/DESILIJIK.
