Page-order notes:
- No missing pages and no duplicated story pages. Everything runs in order.
- Two-page spreads are saved as single 2048×1536 files: p.3 (title), p.28 (#8) and p.71 (#10). All other files are 1024×1536.
- On p.113–115, 117, 118, 120–122, 124 and 128 (#12) the art is printed reduced inside wide white margins. The files are full size and nothing is cut off.
- The scan mixes printings. The p.4 indicia is the March 2001 first edition, but p.142's timeline ad (it lists Knight Errant, The Old Republic and Vector) and the p.141/p.143 circle-frame trade dress are from a later reprint. p.143 is a front cover placed at the end. It is not a duplicate of p.1 (different art, different hash).
- Text inconsistencies, kept as printed: Hett missing "15 years" (p.23, A'Sharad born "fifteen cycles ago" p.106) vs "almost 18 years" on Tatooine (p.72); "exiled" (p.100) vs the flashback showing him choosing to leave; DESILIJIC/DESILIJIK (p.38/41); the "orininal" typo (p.135). Hett's saber is yellow in the p.22 montage and the flashback, but red in the present-day story.

Only notes and short quotes are committed. No images or comic files. This TPB has no letters pages, so no reader names or addresses appear.
