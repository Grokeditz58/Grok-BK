# Legacy of the Jedi: notes part 1 (Part One, ch. 1–6: Dooku and Lorian Nod as Padawans)

Source: owner's MEGA epub (handle oH802TYJ), Jude Watson, Scholastic 2003 (fb2-converted epub, ISBN 0-439-53666-9). Line refs are to the extracted text outside the repo (`legacy.txt`), cited as ch. + approx. line.

## Setting
- Dooku and Lorian Nod are both 13. Dooku has just been chosen by Thame Cerulian (Jedi historian, Sith-watcher) (ch. 1, l.58).
- Thame believes "there will come a time when the Jedi will have to fight the Sith again" (ch. 1, l.94). This is foreshadowing of Dooku's later path.

## Dooku's baseline power (age 13)
- Lorian: "You have a better Force connection than any Padawan. Everyone knows that." (ch. 1, l.106). This is a peer's claim.
- Narration: "Dooku had always been ahead of Lorian in battle skills and Force connection" (ch. 1, l.148).
- Lorian again: "They know you have an extraordinary Force connection." (ch. 3, l.326). Yoda "has taken a persona[l] interest in you".
- Yoda (ch. 6, l.800): "Great are your gifts, Dooku." Flaw: pride.

## Sith Holocron effects (lore)
- Thame's manual: "Some suffer from nausea or dizziness at first viewing… Nightmares and dark visions can result, lasting years…" (ch. 1, l.84).
- Lorian, after merely carrying it: "Do you feel it? I feel so sick" and his hands are shaking (ch. 2, l.218–226). Dooku "could feel the dark power of the Holocron… Just knowing it was behind him in a dark corner was enough to make him feel shaky" (ch. 2, l.228).
- Oppo Rancisis walks in. Dooku "felt the atmosphere in the room change, as though gravity had increased" and Rancisis says "I sense a tremor in the Force" (ch. 2, l.250–252). He pockets the Holocron casually. The Holocron has a passive dark aura that a Council master shrugs off.

## Urban tracking exercise (ch. 4–5): feats
- Dooku's read on the group's Force: "Dooku felt the combined Force from the group, energetic, unfocused, but strong." (ch. 4, l.376).
- Training lightsabers: these are lower-power, but three Padawans cut a turbolift ceiling door: "A training lightsaber did not have the power of a true lightsaber, but it could most likely get through the metal door… Within minutes they had peeled back the metal" (ch. 5, l.554–558).
- Dooku's chain of tags: he leaps a display, dives, rolls, and takes out several Gold members in a row. "Dooku accessed the Force and leaped. Usually his control wasn't the best for this maneuver — he still had much to learn — but he surprised himself with perfect execution." (ch. 5, l.590).
- Force-assisted perception in a crowd: "Some movement so tiny that his senses would pick it up in a sea of information that he couldn't process. But the Force could. / The Force surged. Everything fell away, and he saw Lorian." (ch. 5, l.600–602).
- He garbage-bounces over Lorian's head: "the momentum sent him skyward. He flew over Lorian's head and landed in front of him" (ch. 5, l.610).
- Lorian is no slouch: "his reflexes were excellent, a source of envy among the other students" (ch. 5, l.612). He runs up a wall and flips over Dooku, "a basic Padawan exercise" (ch. 5, l.640).
- Dooku is exhausted and his Force access fades under anger: "He reached for the Force. It was elusive. Instead of flowing through him, he could barely feel it trickle." (ch. 5, l.634). This is an early instance of anger degrading Jedi Force access (contrast with the later Sith use of anger).
- The deliberate wounding blow: "It had been a true blow, designed to hurt." (ch. 5, l.668). It leaves a deep bruise; a training saber "is not meant to wound, yet wound Lorian you did" (ch. 6, l.706).
- Yoda appears unnoticed on the roof: "They hadn't noticed him." (ch. 5, l.676).

## Outcome and seeds of the fall
- Lorian is expelled "not for stealing the Sith Holocron, but for lying and implicating his friend"; Dooku gets a reprimand "for excessive aggression" (ch. 6, l.740).
- Lorian's prophecy: "You will never, never be a great Jedi Master." (ch. 6, l.782). Dooku's lesson: "he was alone" (ch. 6, l.790).
- Blindfold seeker drill: Dooku senses Yoda by "the way the Force gathered in the room", and "knowing he could slice the seeker in two whenever he wanted" (ch. 6, l.792). When Yoda leaves, "The Force drained from the room." (l.804). Yoda's presence is felt as a concentration of the Force.
- Yoda: "Between sureness and pride, a small step it is… Pride it is that blinds you." (ch. 6, l.796–800).
