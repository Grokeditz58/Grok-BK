# Legacy of the Jedi: summary of strongest findings

Source: Jude Watson, *Star Wars: Legacy of the Jedi* (Scholastic 2003; ISBN 0-439-53666-9). This copy is an FB2-converted epub, not a Del Rey ebook. Hand-read in full on 2026-11-03: the annotation and all 27 chapters across four time-jump parts. Setting: four generations (Dooku/Lorian Nod as Padawans → Dooku and Qui-Gon → Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan → Obi-Wan and Anakin about 6 months after Geonosis). Citations are "LJ ch. N".

1. **Obi-Wan ranks Dooku above Maul in felt power.**
   > "He had never met such power in battle before. He had never come up against something that had completely overpowered him. Even meeting the Sith Lord who had killed Qui-Gon had not been the same." (LJ ch. 20)
   - Lorian, an ex-Padawan: "He knew Dooku was vastly more powerful." / "Why use any weapon when Dooku could swat it away like a fly?" (LJ ch. 26)
2. **Dooku's dark-side TK and his killing of Lorian Nod (Clone Wars).**
   > "The dark side surged in a shocking display of power, and Lorian found himself flung like a child's doll in the air. He slammed against the wall" (LJ ch. 26)
   - Dooku senses Lorian's returning Force: "So you haven't lost it completely… Too bad it isn't enough." His missed cut "hit rock and sliced through it". The kill is a silent run-through: "Behind him was a whisper… he had been pierced through." (LJ ch. 26)
   - Lore: Dooku viewed the Sith Holocron as a Knight (LJ ch. 13: "The massive doors opened, and Dooku strode in"). He doesn't deny it in ch. 26: "Why shouldn't I have accessed it?"
3. **Young Master Dooku: precision TK and a dominant duel.**
   > "The vibroblade flew from Eero's unsteady hand and landed on the energy cuffs binding Dooku's wrists. The blade cut through the cuffs easily. With split-second timing, Dooku slipped out his hand" / "his lightsaber flew from the room next door into his palm." (LJ ch. 12)
   > "Anger no longer had the power to make him sloppy. It made him more precise." / "He fired the blaster. Dooku deflected the fire easily." / "He parried Lorian's attacks, barely moving." (LJ ch. 12)
   - His ambition, stated outright: "to surpass Yoda as the greatest Jedi ever" (LJ ch. 7). At 13: "Dooku had always been ahead of Lorian in battle skills and Force connection" (LJ ch. 1).
4. **Anakin and Obi-Wan's Force-amplified villa fight vs. a dozen-plus super battle droids.**
   > "He felt the Force in the room like a pulse, like a heartbeat, like a rolling wave." / Anakin sets Floria down "so lightly and gently in the midst of his soaring leap that not even a hair of Floria's coiled braids was disturbed." (LJ ch. 25)
   > "He caught the surging Force from Anakin and embraced it, doubling it, making it grow." (LJ ch. 25)
   - Anakin finds the hidden keystone by Force feel ("feeling it shimmer from the stones and the wood and the living beings") and eases it out. The fireplace wall and "part of the ceiling" bury the droids, and three survivors are cut down. Later he "moved the hundreds of large stones that had barred their exit" (LJ ch. 25, 27). Earlier he wedged his speeder under a descending durasteel blast shield, "In an amazing display of skill".
   - Anakin's boast: "My connection to the Force is stronger than yours." He wanted to attack Dooku plus a super battle droid landing party. Obi-Wan: "We would be dead before we took two steps on that platform." (LJ ch. 24)
5. **A non-Jedi's residual Force beats a ship's computer.** Lorian's pirate gunship fires exactly as the target drops its particle shield: "Even an onboard computer couldn't obtain that kind of speed and accuracy." (LJ ch. 8) / "Lorian still had Force abilities, which explained the split-second timing of his laser cannon attack." (LJ ch. 10). The book is inconsistent: ch. 12 says "Without his connection to the Force, Lorian could not handle the weapon as he once had". His Force fully "rushed back" at his death (LJ ch. 26).
6. **Qui-Gon/Obi-Wan battle-meld (pre-TPM).**
   > "The Force surged around them, gathering so that it felt like heat and light, making every move easy. / Within seconds, battered and smoking droids littered the floor." (LJ ch. 18)
   - Qui-Gon "within seconds… had estimated velocity, path, and blaster range" of unfamiliar droids. He also uses a Force push, a mind-distraction to hide lightsabers during a search, and filtered hearing (LJ ch. 14, 17, 18).

Other data:
- The Sith Holocron causes nausea, shaking and "Nightmares and dark visions… lasting years". Oppo Rancisis pockets it calmly (ch. 1–2).
- Ship tech (ch. 8): particle and energy shields can't run at once; the energy shield stops turbolasers but not laser cannons. A small craft carries "scaled-down" capital-ship turbolasers.
- A reclumi spider web "could stop a moving vehicle" (ch. 22).
- The Delaluna "Annihilator" superweapon was a hoax Lorian started (ch. 16–17).
- A training lightsaber can cut a turbolift door "within minutes" with three users (ch. 5).
- Anakin parses dozens of overlapping holo-voices at once (ch. 20).
