# Legacy of the Jedi: notes part 3 (Part Three, ch. 14–19: Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan, "Thirty-two Years Later")

Source: owner's MEGA epub (handle oH802TYJ). Line refs are to the extracted text outside the repo.

## Setting
- This is post-Melida/Daan and post-Xanatos (the Telos death is recapped at ch. 14, l.1768). Tahl is blind and doing Temple research (l.1816).
- Junction 5 (capital Rion) vs. its moon Delaluna and the rumored "Annihilator" superweapon, "capable of wiping out cities with one blow" (ch. 14, l.1824). **It does not exist** (ch. 16, l.2324). Lorian Nod heads the Guardians security force and invented the rumor ("a bloodless grab for power", ch. 17, l.2416).

## Feats (Qui-Gon and teen Obi-Wan)
- Force-filtered hearing: "Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan were able to screen out background noise with the help of the Force, concentrating on a conversation at a table behind them." (ch. 14, l.1854).
- Telekinetic diversion plus a group leap: "Qui-Gon used the Force to disturb a precariously stacked column of boxes… The Force propelled them over the mess, the citizens, and the workers" (ch. 15, l.2004–2006).
- Cilia Dil vaults out from under a bridge (non-Force, l.2124). Qui-Gon senses her hidden: "Yet Qui-Gon felt that Cilia was near" (l.2114).
- Lorian, as the Guardians' head, "seems to know things… things he couldn't know, even by surveillance" (Cilia, ch. 15, l.2132). This implies residual Force insight.
- Combat vs. Delaluna airborne droids (ch. 17): Qui-Gon reads the threat instantly: "within seconds he had estimated velocity, path, and blaster range" (l.2350). He "took out two droids with one swift stroke" (l.2362). Obi-Wan's "lightsaber [is] a constant arc of movement" and his kick splits a droid "into pieces" (l.2356–2362).
- Force push: "With a Force push, Qui-Gon sent the officer flying." (ch. 17, l.2370).
- Mind trick during a search: "He and Qui-Gon had used the Force to distract the guards from their lightsabers during the search." (ch. 18, l.2484).
- Lightsabers through a durasteel cell door: "The metal melted and peeled back, glowing orange" (ch. 18, l.2486).
- Master–Padawan battle-meld: "The Force surged around them, gathering so that it felt like heat and light, making every move easy. / Within seconds, battered and smoking droids littered the floor." (ch. 18, l.2506–2508).
- Trained guards using Temple-style flanking: "Lorian's Temple training had come in handy. / Still, the security officers were not Jedi. Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan could keep them at bay." (ch. 18, l.2532–2534). Qui-Gon's order: "Take no lives" (l.2524).

## Lore
- Lorian left prison early as an informant (ch. 15, l.1930). His line: "The galaxy is so immense, but I can't get away from the Jedi." (ch. 18, l.2580).
- Qui-Gon rejects Dooku's creed: "Qui-Gon knew Dooku was wrong — he was not alone." (ch. 19, l.2596).
- Coda before Naboo; Tahl's death on New Apsolon is recalled (ch. 19, l.2620). Qui-Gon on Obi-Wan's flaw: "You wish to please me too much." (l.2638).
