# Legacy of the Jedi: notes part 4 (Part Four, ch. 20–27: Obi-Wan and Anakin, "Twenty-three Years Later", Clone Wars)

Source: owner's MEGA epub (handle oH802TYJ). Line refs are to the extracted text outside the repo.

## Setting and timing
- "The Clone Wars had begun… Many Jedi had lost their lives on Geonosis six months earlier." (ch. 20, l.2658). Anakin still favors his new artificial hand (l.2682). Raxus Prime is mentioned as a missed chance at Dooku (ch. 24, l.3260).
- Lorian Nod, now elected ruler of Junction 5, offers to spy on Dooku's summit at his villa on Null. The prize is Station 88 Spaceport, "a gateway to the Mid-Rim systems" (ch. 20, l.2750).
- Lorian: "I am no longer able to access the Force as I did before." (ch. 20, l.2772).

## Top power-scaling statements
- **Obi-Wan on Dooku at Geonosis:** "He had never met such power in battle before. He had never come up against something that had completely overpowered him. Even meeting the Sith Lord who had killed Qui-Gon had not been the same." (ch. 20, l.2704). This is Obi-Wan's own ranking: Dooku > Maul in raw felt power.
- **Anakin's self-assessment:** "My connection to the Force is stronger than yours. I'm telling you I can do it!" (ch. 24, l.3270). He wants to kill Dooku with Obi-Wan ("We can take him together") and rejects the super battle droid landing as an obstacle. Obi-Wan's count: "We would be dead before we took two steps on that platform." (l.3284). Treat this as a boast, not narration.
- **Lorian's assessment of Dooku:** "He knew Dooku was vastly more powerful." and "Why use any weapon when Dooku could swat it away like a fly?" (ch. 26, l.3532–3538).

## Feats
- Anakin multi-tracks dozens of spinning planetary holograms with narrating voices: "Out of the chaos, Anakin would distinguish one voice… until he could clearly hear each voice amid the babble." Obi-Wan can't follow it (ch. 20, l.2674–2676). He dismisses them one by one with a gesture.
- Anakin boards a moving swoop: he swings from a branch, drops on the back, and steers from behind at max through dense forest, flipping repeatedly (ch. 22, l.2970–2994).
- A reclumi spider web stops the swoop dead: "a web so strong it could stop a moving vehicle" (ch. 22, l.3004). The web sticks to the skin like glue; Obi-Wan cuts free with his saber (ch. 23, l.3020).
- Villa assault (ch. 25): Anakin cuts power, flips his speeder and wedges it under a descending durasteel blast shield, "In an amazing display of skill" (l.3388–3392). Earlier he had blasted through the doors beyond.
- Dark-side sense: "He felt the dark side in the room, how it surged and grew." (ch. 25, l.3402). Dooku's rage at Lorian's betrayal: "His dark eyes seemed to suck in the light around the table and devour it." (l.3420).
- **Anakin's charge and catch:** he charges at Dooku and the droids. Obi-Wan feels "the Force in the room like a pulse, like a heartbeat, like a rolling wave." Anakin "altered his path to scoop up Floria… kept his lightsaber moving, deflecting the blaster fire," then sets her down "so lightly and gently in the midst of his soaring leap that not even a hair of Floria's coiled braids was disturbed" (ch. 25, l.3458–3464).
- Force amplification: "He caught the surging Force from Anakin and embraced it, doubling it, making it grow." They meet in midair (ch. 25, l.3468).
- Obi-Wan: a double-footed somersault kick sends assassin Robior Web flying into the wall (ch. 25, l.3482).
- Anakin hews a chunk off the stone table for cover with one swipe (ch. 25, l.3486).
- **Keystone collapse:** Anakin finds the hidden keystone by Force feel ("feeling it shimmer from the stones and the wood and the living beings") and pulls it telekinetically. The 20 m-class fireplace wall and "part of the ceiling" fall on about a dozen-plus super battle droids. Three are left standing and are cut down (ch. 25, l.3502–3514; the inn's fireplace is called "twenty meters tall" at ch. 21, l.2848, and the villa's is "big enough for a Null to stand erect in", l.3398).
- Afterward Anakin "moved the hundreds of large stones that had barred their exit" (ch. 27, l.3618). This is presumably TK; the book doesn't specify.

## Dooku vs. Lorian (ch. 26), the death of Lorian Nod
- Lorian's Force returns at the end: "Lorian had not felt the Force in many years. When he reached out and felt it move, it startled him… within seconds, it all rushed back" (l.3526–3528).
- **Dooku's dark-side TK:** "The dark side surged in a shocking display of power, and Lorian found himself flung like a child's doll in the air. He slammed against the wall" (l.3582).
- Dooku senses the regained Force: "So you haven't lost it completely… Too bad it isn't enough." (l.3592).
- Lorian dodges Dooku's first cut using childhood knowledge of Dooku's footwork; the saber "hit rock and sliced through it" (l.3594).
- The kill: "Behind him was a whisper. That was all he heard… he had been pierced through." (l.3596). Lorian's shot at the speeder's cable panel goes wild; Dooku escapes.
- Lore: Dooku viewed the Sith Holocron "sometime later". Lorian accuses him and Dooku doesn't deny it: "Why shouldn't I have accessed it?" (l.3558–3560). This closes the ch. 13 thread.
- A Force-death image: "He drew the Force around him like a blanket, and with an explosion of color lighting his vision, he smiled and let go of his life." (l.3608). Obi-Wan: "He was never more a Jedi than at the last." (ch. 27, l.3626).

## Character notes
- Obi-Wan sees "contempt" flicker in Anakin's gaze (ch. 24, l.3280) and "a flash of something much deeper than he'd ever known before" (l.3288).
- Anakin: "Love is always relevant, Master." (ch. 24, l.3322). He reads Floria's hidden love for Samish Kash.
- Ending: "He loved Anakin Skywalker, but he did not truly know him." (ch. 27, l.3662).
