## Chronology note (derived from the text, not stated as BBY)
- The part gaps are 13 + 32 + 23 = 68 years from Part One to the Clone Wars section. Dooku is 13 in Part One, so he is about 81 in Part Four, which is set about 6 months after Geonosis.
- Qui-Gon is 16 in Part Two, so he is about 48 in Part Three, which is set after Melida/Daan and Xanatos's death on Telos. That is consistent with Legends dating (Qui-Gon born about 92 BBY; Melida/Daan about 44 BBY). Part Four then falls at about 21 BBY.
