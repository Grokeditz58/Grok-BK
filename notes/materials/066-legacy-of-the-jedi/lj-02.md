# Legacy of the Jedi: notes part 2 (Part Two, ch. 7–13: Dooku and Qui-Gon, "Thirteen Years Later")

Source: owner's MEGA epub (handle oH802TYJ). Line refs are to the extracted text outside the repo.

## Setting and ambition
- Dooku is now a Master. He picked Qui-Gon at age 10 from lightsaber training; Qui-Gon is 16 (ch. 7, l.830–834).
- Dooku's stated goal: "to surpass Yoda as the greatest Jedi ever" (ch. 7, l.830). And: "Dooku was not as great a Jedi as Yoda — not yet. But he would be one day." (l.828).
- Qui-Gon: "quick to learn and very strong in the living Force" (ch. 7, l.856). He is better at repairs than Dooku (ch. 10, l.1170).

## Space fight vs. Lorian's pirate ship (ch. 8)
- Lorian's small cruiser carries scaled-down turbolasers. Dooku: "This kind of firepower is usually reserved for capital ships. The attacking craft must be custom-fitted with scaled-down versions." (ch. 8, l.1036).
- Ship tech lore: the particle shield must drop to fire laser cannons. The energy shield blocks turbolasers but not laser cannons, and "we can't operate both shields simultaneously" (ch. 8, l.1018).
- Force-assisted gunnery by a non-Jedi: Qui-Gon notes "The ship is firing whenever we drop the particle shield… That would take incredible reflexes… Even an onboard computer couldn't obtain that kind of speed and accuracy." (ch. 8, l.1062). It is later explained: "Lorian still had Force abilities, which explained the split-second timing of his laser cannon attack." (ch. 10, l.1192). **An expelled Padawan keeps enough Force reflex to out-time a ship's computer.**
- Dooku hand-reverses the engines at high velocity to escape the ambush (ch. 8, l.990).

## Boarding (ch. 9)
- Qui-Gon at 16 deflects blaster fire fluidly; "so little of the awkwardness of adolescence" (ch. 9, l.1078).
- Dooku, blinded by smoke grenades: "He took out the droids as he moved, hearing them before he saw them. / … droids littered the ground." (ch. 9, l.1100–1102).
- A blast-proof safe-room door (rated as needing "enough explosives to destroy the ship itself", ch. 7, l.860) is blown open quickly. The weak point was sabotage via Kontag.
- Lorian in armor shoots with "excellent aim"; Dooku has to keep his blade moving. Lorian "turned, even though they'd made no sound" (ch. 9, l.1126–1128). Lorian exploits Dooku's split-second recognition of his trademark move and escapes (l.1132–1138).

## Captivity and the duel (ch. 11–12)
- Force danger-sense: "Dooku felt the rush of the Force as it warned him… Qui-Gon was only a fraction of a second behind him." (ch. 11, l.1438). They surrender to Colicoid Eradicator droids to protect child workers.
- Force healing: "He called on the Force to help him, and he felt the pain ease its grip." (ch. 12, l.1464).
- **Telekinetic precision:** "Dooku summoned the Force. The vibroblade flew from Eero's unsteady hand and landed on the energy cuffs binding Dooku's wrists. The blade cut through the cuffs easily. With split-second timing, Dooku slipped out his hand" (ch. 12, l.1600). Then "his lightsaber flew from the room next door into his palm" (l.1604). This is a summon through a doorway.
- Anger as a tool, a pre-Sith trait: "He was older now, and wiser. Anger no longer had the power to make him sloppy. It made him more precise." (ch. 12, l.1622).
- Duel dominance vs. Lorian, who holds a blaster and vibroblade, then Qui-Gon's saber:
  - "He fired the blaster. Dooku deflected the fire easily." (l.1632).
  - "with an almost casual swipe severed the vibroblade in two." (l.1634).
  - "Without his connection to the Force, Lorian could not handle the weapon as he once had… He parried Lorian's attacks, barely moving." (l.1646). This is in tension with ch. 10's claim that Lorian kept Force reflexes. Read it as Lorian's Force being atrophied, not absent.
  - "He moved forward in perfect form, gathering the Force and molding it to his desires." (l.1654).
- Qui-Gon's "Master. Stop." stops an intended killing (l.1660–1666).

## Dark-side drift (lore)
- Lorian *did* view the Sith Holocron: "what I saw chilled my blood and haunted my days for a long time. It haunts me still." (ch. 12, l.1554).
- Ch. 13: envy drives Dooku into the archives to view the Holocron himself: "the same hunger, the same irresistible urge to know the Sith… Dooku, one of the greatest Jedi Knights" (ch. 13, l.1708–1712). The text cuts away at "The massive doors opened, and Dooku strode in."
- Two years later, Dooku's last mission with Qui-Gon: "their battle minds in perfect rhythm" (l.1714). Qui-Gon on Dooku: "He was grateful to have a Master so strong in the Force." (l.1740).
- Dooku's parting creed: "You are always alone, and betrayal is inevitable." (l.1742).
