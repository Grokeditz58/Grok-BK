# Outbound Flight (Timothy Zahn, Del Rey 2006): notes part 4, Ch. 18–21

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. This part covers FULL.txt l.2854–3602. Citation "OF ch. N (l.x)".

## Ch. 18: Dreadnaught specs, the meld test, Sidious meets Thrawn (l.2854–3052)
- **Dreadnaught specs (Qennto):**
  - Built by Rendili StarDrive, "Six hundred meters long, with heavy shields and a whole bunch of upgraded turbolaser cannons".
  - Most of the guns are "clustered in four midline bubbles ... terrific broadside volley".
  - "Normal crew runs around sixteen thousand, with room for another two or three thousand troops" (l.2864). Outbound Flight carries about 50k in total.
- Qennto's furs are now 2.5 months late to Drixo. Ar'alani secretly takes Car'das to the battle site. The *Whirlwind* "did not return".
- **JEDI MELD GUNNERY TEST:**
  - This is C'baoth's 3rd meld of the day. He links himself (on D-1) with Lorana (on the far side of D-1) and with Ma'Ning plus 2 Duros on D-4.
  - All six targets are destroyed, with "all test-one volleys on target" and one "flier" miss on target two (l.2925–2943).
  - Obi-Wan wonders "if the man might actually be as strong in the Force as he claimed" (l.2929).
  - Obi-Wan notices something odd on shots 2–3: D-4's Padawans were present. C'baoth says they weren't participating. "Such impatience is the mark of the dark side."
- Roxuli is the final Republic stop, 4 days later.
- On the moved *Darkvenge*, with a TC-18 translator droid, Thrawn tells Doriana his "name is not Stratis. You're not your own master".
- **Doriana's reveal:** he is Kinman Doriana; he publicly serves Palpatine but truly serves Darth Sidious (l.3005).
- **FAR OUTSIDERS:** a "massive assault force of dark ships, shadowy figures, and weapons of great power, based on organic technology", with a foothold at the galaxy's far edge (l.3011). These are the Yuuzhan Vong. Vergere disappeared there (l.3017).
- **Sidious hologram:**
  - "are you typical of your species?" Thrawn: "the youngest of my people to ever hold the position of Force Commander" (l.3029).
  - Sidious says the Jedi "reach across great distances to touch and manipulate the minds", and "Master C'baoth will not under any circumstances return".
  - Jedi sensitivity is blunted by thousands of minds around them, and more so when those minds die (l.3038).
- Thrawn takes 2 droidekas, 4 battle droids and a controller datapad (each controller handles 200 droids).
- **Tech:** droideka shields block radiation and high-twist magnetic fields. The Chiss once used reversed-polarity versions as intruder traps but discontinued them (l.3046–3051). This sets up Kav's death.

## Ch. 19: The trap is laid (l.3053–3235)
- The battle site holds 14 wrecks (maybe 13). Ar'alani: "The lives of other beings are not ours to interfere with" (l.3082). She supervises Thrawn while he awaits a tribunal.
- **C'baoth's tyranny grows:**
  - He suspends the Commander's Court and puts Jedi in charge of supervising everything.
  - He bans underlevel-themed messroom décor and docks Pressor 2 days' pay.
  - Ma'Ning senses growing resentment. Lore: the "special bond between Master and Padawan".
- **Thrawn tech prep:**
  - The starfighters are unloaded onto an asteroid. Thrawn programs the droid starfighters for "hull skimming", "no more than five meters above the hull" (l.3138).
  - Kav secretly overlays a secondary attack pattern with relay control. Kav: "in the end he will have to die". Doriana agrees all witnesses must be silenced (l.3156).
  - Thrawn links 20 starfighters in pairs with a "spare fuel tank" between them (l.3159). These turn out to be the radiation bombs.
- **Roxuli:** Palpatine personally arrives. C'baoth refuses a Roxuli mediation. Palpatine has Yoda's permission and takes Obi-Wan and Anakin off the ship, fulfilling Sidious's promise (l.3170–3191). The Skysprite stays in the D-3 hangar. Anakin: C'baoth "finds a way to make everyone do what's best for them."
- Car'das steals a long-range civilian shuttle (he learned to fly it from base tutorials), takes the *Springhawk* nav-data cylinder, and flies 6 hours to a Vagaari-conquered world to offer them a deal. "Jorj Car'das ... from a world called Corellia" (l.3234).

## Ch. 20: Car'das and the Miskara; Outbound Flight arrives (l.3236–3414)
- **Vagaari:** short bipeds with large hands and fright-mask faceplates. Their ruler, the Miskara, sits on a throne in tall masks. The Geroons are a slave species whose world was conquered.
- Car'das betrays the location of Crustai to the Miskara, an echo of "I will do whatever necessary".
- The shuttle carries 4 battle droids, 2 droidekas and a controller, all planted by Thrawn. Car'das keys the droids to the Miskara with "target" and "fire" commands. The Miskara shoots 3 Geroon slaves (l.3308).
- **The ambush system** has a globular cluster. Outbound Flight is 2+ hours late and is yanked out of hyperspace **5 km ahead** by the gravity projector (l.3327–3330). The Chiss can build these; the Republic never had them.
- Thrawn has removed Kav's alternate command layer: "thank you for showing me how such secondary programming is done" (l.3337–3339).
- C'baoth forbids any reply until the hyperdrive failure is explained. He senses "deep malice" and puts Jedi at the weapons stations. "Let me see your face." Thrawn wears a black tunic with silver bars.
- Uliar rides a swoop into a turbolift to reach D-1.
- The Miskara brings his entire fleet. Car'das is locked in the forwardmost hull bubble of the flagship, "twice the size of a coffin" (l.3403–3411).

## Ch. 21: The ultimatum (l.3415–3602)
- Thrawn relays the Far Outsiders warning. C'baoth: "the Sith have long since vanished" (l.3421).
- Thrawn tells them to turn back and never return, "Or I will be forced to destroy you" (l.3424). C'baoth: "Do you truly believe your twelve ships could survive ten minutes ...?" (l.3425).
- Lorana proposes going around, and Thrawn accepts. C'baoth: "We are the Jedi, the ultimate power in the universe ... We will destroy any who dare stand in our way" (l.3435). One hour is given.
- Thrawn to Lorana: "intent alone is meaningless. Your actions are what will determine your fate" (l.3451).
- **C'BAOTH FORCE FEATS:**
  - He Force-freezes Uliar's mouth, tongue and legs (l.3466–3468, 3477).
  - He holds a shuttle hatch shut against Pressor and Mosh shoving it (l.3481).
  - ComOps tracks the swoop.
- Ma'Ning calls a Judgment Circle. C'baoth: "My mission, Uliar."
- 57 "conspirators" plus the children are locked in the storage-core training center (count at l.3699). The students are moved to D-1 ComOps.
- Pressor: D-4's aft reactor storage holds "a couple of droidekas packed away for emergency intruder defense" (l.3517).
- Car'das tells the Miskara that Outbound Flight has "fifty thousand" people and perhaps a few hundred droidekas, plus at least 20,000 service droids (a guess) (l.3548–3550). Miskara: "We are the Vagaari. We take what we want."
- **The Vagaari fleet** is yanked out of hyperspace by the gravity net about 2 hours in, on the Crustai line: "over two hundred ships, at least a hundred ... warships" (l.3572).
