# Outbound Flight (Timothy Zahn, Del Rey 2006): notes part 2, Ch. 7–12

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. This part covers FULL.txt l.936–1969. Citation "OF ch. N (l.x)".

## Ch. 7: Lorana captured; the slinker missile (l.936–1095)
- Anakin talks swoops with Duefgrin (split-X intakes).
- Lorana tails the adult Brolf Jhompfi for about an hour and is ambushed by Vissfil and his brother (a blaster and a quarter-pick ax). She deliberately acts harmless and Force-slides her lightsaber into a hedge (l.985). She is stunned from behind because his "intent [was] masked by his overall anxiety" (l.993). **Anti-feat:** an anxious attacker can mask his intent from Jedi danger sense.
- **Doriana's "slinker" missile:**
  - He claims it guides itself through buildings. Really it follows a "geographically programmed course director" path that he paced out himself (l.1016).
  - The targets are Argente and Guildmaster Gilfrome. He tells the conspirators to go fugitive and sizes them up as "Stupid, angry, and malleable".
- Crustai base lore:
  - The base sits inside the asteroid, reached through a twisting tunnel. The Defense Fleet is building a larger base of the same kind.
  - Aristocra Chaf'orm'bintrano is of the Fifth Ruling Family, whose color is yellow. The First and Eighth families would bring a reprimand.
- Car'das is 23 (l.1076).
- Thrawn on pre-emption: "I and the entire Chiss species disagree on this point" (l.1081). "I merely understand how others think and react" (l.1091).

## Ch. 8: Rescue; Thrawn reads art (l.1096–1272)
- Anakin's intel from Duefgrin points to Jhompfi and the Covered Brush house ring. Riske reports missing mining explosives, which suggests homemade missiles. C'baoth: "There is no anger, Master Kenobi. Not for a Jedi." He senses Lorana isn't dead and suggests mind-forcing the teen (implied).
- **Force feats (Obi-Wan and Anakin):**
  - Both sense the Ilum crystal of a hidden lightsaber at a few metres.
  - Obi-Wan **ignites Lorana's lightsaber remotely with the Force** as bait (l.1188) and closes it remotely (l.1192).
  - He catches the thrown saber, deflects repeated shots and slices a blaster in half.
  - Anakin storms the house and kills one Brolf (the body is on the floor). Lorana is rescued.
  - Obi-Wan sniff-tests the sedative using sensory enhancement.
- **Thrawn feat (art-reading):**
  - From Vagaari loot, he reads a tressle as "created by beings with an extra joint between wrist and elbow, who see largely in the blue-ultraviolet". Car'das confirms it is the signature of the Frunchettan-sai, an Outer Rim species (l.1237–1245).
  - From a Pashvi sculpture he reads "humanoid ... wider torso or longer arms ... drawn to and yet repulsed by or fearful of the physical objects they live among". Qennto confirms Pashvi (a rock-pillar world with a predator avian).
  - Thirteen works by different artists, each species correctly identified. Qennto: "Like some crazy Jedi thing."

## Ch. 9: The missile and C'baoth's grip (l.1273–1374)
- The slinker flies 1 m above the ground. Its nose sprays black "shroud-liquid" into Lorana's eyes, and she misses. Obi-Wan misses too, and his thrown saber is dodged by the locked-on missile. Lorana sends a Force call, "Danger. Danger. Danger.", to C'baoth (l.1301).
- **C'BAOTH FORCE FEAT (headline):**
  - He stops the thrusting missile in mid-air with a Force grip, its thrusters "spitting fire uselessly" until they die (l.1304).
  - He lectures the crowd while holding it. Then "Deliberately, he closed his outstretched hand. With a raucous crackling of stressed metal, the body of the missile crumpled in on itself" (l.1312).
  - Obi-Wan takes its weight in his own Force grip and carries it off. Anakin: "That's telling them."
- **Sidious/Doriana lore:**
  - The plot was always to make C'baoth a hero so Outbound Flight gets its funding. "I don't want Kenobi taking any of the credit". Doriana becomes Palpatine's liaison to the project.
  - "Lord Tyranus" is mentioned as a simpler man who would just assassinate.
  - "why simply dispose of ... C'baoth when you could dispose of him and as many other Jedi as he could talk into accompanying him on Outbound Flight?" (l.1332). "Jorus C'baoth ... was dead. He just didn't know it yet" (l.1333–1334).
- Yaga Minor prep center: Chas Uliar and Brace Tarkosa (among the first 100 signups), and Keely. Funding and families are restored.
- **Jedi roster:** six Masters including C'baoth, plus eleven Knights (l.1351). Windu suspects coincidence: "we suspect there may be a Sith out there". Obi-Wan and Anakin are to ride along through the Unknown Regions. They bring a Delta-12 Skysprite: a two-seat Aethersprite with no weapons and a TransGalMeg hyperdrive ring. Jedi hibernation is to stretch supplies.

## Ch. 10: Chiss society; Ar'alani and Thrass (l.1375–1521)
- After five weeks of lessons, Thrawn's Basic "far surpassed" the humans' progress in his languages. Thrawn: "One can concentrate so closely on the words ... misses the meaning ... never lose focus on the larger landscape." Maris is falling for him.
- **Chiss lore:**
  - Senior Defense Fleet officers are stripped of family name (the Defense Hierarchy, merit-based).
  - Families take merit adoptives, and warriors wear their family color.
  - Thrawn is Eighth Family, "Trial-born", and a possible "ranking distant".
  - The number of families has varied between 3 and 12 over the centuries.
  - Admiral Ar'alani wears white. Syndic Mitth'ras'safis (Thrass) is Thrawn's brother (l.1454); both were "born as commoners" (l.1740). Csilla is named (l.1454).
- Thrawn reads three slave species from ship details. He maneuvers Ar'alani into staying to inventory loot, using a Cheunh lie Qennto can't contradict, so he can slip away to a Vagaari attack six hours off.

## Ch. 11: Thrawn vs. the Vagaari war fleet (l.1522–1758)
- The scene: about 200 Vagaari ships fighting over a planet, 100 more waiting, about 20 hulks.
- **Thrawn deductions:**
  - From the defensive grouping he spots civilians, so the Vagaari are a "completely nomadic species". "There is no homeworld" (l.1544).
  - From delayed escape jumps he deduces a gravity-well projector: "Clearance from the laws of physics" (l.1556). The shadow is cone-shaped, and there are 6 projectors.
- **THRAWN TACTIC (headline):**
  - He jumps 6 heavy fighters into hyperspace aimed so the projector's gravity cone yanks them out at its edge, right beside the projector (l.1585–1590).
  - The fighters wipe out the enemy fighters in under a minute, and the *Springhawk* follows.
  - Assault teams kill the crew, and Chief Engineer Yal'avi'kema collapses the projector in 5 minutes.
  - He reasons the enemy warships can't fire freely without hitting their own projector (l.1606).
- **Living shields:** the hull bubbles hold prisoners (l.1613–1615). Thrawn: "Those people are already dead" (l.1621). He fires missiles anyway and puts shock nets on the bridges.
- Damage: the *Springhawk* canopy is hit and Thrawn takes a chest wound. Maris lies on him to seal his suit. The projector is spark-welded to the hull. All 6 fighters escape, and 4 warriors die (l.1747).
- Qennto is jailed for disrespecting Ar'alani, and Thrass puts the loot under seal.
- Thrawn: "Someday, our Ascendancy and their Republic will make contact" (l.1711). Thrass: "Peaceful watchfulness is the Chiss way." Thrawn: "it's foolhardy to always permit our enemies to choose the time and place of battle" (l.1738); "If I do throw it away ... it won't be for nothing" (l.1742). Also: "I will defeat them, no matter what the cost."

## Ch. 12: Outbound Flight at Yaga Minor (l.1759–1969)
- **Layout:** six Dreadnaughts in a hexagon around the central storage core, joined by massive turbolift pylons (l.1760). C'baoth: "Therein lies the future of the galaxy."
- Lorana was knighted 3 weeks earlier. C'baoth tells her she is coming: "It's not an offer".
- **Cast:**
  - Jedi Master Justyn Ma'Ning.
  - Tech Dean Jinzler, Lorana's brother, who is not coming.
  - Captain Pakmillu (Mon Cal) on D-1, the command ship.
- **Ship systems:**
  - The ComOps Center is about 30 m long between the bow corridors, and the whole system can be run from it.
  - A Podracer-style console runs a fleet of speeders and swoops through the corridors.
  - About 50,000 people aboard (l.1812).
- **Route and supplies:**
  - Lonnaw (Droma), the edge of Glythe, Argai (Haldeen), Kokash, Mondress, Albanin.
  - 3 weeks to Unknown Space, with a nav calibration about 230 ly past the edge of Wild Space.
  - The storage core holds supplies for 10 years. A Dreadnaught can be detached to found a colony.
  - "eighteen Jedi aboard".
- **C'baoth Force feat:** Force-catches a falling conductive grid sheet and holds it while chatting (l.1872–1874). Lorana lifts it back into place.
- The Supreme Chancellor's representative orders "analysis-grade blood and tissue samples for shipment to Coruscant" (l.1878). This hints at a C'baoth clone (cf. Joruus C'baoth in the Thrawn trilogy).
- **Jinzler family:**
  - Dean and Lorana's parents were Temple electronics workers, fired after Lorana was taken at 10 months old. Dean resents it.
  - Her amethyst-crystal lightsaber was secretly a gift from their parents (l.1938–1940).
  - Lorana to Dean: "I want you to stop hating".
