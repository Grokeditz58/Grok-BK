# Outbound Flight: summary of strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. Full text l.1–5928. Citation "OF ch. N (l.x)"; "ME" = *Mist Encounter*.

1. **Thrawn routs a Trade Federation task force with 12 ships and no losses (OF ch. 16).**
   - The enemy: 2 battleships, 6 Hardcells, 7 escort cruisers and "nearly three thousand" droid starfighters (l.2383).
   - He finds the droids' control-range edge and stays outside it (l.2533–2534).
   - He spoofs the rolling code to send droids chasing a decoy (l.2576), hides Connor nets in missiles (l.2585–2591), and puts every droid to sleep with full-spectrum jamming (l.2610–2613).
   - He kills the Hardcells at their fuel-cell junctions (l.2630) and pins *Darkvenge* with a gravity projector (l.2638). He loses no ships (l.2708).
2. **Thrawn's gravity-projector ambush catches two fleets (OF ch. 11, 20–22).**
   - He jumps fighters into the edge of a Vagaari gravity cone to land next to the projector (l.1585–1590).
   - Later he uses the captured projector to pull Outbound Flight out of hyperspace 5 km away (l.3327–3330), and then 200+ Vagaari ships (l.3572).
   - Hull-skimming droid fighters at 5 m cut between living-shield hostage bubbles and take out "over a quarter" of the warships in the first attack (l.3651–3653).
3. **C'baoth's Jedi meld mind-attack (OF ch. 22).**
   - "Stretch out to the Force ... and then, to the Vagaari" (l.3623).
   - The flagship bridge falls into "helpless babbling", across 100+ warships (l.3629–3638).
   - The meld itself "permits a group of Jedi to connect their minds so closely as to act as a single person" (l.2401). The gunnery test across two Dreadnaughts hit all six targets (l.2925–2943).
4. **C'baoth's telekinesis.**
   - He stops a thrusting missile mid-air, then crushes it: "the body of the missile crumpled in on itself" (l.1304–1312).
   - Range feats: a Force push through a comm display (l.2841–2842), and a **Force choke on Thrawn over a comm link from another ship** (l.3712–3716).
   - He fell to the dark side: "Jorus C'baoth, Jedi Master, had gone over to the dark side" (l.3719).
5. **Lorana flies Outbound Flight alone (OF ch. 23–24).**
   - "One single Jedi" (l.3995–3997). She runs D-1's systems through the Force with the hyperdrive "running blazingly hot" and navigates a packed star cluster (l.4031–4043).
   - Then she crash-lands the ship to save 57 survivors (l.4129–4141).
6. **The radiation-bomb kill: about 50,000 dead with the Jedi aboard.**
   - Ramming starfighters warp the blast doors and breach the hulls. Then bombs fill the Dreadnaughts with "a killing wave of radiation ... destroying all life" (l.3735–3759).
   - The Jedi in the weapons blisters were mostly killed earlier, when Thrawn's ships hit the blisters (l.3681–3683).
7. **Thrawn's mind-reading through art and architecture.**
   - He identifies alien species from their art (l.1237–1245) and reads a droideka bluff from the bridge design: "Architecture is merely another form of art" (l.2770–2774).
   - Car'das's betrayal to the Vagaari was itself Thrawn's lure (l.3800).
8. **Obi-Wan / Anakin.**
   - Obi-Wan ignites and closes Lorana's lightsaber remotely with the Force (l.1188–1192). His wall-hop climb comes with a stated ceiling: "a leap like that was well beyond his capabilities" (l.894–897).
   - Anakin is 14 (l.493). Sidious personally removes him from the flight (l.2031–2039, 3170–3191).
9. **Sidious / Far Outsiders lore.**
   - Doriana openly serves Sidious (l.3005). The Far Outsiders (Yuuzhan Vong) have "weapons of great power, based on organic technology" (l.3011).
   - The Chiss have already repelled a Far Outsider recon force (l.4170–4174).
   - Sidious: Jedi sensitivity is blunted by thousands of minds and their deaths (l.3038). His "hatred of nonhumans" (l.4181).
10. ***Mist Encounter*: solo Thrawn vs. a Star Destroyer's landing party.**
    - Berry-lured animals carry sabotaged power packs past the sentries. A monofilament line downs a TIE.
    - He wears a stormtrooper's armor to board the *Strikefast* (l.4499–4521). "Call me Thrawn" (l.4559).
11. **Kav's death:** a reversed-polarity droideka shield sends his own bolt back into him (l.3046–3051, 4159–4164).
