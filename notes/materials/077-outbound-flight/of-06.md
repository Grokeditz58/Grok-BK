# Outbound Flight (Timothy Zahn, Del Rey 2006): notes part 6, *Mist Encounter* (l.4242–4570)

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. The bonus short story (© 1995, first printed in *Star Wars Adventure Journal* 7). Citation "ME (l.x)".

## Setting and cast
- About one week after Palpatine declared himself Emperor (l.4267).
- Booster Terrik and the Borlovian Llollulion (*Starwayman*) are chased into Unknown Space by the Victory-class Star Destroyer *Strikefast* under Captain Voss Parck.
- The landing force is led by Colonel Mosh Barris, with Major Wyan and Lt. Kavren. The Imperials operate under UAE (Unknown Alien Encounters) Orders.
- "K'rell'n traders" is the Chiss name for Corellians (l.4476–4478).

## THRAWN SOLO vs. AN IMPERIAL LANDING FORCE (headline)
Thrawn is in exile, alone and without a ship, and takes apart an entire landing force:
- He takes a dead TIE pilot and stuffs the flight suit with grass and fermented pyussh berries, which lure small nocturnal animals. The animals carry gimmicked blaster power packs, with their "sturm dowels pulled out", past the sentries (l.4336, 4499–4503).
- He uses a sling to throw berries onto troopers so the animals swarm them.
- A monofilament line strung between treetops downs a TIE (l.4505).
- He swaps the pilots' comlinks so the Imperials lock out the wrong one (l.4509–4510).
- He kills ordinary troopers so that stormtroopers, with full-face helmets, are sent in (l.4513–4515).
- He kills one stormtrooper with an explosive under cover of comm jamming and studies the armor. Then he kills a second without visible damage and wears that armor. He props the first set up with branches and a bomb so the count looks right (l.4517–4521).
- He hides in a hollowed-out generator casing and boards the *Strikefast*.
- **Tally:**
  - 3 TIEs and their pilots lost.
  - 5 perimeter casualties: 3 by him and 2 by friendly fire (l.4407).
  - 2 stormtroopers.

## Recruitment
- Parck captures him in a test (a dimmed hangar).
- Thrawn: "I was exiled." "The leaders and I disagreed." "I alone of our warrior leaders accept this concept" (pre-emptive strike) (l.4533). "I do not need their permission to fight on their behalf."
- Parck offers him a fleet position and cites Vader's recruitment of aliens (the Noghri), through Parck's cousin who was promoted to vice admiral (l.4555).
- "Call me Thrawn" (l.4559).
