# Outbound Flight (Timothy Zahn, Del Rey 2006): notes part 1, front matter and Ch. 1–6

- Read 2026-11-03 CST from the owner's epub (Del Rey mass-market ebook, eISBN 978-0-307-79575-5, v3.1_r2; © 2006 Lucasfilm). This part covers FULL.txt l.1–935.
- Citation: "OF ch. N (l.x)". Chapters are numbered in this edition (1–24).
- Big picture: about 27 BBY ("five years after" Naboo). The Outbound Flight Project, six Dreadnaughts led by Jedi Master Jorus C'baoth, is sent to cross the galaxy. Darth Sidious, through his agent Kinman Doriana, wants it destroyed. Young Chiss Commander Mitth'raw'nuruodo (Thrawn) intercepts it in the Unknown Regions. Most of the POV comes from Jorj Car'das, a stranded Corellian smuggler.

## Ch. 1: Bargain Hunter blind-jumps (l.66–192)
- Crew of the *Bargain Hunter*: Captain Dubrak Qennto, copilot Maris Ferasi, and junior crewer Jorj Car'das (about 6 months aboard). They are running from Progga the Hutt over a fur shipment owed to Drixo the Hutt, with firegems smuggled in a fur collar.
- Car'das blind-jumps into "an empty spot with no stars". The hyperdrive runs away (mauvine nullifier instability) for 4+ hours, out through the Outer Rim and Wild Space into the Unknown Regions, about 250 ly past the edge.
- Progga follows them. A missile fired from below deploys a net "over a kilometer across" that dumps high voltage and kills the *Bargain Hunter*'s systems. **Combat:** three Chiss ships destroy Progga's ship in "Six minutes" (Qennto had bet 15). "Whoever these guys are, they're good."

## Ch. 2: Meeting Thrawn (l.193–369)
- The ship is the *Springhawk*, command vessel of Picket Force Two, Expansionary Defense Fleet. "Commander Mitth'raw'nuruodo of the Chiss Ascendancy" (l.222). The setting date is "five years after" Naboo (l.224).
- **Tech:** the jump to lightspeed isn't felt: "We made the jump ... approximately four standard minutes ago" (l.256). The Chiss ship is "Not that much bigger than a Republic cruiser". Forward Visual Triangulation Site One: "Sometimes the eyes of a Chiss are more reliable" (l.306).
- **Thrawn deduction feats:**
  - He reads the smuggling from the ship's economics (l.310) and has already found the firegems.
  - He memorized the phonemes "Dubrak Qennto" from a Huttese transmission he didn't understand (l.327).
- Doctrine: "Chiss don't invade ... We don't make war against even potential enemies unless we're attacked first" (l.347).
- Language: they speak Sy Bisti. Thrawn wants to learn Basic and teaches Car'das the trade language Minnisiat. Cheunh is the Chiss tongue. "my core name, Thrawn" (l.362).

## Ch. 3: Palpatine's office and Sidious (l.370–536)
- C'baoth and his Padawan Lorana Jinzler (22) meet Palpatine. Kinman Doriana is Palpatine's new assistant. **C'baoth Force feat (minor):** slides a datapad across the desk with the Force (l.389).
- **Outbound Flight specs:**
  - "six brand-new Dreadnaughts, straight off the Rendili StarDrive assembly line"
  - a central storage core joined by turbolift pylons
  - crews training on Yaga Minor (l.394)
- Funding was cut to crews only, with no colonists. The goal is another galaxy: search for life and Force-users. C'baoth adds a security angle.
- The bargain: C'baoth mediates the Barlok dispute (Marcol sector; Corporate Alliance vs. the Brolfi over mining rights) in exchange for funding and colonists.
- C'baoth quotes:
  - "Respect and fear are merely two sides of the same coin" (l.422).
  - "When you travel openly as a Jedi, you must behave as a Jedi. Always."
- Lorana senses a young man in the crowd watching her with resentment. This sets up her brother Dean.
- **Sidious lore:**
  - Doriana's holoprojector takes a code of 12 letters plus 18 digits.
  - Sidious: "It will be a distinct pleasure to get Jorus C'baoth out of our way" (l.480); "I have many servants, Doriana, but few as clever and as subtle as you."
  - Doriana "did so enjoy the game."
- Windu briefs Obi-Wan. Anakin is 14 (l.493). C'baoth wants to find Vergere and to move the best Jedi out of the Republic. Windu: "Something deep inside the man that I haven't been able to get a grip on." Obi-Wan is assigned to watch C'baoth on Barlok.

## Ch. 4: Crustai and the first Vagaari (l.537–682)
- Thrawn's Crustai base: Car'das deduces that a wobbling asteroid sweeps a passive sensor array (l.550). The Nine Ruling Families are introduced.
- **Thrawn tactical feats:**
  - An in-system micro-jump drops him right on target "Less than a minute after entering hyperspace" (l.572–577).
  - He kills two fighters with single shots, fired "just as the tubes' protective doors opened, detonating the missiles before they could be launched", after reading their predictable laser-then-missile pattern (l.672).
  - His lasers vaporize 4 missile salvos. A sentry had already disabled the enemy hyperdrive.
- He leads the boarding party personally in a vac suit: "I command these warriors ... Part of my duty is to share in their danger." "Chiss warriors are the best there are" (l.606–608). The ship is taken in under an hour, with the *Springhawk*'s sensors guiding the boarders.
- **Vagaari intro:**
  - The ship is a slaver/pirate treasure vessel with ovoid hull bubbles about 2×3 m.
  - The Vagaari have been "moving through this region ... at least the past ten years, preying mostly on the weak".
  - Car'das deduces from the height of the sealant that slaves did the repairs. The Chiss have no droids (l.645).

## Ch. 5: Barlok (l.683–798)
- Lorana thinks of non-Jedi as "a swirl of dead leaves" (C'baoth's influence). She has medium height, dark hair and gray eyes (l.749).
- Anakin (14) meets C'baoth again and is impressed. Obi-Wan: "he'll be a full Jedi before he's twenty." C'baoth dumps Lorana on Obi-Wan.
- Doriana, under the name "Defender", arms Brolf "Patriot" insurgents and jams the city HoloNet. He recalls Sidious's anger over Naboo and Maul's death.
- Obi-Wan on limits: "a Jedi can gain as much depth in the Force as he or she wants ... I don't believe there are any such limits."
- Jerv Riske is a former bounty hunter and top enforcer for Corporate Alliance Magistrate Passel Argente. **Obi-Wan feat:** uses "Jedi sensory enhancement techniques" to eavesdrop on an Aqualish shakedown. Lorana disappears following Riske.

## Ch. 6: Lorana vs. Riske; Obi-Wan's wall-jump (l.799–935)
- **Combat (Lorana):**
  - She gets a Force warning and cuts all three pieces of a splitting disk "with a quick one-two-three" (l.817).
  - Then Riske takes her in a chokehold from behind with a holdout blaster: "you're no Jedi—you got suckered way too easily for that" (l.824). Riske jabs at Jedi neutrality over Naboo.
- Lorana: "How do you ever know what is truly right?" Obi-Wan: "When you're at peace ... truly attuned to the Force."
- A Brolf teen, Duefgrin, steals burst thrusters.
- **Obi-Wan Force feat (with a limit stated):** he climbs a ~30 m alley. "even with Jedi strength enhancement a leap like that was well beyond his capabilities", so he frog-hops wall to wall, the first hop 4 m (l.894–897).
