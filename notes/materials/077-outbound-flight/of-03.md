# Outbound Flight (Timothy Zahn, Del Rey 2006): notes part 3, Ch. 13–17

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. This part covers FULL.txt l.1970–2853. Citation "OF ch. N (l.x)".

## Ch. 13: Doriana sets the trap (l.1970–2047)
- Doriana gets the final lists: "nineteen and a half" Jedi (Anakin counts as the half). He had nudged C'baoth into inviting "potential troublemakers".
- As "Commander Stratis" he meets Neimoidian Vicelord Siv Kav and sets up a Trade Federation ambush, 2 days to the rendezvous. Kav: Sidious "abandoned us" at Naboo. Doriana implies he is shielding the Trade Federation from the judiciary.
- Pakmillu has two nav checks in the first 800 ly past the Republic, and the ambush is set for the first.
- Sidious on Anakin: "He cannot go on that ship. He must remain here." Then: "I will remove Skywalker from Outbound Flight" (l.2031–2039). And: "eighteen Jedi to kill."

## Ch. 14: C'baoth's rule begins (l.2048–2315)
- D-4 is the Dreadnaught farthest from D-1. Jedi Master Justyn Ma'Ning oversees the D-4 Jedi, Commander Omano commands D-4, and two Duros Knights serve aboard.
- **C'baoth's secret program:**
  - He secretly screened the colonists for Force-sensitive children and found 11 candidates aged 4–10, plus low-sensitivity "secondaries".
  - "Then Master Yoda would be wrong". He cites Master Simikarty's writings. A training center is set up in the storage core.
- The Pressors: Jorad (5–6, a secondary) asks Lorana to retest him; his father Dillian is a hyperdrive tech and his sister is Katarin. Uliar complains about the drills.
- At Lonnaw, six days out, C'baoth tries to take Jedi Master Evrios's candidate child at night.
  - "Ship's law wasn't written to cover this situation." C'baoth: "We merely rewrite it."
  - "Outbound Flight is first and foremost a Jedi project. Jedi requirements therefore supersede all other authority" (l.2234).
- C'baoth's philosophy:
  - "Rule is of the dark side because it seeks personal gain ... Guidance ... seeks the other person's best interests" (l.2261).
  - "Can you truly say that Master Yoda and Master Windu couldn't run the Republic with more wisdom ..." (l.2263).
- Pakmillu sides with C'baoth and the boy is taken. Jobe Keely is named. Jorad's retest is negative. Lorana Force-stabilizes crate stacks in the core.

## Ch. 15: The ambush fleet and the Jedi meld (l.2316–2453)
- The *Bargain Hunter* is 600 hours from home at sublight (650 if careful). Thrawn: Republic hyperdrives are "more fragile", and shock nets disable them.
- Thrawn describes the Jedi to Thrass: "some unknown energy field ... sensory enhancement, insight into others' motivations and thoughts, or as a direct weapon" (l.2336).
- Qennto has secretly learned Cheunh and confronts Maris about Thrawn.
- **Trade Federation task force at the ambush point (fleet numbers):**
  - *Darkvenge* (Kav's flagship) and *Keeper* (a second battleship, once called *Seeker* at l.2560, a text slip), both split-ring battleships.
  - 6 armed Techno Union Hardcell-class transports and 7 Trade Federation escort cruisers.
  - "nearly three thousand" droid starfighters split between the two battleships (l.2383), with minimal crews plus droids.
  - "possibly the largest assembled in one place since the fiasco at Naboo" (l.2379).
- The second nav stop is 600 ly further on. Outbound Flight is due in about 11 days.
- **Dreadnaught weapons:** Weapons Blister One is one of 4 per Dreadnaught, with 15 crew including 3 gunners. Each fires the forward and aft turbolasers plus its own side's guns.
- **JEDI MELD (lore):**
  - It "permits a group of Jedi to connect their minds so closely as to act as a single person" (l.2401).
  - Obi-Wan: it needs a Master of great power to do it "without killing or destroying the minds".
  - C'baoth has done it 4 times: 3 in training and 1 in the field with 5 others (l.2403–2405).
  - He plans a 20-Jedi meld at the gunnery: "Outbound Flight will be virtually unbeatable" (l.2407).
- **C'baoth's vision:** "The time is coming when all Jedi will be forced to take up arms against a great threat to the Republic. I have foreseen it" (l.2409). "we of Outbound Flight will be all who will be left to breathe life back into the ashes" (l.2411). This is an accurate foresight of Order 66.
- C'baoth Force-resets a dislocated shoulder (l.2425–2426). There are about 200 speeders and swoops aboard. He docks a driver's pay without a hearing: "It was justice".

## Ch. 16: THRAWN vs. THE TRADE FEDERATION (l.2454–2652)
- **Chiss force:** the *Springhawk*, 2 cruisers of the same size (one is the *Whirlwind*) and 9 fighters, 12 ships in all (l.2459, 2504). The trip takes 4 hours. Car'das goes along in a combat suit. Thrawn hails them in Basic.
- Kav launches half of *Keeper*'s droids in three attack groups. Thrawn finds the droids' control-signal maximum range, "ten thousand visvia" (about 16,000 km), and holds his ships at 11,000 (l.2533–2534).
- **Tactic 1 (decoy):**
  - A single fighter repeatedly probes the same vector, revealing that the rolling encryption has only 3 patterns for that group.
  - He records the next code and rebroadcasts it at higher power, and the droids chase the decoy into deep space (l.2576).
  - "whenever possible, reduce matters to a single variable" (l.2578).
- **Tactic 2:** each of the 9 fighters fires a missile hiding a Connor net. The droids shoot the missiles, but the nets fly on and kill 9 droid groups, whose dead fighters then crash into others (l.2585–2591).
- **Tactic 3:** Kav concentrates all his fighters, and Thrawn answers with full-spectrum jamming. All the droids go dormant (l.2610–2613), and the Chiss fly through blasting them. Laser comm is blocked by the debris.
- **Tactic 4:**
  - Hardcell missiles chase 5 fighters. The fighters drop Connor nets, and the inertia of the dead missiles slams them into the Hardcells.
  - The weak spot is the "line junction to the massive external fuel cells" (l.2630). All 6 Hardcells are destroyed and the 7 escort cruisers are hunted down.
  - *Darkvenge*'s hyperdrive is blocked by the captured Vagaari gravity projector ("too close to a planetary mass", l.2638). *Keeper*'s hangar droids self-destruct inside it.
  - *Darkvenge* is left unscratched, with a Chiss cruiser parking missiles at its bridge.
- Doriana: "Everything Mitth'raw'nuruodo does is impossible." **Result:** the whole task force is routed and Thrawn loses no ships (l.2708).

## Ch. 17: Aboard Darkvenge; C'baoth's long-distance push (l.2653–2853)
- Thrawn walks a 20 m corridor of battle droids. On the droidekas: "Interesting design. That shimmering sphere—a small force shield?" Then "now you will send them away", and Kav wilts (l.2663–2667).
- Thrawn publicly calls Car'das a prisoner, for the record (protecting Ar'alani).
- Doriana pitches Outbound Flight as a danger and the Jedi as domineering, aiming to get Thrawn to destroy it.
- **Tech:** Expansionary Fleet transmitters are more powerful because there are no booster networks (l.2731). Thrass is informing Ar'alani, who returns about 5 weeks later.
- Car'das has no planetary-occupation numbers to give. The *Whirlwind* carries the Vagaari gravity projector tucked into its hull, pinning *Darkvenge* (l.2759–2760).
- Thrawn: "I will do whatever necessary to protect those who depend on me ... No more. But no less" (l.2765).
- **Thrawn reads the droideka bluff:**
  - The bridge design "spoke of a people who would never willingly put themselves at more risk": "Architecture is merely another form of art".
  - There are triple blast doors, and Kav stood "too close to the line of fire" (l.2770–2774).
- Ma'Ning teaches Padawans (5–8) on a live D-4 reactor board. C'baoth has ordered full Jedi access to all systems through Commander Omano. The storage core is rooms of crates in crash webbing, with the Jedi school in section 124.
- **C'BAOTH FORCE FEAT:** he Force-pushes Uliar the length of a room **through a comm display**, without being there in person (l.2841–2842). Algrann: "We leave a tyranny run by bureaucrats ... only to end up in one run by Jedi." "Not yet."
