# Outbound Flight (Timothy Zahn, Del Rey 2006): notes part 7, back matter (l.4571–5928)

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. Bundled publisher material, not part of the novel. Cited by line. The excerpt titles in [brackets] are identified from content; the text does not name them.

## Author pages (l.4571–4603)
- About the Author, the By Timothy Zahn list, and the Star Wars Legends intro (Del Rey, May 2014).

## *Choices of One* ch. 1, Zahn (l.4604–4781)
- ISD *Chimaera* with Commander Gilad Pellaeon and Captain Calo Drusan. Grand Admiral Zaarin is aboard ISD *Predominant*.
- "Lord Odo" (masked, 50 biomarkers) travels in the Kazellis-class freighter *Salaban's Hope* with a pilot called "Sorro".
- **Combat:** a tractor-beam trick (a helix yaw) throws debris into the pursuers.
- **Reveal at Endor's moon shield generator:** Senior Captain Thrawn, the Emperor, and Car'das, who is now a pilot. Car'das fell ill after a Dark Jedi encounter and quit his organization.
- Thrawn recommends burning the forest for 100 km and using AT-ATs and juggernauts: "don't dismiss too quickly those natives". Emperor: "Unless ... you're setting a trap." Thrawn: "Of course."
- Warlord Nusoesva is active in the Unknown Regions. Thrawn asks for six more Star Destroyers. The Emperor reads his mind and says to "speak to Lord Vader".
- Drusan announces that Lord Odo has ultimate command of the *Chimaera*. The first officer is Senior Commander Grondarle. The first stop is Wroona, for "specialized equipment".
- Pellaeon notes the only alien senior officer he knows of is Senior Captain Thrawn, and he starts tracing Odo, Sorro and *Salaban's Hope*.

## Old Republic intro and [*The Old Republic: Fatal Alliance*, Sean Williams] (l.4782–4911)
- The intro covers 25,000 years, Naga Sadow, Ruusan and Bane's Rule of Two. It recommends *Deceived*, *Knight Errant* and *Path of Destruction*.
- **Cast:**
  - Padawan Shigar Konshi (18, Kiffu tattoos) and Master Satele Shan. Master Nikil Nobil (Thisspiasian) deems him unready.
  - Larin Moxla, an ex-soldier.
  - The Mandalorian Dao Stryver (flamethrower, darts, rockets), hunting for "Lema Xandret". A dying Nautolan says "Cinzia".
- **Feats:**
  - Shigar Force-pushes the Mandalorian into a wall, crushing his jetpack vent, and deflects his darts.
  - He Force-deflects falling monorail metal spears into the ground.
  - Stryver throws him into a wall and escapes.

## Rise of the Empire intro and [*Dark Lord: The Rise of Darth Vader*, Luceno] (l.4913–5037)
- The intro recommends *Labyrinth of Evil*, *Hard Contact*, *Death Troopers* and *The Han Solo Adventures*.
- **Setting:** Murkhana, in the final hours of the Clone Wars. It is Passel Argente's homeworld.
- **Cast:**
  - Jedi Roan Shryne (his former Master was Nat-Sem).
  - Bol Chatak (Zabrak) and her Padawan Olee Starstone.
  - Commander Salvo (32nd air combat wing), the Victory-class *Gallant*, and Ion Team commando Climber.
- **Tech:** a droid-buster weapon flattens droids within 50 m.
- A medcenter sits over the shield generator, and Shryne refuses the bombardment. **Feat:** Shryne makes a Force-guided jump of about 10 m.

## Rebellion intro and [*Death Star*, Reaves & Perry] (l.5038–5144)
- The intro recommends *Death Star*, *The Mandalorian Armor*, *Shadows of the Empire*, *Tales of the Bounty Hunters* and *Shadows of Mindor*.
- **Setting:** ISD *Steel Talon* over the prison planet Despayre.
- **Cast:** Lt. Cmdr Vil Dance (Black-11, Alpha Squad), Capt. Rax Exeter, Benjo, Raar Anyell and Lude.
- **Tech:** a Lambda-class shuttle (20 m, 1400 g acceleration, Class-One hyperdrive, 3 double blaster and 2 double laser cannons) is stolen by prisoners. Dance destroys it. The Death Star's skeleton is seen.

## New Republic intro and *X-Wing: Rogue Squadron* ch. 1, Stackpole (l.5146–5282)
- The intro recommends *Rogue Squadron*, *Heir to the Empire* and *Before the Storm*.
- Corran Horn hears "you're no Luke Skywalker". The Requiem scenario features the *Redemption*, the corvette *Korolev* and the frigate *Warspite*. Also present: Whistler, Ooryl Qrygg, Rhysati, Nawara Ven and Bror Jace.
- **Combat:**
  - Corran kills the bombers and rams Jace's bomber with his aft shield.
  - A mystery TIE pilot shoots down Ooryl, Rhysati and Nawara and disables Corran.
  - Corran's missile gets him, and Corran wins the scenario with 9 kills.
- The pilot has Hoth, Endor and Bakura tabs; the dot on the Endor tab means he survived the Death Star run. He reports to Admiral Ackbar. This is implied to be Tycho Celchu, who is not named.

## New Jedi Order intro and [*Vector Prime* ch. 1, Salvatore] (l.5284–5501)
- The intro covers the Yuuzhan Vong and recommends *Vector Prime*.
- **Cast:** Leia, Mara (sick) and Jaina (16) aboard the *Jade Sabre*, with the Noghri Bolpuhr and C-3PO.
- **Setting:** Rhommamool vs. Osarian. The Mon Cal cruiser *Mediator* is under Commander Ackdool.
- **Combat:**
  - Rodian Captain Grappa flies Z-95 Headhunters.
  - **Jaina** flies evasive maneuvers by the Force, "three moves ahead" (l.5380).
  - Jedi Wurth Skidder kills a Headhunter, and Leia rebukes him.
- The Red Knights of Life stone droids at the Square of Hopeful Redemption.
- **Lore:** Mara senses Nom Anor as a blank: "he had nothing to do with the Force" (l.5485). Nom Anor crushes a stone in his fist (l.5497).

## Legacy intro, [*LotF: Betrayal* ch. 1, Allston] and [*FotJ: Outcast*, Allston] (l.5503–5727)
- The intro recommends *Betrayal*, *Millennium Falcon*, *Crosscurrent* and *Outcast*.
- **Betrayal:**
  - Luke dreams "He doesn't exist", with molten gold eyes, foreshadowing Caedus.
  - Jacen Solo and Ben Skywalker (13) raid the Dammant Killers missile plant on Adumar.
  - **Feats:** Jacen reads Testan's mind to find a hidden door ("true from a certain point of view"). He deflects blaster bolts back into 20+ troopers while advancing. Ben records holocam evidence. They make a Force-cushioned drop of about 12 m.
- **Outcast:**
  - An ISD named *Gilad Pellaeon*. Jagged Fel is the Imperial Head of State, alongside Jaina, the Bothan Senator Tiurrg Drey'lye and Chief of State Daala.
  - Valin Horn sees "Not-Mirax" and a "not" Corran as impostors and lunges. This opens his madness arc.

## Star Wars Legends novels timeline (l.5729–5928)
- It runs from Before the Republic (*Into the Void*) through the Old Republic to Rise of the Empire.
- The 32 BBY header groups **Episode I, *Rogue Planet*, *Outbound Flight*, *The Approaching Storm*** (l.5756–5759), even though the text dates OF five years after Naboo.
- Rebellion era: *Allegiance*, then *Choices of One* (l.5810–5811).
- New Republic era: the Thrawn Trilogy (l.5841–5844), the Hand of Thrawn duology, *Survivor's Quest* (l.5870, the OF sequel).
- It then runs through the NJO and Legacy eras to *Crucible* (45 ABY). The final line is the footnote "*An eBook novella" (l.5928).
