Character quick bios:
- **Mitth'raw'nuruodo (Thrawn):** Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet commander of Picket Force Two (*Springhawk*), Eighth Family, Trial-born. "the youngest of my people to ever hold the position of Force Commander" (l.3029). He believes in pre-emptive strikes against the Chiss doctrine, which leads to his exile (seen in *Mist Encounter*).
- **Mitth'ras'safis (Thrass):** Syndic and Thrawn's brother. He dies with Lorana landing Outbound Flight.
- **Admiral Ar'alani:** Thrawn's superior, in white. She shields him quietly and repelled a Far Outsider recon force.
- **Aristocra Chaf'orm'bintrano:** Fifth Ruling Family. Thrawn faces him down with the gravity projector.
- **Jorus C'baoth:** Jedi Master who leads Outbound Flight. He has an immense telekinetic range and leads the Jedi meld; he turns tyrant and falls to the dark side. A tissue sample is sent to Coruscant (l.1878), the clone hint.
- **Lorana Jinzler:** C'baoth's Padawan, knighted during the book. The last Jedi alive aboard, she flies the ship alone.
- **Obi-Wan Kenobi / Anakin Skywalker (14):** on Barlok and early in the voyage; removed at Roxuli by Palpatine.
- **Justyn Ma'Ning:** Jedi Master on D-4, killed holding the hull together.
- **Kinman Doriana:** Palpatine's assistant and Sidious's agent ("Commander Stratis"). He pushes the button and then recruits Car'das.
- **Siv Kav:** Neimoidian Vicelord, *Darkvenge*. Killed by his own reflected bolt.
- **Jorj Car'das (23):** Corellian junior smuggler and the main POV. He is Thrawn's double agent against the Vagaari and is later recruited to an information network.
- **Dubrak Qennto / Maris Ferasi:** *Bargain Hunter* captain and copilot. Maris loves Thrawn.
- **Dean Jinzler:** Lorana's resentful brother (*Survivor's Quest* setup).
- **Chas Uliar, the Pressors (Dillian, Jorad, Katarin), Pakmillu, Omano:** colonists and crew. The Pressors and Uliar are among the 57 survivors.
- **The Miskara / Vagaari:** nomadic slaver warlord-species that uses captives as living shields.
- **Voss Parck / Mosh Barris:** Imperials in *Mist Encounter* who recruit the exiled Thrawn.
