# Outbound Flight (Timothy Zahn, Del Rey 2006): notes part 5, Ch. 22–24

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. This part covers FULL.txt l.3603–4241. Citation "OF ch. N (l.x)".

## Ch. 22: The battle (l.3603–3790)
- C'baoth on living shields: "The most evil and cowardly defense concept ever created". He plans to "shoot between the hostages".
- **JEDI MELD MASS MIND ATTACK (headline Force feat):**
  - "Stretch out to the Force ... and then, to the Vagaari" (l.3623).
  - The whole Vagaari flagship bridge collapses into "helpless babbling" (l.3629–3630), across **100+ warships**.
  - Doriana: "a terrifying feat ... by a relative handful of Jedi" (l.3637–3638).
- **Thrawn's countermove:**
  - The droid starfighters launch against the Vagaari, not against Outbound Flight.
  - The 6 droids planted with Car'das carry a hidden secondary control pattern and massacre the Vagaari flagship bridge (l.3646–3649).
  - Hull-skimming at 5 m cuts between the hostage bubbles, and all the Geroon hostages in view live (l.3651–3652).
  - "over a quarter of the alien warships" are out of the fight after the first attack (l.3653).
- **Meld backlash:** Lorana is overwhelmed by the death screams of thousands of Vagaari (l.3655–3656). This is the same effect Sidious predicted.
- **Thrawn's "Ch'tra" attack:**
  - The Chiss ships fly through the turbolaser and point-defense zone unchallenged, because the gunners are the melded Jedi. They pass inside the shields.
  - Using tech readouts, they destroy the weapons blisters and then the shield generators (l.3661–3663). Thrawn stops and offers surrender.
  - The weapons blisters were where the Jedi were stationed, so nearly all the Jedi die (l.3681–3683).
- **Ma'Ning Force feat:** dying, he holds metal sheets over a hull gash with the Force while droids weld them. Earlier he lifted a girder off Lorana (l.3676, 3686).
- **C'BAOTH FALLS:**
  - Burned and blood-spattered, he says: "The role of the Jedi is to lead and guide, and to destroy all threats."
  - He **Force-chokes Thrawn over the comm display from another ship** (l.3712–3716).
  - Lorana feels it: "Jorus C'baoth, Jedi Master, had gone over to the dark side" (l.3719).
  - Lorana opens a jammed door with the Force and suppresses her leg pain.
- **The red switch (Doriana presses Thrawn's red-rimmed switch):**
  - Waves 1–2: the starfighters break off from the Vagaari and ram the Outbound Flight hulls at the interior blast-door positions to warp the doors.
  - Waves 3–4: they open the Dreadnaughts to space (l.3735–3744).
  - Then 10 starfighter pairs carrying a "fat cylinder" fly into the holes, and a pale blue haze follows (l.3748–3750).
- **Radiation bombs:**
  - "Once inside the protective barrier of a war vessel's outer armor, they explode into a killing wave of radiation ... destroying all life" (l.3757). One per Dreadnaught is enough.
  - "They were intended for use against the largest of the Vagaari war vessels" (l.3759).
  - C'baoth dies and the choke releases (l.3751–3755).
- Some Vagaari warships and civilians escape. Thrawn: "No warrior ever has the full depth of control that he would like" (l.3764).
- Doriana plans to kill Thrawn in a boarding melee. Lorana senses no life above her, and the pylons are open to vacuum.

## Ch. 23: Aftermath (l.3791–4030)
- Car'das is freed in a rescue ball. Thrawn: "Luring the Vagaari into my trap ... Or had you forgotten that the Chiss do not engage in preemptive attacks?" (l.3800). Car'das's betrayal had been Thrawn's plan.
- The spy accusations against Car'das had protected Ar'alani, who arrives with transports for the freed Geroons (l.3802).
- Doriana: "I was the one who actually pushed the button" (l.3812). Kav gives Doriana a hold-out blaster. About 50,000 dead.
- Thrass to Car'das: "The morality of an action is not determined by the results" (l.3849).
- Aristocra Chaf'orm'bintrano arrives with a Fifth Family fleet of 12 ships, flagship *Chaf Exalted*.
- Thrawn: "It would be better for Outbound Flight to be destroyed than to let any single family claim it" (l.3889).
- Ar'alani detains Thrawn. "The role of a warrior is to protect the Chiss people. The warrior's own survival is of only secondary importance" (l.3918). Pesfavri is the nearest Defense Fleet base.
- Car'das and Thrass stay behind, planning to scuttle the wreck into the sun.
- **Lorana:**
  - She pries a turbolift door open with the Force (l.3943). D-4, D-5 and D-6 are dead, and droids have restored airtightness.
  - Before the Aristocra, Doriana claims "full salvage rights" for the Republic to deny the Fifth Family the prize (l.3956). He sees Thrawn's approving smile.
  - **Combat:** Lorana deflects a blaster bolt from Thrass with her green blade (l.3968–3970).
- There are 57 survivors, including children, in the core.
- **LORANA:** "I can fly Outbound Flight ... One single Jedi" (l.3995–3997).
- Thrass: a star cluster 2 days away is being fortified as a Defense Fleet emergency refuge, with at least 10 habitable worlds (l.4008). Lorana will use "Jedi navigational techniques" (l.4014). Thrass gets the Delta-12 Skysprite.
- Lorana asks Car'das to find Dean Jinzler (Senate Support Services, Coruscant).
- Outbound Flight jumps away while Thrawn stalls the Aristocra (l.4027–4028).

## Ch. 24: The last flight; Kav; Car'das recruited (l.4031–4241)
- **LORANA FORCE FEAT (headline):**
  - She single-handedly runs D-1's systems through the Force, sensing their status and holding the controls.
  - The runaway hyperdrive is "running blazingly hot".
  - She navigates by the Jedi technique through a packed star cluster (l.4031–4043).
- **Chiss politics:**
  - The Aristocra revokes Thrawn's position and demands that he resign and renounce his Trial-born status.
  - Thrawn's leverage is the gravity projector, "the device I used to bring both Outbound Flight and the Vagaari fleet out of hyperspace". It would go to the Eighth Family if he were expelled (l.4073–4077). The Aristocra backs down.
  - Ar'alani: "there may come a point where we can no longer stand with you" (l.4084). This foreshadows his exile.
- **The crash:**
  - The hyperdrive dies midway through the cluster. The other hyperdrives are dead too; the radiation "scorched a great deal of the delicate equipment" (l.4096).
  - The ship is in a decaying orbit around a planetoid (15 orbits). Thrass finds an enclosed, gravel-filled valley.
  - The survivors are on D-4, so D-4 has to be on top and D-1 at the bottom. Lorana and Thrass sacrifice themselves (l.4129–4141).
  - Lorana: "Your people will come here someday ... enough food and supplies to last for generations." Thrass: "I hope that someday humans and Chiss will be able to work side by side in peace."
- **Kav's death (combat/tech):**
  - Doriana refuses to shoot Thrawn: "I would as soon shatter thousand-year-old crystal as kill a being such as this" (l.4156).
  - Kav fires, and Thrawn's **reversed-polarity droideka shield** around the desk bounces the bolt back and kills him (l.4159–4164).
- **Far Outsiders lore:**
  - The Chiss HAVE met them: "a small reconnaissance force ... savage ferocity before it was finally repulsed" by Ar'alani at the far edge of the Ascendancy (l.4170–4174).
  - That is why Ar'alani tolerated the strike on the Vagaari: it avoided a two-front war.
- Doriana notes Sidious's "hatred of nonhumans" (l.4181). Thrawn keeps the blaster as a souvenir.
- **Car'das's future:** Doriana recruits him into smuggling and a "private information network" (l.4191), the start of his Legends career.
- Thrawn asks Car'das never to tell Maris: "There are all too few idealists in this universe" (l.4201). "Farewell ... Jorj." Car'das lies to Maris that Outbound Flight flew away.
- The D-4 survivors live through the crash. Dillian Pressor's "collision warning ... just sort of popped into my head" (l.4235), a Force hint. Uliar will watch over the Pressors. This sets up *Survivor's Quest*.
