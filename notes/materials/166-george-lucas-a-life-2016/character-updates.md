- [Real-world · George Lucas, Bob Iger, and Kathleen Kennedy (Jones, 2016)] Disney sale and *The Force Awakens,* typography locked. Lucas’s condition: Disney must use his story treatments; he will not hand them over until the deal is done. Iger, publicly, said the treatments “had a lot of potential.” Sale signed October 30, 2012, $4.05 billion. Disney then “had disregarded the story treatments he had given them in 2012.” Lucas to Charlie Rose, Christmas Day 2015: “They decided they didn’t want to use those stories, they decided they were gonna go do their own thing.” Kennedy told reporters “he really liked it,” which Jones calls “a tepid response”; she also said “he can’t do that unless he’s running everything.” Watching the film, Lucas said, was “an awkward reality,” “like attending the wedding of your grown child after you’ve been divorced.” “They wanted to do a retro movie. I don’t like that.” “I will go my way, and I’ll let them go their way.” Jones: “George Lucas had given up control. But that didn’t mean he was ever going to be happy about it.” Same Rose interview, on the first line of the obituary: “I *tried,*” he said, laughing.
