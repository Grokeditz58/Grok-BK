# Jones, *George Lucas: A Life* (2016): notes — ch. 3 “The Right Horse,” 1967 (pp. 68–85)

Typography locked from the publisher XHTML. Citations: Jones p. N; endnotes as Jones Notes, ch. 3 n. Y.

Graduate return to USC, January 1967; loathes Jerry Lewis directing class. Bob Dalva recommends him as USIA replacement; works under editor Verna Fields on *Journey to the Pacific* (LBJ / Manila summit). Hates government constraints (Lady Bird angles; LBJ bald spot; a Korea horses shot cut as “a little too fascist”). **Lucas, p. 70 (n.5):** “I don’t like this. At that point, I was really wanting to be an editor and a cameraman… [and] in the course of… doing this, I sort of said, ‘You know, maybe I want to be a director. I don’t want people to tell me what to do.”

Meets Marcia Griffin, assistant editor from Sandler Films, also born with a Modesto connection (born in Modesto). She finds him talented; he is initially wary. Teaching assistant for Gene Peterson’s navy/air force cameraman class; uses the military crew, color film, and clearances to make the *Breakout* idea.

**“Used universe,” p. 73, Jones’s wording:** like Godard’s *Alphaville,* he would use the present as the future, “make 1960s-era clothing and machinery look futuristic yet still somehow shopworn and vaguely familiar—the “used universe” mentality he would later bring to *Star Wars.*” Natural lighting; locations at LAX, Van Nuys, UCLA parking.

## KEY: Arthur Lipsett, *21-87,* and the Force (pp. 73–75)

Lucas watched Lipsett’s NFB montage “twenty or thirty” times (n.14, Pollock, *Skywalking,* 47). **Lucas, pp. 73–74 (n.15, Steve Silberman, “Life after Darth,” *Wired,* May 2005):** “It had a very powerful effect on me. It was very much the kind of thing that I wanted to do. I was extremely influenced by that particular movie.” Sound/picture “free-floating” (Walter Murch, n.16).

**Typography locked from the XHTML, p. 74 (n.17, also Silberman, “Life after Darth”):** “In one memorable moment—especially as it affected Lucas—Lipsett inserts over images of fluttering pigeons a snippet of an existential discussion between Warren S. McCulloch, a pioneer in artificial intelligence, and cinematographer Roman Kroitor. As McCulloch contends that human beings are merely complicated machines, Kroitor counters that it can’t be that simple or soulless, arguing that as humans contemplate the world around them, “they become aware of some kind of force… behind this apparent mask which we see in front of us, and they call it God.” A decade later, Lucas would acknowledge that his own version of the Force, while based on the universal idea of life forces, was a tip of the hat to Lipsett, “an echo of that phrase in *21-87*.””

Lock: lowercase “force” in the Kroitor quotation; ellipsis character … after “force”; capitalized “the Force” and “an echo of that phrase in *21-87*” as Jones’s report of Lucas (Silberman 2005). Jones’s intervening gloss: “while based on the universal idea of life forces.” Do not treat the Kroitor line as Lucas’s own wording.

**Naming, p. 75:** Lucas: “I think that’s one reason I started calling most of my [college] movies by numbers.” (n.18, Pollock 47) Film titled *THX 1138 4EB*; number across the forehead as in Vorkapich. Lucas “always insisted that the letters THX ‘[don’t] mean anything’” (n.19, Kline, *GL Interviews,* 6). Robbins thought he liked the symmetrical letters; others note phone number 849-1138.

Shot over three days in January 1967; guerrilla / exhausted; sleeps at the Moviola. Also makes *anyone lived in a pretty [how] town* (e. e. cummings; color Cinemascope against advice). Sound campaign: later John Williams and theater acoustics (Jones, p. 77). Begins dating Marcia after a non-date at AFI. *The Emperor* (Bob Hudson doc; wanted Wolfman Jack first; fake credits as an attack on the ten-minute limit). Marcia moves in on Portola. **Completed *THX 1138 4EB*:** date flash 5-14-2187 as a nod to Lipsett; “SEXACTE”; “I was into trying to create emotions through pure cinematic techniques…” (p. 81, n.42). George Sr. at a USC festival: “I think we put our money on the right horse.” (p. 81, n.43)

Carl Foreman / *Mackenna’s Gold* scholarship (spelled Mackenna, two n’s, in Jones); Lippincott drops out, Lucas goes; hates the “opulence”; makes tone poem *6-18-67* instead of a promo. Samuel Warner Memorial Scholarship, beating Walter Murch (later hires him on *Graffiti*). Warner lot a “ghost town”; only *Finian’s Rainbow* shooting. Meets Francis Ford Coppola: Lucas, asked if he sees anything interesting, says “Nope. Not yet.” (pp. 85, n.50)
