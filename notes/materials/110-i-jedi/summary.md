# I, Jedi: summary (Michael A. Stackpole, Bantam Spectra 1998)

Source: the owner's epub, eISBN 978-0-307-79642-4, read in full (FULL.txt l.1–10780). Cites are "l.x" in FULL.txt. Feats come from the novel only (l.228–7978); bundled excerpts are excluded, including the X-Wing: Rogue Squadron excerpt in which Corran appears (l.9247–9499).

## Plot
- **Mirax gone:**
  - After an Invid ambush (l.272–352), Corran wakes hearing Mirax scream and knows she is gone (l.488–492).
  - Cracken admits she was undercover against Tavira's Invids and won't say where (l.626–672).
  - Luke tests Corran: his mind is "locked up pretty tightly" (l.884); "flashburns" (l.922).
  - Whistler's message reveals that Corran's grandfather was the Jedi Master Nejaa Halcyon (l.1056–1072).
- **The academy (as Keiran Halcyon):**
  - Corran can't move a pebble (l.1660–1684) but makes the whole class "see" a floating boulder (l.1856–1864).
  - He absorbs gas-vent heat to lift Tionne (l.1994–1996).
  - Gantoris is burned from within after hinting at a hidden teacher (l.2104–2176).
  - Kun, speaking through Kyp, smashes Corran's absorption shield (l.2768–2792). Kyp steals Mara's Z-95 (l.2856).
  - Corran beats a listless Luke in a spar (l.3198–3222). Luke's spirit leaves his body (l.3302–3308).
  - Corran feels Carida die (l.3512–3532) and stops a possessed Streen with an illusion (l.3592–3598).
  - Kun offers Mirax for Luke's death (l.3650–3664). Corran feeds Kun false despair through the Streen link (l.3850–3858).
  - At Kun's temple Kun melts the detonators and breaks Corran (l.3950–4022). Mara rescues him (l.4024–4072), and the students destroy Kun (l.4092).
- **Corellia:**
  - Corran leaves the academy (l.4110–4140). Booster supplies false IDs (l.4264–4462).
  - Rostek Horn reveals Jedi data hidden in flower DNA (l.4592–4596) and Nejaa's trunk (l.4720–4734).
  - Corran dreams Nejaa's last fight against Nikkos Tyris (l.4772–4824).
- **Undercover with the Invids as Jenos Idanian:**
  - Stops the hijacking of Tinta Blue Seven by mind trick (l.4984–4988) and joins the Survivors (l.5254–5336).
  - Flies against Rogue Squadron at Xa Fel (l.5740–5824). Encysts Tavira's advisor probe (l.5958–5960).
  - Beats Remart to save Elegos A'kla (l.6168–6296) and refuses to become Tavira's consort (l.6398–6534).
- **The Jedi of Courkrus:**
  - Builds a lightsaber (l.6664–6708). Ghost of Oonaar (l.6726–6742); Fastblast and Blackstar raids (l.6816–6862).
  - Survives Shala's LX-1 trap with the firestorm vortex (l.6918–6942). The Invid crews break up (l.7046–7066).
  - Five Jensaarai ambush him; Luke arrives (l.7162–7198).
- **Susevfi:**
  - Palace assault; Mirax is woken from hibernation (l.7446–7508).
  - Duel with the Saarai-kaar; Corran's blade shorts on cortosis (l.7610–7650).
  - Corran mind-routs the Invidious (l.7698–7712). The memnis of Nejaa's death reconciles the Jensaarai (l.7786–7884).
- **Epilogue:** Corran stays with Rogue Squadron (l.7930–7938), Luke says "we're even" (l.7940), and Corran destroys Exar Kun's statue and temple with torpedoes (l.7948–7970).

## Strongest feats
1. **Corran routs a Star Destroyer with his mind** (l.7698–7714). From Susevfi's surface he pushes his awareness in a cone up to the Invidious in the gas-giant ring, finds Tavira, plants doubt, and replaces the Errant Venture on her display with a Super Star Destroyer (built from Lusankya memories) plus a Sun Crusher. She orders "It's a trap, a Jedi trap… Far away!" (l.7712). "'size matters not.' And telekinesis is not the only way to move the Invidious" (l.7698).
2. **Absorbs the LX-1 laser-flechette barrage** (l.6922–6942).
   - He takes "every stray erg" into himself and turns it into a telekinetic firestorm vortex. It rips the warehouse roof off, pitches the exploding chemical drums skyward and contains the blast (l.6922–6928).
   - He then projects a giant-Jedi vision into "every mind I could find" across the city (l.6932).
   - Cost: his clothes burn off and he is left Force-burnt-out (l.6936–6942).
   - Related absorption feats:
     - Catches a blaster bolt barehanded and uses its energy to rip the gun away (l.6828–6830).
     - Catches a training-remote bolt 2 cm from Mara's face (l.3044).
     - Absorbs a stun baton (l.4306–4308).
     - Absorbs Kyp/Kun's Force blow into a shield (l.2768–2782).
     - Nejaa drains Tyris's lightsaber and crushes him with the energy while run through (l.4820–4822; memnis l.7824: "the rarest of all Jedi gifts").
3. **Mass and targeted illusions:**
   - An unconscious floating-boulder illusion fools about 11 trainees and briefly Luke (l.1856–1864): "how easily you managed to make us all see" (l.1916).
   - Laanars shoots his own thigh three times (l.4984–4986).
   - An image projected into Tycho's mind mid-battle (l.5740–5742).
   - Kun's spy link edited (l.3850–3858). Tavira's advisor encysted in holodrama memories (l.5958–5960).
   - An 8-second memory blank on hotel staff (l.6764).
   - A Nikkos Tyris image freezes the Saarai-kaar (l.7646–7648).

Also of note:
- Sphere of responsibility: 16 m fine control, 250 m focused for bolts. Puts a vision into Dorsk 81's mind from 0.5 km (l.2638). Senses a whole city (l.6770).
- Shoots down two of Tycho's proton torpedoes with lasers (l.5814–5818).
- Spar win over Luke (l.3198–3222), qualified: Luke was listless.
- Builds a working lightsaber in 2.5 days; silver blade 133 cm (l.6700–6708).
- Beats three Jensaarai: Red, Blue, Orange (l.7188–7192).
- Luke handles five Jensaarai with ease (l.7636), redirects bolts into an E-web (l.7482) and levitates Corran (l.7558).
- Exar Kun melts detonators remotely and hurls 30 kg of charges into the lake (l.3950, 3964).

## Anti-feats
- **No telekinesis:** "couldn't make a ripple in a cup of water" (l.1818). Can't levitate or leap (l.2314). Can only twitch his saber hilt (l.3066). Can't save Ooryl (l.5780).
- **Exar Kun:** goes in and out of Corran's mind before he can armor it (l.3626). Breaks his arm, ribs and leg with a flick (l.3956–4022). Kun's gale crushes his shield (l.2780–2782).
- **Courkrus:** the illusion fails on the Jensaarai (l.7164). "If you hadn't arrived, the five of them would have killed me" (l.7198).
- **Lightsaber:** can't redirect bolts like Luke (l.7474). His blade shorts out on a cortosis bracer (l.7642–7644). The 300 cm variable blade fails because the diamond was synthetic (l.6910–6914, 6990).
- **Force burnout** after the vortex (l.6936–6942). The ysalamiri leave him "back where I started" (l.7228–7232).

## Lore highlights
- **Halcyon line:** weak at telekinesis, with a stubborn rear-guard tradition (l.2316, 4622). Nejaa excelled at mental projection and energy absorption, "very rare" (l.4626).
- **Corellian Jedi:** they strike memorial coins on reaching Master; Corran's Jedi Credit (l.940–942). Data was hidden in plant DNA junk (l.4592–4596). The Halcyon blade is silver (l.1084, 4640).
- **Alter/Control/Sense taxonomy** and Alter Mind (l.1894–1916). Sphere of responsibility (l.2596, 2638). Ranik Solusar's "galaxy-sized" sphere (l.2596).
- **Exar Kun:** Massassi temples as power foci (l.2628). Killed Vodo-Siosk Baas (l.2968–2976). Corran: Kun used the temple "to infect Kyp" (l.7958).
- **Jensaarai:**
  - "Sith word for the hidden followers of truth" (l.7854). Masters learned Sith techniques from an antiquarian (l.7854).
  - Wear spun cortosis armor (l.7286). Ranks: Apprentices, Defenders, Saarai-kaar (l.7286).
  - Luke: the rank and file were taught the Jedi way by masters who had accepted Sith philosophy (l.7860).
- **Caamasi memnii:** shareable emotive memories, limited by consanguinity; transferable to bonded Jedi (l.6568–6576). Ylenic It'kla was Elegos's uncle (l.6576–6584).
- **Lightsaber build:** components bound through the Force on first charge; the Clone Wars two-day method (l.6664–6670). Gems: Durindfire, diamond and emerald (l.6674).
- **Invids:** Tavira's Force-sensitive advisors are limited and half-trained, sensing threats aimed at her (l.5940–5944).
- **Caamas:** destroyed after the Clone Wars; "Caamasi" means "friend from afar" (l.6092–6094).

## Atrocities
- Kyp, under Kun's influence, destroys Carida with the Sun Crusher; millions die (l.3512–3532).
- Kun tortures and kills Gantoris (l.2134–2176).
- Remart murders civilians (l.5704) and loots the Caamasi (l.6168–6244). Tavira has Remart shot (l.6366–6370) and threatens to sterilize Kerilt (l.6398–6408).
