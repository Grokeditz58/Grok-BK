Character quick bios:
- **Corran Horn / "Keiran Halcyon" / "Jenos Idanian":**
  - Ex-CorSec Rogue Squadron pilot; first-person narrator. Grandson of Nejaa Halcyon (l.1068).
  - Nearly no telekinesis (l.1818, 2314, 3066). His strengths are the Halcyon gifts: mental projection and energy absorption (l.4626).
  - Mass illusion of a floating boulder (l.1856–1864). Absorbs the LX-1 barrage into a firestorm vortex (l.6922–6928). Mind-routs Tavira's ISD (l.7698–7712).
  - Broken by Exar Kun (l.3956–4022).
- **Luke Skywalker:**
  - Master of the new academy. Beaten in a spar while distracted (l.3198–3222).
  - Handles five Jensaarai "with such ease" (l.7636). Redirects bolts and kills an E-web (l.7482). Levitates Corran (l.7558).
- **Mirax Terrik Horn:** Corran's wife, an undercover agent for Cracken. Seized by the Jensaarai at Nal Hutta and held in a Jedi hibernation trance (l.7292, 7496–7508). Stuns the Saarai-kaar (l.7650).
- **Exar Kun:** Sith Lord spirit on Yavin 4, 4,000 years dead (l.2628–2636). Speaks through Kyp (l.2774–2778). Kills Gantoris (l.2134–2176). Melts detonators remotely and breaks Corran with a flick (l.3950–3958). Destroyed by the students (l.4092).
- **Leonia Tavira:** ex-Moff's widow, Invid leader aboard the ISD Invidious (l.570–586). Tiny, violet-eyed, carries a quirt (l.5852–5856). Flees from Corran's illusion (l.7712).
- **Saarai-kaar / Jensaarai:** Sith-taught Jedi splinter in cortosis armor (l.7284–7286). The Saarai-kaar is the widow of a Nejaa-era dark Jedi (l.7742–7768, 7850). Offered academy training (l.7878–7884).
- **Nejaa Halcyon:** Corellian Jedi Master (l.1068). Drains Nikkos Tyris's lightsaber and kills him with the energy as he dies (l.4820–4822, 7824).
- **Elegos A'kla:** Caamasi Trustant whom Corran saves from Remart (l.6168–6296). Carries the memnis of Nejaa's death (l.6588, 7790–7838).
- **Kyp Durron:** the strongest apprentice (l.2556, 2618). Kun's pawn; destroys Carida with the Sun Crusher (l.3512–3532).
- **Mara Jade:** Corran resists her probe (l.2680–2682). She rescues him from Kun's temple (l.4024–4072).
- **Others:** Gantoris (l.1482, 2134); Streen (l.3592); Kam Solusar (l.1356); Tionne (l.1500); Booster Terrik and the Errant Venture (l.4264–4378); Rostek Horn (l.4498–4734); Jacob Nive and the Survivors (l.5254–5336); Remart Sasyru (l.5558, 6236–6260, 6366).
