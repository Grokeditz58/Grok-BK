# EA2 The Bartokk Assassins: notes, whole book

## Front matter (OCR l.1–66)
- Cover, series ad (#1–3), title page ("Game Book" logo variant), copyright page: ISBN 0-439-10139-5, first printing October 1999. Then the Introduction heading.

## Ch. 1: To Calamar (p.7–13)
- Qui-Gon reasons that 13 Bartokks are still hunting the ships: "the best place to hide a needle is among other needles."
- The test pilot is **Bama Vook**, a Talz. His navigator droid is LE-PR34 "Leeper", and his son is Chup-Chup. They hang out at the **Ion Sandbox** saloon.
- **Rune Haako** (a lieutenant here) and **Daultay Dofine** arrive disguised as Duros traders, under orders to find and silence the informant. Dofine spots an eye-patched Talz.

## Ch. 2: Bama Vook (p.17–22)
- Bama is just under 2 m tall, wears a vocabulator and uses a fake eye-patch. **He sent the data card.**
- He stole the spike-covered freighter holding all 50 fighters and the **Neimoidian prototype hyperdrive** while Leeper ran a distraction. It turns out to be the Bartokks' own freighter. It sits in **Docking Bay 28** beside his YT-1300 *Metron Burner*.
- The Neimoidians see the group speed off.

## Ch. 3: Docking Bay ambush (p.25–31)
- Bama has a Z-95 refitted for space with two seats.
- Qui-Gon senses "a disturbance in the Force". **He yanks Trinkatta clear of a spear and blurs aside from three more** (p.27). The Bartokks communicate telepathically.
- Chup-Chup is taken hostage. Each Bartokk fires 4 crossbow bolts at once, and three of them hold 12 bolts on Bama: "you know it is unlikely you could prevent all twelve arrows."
- Game Book interstitial (p.32).

## Ch. 4: Obi-Wan alone (p.35–39)
- A fourth Bartokk drops a stun net. **Qui-Gon is knocked unconscious** (anti-feat), along with Bama, and Leeper is shorted out. Obi-Wan dives clear "faster than the eye could follow".
- **Obi-Wan blurs "like a hostile wind" and cuts off all 12 arms of three Bartokks in one pass** (p.37).
- He ruptures a chemical tank, and two of them are "liquefied". He takes the third one's legs, then Force-pushes it into the spill (p.38).

## Ch. 5–6: Saber throw, bomb, launch (p.43–55)
- **Force-guided saber throw** severs the net-thrower's crossbow arm, and the claw fires two bolts into the Bartokk itself. The saber loops back: "used the Force to control his weapon's every move" (p.43).
- The Bartokk freighter is about 70 m by 25 m, with a sensor dish and a six-winged fighter moored to its hull.
- Obi-Wan accidentally re-shocks the net (anti-feat). Trinkatta knows the 10-button Bartokk remote. Trinkatta's count leaves 9 Bartokks.
- Obi-Wan cuts through the lift floor and climbs down a cable. He disarms a proton-grenade booby trap in the Z-95: he studied bomb disposal and traced its contours with the Force (p.53).
- He senses Chup-Chup's fear "from across space" to pick the right sensor blip (p.54).

## Ch. 7: Space battle (p.59–68)
- The Bartokk fighter has a 3-crew hive mind acting as "a single twelve-armed pilot". Obi-Wan loops away from a homing torpedo, then kills the fighter with a concussion missile.
- **3 droid starfighters:** he makes one crash into an asteroid. His engine backdraft rotates two meteoroids together and crushes the second. He shoots out the freighter's sensor dish, which cuts the third off from its control unit. The freighter's spikes shoot that drone down while he takes out the shield generator (p.62–65).
- Docking: poison gas. He uses his breather and cuts through the hatch.

## Ch. 8: Aboard (p.71–76)
- He bisects two Bartokks, throws their poisoned arrows to kill two fragments and minces the rest. He feels revulsion.
- The slaving-collar tracker leads to the hold, which has 22 droid fighters.
- **He Force-flips the magnetic-field switch to blow 2 Bartokks into space, then flips it back** (p.75).
- He saves Chup-Chup from the depressurising airlock (two yellow buttons). **Chup-Chup is 2.2 m tall.**

## Ch. 9: Corulag (p.79–85)
- 25 fighters were moved to a second Bartokk freighter, the Bartokks' backup plan.
- **Anti-feat:** a Force probe of a Bartokk mind finds it "maddeningly intricate, with trillions of nerve cells thinking of nothing but murder". It still blurts out "Corulag", then kills itself with mandible toxin (p.82).
- He plants the proton grenade under the nav computer. The last Bartokk drops its weapons to fight bare-clawed, and Obi-Wan cuts it into a dozen parts. The freighter explodes.

## Ch. 10: Regroup and Sidious (p.88–91)
- Leeper: the prototype could deploy the fighters "almost anywhere within the next three sectors in a matter of minutes". Building them in Republic space would break "more than a dozen treaties".
- Obi-Wan warns **Corulag Academy**. Rhinnal answers only with static, so the group heads to Rhinnal on the *Metron Burner*.
- Sidious to Gunray: "Do not bother. I have someone else lined up for the job." Teaser: *The Fury of Darth Maul*.
- Back cover (partial OCR): "…They have also kidnapped an innocent child. The Bartokks must be stopped."
