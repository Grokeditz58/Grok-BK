# EA2 The Bartokk Assassins: summary

- Full read of the scan (OCR l.1–2322). The epub is complete.

## Top feats (Obi-Wan carries the book)
1. **Cuts off all 12 arms of 3 Bartokks in one blurred pass** (p.37).
2. **Force-guided boomerang saber throw** (p.43).
3. **Force-throws a switch to vent 2 Bartokks into space**, then reseals the hold (p.75).
4. **Wins a space battle alone in a Z-95:** a Bartokk fighter and 3 hyperdrive droid fighters, using an asteroid, meteoroids moved by his engine backdraft and a cut control link (p.59–65).
5. Kills about 9 Bartokks in total, wiping out the hive's freighter crew. Senses fear "from across space" (p.54).
- Qui-Gon blurs aside from 3 spears (p.27).

## Anti-feats
- Qui-Gon is knocked out by a stun net (p.36).
- Obi-Wan's Force mind probe fails on the Bartokk's distributed brain (p.82). He fumbles the net remote twice.

## Lore
- Bartokk tech: spiked freighters, six-winged 3-crew fighters, stun nets, poison crossbows, suicide toxin.
- The TF's prototype hyperdrive is a treaty violation. 25 fighters are still headed for Corulag.
- Characters: Bama Vook, Chup-Chup, Leeper, Daultay Dofine.
