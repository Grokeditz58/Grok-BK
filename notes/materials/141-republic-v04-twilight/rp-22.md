## Feats / Force / combat (#22)
- **Force Lightning (p.77–78) — CRITICAL:** Explicitly named and depicted. Quinlan threatens then channels blue-white jagged lightning that suspends/tortures Pol Secura to confession (Chom Frey Kaa / Senate). Hand gesture: bandaged fingers clawed/extended; continues after name given. Aayla witnesses Master using Sith-coded power. Limit: rage/interrogation use, not battlefield AOE; stops when he has the name then shifts to lethal intent with saber; no lasting lightning after Pol falls.

- **Mace susceptibility warning (p.83):** Council-level assessment; Sheyf Tinte contacted them.
