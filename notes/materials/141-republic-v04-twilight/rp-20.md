# Star Wars (1998) #20 — Twilight, Part 2 (TW p.28–49)

Chapter p.28–49 (22 files). Citation "TW p.N" = N-th TPB file.

## Plot (#20)
1. **Psychometric cascade (p.28–30).** Images cascade: Padawan / Aayla Secura / Ryloth / Glitteryll / caves; "BETRAYED? BETRAYER?"; Bib Fortuna / Twi'lek Hutts; "ATTACK. BETRAYAL. AAYLA. DEAD?"; Tatooine shadows; Sheyf Tinte / Kiffu / Kiffex / Clan Vos / Guardians; secret meetings to withhold from Jedi. Crossed green + pink sabers crackle (SHKKRRR) as Villie snaps him awake: "WAKE UP NOW, JEDI!"
2. **Plan: find Fortuna (p.31–32).** Villie restates Nar Shaddaa bet (his life on Quinlan escaping). Quinlan: can read images off objects; meditating on his saber and companion's; images lack emotional resonance—"scenes for somebody else's life." Companion = Padawan Aayla, Twi'lek. Villie knows Bib Fortuna works for Jabba; Hutt homeworld orbits this moon; Fortuna involvement = "major bad." Quinlan refuses to leave until he finds Fortuna; Villie agrees to help locate him to speed exit.
3. **Bib Fortuna's sanctum (p.33–35).** High Nar Shaddaa: fresh air/sunlight for the rich; Fortuna's temporary sanctum and rising ambitions. Quinlan confronts with crossed green + magenta sabers. Villie holds Fortuna at blaster-point; warns Jabba is not part of this deal. Fortuna refuses: Jedi will not kill without need. Quinlan Force/physically overpowers him (WHAM / KRAK)—boot pins him. Quinlan: "I KNOW NO CODE!" Demands what Glitteryll is. Fortuna: only a facilitator; one "high up on Coruscant" behind it all; presses alarm (TEK). Guards + battle droid arrive. Villie: "PLAN WAS NO GUARDS."
4. **Mercenary fight / droidekas (p.36–37).** Quinlan adapts: deflects red bolts with green saber; kicks mercs; droids explode. Caption: attackers are mercs/droids/"scum… struck down the same way." Fortuna flees. Destroyer droids (shields, twin fire VIIP). Villie priorities: stay alive, keep Jedi alive, stop Fortuna if possible. They crash through glass (CRASH) and steal Fortuna's speeder.
5. **Inferno and NT-600 (p.38–42).** Spaceport: Villie's ship *Inferno* surrounded by security droids—"technically is no longer my ship" (lost betting on Quinlan's death). Firefight; Quinlan deflects (SPONG). Inside, NT (Astronavigation / General Assistance droid hardwired as co-pilot) refuses takeoff—Villie not authorized owner. Quinlan persuades NT that closing ramp/raising shields protects the new owner's investment without violating protocol. Owner Gorzima (Quarren) on viewscreen threatens destruction; ion cannon depleting shields. Quinlan gives word as Jedi that Villie will pay as promised. Gorzima restores original authorization; *Inferno* flees (SHOOOOOSH).
6. **Breakaway and Kiffar readout (p.43–45).** Betting losers chase *Inferno* through Nar Shaddaa sky (THOOM/CHOOM). At breakaway point Villie "wins" then must pay Quinlan—poor again. Quinlan: knows name, that he is Jedi, had apprentice; not what happened to him or her, or if she lives. NT scans: Quinlan is **Kiffar**, Clan Vos, planet **Kiffu** (twin **Kiffex**, Azurbani system); lightning storms power cities; Guardians exile criminals to Kiffex; Sheyf (Clan Vos 300 years)—current **Tinte**, Quinlan's great-aunt; Guardians with psychometry; Quinlan highest known, taken to Coruscant for Jedi training. They land; Quinlan invited to meet Tinte; Villie stays with ship—"Don't mention Villie."
7. **Sheyf Tinte (p.46–48).** Kiffu chamber under orange lightning sky. Tinte: home again, not so proud; place in clan if he assumes duties. Quinlan seeks missing companion (Jedi obligation). Tinte: Jedi are thieves who took him; not one Vos in a thousand born with his psychometry. Shares intel: weeks ago Quinlan + apprentice sought info on **Glitteryll** (synthesis of Glitterstim/Kessel + Ryll/Ryloth); they went to Ryloth; last contact; rumors from Nar Shaddaa and Tatooine; Corporate Sector interested; behind it someone "very highly placed on Coruscant." Quinlan thanks her for justice; she asks justice or revenge. She senses darkness boiling in him; he admits on Nar Shaddaa questioning Fortuna he felt power that fed his anger and fed *from* it, left him unclean. Tinte: Dark Side—Guardians respect it, do not fool with it. Directs him to Ryloth for apprentice/truth; honor justice; beware the darkness.
8. **Cliffhanger (p.49).** On *Inferno*, cousin Holmar Grahrk holocalls: big price on Quinlan Vos's head; enough for two if Villie helps set him up. Villie: "I think maybe we can do business."

## Feats / Force / combat (#20)
- **Psychometry deepened (p.28–31, 45):** Object-reading yields names/places without emotional ownership; NT confirms highest known Guardian psychometric talent + Force affinity.
- **Dual-saber intimidation then single green combat (p.33–37):** Crossed green+magenta vs Fortuna; later green-only deflection vs mercs and droideka fire; kick/physical takedowns. Limit: no Code restraint—threatens lethal force because memory of Jedi ethics is gone.
- **Anger-fed power acknowledged (p.48):** Self-report that power during Fortuna interrogation fed anger and fed from it → unclean; Tinte names Dark Side. Scaling note: same rage channel as #19 Force push, now explicitly dark-tinged.
- **Diplomatic Force-of-personality (p.40–42):** Talks NT into shields via protocol loophole; Jedi word persuades Gorzima—social/ethical leverage, not a mind trick.
- **Limits:** Cannot Force-locate Aayla or Fortuna's Coruscant principal; still fragmentary identity; Villie's loyalty bought/sold; Dark Side pull flagged.
