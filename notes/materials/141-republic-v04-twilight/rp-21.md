# Star Wars (1998) #21 — Twilight, Part 3 (TW p.50–71)

Chapter p.50–71 (22 files). Citation "TW p.N" = N-th TPB file. Careful Read of every rendered page image (+ crops for small text / Force moments). Asante is **Kiffar** (yellow facial band), not Twi'lek; Villie/Holmar are **Devaronian**.

## Plot (#21)
1. **Ryloth arrival splash (p.50).** Starport cavern. Narration: quiet trip; Ryloth Outer Rim near Tatooine, tidally locked, habitable twilight band, Twi'lek mountain cities. Quinlan seeks Aayla Secura. Thanks Villie; Clan Vos will compensate. Villie arms crossed; Quinlan hooded.
2. **Asante Vos reunites (p.51).** Villie plans spice/dancing-girl profit. Asante Vos (Kiffar Guardian, yellow stripe, purple/gold star pauldrons) finds Quinlan; confirms amnesia; was close admirer when he visited home. Escorts him to meet Pol Secura (Aayla's uncle, chieftain). Villie asserts name: "Vilmarh Grahrk!"
3. **Villie vs cousin Holmar (p.52–53).** Villie greets Holmar face-to-face with punch (KRAK), slam (WHUMP), more strikes; explains two Devaronians together look guilty; Holmar wanted Villie's help to kill Jedi for bounty—must not get Villie killed first. Orders Holmar into disguise, meet at Seko Cantina; parting "gentle reminder" punch (KRAK).
4. **Pol Secura audience (p.54).** Ruling five-member head clan; Twi'jani hospitality still holds. Pol asks after Aayla; Quinlan recounts waking on Nar Shaddaa under attack, psychometry off lightsabers, Glitteryll hunt. Pol: they found nothing, went to Kessel, then silence. Asante urges rest; Pol prepares same rooms as last visit.
5. **Seko Cantina conspiracy (p.55).** Villie shows Holmar the cantina. Hooded figure (Villie in disguise negotiating as contact) and Holmar: big money if Jedi dies and never reaches Coruscant; Holmar wants 60-40 split for getting close enough to shoot. Hooded: half. Holmar won't name bounty source (not Bib Fortuna)—if Villie knew he'd kill Jedi himself. Plan: only Villie places death-bet, then contact.
6. **Patron orders death (p.56).** Pol cowers before holocomm of Patron (Gran: ridged forehead, large eyes, ornate robes—later named Chom Frey Kaa). Patron: Jedi's return unacceptable; should have been killed last time. Pol: memory wipe means death now raises dangerous questions. Patron: own position/plans at risk—Jedi must die. Threatens Bright Lands fate for whole head clan if one dies. Orders Pol to see it done; Pol: "As you command, my master."
7. **Wrong rooms / psychometry blank (p.57–59).** Asante and Quinlan in supposed prior rooms overlooking Ryloth city. Quinlan passes hand over every surface—**no psychometric images** of self or Aayla. If age faded room echoes, saber echoes should have faded too → room likely wrong/deceptive. Asante: why would Pol deliberately mislead? Quinlan: "Because he has something to hide." Explodes: no memory, no emotional connection even to images of himself—"MY LIFE IS GONE!" Inner voice directs him: find Aayla = find own spirit. Admits shadow images on Aayla's saber suggest **he shot her**; must know if true. Asante raises Dark Side possibility. Quinlan recalls Nar Shaddaa anger-push pleasure then feeling soiled; commits to truth whatever it is.
8. **Villie summons to rock gardens (p.60–62).** Message: meet Rock Gardens near meridian border—cantina intel, open channel unsafe; costs "little money." Asante distrusts Devaronian; Quinlan goes anyway ("has been of service"). Floating Rock Gardens: quiet, near bright side, little-visited; Quinlan meditates until he senses approach. Holmar (not Villie) aims blaster: "you'll see him soon enough in whatever hell takes you." Fires (BTEW); kills Quinlan's mount; Quinlan ignites green saber. Aftermath: Villie has killed Holmar (own cousin; family feud); learned bounty on Jedi but **not** who placed it—not Fortuna. Big bet Villie Jedi gets off Ryloth. Quinlan: memory flashes point to spice mines first. Villie tags along—"protect investment." Trust? "Of course… Is **money** involved!"
9. **Abandoned mine / Kessel spider eggs (p.63–64).** Tracks contradict "abandoned" signs; strong recent ryll deposits. Psychometric traces: Aayla and Z Secame this way. Cavern full of white webs, translucent reddish-orange pods, broken egg husks. Villie: "Eggs. Spider eggs. From Kessel. Make big spiders." Glitteryll process: feed ryll to spiders, grow them big. Villie bad feeling. Giant multi-eyed energy spiders attack (HSSSS).
10. **Spider combat (p.65–67).** Cramped space; Quinlan plan: face them, size/numbers work against spiders. Villie scoffs. Green saber leaps/cuts (SNEKT severs clawed fingers); Villie blasts (BTEW) while webbed; Quinlan cuts him free. Spiders energy/webbing beams (YOW); Villie falling; narration: too many—without ally Quinlan as good as dead.
11. **Asante betrayal (p.68–69).** Asante appears with long-barreled blaster while Quinlan fights Anzati/spider. He calls "ASANTÉ?" She shoots him in the head/temple (impact spark); he falls, saber drops. Prone, he recognizes: "It was… you I saw in… the vision…! You shot… Aayla…! Why?" Asante: for money; escape Kiffu Guardian life; they learned too much; powerful beings wanted them dead. She and Pol fed them glitteryll to wipe minds—thought no memory = no threat—but Quinlan came back. Tearful: loved him a little; must kill him. Fatal SCHWCK strike cuts her down while she aims; blood from nostrils; she falls; Quinlan (still down) cries "ASANTE!" (strike source: blade SFX while Quinlan prone—attacker not clearly Quinlan standing; later he tells Pol spiders killed her.)
12. **Spider finish / Asante dies / yellow-eye rage (p.70–71).** "Thought and action are one"—Quinlan drives green saber into spider underbelly amid BRAZOW explosion; Villie climbs back from web fall, claims he saved Quinlan. Quinlan cradles dying Asante: she apologizes—betrayals led deeper into darkness past redemption. Dies. Villie: now you have a name—Pol Secura—now what? Close-up: Quinlan's eyes **glow yellow-green**, teeth bared: "NOW I GO TO SEE POL SECURA!"
