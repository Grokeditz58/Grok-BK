## Plot (#19)
1. **Amnesia in the fire (p.6–8).** An unidentified man (Quinlan) wakes in a burning room: "WHO AM I?" He refuses fear—"Calm… Focus… and seek the path… out!"—crashes through an obstacle (KRASH), finds no safe path, looks up to a segmented skylight, Force-leaps through it (KRESH / glass shatter). Hands bandaged.
2. **Hunters on Nar Shaddaa (p.9–10).** He lands amid debris; green blaster bolts (VIP) miss him. An internal sharper voice urges: defend yourself or die without answers. Thugs in goggles/armor mock him ("HO HO HO! KAKO KREESPA, JEDI!"). He reaches for a missing belt weapon, finds none. Anger boils out; he blindly Force-pushes three armed aliens away (SUWAKKK / SHWAKKK)—debris flies; the push is rage-driven, not controlled technique.
3. **Villie appears (p.11–15).** Building collapses; the voice: "Questions later… LEAP!" He meets Vilmarh "Villie" Grahrk (red Devaronian, horns, teal bead necklace): "'Allo, Jedi!" Villie claims to be saving his life. Quinlan distrusts him. Explosions (CHOOM) hammer the area; Villie hijacks Lido Mufrenes's yellow skycycle at blaster-point ("TIME TO SAY 'BYE-BYE!'"). Quinlan mounts; they flee under green bolt fire while Villie laughs that everyone is "cheating."
4. **The Nar Shaddaa betting pool (p.16–17).** Villie explains: everyone knows he is a Jedi whose memories "made to go poof"; they bet how long he lasts and cheat to kill him on their chosen time. Quinlan takes Villie's blaster despite protest; narration: he feels/understands; arm, sight, blaster, and target become one—"in his mind the shot has already taken place." He destroys pursuing speeders (KRAKK / CHOOM / WHAROOM). Villie impressed; they dive to Villie's hidey-place.
5. **Betrayal and psychometry (p.18–21).** Safe room (Black Sun–marked pillar visible). Villie explains Jedi = allies of the Force; memory loss does not erase Force ability; rumor Quinlan killed other Jedi / other Jedi did this to him. Quinlan returns the blaster. Villie suddenly aims it and claims the Jedi bet on holo ("visual confirmation in five seconds")—crowd of aliens reacts. Blaster clicks empty: Quinlan had reversed the power cell. Roundhouse kick (KRAK) drops Villie. Quinlan: "The blaster told me. While I was focused, images came off it." He demands identity and who did this, nearly kills Villie; Villie protests Jedi do not kill unless they must—Quinlan does not know that. Off-panel voice: "That's enough. Put the blaster down, brother."
6. **Fake Jedi and saber reclaim (p.22–27).** A Whiphid in robes (pink/magenta saber) and a green-skinned hooded figure (green saber) claim to be Jedi. Villie: "Them not Jedi. Them bad boys." Green alien lunges; Quinlan blasts him in the head (SPAKOW). Quinlan Force-pulls a fallen lightsaber to his bandaged hand (green Force flash). Touching the hilt floods psychometric images: Padawan Aayla; Twi'leks, Ryloth, Glitteryll; Sheyf Tinte; Mace Windu; his own name just out of reach—then: "I KNOW WHO I AM. MY NAME IS QUINLAN VOS AND I AM A JEDI." Muscle memory wins the uneven duel vs the Whiphid ("the body does… memory lies deep in muscle"). He collects a second saber that "belongs to… Aayla." Villie admits the kill-bet failed and has remade a longer-odds bet that the Jedi escapes alive—Villie's own life is the stake—so Quinlan's survival is now Villie's interest. They leave together under watching thugs: "Lead on, Grahrk."

## Feats / Force / combat (#19)
- **Instinctive Jedi discipline under amnesia (p.6–8):** Suppresses fear, seeks path via calm/focus; Force-augmented leap through skylight while surrounded by fire. Limit: no conscious identity or technique names; guided partly by an inner voice.
- **Rage Force push (p.10):** Area shove throws three armored blaster-armed thugs and shatters local structure. Explicitly anger-driven and "blind"—not a trained controlled push. Possible contribution to nearby building collapse (p.11: "Did I do that?").
- **Force-guided marksmanship with a borrowed blaster (p.16–17):** Precognitive/flow-state accuracy ("shot has already taken place"); destroys pursuer speeders mid-chase while standing on a moving skycycle. Not lightsaber combat.
- **Psychometry (p.20, p.24):** Reads images off Villie's blaster (betrayal/bet) and off his own lightsaber (Aayla, Ryloth/Glitteryll, Sheyf Tinte, Mace, own name). Confirms Kiffar psychometric talent survives memory wipe.
- **Blaster sabotage by feel (p.20):** Reversed power cell before returning the weapon—practical cunning + psychometric tip-off.
- **Telekinetic saber call (p.23):** Pulls lightsaber hilt across the ground to hand under attack; green surge on contact.
- **Lightsaber muscle memory (p.25–26):** Green blade vs Whiphid's pink/magenta blade; uneven match resolved because body remembers forms the mind does not. Outcome "never in question."
- **Limits:** No lightsaber at start (belt empty). Does not know Jedi rules against killing. Trust of Villie remains coerced/transactional. Identity recovers in fragments; full context of Glitteryll / who wiped him still unknown at #19 end.

## Lore
- Setting: **Nar Shaddaa**; open betting pool on amnesiac Jedi's lifespan; cheaters try to force the outcome.
- Quinlan Vos = Kiffar Jedi Knight; Padawan **Aayla Secura**; links to **Ryloth**, **Glitteryll**, **Sheyf Tinte**, **Mace Windu** via psychometry.
- Fake Jedi (Whiphid + green alien) wield lightsabers but are underworld "bad boys" per Villie—sabers do not prove Jedi identity.
- Black Sun mark visible in Villie's safe room (p.18).
- Continuity hook from prior arcs (Emissaries/Outlander): Glitteryll / memory wipe already in play; this issue is the amnesiac aftermath on Nar Shaddaa.
