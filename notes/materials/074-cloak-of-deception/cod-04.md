# Cloak of Deception: notes part 4, Part Three "The Outlying Systems" (Ch. 18–25)

- This part covers FULL.txt l.1551–2321. Citation: "CoD ch. N".

## Ch. 18: The Asmeru trap
- **Forces:** two Judicial diplomatic cruisers, *Prominence* and *Ecliptic*, escorted by Tikiar fighters and 2 House Vandron dreadnaughts. A spiral with a black center (the gravitic sink) is visible. The mines are command-detonated.
- **Seven Jedi go in:**
  - Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan
  - Ki-Adi-Mundi (called a "Jedi Knight" in the text)
  - Yaddle and Saesee Tiin
  - Vergere (a Fosh, former apprentice of Thracia Cho Leem)
  - Depa Billaba
  > Yaddle: "Deal lightly with matters of consequence, and decisively with those of little consequence" (CoD ch. 18)
- Ossan slaves are bred on Karfeddion, and the salon pods can be jettisoned.
- **The trap springs:**
  - The mines detonate and destroy the *Ecliptic*.
  - A commercial tractor beam in a pyramid grabs the ship, backed by an ion cannon.
  - A pilot rams a salon pod into a CloakShape, which hits the tractor pyramid and damages its grid.
  - The ion cannon disables the *Prominence*, which glides down to a lake.
  > Obi-Wan: "I thought you were just being figurative about getting wet, Master" (CoD ch. 18)

## Ch. 19: The island
- **Feat (mobility):** the Jedi swim to an island with a 100 m pyramid. Qui-Gon "launched himself to dry land", Obi-Wan "sprang from the water", Vergere catapults out, and Yaddle rides Ki-Adi.
  > "Drawing on the Force, and taking the judicials with them, the Jedi vaulted and bounded up into the boulders." (CoD ch. 19)
- **Feat (endurance):** the Jedi keep warm through the Force overnight in below-freezing cold, with no drinkable water (the lake is hypersaline).
- **Yaddle lore:** she is 476 years old and became a Master after more than 100 years in an underground prison on Koba.
- Vergere hears slaves approaching, and Depa speaks the Senex traders' tongue.
  > NF spokesman: "Ultimately, you would certainly emerge the victors" (CoD ch. 19). The terrorists concede the Jedi would win a straight fight.
- The NF executes slaves. Then "Seven lightsabers reignited." Tiin: "We're annulling the truce."

## Ch. 20: The Battle of Asmeru
- A holo on Famulus shows the *Ecliptic* destroyed by mines, with 20 judicials dead. Survivors from the *Prominence* are the 7 Jedi plus 5 judicials. Horox Ryyder (an Anx) appears.
  > Palpatine: "the hostages are Jedi Knights." (CoD ch. 20)
- **Feat:** Qui-Gon, Obi-Wan and Ki-Adi fight back to back, their blades "green, blue, and purple" (the text gives Ki-Adi a purple blade, a continuity quirk). They "moved faster than the eye could follow".
- Vergere uses an emerald blade and Tiin a cobalt one.
- **Feat:** Tiin carries a wounded judicial under one arm while deflecting one-handed.
- Qui-Gon's fighting flow is detached: "He left no traces of himself..."
- **Feat (signature):** Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan
  > "leapt ten meters into the air with their swords raised, ripping the repulsorlift engine from the lead craft" (CoD ch. 20). The lead craft is a gunboat.
- At the Council:
  > Yoda: "Moving us around like pieces on a holo-game board." (CoD ch. 20)
  - 20 Jedi are on Eriadu and 10 are sent to Asmeru.
  - Even Piell cites the Rights of Sentience.

## Ch. 21: Cohl's trail
- The Jedi advance in a wedge with Qui-Gon on point.
  - A slave uprising breaks out, while NF moderates (Bith-led) clash with the militants.
  - The Lancet kills a CloakShape, and around 20 Jedi reinforcements rappel in.
- The dying Bith reveals that Havac leads the militants and that the plan involves Cohl. **Cindar**, called a Nikto here, volunteers to lead them to Karfeddion.
  > Qui-Gon, defying the Council: "My duty is to the Force, Master." (CoD ch. 21). He takes the *Hawk-Bat*.
- **Lope** is a Stark Conflict veteran armed with a vibroblade, stun baton and nerve pick. The crew also includes a Gotal.
- **Feat (non-Force quick-draw):** in the Tipsy Mynock cantina, Lope kills Pezzle and 3 others: "Cohl never saw Lope's blaster leave its holster" (CoD ch. 21).
- Rella returns and signs on. The Jedi trace Cohl via Belsavis, Malastare, Clak'dor and Yetoom.

## Ch. 22: Eriadu and Tarkin
- Eriadu (Seswenna sector, where the Rimma Trade Route meets the Hydian Way) is polluted and industrial, and the city lies about 100 km inland.
- Valorum's code name is "The Goods", and he carries a locator implant. His family runs a shipping and ship-construction concern on Eriadu.
- **Wilhuff Tarkin**, lieutenant governor, is Valorum's host; Valorum stays with him because Tarkin is a Palpatine acquaintance. Tarkin is wiry with blue eyes, and served in the military when Eriadu was in the "Outland Regions". The Seswenna Hall dome is about 200 m.
  > Tarkin: "Threats of insurrection must be met with strong, centralized power. One must seize the moment, and strike." (CoD ch. 22)
- **The aurodium leak:**
  - Taa (Allocations and Appropriations committees) hands Bail Antilles (chair, Internal Activities Committee) a holocron.
  - It shows a sudden capital inflow to the Valorum relatives' Eriadu shipping concern.
  - The leak cites Statute 435, Substatute 1759 of the Amended Proprieties Bill.
  - This is the data Pestage planted in ch. 15.

## Ch. 23: Cindar's betrayal (unarmed fight)
- Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan leave their sabers aboard the *Hawk-Bat* to bait Cindar. Qui-Gon tells the Generis sallap-tree parable: a Sith Lord's path through the dark wood is shorter but never arrives.
  > "Nothing was before Yoda's time, Padawan." / "What can be avoided, whose end is purposed by the Force?" (CoD ch. 23)
- **Feat (unarmed):**
  - Qui-Gon Force-shoves Cindar's blaster hand.
  - Obi-Wan leaps over Cindar's head and lands a sweep kick. He blocks punches and kicks, slips a bear hug, and lands a cartwheel roundhouse and a back flip to the jaw.
  - A hold-out blaster nicks Obi-Wan's right leg.
  - Qui-Gon "appeared out of nowhere", and Cindar is killed by his own ricochet.
- At the Council:
  > Yoda: "Always forward, the Living Force. Adjust to Qui-Gon's actions, the future will." / "Become Captain Cohl, Qui-Gon has!" (CoD ch. 23)

## Ch. 24: Into Eriadu
- Rella, Boiny, Lope and the Gotal hijack an Eriadu Customs picket. The mercenaries ride in cargo tubes, and Havac's people run the customs warehouse.
  > Cohl to Rella: "I don't deserve you." (CoD ch. 24)

## Ch. 25: Havac's betrayal
- Adi reports that Tiin, Ki-Adi and Vergere are en route.
  > Qui-Gon: "Cohl is here somewhere. I can feel him." (CoD ch. 25). This is Force tracking at planetary-city scale.
- Havac reveals the plan has been modified by an "advisor", and the mercenaries are only a diversion.
- **The shooting:**
  - Havac shoots Cohl in the chest, but the vest stops it.
  - He kills Rella with a shot to the neck and wounds Boiny in the head.
  - Cohl is also hit in the thigh, abdomen and forehead.
  > "Our advisor prefers as few loose ends as possible" (CoD ch. 25)
- Valorum refuses to postpone the summit or let Jedi replace his guards.
