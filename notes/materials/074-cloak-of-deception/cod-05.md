# Cloak of Deception: notes part 5, Part Three cont. (Ch. 26–33), the Eriadu summit

- This part covers FULL.txt l.2322–2946. Citation: "CoD ch. N".

## Ch. 26: Palpatine, Sidious and the Directorate
- Bor Gracus (Sluis Van) appears.
  > Palpatine: "If events could truly be divined—if one were to be granted such awesome power—then perhaps the future could be directed along one line or another." / "I'm content to play my small part behind the scenes." (CoD ch. 26)
- **The TF Directorate has 7 members:** Gunray, 4 humans (2 from Kuat, 1 from Balmorra, 1 from Filve), a Gran and a Sullustan. Lott Dod is their senator.
- Neimoidian shuttles are organic in design, and there is a shield generator in the TF section of the hall.
  > Sidious: "It is all a charade... Taxation is but a minor obstacle" (CoD ch. 26). The TF is to extend its reach "to the galactic rim itself".

## Ch. 27: Havac's plan
- **The setup:**
  - Spotters (Lope, the Gotal and a woman) go up to a rooftop from a safe house.
  - A sniper in an Eriadu security jacket fires at the floor at the start of the third fanfare.
- **The real shooter** is a custom battle droid built by the TF's own security-droid supplier and brought in by the TF's security detail.
  - The droid "needs to perceive a threat before it can be tasked". The goal is to frame the TF.
- The Gotal's senses are overloaded by droid energy.
  > Qui-Gon: "There is an organizing hand at work here—one that moves Cohl about as effortlessly as it moves us." (CoD ch. 27)
- The freighter carrying the bodies is found through an "anonymous lead".

## Ch. 28: Cohl survives
- **Wounds:**
  - Cohl revives because his vest absorbed the shot. His hair is burned off on the right side.
  - Boiny has a fractured, burned skull and is dosed with expired bacta patches.
  > Cohl: "Some of us were born to be betrayed." (CoD ch. 28)
- Boiny finds an advanced restraining bolt meant for a combat droid, and Cohl works out the plan.
  > Tarkin: "I hope ... Coruscant will one day be a second home to me. All the Core, in fact, from Coruscant to Alderaan." / "Don't concern yourselves with legalities." (CoD ch. 28)
- Verpine trackers (with radio-wave organs) are used. Rella's body is identified as the freighter's captain.
  > Qui-Gon: "he expected us to find Cohl and the Rodian alive... to throw us off the scent." (CoD ch. 28)

## Ch. 29: The rooftop fight (Qui-Gon's best saber showing in the book)
- **Setup:**
  - The rooftop spotters wear mimetic suits, and Lope has a wrist rocket launcher and a vibroblade.
  - The hovercade has 10 vehicles with Valorum in the 5th, under an energy shield.
  - Lope fires rockets at the Jedi airspeeders.
- **Feats:**
  - Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan leap from their speeder and somersault. "In a blur of motion" they bound across a courtyard.
  - Qui-Gon outruns a pair of attackers and deflects "a dozen blaster bolts—along with a hurled blaster" (CoD ch. 29). A Force push then drops both of them.
  - A 1 m vibroblade pierces his cloak and he cuts it in half. He grapples hand to hand while bolts singe his hair.
  - He **Force-pulls a slate tile** to brain one terrorist.
  - He cuts off a sliding woman's right arm **by predicting where she would land**.
- **Feat (Vergere):** she leaps from dome to dome "as if she were wearing a rocket pack".
- **Feat or anti-feat (Obi-Wan):** knocked off the roof, he clings one-handed to an airspeeder's stabilizer.
- **Bondara koan payoff:** Obi-Wan decodes the koan from ch. 9: the skimmer is the Force, the pit is the dangers, the travelers are the distractions. Qui-Gon then realizes that everything so far is a distraction.

## Ch. 30: Sneaking in; Palpatine on Naboo
- Cohl and Boiny wear turbans. The Stark Hyperspace Conflict was "twelve years earlier", and its veterans in repulsorlift chairs from bacta-scarce worlds give Cohl his way in (the east entrance).
- **Naboo politics:** Palpatine reports that King Veruna has abdicated and an acting regent rules until elections. He wants someone "less... traditional".
  > Palpatine on Tarkin: "Power can turn even the meekest of bureaucrats into a raging manka cat." (CoD ch. 30)
- **The money trail:** Taa says Antilles has traced funds to a fraudulent bank account channeling illicit money (the aurodium account).
- **Sidious and Gunray:**
  - Sidious's holo to Gunray is so clear that Gunray "almost believed that Sidious was on Eriadu". This hints that Sidious is physically present.
  - Sidious supplies an extra droid with yellow markings.
  - A "second directive" troubles Gunray. He notes where Lott Dod sits.

## Ch. 31: Finding Havac
- Cohl steals a repulsorchair. There are 13 droids, and only the yellow-blazed one lacks a restraining bolt.
- Havac is a documentarian, so Cohl checks the media booths.
- **Feat (Force sensing):** Qui-Gon: "I can feel him", reading Cohl through the living Force.
  - Havac is in the Eriadu Free HoloDaily booth with a holocam and a scanner aimed at the trumpeters. Havac also tipped off the authorities about the freighter.
- The Jedi confront Cohl and Boiny, and read through the Force that their injuries are real.
- **Tech:** Boiny says the yellow droid is a Baktoid "new and improved" commander droid, a "freethinker" that can sway the droids around it (all 13).
  > Qui-Gon: "Lean on me, Captain." (CoD ch. 31)

## Ch. 32: The booth
- The ceremony has 10 drummers, 20 horns and 3 fanfares.
  > Tiin: battle droids are "not very accurate" (CoD ch. 32)
- **Feats:**
  - Qui-Gon slags the door lock with his saber.
  - He deflects a flurry of fire, and 2 rebounds wound Havac's men.
  - He **Force-summons Havac's falling blaster** and lifts Havac one-handed against a window.
  > Cohl: "I'm glad I never had to go up against you people." (CoD ch. 32)
- A page lures Gunray and Lott Dod out of the hall over a shuttle issue, so they are not in place for what follows.

## Ch. 33: The massacre
- The walkways are 100 m above the floor. The fake security agent is the sniper, and he shoots Boiny in the chest.
- **Anti-feat for Qui-Gon (important in debates):**
  > "blaster bolts were delivered with such speed and precision that he was hard-pressed to deflect all of them. Two whizzed past his blade, grazing his left arm and right leg." (CoD ch. 33)
  - Context: the sniper dual-wields and wears body armor. Qui-Gon is also angling his deflections sideways so they don't hit security agents.
  - Qui-Gon then cuts the walkway supports and dangles one-handed.
- **The twist:**
  > Havac: "What makes you think that we were ever after Valorum?" (CoD ch. 33)
  - The real target was the TF Directorate. The scanner detects the force field switching on, which triggers the holocam.
- **The massacre:**
  - The droids about-face inside the force field and massacre the Directorate. No bolts can enter or leave the field.
- **Deaths:**
  - Cohl and Havac grapple, and Cohl kills Havac with his final bolt.
  - Cohl is shot in the abdomen and dies.
- Qui-Gon ends sitting cross-legged, "staring off into space".
