# Cloak of Deception (James Luceno, Del Rey 2001/2002): notes part 1, front matter, Part One "Dorvalla" (Ch. 1–5)

- Read 2026-11-03 CST from the owner's epub (Del Rey ebook v3.1_r1, eISBN 978-0-307-79570-0; copyright page reads © 2002 Lucasfilm). This part covers FULL.txt l.1–580.
- Citation: "CoD ch. N". The epub has no print page numbers and no chapter headings in the text; chapter numbers follow the contents list (38 chapters in four parts) and the boundaries were mapped by hand.
- Big picture: the whole novel is set in 32 BBY in the months before TPM (the bundled timeline files it under "33 years before ANH"). It explains how the Trade Federation got its droid army, why the Naboo blockade happened, and how Valorum fell.

## Front matter (l.1–100)
- The Del Rey copyright page (© 2002) credits an excerpt from *NJO: Enemy Lines I: Rebel Dream* (Allston), but the excerpt actually bundled after the novel is *Shadow Hunter*. The dedication is "For Karen-Ann".
- Contents: Part 1 Dorvalla (ch. 1–5), Part 2 Coruscant (ch. 6–17), Part 3 The Outlying Systems (ch. 18–33), Part 4 The Inner Circle (ch. 34–38). Back matter: About the Author, By James Luceno, the Legends intro, the *Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter* excerpt, era intros with excerpts, and the novels timeline.
- The opening crawl (l.79–99) sets the scene: the Republic is crumbling, greed fills the senate, and the Trade Federation's "gargantuan vessels" are preyed upon by pirates and terrorists. The Jedi are named as "the Republic's best hope".

## Ch. 1: The *Revenue* at Dorvalla
- The Trade Federation freighter *Revenue* is loading **lommite** (an ore used for transparisteel) at Dorvalla. Commander **Daultay Dofine** shortchanges Dorvalla Mining.
- Tech: the TF freighter is **3 km in diameter**, with hyperdrive reactors in the centersphere. Its droid starfighters are described as "wanting shields" but rapid-firing, and they run off a central control computer.
- Security droids are commanded by an OLR-4 droid. They are a pre-B1 security type with signal-boost backpacks.
- Nebula Front CloakShape fighters and the Tempest-class gunship **Hawk-Bat**, flown by Arwen Cohl's crew, attack. A battering-ram pod breaches the hull, and blaster bolts deflect off the ram. The freighter *Acquisitor* is one system away.

## Ch. 2: Cohl's raid
- **Cohl**: violet eyes, a diamond-pattern tattoo from temple to temple, long black hair, bearded. His team uses mimetic (camouflage) suits and field disruptors. **Rella** is the only woman on the team and **Boiny** is a Rodian slicer. Rasper is already dead.
- TF lore: the quad laser batteries sit on the equator, so the ships are blind above and below, which is chalked up to Neimoidian cheapness. The senate has to sanction starfighter allotments. The freighter's shields drop from 50% to 40%.
- The ship turns its cranes and loadlifters on the raiders. Cohl blows the bridge and slaps the timer of a 60-minute thermal detonator onto Dofine's hand.
- The prize is **two billion in aurodium ingots**, which are TF bribes meant for Outer Rim worlds.
  > Dofine: "The Trade Federation does not deal in violence and death." / Cohl: "Not yet you don't." (CoD ch. 2)
  > "I'd shoot an unarmed Neimoidian—and I'd sleep better for it." (CoD ch. 2)

## Ch. 3: The Jedi aboard
- Qui-Gon (broken nose) and Obi-Wan (nasal voice, cleft chin) ride aboard in a fake pod. Lightsaber hilts are given as 30 cm.
- Force lore: the Force gives "power to move objects at will, to cloud the thoughts of lesser minds, to peer forward in time".
- Aurodium "glowed with a constantly shifting inner light".
- Cohl's trick: he lets the freighter's diagnostic find the detonator, so every pod is jettisoned and the sky becomes a "target-rich environment".
- **Feats:** Qui-Gon decapitates a droid one-handed and **Force-pushes its head across the hangar** as a decoy. Obi-Wan "disappeared in a blur" to plant a tracker on Cohl's pod.

## Ch. 4: The pod chase
- Obi-Wan pilots their pod through a stampede of pods. Flashback: at the Temple he trained while five teachers worked to distract him.
- The CloakShape is described as an atmospheric fighter with a retrofit hyperdrive. Z-95s also appear. The Jedi plant their own thermal detonator on Cohl's pod, with 5 minutes on it.
- **Feat:** with his eyes closed, Obi-Wan **reacquires a lost target through the Force**.

## Ch. 5: *Acquisitor*, and the *Revenue* blows
- The *Acquisitor* (Lagard) has overlapping strengthened shields and twice as many droid craft. Cohl's pod is a husk: a shuttle hides inside it and the lower half blows away.
- The Jedi pod hides a crimson, wingless Corellian Lancet in Judicial Department colors. The Nebula Front crew say "Jedi?" in unison, and Boiny adds "Maybe the Force told them."
- Nute Gunray appears by holo from his mechno-chair (built by Affodies Crafthouse) and calls the loss "a boon".
- **Feat (precognition):** Qui-Gon senses danger and orders "Abort the pursuit" moments before the *Revenue* explodes. The Lancet rides the blast wave.
- Jedi Rules of Engagement given here: 5th "Understand the dark and light in all things", 6th "Learn to see accurately", 7th "Open your eyes to what is not evident", 8th "Exercise caution, even in trivial matters".
  > Qui-Gon: "I sense instead a menacing beginning." (CoD ch. 5)
