# Cloak of Deception: notes part 3, Part Two "Coruscant" cont. (Ch. 12–17)

- This part covers FULL.txt l.1040–1550. Citation: "CoD ch. N".

## Ch. 12: Asmeru; the secret droid army
- Asmeru (Senex sector) is a Nebula Front base, guarded by space mines disguised as asteroids.
- **Havac** is a combat name. He is a former holo-documentarian who was an alien-rights activist during the Stark Hyperspace Conflict, and has "no stomach for violence, but ... talent for treachery".
- **Cindar** is described here as reptilian, with 4 horns and multiple nostrils. (Ch. 21 calls him a Nikto; this is a continuity quirk.) Cohl's team started out at 12 members.
- Cohl suspects a leak in the NF. Havac pitches "something big". Rella wants to retire.
- **Tech and lore:** at Neimoidia, Haor Chall and Baktoid build prototypes aboard the *Acquisitor*. Each is a civilian item reworked:

  | Prototype | Built from |
  |---|---|
  | Landing craft (wings held by tensor fields) | Ore barges |
  | MTT | Cargo pods |
  | AATs | (no source given) |
  | Folding battle droids | Security droids |
  | STAPs | Longspur and Alloi's Bespin airhooks |

  - Droid starfighters run on central control, and the Colicoids have a rolling droid (the droideka foreshadowed).
  - The aim is an "army without giving the appearance of having an army". The rest of the TF Directorate is kept in the dark.

## Ch. 13: Palpatine's double game
- Palpatine walks the underworld incognito,
  > "as if he could see the coiling blackness that inhabited the senate, and the refulgent light that poured from the spires of the Jedi Temple" (CoD ch. 13)
- His friendship with Valorum dates from the start of Valorum's second 4-year term. Naboo peers retire from public service at "the accepted age of twenty", but he stayed on.
- He met the NF through the Bith and meets Havac at a humans-only club. Havac wants a stronger chancellor. Havac's "powerful new ally", who supplied the Dorvalla data, is Sidious.
- **The aurodium laundering chain:** Palpatine routes the aurodium from an outlying bank to the InterGalactic Bank, then into Republic dataries, where the credits pay bribes to senators.
  > "consider me the voice of the Republic." (CoD ch. 13)

## Ch. 14: The senate-plaza assassination attempt
- Force lore: on Coruscant the Force is "a howl—a white noise of sentience".
- **Adi Gallia** has sat on the High Council for more than 10 years and has Corellian diplomat parents. A Dorvalla Space Corps report shows Cohl survived (only 3 sets of human remains were found).
- **Feat (danger sense):** Qui-Gon senses targeted danger and picks out the shooters in the crowd: a Bith, 2 Rodians, 2 Twi'leks, a Bothan and a Rodian taxi pilot.
- **Feat (deflection):** Qui-Gon deflects the Twi'lek and Rodian fire and diverts the bolts *above the crowd*.
- **Feat:** Adi's azure blade turns two darts. The third hits Valorum in the right arm.
- **Feat (mobility):** Qui-Gon fires a liquid-cable launcher onto the taxi, rides it up, and severs two Rodians' cables.
  - He then drops from the top of the tallest Core Founder statue (30 m) to its shoulders, then the pedestal, then the plaza.

## Ch. 15: The medcenter
- Palpatine "held himself accountable", which is ambiguous from his surface POV. Bail Antilles and Com Fordox (Corellia) appear, along with bacta lore (an insectoid species).
- Valorum lists possible successors: "Bail Antilles, Ainlee Teem … even you". Palpatine suggests Jedi help, and Valorum asks him to introduce the motion.
- **Plant:** Pestage's surveillance droid burns a hole through a "blasterproof pane" and plants data in Orn Free Taa's computer, to be "discovered" later.

## Ch. 16: The Council
- Cameo: **Jorus C'baoth** comments on Naboo ("More indigenous species ...").
- Full High Council roster: Depa Billaba, Eeth Koth, Yarael Poof, Adi Gallia, Oppo Rancisis, Even Piell, Yaddle, Saesee Tiin, Ki-Adi-Mundi, Yoda, Mace Windu and Plo Koon.
- Qui-Gon's read of the attack: the assassins aimed at the guards, the graze on Valorum was inadvertent, and the evidence was too easy to find. He concludes it was staged.
- The *Hawk-Bat* is chased by 3 Judicial Corellian space cruisers. Rella: this is "Our last" job.

## Ch. 17: Sith lore; the Eriadu contract
- Valorum has no Force in his bloodline but reveres the Jedi.
  > The Jedi, "Ten thousand strong ... could rule the Republic if they so wished" (CoD ch. 17)
- **Sith lore:**
  - "Two thousand years earlier" there were Sith Lords and armies, "Founded by a fallen Jedi".
  - Their creed: "power disavowed was power squandered".
  - They destroyed themselves over the course of a thousand years.
- **Asmeru briefing:**
  - Houses Vandron and Elegin are slave breeders.
  - The defenses are a gravitic sink plus mines.
  - The force sent is 2 cruisers and 30 judicials, plus Jedi.
- Windu says the TF should not get weapons. Yoda to Qui-Gon: "At war with yourself, you are."
- Cohl takes the Havac job: "It's the life... This is what I know."
  - The plan is to hire a team of non-professionals (spotters, trackers, weapons experts) to kill Valorum at the Eriadu summit, backed by a "powerful outside agency" (Sidious).
  - The Coruscant attempt was meant to scare Valorum into inviting the NF.
  > Havac: "the Jedi are going to be busy elsewhere" (CoD ch. 17). This is the Asmeru trap.
