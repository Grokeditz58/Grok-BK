# Cloak of Deception: summary of strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. Full text l.1–4392. Citation: "CoD ch. N". The back-matter excerpts are cited by line and are not CoD.

1. **Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan rip a gunship's engine off mid-leap (Battle of Asmeru).** They "leapt ten meters into the air with their swords raised, ripping the repulsorlift engine from the lead craft" (CoD ch. 20). In the same fight, three Jedi "moved faster than the eye could follow", and Saesee Tiin deflects one-handed while carrying a wounded judicial under his other arm.

2. **The rooftop fight is Qui-Gon's best multi-opponent showing.** He deflects "a dozen blaster bolts—along with a hurled blaster", then Force-pushes both shooters down (CoD ch. 29). He also halves a 1 m vibroblade, Force-pulls a slate tile into a terrorist's head, and severs a falling woman's arm by predicting where she will land. In the same chapter Vergere leaps dome to dome "as if she were wearing a rocket pack".

3. **Qui-Gon's main anti-feat comes from an armored dual-wielding sniper.** "blaster bolts were delivered with such speed and precision that he was hard-pressed to deflect all of them. Two whizzed past his blade, grazing his left arm and right leg" (CoD ch. 33). Context for debates: he was angling his deflections sideways to protect security agents.

4. **Precognition, sensing and mobility.**
   - Qui-Gon calls "Abort the pursuit" moments before the *Revenue* explodes (CoD ch. 5).
   - He picks seven assassins out of a senate-plaza crowd and diverts their bolts above the crowd. He then drops from a 30 m statue in stages (CoD ch. 14).
   - He tracks Cohl across an Eriadu city: "Cohl is here somewhere. I can feel him." (CoD ch. 25; again in ch. 31).
   - On Asmeru the Jedi keep warm through the Force overnight below freezing (CoD ch. 19).

5. **Sidious engineers both sides.**
   - He leaks the aurodium to the Nebula Front (CoD ch. 8), and Palpatine launders it (CoD ch. 13).
   - He turns Havac's "assassination of Valorum" into a massacre of the TF Directorate by a Baktoid commander droid. Havac: "What makes you think that we were ever after Valorum?" (CoD ch. 33)
   - He then all but admits it: "Perhaps you imagined that I would act with greater subtlety, is that it?" He sets up the Naboo blockade: "We don't want a war. We want an embargo." (CoD ch. 38)

6. **Droid-army origin lore.**
   - Haor Chall and Baktoid build the army from civilian kit: landing craft from ore barges, MTTs from cargo pods, battle droids from folding security droids, and STAPs from Bespin airhooks. The Colicoids supply the rolling droid. The aim is an "army without giving the appearance of having an army" (CoD ch. 12).
   - The TF's quad lasers are equatorial, so the ships are blind above and below (CoD ch. 2).
   - A lightsaber "could cleave duracrete or burn its way slowly though a starship's durasteel blast doors" (CoD ch. 9).

7. **Back-matter bonus: Maul vs 4 Duelist Elite droids (Shadow Hunter excerpt, l.3201–3265).**
   - The droids' reflexes are "a hair faster than human optimum". Maul senses them behind him through the Force and finishes the last one barehanded.
   - It takes about 60 s, with his heart rate up 5 bpm at most: "Not his personal best", and "Jedi were a different matter."
