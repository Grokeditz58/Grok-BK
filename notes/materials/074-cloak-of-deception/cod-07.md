# Cloak of Deception: notes part 7, bundled back matter (excerpts, era intros, timeline)

- This part covers FULL.txt l.3200–4392. It is **not** part of the novel: these are Del Rey promotional excerpts and era intros bundled into the ebook.
- The excerpts mostly print no titles, so the identifications below come from the recommendation lists printed next to them and are hedged accordingly. Cite as "CoD ebook back matter, l.N" and not as CoD canon.

## Excerpt: *Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter* (Michael Reaves), l.3201–3265
- This one is titled in the contents. Sidious is aboard the *Saak'ak* in Wild Space, and Hath Monchar is missing.
  - **Feat:** Sidious senses Gunray lying through the Force, which feels "like an omnipresent mist".
- **Maul vs 4 Trang Robotics Duelist Elite droids**, armed with rapier, cudgel, chain and hachete. Their reflexes are "a hair faster than human optimum", and their inhibitors have been nullified.
  - His double-bladed saber was "first designed by the legendary Dark Lord Exar Kun millennia ago".
  - **Feats:**
    - He senses the droids behind him through the Force, and under the dark side "Time seemed to slow".
    - He kills 3 with his saber using teräs käsi forms (Riding Bantha, Rancor Rising), and his pulse rises only 2–3 bpm. He bisects Chain from crotch to head.
    - He deactivates his saber to fight Hachete barehanded. An ankle hook, then a front flip, and his boot heels crush Hachete's head.
  - The whole fight takes **about 60 seconds** with his heart rate up **5 bpm at most**. He judges it "Not his personal best", and "Jedi were a different matter."
- Sidious praises the Black Sun assignment (Black Sun has been "in disarray for years"). He orders Maul to find Hath Monchar and kill anyone Monchar told.

## Era intro: Old Republic (l.3267–3279) and excerpt (l.3281–3396)
- The Republic began about 25,000 years before ANH. Naga Sadow, Ruusan (1000 BBY) and Bane's Rule of Two are mentioned.
- Recommended reading: *Deceived*, *Knight Errant* and *Darth Bane: Path of Destruction*.
- **Untitled excerpt (SWTOR-era):**
  - Shigar Konshi, 18, is the Padawan of Grand Master Satele Shan. Larin Moxla is an ex-soldier from Kiffu, and Dao Stryver is a Mandalorian.
  - **Feats:**
    - Shigar Force-pushes the Mandalorian into a building, crushing his jet vent.
    - He is grabbed by the throat and hurled into a wall.
    - He deflects falling monorail metal spears "with the greatest exertion of the Force" he could summon, and pushes smoke away.

## Era intro: Rise of the Empire (l.3398–3407) and excerpt (l.3409–3494)
- Recommended reading: *Labyrinth of Evil*, *RC: Hard Contact*, *Death Troopers* and the *Han Solo Adventures*. The Cato Neimoidia raid in the excerpt matches *Labyrinth of Evil*.
- **Feats:**
  - Obi-Wan deflects bolts into 2 sentry droids, and Anakin decapitates a droid past Obi-Wan's head.
  - Anakin uses a Force push and leaps among mines guided by the Force.
  - He uses a "Jedi healing technique" on a clone.
- **Tech:**
  - Harvester beetles carry 5× their weight, and more than 100 STAPs appear.
  - The clone army is 1.2 million strong, with half fit for duty. Droidekas roll at more than 80 km/h.

## Era intro: Rebellion (l.3496–3508) and excerpt (l.3510–3607)
- Recommended reading includes *Luke Skywalker and the Shadows of Mindor*. The excerpt names Taspan and Mindor.
- Rogue Squadron (Wedge, Hobbie with a prosthetic arm and leg, and Janson) fights Shadowspawn's TIE Defenders.
- **Tech:**
  - The TIE Defender is faster than an X-wing and carries twin ion cannons. Its Novaldex deflectors are "nearly as powerful as those found on capital ships", but it has no particle shields.
  - Flechette torpedoes appear, and marauder craft have deadman self-destruct interlocks.
  - Taspan II hosts gravity-well projector tests ("The Big Crush").
- Lando leads the Rapid Response Task Force.

## Era intro: New Republic (l.3609–3618) and excerpt (l.3620–3745)
- The excerpt is Corran Horn in a simulator run, matching *X-Wing: Rogue Squadron* on the rec list.
- **Tech:**
  - The X-wing shield is a demisphere extending about 20 m behind the ship. A TIE bomber glancing off the aft shield is like hitting a ferrocrete wall at 60 km/h.
  - The X-wing has Incom 4L4 fusial thrust engines.
- Corran scores 9 kills. The mystery TIE pilot wears Hoth, Endor (Death Star run) and Bakura tabs.

## Era intro: NJO (l.3747–3757) and excerpt "Fraying Fabric" (l.3759–3965)
- The Vong are biotech-based and cut off from the Force. The excerpt matches *Vector Prime* on the rec list.
- **Feats:**
  - Jaina, 16, flies the *Jade Sabre* with inertial compensation at 95%, using the Force to stay "three moves ahead". She evades 6 Z-95s, including a repulsorlift hop under a concussion missile.
  - Mara's molecular disease takes "tremendous Force energy" to hold in check.
  - Nom Anor crushes a stone to dust in his fist and registers as a Force "blank".

## Era intro: Legacy (l.3967–3977) and excerpts (l.3979–4191)
- The excerpts match *LotF: Betrayal* and *FotJ: Outcast* on the rec list.
- Luke dreams of a hooded figure: "He doesn't exist."
- **Adumar feats (Jacen):**
  - He reads Testan's surface thoughts and gets a precognitive glimpse of the fight.
  - He deflects bolts back, and 4 troopers fall to the ricochets in the first seconds.
  - He makes a Force-cushioned drop of about 12 m.
- **Ben**, 13, saber-cuts a 1.5 m circle out of a wall.
- Jaina and Jag Fel appear aboard the *Gilad Pellaeon*, and Valin Horn sees the "Not-Mirax" imposters.

## Timeline (l.4193–4392)
