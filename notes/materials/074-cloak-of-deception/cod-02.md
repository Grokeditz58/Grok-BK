# Cloak of Deception: notes part 2, Part Two "Coruscant" (Ch. 6–11)

- This part covers FULL.txt l.581–1039. Citation: "CoD ch. N".

## Ch. 6: Valorum and Palpatine
- **Valorum** has been Supreme Chancellor for 7 years across two terms, and comes from a dynasty thousands of years old. The Republic has had no standing military "in generations".
- **Palpatine** has red hair and a provincial manner. He is sectorial senator for Naboo and 36 other planets. He pushes taxing the free trade zones in exchange for letting the TF increase its droid forces. King Veruna of Naboo is mired in scandal.
  > Palpatine: the Republic is "frayed at its far-flung borders and hollowed at its very heart by corruption" (CoD ch. 6). Valorum reuses these exact words in his ch. 11 speech, which is an irony.
  > "My interests are the interests of the Republic." (CoD ch. 6)

## Ch. 7: The Temple and the Council
- Coruscant's underworld has not seen the sun in 25,000 years. The Jedi Temple is a kilometer-high truncated pyramid with five towers arranged in a quincunx.
- The **Reconciliation Council** has 5 members; this meeting has Plo Koon, Oppo Rancisis, Adi Gallia and Yoda.
- Qui-Gon would sit on the Council if he did not bend the rules. He follows the **living Force**, and "rarely entered into a match with a strategy in mind": he improvises. He has a Bith contact inside the Nebula Front, met on Malastare.
- Adi Gallia is Corellian and has close ties to Valorum. Rancisis is a royal of Thisspias. Plo Koon wears his mask in oxygen-rich environments. Both are military strategists.
- The Nebula Front's radical wing hired Cohl. The NF refused to smuggle spice for the Hutts because it opposes slavery.
  > Yoda: "A true Knight, Qui-Gon is... Forever on his own quest." (CoD ch. 7)

## Ch. 8: Neimoidians and Sidious
- Neimoidian lore: they spend 7 years as grubs, raised in hives fed by fungus farms. Hath Monchar is deputy viceroy and Rune Haako is the legal counsel.
- Sidious first contacted Gunray months earlier, after Gunray testified against the Pulsar Supertanker company with its "malicious disregard for profit".
- Sidious arranged for resource worlds to join the TF as signatories. He leaked the aurodium shipment to Nebula Front militants to stir up sympathy for the TF. The droid army will come from **Baktoid, Haor Chall and the Colicoids**.
- Sith lore: "ancient ... black mages", "absent ... past thousand years".
  > Sidious: "Valorum is our strongest ally in the senate." / "there are many that do my bidding" / "foolish friends are no better than enemies" / "my actions will speak for me" (CoD ch. 8)

## Ch. 9: The opera; lightsaber lore; Anoon Bondara
- The opera is *The Brief Reign of Future Wraiths*, staged by a Bith company that Valorum's parents underwrote. Sei Taria is Valorum's aide.
- In Orn Free Taa's box are Toonbuck Toora, Passel Argente (Corporate Alliance), Edcel Bar Gane (Roona) and Palpatine, with his aides **Kinman Doriana** and **Sate Pestage**.
- Palpatine dismisses Valorum as a "bureaucrat at heart" and notes that Valorum's father failed to eradicate the NF.
  > "corrosion... progresses from the outside in" (CoD ch. 9)
- **Lightsaber lore:**
  - Adegan crystals and diatium power cells.
  - A blade "could cleave duracrete or burn its way slowly though a starship's durasteel blast doors".
  - Force talent shows in infancy, children reach the Temple by 6 months, and those who fail go to the AgriCorps.
- **Backstory:**
  - Obi-Wan was on Bandomeer 12 years earlier.
  - Qui-Gon's previous Padawan fell to the dark side. Xanatos is implied but not named.
- **Anoon Bondara:** a Twi'lek "duelist of unparalleled skill". Sparring with him was "more instructive than twenty contests against lesser opponents". His Padawan is Darsha Assant.
  - His teachings: "Expect nothing"; "hold the lightsaber by loosening your grip"; "formless rhythms"; plus a sand-creature koan that pays off in ch. 29.
- **Luminara Unduli** has the same diamond tattoos as Cohl, so Cohl comes from her homeworld. There he was a freedom fighter, imprisoned on a false conspiracy charge. He escaped and became a mercenary, and is said "never to have betrayed a friend".

## Ch. 10: Cohl's escape
- Cohl's team hid for 4 days in the *Revenue*'s severed starboard arm.
- On reentry, Dorvalla Space Corps picket ships shoot down their shuttle. The survivors are Cohl, Rella, Boiny and Jalan. Cohl carries 2 of the 4 aurodium allotments.
- Jalan stays behind with the self-destruct code and takes a picket ship with him. The *Hawk-Bat*, the contingency plan, shoots down another, and the team is extracted.

## Ch. 11: The Senate session
- The Senate Rotunda has 1,024 balconies, 30 m statues and a 30 m dais.
- Valorum proposes abolishing the free trade zones, taxing them, and having member worlds leave TF signatory status. At Palpatine's prompting (a note passed through Pestage), he adds that a percentage of tax revenue goes to relief for the outlying systems.
- Reactions:
  - Lott Dod speaks for the TF.
  - Ainlee Teem (a Gran from Malastare) and Bail Antilles (Alderaan, against augmenting the TF's droids) are both active.
  - The naysayers are Toora, Po Nudo and Wat Tambor.
- Palpatine: "May I suggest … Eriadu?" for a summit. Toora wins a moratorium on the tax vote until the summit. Valorum will attend in person.
