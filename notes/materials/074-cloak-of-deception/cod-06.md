# Cloak of Deception: notes part 6, Part Four "The Inner Circle" (Ch. 34–38) and author pages

- This part covers FULL.txt l.2947–3199. Citation: "CoD ch. N".

## Ch. 34: Aftermath; Amidala
- About a month has passed. Field disruptors had dropped the shield and the droids. Only Gunray and Lott Dod survived from the Directorate.
- Tarkin obstructs the Justice Department investigation, and evidence and witnesses vanish.
- Havac's real name was **Eru Matalis**, an Eriaduan.
- **Commander droid:** it took independent control of the other droids briefly, until the central control shut them all down.
- Gunray is now "commanding viceroy of the entire Trade Federation". The plasma-leak call that lured him out was untraceable, and Qui-Gon remains unconvinced ("So it would appear").
- **Amidala lore:**
  - She was born Padmé Naberrie and is 14, newly elected. King Veruna is dead in an "accidental" death.
  - Captain Panaka is the new security chief, replacing Captain Magneta.
  - Palpatine lives at 500 Republica.
  > Amidala: "I will abide by your decision on the matter, Senator." (CoD ch. 34)
- **The taxation vote:**
  - Valorum had deferred taxation, but Palpatine urges him to pursue it.
  - Palpatine also proposes letting the TF "take whatever steps are necessary to safeguard their vessels", which means augmenting its army.
  - Amidala will not support taxation.
  > Valorum to Palpatine: "should need ever arise, I would do all within my power to render aid to Naboo." (CoD ch. 34). This line sets up TPM.

## Ch. 35: Ratification and the summons
- Taxation is ratified, and Sei Taria fields the press. Valorum's victory statement is interrupted.
- Bail Antilles serves notice that Valorum must appear "before the Supreme Court to answer allegations of corruption and illegal enrichment" (CoD ch. 35).

## Ch. 36: The aurodium surfaces; Taa's cabal
- Two weeks later the Supreme Court (12 judges, Galactic Courts of Justice, Plains of Coruscant) hears the case.
- Antilles shows that Valorum Shipping and Transport received **aurodium ingots** through a blind account and a Coruscant bank "of dubious reputation". The amount matches *exactly* the cache missing from the *Revenue*.
- **Taa's penthouse cabal:** Tikkes (Quarren), Mot Not Rab and a Rodian senator.
  - The scandal was held back until after ratification, so the leak would hurt Valorum the most.
  - Palpatine suggested Mas Amedda (Chagrian) as vice chancellor, because Amedda's fixation on procedure would stifle Valorum.
  - Bail Antilles and Ainlee Teem are already campaigning. Taa proposes nominating Palpatine:
  > "there is a strong hand concealed within those loose sleeves" / "he thinks like one of us." (CoD ch. 36)

## Ch. 37: Valorum broken; the Council
- Valorum is despondent. His family snubbed him over Tarkin (whom they see as a nemesis), and the Jedi are now barred from trade disputes without senate consent.
  > Valorum's dream: "Something was reaching for me out of the blackness, to crush me in its grip." (CoD ch. 37)
- At the Council, Windu concludes the aurodium came from Havac.
  > Yaddle: "If stopped for a moment to listen to the unifying Force, see what was coming you might have." / Yoda: "Blame you, we don't, Qui-Gon. But exasperate us, you do." (CoD ch. 37)
  > Yaddle: "Tipping the balance is." / Windu: "And what unknown hand is doing the tipping?" (CoD ch. 37)

## Ch. 38: Sidious sets up the blockade
- Sidious speaks by holo to the Neimoidians aboard the *Saak'ak* (*Profiteer*).
  > "Perhaps you imagined that I would act with greater subtlety, is that it?" (CoD ch. 38). This openly admits the Directorate massacre was his doing.
  > "Soon I will be taking steps to eliminate some of your other competitors" (CoD ch. 38)
- The TF's droids, starfighters and landing craft come from Baktoid and Haor Chall. Sidious: "We are going to put your new army to the test." The blockade is of **Naboo**, not Eriadu: "We don't want a war. We want an embargo."
  > "Senator Palpatine is adept at dissembling his real nature." (CoD ch. 38). This is dramatic irony, since Sidious is Palpatine.
  > "I have a surprise in store for feeble-tempered Valorum" / on the Jedi: "We will not be subtle in dealing with them." / "you serve yourselves best when you serve me." (CoD ch. 38)
