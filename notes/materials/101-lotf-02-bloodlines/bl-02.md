## Ch.5 (l.2100–2662)
- Epigraph: Ailyn Habuur/Vel quotes 500k each for Han and his son; extra for the Solo women and Skywalkers (l.2102–2105).
- Coronet port: Han repairs Falcon hyperdrive housing bolts (stress microfractures) (l.2117–2133). Ambush by a ~30-yr-old freelance bounty seeker; Han, pinned under the housing, sweeps continuous fire across a 150° arc and wounds him (l.2135–2157). "it wasn't Fett ... He'd have been dead by now" (l.2139).
- FEAT: Leia kills the attacker with three lightsaber strokes (l.2159–2163); they dump the body out the air lock (l.2199). Han is 60 (l.2169).
- Falcon covers 20,000 light-years to Coruscant in 3 hours on a damaged drive (l.2229); beacon at 750,000 km (l.2235). Anonymized transponder as Tatooine freighter (l.2239).
- Lumiya answers summons; Jacen tracks her and her emotions (l.2283–2285). Ben wears a tiny Padawan braid (l.2287). Lumiya's cover name "Shira" (her birth name) (l.2301). Ben: "That's the real enemy ... Chaos" (l.2331); "almost a Sith sentiment" (l.2335).
- Lumiya almost fully conceals her presence (l.2345); bag likely holds her lightwhip (l.2349). Ben doesn't recognize her as Brisha (l.2355).
- Jacen: "there was little—if anything—that could present a serious threat to him now" (l.2385).
- Lumiya passes undetected through the Temple and the Room of a Thousand Fountains (l.2423–2457). Lumiya denies the bombing (l.2477).
- MAJOR FEAT: Jacen cloaks Lumiya in a Force illusion and projects calm as Luke passes; it takes "all his strength" to deceive a Master of Luke's stature (l.2483–2495). ANTI-FEAT (partial): Luke still senses something faintly (l.2499–2501). Lumiya: "If I wanted to get to him, I wouldn't need you" (l.2521).
- Ben in the Corellian quarter: water supply poisoned, 10 pumping stations shut, half central Galactic City without water (l.2565–2575). Riot at the Corellian embassy; CSF dispersal gas (l.2625–2641).
- FEAT: Ben, half-blinded by gas, deflects Barit Saiy's blaster bolt aimed at a CSF officer's back with his lightsaber (l.2647); officer: "You're fast, kid" (l.2653). Ben lies it was a rock (l.2651).

## Ch.6 (l.2663–2879…)
- Epigraph: "The bigger the galaxy, the sweeter the homecoming." (l.2665).
- Jacen uses a Theran technique to hear remotely through the Force (l.2681); sends one word, "Together" (l.2689–2691).
- MAJOR FEAT: Jacen channels his telekinesis *through* Leia from the Temple to hold the Falcon's cracking drive housing together during atmospheric entry; drowns out Jaina's contribution with "the sheer strength of the telekinesis he was projecting" (l.2705–2721).
- Fett: less agile than a year ago, joint pain (l.2761, 2779); tank-gestation memory per Taun We (l.2773).
- Slave I armament: turbolasers, ion cannon, torpedoes, concussion missiles, plus armor-piercing detonite ordnance; "firepower of a small warship" (l.2787).
- Roonadan (Corporate Sector), capital Varlo. Fett uses ID "Vhett" (pure Mando'a "Fett") (l.2811–2825). Disguises with cloak over armor, packs jetpack and rocket launcher in holdall (l.2843–2867). Unhelmeted in public for the first time in his adult life; grey hair, faint Sarlacc scars, looks early seventies (l.2869–2879).
## Ch.7 (l.2881–3176)
- Epigraph: Omas to Luke and Niathal on "that little surprise in the Kiris Cluster" — the true justification, to be kept secret (l.2883–2887).
- Falcon down at landing area 337/B; coolant pipework sheared at the welds (l.2895). Leia: "like getting a Force booster pack from nowhere" (l.2905).
- Jaina back on active duty as a GA fighter pilot, 31 (l.2981–2991); to Jacen: "You don't feel right to me lately ... Are you in trouble?" (l.3003). Han: Jacen's Force senses "seemed to beat scanners" (l.2953).
- Ben home with riot-gas burns; says it's his fault, not Jacen's (l.3039–3071). Han/Luke row: "galactic rubber stamp ... Does the name Palpatine ring a bell?" (l.3121); Han refuses Luke's hand (l.3137).
- Jacen likens the Force channeling to deflecting Killik weapons together (l.3145); Luke: channeling is new to him (l.3147). Luke: Jacen is the only other Jedi he knows who can defeat Lomi Plo's invisibility (l.3149).
- Luke catches a trace of Lumiya (Shira Brie, "more cyborg than human") and understands his dream; the hooded figure is a woman (l.3153–3167); tells Jacen nothing (l.3175).

- Epigraph: Corellian militants claim the Fex-M3 water attack: 456 dead, 5,000+ with nerve damage; full terror alert (l.3179).
- Omas drinks tap water on HNE (l.3187). Calls for Corellian internment (l.3203). G'Sil: "a trillion people on this world" (l.3221).
- Jacen offered command of a force between army and CSF, made Colonel (l.3233–3253). "I'm not ashamed of Anakin Skywalker" (l.3247). Insists on a separate Galactic Alliance special guard (l.3257); wants Shevu, a special-forces company, and Alliance Intelligence data, but no NRI officers (l.3273–3277). SIC vote within 24 h (l.3289). Omas: "step toward martial law" (l.3279).
- Fett unhelmeted in Varlo, says he's an "anatomist" (l.3325); drinks a Corellian ale in public (l.3359). AruMed's shares rose on scientists poached from SanTech, not Kaminoans (l.3369). Rumour that Kaminoans are at Arkanian Micro on Vohai (l.3379–3393). Buys six Upper Parkway homes as an investment (l.3399). Joint pain in fingers (l.3407).
- Helmet HUD opens Slave I's hatch remotely at 30 m (l.3423). Mirta does sit-ups in the prisoner bay (l.3417). Fett skips g-dampers on takeoff (l.3439); flies on manual to check he still can (l.3447).
- Mirta: met a clone last year who fought at Geonosis — light grey Mando armor, grey leather gloves, custom Verpine rifle, three-sided knife, clan name **Skirata**; left the army after Palpatine came to power (l.3449–3517). Kal Skirata was Jango's friend, a commando trainer (l.3497). Fett deduces someone halted that clone's accelerated aging — Ko Sai's data was used (l.3505–3521). Clones matured in 10 yrs and aged at 2x+ (l.3471).
- Pellaeon resigns at 0800 over GAG powers; Niathal becomes Supreme Commander (l.3543–3559). Thrackan: Centerpoint back online in three months (l.3561). Lumiya: "You're more like your grandfather than you think" (l.3567); Vader picked her as an intelligence agent (l.3569).
- Jacen could listen behind closed doors at the far end of the floor (l.3595).
- FEAT (repeat): Jacen cloaks Lumiya from Luke for a full ten minutes ("ball of heat in his chest") (l.3609–3631). Lumiya passes up a chance to kill Luke (l.3635). Jacen: internment next (l.3643); "prepared to be hated in pursuit of a greater good" (l.3649).
## Ch.9 (l.3651–3942)
- Epigraph: Omas's memoirs. GAG formed; he was "decried as the new Palpatine" (l.3653–3655).
- Ben, in plain clothes, confronts Barit at the Saiy workshop: "We are [at war]. Maybe not properly, but we are." Ben doesn't turn him in (l.3659–3735).
- Jabi Town raid, 0400: six CSF ships per skylane junction (l.3745); five squads of 967 Commando (siege-busting, personnel retrieval; all Coruscant-born humans) plus CSF ATU volunteers (l.3759). About 2,000 residents and 500 troops (l.3779). Shevu revises the ROE to "real and immediate threat" (l.3767). 0400 doesn't work on Wookiees (l.3773).
- Ben senses explosives "Dangerous, but not right now. Soon." (l.3813).
- MAJOR FEAT: Jacen sends a Force jolt through three targets at once behind a wall, paralyzing them ("shock to the spinal cord"), then blows the doors open with no shock wave (l.3823–3835). Sgt Wirut: "Nice trick." Troopers coin "REBJ: Rapid Entry By Jedi" (l.3837–3841).
- Jacen considers a "multiple Force grab" to drop every holocam (l.3867). Ben Force-wrenches doors apart (l.3873). No fatalities; 1,500+ to process (l.3877).
- Han buys a slow surplus shuttle; he and Leia watch HNE: "JABI TOWN: DAWN RAID" with Jacen and Ben, sabers drawn; "watched her son turn into a monster" (l.3907–3941). Threepio to fly the Falcon over later (l.3935).
