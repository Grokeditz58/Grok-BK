Character quick bios:
- **Jacen Solo:**
  - Commander of the GAG and Lumiya's apprentice, following the tassel prophecy: sacrifice, ruin those who deny justice, "immortalize his love" (l.8576–8578).
  - Flow-walks in time: 59 years back to Knightfall (l.1918–1952) and to the Council chamber (l.6706–6752). Cloaks Lumiya from Luke (l.2483–2495, 3609–3631).
  - Kills Ailyn Vel by Force interrogation (l.6082–6136) and torpedoes a fleeing civilian freighter (l.7022–7026).
  - Ends resolved to kill Tenel Ka and Allana (l.8689–8701). Han: "you aren't my son anymore" (l.8239).
- **Boba Fett:**
  - Mandalore, in his seventies (l.4627) and dying of an illness that needs Kaminoan data. Looks for Taun We and a gray-armored clone (l.8105).
  - Hired first by Thrackan, then by Gejjen, who pays him 1M to kill Thrackan (l.7717).
  - Backstory: Jango was killed in front of him at 10; married Sintas at 16; killed a superior officer and was exiled from Concord Dawn (l.8434).
- **Mirta Gev:** Ailyn's daughter and Fett's granddaughter. Hunts him at first; ends calling him "Ba'buir" (l.8757).
- **Ailyn Vel / Habuur:** Fett's Kiffar daughter, about 50, and a bounty hunter hunting Han. Dies under Jacen's interrogation.
- **Ben Skywalker:** 13–14, Jacen's apprentice in the GAG. Makes his first kills in the H-90 raid (l.7500–7524) and learns to hide from his parents in the Force (l.7570–7574, 7683–7693).
- **Han and Leia Solo:** On Corellia, they help Fett and Gejjen against Thrackan. Leia stops Mirta's shot with the Force (l.7795–7797).
- **Luke and Mara Skywalker:** Luke senses darkness around Jacen but can't place it; he and Mara lose the Force sense of Ben.
- **Jaina Solo:** XJ7 pilot. Refuses Jacen's order to finish off the freighter and is suspended (l.7000–7036).
- **Lumiya:** Dark Lady of the Sith; uses a safe house on Coruscant. Gives Jacen his "final trial": "kill what you love" (l.8645).
- **Thrackan Sal-Solo:** Corellian President, assassinated in the Gejjen/Fett plot (Mirta fires the first round, l.8446).
- **Cal Omas / Cha Niathal / G'vli G'Sil:** run the Alliance. Over 100 planets withdraw their representatives (l.7977), and war is formalized (l.8331).
- **Goran Beviin, Taun We, Lon Shevu, Jori Lekauf, Barit Saiy, Nov Gejjen, Heol Girdun:** supporting cast (Dramatis Personae, l.127–167).
