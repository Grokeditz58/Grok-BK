- EXCERPT, LotF 3: Tempest, prologue (l.8847–9151): Alema Rar stalks Jacen in the Yuuzhan'tar undercity with wasber-venom cone-darts (eight more darts, one for each Solo and Skywalker plus two) (l.8913–8915). Lumiya Force-hurls Jacen clear of the dart, which hits the World Brain's eye (l.9097–9101). Jacen's Force lightning blasts Alema (l.9107). Lumiya: "You are a servant to your emotions" (l.9137). Alema: "Jacen Solo, apprenticing with a Sith" (l.9149). (Out-of-book material — not counted as Bloodlines feats.)
- Old Republic era intro (5,000–33 BBY) and three starter picks: Deceived, Knight Errant, Path of Destruction (l.9153–9173).
