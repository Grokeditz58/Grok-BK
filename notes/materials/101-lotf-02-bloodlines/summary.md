# Legacy of the Force 2: Bloodlines: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-03/04 CST. Full hand-read, l.1–11855. Citation "BL ch. N (l.x)". Detail is in bl-01 to bl-06.

## Plot
- **Fett's thread (prologue–ch. 3, 11–12, 14, 17, 19–23):**
  - Fett, ill, takes Thrackan's contract on the Solos, then switches to Gejjen's 1M to kill Thrackan.
  - He infiltrates Arkanian Micro to find Taun We and the Kaminoan data (l.4383–4425), learns his daughter Ailyn is hunting Han, and meets Mirta.
  - Ailyn is captured on Coruscant by Jacen's GAG and dies under interrogation. Fett takes her body home by way of Geonosis, reconciles with Mirta, and withdraws the Mandalorians from the war (l.8787).
- **Jacen's thread:**
  - Forms the GAG and carries out raids and internment.
  - Flow-walks to watch Anakin's fall and decides "I'm not Grandfather" (l.1968) / "I'm the second chance" (l.6756).
  - Hides Lumiya from Luke. Fires the first shot of the blockade war (l.5757–5783) and torpedoes a fleeing freighter.
  - Lumiya's final trial is to kill what he loves; he chooses Tenel Ka and Allana (l.8645–8701).
- **Solos:** Han and Leia are hunted on Corellia, join the Thrackan assassination, learn what Jacen has become, and break with him (l.8239, 8396).
- **Ben:** drawn into the GAG, makes his first kills (l.7500–7524), learns to mask himself from his parents, and starts investigating Nelani and Brisha (l.8295–8305).
- **Politics:** Omas's blockade becomes a formal war with Corellia and her allies (l.8331).

## Top feats
1. **Jacen channels telekinesis through Leia** from the Temple, across a planet, to hold the Falcon's cracking drive housing together during atmospheric entry. It drowns out Jaina's contribution (BL ch. 6, l.2705–2721).
2. **Jacen's triple technique in the Jedi Temple:** Fallanassi invisibility, total presence suppression and flow-walking into the past, all at once (ch. 17, l.6706–6744). Also his 59-year time-drift to the exact spot of Operation Knightfall, feeling Anakin's emotions (ch. 4, l.1918–1952).
3. **Jacen cloaks Lumiya from Luke Skywalker** with an illusion and projected calm (ch. 6, l.2483–2495), and again for ten minutes (ch. 8, l.3609–3631). It takes "all his strength", and Luke still senses something faint (l.2499–2501).

## Other feats
- Jacen sends a Force jolt through a wall into three targets and blows the doors open with no shock wave, which the troopers name "REBJ" (l.3823–3841).
- Jacen takes Ailyn's cell: deflects bolts, disarms two shooters and pins Ailyn (l.4889–4893). Holds a Force shield over the crowd and pushes it back with no crush (l.5910–5922). Force-shields an air taxi against a bomb blast (l.1093–1099). Shatters Lumiya's illusion with one clap (l.6418).
- Jacen masks his panic from Lumiya (l.8649–8651) and holds his hand in a flame until it chars (l.8673).
- Fett micro-jumps through the blockade at half lightspeed for about a second (l.5811–5831). Bypasses the Arkanian fence and sensors (l.4393–4413). Escape with flamethrower and a missile bent 90° (l.7252–7276). Slave I captures a Headhunter with its tractor (l.187–189).
- Leia Force-stops Mirta's blaster bolt and strips her weapon (l.7795–7797), and kills an attacker in three strokes (l.2159–2163).
- Ben deflects a bolt while half-blind from gas (l.2647), senses a weapons cache from a holomap (l.7362–7368), and uses a Force shock wave (l.7500–7502).
- Jaina weaves through ion fire and Luke can't land a real shot (l.4681–4697). Force-pushes a captain past Luke's block (l.4737–4741).

## Anti-feats and atrocities
- Jacen kills Ailyn by Force head-slams and mind-probing (l.6082–6136). Jacen misses what Ben finds by plain analysis (l.6174–6186). He torpedoes a fleeing civilian freighter (l.7022–7026).
- Ben kills an unarmed man (l.7502–7524).
- Fett's illness: stomach pain (l.221), forearm ache (l.4581), "I'm dying" (l.8729).
- Han is dropped by Fett's rifle butt and Mirta's knee (l.6636–6638).
- Luke admits "maybe I'm slowing down" (l.4683).
