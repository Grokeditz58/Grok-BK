# MedStar II: Jedi Healer: summary of strongest findings

Source: Michael Reaves & Steve Perry, *Star Wars: MedStar II: Jedi Healer* (Del Rey 2004; ebook v3.1_r1, eISBN 978-0-345-49268-5). Hand-read in full on 2026-11-03: all 42 chapters and the Epilogue, dedication, About the Author, the bibliographies, the Del Rey Legends intro, and the *Jedi Trial* excerpt (prologue and ch. 1). The other back-matter excerpts were skimmed. Setting: Drongar, the Clone Wars. Citations are "MS2 ch. N".

1. **On bota, Barriss Offee's connection to the Force becomes "almost omnipotent".** This is the book's central power claim. It is subjective, but the narration describes it and the Council appears to have felt it.
   > "she felt transcendent, almost omnipotent ... able to sense beyond three, even four dimensions. It felt as if she could grasp the fabric of space and time, and turn it, twist it, any way that suited her ... For that timeless moment, she was the Force." (MS2 ch. 17)
   > "intensifying her connection to the Force by an order of magnitude she couldn't even begin to tally" / "like trading in that lamp for her own personal sun". (MS2 ch. 19)
   > "Master Yoda and several others on the Council mentioned recently that they sensed—not a disturbance, exactly, more like a surge—in the Force. Perhaps this is the explanation." (MS2 ch. 35, Luminara)
   - The first, accidental dose comes during a sustained Force push that pins a reptilian to the ground. On the second, deliberate dose she sees the Force as a tapestry, gets precognition of the spy and the camp's destruction, then loses control. She is "gone" about 12 hours and is pulled back by Luminara's voice ("Let go ... Or—you could die", ch. 26). Her claims that Jedi with bota "could easily defeat Dooku's forces" (ch. 19) or "abolish slavery, transform barren worlds" (ch. 29) are her own boosted, possibly addicted view, not feats. Jos notes the Council link is unprovable (ch. 37).
2. **Barriss refuses the power and becomes a Jedi Knight by recognizing it.**
   > "To go from being a Padawan to becoming the most powerful Jedi in the galaxy in a matter of moments ... There was nothing in the galaxy that could resist the Force, if it could but be channeled properly" (MS2 ch. 39)
   > "Did it make sense to have the powers of a god, without the wisdom of a god?" / "You truly became a Jedi Knight on the day when you realized that you already were one." (MS2 ch. 39)
   - She compares it to "a lightsaber that could shear through a planet" (ch. 37; a metaphor, not a feat). She judges that the power "could not help but be addicting" (ch. 39).
3. **A Republic superweapon test sterilized a world, an early Death Star-program event.**
   > "a solar flare burst from our sun that was over ten light-minutes long ... The atmosphere and oceans boiled away in minutes" / "The Republic ... military leaders were testing a new weapon. A planet buster, a superweapon for some kind of ultimate battle station being developed. They fired it into our sun, and they miscalculated." (MS2 ch. 40, Klo Merit)
   - Nearly a billion Equani died; "a few hundred, maybe a thousand at most" survive. This is the spy's motive. The effect is indirect, through the star.
4. **The Force lecture: what the Force does and its upper bounds.**
   > "It augments our strength, oxygenates our tissues, decreases reaction lag. Once, in Coruscant Park, I saw Master Yoda lift a rock as big as a family-sized electric cart, with what looked like nothing but a simple hand gesture." (MS2 ch. 22)
   > "the Force may create midichlorians, sort of as its conduits into our continuum, rather than the other way around." (MS2 ch. 22)
   > "Four thousand years ago, Exar Kun, a Sith Lord, somehow destroyed an entire stellar system with his misuse of the Force." (MS2 ch. 22; loose attribution, compare the Cron Supernova)
   - On Form III: "a true master of Form Three is invincible" but "The greatest Jedi who ever lived could not stop four bolts fired from different angles at the same moment" (ch. 6, 8). Yoda can sense disturbances "parsecs away". She felt Obi-Wan or Anakin "would have sensed it immediately" when she missed the MedStar bomb (ch. 14).
5. **The limits of Barriss's mind-reading and the strength of thoughtshields.** Kaird: "A mere Padawan, even a healer, would sense nothing about him that he did not allow" (ch. 3). She can't read past the trained shields of Tolk (Lorrdian) or Merit (Equani). Den and Tolk lower their shields voluntarily and she reads them in moments (ch. 38). Squa Tront read Kaird's thoughts only when his shield slipped.
6. **Ordnance and tech data points.**
   > "The readout showed a yield of half a kiloton. The bomb would have turned him and the ship into a cloud of incandescent plasma." (MS2 ch. 36; four thermal detonators linked in series)
   > "Two-hundred-millimeter mortar. They can throw those at us until the local sun burns out—won't dent the shield. But when they crank up the charged particle spitters and the gigawatt lasers, then we'll be in trouble." (MS2 ch. 38; a Rimsoo force-dome, which a lightning strike finally overloads, ch. 41)
   - I-Five runs on a SyntheTech AA-One at 7 petahertz and 5 exabytes, and wrote a program in 6.1 picoseconds (ch. 25). He fires a microwave beam that overloads a droid's capacitor (ch. 9). A drunk Wookiee wrenches off I-Five's arm (ch. 28). I-Five recovers his memory and declares "I am indeed sentient" (ch. 20, 28).

Other data: Black Sun's firepower "couldn't match even that of the Republic's clone army on Drongar alone" (ch. 21). The Surronian assault ship is "comparable to a Naboo N-1" and under 30 m (ch. 25). Kaird describes a small thermal detonator as shredding a room "in about a thousandth of a second" (ch. 32). Bota grows in about 1,000 km² of Southern Tanlassa across 15 Rimsoos; about 2 metric tons were salvaged (ch. 33, 42). The Venator-class *Resolution* ends the Battle of Drongar (ch. 42). The spy Column/Lens is Klo Merit. The *Jedi Trial* excerpt has Anakin sparring Nejaa Halcyon evenly for an hour, landing a backhand "so close I'm not sure whether or not it counts as a touch."
