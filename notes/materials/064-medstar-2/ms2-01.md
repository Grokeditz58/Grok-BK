# MedStar II: Jedi Healer: notes part 1

- Read 2026-11-03 CST from the owner's MEGA epub (handle VasSURja; Del Rey ebook v3.1_r1, eISBN 978-0-345-49268-5). Citation "MS2 ch. N". Setting header: "RMSU-7, The Jasserak Highlands of Tanlassa, Near the Qarohan Steppes, Planet Drongar, Year 2 A.B.O.G."
- Paraphrase plus short quotes only.

## Ch. 1: Ansion flashback (four Jedi disarm two armies); Luminara keeps Barriss on Drongar
- Barriss's memory of Ansion: she, Luminara, Obi-Wan and Anakin disarm the Borokii and Januul hordes without killing (Obi-Wan's order: "Do not lop off arms or legs or heads"). They stop "blaster bolts, arrows, swords, even a few slung rocks" without reflecting any back. "It was much harder to fight and disarm or wound than to maim or kill."
- **Anakin**: "a fair skill with his lightsaber, though his technique was still somewhat rough". She senses "he wanted to strike them all down"; "He seemed to be enjoying this far too much."
- **Obi-Wan**: "shattering durasteel blades almost too fast for the eye to follow".
- **Luminara and Barriss in tandem**: "together, merged by and in the Force, they were a fighting unit far stronger and faster than the sum of its two parts". With all four: "she had never felt the Force flow as strongly as it did now". It felt like hours but was "perhaps ten minutes or less. Dozens of shattered weapons lay at their feet". (MS2 ch. 1)
- Present: the Rimsoo moved "some fifty-odd kilometers to the south" (the MS1 epilogue says 80 km). Luminara by holocomm: "Spirits require healing, too". Barriss stays.
- Drongar warfare: because of bota, fighting is "within hand-blaster range, in small numbers, and without much in the way of artillery"; even nearby thunder can kill a field.

## Ch. 2: Uli Divini; Den and I-Five
- Lieutenant Kornell "Uli" Divini, from Tatooine near the Dune Sea. He trained at Coruscant Medical and did his residency at Big Zoo (Galactic Polysapient, Alderaan, "seventy-three separate environment zones"). He was drafted early ("they were running short on doctors"). Zan has been dead a week.
- "Mimn'yet surgery": Jos can work "twenty hours straight".
- Den wants to get I-Five drunk. I-Five has "Multiple redundancy backups. They're hardwired". "Something about this ... feels so familiar." (This echoes Lorn Pavan.)

## Ch. 3: new weapons; Admiral Kersos; Kaird as a Silent
- **Weed-cutter**: a Dooku smart frag bomb that arcs over defensive grids and bursts at chest height. "Deadly for two hundred meters against soft targets, and the clone trooper armor didn't stop much, if any, of it." Jos on clone armor: "practically useless"; troops call it "body buckets".
- Tolk is a Lorrdian, able to read microexpressions "almost ... like telepathy".
- New commander: Admiral Erel Kersos, Jos's great-uncle on his mother's side, "clan-shunned for sixty years" for marrying an ekster. He is also a surgeon and does a heart-lung transplant himself.
- **Kaird** (Nediji, Black Sun) is disguised as one of The Silent. The Silent's healing effect is real and statistically proven, "had nothing to do with the Force", and is not a placebo.
- Bota by species: better than bacta for Abyssin, a psychotropic for Falleen, "an anabolic steroid that could help Whiphids". Its use at the Rimsoos is still interdicted.
- Lens (the spy) is a double agent and Black Sun's contact, but won't act as a procurer.
- **Kaird against a Padawan's senses**:
> "This woman wasn't a danger to him directly, even though her connection to the Force granted her considerable mind-probing abilities. His thoughtshield techniques were far above average, however ... A mere Padawan, even a healer, would sense nothing about him that he did not allow to be sensed." (MS2 ch. 3)
  - "He could take out this Padawan easily, but the next one might be a Jedi Knight or even a Master". This is Kaird's own claim. Rule of thumb: "kill a Jedi somewhere, anywhere, and other Jedi always came to investigate."
  - Barriss passes him and nods; she senses nothing.

## Ch. 4: Column/Lens; Den and Merit on Ji
- The spy serves "two masters under two aliases—Column to Count Dooku's Separatist forces and Lens to Black Sun". The motive is "righteous justification—still painful after decades". The relocation cost 3 patients, 15 wounded and Zan. Construction droids rebuilt the base in under two local days (a local day is just over 23 standard hours).
- The spy's coded messages go "via sublight waves through a hyperspace wormhole connection".
- **Retcon of Ji's last stand**: according to Den's source, the Salissian mercs Ji killed "were an elite combat team on a training mission ... being prepared for a major covert attack". Den: "He defeated a Jedi Knight in a match once, you know."

## Ch. 5: the agonizer; Thula and Squa Tront
- **The agonizer** is an experimental hand weapon: "high-collimation microsonic beam" on the limbic system, causing pain without trauma. Somaprin can't block it, and patients can die "from sensory overload". The fix is to sever thalamic nociceptor synapses.
- Kaird's disguises: Kubaz flex-mask, Silent robes, and once a "Hutt suit". His Nediji eyes see "deep into both ends of the visible spectrum", including the colours of an Umbaran shadowcloak.
- Hires: **Thula** (a Falleen whose pheromones work across species; Kaird resists by discipline) and **Squa Tront** (an Umbaran, "paramental abilities, to be able to see and even influence others' thoughts"). Tront sees through Kaird's disguise and greets him with the Nest Blessing.

## Ch. 6: Barriss's Form III practice; the cold wind; she cuts herself
- **Form III lore** (Luminara's words):
> "Of all the seven forms ... Form Three, with its emphasis on anticipating and blocking lightspeed energy blasts, requires the greatest connection to the Force. The road is long, but it is worth the journey, for a true master of Form Three is invincible." (MS2 ch. 6)
- Barriss built her saber at 16 (blue crystal) and has "taken it apart and reassembled it using only the Force".
- **A sudden cold breeze** (a dark omen) breaks her focus, and her blade cuts through her spun-plast boot (proof against a vibroknife) into her foot. It is her first self-cut since age nine: "a beginner's mistake".
- **Healing limit**: by Force she stops the bleeding and shunts the pain, but "The edges seemed to draw together a bit, but then gaped again." Uli staples it. Jedi heal in "Two. Two and a half, at the most" days, against 5–6 for a normal human.
- **Uli's anecdote of a Cerean Jedi bodyguard** (Aldara riot): 30–35 rioters tried to flip a Senator's flitter. Interns spent "the rest of the night reattaching hands, feet, arms, and legs". The Jedi had a vibroknife cut to the ulna that "Wasn't bleeding" (Force body control). Barriss: "We are so few now, so few …"

## Ch. 7: sabacc; Nars Dojah; Teedle
- New quartermaster, Twi'lek Nars Dojah; a TDL-501 waitress droid ("Teedle"); an RH7-D CardShark. Uli is 19, "a prodigy". I-Five wins with an idiot's array.
- Clone programming, Jos's view: "no fear of death ... just enough pain receptors to warn them". CT-914 has since died with his whole garrison.

## Ch. 8: Kersos's story
- Kersos married an ekster, Feleema. She died after six years in a mag-lev crash on Coruscant (about 400 dead, at "three hundred kilometers per hour"). He served for nearly 40 years. "Six years with Feleema was better than six hundred years of my family." "there's no easy way to shave a Wookiee."

## Ch. 9: I-Five's microwave beam; the deal with Thula and Tront
- **I-Five** shuts down the CardShark at range: "I simply aimed a microwave beam at one of its EM receptors and overloaded a capacitor." (MS2 ch. 9) Merit, a former "boson wrangler for Industrial Automaton", proposes a way to make I-Five "drunk" by phase-harmonic multipulse.
- Thula can trigger pheromone desire "multiplied a hundredfold" at will. **Squa Tront reads Kaird's bargaining floor**: Kaird's "pretty good thought-shield defense when he concentrated on it" had slipped. Their deal is 4% plus 5,000 credits.
- Kaird also feels the impossible **cold breeze**.

## Ch. 10: Column's orders
- The Separatists have ordered "Extreme violence", and Column must "instigate a vital element of the plan". Column: "the Republic had to lose this war." Most Republic medics are conscripts ("be inducted or be imprisoned").
- Shrapnel extraction accounts for "nearly all the invasive work". Jos has pulled "a good ten kilos" of metal. HoloNet troupe: Jasod Revoc, Epoh Trebor, Eyar Marath, and **Figrin D'an and the Modal Nodes**.
- Kersos orders Tolk to MedStar for a pretext CME class.

## Ch. 11: Column heads to MedStar; Barriss on Form III and the lightsaber ethic
- MedStar frigates are rare, high-end hospital ships built by KDY. Drongaran life evolves at a constant, extremely fast rate: "undedifferentiation properties to virtually every cell". Spore swarms can digest ship seals.
- Uli does "Two bowel resections, a cardiac transplant, a liver repair" in a day.
- **Barriss's practice against four blasters, a key scaling line:**
> "She had just finished the section of Form III in which she danced against four imaginary opponents, all of whom were using blasters. The greatest Jedi who ever lived could not stop four bolts fired from different angles at the same moment, but that wasn't the point. ... If you trained for combat believing that you would always be outnumbered and outgunned and that you could still prevail, you stood a much better chance". (MS2 ch. 11)
- She identifies Uli approaching from behind through the Force, before he speaks. The Force "was not, first and foremost, a protector, or a weapon, or a mentor ... Errors in wielding it belonged to the user."
- Why Jedi use the lightsaber: "if you must slay someone ... then you must be willing to look that being straight in the eyes while you do it." She sees healing and warrior as "opposite sides of the same coin". "all Jedi are taught basic medical skills". "Power alone doesn't corrupt—but it can feed corruption that already exists."
- Dooku is blamed for "planetary genocide" and the destruction of the Museum of Light on Tandis Four (Uli's claim).

## Ch. 12–13: the snow
- Kersos's family lore. A drug, Sinthenol, prevents lasting brain effects from alcohol. **I-Five can now soothe hangovers "with sonic tones"**.
- Ji is now "The Martyr of Drongar", and HoloNet is making a documentary. Den now thinks Ji was "probably ... preventing an assassination attempt".
- The **force-dome malfunctions**: lights out, then a temperature crash and snow inside the dome. (The dome is a sphere, half of it underground.)
- **Barriss's theory for her self-cut**: the force-dome crystals are "similar to those at the heart of a lightsaber", and a flawed dome-field pulse "might have resonated with her weapon's focusing crystals", changing the blade length. "Normally, the shielding in a lightsaber was proof against such interference—enemies had tried to short-circuit Jedi weapons before." (MS2 ch. 13)
- **Kaird**: "decades as an operative" of Black Sun, with a dead-man's-switch dossier. He decides to retire to Nedij after this job. Self-assessment: "eventually he would be a half step too slow".
- **The MedStar explosion**: "Half the flight decks and most of a storage level just got blown to vac!"

## Ch. 14: aftermath; Barriss's Force-sense fails, then fires
- Thula's protein analogs also work by skin contact. Black Sun has a new underlord ("the underlord's temperament"). The quota is another 200 kg in 3 days. The dome replacement parts were destroyed in the MedStar blast. 186 crew were on the affected decks, and at least 26 bodies are in space.
- **Barriss's range limit and the scaling comparisons:**
> "When it had happened, she hadn't felt a disturbance in the Force ... Master Yoda was said to be able to sense major disturbances parsecs away—even, sometimes, of things yet to happen ... She felt certain that Obi-Wan Kenobi or Anakin Skywalker would have sensed it immediately." (MS2 ch. 14)
  - Then, "the Force abruptly rose swirling in her": "The explosion on MedStar had not been an accident." Tests later confirm sabotage (ch. 15).

## Ch. 15: Jos uses Zan's bota
- CT-802 has a mutated Drongaran infection resistant even to Spectacillin. **Barriss keeps him alive with the Force for a limited time**: "I've been keeping him stable by working on him through the Force, but I can't keep that up forever ... I don't think he'll see another sunrise".
- Zan's secret bota had "cured every resistant infection a Fett-clone had developed on this world". Jos takes it from under his cot.
- The dome is at about 10 degrees below either planetary pole. Tolk and Merit are alive. Hospital ships have been "inviolate ever since the Great Hyperspace War".
