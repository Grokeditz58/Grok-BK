# MedStar II: Jedi Healer: notes part 2

- Read 2026-11-03 CST from the owner's MEGA epub (handle VasSURja). Citation "MS2 ch. N".

## Ch. 16: the dome stays frozen
- Temperature has stabilized at minus 6 °C, and three kiosks collapsed under the snow. The sabotage is classified. I-Five speaks Binary. Merit's "drunk" method would only work after I-Five recovers his lost memory, because of "nonlocal control dampeners".

## Ch. 17: Column's toll; Barriss's accidental bota dose and Force "apotheosis" (the key scene)
- **Column's MedStar sabotage**: "thirty-three biological lives, seventeen droids, and several billion credits' worth of damage". Column was ordered to destroy the lower decks so patient uplift would be choked off. Column keeps Black Sun happy, or "Kaird of the Nediji might soon be knocking on Column's door as he had on Admiral Bleyd's".
- Bota cures CT-802 overnight. Smashbone fever: 50% mortality, with spasms that tear ligaments and snap bones.
- **Force restraint**: Barriss pins the spasming Rodian Zheepho with "a controlled and sustained Force push". A mind-sway won't work because the spasms are involuntary. A major spasm "shook Barriss's grasp of the Force", and she accidentally injects herself with bota (some of it into a vein).
- **The bota Force-surge:**
> "With her next breath, Barriss felt sudden energy flow into her—pure, raw, vast power. In that moment, she felt transcendent, almost omnipotent. She was simultaneously in her body and out of it, able to sense beyond three, even four dimensions. It felt as if she could grasp the fabric of space and time, and turn it, twist it, any way that suited her. For one blinding instant she could feel the Force as she had never done before—in its entirety." / "For that timeless moment, she was the Force." (MS2 ch. 17)
  - "Suns were born, planets spawned ... Every detail on every world in all the galaxies to the end of the universe." Just before it, she sees a vision of Luminara 3 m away, which fades.
  - Afterwards: "She felt exhausted, but … wiser". Den notices she seems "older. Much older" (ch. 18).

## Ch. 18: Tolk is changed; Teedle is sacrificed
- Tolk comes back from MedStar cold and distant toward Jos.
- **Teedle volunteered** her YX-Ninety drive to fix the dome's phase-harmonics generator, which means she is permanently gone. The military won't pay to rebuild her. I-Five had asked for her "field substrate parameters".

## Ch. 19: Barriss's fear of the Force after the surge
- She cannot do a routine Force prep for a trooper getting cyber-legs (soothing the autonomic nervous system and "stimulating biological response modifiers"). "Since experiencing that searing, that 'cosmic' connection, Barriss had been afraid to reach out to the Force again."
- **Her own scaling of the bota effect:**
> "the panaceatic chemical concoction had produced yet another miracle, this time by intensifying her connection to the Force by an order of magnitude she couldn't even begin to tally." / "like trading in that lamp for her own personal sun". (MS2 ch. 19)
> "even the small handful of the Order remaining could turn the course of the war, could easily defeat Dooku's forces and restore galactic peace, did they but have access to the kind of power Barriss had experienced ... she had felt as if she could accomplish all that by herself, so she knew that, with such mystical strength in the hands of Luminara or Obi-Wan or Yoda, anything would be possible." (MS2 ch. 19)
  - This is a subjective feeling while she was boosted, not a demonstrated feat. She also fears it "might claim her for itself, and never let her go". Offworld comms are jammed, so she can't reach Luminara. Temptation: "Its call was so tempting."

## Ch. 20: the dome is fixed; I-Five remembers
- Merit nudges Jos to wonder whether Tolk is hiding something. When the dome is repaired, the meltdown fills the air with static: "Jedi's Fire" glimmers on Jos's fingertips.
- **I-Five: "I remember everything."** His memory gaps are filled.

## Ch. 21: bota is mutating; Kaird's theft plan
- A bribed xenobotanist reports a planetwide bota mutation: "within another generation, bota will be, for all intents and purposes, inert". (Comparison: Bogden ironwithe die-offs that reach "parsecs away" through "quantum entanglement" in DNA.)
- **Black Sun's military limits:**
> "Black Sun was a formidable criminal empire, but its ways were those of the poisoned chalice and the hidden dagger, not the blaster and the lightsaber. All of Black Sun's firepower couldn't match even that of the Republic's clone army on Drongar alone." (MS2 ch. 21)
- Kaird plans to steal 50–60 kg of bota in carbonite and a fast ship. He has kept the plan from Black Sun.
- Den and Eyar Marath ("You remind me of my grandfather").

## Ch. 22: Barriss's Force lecture to Uli
- She is "off her game", her lightsaber forms stiff. She senses Uli approaching under the noise of the saber.
- **Force lore:**
> "the Force may create midichlorians, sort of as its conduits into our continuum, rather than the other way around. They're isomorphic on every world that has life." / "it is bound to life and consciousness more deeply than the quantum level." (MS2 ch. 22)
> "It augments our strength, oxygenates our tissues, decreases reaction lag. Once, in Coruscant Park, I saw Master Yoda lift a rock as big as a family-sized electric cart, with what looked like nothing but a simple hand gesture." (MS2 ch. 22)
> "Four thousand years ago, Exar Kun, a Sith Lord, somehow destroyed an entire stellar system with his misuse of the Force." (MS2 ch. 22)
  - The last line is Barriss's in-universe claim and is loose. Exar Kun is usually tied to the Cron Supernova, which Legends normally credits to Kun's Krath allies Aleema and Ulic Qel-Droma.
- On the dark side: "It's more like a steep, slippery slope". "no Jedi had ever been faced with this particular choice before" (taking bota for Force power).
- **Kaird's résumé**: "an assassin who had taken out scores of the organization's enemies without ever once being arrested or even suspected". He can move a repulsor-carried block of "a metric ton or two" easily. "I'm stronger than I appear" (he plans to carry 70–80 kg). His thoughtshield is "solidly in place". A disguise flesh-suit filters Falleen pheromones.

## Ch. 23: I-Five's self-assessment
- I-Five, after his memory is restored: "I'm a machine. A very complex machine, capable of mimicking the thinking processes of a sentient to an astonishing degree ... And I have no real desire to be anything else." He describes his emotions as programmed, plus "extrapolate feelings" through his "lack of creativity dampers and my expanded synaptic grid". Then: "I think I'm having an anxiety attack."

## Ch. 24: Kaird tells Lens; Jos loses a patient
- The spy has many names; "Lens" is the one Black Sun uses. Kaird (in a fat-suit) tells Lens that bota will be inert "By its next generation" and suggests the Separatists might make "a final, all-out battle to secure what's left". Lens has access to medical records.
- An explosion at the AIA hydrogen plant (an industrial accident) brings 30-plus burn cases.
- **Clone physiology**: an exhausted Jos raises a pressor field to 7 and blows a normal human's aorta. A "Fett-clone ... circulatory system's wall strengths had been augmented to help keep wounds from bleeding as much". (MS2 ch. 24)

## Ch. 25: Den's scoop; I-Five's processor
- Den confirms the bota decline from three sources. Eyar proposes that he join her cohabitation cave, and he accepts.
- **I-Five's hardware**: "a SyntheTech AA-One nanoprocessor, operating at seven petahertz, with a five-exabyte capacity ... It took me six-point-one picoseconds to encode the basic algorithm". He can reprogram his autonomic subprocessor to simulate being drunk ("The hardware is protected. I have some leeway with the software, now that my full memories have returned"). (MS2 ch. 25)
- **Kaird's target**: the admiral's ship, Bleyd's old modified Surronian assault ship. "fast in atmosphere ... comparable to a Naboo N-1 starfighter—but ... fast to lightspeed, also", with fire-linked ion and laser cannons, under 30 m. He works through a Strag-Adept couple (the Twi'lek cook Ord Vorra and the pilot Biggs Bogan) to get the codes.
- **Column triggers the attack**: the coded message about bota failing goes to the Separatists. A "state-of-the-art broadband confounder" 5 km away in the jungle is the real cause of the comm blackout ("The official explanation, of course, was sunspots"). The spy hints at a love inside the camp ("one, in particular").

## Ch. 26: Barriss's second, deliberate bota dose; a precognitive vision; she nearly drowns in the Force
- She reasons "Ask the Force" and injects deliberately. "the rush was almost immediate this time."
- **A vision of the Force as a galaxy-wide tapestry with necessary "flaws"**. Reading one flaw gives her precognition:
> "The camp was in danger. There was a spy among them, the same one who had been responsible for the explosions of the shuttle, and on MedStar. Not dead, as they had thought, but still alive. This spy had initiated events that would, if left unattended, cause the destruction of all those who were there." (MS2 ch. 26)
  - "For the briefest of times, less than an eyeblink, she had more—she had the how and why and where and when of it—but then that was gone".
- **Losing control:** "she had not had the power or the skill or whatever was needed to control it properly ... she had somehow disrupted the flow of the Force". Her dark-tinged reaction: "No. She had power. Great power. She would use it!" A voice: "Let go ... Or—you could die." She recognizes it as Master Unduli.
- She is "gone" for **about twelve hours** (midday to midnight), and comes out refreshed, not hungry or thirsty.

## Ch. 27: the farewell party; Jos's despair
- I-Five runs his "inebriation algorithm" (brighter photoreceptors, a happy drunk). The Equani Klo Merit is described as "massive; Klo probably outweighed the Wookiee". Tolk is looking for Jos with an ominous expression.
- Jos thinks about suicide and steps back from it ("Just an hour, the next hour").
