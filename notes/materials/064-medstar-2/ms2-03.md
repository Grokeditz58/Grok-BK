# MedStar II: Jedi Healer: notes part 3 (ch. 28 to the Epilogue)

- Read 2026-11-03 CST from the owner's MEGA epub (handle VasSURja). Citation "MS2 ch. N".

## Ch. 28: a Wookiee rips off I-Five's arm; I-Five declares himself sentient
- Wookiee strength against droid armor: a drunk Wookiee loses at dejarik and "grab[s] I-Five's right arm, and wrench[es] it from the droid's shoulder. Circuitry and servomotor couplings broke free in a shower of sparks". (MS2 ch. 28) Jos: "You're lucky he didn't pull your head off."
- I-Five sobers up instantly by switching the program off. **"That I am indeed sentient."** He remembers his obligation: Lorn Pavan asked him to watch over his son **Jax** (ties into the Coruscant Nights trilogy).
- Kaird drugs the standby pilot Sebairns with a legume allergen.

## Ch. 29: Barriss's afterglow and grandiosity
> "She now understood how it was that the greatest Jedi Masters could sense things even parsecs away, information gained far faster than by subspace packet ... the universe was of an entire piece, each part connected to all the others, webbed together by vibrating strands of the Force". (MS2 ch. 29)
> "with it the Jedi could become more powerful than anyone could possibly imagine. They could stop this war ... abolish slavery, transform barren worlds into lush paradises ... Nothing would be beyond their capabilities—the power was that immense!" (MS2 ch. 29)
  - This is her boosted, arguably addicted viewpoint: "She wondered if that was the desire of a seeker, or an addict." It is an unreliable claim, not a feat.

## Ch. 30–31: I-Five's plan to desert; the Separatists commit to an all-out attack
- I-Five will desert if needed. Barriss gives him an eyes-only message and a vial of bota extract for the Temple.
- Oni females are "electrophoretic" (Uli gets shocked).
- The Separatists verify the bota decline and plan "an all-out attack ... Every mech and mercenary the other side could field". "They would not be taking prisoners."
- Thula and Squa hand Kaird the "bota" case (about 70 kg, "easily within his ability to pick up and carry").

## Ch. 32: Kaird gets the ship codes; Jos confronts Kersos
- Kaird drugs Bogan and Vorra with myocaine. He threatens them with a thermal detonator, which is actually a trainer, "a unit this size ... would shred everything in it, paint the walls with blood and vaporized flesh, and then knock down the walls. All in about a thousandth of a second." (MS2 ch. 32; Kaird's description of a small detonator)
- Kaird: "he had sent more than his share of people back to the Cosmic Egg in his time". He would kill himself rather than face a brain scan that would expose his assassination of Bleyd.
- Kersos lets Tolk watch the recording of Jos's declaration ("What I break, I fix").

## Ch. 33–34: Kaird steals the admiral's ship
- Bota grows only in about 1,000 square km of Southern Tanlassa, served by 15 Rimsoos. Kaird mimics Bogan's voice ("humans were easy, with their limited vocal cord system") and fakes a glitchy holo to clear Flight Control.
- I-Five on the mutation: "This entire planet is one huge transgenic experiment."

## Ch. 35: Barriss reaches Luminara; the Council felt the surge
- **Key scaling and lore beat:**
> "Master Yoda and several others on the Council mentioned recently that they sensed—not a disturbance, exactly, more like a surge—in the Force. Perhaps this is the explanation." (MS2 ch. 35, Luminara)
  - Barriss's bota-amplified connection on Drongar (Outer Rim) may have registered with Yoda and the Council on Coruscant. Jos later notes this can't be proven (ch. 37).
- Barriss: "using this new connection, I can prevent it. I have not the least doubt of that. The power is staggering." The call cuts out before Luminara can give advice.

## Ch. 36: the thieves' bomb
- Thula and Squa swapped the bota for **four thermal detonators linked in series with a timer**. Kaird finds it when a wheel breaks, jettisons it, and "The soundless flare was picked up by his rear array less than two minutes after jettisoning the bomb. **The readout showed a yield of half a kiloton.** The bomb would have turned him and the ship into a cloud of incandescent plasma." (MS2 ch. 36)
  - **Calc note:** about 0.5 kt from 4 detonators, roughly 0.125 kt (about 523 GJ) each, if the yield is split evenly. These may be heavy-class detonators (the earlier "small" trainer was a trigger-class unit), so treat this as a high-end thermal-detonator data point, not a standard one.
- Kaird stays bound to Black Sun and vows revenge on Thula and Squa.

## Ch. 37: Barriss can't convince Vaetes; "a lightsaber that could shear through a planet"
- Comms are jammed both ways ("subetheric disturbance"). Jos raises the possibility that it was a hallucination.
> "Who was she to wield a weapon that was, perhaps, tantamount to a lightsaber that could shear through a planet?" (MS2 ch. 37)
  - This is a metaphor for the bota-Force, not a feat.

## Ch. 38: finding the spy by Force-reading; the dome under fire
- **Limits of her mind-reading**: Barriss can read surface "essences" of the medical staff through healer empathy. She can't scan beneath the trained shields of Tolk (Lorrdian) or Merit (Equani minder). Vaetes "had no thoughtshield". Den and Tolk each open their minds voluntarily, and she reads them in moments. "Any killer with a mind open to her touch would have been like a black lamp among all these healing folk."
- **The force-dome's durability**: "Two-hundred-millimeter mortar. They can throw those at us until the local sun burns out—won't dent the shield. But when they crank up the charged particle spitters and the gigawatt lasers, then we'll be in trouble." (Den, MS2 ch. 38)
- **The spy is Klo Merit**, also known as Column and Lens.

## Ch. 39: Barriss refuses the third dose and becomes a Jedi Knight
- Her temptation in her own words:
> "To go from being a Padawan to becoming the most powerful Jedi in the galaxy in a matter of moments—one who could use the Force in ways no one had ever been able to even comprehend ... like an active volcano channeling molten rock ... There was nothing in the galaxy that could resist the Force, if it could but be channeled properly". (MS2 ch. 39)
- "Barriss was the only Jedi in a hundred cubic parsecs, as far as she knew." The droids aren't centrally controlled ("someone had wised up since" Naboo).
- Luminara's warning about the dark side: "It will feel better than anything you have ever experienced ... Worst of all, it will feel right."
- Her verdict: "That kind of power could not help but be addicting. It would consume anyone who was less than absolutely pure ... Did it make sense to have the powers of a god, without the wisdom of a god?" She puts the popper away.
- **"You truly became a Jedi Knight on the day when you realized that you already were one."** She is self-recognized as a Knight (ch. 42: "as a full-fledged Jedi Knight").
- The dome is expected to fail. Backscatter radiation risk; dosimeters are issued.

## Ch. 40: Merit's motive, a Republic superweapon test (major lore)
> "a solar flare burst from our sun that was over ten light-minutes long ... Equanus was cooked. The atmosphere and oceans boiled away in minutes; the land was turned into a burned-out cinder." (MS2 ch. 40)
> "that solar flare was not a natural disaster ... The Republic ... military leaders were testing a new weapon. A planet buster, a superweapon for some kind of ultimate battle station being developed. They fired it into our sun, and they miscalculated." (MS2 ch. 40)
- Nearly a billion Equani died, leaving "a few hundred, maybe a thousand at most". It happened "Two years, six months, and three days ago". The weapon-test base on the moon was destroyed too. This is an early Death Star-program superlaser test told from Merit's point of view. **Scaling:** a stellar-flare-induced planetary sterilization (atmosphere and oceans boiled "in minutes"). It is an accidental result through the star, not direct superlaser output.
- Jos shoots Merit, who was going for a sleeve hold-out blaster. The roof collapses on Jos and he gets a concussion.

## Ch. 41–42 and Epilogue: the dome fails; the Battle of Drongar ends
- **The dome finally fails** because of a lightning strike, not a beam: "it was a lightning bolt, rather than a beam, that finally overloaded the breakers". I-Five switches off to protect himself from EMP, then carries Den easily.
- Southern Tanlassa is burned. The **Venator-class Star Destroyer *Resolution*** arrives. The Republic salvages about 2 metric tons of bota. "The Battle of Drongar is over."
- I-Five: "All it took was the destruction of half a planet" (hyperbole). He and Den head to Coruscant and the Outlander Club, setting up Coruscant Nights.
- Epilogue: Jos and Tolk go to Corellia together. Kersos transfers to Borellos Base.
