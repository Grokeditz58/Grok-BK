## THE COMPLETE ALEX TAVOULARIS STORYBOARDS

Second draft *Adventures of the Starkiller, Episode I: The Star Wars* dated **28 January 1975**. Mid-February 1975 Lucas hires **Alex Tavoularis** to storyboard. He sketches only a few second-draft panels before the third draft, *The Star Wars: From the Adventures of Luke Starkiller*, August 1975; the majority of the boards are from this version. Scenes substantially different from, or absent in, the finished film; descriptive excerpts from the respective drafts accompany the boards, “however, some storyboards are slightly different from the script, as Lucas was constantly experimenting with new ideas.”

### Second Draft Storyboards

Excerpts typography-locked as printed: small rebel spacefighter chased by **four giant Imperial stardestroyers**; gun crews in heat-protective suits, goggles, breath masks cheer a direct hit. Artoo and Threepio struggle through a shaking passageway; a destroyer surges ahead; fire disintegrates the rebel ship’s “main solar fin”; Threepio lodged under a computer console. Captain’s First Officer, then “the sturdier legs of Captain Starkiller.” **DEAK STARKILLER**: “The shields are down. They’ll be boarding in no time. There is a Sith knight among them. I can feel his para-force. Hurry, secure your assault positions. The BOGAN force is strong with the enemy.” Stormtroopers with “chrome multi-laser rifles”; batons ignite into “a long glowing laser sword.” Deak spins, replaces the sword, draws a laser pistol, four shots. “something unspeakably evil and terrifying approaches the cabin.”

### Third Draft Storyboards

MAIN TITLE and ROLL UP against stars; “awesome yellow planet of Utapau emerges from total eclipse”; tiny silver craft, giant stardestroyer, solar fin disintegrates, “spectacular heavenly display.” Scraping of boarding equipment “run chills up Threepio’s bronze spine.” Blast opens a six-foot hole; stormtroopers with multi-laser rifles; rebels with “chrome laser pistols”; troopers “holding shattered arms and faces.” Robots blown into a power canister, gas from a broken pipe. Typography locked: “An awesome, seven-foot-tall DARK LORD OF THE SITH makes his way into the blinding light of the main passageway. Darth Vader raises his arms and objects that aren't bolted down, including the rebel warriors, are picked up by an invisible force and hurled to the end of the passageway.” Death-white wasteland, two huge twin suns, “LUKE STARKILLER, a farm-boy with heroic aspirations.” Lifepod: Threepio “I’m not getting in there. No more adventures…”; “Flames Lick at Threepio” [capital L as printed]; “That’s funny. The damage doesn’t look as bad from out here.” “The rebel cries out as the spooky looking Sith Lord begins to twist his arm, creating a gruesome snapping and popping of broken bones.” **LEIA ORGANA**, “a beautiful young girl about sixteen,” blasts two troopers, is captured. Jundland / “No Man’s Land”; robots argue and separate (Threepio / Artoo dialogue as excerpted, including Artoo’s “rather rude sound”). “The long ornate pirate starship approached the gray gaseous planet of Alderaan… drifts crab-like”; four Imperial third-fleet fighters, “small cylindrical power shells dominated by two huge solar fins.” Pilot: no life forms, escape pods “four, seven, and oh-three” jettisoned; Commander: “We don’t get many visitors. Let’s take a look at it.” “The Imperial city of Alderaan. The magnificent domed and gleaming city is perched, mushroom-like, on a tall spire which disappears deep into the misty surface of the planet.”

## THE COMPLETE IVOR BEDDOES STORYBOARDS

Once Lucas settled in London, early 1976, he hires **Ivor Beddoes** to storyboard certain fourth-draft sequences. Fourth draft finished **1 January 1976**: *The Adventures of Luke Starkiller as taken from the “Journal of the Whills” (Saga I) Star Wars*. “In it, the fate of Ben Kenobi is different from the final film, and Lucas had Beddoes draw up intricate blocking to illustrate Kenobi’s battle with Darth Vader.” Excerpts accompany the boards. **Do not collapse this survival version with the later decision that Obi-Wan dies.**

### Fourth Draft Storyboards

Two warriors motionless, swords locked, “low buzzing sound.” Threepio sees Ben emerge: “Where are the others? Oh, oh!” Ducks as seven stormtroopers who were guarding the starship rush toward “Ben and the Sith Knight.” Han, Luke, Chewbacca, and the princess run from a tunnel on the other side of the starship. “Vader takes advantage of Ben’s momentary distraction and brings his mighty laser sword down on the old man. But Ben manages to deflect the blow, swiftly turning around and cutting a safety lock on the blast door. Vader is standing in the access tunnel, while Ben is in the huge hangar, and the heavy blast door slams down between them, trapping Vader in the tunnel.” Ben charges the troops; Luke and Han fire from the ramp; droids board. Luke: “Get the ship started!” Leia grabs the laser pistol from Han’s hand. Troops fire point blank; Ben deflects, a bolt explodes near him, “knocking him to the floor.” Luke cuts down the remaining troops and rushes to his side. Leia: “Luke, quickly! There are more coming!” “Luke struggles to help the old man to the ship under the constant fire of the Imperial troops.” Leia hits the safety lock on the blast door, slamming it, “leaving only three troops left in the hangar.” Luke: “Close the main hatch and get this thing moving!”

## SELECTED JOE JOHNSTON STORYBOARDS

December 1975: Lucas writing the end battle; third draft described it “fairly briefly”; fourth draft would contain the details. To illustrate shots and montage he often refers to the eight-minute 16mm film made by videotaping WWII documentaries and movies; then works at ILM with **Joe Johnston**. “approximately 100 out of 366 of those early end-battle storyboards… for the first time complete with Richard Edlund’s penciled notes.” Notes often refer to **22 and 29 December 1975**, “apparently the dates of major revisions.” Boards show “Han Solo’s original pirate ship, which was about to be replaced by the ‘hamburger’ ship, aka the *Millennium Falcon*.” “this set of boards contains the *only visual representation* of Luke Starkiller’s *two* trench runs. In postproduction, the first of his trench runs would be eliminated.” “Luke’s two wingmen are Biggs and Chewie, who would shortly be replaced by Wedge, when *Chewie* became the nickname of *Chewbacca* in the fourth draft.” Omitted-in-review boards included “for completeness.” Descriptions and dialogue from the fourth draft. Abbreviation key: ALT, BD, BG, C/U, D.S., FLOP, FR (24 fps), L–R, POV.

### Fourth Draft End-Battle Storyboards

Selected locked lines (not every panel): Luke trying to spot Blue Leader; “Vader passes Blue Leader firing a laserbolt that creates a small explosion in one engine.” R-2 scrambles back to Blue Leader’s engine. Luke: “We’ve lost Blue Leader…” [control room, Ben urges Luke to trust his feelings]. “Wedge, Biggs, we’re going in. We’ll go in full throttle…” Biggs: “We seem to have upset them.” Luke’s X-wing in the trench; laserbolts suddenly stop; “Watch yourself. Increase your speed full throttle.” “Wedge (Chewie): ‘Coming in … point three.’” **Vader**: “Stay with them … They’ll have to slow down before they reach that tower …” Luke: “Almost home.” Vader: “Take them.” “Wedge, Biggs, split up … it’s the only way we’ll shake them.” Luke hit, Artoo to lock it down; “I think we lost those fighters… Blue Group, this is Blue Five.” Wedge waiting; Biggs: “Hold on, Luke, I’ll be right there.” [war room, Ben urges Luke to trust the Force.] “Biggs, are you all right?” Wedge: “wait a little longer…” Luke: “We can’t wait… I don’t think he made it.” Biggs (radio breaking up): “Hey you guys, what are you waiting for? … Don’t ever give up on old Biggs …” Targeting device; Wedge: “Here we go again.” Biggs: “Hurry, Luke. They’re coming in much faster this time.” Luke: “Close it up… Artoo, try to increase the power to the rear deflectors.” **Vader**: “I’m on the leader, take the other ones …” Wing Man: “Wha …” **Han**: “You’re all clear, Luke. Now blow this turkey and let’s get out of here.” Luke: “Glad you were here to see it … now let’s get some distance before the thing blows.”

## GEORGE LUCAS EXPANDS HIS UNIVERSE

Rinzler EE, “George Lucas Expands His Universe.” After the hit, Lucas “retired,” but the ancillary world was beginning: Alan Dean Foster had ghostwritten the novelization and was working on a sequel; Marvel monthly comics; merchandising deals; “lots of people needed to know more.” Lucas had draft notes, “words in progress.” To get everyone on the same page, he sits down with **Carol Titelman** and records “his knowledge of the world he had created.” Technique “similar to role-playing: He assumed a character and responded to questions about his or her background, personality, home planet, and so on.” **Lucas spoke as C-3PO on 28 July 1977; Leia on 5 August 1977; and as Han Solo on 11 August 1977.**

**Important Note** (typography locked, flag as Rinzler’s warning — these are first thoughts, later modified, *not* official continuity): “The following background stories and ideas, which have been reorganized by subject, were in many cases George Lucas’s first thoughts on what lay beyond the parameters of the first film. Many of these ideas, however, he has since modified to a lesser or greater degree. They are presented here only to provide an idea of how he first began this process in the days immediately following the success of the first film (which, for example, became “Episode IV: *A New Hope*” only upon its re-release in 1981). These stories are *not* part of any official continuity.”

### HAN SOLO: BACKGROUND

Lucas-as-Han, typography locked: “Han is around thirty years old. His parents might have been killed in a space battle with Han as the only survivor. There’s no real record about where the space gypsies found him. They run through the galaxy picking up all sorts of odds and ends. They may have won him in a game. They may have cheated someone out of him and thought they would sell him. He was kind of like a slave. Although the gypsies loved him in their own way, they were also mean to him. They used him for their own ends and he was very unhappy. They would send him out on dangerous missions and they would make him beg in the streets and that sort of thing. They abandoned him to the Wookiees when he was about seven years old; he lived with them for about five years.

“From the time Han was twelve to the time he was fifteen, he was in the Space Academy. That’s where he learned to be a starpilot and he was very, very good—only he was such a hustler that he got caught selling exam answers to the class. He also tried to race one of the professors in a spaceship and crashed it in the process. He didn’t beat the professor, but he was booted out. Then he went around and did a lot of things, finally coming back to the Wookiees and meeting Chewbacca.

“Then he went to the planet of Coonee, where he used to herd large animals called coldppedas, which have fifteen feet. They look something like a giraffe, about twenty-three feet tall, only with scales and a lizard head. Some of them are known to get as high as forty feet. They have huge hooves, which are round and soft. Their meat has an aphrodisiac quality, so they ship it all around the universe. He did that for three years.

“Han then worked as a mechanic in a factory that built spaceships on a planet called Saberhing. He was on an assembly line. He fell into smuggling when he was about twenty. Smuggling was really the result of the Empire, which has such stringent rules about tariffs, and the trade companies, which are so strong, so monopolistic. Most legitimate operators were squeezed out of the business. So Han could be described as a smuggler, but in reality he’s a free-enterprise small businessman who is trading between systems without benefit of a proper license from the trade unions.

“He smuggled everything under the sun. He smuggled spice along with everything else: various foodstuffs and precious gems and mechanical parts—whatever happened to be available… Han outwitted the Empire on numerous occasions and he’s made some very fast deals. One of the problems is that he gambles quite heavily and loses most of his money. He’s tough and sharp, only he somehow never manages to scrape together enough money to get any power. As a matter of fact, I think he enjoys this: He’s slightly self-destructive and enjoys being on the brink of disaster. So he’s constantly putting everything he’s got into some high-risk venture, and sometimes he wins and sometimes he loses. You might meet him and he may be worth ten billion credits one day and the next time you meet him, he’s in debt up to his ears.

“The Empire actually destroyed one of his ships, a bigger ship, not as fast as the *Millennium Falcon*. They blasted him and he had to drop a whole shipment in order to keep from getting caught. The Empire is really the Big Brother who watches over him and causes him trouble. He doesn’t really think of it as a political thing so much as somebody who is getting in his way, although he frequently works with, and for, bounty hunters, gangsters, Rebels, and the Empire if the price is right.

“He has a girlfriend in every port.”

### THE *MILLENNIUM FALCON*

“It’s of Corellian design, from Crell, and is used primarily by the Corellian spice shippers. They transport their goods to the center of the Empire… modifications… mostly to the navigation system to get through hyperspace in the shortest possible distance (par-sects). Crell is a rather grim planet, mostly gaseous. There’s no surface to it. The cities are large satellites and they float inside the planet, like in the fog, or in the gas. The inhabitants have wings and can fly. They are like porpoises with wings, but they also have claw-like hands.”

### C-3PO: BACKGROUND

Lucas-as-Threepio: “I’m about 112 years old and have had about forty-three masters. I was made in a robotic factory on the planet Affa, which is in one of the Outland Regions in the third sector of the Galactic Empire.” Storage in heart/chest, logic in the head (logic replaceable, storage not). All stored information transferred periodically into “a main computer located on Alderaan.” Diplomatic droids transfer once to three times a year. “Sometimes it’s a great relief when I go back to the Alderaan main computer and get my memory purged. To be renewed is everything—what more could one ask for than to have one’s youth back again?” Before the court of Alderaan, “roughly twenty years ago,” a sub-embassy in the Granicus system for a third liaison officer; “a good thirty-seven years before I was actually transferred into protocol.” Started as one of twelve hundred interpreter droids in the Outland; high attrition in strange climates; “I was lucky to be totally reassembled by a young boy working for a junk dealer. After several years I gained new coverings and became as good as new.” Then the court of Alderaan, “special assistant translator to the captain of a diplomatic starship, Captain Antilles.”

### PRINCESS LEIA: BACKGROUND

Lucas-as-Leia: Alderaan “very Earth-like. High snowcapped mountains, green pastures, very modern… about four or five million years old.” “I have two brothers, much younger than I am: One is four and one is seven. My parents are in their fifties.” Father a Senator, “president of that Senate,” “very kind… strict in his own way… very fair; he had a very gentle heart.” Mother “well educated, the head of several relief organizations, beautiful and warm.” Alderaan system: seven planets, Alderaan the capital. “I’ve been in school since I was two… trained by the finest minds… exceptionally bright… gifted schools… used to taking charge… overseen several kinds of operations… ever since I was seven years old. I have an ability for organization and command. Now I am a Senator.”

### LEIA ON THE HISTORY OF THE EMPIRE

Old Republic: systems sent representatives; “It wasn’t an Imperial Senate; it was a Republican Senate… There were 24,372 systems in the Galactic Senate.” Chancellor elected for four years, “only eligible for one term.” One Chancellor, “with the help of some of the large intergalactic trade companies and mining companies and intergalactic power companies,” buys a second term “because of a crisis,” corrupts the Senate, is made “Emperor for the rest of his life.” Rationalizing: elections in an emergency “disrupted the bureaucratic system”; bureaucracy “running amok”; Emperor would “bring the bureaucracy back in line.” He had “let the bureaucracy run amok” and blackmailed the Senate; “the key people in the bureaucracy were working for him and were paid by the companies.” Imperial Senate meetings dwindle to once a year, very short. “a little over half the Senate as it turned out was not involved, was not corrupted”; they try to oust him legally and impeach him. “many of the Senators who were fighting the Emperor at that time mysteriously died. The Jedi Knights were alerted immediately and they rallied to the Senate’s side. But there was a plot afoot and when the Jedi finally rallied and tried to restore order, they were betrayed and eventually killed by Darth Vader.”

### C-3PO ON DARTH VADER AND THE EMPEROR

“The Death Star had readouts about everyone on board, except Darth Vader. Darth Vader is really attached to the Emperor himself, and he was not really a part of the Death Star personnel or any of that system. Lord Vader worked directly for the Emperor and was the Emperor’s emissary. I’m sure people have seen the Emperor. There are no images of him [in Episode IV], but he’s there.”

### PRINCESS LEIA’s HISTORY OF DARTH VADER

“When the Jedi tried to restore order, Darth Vader was still one of the Jedi. What he would do is catch the Jedi off guard and, using his knowledge of the Force, he would kill the Jedi without them realizing what was happening. They trusted him and they didn’t realize he was the murderer who was decimating their ranks. At the height of the Jedi, there were several hundred thousand. At the time of the Rebellion, most of them were killed. The Emperor had some strong forces rally behind him, as well, in terms of the army and the Imperial forces that he’d been building up secretly. The Jedi were so outnumbered that they fled and were tracked down. They tried to regroup, but they were eventually massacred by one of the special elite forces led by Darth Vader. Eventually only a few, including Ben and Luke’s father, were left. Luke’s father is named Annikin.”

### PRINCESS LEIA AND THE REBEL ALLIANCE

“The Rebellion has been going on since before I was born, for about twenty years. My father was about forty at the time of the Rebellion, at the point when the revolution went through the Senate. But since Alderaan is a nonviolent system, we didn’t take up arms against the Empire… The more peaceful systems, like ours, became gatherers of information.” Government ships usually armed against “pirate smugglers”; “royal guard, more like guards than troops.” Men on her ship “handpicked because of their antipathy toward the Empire… that wasn’t really generally known.” “in a big disagreement with my father, I became more involved in [the militant] side, but I’m not a warrior. I’m more of a political leader, mostly doing mercy missions and ambassadorial work.” On the way back from the Senate to Alderaan, they intercept spies from the Death Star; a battle destroys “a lot of the star fleet”; “we flew near the station that we were supposed to fly near and intercepted a message from our spies. We flew on and then were chased, but we didn’t really take part in the battle.”

### C-3PO ABOARD THE *TANTIVE IV*

Hundreds of ambassadors; Threepio an interpreter for alien life-forms or planets without “the universal galactic language.” “I was programmed not to mention having knowledge of the Princess—that she was aboard the ship. That’s why I always seemed ignorant when she was brought up. I knew who she was, but I was programmed not to say who she was.”

### C-3PO TALKS ABOUT R2-D2

“I believe Artoo had his memory erased several times, but I’m sure that horrible fate never befell me. Actually, I met Artoo just recently. I didn’t meet him until we were on the *Tantive IV*. He was attached to that vessel for, I believe, a dozen years before I got there… I wouldn’t say that we have any feelings for each other… He might possibly have feelings for me… I wouldn’t say I’m fond of him.” Emotion program can override “under times of stress.” “Artoo-Detoo’s function is to be a repair robot… Information retrieval is not his main function… Essentially, he is just a plumber, and his primary function is to maintain the plumbing system of the ship,” though he could do anything on most ships in service.

### COIN OF THE GALACTIC REALM

Galactic coin of the realm and credit units “in the more civilized systems.” Coin: “a crystal-like mineral. It is flat and square… only manufactured by the Empire and they are foolproof.” Outland / Tatooine: mostly barter; farmers wouldn’t use the Imperial coin; also local currencies “that aren’t good anywhere else.”

### TATOOINE

“mostly a trading planet and a stopping-off point in the middle of nowhere.” They mine “certain power sources… used in some of the intergalactic ships, and a lot of farmers just farm water… It’s a very hard planet. There are only a few women, wives of the settlers, but generally not that many. Because Mos Eisley is a spaceport, mostly men go there… on the edge of the galaxy, so it’s a natural place to stop while going to and from the center of the galaxy.”

### LEIA ON THE DESTRUCTION OF ALDERAAN

“I would assume that my brothers were killed on the planet, along with my parents. But since I haven’t had contact with my parents in quite a while, I don’t know whether they were on the planet or not. When I got back to the Rebel base, they didn’t know, either, because they hadn’t had any contact.”

### THE FORCE

“The Force really doesn’t have anything to do with the lightsaber. Anybody can have a lightsaber. It’s just a weapon like a pistol, and Leia could use the lightsaber as well as anyone else. But she really hasn’t had any training with a lightsaber because she doesn’t really like them; she prefers pistols. The Force is really a way of feeling; it’s a way of being with life. It really has nothing to do with weapons. The Force gives you the power to have extrasensory perception and to be able to see things and hear things, read minds and levitate things. It is said that certain creatures are born with a higher awareness of the Force than humans. Their brains are different; they have more midi-chlorians in their cells.

“The Force is a perception of the reality that exists around us. You have to come to learn it. It’s not something you just get. It takes many, many years. Luke is on the road to knowing the Force, but it will be another twenty years before he actually begins to cope with it on a real level. He’s still an amateur. As far as Luke knows, Ben is the last of the Jedi Knights. But there could be more. Obviously Darth Vader knows the Force, but as far as we know at this point, Luke is one of the last surviving apprentices of the Force. The Force is always there, however. Anyone who studied and worked hard could learn it. But you would have to do it on your own.”

### STORMTROOPERS

“Some of the stormtroopers are women, but there weren’t that many women assigned to the Death Star. We can assume that there are quite a few elsewhere.”

### C-3PO EXPLAINS AWARDS AND WOOKIEES

Now attached to Leia and Luke “as their personal interpreter and servant.” Reward: refurbished, “a new XB21 memory system, which is about the greatest honor a droid can have,” new outer shell recoated and polished; Artoo cleaned up too. “Chewbacca wasn’t given a medal because medals don’t really mean much to Wookiees… The Wookiee Chewbacca was in fact given a great prize and honor during a ceremony with his own people. The whole contingent from the Rebel Alliance went to Chewbacca’s people and participated in a very large celebration. It was an honor for the entire Wookiee race.”

Closing caption: Marvel’s continuing series as one expansion of the galaxy “until the release of the next film.”
