## Key power-scaling / lore points
1. **Malak's endgame power is borrowed:** in the final chamber he drains eight comatose Jedi in stasis to restore his vitality and Force; destroying them cuts the drain and he loses half his FP (r147/p146; crop `crops/r147-malak-final-fight.png`). No boss stat block is given.
2. **Revan reveal (r132/p131):** the player was Darth Revan, betrayed by apprentice Malak; the Council captured him and rebuilt his memory with a Republic identity.
3. **Malak's rise (r16/p15):** Malak and Revan fought the Mandalorians as young Knights, found the Star Forge and fell; Malak betrayed Revan and took the mantle. Quoted wording "Malak seized the mantle of his fallen Sith Master and proclaimed himself the new Dark Lord" is **UNVERIFIED** (paraphrase).
4. **Bastila's reputation (r7/p6):** "Even in the dark halls where the Sith Lords gather, young Bastila is whispered to be a Jedi of note." Battle Meditation; Sentinel Lv3.
5. **Rakata / Star Forge:** Rakata built the star maps and the Star Forge, which runs on a star's power (r141–143); the guide says the Rakata terraformed Kashyyyk from barren to forest (r87–94).
6. Intro framing (r4/p3): Malak, "last surviving apprentice of Dark Lord Revan", has "unleashed an invincible Sith armada".
7. Juhani's struggle between instinct and training can lead to the dark side (r12/p11); Darth Bandon is a former student of Malak (r15/p14).
