## Thesis

Cade put up a real fight. He "certainly didn't go down without making Krayt work for it". Ant's support:

- Talon's warning means Krayt could not "easily" win;
- Krayt had every reason to go all out: his kill-bend-break plan and the imminent Omega Red toxin;
- Cade withstood three Force attacks (a push, a telekinetic rock attack he dodged, and lightning he bent away);
- losing the saber exchange does not show that Krayt was vastly superior.

Ant concedes Cade "never pressed Krayt offensively".

## Arguments (numbered)

**Part I — Talon's assessment**

1. **Talon understands Krayt's power best.** Ant cites:
   - "purpose of her existence is to execute Krayt's will" (Legacy #0);
   - "an extension of the Dark Lord's personal will" (Galactic Files);
   - she witnessed Krayt dominate Nihl, his galaxy-wide call (Havok and Vul Isen lines), the Wyyrlok duel, and "powers have multiplied".
2. **Why must she warn Krayt?** Ant tests three proposals:
   - **Proposal 1 (ambush): rejected.** Krayt wants Cade to come to him (p46); the Temple fight is loud; the shields are down.
   - **Proposal 2 (Krayt underestimates him): possible.** Even so, Krayt "cannot 'easily' defeat Cade". Cade has to be dangerous to "a not-particularly-trying Krayt". The Skywalker X+10 jump is "baked in", so Talon must fear X+20. Ant also cites "flashes of brilliance", the shuttle thrown at Talon, the gutting, Dark Transfer, and Claws: "THE ONLY THING THAT SAVES YOU THUS FAR IS THE BLOOD OF VADER…"
   - **Proposal 3 (take him seriously): possible.**

**Part II — Krayt's intentions**

3. **Kill → bend → break → join** (p46). "Kill" means sending Cade as near death as possible, echoing Krayt's own death ("EVERY NERVE FIBER ABLAZE", "YAWNING ABYSS OF THE GRAVE", "ASTRIDE THE GRAVE", p2–3). So it is "goal optimizing" to "unload everything on Cade".
4. **Omega Red time pressure.** The toxin kills "within a matter of breaths" (p108), and Sith are immune. It probably keys on dark-side energy, and Cade is still "on the edge of light and dark" (p123), so he might not survive it. Krayt therefore has to finish fast. "If not for Antares Draco stopping the virus, which Krayt did not foresee or anticipate, Fel would have unleashed the virus before Krayt had actually defeated Cade… if Krayt could readily defeat Cade, he would have. The fact he didn't reveals he couldn't."
5. **"Surrender" means Dun Moch or "just give up"**, not an expectation that Cade will drop his saber. Krayt has a new red-hilted saber, "going into this fight with some consideration".

**Part III — fight breakdown**

6. **§1, Force blast (p118, top).** Cade is caught mid-leap. Krayt pairs "SO MUCH MORE NOW" with an attack that "probably" shows the new power; otherwise each of his goals is undermined. "Here's multiple reasons why Krayt should go all out versus here's zero reasons why Krayt should not."
7. **§2, "the most disputed panel" (p118, middle).** Ant reads it as Krayt breaking rocks while Cade jumps and pivots along them, not Krayt "dragging Cade through the air". The dialogue is Cade's boast. If Krayt had him in a TK hold, why switch to lightning and say "surrender" again? (Dooku's hold on Kenobi was decisive.)
8. **§3, lightning (p118, bottom).** Krayt changes attack, grimaces, and the bolded "**SURRENDER**" shows he is raising his voice. Ant gives five options for the spiralling lightning:
   - (1) Krayt losing control through rage, like Sidious in RotJ;
   - (2) Cade bending it, as Duursema drew Shado bending Maladi's lightning (B018);
   - (3) Krayt unwilling to use lightning (rejected);
   - (4) Krayt charging it (rejected);
   - (5) bad art (rejected).

   Both remaining readings "look great for Cade", and Cade exits unscathed.
9. **§4, the fist (p122).** Krayt engineers an opening and punches Cade's exposed chest with a Dark-Transfer fist. Ending one panel does not show how the duel went: the Qui-Gon/Maul mock-up (B020), plus chess and MMA analogies. They moved outdoors, so the fight lasted "some time". Cade is a far better Force user than duelist (Talon p112 is smiling in the saber lock). Krayt uses one saber because:
   - he is a Teräs Käsi master and has a lethal fist;
   - LECG gives him "Dual Weapon Mastery II", not III, so two blades carry a trade-off;
   - Revan and Palpatine are precedents for fighting equally well with one.
10. **Conclusion.** "He certainly didn't go down without making Krayt work for it."

## Common rebuttals / weak points (ANALYSIS — not from the thread)

- **The Omega Red premise is mostly off-page.**
  - Krayt's lines about the toxin are spoken *inside the vision* (p123), and the launch never happens: Draco strikes Fel down at p126 after feeling Krayt die.
  - Maladi built it as Krayt's agent: "PROMISES KEPT. IT IS DONE, MY LORD KRAYT." (p108).
  - Krayt designed Sith immunity in, and his vision *includes* the toxin ("DISPERSED BY THE EMPEROR'S OWN HAND… WAS THE VECTOR").
  - He never voices any hurry: "YES, NIHL...AS I FORESAW..." (p116); "YOUR BATTLE HERE WAS LOST BEFORE YOU ARRIVED" (p118).
- **"Krayt did not foresee Draco" is unsupported.** No page says what Krayt foresaw about the launch.
- **"Zero reasons not to go all out" ignores the stated plan.** Krayt wants Cade to *survive* and turn. Lethal overkill risks the very prize he wants, so measured force is just as "goal optimizing".
- **Teräs Käsi is not in our Legacy material.** It appears in neither the LECG stat block nor Book 1/Claws.
- **Lightning-bending (option 2) is plausible, but Cade *leaps through* the lightning** ("NOT GONNA HAPPEN!"). Our 010 read is "Cade leaping through/over the lightning". The page does not show it bent "into his blade".
- **"Moved outdoors, so the fight lasted some time":** the location change is real (p122 is in open air), but it says nothing about who was winning.

## Characters touched and implied scaling (FORUM OPINION)

- Krayt > Cade (Ant concedes), but Cade "made Krayt work for it".
- Talon is a reliable judge of Krayt's power.
- Cade: Force user > duelist.
- Krayt one-saber ≈ two-saber (DWM II).

| 2 | The warning proves Krayt can't win "easily" | **Partly** | The warning is real (p114). Whether it implies "not easily" depends on Talon's reliability. | 4 | Omega Red forces Krayt to hurry | **Disagree** | See weak points: the in-vision line, a toxin Krayt planted himself, no stated hurry, and immunity Krayt designed. Ant's "uncertain whether Cade survives the toxin" is a fair *risk*, but the comic never treats it as one. |
| 5 | "Surrender" = Dun Moch | **Partly** | It is a reasonable reading of a taunt. It is not decisive either way. |
| 6 | The opening blast shows newfound power | **Disagree (unproven)** | A push that throws Cade across the room fits any power level. Ant himself concedes "Necessarily? No." |
| 7 | Disputed panel = Cade jumping along rocks | **Undecided** | The dialogue favours Ant: Cade is boasting, and Krayt answers "IT DOESN'T MATTER". The art (white streak from Krayt's sphere to Cade; Cade beside a breaking wall, legs folded) favours a TK hit or hold. Neither reading is proven. |
| 8 | Lightning: Cade bends it or Krayt raged | **Partly** | The bolded "SURRENDER" and the grimace are on the page. "Cade bends it" is plausible from the art (bolts arc around him), but the page shows him leaping *through* it. "Krayt lost control like Sidious" is speculation. |
| 9 | Saber loss ≠ vast inferiority | **Agree (on method)** | One panel cannot show the duel's course. That cuts both ways: it does not show a close duel either. The DWM II point is verified. Teräs Käsi is not in our material. |
| 10 | "Made Krayt work for it" | **Partly** | Cade survived a push, an ambiguous TK phase and lightning, then lost to the fist. "Work" is a fair word for multiple techniques; it is not evidence of a close contest. |

**Overall:** the arguments from Krayt's intentions are the weakest part. Omega Red is mostly a vision device, and the toxin was Krayt's own plan. The strongest parts are the method point (one panel ≠ the whole duel) and the bolded "SURRENDER" with Krayt's escalation through several techniques. A probability case built on "likely" cannot carry the full-power conclusion.

## New knowledge

- **Correction to our 010 p118 reading.** "Catches Cade's saber attack bare-handed" is unsupported. The panel is a TK-sphere / white-streak / breaking-wall image with three viable readings. Wookieepedia's "dragged through a wall" is one of them. The page sequence p118 → p122 (outdoors) probably skips no page; the duel just jumps location.
- **Krayt planted the toxin.** p108, "PROMISES KEPT. IT IS DONE, MY LORD KRAYT." That turns Omega Red from a threat to Krayt's plan into part of it, and it decides the 016/019/020 exchange on this point.
- **The bolded "SURRENDER"** (p118 bottom) is a real lettering cue of rising intensity. Ant's observation is correct.
- **The handbook's Talon line** (009 p449) partly matches the Legacy #0 quote.
