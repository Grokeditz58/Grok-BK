# The Phantom Menace (Terry Brooks): notes part 4, Chapters 16–21

- FULL.txt l.1591–2137, hand-read 2026-11-03 CST. Citation "TPM ch. N".

## Ch. 16: Coruscant, Palpatine, the Council hearing
- Coruscant: "atmosphere was filtered through oxygen regulators and purified by scrubbers, and water was gathered and stored in massive artificial aquifers. Native animals ... in the museums". Ric: "A nice place to visit, but I wouldn't want to live there."
- Valorum has "startling blue eyes". Palpatine "glanced over his shoulder at them ... a look of skepticism".
- Palpatine's pitch: "the chancellor has little real power. He is mired in baseless accusations of corruption. A manufactured scandal surrounds him. The bureaucrats are in charge now." / "You could call for a vote of no confidence".
- **Jedi Temple and Council:** "A colossal pyramid with multiple spires". There are twelve Council members, among them Ki-Adi-Mundi, Adi Gallia, Depa Billaba and Even Piell. "The shape of the room and the Council seating reflected the Jedi belief in the equality of and interconnection between all things."
- Qui-Gon: "the one who attacked me on Tatooine is a Sith Lord." Ki-Adi-Mundi: "The Sith have been gone for a millennium!" Yoda: "Threatened, the Republic is, if the Sith are involved." / "Hard to see, the dark side is".
- **"Vergence" (a novel-only term):** "I have encountered a vergence in the Force." / "Located around a person?" (Mace) / "It is possible he was conceived by midi-chlorians."
  > "midi-chlorians formed the link between everything living and the Force" / "a prophecy, so old its origins had long since been lost, that a chosen one would appear, imbued with an abundance of midi-chlorians, a being strong with the Force and destined to alter it forever." (TPM ch. 16)
  > "Finding him was the will of the Force." (Qui-Gon)
- Rabé to Anakin: "This time you won't have to listen from behind a door."

## Ch. 17: Senate session, vote of no confidence, Council test
- Senate chamber: floating repulsor boxes. Trade Federation senator Lott Dod. Aks Moe of Malastare invokes the commission rule ("It is the law"). Vice chair Mas Amedda. "Section 523A takes precedence here."
- Palpatine, whispering: "Enter the bureaucrats, the true rulers of the Republic, and on the payroll of the Trade Federation, I might add". Amidala: "I was not elected Queen to watch my people suffer and die while you discuss this invasion in committee. ... I move for a vote of no confidence in the supreme chancellor." Edcel Bar Gan of Roona seconds. "Vote now!" Valorum shows "the look of betrayal". Lott Dod "exchanged a quick glance with Palpatine, but neither spoke." (This is a novel-only hint of collusion.)
- Anakin meets the Queen (Sabé): "Padmé isn't here, Anakin. I sent her on an errand."
- **Obi-Wan on training age:** "Training for the order after more than a year of life was doomed to fail. At nine years of age, Anakin Skywalker was simply too old." (This contrasts with ch. 11's "first six months of birth".) Obi-Wan thinks "There were hundreds of these old prophecies". "you could be sitting on the Council by now if you would just follow the code." / "You still have much to learn, my young Padawan."
- Council test: Anakin names images before they appear ("A bantha. A hyperdrive. A proton blaster ... A Republic cruiser. A Rodian cup. A Hutt speeder."). Yoda: "See through you, we can." / "To the dark side, fear leads. To anger and to hate. To suffering." / "Much fear in you, I sense, young one."

## Ch. 18: The nomination, the Council verdict, departure
- Jar Jar describes the Gungan army: "Gotta big energy shields, too. Nutten get through. Gotta energy balls, fly outta slings and splat electricity and goo." Amidala: pain exists "To motivate us, I imagine."
- Nominees: Palpatine, "Bail Antilles of Alderaan and Aks Moe of Malastare". Amidala: "Senator, this is your arena. I must return now to mine." / "No place is safe, if the Senate doesn't condemn this invasion." Jar Jar catches "the barest glimpse of a smile on the senator's shrewd face."
- **Council verdict:** Mace: "He is too old. There is already too much anger in him." Yoda: "Clouded, this boy's future is. Masked by his youth." Qui-Gon: "I take Anakin Skywalker as my Padawan apprentice." / "We forbid it." Obi-Wan: "I am ready to face the trials!" / Yoda: "What know you of ready?" Qui-Gon: "Obi-Wan is headstrong, and he has much to learn still about the living Force". Mace: "Go with the Queen to Naboo and discover the identity of this dark warrior". Yoda: "Take him with you, but train him not!" / "do not intercede if it comes to war until we have the Senate's approval."
- Obi-Wan: "The boy is dangerous. They all sense it. Why can't you?"
- R2 uses his "onboard jets" to recover after falling off the platform. (Rocket-booster feat in 1999.)
- **Midichlorians explained:** "microscopic life-forms that reside within the cells of all living things and communicate with the Force." / "We are symbionts with the midi-chlorians." / "Without the midi-chlorians, life could not exist" / "When you learn to quiet your mind, you will hear them speaking to you." Also "your focus determines your reality."
- Sidious to Gunray: "I am sending Darth Maul to join you. He will deal with the Jedi." Haako: "A Sith Lord, here with us?"

## Ch. 19: Naboo; the real Queen; the Gungan alliance; the plan
- Anakin learns the cockpit controls "with astonishing quickness". Qui-Gon reads the Council's real objection: "not his age, but the conflict they sensed within him ... He was vulnerable to his inner conflict, and the dark side would be quick to take advantage of this."
- Panaka: "There are only twelve of us!" Qui-Gon: "The Jedi cannot fight a war for you". "you don't need a blockade once you control the port."
- Handmaidens now wear trousers with blasters. Obi-Wan and Qui-Gon reconcile ("twenty-odd years of friendship. Qui-Gon was like a father to him"): "I have taught you all I can. You will be a great Jedi". Qui-Gon: "The Jedi have no authority to take sides." / "It is a fine line we walk, then."
- Otoh Gunga was "attacked at daybreak two days earlier". The Gungans have fled to the sacred place. Captain Tarpals: "Ouch time, Binks."
- **Padmé reveals herself:** "You did well, Sabé. But I will have to do this myself." / "Sabé serves from time to time as my decoy, my loyal bodyguard." R2 recognizes her first. Boss Nass: "Yous no think yous greater den da Gungans!"
- Sidious: "This is an unexpected move for the Queen ... It is too aggressive. Lord Maul, be mindful." / "Let them make the first move."
- Jar Jar is made "bombad general" and faints. Panaka: "A few hundred officers and guards have formed an underground movement".
- **The plan:** "The battle is a diversion. ... Neimoidians don't think for themselves." / "Cut off the head, and the serpent dies." Knocking out the control ship disables the droids. Obi-Wan: "if the viceroy should escape ... he will return with another droid army". Qui-Gon: "You stay close to me, Annie, do as I say, and you'll be safe."
- Sidious: "Our young Queen surprises me ... She is more foolish than I thought." Maul: "The two Jedi may be using the Queen for their own purposes." Sidious: "Even Qui-Gon Jinn cannot break that covenant." / "Wipe them out, Viceroy. All of them."

## Ch. 20: Armies deploy; Maul goes hunting
- **Gungan tech:** fambaa carry shield generators: "a pulse of red light arced from a generator atop one fambaa to a dish atop the next ... changed from red to gold". **The shield tanks sustained tank cannon fire:** "Searing beams hammered into the shield and shattered ineffectively ... they could not break through the Gungan energy shield."
  - The shield's limit, stated explicitly: "designed to deflect large, slow-moving objects of density and mass such as artillery vehicles and small, fast-moving objects generating extreme heat such as projectiles from weapons fire. But it would not deflect small, slow-moving droids".
- The Federation line spreads "more than a kilometer". Droids unfold from racks, "thousands of droids".
- General Ceel tells Jar Jar to "set a good example for his people and die well."
- Anakin's private vow: "he would keep them safe no matter what. That would be his job." (A recurring protect-them motif.) Padmé: "I was the same person before, whether you knew the truth about me or not."
- **Maul, about to strike:** "I told you there was more to this than was apparent!" / "I have waited a long time for this. I have trained for it endlessly. The Jedi will regret their decision to return here." / "I'm going to the main hangar to rid you of the Jedi once and for all."

## Ch. 21: Hangar assault; the duel begins; Anakin launches
- Padmé in combat: "she fired her blaster with quick, precise moves, bringing down droid after droid ... She moved with the skill and training of a seasoned fighter ... becoming instead a deadly combatant." Anakin recalls his army dream.
- **Maul's entrance:** "He stepped out of the shadows like a large sand panther". The double blade is revealed: "Gleaming blade fire jutted from both ends of the handle ... he swung the weapon before him in an idle, casual gesture, beckoning the Jedi ahead."
- Jar Jar's accidental feats: a decapitated droid tangled on him keeps firing, "finding droid targets more often than Gungans". The troops rally to "Jar Jar Binks!". Destroyer droids are unleashed.
- **First exchange, 2v1:** "a clash of lightsabers that produced the shriek of diamond-edged saw blades cutting through metal. ... The Sith Lord was supple and quick ... more than holding his own against their efforts to bring him down. He was skilled, Anakin saw—more skilled, perhaps, than the men he faced." (TPM ch. 21)
- Anakin and R2: R2 starts the fighter. Anakin kills three destroyer droids with the nose lasers ("Droid blasters!"). He sees "the Sith Lord driving the Jedi across the hangar ... pressing them backward relentlessly". The fighter goes to space on autopilot, and R2 reports "There is no manual override". Bravo Leader is Ric Olié.
