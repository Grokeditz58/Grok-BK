## A Conversation with Terry Brooks (l.2426–2467)
- Lucas asked for an Anakin-POV take: "He had originally thought to tell the story from Anakin's point of view. ... I could write new material and have a free hand in adapting the movie. ... So the book is actually an expansion of the movie." (l.2435) **Debate use:** this counters "it's just a script transcription"; the novel's added narration (Maul's superiority, Sidious's assessment) is author-sanctioned expansion.
- Lucas: "Don't worry, I don't make science fiction movies." (l.2429). TPM was Brooks's first #1 bestseller. He works from an outline. His advice is "Read, read, read. Outline, outline, outline. Write, write, write. Repeat."
- Nothing in this section bears on power scaling.

## Darth Maul: *End Game* by James Luceno (bonus short story, © 2012; starts l.2513)
Legends continuity. It is set between TPM ch. 15 and 21 and ties into Luceno's *Darth Plagueis* era: Hego Damask, Orsis, Trezza.

### Tatooine duel reframed from Maul's POV (l.2518–2544)
- Maul pursues on the speeder and attacks with "some 250 meters separating Qui-Gon and the slave boy" from the ship.
- **Maul's own rating of Qui-Gon:** "Qui-Gon had quieted his mind and brought his imposing might to bear against Maul's agility. He had matched Maul's furious strokes with a disciplined intensity all his own." / "The Force favors this Jedi!" / "finally a worthy opponent. Not since he had fought and been defeated by his own Master, Darth Sidious, had Maul been so committed to a challenge." (l.2524–2526)
- **Fight trend:** "just when Qui-Gon's stamina was beginning to flag and the fight was tipping in Maul's favor ... Qui-Gon had fled." (l.2527). This supports the TPM ch. 15 reading.
- **Excuses Maul considered:** "the leg wound he had sustained during his brief capture by Togorian pirates" (a *Shadow Hunter* / *Saboteur* tie-in?). Also: "the slave boy ... a seeming nexus of Force energy, the boy had somehow abetted Qui-Gon in the fight." (l.2531) **This is a debate caveat: on Tatooine Maul was injured and possibly Force-hampered by Anakin.**
- In hindsight he would "simply race in at top speed with his lightsaber already ignited and decapitate Qui-Gon Jinn".
- **Prior kills (resume):** "single-handedly butchered the trainers and trainees at the Orsis combat academy five years earlier": the Mandalorian Meltch, "his pair of lethal Rodians", Trezza, and the Nautolan Kilindi (l.2529). Also the "massacre of the leaders of the Black Sun crime syndicate" (l.2537), the killing of Hath Monchar (l.2580), and foiling his Nightsister abductors (l.2546). He "had come close to losing to Trezza" in hand-to-hand combat (l.2695).
- Maul's backstory: raised by Sidious's custodial droids on Mustafar ("forged in fire"); a Dathomiri Zabrak. The *Scimitar* has a cloak (l.2518, 2549).

### Naboo operations (l.2545–2750)
- With the *Scimitar*'s cloak, he slips "well within firing range of the control ship and a squadron of drone starfighters, without the Neimoidians even being aware" (l.2550). He believes he could solo-infiltrate and destroy the control ship.
- **Force sound illusions:** "used the Force to create sounds that tricked the droids into moving in one direction or another." (l.2555)
- **Squads of B1s:** "decapitating them with his blade or exploding them by deflecting blaster bolts back at them", multiple patrols in succession. He snaps a security droid's head off and crushes it in one hand as a comlink: "squeezed the head so tightly, it began to fold in on itself" (l.2558–2563).
- Orders OOM-9: "Poison the waters ... then drain the marsh." (l.2616)
- **Magneta fight:** "moving faster than human eyes could follow, breaking Magneta's neck with the edge of his hand", kicks the bodyguard, dodges "a hail of blaster bolts", dives through a window, and kills a group of armed farmhands with "his bare hands" (l.2651–2652).
- Lore: Magneta (ex-security chief for King Veruna) claims Hego Damask and "his puppet" Palpatine have been colluding "for over two decades" and killed Veruna. **Maul does not know of Damask/Plagueis** and wonders "Was it possible that Darth Sidious himself had a clandestine Master?" (l.2646, 2689). Maul fears he is "a mere stealth agent and assassin" rather than a true Sith.
- The Bothan surveyor Leika provides the Gungan city coordinates: Rellias (Boss Ganne, Ankura Gungans), the plasma channel to Otoh Gunga, the Sacred Place.
- **Battle of Rellias Straits feat (l.2692–2699):**
  - Force-jumps to catch a passing STAP one-handed, while evading or deflecting "flights of energy lances and spheres".
  - Drops from 20 m and Force-propels himself "tearing through a score of mounted Gungans" with lots of dismemberment.
  - "launched himself into the air ... five meters from the Boss", beheads the general, knocks Ganne off his mount.
- **Mind trick:** Force suggestion on Ganne ("Responding to the Force suggestion, Ganne's eyes grew even blanker"), so he opens the gates. On the Sacred Place secret, Ganne resists ("something inside him was battling the compulsion"), so Maul tortures him with the saber and kills him (l.2702–2713).
- Lore: Maul reasons "What Sith in the past thousand years had been allowed to wield a lightsaber in open battle?" (l.2697).
- **Force concealment:** he spies on the Sacred Place "a couple of kilometers south ... his presence in the Force deliberately diminished", using probe droids as bugs (l.2744). The Jedi do not detect him.
- Maul now "wanted the Neimoidians to fail, despite his Master's plan" (l.2745). He reads Sidious's "foolish" remark as feigned: Sidious wanted a grand battle and a Maul–Jedi contest "to be assured that his apprentice had what it took to be a true Sith" (l.2737–2739).
- Maul's view of the stakes: "Maul's task remained as before: to kill two Jedi. The rest of it ... was nothing more than theater." / "The real war was, as ever, between the Sith and the Jedi." (l.2751–2752)

### Maul pre-scripts the Duel of the Fates (l.2755–2770). Key Maul-strategy lore.
- **Maul deliberately thins the hangar guard:** "close to four hundred B1 droids", reduced to sixty ("I will personally make up for the reduced count"), so that Amidala's team gets through and the Jedi are not "worrying too much about her safety" (l.2755–2760).
- **He rehearses the arena:** paces the power-station catwalks, "Several times he leapt to higher or lower catwalks. Once he had committed the arrangement to both mental and muscle memory" (l.2764). **He learns the laser-gate timing:** "after he passed through the gates several times ... Maul began to discern a subtle pattern ... twice he came close to being fried" (l.2765). He drops a hydrospanner into the core and never hears it land.
- "He would use the laser gates to separate the Jedi. ... he would kill one of them just there." (l.2768). **Debate implication: Maul had home-field prep. The gates separating Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan were his plan, not luck.** This contradicts Sidious's "bad luck and chance" (TPM ch. 24).

### Maul's defeat, retold (l.2771–2778)
- "Maul had savored the pained surprise in Qui-Gon's blue-gray eyes as the crimson blade ran him through." Obi-Wan dangles "two meters below" on "a nozzle". Maul showers sparks on him by dragging his blade along the rim.
- His fatal error: "Maul had sabotaged himself by drawing out his moment of victory". Obi-Wan "leapt straight out of the core and somersaulted in midair ... with Qui-Gon's Force-summoned weapon in his hand."
- **Here he is "bisecting him at the hips"**, matching the film. The TPM novel has a chest slash (ch. 23). **This is a continuity conflict between the two texts in the same ebook.**
- Maul recalls performing "the same feat ... on Orsis ... the first time he had used the Force among beings others than his Master".
- "Sidious is rid of another problem, for I am not yet a true Sith." / "He would survive his defeat, and grant himself yet another second chance." (This sets up his survival.)

## "Star Wars—The Expanded Universe" promo (l.2780–2787)
- A Legends primer: the Solo kids, Luke's new Jedi order, and Darth Bane as "the lone survivor of a great Sith war and created the 'Rule of Two'". (TPM ch. 10 instead says Bane "had survived" a self-destruction of the Sith.)
