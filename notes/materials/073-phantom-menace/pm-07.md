- FULL.txt l.2789–4435 (the true end; the brief said 4387) hand-read 2026-11-03 CST. Citation is by FULL.txt line. These are **excerpts of other books**, so they are marked as such. Use them only as supplementary feats (and the full books get their own entries).

## Excerpt: *Rogue Planet* by Greg Bear (l.2789–3019). Anakin is 12, three years after TPM.
- An illegal garbage-pit wing race on Coruscant. The pit is "two kilometers wide and three deep". Canisters fire "Every five seconds". "over five thousand such garbage pits on Coruscant ... a trillion tons of garbage every hour" (l.2840–2841).
- Worms "hundreds of meters long and three or four meters wide". "It was said a garbage worm could actually eat a defunct hyperdrive core and survive … for a few seconds" (l.2941, 2966).
- **Anakin's sensing:** he reads air currents, ozone, and the volley pattern ("Like flying between a storm of steel raindrops"). He tunes "to the rhythm of that rotating launcher, bigger than the entire Jedi Temple" (l.2849–2850, 2958).
- A Blood Carver assassin attacks him with a "twister knife" ("Padawan!"). Anakin is hit on the wrist. He falls less than 100 m before controlling the wings, then dodges a second blow by sensing "the noiseless flight ... almost casually" (l.2899–2917, 2980).
- **Anakin at 12 on levitation:** "he had not yet reached the point in his training of being able to levitate, at least not more than a few centimeters." (l.2977). This is a low-end TK limit statement.
- Anakin's inner life: "Fear, hatred, anger … The old trio Anakin fought every day of his life" (l.2813). "The dark empty boots that trod the worst of his nightmares" (l.2957), a Vader foreshadow. "turn in an instant from the light side of the Force to the dark and hardly know they were different" (l.2961).
- **Obi-Wan:** follows Anakin by sensing his presence ("It was never too difficult to sense Anakin's presence", l.2865). He jumps with quarter-charged, ill-fitting wings, falls "Almost three hundred meters" while learning them, and lands unhurt ("had avoided breaking any bones", l.2914, 2949). He kills the Blood Carver's flight with the lightsaber and a two-foot stomp on the wings (l.2990–2992).
- Obi-Wan is "one of the most capable and steady-tempered Jedi Knights in the Temple". Mace elbows Obi-Wan: "you should have known I was here." Yoda: "The chosen one Qui-Gon gave to us all, not proven, full of fear, and yours to save."
- **Climax feat (l.3004–3018):** they pool fuel. Anakin asks Obi-Wan for "a little boost … up and out?" Obi-Wan "did" (a Force boost), while Anakin snatches a worm scale in flight. Then **Anakin TK-lifts both of them through a shield:** "was it his Padawan's newfound skill at levitation?—lifted them through the next shield as if they lay in the palm of a giant hand." / **"Obi-Wan Kenobi had never felt so close to such a powerful connection with the Force, not in Qui-Gon, nor Mace Windu. Not even in Yoda."** (l.3016–3017)
  - Debate use: at 12, Anakin's raw connection is felt as greater than Yoda's (an Obi-Wan impression, in the moment). It pairs with TPM ch. 24. Contrast this with l.2977, a few lines earlier: he can only levitate "a few centimeters", so this is a surge under duress.
- The worms rise "high and wide as a pedestrian walkway".

## Excerpt: *The Old Republic: Deceived* by Paul S. Kemp (l.3034–3291), about 3,653 BBY (not prequel era)
- Zeerid "Z-man" Korr, an ex-Havoc Squadron marine running guns for the Exchange. He kills two with a knife-hand to the throat and a quick-draw; his armored vest stops a pistol bolt; he flees and detonates a dropped grenade crate with plasma cannons, killing six (l.3127–3161).

Eleena (Twi'lek) nursed him "after the Battle of Alderaan, when you lay near death from that Jedi witch". His first kill was as a boy; he was trained at "the Sith Academy on Dromund Kaas". A Mandalorian drops Coruscant's defense grid. He approaches the Temple: "You have been deceived". He senses guards he cannot see (l.3240).
- Aryn Leneer (Jedi Knight, empath, Master Zallow), begins at l.3247.
- The rest of the excerpt (l.3251–3290): the Alderaan peace talks (Master Dar'nala and Satele Shan negotiating). Aryn's empathy feels Sith derision "akin to a pebble in her boot", and their presence is "submerged deep underwater" pressure. She sees the Force as "a matrix of glowing lines". ## Excerpt: *Shatterpoint* by Matthew Stover (l.3304 onward). Already covered in full in 061-shatterpoint, so only the key lines are noted here.

He saw it at age 6–7, while "full-grown Jedi Knights, even wise Masters, could sense such connections only with difficulty". On Jango: "The Force hinted a shatterpoint, and I left a headless corpse on the sand." (l.3324–3329)

- The Depa Billaba massacre holo; the "dead blade" analysis; "I use the night, and the night uses me." / "I have become the darkness in the jungle." (l.3492–3498). He sees stress lines in the data wafer (l.3451).
- Depa's message: "nothing is more dangerous than a Jedi who's finally sane." Mace: "I have more experience in those jungles than any other living Jedi." **Vaapad lore:** Yoda: "the seventh form ... Deadliest of all … But dangerous it is—to its master, as well as its opponent. ... One student alone to mastery has risen." Mace: "I didn't study Vaapad. ... I created it." ## Excerpt: *Death Star* by Michael Reaves and Steve Perry (l.3572–3663). Rebellion era, tech only.
- On the ISD *Steel Talon* at Despayre, the flight deck is kept "slightly below one g". TIEs have "no defensive shield generators, no hyperdrive capability, and no emergency life-support". Vacuum: "lose consciousness in under ten seconds, and die in under a minute".
- **Lambda-class stats:** "twenty meters long, has a top speed of fourteen hundred g, a Class-One hyperdrive ... twenty troops ... three double-blaster cannons and two double-laser cannons" (l.3612–3613). Hyperspace Minimum Safe Distance: "Jump too close to a planet's gravity well and the shift would tear the vessel apart" (l.3633).
- Vil Dance destroys the escaping Lambda. The Death Star skeleton is seen from 100 km, "the size of a small moon when it was finished" (l.3660).

## Excerpt: *X-Wing: Rogue Squadron* by Michael A. Stackpole (l.3676 onward)
- Corran Horn flies the "Redemption/Requiem" simulator; Whistler is his R2. The X-wing has "Incom 4L4 fusial thrust engines". He redirects power between shields, lasers and engines. Proton torpedo locks take out the TIE bombers at about 2 km. "Corran could feel Jace's excitement" (an early Force-sensitivity hint, l.3750). Commander Antilles "has got two Death Stars painted on the side of his X-wing".
- **X-wing shield tech (l.3752–3754):** the shield is "a demisphere approximately twenty meters behind the X-wing. Designed to dissipate both energy and kinetic weapons". It could tank a full missile load "though that would have been enough to destroy the shields". A glancing collision with the bomber costs "half the power of the aft shield". The bomber's impact is "roughly equivalent to a vehicle hitting a ferrocrete wall at sixty kilometers per hour".
- The mystery TIE pilot (Hoth, Endor with a black dot, Bakura tabs; implied to be Tycho Celchu) kills three trainees and disables Corran. Corran wins the scenario with nine kills.

- Jaina (16) flies the *Jade Sabre* at 95% inertial compensation. **Force-assisted piloting:** "feeling the Force coursing through her, was registering every movement of her enemies and reacting instinctively, playing the game three moves ahead" (l.3899). She bounces the shuttle on repulsorlifts to dodge a concussion missile.

- Mara on Nom Anor: "A blank ... More like he had nothing to do with the Force." (l.4002–4004). **Nom Anor crushes a stone to dust in his fist** (l.4017; there is a later in-series explanation). The droid-stoning pit is 30 m across and 10 m deep.

## The Star Wars Novels Timeline (l.4246–4435)
- **Note:** FULL.txt is actually **4,435 lines** (the `wc -l` and `awk NR` counts agree). The task brief and tracker said 4,387. All lines through l.4435 ("*An eBook novella") were hand-read.
- **Prequel-era order as listed (useful for the reading queue):** Rise of the Empire: *Darth Maul: Saboteur** (eBook novella), *Cloak of Deception*, *Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter*. Then 32 BBY: **Episode I: The Phantom Menace**, *Rogue Planet*, *Outbound Flight*, *The Approaching Storm*. Then 22 BBY: Episode II, followed by the Clone Wars block (*The Clone Wars*, *Wild Space*, *No Prisoners*, *Clone Wars Gambit: Stealth/Siege*, *Republic Commando 1–4*, *Shatterpoint*, *The Cestus Deception*, *The Hive**, *MedStar I–II*, *Jedi Trial*, *Yoda: Dark Rendezvous*, *Labyrinth of Evil*). Then 19 BBY: Episode III, *Dark Lord*, *Imperial Commando 501st*, *Coruscant Nights 1–3*, and so on.
- The Old Republic block runs from *Lost Tribe of the Sith** to the Darth Bane trilogy (1032 BBY). The list continues through the Rebellion, the New Republic, NJO (Vector Prime → The Unifying Force), Dark Nest, Legacy of the Force (9) and *Crosscurrent/Riptide/Millennium Falcon*, and ends with Fate of the Jedi (9, *Outcast* → *Apocalypse*).
- *Maul: Lockdown* and the *Republic* comics are **not** on this list (it is a 2012-era novel list; Lockdown is 2014).
