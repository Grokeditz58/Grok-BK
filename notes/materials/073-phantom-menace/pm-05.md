# The Phantom Menace (Terry Brooks): notes part 5, Chapters 22–24 (climax)

- FULL.txt l.2138–2425, hand-read 2026-11-03 CST. Citation "TPM ch. N".

## Ch. 22: Duel of the Fates, part 1 (THE KEY SCALING CHAPTER)
- **Qui-Gon's pedigree (novel-only):**
  > "Qui-Gon Jinn was one of the most able swordsmen in the Jedi order. The Jedi Master he had trained under had considered him one of the best the Master had taught in his more than four hundred years in the order." (TPM ch. 22)
  - Qui-Gon's own Master was a Jedi of more than 400 years, with Qui-Gon among his best students. (Later Legends gives this Master as Dooku, but the "four hundred years" line conflicts with Dooku. This is a 1999 artifact.)
- **The explicit comparison:**
  > "But on this day, he had met his match. The Sith Lord he battled with Obi-Wan was more than his equal in weapons training, and he had the advantage of being younger and stronger. Qui-Gon was nearing sixty; his youth was behind him and his strength was beginning to diminish. His edge now ... came from his long experience and intuitive grasp of how an adversary might employ a lightsaber against him." (TPM ch. 22)
  > "Obi-Wan brought youth and stamina to the combat, but he had fought in only a few contests and was not battle hardened. Together, they were able to hold their own against the Sith Lord, but their efforts at attack ... were woefully inadequate." (TPM ch. 22)
  > "Darth Maul was a warrior in his prime, never to be any better, his powers at their apex. ... He had worked and trained all his life for this moment ... It was an added bonus that he was able to engage two. He had no fear for himself, no doubt that he would win. He was focused in a way that Qui-Gon recognized at once—a Jedi's focus, mindful of the present" (TPM ch. 22)
  - **Debate value:** this is narrator-confirmed that Maul (a) out-skills Qui-Gon in weapons training, (b) is stronger and younger, and (c) holds off Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan together. Note "never to be any better" (a peak statement in this novel; later works such as *Son of Dathomir* and *The Clone Wars* contradict it).
- **Maul controls the fight 2v1:** "while it might seem as if the Jedi were driving him before them, it was the Sith Lord who was controlling the struggle. Wheeling and spinning, leaping and somersaulting with astonishing ease, their enemy was taking them with him, drawing them on to a place of his own choosing." Qui-Gon believes "one day Obi-Wan would be better than he had ever been."
- In the power station: Maul "leapt from the bridge on which they fought to the one above". **He kicks Obi-Wan "completely over the railing"**. Qui-Gon forces Maul over the railing too; Maul lands "hard on a catwalk several levels below ... visibly stunned" but recovers quickly. The novel gives no Force-push exchange here (the film's mutual pushes are absent). Qui-Gon is "worn and battered by now, close to exhaustion".
- Laser gates: "segmented the corridor at five points". Maul is between walls 4 and 5, Qui-Gon one wall back, Obi-Wan behind the first. "Qui-Gon ... dropped into a guarded crouch to meditate and wait."
- Padmé's route: grapple "ascension guns" up from a ledge "six stories above a thundering waterfall", a further four stories. Destroyer droids box them in, and she surrenders deliberately ("They win this round") after a comlink signal.
- Ground war: destroyer droids push the Gungans back. Jar Jar's spilled energy-ball wagon destroys "droid after droid". The fambaa shields are shot down; Ceel orders the retreat. Jar Jar rides a tank turret, and Tarpals saves him.
- **Anakin's space feats:** R2 gives him control. He discovers "overdrive" by accident, then spins the fighter "like a top". **He uses reverse thrust to make a pursuing fighter crash into the battleship:** "engaged the reverse thrusters and banked right ... The enemy fighter ... rocketed past him into the side of the battleship, exploding". The battleship's shields stop a four-fighter run ("Their shields are too strong!"). Hit, he slides into the main hangar and crash-lands; "Everything's overheated".

## Ch. 23: Qui-Gon falls; the throne room
- **Obi-Wan's view:** "They should have won this battle long ago. Against any other opponent, they would have. But the Sith Lord was battle trained and seasoned well beyond anyone they had ever encountered before. He had matched them blow for blow" (TPM ch. 23)
- Qui-Gon "was the best swordsman Obi-Wan had ever seen, but he was growing old."
- **Maul is wounded (novel-only):** "the Sith Lord worked at binding up his wounds, a series of burns and slashes marked by charred tears in his dark clothing." So the Jedi did land hits during the 2v1.
- **Qui-Gon's final surge after meditating:** "He had found a fresh reserve of strength during his meditation, and now he was attacking with a ferocity that seemed to have the Sith Lord stymied ... deliberately engaging in close-quarters combat, refusing to let the other bring his double-bladed weapon to bear ... Darth Maul's ragged face took on a frenzied look, and the glitter of his strange eyes brightened with uncertainty." (TPM ch. 23)
  - **Debate point:** 1v1 at the melting pit, a meditating Qui-Gon temporarily dominates Maul by fighting inside his reach. He fades through fatigue: "beginning to weary now from carrying the battle alone".
- Maul back-flips across the melting pit. The killing blow: "the Sith Lord parried a downstroke, whirled swiftly to the right, and with his back to the Jedi Master, made a blind, reverse lunge. ... caught him directly in the midsection". (A pommel strike precedes it in the film; the novel has only the reverse lunge.)
- Obi-Wan's hard stop at the last laser gate: "A stream of electrons was all that separated him ... it might as well have been a wall of permacrete three meters thick."
- "Obi-Wan Kenobi, seething with rage, rushed to his rescue."
- Throne room: Padmé's decoy gambit. Sabé appears in full regalia ("I will not be signing any treaty, Viceroy! You've lost!") to draw off six droids. Padmé takes blasters "from a hidden compartment in the arm of the throne", and she and Panaka destroy the remaining four droids. "Before they make it through that door, we will have negotiated a new treaty, Viceroy."
- **Obi-Wan vs Maul, 1v1:**
  - The rage rush: "he barreled into Darth Maul with such fury that he almost knocked both of them off the ledge ... lost in a red haze of rage". Maul is "borne backward ... pressed all the way back to the far wall".
  - "Obi-Wan, while not so experienced as Qui-Gon, was quicker. Anticipating each blow, he was able to elude" Maul's leg cuts. "Twice, Obi-Wan went down." Maul "scorched the young Jedi's tunic, shoulder to waist". Obi-Wan answers with "an upthrust counterstrike to the other's midsection".
  - **Telekinetic barrage (novel-only):** "Darth Maul began to use his command of the Force to fling heavy objects at Obi-Wan ... Obi-Wan responded in kind, and the air was filled with deadly missiles. Lightsabers flicked right and left to ward off the objects" (TPM ch. 23). This is the book's only TK-dueling exchange between them.
  - "for a time it was fought evenly. But Darth Maul was the stronger of the two ... Eventually, the Sith Lord began to wear the young Jedi down."
  - Obi-Wan uses side blows "designed to bring the two-bladed lightsaber horizontal", feints left, and **severs Maul's saber**. His killing blow misses: "Darth Maul, anticipating the maneuver, had stepped smoothly away." Maul knocks Obi-Wan into the pit with the remaining half; he catches "a metal rung just below the lip".
  - **The finishing feat:** "Calming himself ... banishing his anger and fear ... he launched himself away from the side of the pit and catapulted back toward its lip. Imbued with the power of the Force, he cleared the rim easily, somersaulting behind the Sith Lord in a single smooth, powerful motion. Even as he landed, he was drawing Qui-Gon Jinn's fallen lightsaber to his outstretched hand." The strike: "Qui-Gon's lightsaber slashed through his chest". (In the novel it is a chest cut, not the film's waist bisection.) Maul "tumble[s] away into the pit". (He survives in later canon.)
- **Anakin destroys the control ship:** R2 brings the systems up from red to green. With "Shields up!" Anakin deflects the droids' fire, then sweeps the hangar with lasers in a "pinwheel pattern".
  - **Precog torpedo shot:** "his instincts kicked into high gear ... he could see what no one else could ... responding to a voice that spoke to him alone, that whispered always of the future ... Acting of its own accord, faster than thought, his hand ... threw a double-hinged switch". He thinks he "Missed everything!", but the torpedoes go through "a broad vent". The battleship explodes "from the inside". Ric Olié: "Wasn't us ... We never hit it."
- Droid army freezes: "Tanks skidded to a halt and STAPs crashed."
- Qui-Gon's death: "Too late, my young Padawan." / "Now you must be ready, whether the Council thinks you so or not. You must be the teacher." / "He is the chosen one, Obi-Wan. He will bring balance to the Force. Train him well."

## Ch. 24: Aftermath, funeral, parade
- The Neimoidians are shipped to Coruscant for trial. Palpatine is elected supreme chancellor. The novel confirms Padmé's plan: she signaled Sabé "to use the service passages to reach the Queen's chambers"; failing that, she "would have triggered the secret compartment release" anyway.
- **Anakin explicitly Force-guided (narrator via Obi-Wan):** he "penetrated their shields, landed in the belly of the Neimoidian flagship, torpedoed the ship's reactor ... Obi-Wan believed Anakin was guided by something more than the thinking of ordinary men. That extraordinarily high midi-chlorian count gave the boy a connection to the Force that even Jedi Masters on the order of Yoda might never achieve." (TPM ch. 24) **This is a strong Anakin potential > Yoda quote.**
- Yoda: "Confer on you the level of Jedi Knight, the Council does." / "A great warrior, was Qui-Gon Jinn ... But so much more he could have been, if not so fast he had run." / "Years, it takes, to become a Jedi Knight. Years more, to become one with the Force." / "Clouded, this boy's future remains ... A mistake to train him, it is." / "Disagree with that decision, I must." (In the novel, the Council overrules Yoda.)
- Funeral attendance includes the Council, "an honor guard of one hundred Naboo soldiers". Obi-Wan: "I am your new Master, Anakin."
- **Council evaluation of Obi-Wan's win:** "Only a Jedi Knight fully in tune with the Force could have saved himself against such an adversary. Obi-Wan Kenobi had proved himself beyond everyone's expectations that day." Mace: "Defeating a Sith Lord in combat is a strong test of his readiness for anything." / "There is no doubt. The one who tested him was a Sith." Yoda: "Always two there are. No more, no less. A master and an apprentice." Mace: "Then which one was destroyed ... the master or the apprentice?"
- **Sidious's assessment of Maul (novel-only):** "Years of training had gone into the preparation of Darth Maul as a Sith Lord. He had been more than the equal of the Jedi Knights he had faced and should have been able to defeat them easily. It was bad luck and chance that had led to his death, a combination that even the power of the dark side could not always overcome." / "he had gotten what he wanted most from this business. Even the loss of Darth Maul was worth that." (TPM ch. 24)
- Parade: the Globe of Peace. Anakin feels "sick in spirit and lost in his heart". He foresees "One day, each of them would play a part in his life that would change him forever. He could sense that." (This is precognitive foreshadowing.) Obi-Wan: "One day you will see her again. But when you do, you will be a Jedi Knight." (This is dramatic irony.)
