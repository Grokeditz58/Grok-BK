# The Phantom Menace (Terry Brooks): notes part 3, Chapters 12–15

- FULL.txt l.1217–1590, hand-read 2026-11-03 CST. Citation "TPM ch. N".

## Ch. 12: The dream, the chance cube, the starting grid
- **Prophetic dream (novel-only):** Anakin dreams he is "of a different, but indeterminate age ... He was cut from stone, and his thoughts were emblazoned with a vision so frightening he could not bring himself to consider it fully". Padmé is "at the head of the dark wave of his dream, and the wave was an army, marching toward him". She is "older, sadder … and something more." / "You were leading a huge army into battle." / "I hope not. I hate fighting."
- **Force probability manipulation:** "As he did, Qui-Gon made a small, surreptitious gesture with one hand, calling on his Jedi power to produce a small inflection in the Force. / The cube bounced, rolled, settled, blue side facing up." (TPM ch. 12). Qui-Gon wagers the Pod against "the boy and his mother", and Watto will put up only one. Blue means Anakin.
- Watto also suspects fixing: "You knew the boy was going to win! Somehow you knew it!" (ch. 14).
- A Sith probe droid is already searching Mos Espa.
- Boonta Eve crowd: "Over a hundred thousand beings". Jabba is with "Gardulla, his female friend". The racer list adds Boles Roor, Ben Quadinaros, Aldar Beedo, Ody Mandrell, Xelbree, Mars Guo, Clegg Holdfast, Bozzie Baranta, Wan Sandage, Mawhonic and Teemto Pagalies. Sebulba bangs on Anakin's left stabilizer.
- Qui-Gon: "Remember, concentrate on the moment. Feel, don't think. Trust your instincts. ... May the Force be with you, Annie." Padmé: "You Jedi are far too reckless". Qui-Gon: "The Queen trusts my judgment, young handmaiden".
- Pod tech: "Energy binders were engaged, powerful electromagnetic currents arcing between coaxial plates, locking the twin engines of each Podracer together as a single unit."

## Ch. 13: The Boonta Eve race
- Anakin stalls at the start ("He'd fed in too much fuel from a dead start") and restarts after clearing the feeder lines.
- **Novel race feats beyond the film:**
  - He passes Gasgano by faking a drop shift, then "rocketed right over the top of Gasgano".
  - "envisioning the course in his mind—each twist, each deviation, each rise and drop. Everything was clear and certain to him. Everything was revealed."
  - Sebulba kills or wrecks Mawhonic (rams him into rock), Xelbree (side-vent flame), Mars Guo (metal in the intake) and Obitoki (flame). Tuskens hit Teemto Pagalies at "Tusken Turn". Ody Mandrell's engine sucks in the pit droid DUM-4. Habba Kee crashes into Obitoki's wreck.
  - **He flies blind through smoke:** "the boy was seeing with more than his eyes, sensing with his mind, calm and steady within himself. He could feel the danger waiting" (TPM ch. 13)
  - **Remote viewing on the final lap:** "In his mind, Anakin could see Qui-Gon and Jar Jar watching him; Kitster ... Padmé ... and his mother, her eyes filled with terror. He could see them all, as if he were standing among them, standing outside himself" (TPM ch. 13)
  - The symbiosis with the Pod gives "insights and understandings that transcended his senses and knowledge, projecting him past the present and into a place others could not reach."
  - He re-hooks the loose cable with the magnetic retriever "After a dozen tries". He jettisons the sabotaged stabilizer and switches to an auxiliary mount, then fakes a triple shift to get alongside. Sebulba's Pod self-destructs when the locked steering rods break.
- **Qui-Gon searches for Anakin through the Force:** "Qui-Gon closed his eyes and blocked everything away ... becoming one with the Force, disappearing into its flow, searching for Anakin." He opens them and says "Here he comes!" just as R2 beeps.
- Result: "at nine years of age, the youngest winner ever of the Boonta Eve race."

## Ch. 15: Maul vs. Qui-Gon, Tatooine duel (KEY FEAT CHAPTER)
- **Qui-Gon's danger sense, before the attack:** "his Jedi training in the ways of the Force warned him now of something far more dangerous. He could feel a shifting in the balance of things ... a dark weight descending like a massive stone."
- Maul's speeder: "a saddle-shaped vehicle with no weapons in evidence ... like nothing the Jedi had ever seen, but vaguely reminiscent of something dead and gone."
- **Maul's entry:** "wheeling the speeder aside deftly at the last moment, closing off its thruster, and leaping from the seat, all in one swift movement. He carried a lightsaber of another make, and the weapon was cutting at the Jedi Master even before the attacker's feet had touched the ground. Qui-Gon, surprised by the other's quickness and ferocity, barely blocked the blow" (TPM ch. 15)
- **Scaling statement, an explicit narrator comparison:**
  > "Even without knowing anything else, Qui-Gon knew this man was trained in the fighting arts of a Jedi, a skilled and dangerous adversary. Worse, he was younger, quicker, and stronger than Qui-Gon, and he was gaining ground rapidly. The Jedi Master blocked him again and again, but could not find an opening that would provide any chance of escape." (TPM ch. 15)
- The ship is "not three hundred meters away". Ric flies low ("barely higher than a speeder bike") with the ramp down.
- Qui-Gon leaps onto the ramp. Maul follows "leaping onto the ramp as the ship began to rise". They fight on the ramp "twenty meters into the air".
- **Qui-Gon's finishing blow:** "swing a powerful, two-handed blow at his attacker. The horned man blocked it, but only barely, and in the process lost his balance completely. The blow's force swept him away, clear of the ramp". **Maul falls about 20 m, "landed in a crouch, and rose instantly to his feet."** (TPM ch. 15)
- **Qui-Gon's own assessment:** "He had barely escaped with his life ... His opponent was strong and had tested him severely. He was getting old, he decided" / "That was a surprise I won't soon forget." / "he was trained in the Jedi arts. My guess is he was after the Queen."
- Debate use: first-round Maul is shown as faster and stronger than Qui-Gon one-on-one. Qui-Gon survives by disengaging and lands one strong blow, and he still knocks Maul off the ramp.
- Coruscant aside: Sio Bibble is taken away ("We are a democracy!"). OOM-9 begins the swamp search. Gunray rides a "mechno-chair".
- The japor snippet pendant: "How could I forget my future husband?" / "Many things will change when we reach Coruscant, Annie. My caring for you will not be one of them." "Space is cold."
