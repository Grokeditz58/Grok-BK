## Ch. 1: Anakin's crash at Metta Drop (novel-only)
- Opens mid-Podrace on Tatooine, about a month before the film. Anakin is nine ("Maybe even next year, when he reached the age of ten"). Rivals are Sebulba, Mawhonic, Gasgano and Rimkar (in a "strange bubble pod").
- **Precognition and slowed perception in the Pod:**
  > "Sometimes, he thought dreamily, he could sense what it would say before it even spoke." (TPM ch. 1)
  > "When he raced, everything around him slowed down rather than sped up. ... He could almost close his eyes and drive" (TPM ch. 1)
- Racing record: "He had crashed twice and failed to finish even once, but after more than half a dozen races he was unharmed." He is "the only human ever to drive in the Podraces".
- The month before, Sebulba tried to cut his control cable with "an illegal razor saw"; Anakin "sensed him coming up from behind and underneath" and pulled away.
- At Metta Drop, Sebulba uses his exhaust to slam Anakin and Rimkar into the cliff. Rimkar dies ("a long black scar"). Anakin reacts "Almost before he knew what he was doing, at the same instant he felt the backwash", lifts clear, and crashes on the flats unhurt.

## Ch. 2: Watto, Shmi, Kitster and Wald, the old spacer (novel-only)
- Watto scolds him in Huttese ("Oba chee ka!", "Rassa dwee cuppa, peedunkel!"). Shmi faces Watto down ("her calm, steady gaze brought him up short").
- **Anakin's gifts, stated outright:**
  > "what no one knew about him except his mother was the way he sensed things. Frequently he sensed them before anyone even knew they would happen. ... He was only nine years old and he could already see the world in ways most adults never would." (TPM ch. 2)
- Physical: "a small boy, even at nine years of age ... sandy hair, blue eyes, a pug nose ... quick and strong for his age". Friends: Kitster, and Wald (a young Rodian, "several years younger"). Their drink is the ruby bliel; the currency is druggats.
- **The old spacer** is a retired Republic fighter-corps pilot with gray eyes. He "left the corps six years back". He flew troops into "Makem Te during its rebellion", flew "four [Jedi] to a place I'm not supposed to talk about even now", and flew the Kessel Run. He prefers "a Z-95 Headhunter every time".
  > "You fly like your name, Anakin. You walk the sky like you own it." / "You're better than I was at twice your age." / "in this life you're often born one thing and die another." (TPM ch. 2)
- Anakin "didn't lie to his mother. Not about anything, not ever." Shmi makes him promise not to ask Watto to race again. His vow: "I'll even fly with Jedi Knights."

## Ch. 3: The Federation flagship: gas, the blast doors, the hangar
- Novel ship detail: Trade Federation battleships are "tubular in shape, split at one end and encircling an orb that sheltered the bridge, communications center, and hyperdrive". The Republic cruiser's red is "the symbol of ambassadorial neutrality". The ship's captain is a woman.
- Political backdrop: the blockade has run "almost a month". Valorum "secretly notified the Jedi Council" after the fact, and "any interference ... required Senate approval", so the mission is covert ("skirting the edges of his authority").
- **Jedi order lore (novel):** the order was "Founded as a theological and philosophical study group so far back that its origins were the stuff of myth" and "only gradually became aware of the presence of the Force". It moved from isolated meditation to social responsibility.
  > "In the time of Qui-Gon Jinn, ten thousand Jedi Knights in service to the Republic carried on the struggle each day of their lives in a hundred thousand different worlds" (TPM ch. 3)
- Ages: Obi-Wan is "in his mid-twenties"; Qui-Gon is "more than thirty years" older. Obi-Wan is "not yet a full Jedi, but ... close to being ready".
- Obi-Wan senses: "It's not about here, Master. ... It's something … elsewhere." Qui-Gon: "Be mindful of the living Force, my young Padawan."
- Sidious on Dofine: "I don't want this stunted piece of slime to pass within my sight again." / "I will make it legal, Viceroy." / "Kill them now. Immediately."
- **Feats:**
  - Qui-Gon "closed his eyes, and retreated deep within himself. ... 'They've destroyed our ship.'" (sensing at a distance)
  - Obi-Wan uses a Force push on a battle droid: "He raised his hand, palm outward, and another of the droids went crashing into the wall."
  - "An unstoppable force, the two men worked in unison ... seemingly able to anticipate every form of attack." (TPM ch. 3)
  - **Blast door:** "lightsabers cutting at the massive doors, melting away the steelcrete like soft butter ... a red spot appeared at its center where the larger man plunged his lightsaber into the metal almost up to its hilt." (Note: the novel calls the blast door material **steelcrete**.)
  - Ten destroyer droids arrive and the Jedi withdraw. Haako: "You've never encountered Jedi Knights before, have you?" / "That won't be enough."
- Amidala's call to Gunray matches the film. The invasion force is six landing ships.

## Ch. 4: Naboo swamp, Jar Jar, Otoh Gunga
- Naboo fauna: "Ikopi, fulumpasets, motts, peko pekos". Qui-Gon flattens himself and Jar Jar under a passing MTT.
- **Deflection:** Qui-Gon "blocked the bolts with his lightsaber and deflected them back into the attack craft. The STAPs exploded".
- **Novel-only detail:** Obi-Wan's saber is shorted out by the swamp. "Sorry, Master. The swamp fried my lightsaber." / "You forgot to turn off your power again, didn't you, Obi-Wan?" / "It won't take long to recharge".
- Jedi breathing devices are "the size of the palms of their hands". Otoh Gunga's bubbles admit bodies without rupturing.
- **Mind tricks on Boss Nass (two):** "passing it smoothly before the Gungan chief's eyes in a quick invocation of Jedi mind power" gets them a bongo, and the second gets Jar Jar released under the life debt.
- Qui-Gon: "Your sensitivity to the living Force is not your strength." Obi-Wan: "One day it would be his undoing." Jar Jar's sentence: "Pounded unto death, dis one."

## Ch. 5: Through the core
- Obi-Wan's view of Qui-Gon: "perhaps the greatest Jedi alive, a commanding presence at Council ... He repeatedly defied the Council". Qui-Gon has been his mentor "for more than a dozen years"; "Obi-Wan was the son he would never have."
  > "There are secrets hidden in the Force that are not easily discovered. ... Sometimes that purpose must be sensed first in order that it may be revealed later." / "Secrets must be exposed when found. Detours must be taken when encountered." (Qui-Gon, TPM ch. 5)
- Opee sea killer, sando aqua monster and colo claw fish, as in the film. Obi-Wan pilots them out between the aqua monster's "teeth the size of buildings".
- **Qui-Gon's Force sleep:** "'Relax,' Qui-Gon Jinn said softly, placing his hand on Jar Jar's twitching shoulder. The Gungan jerked and promptly fainted." / "You overdid it." (TPM ch. 5)
- Sidious: "If they are alive, they will show themselves. ... I will deal with them myself."

## Ch. 6: Anakin and the Tusken (novel-only)
- A little over a week after the race, Watto sends Anakin to trade with Jawas at Mochot Steep in the Dune Sea.
- **Empathic sensing:** "He could tune in to other creatures, bond with them so closely he could sense what they were thinking and what they would do almost before they did."
- C-3PO is skinless and one-eyed. Anakin names him "three because the droid made the third member of his little family". He knows "fifty-one hundred different varieties of droids". The secret Podracer has been in progress "almost two years" under a tarp at the dump, disguised as junk.
- Haul: two mechanic droids, three multipurpose droids and "a damaged hyperdrive converter".
- In Xelric Draw, Anakin frees a Tusken pinned by a rockslide, using droids and a lever off the speeder, and splints his leg. He stays the night by a glow unit. The Tusken tells 3PO to "shut up". At dawn a Tusken band surrounds Anakin; the rescued Raider speaks to them and they leave, taking the rifle.
- Foreshadowing: Anakin's hidden fear for his mother ("What if he were to lose her?"). He dreams of being a Jedi fighting "things so dark and insubstantial", and of commanding an army that frees Tatooine's slaves while his mother "vanished".
- "Anakin Skywalker wasn't afraid of anything. / Was he?"

## Ch. 7: Theed falls; escape from the hangar
- Theed fell "shortly after sunrise"; the Naboo are sent to detention camps. Panaka: "a big, powerfully built man with a dark, smooth face". Battle droid commander OOM-9 orders them to "Camp Four".
- The Naboo choose their ruler "by popular acclaim"; there are no dynasties.
- **Force hold:** "The sergeant turned to flee, but Qui-Gon brought up his hand, holding the droid fast with the power of the Force." (TPM ch. 7)
- The Queen consults her handmaidens; Padmé answers "We are brave, Your Highness". The Queen's transport is "A J-type 327 Nubian" with Headon-5 engines.
- Obi-Wan frees the captive pilots "with ferocious determination"; Qui-Gon holds the ramp alone.
- Jar Jar is locked in the astromech bay and breaks the red R2 unit's head by accident, which is why it fails to deploy (novel-only cause).
