# The Phantom Menace (Terry Brooks): notes part 2, Chapters 8–11

- FULL.txt l.718–1216, hand-read 2026-11-03 CST. Citation "TPM ch. N".

## Ch. 8: Blockade run, Maul revealed, landing on Tatooine
- **Ship feats:** Ric Olié flies the Nubian through the blockade with shields failing. Qui-Gon: "The Trade Federation uses pulser tracking for its weapons. Spin the ship. It will make it difficult for them to get a reading on us." The ship hugs the battleship "so close that the larger guns were rendered useless". Droid starfighters ("twin compartments attached to a rounded, swept-back head") destroy three of the four R2s. R2-D2 restores the shields under fire.
- Panaka: "This is not a warship! ... We have no weapons, Ambassador! We're a nonviolent people".
- The hyperdrive is leaking. Obi-Wan picks Tatooine ("It's controlled by the Hutts").
- **Maul's first appearance (Sidious hologram):** "Now it's out of our range—" / "Not for a Sith, it isn't."
  > "His face was a mask of jagged red and black patterns, the design etched into his skin, and his skull was hairless and studded with a crown of short, hooked horns. Gleaming yellow eyes fixed on the Neimoidians, breaking past their defenses, stripping them bare" (TPM ch. 8)
  > "this is my apprentice, Lord Maul. He will find your lost ship." (Sidious, TPM ch. 8)
- Gunray's motive: "their plans for sabotaging the trade-routes tax did not contemplate risking their lives". Haako: "What will happen when the Jedi become aware that we are doing business with these Sith Lords?" (Both Neimoidians know the word "Sith".)
- R2-D2 is named and thanked; the Queen sends Padmé to clean him. Qui-Gon notices something off about who made the escape decision: "Except, it hadn't been exactly like that …" (He half-perceives the decoy.)
- On Tatooine, the T-14 generator is "shot". Qui-Gon: "Be wary, Obi-Wan. I sense a disturbance in the Force." / "I feel it also, Master."
- Padmé: "I've been trained in self-defense. I speak a number of languages."

## Ch. 9: Mos Espa, Watto's shop, Sebulba, Jira
- Qui-Gon has been to Tatooine "some years ago". He explains Jabba's control of spaceports and the Jawa and Tusken desert.
- Anakin and Padmé: "Are you an angel?" ("They live on the moons of Iego"). "My mom and I were sold to Gardulla the Hutt, but she lost us to Watto, betting on the Podraces." He has been there "Since I was very little—three, I think." "I am a person!" She gives her name as "Padmé Naberrie". "I'm going to marry you." / "I won't always be".
- **A mind trick fails, even at full strength:** Qui-Gon offers "twenty thousand Republic dataries"; then "passed his hand in front of the pudgy blue alien again, bringing the full force of his Jedi suggestive power to bear." Watto: "I'm a Toydarian! Mind tricks don't work on me—only money!" (TPM ch. 9)
- Sebulba seizes Jar Jar over a frog. Anakin bluffs ("This one's very well connected ... as in Hutt") and sees "a hint of fear" in the Dug.
  > "Fear attracts the fearful. Sebulba was trying to overcome his fear by squashing you. ... You can help yourself by being less afraid." (Anakin, TPM ch. 9)
- Jira, a fruit seller (novel-only character): Anakin promises her a cooling unit and buys pallies. He spots Qui-Gon's lightsaber when the poncho opens.
- Sio Bibble's holo plea reaches the ship ("death toll rising, catastrophic"). Obi-Wan: "It is a trick." Handmaidens on board: Eirtaé and Rabé.

## Ch. 10: The Skywalker home; the Sith history; Qui-Gon's night thoughts
- Shmi is "a woman of forty". Qui-Gon gives her "five small capsules" of food. On Anakin: "He's a very special boy." / "Yes ... I know."
- C-3PO is named in his memory banks. Slaves carry transmitters ("and they blow you up! ... Poof!"). Anakin is "working on a scanner to try to locate them". Shmi: "The Republic doesn't exist out here".
- **Qui-Gon's reflexes:** "His hand moved swiftly, and in a heartbeat he had secured the Gungan's tongue between his thumb and forefinger."
- Qui-Gon: "You must have Jedi reflexes if you race Pods." / Anakin: "No one can kill a Jedi." / "I wish that were so …"
- Anakin's dream "I came back here and freed all the slaves". Shmi: "the biggest problem in the universe is that no one helps anyone" / "Maybe he was meant to help you." Qui-Gon: "Greed can be a powerful ally". The race is the next day but one, on Boonta Eve.
- **Sith history, novel version (Sidious's thoughts), a major lore point:**
  > "The Sith had come into being almost two thousand years ago. ... A rogue Jedi Knight had founded the Sith ... soon the ranks of the Sith swelled to more than fifty in number." (TPM ch. 10)
  > "In the end, the Sith destroyed themselves. ... In a matter of only weeks, all of them died. / All but one." (TPM ch. 10)
  > "there would only be two at any one time. ... The Sith who reinvented the order called himself Darth Bane. / A thousand years had passed since the Sith were believed destroyed" (TPM ch. 10)
  - This is the 1999 version of the Sith origin, a rogue Jedi with about 50 followers about 2,000 years ago. Later Legends retconned it (ancient Sith species, Exar Kun, Ruusan). It is useful for "when did the Sith start" arguments, but note the retcon.
- Maul: "Tatooine is sparsely populated. ... If the trace was correct, Master, I will find them quickly". Sidious: "Move against the Jedi first" / "At last we will reveal ourselves to the Jedi. At last we will have our revenge." / "The Jedi will be no match for you."
- **Qui-Gon's Force theory (novel-only):**
  > "the Force existed on more than one plane ... He was too close to the life Force when he should have been more attentive to the unifying Force." / "Obi-Wan was grounded in the need to focus on the larger picture, on the unifying Force. He lacked Qui-Gon's intuitive nature." (TPM ch. 10)
  > "Rules were created to provide a road map to understanding the Force." (TPM ch. 10)

## Ch. 11: The deal, the Pod, Maul's probes, the midichlorian count
- Qui-Gon puts up the ship as the entry fee ("I have acquired a Pod in a game of chance ... The fastest ever built"). Deal: if he wins, Watto keeps the winnings minus the parts.
- Padmé: "Well, I don't approve." Qui-Gon: "The Queen does not need to know."
- Anakin's Pod: "a narrow half cylinder ... Sleek Radon-Ulzer fighter engines with scoop-air stabilizers ... Steelton cables ... like seeing a doop bug attached to a pair of banthas."
- Friends: Kitster, Seek, Amee (a blond girl) and Wald. Jar Jar numbs his tongue on the energy binder. Qui-Gon supplies a power pack lifted from Watto ("Watto has less need for it than you").
- **Qui-Gon on Anakin's gift:**
  > "He can see things before they happen. That's why he appears to have such quick reflexes. It is a Jedi trait." / "The Force is unusually strong with him" (TPM ch. 11)
  > "There is no father ... I carried him, I gave birth to him. I raised him." (Shmi, TPM ch. 11)
  - Novel lore: "all Jedi were identified within the first six months of birth and given over to their training. ... There were no exceptions."
- **Maul's hunt:** his ship (unnamed in the text: "Shovel-nosed and knife-edged, its wings swept back") lands on a mesa. He wears desert garb. **He uses a forearm control panel to set the probes to sense the Force:** "punched in the calculations required to identify the enemy he was looking for. Jedi Knights would manifest a particularly strong presence in the Force." The probe droids fly to three cities.
- **Midichlorians:**
  > "They say the midi-chlorian count is twenty thousand. ... No one has a count that high. Not even Master Yoda." (Obi-Wan, TPM ch. 11)
  - Note the novel's spelling, "midi-chlorian". The blood sample goes through a comlink chip.
- Anakin's precognition: "That strange ability to see what others did not ... told him so. His future was coming up on him in a rush". He is carving the japor snippet for Padmé.
