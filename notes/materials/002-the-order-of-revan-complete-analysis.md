## Thesis

The Order of Revan (the Revanites of SWTOR's *Shadow of Revan*) was a large, disciplined, elite military faction. Its champions and fleets were treated in-game as one of the most dangerous threats the Republic and Empire ever faced. Gameplay mechanics, which the author argues are S-canon, show SoR Revan and his top lieutenants to be extremely powerful.

## Arguments (numbered)

1. **Gameplay is S-canon, not N-canon.** In the Lucasfilm Licensing EU canon system, game mechanics count as S-canon unless contradicted. The author cites Sue Rostoni ("defies Star Wars sensibilities") and Leland Chee: game stats exist "to provide a scale from which to compare how one character stacks up against another" and show "what type of Force powers a character may have". Because the Revanites appear only in SWTOR, the author says no higher canon contradicts the analysis.
2. **Origin and philosophy.** Tari Darkspanner founded the Order after finding Revan's secrets on Korriban. Revanites study Revan's artifacts and holocrons and reject a pure Jedi or Sith identity in favour of using both sides of the Force. The rituals include a "trial of death". The author ties this to love- and positive-emotion-based power (a George Lucas "super energies" remark is paraphrased, not sourced) and to Luke's love amp against Abeloth (author's note).
3. **Revan's real goal was destroying Vitiate.** The Forged Alliances raids and the SoR war only make sense as an anti-Vitiate plan. Revan sacrificed senior officers and fleets for it. The author also theorises that Revan knew about the Eternal Empire (FORUM OPINION / speculation).
4. **Elite performance on Yavin 4.** The Revanites broke through Massassi and then the Emperor's Imperial Guard Training Grounds. Satele, Quinn, Aric Jorgan, Ashara Zavros and Darth Marr all express shock. Marr: "All told, the Revanites may be the fiercest adversaries we've ever faced. We must gather our forces. We must endure."
5. **Cohesion as a power source.** No infighting was reported. The author compares the Revanites to the Knights of Zakuul and Imperial Knights, who draw strength from loyalty. Satele on the Knights of Zakuul: "Devotion makes them strong".
6. **SoR Revan boss fight, read as S-canon feats.** The author breaks down the mechanics: six lightsabers, Force bonds, corruption of companions, the Machine core, HK-47, teleportation, and pinning the whole final strike team, which only the Hero resists. Battle lines from both SoR Revan fights are transcribed (Appendix A).
7. **The Spirdrall prophecy:** "You stand on a moon, defiantly, before one who is not one. You do not stand alone, but you fail all the same." The author reads it as foreshadowing Revan on Yavin.
8. **Hierarchy of champions.** This section profiles each champion:
   - Revanite troops: standard and strong classes, Avengers and Vindicators, walker squadrons.
   - The Star Forge-grafted "virtual immortality" army.
   - Master Obai's "paralyzing Force wave".
   - Commanders Xarovit Tovar and Djeon Thilloch.
   - Deron Cadoruso, Lord Kurse and Sano Thrica (Revan's "most powerful allies").
   - Darth Arkous and Colonel Darok, whose "plan" was "formed over countless decades".
   - Lord Goh (Lana: "flawless").
   - Jensyn, whose "the Force Unleashed" ability lifts four Imperial champions including Nox and the Wrath.
   - Overlord Jenarian, who probably led the assault on the Imperial Guard training grounds.
   - Darth Soverus, whom the author suggests "may also be a Revanite" because Jensyn executes him to stop him talking.
   - Malaphar the Savage ("the mother of all Massassi").
   - The Underlurker. Revan's spirit: "I have faced such beasts before, but never one this strong with the dark side."
9. **Faction size.** Companion lines are used to size the fleet:
   - Mako: "we're fighting an armada"
   - Ashara: "Scores of ships and ten times as many cultists"
   - Quinn: "enough firepower to breach Rishi's crust"
   - Aric: "That many ships could put up a fight against any planet's defenses"
   
   The author compares the Revanite fleet with the Battle of Corellia and with Malgus's expeditionary fleet. He then applies "a greatly conservative five times multiplier" to the Imperial and Republic **command fleets** that the Revanites aimed to destroy. That gives about 200,000 Imperial and Republic troopers, rising to about 2,000,000 under the "ten times multiplier" for ships as shown in-game. His estimate for the **Revanite** fleet itself is 5 Harrower, 5 Gage, 10–30 Thranta, 10 Ajuur and 10 Theoretika-class ships, with an army "from the hundreds of thousands to millions of soldiers depending on the reference". He calls it "a galaxy-class fleet". His estimated demographic split is Empire 40%, Republic 30%, Outlaw 25%, Mandalorian 5%. **All numbers are the author's own estimates (FORUM OPINION), not stated figures.**
10. **Ending (post 9).** The author says this "might be the last Star Wars blog I ever make" and reflects on running Suspect Insight. It contains no argument.

## Common rebuttals / weak points (ANALYSIS — not from the thread)

- **Gameplay as S-canon is contested.** Debate regulars routinely reject boss-fight mechanics (e.g. pinning a strike team, multi-lightsaber phases) as gameplay abstraction. The Chee and Rostoni quotes concern stats and Force-power types, not scripted encounter outcomes.
- **Same mechanics, everyone.** Revan's "pin" is resisted by the player character, so it scales the Hero as much as Revan. Every operation boss also beats eight-to-sixteen-player teams, which dilutes the significance.
- **Fleet numbers are extrapolations.** These include the "ten times multiplier" and the per-class ship counts. No source gives a Revanite headcount.
- **Shock dialogue is relative.** "Fiercest adversaries" (Marr) is context-bound hyperbole from a faction at war.
- **Speculative lore links** (Revan knew of the Eternal Empire; Soverus as a Revanite) are labelled by the author as theories.

## Characters touched and implied scaling

- **SoR Revan:** an extremely powerful boss who pins a strike team; implied ≥ Hero of Tython's allies, < the Hero (who resists). FORUM OPINION.
- **Revanite champions** (Jensyn, Arkous, Darok, Goh, Kurse, Cadoruso, Thrica, Obai) are shown as elite and comparable to Imperial and Republic champions such as Nox and the Wrath. FORUM OPINION.
- **Satele, Marr, Theron, Lana, Shae Vizla, Jakarro:** strike-team members defeated or pinned in the fight mechanics.
- **Vitiate:** Revan's ultimate target. No direct scaling is offered.
