# The Approaching Storm (Alan Dean Foster, Del Rey 2002): notes part 1, front matter and Ch. 1–5

- Read 2026-11-03 CST from the owner's epub (Del Rey ebook v3.1_r1, eISBN 978-0-307-79571-7; © 2002 Lucasfilm). This part covers FULL.txt l.1–590.
- Citation: "AS ch. N (l.x)". The edition has 18 numbered chapters. The chapter ranges are line ranges in FULL.txt.
- Big picture: set shortly before *Attack of the Clones*. Anakin is about Barriss's age, roughly 19–20, and the bundled timeline files the book just before Episode II. Obi-Wan & Anakin and Luminara Unduli & Barriss Offee are sent to Ansion. Ansion's secession would trigger mutual-defense treaties and could pull 40+ systems out of the Republic. To keep it in, they must win the nomadic Alwari overclans' agreement to the city Unity's terms. Behind the plot are the Commerce Guild's Shu Mai and, finally revealed, Count Dooku.

## Front matter (l.1–60)
- Blurb, copyright (Del Rey, © 2002 Lucasfilm), dedication to Shelby Hettinger ("Uncle Alan") (l.19–21), and a contents list of 18 chapters plus back matter.

## Ch. 1: the holomap plot (l.61–106)
- Shu Mai (Gossam, President of the Commerce Guild) meets Senator Mousul of Ansion, a blue Twi'lek female and a Corellian industrialist in front of a galactic holomap.
- **Lore:** Ansion sits at the node of the Malarian Alliance (3 systems) and the Keitumite Mutual Military Treaty (6 systems), among other treaties. If Ansion secedes, the treaty chain could carry "forty systems or more" with it (l.79). The vote is expected within half a standard year (l.89).
- "Small keys can unlock very big doors" (l.62). The keystone-block analogy is at l.90–91. The Trade Federation's failed Naboo move is referenced (l.82).

## Ch. 2: Cuipernam ambush (l.108–199)
- **Luminara's look:** lower lip stained purplish black, a black diamond tattoo on her chin, finger markings, olive skin, blue eyes, and copper/bronze forearm bands (l.109, 113).
- Background: the city Unity of Community vs. the Alwari nomads (l.127–129). Count Dooku is mentioned as a rumor (l.143).
- **Force/combat feats:**
  - Luminara senses a Hoguss dropping from above. She raises her saber and it impales itself (l.145–146).
  - Dozens of hired attackers follow. Luminara and Barriss deflect bolts until bodies pile up (l.150–153). A second wave of about two dozen better professionals comes in, meant to wear them down (l.154).
  - Luminara blocks two shots, then Force-pulls a blaster from a man's hands one-handed (l.157).
  - Barriss disarms a six-handed Wetakk (l.159). She jumps and climbs an "unscalable" wall like a spider (l.156, 161).
  - Luminara alone holds the whole throng back (l.161–162). A Throbe's blaster grazes her; she did not sense that shot (l.164).
  - Barriss senses Obi-Wan and Anakin coming as a strong Force disturbance (l.165). They rout the survivors in under a minute (l.167). Obi-Wan sensed the trouble from across the city (l.172).
- **Doctrine:** non-Jedi beat Jedi only by numbers or by catching them in a lull (l.150). Jedi may not wear personal ornament: "A Jedi's glow comes from within" (l.132).
- **Quotes:**
  - "Anyone can handle a weapon. Reason is much more difficult to wield." (Luminara, l.131)
  - "I prefer settling differences with a lightsaber" (Barriss, l.130)
  - "those who have their mouths open all the time generally have their ears shut" (Barriss, l.189)
- Barriss and Anakin are "more or less the same age" (l.199).

## Ch. 3: Soergg, the Unity meeting, the carafes (l.201–356)
- **Villains:** Bossban Soergg the Hutt; his majordomo Ogomoor; Smotl Ib-Dunn; Yuzzem guards.
  - Soergg on Jedi: "Fighting one is like fighting two. Dealing with two more akin to trying to handle eight." (l.211). Jedi are hard to ambush because they sense disturbances (l.218).
  - There are no proper bounty hunters on Ansion (l.225). Shu Mai is under orders not to draw the Jedi Council's attention, so no offworld professionals are used (l.246). She answers to "another" (l.238, 248). The Commerce Guild covered up niobarium pollution on Vorian Four (l.243).
- **Ansionian physiology (l.258):**
  - Slightly shorter than humans, slim and wiry, pale yellow/golden skin.
  - A hairless body except for a mane strip about 15 cm wide that runs from the forehead down the back to a roughly 15 cm tail. The mane can be any color.
  - Large eyes, usually red (sometimes yellow, rarely mauve); sharp teeth; omnivores leaning to meat.
  - One nostril (l.391, 438). An opaque lid that blinks side to side (l.399). "her hearts" (plural, l.434). Night-sighted (l.331). Most go by one name (l.264).
- **Politics:** nomads and cities have clashed for millennia under a tenuous peace of about 200 local years (l.260). The nomads want to stay in the Republic; the cities lean toward secession (l.261).
- **Delegates:** Ranjiyn; Kandah (female, secretly Ogomoor's informant, bought with the promise of family estates in Korumdah, l.342); Induran; Tolut (an Armalat, green, three-fingered, several hundred kilos).
- **Force feat: the carafes.** Tolut, a non-Jedi, lifts a carafe 0.5 m by telekinesis with visible strain (l.287). Luminara takes it from him with no hand gesture, floats it to the ceiling and dumps it on him, then does the same with all the carafes (l.289–293).
  - Tolut: "I defer to greater power" (l.296). Obi-Wan: "dynamic diplomacy" (l.300). "For those who can make it rain indoors…" (l.318).
- Luminara offers that the Alwari will share half or more of the prairie for Unity development (l.308).
- Soergg: "Jedi cannot be bribed, connived, broken, or swayed" (l.353).

## Ch. 4: Coruscant conspirators; Barriss kidnapped (l.357–486)
- In a Coruscant bar the conspirators meet: Nemrileo of Tanjay, Mousul (promised a sector governorship, l.361), a straw-furred alien politician, and Tam Uliss. Mousul reports that Nemrileo is unreliable (l.378). Later a newsblink reports Nemrileo irm-Drocubac killed in an aircar "software failure" (l.482), and "A grieving Chancellor Palpatine" is to give the eulogy (l.484).
- Soergg's plan is to kidnap a Padawan, because "One Jedi's word binds all Jedi" (l.390).
- **Force lore:** "even a minor mental illness is sometimes sufficient to confuse perception of the Force… Psychosis acts like a fogged piece of transparisteel" (l.394). This is why the kidnappers are Bulgan and Kyakhta, brain-damaged clanless Alwari of the Tasbir of the Southern Hatagai (l.411). Soergg implanted bombs in their necks (l.408).
- Culture: Niruu sanwiwood carving (l.426); Northern Bands wear identifying forehead tattoos (l.432). Oaths: Winds of Whorh, Uldas, Nomgon's Arm.
- **Durability:** Barriss takes three bursts of a knockout atomizer, "enough vapor to put out a whole squad of mounted warriors" (l.438).
- Luminara can't sense Barriss (l.469). Anakin reads footprints, and Obi-Wan tells him: "You are learning to see beyond the obvious" (l.467–468).

## Ch. 5: Barriss heals her captors (l.487–590)
- Soergg claims a lightsaber "can be tuned to its owner's personal electrical field" so it would blow up for anyone else (l.490). That is the Hutt's claim, not narration.
- **Limits:** Barriss cannot Force-open the door ("limited mastery", l.497) and cannot mind-influence Bulgan, because there is "not much mind there" (l.498).
- **Force healing feat:** Barriss is "a practicing healer" (l.507). "The Force compelled a subtle realignment of tissues" in Bulgan's brain (l.524). It takes several minutes, and his speech and intelligence change at once (l.525–529). Kyakhta takes "several moments" (l.549). She cannot fix Bulgan's back or eye (l.509). Bulgan's damage came from a childhood fall from a suubatar (l.541).
- Ansionians have three-fingered hands (l.528) and run several degrees warmer than humans (l.523).
- **Tech:** Barriss's belt scanner disables the neck bombs (l.568–571), and Soergg's detonation then does nothing (l.583). Soergg posts a bounty of 1,000 credits for the Padawan alive or "the head of a dead Jedi" (l.584). Soergg does not know who is behind his own paymaster (l.587–588).
