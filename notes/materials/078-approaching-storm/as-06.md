# The Approaching Storm: notes part 6, back matter, bundled excerpts and timeline (l.2200–3630)

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. Citation "AS l.x".
- Excerpt titles in [brackets] are identified from content. The text gives chapter headings only, not the excerpted book's title.

## About the Author and other books by Foster (l.2200–2261)
- Alan Dean Foster won the Cyber Way Southwest Book Award (1990) for the first SF title. He lives in Prescott, AZ, with JoAnn Oxley, in a brick house that was once a miners' brothel.
- The list of his books runs from *The Black Hole* and *Splinter of the Mind's Eye* through the Flinx/Commonwealth books and *Tipping Point: The Human Blend* (l.2204–2261).
- Legends rebranding note, Del Rey, May 2014 (l.2263–2268).

## [The Clone Wars novelization, Karen Traviss] (l.2270–2479)
- **Ch. One:** Dooku and Ziro plot to kidnap Rotta and frame the Jedi.
  - Dooku: "Welcome to politics… Don't delude yourself that it has to have party labels" (l.2301).
  - Jabba asks the Republic for Jedi (l.2310).
- **Ch. Two:** Palpatine meets Windu, Plo Koon, **Luminara Unduli** and Bolla Ropal. Luminara sides with Palpatine on the negotiating position (l.2346). Palpatine's inner monologue as a Sith is given (l.2330, 2354–2360).
- **Christophsis, Anakin's feats:**
  - He leaps two stories off a roof onto an octuptarra droid and stabs it (l.2414–2418).
  - An instinctive Force push deflects metal fragments, and he rips off a battle droid's head in a Force-assisted stranglehold (l.2420).
  - He leaps from octuptarra to octuptarra (l.2433). "Not rage. Not rage." (l.2434). There is a flashback to the Tusken massacre (l.2420–2422).
- Obi-Wan cuts two droids in half with one sweep (l.2437). Ahsoka arrives: "I'm yours, Master Skywalker" (l.2479).

## Era intro: The Old Republic (l.2481–2493)
- Naga Sadow, Ruusan (1,000 BBY), and Bane's Rule of Two are covered.

## [The Old Republic: Fatal Alliance, Sean Williams] (l.2495–2610)
- Shigar Konshi (18) and Satele Shan are introduced. Satele silences Shigar with a telepathic nudge (l.2503).
- Larin Moxla fights Black Sun. Mandalorian Dao Stryver carries a flamethrower, net, jetpack, darts and rockets.
- **Shigar's feats:**
  - He cuts the net and Force-pushes the Mandalorian sideways into a building (l.2559). The Mandalorian then grips him by the throat and hurls him into a wall (l.2561).
  - With his "greatest exertion of the Force" he deflects falling monorail metal spears into the ground (l.2563), then clears the smoke with a push (l.2565).
- "Never trust a Konshi" (l.2608).

## Era intro: Rebellion (l.2736–2748)

## [Shadows of Mindor, Matthew Stover] (l.2750–2847)
- Hobbie fights six TIE Defenders.
  - **Defender specs:** twin ion cannons, Novaldex shields "near capital-ship strength", no particle shields, titanium hull (l.2771).
  - Flechette torpedoes turn them into a "very, very expensive cheese grater" (l.2772).
- Hobbie has a prosthetic left arm and leg (l.2780–2787).
- Taspan II is a gravity-well test site, "Big Crush". Taspan I is Mindor (l.2829–2833).

## [Vector Prime, R. A. Salvatore] (l.2999–3203)

## Timeline (l.3431–3630)
- *The Approaching Storm* is listed under the 32 BBY block, after *Outbound Flight* (l.3457–3461), even though the story is set shortly before *AOTC* (22 BBY).
