# The Approaching Storm: notes part 4, Ch. 14–16 (l.1576–1989)

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. Citation "AS ch. N (l.x)".

## Ch. 14: the ride, the Yoda debate, shanh attack (l.1576–1657)
- **Obi-Wan's balance feat:** he stands on the saddle of a loping suubatar doing stretches, in "perfect balance", through "mastery of… body's neuromuscular complexities" (l.1577). Luminara could match it (l.1578).
- Anakin vows not to become dour like Obi-Wan: "If I should ever forget this vow… it will be because I am no longer the person I have chosen to be" (l.1582).
- **The Yoda debate:**
  - Yoda is "reputed to be the greatest lightsaber master ever" (l.1591).
  - Anakin doubts Yoda has ever really fought anyone ("Someone Tooqui's size, maybe?", l.1596). This is dramatic irony before *AOTC*.
  - Barriss: "It's not the size of the Jedi… but the strength of her heart" (l.1597). Anakin: "Give me size and power any day, and keep your heart" (l.1598).
- Watto was the "nearest thing to a father figure" (l.1614).
- **The shanh attack (l.1620–1636):**
  - Shanhs are six-legged and striped, with backward serrated teeth and three-clawed paws.
  - Anakin's saber is knocked away and shuts off (l.1624). He can only Force-*deflect* a leaping male aside (l.1628).
  - After Luminara, in her sleepwear, kills the male by searing its skull (l.1635), Anakin "shoved hard with the Force" and knocks the female sideways "several body-lengths" (l.1633). He then kills her in mid-leap.
  - **Lesson / limit:** the Force "was not armor… responded only to conscious effort" (l.1654). "Small sounds often mask large sources… Reality is rife with disguises" (l.1636).

## Ch. 15: Unity delegates; the Borokii test (l.1658–1792)
- **Delegates at Kandah's:** Garil Volune (human), Fargane (eldest), Tun Dameerd, Tolut, Kandah. Support from the Malarian and Keitumite treaty partners is waning (l.1679). They compromise on a fixed vote date (l.1680–1683).
- **The Borokii camp (l.1689–1711):** thousands of people. Their riders carry carbonite standard poles and Malarian laser pistols (l.1701). Six is a significant number to Ansionians (l.1701). Bayaar of the Situng Borokii greets them with a hand over the eyes, then the chest (l.1703).
- **Surepp:**
  - Males are blue with coiled antlers; females are green and larger. Three eyes stacked vertically for sky, herd and ground (l.1691–1697).
  - They sleep standing, packed palm-close (l.1752). The two albino males stay at the exact center of the herd (l.1747, 1757).
- **The test:** bring a handful of ruff wool from a white surepp, taken by hand with no devices or mounts (l.1727–1731).
- **Force limit statement:** "A mastery of the Force would enable one to rise momentarily above a small part of the herd, but it would not permit long-term personal levitation" (l.1759).
- Anakin: "The Force was with me" (Podracing). Barriss: "Luck was with you" (l.1775–1776). Obi-Wan and Luminara embrace (l.1785–1786).

## Ch. 16: the surepp run, the conspirators' flight, the Januul battle (l.1794–1989)
- **Luminara's surepp run (l.1795–1830):** with "strength of a weight lifter, agility of a gymnast", she runs across the backs of thousands of densely packed surepp to the center. She moves too fast for Obi-Wan to keep in focus without macrobinoculars (l.1798). She cuts the albino's ruff, then slips and is knocked out (l.1805).
- **The three-way Force hold-back (l.1808–1817):** Obi-Wan holds both Padawans' hands, and the three of them make thousands of surepp part and hold back. "Faced by… proximity of death, the Force grew within him" (Anakin, l.1811). Anakin carries Luminara out on his shoulders (l.1813).
  - Anakin gets butted through the barrier: "Only my dignity, Master" (l.1819).
  - Obi-Wan admits he couldn't have made Luminara's run: "His particular talents lay elsewhere" (l.1843).
- **The conspirators:** Tam Uliss presses ("Wait too long, and the fruit rots", l.1862). Shu Mai calls Ansion the detonator (l.1865) and quietly decides to "deal with" Uliss (l.1875–1877).
- **The Borokii council (12 elders):** Obi-Wan says "A small crack can lead to the collapse of a huge dam" (l.1897). The Borokii price is to help them defeat their traditional enemies, the Hovsgol Januul (l.1919), but Jedi may not take sides (l.1920). Obi-Wan: "No—there is another [choice]" (l.1936). The Januul have under 1,000 warriors and no heavy weapons, by taboo (l.1945).
- **The three-way battle (l.1963–1988): four Jedi stop two armies without one death.**
  - **Obi-Wan's Force push** stops a charging sadain "as if… run into a wall". Its legs go down and the rider is thrown (l.1974).
  - Arrows, spears and bolts are deflected "as rapidly as the lightning itself", and missiles from overhead are turned with the Force (l.1975).
  - Luminara's 3 strokes: a disarm, a melted blade, a club knocked down (l.1976).
  - Anakin walks into blaster fire from two Borokii and disarms both (l.1977).
  - Obi-Wan: "No maiming and no killing… It's hard to win hearts and minds when you're cutting off heads and hands." (l.1978)
  - It lasts about 10 minutes. Both armies are disarmed with no one harmed (l.1980–1982). "nothing more challenging or exhausting for a skilled fighter than engaging in combat while striving not to kill" (l.1988).
  - Luminara decrees that a summit house be built on the field from both sides' parts (l.1986).
