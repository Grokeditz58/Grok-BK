# The Approaching Storm: notes part 2, Ch. 6–9 (l.591–1089)

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. Citation "AS ch. N (l.x)".

## Ch. 6: plan, suubatars (l.591–731)
- Luminara: "Ansion will not become another Naboo" (l.599).
- **Alwari lore:**
  - The Borokii are the leading overclan, and others follow them (l.603). They will not come within 100 huus of a city (l.605).
  - The clanless are "as rootless as the blowing irgkul bush" (l.607).
  - The Alwari reject landspeeders as traditionalists (l.622).
- Anakin: "I'm a champion Podracer" (l.630). His idea is to lock the leaders in a room and threaten them with a task force (l.652); Barriss argues against it. In private he thinks secession might be a wake-up call (l.644).
- **Obi-Wan on Anakin (l.672–676):** he came late; he was Qui-Gon's dying wish; he is impetuous; he "excels… lightsaber combat. And he's a natural pilot"; politics makes him ill.
- **Suubatar (l.681–708):**
  - Three times human height at the shoulder, six-legged, with six clawed toes per foot (36 digits, l.707).
  - Light bronze with dark-green stripes, four canines, a single nostril, silver-flecked eyes.
  - Wild herds "attack and destroy entire caravans" (l.688). The command is "Elup!".
  - They sprint "outpacing the wind" without breathing hard (l.707–708).
  - Balance feats while riding: Kyakhta stands on a galloping one (l.703); Obi-Wan rides with arms crossed and eyes closed (l.701).
- The Varvvan mercenary refuses to give chase: "You ever try to corner an Alwari mounted on a good suubatar? A quick way to die." (l.713). He lifts Ogomoor one-handed (l.717).

## Ch. 7: prairie, Jedi doubts, gairks (l.732–892)
- **Obi-Wan's doubts about the Order:**
  - "Sometimes I wonder if it isn't a flaw in Jedi training to take infants from their families" (l.741).
  - "No Jedi would be a true devotee who didn't question the system" (l.743). He has disagreed with Yoda before the Council (l.749).
- **On Anakin:**
  - Obi-Wan: "only Anakin can decide what Anakin will become" (l.751); "Someday it will come out" (l.753).
  - Luminara: "All Jedi are lonely to one degree or another" (l.764); "I would as soon have Anakin Skywalker by my side in a fight as any Padawan I have ever met" (l.768). Barriss will be an "exceptional healer" (l.766).
- Ansion has two moons (l.757). The Torosogt River marks the edge of Alwari land (l.780); the Alwari would shoot down any seeker droid (l.784).
- **Gairks (l.802–823):** warty, olive-green river predators that drag prey down by suction and lock their jaws together "like the pieces of a puzzle" to pool their suction (l.821).
  - They drag Barriss in. Anakin dives in, and four linked gairks suck him toward them. Using Obi-Wan's maxim "If you can't defy the storm, go with it" (l.822), he swims into them and cuts them apart (l.823).
  - Barriss says she had it handled and outswims him to shore, bursting "forward like an eel" (l.832–833).
  - Obi-Wan: "Better to be berated by a live friend than absolved by a dead one." (l.839)
- Kyakhta has a prosthetic right arm (l.853). Ansionians find humans odd: no mane, five fingers (l.846–849).
- Ogomoor pushes a human Unity delegate to call the vote while the Jedi are away: "changes are coming to the Republic" (l.868–890).

## Ch. 8: Yiwa camp, entertainments (l.893–996)
- The ongun-nur are huge, harmless fliers that ride the wind on balloon wings (l.896–902).
- **The Yiwa (a clan of the Qiemo Adrangar, l.907):**
  - Huts with solar tiles, kept campfires, comlinks and blasters (l.904–918).
  - Leader Mazong. Their mounts are sadain: four legs, four eyes, tall blood-rich ears for spotting predators, a nose ring (l.916, 928).
  - Ansionians cannot squint (l.927). Knuckle-cracking means applause (l.981). Thieves are cast out to the shanhs (l.939).
  - Guests must provide entertainment. Senate envoys never did, because "they have no souls" (l.951).
- **Barriss's lightsaber dance (l.972–980):** compass-point leaps "as if dancing atop a concealed trampoline". She passes the blade under her own feet in mid-jump and ends with a double flip, landing on her knees an arm's length from Mazong. It is "well beyond basic Jedi training" (l.976).
- Anakin sings his mother's song (l.993–995).

## Ch. 9: story, sand dance, kyren (l.997–1089)
- **Obi-Wan's storytelling** holds the whole clan "as mesmerizing as the most powerful hypnotic drug" (l.1008). "Such is the power of story" (l.1015).
- **Luminara's sand dance (l.1021–1031): a top precision-TK feat.**
  - Sand falls against the wind (l.1025), reverses upward and forms two counter-rotating spirals that split into about thirty threadlike pillars (l.1027).
  - She spins on one foot, levitates a hand-length while tilting, and the sand forms a near-solid globe around her (l.1030): "as unique a demonstration of control over the Force as Barriss had ever seen."
  - Her comment: "The trick, my dear, is not to throw up" (l.1035). Anakin: "It would be useful in a fight" (l.1040).
  - It is control over "thousands of individual grains of sand" at once (l.1051).
- "A Jedi could go without sleep for an amazing length of time" (l.1050).
- **Kyren (l.1069–1089+):** seed-eating fliers in flocks of "one or two hundred million" (l.1085). They never change course: they smash rocks, cut trees and eat through animals (l.1077).
  - **Jedi limit statement (Luminara):** "One kyren… A few thousand? Questionable"; a hundred million is "too vast a number for even several Jedi to stand against" (l.1090–1091). The scene continues into Ch. 10.
