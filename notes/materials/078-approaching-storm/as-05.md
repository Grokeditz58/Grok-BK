# The Approaching Storm: notes part 5, Ch. 17–18 (l.1991–2198)

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. Citation "AS ch. N (l.x)".

## Ch. 17: the accord and the ride back (l.1991–2028)
- Obi-Wan: "You asked us to help you deal with your traditional enemies… Nothing was ever said about helping you defeat them." (l.1993)
- The accord is sealed at a joint feast. Anakin plans a vacation (l.1999). With no comlinks, they race to Cuipernam (l.2000).
- Luminara: "Always know where in your mental closet you've hung your bravery" (l.2006). She would want Anakin guarding her back, but "It was what he might do after such battles that concerned her" (l.2008).
- The delegates set the vote for the end of the week (l.2022–2025).

## Ch. 18: the bomb, the ambush, the vote, Dooku (l.2030–2198)
- Soergg smashes a commdroid and orders his people to hold the Jedi until sunset (l.2035–2043). The city's comms are shut down (l.2059). The inn manager is Dbarian, whose skin changes color (l.2047).
- **Danger-sense feat:** Obi-Wan feels it, and the inn implodes exactly 3 minutes after they leave (l.2063).
- **The street ambush:**
  - Luminara senses the snipers through the Force (l.2066). All rooftop fire is deflected: "Not one of the shots… came close" (l.2070).
  - Then dozens of professionals storm the trading compound with "far greater accuracy" (l.2073–2075). Ogomoor's theory: "Even Jedi would be hard-pressed to deal simultaneously with more than one axis of battle" (l.2072).
  - Tooqui tackles a Vrot assassin (l.2076). The Borokii/Januul honor guard, the "gift", takes out the snipers (l.2085–2094). Anakin: "We would have managed" (l.2095).
- **The vote:**
  - The delegation is expanded to 12 (l.2099). Soergg shoots Ogomoor dead to silence him (l.2102–2108).
  - Barriss accuses Soergg, including "in the pay of the Commerce Guild" (l.2114). She holds back from using "the Force to wrench the smug, bloated Hutt's eyes out" (l.2124).
  - The vote is **9–2** to keep the concordance. Kandah and a southern delegate vote no (l.2125). Soergg: "Business is only business" (l.2128).
- Barriss and Anakin reconcile: "I hope you find whatever it is you're looking for" (l.2139). Luminara says "There was evil afoot" and the Council is unsure of its source (l.2145).
- **Coruscant coda:** Tam Uliss (Corellian industrialist, l.2150) is trapped by Shu Mai between two force barriers on the skywalk between Bror Towers 3 and 4 at the 166th floor. The skywalk is detached and he falls 166 floors (l.2176–2182). Officially it is "Structural fatigue" (l.2183). Shu Mai: "Patience, my friend, is the one weapon we cannot afford to waste" (l.2180).
- **Reveal:** Shu Mai reports to **Count Dooku**: "I wouldn't have thought the Jedi could accomplish it. Not in so short a time" (l.2192); "Destiny awaits us… Great changes are at hand" (l.2196).
