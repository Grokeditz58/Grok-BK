# The Approaching Storm: summary of strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. Full hand-read, l.1–3630. Citation "AS ch. N (l.x)".

1. **Four Jedi stop two armies without a single death** (AS ch. 16, l.1963–1988)
   - About 10 minutes against the Borokii and the Hovsgol Januul (under 1,000 warriors on the Januul side), with no maiming and no killing.
   - Obi-Wan's Force push stops a charging sadain "as if… run into a wall" (l.1974). Projectiles are deflected "as rapidly as the lightning itself" (l.1975).
2. **Luminara's surepp run** (AS ch. 16, l.1795–1805)
   - She runs across the backs of thousands of packed surepp, too fast for Obi-Wan to follow without macrobinoculars (l.1798), and cuts the albino's ruff.
3. **Group Force hold-back** (AS ch. 16, l.1808–1817)
   - Obi-Wan, Anakin and Barriss link hands and hold off thousands of stampeding surepp. Anakin's power surges near death (l.1811).
4. **Luminara's sand dance** (AS ch. 9, l.1021–1031)
   - She controls "thousands of individual grains of sand" at once in counter-rotating spirals and about 30 pillars, while levitating and spinning.
   - Also: she takes a carafe from a non-Jedi telekinetic with no gesture and rains every carafe in the room (ch. 3, l.287–293).
5. **Barriss's Force healing** (AS ch. 5, l.524–549)
   - She realigns brain tissue in minutes and cures the kidnappers' psychosis-level impairment. She can't fix old structural injuries (l.509).
6. **Explicit limits (good for debates)**
   - Mental illness fogs Force perception (l.394).
   - Hundreds of millions of kyren are too many "for even several Jedi" (l.1091).
   - A sedative gas drops all four Jedi (l.1438).
   - The Force "was not armor" (l.1654).
   - No long-term levitation (l.1759).
   - Two-axis ambushes strain Jedi (l.2072).
7. **Anakin's dark-side markers** (l.1552–1556, 2008)
   - He toys with a guard before killing him. Obi-Wan: "lest next time your opponent is even faster than that." Luminara worries about "what he might do after such battles."
8. **Plot reveal**
   - Shu Mai answers to Count Dooku (l.2196). Palpatine gives the eulogy for a conspirator who was murdered (l.484).
