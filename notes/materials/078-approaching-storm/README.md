Character quick bios:
- **Obi-Wan Kenobi:** the diplomat-storyteller. He has a Force push that stops a charging sadain, sensed the danger before the inn bomb, and openly questions the Order taking infants. He admits Luminara's agility exceeds his own (l.1843).
- **Anakin Skywalker:** a Padawan, champion podracer and natural pilot. He is impetuous and dismissive of politics, and doubts Yoda has ever fought (l.1596). He shows great willpower against the gas, Force-shoves a shanh several body-lengths, and toys with an enemy before killing him.
- **Luminara Unduli:** Jedi Master with a chin tattoo and stained lip (l.109). The book's best precision-TK and agility feats are hers: the carafes, the sand dance, the surepp run.
- **Barriss Offee:** Luminara's Padawan and a healer. She performs a lightsaber dance, Force-deflects a hail of stones, climbs walls, and heals brain damage. She tempers her anger at Soergg.
- **Villains:**
  - Shu Mai (Commerce Guild) and her conspirators: Senator Mousul, Tam Uliss (murdered), and Nemrileo (murdered).
  - Bossban Soergg the Hutt and his majordomo Ogomoor (shot by Soergg).
  - The Qulun trader Baiuntu.
  - Count Dooku, revealed at the end.
- **Allies:**
  - Kyakhta and Bulgan, Alwari guides who were healed.
  - Tooqui of the Gwurran.
  - Mazong of the Yiwa.
  - Bayaar of the Borokii.

Continuity quirks noted in the text:
- Anakin calls himself a "champion Podracer" (l.630).
- Soergg claims a lightsaber can be tuned to its owner and would explode for anyone else (l.490). That is the Hutt's claim, not narration.
- The timeline places the book at 32 BBY, while the story is set just before AOTC.
- In Ch. 3 a non-Jedi (Tolut) uses telekinesis (l.287).
