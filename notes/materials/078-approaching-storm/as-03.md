# The Approaching Storm: notes part 3, Ch. 10–13 (l.1090–1575)

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. Citation "AS ch. N (l.x)".

## Ch. 10: kyren swarm, Coruscant lake, chawix (l.1090–1217)
- **Kyren limit (cont.):** the shanhs flee. Luminara says a hundred million of anything is "too vast a number for even several Jedi to stand against" (l.1090–1091).
- **Kyren anatomy:** jet black with four membranous wings and fur sled-runners instead of legs (l.1109–1110). Not even an armored landspeeder could force a way through (l.1099).
- The party shelters behind jijite mounds, built by tiny collective reptiles (l.1095). The swarm takes under an hour to pass (l.1114), and a pile of kyren bodies forms a buffer (l.1120). Kyren taste "like boiled mud" (l.1123).
- Bath scene: Obi-Wan Force-sprays water over the suubatars, and the Padawans Force-squirt each other (l.1129–1132).
- **Coruscant:** Shu Mai and Mousul sail on the artificial Savvam Lake, set atop 200-story buildings (l.1150). Shu Mai deals with "the unseen one" (l.1136). "Impatience is a potentially fatal disease" (l.1139). Her hint at Dooku: "It may be that some [Jedi] are not so staunch as you believe" (l.1146).
- **Chawix:** a carnivorous tumbleweed. Its thorns extend when they sense flesh, inject nerve poison, and suck up the liquefied flesh (l.1159–1177).
- **Quotes:**
  - Anakin: "Believing in the Republic isn't the same as believing in the Senate" (l.1206).
  - Barriss on the Senate: "that overstuffed hothouse full of declamatory blowhards!" (l.1207).
  - Anakin on his mother: "Just know that it hurts" (l.1212).

## Ch. 11: Tooqui and the Gwurran (l.1218–1359)
- Barriss chases the thief Tooqui into a slot canyon. The Gwurran are waist-high, furred Ansionian cousins with tails; each has a unique fur pattern.
- **Force feat:** Barriss deflects a hail of stones thrown by dozens of Gwurran from the canyon rim, using a raised hand. The strain nearly makes her pass out (l.1234–1240).
- The Gwurran hate the Alwari ("Nomads kill Gwurran", l.1254). They have three fingers, climb like spiders and live in hill caves. They don't know what Coruscant or even Ansion is (l.1326–1328).
- Obi-Wan: "A Padawan's promise does not bind a Jedi, but that doesn't mean it should not be honored" (l.1308).
- Luminara points out the mirror: the Alwari see the Gwurran the way the cities see the Alwari (l.1316–1317). She vows to get the Gwurran a school (l.1329).
- Tooqui: "Tooqui eat danger for morning meal! Make good pet, too." (l.1343). Barriss, when told Jedi can't keep pets: "Where does it say that in the regulations, Master?" (l.1350).

## Ch. 12: the Qulun trap (l.1360–1439)
- The Qulun are a trader clan who deal with both the Alwari and the cities (l.1368). Their chief trader is Baiuntu: "Any merchant can be bought… loyalty is just another commodity" (l.1416).
- **Tech:** self-erecting houses, atmospheric water condensers, and perfumed dance pixies (l.1399–1406). An Ansionian wink is a hand passed across both eyes (l.1400).
- **The Jedi are knocked out:** paluruvu, a violet sedative essence from the Dzavak Lakes, is released in a sealed room. It downs all four Jedi, their guides, and Baiuntu himself (l.1425–1438).
  - Obi-Wan fails to draw his saber (l.1431).
  - **Anakin's willpower feat:** he gets up and rams the door, one of the last standing, with an "enormous reservoir of willpower" (l.1434–1435). Luminara is the last one conscious (l.1435).
  - **Limit:** "Even Jedi training could be overcome… a lightsaber was useless against the… fragrance" (l.1438).

## Ch. 13: captivity, lorqual stampede, escape (l.1440–1575)
- Tooqui escapes the Qulun's blaster fire. The Gwurran gods are Miywondl (wind) and Kapchenaga (thunder) (l.1444).
- **Captivity:**
  - The prisoners are hooded and gagged and their comlinks destroyed (l.1475). "At the first sign of Jedi tricks, the perpetrator will be shot" (l.1473).
  - Baiuntu has been paid to delay their return (l.1472). Luminara deduces the Hutt is behind it (l.1481).
  - Their transport is pulled by 8 sadain (l.1485).
- **Lorqual (l.1496–1501):** the largest plains beasts. Slightly taller than a suubatar at their two sets of shoulders and as heavy as four suubatars. Six skull knobs; 6 feet with 6 toes each. Calves are too big for anything less than a full shanh pack within two weeks.
- **Tooqui's stampede (l.1503–1518):** in a rainstorm he climbs a lorqual with a basket of stones and pelts the herd into a panic. He steers the stampede through the Qulun camp. One shot can't drop a lorqual; it takes multiple laser bursts.
- He frees Barriss with Bulgan's knife (l.1520). Jedi regain circulation faster than the untrained (l.1522).
- **The corral fight (l.1550–1563):**
  - Obi-Wan parries a rifle burst with "unnaturally swift" speed and kills his guard.
  - **Anakin toys with his guard** before killing him with a neck cut (l.1552).
  - Anakin: "He was faster than I thought." Obi-Wan: "Have a care, Anakin, lest next time your opponent is even faster than that." (l.1555–1556). Dark-side foreshadowing.
  - Four Qulun sentries die. Barriss is cut on her left hand. The suubatars jump the electrified corral.
- Baiuntu is thrown from his sadain and killed by a shanh pack before he can reach his blaster (l.1567–1574).
