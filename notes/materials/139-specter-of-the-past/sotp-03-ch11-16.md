Meditates → overwhelming Force vision kaleidoscope: Wedge/Rogues in battle; Jedi students leaving Yavin; self on canyon balcony over lights; **Han/Leia vs mob** + rooftop sniper; Mara floating limp in water as if dead (ll.2313–2317). Identifies locale as **Canyonade on Cejansij**; jumps there despite Mara vision (ll.2330–2342).
