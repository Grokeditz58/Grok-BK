# Young Jedi Knights 5: Darkest Knight: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-20 CST. Full read of READ l.1–1746 (every line, in order): front matter, all 22 chapters, the Jedi Under Siege blurb and preview, the series ad and the back cover. Notes are limited to scaling-relevant material (owner's ruling, 2026-11-20). The quotes are in dk-01 to dk-04; this summary is in our own words and cites them by chapter.
- Authors: Kevin J. Anderson & Rebecca Moesta. Fifth Young Jedi Knights book. Copyright page (scan p6): Boulevard edition, June 1996, © 1996 Lucasfilm Ltd. 22 chapters.
- Era: directly after Lightsabers. Tenel Ka has her new turquoise lightsaber and one arm; Zekk has just won the Darkest Knight title. No ABY year is given.
- Big events: the Shadow Academy raids the Wookiee computer fabrication facility on Kashyyyk while Lowie's sister Sirra faces her rite of passage. Jaina defeats Garowyn, who falls into the forest depths. Vonnda Ra is killed by a syren plant. Zekk spares Jaina and Chewbacca and lies to Brakiss that the young Jedi are "no longer a problem." The "Emperor" orders the Shadow Academy to attack Luke's academy on Yavin 4.

## Plot
- **Ch. 1–2:** Lowie broods over Sirra's coming rite of passage; Tenel Ka spars one-handed with Tionne; Han arrives in disguise with shield-generator parts; Chewbacca is to take the four to Kashyyyk.
- **Ch. 3:** the trip in the Shadow Chaser; Jacen finds a rodent nesting in the ion shield generator; an ion storm knocks out the shields and navicomputer; the Wookiees navigate home from memory.
- **Ch. 4:** Brakiss gives Zekk the Darkest Knight cape and the Kashyyyk raid; Tamith Kai adds orders to remove the twins; the Nightsisters argue over Zekk's loyalty.
- **Ch. 5–7:** Lowie's family; Sirra's resentment and wish for adventure; the tour of the computer facility and the Y-wing demonstration.
- **Ch. 8–9:** a coded message tells Brakiss the Emperor is coming; the Emperor's shuttle and red guards shut him out; Qorl trains the reckless Norys.
- **Ch. 10:** Lowie and Sirra mourn Raaba.
- **Ch. 11–13:** the disguised raiders take the control tower and cut the orbital defenses; TIEs attack; Jacen and Tenel Ka man the perimeter guns; Lowie and Sirra find the single-point failure in the tower; Zekk and Vonnda Ra face the Jedi; Sirra opens an escape hatch to the underlevels.
- **Ch. 12, 15:** Garowyn tries to steal the Shadow Chaser; Jaina and Chewbacca beat her; Jaina's Force furrow tricks her into a fall; they ride her speeder bike to the burning factory.
- **Ch. 14:** the Emperor rebukes Brakiss over the comm and calls him expendable.
- **Ch. 16–20:** the chase through the underlevels; the vine creature; Jacen turns a giant slug on the troopers; Tenel Ka drops four troopers by cutting their branch; Vonnda Ra dies in the syren plant after Sirra tackles her, and Sirra comes out with syren fibers; Chewbacca falls and breaks his leg; Zekk finds Jaina, spares her and warns her away from Yavin 4; the others arrive and carry Chewbacca up.
- **Ch. 21–22:** Zekk reports to Brakiss and covers for his friends; the Emperor's hologram orders the move against Yavin 4; Sirra stays home; the friends fly back to warn the academy.

## Strongest findings (see the part files for the quotes)
- **Tenel Ka:** fights one-handed with precision over brute force (Tionne, ch. 1); mans a Wookiee quad-laser one-handed in seconds and downs two TIE fighters (ch. 13); knocks Vonnda Ra aside with a thrown pipe (ch. 13); cuts free of the vine creature and knocks Jacen out of a blaster bolt's path (ch. 16); cuts the branch under four stormtroopers with one stroke (ch. 18). Luke's preview statement: she should teach ground combat against assassins and commandos (back matter).
- **Jaina:** Force-deflects Garowyn's crates and Force-shoves her across the floor; her Force "stampede" sends Garowyn into the depths (ch. 12). Limitations: can't carry a Wookiee even with the Force (ch. 19); tests no branch and falls (ch. 17).
- **Jacen:** kills a loaded TIE bomber and more TIEs at the perimeter guns but misses evasive ones (ch. 13); nudges a trooper with the Force, then turns a giant slug on the troopers (ch. 16); freezes under fire (ch. 16).
- **Lowie / Sirra:** they find the tower's single-point failure (ch. 13); Sirra lifts the heavy hatch, rips vines free and tackles Vonnda Ra into the syren plant (ch. 13, 16, 18). Lowie is stunned by Vonnda Ra's lightning (ch. 18).
- **Chewbacca:** pulls the initiator module, hurls crates without the Force, and leaps through the air to catch Garowyn's speeder bike (ch. 12); tracks by scent in the dark (ch. 17).
- **Zekk:** turns Jaina's Force rustle into a whirlwind and a leaf storm (ch. 19); Brakiss calls him "an instrumental fighter" (ch. 21). Portrayal: still protects his friends (ch. 18–21).
- **Garowyn / Vonnda Ra / Tamith Kai:** Garowyn's telekinetic barrage and Force lightning (ch. 12); Vonnda Ra's vibroblade sabotage, dark lightning and superheating of the pipe (ch. 11, 13); Tamith Kai's statements on Zekk's loyalty (ch. 4, 13).
- **Brakiss / the Emperor:** Brakiss can't detect the cloaking field with the Force and is cowed by the Emperor (ch. 8, 14). The Emperor claims ten times Brakiss's power to protect himself and says he can destroy the Academy on a whim (ch. 14). As in LO and LS, the Emperor's lines are kept in the notes only and not routed to darth-sidious.md, because this book presents him only through a voice, guards and a hologram.
- **Qorl / Norys:** Qorl's droid arm could crack armor "like an eggshell" (ch. 4); Norys misses repeatedly, then hits friend and foe alike (ch. 9).
