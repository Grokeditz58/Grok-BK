## Ch. 1 (READ l.146–230) — Lowie alone atop a Massassi tree; dinner; Tenel Ka spars with Tionne; the Falcon lands
- Plot: Lowie climbs the tallest Massassi tree near the Great Temple to think about Kashyyyk and his sister Sirrakuk, whose dangerous time is near. At dinner Jacen chatters about his pets while Jaina worries about Lowie, who sits alone and will not talk. At night Tenel Ka spars with Tionne using her new turquoise lightsaber. The disguised Millennium Falcon lands; Tenel Ka realizes Lowie knew it was coming.
