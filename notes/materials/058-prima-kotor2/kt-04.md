# Prima *KOTOR II* guide, part 4: Endgame (PDF pp. 194–212)

Source and copy as in [kt-01](kt-01.md). Citations use **PDF pages**; printed page = PDF − 3.

## Headline finding: in this guide, *Atris* is "Darth Traya"
- **Atris's fall (p. 203):** The guide says Atris "has taken the moniker of Darth Traya". It goes on: "Traya has gathered dozens of Sith holocrons".
- **The Traya fight (pp. 203–204):** The duel at the Jedi Academy on Telos is headed "DARTH TRAYA BATTLE". The tips are written for Atris, who is called "a former member of the Jedi Council" and "the Queen of Betrayal".
- **What the guide never says:** A full-text search finds no passage that names **Kreia** as Darth Traya. Kreia's final fight (pp. 211–212) is headed simply "KREIA BATTLE".
- **Comparison with the shipped game (outside knowledge, not from the guide):** In the released game, Kreia is the one who reveals herself as Darth Traya on Malachor V. Atris falls to the dark side but never takes that name. This guide went to press early and appears to keep an earlier draft of the reveal. **Treat it as a guide-only variant.**
- **The Council scene (p. 203):** This also differs from the game as many players remember it. The Council plans "permanently banishing you from all connection to the Force. Kreia saves you". If you have already killed the masters, the guide says "it's the death of the Force itself that she's after."

## The Ravager (PDF pp. 194–208)
- **The ship (p. 197):** "The Ravager was used in the Mandalorian Wars". Mandalore leads the boarding party and brings four proton cores. "After you defeat Darth Nihilus and escape, you can detonate the cores by remote and completely destroy the Ravager once and for all" (p. 205).
- **Visas (p. 205):** "Visas Marr arrives (if she wasn't with you already) and vows to join your confrontation with Darth Nihilus, her former master."
- **Tobin (p. 206):** Tobin "is now practically a prisoner, chained to Darth Nihilus and the Ravager by the dark side". You can redeem him, but "this only works if you bring up the safety of Onderon."
- **Nihilus (pp. 207–208):**
  - "Darth Nihilus is so corrupted by the dark side that his very speech causes pain and death to all who hear it."
  - On first contact he "effortlessly stuns you with a wave of his hand".
  - Visas can "block the flow of power Nihilus absorbs from the Ravager".
  - At the end, "The Lord of Hunger evaporates in a storm of dark energy". "The Lord of Hunger leaves behind no remains". His mask, which Visas gives you, "slightly increases your maximum Force points".
- **Carth cameo (p. 208):** "Admiral Carth Onasi, who fought with Revan in the Mandalorian Wars". He points you to "Malachor V". The text's typo reads "you most follow the Ravager's course".

## Malachor V and Trayus Academy (PDF pp. 208–212)
- **Arrival (pp. 208–209):** "The Ebon Hawk crashes on the Malachor V's jagged surface." The fight with the greater storm beast follows. At the academy, "A dozen Sith assassins uncloak as you approach, but they don't attack; instead, they reverently bow to you."
- **Mass shadow generator (p. 210):** "Bao-Dur has programmed his trusty remote droid to accomplish one final task: the destruction of Malachor V." The remote powers four wrecked Republic ships. Then "G0-T0 arrives and interrupts the plan. Ultimately, the choice of whether or not to destroy Malachor V is up to you."
- **Sion (p. 211):** "To end the battle for good, you must slay the Lord of Pain four times. After that, he surrenders to your greater strength of will and, with some relief, voluntarily relinquishes his life." A sidebar adds: "This duel isn't about strength, it's about belief."
- **Kreia (p. 212):**
  - "Kreia reveals the hidden motive that's driven every step she's taken during your quest: her utter hatred of the Force."
  - "Only death can free her from this hatred, but it must come at your hands."
  - Tactics: "Kreia is a Consular". In her second phase she "summons a trio of lightsabers and, using her Kinetic Combat feat, mentally commands them to attack you".
- **Ending (p. 212):** "Before she dies, Kreia answers all of these questions for you, thanks to her ability to see dimly into the future." There are two endings, and the guide doesn't describe them: "we won't spoil them by telling you what happens."
