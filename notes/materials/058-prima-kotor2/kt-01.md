## Jedi Companions (PDF pp. 5–35)
- **Scope (p. 5):** "This section reveals the 12 party members available during your questing, 10 of whom can be brought into your fold during a single adventure across the galaxy." A spoiler box warns that the section "reveals which party members can be trained in the ways of the Jedi", and it points to Appendix XII for companion quests. Influence is explained as "praise (high influence) or contempt (low influence)."
- **T3-M4 (pp. 5–7):** "Built by Janice Nall, a Twi’lek droid mechanic on Taris, T3-M4 is a robust expert droid". It works as "a portable workbench for upgrading items". It is "immune to mental Force projection". Starting level is Expert Droid 3, with a Droid Shock Arm and a Mining Laser.
- **Kreia (pp. 7–9):**
  - "the mysterious old woman plaguing your dreams and abducting you from your ship reveals herself to be a powerful Jedi Consular named Kreia."
  - Mentor gives an XP bonus. Force Chain is "a unique psychic bond between herself and you".
  - "When your character reaches Level 15, Kreia offers to teach you her deepest secrets, allowing you to branch out into the Prestige classes."
  - A spoiler hidden in the build advice: "Ignore Two-Weapon Fighting, as Kreia loses her left hand early in the adventure" (p. 8).
  - Her starting Force is 49, the highest listed for any companion.
- **Atton Rand (pp. 10–12):** "Tumbling through disaster after disaster ever since he was five and counting on his incredible luck to pull him through each time, Atton “Jaq” Rand was once an elite pilot but has always been a scoundrel." His unique feats are Scoundrel's Luck, Survival and Spirit. The tip is to heal him "only to half his regular health so he takes more damage, thus making him tougher." The caption notes that his appearance shifts with alignment.
- **Bao-Dur (pp. 13–15):** "Bao-Dur is a Zabrak tech specialist and refugee from a colony world in the Mid Rim that was bombed out of commission by Malak." He has Shield Breaker, Repulsor Strike and "a small remote droid". "He builds your lightsaber and upgrades your droids." His dark-side shift shows as a "change in tattoos" (p. 14).
- **Handmaiden (pp. 16–18):** "One of the six Echani handmaidens who serve the Jedi Knight Atris". She is "Only available to male player characters after you visit the Jedi academy on Telos". Her unique feat is Echani Strike. Once trained she "dons her mother’s robes".
- **Mira (pp. 19–21):** She is a Nar Shaddaa bounty hunter who "joins you if your destiny has taken a light side or neutral path". She has a wrist launcher, the eight-tier Targeting feat, and "Mira’s Point Guard ability–an extremely useful bonus when trekking through mine fields."
- **Hanharr (pp. 21–23):**
  - The section header misspells him as "HANHARR: WOOKIE SCOUT" (p. 21).
  - "Hanharr is an anomaly of Wookiee culture; he is a dishonorable predator who came to see the Czerka Corporation’s attempts to enslave his homeworld as a great learning experience for how galactic trade works." He joins "only... if you are strongly committed to the dark side".
  - The guide's joke: "a truly unstoppable walking carpet" (p. 22).
  - His starting Strength and Constitution are both 20.
- **G0-T0 (pp. 24–25):** "it has managed to become a prominent Exchange crime lord on Nar Shaddaa." He has a personal cloaking shield and Droid Scramble. Talking to him earns "credits that establish stability on various planets."
- **HK-47 (pp. 25–27):** "the combat droid rumored to be in the service of Darth Revan appears to be completely deactivated." The text says to steer him "away from conversations about party-member assassination and toward the droid’s role in the Mandalorian Wars". A "pacifist package... unlock[s] a side of HK-47 you never knew existed!" His four component sources are listed on p. 26:
  1. HK-50 remains at Peragus.
  2. Kodin at Refuge Landing Pad.
  3. HK-50 remains at Vogga's Warehouse.
  4. 1B-8D in Onderon's Western Square.
- **Mandalore (pp. 27–29):** "Once a mercenary-for-hire, this Mandalorian foot soldier abandoned these practices to seek a new purpose: the gathering of scattered Mandalorian warriors across the galaxy and a return home. Now the Mandalore or “chief,” this soldier joins your party on the beast moon of Dxun". The guide never names him Canderous here. "A true Mandalore never takes off his ceremonial armor, even when he sleeps". His unique feats are Implant Switching and Mandalorian Courage.
- **Disciple (pp. 30–32):** "The Disciple is a historian currently researching on the planet Dantooine, and he joins your party only if your player character is female." He supplies "a constant source of medpacs" and works as "a mobile and instantly accessible" lab station.
- **Visas Marr (pp. 32–35):** "Have you checked your space craft recently? There may be a Sith Apprentice aboard by the name of Visas Marr." She is Miraluka and "effectively blind but uses her Force Sight talents". She carries her own lightsaber, but "this must be destroyed". The guide calls her "arguably the best character to support you in combat."
- **Format:** each companion gets a starting and wounded appearance, recommended "ultimate appearance" screenshots (melee, ranged, light, dark), starting stats and skills, 30-level feat and power charts, a preferred-equipment chart and item upgrades. The gear picks repeat across companions: Zeison Sha warrior armor, the Verpine prototype shield, the Echani vibrosword with the Sith tremor sword, the Mandalorian disintegrator and the Kaiburr crystal.

## Jedi Training (PDF pp. 36–53)
- **Framing (p. 36):** "The d20 Game System". The six attributes are explained with game examples.
  - INT reveals the Peragus lift code: "If your character has high INT, the decoded solution appears automatically as one of your input choices" (p. 37).
  - WIS is "particularly useful for detecting when someone is lying to you" (p. 37).
  - CHA: "Anyone in your party gets to add your CHA modifier to all their attacks and saves" (p. 38).
- **Classes (pp. 38–39):**
  - Guardian, Sentinel and Consular. "For skills, boost your Persuade, as only the player has this" (p. 38).
  - The Consular advice borrows Palpatine's line: "maximize your wisdom to become more powerful than you can possibly imagine" (p. 39).
- **Prestige classes (pp. 40–41):** "When you reach Level 15, and if you’ve attracted Visas Marr to your party, you have qualified for a Prestige class. Talk to Kreia on the Ebon Hawk to trigger the advancement."
  - The six classes are Jedi Weapon Master and Sith Marauder, Jedi Watchman and Sith Assassin, and Jedi Master and Sith Lord.
  - The marauder is for "the most melee murdering in the Old Republic". The assassin's Sneak Attack goes to Level 10, "enough to almost bring down a bantha with a single strike." The Sith Lord gets "Crush Opposition" and "Dark Side Corruption".
- **Skills (pp. 42–45):** Skill checks use "take 20" when failing has no consequence. Other notes:
  - Korriban's academy terminals: "you won’t need to memorize your complicated ID number if you have high Computer Use" (p. 43).
  - Mind Trick is the one power that "does not cancel stealth mode" (p. 43).
  - "there are two places on Nar Shaddaa where you can overhear whispered conversations by approaching unseen" (p. 43).
- **Feats (pp. 45–47):** "Every character except Kreia can and should master Two-Weapon Fighting as soon as possible" (p. 46). Force Jump "only works if you execute a standard attack while equipped with a lightsaber."
- **Force powers (pp. 48–50):**
  - "Only Jedi or Sith can use Force powers".
  - On Shock: "Above all, Shock is essential to learn" (p. 49).
  - "The save DC for Plague is 100, making resistance impossible" (p. 49).
  - Affect Mind "Every player should learn this power when they qualify (Level 6)" (p. 49).
  - Force Wave: "Late in your quest you are often alone against overwhelming numbers; Force Wave saves your life every time" (p. 49).
  - Breath Control: "you’ll use this power extensively on Nar Shaddaa, Korriban, and Malachor V" (p. 50).
- **Forms (pp. 50–51):** Shii-Cho is the default form, learned at Level 7. Makashi trades deflection for attack. "Of these, Juyo and Niman are the strongest". Juyo brings attacks per round "to four". Force forms are Force Channel/Affinity and Potency/Mastery.
- **Equipment (pp. 51–53):**
  - Loot is random: "even the remains of unique enemies like Revan or Darth Sion may contain an incredibly rare and powerful lightsaber…or nothing at all" (p. 51). Naming *Revan* as an enemy whose remains you loot is odd, since Revan is never fought in KOTOR II. This is probably a slip for another Sith Lord.
  - Lightsabers take "three crystals (one of which determines the lightsaber’s color) a lens, an emitter, and an energy cell" (p. 52).
  - "almost all droid battles can be ended quickly with a few ion grenades" (p. 53). The Disciple acts as "a mobile lab station" (p. 53).
