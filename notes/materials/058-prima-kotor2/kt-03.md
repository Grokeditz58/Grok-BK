# Prima *KOTOR II* guide, part 3: Dxun, Onderon, Dantooine, Korriban, Galactic Tour (PDF pp. 135–193)

Source and copy as in [kt-01](kt-01.md). Citations use **PDF pages**; printed page = PDF − 3.

## Dxun (PDF pp. 135–146)
- **Overview (p. 135):** "When you enter Onderon space, a fleet of ships immediately attack forcing you to land the damaged Ebon Hawk on Dxun". The moon "conceals a secret Mandalorian base."
- **Arrival (p. 140):** The attackers are "commanded by the ambitious Colonel Tobin". Kreia tells you "the moon is where the Mandalorian Wars began." The guide also spells the Ebon Hawk's former owner "Ratrin Vehk" here (p. 140), against "Ratrin Vhek" on p. 108.
- **Beasts:**
  - Maalraas are "large carnivores that resemble a cross between a dog and lizard" (p. 136).
  - The boma and the zakkeg appear on p. 137.
  - Kreia teaches the secret power Beast Trick: "Kreia teaches you a secret Force power, Beast Trick, when you encounter a young boma" (p. 138).
- **Mandalorian camp:**
  - The Mandalorians are "generally distrustful and unfriendly" (p. 139).
  - The deal: Mandalore "flies you to Onderon in his private shuttle while Atton stays behind to complete repairs on the Ebon Hawk" (p. 139).
  - "There are seven different missions you can accomplish to impress Mandalore" (p. 141).
  - Kumus can be saved or exposed: "For a light side bonus, don't reveal Kumus' humiliating secret" (p. 145).
  - The battle circle has five bouts: Davrel, Kex, Tagren, Kelborn, then Bralor (pp. 145–146).
- **The Sith attack (p. 146):** "Don't worry about the Mandalorians; they can't be slain." Mandalore wants you gone: "After all, the Sith only attacked because you were there."

## Onderon, first visit (PDF pp. 147–158)
- **Overview (p. 147):** "Onderon is torn by conflict between Queen Talia, the rightful ruler, and her cousin General Vaklu". Light-side players back Talia and dark-side players back Vaklu.
- **Mandalore joins (pp. 152, 156):** His tooltip reads "Mandalore vows his allegiance to you, but not his servitude." Back on Dxun, "Mandalore informs you that he's now joining your quest on a permanent basis."
- **The Sullio murder (p. 154):**
  - Nikko says the doctor and captain "were renowned for their mutual abuse—it was their way of flirting."
  - The case ends unresolved: "While the murder remains unsolved, at least Dhagon Gent's name is cleared."
  - Elsewhere the text misspells "Captian Sullio" (p. 152).
- **Dark-side hook (p. 155):** Tobin "asks you to help General Vaklu defeat a Sith Lord who's been pushing them around and to help overthrow Queen Talia". Vaklu's men keep attacking you to "keep up appearances."
- **HK-47 (pp. 150, 156):** 1B-8D sells "the HK vocabulator". The guide's comment: "It sounds suicidal, but when HK47 starts taking your orders, you're on your way to commanding the strongest ranged attack specialist in your group."
- **Starport visas (pp. 157–158):** There are two visas and five claimants:
  - Terlyn, whose "husband was eliminated for trying to assassinate General Vaklu".
  - Gormo, who pays 500.
  - Tolas, who pays 2,000, or 3,000 with Persuade.
  - Sakarie, who offers "a premium weapon, 5,000 credits, or a rare Qixoni crystal".
  - Xaart, "a secret Republic operative". "If you say anything against the Republic, he will never talk to you again!"
- **Name slips:**
  - Bakkel and Bikkel are both used (pp. 155, 157).
  - Bostuko and Bostuco are both used (p. 158).
  - Gelesi and Gilesi are both used (p. 158).

## Dantooine (PDF pp. 159–174)
- **Overview (p. 159):** "The renowned Jedi Enclave, destroyed during the Mandalorian Wars, has attracted a host of scavengers and explorers to its ruins." KOTOR I had the Enclave bombarded by Malak's fleet during the later Jedi Civil War, so this wording looks like a slip in the guide.
- **Azkul (p. 159):** "a hardened veteran of the Mandalorian Wars who fought for the Sith".
- **The Disciple (pp. 162, 165):** He is "a historian and scientist working for the Republic". He has been "scouring the galaxy for lost Jedi", and he "has been searching for you as well." He joins only a female PC. "If you're playing a male character, the disciple doesn't join you" (p. 165).
- **Vrook (pp. 166, 170):**
  - "Vrook is angry with you for rescuing him."
  - Siding with Azkul means "You must defeat Jedi Master Vrook". Afterward, "The mercenary captain slays Administrator Adare".
  - Either way, "you learn a new lightsaber form, Shion". The guide spells the form "Shion" (the game's name is "Shien").
  - Vrook "wields a double-bladed lightsaber".
- **Mandalorian aside (p. 166):** "Esok challenges him for the right to wear the helm of the Mandalore."
- **Sidequest humor (p. 171):** "Ahrnell describes the fascinating properties of the kinrath rectal gland."

## Korriban (PDF pp. 175–182)
- **Overview (p. 175):** The Sith "turned on each other in mutual self-destruction". Also, "Kreia will not set foot on the surface of Korriban, but her Force Chain with you remains intact."
- **The Sith Academy trap (p. 176):** "Darth Sion has kept the Jedi Master here as bait, hoping to lure you inside."
- **Recruit test crib sheet (p. 179):** The student ID is "3401726-B853S5O0X001". The answers are "1 Freedon Nadd", "2 Twenty", "3 Gizka", "4 I always lie" and "5 Passion, strength, power, victory". A "glitch in the system causes the Level Sixteen Test to be administered instead."
- **Vash (p. 180):** "Of all the lost Jedi you've sought, you are too late to speak with her." Sion "can restore his health at will", and the text has the typo "Kriea".
- **The secret tomb (pp. 178, 181–182):**
  - "Ludo Kressh, Sith Lord, is buried here".
  - Entry requires strong alignment: "the hidden tomb within blocks entry to those who haven't dedicated themselves to one side of the Force."
  - "Malak himself appears, along with a group of his most loyal companions: Bastila, Nisotsa, Cariaga Sin, and two other random Jedi".
  - "Malak probes you regarding the choices you made during the Mandalorian Wars".
  - The mined-bridge vision is "the heart of the Mandalorian crusade during a critical moment when you ordered your troops to charge across a mined bridge".
  - Revan: "Darth Revan's appearance depends on your dialogue with Atton back on Peragus." Also, "Revan is only a ghost and can't fight at full strength." The ghost drops "Bastila's lightsaber".

## Galactic Tour (PDF pp. 183–193)
- **Freedon Nadd (p. 184):** "Freedon Nadd was a Dark Jedi who conquered Onderon long ago and established the royal line from which Queen Talia is descended. The Sith have gathered here to conduct a blood ritual intended to resurrect the entombed tyrant."
- **Party split (p. 187):** "Over Mandalore's objections, it's decided to split the party into two groups." Puzzle solutions follow: the terminal math is "6 x 2 = 12" through to 13 (p. 188), and the vault code is "65, 45, 39" (p. 191).
- **The Basilisk (p. 189):** "An armored dropship designed for quick penetration into hostile territory."
- **Kavar (p. 192):** "Jedi Master Kavar doesn't want to fight you, but he does what he must to protect Queen Talia". He "isn't as much of a lightsaber virtuoso as the other Jedi masters". On the light path, "Queen Talia declares a death sentence for her cousin", and you can persuade her "to grant General Vaklu a fair trial."
