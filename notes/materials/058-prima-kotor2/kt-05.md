# Prima *KOTOR II* guide, part 5: Appendices I–XII and "Paths of Destiny" (PDF pp. 213–304)

Source and copy as in [kt-01](kt-01.md). Citations use **PDF pages**; printed page = PDF − 3. Appendices I–XI are mostly stat tables. I read the item and power descriptions for their lore, and I list only the notable ones here. Appendix XII ("Party Members' Paths of Destiny", PDF pp. 292–304) is a dense dialogue and influence guide, and it is summarized in more detail.

## Appendices I–XI: lore in the item text
- **The guide's own instructions (p. 213):** "The items highlighted in red are items we strongly recommend you locate and equip."
- **Handmaiden's family (pp. 213, 243):**
  - Her staff "once belonged to her father, a powerful Echani general" (p. 213).
  - Her robe "was given to a handmaiden by her mother, Errin. Though unknown to handmaidens, Errin was a Jedi Knight" (p. 243).
- **Old Republic deep lore in the item notes:**
  - "Freedon Nadd trained with (and later destroyed) Naga Sadow's spirit" (p. 220).
  - A blaster "once belonged to Freedon Nadd" (p. 230).
  - The Ludo Kressh war sword (p. 215). Ludo Kressh "opposed fellow Sith lord Naga Sadow in the Great Hyperspace War" (p. 261).
  - Exar Kun's armor dates "his defeat forty years ago" (p. 247).
  - The Crado robe: "Given how completely Crado was killed, it is questionable whether this robe ever actually belonged to him" (p. 244).
  - Sylvar, Thon, Nomi and Andur, Aleema Keto and Arca Jeth also appear in robe notes (p. 244). Ulic Qel-Droma is mentioned in robe and armor notes (pp. 244, 246).
- **Gray Jedi definition (p. 244):** "Gray Jedi, although having completed the teachings of the Jedi, operate independently and outside of the Jedi Council. They are typically seen as misguided, though they have not necessarily succumbed to the dark side."
- **KOTOR I tie-ins:**
  - "This blaster bears the symbol of the Onasi family. It perhaps once belonged to Carth Onasi, a former companion of Revan" (p. 229).
  - Jolee Bindo's robes, "though his current whereabouts are unknown" (p. 243).
  - A garment "Darth Malak, the fallen former apprentice of Darth Revan, once possessed" (p. 245).
  - An armband "Crudely engraved upon this armband are the words “For M Vao—Z”" (p. 261). This reads as Zaalbar's gift to Mission Vao; that's my inference.
  - The exile's own band: "This band belonged to the Jedi <Your Character>, who was exiled from the Jedi Order following the Mandalorian Wars" (p. 261). The `<Your Character>` placeholder was printed as-is.
- **Lost Jedi of the Great Hunt (pp. 222, 257):**
  - Ood Banr "bequeathed the Solari crystal to his most promising student, a young female Jedi named Shaela Nuur" (p. 222).
  - The circlet of Saresh belonged to Guun Han Saresh, who "disappeared shortly after the time of the Great Hunt" (p. 257).
- **Force traditions in the armor notes (pp. 243–245):** The Baran Do ("Force-sensitive members of the Kel Dor race"), the Matukai, the Zeison Sha (from "The Outer Rim planet Yanibar") and the Jal Shey ("seeking to understand it at a mental level rather than at a spiritual one").
- **Jokes and oddities in the item text:**
  - Mira's darts: "Once thought to be harmless children's toys, lawn darts were banned from all civilized worlds" (p. 239).
  - "Thirty-seven Mandalorians who looked like Jigger were accidentally executed until sightings declined" (p. 231).
  - A shielding device was nicknamed "Little Shocky" (p. 255).
  - Medical gear: "If you feel better, you are better" (p. 254).
  - A Force power whose description says "A successful save is impossible—it should never happen" (p. 290).
  - "Kyber darts hail from the little-known world of Kamino" (p. 239).
- **Party mechanics in the feats:**
  - "Kreia can use weapons without her hands" (p. 278).
  - Kreia also gives the party an XP bonus: "3 percent at Level 1" (p. 278).
  - Mandalore's implants: "Mandalore has four implant options" (p. 277).
  - Atton gets back up after being knocked out: "When knocked unconscious, Atton has a chance to get back up" (p. 281).
- **Force forms (pp. 291–292):** The table spells it "Shien" (against "Shion" on p. 170). Makashi gives "Attack modifier: +3 with lightsaber" and "Blaster bolt deflection: -5" (p. 292).

## Appendix XII: Party Members' Paths of Destiny (PDF pp. 292–304)
- **Spoiler warning (p. 292):** "The following party member information reveals all of their secrets and should ONLY be witnessed if you wish to know EVERYTHING about your teammates".
- **T3-M4's holograms (p. 293):** What T3 shows depends on how you set up Revan:
  - "If Revan was male and good: A hologram of Bastilla appears in Jedi robes".
  - "If Revan was female and evil: A hologram of Carth Onasi appears in an old outfit, talking about evil Revan as the original party heads to the Unknown World before she kills him."
  - The guide spells Bastila "Bastilla" throughout.
- **Kreia:**
  - Asking her whether she is a Jedi or a Sith pays off with "Award: 1,000 XP and movie revelations!" (p. 294).
  - "Did you know Atris at all?" leads to "questions about her training Darth Revan" (p. 294).
  - Asking about the pursuing Sith gets a response that "includes information on Sion, Traya, and Nihilus" (p. 295). This fits the guide's separate Atris-as-Traya account; see [kt-04](kt-04.md).
  - The "lesson of strength" with Hanharr: "Acceptance award: 500 XP, minor influence gain with Kreia, small dark side bonus, +2 STR." Versus "Denial award: 1,000 XP, major influence gain with Kreia" (p. 295).
- **Atton (p. 295):** Atton confesses "to being a deserter", and later speaks of "a Jedi who gave her life for him". He can then be trained as a Jedi Guardian.
- **Bao-Dur (pp. 295–296):** He greets you with "General. Need something?" (p. 295). With very high influence, "he may talk to you about Malachor V. This can eventually lead to an ultimate sacrifice on the part of this Zabrak" (p. 296).
- **Handmaiden (p. 297):** Kreia "believes she shares her the handmaiden's mother's power" (sic). A male PC can then convince the handmaiden to train as a Jedi.
- **Mira (p. 297):** Taking her to the spot of Kreia's Nar Shaddaa vision makes her a Jedi: "A vision grips Mira, and she turns into a Jedi."
- **Hanharr (p. 299):** Hanharr explains why he killed Mira. Pursued further, "unless you know that Hanharr murdered his own people, the conversation stops."
- **G0-T0 (pp. 299–300):** On Onderon under Vaklu, "Onderon will secede from the Republic, denying the ecosystems and life required for the other decaying worlds" (p. 299). The "five pieces of evidence" route exposes Goto as a droid (p. 300).
- **HK-47 (pp. 300–301):** The pacifist package gives a "very bizarre conversation" (p. 300). HK can "mock Revan's previous party members: Carth and Bastilla (if Revan was male) and Carth only (if Revan was female)" (p. 301).
- **Mandalore (p. 301):** With very high influence you learn "Mandalore's real identity". The guide never names him; in the game he is KOTOR I's Canderous Ordo. He says: "You helped me gather the clans together, so I feel I owe it to you to tell you the whole truth about my mission and Revan."
- **The Disciple (pp. 302–303):** His real role: "he's an agent!" (p. 303). He also "reveals he used to be a Padawan" (p. 303).
- **Visas (pp. 303–304):** "Regardless of whether you are male or female, Visas Marr forms a romantic attachment to you" (p. 303). She teaches "Force Sight" (p. 304).
- **Extras (p. 304):**
  - Third playthrough: "Complete the game with a light side and a dark side character, and on your third play through the game, expect some extra dialog from the party members."
  - Start screen: "After Visas detects your character in any game, the Start Menu changes to feature Darth Nihilus instead of Darth Sion."
  - Visas's sacrifice: Equip her original clothing and a melee weapon, and "you gain the option for her to sacrifice herself, distracting Nihilus long enough for you to finish the battle."
