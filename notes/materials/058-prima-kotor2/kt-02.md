# Prima *KOTOR II* guide, part 2: walkthrough intro, Ebon Hawk prologue, Peragus, Telos, Nar Shaddaa (PDF pp. 54–134)

Source and copy as in [kt-01](kt-01.md). Citations use **PDF pages**; printed page = PDF − 3. The walkthrough is a step-by-step solution with maps, sidebar tips and "Side Step" light/dark boxes. Below are the lore, design and quirk findings, not the button-by-button route.

## Walkthrough introduction (PDF p. 54)
- **Opening crawl:** "It is a perilous time for the galaxy. A brutal civil war has all but destroyed the Jedi Order, leaving the ailing Republic on the verge of collapse." The crawl ends: "the last known Jedi clings to life aboard a battered freighter near the ravaged world of Peragus…"
- **Branching:** "Most events unfold in different ways depending on your class, gender, and alignment." Also, "when you first pick your destination on the Ebon Hawk, you could land in one of five different worlds."
- **Recommended order:** Peragus, Telos, Nar Shaddaa, Dxun, Onderon, Dantooine, Korriban. "You must visit Peragus first and Telos second, but you can do the remaining five in any order."
- **Alignment scale:** "This is reflected on a scale from 1 to 100, with 1 being unadulterated dark side and 100 being absolute light side." The visual cue: "Light side devotees have a healthy glow, while dark side characters appear pale and diseased."
- **Sample Side Step lines:** "How terrible for you, is there any way I can help?" (light) versus "If you want to die, keep talking" (dark).

## Ebon Hawk prologue (PDF pp. 55–58)
- The prologue is optional: "skip the Prologue by heading toward the front of the cockpit." The guide recommends playing it "to find extra items and equipment for T3-M4".
- You play T3-M4 with 3C-FD's help. The three sidequests are "Save the Jedi", "Garage Access" and "Repair 3C-FD". "The Jedi (your character) is found in the med bay in critical condition."

## Peragus (PDF pp. 59–74)
- **Setting (p. 59):** "a maze of tunnels and rooms carved from one of the larger asteroids orbiting the remains of Peragus."
- **Kreia's entrance (p. 65):** "Kreia, the secretive old woman who is destined to be your guide and mentor, has feigned death using a Jedi trance." The med-bay database shows you "were administered a massive dose of sedatives. But by who?"
- **Darth Sion (p. 65):** "Darth Sion, Lord of Pain and new captain of the Harbinger, nearly catches up with you here—but the time to fulfill your destiny is yet to come." Kreia "departs to confront him alone" (p. 72).
- **Setting KOTOR I's canon (p. 66):** "your responses dictate whether Revan (the protagonist of Knights of the Old Republic) was good or evil, male or female." Atton survives "thanks to the Force cage he's imprisoned in".
- **HK-50 (p. 70):** "it is revealed to be HK-50, an assassin droid sent to capture you by powerful crime lords." The dormitory turbolift code is printed as "7 5 13 17 3". HK-50's remains drop "one of four vital pieces needed to activate the secret character HK-47" (p. 71).
- **Destroying Peragus (p. 74):** "risk collision with the asteroids (light side) or deliberately fire on the fuel-packed asteroids, blowing up the entire colony (dark side). Even if you do your best to avoid it, the Harbinger's wayward guns trigger a massive reaction that destroys everything…except your ship."
- **Force Chain (p. 74):** "any Force powers used on one of you affects the other."

## Telos (PDF pp. 75–104)
- **Overview (p. 75):** Lieutenant Grenn detains you, and your ship is stolen. The search ends at "a hidden Jedi academy buried under the polar ice."
- **Citadel Station cast:**
  - Loppak Slusk of the Exchange (p. 77).
  - Czerka's Jana Lorso. "Taking on jobs for her will propel you along the dark side" (p. 77).
  - T1-N1, "a mildly psychotic utility droid" (p. 78). The name also appears as "T-1N1".
  - Ithorian leader Chodo Habat (p. 78).
  - The Dobo brothers (p. 80).
- **Luxa (p. 82):** "As a Zeltron, Luxa has the ability to read your emotions; tricks like Force Persuade won't work on her." She finally "reveals her true colors by confessing that she never knew where your ship was" (p. 94).
- **The cell assassin (p. 87):** he came "to collect the Exchange's bounty on Jedi", and "The assassin, who called himself Batu Rem, is actually an impostor." Docking records show "the assassin came from Nar Shaddaa" (p. 102).
- **Bao-Dur (pp. 83, 95):** "recognizing you as his former general in the Mandalorian Wars, offers to renew his allegiance". He is "a muscular Zabrak with a cybernetic arm".
- **Atris (pp. 87, 99):** She "reveals that she was responsible for stealing the Ebon Hawk." Also, "She has your old lightsaber, but nothing you can say will convince her to give it to you." In the cells, "Kreia bores into Atton's mind and finds dark secrets lurking there" (p. 99).
- **Handmaiden hook (p. 99):** "look for the girl who isn't wearing a hood". The tip: "If she falls for you, her fate becomes tied to yours."
- **Sidequest tone:** "No Jedi should be without their own" Twi'lek dancing girl (p. 103). This is the Ramana/Harra slave quest, and the guide's text layer garbles "Twi'lek" there.
- **Editing slips:**
  - "Batano" and "Batono" are both used for one fugitive (pp. 100–101).
  - Chodo's aide appears as Moxa (p. 88), Mozo (p. 92) and Moza (p. 104).
  - "When Odo gives you the credentials" (p. 92) means Opo Chano.

## Ebon Hawk interlude (PDF pp. 105–106)
- **Exile backstory (p. 105):** "T3-M1 displays a holo record of your trial before the Jedi Council that hints at a hidden motive behind your exile." "T3-M1" is a typo for T3-M4.

- **Ship and handmaiden (pp. 105–106):** The Ebon Hawk is "worth its weight in spice". If you are male, the Handmaiden "stowed away aboard your ship".

## Nar Shaddaa (PDF pp. 107–134)
- **Setup (p. 107):** "The Exchange boss, G0-T0, tracks your arrival and warns the bounty hunters to keep their distance from you, but your friends remain fair game." The search for Zez-Kai Ell is framed this way: "After all, the Jedi Master came here to hide" (p. 116). "There are 14 different methods of causing trouble you can try" (p. 116).
- **Local color:**
  - Geeda the Rodian "actually likes humans (considered a perversion by other Rodians)" (p. 107).
  - Quello: "As a Toydarian, he is immune to Force Persuade" (p. 116).
  - Ratrin Vhek is "the Ebon Hawk's original owner" (p. 108).
  - Kodin sells the HK chassis "for 1,500 credits" (p. 107).
- **Vogga:**
  - "Female characters can gain an audience by donning a slave outfit and dancing for the bloated fiend; male characters can thankfully let the handmaiden do the job" (p. 110).
  - "For an amusing reply, try volunteering Kreia or Visas for the job" (p. 120).
  - "Vogga is obsessed with G0-T0" (p. 120).
- **The Champ (p. 119):** "you must beat the Champ three times–and mind tricks don't count". The Persuade route: "Convince the Champ that winning every match takes all the fun out of the game".
- **Kreia (p. 109):** At the red door, "you sense the powerful Force echoes that reverberate around Nar Shaddaa and permanently gain 20 Force points."
- **Jekk'Jekk Tarr (p. 114):** "a poisonous spa for those species that can survive the toxic (and intoxicating) fumes". Breath Control comes from Kreia's telepathic contact (p. 126).
- **Visas Marr (p. 124):** "If you've held strongly to the light or dark side to this point, Visas Marr will be waiting for you on the Ebon Hawk". After "fending off her half-hearted attack, she offers her life to you and joins your party."
- **Twin Suns (p. 125):** The tip is to pre-mine the cantina, "ending the duel in less than one second". Hiding behind booths "may be considered unsporting". A caption reads "The Twin Suns were a threat. The Zhugs are not."
- **Mira vs Hanharr (p. 127):** "light side players get Mira, while dark side players take the role of Hanharr". A caption says "One of these bounty hunters is departing for the Shadowlands." Hanharr's stat box lists the ability "Wookie".
- **G0-T0's trap (pp. 128–131):**
  - G0-T0 wins by "electrocuting you into submission and taking you on board his orbiting yacht as a hostage…" (p. 128).
  - "There are twelve computer terminals on G0-T0's yacht" (p. 129). The guide prints a binary "count" table (p. 130).
  - Afterward, G0-T0 "has the nerve to join your party!" (p. 131).
- **Zez-Kai Ell (p. 131):** He fights with "his dual-bladed lightsaber". "Whether in friendship or in battle, you learn the lightsaber stance Ataru after meeting with Zez-Kai Ell."
- **Sidequests (pp. 132–134):** The dark option for Geriel: "Persuading Geriel to take his own worthless life, an act of savage mercy that really impresses your companions" (p. 133). Geeda's expanded stock includes "the unique HK pacifist module" (p. 133).
