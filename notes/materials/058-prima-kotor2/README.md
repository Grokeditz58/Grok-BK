## Strongest findings
1. **The guide calls Atris "Darth Traya", and never gives that name to Kreia.** It says Atris "has taken the moniker of Darth Traya" (p. 203), and the fight is headed "DARTH TRAYA BATTLE" (pp. 203–204). Kreia's final fight is just "KREIA BATTLE" (pp. 211–212). In the shipped game, Kreia is Traya, so this is a pre-release variant or a guide error. See [kt-04](kt-04.md).
2. **Kreia's motive, in the guide's words:** "her utter hatred of the Force" … "Only death can free her from this hatred, but it must come at your hands" (p. 212).
3. **Lore in the item text:**
   - The Handmaiden's mother "Errin was a Jedi Knight" (p. 243).
   - "Freedon Nadd trained with (and later destroyed) Naga Sadow's spirit" (p. 220).
   - The exile's band was printed with the raw placeholder "the Jedi <Your Character>" (p. 261).
4. **Revan-dependent content (p. 293):** T3's hologram shows Bastila or Carth depending on Revan's sex and alignment. With a female, evil Revan, it shows evil Revan "before she kills him".
5. **Slips and typos:**
   - The Enclave is "destroyed during the Mandalorian Wars" (p. 159).
   - The form is spelled "Shion" on p. 170 and "Shien" on p. 291.
   - Bastila is spelled "Bastilla" throughout.
   - Smaller typos: "Captian Sullio" (p. 152), "Kriea" (p. 180), "you most follow" (p. 208).
6. **Extras:**
   - The start menu switches from Sion to Nihilus once Visas detects you.
   - Visas can sacrifice herself against Nihilus.
   - A third playthrough unlocks extra dialogue (p. 304).
