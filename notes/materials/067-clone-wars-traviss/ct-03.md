# The Clone Wars (Traviss): notes part 3 (ch. ELEVEN–FIFTEEN: Ventress, Rex's last stand, the escape)

Source: as ct-01 (handle 5ftWhT7C; extracted `cwt.txt`, line refs "l.").

## Ch. ELEVEN (l.2578): Ventress moves in
- Ventress: "Hutts were hard to kill even if you tried" (l.2600).
- Lore: Dooku told her that Ky Narec was abandoned by the Jedi, specifically Mace Windu (l.2620–2622). Her hatred of the Order depends on Dooku's account.
- Anakin senses "something malevolent and dark lurking at a distance" (l.2672) before Ventress shows herself.
- **Ventress cuts a massive monastery door free with one saber**, slicing the frame until the door gives (l.2740). She reasons: "Skywalker would run out of troops before she ran out of droids" (l.2742).

## Ch. TWELVE (l.2749): Rex's last stand
- Rex's trip-wire detonators and his fight against super battle droids and spider droids. Anakin, inside, "felt death, pain, and fear rip through the fabric of the Force" (l.2791).
- **Ventress vs. Rex:** she deflects his sidearm fire immediately, disarms him with the Force and holds him by the throat:
  > "Ventress had grabbed his throat in a stranglehold. She didn't even need to touch him." (l.2875)
- Her mind influence fails on Rex: "Only works on the weak-minded… I ain't" (l.2913). He fakes compliance, and the clones' codeword is "Anakin". This counts as a Traviss data point that clones resist mind tricks, though it's anecdotal.

## Ch. THIRTEEN (l.2926): Force signatures and the vulture duel
- Anakin knows "from disturbances in the Force" that his troopers were slaughtered.
- **Identifying Force-users by signature:**
  > "it wasn't always possible to identify other Force-users simply from the impression they left in the Force" (l.2986)
  Ventress's signature is unmistakable to him: raging pain and "absolutely obsessive hatred", "like looking into the heart of a diamond" (l.2990).
- Teth has insects 3 m long.
- **A walker-mode vulture droid nearly beats Anakin** while he carries Rotta on his back:
  > "A living opponent could be sensed and gauged in the Force, but a droid… a really smart one could give a Jedi a serious run for their creds." (l.3094)
  > "He was close to beaten. He felt it. He was losing." (l.3096)
  Ahsoka swings back up on her fingertips, Force-pulls her saber (l.3114), and cuts the vulture's leg.
- Hutt durability (Anakin's thought): "Rotta was a Hutt, and they were far tougher than any puny human" (l.3090).
- Dooku and Jabba: "I have more firepower in orbit around Teth than the Republic can muster" (l.3178). Jabba wants Skywalker's head. Dooku withholds updates from Sidious (he has an "image to keep intact", l.3234).
- Rex has broken ribs and judges Ventress "Sith, or a dark adept" (l.3260). He rips a droid's head up barehanded and uses his vibroblade.
- Theme line: "one side in this war was making droids more like men, and the other was making men more like droids" (l.3252).

## Ch. FOURTEEN (l.3337): Ventress vs. Ahsoka
- **Ventress's dual-saber cutting:** she cuts through the door with both sabers "simultaneously, sawing a circle" (l.3397). Later she makes "a perfect freehand circle" and Force-pushes the disk out (l.3467–3469).
- Anakin hitches a ride on a 3 m hunting fly that R2 lured by imitating a gunship drive. He steers it with "Force-pull here, Force-push there" (l.3457).
- **The duel (about 45 seconds):** "these matters never lasted long" (l.3487). Ventress disarms Ahsoka with a figure-eight, Force-pushes her flat and holds her down (l.3487–3489). This is a clean win over a 14-year-old Padawan.
- Sensing: Ventress senses Anakin only as "a faint tingle deep behind her eyes" (l.3499). The hunting fly hits her from behind, unsensed. Animals are a Force blind spot like droids.
- The platform collapses over an 800 m drop. Her ship is the *Ginivex*; 4A-7 is the only being she trusts.
- "an army had failed to suppress a company of the 501st Legion—not even battalion strength" (l.3567, Ventress's POV). Rex's six men turn a super battle droid into a walking bomb with thermal detonators ("Republic—one", l.3693). Rex: "Torrent Company," … "Stand to." (l.3729)

## Ch. FIFTEEN (l.3730): the fly, the Twilight, and Kenobi's arrival
- **Anakin holds the hunting fly in a Force grip:** "It took all Anakin's concentration" (l.3743). He tries to calm it through the Force, but its "mind was so alien" (l.3791). He lands it with Force pressure and a "headwind in the Force" (l.3801).
- Hutt biology, as Anakin states it to Ahsoka: "You can't even poison them. They regrow body parts. They can live for a thousand years" (l.3759). This is a character's claim, not narration.
- Restated rule: "droids didn't leave impressions in the Force the way living objects did" (l.3833). Ahsoka still senses hidden droids through danger sense.
- Ahsoka beheads 4A-7. They seize the freighter *Twilight*.
- Rex's square of six faces a flamethrower. The 212th arrives. **Kenobi** jumps from an Interceptor and lands "perfectly, like a gymnast", then "Force-threw it back across the lines" (a droid; l.4017–4021).
- At the end, Rex's six are all that's left of Torrent Company.
