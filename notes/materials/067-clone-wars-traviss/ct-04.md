# The Clone Wars (Traviss): notes part 4 (ch. SIXTEEN–TWENTY: Kenobi vs. Ventress, Tatooine, Anakin vs. Dooku)

Source: as ct-01 (handle 5ftWhT7C; extracted `cwt.txt`, line refs "l.").

## Ch. SIXTEEN (l.4078): Kenobi vs. Ventress
- Ventress on Kenobi: "this is the man who won't train me as a Sith" (l.4128). She is confusing him with Dooku's own reluctance; it's her framing. She senses him as "a brash fanfare of a presence" (l.4138).
- **The duel in the column chamber:**
  - She opens with a scissor attack while thinking "He couldn't take Fett" (l.4178), referring to Kamino. Also: "he's not trying this time".
  - She Force-pushes off a wall and hacks "with all the raw strength she could muster", fuelled by rage over Narec, and knocks Kenobi's saber away. Her blade is at his throat (l.4182–4184).
  - Kenobi answers by Force-throwing her into a column, "hard enough to hear something crack" (l.4190). He recalls his saber and grins.
  - He later stands in the hangar window; to Ventress it is "a gesture of contempt. It said that he could take her any time he liked" (l.4308).
  - **Result:** mixed. She briefly had him disarmed, but he recovered at once and controlled the fight.
- The *Twilight* comes under fire from its own side because of its CIS transponder, and a vulture fireball explodes in the hangar.

## Ch. SEVENTEEN (l.4313)
- Ahsoka jettisons 5 t of water and hangs on by her fingertips. The ship jumps to Tatooine.
- Ventress was "too busy and angry to concentrate on feeling the subtler variations in the Force" (l.4437) but still senses that Skywalker has left the system.
- Kenobi knocks one of her sabers away. She Force-pulls it back and leaps onto a passing vulture to escape (l.4455–4461).
- **Dooku's mind trick:** he "mind-influenced the two servants… to go away and forget" (l.4471).
- **Dooku's empathy:** when Ventress falsely reports Rotta dead, he "felt the Hutt's shock and grief in the Force, like standing too close to a detonation" (l.4511). Galidraan comes back to him.

## Ch. EIGHTEEN (l.4530): MagnaGuards and the Dune Sea
- Dooku: "I have MagnaGuards ready for him" (l.4525–4533 area). Jabba wants "His skull".
- Rotta has an unknown bacterial infection, treated by the med droid TB-2. "Hutts didn't have bones… immensely strong bag of muscle" (l.4676). Rotta might survive a crash that would kill humanoids.
- Two MagnaGuard fighters (Grievous's elite) attack. Anakin downs one with the forward cannon; R2 swings the cannon past its safe arc to down the other. The *Twilight* crash-lands, and Jawas strip it.
- **Palpatine on Yoda:** "So this is where centuries of wisdom—and power—gets you. Oblivious." (l.4688) Also: "the greatest Jedi Master failed to recognize" him (l.4686). Yoda, standing face to face with Sidious, doesn't sense him. Palpatine reads Padmé's reaction and guesses she and Anakin are close.
- Yoda on Anakin: "in dangerous situations, the most likely to succeed" (l.4704).
- Anakin and Ahsoka both sense Dooku coming: "It's the dark side." (l.4790–4792). They split up. Ahsoka takes Rotta and R2 on foot, and Anakin carries a decoy backpack full of rocks.

## Ch. NINETEEN (l.4819): Anakin vs. Dooku
- Dooku "cast around in the Force for Anakin" (l.4867) and tells Ziro to sell Padmé. He tells the captive Padmé: "I'm not a traitor. I was never on your side." (l.4881)
- **Anakin's meditation (l.4891–4905):** his pulse drops and his awareness layers. He can't sense R2 (the droid rule again). He feels Dooku as "precise, targeted, controlled, a firaxa shark" (l.4893) and also Tatooine's accumulated misery. "neither of them needed physical evidence to find one another" (l.4905). They track each other mutually across a desert.
- **The duel (l.4915–4951):**
  - Dooku attacks with Force lightning: "Anakin evaded the bolts and channeled the lightning to his lightsaber" (l.4915). Dooku: "You're making progress."
  - Dooku somersaults over him.
  - Anakin "sent a Force whirlwind of sand sweeping from the dunes… almost knocking him to the ground" (l.4919). This is weather-scale TK used against a Sith Lord.
  - Anakin attacks ferociously, almost blind with fury. "Dooku had been a master duelist, even among Jedi" (l.4925). He ducks under and slashes the backpack "almost to Anakin's spine": "I seem to have cut Rotta in half."
  - Dooku is the decoy, stalling while MagnaGuards chase Ahsoka. Anakin escapes on Dooku's speeder.
  - Anakin's doubt: "Am I really stronger than him, or did he choose to let me escape?" (l.4951). The text leaves it open.
- Rex and Cody's debrief: "lost a quarter of our men before we even started" and "needed battalion strength, not a company". Clone "faith" is "getting thinner the higher it went". Rex: "tinnies really are useless in nonstandard situations and confined spaces" (l.4993). "Tinnies" is clone slang for droids.

## Ch. TWENTY (l.5006): resolution
- Epigraph (Commander Fox): Ziro is in custody and Padmé is freed (l.5009).
- **Anakin in Jabba's court:** he surrenders his saber. When Jabba refuses to listen, "The lightsaber TC-70 was holding flew across the chamber and into the Jedi's grip, and within seconds the Nikto guards had crashed against the wall as if thrown by an invisible hand." (l.5042) He deflects blaster fire and holds the blade to Jabba's throat.
- Ahsoka arrives, battered, with Rotta. Anakin: "MagnaGuards weren't battle droids. Anakin was amazed she'd survived." (l.5102) Her MagnaGuard fight happens off-page, so there are no specifics.
- Jabba "understood Basic just fine" (l.5108). Padmé brokers the deal: Republic use of the Hutt routes, and Jabba wants Dooku brought to justice (l.5140).
- **Sidious, pragmatic:** "Then allow them this victory… It stretches their forces more thinly. It makes them overconfident." (l.5156) Dooku's closing vow: "until Mustafar itself froze as white and cold as Galidraan" (l.5168).
