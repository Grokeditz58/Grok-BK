# The Clone Wars (Traviss): notes part 1 (prologue, ch. ONE–FIVE: setup and Christophsis)

Source: the owner's MEGA epub (handle 5ftWhT7C), Karen Traviss, *Star Wars: The Clone Wars* (Del Rey 2008; ebook v3.1, eISBN 978-0-307-79596-0). Line refs ("l.") point to the extracted text kept outside the repo (`cwt.txt`). This is the novelization of the 2008 theatrical film, Legends continuity, set shortly before the TV series, with Ahsoka newly assigned. The chapters have spelled-out headings (ONE–TWENTY), and each chapter opens with an in-universe epigraph.

## Prologue (Jabba's palace, l.107)
- Hutt lifespan: Jabba "might live to be a thousand years old. No Hutt was in a hurry with a life span like that" (l.152). Hutts reproduce without a partner ("Hutts needed no partner to produce a child").
- Rotta is taken during his daily sail-barge excursion. Teniline granules are traded for hyperdrive use (minor tech lore).

## Ch. ONE (l.189): Dooku and Ziro's framing plot
- Dooku and Jabba's uncle Ziro plan to kidnap Rotta, frame the Jedi, and put Ziro in Jabba's place.
- Dooku's Force sensing at a casual level: "He sensed anxiety; no unusual thing in a Hutt's palace" (l.235). This is ambient empathic reading through doors.
- Dooku speaks Huttese (later: "Dooku's grasp of Huttese", l.2329, which has limits; he can't fully follow Jabba's rage).

## Ch. TWO (l.288): Palpatine and the Council
- Sidious's cover: "the ease with which he moved undetected among them as a Sith Lord made him pause and marvel" (l.304). This feeds the Sith-concealment-vs-Jedi-perception debate. Windu senses that "something" is wrong but nothing specific.
- Palpatine's stated plan is to keep the war balanced, then end it and the Jedi Order at once. The Hutt space lanes are the strategic prize.

## Christophsis (ch. TWO, l.~400–602): Anakin's battle trance
- Anakin jumps off a roof (Rex uses a rappel line) and lands on an octuptarra droid, "just hard enough to balance" on it (l.~474).
- The battle-trance passage (l.482–500) is the book's key Anakin combat-state description:
  > "He was aware of the position of every droid, every clone trooper, but not consciously" (l.482)
  > "Some veered away at angles, deflected not by his lightsaber but an instinctive and unthinking Force push." / "ripping off its head with a Force-assisted stranglehold." (l.484)
  > "He swung his blade through metal bodies as easily as if he were cutting grass." (l.500)
- Psychology: he flashes back to the Tuskens and to Ke Daiv, the Blood Carver he killed years earlier. He tells himself "It's not darkness." / "I'm not dark." / "This isn't anger". The troopers "felt unlike him in the Force, devoid only of that singular crazed frenzy, that throat-closing … rage" (l.500). Traviss frames his peak output as rage-fuelled.
- Obi-Wan takes out two droids in a single sweep and is "invigorated by a fight". About a dozen troopers fall; the text notes weak points at plastoid armor joints.
- Ahsoka arrives by shuttle (l.596–602).

## Ch. THREE (l.603): Jabba and Dooku
- Jabba on Hutt toughness: "Most poisons didn't work on them" (l.641). Jabba executes the sail-barge crew. Slicers trace Rotta through Hutt-illness records (GASSH lookups, l.643) to Teth.
- **Dooku's Galidraan flashback** (l.~700–740): the Jedi massacred Mandalorians on behalf of a corrupt governor, and this is framed as his turning point. Key lines: "What have we done?" (l.737) and Yoda-era training's "Don't think, Padawan Dooku". He reflects on "millions of troops cloned from Fett" (l.755) and on Jango's "formidable body". "Make it happen soon, Darth Sidious" (l.713).
- He calls Ventress, who is stationed at the Teth monastery (l.763).

## Ch. FOUR (l.764): Ahsoka, Rex, and the shield
- Ages: Ahsoka says "I'm fourteen" (l.806), Rex "I'm ten" (l.808), and Anakin is 20 (l.1064, restated l.2825).
- On the Council's reluctance about Anakin: "this terrible, galaxy-changing power of his" (l.868).
- Togruta senses (Rex's POV): "her head eerily steady—unnaturally steady… still a predator, a fast and precise hunter" (l.970); "she'd have terrific long-distance vision" (l.976).
- The Separatist energy-field dome: "Cannon won't penetrate that" (l.986).
- Anakin's empathic reads: on Rex, "He felt it: a kind of sad guilt" (l.1024). He also reads Ahsoka's fear by testing "the eddies in the Force around her" (l.~1060).
- Plan: "let their lines cross over us" (l.1090). The Jedi go under the moving shield to the generator.

## Ch. FIVE (l.1091): under the shield
- "The stream of cannon fire hit the energy shield and simply spilled off it like white-hot liquid." (l.1101)
- Rex believes "Kenobi would certainly sense if they got killed" (l.1129).
- Palpatine "understood Huttese" but feigns ignorance with TC-70 (l.1155). He thinks "Jabba might not have been in Palpatine's own league—was anyone?" (l.1165) and calls it a "meticulously planned war in which he controlled both sides" (l.1185).
- Anakin finds the shield generator "by a combination of his Force senses and the odd infrasonics" (l.1217).
- Droideka: it can't fight and move at once, so the pair let it roll between them and cut it apart before it deploys (l.1237–1245). This is a tactical, not raw-power, feat.
