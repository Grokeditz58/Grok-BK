# The Clone Wars (Traviss): summary of strongest findings

Source: Karen Traviss, *Star Wars: The Clone Wars* (Del Rey 2008; ebook v3.1, eISBN 978-0-307-79596-0). This is the novelization of the 2008 film, Legends continuity. Hand-read in full on 2026-11-03: the unnumbered opening, chapters ONE–TWENTY with their epigraphs, and all back matter. Citations are "CW ch. N, l." against the extracted text, which is kept outside the repo.

1. **Anakin's largest TK feat: a burning AT-TE thrown into the droid lines.**
   > "He sent the wreckage skidding across the ground with a massive Force push. The kinetic force of the impact and the sheet of flame released when it slammed into the droid ranks had the effect of a bomb going off." (CW ch. TEN, l.2525)
   - On the Teth cliff top: "Anakin swept a dozen of them aside with a raw surge of Force power." His stated limit: "the surge of Force power almost squeezed the air from his lungs in his all-out effort to crush the next rank of droids, he felt suddenly light-headed" (ch. EIGHT, l.1811, l.1833).
   - He hops between STAPs: "He didn't even need to draw his lightsaber." (l.1803). He opens a massive cell door "with a Force push to leave his hands free" (l.2092).
2. **Anakin vs. Dooku on Tatooine: lightning redirected, a sand whirlwind, and Dooku still the better duelist.**
   > "Anakin evaded the bolts and channeled the lightning to his lightsaber" / "You're making progress." (CW ch. NINETEEN, l.4915)
   > Anakin "sent a Force whirlwind of sand sweeping from the dunes" (l.4919)
   - But "Dooku had been a master duelist, even among Jedi". He ducks Anakin's ferocious attack and slashes the backpack "almost to Anakin's spine" (l.4925). Dooku was a stalling decoy. The text leaves the outcome open: "Am I really stronger than him, or did he choose to let me escape?" (l.4951)
   - Mutual Force-tracking across a desert: Anakin feels Dooku as "precise, targeted, controlled, a firaxa shark"; "neither of them needed physical evidence to find one another" (l.4893, l.4905).
3. **Sidious's concealment beats Yoda face to face.**
   > "the ease with which he moved undetected among them as a Sith Lord made him pause and marvel" (ch. TWO, l.304)
   > "So this is where centuries of wisdom—and power—gets you. Oblivious." (ch. EIGHTEEN, l.4688)
   - Dooku's epigraph on Yoda: "No being can wield that kind of power for centuries without becoming complacent at best or corrupt at worst." (ch. NINE, l.1953)
4. **Ventress: strong against clones and a Padawan, mixed against Kenobi.**
   - She Force-chokes Rex: "She didn't even need to touch him." (l.2875) Her mind influence fails on him ("Only works on the weak-minded… I ain't", l.2913).
   - She beats Ahsoka in about 45 seconds ("these matters never lasted long", l.3487): a figure-eight disarm, then a Force push that pins her (l.3487–3489). She cuts a "perfect freehand circle" through a door with both blades (l.3467).
   - **Against Kenobi** (ch. SIXTEEN), she briefly disarms him with a wall-launched, rage-driven assault, blade at his throat (l.4182–4184). He Force-throws her into a column, "hard enough to hear something crack" (l.4190), recalls his saber and grins. To her, his stance says "he could take her any time he liked" (l.4308). She flees on a vulture (ch. SEVENTEEN).
   - Her Force sense is limited: she reads Anakin only as "a faint tingle deep behind her eyes", and an animal blindsides her (l.3499).
5. **Traviss's sensing rules: droids (and alien animals) are Force blind spots.**
   > "A living opponent could be sensed and gauged in the Force, but a droid… a really smart one could give a Jedi a serious run for their creds." / "He was close to beaten." (ch. THIRTEEN, l.3094–3096, against a vulture droid in walker mode)
   - "droids didn't leave impressions in the Force the way living objects did" (l.3833). Anakin can't sense R2 even in deep meditation (ch. NINETEEN). A 3 m hunting fly's "mind was so alien" he can't calm it, though he holds it in a Force grip that "took all Anakin's concentration" (l.3791; grip l.3743).
   - Force signatures aren't always identifiable: "it wasn't always possible to identify other Force-users simply from the impression they left in the Force", but Ventress's hatred is "like looking into the heart of a diamond" (l.2986, l.2990).
6. **Anakin's Christophsis battle trance, which runs on rage.**
   > "He was aware of the position of every droid, every clone trooper, but not consciously" / "an instinctive and unthinking Force push" / "a Force-assisted stranglehold" / "as easily as if he were cutting grass" (ch. TWO, l.482–500)
   - The Force reads it as "that singular crazed frenzy, that throat-closing … rage" (l.500). Rex calls it "killing-machine mode. He was unstoppable" (l.1757).

Other data:
- Ahsoka (14) Force-pulls a wall onto Anakin and the droids, trusting Togruta "visuospatial awareness" to clear him through the window gap (l.1382). She Force-pushes a droid "like a shock-ball" (l.1324).
- Kenobi's Force push: "as if punched by an invisible fist" (l.1278). He lands "like a gymnast" and Force-throws a droid back across the lines (l.4017–4021).
- In Jabba's court, Anakin's saber flies to him and "the Nikto guards had crashed against the wall as if thrown by an invisible hand" (l.5042).
- Dooku mind-wipes servants ("go away and forget", l.4471). He feels Jabba's grief "like standing too close to a detonation" (l.4511). Lore: the Galidraan massacre was his turning point (l.735–757).
- A Separatist energy-field dome sheds artillery: "spilled off it like white-hot liquid" (l.1101).
- Hutt biology: Jabba's "Most poisons didn't work on them" (l.641), "Hutts didn't have bones… immensely strong bag of muscle" (l.4676), and Anakin's claim "They regrow body parts. They can live for a thousand years" (l.3759).
- Clone attrition: Torrent Company is ground down from "Forty-two men remaining" (l.2557) to six (ch. FIFTEEN). "an army had failed to suppress a company of the 501st Legion" (l.3567).
