# The Clone Wars (Traviss): notes part 2 (ch. SIX–TEN: shield generator, Teth assault, the frame-up)

Source: as ct-01 (handle 5ftWhT7C; extracted `cwt.txt`, line refs "l.").

## Ch. SIX (l.1256): Christophsis climax
- Epigraph (Ventress): the Jedi abandoned Rattatak and her Master Ky Narec (l.1261).
- **Obi-Wan's Force push:** a battle droid holding a trooper "fell back as if punched by an invisible fist—Rex knew the Force in action when he saw it" (l.1278). Kenobi then holds the line alone against droids and a tank to buy time ("I'll slow these clankers down", l.1288).
- **Ahsoka's TK:** she "Force-pushed another away from her like a shock-ball" (l.1324). Then she **Force-pulls a bombed-out wall down onto Anakin and a crowd of sentry droids**. Anakin survives in the window gap and the droids are crushed (l.1350–1382). Her explanation: "We've got much better visuospatial awareness than humans. I knew the window gap would clear you" (l.1382). This is a precision structural pull by a fresh Padawan.
- Anakin's trance again, mortal framing: "I can think that I might have to die." / "And I can die like any man." (l.1332–1340). He holds off "an orange sea of droids", but "in these numbers their persistence would eventually overwhelm even a Jedi" (l.1346).
- When the shield falls, the 501st is "ludicrously outnumbered… hundreds to one" (l.1444). Yoda's fleet breaks the blockade, and Loathsom surrenders to Kenobi.

## Ch. SEVEN (l.1457): mission to Teth
- Ahsoka: "Can't you feel their pain?" (l.1495). She has exceptional peripheral vision (l.1505).
- Yoda threatens to reassign her to Obi-Wan. Anakin resents that the Jedi never freed Shmi. On the Tuskens: "He didn't feel guilt about it at all." (l.1595)
- **Jabba's view of Jedi:** "The mystic side of them made him wary, because he could never get the measure of their physical limits." (l.1613) His top bounty hunters come back as a crate of heads ("the finest bounty hunters creds could hire", l.1605). Ventress's droids killed them.
- Jabba gives the Republic "one planetary rotation… A Tatooine rotation" (l.1643).

## Ch. EIGHT (l.1716): the cliff assault on the Teth monastery
- Epigraph (Sidious to Dooku): the Council is "storing up trouble" by alienating its Chosen One (l.1721).
- Rex on Anakin's pre-combat trance: "he came out of it in what Rex thought of as his killing-machine mode. He was unstoppable, all lethal movement" (l.1757).
- AT-TEs climb a vertical cliff ("as near to a last-resort deployment as he'd seen", l.1765). One falls through the climbing troopers.
- **Anakin's STAP-hopping:** he leaps from the cliff onto droid STAPs, one after another. "The sheer inertia of his body sent the droid plummeting. He didn't even need to draw his lightsaber." (l.1803) He then strafes the cliff top with a captured STAP's cannon to clear a lane for his troopers.
- **Raw Force output:**
  > "Anakin swept a dozen of them aside with a raw surge of Force power." (l.1811)
  > "He had the strength and skill to wipe out legions of droids, but he hadn't used it for the person who mattered most." (l.1827)
  > "As the surge of Force power almost squeezed the air from his lungs in his all-out effort to crush the next rank of droids, he felt suddenly light-headed" (l.1833)
  This states his limit outright: all-out TK makes him light-headed.
- Sensing limits: he "couldn't even sense her in the maelstrom of Force disturbances churned up by desperate combat" (l.1835). He shields himself with the Force from destroyer-droid shrapnel (l.1849).
- He is exhausted when three droidekas arrive, and Ahsoka's AT-TE saves him. Losses: "He'd lost nearly half his men." (l.1859)
- **Ventress staged the fight:** "He'd encountered just enough resistance… without the droids getting lucky and actually killing the Jedi… She'd had to balance the battle carefully." (l.1883) She is willing to kill the Huttlet. "Rattatak had broken her long, long before she met him" (l.1935).
- Dooku: "The galaxy will be torn apart by orphans… everyone I know who has the potential to bring down empires has been robbed of parents." (l.1941)

## Ch. NINE (l.1948): inside the monastery
- Epigraph (Dooku on Yoda): "No being can wield that kind of power for centuries without becoming complacent at best or corrupt at worst." (l.1953)
- Anakin navigates in the dark: "I can sense my way" (l.1970). Togrutas have good low-light vision.
- **Droid blind spot (a recurring Traviss rule):** "Anakin couldn't sense droids as entities in the same way that he could feel organic beings, but his Force sense of danger knew something was wrong." (l.2034)
- Ahsoka destroys two battle droids without a sound: "She really could be a silent hunter." (l.2052)
- Anakin opens a massive cell door "with a Force push to leave his hands free" (l.2092). He calms Rotta in Huttese.
- Rotta is gravely ill: "Dooku was much, much subtler than even Anakin had imagined." (l.2202)
- Jabba on Dooku: "I hate beings who can't be bought." (l.2218)

## Ch. TEN (l.2253): forged evidence and the courtyard siege
- 4A-7 splices the holocam footage so Anakin seems to say he hates Hutts and wants to "destroy them" (l.2287). Jabba promises the CIS "full access to Hutt space" (l.2359).
- Dooku's motives are sincere. He "wasn't in this to grow wealthier" and calls the Republic "a dictatorship" (l.2353).
- A Separatist landing ship and a vulture squadron hit the courtyard. Anakin: "I can sense rounds coming, Rex." (l.2451) He runs "trusting his Force senses to steer him between the strafing runs of the vulture fighters" (l.2503).
- **Biggest single TK feat in the book:** after spider droids knock out an AT-TE, "He sent the wreckage skidding across the ground with a massive Force push. The kinetic force of the impact and the sheet of flame released when it slammed into the droid ranks had the effect of a bomb going off." (l.2525) An AT-TE weighs on the order of tens of tons (the mass isn't given in the text).
- Rex fires into the door controls and the portcullis slab drops (l.2537). "Forty-two men remaining" (l.2557). Anakin thinks "Three-quarters of my men dead. For a Hutt." (l.2561) The two figures are rough and don't strictly agree. The troopers wire themselves with ordnance.
