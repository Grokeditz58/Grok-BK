## Front matter
- Dedication: "For the A Team at PCC," / "now scattered to the four winds".
- The acknowledgments thank Shelly Shapiro (Del Rey), Sue Rostoni (Lucasfilm), Russ Galen, Jim Gilmer and Karen Miller, among others. They contain no lore.
- The contents list Chapters One–Twenty. The opening Jabba's-palace section (l.107–188) has no TOC entry; these notes call it the prologue.

## Structure (hand-verified)
- Unnumbered opening plus chapters ONE–TWENTY. Each chapter opens with a short in-universe epigraph attributed to a character, for example Palpatine (ch. ONE), Ventress on Ky Narec (ch. SIX), Sidious (ch. EIGHT), Dooku on Yoda (ch. NINE), Kom'rk/Mando'a (ch. FOUR) and Commander Fox (ch. TWENTY). The epigraphs carry lore in their own right; the notes cite them where relevant.
- Arcs: Christophsis (opening–ch. SIX) → Teth (ch. SEVEN–FIFTEEN) → escape and Tatooine (ch. SIXTEEN–TWENTY). This is the novelization of the 2008 theatrical film, with added POV material (Rex, Jabba, Dooku's Galidraan memories, Ventress's Rattatak backstory, Palpatine's interiority).

## Back matter (read and skimmed; l.5169–7689)
- About the Author and the "BY KAREN TRAVISS" list (Republic Commando, Legacy of the Force, The Clone Wars, No Prisoners, Gears of War, Wess'har Wars).
- "STAR WARS—The Expanded Universe" promo page (EU intro; it mentions Darth Bane's Rule of Two).
- **Wild Space excerpt (Karen Miller), chapter ONE "THEN: THE BATTLE OF GEONOSIS, AFTERMATH" (l.5228–5415).** This is the next book in the queue (068). Data points:
  - Yoda: "Too old am I to be the last hope of the Jedi."
  - Vokara Che diagnoses Padmé's injuries by Force-sense without touching her ("I'm not even touching you and I can feel your discomfort").
  - Yoda on Anakin and Obi-Wan: "Together they appeared invincible. Or they had … until Geonosis."
  The excerpt is read here; full notes belong to 068.
- Era intro pages (Old Republic, Rise of the Empire, Rebellion, New Republic, New Jedi Order, Legacy), each followed by a first-chapter excerpt. The excerpts carry no titles in the text (only the era intro's recommendation list); I skimmed them and identified each one by its content:
  - *The Old Republic: Fatal Alliance* (Sean Williams), ch. 1: Shigar Konshi and Larin Moxla against the Mandalorian Dao Stryver. Shigar Force-pushes him into a wall, then "Only with the greatest exertion of the Force that Shigar could summon" deflects falling monorail spears; Stryver throws him by hand. This is non-Clone Wars era, so it is only noted.
  - *Republic Commando: Hard Contact* (Traviss), ch. 1: Darman, Etain, Niner and Fi after Geonosis. Etain can't manage a mind trick under panic ("try as she might to concentrate"). The Geonosis casevac signal gives "TWELVE THOUSAND" serious injuries and "SEVENTY-TWO THOUSAND" combat-fit troops.
  - *Luke Skywalker and the Shadows of Mindor* (Stover), the Lando/Taspan opening.
  - *X-Wing: Rogue Squadron* (Stackpole), ch. 1.
  - *The New Jedi Order: Vector Prime* (Salvatore), ch. 1 "Fraying Fabric": Leia, Mara and Jaina; Nom Anor "had nothing to do with the Force".
  - *Legacy of the Force: Betrayal* (Allston), ch. 1: Luke's Vader dream; Jacen and Ben on Adumar, with Jacen batting blaster bolts back at troopers.
  - *Fate of the Jedi: Outcast* (Allston), opening: Valin Horn and the "Not-Mirax" double.
- The Star Wars Novels Timeline, which runs through Fate of the Jedi.
- **No power-scaling content in the back matter bears on the Clone Wars cast**, apart from the Wild Space excerpt, which 068 covers in full.
