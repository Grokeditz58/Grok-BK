# Jedi Apprentice 2: The Dark Rival: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-20 CST. Every line of READ l.1–817 was read in order (ch. 1–19). Notes are limited to scaling-relevant material (owner's rulings, 2026-11-20). The quotes are in dr-01 and dr-02; this summary is in our own words.
- Author: Jude Watson. Second Jedi Apprentice book (Scholastic, 1999). It follows straight on from The Rising Force on Bandomeer. Cites are DR ch. N, with no page numbers.

## Plot
- **Bandomeer (ch. 1–5):** Qui-Gon has a recurring nightmare about K-7, Core 5 and a broken circle. Governor SonTag never asked for a Jedi. Qui-Gon sends Obi-Wan to the Agri-Corps. Xanatos turns up as Offworld's charming representative, and the Home Planet Mine's main building blows up. The meeting had been moved at the last minute, which saved them all. Xanatos pays for the rebuilding.
- **Clues (ch. 6–9):** Obi-Wan opens a hidden annex in the Agri-Corps dome with the Force and finds Offworld explosives and a broken-circle box. VeerTa tells Qui-Gon about a secret ionite vein. Xanatos works on Obi-Wan's resentment, and Obi-Wan is knocked out fighting Offworld guards. Qui-Gon mind-tricks his way into Xanatos's hidden office, guesses the password "Crion", and realizes Xanatos controls Offworld.
- **Captivity and the duel (ch. 10–13):** Yoda advises patience. Obi-Wan is enslaved on a deepsea platform with an electro-collar, alongside the Phindian Guerra. Qui-Gon duels Xanatos in a mineyard and wounds his shoulder. Xanatos draws Obi-Wan's saber as a second blade, but Qui-Gon takes it from him and runs to find the boy. Obi-Wan mind-tricks an Imbat guard, is betrayed by Guerra, and is sentenced to be thrown overboard.
- **Rescue (ch. 14–15):** Guerra's sling and both Jedi's Force catch Obi-Wan's fall. Qui-Gon neutralizes the collar, and they fight their way off the platform. Qui-Gon tells the story of Telos: Crion's war, Qui-Gon killing Crion, Xanatos branding his cheek with the broken ring, and their duel to exhaustion.
- **The mine (ch. 16–18):** Qui-Gon frees the platform slaves. Xanatos chases them on a laser-cannon speeder bike into the mine. Obi-Wan burns his hand, and they fight Xanatos in the dark until their Force energies merge and drive him off. Sealed in, Obi-Wan offers to blow the door with his collar. His Force will astonishes Qui-Gon, who closes the broken circle on the lock with the Force. At the master ion bomb, Obi-Wan's ionite stops the clock so Qui-Gon can disarm it.
- **Ending (ch. 19):** VeerTa is exposed as Xanatos's informant, and Xanatos escapes, leaving a note. Qui-Gon accepts Obi-Wan as his Padawan.

## Strongest findings
- **Xanatos:** "mastery of the lightsaber was unsurpassed in his class. His ability to focus on the Force matched his Master's." After leaving the Order he "had only grown more powerful". Qui-Gon cannot tire him. He is "faster than any fighter Obi-Wan had met", flips over both Jedi, and fights blind ("best at the blindfold test"). He dual-wields briefly. Anti-feats: weak footwork, overconfidence, wounded shoulder and hand, driven back by the two Jedi's melded Force, and twice he flees.
- **Qui-Gon:** an evenly matched duel with Xanatos, a shoulder hit, and taking away the second saber. A mind trick on a Hutt. Force transparency on Telosian walls and the circle lock (with Obi-Wan's power). He neutralizes a collar's charge, anticipates cannon fire with the Force while driving, fights blind, and disarms three Imbats instantly. Limits: he cannot open the stronger lock or cut the collar, the ion trigger is too delicate for the Force, and he lost focus when the second saber appeared.
- **Obi-Wan:** turns walls transparent with the Force, mind-tricks an Imbat ("lacks the power to alter physical objects"), and steers his fall with the Force. He leaps a rolling cart to burn Xanatos's hand. His Force will "like a breaking wave" astonishes Qui-Gon, and he works out the ionite trick. Limits: fails for two days against the collar, and is beaten down by electro-jabbers.
- **New file:** Guerra (long rubbery arms, the sling rescue).
