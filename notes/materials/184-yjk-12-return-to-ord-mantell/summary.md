# Young Jedi Knights 12: Return to Ord Mantell: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-20 CST. Every line of READ l.1–1676 was read in order: the front matter and the whole story. The epub has no back matter. Notes are limited to scaling-relevant material (owner's rulings, 2026-11-20). The quotes are in rm-01 and rm-02; this summary is in our own words.
- Authors: Kevin J. Anderson & Rebecca Moesta. Twelfth Young Jedi Knights book (Boulevard Books, 1998). It opens the 'Under a Black Sun' arc. **The source has no chapter headings, so citations use the epub's 83 section breaks (RM sec. N).**

## Plot
- **Opening (sec. 1–12):** On Yavin 4, Zekk trains with a staff and the Force against a tentacle tree; he has given up his lightsaber. Kyp Durron and Streen visit. Han invites the Solo children to Ord Mantell, where he is Grand Marshal of the Blockade Runners Derby, and tells how he once beat the smuggler Czethros there.
- **Ord Mantell (sec. 13–27):** Czethros, now a 'businessman' with a cyber-visor, greets Han. On the Falcon's pace-craft run the children sense danger just before a hidden space-mine field. In the race, Zekk, Tenel Ka and Lowie win in the Rock Dragon with Force-guided flying. Chameleon creatures bearing a Black Sun-like mark steal the mine evidence and fight the friends. The berserk lightsaber fighter Anja Gallandro joins the fight, then accuses Han of murdering her father.
- **The plot (sec. 31–36):** Anja is Czethros's andris-spice-dependent agent, and Czethros is rebuilding Black Sun. Han explains that Gallandro died in Xim's vault trap. Han takes the young Jedi Knights and Anja on a fact-finding mission to Anobis, a world in a decades-long civil war between farmers and miners.
- **Anobis (sec. 37–72):** The friends intercept Lilmit, who runs Black Sun's guns to both sides. In the farm village, a knaar stampede drives everyone through a minefield; Jacen maps safe paths through the Force and turns the pack back. The forest is full of traps. The mountain miners take the farmers hostage. Anja and Protas raid the empty farm village, and Protas dies on Anja's own mine. Grief and the arms-dealing revelation bring Elis and Ynos to a cease-fire.
- **Clearing the traps and ending (sec. 72–83):** Jacen and Zekk disarm sonic punchers with telekinesis. Lowie and Tenel Ka clear the forest traps. Jaina and Anakin sense the field mines while the Falcon and the Rock Dragon (with Kyp, Streen, Lusa and Raynar) crush them with deflector shields. Anja accepts an invitation to the Jedi academy but secretly reports to Czethros that she will win the Jedi's trust there.

## Strongest scaling findings
- **Jacen:** maps a whole minefield by sensing the burrowers' movements and leads villagers through it. He senses the knaar pack's territorial unease and sends it home with a mental nudge. He freezes a triggered sonic puncher's mechanism with TK, and has repeated danger premonitions.
- **Zekk:** wins the Derby by Force-guided flying in an unmodified ship, with an unbeatable time. He disarms a triggered sonic puncher by ripping out its circuits through the Force, and shows blaster marksmanship (stunning camouflaged creatures, shooting from the gun well).
- **Jaina:** her Force-sensed docking of the Falcon; senses the field mines; shoulder-to-ribcage cut through a knaar; leads the minefield retreat.
- **Tenel Ka:** senses the camouflaged intruders; runs 'like a hunting cat'; clears the forest traps.
- **Lowbacca:** spots monofilament wire, severs a knaar at the waist, and tears trap controls from a tree barehanded.
- **Anja Gallandro** (new): no Jedi training, an ancient lightsaber, and andris-spice boosts to senses, reflexes and fighting ability. She outruns knaars and lops off a knaar's forearms. Limits: berserker frenzy and spice dependence.
- **Anakin Solo:** 'uncanny' prediction and pattern-reading, and sensing of the detonators.
