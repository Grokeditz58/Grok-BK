# EGPM 1998 — notes150: Ammuud → Ando (printed 14–17 / scan 028–031)

## Printed 14–15 / scan 028–029 — AMMUUD
- **Stats:** Kumasi system; mountains; Humans; Basic; Glayyd clan stronghold. Corporate Sector; settled >10kya from Thokos.
- Seven colony ships → seven clans (Reesbon, Tikeris, Owphrin, Melchett, Almowri, Odoon, Glayyd); hereditary patriarch = **Mor**. Millennia of clan war → Code of Ammuud (~100 BBY): death duels replace wars. CSA subcontract kept local laws.
- Han/Chewie + **Fiolla** (CSA) chase slavery ring; Mor Glayyd clearing slavers; Han stands in for Mor under code; **Gallandro** withdraws after speed-draw demo. Chewie hangs-glides pterosaur carcass from grazer stampede. Slavers poisoned prior Mor; evidence shuts ring; +10k credits.
- Art: Mor Glayyd & Mor Reesbon; pterosaurs.

## Printed 16–17 / scan 030–031 — ANDO
- **Stats:** Ando system; seas, small islands; Aqualish; Andoan; Quantill City, offshore fishing platforms.
- Needlessly belligerent (Ponda Baba/Mos Eisley). Two breeds: **Quara** (fingered, ~10%, islands) vs **Aquala** (fins, rafts). Mineral-fish secrete ore shells; overfishing → fratricidal war → Duros(?) scout ship reverse-engineered → failed conquest of Republic → demilitarized. Offworld as strikebreakers/bill collectors. Evazan brain-switch plot on Ando: senator trapped in Ponda’s body; Ponda vanishes; Evazan flees.
- Art: Quara+Aquala; Andoan mineral-fish.
