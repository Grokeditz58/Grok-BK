## Introduction (paraphrase; edition-specific)
- Opens with theatrical-preview “epic of heroes, villains, and aliens from a thousand worlds.”
- Films spotlight few climates (Tatooine desert, Hoth ice, Dagobah mudhole, Endor forest moon); EU expands Dantooine/Kessel/Taanab etc.
- Covers **100** notable planets/moons Abregado-rae→Zhar; coded bibliography at back. “Enjoy the bus tour, and don’t feed the Wookiees.” — Daniel Wallace, Detroit.
