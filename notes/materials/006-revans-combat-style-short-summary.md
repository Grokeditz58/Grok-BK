## Thesis

The author sets out to explore "(A) Revan's combat style and (B) Revan's proficiency at combat", using his military strategy as evidence. Revan's military genius "is explicitly translatable to his personal combat abilities". He should therefore be "one of the best pure fighters in the verse". His style is "very aggressive yet subtle", "all about deception and sacrifice". Resurrected Revan combines immortality and teleportation with that style.

## Arguments (numbered)

1. **(I) Military tactics are relevant to personal combat.**
   - In the KOTOR Star Map Q&A, the "correct" Darth Revan answers sacrifice a city and let attacks happen. Even when Revan answers otherwise, the computer's "Secondary scans during battle have revealed much".
   - The Handmaiden (KOTOR 2): "Stance, form, discipline are a means of expression and communication." Revan "spoke through battle and tactics". On Echani battle precognition: "Echani battles are fought several minutes in advance… Only Revan ever demonstrated such skill in war".
   - The author's conclusion: Revan can "anticipate duels many minutes in advance". This is FORUM OPINION and an inference.
2. **(II) Revan's military genius.**
   - Canderous: "the greatest single warrior the Republic has ever known".
   - Dorak: "won victory after victory".
   - Kreia: Revan marked the war's beginning and end.
   - In SoR, Marr calls the Revanites the "fiercest adversaries we've ever faced". The battle was described as "conflict on a scale we've never seen". The Outlander: "Have to hand it to you, Revan --you do know how to strategize."
   - Yavin reaction lines from Satele, Ashara, Aric and Torian.
3. **(III) The characteristics of his tactics.**
   - Canderous: "Feints, counterattacks, mass deceptions. Revan was a genius on the field." Mandalore was "taken aback by the ferocity, the tenacity and the subtlety of Revan's plans."
   - HK-47: "Revan was a human capable of subtle, intelligent cruelties."
   - Revan Reborn codex (SWTOR): Revan "was willing to sacrifice his own troops".
   - Kreia: "the Mandalorians taught the Jedi much through battle".
   - Mandalorian Wars codex: the "gradual adoption of Mandalorian tactics".
   - Summary list: feints and counter-attacks; ferocious and tenacious; subtle; accepts sacrifices; Mandalorian aggression.
4. **(IV) How other works abstract his combat style.**
   - **Star Wars Miniatures (WotC previews):** Triple Attack, Lightsaber Duelist, Master Tactician. "Only Count Dooku (Clone Strike) and Darth Revan (The Force Unleashed) manage a higher Defense against these foes."
   - **Galaxy of Heroes (dev team):** "ruthless", "very aggressive", "an exceptional strategist, master fighter, and a ruthless Sith Lord". The Ferocity mechanic trades defence for offence.
   - **SWTOR: Shadow of Revan:** Revan is the only prominent NPC with Force Speed, at "400% movement speed" (B003, B004). The author reads it as a possible "game representation of battle precognition".
   - In the SoR trailer, Revan takes Satele's lightsaber strike without breaking stride (video).
   - He uses teleport-then-Force-grapple tactics. A *Crucible* Luke-choke passage illustrates why a sudden choke from an unseen position is dangerous.
5. **(V) Conclusion.** Chris Avellone: "Revan's a master tactician, and he's kind of a genius, and an incredible lightsaber wielder."

## Common rebuttals / weak points (ANALYSIS — not from the thread)

- **Strategy ≠ dueling.** Military genius does not establish lightsaber skill. The Handmaiden's Echani line concerns "skill in war".
- **Gameplay abstractions** (Miniatures stats, GoH kits, a 400% speed buff) are mechanics. Their canon weight is disputed.
- **"Anticipate duels many minutes in advance"** is the author's extrapolation from Echani lore.
- **The Crucible passage is about Luke**, not Revan. It only illustrates a tactic.
- **Accolades are mostly about the Mandalorian Wars era.** They don't directly measure Reborn/SoR Revan against other top-tier duellists.

## Characters touched and implied scaling (FORUM OPINION)

- **Revan:** among the best pure fighters, with Defense tied with or just below Dooku (Miniatures).
- **Malak:** described as "more like an angry club" (HK-47); implied inferior in subtlety.
- **Dooku (Clone Strike):** a Miniatures defensive peer.

**A1. Source as labelled by blogger: KOTOR, DS question-and-answer** (raw thread.txt line 19)

> Star Map computer: Hypothetical: you are at war. Deciphering an intercepted code, you learn two things about your enemy. A single spot in their defense will be at its weakest in ten days, and they will attack one of your cities in five days. What do you do with this information? What is the most efficient course of action?
> Darth Revan: I prepare my forces to attack in ten days. I do nothing in the city.
> Star Map computer: Very good. If you had moved to evacuate the city, you would have alerted the enemy to their lost codes.
> Carth Onasi: You mean you'd just let all those people die? That's monstrous!
> Mission Vao: I can't believe you'd just let all those people die!

> Darth Revan: That's right. The deaths of those people would spur my forces on.
> Star Map computer: Very good reasoning. I will apply it to the rest of the evaluation. You begin to match the pattern in memory. Hypothetical: remove the ongoing war from the previous example. Consider enemy states to be weak and remote. With no external threat, your empire stagnates. Your people become complacent and begin to question you. Same scenario as before; you discover an impending attack, but also a weakness that will come after. How do you react?
> Darth Revan: I let the attack happen.
> Star Map computer: Of course you do. It makes the most strategic sense. Your people will rally beneath you against the common foe. As their eyes turn outward, your rule will strengthen. The trappings of war grant many opportunities. You have matched the pattern in memory. I recognize you, and will fulfill my designated function.

**A17. Source as labelled by blogger: Galaxy of Heroes Dev Team** (raw thread.txt line 237)

> **Darth Revan is ruthless Sith Lord that is extremely effective at taking down the enemy leader. **
>
> So who is the Darth Revan character?
>
> Darth Revan is a dominant leader for the new Sith Empire faction and **powers up a team by weakening its own defensive capabilities. Darth Revan will lead a very aggressive Sith Empire team that aims to take down enemies quickly but with a different approach than most. Darth Revan’s kit is focused on effectively taking down the enemy leader while applying beneficial debuffs. We think this will be a very powerful character with longevity in PvP and do fairly well in raids. Check out Darth Reva**n's kit in a separate forum post here.
>
> SBC: What makes the character unique/special?
>
> **Darth Revan is focused on taking out enemy leaders and using Ferocity, which sacrifices allies’ defensive capabilities for more Offense. This is the first character who uses debuffs to boost their own team’s damage, a fitting theme for such a ruthless faction.**
>
> SBC: What scenes or moments were you trying to capture?
>
> Everyone experienced Revan’s journey differently but there are core roles that Revan always fills: **an exceptional strategist, master fighter, and a ruthless Sith Lord.** In short, we tried to narrow down what makes Darth Revan, Revan, but this was quite a difficult task since each playthrough can be different and it was challenging to capture everyone’s unique experience.
>
> How did those moments inform the creation of Darth Revan’s abilities?
>
> In Knights of the Old Republic, Darth Revan was portrayed as an exceptional strategist who undermined enemies by carefully striking at the key figures masterminding the plots against the Sith Empire. We felt this should be represented in battle with a kit focused on taking out leaders, and Darth Revan should, of course, be a leader of a powerful Sith Empire team. As a Sith Lord, Darth Revan is a powerful fighter with a lightsaber and uses negative emotions to gain power, much like how the debuffs in game will actually makes you stronger.
