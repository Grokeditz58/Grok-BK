S458 CHAPTER 7 THE NEW JEDI ORDER. Drop cap A (or W — lock from the leaf). After Bantam, the license moves to Del Rey. Original plan 29 books / 5 years, whittled to 19. Hero’s Journey / Campbell. Early outline (mostly discarded): Luke sends the Solo children on a quest; an unnamed invading-species female corrupts Anakin, falls in love, is redeemed; Jacen and Jaina turn on Anakin in sibling rivalry; Jacen dies. Early invaders perhaps the source of the original Sith, predisposed to the dark side more from necessity/survival than evil; 1998 outline: they watched “our” galaxy for a thousand generations, feared a “light-bringer” prophecy, and struck when the light returned. MAJOR LUCAS: “George Lucas commented on the story overview, making a number of specific changes. Among them, he disapproved of the idea that the invaders were dark side users. Per Lucas, such dark siders would ultimately vie for power, which would be their undoing as a group. Thus no Sith or proto-Sith appear in the series.” Five yearly hardcovers plus mass-market paperbacks; the first book includes “one of the most polarizing moments in Expanded Universe history.” Tight-crop.
