## S565–S577 — APPENDIX A WORKS IN PUBLICATION ORDER

The list opens with *Star Wars: From the Adventures of Luke Skywalker* (1976) and *Splinter of the Mind’s Eye* (1978), runs through the Han Solo and Lando adventures, the Thrawn, Jedi Academy, and later novel programs, and closes on S577 with 2013: *The Clone Wars: Darth Maul: Shadow Conspiracy*. S577 is otherwise blank after that final 2013 line.

**1983 typography, tight-cropped (typ565_return3.jpg):** the 1983 film-novelization line prints **Return of the Jedi** (with the *r*). This is not the **Retum of the Jedi** typography locked on the Chapter 6 opener (S313) and on some later Chapter 6 film-reference lines (S333–S335). Lock each leaf as printed.

**CBR/ebook left-trim artifact (Appendix A):** on several Appendix A leaves, hanging-indented short-story titles lose their opening quote and first letter at the left edge of the raster (e.g., S565 “irst Contact” for “First Contact,” “scape from Balis-Baurgh” for “Escape from Balis-Baurgh,” “hispers in the Dark” for “Whispers in the Dark”). Book-length titles on the same leaves (for example *The Truce at Bakura*) are complete. Appendix B prints the same short-story titles in full. Recorded as a CBR/ebook left-trim of hanging indents, not as a typography lock of the works’ names.

Other typography locks visible in the publication-order list (do not regularize): **Legacy of the JediSecrets of the Jedi omnibus** (run-together, S574 and again in Appendix B on S589); *The Clone Wars: Secret Missions 1  Breakout Squad* (possible extra space); *Junior Jedi Knights 1: The Golden Globe* uses arabic **1** in this list (the chapter entries used the juvenile-series **#I** habit). ## S594 — ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Title **ABOUT THE AUTHOR**. **PABLO HIDALGO** started writing about fantastic subject matters professionally in 1995 as a freelance author for role-playing games. In 2000, he changed careers from working as a visual-effects concept artist and digital compositor in Canada to being a full-time *Star Wars* authority at Lucasfilm Ltd., working with their marketing and online department. Since then, he has overseen the development of websites, authored several books, and developed a number of online comic books and strips. He lives in San Francisco. Remainder of the leaf blank.
