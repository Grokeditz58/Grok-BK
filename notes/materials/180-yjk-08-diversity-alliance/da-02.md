- Plot: Framed by burning waterfalls, Nolaa speaks to tens of thousands of Chromans whom the Empire enslaved as miners. She lists the crimes of humans against alien species and promises freedom 'from the tyranny of all humans—for all time.' The Chromans hail her as their savior.
