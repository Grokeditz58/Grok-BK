# Young Jedi Knights 8: Diversity Alliance (Kevin J. Anderson & Rebecca Moesta, 1997): notes part 1, front matter and ch. 1–10

- Read 2026-11-20 CST, every line in order. The reading text, READ.txt (1,625 lines), was built from the owner's library epub of the 1997 Boulevard Books paperback, a "not retail" calibre conversion. The library's mobi copy has the same text. This part covers READ l.1–870.
- **Source gap:** the English epub and mobi both break off mid-sentence early in ch. 19 (READ l.1527: "I suggest we discuss the merits of our escape plans aft…"). The rest of ch. 19 and all of ch. 20 are missing. To follow the story, that ending was read in the library's German translation (*Allianz der Vergessenen*, trans. Michael Kubiak). It is given in part 2 as an English paraphrase only: nothing from it is quoted or routed.
- **Page numbers:** "p.X" counts the epub's own page-break markers (softbreaks), so it only approximates the printed paperback pages; no scan was available to check them. The citation gives the page where the paragraph starts.
- Scope (owner's rulings, 2026-11-20): every line was read, but only scaling-relevant material is noted. That means feats of every kind (Force, lightsaber, strength, durability, speed, reflexes, agility, stamina, pain tolerance, marksmanship, hand-to-hand, tech and tactics, senses), skills, statements about characters, narrative portrayal, and anti-feats and limitations. Ordinary non-Force piloting is not noted; Force-assisted piloting is. Each chapter keeps a short Plot bullet (our words) so the story can be followed.
- Quotes are pasted by script from READ.txt (anchor expansion), never retyped. Conversion and OCR misreadings (e.g. "Raynat", "Boman"/"Bomaryn" for Bornan/Bornaryn, "Twflek", "daws" for claws, stray ‘ ’ marks) were corrected in READ.txt before pasting. "…" inside a quote is the book's own ellipsis. Straight double quotes wrap each quote; the quotes inside belong to the book.
