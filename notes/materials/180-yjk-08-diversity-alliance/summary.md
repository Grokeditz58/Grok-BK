# Young Jedi Knights 8: Diversity Alliance: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Authors: Kevin J. Anderson & Rebecca Moesta. Eighth Young Jedi Knights book (Boulevard Books, 1997), the second in the Bornan Thul / Diversity Alliance arc. 20 chapters. The dedication is to Steve Sansweet.

## Plot
- **Ch. 1, 3–5, 7:** Raynar is escorted to his mother's hidden Bornaryn fleet. The guard Kusk betrays them to his bounty-hunter brother on the High Roller. Jacen, Tenel Ka and Raynar disarm Kusk with the Force, and Jaina and Lowie destroy the High Roller from the gun wells with Jedi-enhanced aim. Aryn reveals Bornan's last meeting place: Kuar.
- **Ch. 2, 12, 15:** Nolaa Tarkona rules the Diversity Alliance from Ryloth, with Hovrak as her lieutenant and Boba Fett hired to find Thul. She rallies the Chromans against humanity.
- **Ch. 6, 9, 16:** Zekk takes his first bounty job, tracing the scavenger Fonterrat to the plague-killed human colony of Gammalin. Fett captures and then spares him. Fonterrat's dying message shows that Nolaa planted the plague, which kills humans only, as a test, and that Bornan Thul has the secret.
- **Ch. 8, 10–11, 13–14, 17:** on Kuar the friends find Thul's sash warning that "all humans" are in mortal danger. They survive combat arachnids with the help of Raaba, Lowie's friend who was thought dead and who now serves Nolaa. Tyko Thul arrives, insults Nolaa, and Raaba leaves.
- **Ch. 18–20:** IG-88's assassin droids raid the camp, wrecking Tyko's ship and the Rock Dragon's drive. After a three-way battle in the catacombs with the arachnids, IG-88 and his last droid abduct Tyko as their bounty and warn the Jedi off. The friends repair the Rock Dragon with a repulsor module Lowie salvages and head home to Yavin 4 (ch. 19–20 from the print edition).

## Strongest findings (by character)
- **Lowbacca:** lifts and tosses a heavy slab (ch. 10). He fights and kills a combat arachnid, swinging around it on a chain, and holds the rear on the chain crossing (ch. 11, 13). He keeps going through his rib wound (ch. 13–14), and his Jedi-enhanced gunnery helps destroy the High Roller (ch. 7). In the ch. 19 battle he cuts an assassin droid's leg off with a blind, instinctive backward sweep, then, attuned to the Force, leaps over its blaster fire and cleaves its head in two (print pp.212–213).
- **Tenel Ka:** cuts the arachnid's legs and spines (ch. 11). She catches Jacen one-armed and cuts the chain under the last arachnid (ch. 13), sleeps on a warrior's edge of alertness (ch. 18), crosses blades with Jacen to deflect IG-88's rifle bolt, and beheads the arachnid that reared behind Jaina (ch. 19). Her read after the abduction: someone wants Thul hostages, 'We should have expected it.'
- **Jacen:** Force-disarms Kusk with Tenel Ka and Raynar (ch. 5). He senses danger and others' emotions repeatedly (ch. 5, 17–19), and flows with the Force to block IG-88's shot (ch. 19). He slashes off the mandibles of the arachnid about to pounce on Jaina, but an earlier cut that clips a leg off another does not even slow it (ch. 19).
- **Jaina:** Jedi-enhanced quad-laser gunnery (ch. 7) and tech skill. In ch. 19 she moves too quickly for an assassin droid, cutting its rifle tip before it can fire and running it through; she is then caught off guard by an arachnid behind her and has to be saved.
- **Raynar:** sensing and Force calming, and his Force push in the Kusk disarm (ch. 4–5).
- **Zekk:** a Force warning lets him dodge Fett's first shot (ch. 9). He senses that Fett is sincere and Force-nudges a cell lock (ch. 16).
- **Boba Fett:** "Few have shot at me and lived"; "Had I truly intended to hit you, I would have succeeded"; the Bounty Hunters Creed (ch. 16).
- **IG-88 / assassin droids:** drop from heights no human could survive; "the worst of all the assassin droid bounty hunters" (ch. 18). In ch. 19 IG-88's grenade punches through an arachnid's body armor; one droid rips an arachnid limb from limb (both die); IG-88 crushes an arachnid's brain with his fist and holds a grenade in another's mandibles until it explodes, mangling his own hand; he and his last droid beat the young Jedi to Tyko, cut him free with precise shots, take him and leave the four Jedi frozen. Three of the four assassin droids with him are destroyed in the fight (one by an arachnid, one each by Jaina and Lowie).
- **Tyko Thul:** helpless in the fight: 'no Jedi powers, no lightsaber, not even a blaster' (ch. 19).
- **Em Teedee:** runs the Rock Dragon's repair diagnostics and plots the fastest route home; he set the Tradewyn's navicomputers to full randomization (ch. 20).
- **Nolaa Tarkona:** her oratory and charisma (ch. 2, 15), and the plague plot (ch. 16).
