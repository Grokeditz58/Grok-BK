# Young Jedi Knights 8: Diversity Alliance: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-20 CST. Full read of READ l.1–1624, every line in order: front matter, ch. 1 to the break in ch. 19, the blurb fragment and the Delusions of Grandeur preview. The missing ending (the rest of ch. 19 and ch. 20) was read in the German edition for the plot only; see the README. Notes are limited to scaling-relevant material (owner's rulings, 2026-11-20). The quotes are in da-01 and da-02; this summary is in our own words.
- Authors: Kevin J. Anderson & Rebecca Moesta. Eighth Young Jedi Knights book (Boulevard Books, 1997), the second in the Bornan Thul / Diversity Alliance arc. 20 chapters. The dedication is to Steve Sansweet.

## Plot
- **Ch. 1, 3–5, 7:** Raynar is escorted to his mother's hidden Bornaryn fleet. The guard Kusk betrays them to his bounty-hunter brother on the High Roller. Jacen, Tenel Ka and Raynar disarm Kusk with the Force, and Jaina and Lowie destroy the High Roller from the gun wells with Jedi-enhanced aim. Aryn reveals Bornan's last meeting place: Kuar.
- **Ch. 2, 12, 15:** Nolaa Tarkona rules the Diversity Alliance from Ryloth, with Hovrak as her lieutenant and Boba Fett hired to find Thul. She rallies the Chromans against humanity.
- **Ch. 6, 9, 16:** Zekk takes his first bounty job, tracing the scavenger Fonterrat to the plague-killed human colony of Gammalin. Fett captures and then spares him. Fonterrat's dying message shows that Nolaa planted the plague, which kills humans only, as a test, and that Bornan Thul has the secret.
- **Ch. 8, 10–11, 13–14, 17:** on Kuar the friends find Thul's sash warning that "all humans" are in mortal danger. They survive combat arachnids with the help of Raaba, Lowie's friend who was thought dead and who now serves Nolaa. Tyko Thul arrives, insults Nolaa, and Raaba leaves.
- **Ch. 18–20:** IG-88's assassin droids raid the camp, wrecking Tyko's ship and the Rock Dragon's drive. After a three-way battle in the catacombs with the arachnids, the droids abduct Tyko (German edition for the ending). The friends repair the Rock Dragon and head home.

## Strongest findings (by character)
- **Lowbacca:** lifts and tosses a heavy slab (ch. 10). He fights and kills a combat arachnid, swinging around it on a chain, and holds the rear on the chain crossing (ch. 11, 13). He keeps going through his rib wound (ch. 13–14), and his Jedi-enhanced gunnery helps destroy the High Roller (ch. 7). In the German-only ending he makes a Force leap over blaster fire.
- **Tenel Ka:** cuts the arachnid's legs and spines (ch. 11). She catches Jacen one-armed and cuts the chain under the last arachnid (ch. 13), sleeps on a warrior's edge of alertness (ch. 18), and crosses blades with Jacen to deflect IG-88's rifle bolt (ch. 19).
- **Jacen:** Force-disarms Kusk with Tenel Ka and Raynar (ch. 5). He senses danger and others' emotions repeatedly (ch. 5, 17–19), and flows with the Force to block IG-88's shot (ch. 19).
- **Jaina:** Jedi-enhanced quad-laser gunnery (ch. 7) and tech skill. Her lightsaber work against the droids is in the German-only ending.
- **Raynar:** sensing and Force calming, and his Force push in the Kusk disarm (ch. 4–5).
- **Zekk:** a Force warning lets him dodge Fett's first shot (ch. 9). He senses that Fett is sincere and Force-nudges a cell lock (ch. 16).
- **Boba Fett:** "Few have shot at me and lived"; "Had I truly intended to hit you, I would have succeeded"; the Bounty Hunters Creed (ch. 16).
- **IG-88 / assassin droids:** drop from heights no human could survive; "the worst of all the assassin droid bounty hunters" (ch. 18).
- **Nolaa Tarkona:** her oratory and charisma (ch. 2, 15), and the plague plot (ch. 16).
