> "One had a crushed larynx, one a broken neck, and the third had taken an elbow to the temple that had surely cracked the skull." (MS1 ch. 14)
- The fight is too fast to follow at normal speed and is studied at "one-quarter speed". Ji then takes trophies, possibly fingers or ears.
- **Barriss's assessment:**
> "The Jedi part of her had been impressed by Phow Ji’s efficiency as a fighter. His moves had been fluid and powerful; against an opponent unaided by the Force, he would be formidable indeed." / "A Jedi who was worried that the Force couldn’t protect her against a trained thug was definitely overfatigued." (MS1 ch. 14)

## Ch. 15: Bleyd disposes of Mathal
- Mathal gave Bleyd "two long, shallow cuts on his right forearm". Bleyd stops the bleeding with thumb pressure on a nerve ganglion.
- He **carbon-freezes the corpse**, disguises himself as one of The Silent (a mute caste of medical servers), puts Mathal's KDY Starspin on autopilot carrying the block, and **spacewalks** back to the trash lock in a thinskin suit with only "five minutes of life" of air, enduring the cold "like a thousand needles of frozen nitrogen". The Starspin flies into Separatist orbital space and is destroyed by a "thermonuclear explosion".
- Rules of war: neither side may attack the other's orbiting medical frigate.

## Ch. 16: Barriss trips Phow Ji with the Force
- Ji asks for the holo so he can "self-critique" his moves and says "I have a wall full of trophies". He justifies the killings: "I was unarmed, one against three". Barriss: "your hands and feet are as much weapons as a vibroblade".
- Ji challenges her ("demonstrate the efficacy of your mystical Force against my expertise?"), then turns away: "it wouldn’t really be fair for me to pick on a lowly Padawan".
- **Barriss uses the Force to twist his boot. He turns the fall into a roll:**
> "his boot twisted inward, no more than a few degrees—just enough to catch the heel of his forward boot. He tripped. A man of lesser skill would have fallen flat on his face ... But even as he fell, Ji tucked into an ovoid shape ... a neat gymnastic move that left him standing in balance and facing her." (MS1 ch. 16)
- She regrets it: "It was chasing a fire gnat with a turbolaser." Qui-Gon's maxim: "Be ever mindful of the living Force".

## Ch. 17: I-Five's past (*Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter* link)
- I-Five quotes Kai Konnik's *Beach of Stars* metaphorically. He has more than 200,000 works in memory, and uses only "one percent" of his cognitive module on his chores.
- He was the partner, not the property, of **Lorn Pavan** (Coruscant underworld info-broker, Crimson Corridor). They were hunted by an assassin:
> "The assassin was an Iridonian Zabrak. Quite deadly; a martial arts master skilled enough to make Phow Ji look like a drunken Jawa. He had … other skills as well." / "Black Sun?" "Worse. Far worse." (MS1 ch. 17)
  - This is Darth Maul (*Shadow Hunter*), "more than a decade ago". The assassin "stole an item of value from us and fled Coruscant". A woman (Darsha Assant) "sacrificed herself to save us". I-Five thinks Lorn deactivated him to protect him, and it was "hushed up at an extremely high level".
- Afterwards he spent 12 weeks deactivated, 6 years on the Kessel Run, then had several years missing from his memory. He was a house droid for a Naboo noble family until the Republic confiscated droids, and was given medical training. He can "hack past pyrowalls". He is nervous around Zabraks (Zan).

## Ch. 21: the spy's motive; Bleyd's lighter
- The spy finds the Rimsoo staff "good people" ("There wasn’t a demon among them") but acts on "old and far away" reasons: "the atrocity that had made all this necessary ... just as the spy’s people had died".
- **Bleyd's lighter** is a modified **Surronian Conqueror** assault ship under 30 m long with eight Surronian ion engines (four A2, four A2.50), "the fastest thing in the atmosphere on this planet by far". It has a Class One CEC H1.5 hyperdrive, fire-linked ion cannons and laser cannons, and was captured from a pirate.
- Bleyd's side schemes: stash bota in carbonite on the MedStar ("A thousand kilos ... worth millions"), double-order medical systems, and sell the MedStar's operating software. There are about a dozen Rimsoos on Tanlassa. Bleyd's ship is MedStar Nineteen.

## Ch. 22: Bleyd and Phow Ji size each other up
- Bleyd feels "a distinct sense of danger" from Ji at 20 m: "Here was a warrior—not just a soldier. A smiling killer". Ji's rank is Lieutenant.
- Bleyd's estimate: "This man could take on twenty Black Sun thugs like the one Bleyd had bested orbitside without breaking a sweat." (MS1 ch. 22)
- Bleyd's honest self-assessment:
> "he wasn’t at all sure he could beat Phow Ji in a fair match. There was no doubt that he was stronger and faster, but the human was a combat champion, and his skills had been honed in dozens of matches, some of which had been to the death. Size and strength and speed all mattered, of course, but an opponent with enough skill could level that field." (MS1 ch. 22)
- Ji: "Adept enough for anyone on this planet. Sir." Bleyd puts off the confrontation and considers hunting Ji in the rain forest someday.
- The Rimsoo energy shield stops missiles and gamma and X-rays, but heat, rain, spores and insects get through.
- Den has evidence that Filba diverted 500 hectolitres of Anticeptin-D. He spots a glance between Bleyd and Filba and realizes "how high up the help was".

## Ch. 23: the moon-moth spycam
- A thumbnail-sized biomimetic spycam with a 10,000 m range and a confounder that defeats tangle fields and sensor screens. It cost three months' pay and was first used on a "Wild Space smugglers" story.
- **Bleyd's speed:** "Bleyd was suddenly across the room and at the Hutt’s throat. He’d moved so fast that the moon moth had only registered a blur." Filba collapses, **poisoned through his ale jug**; Bleyd sniffs a shard, baffled. Bleyd then plucks the flying moon moth out of the air: "A hand came out of nowhere, moving so fast it just seemed to appear." He crushes it. (MS1 ch. 23)

## Ch. 24: Phow Ji vs Barriss, unarmed and without the Force (the key fight)
- Barriss senses Ji hidden in the shadows: "the Force’s prompt was clear ... almost the psychic equivalent of a hand on her shoulder"; he is "a pulsing obstruction in the Force’s smooth continuum".
- **She cannot mind-trick him:**
> "she could feel the discipline of his thoughts. They were a dense weave, as impenetrable as spin-worm silk. Ji was not weak-minded enough for a Padawan’s ability to sway him easily, if at all." (MS1 ch. 24)
- **Her own view of the Force against Ji:**
> "One gesture, and Ji would fly into the nearest tree like a rabid rockbat. No fighter, no matter what his physical strength, could pit muscle against the Force and prevail. Maybe she couldn’t control his mind, but she could control his body." (MS1 ch. 24)
- She won't use the lightsaber: "A Jedi Knight or Master would have the skills to stop him without injuring him, but as a Padawan, she was not confident in her ability to do so."
- **The exchange (Barriss with no Force and no saber):**
  - Ji "leapt, covering the two strides separating them with phenomenal speed". She "barely" dodges, and the punch glances off her shoulder.
  - She lands a low punch to his belly: "it was like punching a wall—there was no give. His abdominals were like ridged plasteel." She takes a neck slap that makes "her vision flare red".
  - Ji ends it with an open-hand combination to head, torso and hip plus an ankle hook: "Barriss went down, hard." Incoming medlifters stop it there. "My point has been made."
- **The dark side surges:**
> "Phow Ji had no idea how close to dying he was just then. Her rage was such that a flicker of a finger would suffice." / "she resisted the dark side. She lost the battle, but won the war. This time …" (MS1 ch. 24)
  - Foreshadowing for Barriss: "The dark side was always there ... Give in once, it would be twice as powerful the next time."
