# MedStar I: Battle Surgeons: summary of strongest findings

Source: Michael Reaves & Steve Perry, *Star Wars: MedStar I: Battle Surgeons* (Del Rey 2004; ebook v3.1_r1, eISBN 978-0-345-46307-4). Hand-read in full on 2026-11-03: all 40 chapters and the Epilogue, dedication, author pages, and the Del Rey back matter (the *MedStar II* and *Hard Contact* excerpts were hand-read; the non-Clone Wars excerpts were skimmed). Setting: Drongar, "Year 2 A.B.O.G." Citations are "MS1 ch. N".

1. **Phow Ji, a non-Force teräs käsi master, beat a Jedi and wins outright against a Padawan who fights without the Force. The book is equally clear that the Force would have beaten him.**
   > "Danva had the peculiar skill of being able to divorce himself from the Force at times. He never used its power in his matches ... a virtuoso with twin lightsabers, one of the few ever to master the Jar’Kai technique ... Master Danva was beaten by the local teräs käsi champion, Phow Ji." (MS1 ch. 5)
   > "it was like punching a wall—there was no give. His abdominals were like ridged plasteel." / "Barriss went down, hard." (MS1 ch. 24)
   > "No fighter, no matter what his physical strength, could pit muscle against the Force and prevail. Maybe she couldn’t control his mind, but she could control his body." (MS1 ch. 24)
   - Barriss's view: "against an opponent unaided by the Force, he would be formidable indeed" (MS1 ch. 14). A small Force twist trips him, but he rolls back to his feet (MS1 ch. 16). His mind is "a dense weave, as impenetrable as spin-worm silk", so a Padawan's mind trick fails (MS1 ch. 24).
2. **Phow Ji's last stand is the book's peak for a non-Force fighter. He fights roughly 20 or more soldiers, mercenaries and droids alone, then takes the last of them with him.**
   > "Ji snatched the blasters from their holsters so fast that the action was a blur" / "Ji drew both blasters and shot the guns out of their hands!" / "Ji shot eight of them, leaping, dodging, and rolling as plasma bursts peppered the ground all around him. It was a Jedi-like display of acrobatic skill" (MS1 ch. 36)
   - Earlier that day he killed 7 Separatist soldiers and got only a shallow vibroblade cut and a laser burn on one cheek. He claims mastery of "teräs käsi, of Hapan, echani, tae-jitsu, and a dozen other deadly styles". His limit: "there was no way Ji could have dodged something he hadn’t seen coming" (a neurotoxin dart, MS1 ch. 36).
3. **Barriss Offee's Force abilities and limits.** She heals CNS trauma and neurotoxin poisoning, reads machinery down to the quark level, leaps about 10 m, hears a death cry at a distance, and has raw dark-side power ready. She cannot heal brain-stem destruction or handle mass suffering.
   > "healing energy was pouring from her into him. No—not from her; through her." (fried CNS restored, MS1 ch. 6)
   > "the Padawan leapt across the bota field, covering a distance of at least ten meters in a single bound ... a single blow ... sheared halfway through the droid’s torso." (MS1 ch. 33)
   > "She wanted nothing more than to turn him into a pile of ashes, and, worse yet, she knew that she could ... It wouldn’t even require an effort" (MS1 ch. 36)
   > "extrasensory experience of this much agony at this close range carried a real possibility of synaptic overload." (MS1 ch. 13) / "she had still not been able to save him, though she had used every bit of the power supposedly under her control." (MS1 ch. 40)
   - "The Force could not in itself close a gaping wound—at least she did not have that kind of control" (MS1 ch. 9). She floats half a body-length in meditation (MS1 ch. 12). Rage blinded her danger sense (MS1 ch. 36).
4. **Speed and strength ladder among non-Force fighters: Kaird (Nediji) is faster than Bleyd (Sakiyan), who is faster than humans. Bleyd still doubts he could beat Ji.**
   > "a Sakiyan’s muscles and ligament-attachment angles were mechanically superior to those of a human’s, making Bleyd at least half again as powerful as a strong man, and a good deal quicker as well." (MS1 ch. 11)
   > "There was no doubt that he was stronger and faster, but the human was a combat champion ... an opponent with enough skill could level that field." (MS1 ch. 22)
   > "he lunged, impossibly fast, and the short knife flicked out in a blur. Bleyd saw it coming, but even so, he barely had time to get his own knife in position for the block." (Kaird, MS1 ch. 35)
   - Bleyd plucks a flying spycam from the air: "A hand came out of nowhere, moving so fast it just seemed to appear" (MS1 ch. 23). His record: a rancor with "only three quarrels" and Noghri with hook-blades (MS1 ch. 11). Kaird kills him with dendriton toxin.
5. **Link to Darth Maul via I-Five and Lorn Pavan, and the Order still doesn't know a Sith killed Darsha.**
   > "a martial arts master skilled enough to make Phow Ji look like a drunken Jawa. He had … other skills as well." (MS1 ch. 17)
   > "Obi-Wan Kenobi says she died bravely, battling an unknown foe." (MS1 ch. 29)
   - I-Five finds a hidden finger laser and "harmonic vocalization abilities" (MS1 ch. 38). Barriss can sense his hardware but "the droid’s mind ... was untouchable" (MS1 ch. 29, 40).
6. **The Force perceives clone individuality, and lore on the dark side.**
   > "You might all look alike, but you aren’t all the same ... The Force can recognize this." (MS1 ch. 19)
   - Luminara admits she once used the dark side to survive: "it will feel right" (MS1 ch. 26). "The Force was not an instrument of any but the most basic of ethics and morality." (MS1 ch. 29)

Other data: Rimsoo survival rate is 87% (ch. 10). Bota is an adaptogen that "can cure everything but a rainy day" (ch. 30). Separatist ciphers end in Symbian, dead for 30,000 years (ch. 30). The spy, "Column" or "Lens", works for both Dooku and Black Sun and is still unrevealed at the end. The *Hard Contact* excerpt gives Geonosis casualties: 12,000 seriously injured, 8,000 walking wounded, 72,000 combat-fit awaiting redeployment.
