# MedStar I: Battle Surgeons (Michael Reaves & Steve Perry, Del Rey 2004): notes part 1

- Read 2026-11-03 CST from the owner's MEGA epub (handle YL0GmRIK; Del Rey ebook v3.1_r1, eISBN 978-0-345-46307-4). Citation "MS1 ch. N".
- Setting: RMSU-7 ("Rimsoo Seven"), Jasserak Lowlands of Tanlassa, near the Kondrus Sea, planet Drongar, "Year 2 A.B.O.G." (after the Battle of Geonosis). The Cestus timeline puts MedStar at +24 months.

## Ch. 1: the assembly line
- Surgeon Capt. Jos Vondar (Corellian), Zan Yant (Zabrak, plays the quetarra), Tolk le Trene (Lorrdian scrub nurse), Klo Merit (Equani empath), Leemoth (Duros).
- Clone armor gets little respect:
> "There’s enough scrap metal in this guy to build two battle droids ... I don’t know why they even bother putting armor on ’em." / "Got that right," Zan said. "Stuff won’t stop anything stronger than a kid’s pellet gun." (MS1 ch. 1)
- Clones are "even more identical than monozygotic twins", so cloned organs from the nutrient tanks swap in "with no worry about rejection syndrome". A lung transplant takes "less than an hour".
- Lightning on Drongar: "More than a few troopers had been cooked where they stood". One trooper's boots were left standing "five body-lengths away from the blackened form".

## Ch. 2: Barriss arrives; Drongar
- Padawan **Barriss Offee** (Mirialan healer; Master Luminara Unduli) suspects the posting is her trials. Luminara on when the trials start: "Anytime. All the time. No time."
- Drongar: adaptogenic spores mutate around every treatment and eat ship filters in "a hour, mebbe less". Humidity is over 90 percent. Because the air is oxygen-rich, B1/B2 battle droids **rust** ("even the incredibly tough durasteel alloy ... would oxidize"), and the fire risk keeps most fighting to small arms (sonic pistols, small blasters, slugthrowers). Very little air war happens because of the spores.
- **Bota** is a mold/fungus wonder drug: an antibiotic for humans, a narcotic for Neimoidians, and for Hutts a stimulant "almost as powerful as glitterstim". It burns "like rocket fuel" and dies from nearby explosions.
- Barriss watches a battle of about 200 troopers against 70–80 droids, with B1s and B2 quads. Luminara's maxim: "It does not matter how fast you are if you miss the target. It is the one who hits the most who will have the victory …"
- A medlifter carries up to 6 wounded.

## Ch. 3: Admiral Bleyd
- Admiral Tarnese Bleyd (a Sakiyan who sees into the infrared) commands the MedStar frigate and resents the posting. He thinks bota will make him rich. His clan was ruined when Shiltu the Hutt, a Black Sun vigo, double-crossed his pride-father.
- Drongar: radius 6,259 km, gravity 1.2 standard, two captured-asteroid moons.
- Bleyd is conspiring with Filba the Hutt (supply officer) and Black Sun over bota, expecting "billions of credits". He has hunted razor-tailed tigers and lyniks, and trapped a nexu.
- Den Dhur (Sullustan, reporter for the *Galactic Wave*) arrives. He has history with Filba.

## Ch. 4: the clones as "meat droids"; Barriss senses the camp
- Jos's view of the clones: "subtle behavioral adjustments mass-programmed into the amygdala"; "No one ever heard of a trooper freezing under fire". He suspects serotonin and dopamine were adjusted. "meat droids … as a description, it seemed apt". He has seen some individuation.
- The front is about 70 km from the Rimsoo. Droids rarely last long on Drongar because of the humidity and oxygen.
- Barriss "felt his presence in the Force before she could see him". At night she can "feel the reverberations of death and destruction quite clearly through the Force" from distant fighting.
- Lore: Yoda "has been a mentor to nearly all the Jedi currently in the Order. One student ... left the Order and turned to the dark side" (Dooku).

## Ch. 5: Phow Ji, the teräs käsi master who beat a Jedi
- Barriss senses "impending combat" through the Force from about 100 m away. It turns out to be a teräs käsi match. The roil of menace comes from the blond fighter.
- **Joclad Danva and the Bunduki match:**
> "Danva had the peculiar skill of being able to divorce himself from the Force at times. He never used its power in his matches; only his personal skill, which was considerable. He was a virtuoso with twin lightsabers, one of the few ever to master the Jar’Kai technique. ... He could hold his own with most of the Jedi in practice." / "Master Danva was beaten by the local teräs käsi champion, Phow Ji." (MS1 ch. 5)
- Phow Ji drops Usu Cley (Ninth Fleet Middle-Mass Champion two years running) with a cross-deflection, a nose punch, a throat-grip sweep and an elbow to the solar plexus: "the entire sequence had taken maybe three seconds, total."
- Ji: "The Force is a tale made up by the Jedi ... Jedi are not impressive fighters. I hardly broke a sweat, dropping one awhile back." He throws his towel at Barriss, and she stops it in midair for about two seconds with the Force: "The Force is real". Ji dismisses it as a carnival illusion. Barriss calls the display "An error in judgment".
- Jos's hearsay: Jedi can "anticipate such attacks before they were launched ... seeing the immediate future".
- Fauna: the "wriggler" is a slug about 5 m long whose cilia deliver an electric shock. All land fauna seen so far are invertebrates.

## Ch. 6: first Force-healing on Drongar
- A clone hit by a disruptor field has his "central nervous system ... fried" and would be "nothing but meat". Zan was going to harvest his organs. Barriss lays her palms on him:
> "healing energy was pouring from her into him. No—not from her; through her. She was only the vessel" / "His brain wave pattern’s within normal limits, and most of the secondary trauma seems gone." (MS1 ch. 6)
- Childhood memory: at age 3–4 she telekinetically called a rolling ball back to her in the Temple, and Yoda saw it and "smiled and nodded".
- Equani: few remain "after a solar flare had scorched their homeworld". They are intensely empathetic.
- Jos: Filba controls bota processing, harvesting and shipments under Bleyd. Den is nervous about it. Epoh Trebor's HoloNet tour is coming.

## Ch. 7: the spy; the bota transport destroyed
- An unnamed **Separatist spy**, "high enough" in the chain of command, has been at the Rimsoo for 2 months, placed through "agents in the higher echelons of the Republic military" as part of a Dooku scheme. The spy watches the bota shuttle explode at about 1,000 m: "brighter than Drongar Prime". It kills 3 and destroys "More than seventy kilos of bota". The text implies the spy did it.
- Den knew Filba from **Jabiim**: Filba sold on the weapons black market and nearly got Den killed "trying to curry favor with the rebel Alto Stratus". Filba is the quartermaster, runs the holocomm station, and handles bota.
- Den on clone toughness: "cut off both arms and they’ll kick you to death."

## Ch. 8: Barriss feels the sabotage; the carbonite scheme
- > "I—felt the destruction. Through the Force. Not the agony of their deaths—that was almost instantaneous. But the recoil in the Force, the reaction to whatever motivated this heinous act ... It was sabotage. It was murder." (MS1 ch. 8)
- Bleyd's scheme: Filba skims bota, which decays unless it is **embedded in carbonite**. Black Sun supplies a YT-1300f with a modified hyperdrive to run both blockades. Bota's medical use at the Rimsoos is "strictly interdicted". Bleyd suspects Black Sun, or that Filba is betraying him. Bleyd's view: the war "was all about economics".

## Ch. 9: Barriss on the medical ward
- Her limits: "The Force could not in itself close a gaping wound—at least she did not have that kind of control—but it could help a sick person’s weakened immune system".
- She can't read Hutts: "like trying to pick up a transparisteel ball covered in ramjet lube"; "She was best with her own kind".
- CT-914 faints whenever he sits up (orthostatic syncope). With "tidal breathing" she enters the Force and finds a hypothalamus lesion. She "stroked it with etheric ripples until the glow faded" and cures it. Clones are "literal and taciturn". He makes the first clone joke she has heard.
- Healing time: "a little more than an hour had passed ... yet it had felt as if only a few seconds had passed."

## Ch. 9–11: Bleyd hunts the Black Sun envoy
- Mathal, Black Sun's envoy, says Black Sun wants all the bota, to be loaded into a "Damorian Nine Thousand, carry half the planet". Bleyd plans to kill him.
- **Sakiyan physique:**
> "a Sakiyan’s muscles and ligament-attachment angles were mechanically superior to those of a human’s, making Bleyd at least half again as powerful as a strong man, and a good deal quicker as well." (MS1 ch. 11)
- Bleyd's hunting record: with only a lance he stalked Shistavanens on Uvena III. He "had taken a rancor with a pulley bow and only three quarrels" and "had tracked and killed unrepentant Noghri with a pair of hook-blades" no longer than his middle fingers.
- He gives the armed Mathal (an expert knife fighter) a knife and a 2-minute head start, waits upside down in a vent, and dives headfirst with the clan cry "Taarrnneeesseee—!". The kill happens off-page.
- Den learns from the drunk Ugnaught tech Rorand Zuzz that Filba is skimming credits.
- The spy is building a **frame**: "What Vaetes and his people needed to see at this point was an actual villain." The spy claims "more abilities in that direction than anyone here could possibly know".

## Ch. 10: I-Five arrives
- Jos's clan is *enster* (no marriage outside one's home system), so he holds back from Tolk.
- The Rimsoo keeps "eighty-seven percent of those who rotate through our surgery alive" (Tolk).
- The droid **I-5YQ ("I-Five")** comes down the ramp with a sarcastic aside ("Blind luck, I'd say"). It calls itself "A modification of the Threepio series ... borrows somewhat from the old Serv-O-Droid Orbots model ... discontinued ... due to litigation", has Sector General medical files, and seems "unnerved" by Zan.

## Ch. 12: floating meditation; Luminara's lesson
- Barriss's **Rising/floating meditation**: on good days she can "rise like a balloon, to hover weightless half her body-length in the air". At the end of the chapter she does it.
- She sees herself as an average Padawan. Luminara's balcony lesson: "By saying ‘try,’ you limit yourself. Jedi do not limit themselves by choice." / "A Jedi does not put limits on herself, but there are limits, and you must find them". The river-crossing parable: "I could have simply stood up and looked over the wall".

## Ch. 13: mass casualties
- Klo Merit: "The Equani species is—was—by nature rather resistant to the powers of the Jedi."
- 35–40 wounded at once. Rimsoo triage runs categories 1–6, plus X for mortal or too time-consuming.
- **Barriss's Force-limit under mass suffering:**
> "She had damped down on her connection to the Force as much as she could; extrasensory experience of this much agony at this close range carried a real possibility of synaptic overload." / "There were some here she knew she could heal with the Jedi arts she had learned, but it would take too long. Not even the Force could mitigate the cold and brutal equations of triage." (MS1 ch. 13)
- A droid at triage remarks "A purgation of fire". Jos loses six patients in a row.
