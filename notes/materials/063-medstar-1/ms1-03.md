# MedStar I: Battle Surgeons: notes part 3 (Chapters 25–Epilogue, back matter)

- Read 2026-11-03 CST from the owner's MEGA epub (handle YL0GmRIK). Citation "MS1 ch. N".

## Ch. 25: aftermath of Filba's death
- The autopsy confirms poison. Bleyd will now use the dead Filba as the "scape-Drall" and goes hunting for whoever ran the spycam (operator range about 10 km).
- Den sends the receiver down the waste disposal and hides the recording disc behind a wall brace in the south refresher. Black-marketing in wartime counts as treason and carries death. He is stuck on Drongar, because leaving suddenly would invite a brain-scan, and decides on a Phow Ji story as cover.
- Rumour at the base: Filba blew up the transport and stole bota, then took poison.
- Barriss: "the Force was not quiescent. Something was still roiling in its invisible folds ... the matter was not yet settled." (MS1 ch. 24)

## Ch. 26: Luminara on the dark side (Oa Park flashback)
- Oa Park on Coruscant has "more than thirty different environments" behind energy boundaries.
- **The blindfolded remote drill:** "the only way to avoid being shocked was to let the Force guide your hands." Barriss could have destroyed the remote "Easily". **"One student in eight does eventually reach out to destroy a remote."** Luminara: "Some of the more gifted students do just that". It is acceptable if done with logic and a calm mind; done out of anger, it is the dark side.
- Barriss notes: "if I could not block that, I might not have the power to stop a shooter fifty or a hundred meters away".
- **The Luminara quote:**
> "Power wants to be used. It must be kept under constant vigil, else it will seduce and corrupt you. One moment you’re swatting an annoying training toy; the next you’re paralyzing an offending being’s lungs and choking him to death. You do it because you can." (MS1 ch. 26)
- **Luminara admits she once used the dark side:**
> "Yes. In a moment of weakness and pain, I did. It allowed me to survive when I might have perished otherwise, but that one taste was enough for me to realize I could never do it again." / "It will feel better than anything you have ever experienced ... Worst of all, it will feel right." (MS1 ch. 26)
- Barriss: "Without the Jedi, she was nothing." (This ironically foreshadows her later fall.)
- CT-914 to Jos: "I—personally—was pained by Nine-one-five’s death ... I expect I’ll miss him until I die."

## Ch. 27: Kaird of the Nediji (Black Sun)
- Colonel Kohn Doil (Vunakian, ritual scars, no pain inhibitor) heads Bleyd's security. A vortex storm wrecked a transport.
- **Kaird** is a Nediji avian Black Sun agent, disguised as one of The Silent and given away by a spiceflower scent. Nediji are from Nedij on the east spinward arm; leaving the system is taboo for those "properly nested".
- **Kaird's speed (he outmatches Bleyd):**
> "The being had moved, but so fast that Bleyd couldn’t believe what he was seeing. Of a moment, he was sitting on the cot, and then in an eyeblink he stood next to Bleyd, out of the blaster’s line of fire, close enough to touch." / "If the fellow could move that swiftly in a one-gravity field, he would never be able to line up on him in time" (MS1 ch. 27)
  - Bleyd lowers his blaster: "The Nediji was much faster than he was, and tricky as well." Kaird is "a much better flier. In the genes".
- Mathal had been working against Black Sun, planning to grab all the bota and run. Black Sun keeps the original small-flow deal. Bleyd falsely claims he killed Filba.

## Ch. 28: the spy "Lens" / "Column"
- The spy is "Lens" to Black Sun and "Column" to the Separatists, and claims the **Filba poisoning**: "The Hutt was more useful to us dead than alive." Lens is charting every bota field, including unlogged wild patches that Lens marked "found empty" in the records.
- The Silent: when one is present, "mortality figures dropped more often than not ... Perhaps it had something to do with the Force". The text treats this as documented.
- Nediji are "fast, ruthless, and clever". Lens knows the Nest Blessing ("Fly free, fly straight, Brother of the Air") and claims "A kind of kinship ... between the two species". Data goes onto an implant chip that only a "doppraymagno scanner" can detect.
- Kaird: "Wars come, wars go, but business continues." The spy's goal: "What, after all, did a single admiral matter when you were after the entire Republic?"

## Ch. 29: Barriss probes I-Five with the Force
> "She could sense the almost undetectable susurrus of countless quarks and bosuns shifting spin and polarity, providing the synaptic grid with nearly unlimited potential connections. She could feel the hum of circuitry, the smooth pulse of hydraulic fluid, and the restrained power of the servos." / "something too subtle even to be called an aura. The merest hint that somehow ... the sum of I-Five was greater than his parts." (MS1 ch. 29)
- **A Force-sensing scale point:** she reads a droid's internals down to the quark and boson level.
- I-Five remembers "Darsha Assam/Assant". Barriss: "Obi-Wan Kenobi says she died bravely, battling an unknown foe." (The Order does not know it was a Sith.)
- I-Five: Sakiyans, Trandoshans and Nikto are "fearless, but not necessarily brave" because it is in their genes. Clones are "genetically modified to feel little or no fear on the battlefield".
- "The Force was not an instrument of any but the most basic of ethics and morality."

## Ch. 30: Zan uses bota on a patient
- Separatist ciphers are Feraleechi one-time loops that end in Symbian, a language dead for 30,000 years.
- Zan saves Major N'do Maetrecis from a drug-resistant hospital infection with **hand-processed bota** in an unmarked skinpopper. **Bota is "an adaptogen that can cure everything but a rainy day in most humanoid phenotypes."** Rimsoos are forbidden to use it.
- Drakñahr Syndrome was "something that all of Coruscant Med and a room full of Jedi and Silents couldn’t treat", which marks a limit of Jedi healing.

## Ch. 31: Ji's class
- First Rule: "Always be ready!" Ji punches a student without warning: "Duels are fun ... Duels are neat, clean, and have rules ... In real life, you don’t have those luxuries."
- Den sees Ji as "a being who could, if he wished, exterminate you without even raising his heart rate".

## Ch. 32–33: Barriss calms Jos's mind; leap and saber against a battle droid
- Tolk and Jos: "Kuuta velomin" ("Seize the moment"). Jos refuses because of enster duty. Bota is a broad-spectrum antibiotic, hallucinogen or nutrient depending on species, and the fields are guarded by a "low-level zap field".
- **Calming another mind with the Force:**
> "Flowing with the Force, very lightly, she touched the tight, knotted strings of his dilemma, quieting their thrumming as a finger passed over the strings of a quetarra could subdue a chord." (MS1 ch. 33)
- **The attack:** a battle droid's chest-cannon blast superheats a tree's sap and turns it into "an organic bomb". Barriss is knocked unconscious, then:
> "She seemed to levitate—one moment she was sprawled on the ground, and the next she stood upright." / "the Padawan leapt across the bota field, covering a distance of at least ten meters in a single bound." / "she struck a single blow with the energy weapon that sheared halfway through the droid’s torso." (MS1 ch. 33)
- Her blade is "an azure streak about a meter in length" (blue in this book; it is green in other canon).
- She blames herself: "Had I been more mindful of the Force, that droid would never have gotten close enough to attack us." The droid was battle-damaged with poor targeting.

## Ch. 34: Ji the media "hero"
- Den's hit piece on Ji is "twirled" by his editors into a hero story ("The Hero of Drongar"), because the public is tired of grim news: "battles lost here, systems cut off there".
- I-Five's recovered memory fragments suggest he did "heroic" things in the past. Constructed as he is, he "could last five hundred, a thousand years or more" (Jos). Zan calls himself a "devout agnostic".

## Ch. 35: CT-914 dies; Kaird kills Bleyd
- Lore: the Drall scientist Tiran proved light speed was the barrier "more than thirty-five thousand years ago". Early colonization used generation ships. Hyperdrive came from "negative pressure fields", which "led to the Great Hyperspace War".
- CT-914 and most of his platoon are killed in a Separatist guerrilla ambush. Clones are decanted at Tipoca City.
- Bleyd works out that Den was the spycam operator.
- **Bleyd vs Kaird, knife duel:**
  - Bleyd has a ryyk blade (a small Wookiee-style knife) and claims "I’ve killed half a dozen opponents with this very blade".
  - Kaird: "You’re larger and much stronger, true, but I’m far faster."
  > "he lunged, impossibly fast, and the short knife flicked out in a blur. Bleyd saw it coming, but even so, he barely had time to get his own knife in position for the block." (MS1 ch. 35)
  - Kaird throws his knife. "It came too fast to dodge. Bleyd ... with reflexes honed by centuries of natural selection, managed to deflect it—barely. It nicked him on the hand".
  - The knife was coated in **dendriton toxin**, which "immolates" from the inside. Kaird cuts his throat out of mercy: "I’m an assassin ... getting the job done is more important than how I do it." Bleyd says he is "immune" to Jedi mind tricks (his claim only; never tested).
- **Speed ladder in this book:** Kaird > Bleyd (who blurs and plucks a flying spycam from the air) > ordinary humans. Bleyd thinks Ji has more skill than him but is slower.

## Ch. 36: Barriss at the edge of the dark side; Phow Ji's last stand
- **Force dreams:** when the Force first woke in her, the dreams were "frequent and powerful ... always leaving her with a sense of increased strength and control".
- **She hears a death cry across the marsh and sees the killer's face:**
> "It had been a cry for help, nonverbal, but no less strong ... she knew where it had come from—the edge of the Kondrus Sea ... she could see, as clearly as with her own eyes, the killer’s face as he loomed over his victim. Phow Ji." (MS1 ch. 36)
- **Ji alone vs 7 Separatist soldiers:** all seven are dead. Ji has only "a shallow vibroblade wound on his right forearm, and a blistered left cheekbone where a laser beam had narrowly missed".
- Ji names his styles: "I am a master of teräs käsi, of Hapan, echani, tae-jitsu, and a dozen other deadly styles."
- **Barriss's dark-side power:**
> "She wanted nothing more than to turn him into a pile of ashes, and, worse yet, she knew that she could. The dark power lived and shouted within her. It wouldn’t even require an effort—all she had to do was release it." (MS1 ch. 36)
  - It is never resolved: "would she have succumbed to the dark side or not? She never got the chance to find out."
- **Ji's limit:** a hidden sniper hits him with a neurotoxin dart. "For all his vaunted strength, skill, and speed, there was no way Ji could have dodged something he hadn’t seen coming." Barriss: "had she not been blinded with rage at Ji, she might have sensed the intended attack in time", so **rage blinds her danger sense**. She uses an "expanded ... bubble of awareness".
- **Barriss Force-heals neurotoxin poisoning** (Paraleptin or Titroxinate-class CNS paralysis) and saves him. Ji is "furious".

### Phow Ji's last stand (droid holo footage)
- His draw is "so fast that the action was a blur". He blinds a combat droid scout with twin shots to its sensors, then kills it with six or seven bolts into "the chink under its control box".
- Running and firing two-handed, he kills 3 Salissians with "fatal body strikes", body-slams one, shoots another, destroys a **super battle droid** with three shots, and snaps a neck.
- "two more mercs emerged from the woods, and Ji drew both blasters and shot the guns out of their hands!" Then a hammer fist to the temple and an elbow to the throat. He empties both blasters into the trees, then fights hand-to-hand in the woods (a merc "pinwheeled into the clearing").
- He takes a slug to the thigh and a blaster bolt through the right side, and keeps fighting.
- A drop ship lands 12 soldiers by repulsor pack: "Ji shot eight of them, leaping, dodging, and rolling ... It was a Jedi-like display of acrobatic skill". He goes down, then **sets off a thermal grenade** and takes the rest with him.
- **Tally:** roughly 20 or more enemies (7 earlier that day, then about 3+2+1+2+2+several+8+the remaining 4) and 2 droids.
- Barriss: "He was m’nuush" (Wookiee). Trandoshan: "davjäan inyameet—the ‘burning in the blood’", berserk. She tells Den his aura "has definite glimmerings of hero" (aura-reading).

## Ch. 37: Rimsoo Seven evacuates
- A battle droid force of "more than eight hundred" is under 10 km away across Jackhack Slough. Particle-weapon hits overload the main shield generator, then the backup.
- Scorched-earth policy: herbicide is sprayed on any bota left behind. A thermal bomb lands 500 m away.

## Ch. 38–39: I-Five's hidden abilities; Den saves the quetarra
- **I-Five's hidden systems come back online:**
> "the droid’s right index finger ... fired a needle beam of bright red coherent light ... incinerating a one-meter-wide path" / "I didn’t know you packed a laser." "I didn’t either, until about thirty minutes ago ... Another link in my grid became accessible. I also have some unique harmonic vocalization abilities" (MS1 ch. 38)
  - His skin electrostatically repels mud, and he can produce cleaning ultrasound. His recovery is "heuristic" and speeds up as it goes.
  - He worries he has been "programmed ... to sacrifice myself"; he wants to choose for himself.
- Den runs into shrapnel and fire to save Zan's quetarra (the case's scorched lid saves him), which undercuts his cynicism.

## Ch. 40: Zan dies
- The carrier is hit; Zan takes "a piece of shrapnel in the brain stem" and dies instantly, smiling. Jos refuses Barriss's Force comfort: "I won’t be anesthetized. I owe him that much."
- **Column, the spy, didn't decode the warning in time:** "The message the spy had not gotten around to translating earlier had been, of course, a warning of impending attack." The spy works for "Dooku [and] Black Sun", and is now on the evacuation transport, so the spy is among the regular characters.
- **Barriss's limits, stated outright:**
> "She had not been able to deflect the attack that had killed Zan—she hadn’t even felt it coming. And after the metal splinter had lodged in the base of his skull, she had still not been able to save him, though she had used every bit of the power supposedly under her control." (MS1 ch. 40)
  - She cannot heal brain-stem destruction. "The immensity of the war seemed far beyond the capabilities of the few remaining Jedi".
  - "Barriss reached out with the Force, but the droid’s mind, as others like it, was untouchable." She **cannot touch droid minds**, which contrasts with her physical reading of I-Five's hardware in ch. 29. I-Five's memory recovery has stopped after the impact.
- I-Five: "if droids ran things, war would not be an approved activity."

## Epilogue
- Rimsoo Seven reopens 80 km southeast. Jos and Tolk get together ("Life is too short"), then go back to work as medlifters arrive.
