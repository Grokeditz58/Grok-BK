# 056 · *Dark Forces Official Player's Guide* (1995), filed in MEGA's "Prima Guides" folder but **not a Prima book**

- **Status:** DONE. I hand-read all 259 PDF pages: front matter, Chapter 1 Strategy Briefing, the Chapter 2 shoot-through of all 14 missions, the 16-page color map insert, Chapter 3 The Making of Dark Forces, Chapter 4 Script & Conceptual Art, and the back matter (ad page and back cover).
- **Source:** MEGA handle `QC1G0ByK` (`Star_Wars_Dark_Forces_Official_Guide.pdf`). It is an image-only scan with no text layer. I ran two OCR passes over every page (a plain pass, then a thresholded pass that recovers the gray Jedi Tip boxes) and checked the layout pages, sidebars and credits against the page images. The source file is not committed.
- **Bibliographic details:** by Jeff Hoff; Infotainment World, Inc., San Mateo CA, 1995; ISBN 1-57280-022-4; $18.95 USA / $26.95 Canada. **Publisher note:** the MEGA folder is named "Prima Guides", but the imprint page credits Infotainment World (an International Data Group company). The guide also disclaims official sanction by the game companies.
- **Page mapping:** printed page = PDF page − 6 up to the color insert (PDF pp. 51–66, unnumbered). After the insert, printed page = PDF page − 22. All citations give the PDF page.
- **Quote checking:** every double-quoted passage was matched against the OCR text, and every cited page was checked. The few quotes that do not match the OCR exactly are cases where the OCR garbles a character ("6O-hour", "Mohe", "\V", "(Vm") or where the text appears only as art (cover type, the "Mission Maps" heading). Each of these is flagged in the notes and was checked on the page image.

## Files
- `df-01.md`: front matter, cheat codes, Strategy Briefing (weapons and bestiary), the mission shoot-through with all of Kyle's in-game lines and the story beats, and the Jedi Tips recovered by the second OCR pass (PDF pp. 1–222).
- `df-02.md`: Chapter 3, The Making of Dark Forces. It covers the team, the engine, how the story went from Luke to Kyle, the 32 → 14 missions, why Jan Ors was created, the AutoCAD levels, testing, Bajakian's sound and themes, the sequel tease, and the full credits with the voice cast (PDF pp. 223–234).
- `df-03.md`: Chapter 4, Justin Chin's game script. It covers the opening crawl, Operation Skyhook, Mohc's introduction and backstory (Clone Wars veteran, opposed the Death Star), Kyle as a former Imperial officer, Moff Rebus, Phrik, the three-phase supply chain, Boba Fett as "Jabba the Hutt's hired gun", the ending, and the back matter (PDF pp. 235–259).

## Strongest findings
- The story first "revolved around Luke Skywalker and the original Star Wars plot" before Kyle Katarn was created (PDF p. 227).
- Jan Ors exists because the cut scenes were moved into their own engine (PDF p. 229).
- Chin's first design document had "descriptions for 32 missions", which were cut to 14 (PDF p. 228).
- Mohc is "A decorated soldier in the Clone Wars" and "one of the few generals to oppose the construction of the Death Star" (PDF p. 247).
- Mohc on Kyle: "Katarn was once an impressive Imperial officer" (PDF p. 246).
