# *Dark Forces Official Player's Guide* (1995): part 3, Chapter 4 "Dark Forces Script & Conceptual Art" and the back matter (printed pp. 213–234, PDF pp. 235–259)

**Source:** Jeff Hoff, *Dark Forces Official Player's Guide*, Infotainment World, 1995. MEGA `QC1G0ByK`. I read this from OCR and checked it against the page images. Citations give the PDF page; printed page = PDF page − 22. The chapter reprints Justin Chin's screenplay-format game script, with all the cut-scene dialogue, and Hoff's linking paragraphs between the missions. Concept sketches run along the margins. I read every page, then the back matter (PDF pp. 257–259).

## Chapter opener (PDF pp. 235–236)
- PDF p. 235 is the chapter title over a full-page ink-wash sketch of a figure in a long coat and cap.
- **Credit for the dark troopers:** "General Mohc, Darth Vader, and the Emperor are not responsible for dark troopers threatening the Rebel Alliance. The blame for introducing the doomsday army into the universe falls on the narrow shoulders of Justin Chin." He "hammered out the original script and design documents in five days, then worked with others at LucasArts to cut missions and create the Dark Forces script. This is the document the level designers, artists, programmers, animators — everyone — used as a guide to create Dark Forces." The OCR misses the first letter of "General" (a drop cap).
- The sketches are "original conceptual sketches by Justin Chin, Paul Mica, and others on the art team."

## Introduction and Mission I (PDF pp. 236–238)
- **The opening crawl** introduces Kyle: "the Rebels have employed the skills of Kyle Katarn. Known to most as a mercenary for hire, Katarn is a rogue figure who has a partial alliance with the Rebels. Armed only with a blaster pistol and an intimate knowledge of Imperial methods, Katarn prepares to infiltrate the Imperial base in which the plans are kept". The planet is "Danuta".
- **The Mission I briefing is titled "The Death Star Plans, Operation Skyhook, Phase 2."** Mon Mothma: "Operation Skyhook will deliver the plans to Princess Leia, on the consular ship Tantive" IV. The OCR reads "\V". (PDF p. 237)
- The narrator ties it to the film: "Kyle delivers the plans to the Rebel Alliance. Soon afterwards, the Death Star is destroyed.... But even as the Alliance celebrates this victory, another sinister plot is set in motion". (PDF p. 238)
- **Mohc's introduction:** "DARTH VADER addresses GENERAL MOHC, a soldier from the old school who prefers mechanized combat troops over antiseptic, hands-off weapons such as the Death Star. Its failure is his opportunity." The OCR reads "cover" for "over". Mohc: "We will be able to decimate the Rebels just as we did the Jedi Knights. At last the Emperor's war will be filled only with the glory and beauty of decisive victory." Then: "Dark trooper release." (PDF p. 238)

## The Talay attack and Kyle's recruitment (PDF pp. 239–241)
- The launch sequence: "INT. ARC HAMMER — DARK TROOPER LAUNCH TUBES". Vader: "Very impressive, General. The Emperor will be most pleased. Continue with your project." (PDF p. 239)
- Mon Mothma: "Five days ago the Empire attacked one of our secret bases in the city of Talay." The Rebel on the hologram: "This is Tak Base to anybody out there ... please ... we're under Imperial attack". Then: "Tak Base was destroyed in minutes." (PDF p. 240)
- **Madine as a defector:** "This Imperial officer, Crix Madine, wishes to defect to the Alliance. He has supplied us with information on the development of a new Imperial weapon." The weapon is "a new type of stormtrooper, the dark trooper." (PDF p. 240)
- **Kyle's two best lines (PDF p. 241):** "I should've kept working for the Empire." and "All right, I'm in. But I think I'll need some help on this one. I want Jan Ors as my mission officer." Hoff's link says the script holds "story descriptions for each of the 13 missions" that follow.

## Missions II–V: the dark trooper supply chain (PDF pp. 242–245)
- **Mission II** (Talay: TAK Base: After the Massacre): Jan says, "Good job on" "Operation Skyhook, the Rebels were very impressed. Now it’s back to mission hopping, \ just like the old days." The prize is "a broken repeater rifle abandoned by the dark troopers during the massacre". (PDF p. 242; the "\" is OCR noise from a sketch.)
- **Mission III** (Anoat City): "I found the symbol “M.R.” on the barrel — the signature of an infamous weapons specialist named Moff Rebus, a real character known for paranoia and extreme loyalty to the Empire." (PDF p. 243)
- **Mission IV** (The Planet Fest): Rebus, under questioning, said: "You shoulda seen the results of our tests on Fest — my repeater's gonna put a lot of holes in a lot of Rebel scum." The goal is "a hunk of rare metal alloy called Phrik." (PDF p. 244)
- **Mission V** (Gromas): "The metal that you found is called Phrik, a rare material that they must be using for armoring the dark troopers." In Hoff's link: "It's only a Phase One dark trooper (Phase Two is nearly complete and Phase Three is the real McCoy)". (PDF p. 245)

## Mission VI: Boba Fett enters (PDF pp. 246–247)
- The scene: "GENERAL MOHC points a pistol at CRIX MADINE, the captured Rebel spy. Darth Vader looks on ominously." The OCR reads "MOH". The setting is "INT. ARC HAMMER — LAUNCH BAY", yet Jan's briefing on the same page says Madine is "being held at the Imperial Detention Center on Orinackra".
- Vader: "Katarn will not be as easy to deal with. He is very" "resourceful — more resourceful, it seems, than" "your dark troopers." Part of the line is overprinted by a sketch.
- **Mohc confirms Kyle's backstory:** "Katarn was once an impressive Imperial officer, but he was weak and gave up on the struggle for our new order." Then: "My new hire will see to that...". "From the darkness behind Madine emerges BOBA FETT, Jabba the Hutt's hired gun, looking silent and deadly." (PDF p. 246)
- The mission is titled "Imperial Detention Center, Orinackra: Crix Madine’s Fate." Madine is in "the XX block". (PDF pp. 246–247)

## Mission VII: Rom Mohc's backstory (PDF pp. 247–248), the key lore passage
- "Crix Madine informs us that the leader of the dark trooper project is a general named Rom Mohc. You might remember him from your time with the Empire. A decorated soldier in the Clone Wars, Mohc became very prominent during the Emperor's rise to power. He was one of the few generals to oppose the construction of the Death Star because of his distrust of any technology that separates the warrior from battle and engaging the enemy. He's absolutely obsessed with the honor of personal combat." (PDF p. 247)
- **The three phases of construction:** "The first one is the Gromas Mines, which you so effectively blew to bits, where they mine the alloy resource, Phrik. The resulting alloy is then transported to the Robotics Facility, where it is treated and refined. The alloy is then taken to a special cruiser, the Arc Hammer, where the final composition and assembly of the dark trooper takes place." (PDF p. 248)
- "General Mohc has solicited the help of an underworld kingpin, unknown to us at this time. This kingpin supplies" Mohc "with smugglers". The OCR reads "Mohe". The route starts at "Ramsees Hed, a spaceport on the planet Cal-Seti." (PDF p. 248)

## Missions VIII–X: Anteevy, Nar Shaddaa, Jabba (PDF pp. 249–251)
- **Mission VIII** (Robotics Construction Facility, Anteevy: Ice Station Beta): Jan says, "It looks like it’s freezing out there. I'm glad I'm not you." (PDF p. 249)
- **Mission IX** (Nar Shaddaa, the Vertical City: The Death Mark): "I got word that he has placed a healthy death mark on your head. Every bounty hunter in the system will be out for your hide." The objective "is called a nava card. And it’s your only ticket to the Arc Hammer." Then "The ENORMOUS SHIP of Jabba the Hutt fills the screen." (PDF p. 250)
- **Mission X, Jabba:** "So this is Katarn, the one that has been a thorn in Mohc's side. Your reputation has traveled far. Too bad you have interfered with my affairs." Kyle: "There's nothing like roast Kell dragon." The OCR garbles the speaker tag and the start of Kyle's line. Jabba: "Throw him in!" (PDF p. 251)
- **Reading note:** this is as close as the guide comes to naming Jabba as Mohc's "underworld kingpin". Jabba's line implies a link, and the Mission VI stage direction calls Fett "Jabba the Hutt's hired gun", but the guide never states that Jabba is the kingpin.

## Missions XI–XIV and the ending (PDF pp. 252–256)
- **Mission XI** (Imperial City, Coruscant: The Imperial Mask): "The card is an Imperial military encrypted navigation device that plugs into a decryption key box". The target is "the Imperial Security Operations building, the center of covert operations and intelligence for the Empire". The Boba Fett tease: "Remember the other mercenary that General Mohc hired a while back? When you try to reach your ship, you won't be alone." (PDF p. 252)
- **Mission XII** (Imperial Fuel Station, Ergo: Smuggler’s Hijack): "for security reasons, the Arc Hammer makes periodic jumps across the galaxy, always hiding its location. The only ship that knows those locations is the Executor." Jan: "It would get pretty lonely back at the Rebellion without you to worry about." (PDF p. 253)
- **Mission XIII** (The Executor: The Stowaway): Hoff's PDA joke: "(Personal Digital Assistant — apparently the term has survived a few hundred years since its coining in the early 1990s on the planet Earth)". (PDF p. 254)
- **Mission XIV** (The Arc Hammer: The Dark Awakening): "Maybe he can finally rid the universe of Darth Vader as well. (Maybe not.)" Jan: "The Arc Hammer is a large cruiser that acts both as the construction facility and as a launch platform for the dark trooper." (PDF p. 255)
- **Ending:** Vader watches the Arc Hammer explode: "This is an unfortunate setback. The Force is strong with Katarn." Then "MON MOTHMA stands before KYLE and JAN. Mon Mothma hands them a MEDAL." The last line is "What's next for our reluctant hero?" (PDF p. 256)
- **Note:** the script never shows Mohc's death. The finale fight is covered only in the shoot-through (part 1, PDF pp. 217–218).
