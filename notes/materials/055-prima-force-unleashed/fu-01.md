## The Cast (pp. 8–20). The bios are long and LucasArts-supplied; key lore below.
- **The Secret Apprentice (p. 8):** Vader "kidnapped the boy after striking down his Jedi father"; "no other Imperials—including the Emperor—know of his existence"; "Vader subjected the boy to rigorous physical tests bordering on torture"; code name "Starkiller" and the *Rogue Shadow*. Vader's hope: "standing at Vader’s side as they confront and destroy the Emperor together."
- **Juno Eclipse (pp. 8–9):**
  - Born on Corulag, with a cold pro-Imperial father. "At fourteen, Juno was the youngest student ever accepted into the Corulag Academy."
  - **The Battle of Callos:** Vader ordered her to destroy "a massive planetary reactor" and "the resulting explosion caused global climate changes, wiping out nearly all life on the planet".
  - The bio reaches **past the game's opening into the endgame**: Vader "had branded Juno a traitor", she was transported to "the Empirical", and she was saved by Starkiller, "who was indeed alive". The bio spoils the mid-game.
- **PROXY (p. 9):** "a prototype holodroid ... Although PROXY’s origins are unknown". He is "programmed with a sinister prime directive to kill his master at the first available opportunity ... never realizing that success would end their friendship forever." Box: "The battle with PROXY occurs only in the Xbox 360 or PlayStation 3 versions".
- **Rahm Kota (p. 9):**
  - Fought on his homeworld "before his tenth year"; at 18 he met Mace Windu, who brought him to Coruscant to train "under the watchful eye of Jedi Master Yoda".
  - Built a non-clone army "from local militias, hardened mercenary groups, and even Separatist prisoners of war". "His misgivings about the clones saved Kota’s life when Order 66 was issued"; he "hijacked a Republic Cruiser" afterward.
- **Bail Organa (p. 10):** "weapons have been banned from Alderaan". He funnels supplies "to Shaak Ti, Rahm Kota, and others" and later "ensured that the blinded Jedi Master was safely transported to Cloud City". "When the drunk and blind Kota refused, Bail set out in search of Shaak Ti for aid, only to be captured by Maris Brood on Felucia."
- **Leia (p. 10):**
  - "at only 16 she became the youngest senator in galactic history".
  - Sent to Kashyyyk "as a “senatorial observer,” essentially putting her directly in harm’s way. If Bail spoke out against the Emperor again, Princess Leia would meet an untimely end, allegedly killed by berserk Wookiee Slaves...."
- **Mon Mothma (p. 10):** "the youngest person elected to the Republic Senate, an honor she held until the rise of Princess Leia Organa".
- **Garm Bel Iblis (p. 11):** "Garm was forced to watch as his wife and children were executed". His escape was "secretly aided by his allies in the senate—Bail Organa and Mon Mothma. Inspired by the mysterious Starkiller, the three have begun to discuss taking more direct action".
- **Master Kento (p. 11), the Apprentice's father:**
  - Met "a young Jedi freedom fighter named Mallie" while fighting on Talus. "Kento and Mallie secretly married".
  - "On the eve of Order 66, Kento and Mallie disguised themselves as medics and slipped away into the Outer Rim".
  - Settled on Kashyyyk. "Mallie was killed while defending several Wookiees from a group of mercenary slavers". Vader then "discovered and kidnapped the powerful boy".
- **Imperial enemies (pp. 12–14):**
  - Stormtrooper Commanders "continue the reputation established by the Clone Wars’ fearsome ARC troopers".
  - Shadow Troopers have "experimental cloaking armor".
  - EVO Troopers: "many EVO Troopers are handpicked from Stormtroopers who have displayed a hatred toward nonhumans".
  - Incinerator Troopers.
  - Jumptroopers carry a "rail detonator".
  - Purge Troopers are "part of the top-secret Imperial Dark Trooper project ... protected by refined cortosis".
  - Senate Guards use "cortosis pikes".
  - Royal Guard: "suggestions as to its numbers have ranged from 50 to tens of thousands".
  - Interrogation droids appear aboard "the ISS Empirical".
  - **Shadow Guards (p. 14):** "some Imperials ... speculate that the Shadow Guard are, in fact, Jedi who have been captured, tortured, and brainwashed by the insidious Palpatine. Others believe the Shadow Guard are simply loyal members of Palpatine’s red-robed Imperial Royal Guard, who have been secretly trained in the basic Sith arts."
  - AT-CT; AT-KT: "(since dead Wookiees make poor slaves)".
- **Kashyyyk, Raxus, Felucian and Cloud City enemies (pp. 15–17):**
  - Rodian salvage cartel: Rippers and Heavy Defenders, who "have run afoul of Kazdan Paratus". Jawas were transplanted to Raxus Prime.
  - Kazdan's Force-animated "Scrap Guardians", "Scrap Drones" (the text calls them "Scrap Guardians" again by mistake: "Another of Kazdan Paratus’s Force-imbued creations, the Scrap Guardians are devious flying golems designed specifically to combat Darth Vader"), and the Junk Titan.
  - Felucians: "When Shaak Ti was murdered, the Felucians succumbed to anger and hatred ... their features twisting into hideous masks." Yerdua poison spitters.
  - "the near-mythical Bull Rancor ... guards the legendary “Rancor graveyard” on Felucia"; Maris Brood "has formed a strong bond with the ferocious beast."
  - Ugnaughts and "Uggernaughts" (exoskeletons by Bespin Motors) on "the half-finished Cloud City".
- **Kota's Militia (p. 18):** Saboteurs, Elites and Troopers.
- **Bosses (pp. 18–20):**
  - **Kazdan Paratus (pp. 18–19):**
    - Passed the Jedi Trials by "constructing a sophisticated and deadly combat droid"; became an instructor "at the School of Padawans"; used a spider-leg harness and a "lightsaber pike".
    - At Order 66 he "suffered a moment of panic ... fled Coruscant in a nondescript freighter". On Raxus Prime "He even constructed a version of the Jedi Temple, complete with replicas of the Jedi Council members."
  - **Shaak Ti (p. 19):**
    - Fought at Geonosis, Kamino, "Centares and Dagu". "Shaak Ti was deep in meditation when Darth Vader and his forces stormed the Jedi Temple"; she fled after saving Padawans.
    - "she had already trained two Padawans in her career, only to see both fall victim to criminal killers shortly before their graduation to Knighthood." "Shaak Ti became a respected Felucian Chieftain".
  - **Captain Ozzik Sturn (p. 19):** "Born to a long line of celebrated trophy hunters". As "chief magistrate on Malastare" he ran "his own secret hunting reserve" with prisoners, which caused "a revolt among the native Dug and Gran populations". Now on Kashyyyk he hunts Wookiee warriors.
  - **Maris Brood (pp. 19–20):**
    - "tutored by a Jedi warrior aboard the starship Gray Pilgrim". Her master "took his starfighter in search of answers. He never returned".
    - Captured Bail "hoping to trade him to Darth Vader in exchange for her life." "She has also learned to use the Force to mask herself and become invisible".
  - **Vader (p. 20):** "harbors much anger toward his master and secretly plots to overthrow him."
  - **The Emperor (p. 20):** "Palpatine promoted the doctrines of hatred, speciesism, and tyranny."

## Wielding the Force / Becoming the Sith (pp. 21–35)
- **Controls (pp. 21–25):** the guide lists Xbox first, then PS3, covering Force Dash, lock-on, blocking ("You cannot move while blocking."), Lightsaber Throw, grapples, Lightning, Push, Grip, Force Throw, Counter Force Grip ("repeatedly pressing the Jump button"), Force Lock and Lightsaber Lock, Repulse and Shield, plus the HUD.
- **Upgrade system (p. 26):** Force powers, combos and talents. Leveling grants "three upgrade spheres"; Holocrons hold "anything from 1,000 Force Points to Force ability upgrade spheres."
- **Combos (pp. 26–31):**
  - Sith Shien: "An ancient Sith variation of a classic lightsaber fighting style".
  - Sith Saber Flurry, Dashing Slash, Leaping Slash and Slam, Saber Blast, Cannonball.
  - Lightning Grenade: "the perfect way to turn an enemy on his comrades...as an explosive".
  - Aerial Strike, Saber Sling, Dashing Blast, Sith Strike, Sith Slash, Sith Saber Smash, Lightning Bomb, Dashing Shock, Saber Slam, and the Aerial Ambush, Assault, Blast, Shock, Throw and Slam family.
- **Talents (p. 31):** Fortitude, Force Focus, Force Affinity, Vitality, Resilience, Battle Meditation, Saber Mastery, Defense Mastery, Combo Mastery.
- **Crystals (p. 32):** opens with an epigraph from Luminara in *Clone Wars Vol. 1*: "The crystal is the heart of the blade. The heart is the crystal of the Jedi."
  - The eight combat crystals are Ilum (no bonus), Rubat, Ruusan, Lorrdian, Sigil, Vexxtal, Firkrann and Katak. Example: "Katak combat crystal: Lightsaber attacks have a chance to siphon health from opponents."
- **Training Room (pp. 32–35):** five tutorials and challenges (Push, Flourish, Grip, Lightning, Repulse) plus combat modules (Stormtrooper, Rodian, Felucian, Imperial Assault and Armed Recon, Royal Guard, AT-ST, Junk Titan, Kota's Militia, Rancor, Raxus Droids). "By completing all the tutorials and challenges, respectively, you’ll get two Xbox 360 Achievements!"
