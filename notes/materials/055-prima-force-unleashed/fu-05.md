# Prima *The Force Unleashed* guide — part 5: the bound-in **Wii** guide, front matter to Shaak Ti (Wii pp. 1–49)

This PDF contains a second, complete guide after the PS3/360 one: *Prima Official Game Guide for the Wii*, PDF pp. 114–226. It has its own page numbers, and **Wii printed page = PDF page − 114**. Citations below are "Wii p. N". The Wii game has a different level list from the PS3/360 game: it adds the Jedi Temple Trials, Nar Shaddaa and Duel mode. That is the reason to read this guide in full rather than skim it.

## Front matter (Wii pp. 1–3)
- **Credits page:** "Written by Fernando Bueno". It gives the same ISBN as the PS3/360 guide, "978-0-7615-5916-0", and the same LCCN "2008920935". "Texture Maps: David Bueno" is credited again.
- **Author bio:** the same text as the main guide, ending with the author's work e-mail address, which I have left out. It includes "Fernando “Red Star” Bueno (aka dukkhah)" and "In his time off he enjoys the works of Hermann Hesse, Johann Van Goethe, Franz Kafka, and EGM."
- **Contents (Wii pp. 2–3):** the level list is:
  - Prologue; TIE Construction Yard; **Trial of Skill**; Raxus Prime; **Trial of Insight**; Felucia; Vader’s Flagship; **Nar Shaddaa**; **Trial of Spirit**; Kashyyyk; Dark Felucia; Imperial Raxus Prime; Cloud City; Corellia; Death Star.
  - Then **Duel Mode** (Wii p. 93) and Sith Secrets (Wii p. 99).
  - Unlike the PS3/360 game, there is no Empirical level and no Imperial-era Felucia under that name; the guide calls it "Dark Felucia".

## Controls: motion-control text (Wii pp. 4–12)
- "Only the Wii’s motion-sensing controls allow you to use the Wii Remote and Nunchuk controllers to truly emulate battle with the lightsaber and Force powers." (Wii p. 5)
- Force Push: "thrust the Nunchuk forward (much like a lunge attack with your lightsaber) as if you’re pushing the air." (Wii p. 6)
- Force Choke: "Hold 6 then turn the Nunchuk upside down." The guide calls it "by far, one of your most underrated powers." (Wii p. 10)
- Force Maelstrom is a Wii-only power: "Activate Force Maelstrom by holding down 3, 4, 5, and 6 to focus your Force, then swing both the Wii Remote and Nunchuk in a downward motion simultaneously." (Wii p. 7)
- Sith Scorcher: "If you upgrade only one Force power, make it this one." (Wii p. 11)
- Upgrade cost: "20,000 to activate, then 40,000, 60,000, and 100,000 to upgrade each subsequent level" (Wii p. 9).
- **Combat crystals (Wii p. 12):** there are six: Kaiburr (Push), Qixoni (Choke), Opila (’saber throw), Dragite (’saber damage), Firkrann (Lightning) and Damind ("Greatly increases strength of lightsaber damage").
  - Note: on the PS3/360 side, Firkrann is a crit-chance crystal (main guide). On the Wii it "increases Force Lightning strength".
- **Epigraph (Wii p. 12):** "“The crystal is the heart of the blade. The heart is the crystal of the Jedi. The Jedi is the crystal of the Force. The Force is the blade of the heart. All are intertwined. The crystal, the blade...the Jedi. You are one.” —Master Luminara Unduli, Star Wars: The Clone Wars Volume 1"

## Prologue: Vader on Kashyyyk (Wii pp. 13–17)
- "There are 200 Holocrons to collect in Star Wars: The Force Unleashed." The PS3/360 guide's total is 115.
- Vader leads "Triton Squad".
- **Ending (Wii p. 17):** "the lightsaber flies out of your hand and into the hands of a child! You turn to find that it was not his master you sensed, but his son. Rather than let the child perish at the hands of the Imperial forces or, worse, the Emperor, you resolve to make him your secret apprentice."

## TIE Construction Yard (Wii pp. 18–26)
- Juno is introduced here: "According to PROXY’s personnel file, Captain Juno Eclipse is a capable, war-hardened, highly decorated combat pilot. You need no more than a glance to see that she is beautiful as well." (Wii p. 19)
- The order: "you must leave no witnesses—eliminate Rebel and Imperial forces alike."
- **Kota's look (Wii p. 25):** "His attacks on the Imperial shipyards weren’t meant to get your attention, but rather your master’s."
- **End (Wii p. 26):** "He rambles on about your future and how he sees only...himself? It’s too late. Your ’saber burns his eyes, and your final attack sends him flying out into space."

## Trial of Skill: Darth Desolous (Wii pp. 27–29), a Wii-only level
- "The Jedi Temple holds many secrets; there is much to fear within its walls."
- Vader "steals Kota’s ’saber from you and quickly draws the blade on you... your lack of focus concerns him" (Wii p. 27).
- **Darth Desolous (Wii p. 28):** "you’re mysteriously surrounded by a red electrical current and tossed aside. It is no normal current, however, but a Force attack! Ironically, this is no Jedi attack, but rather a Sith assault. Your next opponent, Darth Desolous, emerges from the Temple’s shadows."
  - Closing line: "You’ve destroyed a Jedi. Now you’ve destroyed a Sith as well." (Wii p. 29)

## Raxus Prime: Drexl and the junk titan (Wii pp. 30–37)
- The section title is "“Where All Droids Go to Die...”". The enemies are Junk Golems, a Junkyard Dog Golem, a Junk Behemoth, "Drexl: Minor Boss" and a Junk Titan. These differ from the PS3/360 Jawas and scrap.
- From the air, "Paratus’s home looks much like a Jedi Temple" (Wii p. 31).
- **Drexl (Wii pp. 32, 35):** he is a Rodian bandit boss who "offers to help you find Paratus—for a price." He later learns your identity: "Somehow he’s managed to discover that you’re Vader’s apprentice and, being a greedy Rodian, has decided to sell you to the Emperor."
  - Guide joke: "If you attempt to overpower the Rodian, he’ll cut you like a block of Coruscant’s finest cheese."
  - "The battle with Drexl is one of the only boss encounters where you won’t have to follow onscreen prompts to finish him off."
- **Paratus (Wii pp. 36–37):** "Paratus and the Puppet". "The pint-sized puppet master creates several large rock-shard pillars".

## Trial of Insight: Darth Phobos (Wii pp. 38–42), a Wii-only level
- Afterwards, Paratus "is nothing more than “an outcast.”" (Wii p. 39).
- In the Temple, "you hear a mysterious voice warning you to not be blinded by anger." (Wii p. 40)
- **Face Your Fears (Wii p. 41):** "Inside, you spot Juno. She looks hurt, bent over as if in pain. When you approach, Juno leaps into the air and transforms in a blue puff of smoke. She’s not Juno—it’s Darth Phobos! Just as her name suggests, she feeds on phobias. Your fears give her strength."
- **End (Wii p. 42):** "in a weak attempt to dissuade you from destroying her, she once again transforms into Juno. You ignore her pleas and run your ’saber through her chest, thus ending the confrontation with fear itself."

## Felucia: Juno and Callos (Wii pp. 43–49)
- **"It Reminds Me of Callos." (Wii p. 44):** "There, she led her troops into battle and emerged victorious—but not before she, as part of the Imperial force, razed the planet and decimated it." Then: "What was once a lush, green planet teeming with life is now a barren world. And it was because of her."
- Brood: "There’s a hint of darkness in Brood, and Shaak Ti knows this."
- **The Last of the Council (Wii p. 49):** the Sarlacc grabs Shaak Ti's rancor. She "creates a protective bubble around herself" to regenerate. At the end, "she tries convincing you that you could be so much more than a Sith Lord and Vader’s puppet. Her words fall on deaf ears, and she falls into the chasm."
