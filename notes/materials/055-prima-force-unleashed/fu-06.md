# Prima *The Force Unleashed* guide — part 6: the Wii guide, Vader's Flagship to the end, Duel mode and Sith Secrets (Wii pp. 50–112)

Citations are "Wii p. N" (PDF page − 114).

## Vader's Flagship (Wii p. 50)
- The text matches the PS3/360 guide's: "this chapter is a bridge between Acts 1 and 2 ... a bridge between the prequel trilogy and the original trilogy."
- "Lord Vader believes that only a rebellion could sufficiently distract the Emperor. Before you and Vader strike, you must raise an army. A rebel army. You must form a Rebel Alliance...."
- "Back on the Rogue Shadow, you remember something Rahm Kota said during your first encounter. He said that he saw himself in your future. If what he said was true, then he must still be alive!"

## Nar Shaddaa: the drunk Kota (Wii pp. 51–54), a Wii-only level
- **Objective:** "Find General Kota on Nar Shaddaa and enlist him in your cause."
- **The bartender (Wii p. 52):** "after some “aggressive negotiations,” he admits that the run-down Jedi is “never more than stumbling distance” from the bar."
  - Once he is questioned, "Having used your Force powers to interrogate the bartender, you’ve exposed yourself as a Jedi, and now your head will bring in a hefty bounty!"
- **Kota (Wii p. 53):** "The run-down Kota is nothing more than a blubbering mess now. He’s been drinking his troubles away and has seemingly given up on the Jedi" … "ways. Ever since you took his sight in your first battle, he’s been on a downward spiral, even though he managed to salvage his life." (The text is split by a sidebar in the source.)
- **Shadow Guard boss (Wii p. 54):** "When the fight is over, Kota emerges half-drunk from behind the bar. ... he does know someone in the senate who could use your skills. He joins you... hesitantly."

## Trial of Spirit: the father (Wii pp. 55–58), a Wii-only level
- **Objective:** "Something is drawing you back to the Jedi Temple on Coruscant. ... You must prepare to confront your deepest fears."
- **Reunion (Wii p. 58):** "you exit to find your master battling a familiar figure. The rogue Jedi seems familiar, but you can’t place him. Apparently, you’ve arrived just in time, as the Jedi strikes down Darth Vader".
  - "Unfortunately, it is not until the Jedi lies dying on the ground that you realize who he is. His final words send shock waves of fear and regret through you. Just as his body fades, he says, “I’m sorry, son.”"
- **Comparison:** the PS3/360 guide gives this line to a ghost in the Kashyyyk hut (main p. 68). The Wii version stages a full boss duel with Kento in the Temple, where the apprentice kills him.

## Kashyyyk (Wii pp. 59–64)
- "Before touching down on Kashyyyk, upgrade your Force Lightning and Push powers to Level 3." (Wii p. 60)
- Sturn's walker "has backup shield generators as well as ground forces" (Wii p. 63).
- **End (Wii p. 64):** "You run your lightsaber through the pitiful captain and enter the building in the corner. Inside the building is what you’ve been after. It’s not a thing, but a young woman named Leia Organa". There is no Skyhook sequence. "Rather than joining you on the Rogue Shadow, she decides to go her separate way."

## Dark Felucia: Maris Brood (Wii pp. 65–69)
- The setup matches the PS3/360 one: Bail is held "as a prize for Darth Vader".
- **Bull rancor (Wii p. 68):** "You climb atop his back, slashing away, then leap from his tusk into his mouth where you cut your way out. Maris Brood’s pet bull rancor is nothing more than a pile of bull bones now...."
- Brood calls "another rancor beast". When she returns, "she has a full Health bar".
- The ending matches the main guide: "you decide to let her go."

## Imperial Raxus Prime (Wii pp. 70–76)
- **"You Still Serve Me." (Wii p. 71):** "Juno walks in on you while you seek Vader’s counsel, and though she’s disappointed in you for remaining loyal to him, she still remains loyal to you." In the next paragraph: "Perhaps she sees good in you still."
- The plan: "aim the cannon at the facility floating high above Raxus and let ’er rip!" The second Shadow Guard is the boss.
- **End (Wii p. 76):** "you rush out and use your Force Grip to bring the destroyer crashing down onto the planet’s surface. Mission accomplished."

## Cloud City: Chop'aa and Kleef (Wii pp. 77–84), different from the PS3/360 level
- **Objective:** "Go to Cloud City on Bespin and meet with Senator Garm Bel Iblis."
  - "In Cloud City, you’ll need to contact the chief administrator, Lobot." (Wii p. 77)
- **Enemies:** Twi’lek Mercenary, Mandalorian Spy, Gran Grenadier, Talz Medic, Whiphid Thug, Ithorian Smuggler, "Kleef and H-TFU Droids: Minor Boss", "Chop’aa and Minions: Boss".
- **Plot:**
  - Bel Iblis "is currently at the loading bay trying to negotiate with Chop’aa, the leader of the mercenaries on Cloud City" (Wii p. 78).
  - Then: "Senator Garm Bel Iblis was captured by a rogue named Kleef" (Wii p. 81).
  - Kleef: "Fry Kleef every chance you get; it’s the quickest way to whittle down the Gungan’s health." (Wii p. 82)
- **Chop’aa (Wii p. 84):** "Chop’aa hops in his basilisk and powers up." Later: "Chop’aa comes running out of the fire with his bo-staff".
- **End:** "Senator Bel Iblis has had enough of the Emperor. With threats against him and his family, he’s ready to take action and join the Rebellion."

## Corellia and the Death Star (Wii pp. 85–92)
- **Corellia (Wii p. 85):** "Standing around the table are Senator Organa, his daughter, Senator Bel Iblis, Kota, you, and Mon Mothma."
  - "You were just a tool to gather the Emperor’s enemies together to destroy them all at once."
  - "Kenobi’s body disappears, revealing PROXY’s fallen form."
- **"I’ve never been a Jedi before." (Wii p. 87):** "Realizing that this might be the last time she sees you, she finally reveals her true feelings...with a kiss."
- **A Choice (Wii p. 92):** the guide again declines to describe the endings: "Rather than give away the two possible endings, the following section provides tips on how to defeat the Emperor and Darth Vader."
  - Emperor tip: "The most effective attack against the Emperor is a Ground Slam."
  - Vader tip: "Vader relies less on the Force than the Emperor. The majority of his attacks are lightsaber attacks."

## Duel mode (Wii pp. 93–98)
- "Duel mode is a Nintendo Wii exclusive mode!" It has "a combined 27 Jedi and Sith characters from the Star Wars universe, you can relive or rewrite the Star Wars saga!" (Wii p. 93)
- **Force Unleashed state:** "Once Unleashed, your Force meter becomes unlimited, and your Force powers function at their maximum."
- **Nine arenas:** TIE Construction Yard, Jedi Temple, Raxus Prime Junkyard, Bespin Carbonite Chamber, Geonosis Colosseum, Hoth Battlefield, Kashyyyk Wookiee Village, Tatooine—Ben’s Hovel, and Felucia—Sarlacc Pit.
  - Ben's Hovel: "Obi-Wan Kenobi’s hovel makes for a great dueling place" (Wii p. 95).
  - Sarlacc Pit: "Force Throw him into the center of the Sarlacc Pit! Your enemy will fall straight into the beast’s mouth and be chewed into a pulp!"
  - Note: on Wii pp. 94–95 the paragraphs and headings for Raxus/Bespin/Geonosis/Hoth/Kashyyyk are offset by one, a layout error in the source. The Bespin-headed text describes the junkyard, and so on.
- **The profiles spoil both endings, which the walkthrough had kept secret (Wii p. 96):**
  - **Ultimate Good:** "Having walked the path of the dark side as Darth Vader’s slave, the secret apprentice turned on his master and the Emperor. Though he failed to crush the dark side once and for all, he made the ultimate sacrifice: He gave his life so that the Rebel Alliance might survive."
  - **Ultimate Evil:** "Rather than save his friend Rahm Kota, he gave in to his hatred and destroyed his old master, Darth Vader. When he realized what he’d done, he regretted his decision and turned his ’saber on the Emperor. Unfortunately, it was too late. The Emperor struck him down. When he woke up, the secret apprentice had been rebuilt into a symbol of ultimate evil."
- **Other profiles:**
  - Apprentice: "no other Imperials—including the Emperor—know of his existence" (Wii p. 95).
  - Kento's Robes: "the secret apprentice in Master Kento’s (his father’s) robes."
  - Darth Phobos: "her mind tricks can fool a person into thinking she is someone else." (Wii p. 98)
  - Shaak Ti "has since become a respected Felucian chieftain, commanding tribes of Force-sensitive natives." (Wii p. 97)
  - **Mara Jade (Wii p. 98), an anachronistic Legends summary:** "She first experienced the Force at the Emperor’s side as his Hand. Later she married Luke Skywalker and gave birth to their son, Ben. After becoming a Master, Mara Jade trained her niece in the ways of the Jedi."
  - Fighter stats are rated Low to Very High. Young Luke and Jedi Luke both have "Force Push: Very High".
- **Leftover cross-reference:** "For detailed histories of all these characters, refer to the “Cast” chapter of this guide." (Wii p. 95). The Wii guide has no Cast chapter; it is in the PS3/360 guide bound in front.

## Sith Secrets and Level Unlockables (Wii pp. 99–112)
- "The following pages were ripped from secret scrolls known only to the Sith. They reveal the locations of all Holocrons and color crystals, and how to obtain all unlockables." (Wii p. 99)
- **Checklists:** Prologue 20; TIE Yard 20 ("Holocron 19: When the door opens to Area 6, defeat all 90 enemies in the level to reveal the Holocron."); Trial of Skill 6 ("Holocron 6: Hidden behind the Sidious statue in Area 3."); Raxus 20; Trial of Insight 6; Felucia 27; Nar Shaddaa 10; Trial of Spirit 8; Kashyyyk 20; Dark Felucia 13; Imperial Raxus 20; Cloud City 20; Death Star 10.
  - These add up to exactly 200, which matches Wii p. 14. Health and Force Holocrons, colour crystals and hilts are listed separately.
- **Level Unlockables (Wii p. 112):** Duel-mode characters and costumes by level, for example:
  - "Darth Phobos character—Duel Mode", "Asajj Ventress character—Duel Mode", "Mara Jade character—Duel Mode", "Qui-Gon Jinn character—Duel Mode", "Mace Windu character—Duel Mode";
  - "Ceremonial Jedi Robes Costume", "Sith stalker costume".
  - "Unlocking the Ultimate Good and Ultimate Evil characters depends on which side you choose in your final battle."
- The Wii unlockable list calls the level "Imperialized Raxus".
