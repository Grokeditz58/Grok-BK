# 055 · Prima Official Game Guide: *Star Wars: The Force Unleashed* (2008)

- **Status:** DONE. I hand-read all 226 PDF pages. They are the PS3/Xbox 360 guide (pp. 1–113, including an 8-page art gallery) and the complete bound-in Wii guide (PDF pp. 114–226, its own pp. 1–112).
- **Source:** MEGA handle `1W1WxTiY`, a Prima eGuide PDF of 226 pages with a text layer. Main guide: printed page = PDF page − 1. Wii guide: Wii page = PDF page − 114. The source file is not committed.
- **Bibliographic details:** written by Fernando Bueno; Prima Games / Random House, 2008; ISBN 978-0-7615-5916-0.

## Files
- `fu-01.md`: front matter, the story-so-far primer, the cast and bestiary, and the combat systems (pp. 1–35).
- `fu-02.md`: the PS3/360 walkthrough from the Prologue to the Cloud City arrival (pp. 36–65).
- `fu-03.md`: the walkthrough from Cloud City to the Death Star choice (pp. 65–92).
- `fu-04.md`: PS3/360 Sith Secrets (the 115-Holocron tables), the Xbox 360 Achievements and the art gallery (pp. 93–113).
- `fu-05.md`: the Wii guide from its front matter to Shaak Ti, including the Wii-only Trials of Skill and Insight (Darth Desolous, Darth Phobos) and Drexl (Wii pp. 1–49).
- `fu-06.md`: the Wii guide from Vader's Flagship to the end: Nar Shaddaa, the Trial of Spirit (where the apprentice kills Kento), the Chop’aa and Kleef version of Cloud City, Duel mode with profiles that spoil the endings, and the 200-Holocron checklists (Wii pp. 50–112).

## Roster additions
- Mallie (Kento Marek’s wife), Chop’aa Notimo and Kleef. Darth Desolous, Darth Phobos, Drexl Roosh and Ozzik Sturn were already in the roster.
