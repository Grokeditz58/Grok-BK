# Prima *The Force Unleashed* guide — part 2: PS3/360 walkthrough, Prologue to Cloud City arrival (printed pp. 36–65)

Citations use printed pages (PDF page − 1). I read the walkthrough prose in full. Below are story beats, lore, quotes, and the bonus objectives for each level. Holocron positions are summarized, not copied.

## Prologue: Kashyyyk as Vader (pp. 36–39)
- **Crawl (p. 36):** "The Emperor’s spies have located a lone Jedi Knight on the Wookiee homeworld of Kashyyyk. The Sith Lord DARTH VADER has been sent to destroy him...." There are no Holocrons.
- **"Dark Plans" (p. 37):** "Ignoring the fallen Stormtroopers at his feet, Vader’s gait is strong, confident, and full of resolve. Nothing can stop him now. Not even one of the last remaining Jedi."
  - Wookiees are "troublesome fuzz balls" (p. 37), and the text says "Force Choke" for Grip there.
- **Rogue Jedi Duel (pp. 38–39):**
  - Kento "simply tells you to turn back as he Force Pushes a pair of Stormtroopers".
  - Vader senses a presence. "At first you think it might be the Jedi’s master, but it’s not; you destroyed him years ago. Suddenly ... your weapon inexplicably flies out of your hand! You turn to find a young boy holding your ’saber. It wasn’t the Jedi’s master you sensed; it was his son!" (p. 39)
  - "In a split-second decision, you decide to spare the boy’s life so that he may suit your own purposes later. ... With all witnesses removed, you take the boy and claim his future as your Secret Apprentice." (p. 39)

## TIE Construction Yard: Kota (pp. 40–46)
- **Objective:** "Find and eliminate General Kota." Bonus: 5 TIE fighters, 90,000 Force Points, 15 Holocrons.
- **Opening (p. 41):** "Your years of tutelage under Darth Vader’s care have remained a secret even to the Emperor, for your destiny poses a threat to the Dark Lord."
- **"Leave No Witnesses." (p. 41):** "you must leave no witnesses—Rebel and Imperial forces alike."
  - PROXY, "disguised as Obi-Wan Kenobi, lunges out at you". Then the personnel file on Juno: "Captain Juno Eclipse is a capable, war-hardened, highly decorated combat pilot. But you can see that she is beautiful as well."
- **Kota fight (p. 46):** "His attacks on the Imperial shipyards weren’t meant to get your attention, but rather your master’s." He "uses the Force to unhinge the control room and separate it from the rest of the TIE construction facility!"
  - If the fight runs long, the room reaches the atmosphere and superheats.
  - Ending: "He rambles on about your future and how he sees only...himself? It’s too late. Your ’saber burns his eyes ... You take his lightsaber for your master and leap out of the broken window, landing on the Rogue Shadow as it speeds by below you."

## Raxus Prime: Kazdan Paratus (pp. 47–53)
- **Objective:** "Seek out and destroy Kazdan Paratus." Bonus: 10 scavenger skiffs, 150,000 FP, 15 Holocrons.
- **Setting (p. 47):** "a world whose entire surface resembles the trash heaps and junkyards of Coruscant. From high above the planet, Paratus’s home looks much like a Jedi Temple." "transport beams flow overhead like a river of trash."
- **"Paratus and His Puppet" (p. 53):** "a nostalgic, if not sullen, re-creation of the Jedi Temple on Coruscant. Not only has the cowering Jedi re-created the Jedi Council chamber, but he’s also sculpted many of the Jedi Council members out of trash."
  - He summons Junk Titans twice.
  - Death: "he sighs his last words: “I’m sorry, Masters. I’ve failed you again....” And with that, a bright blue beam of light shoots out of his body and into the air. The mysteries of the Jedi…."

## Felucia: Shaak Ti (pp. 54–59)
- **Objective:** "Hunt down and destroy Shaak Ti." Bonus: 4 Rancors, 250,000 FP, 15 Holocrons. Enemies include the "Saddle Rancor" and "Felucian Slug".
- **"Then There Is but One More Test." (p. 55):**
  - Vader "dismisses your victories over both Jedi, claiming they were nothing more than “an old man and an outcast.”" The next test is "a true Jedi Master, one of the last of the Jedi Council."
  - "Maris Brood, Shaak Ti’s Padawan, is eager for a fight. She’s not pleased when her master sends her away. There’s a hint of darkness in Brood, and Shaak Ti knows this."
- **"The Last of the Council" (pp. 58–59):**
  - Ti is found "meditating in front of a large open chasm containing a Sarlacc creature". She later drops into the pit and "she’ll use the Sarlacc’s tendrils as weapons".
  - Ending: "she tries convincing you that you could be so much more than a Sith Lord and Vader’s puppet. Her words fall on deaf ears, and she falls into the chasm."

## Vader's Flagship (p. 60)
- The objective reads "Rejoin Darth Vader, challenge the Emperor, and fulfill your destiny."
- **Framing:** "This chapter is a bridge between Acts 1 and 2 ... During Act 1, you were tasked with hunting down Jedi in accordance with Order 66, thus bringing a tidy end to the first three episodes of the Star Wars saga. During Act 2, you’ll take part in setting up the last three episodes of the saga."
- **Betrayal:** "Just as the Emperor enters the chamber, Vader runs his lightsaber into your back." "Either Vader destroys you to prove his loyalty or the Emperor will eliminate you both. Without hesitation, Lord Vader sends you through a glass pane and into the darkness of space…."
- **Rebuilt:** "the Emperor failed to notice a small droid zip out into space toward your lifeless floating body." "Lord Vader salvaged your body and rebuilt you."
- **The new plan:** "Lord Vader believes that only a rebellion could sufficiently do this. Before you and Vader strike, you must raise an army. A rebel army. You must form a Rebel Alliance…."
- PROXY is "pleased that you’ve survived, but only because he can continue to try and destroy you himself". On Juno: "Besides, she’s much more than a pilot to you."

## Empirical Lab (pp. 61–63)
- **Objective:** "Escape the ISS Empirical." Bonus: "Destroy all six remaining escape pods", 150,000 FP, 5 Holocrons.
- "Ah, yes, the pilot." (section title, p. 62)
- Juno's rescue: "She’s weak but alive. Even though Vader told her you were dead, she held on to hope. Hope that you were still alive. Hope that you would come for her. And you did." (p. 63)

## Cloud City, opening (pp. 64–65)
- **Objective:** "Escape Cloud City with General Kota." Section title "I’m no Jedi now. Not since...this." Bonus: "Freeze 10 Imperials in carbonite", 200,000 FP, 5 Holocrons. The boss is a Shadow Guard.
- "Cloud City is not as vibrant as Felucia, but it is teeming with life nonetheless. Unfortunately, the life there is drunk and angry." (p. 64)
- Cutaway: "Captain Ozzik Sturn stands at high attention to welcome his newest prisoner. Princess Leia Organa ... arrives and is taken into custody." (p. 64)
- Kota: "His blind eyes have been bandaged, and he’s drunk himself into a stupor." (p. 65). "if he dies, your mission is over."
