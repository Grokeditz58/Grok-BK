# Prima *The Force Unleashed* guide — part 4: Sith Secrets, Xbox 360 Achievements and the art gallery (printed pp. 93–113)

Citations use printed pages (PDF page − 1). This part closes the PS3/Xbox 360 guide. I read every table row; this note summarises them instead of copying them out.

## Sith Secrets: Holocron Locations (pp. 93–103)
- **Opening line (p. 93):** "The following pages were ripped from secret scrolls known only to the Sith. They reveal the locations of all Holocrons and how to attain all Xbox 360 Achievements."
- **How the tables work:** "The Holocrons are listed in order as you progress through the level." Each row gives the sector, the Holocron number, the reward and the location.
- **Total (p. 104):** "There are 115 Holocrons to collect in total." That is 15 for each of the TIE Construction Yard, Raxus Prime, Felucia, Kashyyyk, Imperial Felucia, Imperial Raxus Prime and Death Star tables, plus 5 each for Empirical and Cloud City. The Prologue has no table.
- **Reward types:** Force Points, Force Power / Force Combo / Force Talent Spheres, ’saber colour crystals, Power (combat) crystals and Bonus Costumes.
- **Notable rewards:**
  - Raxus Prime 12, "Bonus Costume: Sith Robe": "Through a wall of girders and pipes, located on a distant platform that requires a double-jump and dash to reach" (p. 94).
  - Raxus Prime 15, "Power Crystal: Firkrann", in the Jedi Temple Shaft: "Use AT-ST head to jump up to reach" (p. 94).
  - Kashyyyk 2, "Bonus Costume: Kento’s Robes": "Hidden behind wreckage, behind your spawn point" (p. 99).
  - Kashyyyk 8, in the Commander’s House: "Inside one of the glass trophy cases" (p. 99).
  - Cloud City 2, "Power Crystal: Ruusan": "Hidden on platform above bar" (p. 98).
  - Imperial Raxus Prime 1, "Power Crystal: Sigil" (p. 101); Death Star 2, "Power Crystal: Katak" (p. 103).
  - Death Star 12, "‘Saber Crystal: Black": "Of the eight laser tubes, this Holocron resides at the very top, above a rail, near the chamber’s far end" (p. 104).
  - Empirical 2: "Inside specimen “jar”; use Force Grip/Throw to smash glass to acquire Holocron" (p. 97).
  - Death Star 15 is the last one: "the final Holocron is in the center of a console cluster along the platform’s edge" (p. 104).
- **Named colour crystals:** Compressed Red, Gold, Compressed Yellow, Unstable Red, Compressed Gold, Blue, Lorrdian, Unstable Orange, Unstable Yellow, Compressed Blue, Purple, Green, Compressed Purple, Vexxtal, Unstable Blue, Compressed Green, Unstable Purple, Unstable Green and Black. The TIE Construction Yard table also lists a "‘Saber Crystal: Damage" (Holocron 10, p. 93).

## Xbox 360 Achievements (pp. 104–105)
- The table is headed "Achievment Name" (misspelt in the source). It lists Gamerscore and "User Requirements", and the total is "1000" (p. 105).
- **Story and difficulty:**
  - "Revenge" (25): "Complete Game: Dark Side"; "Redemption" (25): "Complete Game: Light Side".
  - "Sith Master" and "Sith Lord" (100 each) are for the top difficulties.
  - "Holocron Collector" (75): "Collect all Jedi Holocrons in the game".
- **Joke names (pp. 104–105):**
  - "PROXY Won’t Be Happy" (15): "Destroy 35 droids".
  - "Red Five" (15): "Destroy 20 attacking TIE Fighters".
  - "Bossk" (15): "Defeat 200 Wookiees during the Kashyyyk Prologue level".
  - "The Bigger They Are" (15): "Defeat 6 Rancors". "The Harder They Fall" (15): "Defeat 10 AT-STs or AT-KTs".
  - "Bully" (15): "Defeat 25 Ugnaughts or Jawas".
  - "Worst Day-Shift Manager Ever" (10): "Kill 12 Stormtroopers as Vader during the Prologue".
- **Skill tiers:** "Legend" (25) is "Earn 600,000 Force Points on a single level". There are mastery pairs for each power, e.g. "Force Grip Mastery" (500 kills) and "Gripped" (100), and Frenzy tiers up to "Sith Lord Frenzy" (20): "Get a Frenzy x12 bonus".
