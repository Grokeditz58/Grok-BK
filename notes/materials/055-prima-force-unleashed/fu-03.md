# Prima *The Force Unleashed* guide — part 3: PS3/360 walkthrough, Cloud City to the Death Star choice (printed pp. 65–92)

Citations use printed pages (PDF page − 1). I read the walkthrough prose in full.

## Cloud City, conclusion (pp. 65–66)
- **Achievement aside:** "The Ugnaughts scuttling about the bar don’t really pose much of a threat, but if you get rid of them, they’ll count toward one of your Xbox 360 Achievements. If you really want to make things a bit more fun, try using Sith Punt attacks on them." (p. 65)
- Carbonite barrels "freeze the Imperials upon explosion". The construction "Uggernaught" is "AT-ST-like" (p. 66).
- The boss is a Shadow Guard on the loading docks.
- **End (p. 66):** "General Kota still tries to convince you that defeating the Empire is a fool’s errand. Unmoved by his doubt, you insist that the Empire must be opposed. Reluctantly, Kota decides to help."

## Kashyyyk: Leia and the Skyhook (pp. 67–72)
- **Objective:** "Prove yourself to Kota’s Senate contact by retrieving a mysterious object on Kashyyyk." Bonus: destroy the Imperial Comm Tower, 300,000 FP, 15 Holocrons. The boss is Captain Ozzik Sturn.
- **"We’re heading to Kashyyyk." (p. 68):** Kota's Senate contact "doesn’t say much other than the name of your new destination."
- **The hut and the father's ghost (p. 68):**
  - "Its walls are decorated with a strange symbol resembling a bird inside a circle. ... amid the chaos and destruction, you sense something familiar. Something dark. Something sad."
  - "His face is hidden by a low cowl, but his voice is familiar. “I never wanted any of this for you,” he says. “I’m sorry, son.” And with that, he fades back into the hut’s ruins."
  - The section ends: "Your father’s apparition shakes you up a bit, but you’re able to continue."
  - Note: the guide frames the bird-in-circle (the future Rebel crest) as a symbol on the hut. That is the game's origin story for the emblem.
- **Wookiee Warning (p. 68):** "Do not allow these fiery foes to surround you! If they do, you’ll be Apprentice flambé!"
- **Sturn's quarters (p. 70):** there are "glass trophy cases", and an Imperial Royal Guard stands inside.
- **Leia (p. 70):** "she assumes you’re one of the Emperor’s assassins." "She’s concerned that if the Empire completes the Skyhook transport, it will begin to shuttle Wookiee slaves off the planet. If you promise to destroy the Skyhook, however, she’ll willingly leave."
- **Skyhook (pp. 71–72):** remove seven traction hooks. "Captain Ozzik Sturn decides to take matters into his own hands. He exits the Skyhook facility in a large walker". At the end, "the Skyhook facility breaks down and the Wookiees are once again free."

## Imperial Felucia: Maris Brood (pp. 73–79)
- **Objective:** "Find and rescue Senator Bail Organa." Bonus: "Release the Sarlacc", 375,000 FP, 15 Holocrons. Bosses: the Bull Rancor and "Corrupted Maris Brood".
- **"Your master would be disgusted...." (p. 75):** "Since her master’s demise, Brood has become a dark, angry creature. ... she holds Organa as a prize for Darth Vader should he ever visit Felucia. She tells Organa that if he attempts to escape, she’ll feed him to her new pet—a monstrous Bull Rancor beast!"
- **The level path:** Imperial base camp, the stabilizing towers (cut the chains to free the Sarlacc), then into the Sarlacc itself: "Ride it down into the Sarlacc’s body", "the Sarlacc’s stomach", "the Sarlacc’s lung room", "breathing chamber". "a gust of wind will blast you out of the Sarlacc and into a Rancor graveyard." (pp. 76–78)
- **Brood (p. 79):** "Maris Brood is highly skilled with her light-tonfas"; "her tonfa toss".
  - Ending: "With Maris Brood under your lightsaber, it becomes clear that she can still be saved. Rather than strike her down for good, you decide to let her go."
  - "Organa knows another Senator who has spoken out against the Emperor, but in order to rally troops, you must show them that the Emperor is vulnerable and must instill a sense of hope in the dissidents."

## Imperial Raxus Prime: the Star Destroyer (pp. 80–85)
- **Objective:** "Destroy the Orbital Shipyard." Bonus: destroy the Imperial tractor beam tower, 430,000 FP, 15 Holocrons. The boss is PROXY; the "Imperial Star Destroyer" and "GNK Power Droid" are listed as enemies.
- **"You Still Serve Me." (p. 81):**
  - Vader "senses conflict in you. Your feelings for your new allies grow while your allegiance to the dark side remains."
  - Juno "though she’s disappointed in you for remaining loyal to him, she remains loyal to you. ... she knows that sooner or later, you will decide the fate of the Rebellion, not Darth Vader."
  - The target is "the Star Destroyer construction facility on Raxus Prime".
- **PROXY boss (pp. 84–85):**
  - "Your clever training droid’s tactical computer suggested that this would be the best time for him to attack you. He was programmed, after all, to kill you."
  - He cycles through Brood, a Shadow Guard, Kota and Shaak Ti, and then "he’ll transform into one you haven’t seen in combat before—Darth Maul." The guide calls him "Darth PROXY" (p. 85).
  - Maul's "amplified lightsaber throw (which cannot be blocked)".
- **The Star Destroyer pull (p. 85):** overload four rail-gun tracks so "a blast from the ore cannon" hits the orbital station. Then "Force Grip the massive Star Destroyer" with both sticks, destroying TIE waves between grips: "You won’t be able to bring down the ship on the first try."
- **Aftermath (p. 85):** "a shadowy figure stumbles out of the wreckage and falls at your feet. PROXY sustained some damage and is no longer capable of executing his program to full capacity, but he’s still your droid and you can’t leave him behind."

## Corellia: the founding (p. 86; cutscene, no gameplay)
- "Standing around the table are Senator Organa, his daughter, Senator Bel Iblis, Kota, you, and Mon Mothma. Everything has fallen into place, and the Rebel Alliance is taking shape."
- "Just as Organa makes an official declaration of rebellion ... Darth Vader comes storming in."
- "Vader confirms your greatest fears: He’s been lying to you since the very beginning. He never planned to destroy the Emperor with you. You were just a tool to gather the Emperor’s enemies together to destroy them all at once."
- **PROXY's sacrifice:** "A Jedi with shaggy brown hair and a full beard comes leaping out of the Corellian meeting room. It’s Obi-Wan Kenobi! ... Kenobi’s body disappears, revealing PROXY’s fallen form."
  - "He knew that he was no match for Vader, but his surprise attack bought you enough time to drop down from the cliff’s edge". Juno then rescues you.
  - An epigraph quotes Vader's ANH line "I’ve been waiting for you, Obi-Wan."

## Death Star (pp. 87–92)
- **Objective:** "Find General Kota and the Founders of the Rebel Alliance." Bonus: 450,000 FP, 15 Holocrons. Bosses: Darth Vader and the Emperor.
- **"I’ve never been a Jedi before." (p. 88):**
  - "You’ve never been a true Jedi before, but somehow your meditation pays off. You see visions of Kota and a large space station still under construction."
  - "You set course for the Outer Rim and find Vader’s Death Star." Juno: "Realizing that this might be the last time she sees you, she finally reveals her true feelings…with a kiss."
- Level features: firing tubes, "pulsarium rings" that must be aligned (p. 90), the "convergence chamber", and the "Emperor’s observation dome" (p. 90).
- **"The Reckoning" (p. 91):** "The Emperor, sure of himself and his power, insists that the Rebellion will fail. But he fails to sense what Kota is immediately aware of—you."
  - The Vader fight continues in an antechamber where explosive barrels stun him. "Your final attack destroys Vader’s protective mask and sends him flying into the Emperor’s chamber." (p. 92)
- **"A Choice" (p. 92):**
  - "Rather than give away the two possible endings, the following section provides tips on how to defeat the Emperor and Darth Vader. ... Will you join the light side by defeating the Emperor and freeing the Rebels, or will you join the dark side by destroying Darth Vader and taking his place next to the Emperor?"
  - "Vader is still at your mercy, but the Emperor is destroying Kota!" The guide never narrates either ending.
  - Emperor tips: "The Emperor doesn’t wield a lightsaber; his attacks are all Force power–focused." Note his "purple Force Lightning shield".
