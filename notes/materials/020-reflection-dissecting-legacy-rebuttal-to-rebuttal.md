## Common rebuttals / weak points (ANALYSIS — not from the thread)

- **"Firestorm of raw emotion and cold rage" is overused.** It is a p15 *caption* describing what Wyyrlok senses as Krayt approaches. Ant attaches it to each attack as if it measured the attack's power.
- **Concession counters overclaim.**
  - #1: ILS wrote "even assuming that's what happened", which is not agreement.
  - #2: ILS paired "no evidence he can" with "no evidence he can't either".
  - #3 is partly fair.
- **Shields and deadline narrative:** no page shows Krayt luring Cade to drop the shields or working to a timetable. Draco stalling Fel *is* on the page (p121–126), but it was not something Krayt planned around.
- **Dan Parsons:** he is the inker, not the colourist, and gave a personal view on "fighter" parity. It does not count as authorial intent.
- **Ant's best new material:** the horn colour sampling, the reserves after 40 ft, the Memory Walk grammar, and the p4 corpse correction.
