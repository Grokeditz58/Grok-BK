# Young Jedi Knights 6: Jedi Under Siege: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-20 CST. Full read of READ l.1–1443 (every line, in order): dedication, acknowledgments, all 26 chapters, the authors' note, the Shards of Alderaan preview, and the scan's cover, copyright page, "About the Authors" and back cover. Notes are limited to scaling-relevant material (owner's rulings, 2026-11-20). The quotes are in jus-01 to jus-03; this summary is in our own words and cites them by chapter.
- Authors: Kevin J. Anderson & Rebecca Moesta. Sixth Young Jedi Knights book and the end of the first arc. Copyright page (scan p6): Boulevard edition, September 1996, © 1996 Lucasfilm Ltd. 26 chapters.
- Era: directly after Darkest Knight. The Shadow Academy attacks Luke's Jedi academy on Yavin 4.
- Big events: Tenel Ka beats Tamith Kai, and Tamith Kai dies when the battle platform is destroyed (Brakiss reports her dead). Luke beats Brakiss in a lightsaber duel. Qorl kills Norys to save Jacen and Peckhum. Brakiss learns the "Emperor" was a hologram hoax run by four of Palpatine's red guards. The Shadow Academy self-destructs, and Brakiss is believed dead. Zekk, believing everything lost, nearly kills Jaina but saves everyone from Orvak's bomb and is hurt by the falling rock.

## Plot
- **Ch. 1–3:** the friends return from Kashyyyk; Luke gives everyone a task; the Shadow Academy decloaks; Orvak's stealth TIE gets under the shield and blows up the generator (his partner Dareb dies against the field).
- **Ch. 4–6:** long-range transmissions are jammed; Luke evacuates the temple into the jungle; Zekk's Dark Jedi drop from Tamith Kai's battle platform; Qorl leads raw TIE pilots, Norys among them; jobs are handed out under fire.
- **Ch. 7:** Lowie's T-23 is shot down near the platform; Tenel Ka climbs up to it on a grapple line.
- **Ch. 8:** Luke's alien students beat three groups of Dark Jedi (the Cha'a with river water, Tionne's students with a levitated slab, a plant Jedi with a felled tree).
- **Ch. 9, 16:** Jaina steals Orvak's TIE, shoots down four TIE fighters in all, and is saved at the last moment by the arriving New Republic fleet.
- **Ch. 10, 17:** Luke agrees to meet Brakiss alone, duels him, wrecks his lightsaber, and Brakiss escapes by repulsorpack.
- **Ch. 11–13:** Jacen sends the distress call from the Lightning Rod; Zekk's storm fails when he sees Peckhum's ship falling; Zekk throws Raynar into the river rather than let him be killed; Qorl shoots Norys down.
- **Ch. 14, 23–24:** Orvak plants a bomb in the grand audience chamber, but Jacen's freed crystal snake puts him to sleep; it goes off and wrecks the top of the temple.
- **Ch. 15:** Tenel Ka beats Tamith Kai; the TIE bomber she holed crashes into the platform.
- **Ch. 18:** the Second Imperium fleet uses stolen IFF to dodge New Republic targeting; Jaina's override idea turns it around; Qorl's suicide run fails and he crashes.
- **Ch. 19:** Brakiss uncovers the hoax; the last guard flees and blows up the station.
- **Ch. 20–22, 24:** Zekk's despair; the wounded students gather; Zekk bars the temple door and fights Jaina briefly; she throws down her blade; he saves them from the blast.
- **Ch. 25–26:** Qorl goes back to the jungle; the academy will be rebuilt; the friends go to GemDiver Station for bacta; Tenel Ka tells her first joke.

## Strongest findings (see the part files for the quotes)
- **Tenel Ka** (ch. 6–7, 15, 26): her best showing so far. One-armed, she climbs the grapple line with Force levitation, blocks Tamith Kai's lightning, knocks her off the command deck with a Force nudge, dodges TIE bomber cannon fire by Force-sensing where the bolts will hit, holes the bomber with a thrown lightsaber, and puts Tamith Kai down bare-handed. Later she finds her lost lightsaber by sensing its rancor tooth. Limits: the bomber's guns are too strong to deflect, and the fall leaves her with a head gash and a thigh wound.
- **Luke** (ch. 10, 17): Force awareness of the whole jungle battle, with support sent to his students from a distance. He out-duels Brakiss calmly and deliberately wrecks Brakiss's lightsaber hilt instead of taking his hand. Brakiss, for his part, calls Luke only partly trained.
- **Brakiss** (ch. 2, 17, 19): loses to Luke, begs, and flees. Back on the station, his anger lets him cut down two red guards in under a second, blow the door circuits with the Force, wrench the door open by hand and cut through the armored chamber. His limitation is that he cannot reach the calm his full power needs.
- **Jaina** (ch. 9, 16, 18): Force-heightened gunnery and evasion in the stolen TIE (four kills). She also works out the Second Imperium's IFF trick and the override that beats it. Limits: she cannot repair the generator, and two TIEs would have finished her.
- **Zekk** (ch. 5, 12, 20, 22, 24): leads Dark Jedi in conjuring a storm, which collapses when his concentration breaks. He hurls Raynar with telekinesis and has Jedi-instinct reflexes (the rodent). His lightsaber draw is eerily smooth, but he cannot outrun the rockfall.
- **Lowie** (ch. 4, 7, 15, 21): senses danger ahead of time and flies through fire with the Force, crash-landing the T-23 under Force guidance. He throws stormtroopers one-handed and holds off fifteen. Durability: a dislocated shoulder and three broken ribs from catching Tenel Ka.
- **Qorl** (ch. 5, 13, 18, 25): one shot to Norys's reactor exhaust, and he survives a second TIE crash. **Norys:** a top simulator scorer with weapon aptitude who wastes his shots.
- **Others** (ch. 8, 12): the Cha'a, avian, plant and Tionne's students all win their fights with the Force. Raynar's Force push goes unnoticed (ch. 12).
