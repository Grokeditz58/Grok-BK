## Feats / scaling
- **Mind trick on a group (partial):** pushes his will on ~8 outsiders; at least the Rodian starts parroting him before the horned leader snaps them out (p.16). Not a clean win.
- **Force door-unlock:** opens a lock with the Force — "NEVER HAVING TO CARRY A KEY" (p.6).
- **Force-sense:** feels a "disruption in the Force" at the house (p.7); reads unease in passing minds (p.6).
- **Unarmed combat vs ~8 thugs:** throws, disarms, brick-strike, one-punch KO of the horned brute (p.17–20); takes a slam and a knife cut to the hand. Narration admits he's aging: "TWENTY YEARS AGO, WHEN HE KNOCKED THEM DOWN, THEY STAYED DOWN" (p.18).
- **Animal summoning / calming:** calls his arax to the fight from a distance, then Force-calms the battle-hot carnivore (p.18–19).
- **Hearsay (non-feat):** cantina rumors that Jedi can make you put a hand in boiling water or drink poison — narration says Ki never did and never would (p.13).
- **Tech:** forensics (skin cells on the saber hilt; surveillance cameras) used to clear Ki (p.5).

## Lore
- Maj's full name: **Maj-Odo-Nomor**. Gang members: Kordren, Skeel. Ydde: Ortolan dockworker/informant with a wife and babies.
- Ki was a poor Padawan on some days (couldn't levitate a leaf, nearly burned off his ear).
- Bron is working with Ephant Mon and is shielding Maj only for as long as he's useful.
