## Feats / scaling
- **Air-battle tactics (p.7–8):** outnumbered by atmospheric fighters, Ki and Fltch kick up a sandstorm with stern blasters on full, choking the intakes until the pursuers "SELF-DESTRUCTED". Tech note: atmospheric fighters rely on air-cooled engines and overheat "IN A MATTER OF SECONDS".
- **Ion-storm rescue (p.11–14):** Ki drops from his ship onto a lightning-struck tower mid-storm, cuts down Jabba's guards and **deflects blaster bolts with his saber**, and frees both girls.
- **Strategic layer:** the "fumbled card" decoy and covert droid insertion (J1M ejected as debris, smuggled aboard Jabba's shuttle, decrypting the manifest). He exposes the **Trade Federation** buying weapons forbidden by the Republic's rules of engagement. The Council credits this as the reason for his seat (p.16, 18).
- **Force sense:** a disturbance in the Force over the Republic core, "TOO *DIFFUSE*" to read (p.17).
- **Young Ki (VoJ):** Force-loosens rope knots (p.20). Quickly takes out two guards unarmed (p.21). Beats the raider chief who had beaten him the night before (p.22). **Telekinetically turns a thrown knife "WITH BARELY MORE THAN A THOUGHT"** (p.24). Bluffs a whole raider band into disbanding (p.23). Narration admits he doubts he could beat them all by "SHEER FORCE OF *NUMBERS*" (p.22). Useful as a limit statement for a newly knighted Ki.

## Lore
- **Jabba's "new associates" = the Trade Federation**: the merchandise shipped off Tatooine goes to a Federation vessel, and the cargo includes items forbidden under Republic rules (p.15–16). This is the arc's tie-in to *The Phantom Menace*.
- **Ki is proposed for the Jedi Council**, with Yoda backing him: "VALUABLE HE HAS BEEN ALREADY. *MORE* VALUABLE HE YET WILL BE." (p.18).
- Senator **Silais recalled**, senators in seclusion, unrest in the Republic core (p.17). **Bron** drops his crusade "for now". Maj's name is cleared by Sylvn's testimony.
- **Shea** (Ki's first/bond-wife) is pregnant (p.18). Ki is shown with honor-wives and several daughters.
- Ephant Mon escapes ("SOME OTHER DAY").
- Droids: **J1M** appears by name and is the hero of the infiltration. B3NK's cynicism about Jedi treating droids as "ONLY MACHINES" is shown up when Ki reveals J1M's mission.
