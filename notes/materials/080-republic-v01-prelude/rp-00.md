## Feats / scaling
- **Feat (Yoda, implied):** Yoda overrides the training droids' movement parameters, making them move beyond spec and attack simultaneously (Ki-Adi's complaint, p.6). Implied Force/tech manipulation; not shown how. (ANALYSIS: likely Force-driven.)
- **Feat (Padawan Ki-Adi):** fights at least two gold training droids at once with a sparring-mode blade; destroys one (explosion panel, p.6). Low-end.
- **Anti-feat (Ki-Adi, present):** clipped in the shoulder by a training remote's bolt (p.3) because his concentration slipped.

## Lore
- Yoda trained Ki-Adi personally (Padawan scene).
- "Vuras": a Jedi who sought revenge, adopted his enemies' tactics, won but destroyed himself (Yoda's cautionary tale; unique to #0).
- Cerea is under "pressures from the Republic"; the Twi'lek representative Bron is stirring Cereans against tradition.
