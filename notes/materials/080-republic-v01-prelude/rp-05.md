## Feats / scaling
- **Divvik gauntlet (p.4–9):** in a pressure suit and still healing, Ki cuts through the swarm in the docking tube and corridors and reaches the pod. #4 set the bar at "four or five" divviks for a trained Jedi, and he is shown fighting through far more (with Fltch's fire support and a demo charge that stuns the pod-door group). The suit gives only "SLIGHT" protection from bites.
- **Force navigation:** "THE FORCE HAS LED KI *HERE*" (p.8); he senses an empty dark chamber (p.6).
- **Saber vs starship hull:** cuts his way out of the freighter's hull into vacuum (p.12).
- **Mind trick on a starship crew over comms (p.13):** persuades a Trade Federation picket ship to let him pass — "YOUR BUSINESS IS NO CONCERN OF OURS." Good evidence for a remote/comm-range mind trick, though it's framed as convincing them "OF WHAT THEY ALREADY *KNOW*" (they had no jurisdiction).
- **Young Ki loses (VoJ p.24–25):** a newly knighted Ki, hands tied and unarmed, is beaten by the raider chief. Narration admits that later he "WOULD *WIN* SUCH A FIGHT… USE THE *FORCE* TO… *DEFLECT* HER WEAPON." This is the low end of his range.
- **Tech:** divviks plant larvae in brains (p.6); an ion attractor draws storm lightning (p.19); a Trade Federation ship patrols near Tatooine (p.13).

## Lore
- **Traitor Elder: Dorr-Femi-Bonmi**, smuggling swoops and feeding information to the pro-tech movement. He had Ydde killed, and the swoops that killed Skeel (and hurt Kordren) were sabotaged (p.7).
- **Maj-Odo-Nomor is killed by Ephant Mon** (p.10–11, 15).
- **Jabba the Hutt** is Ephant Mon's employer. Mon was sent for malium, and the contraband sold to the pro-tech movement financed it. Jabba has "new associates" taking merchandise off-world (p.16–17) — the setup for #6.
- Yoda's justice/revenge doctrine (VoJ p.20). Raiders' leadership is decided in the **kyana** (combat). Their livestock are **oorgs**. Young Ki's saber is **green** (VoJ p.22).
