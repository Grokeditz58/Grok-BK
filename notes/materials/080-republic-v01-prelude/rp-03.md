## Feats / scaling
- **One-strike droid kill:** wounded (leg shot, buried under a ceiling collapse), Ki gets his saber back and destroys a heavy merc droid with one slash (p.23). Then passes out.
- **Unarmed survival vs a merc droid:** dodges wrist-blaster fire by swinging on pipes, kicks its head, uses a manhole cover as a bolt shield (p.17–18). Narration: he'd "BE DEAD NOW" if the droid's targeting had been calibrated.
- **Force coercion on a Gran:** raises the prisoner's fear until his heart pounds, chest tightens and breathing goes irregular, then calms him (p.10–12). Narration calls it "COERCION, OF A SORT," making the testimony inadmissible. Useful for the "Jedi mind-pressure" debate.
- **Padawan training:** deflects three remotes at once; Yoda trips him (p.13–14).
- **Weakness:** missed the droid's approach — "too wrapped up in his own thoughts" (p.16). His Force-sense isn't automatic when he's distracted.
- **Tech:** the merc droid recalibrates its targeting mid-fight, has a helmet HUD, missiles, and wrist blasters (p.17–21). Ki is healed in a bacta tank (p.24).

## Lore
- Elder **Nar-Somo-Dali** (named p.20) gets Ki's saber back and has the Jedi Council extend his authority off-world; Council pays for a starship and crew (p.24–25).
- Ephant Mon is called "THE CHEVIN" (p.17). Skeel is killed and Kordren hurt in sabotaged swoops (p.4–7).
- Spelling: Ki's mount is "ARAX" in #2 and "ARYX" in Silais's line here (p.24).
