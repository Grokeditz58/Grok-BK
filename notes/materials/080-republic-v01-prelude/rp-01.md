## Plot

TO TAKE THE PLANET BY FORCE WOULD MEAN WAGING A COSTLY LONG-DISTANCE WAR. FOR NOW, THE REPUBLIC CHOOSES TO NEGOTIATE WITH THE ELDERS OF CEREA. FOR NOW." Tilted Elders' hall.

## Feats / scaling
- **Restraint, not a feat:** Ki chooses not to fight a crowd of Cerean youths; plans to use a mind-calm ("CALM THEM WITH HIS MIND") but is disarmed first (p.22). **Anti-feat:** a teenager snatches the lightsaber off his belt (p.22).
- **Force-calming self:** uses the Force to dispel his own anger (p.10).
- **Shea is Force-sensitive** ("A BIT OF THE FORCE IN HER", p.11).
- **Lightsaber lethality:** an untrained youth kills a bystander with one accidental ignition (p.23).
- **Tech:** repulsorlift platform; smuggled swoop; analyzer detecting malium at parts per trillion.

## Lore
- Cerean polygamy: 1:20 male-female ratio; one bond-wife, several honor-wives. Ki has 4 honor-wives, 7 daughters, no son.
- Cerea bans high technology; the Republic has built "Outsider Citadels" as enclaves.
- Malium (Cerea-only) → tecave plant → guilea (mild euphoric). Ephant Mon (Vinsoth) smuggles malium out and tech in.
