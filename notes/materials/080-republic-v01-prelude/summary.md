# Republic Vol. 1 — Prelude to Rebellion: summary of findings

Sources: Star Wars (1998) #0 (online preview), #1–#6 (Dec 1998 – May 1999), with the "Vow of Justice" backup in #4–#6. Hand-read page by page; citations are "SW #N p.X" = file page (see each rp-0N.md).

## Arc in one paragraph
On Cerea, Jedi Knight Ki-Adi-Mundi leads the Elders in rejecting Republic membership. Republic Representative Silais and the Twi'lek agitator Bron stir up a pro-tech youth movement. At a rally, a teen grabs Ki's saber and kills a boy by accident (#1). Ki's saber is held as evidence, and his informant Ydde is murdered (#2). Swoop riders die in sabotaged swoops, and Ki barely survives Ephant Mon's merc droid (#3). Ki's daughter Sylvn flees with Maj-Odo-Nomor, Twin and Mon on a freighter full of hatching divviks in Tatooine orbit (#4). Ki fights through the divviks. Mon kills Maj, blackmails Ki into launching the pod, and hands the girls to Jabba (#5). The traitor Elder is revealed as Dorr-Femi-Bonmi. Ki saves the girls from an ion-storm tower and uses the droid J1M to expose the **Trade Federation** buying forbidden weapons. Yoda backs him for a **Jedi Council seat** (#6). In "Vow of Justice", the newly knighted Ki comes home, loses to the raider chief, then wins with the Force and leaves Bin-Garda-Zon "INCONSEQUENTIAL".

## Top feats (Ki-Adi-Mundi)
1. **Divvik gauntlet** (SW #4 p.7 benchmark; #5 p.4–9). An Elder says a trained Jedi could "PROBABLY FEND OFF *FOUR* OR *FIVE*". Divviks are immune to hand-blasters, have a paralysing bite and acid saliva, and number about 2,000. Ki is still healing after being pulled from bacta early, and fights through the swarm to the survivors. He has drone fire support and uses a demolition charge to clear the pod door.
2. **Saber through a freighter hull and out into vacuum** (#5 p.12), then picked up by Fltch.
3. **Mind trick on a starship crew over comms** (#5 p.13): a Trade Federation picket ship tells him "YOUR BUSINESS IS NO CONCERN OF OURS. YOU MAY *PROCEED*." Caveat: he frames it as convincing them "OF WHAT THEY ALREADY *KNOW*".
4. **One-slash kill of a merc droid while badly wounded** (#3 p.23). He has been shot in the leg and buried under a ceiling collapse, then passes out.
5. **Force coercion of a Gran prisoner** (#3 p.10–12). He drives up the prisoner's fear until his heart pounds and he struggles to breathe, then calms him. The narration calls it "COERCION, OF A SORT", with testimony "INADMISSABLE IN COURT".
6. **Ion-storm rescue** (#6 p.11–14). He drops onto a lightning-struck tower, deflects blaster bolts and cuts down Jabba's guards.
7. **Group mind trick, partial** (#2 p.16). About 8 thugs; the Rodian starts repeating his words before the horned leader breaks the effect. He then wins **unarmed** with a one-punch KO (#2 p.17–20).
8. **Ship-wide Force search** (#4 p.17). Sensors can't tell divviks from survivors; "KI EXTENDS HIS MIND… FINDING. EPHANT MON!"
9. **Young Ki (VoJ):** Force-loosens rope knots (#6 p.20) and **turns a thrown knife "WITH BARELY MORE THAN A *THOUGHT*"** (#6 p.24). As a child he floated objects with his mind (#4 p.21).
10. Smaller Force feats: opening locks ("NEVER HAVING TO CARRY A KEY", #2 p.6); summoning his arax and calming it (#2 p.18–19); sensing a diffuse disturbance in the Force over the Republic core (#6 p.17).

## Anti-feats / limits (use honestly)
- A teenager snatches his saber off his belt (#1 p.22).
- Hit by a training remote when his concentration slipped (#0 p.3).
- Didn't sense the merc droid because he was "TOO WRAPPED UP IN HIS OWN THOUGHTS" (#3 p.16).
- "TWENTY YEARS AGO, WHEN HE KNOCKED THEM DOWN, THEY STAYED DOWN" (#2 p.18). He is ageing.
- Young Ki, tied up and unarmed, **loses** to the raider chief (#5 VoJ p.24–25). The text adds that later he "WOULD *WIN*… USE THE *FORCE* TO… *DEFLECT* HER WEAPON". He wonders how many more he can beat "BEFORE HE'S OVERWHELMED BY SHEER FORCE OF *NUMBERS*" (#6 p.22).
- Sylvn: "JEDI KNIGHTS AREN'T *ALL-POWERFUL*… THEY JUST DO THEIR *BEST*" (#5 p.19).

## Other characters
- **Yoda:** trained Ki personally for 21 years. He overrides training droids' limits so they attack all at once (#0 p.6) and trips Ki: "YOUR BLIND SPOT, I WAS." (#3 p.14). He teaches justice over revenge (#5 VoJ p.20) and backs Ki's Council seat (#6 p.18).
- **Ephant Mon** (Chevin from Vinsoth): smuggles malium out and tech in for Jabba. He is "SOMEWHAT *FORCE ADEPT*" (#4 p.19), kills Maj (#5 p.10), and escapes.
- **Jabba the Hutt:** sees through Ki's decoy-debris trick but lets it play out to cover a shuttle launch to the Trade Federation (#6 p.10–13).
- **Bron** (Twi'lek "diplomat"): agitator working with Mon (#2 p.23). **Silais**: Republic representative, recalled at the end (#6 p.17).
- **Elder Nar-Somo-Dali:** Ki's ally. **Elder Dorr-Femi-Bonmi:** traitor who smuggled swoops, had Ydde killed, and sabotaged the swoops (#5 p.7).
- **Sylvn** (Ki's daughter): pro-tech runaway, reconciled with Ki ("SO AM I, PRINCESS", #6 p.14). **Maj-Odo-Nomor**, **Twin**, **Skeel** (killed), **Kordren** (hurt).
- Crew droids: **Fltch R4** (the #3 merc droid, reprogrammed), **B3NK**, **2-ROB**, **SIM**, **J1M**.
- Family: bond-wife **Shea** (has "A BIT OF THE FORCE IN HER", pregnant by #6), honor-wife **Mawin**, 4 honor-wives and 7 daughters; sister **Proe**.

## Lore
- Cerea: 1 male in 20 births, so polygamy (one bond-wife, several honor-wives). Bans high tech, has Republic "Outsider Citadels", and is not yet a Republic member (#6 letters).
- Malium (found only on Cerea) feeds the tecave plant, which produces guilea, a mild euphoric.
- Divviks: pupae are a Hutt delicacy; they plant larvae in living brains (#5 p.6).
- Ki was found at about 4 for floating things with his mind and was trained under Yoda for 21 years (#4 VoJ).
- Yoda's tale of **Vuras**, a Jedi destroyed by his own revenge (#0 only).
- The Trade Federation is secretly buying weapons forbidden by the Republic (#6), which sets up *The Phantom Menace*. **Next arc:** Ki goes to Coruscant and is sent after a lost Jedi in the Dune Sea (*Outlander*).

## Page order / file integrity

- #1's file numbering jumps from `-001` to `-009` (TPB numbering, not a gap). #1 also contains an extra TPB cover file (`z001_trade`).
- Lower-res scans: #3 `-052` and its letters page, and #6 `-116`.
- #0 is a fan-remastered online preview, never reprinted.
- Lettering inconsistencies: Silais/Bron in #0; ARAX/ARYX; FLTCH R4/FLTCH; Ki's saber colour (blue in #0, violet/white in the main arc, green in VoJ).
