## Feats / scaling
- **Official divvik benchmark (p.7):** a Cerean Elder says a trained Jedi with a saber could "PROBABLY FEND OFF *FOUR* OR *FIVE*" before being overwhelmed. Divviks shrug off hand-blaster fire, have paralytic bites and acid saliva that eats plastisteel, and release toxic gas if crushed. A lightsaber cauterises the wound and seals the gas in. About 2,000 aboard. This is the bar for #5.
- **Force-sensing across a ship (p.17):** B3NK's sensors can't tell survivors from thousands of divviks, but Ki "SENDS MENTAL FINGERS INTO THE FORCE" and finds Ephant Mon. Mon also senses Ki ("HE KNOWS *WE'RE* HERE," p.18). Ki calls Mon "SOMEWHAT *FORCE ADEPT*" (p.19).
- **Ki goes in still injured:** pulled out of the bacta tank early, "STILL WEAK, STILL *HEALING*" (p.8).
- **Childhood telekinesis:** little Ki "COULD SET THINGS AFLOAT… FROM HIS *MIND*" with no training. He also precognitively senses the raiders coming (VoJ p.21–22).
- **Tech:** Fltch R4 is the #3 merc droid, mind-wiped and reprogrammed; it says its "FRAME IS LESS SUSCEPTIBLE TO DAMAGE" (p.14, 19). 2-ROB holds data on 12 million+ species (p.13). The ship reaches the freighter in about 10 hours via hyperspace (p.15–16).

## Lore
- Ki spent **21 years** away from Cerea training under **Yoda**. He was taken late, at about 4 ("MISSED FOUR YEARS"), and tested on Coruscant (VoJ p.20–21).
- Cerean birth ratio repeated: one male child in twenty (VoJ p.22). Ki's sister is **Proe**. Raider chief: **Bin-Garda-Zon** (half a hand).
- Divvik pupae are a Hutt delicacy (p.7). New crew: Fltch R4, B3NK, SIM and 2-ROB.
- Bron is still stirring up the pro-tech crowd; the Elders are "VIRTUAL CAPTIVES" (p.8–9).
- Ephant Mon "saves" Sylvn as leverage — "THE TYRANNY OF MATHEMATICS" (p.16–18).
