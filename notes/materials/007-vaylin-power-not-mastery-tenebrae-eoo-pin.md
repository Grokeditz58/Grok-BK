## Thesis

Vaylin's raw power approaches Tenebrae's (EOO). Her KOTET Ch. VIII maelstrom, released equally in all directions, would match Tenebrae's strike-team pin if it were focused. What she lacks is mastery and consistency, not power: "power vs. mastery", like AOTC Anakin. The author's own framing is a "hot take" (FORUM OPINION).

## Arguments (numbered)

1. **The KOTET Ch. VIII maelstrom.** Vaylin's maelstrom knocks out the Outlander's companions. Points made:
   - The Outlander advances only with effort.
   - Frame-by-frame, Valkorion shields the Outlander as the wave hits. Boyd tweets are cited as confirming this (B001–B004; only B004 still loads).
   - Since the energy is released "**equally in all directions**", the Outlander takes only a small fraction. "If all the power behind Vaylin's maelstrom were concentrated on a small target, all that would be left of the Outlander would be a pile of smoking ash."
   - This is the "very same outcome" as Tenebrae's full-might pin in EOO.
2. **Innate power from birth.**
   - Senya: "She made furniture move while she was still in the womb. Tore droids apart as a toddler." (B006)
   - As a pre-teen she nearly crushed five Knights, and was conditioned on Nathema (B005, Codex: Vaylin's Conditioning).
   - She is "the only The Old Republic-centric character who has the potential to rival Valkorion's Nathema-augmented strength without rituals".
   - TOR's Facebook page called the Eternal Family "the most powerful family in Star Wars".
3. **Released after Vitiate's defeat.** Her KOTFE Ch. III debut is called "the greatest feat of environmental telekinesis in all of TOR up to that point". Boyd: "so incredibly powerful in the Force, probably the most powerful figure that we've introduced short of Valkorion himself". Valkorion: "Her power grows exponentially." (B009, KOTET Ch. III)
4. **The Outlander calls Vaylin stronger than her father (KOTET Ch. III)**, despite having witnessed Ziost. Valkorion had endorsed her ability to possibly replicate Ziost (KOTET Ch. I). Ziost itself is "a clear display of the corrosive power of the dark side of the Force taken to its extreme" (Codex: Death of a World).
5. **The KOTET Ch. III fight was a quick beatdown.** Timing from the video (10:45 bomb in three minutes, fight 11:00–11:45, time-stop 11:55–13:30, relay at 14:05 with "Two Minutes Until Detonation") implies the fight took "less than five seconds" of in-universe time. FORUM OPINION and inference.
6. **The Nathema unchaining.** Vaylin endures minutes of beams. Her stray power disintegrates scientists and shakes the Sanitarium. Jarak: her power "is racing out of control. When it erupts, the entire asylum will be destroyed." Valkorion panics.
7. **Power vs. mastery.** The analogy is AOTC Anakin, from the starwars.com piece "Teacher of the Masters" and the *Galactic Campaign Guide* (B020): "perhaps even more powerful than Yoda. However, he doesn't yet have Yoda's experience". Vaylin's outbursts are "flashes of brilliance". Boyd: "Vaylin has more raw power - she's probably second only to Valkorion on the scale of SWTOR characters - but the player characters have far more fighting experience, which is why they're able to defeat her". The swtor.com short story "A Mother's Hope" shows Senya out-fighting a stronger Vaylin.
8. **Conclusion:** "I don't really have any concluding remarks. I think the contents of the blog speak for themselves."

## Common rebuttals / weak points (ANALYSIS — not from the thread)

- **The fraction-of-output argument is geometric speculation.** No source says the maelstrom's total output equals Tenebrae's.
- **Valkorion shielding the Outlander** depends on frame-pausing and Boyd tweets that mostly no longer load.
- **The Outlander's "stronger than her father"** may be a reaction line; Valkorion himself answers "She is not. She is merely powerful." (cited in Material 004).
- **The Tenebrae comparison:** Vaylin is pinned by Tenebrae alongside her team twice (KOTET Ch. IX, EOO), which the author has to explain away as a lack of mastery.
- **Timing analysis** treats cutscene and gameplay time as real time.

## Characters touched and implied scaling (FORUM OPINION)

- **Vaylin:** raw power ~ approaching Tenebrae; second only to Valkorion (Boyd); above Revan, Arcann and the Dread Masters.
- **The Outlander:** beats Vaylin through skill and experience plus Valkorion's shielding. Raw power is lower.
- **Senya:** a superior duelist, a weaker Force user.
- **AOTC Anakin:** the analogy (potentially > Yoda in raw power, less experience).
- **Tenebrae/Valkorion:** at the top.
