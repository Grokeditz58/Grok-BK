# No Prisoners (Karen Traviss, 2009): summary of strongest findings

Source: the owner's MEGA epub (handle 5KtkQbDY), Del Rey ebook v3.1, eISBN 978-0-307-79597-7. Legends. Line refs point to the extracted text kept outside the repo. Detail is in np-01 to np-04.

This is a low-feat book. Its value is lore on Djinn Altis's sect of married, non-Council light-side Jedi and their unusual Force techniques.

1. **Combined Force push strong enough to sink the street and drop a building on a droid line.** Altis, Callista and Ahsoka:
   > "We're generating so much pressure that it's causing subsidence." … "As soon as the Jedi released the Force push, the building to the right of the droid front line collapsed in a rumbling plume of dust" (ch. SEVEN, l.2619–2639)

2. **Callista's machine-meld: one Jedi becomes a warship's targeting system and kills seven ships.**
   > "She was the machine—she was the whole ship… saw the barriers, and opened them with a breath" (ch. ELEVEN, l.4105); "You took out seven Sep ships." (l.4127)
   It costs her Force senses ("She could barely sense anything in the Force", l.4135). The technique itself: "We influence inanimate objects—machines, computers—by telekinesis, but we can also sense where there is no Force… and so feel what it is to be a machine… It's a rare skill even among Jedi." (ch. TEN, l.3631–3635). The same group also hand-guides a manual hyperspace jump with the nav computer down (ch. TEN, l.3643–3669).

3. **Anakin's power read by an outsider Master.** Altis: Skywalker's presence was "like being trailed by a whirlpool… something that might drag in everything it touched" (ch. NINE, l.3215). His attachment "could be used to bring him to heel as surely as a choke-chain on an akk" (l.3257). "Anyone with that amount of raw power in the Force needed to be carefully directed, not put in harness." (ch. THIRTEEN, l.4885)

4. **Sidious leaked the fleet codes to Grievous** (ch. ELEVEN epigraph, l.3891–3895): Palpatine "after passing the code keys to General Grievous… in his alternative guise as Darth Sidious".

Other data:
- Anakin: casual 15 m Force-cushioned drop and "a little Force wipe" of security cam recordings (ch. TWO, l.595–605). He navigates by sensing presences: "a bearing now that no ship's nav computer could give him" (ch. SEVEN, l.2745).
- Ahsoka's droid doctrine: "A good explosive Force push can bring down the front two ranks… the heads come off ever so easily." (ch. SIX, l.2357)
- CORRECTED: Anakin (not Altis) opens the CR-20 lock by Force vibration and an energy surge (ch. NINE, l.3463). Altis senses the droids aboard through their electrical fields. Anakin: "You can't sense droids in the Force …" Altis: "I feel an electrical field and variations in it. It's a useful skill to practice." (l.3465–3469)
- Altis: "Yoda, you fool… We are the dark side, all of us." (ch. THIRTEEN, l.4793–4809)

## Full line-by-line read additions (2026-11-03)
- **Title sources:** the recruiter epigraph "when we say no prisoners, we mean it" (l.1383–1385); the Irmenu doctrine "Neither take a prisoner nor let yourself become one." (l.1705–1707; Hallena repeats it at l.2677); Hallena's toxin tablets, "No prisoners... Insurance" (l.4435); her farewell note, "No prisoners. No hostages." (l.4749).
- **Altis's theology, most quotable lines:**
  - Letter to Yoda: "it's fixation—obsession—that leads to darkness and evil" (l.4495–4497). "their problem isn't attachment. Their problem is obsession." (l.4565)
  - Rejecting the Code line "There is no passion, only serenity.": "Garbage... We need passion!" (l.4801–4803)
  - "Dooku was a good man when I knew him... We are the dark side, all of us." (l.4809)
  - "Sora Bulq has a point. That the Republic might well need bringing down." (l.4835)
  - "Serenity, my backside... We need more attachment, not less." (l.4849)
- **Sect lore:**
  - The sect probably numbers "thousands" (l.4539) and has no limit on Padawans per Master (l.2147).
  - It takes adult recruits and non-Jedi students (Gali, l.4853).
  - Altis's late wife is named Margani (l.4859) and was not Force-sensitive (l.4857).
  - Qui-Gon named Altis and Dooku among "the thinkers, the debaters, the iconoclasts" (l.2739).
  - Altis to Anakin: "We never close." (l.4587)
  - At the end, Hallena has left Pellaeon and joined the Altisians on Yarille (l.4877–4879).
- **Who paid for the clone army?** A running thread:
  - "Someone knew we'd need them." (l.3131–3133)
  - "The Jedi paid up front, I hear." (l.3155)
  - "Or maybe the Jedi sensed what was coming... But for whose benefit?" (l.3709)
  - "Who paid for the Grand Army?" (l.4423)
  - "Who planned such an army?" (l.4815)
- **Other Force feats:**
  - Altis: shuttle steering through the Force (l.2207–2225); emotive mind influence on Neimoidians (l.3441); flying the CR-20 eyes shut (l.3787); deflecting cannon fire off the hull (l.3863–3867).
  - Callista: hacking Separatist encryption (l.2267); deflecting debris (l.3529); Force first aid (l.3527, technique at l.3839).
  - Anakin: navigating by "the distinctive trail of Jedi" (l.3003–3011).
  - The Jedi trance that cuts oxygen use (l.2965).
  - All the Jedi together sense Ince's death (l.4251–4255).
- **Corrections from the full read:**
  - l.2139 is Callista's line, not Altis's.
  - l.2445: Geith catches Hallena, not the troopers catching Geith.
  - l.3463: the lock is Anakin's feat.
  - The choke-chain quote is at l.3257.
  - The seven-ships line is at l.4127.
  - l.4691 is Geith's line, not Ahsoka's.
  - The Rebellion back-matter excerpt is Zahn's *Allegiance*.
  - Text inconsistency: Geith's surname is "Eris" at l.2523 but "Ens" at l.3287.
- **Back matter:** the *Gambit: Stealth* excerpt cross-references "The loss of greenies Vere and Ince during the Jan-Fathal mission" (l.5170). The *Outcast* excerpt's "rival schools, schismatic branches, and places for former Jedi to go" (l.7177) echoes the Altisians.
