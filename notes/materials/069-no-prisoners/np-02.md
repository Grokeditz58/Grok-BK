## Ch. SEVEN (l.2465): the droid line; Anakin follows
- **KEY GROUP FEAT: a combined Force push that causes ground subsidence.** Altis, Callista and Ahsoka hold back the droid line with a sustained push: "The ground underneath his boots was shaking… cracks in the paving… soon gaping apart in places to form wider fissures." Altis: "Oh, that again… We're generating so much pressure that it's causing subsidence." Then: "As soon as the Jedi released the Force push, the building to the right of the droid front line collapsed in a rumbling plume of dust that raced across the street like a volcanic eruption." (l.2619–2639). Earlier, the barricade of "shattered droid parts" was held by "two humans and a… Togruta… with lightsabers drawn and their free hands extended" (l.2549).
