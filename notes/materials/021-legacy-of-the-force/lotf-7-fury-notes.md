# Legacy of the Force 7: Fury (novel, read in full): feats, scaling and story notes

<!-- read up to: chapter thirty-eight (L7067), END OF BOOK; restructured into house style -->

- **Book:** *Star Wars: Legacy of the Force: Fury* by Aaron Allston (2007). Seventh of the nine Legacy of the Force novels.
- **Continuity:** Legends. Set in 40–41 ABY, during the Second Galactic Civil War, a few weeks after *Inferno*. Caedus is still recovering from his fight with Luke at the start (L343–351) and is only «fully healed for the first time since his fight with Luke» by Ch31 (L5573). It comes after *Inferno* (LOTF 6) and before *Revelation* (LOTF 8). The dramatis personae lists him as «Jacen Solo / Darth Caedus; Sith, joint Chief of State of the Galactic Alliance (human male)» (L51).

- **Method:** I read every line in order: front matter, then Ch1–Ch38. There is no back-matter interview in this edition. `L####` is the line number in that file. Text inside «…» is copied exactly from the file and was checked by script (see the end of this file). The raw reading log is kept as an appendix.
- **Chapter heading lines:** Ch1 L83, Ch2 L313, Ch3 L501, Ch4 L675, Ch5 L847, Ch6 L999, Ch7 L1101, Ch8 L1211, Ch9 L1325, Ch10 L1465, Ch11 L1655, Ch12 L1935, Ch13 L2189, Ch14 L2337, Ch15 L2521, Ch16 L2715, Ch17 L2963, Ch18 L3113, Ch19 L3275, Ch20 L3439, Ch21 L3591, Ch22 L3733, Ch23 L3979, Ch24 (heading lost in OCR; it starts around L4339), Ch25 L4347, Ch26 L4471, Ch27 L4635, Ch28 L4923, Ch29 L5177, Ch30 L5385, Ch31 L5565, Ch32 L5763, Ch33 L5929 (sic «Chaptaer»), Ch34 L6179, Ch35 L6343, Ch36 L6583, Ch37 L6759, Ch38 L6979.
- **Feat vs. statement:** Section 2 covers what the narration shows happening. Section 3 covers what characters say or think, marked **STATEMENT**. Allston writes in rotating tight third person, so a lot of "narration" is a character's view; those are marked *(POV)*. **Allston's framing of Caedus is kinder than Denning's.** In Caedus's POV the Luke duel was even (L345), and in Luke's POV «Jacen was Luke's match» (L6729). Weigh that against *Inferno*, where Caedus's own POV says Luke beat him.
- **Headline:** this is the **Alema Rar endgame and Allana rescue** book. Caedus kidnaps Allana (Ch5–6). He beats a four-Jedi ambush led by Kyle Katarn and beheads Mithric (Ch10–11). Luke hunts him in space but breaks off on sensing Allana (Ch18–19). **Jag Fel kills Alema Rar** using anti-Jedi tactics (Ch27). Luke, Saba and Ben storm the *Anakin Solo*'s bridge while Han, Leia and Iella take Allana back (Ch35–37). Centerpoint destroys itself (Ch37). Caedus Force-chokes Lt. Tebut (Ch37). Jaina leaves to find «her teacher» (L7067), Boba Fett.

## 2. Combat and Force feats, in chapter order

### Ch1–Ch4
1. **Ch1 · L153, L277–285 · Alema (phantom)**: appears on the *Falcon* undetected. Her ruined arm is whole («Her left arm was useless, had been ruined years earlier-but now it was fine.», L153). She shrugs off Leia's push: «Alema merely rocked back on her heels and looked faintly amused.» (L285). *Shows:* the phantom ignores physics; it is a projection, not a body.
2. **Ch1 · L291–309 · Leia vs Alema phantom**: Leia defends «purely defensively» (L303); the phantom's «strength seemed to» fade (L305) and it vanishes.
3. **Ch4 · L693, L711, L717 · Luke (Masters' sparring)**: pulls a tree branch onto Kyp (L693), disarms Cilghal by catching her hilt (L711), and against a swarm of Force-thrown bolts «he calculated which objects had a chance of hitting him and incinerated only them with his blades» (L717). *Shows:* casual Master-tier control, even while depressed.

### Ch5–Ch6 — Allana kidnapped (Hapes)
4. **Ch5 · L871–875 · Caedus**: finds Allana as «a bright, joyous flare in the Force» and sees through her eyes. He uses a stun hold-out because «stun bolts were far better than lethal attacks, lightsabers, or anything that manifested strongly in the Force» (L897). *Shows:* stealth infiltration of the Queen Mother's palace.
5. **Ch6 · L1003–1061 · Caedus**: kills three palace staff (L1003, L1013), cuts through a floor, blows up the diplomatic shuttle and escapes in a StealthX with Allana. Tenel Ka arrives too late (L1009–1039).

### Ch10–Ch11 — DUEL: Caedus vs Katarn, Valin, Mithric, Hu'lya (Senate plaza, L1545–1745)
6. **L1557 · Caedus**: «Contemptuous, he flicked his hand toward the darkness and felt his own power turn the cylinders around.» (He turns back the rolling grenades.)
7. **L1573–1577 · Caedus**: blocks Katarn, Force-hurls Hu'lya into Mithric, and kicks Valin off his feet. «Two seconds had passed since the attack began.» (L1577)
8. **L1641–1651 · Caedus**: feints a push at Katarn and instead yanks a GAG speeder into Katarn's back, driving him onto his blade: «Katarn looked down to see Caedus's lightsaber buried to its hilt in his chest.» (L1649). Katarn survives. Cilghal on the wound: «The attack severed two ribs, penetrated his left lung, and exited through his left shoulder blade. A few centimeters off, and it would have gone clean through his heart.» (L2397).
9. **L1719–1723 · Caedus**: catches a bolt and Mithric's blade in one motion, deflects the bolt into Mithric's chest, then «Mithric's head fell from his shoulders.» *Caveat:* Caedus «wasn't yet recovered from his duel with Luke» (L1715). He is grazed by a bolt (L1685), and Mithric «was starting to beat down his parries» (L1713).

### Ch12 — Jaina vs Jag (training)
10. **L2035, L2065–2081 · Jaina / Jag**: «Almost faster than the eye could follow, Jaina lunged.» (L2035). She wins two bouts, then Jag takes her blade on beskar, crushes the practice hilt and wins (L2065–2075). Jaina Force-throws him into a tree (L2079–2081). *Shows:* Jaina's speed; the anti-Jedi lesson is set up.

### Ch13–Ch17
11. **Ch13 · L2207 · Caedus**: «his Sith battle meditation ability helped his forces inflict heavy losses in both theaters of operation».
12. **Ch15 · L2543 · Leia**: «She glanced at the button, and it clicked down without Caedus's help.» (TK in front of Caedus).

## Story and prose notes

### SP1. Plot summary, in order
See Section 5 and the raw log. The A-plot (Allana) and B-plot (Alema) converge on the theme of *who can be rescued*: Allana and Luke can, Alema and Jacen cannot.

### SP2. Character profiles
- **Caedus:** a father first in this book. His tenderness with Allana (L3843) and «I love you, Allana.» (L1773) sit beside murders (L1003, L6969). Allston shows his self-image as the best swordsman (L1717) and his rage leaking out (L3251, L6965). The arc runs from «In a day, he would own Centerpoint.» (L5575) to kneeling on the deck (L6859).
- **Luke:** depressed and death-seeking («You want to be dead. At peace. With her. Dead.», L5097). He steps back into duty in Ch28. He keeps his «without hate» vow (L1881, L3283, L6469).
- **Ben:** maturing. His trials are counted as done (L5169), he insists his motive is «Not revenge» (L5353–5355), and he finds the tactical solutions on the bridge.
- **Jaina:** hardening into the "Sword of the Jedi" (L1199–1203). Jag teaches her that she is «training yourself to lose» (L2097). She ends by choosing a "last resort" teacher.
- **Jag:** the Jedi-hunter. Killing Alema ends his clan duty and his last tie to it (L5421).
- **Alema:** the tragic end of the Dark Nest. «Remember.» (L4793).
- **Zekk:** his Shadow Academy past resurfaces (L4383) and is a vulnerability.

### SP3. Themes
- **Fury / anger as weakness:** Caedus's rage breaks his concealment (L3251) and leads him to murder (L6965–6971). The Jedi win by *not* hating (L3283, L6469).
- **Attachment:** Ben's definition (L5107–5109); Caedus's attachment to Allana is what defeats him on the bridge (L6733).
- **Let the enemy do the work:** the operation's catchphrase (L5707, L5887) mirrors Jag's anti-Jedi lessons and Seyah's self-targeting trick.
- **Fathers and children:** Luke/Ben, Caedus/Allana, Wedge/Syal, Han/Leia/Allana.
