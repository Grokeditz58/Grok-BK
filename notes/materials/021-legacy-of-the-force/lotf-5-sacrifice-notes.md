# Legacy of the Force 5: Sacrifice (novel, read in full): feats, scaling and story notes

- **Book:** *Star Wars: Legacy of the Force: Sacrifice* by Karen Traviss (2007). Fifth of the nine Legacy of the Force novels.
- **Continuity:** Legends. Set in 40 ABY, during the Second Galactic Civil War (Galactic Alliance vs. the Confederation). It comes after *Exile* (LOTF 4) and before *Inferno* (LOTF 6). Jacen Solo becomes Darth Caedus on the last line (L9584).

- **Method:** I read every line in order: Prologue, then Ch1–Ch24. `L####` is the line number in that file. Text inside «…» is copied exactly from the file. A script checked every «quote» against the source (see the end of this file). The raw reading log I wrote while reading is kept as an appendix at the bottom.
- **Chapter heading lines:** Prologue L64, Ch1 L136, Ch2 L623, Ch3 L1252, Ch4 L1881, Ch5 L2698, Ch6 L3291, Ch7 L3826, Ch8 L4397, Ch9 L4767, Ch10 L5094, Ch11 L5523, Ch12 L6164, Ch13 L6309, Ch14 L6662, Ch15 L6957, Ch16 L7412, Ch17 L7645, Ch18 L7868, Ch19 L8081, Ch20 L8264, Ch21 L8447, Ch22 L8580, Ch23 L9075, Ch24 L9400.
- **Feat vs. statement:** Section 2 covers what the narration shows happening. Section 3 covers what characters say or think about power, marked **STATEMENT**. Traviss writes in tight third-person POV, so a lot of the "narration" is one character's opinion; those entries are marked *(POV)*.
- **Headline:** this is the **Mara vs. Jacen** book. It has three real fights: Mara vs. Lumiya (+ the Sith sphere) on Hesperidium in Ch10, Mara vs. Jacen in the Kavan tunnels in Ch20–21, and Luke vs. Lumiya on Terephon in Ch22. On points, Mara is beating Jacen when he wins with an illusion of Ben's face and a poison dart.

## 2. Combat and Force feats, in chapter order

### Ch1–Ch4 (setup)
1. **Ch1 · L224 · Jacen (POV Ben)**: «Jacen's trick of hiding in the Force had become permanent; Ben found he was impossible to track unless he wanted to be found». *Shows:* Jacen's presence suppression is constant. This sets up the whole book's stealth game.
2. **Ch1 · L258–276 · Jacen**: senses Ben across the Perlemian corridor, then magnifies his own presence to reach him. *Shows:* long-range sensing.
3. **Ch1 · L226, L312 · Ben + Sith sphere**: Ben flies the sphere by thought, and it destroys a pursuing ship in answer to his thoughts. *Shows:* the ship's weapons acting on intent, not a personal feat.
4. **Ch2 · L861 · Lumiya**: senses Alema «like a beacon even at this range». *Shows:* sensing.
5. **Ch2 · L989–991 · Lumiya**: rams a vibroblade into her own thigh and hits metal: «I'm actually more machine than organic». *Shows:* how much of her is cybernetic, which explains her durability later (L5256 «She hit solid durasteel.»).
6. **Ch3 · L1434–1436 · Fett/Mirta**: Fett lands a left hook on a barkeep reaching for a disruptor, and both draw first. *Shows:* baseline unpowered skill.
7. **Ch4 · L2057 · Jacen**: «Force-enhanced hearing» to read a procurement meeting. Minor.
8. **Ch4 · L2545–2573 · Ben**: learns **Force camouflage** from Jacen on his first try. Jacen: «"Now," Jacen said softly, "you see why practice is necessary. But full marks for technique."» (L2565). *Shows:* Ben learns this very fast. The technique decides the book's climax.
9. **Ch4 · L2297 · Fett**: fibercord-loops the gangster Fraig over a balcony. **L2381–2397:** an unseen sniper (Jaing) silently drops the pursuing speeders. *Shows:* Mando skill set.

### Ch5–Ch9 (preparation, the Gejjen hit)
10. **Ch5 · L3188 · Jacen vs. Mara (deception)**: he projects «a veneer of weary good humor in the Force» at Mara, and she does not see through him this time. *Shows:* Jacen fools a former Emperor's Hand.
11. **Ch5 · L3248–3282 · Lumiya**: Force-influences the Senate P&R Council from the gallery so it passes Item 357. «I just redirected a little opposition from the gallery.» (L3282). *Shows:* subtle mass influence on a committee.
12. **Ch5 · L2860 · Jaing Skirata**: ambushes Mirta with no warning. Mirta: «I didn't pick him up on my sensors and I didn't even see him coming at me.» *Shows:* unpowered stealth.
13. **Ch7 · L4140–4168 · Ben (sniper training)**: «It was just like using a lightsaber, really. Letting the Force guide the hand, the eye . . .» (L4140). After a couple of hours he hits the five-centimeter zone 95 percent of the time (L4168), and later 97 percent (L4423). *Shows:* Force-guided marksmanship.
14. **Ch8 · L4529–4543 · Luke vs. Lumiya (sensing)**: Luke senses Lumiya at his apartment, but only because she lets him (L4527). He Force-jams the corridor doors (L4535), but she is already gone and leaves Ben's boots behind. *Shows:* Lumiya's stealth beats Luke's sensing until she chooses to show herself. Luke also gets an impression of Ben from the boots (L4543).
15. **Ch9 · L4953–4955 · Ben**: counts six beings in a sealed room through the Force.
16. **Ch9 · L5079 · Ben**: kills Dur Gejjen with a rifle shot from about 100 m: «Yes, he'd killed Gejjen, a textbook head shot.» *Shows:* assassination skill. (Lekauf then blows himself up as the cover story, L5516.)

### Ch10 — DUEL A: Mara vs. Lumiya (and the Sith sphere), Hesperidium
17. **Ch10 · L5248 · Mara**: «She Force-leapt five meters at Lumiya, slashing down right to left, two-handed.» The stroke clips Lumiya's headdress, and Mara slows the whip tails with a Force push.
18. **Ch10 · L5250 · Lumiya (weapon)**: the lightwhip is «leather strips studded with impervious Mandalorian iron fragments and tendrils of sheer, raw, murderous dark energy». It gouges through a parked ship's durasteel hull.
19. **Ch10 · L5252 · Mara**: a blaster hit on Lumiya's shoulder: «A puff of white vapor burst from Lumiya's shoulder».
20. **Ch10 · L5256–5258 · Mara**: «cannoned into Lumiya's legs with all the power she could muster from the Force. She hit solid durasteel.» She then head-butts the cybernetic jaw.
21. **Ch10 · L5258 · Mara**: the hilt-dagger trick. «she killed the lightsaber blade for a second and held the hilt like a dagger, stabbing it down into Lumiya's chest before flicking the energy back on. Lumiya pulled to the side as the blade punched through flesh.» *Shows:* Mara lands a near-kill blow.
22. **Ch10 · L5268 + Ch14 L6816 + Ch17 L7775 · Sith sphere**: a cable wraps Mara's neck from behind and throws her. Mara: «It wasn't a droid. I got bushwhacked by a living ship, a Sith ship.» (L6816). The sphere later confirms it «stopped her fighting» (L7775). **Context: 2v1.** Lumiya was on a decoy mission (L3910, L3940, L6599). She flees wounded and does not finish Mara (L5302).
23. **Ch10 · L5278 · Mara**: with a concussion and a black eye, she mind-tricks two joggers: «You didn't see a fight at all.»

### Ch18–Ch19 (pursuit and space fight, Hapes Cluster)
30. **Ch18 · L7964–7970 · Ben**: tails the sphere while shut down in the Force, and maps its position by its silent "voice".

36. **Ch20 · L8255 · Mara (prep)**: picks the terrain. «If I get him in a confined space, he can't use all his Force skills, but I can make the most of what I've got.» She hides in the Force, then makes her presence loud to lure him (L8259–8261).

39. **Ch20 · L8402, L8426 · Mara (TK)**: «An intense Force shake of the brickwork and arches in front of and behind the metal plate weakened them, and then she stopped them from collapsing by Force pressure.» Then «She brought down both sections of tunnel, before and behind, with a massive exertion in the Force that made her breathless.» *Shows:* she buries Jacen under a tunnel collapse.
40. **Ch20 · L8438–8444 · Mara**: fires her blaster point-blank at the eye of the buried Jacen.

Bricks hammered the walls and rained fragments, knocking Mara flat like a bomb blast.» *Shows:* while crushed, he deflects a point-blank bolt and throws off the rubble in one burst. It costs him: «The effort froze Jacen for two vital seconds.» (L8473).
42. **Ch21 · L8485 · Mara**: shoto in her left hand, fencing-style, plus the vibroblade: «seared the angle of his cheekbone, and caught him under the tip of chin with the vibroblade».
43. **Ch21 · L8487 · Mara vs. Jacen**: he Force-pushes her into a wall, «but she kept coming at him, one-two, one-two with the shoto and the blade, and he was being driven back, his strength ebbing.»

49. **Ch21 · L8567, L8577 · Ben, Luke**: both feel her death at a distance. Luke «felt a hand brush his hair».

## 5. Plot summary relevant to the fights
1. Lumiya reads the prophecy «He will immortalize his love» (L140–158) as an order to kill what Jacen loves most. Jacen dreads it means his daughter Allana, so he settles on Ben (L1558).
2. Jacen teaches Ben Force camouflage (Ch4). Ben assassinates Gejjen (Ch9). Jacen and Niathal depose Omas with a legal coup (Ch12–13).
3. Lumiya fights Mara on Hesperidium as a decoy (Ch10). Mara plants a transponder in the sphere.
4. Ben confesses the assassination to Mara (Ch11), eavesdrops on Jacen and Lumiya (Ch13), and teaches Mara camouflage (Ch14). Mara decides Jacen must die (L7389), gives him one last chance (Ch16), and then hunts him.
5. Jacen visits Allana on Hapes. Mara follows Lumiya's transponder there. They dogfight, and Lumiya's sphere disables Mara's fighter. Jacen fires on Lumiya and fails to kill her (Ch19).
6. On Kavan, Mara ambushes Jacen and nearly kills him. He uses an illusion of Ben and a poison dart. Mara dies saying "Ben" (Ch20–21).

### SP2. Character profiles

## 6. Verdict-relevant takeaways
1. **Caedus vs. Mara:** the only on-page result is a Jacen win, but on conditions that favored Mara (her terrain, her ambush, his injuries). She was winning the physical fight. He was Jacen, not yet the full Sith Lord: his Sith battle-sight only arrives after her death (L9293), which by the book's own logic is a power-up. Both POVs agree that Jacen is the stronger Force user (S5, S7). **Fair 1v1 at full power: Caedus wins most of the time. Mara with prep can kill pre-Caedus Jacen.**
2. **Caedus vs. Luke:** this book only gives statements. Lumiya's «Luke can still take you in a lightsaber fight.» (L3958) and Jacen's own poison planning (L6948) both point to Luke.
3. **Jaina vs. Caedus:** no data here.
4. **Pre-Caedus Jacen's top Force feat in the book:** while crushed and bleeding, he deflects a point-blank bolt and blasts off a tunnel's worth of rubble (L8471).
5. **Stealth matters a great deal at this tier.** Camouflage let Ben, then Mara, get past Jacen's senses several times.
