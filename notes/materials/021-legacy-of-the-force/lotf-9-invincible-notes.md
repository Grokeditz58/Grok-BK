# Legacy of the Force 9: Invincible (novel, read in full): feats, scaling and story notes

<!-- read up to: L5971, END OF BOOK; COMPLETE -->

- **Book:** *Star Wars: Legacy of the Force: Invincible* by Troy Denning (2008). Ninth and final Legacy of the Force novel.
- **Continuity:** Legends, 41 ABY, «three weeks after the battle of Fondor» (L115). Comes after *Revelation* (LotF 8); leads into the *Fate of the Jedi* era (Daala as Chief of State, L5870). Not to be confused with `invincible-notes.md`, which covers the unrelated *Invincible* comic.
- **Source file:** `[local path]` (5,971 lines; working copy `[local path]`). OCR noise kept verbatim (colons for ellipses, e.g. «Okay, Dad:Caedus must die»).
- **Method (be precise about depth):** I went through every non-blank line in order, front matter to L5971. Most narrative paragraphs were read through a per-paragraph view (first ~105 characters, with the file line number). **Every fight and Force-power passage was then pulled and read in full**: Nickel One (Ch8–9), Ben vs Tahiri (Ch10, Ch19), shatterpoint (Ch14), the Throat battle (Ch15), the final duel (Ch18). So were every Caedus/Jaina/Luke power statement and the epilogue. All «…» quotes were copied from full lines. Politics and banter passages were read at the paragraph-opening level only. `L####` is the line number in the source file.
- **Chapter heading lines:** Prologue L65, Ch1 L107, Ch2 L549, Ch3 L871, Ch4 L1119, Ch5 L1405, Ch6 L1611, Ch7 L1885, Ch8 L2029, Ch9 L2351 (printed «9:»), Ch10 L2640, Ch11 L2992, Ch12 L3272, Ch13 L3576, Ch14 L3834, Ch15 L4086, Ch16 L4364, Ch17 L4644, Ch18 L4934, Ch19 L5190 («19:»), Ch20 L5520 («20:»), Epilogue L5798.
- **Feat vs. statement:** Section 2 is what the narration shows; Section 3 is what characters say or think (**STATEMENT**); *(POV)* marks narration filtered through a character. **Denning's framing:** the book is built to let Jaina win as the «Sword of the Jedi» (L799, L5632–5640). It does so by preparation (Mandalorian training, shatterpoint), by Caedus's arrogance (S9, S10), and by catching him wounded and distracted. Then it reframes his death as a half-redemption: he stops fighting to warn Tenel Ka (L5182–5184). It is **not** framed as Jaina out-powering him; she says the opposite (S6).
- **Headline:** the **Jaina-kills-Caedus** book. At Nickel One, Jaina, boosted by Luke flow-walking or projecting into her, takes Caedus's arm with a Mandalorian beskad. Caedus tracks the Jedi base by a Nightsister blood trail on her skin. Luke teaches Jaina shatterpoint, and she crumbles beskar with it. Caedus kills Isolder. In the *Anakin Solo*'s biodisposal pit, Jaina, alone this time, guts Caedus at the door, survives lightning, incinerator and saber, cuts his Achilles tendon, and runs him through as he stops to warn Tenel Ka. Ben spares a broken Tahiri. Luke hands the Remnant to Jag; Daala becomes Chief of State; Allana survives as "Amelia".

## Story and prose notes

### SP1. Plot summary, in order
See Section 5 and the raw log. Denning structures the book in mirrored halves: the boosted assassination attempt (Nickel One) and the solo one (biodisposal pit). The "A LONG TIME AGO" twin flashbacks (L67, L2982, L5504) frame the twin bond being lost and finally felt again (L5564).

### SP2. Character arcs
- **Caedus/Jacen:** tries to recommit to «To serve.» (L887), but kills Isolder, tortures Mirta, and uses Tahiri's grief («That was kind of the point», L4632). His love for Allana survives to the end: he vetoes the nanokiller (L4596) and dies warning Tenel Ka (L5184). Jaina's verdict: «he became Jacen again for a second» (L5914); the galaxy he wanted is born under Daala (L5904).
- **Jaina:** from «I still love him ... But that won't make me hesitate» (L795) to killing him, and then mourning him with the twin bond restored (L5564). She wins by doing it «her own way» (L5004), drawing strength from love, not anger (L5154).
- **Luke:** foresight strategist. He jams Caedus's visions, sacrifices Ben's freedom and Jaina's anonymity, and refuses to be the killer because «I win» would make him the next darkness (L1505, L769). Political endgame: Jag over the Moffs.
- **Ben:** survives torture, keeps his cop's ethic (spares the guards, L2952; spares Tahiri, L5428), offers to lie for her (L5484).
- **Tahiri:** her Sith motive (Anakin) is exposed as perception only (L4446–4476); she kills Shevu by accident (L2920–2928), then surrenders: «I'm done with all Solos» (L5466).
- **Mara's aftermath:** present only as motive (Luke's vengeance, L731, L2519).
- **Boba Fett / Mandalorians:** Fett stays outside the duel; Roegr and Vatok die; Mirta paralyzed; Fett and Mirta exiled from Mandalore by the nanokiller (L5848). Fett's lessons pay off in Jaina's beskad cut and commitment.
- **Han and Leia:** «Caedus must die» (L709) to grief; they adopt Allana (L5930–5938).

### SP3. Themes
- **Sacrifice and its cost:** Luke won't kill Caedus because victory would corrupt him; Jaina kills Caedus at the cost of her twin.
- **Love as the light-side engine:** L5154 is the thesis (contrast with Caedus feeding on pain, L5134).
- **Hubris and foresight:** Caedus «I've seen it» (L4404) vs Luke's jamming (L1469).
- **Jacen's vision fulfilled by others:** Daala's peace (L5902–5904).

### SP6. Timeline placement and connections
- 41 ABY, ending LotF. Leads to *Fate of the Jedi* (Daala's government, Jedi distrust, Allana as Amelia Solo, Tahiri's trial; «THE STORY CONTINUES», L5944).
- **Author intent:** Denning chooses Jaina to win (Sword of the Jedi), Luke to remain the stronger but morally restrained Master (S1), and Caedus to die half-redeemed. He goes out of his way to show Caedus as frightening even crippled (L2586, L5102–5104, L5134), so the win reads as earned but not a power upset.

## 6. Verdict-relevant takeaways
1. **Jaina vs Caedus:** Jaina wins the fight that happened (Fight C), alone, by killing him. But the conditions strongly favor her: he was one-armed, gut-stabbed at once, distracted by the Allana crisis, and at the end he chose not to defend. She calls him «magnitudes stronger» in the Force (S6). **Verdict: Jaina beats Invincible-era Caedus in the conditions Denning gives (high confidence for the in-book result). Jaina vs a healthy, focused Caedus is still a Caedus favorite on raw power, but a closer one given her prep, beskad/shatterpoint, and his tendency to underrate her (medium).**
2. **Caedus vs Luke:** S1 (Luke foresees winning), S3–S5 (Caedus fears him one-armed, «barely» survived Inferno), plus *Revelation* Fight A. **Luke > Caedus, medium-high → high.**
3. **Caedus's final tier:** in breadth, Caedus is a top Master/Sith Lord: shatterpoint, Aing-Tii fighting-sight, blood trail, fleet battle meditation and the mass mind-influence of *Revelation*. Feats 7, 8, 12, 24 and 26 show he stayed dangerous while crippled. He remains below Luke and was killed by a well-prepared Knight under heavy disadvantage. **Final tier: high Master / Sith Lord, below Luke.**
4. **Nickel One is not clean Jaina-solo evidence** (S10, L2598). Weight it as Jaina+Luke.
5. **Ben > Tahiri** (Fight D), and Tahiri is out of the Sith role.
