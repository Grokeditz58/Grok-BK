# Legacy of the Force 6: Inferno (novel, read in full): feats, scaling and story notes

<!-- read up to: chapter twenty-one + back-matter interview (L5525), END OF BOOK; restructured into house style -->

- **Book:** *Star Wars: Legacy of the Force: Inferno* by Troy Denning (2007). Sixth of the nine Legacy of the Force novels.
- **Continuity:** Legends. Set in 40 ABY, during the Second Galactic Civil War, about a week after Mara's murder (L95). It comes after *Sacrifice* (LOTF 5) and before *Fury* (LOTF 7). Jacen is already Darth Caedus throughout; the dramatis personae lists him as «Jacen Solo; Sith Lord (male human)» (L33).
- **Source file:** `[local path]` (5,526 lines; OCR noise such as "It Caedus" for "If Caedus" is kept verbatim and marked sic).
- **Method:** I read every line in order: front matter, Prologue, Ch1–Ch21, then the back-matter "Round-Robin Interview" with Allston, Traviss and Denning. `L####` is the line number in that file. Text inside «…» is copied exactly from the file and was checked by script (see the end of this file). The raw reading log is kept as an appendix.
- **Chapter heading lines:** Prologue L59, Ch1 L183, Ch2 L423, Ch3 L563, Ch4 L931, Ch5 L1333, Ch6 L1531, Ch7 L1703, Ch8 L1869, Ch9 L2131, Ch10 L2347, Ch11 L2479, Ch12 L2779, Ch13 L3007, Ch14 L3167, Ch15 L3421, Ch16 L3645, Ch17 L4065, Ch18 L4329, Ch19 L4615, Ch20 L4947, Ch21 L5281; interview L5381.
- **Feat vs. statement:** Section 2 covers what the narration shows happening. Section 3 covers what characters say or think, marked **STATEMENT**. Denning writes in rotating tight third person, so a lot of "narration" is a character's view; those are marked *(POV)*.
- **Headline:** this is the **Luke vs. Caedus** book. Luke humiliates Caedus with raw telekinesis in Ch11, hides from him completely (Ch8, Ch17, Ch19), and then beats him in a no-quarter duel in the Embrace-of-Pain chamber (Ch19). Caedus's own POV concedes: «Luke had beaten him.» (L4961). Also: Ben's assassination attempt on Caedus, Caedus burns Kashyyyk, Jaina "kills" Luke (he faked it), Tenel Ka turns on Caedus, Leia vs. Tahiri, and the One Sith on Korriban.

## 2. Combat and Force feats, in chapter order

### Prologue–Ch5
1. **Prologue · L73–L159 · Caedus**: flow-walks Tahiri into the past at Myrkr: «I've been flow-walking for years» (L77). *Shows:* time-perception power. Lore: «Any change they made in the past would be corrected by the Force» (L73).
2. **Ch1 · L187–211 · Tenel Ka**: detects the camouflaged Jacen: «a void so subtle she recognized it only by the surrounding stillness.» (L187). *Shows:* Tenel Ka's sensing beats his camouflage, helped by intimacy.
3. **Ch2 · L479–543 · Leia (+Han) vs. ~12 GAG troopers**: Force-hurls the lieutenant into his squad (L491), sweeps shock nets away (L509–511), slashes off a recruit's arms at the elbows (L525), deflects bolts, and steers a grenade (L541–543). Her mind trick is broken by a trained corporal: «I'm not weak-minded, you know.» (L485). *Shows:* Leia as a competent combat Knight; GAG has anti-mind-trick training.
4. **Ch3 · L721, L771 · Mara (posthumous)**: her body dematerializes at the funeral. *Shows:* she became one with the Force.
5. **Ch4 · L1159–1165 · Jaina**: «batted half a dozen stun bolts back at the men who had fired them» (L1159) and Force-hurls a trooper «across two control stations» (L1165).
6. **Ch5 · L1381–1411 · Alema**: mind-wipes guards (L1381); overpowers the Sith sphere's will: «She pushed harder against its resolve, this time forsaking subtlety for sheer power.» (L1399). *Shows:* Alema's raw strength against a sentient Sith artifact.
7. **Ch5 · L1449–1471 · Alema**: gets through Lumiya's trap gauntlet, including a damage-reflecting Force illusion (L1471).

### Ch6–Ch9 (Balmorra, Omas)
8. **Ch8 · L1917–1925 · Caedus**: precognition. He pushes «his Force-awareness forward into the next few seconds» (L1917) and calls the enemy flight's destruction.
9. **Ch8 · L1945, L1981 · Caedus (limits)**: his battle meditation fails: «The fleet reflected back only confusion and fear» (L1945); «a humbling reminder that battle meditation was not infallible» (L1981).
10. **Ch8 · L2007–2009 · Caedus**: reacts to a sudden mass of Jedi presences with a «reverse flip over the seat back», landing «three meters inside his day cabin, his lightsaber in hand».
11. **Ch8 · L2061–2127 · Luke**: appears in Caedus's day cabin, speaks, and vanishes. Caedus searches «the entire Anakin Solo. He felt no sign of his uncle's presence anywhere.» (L2117). *Shows:* Luke's concealment or projection beats Caedus's senses on his own flagship.
12. **Ch9 · L2139–2163 · Ben vs. Guardian droid**: his saber cuts only halfway through a laminanium arm (L2153); Force-guided second strike through the same cut (L2155); he Force-throws the droid off (L2163) and Force-holds Omas mid-step (L2227–2229).
13. **Ch9 · L2317–2343 · Omas / Ben**: Omas impales himself on Ben's saber to make Ben's cover real; Ben throws back stun grenades and leads CorSec out before the death-trap blows.

### Ch11 — Luke humiliates Caedus (day cabin, Kuat)
14. **Ch11 · L2559 · Luke**: «Luke had not gestured, had not flinched, had not even shifted his gaze; he had simply grabbed Caedus in the Force and hurled him five meters into his chair.» *Shows:* effortless TK on a Sith Lord.
15. **Ch11 · L2563–2569 · Luke**: Caedus «found himself struggling against an invisible weight» (L2563). «While it was true that Luke had taken him by surprise, it was equally true that he had done so with no visible effort-and that he was continuing to hold him with no apparent exertion.» (L2569). *Shows:* Luke's TK hold beats Caedus's active resistance. The book flags the surprise caveat itself.
16. **Ch11 · L2613–2619 · Luke**: keeps Caedus pinned while walking to the door, with only part of his attention.
17. **Ch11 · L2631–2635 · Luke**: crushes the chair pedestal by tapping a finger: «the pedestal collapsed with a low loud crump, depositing Caedus on the floor with his feet sticking out in front of him like a child.»

## 5. Plot summary relevant to the fights
- **Ch1–Ch5:** Caedus corrupts Tahiri by flow-walking; Tenel Ka gives him the Home Fleet on condition of peace with Luke. At Mara's funeral her body vanishes; GAG tries to arrest the Solos. Serpa seizes the Ossus academy. Alema takes the Sith sphere and finds Lumiya's datachip pointing to more Sith.
- **Ch6–Ch10:** Balmorra. Caedus's battle meditation falters; Luke "visits" his cabin and vanishes. Caedus doctors a recording to aim Ben at Omas. Omas dies on Ben's blade by choice.
- **Ch11–Ch14:** Luke pins and humiliates Caedus, then the Jedi desert at Kuat. Leia wins over Kashyyyk. Ben returns to Caedus to kill him and betrays Luke's Kashyyyk plan to win back trust. Jaina, Zekk and Jag retake Ossus; Kam Solusar and Tionne are shot down (they survive, per *Fury* L683).
- **Ch15–Ch18:** Caedus burns Kashyyyk. Ben strikes and fails, is lightning-blasted, and goes into the Embrace of Pain. Alema meets the One Sith on Korriban. Luke "dies" to Jaina's fire. Leia fights Tahiri; Tenel Ka turns.
- **Ch19–Ch21:** Luke attacks Caedus in the torture chamber and wins; Ben stabs Caedus; Luke spares him. Tenel Ka demands Caedus's surrender and fires on him. Caedus and Alema push the Bothans into a mistake, and the Fifth escapes. The Jedi declare «As long as Jacen controls the Alliance, there is no Alliance.» (L5325).

## Story and prose notes

### SP1. Plot summary, in order
See Section 5. Structurally, the book alternates three hunts: the Jedi hunting Caedus (Luke, Ben), Jaina hunting Alema, and Alema hunting for Sith help for Jacen. They meet at Kashyyyk.

### SP2. Character profiles
- **Caedus:** Denning's Caedus is a self-sacrificing zealot who still loves. «This was what it meant to be Sith: to use friends without hesitation, to sacrifice family for destiny, to live with a stained soul.» (L179). He powers his meditation «through his love of them all» (L5269). He tests Ben by presenting his back (L3471, L3519). He takes Tenel Ka's betrayal as «one more offering to his Sith destiny» (L5153).
- **Luke:** goes from grief-paralysis to decisive action. He uses deception (fake reconciliation, fake death) where *Sacrifice* had him trusting. He still refuses vengeance: «I did that with Lumiya, and all it did was make me weak.» (L4909).
- **Ben:** plays double agent and trades lives to keep his cover (L4691), which makes him «more like Jacen than even Jacen knew» (L4695). Luke pulls him back from the kill (L4933–4943).
- **Jaina:** commando and ace. She decides Jacen «become another Emperor, and he simply had to be stopped» (L4175). She ends Ch17 believing she killed Luke.
- **Leia / Han:** «Jacen Solo is dead. My son wouldn't do this.» (L3663). Leia mourns her living son as she mourned Anakin (L3669). She nearly kills Tahiri in rage (L4479).

## 6. Verdict-relevant takeaways
1. **Caedus vs. Luke:** the strongest evidence in the series so far for Luke. Luke out-muscles Caedus in raw TK with no visible effort (Fight A), out-hides him (S9), and beats him in a real duel (Fight E). Caedus says so himself (S1). Caveats: surprise both times, Luke's rage, and Caedus not yet at his peak (see *Invincible*). **Luke > Caedus, high confidence for this point in the timeline.**
2. **Caedus vs. Mara:** Caedus's own admission that «Mara had demonstrated when she nearly killed him on Kavan» (L2011) supports the view that the *Sacrifice* duel was close and that prepared Mara is a real threat.
3. **Jaina vs. Caedus:** only the dogfight. Caedus anticipates her shots (S14) but she still damages him (L4301, L4315). Not a duel data point.
4. **Caedus's best tools are tricks and endurance:** pain-feeding (S21), feints (thorn bed, garrote), lightning, and the willingness to run. When cornered by a stronger opponent he relies on cunning.
5. **Caedus's battle meditation is a strategic multiplier** (Fight F) and lets him escape a four-fleet trap while badly wounded.

### 2. Luke Skywalker
- **Role/arc:** goes from grief to decisive deception (a fake reconciliation, then a fake death). Refuses vengeance: «I did that with Lumiya, and all it did was make me weak.» (L4909).
- **Key feats:** pins Caedus without effort: «he had simply grabbed Caedus in the Force and hurled him five meters into his chair.» (Ch11, L2559). Holds him «with no apparent exertion» (L2569), then collapses the chair pedestal with a finger tap (L2631–2635). Hides from all but Ben: «Not even Jacen had that kind of control.» (Ch19, L4741). In the duel he lands «an elbow smash to the temple that dropped Jacen to his knees» (L4821) and «a snap-kick to the stomach that lifted Jacen a meter off the deck» (L4831), and escapes the garrote (L4893). Flying: «ducking cannon bolts as though he had a telepathic connection to the gunner's mind» (Ch17, L4227).
- **Statements:** Caedus's POV: «the galaxy was littered with the body parts of those who had underestimated the strength of Luke Skywalker.» (L713). Jaina's POV: he «hadn't revealed half of what he could do. Maybe not even a quarter.» (L4227).
- **Fights/results:** beats Caedus (Ch19). Injuries include a broken nose, a popped knee and a garrote wound. He had surprise and was in a battle rage.
- **Scaling:** the decisive Luke > Caedus data point (high confidence).

### 3. Ben Skywalker

- **Key feats:** a Force-guided second strike through the cut in a Guardian droid's laminanium arm (Ch9, L2155). Surprise saber strike that only grazes Caedus (L3627). Vibrodagger between Caedus's shoulder blades (Ch19, L4895–4901).
- **Statements:** Caedus to Ben: «it will take decades to develop the power to confront me openly.» (L4639).
- **Scaling:** far below Caedus head-on. Dangerous through stealth.

### 4. Jaina Solo
