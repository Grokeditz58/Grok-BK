## 1. Plot and themes

### Plot (in order)
- **Ch1–2:** Luke dreams of a shadowed, Anakin-like figure who «brings great pain to the galaxy» (L93). Jacen and Ben raid an Adumari munitions factory and escape a missile-armed airspeeder.
- **Ch3–6:** Solo–Skywalker family dinner; Han resents the GA's pressure on Corellia. The GA plans a show of force; Jacen is sent to sabotage Centerpoint Station.
- **Ch7–16:** Klauskin's fleet arrives off Corellia. Jaina, Zekk, Kolir and Thann fail to kidnap Prime Minister Saxan's convoy party (Ch9, Ch14). At Centerpoint, Thrackan Sal-Solo floors Jacen with a sonic weapon; Jacen fights through, then is tricked twice by Thrackan's recordings (Ch10–15). Ben, separately, faces the "Anakin Solo" droid (Ch15). Centerpoint's self-destruct is triggered; Thrackan escapes.
- **Ch17–21:** Peace talks at Toryaz Station. A commando raid hits every delegation at once; Luke, Mara, Jacen, Jaina, Zekk, Han and Leia repel it (Ch19–20). Investigation follows.
- **Ch22–28:** Luke and Corran in Coronet; Han throws in with Corellia; Jacen and Ben, with Nelani Dinn, track Jedi-linked "phantom" incidents on Lorrd and meet "Brisha Syo".
- **Ch29–33:** The Bimmiel-area asteroid. Phantoms separate Ben and Nelani; a phantom Luke tests Jacen. Simultaneously phantom Jacen and phantom Ben attack Luke and Mara on Coruscant. Brisha reveals she is Lumiya; Nelani fights her; Jacen's foresight shows Luke dying by his hand along every path where Nelani lives; he kills Nelani.
- **Ch34:** The Tralus battle ends; Leia renounces doing evil to protect family. Lumiya, alone, talks with a phantom of the Sith Jacen will become. Jacen lies to Ben. Luke senses «He exists» (L8269).

### Themes
- **The reasonable road to the dark side.** Lumiya never uses a mind trick on Jacen; Nelani herself confirms «Lumiya isn't using any Force techniques against you» (L7917). Jacen falls to an argument plus foresight.
- **Sacrifice of one to save many** (Lumiya: «sometimes this means sacrificing one so that twenty may live», L7901), the same logic Leia rejects in Ch34 («she would not give up a life she did not have the right to sacrifice», L8183).
- **Attachment.** Leia's Ch34 meditation mirrors Lumiya's Sith «balance» speech (L8203, L8237).
- **Luke's denial.** Lumiya: Luke can't see the dream figure's face because he won't accept «your face where the next Lord of the Sith stands» (L7905).

## 5. Scaling connections
- **Luke vs Jacen (Betrayal-era):** S3 (Jacen concedes), D10/S8–S10 (a phantom at roughly Luke's level, boosted by the place's dark side energy, beats Jacen; Jacen judges it «a fair match for Luke»). Indirect but consistent: **Luke > Jacen at 40 ABY start**, matching `lotf-2..4` and the high-confidence Luke > Caedus verdict.
- **Jacen's growth line:** Betrayal Jacen = elite Knight (bolt-catching F11, decisive TK F10, perfect foresight F33, kills Nelani without effort). Already a top duelist below Luke, far below his Sacrifice–Invincible Caedus peak.
- **Lumiya:** superior technique vs a Knight (D11), multi-phantom coordination (F27), even TK push-of-war with Nelani while talking (F31, she is visibly straining, L7897). Fits the existing `verdicts.md` entries Mara ≥ Lumiya and Luke > Lumiya.
- **Mara:** routs a phantom Ben (D8) and kills an armored commando (F17) in the opening second of a raid.
- **Ben (13–14):** competent apprentice, KO'd by phantom Mara (D9, S20).

## Character analyses (10 characters)

### 1. Jacen Solo (future Darth Caedus)
- **Role/arc:** a disillusioned Knight whose «life of the Jedi hadn't given him the sense of purpose it seemed to have provided his uncle» (L7785). Persuaded by argument, not manipulation (S15), he frees Lumiya and kills Nelani to avert the future where he kills Luke (L7983).
- **Key feats:** bolt redirection and saber deflection (F1–F2, F8); pain-mastery recovery from the sonic trap (L2263: «A sudden infliction of pain could surprise him, surely. But it couldn't keep him down.»); bare-handed bolt catch (F11); TK disarm and wall-slam of Thrackan (F10, F12); thrown-saber kill (F20); long-range foresight across «Scores of time lines» (F33, L7955); memory editing (F36).
- **Statements about him:** Thrackan, impressed (S4). Lumiya: «the first one in centuries with the strength to use the Sith techniques to help others» (S13, pitch). Luke's dream: Anakin-like features (S2).
- **Quote:** «The law is what we make of it.» (L7933).
- **Fights/results:** wins D1–D3 (with Thrackan's tricks costing him twice), D12 (kills Nelani). Loses D10 to phantom Luke.
- **Scaling:** high Knight / near-Master; clearly below Luke here (S3, D10). LotF 1 adds no new peak to Caedus's tier (verdicts.md unchanged on tier).

### 2. Luke Skywalker
- **Role/arc:** Grand Master haunted by a dream (S1–S2); politically sidelined; at the end he knows the figure «finally exists» (S21).
- **Key feats:** reverses a blast-propelled door (F13); simultaneous Force sweeps (F14); tears down a ceiling on attackers (F15); rips a Jedi-trap net (F16); fends off phantom Jacen «with little effort» (F21, F23).
- **Statements:** Jacen: «No, just educated in different directions» when asked if he's stronger than Luke (S3). The phantom built from him nearly kills Jacen (S9–S10).
- **Quote:** his waking words open the book (L81): he doesn't exist (printed in capitals in the source).
- **Fights:** D5, D7 (wins).
- **Scaling:** top of the LotF ladder; supports Luke > Jacen/Caedus (high).

### 3. Ben Skywalker
- **Role/arc:** Jacen's apprentice; capable but young; his memories of Brisha erased (F36).
- **Feats:** helps Jacen through the factory and turbolift (F3); cuts the Adumari airspeeder (L417–L421); outmaneuvers phantom Mara's TK (F24).
- **Statements:** Lumiya: «not yet strong enough to face a Sith» (S5). Ben: «She kicked the stuffing out of me.» (S20).
- **Fights:** D4 (stopped by shock tech), D9 (KO'd by phantom Mara).
- **Scaling:** apprentice tier; below every Knight in the book.

### 4. Mara Jade Skywalker
- **Role/arc:** Luke's partner; protective of Ben («is more open. More trusting.», L673).
- **Feats:** one-motion kill through rifle and neck (F17); Force-throws phantom Ben (F22); Force-pulls her saber from sleep (L7229).
- **Statements:** Mara: «We'll find him,» … «And we'll beat him.» (L8275).
- **Fights:** D5, D8 (wins).
- **Scaling:** Master-level combatant; nothing here changes Mara ≥ Lumiya.

### 5. Lumiya (Shira Brie / "Brisha Syo")
- **Role/arc:** the architect. Trained by Vader (L7687), cyborg (L7715), she claims she can never be a true Master (S11, L7735), and wins: «I've won» (L8217).
- **Key feats:** coordinates multiple phantoms at once on Coruscant and the asteroid (F27–F28); lightwhip superiority over Nelani (F29–F30); TK stalemate with Nelani while debating (F31).
- **Statements:** «If it had been an assault, I would have let the Luke phantom kill you.» (L7521). Her own lore claim that cyborgs can't reach «true Mastery» (S11, L7735) also downgrades Vader (S12); treat as self-serving.
- **Fights:** D11 (ahead of Nelani); never fights Jacen.
- **Scaling:** strong dark-sider with exceptional illusion range; below Luke and Mara in direct combat (other LotF books).

### 6. Han Solo
- **Role/arc:** Corellian loyalist drifting from the GA; flies with Wedge at Tralus; sees Jaina «didn't need him» (L8019).
- **Feats (non-Force):** shoots two raiders before Leia lands (L4171–L4173); hits a raider's armor to set up Jacen's thrown saber (L4191–L4193).
- **Statement:** «C'mon, kid» (L4187), to Jacen as they run to the Corellian suite.
- **Scaling:** non-Force; useful as setup man in D6.

### 7. Leia Organa Solo
- **Role/arc:** Jedi Knight and diplomat who, at Tralus, manipulates and lies to protect Han and Jaina, then rejects that path (L8183).
- **Feats:** Force-assisted leap to the ceiling with saber lit (F19); kills/disarms raiders (D6, L4181).
- **Quote:** «Both sides . . . are us.» (L8163).
- **Scaling:** Knight; below Luke/Mara/Jacen.

### 8. Jaina Solo
- **Role/arc:** Knight and pilot on Team Purella; dominant in the Tralus air war (Han's POV, L8021).
- **Feats:** destroys YVH droids in the failed kidnap (L2033–L2041); emerges in the Toryaz raid with saber lit (L4161). Han wonders whether she downed missiles «with a flip of her hand and a Force technique» (L8015).
- **Quote (about her):** «Part Han, part Leia, and all herself, she dominated the air around her.» (L8021).
- **Scaling:** top Knight / ace; no saber duel in this book.

### 9. Nelani Dinn
- **Role/arc:** Jedi Knight from Lorrd; the only witness to Jacen's turn; killed for it.
- **Feats:** TK bust push-of-war with Lumiya (F31); attacks Lumiya first (L7853).
- **Statements:** «Like any dark-sider, she mixes truth with lies until you can't separate them.» (L7793). «If you do this, you'll become something bad. Something destructive.» (L8109).
- **Fights:** D11 (losing to Lumiya), D12 (killed by Jacen).
- **Scaling:** low-mid Knight benchmark.

### 10. Thrackan Sal-Solo
- **Role/arc:** Corellian Chief of State; Centerpoint's restorer; Lumiya names him behind the Toryaz attack (L7893).
- **Feats (non-Force):** a sonic weapon that floors a Jedi (F5); voice-recording decoys that beat Jacen twice (L3239, L3295).
- **Quote:** Jacen: «You can never prepare against Jedi.» (L1967). Thrackan: «Yes, you can.» (L1969).
- **Scaling:** non-Force; shows anti-Jedi tech can neutralize even Jacen briefly.

### 11. Zekk, Kolir Hu'lya, Thann Mithric (Team Purella)
- **Feats:** Kolir «could telekinetically lift tons» (F4, L1761) and kills YVH droids; Zekk cuts into an inverted speeder (L1743).
- **Scaling:** Knight calibration below Jaina.

## 6. Verdict-relevant takeaways
1. **Luke > Jacen (40 ABY start)**: S3, D10, S8–S10. Confidence high.
2. **Jacen's Betrayal tier:** elite Knight, below Luke; this book cannot raise Caedus's peak (it can only fill in his early arc). **Caedus's final tier is unchanged** (top of tier 5, below Luke); LotF 1 was the last gap.
3. **Lumiya** is a skilled illusionist who beats a Knight on technique (D11), consistent with the existing Mara ≥ Lumiya entry.
