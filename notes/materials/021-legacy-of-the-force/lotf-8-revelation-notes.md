- **Feat vs. statement:** Section 2 is what the narration shows; Section 3 is what characters say or think (**STATEMENT**); *(POV)* marks narration filtered through a character. **Traviss's framing:** Caedus is shown as powerful but brittle, increasingly self-deceiving, and repeatedly beaten by *non-Force* means (mines destroyed undetected, Mandalorian beskar, a kneecapping). Luke is shown restraining himself, not lacking power. Treat Caedus-POV self-assessments as unreliable when they flatter him (see S6).

## Story and prose notes

### SP1. Plot summary, in order
See Section 5 and the raw log. Two converging investigations: Ben's (proof of Mara's murder) and Jaina's (how to beat Jacen). The "revelation" is plural: Jacen's guilt, Gotab's secret, Sintas's memory, and Jaina's acceptance.

### SP2. Character arcs
- **Caedus/Jacen:** dissociating. He forgets Tebut is dead (L1417), rebrands his sacrifice as Allana (S21), stops feeling Jaina and declares Jacen «Gone» (S22), sees phantom fleets, spews his confession to the one honest man he trusts. Power grows in breadth (Fight B) while judgment and control decay (yellow eyes L4197, L5859; holozine rage L7409–7421). He still values loyalty (rescues Tahiri personally, L6415, L7089).
- **Luke:** older, grieving, but capable and controlled. Beats Caedus in Fight A and refuses to kill civilians to finish him (L5359). Weeps with pride at Ben (L4271). Ends comforted by Mara's ghost (L8479).
- **Ben:** cop, not avenger: «he didn't mean killing him» (L851); «I'm not the jury or the judge» (L4263). Luke calls him «a moral compass» (L4351). Sees Mara's ghost first.
- **Jaina:** from «I'll bring him in» (L779) to «this is as far as it goes. I have to stop him now» (L8373). Learns to «Be someone else» (L2983), to commit without restraint, and to separate love from approval (L5559–5561).
- **Mara's aftermath:** her ghost (L1843, L8479), her hair (L3461, L4447), her threat (L4207), and Caedus's own confession (S11) re-establish her as the one who nearly won.
- **Boba Fett:** father/grandfather arc (Sintas, Mirta, Ailyn); principled refusal to kill Jacen himself (L7681); professional respect for Jaina (L7359).
- **Mandalorians:** Beviin (heart), Carid (violence), Mirta (redeemed hatred), Gotab/Venku (secret Force-users). Traviss's thesis: armor, culture and attitude beat Force power.
- **Tahiri:** blooded by Pellaeon's murder, named apprentice (L6413), nearly killed by Mirta.
- **Niathal / Pellaeon / Daala:** Niathal's guilt over the leaked minelayers (L4635–4655); Pellaeon dies on principle (L6281–6289); Daala returns as kingmaker.

### SP3. Themes
- **Family vs justice:** «Most murders happen within families» (L857); Gotab: «I'd kill him, out of love.» (L8351).
- **Attitude over power:** Fett: «It's not about their powers. It's about their attitude.» (L6577); S19.
- **Self-deception of power:** Fett: «they have to tell themselves lies to explain how they got there» (L7667).
- **The cost of restraint:** Luke's mercy lets Caedus escape and bomb Oridin; Gotab calls the Jedi's restraint moral cowardice (L8339).

- **Author intent:** Traviss consistently frames Jedi/Sith Force power as overrated against preparation and will (S19; L6577; L8339). She gives Luke the clear win in Fight A but makes his restraint the reason Caedus survives.

## 6. Verdict-relevant takeaways
1. **Caedus vs Luke:** *Revelation* restores a clear Luke edge after Allston's *Fury*. Luke out-illusions, out-hides and out-muscles Caedus in TK (feats 9–11, S5), and says he could have killed him «several times over» (S4). Caveat: it is a fighter duel, not a saber duel, and Luke had a wingmate. **Luke > Caedus, confidence back to medium-high.**
2. **Caedus vs Mara:** Caedus's recorded confession (S11) and private thought (S10) confirm she had him injured and the poison was a last resort. Fett: she «nearly succeeded» (S12). Keeps *Sacrifice* Duel C as "Mara nearly won".
3. **Jaina vs Caedus:** equal power, less training (S1, S2), but her Force-hiding beats his senses (feat 4), she breaks a drained Caedus's choke (feat 19), she is forewarned about his illusion trick (S20), and she adopts Mandalorian commitment (feats 3, 6, 18). Caedus's weaknesses are mapped: arrogance (S6), sensory overload (S23), reliance on illusion (feat 20).
4. **Caedus's peak range:** breadth is now enormous (planetary-shield mind-influence, fleet battle meditation), but he is repeatedly beaten by Luke, by collective Jedi concealment, and by non-Force prep, and is drained by his big feats. **High Sith Lord / Master tier, below Luke.**
5. **Tahiri** is a dangerous Knight but well below Jaina (Fight D).
