# Legacy of the Force 2: Bloodlines (novel), feats and scaling notes

**read up to: COMPLETE (Prologue to Ch23, L1–L8684)**

- **Book:** *Star Wars: Legacy of the Force: Bloodlines* by Karen Traviss (2006). Book 2 of 9 in Legacy of the Force (LotF).
- **Continuity:** Legends. The time is 40 ABY: «ten standard years after the Yuuzhan Vong war» (L63), and the main story opens «SIXTEEN DAYS AFTER THE RAID ON CENTERPOINT STATION» (L143), which was the climax of *Betrayal*.
- **Source file:** `[local path]` (8,684 lines). A line-identical working copy is at `[local path]`.
- **Method:** I read every line of the file in order, in chunks of about 700–1,200 lines. `L####` is the line number in that file. Quotes inside «…» are copied exactly from the file, including its OCR typos (for example "be fell" at L1863, "he back" at L8049, "Fat" for Fett at L8359). Where a quote leaves out words, it is split into separate «…» fragments or marked with "…". A script checked every quote against the line(s) cited for it (see the end of the file).
- **Structure:** Prologue (Fett, first person), then Chapters 1–23. Each chapter opens with an in-universe epigraph (news reports, memoirs, messages). The POVs alternate between Jacen, Luke, Ben, Han/Leia, and Boba Fett. Karen Traviss gives Fett and the Mandalorians about a third of the book.
- **Feat vs. statement:** Section 2 lists things the narration shows happening (**FEAT**). Section 3 lists what characters say or think about power or skill (**STATEMENT**, not a feat). POV narration that reports what a character believes is marked *(POV)*.
- **Headline:** this book has **no lightsaber duel between major Force users**. No Jacen vs. Luke, no Mara vs. Lumiya, and Alema Rar does not appear. Its scaling value comes from Jacen's stealth, illusion, and mind feats against Luke, from narration that directly compares Jacen with Luke and with Anakin/Vader, and from the Jacen–Lumiya power relationship.

## 3. Statements and hype (STATEMENT: not feats)

- **S1 · L295 · Narration on Ben**: Ben's Force senses are «a fraction of Jacen's». *Reliable* (narration).

- **S7 · L3035 · Luke (POV)**: «Jacen was the only other Jedi Luke knew who could defeat Lomi Plo's illusion of invisibility.» *Reliable.* This connects to the *Dark Nest* events.
- **S8 · L5127 · Kyp**: «It's time he became a Master. We all know what he can do.» *The Council's consensus on Jacen's power.*
- **S9 · L5179 · Luke (POV)**: «Jacen would still be a massively powerful Force-user, and nothing could take that from him.»
- **S10 · L6243 · Jacen (POV) on Lumiya**: «She could change nothing permanently. He could, though.» *Reliable as a description of their power styles.* Feat #28 supports it.
- **S11 · L6265 · Lumiya on Anakin**: «He also lacked your breadth of education in the Force.» *Self-serving.* She is grooming Jacen, but the book supports the claim that Jacen is unusually broadly trained (Fallanassi, Theran, Aing-Tii, Vergere).

- **S17 · L7853 · Jacen (POV) on Fett**: «He has a Mandalorian army that could take on the Yuuzhan Vong. Avoid him for as long as you can.» *Jacen's caution reflects Fett's resources, not personal combat.*
- **S18 · L8013 · Fett on Vader**: «Vader had been the master of causing pain and death without so much as a touch of his finger.» Fett connects this to Jacen's Force-killing of Ailyn.
- **S19 · L8369 · Han**: «If you're thinking of going after Jacen, he's too tough a quarry, even for you.» *A father's opinion.*
- **S20 · L8543 · Lumiya**: «I can't teach you any more skills.» Also L6233: «Technique is for apprentices. You know all you need to know.» *By the end of Bloodlines, the student has outgrown Lumiya's teaching. Jacen only needs the "final trial."*

## 4. Duels and fights

There are **no saber duels between Force users**. The fights:
- **A. Jacen vs. Ailyn Vel (Ch11 capture, L4771–4777; Ch15 death, L5969–6023).** She is a non-Force-sensitive bounty hunter. He wins easily: deflection, disarm, and pin. Her later death in custody is a torture-by-Force kill. The point is character, not power.
- **B. Jacen/Jaina/Zekk vs. 3 Corellian fighters (Ch14).** The Jedi win. Jacen chooses to kill even though he could have disabled.
- **C. Fett + Mirta vs. Han (Ch16, L6441–6527).** Fett wins easily, 2v1 at the end. Fett is old and dying.
- **D. Thrackan's assassination (Ch17).** Han, Fett, and Mirta against Thrackan. It is an execution.
- **E. Ben at H-90 (Ch18).** Ben, 13, against armed rioters: two kills with the saber and a shock-wave entry.
- **F. Contests of will rather than fights:** Jacen's illusions vs. Luke (Ch5, Ch8, partly successful). Jacen vs. Jaina's mind (Ch17, Jaina pushes him out). Jacen's dispel vs. Lumiya's illusion (Ch16). Jacen's masking vs. Lumiya's reading (Ch23, Jacen wins).

## 5. Scaling connections

### A. Luke vs. Jacen/Caedus, status at the end of Bloodlines
- Narration ranks Luke above Jacen, with Jacen closing in: «Jacen was more powerful than any of them except Luke. And he was growing closer to Luke's strength by the day.» (L6653).
- Jacen's stealth wins over Luke all come with conditions: concealment stacked with Lumiya's, Luke distracted, and Jacen using «all his strength» (L2379). Luke still half-senses Lumiya (L2389) and later identifies her (L3045).
- **Reading:** Luke > Jacen in overall power. Jacen ≥ Luke at hiding and deception. This sets up Exile and Sacrifice. See `[local path]` and `luke-legends-quotes.txt` for the later endpoint (Invincible).

### B. Jacen vs. Lumiya
- Jacen's raw, physical Force exceeds Lumiya's. She is an illusion and stealth specialist (S10, feat #28). She tells him she has nothing left to teach (S20). He hides a secret from her at full strain (feat #38). **Jacen > Lumiya** in power by the end of Book 2, while she keeps the edge in Sith lore and manipulation.

### C. Jacen vs. Mara (pre-Sacrifice)
- Mara: Jacen's «developing powers way beyond mine» (L837). Mara is «far more attuned to Jacen than Luke was» (L1051), which foreshadows her being the one to find him out. No fight happens in this book.

### D. Caedus vs. Vader / Anakin
- The book draws the parallel constantly: Order 66 flow-walk (L1813–1869), Anakin's Mastery debate (L6609–6655), Lekauf's grandfather (L4031), Omas's memoir (L3539), Beviin's «he fancies himself as the new Vader» (L7987), and Fett's «pale imitation» (L8679).
- **Power claims:** S11, S13, and S14 say Jacen is better trained than Anakin was. Those claims come from Lumiya and from Jacen himself. The authorial verdict (S21) says Jacen is a *lesser* Vader. Treat Bloodlines as framing that favors Vader on stature, with no head-to-head data.

### E. Sidious and Krayt
- Nothing direct. Lumiya's dismissal of Palpatine (S15) is ideology. For existing comparisons, see `debates-sidious-plagueis-tenebrous-vs-krayt-caedus-valkorion.md`.

### F. Common opponents and other links
- Lomi Plo (S7): Luke and Jacen both beat her invisibility. That links to Dark Nest feats.
- Ben's level: deflects bolts at 13 and can clear a room with a shock wave. He is well below Jacen (S1).
- Jaina: pushes Jacen out of her mind (feat #31) and challenges Luke in the air (feat #19). This foreshadows her role in Invincible.

### Quick ladder (this novel only)
Luke > Jacen (closing fast; best in the book at stealth and mind work) > Lumiya (illusion specialist) ≈ Mara (no feats; statement places Jacen above her) > Jaina > Leia > Ben. Fett is the top non-Force fighter, though dying.

## 6. Verdict-relevant takeaways
1. At 40 ABY (Bloodlines), **Luke is still above Jacen** in reliable narration (L6653). Jacen's wins over Luke are stealth-only and costly.
2. **Jacen > Lumiya** in raw power by the end of Book 2 (L6243, L6305, L8543).
3. Jacen's toolkit is the broadest of the era: Fallanassi illusion, Theran remote hearing, flow-walking, presence-erasure, memory wipes, Force interrogation, and channeling telekinesis through another person.
4. The author frames Jacen as a «pale imitation» of Vader (L8679). Use this against "Caedus > Vader" claims that rest on breadth of training.
5. No Mara, Alema, Kyp, Corran, or Tenel Ka combat data in this book.

## Story and prose notes

### SP1. Plot summary, in order
- **Prologue–Ch3 (L61–1626):** Fett is told he is dying and has a «standard year or two» (L359). Thrackan puts a contract on the Solo family, and Fett's estranged daughter Ailyn takes it. A Corellian bombing hits Coruscant (the Elite Hotel). Jacen shields Niathal. Gejjen recruits Han against Thrackan.
- **Ch4–Ch8 (L1627–3536):** Jacen flow-walks to Order 66 and summons Lumiya, who arrives disguised as «Shira». Jacen hides her from Luke twice. Jacen channels his telekinesis through Leia to save the Falcon. Luke works out that the hooded man is Lumiya. Jacen becomes a colonel heading the Galactic Alliance Guard (GAG), and Pellaeon resigns.
- **Ch9–Ch13 (L3537–5366):** The GAG raids Corellian neighborhoods. Fett hunts Taun We and Ko Sai's anti-aging data and learns of a gray-armored "Skirata" clone. Ben betrays Barit Saiy's family. Jacen captures Ailyn. The blockade of Corellia begins.
- **Ch14–Ch18 (L5367–7478):** The Mandalorians and Kad'ika come into the story. Jacen fires the «opening shot of the real war». Jacen kills Ailyn by Force interrogation. Fett takes contracts from both Thrackan and Gejjen. Lumiya teaches the "breaking of limits." Jacen sees Anakin denied the rank of Master. Jaina refuses Jacen's order and is suspended. Thrackan is assassinated. Ben makes his first kills.
- **Ch19–Ch23 (L7479–8684):** Mirta is revealed as Fett's granddaughter. Fett learns Jacen killed Ailyn and chooses to let the Jedi tear themselves apart. Han disowns Jacen. The GA declares war on Corellia. Lumiya gives Jacen the final trial: «You have to kill what you love.» (L8535). Jacen concludes, wrongly at this point, that it means Tenel Ka and Allana.

### SP2. Character profiles
- **Jacen:** a utilitarian who believes he is choosing the Sith path for the galaxy's sake. «I did it for the galaxy, not for my own selfish love of a woman.» (L8529). He gets colder in stages: the shield (Ch2), the kill (Ch14), the torture death (Ch15), the torpedoes (Ch17), and the heart-break acceptance (Ch23). Early on he still doubts himself in his deleted journal: «Am I making the same mistake as Grandfather?» (L565).
- **Lumiya:** a patient, intellectual Sith. «I don't need to create chaos, Jacen». She says people create it themselves (L2365). She calls the Sith way «acceptance» (L7055). Vader saved her after Luke shot her down (L7035).
- **Luke:** ages and hesitates. He is constrained by the politics of his position and is reluctant to force Ben. «I'm not a great student of history … But I think we've been here before somehow.» (L7887).
- **Mara:** defends Jacen out of loyalty and because of her own past as the Emperor's Hand (L833).
- **Ben:** loses his innocence. «Ben knew at that moment that he would never be a child again.» (L5365). «I killed someone … And I feel really bad about it.» (L8217).
- **Fett:** a Traviss focus. He is dying, Mand'alor without any interest in the role, and finds family. «Jedi always take everything from me.» (L8419).
- **Han and Leia:** «I don't know who you are, but you aren't my son anymore.» (L8129).

## Character analyses (added 2026-10-17 under the 7+ characters rule)

*Method:* every entry below was built from the verified feat/statement log above (Sections 2–5) and checked again against the source file. `L####` = line in `838ba53a…txt` (chapter given alongside). Quotes in «…» are verbatim and were re-checked by script (`[local path]`) after writing. Nothing here comes from a wiki unless marked **[wiki summary]**. Before this pass the file had 7 short story profiles (SP2) but **no** per-character analyses with feats, fights and scaling; this section adds **11**.

### 1. Jacen Solo
- **Role/arc:** GAG colonel and Lumiya's apprentice. He goes from shielding a taxi (Ch2) to his first deliberate kill (Ch14), the Force-interrogation death of Ailyn Vel (Ch15), torpedoing a freighter (Ch17), and accepting that his Sith trial means killing someone he loves (Ch23).
- **Key feats:** Force shield around Niathal's taxi in a bomb blast (Ch2, L989). Summons Lumiya from the Bimmiel system «as a commanding fist» (Ch4, L1879–1883). Hides Lumiya from Luke twice by stacking illusion on her cloak, at «all his strength» (Ch5, L2379; Ch8, L3495). Channels telekinesis through Leia to keep the *Falcon*'s drive housing together (Ch6, L2599–2605). Paralyzes three unseen targets at once (Ch9, L3713). Shields a crowd of about half a million (Ch15, L5797–5799). Holds invisibility, presence-erasure and flow-walking at once (Ch17, L6595–6631). Dispels Lumiya's illusion with a clap (Ch16, L6305).
- **Statements about him:** Mara: «No, I feel that Jacen's developing powers way beyond mine» (Ch2, L837). Narration: «Jacen was more powerful than any of them except Luke. And he was growing closer to Luke's strength by the day.» (Ch17, L6653). Luke: «You've developed some impressive skills lately.» (Ch6, L3033). Fett's closing line calls him «a pale imitation of his grandfather, Lord Vader» (Ch23, L8679).
- **Notable quotes:** «I did it for the galaxy, not for my own selfish love of a woman.» (Ch23, L8529). «Am I making the same mistake as Grandfather?» (Ch2, L565).
- **Fights/results:** beats Ailyn Vel easily (Ch11, L4771–4775). With Jaina and Zekk, beats three Corellian fighters and kills one he could have spared (Ch14, L5659). Jaina pushes him out of her mind (Ch17, L6817–6819).
- **Scaling:** Luke > Jacen in overall power, but Jacen is the best in the book at stealth, illusion and mind work, with the widest toolkit of the era. Jacen > Lumiya in raw power by the end of the book.

### 2. Lumiya
- **Role/arc:** a Sith teacher who comes to Coruscant when Jacen summons her and admits by the end that she has nothing left to teach him.
- **Key feats:** cloaks herself inside the Jedi Temple and no Jedi reacts (Ch5, L2311, L2345). Fills a room with an underwater illusion (Ch16, L6243). Long-range emotional projection is implied by Jacen (Ch4, L1777).
- **Statements:** «I can hide indefinitely-anywhere.» (Ch5, L2299). Jacen's POV: «She could change nothing permanently. He could, though.» (Ch16, L6243). Her own: «I can't teach you any more skills.» (Ch23, L8543); «Technique is for apprentices.» (Ch16, L6233).
- **Notable quotes:** «I don't need to create chaos, Jacen» (Ch5, L2365). On Anakin: «He also lacked your breadth of education in the Force.» (Ch16, L6265).
- **Fights/results:** none. Jacen breaks her illusion (Ch16, L6305, a lesson rather than a contest), and she fails to read his hidden secret (Ch23, L8541).
- **Scaling:** an illusion and stealth specialist, below Jacen in power. Luke still identifies her (Ch7, L3045), so Luke > Lumiya at sensing. This lines up with Luke vs. Lumiya in Exile.

### 3. Luke Skywalker
- **Role/arc:** a Grand Master held back by politics and by his reluctance to push Ben. He senses a darkness and works out that the hooded figure is Lumiya.
- **Key feats:** half-senses Lumiya through two layers of concealment (Ch5, L2385–2389) and then identifies her: «I know who the hooded man is. I know now, and it's not a man at all.» (Ch7, L3045). Briefly feels Ben's masked pain (Ch19, L7571–7581).
- **Statements:** narration still ranks him first (L6653, above). He admits to Jaina's flying, «Or maybe I'm slowing down.» (Ch11, L4563). Luke on Jacen: «I know Jacen is turning to the dark side, and that it's beyond anything that you or I did when we ventured there.» (Ch19, L7533).
- **Notable quote:** «I'm not a great student of history … But I think we've been here before somehow.» (L7887).
- **Fights/results:** a training dogfight in which Jaina is «a serious challenge» (Ch11, L4561). Jaina's Force push lands while he is distracted (Ch11, L4621). No real duel.
- **Scaling:** the top of this book on narration alone. Losing to Jacen's illusions came with conditions (distracted, double concealment), so it does not show Jacen > Luke.

### 4. Mara Jade Skywalker
- **Role/arc:** a Jedi Master who defends Jacen to Luke out of loyalty, though she is the one most «attuned» to him (L1051), which sets up Sacrifice.
- **Key feats:** none in combat. She senses Jacen «just blinked out» (Ch2, L1049).
- **Statements:** her own, Jacen's «developing powers way beyond mine» (L837). Narration: Mara is «far more attuned to Jacen than Luke was» (L1051).
- **Fights/results:** none.
- **Scaling:** her own statement puts Jacen above her. That is an early data point for Caedus > Mara (Sacrifice), but it is a statement, not a feat.

### 5. Ben Skywalker
- **Role/arc:** 13 years old (L145), a GAG recruit under Jacen. He makes his first kills and loses his innocence.
- **Key feats:** deflects a shot while half-blinded and being hauled off the ground (Ch5, L2535). The officer says «You're fast, kid.» (L2541). Force-wrenches doors apart (Ch9, L3759). At H-90 he takes a Force-assisted 10 m drop and «sent a surge of energy ahead of him in a shock wave to flatten anyone inside» (Ch18, L7351, L7389), then kills two men.
- **Statements:** narration says his senses are «a fraction of Jacen's» (L295).
- **Notable quotes:** «Ben knew at that moment that he would never be a child again.» (L5365). «I killed someone … And I feel really bad about it.» (L8217).
- **Fights/results:** beats armed rioters (Ch18).
- **Scaling:** a gifted teenager, far below Jacen. This is his baseline for his growth through Revelation and Invincible.

### 6. Jaina Solo
- **Role/arc:** a 31-year-old fighter pilot (L2867) who refuses Jacen's order to fire, an early sign of her role as the Sword.
- **Key feats:** in training, Luke «could have taken a serious shot and he still might not have hit her» (Ch11, L4577). She lands a Force push on Luke (Ch11, L4621). She expels Jacen from her mind: «Get out of my head» (Ch17, L6817–6819).
- **Notable quote:** «Colonel Solo, I'm refusing that order.» (Ch17, L6909). To Jacen: «You fired the opening shot of the real war.» (Ch14, L5671).
- **Fights/results:** with Jacen and Zekk, beats three Corellian fighters (Ch14).
- **Scaling:** an elite pilot, close to Luke in a fighter. Her mental resistance to Jacen foreshadows the Invincible duel.

### 7. Leia Organa Solo
- **Role/arc:** hiding on Corellia with Han. She chooses family over the GA.
- **Key feats:** kills an ambusher with three saber strokes (Ch5, L2051). Holds the *Falcon*'s cracking drive housing together with the Force, «a massive task» (Ch6, L2593), with Jacen channeling through her. Disarms Mirta mid-shot and pins her (Ch19–20, L7683–7693).
- **Statements:** Han: «If Jacen hadn't projected the Force through Leia, you'd have needed a shovel to pick us up at the spaceport.» (L2985).
- **Notable quote** (with Han, to Jacen): «I don't know who you are, but you aren't my son anymore.» (L8129).
- **Scaling:** a competent Knight-level user with fast reactions against non-Force threats. She sits below Jaina and Jacen.

### 8. Han Solo
- **Role/arc:** about 60 (L2059). He plots and carries out Thrackan's death and breaks with Jacen.
- **Fights/results:** Fett and Mirta beat him 2v1 (Ch16): «Fett brought Han down with a forearm smash under the chin and sent him sprawling.» (L6441). Mirta: «Don't take on two bounty hunters at once.» (L6527). He tackles and head-butts Thrackan (Ch17, L6971–7007).
- **Statements:** to Fett about Jacen: «If you're thinking of going after Jacen, he's too tough a quarry, even for you.» (L8369).
- **Scaling:** a non-Force veteran and an elite pilot. In melee he is below Fett even with Fett ill.

### 9. Boba Fett
- **Role/arc:** a reluctant, dying Mand'alor (71, L503) who finds a granddaughter.
- **Key feats:** cuts open a Z-95 canopy with his gauntlet laser (Prologue, L111). Roof infiltration with terahertz radar that «could see through walls» (Ch10–11, L3909). Hyperspace micro-jump through the blockade at «half the speed of light» (Ch14, L5699–5703). Collapses an escape tunnel with a jetpack missile (Ch18, L7165).
- **Statements:** Jacen's POV: «He has a Mandalorian army that could take on the Yuuzhan Vong. Avoid him for as long as you can.» (L7853). Fett on Vader: «Vader had been the master of causing pain and death without so much as a touch of his finger.» (L8013).
- **Notable quotes:** «It hurts deep in my stomach. I don't let anyone see that.» (L113). «Jedi always take everything from me.» (L8419). «You should learn to shoot first, Solo» (L7001).
- **Fights/results:** beats Han (Ch16). Takes part in Thrackan's execution (Ch17).
- **Scaling:** the top non-Force fighter in the book, though dying. His threat to Jacen is his army, not personal combat.

### 10. Mirta Gev
- **Role/arc:** a bounty hunter who turns out to be Fett's granddaughter. She shoots at him, then joins him.
- **Feats:** finishes Thrackan, «rolled across the desk to fire three bolts into Sal-Solo's forehead» (Ch17, L6997). Leia disarms her when she shoots at Fett (L7683–7685).
- **Scaling:** a skilled non-Force fighter, below Fett and Leia.

### 11. Ailyn Vel ("Habuur")
- **Role/arc:** Fett's estranged daughter, hired against the Solos. Jacen captures her and she dies under his interrogation.
- **Fights/results:** loses to Jacen at once: «Jacen slammed her flat against the wall with the Force and pinned her there.» (Ch11, L4775). Death: «Suddenly Habuur's head slammed down on the table without warning … Jacen hadn't lifted a finger.» (Ch15, L5969). Jacen: «She died of an aneurysm.» (L8093).
- **Scaling:** a character beat, not a power marker. It shows that Jacen can kill a non-sensitive through mind-probing, the parallel to Vader that Fett draws (L8013).
