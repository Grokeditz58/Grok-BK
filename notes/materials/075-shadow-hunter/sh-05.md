## *Darth Maul: Saboteur* (l.2020–2457; © 2001 e-book; this edition's text names no author, so none is asserted here)
- Setting: Dorvalla (Videnda sector), whose lommite ore is used for transparisteel. Lommite Limited (Patch Bruit, Jurnel Arrant) is at war with InterGalactic Ore (Falleen security chief Caba'Zan). Hath Monchar negotiates for the TF.
- Sidious's plan: "A push here, a shove there, and both mining companies will collapse", giving the TF Dorvalla and Gunray a permanent Directorate seat. Sidious: "Your skill as a sword master is peerless." "The power of the dark side is limitless, but only to those who accept uncertainty."
- **Sidious feat:** he Force-pulls Maul's saber off his belt "before Maul could prevent it—even if he had chosen to do so", stops it in the air, spins it and ignites both "meter-long" blades.
  > Maul: "The single blade has limitations... It made sense to me to be able to strike with both ends." / Sidious: "A sword master knows that when he flourishes his blade, he reveals his intent." (Sab.)
- **Maul feats and lore:**
  - Snatches a fist-sized insect mid-flight. He is "the only Iridonian in the place".
  - "Discipline was what enabled him to defy gravity and slow the inrush of sensory input, so that he could move between the moments."
  - Extended hearing; Force-probes a fake flitter-bug listening device.
  - **Leaps straight over a 5 m electrified fence**, moving so fast that holorecordings "wouldn't show him unless they were played in slow motion".
  - **Telekinetically rolls a drunk Bruit over and lifts his hand** to unlock a fingerprint-coded computer.
  - Force-walks the bug across the agents' table, then plays spliced fake recordings to pit both companies against each other.
- The Toom clan, pirates/ex-salvagers with an **Interdictor ship** that mimics a mass shadow, wreck both fleets at Eriadu. **Lt. Governor Tarkin** cameo: "blue eyes held neither humor nor empathy."
- **Riome raid:** Maul Force-twirls himself deep into a snowdrift and dodges fire without his saber.
- **Maul vs 7 survivors:** returns a bolt with his lower blade; "leapt and jinked, spun and rolled, an acrobatic wonder, impossible to target"; "turned blasters against each other"; Force-slams a man hard enough to snap his spine; one kick breaks Caba'Zan's arm and then, "without lowering his leg", the security chief's neck. He Force-chokes Bruit so as not to leave saber wounds: "It is something that had to be done—for a larger purpose."
- Ending: the companies merge into Dorvalla Mining with TF exclusivity. Sidious brushes off Monchar as "inconsequential" (seeding SH) and gives Maul his next task, studying Black Sun. "You need fear me only if you fail me."

## *Restraint* (James Luceno, "An all-new Darth Maul short story", © 2012; l.2458–2806)
- **Age and bio:** Maul is **15**, with "almost a decade in combat training" at Trezza's academy on **Orsis**. Sidious delivered him there **eight years earlier**, carrying out **Darth Plagueis's order** to move him from **Mustafar** (raised by Sidious and droids; dark-arts trips to **Tosste**). He does not know who Sidious is. Instructor **Meltch Krakko** is a human Mandalorian trained in Death Watch techniques.
- **Feats (while hiding his Force use):**
  - Force sight sees through mimetic camouflage; he soaks repeated low-power bolts and climbs 400 m.
  - **Leaps an "impossible" chasm**, then Force-launches out of it to land behind three hunters and shoot all three.
  - Trezza: "The vault from the chasm was a brilliant move. But you demeaned it by giving in to your emotions."
  - Refuses bacta: "There is no pain." Slams his fist through a table.
  - Seventh major kill in seven days on his Gora solo. Trezza: "No one I've trained ever fared as well on a solo."
- **Sidious feats:** chokes Maul "with a negligent gesture": "your little ploy at the chasm would have elicited little more than laughter from a Jedi Master." He has been burning Maul in saber practice for a year. Later the hangar is full of dead Weequays "discolored" with no wounds, and warlord Kirske overheats with fogging vision.
- **Dathomir lore:** **Mother Talzin** comes for Maul. **Kycina is Maul's birth mother**, and he shares a clan with **Savage Opress and Feral** ("You already took Savage and Feral from me"). Talzin sold infant **Asajj Ventress** to slavers. Kycina gave Maul up to "An elegantly dressed human... Influential—and powerful in his own right." Nightsisters are "Tainted descendants of an ostracized Jedi".
  - Talzin **holds Maul mid-air with the Force** and knocks him out by touch. Promise: "Your strength will be enhanced tenfold, and those puny horns... will become long and lethal."
- **Station fight (OOS):**
  - Meltch sells Talzin and Maul to Rattatak warlord Osika Kirske.
  - Talzin projects an illusion of dozens of ancient Nightsister warriors ("Our magicks don't work in this sterile place"), then turns floodwater into a soporific steam.
  - **Maul feat:** leaps up, drives a dark-side-charged Nightsister blade into a transparisteel window, rides it down to tear the pane, then slices the tractor-beam program so cargo containers crush the mercs. He then fights **unarmed**, "breaking bones".
- **Ending:**
  > Sidious: "This one does not belong to Dathomir... He is mine." (Res.)
  - Talzin keeps Maul's blood on a talisman: "With this, I will always know where to find you."
  - Sidious names himself and says the Grand Plan has been "in the making for a millennium".
  - He orders Maul to kill Trezza and everyone at the academy, "Instructor and trainee alike, to the last of them"; the saber is forbidden, but "nothing about exercising restraint".
