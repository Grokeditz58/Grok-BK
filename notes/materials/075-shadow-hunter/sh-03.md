# Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter (Michael Reaves, Del Rey 2001): notes part 3, Part II "Labyrinth", Ch. 16–25

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. This part covers FULL.txt l.931–1394. Citation "SH ch. N".

## Ch. 16: Neimoidians and the Council (l.931–978)
- Gunray learns a Neimoidian was killed and Mahwi Lihnn's body has been identified.
- Yoda, "well over 800 standard years": "Yoda's skill with a lightsaber was still second to none on the council." Qui-Gon: "Anoon has always been one to chafe at rules." Qui-Gon suggests Obi-Wan: "If Black Sun is involved, he will be able to sense it." Yoda: "No accident this was."

## Ch. 17: Underground (l.979–1033)
- The Jedi Code plus Bondara's teaching: "one can feel anger... without being controlled by it"; "Chance favors the prepared mind."
- They wake in an automated freight conduit where repulsor transports rush past at over 100 km/h. I-Five: "I wouldn't count him out until I saw his dead body... And even then I'd want him frozen in carbonite just to make sure." Darsha: "Anoon Bondara died saving your life."

## Ch. 18: Cthon ambush (l.1034–1056)
- Lorn's hatred of the Jedi: "Everything that had made him human had been taken from him five years ago."
- I-Five has headlight photoreceptors and radar. Darsha senses "we're not alone" before the radar does. Her saber cuts an electroshock net, but its field knocks her out.

## Ch. 19: Maul survives the fall (l.1057–1109)
- Maul's maxims: "Discipline is all. It conquers pain. It conquers fear. Most important of all, it conquers failure." / "There is no pain where strength lies." / "What is done in secret has great power." / "Anger is a living thing. Feed it and it will grow."
- **Fall feat (about 30 m):** midair acrobatics plus "the discipline of the dark side, which let him manipulate gravity itself, slowing his descent". He is half-stunned by the blast.
- Bio: Sidious "molded him from a weak, puling child". Teräs käsi forms: Charging Wampa, Rancor Rising, Dancing Dragonsnake. Gymnastics from zero g to 2× Coruscant gravity. As a child he stood outside the Temple all day with Sidious, cloaked "as long as they did not enter the building".
- **Kill count:** Bondara "had not been the first Jedi he had crossed lightsabers with, but he was not far from having that honor." He longs to fight Plo Koon or Mace Windu.
- **Reflex feat:** dodges a freight transport doing over 100 km/h: "seized the Force, let it whip him up and to the side as if he were attached to a giant elastic band. The metal behemoth missed him by millimeters."
- Cthons: eyeless, albino, cannibal tunnel-dwellers. Lorn was once "a prosperous business affairs clerk in the employ of the Jedi". "I'm sorry, Jax."

## Ch. 20: Obi-Wan investigates (l.1110–1154)
- Obi-Wan has mock-dueled Darsha twice (1–1). Uses Force "psychic radar" and an easy will-seizure on a Raptor ("the skill of a novice"); senses "powerful evil". "I've got a bad feeling about this."

## Ch. 21: Darsha breaks free (l.1155–1199)
- **Feats:** kneeling two-handed Force waves repeatedly batter Cthons off their feet; she **telekinetically flips I-Five's master switch** several meters away while being held; Force-pulls her saber about 5 m; figure-eight defense.

## Ch. 22: Maul in the dark (l.1200–1252)
- Maul plans to kill Pavan first, then "toy with" Darsha, "naught but an apprentice".
- **Feat:** he shoulder-rolls under an electroshock net (knowing a cut would conduct into him), then **kills nine Cthons** in pitch darkness.
- Cavern about 700 m wide with stalactites 40–50 m up. I-Five: "Not unless you've suddenly gained greater levitation powers than our Sith friend." Darsha: "Creatures who make very little ripples in the psychic flow are sometimes as good as invisible."

## Ch. 23: Backstories on the bridge (l.1253–1301)
- **I-Five's origin:** a rich family's protocol droid whose children made him jump off a roof. Lorn bought him from a junk shop days after being fired from the Temple; a friend installed a top-end AI cognitive module ("as close to full sentience as any droid can be").
- **Lorn = Jax's father:** five years ago a 2-year-old with a high midi-chlorian count came to the Temple; his father, a Temple employee, was fired after agreeing.
- Bondara: "As a Padawan, I could push boulders around with ease, but seeds were next to impossible."
- Darsha's scan of Maul: "A cold pillar of darkness... like electricity from a thundercloud."

## Ch. 24: The taozin (l.1302–1349)
- Maul on Bondara: "a worthy adversary. Not as good as Maul, of course". He feels Darsha's "clumsy probe... weak and hesitant".
- **Taozin lore (unique to the book):** a giant invertebrate as long as a hoverbus, with transparent segmented chitin, 2 cm skin nodules, no skeleton and leg-length mandibles; it spins the bridge silk. It is **invisible to the Force**: "no psychic reverberation at all". Lorn: "supposedly extinct... could, after a fashion, hide from the Force."
- **Anti-feat for sabers:** Darsha's blade "diffused as she sank it into the creature, losing its coherency and radiating in all directions".

## Ch. 25: Escape across the chasm (l.1350–1394)
- Darsha's flow state; Bondara: "A calm in the storm, a pivot to the lever." She shaves nodules off the taozin (set-up for ch. 34).
- Maul knows taozin from Sidious-mandated study: "They were supposed to be extinct—but then, so were the Sith."
- The trio swings across on a cut cable while Darsha Force-slows them.
- **Maul feat:** runs a single cable, catches it one-handed when the taozin shakes it, cuts it with "Slashing Wampa" and drops the taozin into the abyss. Limit: "Even with the Force to aid him he could not leap across a chasm this wide."
