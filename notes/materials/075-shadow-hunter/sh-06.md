# Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter (Michael Reaves, Del Rey 2001): notes part 6, author pages, Legends intro, bundled excerpts, novels timeline

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. This part covers FULL.txt l.2807–4262. Excerpts are cited by line and are not part of SH.

## Author pages and Legends intro (l.2807–2846)
- About the Author: Michael Reaves won an Emmy for *Batman: The Animated Series*. His other SW books include *Coruscant Nights*, *Death Star* and *MedStar* (with Steve Perry). "By Michael Reaves" list at l.2811–2838; the Legends intro is dated May 2014.

## *Darth Bane: Path of Destruction* ch. 1–2 (Karpyshyn, l.2847–2967)
- Dessel mines cortosis on Apatros. Cortosis is "Impervious to both heat and energy... supposedly could withstand even the blade of a lightsaber." His father called him "Bane".
- **Precog feat (untrained):** "like a vision, a brief glimpse into the future" in a brawl with Gerd; he senses at sabacc that "he couldn't lose".

## Old Republic intro and *Deceived* (Kemp, l.2968–3240)
- Smuggler Zeerid Korr's Ord Mantell run turns into an ambush; he kills six with his ship's cannons.
- **Darth Malgus:** "The Force is conflict. The Empire is conflict. The two are congruent." He advances on the Temple: "You have been deceived... Your time is past." Aryn Leneer is at the Alderaan talks.

## Rise of the Empire intro and *Dark Lord* opening (Luceno, l.3241–3365)
- Murkhana, last hours of the Clone Wars: Roan Shryne, Bol Chatak (Iridonian Zabrak Master), Olee Starstone. Shryne makes a Force-guided drop of about 10 m; a "droid buster" flattens droids in a 50 m radius.

## Rebellion intro and *Shadows of Mindor* ch. 1 (Stover, l.3366–3478)
- Rogue Squadron in a bait ship vs TIE Defenders ("capital-ship-grade" shields). Flechette torpedoes; the Taspan gravity-well test site; Shadowspawn's marauders.

## New Republic intro and *X-Wing: Rogue Squadron* ch. 1 (Stackpole, l.3479–3616)
- "You're good, Corran, but you're no Luke Skywalker." In the Redemption sim, a TIE bomber ramming an X-wing's aft shield is like hitting a "ferrocrete wall at sixty kilometers per hour" and crumples. Corran scores 9 kills.

## NJO intro and *Vector Prime* ch. 1 (Salvatore, l.3617–3834)
- Jaina (16) flies with Force precog "three moves ahead". Jedi Wurth Skidder. **Nom Anor crushes a stone to dust in his fist**; Mara: he "had nothing to do with the Force".

## Legacy intro, *Betrayal* ch. 1 (Allston, l.3836–3967) and *Outcast* opening (Allston, l.3969–4061)
- *Betrayal:* Luke dreams of a figure with eyes turning molten gold. Jacen reads a mind ("Your thoughts betray you") and returns bolts on Adumar; Ben (13) cuts a 1.5 m hole in a wall.
- *Outcast:* Jagged Fel is Imperial Head of State and wants a Jedi presence in the Empire. Valin Horn sees his parents as impostors: he scorches hotcakes with his saber ("Flesh does the same thing"), tells "Corran" that "they can't have given you his expertise with the lightsaber", and lunges.

## Star Wars Legends novels timeline (l.4063–4262)
- Runs from Dawn of the Jedi (25,793 BBY) through the Old Republic (Revan 3954, *Deceived* 3650, Bane trilogy 1032) to *Plagueis* (67 BBY).
- **33 BBY: *Darth Maul: Saboteur** (eBook), *Cloak of Deception*, *Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter*, *Maul: Lockdown*.** 32 BBY: TPM, *Rogue Planet*, *Outbound Flight*, *Approaching Storm*.
- Continues through the Clone Wars (incl. Reaves/Perry *MedStar*), 19 BBY (ROTS, *Dark Lord*, *Coruscant Nights*), the Rebellion, the New Republic, NJO and Legacy, ending with FotJ (*Outcast* to *Apocalypse*), *X-Wing: Mercy Kill* and *Crucible* at 45 ABY. Footnote: "*An eBook novella".
- Continuity note: *Restraint* is not listed anywhere in the timeline, even though it is bundled in this edition.
