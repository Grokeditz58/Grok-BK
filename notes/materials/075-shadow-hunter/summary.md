# Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter: summary of strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. Full text l.1–4262. Citation "SH ch. N"; bundled stories "Sab." (*Saboteur*) and "Res." (*Restraint*).

1. **Maul's mobility peak.**
   - He runs "easily five times faster than a human could travel at a dead run" (SH ch. 13).
   - He leaps to grab a skycar "a good ten meters off the ground" (SH ch. 14).
   - He lands a 30 m fall by using the dark side to "manipulate gravity itself, slowing his descent" (SH ch. 19).
   - He Force-whips aside from a freight transport doing over 100 km/h, missing it "by millimeters" (SH ch. 19).
   - Limit: "Even with the Force to aid him he could not leap across a chasm this wide" (about 200–700 m; SH ch. 22/25).
2. **Maul vs Anoon Bondara, a Master whose "skill with a lightsaber was second to none" (SH ch. 3).** Maul "knew that he himself was the superior fighter. He could tell that the Jedi knew it, too", yet "was still unable to slash through his guard". Bondara dies by blowing the bike's power cell, not by Maul's blade (SH ch. 15).
3. **Maul vs Darsha Assant (Padawan).** "No Jedi Master lived within the Force more fully than she did at this moment." Her counter nearly guts him; she Force-pushes two allies "dozens of meters" mid-fight, then sacrifices herself (SH ch. 30–31). Maul's kill count: Bondara was "not the first Jedi he had crossed lightsabers with, but he was not far from having that honor" (SH ch. 19).
4. **Blaster-defense feats and limits vs Mahwi Lihnn.** He might "dodge a single blast, maybe even two" without his saber against "a dozen bolts... in half a second", but once armed he deflects "the next blaster bolt and half a dozen more" and outruns a wrist rocket "at unnatural speed" (SH ch. 10). His admitted error was underestimating her because she was not Force-strong (SH ch. 11).
5. **Other combat showings.**
   - 4 Duelist Elite droids (reflexes "a hair faster than human optimum") in about 60 seconds, the last barehanded, +5 bpm (SH ch. 1).
   - Nine Cthons killed in pitch darkness (SH ch. 22).
   - **Saber thrown through an armored officer and Force-recalled mid-jump** (SH ch. 28).
   - Seven fighters at once, turning "blasters against each other" (Sab.).
6. **Anti-feats.**
   - Lorn Pavan, shielded by a taozin nodule, stuns Maul with two bolts: "For possibly the first time in his life, Darth Maul had been taken completely by surprise" (SH ch. 36).
   - Repulsor beams knock him out of a leap (SH ch. 13).
   - Taozin are invisible to the Force and diffuse saber blades (SH ch. 24).
7. **Sidious.**
   - Force-pulls Maul's saber "before Maul could prevent it—even if he had chosen to do so" and ignites it mid-air (Sab.).
   - Chokes the teenage Maul "with a negligent gesture" and kills a hangar of Weequays without leaving a wound (Res.).
8. **Yoda feat (flashback).** Unarmed against Plo Koon, Saesee Tiin and Depa Billaba, he moved barely a meter and none could touch him: "Better than training, the Force is" (SH ch. 30).
9. **Lore unique to this edition.**
   - Exar Kun invented the double-bladed saber (SH ch. 1).
   - Maul's saber uses **four synthetic crystals** forged in a Sith-holocron furnace (SH ch. 28).
   - From *Restraint*: Maul is 15 at Orsis, moved from Mustafar on Plagueis's order. His birth mother is Kycina and his clanmates are Savage and Feral. Talzin keeps a blood talisman to find him.
