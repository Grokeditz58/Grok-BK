# Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter (Michael Reaves, Del Rey 2001): notes part 2, Ch. 9–15 (end of Part I)

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. This part covers FULL.txt l.509–930. Citation "SH ch. N".

## Ch. 9: The Dewback Inn (l.509–588)
- **Maul's stealth:** he uses the Force to deflect idle glances, "effectively invisible" in "dens of mental weakness", but cannot wipe a public killing from dozens of minds: "here and there burned an intellect too strong to be swayed by simple mind-control techniques... like photonic lamps on a darkling plain." "The Sith were powerful, but there were only two of them. Stealth was therefore one of their greatest strengths."
- Monchar (with Trandoshan Gorth) wants half a million for a data crystal proving "the Sith are behind it".
- I-Five's plan: phantom bank transfer of 500k before auditor droids catch it; he "had somehow evolved a greed circuit". Lorn's lost son **Jax** first mentioned.

## Ch. 10: Massacre at Monchar's cubicle (l.589–689)
- Maul senses Lihnn tailing him but dismisses her: "He felt no real concentrations of the Force emanating from the hidden watcher" (his key error).
- **Feats:**
  - Senses four beings through a wall ("Walls could not stop the dark questing fingers of the Force"); **Force blast shatters the door** "as though hurling an invisible ball".
  - Deflects a bolt into a Quarren "two centimeters lower than he had aimed. Poor control on his part"; returns the next into the Trandoshan's face; leaps and decapitates the other Quarren. "His dueling droids had given him a better fight than these three had. Pitiful."
  - **Force choke** interrogation of Monchar, Force-lift "like a puppet on strings", decapitation; cuts open a durasteel safe.
- **Lihnn gets the drop on Maul.** Her mind is "too sharp... to fall for mind tricks". He reckons he "might be able to dodge a single blast, maybe even two" against someone who can put "a dozen bolts into the air from two semiautomatic blasters in half a second". He Force-hurls a blaster at her face, recalls his saber, deflects "the next blaster bolt and half a dozen more", then against a wrist rocket "slipped along the lines of the Force, moving at unnatural speed", cutting through a wall and the floor to drop out of the blast.
  > Lihnn: "He knocked blaster bolts away as if swatting flies!" His "expertise... far exceeded the skill of any Jedi Lihnn had ever heard of." (SH ch. 10)
- Lorn grabs the crystal from the half-open safe.

## Ch. 11: Admitted error; Sidious's rebuke (l.690–759)
- Maul: "He had underestimated the bounty hunter because the woman had not been strong in the Force. Such a mistake had almost cost him his life." Droid minds are harder to cloud than organic ones.
  > Sidious: "You disappoint me, Lord Maul... Destroy this Lorn Pavan and find the crystal... Do not fail me again." (SH ch. 11)
- Lorn reads cauterized wounds as a lightsaber; I-Five: the last Sith "died over a thousand years ago". Lorn: "it wasn't a question of being paranoid, it was a question of being paranoid enough."

## Ch. 12: Sidious on Maul; Yanth (l.760–800)
- Sidious: Maul's "skills as a warrior were nonpareil"; chief flaw "hubris"; his aura pulses "with the dark stain of impatience".
- Maul Force-cloaks past a checkpoint and probes investigators' minds. Hutt **Yanth** (Black Sun survivor, under 500 years old) buys the crystal for 1 million; it holds Naboo N-1 and TF ship schematics.

## Ch. 13: Tusken Oasis rampage (l.801–845)
- Maul: about 2 m tall, "ten short horns", yellow eyes. "None shall survive."
- I-Five throws the money case into Maul's swing to save Lorn; the blade sears Lorn's hair.
- **Feats:** one-hand spin-blocks two blasters while the free hand Force-disarms both Gamorreans; halves a thrown force pike; a flip covering "the length of the large chamber"; impales Yanth; Force-pulls the crystal; hurls guests "with savage Force-laden gestures", "using the Force like a battering ram".
- **Speed feat:** "moving easily five times faster than a human could travel at a dead run", with "gargantuan" strides; clears a burned landspeeder. He is hit mid-leap by skycar repulsor beams ("invisible hammer").

## Ch. 14: The skycar chase (l.846–901)
- Darsha and Bondara feel his "empathic burst"; "He wielded the Force with the surety of a Master." Darsha "could scarcely believe he had recovered... so fast" from a stun pass.
- **Jump feat:** Maul cuts the undercarriage drive and Force-leaps to grab the rear gunwale when the car is "a good ten meters off the ground"; **Darsha's two-handed push** breaks his grip. He lands lightly and remote-summons his bike.
- Maul: "Finally, a foe that might be worthy... It had been far too long."
- **Bondara Force-leaps from the speeding skycar onto Maul's speeder bike.** Lorn: "Jedi... Why did it have to be Jedi?"

## Ch. 15: Maul vs Bondara (l.902–930)
- Bondara lands on the engine housing; Maul fights twisted in the saddle on autopilot, then sets down on a 10×15 m ledge about 30 m up.
- **Duel:** Bondara is "obviously a master of the teräs käsi fighting arts", but "within the first few moments... Darth Maul knew that he himself was the superior fighter. He could tell that the Jedi knew it, too." "The Jedi gave ground, but Maul was still unable to slash through his guard."
- Bondara stabs the bike's power cell; the explosion vaporizes him "in a microsecond". Maul leaps off "enfolding himself" in the dark side.
- Darsha (planning a 10 m leap; she has jumped higher in training) is thrown by the blast; Lorn catches her; I-Five lands the car and drags them to cover before the platform collapses.
