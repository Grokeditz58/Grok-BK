## Ch. 1: The Saak'ak and the dueling droids (l.97–163)
- Tech: Neimoidian freighter *Saak'ak* in Wild Space has a horseshoe hull, a **cloaking device**, hidden durasteel armor, blaster turrets and military-grade comms.
- Sidious by holo to Gunray, Haako, Dofine: "There are only three of you." Gunray lies that Monchar is ill. Narration: Sidious "might not be able to read another's inmost thoughts, but he certainly could tell when someone was lying." A Force ripple from his gesture makes the transmission grid glow.
- **Feat: Maul vs 4 Trang Robotics Duelist Elite droids** (rapier, cudgel, chain, twin hachete blades). They carry the programmed skills of a dozen martial-arts masters, reflexes "a hair faster than human optimum", durasteel blaster-resistant chassis, inhibitors removed.
  - He senses the two behind him "as plainly as if he had eyes in the back of his head"; "Time seemed to slow."
  - Skewers two with a back/forward thrust; teräs käsi stance "Riding Bantha"; "Rancor Rising" bisects Chain crotch to head.
  - Switches off and kicks away his saber to finish Hachete barehanded (flip, both heels crush its head).
  - About 60 seconds, pulse up 5 bpm at most: "Not his personal best, by any means... Jedi were a different matter. He would have to do better."
- Lore: the double-bladed saber was "first designed by the legendary Dark Lord Exar Kun millennia ago"; only a master fighter can use one.
- Maul has just gutted Black Sun: Sidious says "The organization will be in disarray for years." Order on Monchar: "If he has—kill him, and everyone he has spoken to." Narration: "failure was not an option to Darth Maul. It was not even a concept."

## Ch. 2: Lorn Pavan and I-Five (l.164–224)
- Coruscant lore: oceans drained into subterranean caverns over 1,000 generations ago; thousands of CO2 reactive dampers prevent heat runaway; hawk-bats with up to 1.5 m wingspan.
- Lorn and droid **I-FiveYQ** try to buy a "chronon-dated" 5,000-year-old holocron from Toydarian fence Zippa (with Gamorrean Bilk). Zippa: "A Jedi Holocron can be activated only by someone who can use the Force... Are you perhaps a closet Jedi?"
- **I-Five tech:** hair-thin laser from his index finger; ultrasonic vocabulator scream disables Bilk; I-Five catches the blaster and kills Bilk. Zippa still cons Lorn out of the cube and his cash (15k lost).

## Ch. 3: Darsha's trial; Maul lands (l.225–262)
- Council: Adi Gallia, Plo Koon, Eeth Koth, Yoda, Mace Windu. Final Padawan trial: go alone to the Crimson Corridor (Zi-Kree sector) and bring a former Black Sun member back alive.
- **Anoon Bondara** (Twi'lek): "one of the best fighters in the order. His skill with a lightsaber was second to none." Darsha (orphan from Alderaan, found by Bondara) also trained under Qui-Gon.
- **Darsha's stated Force kit:** leap twice her own height in 1 g; slow a fall; move objects "a dozen meters and more away"; cloak herself, "hiding in plain sight".
- **Ship: Maul's Infiltrator** (new gift from Sidious): stygium-crystal cloaking device plus thrust-trace dampers that confound Coruscant's warning grids; extended cloaking drains power. Carries a speeder bike.
- Bio: Maul is from Iridonia; "his life began with Lord Sidious".

## Ch. 4: Hunting the hunters (l.263–309)
- Maul outruns a police droid and dives through the fog layer about 30 m up. Blinding a crowd to his presence "would take considerable concentration... though it could be done."
- He uses Sidious's password at a Customs terminal to slice the planetwide holocam grid.
- Haako proposes bounty hunter **Mahwi Lihnn**. Lorn drinks at the Green Glowstone: "I was somebody, once... Damn Jedi—they did this to me." Lorn has disconnected I-Five's creativity damper.

## Ch. 5: Sith philosophy; Darsha in the Corridor (l.310–367)
- Sidious: "The Jedi saw the Force as an end in itself; the Sith knew that it was a means to an end. And that end was Power." Rule of one master, one apprentice after the Great Sith War.
- Darsha finds Fondorian informant Oolth. **Mass mind trick on about 12 Raptors** works ("We're not interested in her, or her vehicle") until Oolth's scream breaks it.

## Ch. 6: Monchar, Lihnn, the bartender (l.368–416)
- Monchar is selling the blockade data plus the fact that "the man behind it all was a Sith Lord".
- **Lihnn's kit:** shell-spider-silk garb that resists vibroblades and reflects low-power bolts; twin DL-44s; ankle disruptor; 2 MM9 wrist rockets; palm flechette shooter; stun cuffs, baton, 3 glop grenades. Coruscant population about 1 trillion.
- Maul uses Force truth-sensing on a Baragwin bartender ("sensed no negative vibration in the Force"). Training: punished for flinching; "pain as his teacher". He would "wipe out this entire city sector" without a qualm.

## Ch. 7: Darsha's mission fails (l.417–461)
- **Darsha feats:** Force push sends Raptors back in "reverberating ripples"; senses a Gotal leaping over the car and cuts off his wrist; cuts a hole in transparisteel and rolls through.
- Tech: ascension gun (liquid-cable reservoir, 200 m max, monofilament).
- A hawk-bat flock takes Oolth's eye; he falls 7–8 levels to his death.

## Ch. 8: Shadowing (l.462–508)
- I-Five's trill cures hangovers. Monchar sets a meeting at the Dewback Inn.
- Maul tails Lorn using shadows and "the cloaking power of the Force"; "He had been bred and trained to fight and kill Jedi." He sees the Temple as "a ruin of smoking rubble", a "future reality".
