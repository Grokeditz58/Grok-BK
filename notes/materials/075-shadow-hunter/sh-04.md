# Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter (Michael Reaves, Del Rey 2001): notes part 4, Ch. 26–37

- Read 2026-11-03 CST. This part covers FULL.txt l.1395–2008. Citation "SH ch. N".

## Ch. 26: Obi-Wan at the Tusken Oasis (l.1395–1456)
- **Obi-Wan feat:** senses a blaster under the bar; drops flat as the bartender fires; cuts through the bar and the gun without touching the bartender; rises "almost levitating"; Force-pulls one Rodian's blaster, deflects the other's shot (blue blade), pulls the second gun. Nobody is hurt.
- He cannot read the scene ("A Master such as Mace Windu could probably make sense of it"). Witnesses "couldn't get a good look at him, even when he ran right by them." Bluff: "In terms of fighting skills, I'm probably least among the Jedi."

## Ch. 27: Raptors again (l.1457–1522)
- Darsha knows "what her attackers were going to do before they did it", deflects bolts and Force-passes a blaster to Lorn. I-Five's finger laser heats a vibroblade to incandescence.
- Darsha: "I was chosen... Chosen by the Force." Jax was taken and Lorn fired "because they thought it best that his son have no attachments."

## Ch. 28: Maul's saber and the police (l.1523–1565)
- **Lore: building Maul's saber.** A furnace from plans in Sidious's Sith holocron; synthetic crystals purified by the Force; **four crystals** for the double blade; days "without food or water or sleep". Jedi use natural Adegan crystals; mismatched crystals can destroy saber and maker.
- Force-jumps "several body lengths" out of a vent. Sidious: "Be very careful not to reveal our presence, Lord Maul—it is not yet time."
- Maul: "He had little doubt that he could defeat any living Jedi, but there were many of them here... Even he was not foolish enough to try to take them on all at once."
- **Feat sequence:** a fake dispatch lures two police bikes and a PCBU (droid cruiser with two swivel laser cannons). Maul Force-leaps onto it, shears a gun off its mount and cleaves the cockpit. He **throws his saber (one blade lit) like a spear through an officer's armored chest** while jumping to the other bike, **recalls it in flight** ("feathery runner of the Force") and kills the second officer. (Ch. 32 puts the PCBU 15 m up.)

## Ch. 29: Up through the levels (l.1566–1641)
- Darsha's mind trick ("You will show us the way uplevels, with no more tricks"), then she **telekinetically jams a security cam's aperture**.
- An old power station holds a small carbon-freezing chamber plus welding gas and fuel. Maul arrives.

## Ch. 30: Maul vs Darsha (l.1642–1723)
- **Maul suppresses his own Force presence** ("naked and blind") to approach unseen.
- **Yoda flashback:** "Better than training, the Force is. More than experience or speed it gives." **Unarmed Yoda vs Plo Koon, Saesee Tiin and Depa Billaba:** he moved barely a meter and none could touch him.
- Darsha **Force-pushes Lorn and I-Five "dozens of meters"** into the waste chamber and scrambles the lock.
- **Duel:** her counter to "Striking Sarlacc" nearly guts him and he backflips.
  > Maul: "For a Padawan, she was a worthy opponent. No Jedi Master lived within the Force more fully than she did at this moment." (SH ch. 30)
  - He Force-throws a wrench and a bucket of fasteners plus the teräs käsi "Death Weave"; she deflects most and leaps 5 m to a catwalk. His blades spin "so fast they seemed to merge into a crimson shield."
  - Darsha: "The Sith was the best fighter Darsha had ever seen... control of the Force that of a musician."
- I-Five: "Even my reactions are no match for the Sith's—and I am far faster than you and Padawan Assant." He freezes himself and Lorn in carbonite.

## Ch. 31: Darsha's sacrifice (l.1724–1757)
- She herds fuel cells and gas cylinders with Force waves, deliberately lets him run her through, then Force-throws her lit saber into a cylinder. Last thought: "There is no death; there is the Force."
- Maul Force-leaps out a high window ahead of the blast and clears the smoke with the Force. "A far more worthy opponent than he'd realized... this was to be honored." His scan finds no life, because the carbonite hides them.

## Ch. 32: Aftermath (l.1758–1804)
- PCBU standard procedure: hover 10 m back and 15 m up. Obi-Wan Force-pulls Darsha's melted hilt.
  > Maul: "The Jedi Padawan died in combat. She fought well, for a neophyte." (SH ch. 32)

## Ch. 33: Thawed (l.1805–1871)
- I-Five: carbon freezing "lowered all biological and electronic processes to a level far too faint for even a master of the Force to detect."
- Sakiyan Tuden Sal gives them a lift. The Infiltrator's cloak (I-Five: "high-grade cloaking device. Probably crystal-based"). Lorn "would never have called any Jedi a hero—until he met Darsha Assant."

## Ch. 34: The taozin nodule (l.1872–1906)
- **Infiltrator:** "nearly thirty meters long", palm-plate start, ion drive; must drop the invisibility field in orbital traffic.
- I-Five gives Lorn a **taozin nodule** ("specially adapted cells that block receptivity to the Force... It won't protect you from his telekinetic powers"). Lorn switches I-Five off and sends him to the Temple.

## Ch. 35–36: Ambush on the station (l.1907–1970)
- Tuden Sal betrays them to keep I-Five. Lorn's blaster has 1 full shot or 3 stuns.
- **Anti-feat for Maul:** two stun bolts in the back slam him into a bulkhead. "For possibly the first time in his life, Darth Maul had been taken completely by surprise." He recovers almost at once, Force-blasts Lorn and cuts off his right hand. Lorn seals a hatch, which Maul melts through.
- Lorn hands the crystal to **Senator Palpatine**: "Don't worry, my brave friend. I'll take care of it. Everything will be all right now."

## Ch. 37: Ending (l.1971–2008)
- Obi-Wan and Qui-Gon are assigned to Naboo; the Council blames Black Sun.
- Lorn is promised a prosthetic and a stipend, then Maul opens the door. Pavan was "more of a challenge to Maul than many of the professional killers of Black Sun... had earned the right to die quickly." "his mission at last complete."
