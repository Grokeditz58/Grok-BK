Character quick bios:
- **Darth Maul:** Iridonian Zabrak, about 2 m tall. Raised by Sidious; teräs käsi master; wields a double saber with four synthetic crystals.
- **Darsha Assant:** human Padawan from Alderaan, on her final trial. Yellow blade.
- **Anoon Bondara:** Twi'lek Master in his mid-to-late 40s and a teräs käsi expert.
- **Lorn Pavan:** Corellian info broker, formerly a Jedi Temple clerk, fired when his son Jax was taken for training.
- **I-Five (I-FiveYQ):** near-sentient protocol droid with finger lasers, an ultrasonic scream and radar.
- **Darth Sidious:** Senator Palpatine.
- **Supporting cast:** Hath Monchar (defecting Neimoidian), Mahwi Lihnn (bounty hunter), Obi-Wan (investigator).
