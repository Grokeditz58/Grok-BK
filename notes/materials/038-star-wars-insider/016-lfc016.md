# Owner Material 038 — Star Wars Insider #1–153 plus specials (with The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #1–22): The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #16 (1992)

- **Source:** *Star Wars Insider* magazine (issues 23–153, 1994–2014) and its predecessor *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine* (issues 1–22, 1987–1994), plus seven special editions: articles, interviews, comics and short fiction Secondary source (licensed Legends reference). Scan OCR'd on the box; the scan stays box-only and is **not** published.
- **Method:** every scanned page was OCR'd and machine-screened sentence by sentence for power-relevant statements (fights, kills, defeats, Force feats, rankings, superweapons) that name a character on our roster; each kept sentence is quoted verbatim from the OCR text and has a crop of the printed passage directly under it. Pages with nothing power-relevant are still listed with what is on them.
- **Citation:** "*The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #16* (1992), p.X" by printed page ("scan p.X" where no printed number applies). **Weight:** Licensed magazine: a secondary source. Its short fiction and comics are Legends stories; its articles and interviews sum up or discuss lore and rank below the novels, comics and games they describe.

### Stat block notes (game mechanics, not feats)

- Dark Empire #1 synopsis: "It's six years after Return of the Jedi and the battle over Endor. Yet the battle with the Empire is far from over. Civil war is rampant, especially within what remains of the Empire. The Alliance is using this opportunity to sow confusion among the feuding Imperials, using confiscated Star Destroyers and other Imperial starships to conduct hit and run raids on Imperial outposts. As the story opens in issue #1 of Star Wars: Dark Empire, one such raid has ended in near disaster; an Alliance Star Destroyer commanded by Luke Skywalker, a now fully accomplished Jedi Knight, and Lando Calrissian, has crashed on the Imperial throneworld's surface." (Joe Terwilliger, "Star Wars Returns to Comics", Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine 16, p. 16) (Source: *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #16* (1992), scan p.16)

### Stat block notes (game mechanics, not feats)

- Dark Empire: the cloned Emperor: "Because of the Clone Wars the next thing that occurred to us was that the Emperor we saw in Jedi was a clone. Perhaps he has even been cloning himself for a long time, over and over, because the Dark side eats up his physical body." ... "Suffice to say," Veitch goes on, "by issue #5 we will see a young Emperor emerge from the Clone labs and face off with Luke Skywalker." (Tom Veitch, quoted in "Star Wars Returns to Comics", Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine 16, p. 17) (Source: *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #16* (1992), scan p.17)
- Dark Empire: Salla Zend and Nar Shaddaa: "We bring in a female character from Han's past, Salla Zend, who is a Corellian pirate like Han." ... "we visit its spaceport moon, Nar Shaddaa, a kind of distant cousin of the Death Star, with cities covering its entire surface. ... According to the history we have described, Han actually hung out on the moon for a few years back when he was younger, after he got booted out of the academy. This moon is also where he met Lando." (Tom Veitch, quoted in "Star Wars Returns to Comics", Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine 16, p. 17) (Source: *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #16* (1992), scan p.17)
