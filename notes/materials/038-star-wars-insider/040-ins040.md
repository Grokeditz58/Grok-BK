# Owner Material 038 — Star Wars Insider #1–153 plus specials (with The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #1–22): Star Wars Insider #40 (1998)

- **Source:** *Star Wars Insider* magazine (issues 23–153, 1994–2014) and its predecessor *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine* (issues 1–22, 1987–1994), plus seven special editions: articles, interviews, comics and short fiction Secondary source (licensed Legends reference). Scan OCR'd on the box; the scan stays box-only and is **not** published.
- **Method:** every scanned page was OCR'd and machine-screened sentence by sentence for power-relevant statements (fights, kills, defeats, Force feats, rankings, superweapons) that name a character on our roster; each kept sentence is quoted verbatim from the OCR text and has a crop of the printed passage directly under it. Pages with nothing power-relevant are still listed with what is on them.
- **Citation:** "*Star Wars Insider #40* (1998), p.X" by printed page ("scan p.X" where no printed number applies). **Weight:** Licensed magazine: a secondary source. Its short fiction and comics are Legends stories; its articles and interviews sum up or discuss lore and rank below the novels, comics and games they describe.

### Stat block notes (game mechanics, not feats)

- Vader Force-chokes Motti with barely a finger: "First, Vader walks into the ship, and he picks a guy up by his throat, so you think, 'Oh, he's just a big brute.' But then you see him barely lift his finger, and I'm choking. You see that the Force is something else again." (Richard Le Parmentier, in Scott Chernoff, Star Wars Insider 40, p. 18) (Source: *Star Wars Insider #40* (1998), scan p.18)
- Yoda lifts the X-wing (article aside): "But while most of the saga's examples of the power of the Force are illustrated with special effects (such as Yoda lifting Luke's X-wing fighter out of the Dagobah swamp, or Luke making his lightsaber edge toward him in the wampa's cave on Hoth)" (Scott Chernoff, Star Wars Insider 40, p. 18) (Source: *Star Wars Insider #40* (1998), scan p.18)
