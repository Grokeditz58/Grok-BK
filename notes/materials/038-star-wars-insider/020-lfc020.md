# Owner Material 038 — Star Wars Insider #1–153 plus specials (with The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #1–22): The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #20 (1993)

- **Source:** *Star Wars Insider* magazine (issues 23–153, 1994–2014) and its predecessor *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine* (issues 1–22, 1987–1994), plus seven special editions: articles, interviews, comics and short fiction Secondary source (licensed Legends reference). Scan OCR'd on the box; the scan stays box-only and is **not** published.
- **Method:** every scanned page was OCR'd and machine-screened sentence by sentence for power-relevant statements (fights, kills, defeats, Force feats, rankings, superweapons) that name a character on our roster; each kept sentence is quoted verbatim from the OCR text and has a crop of the printed passage directly under it. Pages with nothing power-relevant are still listed with what is on them.
- **Citation:** "*The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #20* (1993), p.X" by printed page ("scan p.X" where no printed number applies). **Weight:** Licensed magazine: a secondary source. Its short fiction and comics are Legends stories; its articles and interviews sum up or discuss lore and rank below the novels, comics and games they describe.

## scan p.4 (scan page 4)

Page contents: Pamela E. Roller, "George Lucas' Super Live Adventure: An Action-Packed Spectacle Tours Japan": a Feld Productions (Kenneth Feld) arena show, late April-September 1993; director/supervising producer Scott Faris, production designer Douglas Schmidt. The stage was over a quarter acre; cast, crew and staff of 150; more than 400 costumes; score recorded by over 140 musicians including the London Symphony Orchestra; more than 1,000 laser beams; a 40-ft-wide Millennium Falcon replica weighing almost a ton. Segments ran from Willow through Star Wars. (hand-read)
