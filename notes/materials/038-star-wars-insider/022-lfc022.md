# Owner Material 038 — Star Wars Insider #1–153 plus specials (with The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #1–22): The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #22 (1994)

- **Source:** *Star Wars Insider* magazine (issues 23–153, 1994–2014) and its predecessor *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine* (issues 1–22, 1987–1994), plus seven special editions: articles, interviews, comics and short fiction Secondary source (licensed Legends reference). Scan OCR'd on the box; the scan stays box-only and is **not** published.
- **Method:** every scanned page was OCR'd and machine-screened sentence by sentence for power-relevant statements (fights, kills, defeats, Force feats, rankings, superweapons) that name a character on our roster; each kept sentence is quoted verbatim from the OCR text and has a crop of the printed passage directly under it. Pages with nothing power-relevant are still listed with what is on them.
- **Citation:** "*The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #22* (1994), p.X" by printed page ("scan p.X" where no printed number applies). **Weight:** Licensed magazine: a secondary source. Its short fiction and comics are Legends stories; its articles and interviews sum up or discuss lore and rank below the novels, comics and games they describe.

### Stat block notes (game mechanics, not feats)

- Cooper on Exar Kun reviving the Dark Lords: "On one of these planets, Exar Kun resurrects Dark Lord traditions. He travels to the Valley of the Dark Lords where he finds ancient Sith temples and artifacts, including a group of mummified Dark Lords. To make a very long story shorter, Exar Kun acquires the power of the Dark Lords (Tales of the Jedi's Ulic Qel-Droma plays a major role in helping him revive the Dark Lords)." (Bob Cooper, Dark Horse column, Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine 22, p. 4) (Source: *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #22* (1994), scan p.4)
- Cooper on Dark Lords of the Sith vs. Dark Jedi: "There can only be one Dark Lord of the Sith at a time, as compared with the possible existence of many Dark Jedis. Each Dark Lord passes the title to another Jedi, and acts, ostensibly, as the ruler of the Sith people—an ancient race given to arcane rites and a proclivity for dark-side powers. A Dark Jedi, on the other hand, is simply a Jedi who has succumbed to the dark side of the Force. Upcoming in Dark Empire II, for instance, the Emperor creates an entire special unit of Dark Jedi to fight for him." (Bob Cooper, Dark Horse column, Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine 22, p. 4) (Source: *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #22* (1994), scan p.4)
- Cooper's Dark Empire recap: "In Dark Empire, events unfold that are simply too important to dismiss: Han and Leia have a third child, the Emperor Palpatine comes back to life and Luke succumbs to the dark side." (Bob Cooper, Dark Horse column, Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine 22, p. 4) (Source: *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #22* (1994), scan p.4)

### Stat block notes (game mechanics, not feats)

- Lucas on Anakin's fall: "The first three episodes begin with the friendship of a young Ben Kenobi and Luke's father, Anakin Skywalker. When Ben meets Anakin, he sees that the talented pilot is very strong with the Force and decides to train Anakin in the ways of a Jedi. But the Emperor senses Anakin's growing power and, determined to harness it for the Empire, sways the young Jedi to the dark side. Anakin Skywalker ceases to exist, and in his place Darth Vader emerges." (George Lucas, Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine 22, p. 6) (Source: *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #22* (1994), scan p.6)
- Lucas on Vader's rank: "We watched as Luke learned about the Force from Obi-Wan Kenobi and Yoda, and felt his pain when he discovered that Darth Vader, the most highly regarded commander in the Imperial Navy, was his father." (George Lucas, Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine 22, p. 6) (Source: *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #22* (1994), scan p.6)

### Stat block notes (game mechanics, not feats)

- Holland on Admiral Zaarin: "One is Admiral Zaarin. He's very important in the Empire's R&D technology corps. He's in charge of testing out new technology and developing new starfighters during a war time. ... [Zaarin] is a brilliant technician and researcher, but is very ambitious and eventually seeks to overthrow the Emperor." ... "Zaarin thirsts for power and thinks he can do a better job of bringing order and harmony to the galaxy than the Emperor. The Emperor is using these mystical powers, whereas Zaarin has access to the technology. In the game, there's a climax where Zaarin attempts to dispose of the Emperor and Darth Vader." (Larry Holland interview, Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine 22, p. 32) (Source: *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #22* (1994), scan p.32)
- Holland on Admiral Harkov: "Another main character is Admiral Harkov. He's actually quite a despicable character who uses his command over several fleets for profit. ... Harkov is involved in a civil war, but instead of using his power to settle things, he's feeding arms and resources to both sides to keep the conflict going for his own profit." (Larry Holland interview, Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine 22, p. 32) (Source: *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #22* (1994), scan p.32)
