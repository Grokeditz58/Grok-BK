# Owner Material 038 — Star Wars Insider #1–153 plus specials (with The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #1–22): Star Wars Insider #24 (1995)

- **Source:** *Star Wars Insider* magazine (issues 23–153, 1994–2014) and its predecessor *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine* (issues 1–22, 1987–1994), plus seven special editions: articles, interviews, comics and short fiction Secondary source (licensed Legends reference). Scan OCR'd on the box; the scan stays box-only and is **not** published.
- **Method:** every scanned page was OCR'd and machine-screened sentence by sentence for power-relevant statements (fights, kills, defeats, Force feats, rankings, superweapons) that name a character on our roster; each kept sentence is quoted verbatim from the OCR text and has a crop of the printed passage directly under it. Pages with nothing power-relevant are still listed with what is on them.
- **Citation:** "*Star Wars Insider #24* (1995), p.X" by printed page ("scan p.X" where no printed number applies). **Weight:** Licensed magazine: a secondary source. Its short fiction and comics are Legends stories; its articles and interviews sum up or discuss lore and rank below the novels, comics and games they describe.

### Stat block notes (game mechanics, not feats)

- McQuarrie on the origin of Vader's armor: "He is supposed to have jumped from one spaceship to another, blasted through a hole in the wall and entered the other spaceship. I figured he couldn't have survived being in zero atmosphere, so George said give him some kind of breathing apparatus. The sort of padded, armored suit that he has on is what I conceived of as being an armored suit and a space suit with all of the life-support systems and communications systems in it." (Ralph McQuarrie, quoted by Pam Roller, Star Wars Insider 24, p. 47) (Source: *Star Wars Insider #24* (1995), scan p.51)
