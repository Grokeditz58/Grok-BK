# Owner Material 038 — Star Wars Insider #1–153 plus specials (with The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #1–22): Star Wars Insider #151 (2014)

- **Source:** *Star Wars Insider* magazine (issues 23–153, 1994–2014) and its predecessor *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine* (issues 1–22, 1987–1994), plus seven special editions: articles, interviews, comics and short fiction Secondary source (licensed Legends reference). Scan OCR'd on the box; the scan stays box-only and is **not** published.
- **Method:** every scanned page was OCR'd and machine-screened sentence by sentence for power-relevant statements (fights, kills, defeats, Force feats, rankings, superweapons) that name a character on our roster; each kept sentence is quoted verbatim from the OCR text and has a crop of the printed passage directly under it. Pages with nothing power-relevant are still listed with what is on them.
- **Citation:** "*Star Wars Insider #151* (2014), p.X" by printed page ("scan p.X" where no printed number applies). **Weight:** Licensed magazine: a secondary source. Its short fiction and comics are Legends stories; its articles and interviews sum up or discuss lore and rank below the novels, comics and games they describe.

### Stat block notes (game mechanics, not feats)

- Nomi Sunrider single-handed on the battlements; a Jedi's lightsaber through a door (Freedon Nadd Uprising, per artist Tony Akins): "The big battle, where the Jedi break the siege, is a high point for me," he says, as are "the trundling, heavily manned siege engines and Nomi Sunrider's single-handed fighting on the battlements against a squad of conventional armed pikemen." His favorite contribution to Star Wars was inventing a new use for a lightsaber: having it bore through a heavy metal door. "I like to think the prequel filmmakers first saw this in my scene that involved a Jedi pinning a group of bad, bad men against a wall using a heavy panel and then eviscerating them by drawing his lightsaber through and across the door," Akins says. (Authors of the Expanded Universe: Tales of the Jedi, Star Wars Insider 151, p. 36) (Source: *Star Wars Insider #151* (2014), scan p.36)

### Stat block notes (game mechanics, not feats)

- [canon-era story] Sixteen-year-old Anandra disarms and grapples a stormtrooper: As he reached for his belt, Anandra sprang for the blaster in his holster. She didn't try to retrieve the weapon. The trooper would have caught her, broken her wrists and pried the blaster back. She only needed to pull it out of its holster, maintain momentum and release. It went sailing, skidding across the catwalk and quivering at the metal's edge. For that moment, Anandra had the advantage. Then the stormtrooper kneed her in the chest. She fell back, barely tried to break her fall. [...] Two solid kicks to her midsection, and her whole body seemed to fold. When he hesitated, she was up again, jumping forward, wrapping her arms around his neck and jabbing her thumbs under the rim of his helmet. His fingers dug into her sides as she tried to crack the helmet's seal. She slammed her head against the black eyeplate, saw red, hoped she'd bought herself a few moments. (One Thousand Levels Down by Alexander Freed, Star Wars Insider 151, pp. 55-56) (Source: *Star Wars Insider #151* (2014), scan p.55)
