# Owner Material 038 — Star Wars Insider #1–153 plus specials (with The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #1–22)

- **Source:** *Star Wars Insider* magazine (issues 23–153, 1994–2014) and its predecessor *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine* (issues 1–22, 1987–1994), plus seven special editions: articles, interviews, comics and short fiction Secondary source (licensed Legends reference). Scan OCR'd on the box; the scan stays box-only and is **not** published.
- **Method:** every scanned page was OCR'd and machine-screened sentence by sentence for power-relevant statements (fights, kills, defeats, Force feats, rankings, superweapons) that name a character on our roster; each kept sentence is quoted verbatim from the OCR text and has a crop of the printed passage directly under it. Pages with nothing power-relevant are still listed with what is on them.
- **Citation:** "Star Wars Insider, p.X" by printed page ("scan p.X" where no printed number applies). **Weight:** Licensed magazine: a secondary source. Its short fiction and comics are Legends stories; its articles and interviews sum up or discuss lore and rank below the novels, comics and games they describe.

## Notes

- One section note per issue, in issue order (Fan Club Magazine 1–22, Insider 23–153, then specials). The per-issue checklist is in this folder's PROGRESS.md.
- Only the archive.org "Image Container PDF" of each issue was used (160 files). Those PDFs hold low-resolution page images (584 x 754 pixels, about 69 ppi), so OCR has more misread letters than in the other materials. Quotes keep the OCR text as read, with only sentences that are almost entirely clean kept; the crop under each quote is the printed page itself and is the authority where a letter differs.
- Citations give the issue and the printed page where the scan has page numbers, otherwise the scan page.
- Every page has a "Page contents" line listing its headings, so pages with no quote are still shown as read. Ads, letters and merchandise pages were read but rarely hold a power statement.

## How much weight

Licensed magazine: a secondary source. Its short fiction and comics are Legends stories; its articles and interviews sum up or discuss lore and rank below the novels, comics and games they describe. Quotes are what the book says, not feats shown on the page; where a novel or comic shows the event, that source outranks this one. RPG statistics, where quoted, are game mechanics and rank lowest.
