# Owner Material 038 — Star Wars Insider #1–153 plus specials (with The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #1–22): The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #17 (1992)

- **Source:** *Star Wars Insider* magazine (issues 23–153, 1994–2014) and its predecessor *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine* (issues 1–22, 1987–1994), plus seven special editions: articles, interviews, comics and short fiction Secondary source (licensed Legends reference). Scan OCR'd on the box; the scan stays box-only and is **not** published.
- **Method:** every scanned page was OCR'd and machine-screened sentence by sentence for power-relevant statements (fights, kills, defeats, Force feats, rankings, superweapons) that name a character on our roster; each kept sentence is quoted verbatim from the OCR text and has a crop of the printed passage directly under it. Pages with nothing power-relevant are still listed with what is on them.
- **Citation:** "*The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #17* (1992), p.X" by printed page ("scan p.X" where no printed number applies). **Weight:** Licensed magazine: a secondary source. Its short fiction and comics are Legends stories; its articles and interviews sum up or discuss lore and rank below the novels, comics and games they describe.

## scan p.6 (scan page 6)

Page contents: Lucas interview continued: no time to direct an episode; Spielberg may direct one someday; plans to show Indy at age five or six; the series as introspective and true to life rather than artifact-hunting; 32 shows committed, stories for at least 70, a master plot running January 1905 to October 1954. (hand-read)


## scan p.7 (scan page 7)

Page contents: Lucas interview continued: for Star Wars he developed a huge back story first; he will try to do "the next trilogy, which is the first trilogy", writing in the next couple of years, release in "seven or eight years"; denies he is done with Star Wars; Young Indy production techniques and ILM technology development will be used for the new films, planned to shoot back to back. (hand-read)


## scan p.8 (scan page 8)

Boxed "Star Wars Prequel Preview" (crop-verified) quotes the Return of the Jedi novelization by James Kahn; see T lines. (hand-read)


### Stat block notes (game mechanics, not feats)

- ROTJ novelization: Ben on Anakin's power and fall: "...Your father, Anakin, was seduced by the dark side of the Force — he ceased to be Anakin Skywalker, and became Darth Vader. When that happened, he betrayed everything that Anakin Skywalker believed in. The good man who was your father was destroyed. "...When I first encountered your father," Ben continued, "he was already a great pilot. But what amazed me was how strongly the Force was with him. I took it upon myself to train Anakin in the ways of the Jedi. My mistake was thinking I could be as good a teacher as Yoda. I was not. Such was my foolish pride. The Emperor sensed Anakin's power, lured him to the dark side." (Return of the Jedi novelization by James Kahn, quoted in "Star Wars Prequel Preview", Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine 17, p. 8) (Source: *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #17* (1992), scan p.8)
- ROTJ novelization: the duel and the molten pit: "...You should not think of that machine as your father...when I saw what had become of him, I tried to dissuade him, to draw him back from the dark side. We fought...your father fell into a molten pit. When your father clawed his way out of that fiery pool, the change had been burned into him forever — he was Darth Vader, without a trace of Anakin Skywalker. Irredeemably dark. Scarred. Kept alive only by machinery and his own black will..." (Return of the Jedi novelization by James Kahn, quoted in "Star Wars Prequel Preview", Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine 17, p. 8) (Source: *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #17* (1992), scan p.8)
