# Owner Material 038 — Star Wars Insider #1–153 plus specials (with The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #1–22): The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #15 (1992)

- **Source:** *Star Wars Insider* magazine (issues 23–153, 1994–2014) and its predecessor *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine* (issues 1–22, 1987–1994), plus seven special editions: articles, interviews, comics and short fiction Secondary source (licensed Legends reference). Scan OCR'd on the box; the scan stays box-only and is **not** published.
- **Method:** every scanned page was OCR'd and machine-screened sentence by sentence for power-relevant statements (fights, kills, defeats, Force feats, rankings, superweapons) that name a character on our roster; each kept sentence is quoted verbatim from the OCR text and has a crop of the printed passage directly under it. Pages with nothing power-relevant are still listed with what is on them.
- **Citation:** "*The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #15* (1992), p.X" by printed page ("scan p.X" where no printed number applies). **Weight:** Licensed magazine: a secondary source. Its short fiction and comics are Legends stories; its articles and interviews sum up or discuss lore and rank below the novels, comics and games they describe.

## scan p.4 (scan page 4)

Page contents: Dan Madsen, "The Young Indiana Jones Chronicles" cover feature (byline viewed): ABC series created by George Lucas, with Indy at 93 framing each episode; Corey Carrier (9) and Sean Patrick Flanery (16). Lucas on the series' origin in his educational foundation, the turn of the century as his favorite period of history, and the series as a production experiment. (hand-read)


## scan p.5 (scan page 5)
