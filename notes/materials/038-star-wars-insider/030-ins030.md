# Owner Material 038 — Star Wars Insider #1–153 plus specials (with The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #1–22): Star Wars Insider #30 (1996)

- **Source:** *Star Wars Insider* magazine (issues 23–153, 1994–2014) and its predecessor *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine* (issues 1–22, 1987–1994), plus seven special editions: articles, interviews, comics and short fiction Secondary source (licensed Legends reference). Scan OCR'd on the box; the scan stays box-only and is **not** published.
- **Method:** every scanned page was OCR'd and machine-screened sentence by sentence for power-relevant statements (fights, kills, defeats, Force feats, rankings, superweapons) that name a character on our roster; each kept sentence is quoted verbatim from the OCR text and has a crop of the printed passage directly under it. Pages with nothing power-relevant are still listed with what is on them.
- **Citation:** "*Star Wars Insider #30* (1996), p.X" by printed page ("scan p.X" where no printed number applies). **Weight:** Licensed magazine: a secondary source. Its short fiction and comics are Legends stories; its articles and interviews sum up or discuss lore and rank below the novels, comics and games they describe.

### Stat block notes (game mechanics, not feats)

- Swallow on Fett: "Forever hidden behind a battlesuit of Mandalorian armor lies the face of a stone killer, a bounty hunter of steely nerves and cold heart: Boba Fett. His name is enough to strike terror in the hearts of bail jumpers across the galaxy, for he is the most feared skip tracer in all the Empire." (James Swallow, "Head Hunter: An Interview with Jeremy Bulloch", Star Wars Insider 30, p. 48) (Source: *Star Wars Insider #30* (1996), scan p.53)
- Bulloch: slow but deadly, terribly quick: "The one thing that I always remember, in talking with the director Irvin Kershner and George Lucas, was that this particular character is very slow in his movements, but deadly—which means underneath it all he's terribly quick, he's aware of everything going on around him." (Jeremy Bulloch, quoted in James Swallow, "Head Hunter: An Interview with Jeremy Bulloch", Star Wars Insider 30, p. 48) (Source: *Star Wars Insider #30* (1996), scan p.53)
- Bulloch: methodical but fast: "The character was explained a great deal: what sort of person he was, deadly but very methodical in movement and fast, even though you never saw him move quickly." (Jeremy Bulloch, quoted in James Swallow, "Head Hunter: An Interview with Jeremy Bulloch", Star Wars Insider 30, p. 48) (Source: *Star Wars Insider #30* (1996), scan p.53)
