# Owner Material 038 — Star Wars Insider #1–153 plus specials (with The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #1–22): The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #21 (1994)

- **Source:** *Star Wars Insider* magazine (issues 23–153, 1994–2014) and its predecessor *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine* (issues 1–22, 1987–1994), plus seven special editions: articles, interviews, comics and short fiction Secondary source (licensed Legends reference). Scan OCR'd on the box; the scan stays box-only and is **not** published.
- **Method:** every scanned page was OCR'd and machine-screened sentence by sentence for power-relevant statements (fights, kills, defeats, Force feats, rankings, superweapons) that name a character on our roster; each kept sentence is quoted verbatim from the OCR text and has a crop of the printed passage directly under it. Pages with nothing power-relevant are still listed with what is on them.
- **Citation:** "*The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #21* (1994), p.X" by printed page ("scan p.X" where no printed number applies). **Weight:** Licensed magazine: a secondary source. Its short fiction and comics are Legends stories; its articles and interviews sum up or discuss lore and rank below the novels, comics and games they describe.

### Stat block notes (game mechanics, not feats)

- Tyers on Dev Sibwarra: Force-strong captive of the Ssi-ruuk: "Dev Sibwarra is a human who was taken prisoner by aliens at the age of 10. He is 15 at the time of the story, and he's been brainwashed by hypnosis drugs into serving these aliens, the Ssi-ruuk. They intend to take over the human universe, beginning with the Imperial planet, Bakura. Dev tries hard to serve the Ssi-ruuk, but they mistreat him. He is very much an abused person, but he is also strong in the Force and dreams of becoming a Jedi Knight." (Kathy Tyers interview, Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine 21, p. 13) (Source: *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #21* (1994), scan p.13)
- Tyers on Gaeriel Captison and Luke: "I also created Gaeriel Captison, a young Imperial Diplomat. Luke falls head-over-heels in love with her." ... "Gaeriel sparks the Force in Luke in a way that makes his whole being resonate when he is around her." (Kathy Tyers interview, Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine 21, p. 13) (Source: *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #21* (1994), scan p.13)
