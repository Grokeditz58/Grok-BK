# Owner Material 038 — Star Wars Insider #1–153 plus specials (with The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #1–22): Star Wars Insider #29 (1996)

- **Source:** *Star Wars Insider* magazine (issues 23–153, 1994–2014) and its predecessor *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine* (issues 1–22, 1987–1994), plus seven special editions: articles, interviews, comics and short fiction Secondary source (licensed Legends reference). Scan OCR'd on the box; the scan stays box-only and is **not** published.
- **Method:** every scanned page was OCR'd and machine-screened sentence by sentence for power-relevant statements (fights, kills, defeats, Force feats, rankings, superweapons) that name a character on our roster; each kept sentence is quoted verbatim from the OCR text and has a crop of the printed passage directly under it. Pages with nothing power-relevant are still listed with what is on them.
- **Citation:** "*Star Wars Insider #29* (1996), p.X" by printed page ("scan p.X" where no printed number applies). **Weight:** Licensed magazine: a secondary source. Its short fiction and comics are Legends stories; its articles and interviews sum up or discuss lore and rank below the novels, comics and games they describe.

### Stat block notes (game mechanics, not feats)

- Perry on Xizor: "He's not like Marlon Brando but more like a young Al Pacino. He isn't old, used up, and afraid. He is still on the way up, like Pacino in Godfather II or III. He's running the business, and running it hard. I wanted him to be powerful, relatively evil, but at the same time he has to have reasons to be doing what he's doing." (Steve Perry, quoted in Kevin Fitzpatrick, "Into the Shadows: A Profile of Author Steve Perry", Star Wars Insider 29, p. 53) (Source: *Star Wars Insider #29* (1996), scan p.48)
- Dash Rendar and Guri: "Perry also dreamed up two more key figures in Shadows: Dash Rendar, a smuggler pilot cut from the same cloth as Han Solo; and Guri, a human-like droid that is just as deadly as Boba Fett." (Kevin Fitzpatrick, "Into the Shadows: A Profile of Author Steve Perry", Star Wars Insider 29, p. 53) (Source: *Star Wars Insider #29* (1996), scan p.48)
- Perry on Dash: "Dash is basically very much like Solo's brother, has the same general look and attitude." (Steve Perry, quoted in Kevin Fitzpatrick, "Into the Shadows: A Profile of Author Steve Perry", Star Wars Insider 29, p. 53) (Source: *Star Wars Insider #29* (1996), scan p.48)
- Perry on Guri: "She is my drop-dead gorgeous blonde, the ice queen." (Steve Perry, quoted in Kevin Fitzpatrick, "Into the Shadows: A Profile of Author Steve Perry", Star Wars Insider 29, p. 53) (Source: *Star Wars Insider #29* (1996), scan p.48)
- Perry on Luke after Bespin: "He's a whole lot closer to being a Jedi Knight. How did that happen? From the end of Empire and falling out of Bespin, and the time he shows up on Tatooine. That needed telling." (Steve Perry, quoted in Kevin Fitzpatrick, "Into the Shadows: A Profile of Author Steve Perry", Star Wars Insider 29, p. 53) (Source: *Star Wars Insider #29* (1996), scan p.48)
- Luke slows his fall with the Force: "At one point in Empire, Luke drops 30 or 40 feet off an Imperial walker. Why didn't he break something? Well, he used the Force to slow himself down." (Steve Perry, quoted in Kevin Fitzpatrick, "Into the Shadows: A Profile of Author Steve Perry", Star Wars Insider 29, p. 53) (Source: *Star Wars Insider #29* (1996), scan p.48)
- Perry on Vader: "I always thought it would be interesting to make Darth Vader have some real desires, rather than just a mustache-twirling villain." (Steve Perry, quoted in Kevin Fitzpatrick, "Into the Shadows: A Profile of Author Steve Perry", Star Wars Insider 29, p. 53) (Source: *Star Wars Insider #29* (1996), scan p.48)
