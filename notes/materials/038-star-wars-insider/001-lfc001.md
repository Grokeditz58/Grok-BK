# Owner Material 038 — Star Wars Insider #1–153 plus specials (with The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #1–22): The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #1 (1987)

- **Source:** *Star Wars Insider* magazine (issues 23–153, 1994–2014) and its predecessor *The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine* (issues 1–22, 1987–1994), plus seven special editions: articles, interviews, comics and short fiction Secondary source (licensed Legends reference). Scan OCR'd on the box; the scan stays box-only and is **not** published.
- **Method:** every scanned page was OCR'd and machine-screened sentence by sentence for power-relevant statements (fights, kills, defeats, Force feats, rankings, superweapons) that name a character on our roster; each kept sentence is quoted verbatim from the OCR text and has a crop of the printed passage directly under it. Pages with nothing power-relevant are still listed with what is on them.
- **Citation:** "*The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine #1* (1987), p.X" by printed page ("scan p.X" where no printed number applies). **Weight:** Licensed magazine: a secondary source. Its short fiction and comics are Legends stories; its articles and interviews sum up or discuss lore and rank below the novels, comics and games they describe.


## scan p.1 (scan page 1)

Page contents: Cover (viewed): The Lucasfilm Fan Club, 1st Collectors Issue; C-3PO and R2-D2 photo; cover lines for the Anthony Daniels exclusive interview and an exclusive look at Willow. (hand-read)


## scan p.2 (scan page 2)

Page contents: Welcome letter from George Lucas on the club's return and Lucasfilm's second decade (Willow and Tucker in production); staff credits (Fall 1987, issue #1; president and publisher Dan Madsen, associate editor John S. Davis); "Welcome Back" editorial on the revived club, its magazine and plans. Real-world. (hand-read)


## scan p.3 (scan page 3)

Page contents: "Looking At Lucasfilm" division overview: Film & Television (Tucker, Willow, Indiana Jones III in pre-production; no dates announced for continuing the Star Wars saga), ILM, Sprocket Systems, Theater Operations (THX, TAP), Licensing (Tonka Willow toys, 10th-anniversary coins, John Alvin poster) and Games Division. Real-world. (hand-read)


## scan p.4 (scan page 4)

Page contents: Anthony Daniels exclusive interview by Dan Madsen and John S. Davis, part 1 (photos viewed): Japanese C-3PO commercials, his anti-smoking commercial, wanting to act from childhood, first reaction to the C-3PO role, and how the character "happened" on the first day in the suit. Real-world; a personal medical aside is omitted. (hand-read)


## scan p.5 (scan page 5)

Page contents: Daniels interview continued: little peripheral vision in the suit, Tunisia heat, the voice and nervous personality, "We're doomed!", the sense of remoteness working with R2, Lucas not liking his first performance, 3PO and R2 as a Laurel-and-Hardy odd couple, 3PO's vulnerability and loyalty. Real-world actor interview, no in-universe feats. (hand-read)


## scan p.6 (scan page 6)

Page contents: Daniels interview continued: the Sesame Street R2 fire-hydrant sketch, the radio drama (praise for Brian Daley's scripts and Mark Hamill), voicing Star Tours and the animatronic 3PO "looking at" him, not being recognized, and the 10th anniversary convention where he coaxed George Lucas off stage. Real-world. (hand-read)


## scan p.7 (scan page 7)

Page contents: Daniels interview concluded: a possible prequel trilogy, wanting to see why 3PO is like he is and keep him with R2, favorite memories from each film (the Mos Eisley overlook in Star Wars, Irvin Kershner on Empire, the Jabba's palace cooking scene on Jedi), and his framed Trivial Pursuit card. Real-world. (hand-read)


## scan p.8 (scan page 8)

Page contents: Willow preview part 1: Willow Ufgood the Nelwyn, the Daikini war and the sorceress Queen Bavmorda; principal photography from April 27, 1987 at Elstree, Wales and New Zealand under director Ron Howard; crew (Nigel Wooll, Joe Johnston, Allan Cameron, Adrian Biddle, Nick Dudman); Val Kilmer as Madmartigan. Non-Star Wars Lucasfilm production, real-world. (hand-read)


## scan p.9 (scan page 9)

Page contents: Willow preview part 2: cast (Warwick Davis, Joanne Whalley, Jean Marsh, Gavin O'Herlihy, Billy Barty), MGM and Alan Ladd Jr.; a list of Willow convention presentations with Terry Erdmann. Real-world. (hand-read)


## scan p.10 (scan page 10)

Page contents: Star Wars 10th Anniversary Convention report (Los Angeles, May 23-25, 1987): Lucasfilm models room, ILM seminar (Ralph McQuarrie, Dennis Muren, Bruce Nicholson), guest panels, a marketing panel including Howard Roffman, the costume contest, and George Lucas's first public fan appearance with a staged C-3PO, R2-D2 and Darth Vader skit and birthday cake. Real-world event; the line about "a man more powerful than Vader himself" refers to George Lucas in the skit, not an in-universe feat. (hand-read)
