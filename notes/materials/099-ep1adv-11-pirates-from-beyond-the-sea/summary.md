# EA11 Pirates from Beyond the Sea: summary

Full read (1268 lines). Swagg's 10 remaining droids are all destroyed; Swagg escapes.

## Feats
- **Tarpals:** mine disarmed and thrown to cut off a droid's head; 2 droids lured into quicksand; 2 crushed; Swagg tackled (shoulder dislocated); flies an unfamiliar starship.
- **Jar Jar** (by luck): 3 droids on the heyblibber; 2 under the obelisk; dodges a veermok; 3 m spear vault; saves the Dobbis statue.
- **Nass:** the interrogation ruse; outruns an opee.

## Anti-feats
- Jar Jar is snared and left hanging. Swagg gets away.

## Lore and continuity
- New characters: Rep Been, Lob Dizz, Commander Wollod.
- The sacred place's statues: Dobbis, Big Maxiboss Gurunda, and 3 effigies. Cave paintings; the veermok lullaby legend; baking pits from before gasser cooking.
- Gungans regard mines as cowardly (consistent with EA10).
