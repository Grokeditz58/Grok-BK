# EA11 Pirates from Beyond the Sea: notes

## Ch. 1
- **Rep Been** is the Rep Council's historian and keeper of the sacred place. He finds Swagg's camouflaged angular starship and 6 chrome droids loading statues. A vine snare catches him. **Captain Swagg** (blue skin, red eyes, red uniform) wants the Rep heyblibber to replace his lost sub and keeps Been as a hostage.

## Ch. 2–3
- **Lob Dizz** is a sub-engine and guidance engineer, famous for high-speed bongos. The 3rd droid's circuits are fried.
- **Boss Nass's ruse:** the dead droid's head goes on a "garbage disposal", with Jar Jar voicing it over a comlink. The two surviving droids talk. They are not afraid of becoming appliances but will not "wind up talking like you."
- **What they reveal:** Swagg's crew is 14 droids. 4 were sent as decoys and 10 remain.
- Nass: "Pirates from beyond da sea" (the title line). Commander Wollod pilots Nass's private heyblibber.

## Ch. 4
- Tarpals realizes he is attracted to Fassa ("Beautiful?!", "Romance?!"). She outranks him.
- They take the Paonga Strait tunnel. **Nass outruns an opee sea killer** and claims he always wins that race.
- Nass sends Jar Jar to scout because he is "da most expendable". Fassa: "Yousa hopen Jar Jar is ganna get pasted."

## Ch. 5
- **Jar Jar takes down 3 droids by luck:** he yanks a hip cable so one falls onto a console and shorts out. Two shoot at him, their bolts ricochet off a stowage hatch, and they hit each other. Nass: "Luckiest fellow mesa know… Yousa still on probation."

## Ch. 6–7
- Jar Jar kicks off an eel and falls into an old baking pit (from before gasser cooking); a jaboon lifts him out by its tusk. He falls into a pond and kicks a fanback in the nose.
- **The cave:** Gungan cave paintings of a battle with a forgotten alien enemy. A veermok's lair; legend says Gungans can calm veermoks with lullabies. Jar Jar dodges it and it flies over the ledge into the fanbacks.
- **He vaults a 3 m chasm with an ancient spear** and climbs the airshaft after the ancient lift breaks.
- **He topples the obelisk onto 2 droids;** one explodes. He stops the runaway cart with the statue of **Dobbis** millimeters from the waterfall.
- Three great effigies are missing. Jar Jar follows planted tracks into a vine snare.

## Ch. 8
- **Tarpals** is standing on a pressure mine (not motion-sensing). He has trained to disarm mines; Gungans think only cowards use them and Otoh Gunga has none.
- **He throws the disarmed mine and cuts off a droid's head.** He leaps a quicksand pool and 2 pursuing droids sink in it. He leaves Jar Jar hanging.

## Ch. 9
- The statue Big Maxiboss Gurunda has been taken. Tarpals boards on the rising ramp and **crushes 2 droids** by reversing a repulsor transport.
- **He tackles Swagg and dislocates his shoulder**, then flies the alien ship by trial and error back from near space ("Dis is izzy").
- Swagg: "You'll never bring Captain Swagg to a court of law!" He escapes in a pod into space. Tarpals frees Rep Been.
- **Droid count: 14 in all.** 4 decoys (the EA10 mine droid plus 3 on the sub), then 3 + 2 + 3 + 2 = 10 destroyed here.

## Ch. 10–11
- 30 Grand Army platoons arrive. Nass: "Better dat hisen off Naboo dan on it!" Lob Dizz inspects the ship.
- Tarpals remembers Jar Jar and cuts him down. Jar Jar fakes dying to get a promise of front-row seats at the bongo rally and free munches ("Nice try, Binks"). Sets up EA12.
