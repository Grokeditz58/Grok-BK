# Revenge of the Sith (Matthew Stover novelization): Power-Scaling Research Notes

## 4. Character profiles (personality, arc, relationships, abilities and limits, what the book adds)

### Darth Sidious / Palpatine (19 BBY)
- **Personality:** a performer with total self-control. He plays the «tired, frightened old man» (L6589) right up to the kill. He enjoys cruelty as theatre («Happy Empire Day!», L8163; «Please say it's Kenobi», L8171). Stover's thesis line is «The dark is generous» (part epigraphs), and Sidious is that generosity weaponized.
- **Arc:** Concealed Chancellor (Ch1–14) → semi-reveal to Anakin (Ch15, L5739) → unmasked killer (Ch17) → scarred Emperor (Ch19, L7693) → duel with Yoda that leaves him «gasping» (Ch20) → master of the rebuilt Vader (Ch21).
- **Relationships:** Dooku is a tool (Ch3 betrayal; Dooku POV: «Lord Sidious could come up with a new plan more easily than a new apprentice», L1505). Anakin is his true project (S2, S24). Yoda is the real rival, the only one who costs him his stamina on page.
- **Abilities shown:** Force concealment (event horizon), deceptive speed (Tiin and Kolar blitz), sword parity with peak-Vaapad Mace, lightning strong enough to bend Mace's own blade toward his face, endurance lightning-cage contests with Yoda, mass TK (Senate pods), master manipulation.
- **Limits shown:** he is disarmed once (Mace, ledge). He takes reflected lightning (Mace, and Yoda's rocket-blast). He is exhausted after Yoda. He fails to find Yoda's body. He relies on Anakin as an insurance policy in the office (L6759–6765).
- **What the book adds to his scaling:** Stover is the only source that (a) calls the Mace sword phase a literal impasse, (b) puts Mace's admission «too strong for me» *before* the faked surrender, (c) makes the office fight's fear ambiguous («Do you think the fear you feel is mine?»), and (d) shows him drained after Yoda. Net: **Sidious > Mace (power), Sidious ≥ Yoda (endurance/power), Sidious ≫ Dooku (presence).**
- *Line-by-line additions:* Sidious treats Dooku as expendable: «Dooku was not lost, he was sacrificed» (L3509). His pitch to Anakin flatters power more than skill: «You may become more powerful than all of them together.» (L3697), and Anakin is «the most powerful Jedi in the galaxy» (L5033, D). After Mustafar he lifts Vader «with effortless strength» and keeps him alive by will: «Live, Lord Vader. Live, my apprentice.» (L8613–8617).


### Anakin Skywalker / Darth Vader (19 BBY)
- **Personality:** proud and insecure («Such a fragile little ego», L1443). Love for Padmé is his root and his lever. He fears his own fury («Dread, Dooku surmised, of himself.», L1561).
- **Arc:** Hero (Ch1–7) → shut out of Mastery (Ch9–11) → courted (Ch12, 14, 15) → betrays Mace (Ch17) → Vader (Ch17–20) → maimed and suited (Ch20–21).
- **Relationships:** Obi-Wan («complementary halves of a single warrior», L8413); Palpatine (surrogate father, then master); Padmé (choked, L8283); Mace (mutual distrust; Mace saw him as «The shatterpoint»).
- **Abilities:** top-tier raw TK (the table vs Dooku), Djem So power, the rage amp ("gift of fury"), technopathy (unlocking the shackles), battle-scale Force sense, piloting, dark-side strength (Obi-Wan's arms near breaking), Force "roar".
- **Limits:** emotionally exploitable (the taunt at L1567 costs him his edge); arrogance (L8435); the leap at the end, «Half a second too slow» (L8569).
- **Book adds:** the clearest prose evidence for **restrained Anakin ≈ Dooku** and **rage-Anakin > tired Dooku**. Blade parity with Obi-Wan, and Vader ahead in raw power. Dooku attests to his potential (S9, S10) and Sidious too (S2).
- *Line-by-line additions:* Mace has the most balanced Jedi read: «arguably the most powerful Jedi alive, and he is still getting stronger. But he is not stable.» (L2941). His Council outburst is narrated as «the voice of Dooku's destroyer» (L3953). His boasts escalate from «No Jedi in this room can match my power» (L3955) to «the power of any five Masters. Any ten.» (L4053) and then collapse into «I'm one of the most powerful Jedi alive, but it's not enough.» (L5199). His utility includes psychometry («the whole apartment stank of Obi-Wan», L5095) and prophetic dreams that «had never been wrong» (L3529). The Padmé «bug that he could crush» beat (L5153) foreshadows the choke. Ch21's second-person coda states that suited Vader is «so far less now than what you were» (L8867).


### Obi-Wan Kenobi (19 BBY)
- **Personality:** calm, wry, self-effacing («I'm very flattered that you would consider me a master, but really-», L6027). His fatal flaw is attachment to Anakin, which he resolves on page («He let it go», L8531).
- **Arc:** Hero → Utapau victor → Order 66 survivor → Mustafar executioner who cannot finish the job.
- **Abilities:** best-in-Order Soresu (S16–S17); the polarity trick (Grievous, Vader); TK precision; Force-amped strength; mind tricks.
- **Limits:** loses most pure-power exchanges with Vader (TK roar, grip). He is Force-thrown by Dooku twice and knocked out. Grievous throws him twice in melee.
- **Book adds:** the strongest Legends prose for **Obi-Wan > Grievous (low-mid diff with sabers)** and **Obi-Wan = Vader in blades**. Mace rates Obi-Wan the Jedi most likely to beat Grievous.
- *Line-by-line additions:* Mace calls him «our most cunning and insightful Master-and our most tenacious» (L3849). His faith in Anakin is total: «Anakin can do anything. Except betray a friend.» (L4243). The clones fear him: Cody plans heavy weapons from five companies (L7173). On Utapau's aftermath he survives through his mount's sacrifice, TK, a beast-suggestion and a rebreather, ending in «Empty, he found clarity.» (L7545), which sets up «He let it go.» (L8531).


### Count Dooku (19 BBY)
- **Personality:** aristocratic, amused, contemptuous («this is pathetic.»), tired: «It is ... fatiguing, to play the villain for so long, Master.» (L1069).
- **Arc:** Expects a staged win (Ch2) → nearly loses for real (Ch3) → betrayed and beheaded (Ch4).
- **Abilities:** Makashi; directing the Force subtly into the opponents' balance (L1463); strong TK (furniture, the wall-throw, accelerating a fall); self-recharge (L1545).
- **Limits:** **stamina/age** («each block aged him a decade»; «Force exhaustion»). Makashi can't meet Djem So's power.
- **Book adds:** a 2v1 that turns against him, and a solo fight against Anakin decided by Dooku's stamina. Use it to cap Dooku's endurance in long fights.
- *Line-by-line additions:* Palpatine's verdict on Dooku's end is «a trace overconfident» (L2781), and Sidious privately calls him «sacrificed» (L3509). The Council reads Anakin's win as proof that he can «battle a Sith Lord alone» (L3837).


### General Grievous (19 BBY)
- **Personality:** sadistic, contemptuous of Neimoidians, a pragmatic runner («very good at running away», L6065).
- **Abilities:** 12–20 strikes/sec with four arms; combat-algorithm adaptation; staff-immune armor; strength (the console, throws); holdout-blaster draw speed.
- **Limits:** no Force; the gut sac is a soft point; he panics when his blades are cut.
- **Book adds:** concrete numbers (12/sec, 20/sec), plus Mace's report that Grievous adapted to Vaapad «after a single exchange». Obi-Wan's win is clean in the saber phase.
- *Line-by-line additions:* Yoda wants Masters, not Knights, sent against him (L4761). His burst speed «seemed to teleport» (L3449) is shown only against Gunray. He boasts the Utapau base «can stand against a thousand Jedi. Ten thousand.» (L3459, D). Obi-Wan goes in with a read on him: «how he fights now-and for how he runs away» (L4773).


### Mace Windu (19 BBY)
- **Personality:** stern, principled, distrustful of Palpatine and Anakin. Willing to kill a «Sith disease» without trial.
- **Abilities:** Vaapad (its creator and only master); the loop that turns the enemy's darkness into his power; shatterpoint perception; lightning reflection through Vaapad.
- **Limits:** he has to rely on the opponent's darkness. He loses the lightning struggle by his own POV (S21). His shatterpoint tunnel vision kills him (L6813).
- **Book adds:** Stover's text is the canonical source for "Impasse", and it explicitly states that the loop runs on Sidious's own power. That means the office Mace is a matchup-specific peak version.
- *Line-by-line additions:* his shatterpoint gift is defined in Ch8 (L2799), and he had seen Palpatine as a shatterpoint since the election (L2801). He senses that «The dark side of the Force surrounds the Chancellor.» (L3359). Obi-Wan's POV ranks Mace and Kolar «among the greatest bladesbeings the Jedi Order had ever produced» (L4767).


### Yoda (19 BBY)
- **Personality:** severe and sorrowful. He accepts responsibility (S38).
- **Abilities:** lightning redirection; a body-blast counterattack; high-level saber and hand-to-hand; the lightning cage; Force-sprint escape; he survives a 100 m fall.
- **Limits:** endurance against Sidious («reached the limits of his strength»); strategic despair («just-didn't-have it»).
- **Book adds:** the most even prose account of the duel. Every Yoda success is matched by a Sidious success, and the end comes from a stamina break plus an overcommitted leap. Sidious ends it exhausted.
- *Line-by-line additions:* «Sense the future, once all Jedi could; now few alone have this skill.» (L3557). «What you fear to lose, train yourself to release.» (L3573). Kolar: «It is your sensitivity to the broader currents of the Force that a Sith Lord has most reason to fear.» (L3845). Ch21 has Yoda own the loss as a failure of method («Too old I was,» Yoda said. «Too rigid.», L8641) and become Qui-Gon's apprentice in retaining identity after death (L8651–8667). Last word on the bloodline: «Strong the Force runs, in the Skywalker line.» (L8767).


### Padmé Amidala (19 BBY)
Non-combatant. Her political arc (the Delegation of 2,000) and her death (an «inexplicable» systemic failure) have no scaling content.
- *Line-by-line additions:* Mon Mothma credits her with «the heart of a warrior» (L3889). Her Ch21 dying insistence: «there ... is still good in him» (L8721).

### Bail Organa (19 BBY)
- **Personality:** principled, practical and brave under fire. He is cautious in politics («Let us see what we can accomplish in the Senate before we involve the Jedi.», L4861) and quick to improvise when it counts («Listen to me, Sergeant, I am a Senator of the Galactic Republic,» Bail said, improvising, L7101).
- **Arc:** a loyalist Senator who co-founds the Delegation of 2,000 with Mon Mothma (Ch11, L4025) → eyewitness to Order 66 at the Temple (Ch18, L7115–7121) → rescuer of Yoda and Obi-Wan aboard *Tantive IV* (Ch18–19) → adoptive father of Leia (Ch21, L8789).
- **Relationships:** Padmé (political ally and confidante); Mon Mothma (co-conspirator); Captain Antilles (his officer, sent to steal Saesee Tiin's homing beacon, L7133); Yoda (he witnesses Yoda's pain after the Senate duel, L8491).
- **Abilities and limits:** no Force ability and no combat feats. His assets are nerve, a ship and political cover. He survives the Temple ambush only by running: «Bail ran.» (L7123).
- **Feats (cites):** escapes a platoon of clones under fire by speeder (L7123–7125); thinks to steal a Jedi homing beacon to stop Jedi returning to the trap (L7133); picks up the escape pod carrying Yoda (L7439–7445).

(L7391); «I am,» Bail said slowly, «concerned it might be a trap.» (L7599). Bail's layman bet on Yoda is in §3 (S49, L7395).
- **Scaling implications:** none directly. He is a witness, not a combatant. His Temple scene is the book's on-page proof that a Padawan could kill several clones before being shot down (L7119), which scales clone troopers below even a young Jedi in melee.

### Agen Kolar (19 BBY)
- **Personality:** gentle, measured, a strategist on the Council («said Agen Kolar in his gentle Zabrak baritone», L3823). He is calm before the arrest («"Peace," said the third Master, a Zabrak.», L6401).
- **Arc:** Council member who helps design the trap for Sidious (Ch11) → stays on Coruscant as one of the Temple's best blades (Ch13) → joins Mace's arrest party and is killed in the first seconds (Ch17).
- **Relationships:** Mace (trusted colleague); Obi-Wan (they confer after the Council session, L3981); Saesee Tiin and Kit Fisto (fellow members of the arrest party).
- **Abilities (as stated):** Obi-Wan's POV ranks him with Mace: «both among the greatest bladesbeings the Jedi Order had ever produced» (L4767). Obi-Wan later lists him among «four of the greatest swordsmen our Order has ever produced» (L7931).
- **Limits shown:** he never gets a parry in. Sidious kills him with a single thrust after Tiin falls: «A small, neat hole in the middle of his forehead leaked smoke, showing light from the back of his head.» (L6605). His last words: «"It doesn't . . ." Agen Kolar swayed.» (L6603) / «. . . hurt . . .» (L6607).
- **Key quotes (own lines):** «Anakin is the one Jedi we can best hope would survive an encounter with a Sith Lord.» (L3839); «It is your sensitivity to the broader currents of the Force that a Sith Lord has most reason to fear.» (L3845, to Yoda); «Perhaps that is itself the reason,» Agen Kolar offered. (L4743).
- **Scaling implications:** Kolar is the book's clearest "high-hype, instant-loss" fodder. A Master rated among the Order's greatest bladesbeings dies before he can react, alongside Tiin, while Sidious is still playing the frightened old man. That is evidence for Sidious's speed and surprise, not a fair duel. Do not read it as "Kolar is weak": Stover sets up the kill with distraction (the telepathy question to Tiin, L6591) and a hidden blade.

### Saesee Tiin (19 BBY)
- **Personality:** terse and dutiful. He is the arrest party's voice of resolve: «Think not of if, Master Fisto; this duty has fallen to us. We will suffice.» (L6399).
- **Arc:** Iktotchi Master and pilot, recently wounded («One had been amputated after being shattered in battle a few months before», L6399) → arrives on Coruscant in *Sharp Spiral* (L7133) → joins the arrest → killed first.
- **Relationships:** Mace, Kolar, Kit Fisto. Palpatine taunts him directly before the kill: «Master Tiin-I see your horn has regrown; I'm very glad.» (L6545); «"Master Tiin-you're the telepath. What am I thinking right now?"» (L6591).
- **Abilities:** a telepath (per Palpatine, L6591), a Council-grade swordsman («four of the greatest swordsmen our Order has ever produced», L7931).
- **Limits shown:** the telepathy question is a trap. He reaches for Palpatine's mind and lowers his guard: «Tiin frowned and cocked his head. His blade dipped.» (L6593). Then: «Saesee Tiin's head bounced when it hit the floor.» (L6595).
- **Scaling implications:** like Kolar, a surprise kill. Sidious turns Tiin's own gift against him, which is a manipulation feat more than a speed feat. Tiin's homing beacon later matters to the plot: Bail sends Antilles to steal it (L7133).

## 6. Verdict implications (diff wording with confidence words)

*Scale used throughout:* **diff** = how hard the winner has to work (low / low-mid / mid / mid-high / high). **Confidence** = how sure the call is (low / low-medium / medium / medium-high / high). No numeric scores are used.

**Mace (Vaapad) vs Sidious, office, no Anakin → Sidious, mid-high diff. Confidence: medium, leaning medium-high (verdicts.md has Sidious, medium).**
- *For Mace:* the sword phase is a true stalemate (S20, «Impasse»). He wins the ledge disarm (L6725). Vaapad reflects lightning (L6751). Obi-Wan's POV rates him «among the greatest bladesbeings the Jedi Order had ever produced» (L4767, S45).
- *For Sidious:* the impasse only breaks through a shatterpoint that **leads to Anakin** (L6711–6713). The disarm comes after Mace's window-leap, and Sidious's surge «nearly blew him right out» (L6717). The «pulsar of fear» Mace exploits is put in doubt by «Do you think the fear you feel is mine?» (L6745). In the lightning phase Mace's own POV says «This was beyond Vaapad» (L6773) and «he's too strong for me» (L6779), *before* the fake surrender (L6783–6785). Throughout, Sidious «was not afraid. Mace could feel it: he wasn't worried at all» (L6759). Kolar, whom Obi-Wan ranks alongside Mace (L4767), dies in seconds (L6605).
- *Novel-vs-film nuance for verdicts.md:* the existing entry cites the film to argue for a legit disarm ("The film puts Anakin's arrival after the disarm"). **In the novel, Anakin is already in the doorway before the disarm** (L6711 → L6725). So the novel weakens the "legit disarm" reading. That supports keeping Sidious, and nudging confidence up from medium toward medium-high. Sidious also killed Tiin and Kolar in seconds (L6595, 6605), and Fisto off-page (L6649).

**Yoda vs Sidious → Sidious, high diff. Confidence: medium (confirms verdicts.md).**
- The novel's ledger is even until the end. Yoda redirects the opening lightning (L8301). Sidious scorches him (L8305). Yoda's rocket-body counter floors Sidious (L8311). Blades and hand-to-hand are traded (L8385). The end is endurance plus an overcommitted leap («reached the limits of his strength.» … «Half a second too slow.», L8453). Sidious is left «a very old, very tired man, gasping for air» (L8459).
- Yoda's exit is strategic: «if he fell here, that hope would die with him» (L8411), «Failed I have, and kill me they will» (L8475). The "just-didn't-have it" passage (L8397) is about the Jedi Order's way of fighting the dark (L8403–8409), not raw power. Ch21 reframes the loss as one of method: «Too old I was,» Yoda said. «Too rigid.» (L8641). Kolar calls Yoda's sensitivity the thing «a Sith Lord has most reason to fear» (L3845, S41).
- *Nuance:* the verdicts.md entry cites the JN disarm and the podium push. **Those beats are not in Stover's text.** Stover replaces them with the Redrobes redirect and the "rocket" counter. The net read is unchanged.

**Dooku vs Anakin (pre-fall Knight, 1v1, ROTS) → Anakin, mid diff. Confidence: medium (new; not in verdicts.md as its own entry).**
- Restrained Anakin is Dooku-level: Dooku «met the boy's charge easily» once taunted (L1567). Fury-amped Anakin crushes an exhausted Dooku (Ch4). Dooku's age and stamina are explicit liabilities (L1069, L1465, L1513, L1517). Dooku also admits that Anakin was «holding himself back» (L1559).
