### SP1. Plot summary, in order

The POV threads are: **Roan Shryne** (and later his mother **Jula**), **Olee Starstone**, **Darth Vader**, **Darth Sidious/Palpatine**, **Bail Organa**, the clone commando **Climber** and **Commander Salvo** (Part I), the crime boss **Cash Garrulan**, **Chewbacca** (one section), the Interdictor captain **Ugan** (one section), and **Obi-Wan Kenobi** (last chapter).

**Part I: The Outer Rim Sieges (L15–L878), Murkhana, the last hours of the Clone Wars**

- **Ch1 (L19).** *Shryne:* Jedi Knight Roan Shryne rides a gunship down through Murkhana's man-made clouds and remembers his late Master Nat-Sem's lessons about seeing past the white (L23–L39). He has a vision of a green forest world, ships, and «a lone figure stretched out his hand, tearing aside a curtain black as night...» (L47). He banters with clone Commander Salvo of the Thirty-second air combat wing. Salvo wants an orbital bombardment; Shryne defends Palpatine's hearts-and-minds policy (L79).
- **Ch2 (L101).** *Shryne:* Murkhana is the Corporate Alliance's headquarters and home of Passel Argente (L105). Shryne drops with Master Bol Chatak (an Iridonian Zabrak) and her Padawan Olee Starstone (L111–L123). He mentions that «Until recently, Shryne, too, had had a Padawan learner...» (L125). Commando Climber's Ion Team brings in a captured mercenary, Idis of the Vibroblade Brigade, who gives up the location of the landing-platform shield generator inside a medcenter (L195–L199). Shryne vetoes bombing the medcenter (L207–L213).
- **Ch3 (L245).** *Shryne:* Shryne, Climber, and a spec-three take the generator room. Shryne kicks his dropped lightsaber into Climber's hand to save him (L327) and notes that his Force strength is fading: «with the death of every Jedi, the war was leaching some of the Force out the universe» (L331).
- **Ch4 (L365).** *Climber:* Salvo radios a new priority: «The Jedi are to be killed.» (L403). Climber decides to obey on the surface only (L445).
- **Ch5 (L447).** *Shryne:* The three Jedi joke on the way to the rally point. Shryne and Chatak sense a trap (L503–L521). Ion Team blinds Salvo's platoon with electrostatic detonators and lets the Jedi go: «We received orders to kill you.» (L545), «We don't kill our own.» (L575).
- **Ch6 (L597).** *Climber / Shryne:* Salvo tells Climber the order came from the Chancellor (L613–L629). The Jedi flee. Shryne finds that Master Loorne and the other Jedi are dead (L679), and the three disguise themselves as mercenaries (L715).
- **Ch7 (L717).** *Climber:* Salvo passes on the official story that four Jedi Masters tried to assassinate Palpatine and that the Jedi engineered the war (L729).
- **Ch8 (L763).** *Shryne:* A coded burst from the Temple recalls all Jedi to Coruscant (L785). The three leap across broken bridge pylons to the landing platform (L821–L833). A second burst orders the opposite: «All Jedi are ordered to avoid Coruscant at all costs. We're to abandon whatever missions we're involved in, and go into hiding.» (L859). Every droid shuts down (L869), clones surround them, and Shryne drops his lightsaber, comlink, and beacon into the sea (L877).

**Part II: The Emperor's Emissary (L879–L1824)**

- **Ch9 (L883).** *Vader:* Four weeks after the war (L889), Vader kneels before Palpatine's hologram on the new Imperator-class *Exactor*. He is ordered to punish the clones on Murkhana who refused Order 66, because of «Contagion» (L927). Palpatine adds that Vader may kill any escaped Jedi he finds (L943).
- **Ch10 (L945).** *Vader:* Vader catalogs every flaw of the suit ("this is not walking / seeing / hearing / breathing / living", L965–L1011) and decides he is «merely a minion, an errand boy» (L955). He leaves with Commander Appo of the 501st (L1019).
- **Ch11 (L1029).** *Shryne:* After four weeks in a prison warehouse, Shryne sees Vader land. Vader kills two Ion Team commandos, and Climber and the third are captured (L1141–L1199). Chatak reveals herself («But I am!», L1151), duels Vader, wounds his forearm (sparks come out, L1173), and is beheaded (L1185). Vader eyes Starstone, and the prisoners are sent toward Agon Nine (L1201).
- **Ch12 (L1203).** *Shryne:* Shryne tells grieving Starstone that her Master acted in vengeance (L1215–L1223). Starstone, trained in Sith history under Jocasta Nu (L1277), insists that Vader is a Sith. Shryne uses a mind trick on the boarding troopers, and the two of them escape (L1297–L1311).
- **Ch13 (L1313).** *Vader:* Vader repairs his arm and argues with the "specter of Anakin" (L1321). He wonders whether Sidious built the suit as a prison, blames Padme and Obi-Wan, curses the Force (L1355), and studies his new lightsaber, which mimics Anakin's but fits his large hands badly (L1359–L1365). Appo reports two missing prisoners. Vader: «I will handle it myself.» (L1381)
- **Ch14 (L1383).** *Starstone / Shryne:* They go to Cash Garrulan, a Twi'lek and former Black Sun vigo who was Shryne's informant (L1397). Garrulan tells them the Jedi are blamed for the whole war and almost all are dead (L1483–L1487). Shryne realizes «The order was finished.» (L1493)
- **Ch15 (L1539).** *Vader / Garrulan:* Vader reads Temple records on Starstone and Shryne (L1541–L1565). Vader confronts Garrulan, lifting him with the Force (L1601). Garrulan points him at a decoy ship, the *Dead Ringer* (L1661).
- **Ch16 (L1675).** *Vader / Shryne:* The decoy is exposed (L1709). Vader orders Garrulan's ship destroyed with everyone aboard (L1733) and chases the real escape craft, Brudi Gayn's CloakShape, in a black Jedi interceptor (L1783–L1787). The CloakShape slips into a hyperdrive booster ring (L1815). Vader: «If the Jedi are so determined to disappear, then let them.» (L1823)

**Part III: Imperial Center (L1825–L4158)**

- **Ch17 (L1829).** *Bail:* The Emperor tells a group of Senators that his decrees will now be final and «The Republic is extinct.» (L1839). Afterward Mon Mothma talks resistance. Bail urges patience because he is protecting Leia: he knows the twins survived (L1901). Bail sees Vader on a landing platform and nearly collapses: «You're right. Some Jedi did survive.» (L1949)
- **Ch18 (L1951).** *Shryne:* Gayn brings them to the smuggler freighter *Drunk Dancer* and its crew (L1953–L1971). Starstone proposes broadcasting a 913 code through the ship's comm suite (L2003). Shryne falsely promises Jedi funds to pay for it (L2059). The captain arrives: «I gave birth to you. I'm your mother, Jedi.» (L2075)
- **Ch19 (L2077).** *Sidious / Vader:* In the medical lab atop Palpatine's building, Vader throws a med droid (L2099), blames his equipment, and accuses Sidious over Padme (L2143). Sidious answers that Padme died of Vader's anger (L2145), that «power is joy» (L2153), and that Obi-Wan won on Mustafar because he came to kill (L2165).
- **Ch20 (L2171).** *Shryne:* Jula explains that Shryne's father Jen gave him to the Temple and that she sensed him through the Force (L2207–L2227). She urges him to live a real life (L2249). Starstone's signal gets an answer from a group of Jedi (L2265). Starstone scorns the "Lost Twenty" who left the Order (L2289–L2293). Jula warns Shryne off Force persuasion (L2305).
- **Ch21 (L2307).** *Sidious:* Sidious recalls saving Anakin and dreams of unlocking the Sith secret of cheating death (L2323–L2325). He reflects on why he sent Anakin to the Temple (L2337) and on the Rule of Two (L2339), and he resolves to shake Vader out of his despair even at his own peril (L2355–L2357). Mas Amedda reports the Jedi signal, and Sidious keeps Vader on Coruscant (L2369).
- **Ch22 (L2371).** *Shryne:* At the rendezvous, ARC-170s and a Republic light cruiser jump the Jedi transport. The *Drunk Dancer* fights them off with Shryne directing fire, which troubles him because it means killing clones (L2467), and recovers the transport before jumping away (L2571).
- **Ch23 (L2573).** *Shryne:* Six battered Jedi come aboard: Siadem Forte, Deran Nalual, Klossi Anno, Iwo Kulka, Jambe Lu, and Nam Poorf (L2581). They talk about the dead Council members (L2597) and the Chosen One prophecy (L2607). Starstone wants to keep gathering survivors. Jula forbids further broadcasts from her ship (L2661). Filli Bitters knows where to find a hyperwave transceiver powerful enough to reach the Temple data banks (L2673).
- **Ch24 (L2675).** *Vader:* Sidious sends Vader to the empty Temple, officially to fetch Sith holocrons (L2679). Vader relives the massacre, insists to himself that he did it for Padme and had planned to kill Sidious with her (L2719–L2731), and his anguished moan topples the archive busts (L2759). Armand Isard reports that someone is remotely accessing the beacon (L2767).
- **Ch25 (L2769).** *Shryne:* On Jaguada's moon, in an ancient complex Starstone links to the Sith (L2779), Shryne broods about leaving. Jula reveals that her crew once delivered comm hardware there for "Tyranus" (L2855). Starstone has the slicers boost power to the transceiver, and the whole facility, droids included, comes back on (L2885–L2899).
- **Ch26 (L2901).** *Vader / Shryne:* Vader traces the access to Bol Chatak's transceiver (L2911) and sends the Jaguada garrison. The Jedi cut the link, escape, and leave the troopers sealed in with reactivated droids (L3043). Vader: «Sidious was wrong, he told himself as he hurried through the empty hallways.» (L3067)
- **Ch27 (L3071).** *Shryne:* With data on hundreds of Jedi (L3079), Starstone plans a search. Shryne refuses to lead it and says they will part at Mossak (L3115). Jula asks him to join her crew and offers a "good deed" contract: smuggling a former Senator home. She also tells him «Cash is dead.» (L3161)
- **Ch28 (L3163).** *Sidious:* The holocrons Vader brought back are forgeries (L3167). Mas Amedda reports that Fang Zar plans to slip off Alderaan through a Murkhana crime contact. Sidious sends Vader, «To sharpen» his hunger for the Jedi (L3247).
- **Ch29 (L3249).** *Bail:* Refugees march in Aldera (L3259–L3275). Bail and Captain Antilles wonder whether Vader is Anakin (L3293). The *Exactor* arrives, and Sate Pestage announces Darth Vader (L3359). Bail orders Breha, Leia, and the droids hidden (L3377–L3385).
- **Ch30 (L3387).** *Shryne:* Shryne, Skeck, and Archyr try to collect "our bundle", Fang Zar (L3443), but the crowd turns rowdy at the south gate (L3465).
- **Ch31 (L3483).** *C-3PO and R2-D2 / Vader:* The smugglers force the droids to let them into the palace (L3515–L3549). Vader meets Bail, and they spar over protests and freedom (L3589–L3647). Breha appears on a holo with the baby, and Vader asks to meet the Queen (L3663).
- **Ch32 (L3665).** *Shryne / Bail:* R2-D2 leads the intruders in circles and then straight into Breha and Leia, setting off alarms (L3731–L3737). Bail decides «This is Leia's father» (L3745). Security footage shows Shryne, and Vader concludes the Jedi have come for Zar (L3789). Breha cancels her visit, which saves Leia from meeting Vader (L3793).
- **Ch33 (L3809).** *Shryne / Vader:* Shryne shows himself to Vader as bait while Skeck and Archyr move Zar (L3881). Vader turns blaster bolts with his bare hand (L3907).
- **Ch34 (L3917).** *Bail / Shryne:* Pestage forces Bail to seal the palace (L3957). Vader hurls his lightsaber over the closing gate. It boomerangs, kills Fang Zar, and cuts Jula across the back (L3985).
- **Ch35 (L3995).** *Sidious / Shryne / Vader:* Sidious hears his advisers' reports (L4003–L4019) and is pleased that Vader is «Even more troubled by the outcome.» (L4047). Zar dies and Jula survives (L4053). Shryne concludes that Vader is a Sith serving Palpatine (L4073) and that the Force as the Jedi knew it has been defeated (L4085). Sidious goads Vader to admit that he needs his Master «For a time» (L4147), then points him at the Jedi bound for Kashyyyk (L4153).

**Part IV: Kashyyyk (L4159–L5474)**

- **Ch36 (L4163).** *Starstone:* Starstone now leads the seven survivors (L4199). Their search has found only dead Jedi (L4207). They fly to Kashyyyk hoping Yoda survived there (L4217–L4219). The middleman Cudgel meets them, and Wookiees draw weapons (L4331).
- **Ch37 (L4333).** *Vader:* Aboard the *Executrix*, Moff Wilhuff Tarkin reveals that he let the Jedi transport through on purpose, and proposes using the hunt to get skilled labor for the Emperor's battle station (L4391–L4433).
- **Ch38 (L4435).** *Starstone:* Starstone admits «We're Jedi» (L4447). Tarfful and Chewbacca tell her Vos and Unduli died and that Yoda escaped in a pod (L4517–L4523), and they offer sanctuary (L4559–L4565). An Imperial troop carrier lands (L4569).
- **Ch39 (L4573).** *Cudgel / Vader / Starstone:* A stormtrooper commander provokes the Wookiees and the fighting starts. The Jedi ignite their lightsabers (L4633). Vader orders the Wookiees taken alive and Interdictors deployed (L4649–L4669). Starstone sees Vader's shuttle and decides: «Then it's up to us to make this about Vader» (L4727).
- **Ch40 (L4729).** *Chewbacca / Vader:* Chewbacca leads an evacuation and reflects that the war was «a struggle between two evils» (L4739). Vader allows orbital bombardment but «save Kachirho for last» (L4761). He cuts through the Wookiees on a balcony and faces the Jedi (L4771–L4791).
- **Ch41 (L4793).** *Starstone:* Vader kills Forte and Kulka and maims Klossi Anno, Jambe Lu, and Nam Poorf (L4811). He toys with Starstone (L4817–L4833). Shryne arrives on the drop ship with Archyr, pulls Forte's lightsaber to his hand with the Force, beheads Appo (L4837–L4839), and offers himself: «Me for them.» (L4851)
- **Ch42 (L4867).** *Starstone:* The wounded are flown down. Orbital barrage sets the forest ablaze (L4925). Chewbacca agrees to pilot the transport (L4949–L4955).
- **Ch43 (L4973).** *Shryne / Vader:* Shryne and Vader duel. Shryne's hits draw sparks (L4997–L4999). Turbolaser fire destroys the balcony and Vader's shuttle (L5001). Shryne decides the chest control panel is the target (L5037).
- **Ch44 (L5039).** *Starstone:* Starstone senses Shryne still alive inside the tree (L5083) but leaves on Cudgel's prompting. The Interdictor traps the fleeing ships (L5109–L5115). Filli has an idea (L5121).
- **Ch45 (L5123).** Omniscient: «Kashyyyk had fallen.» (L5137)
- **Ch46 (L5139).** *Shryne:* Vader rips the wooden ramps apart with the Force and pelts Shryne until he falls, mortally hurt (L5159–L5177). Vader reveals «Lord Sidious is the Emperor.» (L5201) and that he is Anakin Skywalker, «the twenty-first» (L5225).
- **Ch47 (L5227).** *Starstone:* The group takes over a derelict Recusant-class destroyer whose droid brain is trying to turn the ship into a bomb, and they aim it at the Interdictor (L5325–L5335).
- **Ch48 (L5339).** *Jula / Ugan:* Jula takes in the wounded and grieves for Roan (L5397). On the Interdictor, Ensign Nullip warns Captain Ugan too late (L5433).
- **Ch49 (L5435).** *Vader:* Vader feels he has crossed a threshold into the dark side (L5443). The Interdictor explodes, hundreds of ships escape, and the dying Shryne recognizes the vision he had at the start (L5467). Vader orders the bombardment to continue (L5473).

### SP2. Character profiles

- **Darth Vader (Anakin Skywalker).**
  - *Background:* About four weeks after Mustafar, he is cut off from his old life and lives inside a badly made suit (L889, L967–L1011). Sidious's med droids rebuilt him, and he wrecked them with a scream when he learned of Padme's death (L2087).
  - *Motivation:* power, plus escape from Anakin's memory. He tells himself «Anakin is dead» (L953), but Anakin lingers «like pain from a phantom limb» (L915).
     - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part II, Ch10, Part II, Ch9
  - *Arc:* At first he is clumsy («Vader, by contrast, was clumsy», L1169), self-pitying («Look at us. Are these the faces of victory?», L2133), and easily fooled (Garrulan, Jaguada). He finds power by embracing rage (L4347). By Kashyyyk he is «faster than Starstone remembered him being on Murkhana against Master Chatak, but also more agile» (L4803). After killing Shryne he feels his suit is «little more than an outfit» (L5443).
     - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part II, Ch11, Part III, Ch19, Part IV, Ch37, Part IV, Ch41, Part IV, Ch49
  - *Relationships:* Sidious (below). He claims Padme and Obi-Wan sentenced him to the suit (L1335). He resents Bail and suspects him of helping Yoda (L3563–L3565). He feels more at home with clone troopers than with other people (L1023), and with Appo especially («I was rather fond of Commander Appo», L4975).
     - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part II, Ch13, Part III, Ch31, Part II, Ch10, Part IV, Ch43
  - *Fate:* unveiled to the galaxy (L5565, L5651). He dreams of his own apprentice and of overthrowing Sidious (L5727).
- **Vader's inner struggle with Anakin's memory.**
  - The "specter of Anakin" asks whether he has lost strength in the Force (L1319–L1321) and whether Sidious engineered his prison (L1327).
     - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part II, Ch13
  - He splits himself in two: «Anakin Skywalker had died on Coruscant.» (L1345) / «But the Chosen One had died on Mustafar.» (L1347).
     - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part II, Ch13
  - In the Temple he relives Anakin's acts, and the memory «curdled his blood» (L2715).
     - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part III, Ch24
  - On Alderaan he reasons «it was Skywalker, not Vader, who had lived on Tatooine» (L3577), yet grieves that he can never go to Naboo or Tatooine (L2759).
     - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part III, Ch31, Part III, Ch24
  - By Ch53: «Anakin was gone; a memory so deeply buried he might have dreamed rather than lived it.» (L5717).
  - Qui-Gon's verdict: «the Anakin you and I knew is imprisoned by the dark side» (L5861).
     - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part IV, Ch54
- **Darth Sidious / Emperor Palpatine.**
  - *Background:* He is publicly scarred by the "four treasonous Jedi Masters" and by Yoda (L891). He was trained by Plagueis, whom he killed in his sleep (L5545).
     - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part II, Ch9, Part IV, Ch50
  - *Motivation:* absolute rule, and the secret of life over death (L2323, L5549).
  - *Relationship with Vader:* Sidious deliberately manipulates him («Powered by treachery», L2349). He sends Vader on missions meant to sharpen his hunger (the Temple, Alderaan) and goads him to admit «I need you» (L4143). He knows «Fundamental to Vader's growth was the desire to overthrow his Master.» (L2353) and keeps open the option of a replacement apprentice or weapon (L5559–L5561).
     - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part III, Ch21, Part III, Ch35, Part IV, Ch50
  - *Fate:* consolidating power, with the battle station under construction (L5535).

  - *Background:* He was found on Weytta after an unexplained "incident" (L1551) and refused service in the Acquisition Division (L1553). He studied weapons and crime syndicates and ran Garrulan as an informant (L1555–L1557). He was a peripheral member of the "Old Guard" (L1559). He lost his Master Nat-Sem and his first Padawan at Geonosis, and a second learner at Manari (L1561–L1563).
     - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part II, Ch15

He drifts into Jula's crew; Alderaan is «the first day of the rest of his life» (L3419). Watching Vader's thrown lightsaber, he concludes the Force itself has been defeated (L4085). He returns to save the Jedi on Kashyyyk: «He is a Jedi again.» (L4865).
     - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part II, Ch14, Part III, Ch27, Part III, Ch30, Part III, Ch35, Part IV, Ch41

     - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part II, Ch15, Part I, Ch8

She gets the Jaguada facility reactivated and becomes the group's reluctant leader (L4199). She blames herself for Kashyyyk («She should have listened to Shryne», L4699) and comes to accept that «there are no more Jedi» (L5675).
     - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part II, Ch12, Part IV, Ch36, Part IV, Ch39, Part IV, Ch52

  - *Fate:* stays aboard the *Drunk Dancer* to help Wookiees and smuggle Jedi to safety (L5681–L5685).

   - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part I, Ch2, Part I, Ch5, Part III, Ch27, Part IV, Ch41

  - *Background:* Force-sensitive. Her own parents hid her from the Jedi (L2219–L2221). Her husband Jen gave baby Roan to the Temple, which ended their marriage (L2225–L2227). She took up piloting partly hoping to find him (L2221).

### SP3. Themes: what the book is about

- **Vader's self-loathing and the suit.**
  - The suit is first a prison: «This was solitary confinement. Prison of the worst sort. Continual torture.» (L1013).
     - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part II, Ch10
  - It is also a mirror of his guilt: «I brought about the very thing I feared for her. I'm to blame.» (L2147).
     - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part III, Ch19
  - By the end the suit is a costume and then an outfit (L3561, L5443). The book tracks the shift from physical complaint («This is not living», L1011) to psychological victory over Anakin.
     - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part III, Ch31, Part IV, Ch49, Part II, Ch10
  - Sidious reads it the same way: «Vader's real weaknesses were psychological rather than physical» (L2347).
     - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part III, Ch21
- **Order 66 survivors: hide or band together.**
  - The beacon's last word was to hide (L859, L3095). Starstone's instinct is to gather and rebuild (L2621).
  - Gathering brings disaster: Jaguada, then Kashyyyk, where the Jedi become the Empire's pretext (L4695).
  - The survivors' end state is dispersal and infiltration (L5675–L5697). Obi-Wan's verdict mirrors Shryne's: they should have gone to ground (L5785).
- **The Jedi were played.** Characters repeat the idea: Eyl Dix at L2027, Shryne at L2195, Obi-Wan at L5789–L5793. The Order's privilege blinded it: «And that was why we didn't see it coming» (L2191).
   - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part III, Ch18, Part III, Ch20, Part IV, Ch54
- **Attachment and love.** Starstone recites the doctrine «Love leads to attachment; attachment to greed.» (L2297), and Jula answers «Olee, love is about all we have left.» (L2301). Vader's attachment to Padme drove his fall (L2331–L2337). By the end Starstone admits she has «lost the skill» of avoiding attachment (L5359).
   - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part III, Ch20, Part III, Ch21, Part IV, Ch48
- **Clones and independent thought.** Palpatine diagnoses «Contagion» (L927). Climber chooses comrades over orders (L631). Salvo warns him: «You shouldn't be thinking for yourself, brother. It's more dangerous than you know.» (L637).
   - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part II, Ch9, Part I, Ch6
- **Sith power and treachery.** Power through understanding versus understanding through power (L909). The dark side flows from «appetite, rivalry, avarice, and malice» (L4341). The Master–apprentice bond runs on betrayal (L2349, L4145–L4147, L5727).
   - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part II, Ch9, Part IV, Ch37, Part III, Ch21, Part III, Ch35, Part IV, Ch53
- **The Empire's legitimacy built on fear.** Vader states the aim directly («I am something to be feared.», L4647). The Emperor wants Vader seen so that others ask how powerful the Emperor must be (L4027). The Kashyyyk spin follows (L5643).
   - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part IV, Ch39, Part III, Ch35, Part IV, Ch51
- **The Force endures.** Shryne's closing realization: «the Force will never die» (L5467). Bail's «keep hope hidden» (L5661) and the Luke thread on Tatooine point forward.
   - Chapter (added 2026-10-11, from the L-numbers above and the chapter headings in the source text): Part IV, Ch49, Part IV, Ch51
