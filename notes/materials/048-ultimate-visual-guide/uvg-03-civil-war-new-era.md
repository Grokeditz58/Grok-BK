# Star Wars: The Ultimate Visual Guide (DK, 2005): The Civil War and A New Era (pp. 72–111), hand-read notes

Ryder Windham, *Star Wars: The Ultimate Visual Guide* (DK, First American Edition 2005; ISBN 0-7566-1420-1; foreword by Ian McDiarmid; Lucasfilm continuity supervisor Leland Chee). This is an official licensed DK/Lucas Books title.
Citation convention: "UVG p. NN" is the printed page number. Text in quotation marks is verbatim from the page. [KEY] marks power-scaling or lore-critical passages, and [sic] marks printing errors exactly as printed.

- **p72-73** Section opener The Civil War: Alliance to Restore the Republic born from "shared whispers between individuals such as Senators Bail Organa and Mon Mothma"; Leia's "so-called 'mercy missions'"; Vader "found himself in orbit above his despised homeworld." Art: Star Destroyer, thumbnails (exogorth, Palpatine lightning).
- **p74-75** Leia's Mission (0 BBY): Leia learns of the superweapon "on a mercy mission to the planet Ralltiir". Blockade runner (Tantive IV vs Devastator). DARK ENCOUNTER: on her first visit to Coruscant Leia meets the Emperor and Vader; "Despite their powers, the Sith Lords do not sense anything unusual about Leia, who is unaware of her true heritage." Enter the Dark Lord: "As he orbits the world that was once his home, long-suppressed memories fill him with silent rage." KYLE KATARN: "infiltrates a top-secret Imperial complex on the planet Danuta to steal technical readouts for the Death Star, then transmits the plans to Princess Leia's ship. In the years that follow, Katarn wins many battles against the Empire, and discovers that he is Force-sensitive." Alderaan: "The planet has no weapons or military force, but this was not always the case." Rebel Princess: "an expert shot with a blaster". Leia's denial; Vader fakes distress signal; escape pod.
- **p76-77** Luke's Destiny (0 BBY): Luke and "childhood friend Windy" lost in the Jundland Wastes, rescued by Ben; Owen "warns him not to come back". Restless spirit: father "a navigator on a spice freighter". Luke's guardians. Caption: C-3PO is "the same protocol droid who once served Shmi Skywalker" (Owen doesn't recognize it). Jawas "rodentlike creatures". Workshop revelations: "Having had his memory wiped years before, C-3PO does not remember the Lars homestead or the name 'Skywalker.'" Biggs Darklighter: "First Mate aboard the starship Rand Ecliptic ... plan to jump ship and join the Rebel Alliance"; friends Fixer, Camie at Tosche station.
- **p78-79** A Jedi in Exile (0 BBY): "accounts of his apparent abilities to roam without fear and communicate with beasts have earned him a reputation as a wizard ... After learning how to merge his consciousness with the Force from the spirit of Qui-Gon Jinn, he becomes even more mindful of the present". Sand People strike; The wizard emerges; Ben recognizes C-3PO and R2 "but chooses to keep this information to himself." Ben's home (cellar workshop). ANAKIN'S LIGHTSABER: "'Your father wanted you to have this,' ... In fact, Vader remains unaware of Luke's existence, but Ben thinks it best to protect Luke from the truth about his father." KRAYT DRAGONS: "claws that can shred through durasteel ... Krayt dragon gizzards contain beautiful pearls ... Young Sand People hunt krayt dragons in an initiation rite". Slaughtered Jawas; Victims of the Empire.
- **p80-81** Passage to Alderaan (0 BBY) [running head reads "Passage to Alderaan" rather than "The Civil War" — printing quirk]: Bestine; SCUM AND VILLAINY: Ben "ignites his lightsaber and moves fast, disabling both attackers and leaving Ponda Baba minus one arm." Imperial road block (mind trick). Hiring Han Solo; Cantina band (Figrin D'an, Lady Valarian). Destruction of Alderaan: Leia says Dantooine; Tarkin "orders Admiral Motti to fire". GREEDO'S END: "Failing to notice Solo drawing his own blaster, a fatal blaster bolt puts paid to the Rodian's plans." Disgruntled Hutt (Jabba with Boba Fett at the Falcon). Fast escape: Kubaz spy Garindan; Ben feels "something terrible has happened." "That's no moon!"
- **p82-83** "Now, I Am the Master" (0 BBY): Breakout (cell 2187). Trash woes; dianoga ("seven tentacles that—if severed—will grow back"; native to Vodran). Ben's mission: power terminal "atop a 35-kilometer-tall generator tower". THE DUELISTS [KEY]: "Vader is confident that he will be the victor, asserting that the roles have reversed since their last fateful encounter on Mustafar—and that now, he is the master. As their battle brings them to the corridor outside the docking bay, the Sith Lord does his best to wear down the aged Jedi, and when Ben appears to surrender, Vader does not hesitate to strike. But he is surprised when the red blade of his Sith lightsaber connects only with his opponent's empty robes, leaving him to wonder if Obi-Wan has somehow become even more powerful." A narrow escape: "In a diversionary move, Ben allows Vader to cut him down".
- **p84-85** The Battle of Yavin (0 BBY→0 ABY): Great Temple: "During the Clone Wars, Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker dueled with the deadly, Force-sensitive Separatist commander Asajj Ventress at this same location." Battle plan: General Dodonna; "two-meter wide exhaust port". Alliance starfighters: "Of the 30 Rebel pilots who fight for the Alliance at the Battle of Yavin, only three will survive the assault on the Death Star." Caption: Biggs "killed during the battle by fire from Darth Vader's fighter." Death Star trench: Vader's "TIE Advanced x1 prototype". Chain reaction. Victory ceremony. VADER'S ESCAPE: "Vader is forced to crash-land on the planet Vaal ... attack from vicious creatures ... reaches a shuttle that carries him to Coruscant."
- **p86-87** Encounter on Mimban (2 ABY) [Splinter of the Mind's Eye]: Circarpous V "commonly known as Mimban"; Halla "an old woman who claims to be Force-sensitive. Halla possesses a small splinter of the legendary Kaiburr crystal, which greatly magnifies one's perception of the Force." Monstrous wandrellas, Coway. Caption: "Vader's limited knowledge of Padmé Amidala's death precludes initial suspicions that Luke is his son." Temple of Pomojema. FIGHTING THE DARK LORD: Leia fights Vader, "felled by his merciless blade". Caption [KEY]: "Despite Luke's lack of formal training in lightsaber combat, he is assisted and enabled by the spirit of Ben Kenobi to defeat Darth Vader. The Dark Lord loses his right arm and topples into a deep, crumbling well, but Luke's instincts tell him Vader survived the fall." Healing powers: Kaiburr's "power decreases in direct proportion to its distance from the Temple of Pomojema." From Yavin to Hoth: Skorr; Ord Mantell; Boba Fett meets Vader on Ord Mantell "to suggest using Solo as bait to capture Skywalker" (comic strip captions 1–3).
- **p88-89** Battle of Hoth (3 ABY): Ozzel brings fleet out "too soon"; tauntaun patrol; Ice creature's cave ("Luke has learned to use the Force to move objects"). Echo Base "(so-named because of the huge, echoing caves in which it was built)"; "Luke Skywalker single-handedly brings down an AT-AT after his snowspeeder is shot down". Bacta: "red alazhi and kavam bacterial particles mixed with the colorless liquid ambori". Hoth predators: wampas "averaging over two meters (6.5 feet) in height ... can barely smell beyond their own stench". Gallofree Yards transports. Dark Lord on Hoth: Vader decides "to capture Solo and use him as bait to trap his Force-powerful friend".
- **p90-91** A Jedi in Training (3 ABY): Yoda "went into hiding to escape execution"; Luke "imagines him to be a great warrior." Mud house (Republic-era salvage). Alien ecosystem: knobby white spider is "a mobile root that is part of the gnarltree's life cycle". RIGOROUS EXERCISE: "With 800 years of experience training Jedi ... Because his training did not begin in infancy, he finds it difficulty [sic] to contain his emotions." THE LAST JEDI MASTER?: "Yoda's powers have helped him survive but, at 900 years old, he cannot escape old age. If he fails to train Luke, the legacy of the Jedi could die with him." THE CAVE: "The cave is possibly a vestige of power from a dark Jedi who landed on Dagobah during the Clone Wars." Captions: "In the cave, Luke confronts his worst nightmare." "Unleashing his anger, Luke ends the duel swiftly." "Vader's ruined helmet reveals Luke's own visage." BOUNTY HUNTER ON DAGOBAH: droid bounty hunter Milko; "their encounter was a test arranged by Yoda, but is uncertain whether he passed or failed." Failing to believe; Urgent departure.
- **p92-93** "No Disintegrations" (3 ABY): Vader suggests to the Emperor "that Luke would be a powerful ally if he could be turned"; "Because of Fett's reputation for disintegrating their targets, the Dark Lord warns them". Space slug. The Bounty Hunters: "Boba Fett has already sabotaged Dengar's starship, and IG-88—having calculated that Fett will be first to find the Falcon—has placed a tracking device on Slave I." Recruiting Dengar. BOUNTY HUNTERS' GUILD: headed by Bossk's father Cradossk; "Prince Xizor had schemed to create a mercenary force of freelance hunters, contracting Boba Fett to join the Guild and destroy it from within ... Bossk devoured his own father, and the Guild divided into two warring factions." 4-LOM, Zuckuss (Gand findsman), Bossk ("intends to obtain Chewbacca's pelt, despite Vader's orders"), Dengar ("Banned from racing, he became an assassin"). BOBA FETT: "Jango Fett's unmodified clone has reached the age of 35 standard years". IG-88: "Holowan Laboratories ... one of four identical robots who share a collective consciousness as well as a goal of galactic conquest. It is the droid designated IG-88 B that accepts Vader's assignment." IG-2000 (Trilon Aggressor). Camouflage technique.
- **p94-95** Betrayal in the Clouds (3 ABY): Lando "Baron Administrator"; Falcon "traveling at sublight speed". Unexpected meeting: Vader "proves that a blaster is no match against the dark side of the Force." FREEZING SOLO: Vader "decides to test it on Han Solo to see if a human can survive the freezing process." Duel on Cloud City: Luke "avoids being frozen and impresses Vader with his fighting prowess." NIGHTMARISH REVELATION: "'No, Luke. I am your father.' Vader believes Skywalker can destroy the Emperor, and invites Luke to join him so that they can overthrow Palpatine and rule the galaxy as father and son." THANK THE MAKER? [KEY]: "When C-3PO's shattered parts are presented to Darth Vader, he instantly recognizes the droid he built as a child. He orders the parts to be destroyed, but after capturing the Rebels, he learns that the droid's pieces have been recovered by Solo's copilot. The Dark Lord uncharacteristically rescinds his previous order, and has C-3PO's remains delivered to the Wookiee's cell." Uncertain future: "Luke tells no one of Vader's claim."
- **p96-97** Shadows of the Empire (3 ABY): "Ten years earlier, Vader exterminated 200,000 Falleen to contain a lethal bioagent that had been accidentally released by Imperial scientists on Xizor's homeworld." Xizor plans to frame Vader "for the assassination of Palpatine's would-be prize, Luke Skywalker." Black Sun contract (second Death Star materials). GURI: "A human replica droid programmed to be an assassin ... When Guri is left without a master, she seeks out a droid engineer to restructure her programming". Dash Rendar (Outrider; Gall). A NEW LIGHTSABER: built from instructions in Ben's home; "his new lightsaber follows the design of Ben's weapon." Undercover assignment (Snoova, Boushh). XIZOR'S LAST STAND: Vader "directs the Executor to obliterate the skyhook [Falleen's Fist] and everyone on board." Delivering the goods: Jabba "so delighted by the idea of adding Solo to his art collection that he even agrees to Fett's demand for a higher fee."
- **p98-99** Prisoners of Jabba the Hutt (3 ABY): Luke "deliberately leaves C-3PO unaware of various details". JABBA AND THE GAMORREANS: Jabba hired 12; "it was Hutt tradition to fight blindfolded ... Jabba had his henchmen batter the Gamorreans. The nine survivors remain in awe of Jabba's fighting skills." Jabba's palace: B'omarr monastery; "ultra-strong duranium". ENTER THE JEDI: "Jabba is immune to Jedi mind tricks". Rancor "A birthday gift to Jabba from Bib Fortuna". Hutt's last gasp. BOBA FETT LIVES!: "After several agonizing days, Fett blasts his way out of the creature and is rescued by his fellow bounty hunter, Dengar." Sail barge Khetanna; Bib Fortuna escapes "in a small patrol craft".

- **p104-105** A NEW ERA (section opener): "Some Imperials went into hiding or became warlords, but the most dangerous remained loyal to the Emperor long after his apparent demise. After Luke learned that Palpatine's consciousness had survived beyond the Battle of Endor, he had to conquer his own temptation toward the dark side ... He came to believe that the Jedi Order might thrive once more, not as it was, but in an adapted form to suit the needs of the New Republic. ... the government, which has become the Galactic Federation of the Free Alliances." Art: montage strip (no text).
- **p106-107** The New Republic (4–9 ABY): "Within days of its victory at the Battle of Endor, the Rebel Alliance officially becomes the Alliance of Free Planets." Declaration of a New Republic signed by Mon Mothma, Leia, Borsk Fey'lya, Ackbar, reps of Corellia, Kashyyyk, Sullust, Elom; "The eight signatories form the New Republic Provisional Council". "Two and a half years later, the New Republic launches a series of attacks to capture the city planet Coruscant". The Rogue and the Princess (Isolder; "a jealous Han Solo kidnaps Princess Leia"). Coruscant reclaimed; Political infighting. GRAND ADMIRAL THRAWN: "a Chiss named Mitth'raw'nuruodo demonstrated such impressive tactical skills that the Emperor approved his formal Imperial military training ... Shortly before the Battle of Hoth, a successful collaboration with Darth Vader led to Thrawn being rewarded with the authority to call upon the services of the Dark Lord's private assassins, the Noghri death commandos." Caption: ysalamiri "native to the planet Myrkr". JORUUS C'BAOTH: "Five years after Palpatine's death"; caption "The clone of a deceased Jedi Master Jorus C'baoth was created by Palpatine to guard Imperial treasure and a cloning facility on Wayland. C'baoth suffers from clone madness, the result of being grown too quickly". C'baoth's battle meditation at Sluis Van. Jade and Karrde (Celina Marniss). NOGHRI WARRIORS: "Vader offered aid to the surviving Noghri, who were so grateful that they became his private assassins. Decades later, Princess Leia's encounter with a Noghri leads her to Honoghr, where she discovers that Vader deceived the Noghri". Twins Jaina and Jacen. THE DEATH OF THRAWN: "Mara Jade slays Luke's clone and C'baoth, while Thrawn's Noghri bodyguard—determined to avenge his people's betrayal by the Empire—fatally stabs his master." [Bilbringi]

World Devastators (R2-D2 coded signal). Bounty hunter team: "Dengar proclaims he will never work with Fett again." New war droids (Shadow Droids, X-1 Viper Automadons). DARK JEDI?: "Luke allows Leia to believe that he has joined the dark side." ANAKIN SOLO: Galaxy Gun destroys Pinnacle Moon; "Although her husband suggests the boy should be named Han Solo, Jr., Leia insists that his name is Anakin." - **p110-111** The New Jedi Order (11–29 ABY): Kyp Durron "falls under the spell of the spirit of the long-dead Dark Lord, Exar Kun, but finally prevails and becomes a Jedi Knight." JEDI ACADEMY: "Eleven years after the Battle of Yavin, Luke returns to Yavin 4 to found his praxeum ... students include the sky-hermit Streen, the brash Kyp Durron, the self-doubting clone Dorsk 82, and Kirana Ti of Dathomir. Although Luke draws from his instruction with Yoda and knowledge of the Jedi Order, he does not adhere to its traditional mandates of selecting only Force-sensitive infants, training one apprentice at a time, and eschewing marriage." MARRIAGE OF MARA AND LUKE: "In 19 A.B.Y. ... many regard their union as symbolic of a bright future". Skywalker's students: Massassi temples; lore-master Tionne. Caption (alt crop verified): Corbos "murderous leviathan that seems to thrive off the spirits of its victims." THE YUUZHAN VONG: "Twenty-five years after the Battle of Yavin ... deadly aliens who worship merciless gods. The Yuuzhan Vong are refugees from a destroyed home planet"; dovin basals; Dobido moon pulled onto Sernpidal; "the Yuuzhan Vong conquer Coruscant, rename it Yuuzhan'tar". DEATH OF CHEWBACCA: "Chewbacca dies in a planet-shattering explosion." HAN AND LEIA'S CHILDREN: "Nearly two years into the war ... Jacen ... and Jaina ... are 18, and their younger brother Anakin ... is in his 16th year ... Anakin sacrifices himself to help destroy the voxyn. Anakin's death prompts Jaina to be temporarily drawn to the dark side, and leaves Jacen to question the path of a Jedi."
