# 048 summary: Star Wars: The Ultimate Visual Guide (DK, 2005)

## Strongest findings (verbatim, cited)
1. **Vader vs Obi-Wan on the Death Star** (UVG pp. 82–83, "THE DUELISTS"): "Vader is confident that he will be the victor, asserting that the roles have reversed since their last fateful encounter on Mustafar—and that now, he is the master. ... But he is surprised when the red blade of his Sith lightsaber connects only with his opponent's empty robes, leaving him to wonder if Obi-Wan has somehow become even more powerful."
2. **Palpatine vs Mace and the Sith rituals** (UVG pp. 56–57): "Palpatine surprises them by igniting his own lightsaber, quickly slaying all but Mace Windu, who learns too late that his opponent's greatest weapon is Anakin Skywalker." The caption adds that Anakin, "disarming the Jedi and, unintentionally, allowing Palpatine to kill Windu." On Sith rituals: Palpatine studied Holocrons that "enabled him to channel Sith spirits (right), who taught him how to harness dark-side energy and release lethal bolts of lightning."
3. **Vader's turn at Endor** (UVG pp. 102–103): "Vader realizes his own destiny is simply to be a pawn in his Master's schemes." Under A DESTINY FULFILLED: "Fulfilling the prophecy that he will bring balance to the Force, Anakin seizes the Emperor. Mortally wounded by Sith lightning, the Chosen One hurls Darth Sidious down an elevator shaft, and the Emperor explodes in a violent release of dark energy." Earlier, on Mustafar (p. 61): Vader "will not stray from this viewpoint until the last day of his life, when he finally realizes that there is a power stronger than the dark side."
4. **Maul before Naboo** (UVG pp. 32–33): Maul "survives for a month against a legion of assassin droids". At Black Sun's base, the Vigos "believe they are more than a match for the Sith Lord. Maul slays them all." Mighella "is felled by a lethal slash of Maul's lightsaber" once she realizes he is "a Sith Lord with far superior dark-side knowledge".
5. **Force lore** (UVG pp. 18–21): "The Sith Lord Darth Plagueis discovered it was possible to merge with the Force and still retain individual consciousness, but was not interested in the nonmaterial world. Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn succeeded" (p. 19). "only Sith Lords have ever demonstrated the ability to cast lethal energy charges ... it is a challenge for even the most powerful Jedi Masters to deflect such bursts" (p. 21).
6. **Lucas on the saga** (UVG p. 131, Celebration III, 2005): "to be very honest with you, I never ever thought of anything that happened beyond Episode VI. It's the Darth Vader story." From p. 117 on Episode III: "'Oh my God, that's Anakin. The poor guy is still stuck in that suit.' So the tension and drama is completely reversed."

## Other notable points
- **Grievous's crash** (p. 55 caption): "Grievous is unaware that the ship crash that led to his cybernetic reconstruction was caused by Dooku."
- **Dooku on Geonosis** (p. 49): "Dooku proves to be the master swordsman when he cuts Anakin's right arm off at the elbow."
- **Vader's other kills**: he tracks down the Dark Woman: "After a pitched battle, Vader claims another grim victory for his Master" (p. 65). Also, "Vader exterminated 200,000 Falleen" (p. 96).
- **Vader recognizes C-3PO** (p. 94): "he instantly recognizes the droid he built as a child ... The Dark Lord uncharacteristically rescinds his previous order".
- **Splinter of the Mind's Eye canon placement** (p. 86 caption): "The Dark Lord loses his right arm and topples into a deep, crumbling well".
- **Palpatine's end** (p. 109): "the cyborg Jedi Empatojayos Brand, absorbs and vanquishes Palpatine's evil spirit."
- **Production history**: in the original ANH script Obi-Wan survived (p. 116). Lucas explains the ROTJ title (p. 125): "We'd had so many difficulties with people trying to report stuff to the media that we called the film Revenge of the Jedi to throw people off." Splinter was "the first official Star Wars 'expanded universe' novel" (p. 132). Dermot Power's curved-hilt Sith sketches became Asajj Ventress (p. 118).

## Inconsistencies and printing quirks flagged
- **Galidraan date:** p. 28 says 44 BBY, but p. 45 says "two years before the Battle of Naboo".
- **Sharad Hett's death:** p. 40 says Aurra Sing slew him; p. 47 says he died in Gardulla's attack.
- **Death Star tractor beams:** p. 16 says "over 700"; p. 70 says "768".
- **Bane's dating:** p. 24 says "A thousand years after the Great Sith War", which clashes with the 3996 BBY and 1000 BBY dates on the same spread.
- **KOTOR II "Shades of History":** p. 27 calls Sion Nihilus's apprentice, calls Visas Marr "a Jedi", and says Bao-Dur's homeworld was bombed by Malak.
- **ROTJ dating:** the running heads on pp. 100–103 read 3 A.B.Y.
- **Running head:** p. 80 reads "Passage to Alderaan".
- **Typos marked [sic]:** "superceded" (p. 11), "organized mental facilities" (p. 19), "occured" (p. 37), "resouces" (p. 66), "finds it difficulty" (p. 90), "The Empire Stikes Back" (p. 137).
