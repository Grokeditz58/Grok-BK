# Star Wars: The Ultimate Visual Guide (DK, 2005): The Clone Wars and The Dark Times (pp. 50–71), hand-read notes

Ryder Windham, *Star Wars: The Ultimate Visual Guide* (DK, First American Edition 2005; ISBN 0-7566-1420-1; foreword by Ian McDiarmid; Lucasfilm continuity supervisor Leland Chee). This is an official licensed DK/Lucas Books title.
Citation convention: "UVG p. NN" is the printed page number. Text in quotation marks is verbatim from the page. [KEY] marks power-scaling or lore-critical passages, and [sic] marks printing errors exactly as printed.

- **p50-51** Section opener The Clone Wars: Geonosis "marked the first time in history that the Republic deployed an army"; "The Clone Wars raged throughout the galaxy for over three standard years ... Few representatives of the opposing factions lived to learn that the entire war was meticulously engineered by a Sith Lord." Coruscant space battle art; thumbnails (Ventress comic, Anakin "I pledge myself to the ways of the Sith" / "Henceforth, you shall be known as ... Darth Vader", Cody hologram, Mustafar).
- **p52-53** Battles and Campaigns (22–19 BBY) [KEY]: "many Knights refuse to wage war in the name of the Republic and instead choose to abandon the Order"; "the clones live up to the Kaminoans' promise to be immensely superior to droid soldiers"; Dooku "recruits two living beings to serve as his lieutenants: the ancient bounty hunter Durge and Force-sensitive Asajj Ventress, both of whom are adept at killing Jedi." DURGE: "A 2,000-year-old Gen'Dai bounty hunter, Durge has learned many fighting techniques over the ages, and also developed a bloodlust for killing Jedi. He first sparred with Obi-Wan Kenobi on Ohma-D'un, and was eventually bested by Kenobi on the planet Muunilinst." Civil War on Jabiim: "Many Jedi and Padawans perish ... Obi-Wan is believed to be killed in action. Moments before Anakin Skywalker leaves Jabiim, he realizes he can use the Force to crush a man's windpipe." Battle of Kamino: Mon Cal Commander Merai ambushed by the Jedi; his death "part of Darth Sidious's scheme to prolong the war". Genocide on Ohma-D'un (chemical weapon; antidote from Queyta). Conflict on Aargonar: "A'Sharad subdues Anakin". ASAJJ VENTRESS: "Initially trained by the Jedi Ky Narec, Asajj Ventress turned to the dark side after Narec's murder. Years later, Ventress defeats many gladiators in a contest to impress Dooku, who gives her a pair of lightsabers and makes her a commander of the Separatist army." Caption: "On the moon Rood in the Sriluur system, Ventress tests three renegade Jedi as potential recruits for the Separatists." JEDI GENERALS: "On a mission to Dantooine, Mace Windu single-handedly destroys a huge seismic tank to defeat an army of Federation droids. Obi-Wan survives Jabiim but becomes obsessed with hunting Asajj Ventress. Anakin gains a reputation as a great starpilot and proves himself to be a courageous warrior, but a lightsaber duel with Ventress leaves him facially scarred." THE DROID GENERAL: "Born a Kaleesh, General Grievous led his people in a war against ... the Huk ... In a mysterious aircraft accident Grievous was mortally wounded ... Grievous accepted the Banking Clan's offer of a new droid body ... he became the leader of the Confederacy's droid armies, and received lightsaber instruction from Count Dooku."
- **p54-55** Kidnapped! (19 BBY): Gunray's mechno-chair; Sidious "has diverted their attention in order to carry out his most complicated scheme—the kidnapping of Supreme Chancellor Palpatine. The Sith assigns the abduction task to General Grievous, who remains unaware of the true motives". Eta-2 Actis interceptors; ARC-170, V-wing. JEDI FIGHTER ACE: Anakin "recognized by Grievous as the 'hero without fear.'" DOOKU'S FATE: "Dooku knocks out Obi-Wan, but is soon at Anakin's mercy. Palpatine then orders the Count's execution—and Anakin complies." Grievous retreats (Belderone defeat earlier). CAPTION [KEY]: "Grievous is unaware that the ship crash that led to his cybernetic reconstruction was caused by Dooku. The Count (above) used his powers to keep the unconscious General alive (right) as part of his plan to recruit Grievous to the Separatist cause." THE GENERAL'S TROPHIES: "Grievous has added many lightsabers to his collection since his first Jedi kills on Geonosis. Because so many Jedi perished in that battle, these particular murders were not initially attributed to Grievous." Crash-landing on Coruscant.
- **p56-57** Power of the Dark Side (19 BBY) [KEY]: Anakin as Palpatine's representative; Council asks him to spy. Loyalist Committee (Mothma, Amidala, Organa). A Night at the Opera: "The Tragedy of Darth Plagueis the Wise, the story of a Sith Lord who had the power to keep people safe from death." Battle on Utapau: "After a ferocious lightsaber duel and high-speed chase ... the Jedi finally destroys Grievous using the cyborg's own blaster." SHOWDOWN ON CORUSCANT: "Palpatine surprises them by igniting his own lightsaber, quickly slaying all but Mace Windu, who learns too late that his opponent's greatest weapon is Anakin Skywalker." Caption: "Palpatine uses Sith lightning to attack Mace Windu, but the Jedi Master uses his lightsaber to force the lethal energy bolts back upon the Sith Lord, deforming his features. Palpatine summons aid from Anakin, who betrays Mace Windu, disarming the Jedi and, unintentionally, allowing Palpatine to kill Windu." A New Name: Vader's first assignment to eliminate all Jedi at the Temple. SITH RITUALS: "Chancellor Palpatine spent many years studying ancient Holocrons to learn the secrets of the Sith. The Holocrons enabled him to channel Sith spirits (right), who taught him how to harness dark-side energy and release lethal bolts of lightning. During the Clone Wars, Palpatine conducted Sith rituals on Coruscant that radiated unnerving ripples in the Force". Defending Kashyyyk: Yoda's departure "part of a plan to lure Darth Sidious into the open".
- **p58-59** The Jedi Purge (19 BBY) [KEY]: "Although Supreme Chancellor Palpatine vanquishes the four Jedi Masters who attempt to arrest him ... thousands of Jedi Generals remain scattered on distant worlds ... he activates a secret plan that he designed many years before to eliminate the entire Jedi Order." ORDER 66: "Cody receives a holographic transmission from Palpatine, who states, 'It is time. Execute Order 66.' An emergency protocol, Order 66 identifies all Jedi as traitors to the Republic that must be eliminated with extreme prejudice ... hundreds of Jedi are murdered by their own troops." JEDI SLAYER: "Transformed into Sidious's agent of evil, Darth Vader, he cuts down every Jedi in his path, including many younglings." Caption: "a youngling realizes too late that Anakin's mission is not to help the besieged Jedi—but to exterminate them." Unexpected betrayal (Aayla Secura). A New Regime: "an Empire that he will rule for life." The Survivors: Yoda and Obi-Wan disable the recall signal. THE SEPARATISTS' FATE: "executed by Darth Vader"; caption "Vader kills Shu Mai and Wat Tambor (above). The Sith Lord executes all of the Separatist leaders, leaving Nute Gunray for last." Burning Temple: security scans. DUEL IN THE SENATE: "Palpatine uses both his lightsaber and Sith lightning to attack Yoda, who is ultimately forced to retreat. Although Yoda fails to destroy the Emperor, his survival ensures that he will live to train at least one more student in the ways of the Force."
- **p60-61** Dark Lord Reborn (19 BBY) [KEY]: Chokehold. DUEL ON MUSTAFAR: "As every step becomes more perilous and Vader's attacks more ferocious, Obi-Wan realizes that he still cares for Anakin, and that the only way he can defeat his opponent is to let go of his feelings for his former friend. When Obi-Wan releases this emotional attachment, the battle turns for the Jedi." A PROPHECY FULFILLED?: "Vader believes he has restored peace and justice to the galaxy and so brought balance to the Force ... He will not stray from this viewpoint until the last day of his life, when he finally realizes that there is a power stronger than the dark side." BURNING RAGE: "Despite his fighting skills, Vader is defeated by Obi-Wan. With both legs and an arm severed, Vader lies helpless as red-hot lava burns his flesh and sears his lungs ... Fueled by anger and hatred, Vader never relinquishes his embrace of the dark side." REBUILDING VADER: "incorporates advanced technology with arcane Sith healing techniques ... Vader constructs a new, red-bladed Sith lightsaber to replace the weapon he lost on Mustafar." Ubrikkian DD-13 surgical droid. Polis Massa: Padmé "does not survive". MORE MACHINE THAN MAN: "Vader is filled with fury, not because of his imagined role in Padmé's death, but because he feels cheated by her loss. In his incredible selfishness, Vader is completely consumed by the dark side of the Force, and his humanity becomes almost entirely irretrievable." Death Star construction under Tarkin; twins split up.
- **p62-63** Section opener The Dark Times: "the Sith at long last accomplished their revenge"; Palpatine "opened the previously restricted Deep Core region of the galaxy for exploration"; Presidential Palace reconstructed into Imperial Palace; "By the time most beings realized that the Republic's fire had been extinguished, the Empire's shadow had fallen over thousands of inhabited worlds." Art: screaming Sidious; Dark Woman comic thumb.
- **p64-65** Empire of Evil (19–0 BBY) [KEY]: "the Emperor's New Order is based on tyranny, brutality, and hatred of nonhumans"; support of Imperial Navy "as well as the crime syndicate Black Sun". Caption: new Imperial Palace "has open areas large enough to house a Victory-class Star Destroyer, and it is higher than any other structure on Coruscant." THE EMPEROR: "Acting without any legal, political, or financial restraints, Emperor Palpatine develops the largest military force in galactic history ... As he strives to discover the secrets of eternal life, he also begins experimenting with cloning technology." Tarkin "first military leader to be promoted to Grand Moff ... The Emperor eventually grants him limited authority over Darth Vader." "Following the death of Alexi Garyn, Prince Xizor rises through Black Sun's power structure". "Discovered and raised by Palpatine, Force-sensitive Mara Jade is one of her Master's elite agents and assassins ... 'The Emperor's Hand.'" Kir Kanos (Red Guard). Imperial Dignitaries: "never meets with more than 12 at a time ... When the Emperor desires new advisors for his circle, he kills the old ones at whim." DARTH VADER: "The Emperor's chief enforcer and most diabolical creation, Darth Vader is the embodiment of fear itself ... uses Imperial propaganda to recruit allies such as the Noghri of Honoghr, who become his private assassins." JEDI KILLER: "Numerous Jedi abandoned the Order rather than fight for the Republic in the Clone Wars, and many others went into hiding after Palpatine's implementation of Order 66. The Emperor assigns Darth Vader with the task of eliminating the remaining Jedi, and Vader kills many over the years. Acting on information from Mara Jade, Vader tracks down the Dark Woman in the Outer Rim. After a pitched battle, Vader claims another grim victory for his Master." [Dark Woman's death per this text; art shows her with white-blade saber vs Vader.] Lambda shuttle; Emperor's shuttle cloaking device.
- **p66-67** Imperial Might (19–0 BBY): "Human males—especially those prejudiced to aliens—are recruited from Military Academies to command the clone troops; those who refuse to voluntarily enlist are either drafted to serve alongside the clones as footsoldiers or eliminated." "resouces" [sic] in para 1 ("throw all of his resouces"). Imperial Academy: students "fed propaganda to believe that all alien species are inferior beings". Carida "a high-gravity world". TIE-FIGHTER ACE: Soontir Fel, "rivaled a fellow Corellian, Cadet Han Solo, in the flight simulators ... disgraced by the mutiny of two of his graduates, Derek 'Hobbie' Klivian and Biggs Darklighter ... 181st Imperial Fighter Wing ... Baron of the Empire." Star Destroyers "reduce an entire civilization to molten slag". Imperial forces: stormtroopers "cannot be bribed, seduced, or blackmailed into betraying the Emperor." AT-AT "over 15-meters (49 feet) high"; speeder bike "up to 500 kph (311 mph)". TIE pilots "committed to accomplishing their missions, even if it requires self-sacrifice."
- **p68-69** [read from alt EHskzZ7I scan files _0072/_0073; missing from main c2c] Rogues and Villains (19–0 BBY): "The good become flexible, the bad become deadly, and the clever are never without a blaster and an escape route." Xizor Transport Systems; Palpatine "contracts Xizor to coordinate shipping for various clandestine Imperial operations." HAN SOLO AND CHEWBACCA: "When Corellian pilot and former thief Han Solo graduates at the top of his class from the Military Academy on Carida ... Lieutenant Solo refuses to kill a renegade Wookiee he finds on a slave ship ... Solo is drummed out of the Imperial Navy for insubordination. Chewbacca—bound to honor Wookiee custom—pledges a life debt". LANDO: "once won the Millennium Falcon ... in a sabacc game. Unfortunately, he did not know how to fly it. On Nar Shaddaa, he stopped bounty hunter Boba Fett from apprehending the smuggler Han Solo, and Solo returned the favor with free piloting lessons." Comic panel "Four million on the table. Thinking of raising, Calrissian?" / "You have no idea what I'm thinking, Raynor." GREEDO: son of Greedo the Elder; scuffle with Anakin after Boonta Eve; "his only reputation is therefore as a rude, foul-smelling punk"; Dyyz Nataz, Spurch "Warhog" Goa. BOBA FETT: "In the years since Jango Fett's death, Boba Fett has become so notorious that he is more widely known than his father ever was ... willing to work in exchange for rare Mandalorian weapons and armor". Caption: "In an early encounter on Maryx Minor, Boba Fett fights Darth Vader after the Sith Lord double-crosses him. Both survive, and Vader develops a grudging respect for the bounty hunter." Droid rebellion (Boonda the Hutt). Black Sun and the Hutts: "Xizor believes that Jabba answers to him. However, Jabba is confident Xizor will soon be a footnote in history." Ephant Mon (Chevin, Glakka).
- **p70-71** The Death Star (19–0 BBY): "Measuring 120 kilometers (75 miles) in diameter ... exceed a million beings, not including a security force of more than 25,000 stormtroopers and a support 'crew' of more than 400,000 droids. Weaponry includes 10,000 turbolasers and heavy turbolasers, 2,500 laser cannons, 2,500 ion cannons, and 768 long-range tractor beams." [Note: p16 says "over 700 tractor beam generators".] Tarkin "former Lieutenant Governor of Eriadu ... Tarkin believes that humans are superior to aliens ... Tarkin Doctrine: 'Rule through fear of force rather than by force itself.'" Destroyer of Worlds: "eight combined beams ... more firepower than half of the Imperial fleet ... recharge period of 24 standard hours". Officers: Tagge, Motti. Turbolaser defenses. IMPERIAL ENGINEER: Bevel Lemelisk "previously helped the Geonosians produce their attack craft and also designed the Death Star's prototype, the first weapon ever to wield a superlaser ... When it is eventually discovered that the Death Star has a major design flaw, the Emperor will hold Lemelisk responsible." Caption: blueprints on "reusable durasheet scrolls ... would fade away and disappear after a short period."
