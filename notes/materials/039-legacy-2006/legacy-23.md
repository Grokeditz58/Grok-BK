# Star Wars: Legacy #23 — "Loyalties" (Dark Horse, May 2008)

- Script John Ostrander · art Jan Duursema · inks Dan Parsons · colors Brad Anderson · letters Michael Heisler · cover Duursema & Anderson. 24 pages read (p1 cover, p2 credits/recap, p3–24 story). Set 137 ABY, after Cade's escape from the Sith Temple (#20–21 thread on Coruscant).
- Page numbers are file pages (p1 = cover).

## Rav (Legacy era)

> "AS FOR THE GRINNING LIAR...THE IMPS GAVE ME THE SHIP FOR SERVICES RENDERED. I OWN IT -- LIKE I OWN YOU, CADE." · "BLACK SUN'S GOT A PRICE ON CADE'S HIDE SO HIGH, IT NEARLY MADE ME WEEP"
> — Rav, Star Wars: Legacy #23, p. 4

> "COULDA KILLED YOU WAY BACK, CADE -- BUT I DIDN'T, DID I? ... SAVED YOUR LIFE -- TOOK YOU IN WHEN THE JEDI ABANDONED YOU TO COLD SPACE."
> — Rav, Star Wars: Legacy #23, p. 7

> "BY THE BLOODY BONES, I SWEAR I WILL BE TRUE AND LOYAL TO YOU OR FORFEIT WILL BE MY LIFE."
> — Rav, Star Wars: Legacy #23, p. 8

He breaks the oath right away. He sells Cade out to Darth Maladi and plays her the recording of the oath:
> "I'VE FOUND A BOND OF TRUST IS ALWAYS A USEFUL TOOL."
> — Rav, Star Wars: Legacy #23, p. 10

> "I DON'T MIND CRIME LORDS, BUT I PURELY HATE STUPID ONES!"
> — Bantha Rawk, Star Wars: Legacy #23, p. 21

> — Azlyn Rae, Star Wars: Legacy #23, p. 24

(She stands over dead Black Sun fighters on p24, so she has evidently killed some of them.)
