# Star Wars: Legacy #29 — "Vector, Part 10 of 12" (Dark Horse, October 2008)

- Story John Ostrander & Jan Duursema · script Ostrander · pencils Jan Duursema · inks Dan Parsons · colors Brad Anderson · letters Michael Heisler · cover Dustin Weaver ("A PLAGUE FROM THE PAST!"). 24 pages read (p1 cover, p2 credits/recap, p3–24 story). Set 137 ABY. The *Mynock* raids the Imperial garrison on Had Abbadon (Deep Core). Later scenes take place aboard the *Iron Sun*.
- Page numbers are file pages (p1 = cover).
- New in roster: Darth Reave (added during the #30 read). Also present: an unnamed red-and-black horned Zabrak Sith (red lightsaber plus a spiked chain weapon). He isn't named in this issue. #30 p3 names him as **Darth Reave** (added to the roster). Also present: Roan Fel (by hologram) and his blonde bodyguard (unnamed here, sparring with Fel, p10). Named: Princess Sia (Marasiah Fel, p11) and Bastion.
- Muur's balloons are red-bordered, as in #28.
