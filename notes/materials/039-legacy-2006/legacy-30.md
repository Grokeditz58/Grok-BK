# Star Wars: Legacy #30 — "Vector, Part 11 of 12" (Dark Horse, November 2008)

- Story John Ostrander & Jan Duursema · script Ostrander · pencils Jan Duursema · inks Dan Parsons · colors Brad Anderson · letters Michael Heisler · cover Omar Francia ("SKYWALKER BETRAYED!"). 24 pages read (p1 cover, p2 credits/recap, p3–24 story). Set 137 ABY. The issue moves from the Temple of the One Sith on Coruscant to Had Abbadon.
- Page numbers are file pages (p1 = cover).
- New in roster: **Darth Reave**, the horned Zabrak Sith from #29. He is named in the p3 caption. Already in the roster: Krayt, Talon, Stryfe, Wyyrlok, Maladi ("LADY MALADI", p4), Andeddu, and XoXaan. Named in Wyyrlok's history: the Hundred-Year Darkness, Corbos, Leviathans, and Korriban.
- Muur speaks through Celeste with red-bordered balloons. Celeste's own lines have normal balloons.
