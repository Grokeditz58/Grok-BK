## Power statements (ANALYSIS)
- The only Force feat in this issue is Azard's: a telekinetic lift and choke, then an execution with a double-bladed saber (p4). There is no duel. Everything else is fleet strategy, plus Kol's flashback warning that the Force pointed to a trap (p16).
