# Star Wars: Legacy #25 — "The Hidden Temple" (Dark Horse, June 2008)

- Script John Ostrander · art Jan Duursema · inks Dan Parsons · colors Brad Anderson · letters Michael Heisler · cover Sean Cooke ("THE HIDDEN TEMPLE..."). 23 pages read (p1 cover, p2 credits/recap, p3–23 story; p6 is a two-page spread in one file). Set 137 ABY. The Mynock flies to the Hidden Temple on Taivas in the Zhar system.
- Page numbers are file pages (p1 = cover).
- Captions on p3–6 are Bantha Rawk's recorded message to Droo.
- New in roster: Rasi Tuum, Tobias Sun. Also named: Master Mai (p13, no other detail), the Neti T'ra Saa, Tili Qua (lettered "TILI QUA", p13), the dead Master Tholme (p13), and Zho, Rasi's surviving littermate (p19).
