## Power statements (ANALYSIS)
- Azard says he outclasses an Imperial Knight Master: "YOU CANNOT DEFEAT ME. WE BOTH KNOW IT." (p19). Sinde doesn't dispute it and fights only to stall him. The duel isn't resolved on panel. Azard's other feats are sensing the Knights from across the hangar complex (p15) and Force-shielding himself from a fuel-tank explosion (p22).
- Krayt says he draws strength from mass fear (p7). That's a statement about how his dark-side power works, not a feat.
