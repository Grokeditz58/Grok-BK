# Star Wars: Legacy #27 — "Into the Core" (Dark Horse, August 2008)

- Script John Ostrander · art Omar Francia · colors Brad Anderson · letters Michael Heisler · cover Omar Francia ("SITH vs. SITH"). 24 pages read (p1 cover, p2 credits/recap, p3–24 story). Set 137 ABY. The Deep Core and Andeddu's Keep on Prakith, with a flashback to the Sith Temple on Coruscant just after Cade's escape (p4–6).
- Page numbers are file pages (p1 = cover).
- New in roster: Gerlun and Demtri, brothers among the feral "Malevolence" cultists. Gerlun is named on p11, p12, and p16. Demtri is named on p11. Also named: the Malevolence (a group, not added), the planet Prakith, and Prakith City.
