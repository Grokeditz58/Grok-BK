## Front matter (p2)
> "WELCOME TO THE FUTURE . . ." / "After the deaths of Palpatine and Vader, Luke Skywalker's New Jedi Order became the agent of peace in the galaxy. The remnant of the Empire made peace with a new Republic. But peace was shattered by the invasion of the extra-galactic Yuuzhan Vong. The Republic fell, but was replaced by a Galactic Alliance, which allied itself with the Imperial Remnant and others to defeat the alien invaders."
— Star Wars: Legacy #0, p. 2

> "Peace was restored, and the Imperial Remnant once more proclaimed itself the Empire—but with a more benign Emperor on the throne." / "Meanwhile, a new Sith Order was secretly forming on the planet Korriban, infusing themselves with the dark knowledge found in the ancient Sith temples there, honing a plan to retake the galaxy. All that remained in their way were the Jedi . . . especially the one named Skywalker . . ."
— Star Wars: Legacy #0, p. 2

- Note: sidebar dates the setting "approximately 130 years after the Battle of Yavin" (the series proper opens 130 ABY; #50 front matter says ~137 ABY).

## Coruscant & the Ossus Project (pp. 8–9, spread)
> "CORUSCANT" / "Seat of government for the Old Republic, the Empire, and the New Republic." / "Alien invaders, the Yuuzhan Vong, made it their throneworld, (renaming it Yuuzhan'tar), moving its orbit closer to the sun and crushing one of its moons to make their 'rainbow bridge.'" / "The planet was terraformed to accommodate their native vegetation which still uneasily co-exists with the planet's durasteel canyons." / "The Galactic Alliance displaced the Vong and, in turn, have been displaced by the Empire once more."
— Star Wars: Legacy #0, p. 8

> "Though sectors still bear scars of devastation, the Rings of Coruscant remain a popular tourist destination. Tourists are cautioned to remain with their group as wild nexu have been seen among the ruins."
— Star Wars: Legacy #0, p. 8

> "When the Jedi revealed Ossus' transformation, other systems vied for the opportunity to renew planets devastated by the Yuuzhan Vong Invasion."
— Star Wars: Legacy #0, p. 8

> "One hundred planets were chosen for terraforming. The Jedi accompanied the Shapers and, at first, the effects were wondrous to see." / "But things went terribly wrong. Plants became deformed, and painful bony growths—previously seen only in victims of the Vong—sprouted from inhabitants' skin." / "Though the Jedi suspected sabotage, the Yuuzhan Vong were blamed. The Jedi convinced the Galactic Alliance to back the Vong." / "Outraged, the Moffs of the Empire called for retribution and invoked the Treaty of Anaxes." / "The galaxy was again at war."
— Star Wars: Legacy #0, p. 9
