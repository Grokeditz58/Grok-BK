# Star Wars: Legacy #28 — "Vector, Part 9 of 12" (Dark Horse, September 2008)

- Story John Ostrander & Jan Duursema · script Ostrander · pencils Jan Duursema · inks Dan Parsons · colors Brad Anderson · letters Michael Heisler · cover Travis Charest ("WAITING FOR THE END OF THE GALAXY"). 24 pages read (p1 cover, p2 credits/recap, p3–24 story). Crossover with the Vector event. Set 137 ABY aboard the Star Destroyer *Iron Sun* in the Deep Core. The *Mynock* was headed for Had Abbadon.
- Page numbers are file pages (p1 = cover).
- New in roster: none. Celeste Morne, Karness Muur, and Zayne Carrick are already in. Also named: the ISD *Iron Sun*, which went missing ten years earlier (p3), rakghouls, and the Muur Talisman ("THIS TALISMAN I WEAR").
- Karness Muur appears as a red-tinted spirit only Celeste interacts with. His balloons have red borders.
