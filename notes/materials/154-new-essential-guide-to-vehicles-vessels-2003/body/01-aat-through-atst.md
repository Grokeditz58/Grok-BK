# NEGVV 2003 — notes154: AAT → AT-ST (printed 2–13 / scan 024–035)
2003-source paraphrase; quotes only with crop+locator. Typical spread: even=color art+TECHNICAL READOUT; odd=text+schematics.

## Printed 2–3 / S024–025 — AAT (Trade Federation Armored Assault Tank)
- **Readout (P2):** Size **9.75 m** long; Max **55 kph**; Mfr **Baktoid Armor Workshop**; Affil **Trade Federation**.
- Battle tank for Naboo invasion/occupation; heavily armed; thick armor + reinforced nose ram; reactor/power converter aft.
- **Weapons:** Turret primary laser (long-range; tracking acquires small/fast targets incl. Naboo starfighters escaping Theed hangar — callout #1); secondary lasers vs smaller vehicles; short-range antipersonnel blasters; **6 launch tubes** for high-energy / armor-piercing / “bunker buster” shells (≤**55** shells).
- **Crew:** 4 battle droids (pilot, 2 gunners, commander); ≤6 more on external handholds. Commander/pilot: battlefield sensors, comms, secondary controls, stereoscopic cams.
- Tested Outer Rim; vs pirates on **Lok** (Karthakk) before Naboo.
- **Pull-quote:** “Ouch Time…” — Gungan reaction to seeing AATs.
- **Schematics (P3):** 1 Primary laser; 2 Secondary laser blaster; 3 Rangefinder; 4 Control antennas; 5 Launch tubes; 6 Reactor; 7 Armored hull.

## Printed 4–6 / S026–028 — Anakin’s Airspeeder (+ Zam’s on P6)
- **Readout (P4):** Custom-Built **Narglatch XJ-6**; Size **6.23 m**; Max **720 kph**; Mfr Senator Greyshade’s Personal Mechanics; Affil Senator Simon Greyshade (Vorzyd Sector). Yellow open-cockpit two-seater; twin turbojets; electrogravitic gyro flywheels.
- Airspeeders general: ≤~900 km/h; altitudes >250 km. Coruscant air taxis: robust repulsors >3 km hover; range 210 km; EM field vs insects/debris.
- Mod subculture / young Senators led by Greyshade. Anakin “borrowed” XJ-6 to chase Zam Wesell.
- **Anakin quote (P5):** “Oh, you know, Master, I couldn’t find a speeder I really liked, with an open cockpit… And with the right speed capabilities… And then, you know, I had to get a really gonzo color…”
- **Schematics XJ-6 (P5):** 1 Anti-collision scanner; 2 Comm antenna; 3 Thrust outlet; 4 Turbofan; 5 Twin turbojet engines; 6 Narrowbeam headlight; 7 Open cockpit (enabled catching falling Obi-Wan & leaping to Zam’s vehicle).
- **Zam’s Koro-2 Exodrive (P6):** Desler Gizh Outworld Mobility Corp.; wasteland/thin-corrosive atmospheres; self-contained life support; repulsor + EM generators in forward mandibles charge air for thrust. Stolen from Mining Guild Outer Rim colony; planned concealed blaster (not fitted); crashed Coruscant street → stripped by thieves.
- **Zam quote:** “My speeder might be built like a dung beetle, but it could survive an acid storm on Mordis VI.”
- **Schematics Zam (P6):** 1 Air intake; 2 Power systems; 3 Heat exchanges; 4 Electromagnet propulsion; 5 Radiators; 6 Canopy (auto-tints to conceal identity).

## Printed 8–9 / S030–031 — AT-AT (Imperial All Terrain Armored Transport)
- **Readout (P8):** **15 m** tall, **20 m** long; **60 kph** flat; **Kuat Drive Yards**; Galactic Empire.
- Lineage Clone Wars AT-TE/AT-PT; Veers resurrected for Imperial Army / Blizzard Force. Secret KDY plants Kuat, Carida, Belderone.
- Capacity: **40** stormtroopers, **5** speeder bikes, heavy weapons; or **2 AT-STs**. Crew in head: pilot, gunner, commander; 360° holographic targeting.
- Weapons: long-range heavy lasers + short-range medium blasters. Two drive motors; thick armor vs conventional weapons. Variant “dune walkers” with Sienar z23 heat dissipation for hot worlds.
- **Soft spot (callout 7):** flexible neck — Wedge tow-cable topple at Hoth then shot unarmored neck.
- **Quote (Bren Derlin):** “We had the Battle of Gormen won, until the AT-ATs arrived. They came out of the fog and ripped apart the front lines. The locals ran in terror, but the experienced soldiers surrendered. We knew that you can’t outrun an AT-AT.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Heavy laser; 2 Medium blaster; 3 Sensor array; 4 Drive motor; 5 Armor plating; 6 Escape hatch; 7 Soft spot (neck).

## Printed 10–11 / S032–033 — AT-PT (Republic All Terrain Personal Transport)
- **Readout (P10):** **3 m** tall; **60 kph** flat; **Rothana Heavy Engineering (KDY)**; Old Republic.
- Clone Wars mobile weapons platform for high/low gravity or magnetic fields where repulsors fail. Single trooper; twin blasters + concussion grenade launcher; legs climb **45°**.
- Experimental fleet mostly lost with *Katana*; design philosophy → AT-AT/AT-ST. NR recovered some vs Thrawn.
- **Quote (Brenn Tantor):** “Sure, they don’t look like much. But when you’re trapped on Eos, where the electromagnetic fields keep ‘advanced’ airspeeders grounded, you’ll wish you had a whole fleet of AT-PTs.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Twin blaster; 2 Concussion-grenade launcher; 3 Comm antenna; 4 Main drive joint; 5 Cooling vents; 6 Emergency flare (retrieval when disabled/separated).

## Printed 12–13 / S034–035 — AT-ST (Imperial All Terrain Scout Transport)
- **Readout (P12):** **8.6 m** tall; **90 kph**; Imperial Department of Military Research; Galactic Empire.
- Recon/support; descendant of AT-PT via Clone Wars **AT-XT** prototype. Head rotates ~**240°**; pilot+gunner. Vulnerable gyro under command module; poor on steep/dense foliage (Ewoks/sharpshooters).
- Variant **AT-ST/A** assault: ~10 m; heavy blaster; reinforced leg braces.
- **Quote (Imperial manual post-Endor):** “AT-STs will no longer be deployed on planets with an abundance of trees or other known obstacles such as rock-wielding primitives.”
- **Hatch callout:** Chewbacca + Ewoks used Endor trees to drop onto hatch from above.
- **Schematics:** 1 Light blaster; 2 Concussion grenade launcher; 3 Twin chin blasters; 4 Drive engine; 5 Sensor pod; 6 Armor plate; 7 Hatch.
