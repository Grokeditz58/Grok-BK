# NEGVV 2003 — notes154: Other Vehicles Chu'unthor → Speeder Bus (printed 190–195 / scan 212–217)

## Printed 190 / S212 — Chu'unthor through Guardian Mantis
- **Art:** Hapan Battle Dragon (two stacked saucer discs with connecting pylons; labeled on the art).
- **Chu'unthor:** massive cruiser; mobile Jedi training academy. Built about **400 years** before Yavin; eventually vanished with nearly **10,000** Jedi aboard. Luke later discovered it had crashed on Dathomir, home to Force-sensitive “Witches” descended from a Jedi exile.
- **Combat Cloud Car:** armed cloud car in the niche between standard airspeeders and space-capable starfighters. Max altitude **100 km**.
- **Coral Vanda:** subocean liner / pleasure cruiser in the oceans of Pantolomin. **8** decadent casinos, **16** luxury suites, cabins for **600** passengers, and **4** “adventure rooms” using holography to re-create exotic locales. Han Solo and Lando boarded it while searching for the lost *Katana* fleet during the Thrawn conflict.
- **Dreadnaught:** massive ancient heavy cruisers of the Old Republic. Fairly weak vs Star Destroyers, but remain active because they can be refitted. Modified Dreadnaughts used by both Empire and Alliance in the GCW; Rebels focused on increasing speed and maneuverability.
- **Enforcer One:** Bogga the Hutt’s flagship, in use thousands of years before Naboo. Notable fixed heavy turbolaser with a dedicated power core, advanced cooling, and a long barrel fitted with experimental galven circuitry for a tightly focused, tremendous-range beam.
- **Flurry:** carrier donated to the Alliance by sympathetic Virgillians; destroyed by the Imperial Carrack-class cruiser *Dominant* over Bakura. All hands lost.
- **Guardian Mantis:** starfighter piloted by mercenary Vana Sage. Stealth capabilities, tracking devices, modular weapons. Vana’s R2 hardwired into the ship; torpedo launchers fired small nano-missiles.

## Printed 191 / S213 — Hapan Battle Dragon through I'friil Ma-Nat
- **Hapan Battle Dragon:** saucer-shaped Hapes Cluster starships; each about **500 m** in diameter; nearly **100** weapons emplacements. Hapans also deploy pulse-mass mines that simulate a mass shadow in space to prevent hyperspace jumps.
- **Hapes Nova Battle Cruiser:** **400 m** patrol ship in the Hapes Cluster; designed for swift, brutal attacks. Prince Isolder, seeking Leia’s hand, once offered Han Solo a Nova-class battle cruiser to cease his own pursuit of Leia; Han declined.
- **Hell's Anvil:** dangerous starfighter of bounty hunter and ex-Mandalorian Montross. Solar ionization cannons ignored deflector shields and could melt durasteel. Used in a failed attempt to kill Jango Fett around the Battle of Naboo. (Bottom art: *Hell's Anvil*, rust-orange angular fighter with spherical wingtip pods.)
- **Heyblibber:** luxury Gungan bongo. Jar Jar Binks crashed Boss Nass’s heyblibber shortly before being exiled from Otoh Gunga.
- **Hornet Interceptor:** perfectly aerodynamic ship favored by pirates, smugglers, and other criminals. Thin daggerlike body with insectlike wings for atmospheric flight; more maneuverable than an X-wing.
- **Hutt Caravel:** short-range space transport used by Hutt crimelords, most often between Nal Hutta and Nar Shaddaa.
- **Hyperspace Marauder:** converted freighter owned by smuggler Lo Khan, who often allows the slow unarmed vehicle to be overtaken by pirates, then uses the *Marauder*’s computer to take control of the enemy ship’s systems for his personal use.
- **I'friil Ma-Nat (Yuuzhan Vong Corvette):** YV analogs to the Corellian corvette; roughly pyramidlike; jet-black yorik coral. Lightly armed; typically strike quickly and retreat to fleet safety before enemies regroup. Two participated in a battle at Ord Mantell.

## Printed 192 / S214 — Incom Y-4 Raptor through Marauder Corvette (start)
- **Incom Y-4 Raptor Transport:** military transport shuttle used extensively by Warlord Zsinj. Ferry troops and supplies between starships or bases; allowed quiet, quick infiltration of planetary defenses for surgical strikes.
- **Ithullan Ore Hauler:** ancient vehicle from the carapaces of kilometer-long Ithullan colossus wasps; transported precious mutonium ore. Main weapons: a pair of heavy turbolasers in the forward section of the wasp’s chest.
- **Jabba's Space Cruiser:** customized Ubrikkian starship for Jabba’s “personal business trips”; hidden gunports as an unpleasant surprise for would-be pirates.
- **Jade's Fire:** Mara Jade Skywalker’s first shuttle. Destroyed when Mara intentionally crashed it into the *Hand of Thrawn*’s docking bay on Nirauan. Unique “shoot-back system”: when laserfire hit the hull, a small turret blaster automatically fired in the general direction of incoming shots. Replicated on her current ship, *Jade Shadow*.
- **Jade Sabre:** Mara’s second shuttle, built for her by Luke. Green; inertial compensator, etheric rudder, and expanded docking bay to carry Luke’s XJ X-wing. Yuuzhan Vong discovered it on Dantooine and destroyed it.
- **Jaster's Legacy:** Jango Fett’s first personal starship, named for his Mandalorian mentor. Lost on Oovo IV; he then stole a Firespray-class patrol ship that became *Slave I*. (Bottom art: *Jaster's Legacy*, tan/red hull, two large circular intakes with red X markings; “JANGO” on the nose.)
- **Lancer Frigate:** **250 m** Imperial capital ship designed to combat Rebel starfighters. **20** tower-mounted quad laser cannons; little defense against other combat starships. Five years after Palpatine’s death, Thrawn used Lancer-class frigates as a major component of Imperial raiding missions.
- **Legacy of Torment:** YV vessel destroyed at Ithor. Like the kor chokk (another YV capital / “grand cruiser”), roughly the size of a Super Star Destroyer.
- **Marauder Corvette:** capital ship used extensively by the CSA’s Picket Fleet. **195 m**; streamlined for atmospheric combat. Text breaks: “A stan-”

## Printed 193 / S215 — Marauder Corvette end through Nebulon Ranger
- Continuation: “dard Marauder carries eight double turbolasers and three tractor beam projectors, twelve fighters for long-range assault and patrol missions, and two platoons of forty troopers.”
- **Marauder Starjacker:** ore-raiding ship of pirate captain Finhead Stonebone some **4,000 years** before the GCW. Resembled an insect, about **100 m** long.
- **Miy'til Assault Bomber:** powerful bomber used exclusively by the Hapes Consortium. Faster and more agile than the Empire’s TIE bomber. Reserved for major offensives; few outside the Hapan Cluster understand its capabilities.
- **Miy'til Fighter:** Hapes Consortium’s fast, maneuverable patrol/attack starfighter. Hyperdrive, two fire-linked laser cannons, and a small concussion missile tube; frequently deployed for antipirate missions.
- **Moldy Crow:** starship used by Rebel operative Jan Ors and Kyle Katarn during the Dark Trooper investigation. Jan often piloted it to rescue Kyle from Imperial forces.
- **Mon Remonda:** MC80B Mon Calamari star cruiser delivered to the NR about **18 months** after Endor. Major component in the battle to retake Coruscant; also flagship of an NR fleet sent to repel Warlord Zsinj.
- **MorningStar:** tri-winged starfighter often used by mercenaries in the Naboo system.
- **MT-AT:** Imperial walker designed to navigate steep inclines with independently articulated legs and clawed footpads. Eight-legged design earned the nickname “Spider Walker.”
- **Naboo Bomber:** prototype seldom used by the Naboo Royal Security Forces. Combines deadly Nubian technology with Naboo spaceframe design; heavily armored; devastating energy bombs for air-to-ground combat.
- **Nebulon Ranger:** large courier used by Jedi brothers Ulic and Cay Qel-Droma and Twi'lek Jedi Tott Doneeta, **4,000 years** before the rise of the Emperor. Retractable wings extended for atmospheric flight.
- **Art:** *Legacy of Torment* — dark organic mass with many hanging spires, kor-chokk-like.

## Printed 194 / S216 — Neimoidian Shuttle through Shieldship
- **Neimoidian Shuttle:** transport used by Neimoidian TF officials such as Nute Gunray and Rune Haako. Vaguely resembles a large insect; pointed landing claws; does not require a pilot. Gunray’s shuttle was a Sheathipede-class vessel called the *Lapiz Cutter*. (Art: tan insectoid shuttle with a tall fin, labeled Neimoidian Shuttle.)
- **Phantom TIE:** secret Imperial weapon; tri-winged TIE fighter with cloaking ability. Prototype destroyed by Rebel agents.
- **Phoenix Hawk Light Pinnace:** one of Kuat Systems Engineering’s first starships, for a small group of passengers on long-distance trips. Affordable and moderately armed but cramped and ugly; has virtually disappeared in the NR era.
- **Republic Interceptor TX-130S Fighter Tank:** repulsor craft used extensively by Jedi generals in the Clone Wars. Developed to combat CIS droid armies and lead Republic ground troops. Fast and agile, enabling Jedi to reach any battlefield area quickly; also an effective front-line combat vehicle.
- **Ro'ik Chuun M'arh:** YV frigate analog **200–500 m**. Size varies because they fulfill many roles (cargo, combat support, even long-range recon). Five participated in the Battle of Ord Mantell. Boarding skiffs capable of carrying up to **100** warriors.
- **Sabaoth Fighter:** deadly starfighters of Captain Cavik Toth’s mercenary army around Geonosis. Jedi Master Adi Gallia tested her prototype Jedi starfighter against sabaoth fighters in the Karthakk system.
- **Scarab:** Trade Federation starfighters used in a plot to explore and conquer lawless Outer Rim worlds.
- **Scimitar Assault Bomber:** Imperial bomber used by Thrawn’s forces. Dedicated atmospheric and space bomber with better performance than a standard TIE bomber. Designed partly by members of the elite, highly decorated Scimitar bomber assault wing.
- **Shieldship:** custom-built escort protecting ships traveling to Nkllon in the Athega system. The system’s sun emits dangerous rays that destroy any vessel not protected by a shieldship. Lando later converted shieldships into war machines to battle the Yuuzhan Vong.

## Printed 195 / S217 — Skipray Blastboat through Speeder Bus / Sith / Sh'rip / Shriwirr
- Layout is not strictly left-to-right alphabetical (left: Sith Meditation Sphere, Sh'rip Sh'pa, Shriwirr; right: Skipray, Slave II, Speeder Bus).
- **Skipray Blastboat:** Imperial assault gunship. Larger and more powerful than starfighters, still small enough to be carried aboard capital ships. **25 m**; inordinate weapons for its size: three medium ion cannons, a proton torpedo launcher, two laser cannons, and a concussion missile launcher.
- **Slave II:** Boba Fett’s primary vessel after escaping the Sarlacc on Tatooine. *Pursuer*-class patrol starship that, decades earlier, had been popular with Mandalorian mercenaries. After *Slave II* was damaged over Byss, Fett recovered *Slave I* from a New Republic impound. (Bottom art: *Slave II*, angular gray-green wedge with forward guns and an angled tail.)
- **Speeder Bus:** large repulsorlift transports in Coruscant’s skylanes. Largest can carry up to **1,000** passengers.
- **Sith Meditation Sphere:** large round starship used by ancient Sith warlords to focus Sith “battle meditation” and other abilities. Naga Sadow’s sphere, about **5,000 years** before Yavin, appeared as a giant floating eye with batlike wings.
- **Sh'rip Sh'pa (Yuuzhan Vong Spawn Ship):** large YV cruiser carrying supplies necessary for terraforming captured planets. Enormous faceted polyhedron with onyx skin. (Top art: crystalline/faceted white-silver cluster labeled Sh'rip Sh'pa.)
- **Shriwirr:** large customized Ssi-ruuvi battle cruiser; flagship during the Ssi-ruuk attack on Bakura. Oval; warship and transport carrying about **500** battle droids. Contained a disturbing “entechment lab” that allowed the Ssi-ruuk to steal living energy from prisoners to power their equipment.
