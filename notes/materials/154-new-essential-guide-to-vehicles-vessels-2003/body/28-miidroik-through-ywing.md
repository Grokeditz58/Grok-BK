# NEGVV 2003 — notes154: Miid Ro'ik → Y-wing (printed 172–177 / scan 194–199)

## Printed 172–173 / S194–195 — Yuuzhan Vong Warship / Miid Ro'ik
- **Art title:** YUUZHAN VONG WARSHIP; **text:** Yuuzhan Vong Miid Ro'ik
- **Readout (S194, visual):** **1,600 m** long (average); **2,340 G / Class 3 hyperdrive (equivalent)**; Yuuzhan Vong; Yuuzhan Vong.
- Most powerful in YV arsenal; analog to ISD. Command/blockade/assault. Yorik coral core; dovin-basal propulsion. Unique recycle: dying coralskipper / kor chokk / worldship fed to miid ro'ik → fuel for magma weapons + viscous secretions harden into glossy black armored plates (reflect blasterfire; withstand collisions). Magma weapons in hull crags; typical supports up to **60**. Many hundred dovin basals (faster than other YV ships; command mobility). Perimeter: unified gravity well prevents hyperspace retreats. **Dread weapon:** retractable serpentine appendage several times longer than the warship; rough dark-flecked skin; end opening creates vacuum that can consume small starships or individuals; used on populated areas when hungry; also convoys troops between ships or onto a planet. Midsize transports: nearly **3,000** troops + vehicles; carrier via long coral arms (deep red / dark blue from nose, spine, posterior) for hundreds of coralskippers.
- **Quote (YV shaper):** “From the dead, we grant life.”
- **Callout 5:** “Dovin Basal: Arguably the most powerful and important component of any Yuuzhan Vong starship, the dovin basal both seeks out and projects gravity wells.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Armored Plate; 2 Plasma Projector; 3 Coral Arms; 4 Coralskippers; 5 Dovin Basal.

## Printed 174–175 / S196–197 — Yuuzhan Vong Worldship / Koros-Strohna
- **Art title:** YUUZHAN VONG WORLDSHIP; **text:** Yuuzhan Vong Koros-Strohna
- **Readout (S196, visual):** **10 km** long; **1,100 G / Class 1.5 hyperdrive (equivalent)**; Yuuzhan Vong; Yuuzhan Vong.
- Staging ground / Super Star Destroyer analog: transport + battleship + psychological weapon; carries entire YV population (refugees from destroyed homeworld seeking new planets). Single yorik-coral mass ~10 km; symbiotic organics for weapons/propulsion/defense. Dozens of dovin basals **1–3 m** diameter; lock onto gravity fields at any distance. Weak-gravity / intergalactic: membranous **outrider ganglia** tendrils, unfurled by piloted coralskippers, serve as cosmic sails. Hundreds of magma weapons (small = blaster-cannon analog; large shoot burning rocks the size of small freighters); spaced sporadically, recharge slowly, but “incredibly accurate.” Dovin basals as weapons: collapse a space station or force a moon to collide with its orbital partner; strip conventional shields; intercept incoming torps. Each worldship: **>5,000** warriors + coralskippers. Rudimentary consciousness; communicate with pilots. Lifespan **well over 500 years**; grow with age. **Baanu Miir**, largest yet encountered by the NR, **more than 1,000 years old**. Dying ships: color variations in dovin basals and hallway **myogens**.
- **Quote (anon YV warrior):** “The Koros-Strohna is all I've ever known. I have lived within the ship my entire life.”
- **Callout 6:** “Gorros' Fen: The worldship carries a gigantic, tubular worm that can extend from the vessel to a planet's surface in order to collect nutrients for the worldship.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Outrider Ganglia; 2 Coralskippers; 3 Plasma Projectors; 4 Dovin Basals; 5 Primary Cargo Hold; 6 Gorros' Fen.

## Printed 176–177 / S198–199 — Y-wing (Koensayr BTL-S3 Y-Wing Attack Starfighter)
- **Readout (S198, visual):** **16 m** long; **2,700 G / Class 1 / 1,000 kph** (print font can look like “2,700S”); Koensayr; Rebel Alliance.
- Pre-X-wing Alliance primary attack snubfighter (triangular cockpit + two engine pylons). Outdated by Yavin but durability + heavy firepower kept it in the NR fleet into the YV invasion. Originally close-quarters vs stations/capitals; also light bombing / surgical strikes; escort / recon. Modular arms: most Rebel Y-wings = pair of lasers + rotating ion; can take proton torps, concussion missiles, proton bombs. R2 or R4 in socket: flight/nav/power, fire control, in-flight maintenance, power reroute, several hyperspace jump coords. Siege of **Ank Ki'Shor**: held TIE bombers at bay. Could not be sole fighter (payload/speed/stealth/maneuver vs Imperials; TIE interceptors). After X-wing: soften SD targets before capitals. Easily modified: strip armor/generators for convoy assaults, or add stronger shields + payload for bombing. Diplomatic escort / long-range: **BTL-A4 Y-wing (LP) / Longprobe** — extra provisions, stronger sensors, sophisticated nav computer. Extended-cockpit version supports a copilot/gunner. Some versions: detachable cockpit as emergency escape pod.
- **Quote (Tiree, before Yavin):** “They're the workhorses of this outfit, I can tell you that.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Laser Cannon; 2 Ion Cannon; 3 Proton-Torpedo Launcher; 4 Sensor Array; 5 Ion Jet Engine; 6 Power Generator; 7 Detachable Cockpit.
