# NEGVV 2003 — notes154: Star Destroyer → Swoop Classification (printed 142–147 / scan 164–169)

## Printed 142–143 / S164–165 — Star Destroyer (KDY Imperial I-class)
- **Readout:** **1,600 m**; **2,300 G / Class 2**; Kuat Drive Yards; Galactic Empire.
- KDY + Rendili aid late Old Republic. Predecessor Victory-class (900 m). Arms: 60 Taim & Bak XX-9 TL; 60 Borstel NK-7 ion; 10 Phylon Q7 tractor. Two deflector domes on bridge. Sienar I-a2b solar ionization reactor (ventral shielded dome). Complement: 9,700 soldiers; 72 TIE; 20 AT-AT; 30 AT-ST; support craft; prefab garrison. Later: Imperial II (post-Yavin); Super SD; NR Defender-class (REC; smaller/faster; first *Obi-Wan*). Hangar ≤150 m vessels (e.g. *Tantive IV*).
- **Quote (Lando, Endor):** “Yes! I said *closer*! Move as close as you can and engage those Star Destroyers at point-blank range... We'll last longer than we will against that Death Star, and we might just take a few of them with us!”
- **Schematics:** 1 Deflector shield generator dome; 2 Comm tower; 3 Solar ionization reactor; 4 Ion engine; 5 Targeting system; 6 Central hangar.

## Printed 144–146 / S166–168 — Swoop Bikes (TaggeCo. Air-2 + variants; Mobquet Flare-S)
- **Readout (art):** **3 m**; **600 kph**; Mobquet Swoops and Speeders; None.
- “Repulsorlift engines with seats”; Han champion youth; outlaw gangs Tatooine. >600 kph; hard to control; almost no armor. Air-2: alloy frame + ion + RL; hand-grips/knee/foot pegs + body lean. Mods: waterproof (Bestine); sand filters (Tatooine); rare sealed canopies. Theory: Taris ~4,000 BBY gambling. Gangs: Dark Star Hellions, Nova Demons, Bloodsniffers; Shesharile Raging Banthas/Spiders/Rabid Mynocks. Aduba-3 Cloud Rider “skyspeeders” (Aratech Peregrine 240 + Mobquet Nebulo-Q). Owen Lars Mobquet Zephyr-G (350 kph; Anakin tracked Tuskens). Flare-S hybrid; Dengar crash vs Han Agrilat → bounty hunter. Combat: forward armor; rare shields (<350 kph); Aurra Sing skull shield; Durge energy lance; NR experiments (targeting/weapons/droid — costly/slow).
- **Quote (Han to Anakin Solo):** “Swoop jockeys have the brains of a blister gnat—and about the same life expectancy.”
- **Schematics (Air-2):** 1 Leg shield; 2 Main battery; 3 Ion/RL engines; 4 Cowling; 5 Instrument readout; 6 Engine intake (rontos startle).
- **Schematics (Flare-S):** 1 Turbothrusters; 2 RL engine; 3 Handlebar controllers; 4 Accelerator pedal; 5 Steering vanes; 6 Central repulsor pod.
