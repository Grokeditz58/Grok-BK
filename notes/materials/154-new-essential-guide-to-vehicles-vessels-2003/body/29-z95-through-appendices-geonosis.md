## Printed 178–179 / S200–201 — Z-95 Headhunter (Incom/Subpro Z-95-AF4)
- **Readout (S200, visual):** **11.8 m**; **2,180 G / 1,150 kph**; Incom Corporation / Subpro Corporation; **Affiliation: None**.
- Oldest snubfighter still used by the NR; predates Yavin by several decades; several revivals; legendary resilience; mercenaries / security. Original **Z-95 Mark I** marketed as atmospheric fighter, easily modified for space: twin engines + two swing-wings; bubble canopy (like later X-wing); initially unarmed; outlaw/police popularity → triple blasters on each wing. Mark I not actually suitable for space combat despite Incom claims. Later variants: fixed wings + rear-mounted maneuvering jets; transparisteel canopies; improved tactical displays; aftermarket weapon/engine kits. Military-grade **Z-95-AF4**: Incom 2a fission engines; Taim & Bak KX5 lasers; pair of Krupx MG5 concussion missile launchers — on par with Y-wing destructive capability. Rebels converted some for short-range bombing; also training (controls nearly identical to X-wing and Y-wing). Mara Jade: modified Headhunter with hyperdrive. Han Solo (Corporate Sector days): Mark I vs CSA fighters; atmospheric capabilities exceeded most conventional starfighters including Authority **IRD** fighters.
- **Quote (Outlaw mechanic Doc):** “As long as mechanics keep finding ways to improve this baby, the Headhunter will never be outdated.”
- **Callout 7:** “Maneuvering Jets: The maneuvering jets provide the Headhunter with surprising maneuverability during atmospheric combat.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Laser Cannon; 2 Concussion Missile Launcher; 3 Power Generator; 4 Ion Engine; 5 Combat Targeting Computers; 6 Sensor Array; 7 Maneuvering Jets.

## Printed 180 / S202 — APPENDICES: A Short History of War (opens)
- Hyperdrive invented **more than 25,000 years** before the YV invasion = turning point. Pre-HD: isolated worlds, no galactic community. HD let Core systems build massive starships, first contact, “single benevolent government,” Republic born.
- Early Republic not peaceful; fleet “spread out the far corners” (print as written; “to” omitted) met warlike civilizations → armed warships. Early innovations: turbolasers, ion cannons, proton torpedoes. Proliferation increased violence.
- Later peace/prosperity; only small Outer Rim / non-Republic conflicts; tech mostly noncombat until major emergencies (Sith armies).
- Sith War → Clone Wars → Galactic Civil War → YV invasion: each conflict spurred vehicle tech/tactics. “For every superweapon there must be a countermeasure; for every Imperial starfighter, a New Republic counterpart.”
- Frame: **five key conflicts** from recent history — tech innovations + vehicles that turned the tide.

### The Battle of Geonosis
- **Date:** 22 B.B.Y. **Factions:** CIS vs. Old Republic. **Key Vehicles:** Confederacy homing droids, hailfires, and droid tanks; Republic gunships, AT-TEs, and SPHA-Ts.
- Pre-battle: systems dissatisfied with Republic; ex-Jedi Count Dooku’s separatist movement; Geonosis foundries for weapons/vehicles; Commerce Guild and others; CIS founded at a Geonosis meeting.
- Republic discovered clone army on Kamino, “Allegedly ordered by a mysterious Jedi,” fully trained and equipped. Situation critical → Republic assumed control and attacked; start of the Clone Wars.
- Primarily a ground campaign: sudden overwhelming assault. Military assault ships penetrated upper atmosphere; gunships dropped thousands of clone troopers + dozens of AT-TE walkers. Advanced on Geonosian arena (surrounded Jedi strike team), then attacked TF core ships.
- CIS + Geonosians refused to surrender. Geonosian starfighters vs clones; AT-TEs, gunships, and a handful of Jedi starfighters kept them at bay. Heavy platforms (tank droids, hailfires, homing droids) slowed clones; Jedi flanked slow droids. Recon troopers on speeder bikes gathered intel.
- CIS tried to launch several core ships (untold battle droids/weapons/vehicles). Clone scouts warned Yoda and Jedi. Every core ship = a small army; priority targets. Clones repositioned self-propelled heavy artillery with advanced turbolasers (needed constant gunship/AT-TE protection); downed at least one behemoth core ship, crippling the CIS army.
- Heavy Republic toll: numerous Jedi + legions of clone troopers dead. Confederacy war effort damaged, but Separatists galvanized; Clone Wars escalated quickly.
