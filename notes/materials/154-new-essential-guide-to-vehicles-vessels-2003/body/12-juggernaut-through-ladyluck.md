# NEGVV 2003 — notes154: Juggernaut → Lady Luck (printed 78–83 / scan 100–105)

## Printed 78–79 / S100–101 — Juggernaut (KDY HAVw Juggernaut Troop Carrier)
- **Readout:** **15 m** tall / **22 m** long; **200 kph**; Kuat Drive Yards; Old Republic.
- Clone Wars heavy assault for demilitarized systems vs CIS. Larger wheeled AT-TE analog; 10 drive wheels; flexible shocks; rocky terrain; 200 km/h but slow to 25 for turns; F+R command cabins (full reverse); 1 driver/cabin. 50 troops; reconfigurable for bikes/assault speeders. Arms: 3 rotating lasers, turret blaster (AA), 2 retractable concussion grenade launchers (10 ea). Nicknamed “rolling slab”; eclipsed by AT-AT; few left by YV invasion (parts/maintenance).
- **Quote (Imperial machinist Dathomir log):** “DAY 17: Forward drive shaft on juggernaut-11 has snapped… DAY 123: Awaiting drive shaft.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Laser cannons; 2 Medium blaster cannon; 3 Concussion-grenade bay; 4 Comm antenna; 5 Fuel slug chamber; 6 Sentry tower (wide FOV / sharpshooter target).

## Printed 80–81 / S102–103 — K-Wing (Koensayr BTL-S8 K-Wing Assault Starfighter)
- **Readout:** **16 m**; **2,100 G / 1,000 kph**; Koensayr; New Republic.
- Heavy bombardment (planets/stations/slow capital ships); Y-wing descendant; Black Fleet Crisis 16 ABY debut. Two maneuvering wings + fixed stabilizer; detachable command module = escape pod. Y-wing-class sublight + secondary ion burst; **no hyperdrive** (carrier *Endurance*). 18 hardpoints (concussion/flechette/sublight torps/thermal/mines); then short-range quad turbolaser + medium twin laser on command module. Pilot + dedicated gunner in separate cockpits. Fifth Fleet flechettes vs hypervelocity gun Bessimir training.
- **Quote (Miranda Doni, pre-death Doornik-319):** “We’re the heavy hitters. When you need a command bridge leveled or a convoy of tanks wiped out, the K-wings get the call.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Twin lasers; 2 Flechette launchers; 3 Concussion launchers; 4 Quad laser; 5 Proton torpedo launcher; 6 Ion engines.

## Printed 82–83 / S104–105 — Lady Luck (Modified SoroSuub Personal Luxury Yacht 3000)
- **Readout:** **50 m**; **2,200 G / Class 1 / 820 kph**; SoroSuub; Lando Calrissian.
- Bought from Orthellin royal mistress post-Endor; combat-converted. 5 retractable lasers + small ion turret; Chempat-6 shield pair; sensors; 3 false-ID transponder (alias “Stardream”); droid brain emergency/remote via belt. Luxury: observation deck + exterior (force-field vacuum), private suites/rare art, Conform couches, jet-stream meditation pool, crystal garden; Corellian leather escape pods.
- **Quote (Lando):** “Her name says all you need to know. The Lady Luck’s fickle, but when she’s smiling down on you, you’re bound to win. She hasn’t given up on me yet.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Laser cannon; 2 Ion engine; 3 Hyperdrive; 4 Long-range sensor transceiver; 5 Sensor array; 6 Comm transceiver; 7 Exterior observation deck.
