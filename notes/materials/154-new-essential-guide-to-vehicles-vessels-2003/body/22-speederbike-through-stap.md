# NEGVV 2003 — notes154: Speeder Bike → STAP (printed 136–141 / scan 158–163)

## Printed 136–137 / S158–159 — Speeder Bike (Aratech 74-Z Military Speeder Bike)
- **Readout:** **4.4 m**; **500 kph**; Aratech Repulsor Company; Galactic Empire.
- Clone army Geonosis recon/coordination; Imperial scout-trooper signature. Preferred over 64-Y Swift 3 / Mobquet TrailMaker III / Ikas-Adno Starhawk for reliability + weeks-long independent range. Handlebars + foot pedals; 4 directional steering vanes on 2 outriggers (sever → spin/crash; Luke Endor lightsaber exploit).
- **Quote (Rebel Endor joke):** “What’s the last thing to go through an Imperial scout trooper’s head when he hits a tree? His afterburner.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Blaster cannon; 2 Control panel; 3 Steering vane; 4 Repulsorlift engine; 5 Power cell; 6 Brake pedals.

## Printed 138–139 / S160–161 — SPHA-T (RHE Self-Propelled Heavy Artillery-Turbolaser)
- **Readout:** **20.6 m** tall; **35 kph**; Rothana Heavy Engineering; Old Republic.
- Geonosis debut vs TF core ships. Fixed turbolaser (vehicle turns to aim) + gargantuan reactor; locomotive + weapon; 4 stabilizing struts when firing. Crew 15 clones; 12 retractable antipersonnel blasters (10 gunners); +20 passengers. Focusing dish tech scaled → Death Star superlaser.
- **Quote (Mace Windu):** “Using this weapon against droids is a necessary evil. But what if such destructive power should be turned against living creatures? A sad thought, indeed.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Turbolaser; 2 Focusing dish; 3 Antipersonnel blaster; 4 Targeting scanners; 5 Drive power generator.

## Printed 140–141 / S162–163 — STAP (Baktoid Single Trooper Aerial Platform)
- **Readout:** **2 m** tall; **400 kph**; Baktoid Armor Workshop; Trade Federation.
- Military airhook (Longspur & Alloi civilian origin). Rounded control + long airfoil; RL bottom (max alt 25 m); 2 drive turbines. Twin lethal blasters + light armor (vs civilian stun). Battle-droid only; high-voltage cells frequent recharge. Heavy STAP (Naboo era): larger lasers + missile, less agile. Historic airfoils: Empress Teta 5,000 BBY; Jedi Ruusan; Rebels Dantooine (abandoned for Hoth). Qui-Gon deflects bolts destroy attacker.
- **Quote (Tobias Pall, Naboo RSF):** “We could have stayed hidden in the swamps forever, if not for those accursed STAPs. They rooted us out of the trees like Gungans searching for gumbols.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Twin blasters; 2 Drive turbine; 3 Antigravity repulsor projector; 4 Blaster power supply; 5 Power cell housing.
