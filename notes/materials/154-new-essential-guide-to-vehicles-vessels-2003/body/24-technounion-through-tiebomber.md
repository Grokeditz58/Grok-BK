# NEGVV 2003 — notes154: Techno Union Starship → TIE Bomber (printed 148–153 / scan 170–175)

## Printed 148–149 / S170–171 — Techno Union Starship (Hardcell-class Interstellar Transport)
- **Readout:** **220 m** tall; **400 G / Class 1**; Techno Union; Confederacy of Independent Systems.
- Dual-purpose; Skakoan pressurized cabins. Rocket-shaped; 6 loud fuel-inefficient eco-damaging thrusters; status symbol. Wat Tambor personalized: powerful deflectors; internal bug scanners; advanced HD (hyperspace calc in seconds) → Geonosis escape. Modular engine-ring reconfiguration.
- **Quote (TU memo):** “The Hardcell will get you noticed as you travel the space-lanes. Even Corellian cruisers will get out of your way. And if they don’t, you can run them over.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Thruster; 2 Fuel slugs; 3 Hyperdrive; 4 Command module; 5 Primary sensor array; 6 Shield generator; 7 Engine ring.

## Printed 150–151 / S172–173 — TIE Advanced (Darth Vader’s TIE / SFS TIE Advanced x1 Prototype)
- **Readout:** **9.2 m**; **4,150 G / Class 4 / 1,200 kph**; Sienar Fleet Systems; Galactic Empire.
- Vader Yavin prototype; HD + deflectors; template for TIE interceptor. Rounded cockpit; bent solar wings; durasteel; elongated rear; twin ions + stronger solar ionization reactor. Only slightly faster than std TIE (heavy armor + power-hungry shields). Slow HD; limited life support. Repaired post-Yavin → *Executor*; destroyed Endor when SSD hit DS2. Few produced (cost); Lando owned modified (likely last) by YV era. Survived *Falcon* quad brush at Yavin via shields.
- **Quote (Vader):** “I have you now.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Laser cannon; 2 Sensor array; 3 Transparisteel viewport; 4 Solar array wings; 5 Deflector shield generator.

## Printed 152–153 / S174–175 — TIE Bomber (SFS)
- **Readout:** **7.8 m**; **2,380 G / 850 kph**; Sienar Fleet Systems; Galactic Empire.
- Heavy assault surface/deep-space. Bent wings + dual hull (cockpit + ordnance pod). 2 forward lasers; pod: proton torps / guided concussion / orbital mines / thermal dets. Dedicated life support (no sealed suit). Capital targeting (shields/engines) + precision ground. Post-Palpatine largely replaced by Scimitar assault bomber. Proton bombs dropped on Hoth asteroids hunting *Falcon*.
- **Quote (Tigran Jamiro):** “When the TIE bombers first started shelling Yavin 4, I thought the planet would crack wide open. Those egg layers leveled the jungle.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Bombing chute; 2 Ordnance pod; 3 Missile port; 4 Targeting sensors; 5 Twin ion engines; 6 Solar array wing.
