# NEGVV 2003 — notes154: Inferno → Jabba’s Sail Barge (printed 66–71 / scan 088–093)

## Printed 66–67 / S088–089 — Inferno (Custom “Villie Special” Big Wing)
- **Readout:** **27 m** wingspan; **3,730 G / Class 0.8 / 1,200 kph**; Vilmarh Grahrk (or so he claims); Vilmarh Grahrk.
- Devaronian smuggler Villie’s pink/magenta big-wing; gackle-bat silhouette; narrow hard to target. 3 lasers/wing concealed; short-range belly ion; strong shields/speed over firepower. Holoprojector; cargo tractor; escape pod. Hardwired to **NT 600** astronavigation droid “NT” (ethics circuit / moral compass / remote probe). Aided Quinlan Vos memory recovery; escorted Princess Foolookoola to Dur Sabon. Hyperdrive never fully recovered from pit-droid incident → constant repairs.
- **Quote (Villie to Vos):** “The bet I made on you dying—I bet the ship. You didn’t die. If you had just died I not be in this mess!”
- **Schematics:** 1 Primary ion drive; 2 Concealed ion cannon; 3 Concealed lasers; 4 Deflector shield gen; 5 Hyperdrive generator; 6 Sensor jammers.

## Printed 68–69 / S090–091 — Interdictor Cruiser (SFS Immobilizer 418)
- **Readout:** **600 m**; **1,210 G / Class 2**; Sienar Fleet Systems; Galactic Empire / New Republic.
- Gravity-well projectors block Rebel hyperspace escapes; developed ~2 yrs pre-Yavin; Hoth & Yavin 4. Weakness: **60-second** projector recharge. Defenses: **20** quad lasers + >12 shield gens. Thrawn Outer Rim use; NR captured → Immobilizer 418A vs Yuuzhan Vong / Fondor.
- **Quote (Sienar official):** “Never again will the Rebels leave you behind, looking flat-footed and foolish. The Immobilizer will see to that.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Gravity-well projector; 2 Comm tower; 3 Sensor/targeting; 4 Main reactor; 5 Drive engines.

## Printed 70–71 / S092–093 — Jabba’s Sail Barge (*Khetanna*) (Ubrikkian Luxury Sail Barge)
- **Readout:** **30 m**; **100 kph**; Ubrikkian; Jabba the Hutt.
- Status-symbol repulsorlift; 3-chamber engines ~100 kph; retractable sails. Civilian unarmed; Jabba’s armed for parties/Sarlacc executions. Decks: top (crew/controls, heavy blaster + antipersonnel vs Tuskens, sensors); passenger (banquet/kitchen/private quarters, tapestries, retractable viewports); lower (engines/steering/prison/droid closet/machine shop/live-food tanks). Destroyed when Leia fired deck cannon into deck → chain reaction (Han rescue).
- **Quote (Huttese):** “A Hutt without a sail barge is like a smoke moth without wings.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Main-deck mounted blaster (Leia used to destroy barge); 2 Sensor array; 3 Drive system; 4 Steering vanes; 5 Sails.
