# NEGVV 2003 — notes154: Luke’s Landspeeder + classification (printed 84–87 / scan 106–109)

## Printed 84–86 / S106–108 — Luke’s Landspeeder (SoroSuub X-34) + market context
- **Readout (art page):** **3.4 m**; **250 kph**; SoroSuub; Luke Skywalker.
- Most widespread ground vehicle; ~1 m float. Open cockpit; retractable duraplex windscreen; 3 turbines @ 250 kph. Starboard engine often shown with cowling off (repair motif).
- **V-35 Courier** (same entry body): Lars family vehicle; 100 kph; 3 rectangular thrust turbines. Qui-Gon & Obi-Wan used a V-35 on Ord Mantell ~5 yrs pre-Naboo.
- Market when Luke sold X-34: Bespin Motors Void Spider TX-3, Ubrikkian 9000 Z001, Mobquet Deluxe; X-34 largely replaced by **XP-38** (sleek; astromech-look autopilot; better accel/turn; rigid RL → All-Terrain Roughrider variant). Specialty: SoroSuub Duneblaster (desert); Mobquet ArcticExplorer-4 (heaters + icebreaker laser). Military: Naboo Flash & Gian; Imperial Chariot LAV; Rebel Arrow-23.
- **Quote (Luke):** “He says this is the best he can do. Since the XP-38 came out, they just aren’t in demand.”
- **Quote (XP-38 Tatooine ad):** “Don't be left in the dust!”
- **Schematics (X-34):** 1 Turbine engines; 2 Duraplex windscreen; 3 Repulsor vents; 4 Repulsor lift generator; 5 Scanning device (Luke searched Tatooine for R2).
- **Schematics (Lars’ landspeeder / V-35-style):** 1 Scanner scope; 2 Booster coils; 3 Thrust turbines; 4 Air intakes; 5 Repulsor-field generator; 6 Cargo compartment (farm↔Anchorhead).
