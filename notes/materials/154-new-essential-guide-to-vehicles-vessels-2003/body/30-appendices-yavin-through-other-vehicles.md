## Printed 185 / S207 — Battle of Yavin body + Battle of Hoth opening
- First major GCW engagement; “incredibly powerful” Empire vs “underequipped” Alliance. First significant Rebel tactical victory via superior planning, versatile starfighters, and the Force, destroying the Death Star.
- Early war: Alliance avoided conflict when possible. Learned of the Death Star (planet-destroying secret weapon). Rebel spies stole the plans; Empire traced the Alliance to Yavin 4 (jungle moon near a large gas giant).
- Only **30 standard minutes** before the Death Star was in range of Yavin 4. Very small force of Y-wings and X-wings; pilots from bases across the galaxy, including new recruit Luke Skywalker. Many never in combat, but had flown small repulsorlift craft; **skyhopper** controls “nearly identical” to the X-wing.
- Plan: reach the Death Star, dodge TIEs and turbolaser fire, descend into a long trench, fire a proton torpedo into a **two-meter-wide** exhaust port; hit the unshielded thermal exhaust vent → chain reaction collapsing the power core.
- **Red Squadron** = X-wings; **Gold Squadron** = Y-wings. Approached in tight wings (central fighter + two wingmates). Gold commander + wingmates toward the trench; remaining fighters occupied TIEs and destroyed countless turbolasers. Once the plan was uncovered, Vader boarded his TIE prototype with two hand-selected wingmates to defend the trench.
- Outset looked doomed: X/Y-wings avoided turbolasers and outgunned many TIEs, but Imperial numbers told.
- Trench too narrow for less-maneuverable Y-wings; first strike failed. Second run: trio of X-wings headed by Red Leader; Vader easily overtook them. Only three X-wings remained, including Luke’s. Luke entered the trench as the Death Star prepared to destroy Yavin 4.
- Luke used the targeting computer; Vader’s faster TIE prototype was on him in moments. About to obliterate Luke when the *Millennium Falcon* appeared; quad lasers opened fire. Would have demolished any other TIE, but Vader’s ship had deflector shields; prototype spun out of control instead of exploding. Luke, using the Force, unleashed proton torpedoes into the exhaust port; chain reaction destroyed the main reactor and the superweapon.

### The Battle of Hoth (opens; continues 187)
- **Date:** 3 A.B.Y. **Factions:** Galactic Empire vs. Rebel Alliance. **Key Vehicles:** Imperial AT-ATs, AT-STs, and Star Destroyers; Rebel snowspeeders and transports.
- After Yavin, Alliance spent three years running. Relocated to Hoth; Vader searched for Luke and Alliance leaders from the Super Star Destroyer *Executor*; located the base and launched one of the largest ground offensives of the GCW. “The Battle of Hoth would become the Rebel Alliance's most terrible defeat.”
- Vader’s fleet moved immediately. Admiral Ozzel (Executor’s commander) blundered: emerged from hyperspace too close to the Hoth system, allowing Rebel detection. Alliance erected a planetary shield vs space bombardment and prepared a full-scale retreat.
- Ground: General Maximilian Veers (tactical brilliance) vs Alliance General Carlist Rieekan (pragmatism). Veers: overrun the base and capture all personnel. Rieekan: get Rebels to safety; prepare transports; send Rogue Squadron to delay.
- Veers deployed AT-AT landing barges and Imperial transports. AT-AT squad dubbed **Blizzard Force**, “consisted of sev-” (breaks mid-word).

## Printed 187 / S209 — Hoth conclusion + Battle of Endor (complete)
- Blizzard Force: several AT-AT walkers, each commanded by a high-ranking Imperial officer. Several modified with unique features; Veers’s own **Blizzard One** more heavily armored than a standard AT-AT. AT-STs protected the AT-ATs’ flanks.
- Rogue Squadron in modified T-47 airspeeders (“snowspeeders”) in a loose delta formation to draw AT-AT fire from ground troops. Snowspeeder lasers could not penetrate AT-AT hides. Wedge Antilles used harpoon and tow cable to entangle an AT-AT’s legs, forcing it to the ground.
- Transports began launching individually at first; ion cannon provided covering fire against blockading Star Destroyers; a pair of X-wings as escorts. As AT-ATs closed, transports launched in pairs. Final transport *Bright Hope* was disabled fleeing Hoth; bounty hunters **4-LOM** and **Zuckuss** eventually aided its escape.
- Many transports escaped, but heavy casualties. Defeat severely weakened the Alliance; remained understaffed for the rest of the GCW. A year later, still suffering aftereffects, forced into the risky second-Death-Star assault at Endor.

### The Battle of Endor
- **Date:** 4 A.B.Y. **Factions:** Galactic Empire vs. Rebel Alliance. **Key Vehicles:** Imperial TIE fighters, TIE bombers, TIE interceptors, Star Destroyers, and AT-STs; Rebel X-wings, Y-wings, B-wings, A-wings, Mon Cal cruisers, and the *Millennium Falcon*.
- “The most decisive, and final, engagement of the Galactic Civil War”; major space battle and ground offensive. Unlike Hoth, initiated by the Alliance as a last-ditch effort against the Emperor. Strike on the second Death Star (still under construction) in orbit around Endor’s forest moon. Shield generator on Endor projected a defensive screen, preventing a direct assault.
- Plan part one: Rebel strike team led by Han Solo to destroy the shield generator moments before the fleet arrived. After misadventures, Solo’s group reached the generator and found a squad of the Empire’s finest waiting, armed with AT-STs and speeder bikes, who quickly surrounded the Rebels.
- Unaware of Solo’s plight, the Rebel fleet exited hyperspace near the Death Star, led by Mon Cal cruiser **Home One**, “and including” (print as written) starfighter squadrons of X-wings, Y-wings, A-wings, and B-wings. Lando Calrissian aboard the *Falcon* led the starfighter attack as Gold Leader. Shield still active: Admiral Ackbar advised breaking off; Lando urged them forward. Met by a massive TIE fleet (standard TIEs + new TIE interceptors). Versatility (powerhouse B-wings + agile A-wings) let the Alliance form small wings to crash headlong into the wave. A-wings pursued interceptors; B-wings and other craft provided covering fire.
- On Endor, Solo’s squad rescued by Ewoks. Ewoks capitalized on AT-ST inability to cross shifting terrain by rolling logs under the walker’s metal feet; also primitive traps vs speeder bikes. Chewbacca leapt from the trees to capture an AT-ST from above, turning the ground-battle tide. Solo’s team destroyed the generator, but the space battle was still in doubt.
- While the ground team fought for access to the facility, the fleet discovered the Death Star’s superlaser was fully functional. It destroyed one Mon Cal cruiser and was preparing to fire again; Ackbar ordered a full retreat. Lando suggested closing with the Imperial Star Destroyers so the Death Star wouldn’t fire on ships in close proximity to the SD fleet. An A-wing barreled through the *Executor*’s bridge; the ship crashed into the Death Star. *Executor*’s demise was a major turning point.
- When the shield dropped, Lando and Wedge Antilles flew into the Death Star and attacked the main reactor core with proton torpedoes. As with the first Death Star, reactor destruction caused an explosive chain reaction.

## Printed 189 / S211 — Other Vehicles and Vessels of Note (A-9 through Chariot LAV)
- Intro: galaxy replete with vehicles from single-person repulsorlift transports to massive starships that “almost defy logic.” A full account since earliest Old Republic “would fill volumes.” This appendix—though not exhaustive—shows range in function and form of vehicles piloted by the galaxy’s denizens.
- **A-9 Vigilance Interceptor:** short-range fighter used by Imperial Remnant forces; appeared shortly after Thrawn’s defeat. Two laser cannons; no hyperdrive or shields. Can match A-wing speed, less maneuverable. Small, light; sudden strikes vs enemy bases or capital ships. (Bottom art: **A-9 Vigilance**, white twin-boom interceptor.)
- **Amphibion:** water assault craft used extensively at the Battle of Mon Calamari. Troop transport; land and water up to **100 kph**. Rotating antipersonnel blaster cannon.
- **A-vek Iiluunu (Yuuzhan Vong Fighter Carrier):** bioengineered YV capital ship. Coralskippers attach by branchlike projections. Standard carrier nearly **800 m** long can hold **200** coralskippers.
- **Bria:** SoroSuub Starmite owned and piloted by Han Solo before the *Millennium Falcon*; named for Solo’s first love.
- **Carrack Cruiser:** small combat cruiser integral to the Imperial fleet. Heavily armed and armored: **10** heavy turbolasers and **20** ion cannons. No docking bay; must carry **five** TIE fighters on external racks.
- **Chariot LAV:** modified military landspeeder as a command vehicle in ground engagements. Chariot Light Assault Vehicle has minimal weaponry and “relies rather on other craft for defense” (print as written). Favored by Imperial commanders for low cost, sophisticated communications bay, and ability to navigate most terrain. Thrawn upgraded several to more formidable combat vehicles.
