# NEGVV 2003 — notes154: CloakShape → Spider Tank (printed 24–29 / scan 046–051)

## Printed 24–25 / S046–047 — CloakShape Fighter (Kuat Systems Engineering)
- **Readout:** **15 m**; **3,200 G / Class 1.5 hyperdrive (with upgrade) / 950 kph**; KSE; Affiliation **None**.
- Pre–Clone Wars atmospheric/short-range; easy to modify → pirates/smugglers/militias. Original lacked shields/hyperdrive; small generator. Common upgrades: rear maneuvering fin (so common mistaken for stock); self-powered hyperdrive sled (like Delta-7). Lore: Jedi Jaizen Suel *Dawn Raider*; bounty hunters vs Han to Nar Shaddaa; Nebula Front attack on *Revenue* at Dorvalla.
- **Quote (Shug Ninx):** “I once had a guy come in here looking to buy an ‘original’ CloakShape. I just laughed and told him that there’s no such thing as an ‘original’ CloakShape.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Dual lasers; 2 Concussion missile launcher; 3 Maneuvering fin; 4 Ion engine; 5 Turbo impellor; 6 Power generator; 7 Sensor array.

## Printed 26–27 / S048–049 — Cloud Car (Bespin Motors Storm IV Twin-Pod)
- **Readout:** **7 m**; **1,500 kph**; Bespin Motors; Bespin Wing Guard.
- Cloud City patrol: greet vessels, investigate, defend. Twin armored cockpits + central engine block; twin blasters (gunner starboard); pilot port with nav. Pressurized high-altitude cockpits; limited life support. Ion primary >1,500 kph + secondary repulsor. Combat variants (e.g. Talon I): upgraded armor/lasers; 100 km ceiling; dedicated life support. Blasters “sufficient to keep… *Millennium Falcon* in line.”
- **Quote (Ullok’s Underground Guide to Bespin):** “If you can bribe a member of Bespin’s Wing Guard into taking you on a spin around Cloud City, you won’t regret spending the credits. The views are stunning.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Blaster; 2 Sensor suite; 3 Engine block; 4 Armor; 5 Gunner’s pod; 6 Pilot’s pod; 7 Cockpit.

## Printed 28–29 / S050–051 — Commerce Guild Spider Tank (Baktoid Homing Spider Droid)
- **Readout:** **7.32 m** tall; **90 kph**; Baktoid Armor Workshop; CIS.
- Geonosis CIS army (Commerce Guild / Banking Clan / Techno Union / TF / Corporate Alliance). Baktoid design purchased by Commerce Guild post-Naboo dissolution.
- **Weapons:** Homing laser (sustained precision beam vs shields/armor); antipersonnel cannon; retractable ion cannon (disable ships/vehicles). Four hydraulic legs — height for over-obstacle / atmospheric targeting; one hydraulic failure crashes unit. Minimal AI; central computer control. Works with burrowing spider droid scouts (IR photoreceptors).
- **Armored core callout:** volatile reactor — breach → violent explosion.
- **Quote (Ki-Adi-Mundi):** “It is fortunate that these clone troopers know no fear.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Homing laser; 2 Antipersonnel cannon; 3 Comm antenna/control receiver; 4 Armored core; 5 Hydraulics; 6 Signal tracking dish.
