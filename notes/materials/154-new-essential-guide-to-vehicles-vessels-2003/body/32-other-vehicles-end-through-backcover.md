## Printed 196 / S218 — Other Vehicles: Ssi-Ruuvi Assault Carrier through TIE/gt Fighter
- **Ssi-Ruuvi Assault Carrier:** *Sh'ner*-class planetary assault carriers of the reptilian Ssi-ruuk. Ovoid; nearly **750 m**. Believed rare; only one used in the Bakura attack.
- **Ssi-Ruuvi Picket Ship:** small Ssi-ruuvi Imperium combat ships; typically disable enemies and guard fleet perimeters. Fairly delicate; inefficient shields. Crewed by droids and P'w'eck slaves; Ssi-ruuk consider them expendable and often send them on suicide missions.
- **Star Home:** four-thousand-standard-year-old transport designed for the Hapes Cluster’s Queen Mother. Unique: perfectly replicates the Queen Mother’s castle on Hapes, all atop a giant basalt base housing traditional starship systems.
- **Starlight Intruder:** Salla Zend’s modified Mobquet transport. Converted for smuggling; carried **seven times** the cargo of other light freighters; four military-grade ion engines for quick escapes. Captured by Imperial forces on Byss, who stripped the ship and used her to fuel furnaces.
- **Stinger:** personal transport of Prince Xizor’s cyborg aide, Guri. Modified Surronian Conqueror assault ship; Guri once used its proton torpedoes to single-handedly destroy an Imperial Star Destroyer.
- **Storm:** Prince Isolder’s starfighter, designed by the prince himself. Miniaturized components keep her size just over **seven meters**. Extremely well armed: triple-linked laser cannons, a mini concussion missile launcher, two ion cannons, and a thermal detonator bomb chute.
- **Sun Crusher:** Imperial superweapon with resonance torpedoes capable of destroying a star. Long, dagger-shaped; protected by rotating laser cannons. Destroyed when trapped in a gravity well produced by the Maw’s black holes.
- **Suuv Ban D'krid (Yuuzhan Vong Cruiser):** YV capital ship similar to a cruiser; multicolored, bioengineered of yorik coral. At the Battle of Duro, a cruiser rammed an unshielded orbital city.
- **Tafanda Bay:** Ithorian herd ship. Ithorians have lived in herd ships cruising slowly over Ithor for generations. Each is hundreds of meters tall and holds thousands of Ithorians. Shortly before the Battle of Ithor, *Tafanda Bay* hosted a reception for NR and Imperial Remnant forces. When the YV fleet arrived, warriors boarded and clashed with Luke and his Jedi allies. Soon after, Ithor was consumed in a fireball and *Tafanda Bay* was presumably destroyed.
- **TIE Avenger:** TIE design based on Vader’s prototype. Like Vader’s TIE, had deflector shields and a hyperdrive. Expensive; produced in very limited numbers; eventually replaced by the TIE Interceptor.
- **TIE Crawler:** tank based on the TIE fighter design; round cockpit connected to two large treads. Also known as the “century tank”; essential to the Empire’s efforts to retake Coruscant.
- **TIE/D Fighter:** automated TIE with an integrated Cybot Galactica Ace-6 fighter unit. Computerized pilots can be programmed with the latest tactics and combat protocols. Distinctive rectangular wings and heavy armor.
- **TIE/fc Fighter:** modified TIE providing fire control and targeting aid for long-range naval artillery. Protected comlinks and advanced targeting computers. Can hover near a target and transmit precise aiming adjustments to an attacking fleet ship.
- **TIE/gt Fighter:** predecessor of the TIE bomber. Essentially a standard TIE fighter with an expanded hull loaded with proton torpedoes and bombs.

## Printed 197 / S219 — Other Vehicles: TIE/rc through Zoomer (appendix ends)
- **Art (upper left):** labeled **Yorik-Stronha Spawn Ship** — rocky asteroid-like organic hull with reddish markings. *Note:* the facing text entry is **Yorik-Stronha (Yuuzhan Vong Spy Ship)**; the spawn ship in this same appendix is **Sh'rip Sh'pa** (P195). Art caption and text title disagree; both recorded as printed.
- **Tsik Vai (Yuuzhan Vong Flier):** atmospheric bioengineered flying vehicle. Used to search for Anakin Solo on Yavin 4. Looks like a winged sea creature with several tentacles for grasping prey. Intake vents function like gills and create an audible whine.
- **Uro-ik V'alh (Yuuzhan Vong Battleship):** massive YV vessel; larger than the YV warship; resembles a long rocky slab of hardened slag. Carries up to **80** plasma projectors; designed specifically for direct confrontations with capital ships.
- **TIE/rc Fighter:** reconnaissance TIE with advanced sensor packages and communications devices. Eventually replaced by the TIE vanguard.
- **TIE Shuttle:** personal shuttle for high-ranking Imperials on high-priority missions. **7.8 m**; can carry a pilot and two passengers. In heavy use around the Battle of Hoth.
- **TIE Vanguard:** descendant of the TIE/rc; designed for reconnaissance and spy missions. Deflector shields protected any data gathered by the starfighter.
- **TIE-wing:** combines a TIE fighter cockpit with a Y-wing’s engine nacelle. Considered “ugly” because it is cobbled together from other vehicles. Used by Kavil’s Corsairs in conflicts with Rogue Squadron.
- **Trade Federation Gunboat:** seaworthy vessel to explore swamps and other waterways. Twin laser cannons and a rotating turret; used vs RSF resistance fighters and Gungan warriors during the Naboo invasion.
- **Uumufalh (Yuuzhan Vong Gunship):** escort starship used to protect cruisers and other larger starships; can spray volleys of plasma.
- **Yorik-Stronha (Yuuzhan Vong Spy Ship):** asteroid-shaped scout, usually sent into enemy territory well in advance of an attack fleet to gauge capabilities. Yorik coral covered with layers of rock; often equipped with a cloaking shadow. Anakin Solo and his allies commandeered a spy ship called the *Stalking Moon*.
- **Yorik-ta (Yuuzhan Vong Escape Pod):** constructed from black yorik coral; incorporates a dovin basal for travel and protection. Roughly the size of a landspeeder; transparent canopy; no weapons.
- **Zoomer:** salvage ship of Toydarian mechanic Reti around the Battle of Naboo. Retrieved Naboo pilot Rhys Dallows after he was ambushed by mercenaries hired by the Trade Federation. Several standard years later, Reti and his starship joined forces with pirate captain Nym.

## Printed 198–199 / S220–221 — Hyperdrive Classification (two-page bar chart)
- Header (S220): **HYPERDRIVE CLASSIFICATION**. Footer both pages: “Ships not to scale.” Printed 198 / 199.
- **Axes (visual, both pages):** top scale **Sublight Speed (×1000)** from **0 to 6** (yellow-orange-red bars). Bottom scale **Hyperdrive Speed (class)** running **4 → 0** left to right (purple bars). Lower class number = faster hyperdrive, so the HD axis is inverted relative to the sublight axis. Ships with no HD are labeled **No Hyperdrive** and show only a sublight bar.
- Exact bar-end values are not transcribed as numbers (150 dpi chart; ticks at 1,000-sublight / class intervals). Notes below list every labeled row in visual order and flag “No Hyperdrive.” Comparative standouts that are visually clear are noted; they are not a substitute for the chart itself.

## Printed 200 / S222 — About the Author / About the Illustrator
- **About the Author:** W. Haden Blackman is the author of *The Field Guide to North American Monsters* and *The Field Guide to North American Hauntings*. He has also worked extensively “in” the Star Wars universe on a variety of video games and comic books, including LucasArts’ *Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided* and Dark Horse Comics’ *Jango Fett: Open Seasons*. “He thinks Lando is completely misunderstood.”
- **About the Illustrator:** color photo of Ian Fullwood at dual CRT workstations (an AT-AT on the left screen). “Ian Fullwood lives and works in Herefordshire, England, and has clients both at home and in the USA. With more than fifteen years’ experience in technical illustration and commercial art, he works with a range of clients, who include publishing and engineering companies. He produces a variety of work ranging from science fiction to product visualizing and animation. Ian uses traditional drawing skills combined with computer programs—Illustrator, Photoshop, and Lightwave 3D—to produce technically demanding and visually exciting pieces of work.” Closing line as printed: “Vist www.if3d.com for more visual indigestion!” (print “Vist”; pun on “Visit”).
