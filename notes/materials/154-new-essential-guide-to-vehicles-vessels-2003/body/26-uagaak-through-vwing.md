# NEGVV 2003 — notes154: Uagaak/Vangaak → V-wing (printed 160–165 / scan 182–187)

## Printed 160–161 / S182–183 — Uagaak / Yuuzhan Vong Vangaak
- **Art title:** UAGAAK; **text title:** Yuuzhan Vong Vangaak (aquatic trawler).
- **Readout:** **30 m** (avg); **35 kph**; Yuuzhan Vong; Yuuzhan Vong.
- Yorik coral frame + **kera-boa** gelatinous organism (microscopic tendril propulsion). Armor: dull green chitinous **dora-mu** scutes (self-healing). Feeds via **maw** filtering water; digestive membrane fuels craft. Combat: miniature plasma + kera-boa immobilize for boarding. Lore: Vua Rapuung (shamed tender); Shamed One **Qe'u** on Yavin 4 rivers. Sensory dome often only visible part.
- **Quote:** “Feed the vangaak, feed yourself.” — Translated from the Yuuzhan Vong
- **Schematics:** 1 Propulsion tendrils; 2 Maw; 3 Armored shell; 4 Filtering membrane; 5 Forward kera-boa tendrils; 6 Sensory dome.

## Printed 162–163 / S184–185 — Virago (Prince Xizor’s MandalMotors StarViper-class Attack Platform)
- **Readout:** **9 m** (wings collapsed); **1,240 G / Class 1 / 880 kph**; MandalMotors; Black Sun.
- Xizor commissioned; bought all design rights (unique). 4 adjustable wings + 2 microthrusters each; “bizarre living creature” illusion; fold for speed/atmosphere, expand combat. 4 power generators + reserve fuel. Advanced targeting + experimental laser sighting (not new arms). Arms: Taim & Bak Ht-12 double heavy lasers (forward arms, 180° fire); 2 Borstel proton torp launchers. Believed destroyed by Vader at Xizor’s skyhook; MandalMotors later produced weaker versions. Wings use S-foil tech like X-wing.
- **Quote (Dash Rendar):** “I think that's just about the most disturbing thing I've ever seen.” — upon seeing the VIRAGO for the first time
- **Schematics:** 1 Double laser cannon; 2 Proton torpedo launcher; 3 Deflector-shield generators; 4 Armor plating; 5 Power generator; 6 Microthrusters; 7 S-foil actuator.

## Printed 164–165 / S186–187 — V-wing (Slayn & Korpil V-Wing Airspeeder)
- **Readout:** **5.7 m** long, **9.6 m** wingspan; **1,400 kph**; Slayn & Korpil; New Republic.
- First NR high-atmosphere combat fighter (replace T-47 air-to-air); Verpine designers (B-wing); ready for Mon Calamari. Combat speed 1,000 kph (slightly < T-47); **Chab-Ylwoum scramjet** → 1,400 kph (break-apart risk; escapes only). Max altitude **100 km** (vs T-47 250 m). 2 fire-linked blasters (2 km). No shields; rely on speed/size. Carried by Slayn & Korpil speeder transports (4–6 each).
- **Quote (anon Rogue):** “We were told not to use the scramjets during combat, but it was just too tempting at Mon Cal. It doesn't get much more exciting than using the scramjet to overtake one of those droid TIE fighters.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Blaster cannon; 2 Main generator; 3 Ion engine; 4 Cooling intake; 5 Scramjet intake; 6 Cockpit.
