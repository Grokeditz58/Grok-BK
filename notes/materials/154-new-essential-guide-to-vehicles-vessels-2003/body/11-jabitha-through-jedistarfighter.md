## Printed 72–73 / S094–095 — Jabitha (Handcrafted Sekotan Personal Starship)
- **Readout:** **25 m** long / **30 m** wingspan; **5,200 G / Class 0.4 / 13,000 kph**; Zonama Sekot Shipbuilders; Anakin Skywalker.
- Living vehicle; Obi-Wan & Anakin to Zonama Sekot ~3 yrs post-Naboo. Seed-partners (15 chose Jedi); organoform + mechanical; 3 lobes under green skin; 2 modified Haor Chall Silver-class engines + hyperdrive. Telepathic bond via seed-partners. HCE Class 1 rated but Anakin bond → **Class 0.4**. Damaged in Tarkin attack; planet fled hyperspace; *Jabitha* “died” at Seline.
- **Quote (Fitch to Anakin):** “You're not the brain, young owner, not in literal truth. The ship does think for herself, after a fashion, but she needs you while she's still young, and while she's being finished, or she gets, let's say, confused. Like a baby. You're her guardians now.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Engine; 2 Hyperdrive; 3 Shield ducts; 4 Cockpit; 5 Microthrusters.

Bridge: pilot/copilot/navigator under large canopy. Force-only true hatch release (cosmetic external).

- **Schematics:** 1 Twin ion drive; 2 Sensor suite; 3 Concealed laser; 4 Concussion launcher; 5 Jammers; 6 Tractor; 7 External hatch release (cosmetic; true Force-activated).
