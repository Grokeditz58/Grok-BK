# NEGVV 2003 — notes154: TIE Defender → TIE Interceptor (printed 154–159 / scan 176–181)

## Printed 154–155 / S176–177 — TIE Defender (SFS)
- **Readout:** **6.6 m**; **4,220 G / Class 2 / 1,680 kph**; Sienar Fleet Systems; Galactic Empire.
- Experimental tri-wing assault with HD + protection; pre-Endor; X-wing influence. P-sz9.7 engines; 40% faster than std TIE in atmosphere; maneuvering jet arrays. Arms: lasers on side wings; ion on top wing; proton + concussion on cockpit. Secret project; first full combat vs Admiral Zaarin; Endor handful (Onyx Squadron); Thrawn resurface; hard to produce/maintain. Rare TIE deflectors.
- **Quote (Rexler Brath, Onyx):** “Imperial High Command decided that defender pilots would only be selected from TIE interceptor pilots who had flown at least twenty combat missions and survived. We’re either the best pilots in the Imperial fleet or the luckiest.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Laser cannon; 2 Ion cannons; 3 Missile launchers; 4 Targeting sensors; 5 Solar ionization reactor; 6 Deflector shield projector; 7 Sensor array.

## Printed 156–157 / S178–179 — TIE Fighter (SFS TIE/ln Space Superiority Starfighter)
- **Readout:** **6.3 m**; **4,100 G / 1,200 kph**; Sienar Fleet Systems; Galactic Empire.
- Attrition doctrine: speed/affordability/numbers over durability. Twin ion tech; no HD/shields/life support (sealed suits); 2 L-s1 lasers. Fastest/most maneuverable except A-wing/E-wing. Variants: bomber, interceptor, recon, TIE crawler tank. Flank → target solar wings.
- **Quote (Baron Fel):** “For every TIE fighter you shoot down, a thousand more will take its place.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Laser cannon; 2 Sensor array; 3 Command pod; 4 Solar array wing; 5 Transparisteel viewport.

## Printed 158–159 / S180–181 — TIE Interceptor (SFS)
- **Readout:** **9.6 m**; **4,240 G / 1,250 kph**; Sienar Fleet Systems; Galactic Empire.
- Response to A-wing; TIE Advanced x1 features. Ion stream projector + steering port deflectors → tight turns/rolls. Fastest Imperial (slightly slower than A-wing). Bent dagger wings (smaller profile; Sith Infiltrator reminiscence). 4 fire-linked wingtip lasers. No armor/shields/HD/life support; Thrawn later added some shields. ~20% Imperial fleet by Endor.
- **Quote (Kyle Katarn):** “Your generic TIE grunt is just plain suicidal. And the TIE defender jockey is bloodthirsty. But the TIE interceptor pilot, he's suicidal *and* bloodthirsty. When you see a squad of those maniacs flying your way, you'd better hope your hyperdrive is operational.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Blaster cannon; 2 Targeting sensors; 3 Laser cannon; 4 Transparisteel viewport; 5 Bent wings.
