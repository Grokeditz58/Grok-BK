# NEGVV 2003 — notes154: Coralskipper → Desert Skiff (printed 30–35 / scan 052–057)

## Printed 30–31 / S052–053 — Coralskipper (Yuuzhan Vong Yorik-Et)
- **Readout:** **13 m** long (average); **3,700 G / 400 kph**; Yuuzhan Vong; Yuuzhan Vong.
- Living bioengineered craft grown from yorik coral in vine nurseries; no two identical; tapered nose, dark canopy, triangular body.
- **Weapons/propulsion:** Pilot fires flaming molten rocks from front appendage; magma also propels; “refuels” by consuming rocks/asteroids/debris.
- **Dovin basal:** front creature creates mini black-hole gravity field — overload enemy shields or intercept fire.
- Pilots use **cognition hoods**; mask is part of the ship (callout 1).
- **Quote:** “In the yorik-et, two minds become one.” —Yuuzhan Vong pilot’s creed
- **Schematics:** 1 Cockpit with mask; 2 Firing appendage; 3 Dovin basal; 4 Propulsion appendage.

## Printed 32–33 / S054–055 — Corporate Alliance Tank Droid (Persuader-class)
- **Readout:** **6.2 m** tall (**9.5 m** with control receiver); **60 kph** (body text also cites **50 km/h** top — both logged); Corporate Alliance; CIS.
- Pre-CIS Outer Rim corporate violence tool. Single treaded drive + stabilizing outriggers; slow accel. Armament: ion cannons, homing lasers; optional concussion/homing missiles, dumbfire torpedoes, thermal grenades. Short-range recon + urban; stereoscopic scanners. Often pair-flank spider/hailfire droids. Late-war voice modulators for terror.
- **Control receiver callout:** Droid Control Ship orders + backup processors if ship destroyed.
- **Quote (Plo Koon):** “These are automated agents of evil!”
- **Schematics:** 1 Weapons outrigger; 2 Homing laser; 3 Ion cannon; 4 Drive axis hub; 5 Stabilizing outrigger; 6 Comm/control receivers; 7 Tracking transmitter; 8 Visual sensor.

## Printed 34–35 / S056–057 — Desert Skiff (Ubrikkian Bantha II Cargo Skiff)
- **Readout:** **9 m**; **250 kph**; Ubrikkian; Jabba the Hutt.
- Open-deck utility repulsorlift; hover ≤**50 m**; thrust nozzles ~250 kph; dual steering vanes. Cargo >**100 tons**; magnetic lifters/fasteners; ≤16 seats. Variants: enclosed, water (rain screens), desert (sand filters + cooling — Jabba). Poor combat (vulnerable vanes/repulsors); Jabba’s: light armor + portable lasers for raids/sail-barge escort.
- **Plank callout:** Jabba forced enemies to walk plank into Sarlacc.
- **Quote (Ubrikkian ad):** “A skiff so easy to pilot, even a labor droid can handle it!”
- **Schematics:** 1 Armor; 2 Thrust nozzles; 3 Steering vane; 4 Repulsor coils; 5 Repulsor drive unit; 6 Control pedestal; 7 The plank.
