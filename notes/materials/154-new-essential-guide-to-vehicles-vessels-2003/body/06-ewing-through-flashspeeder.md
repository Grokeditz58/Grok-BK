## Printed 42–43 / S064–065 — E-Wing (FreiTek Escort Starfighter)
- **Readout:** **11.2 m**; **4,200 G / Class 2 / 1,300 kph**; FreiTek, Inc.; New Republic.
- Early NR vs Thrawn; by ex-Incom X-wing engineers. Speed≈A-wing, armor≈X-wing, armament≈B-wing. **3** fire-linked lasers (wings+above cockpit); **16** proton torpedoes; fixed wings; exhaust-nacelle flaps for maneuver. Single pilot + **R7** (sealed compartment — Type B then Series 4 later allows R2; Series 4 after Thrawn: stabler tibanna, secondary generator). Early Type B fixed synthetic tibanna issues.
- **Quote (Wedge):** “I say let the Imperials keep coming, because every time a new admiral crawls out of the Outer Rim or some Imperial goon finds a lost superweapon, Rogue Squadron gets a new starfighter. Seems like a good deal to me.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Laser; 2 Proton torpedo launcher; 3 Deflector shield gen; 4 Sensor array; 5 Drive engine; 6 Exhaust nacelle; 7 R7 astromech.

## Printed 44–45 / S066–067 — Executor (KDY Super Star Destroyer / Star Dreadnought)
- **Readout:** **12,800 m**; **1,230 G / Class 2**; KDY; Galactic Empire. Scale silhouette vs ISD in readout.
- Vader’s flagship; Lira Wessex design; Fondor yards. >1000 weapons (turbolasers, ion, concussion tubes, tractor). Fighters: 144 TIE initially; Endor mix interceptors/bombers/generic; ≤200 support in secondary bays. Ground: full stormtrooper corps, 24 AT-ATs + barges, 50 AT-STs, bikes/speeders/AT-PTs, 3 prefab garrisons, 38,000 more troops. Ozzel (Force-choked) → Piett. Destroyed Endor: disabled A-wing into bridge → collide Death Star II.
- **Meditation chamber callout:** Vader tracked Luke via Force.
- **Quote (Ozzel):** “With the *Executor* under my command, I will be the ultimate power in the universe!” —moments before Vader kills him
- **Schematics:** 1 Deflector-shield dome; 2 Command tower; 3 Drive engines; 4 Main landing bay; 5 Meditation chamber.

- **Readout:** **4.5 m**; **200 kph**; SoroSuub; Naboo Royal Security Forces.
- Civilian→light combat/law; ~30 yrs pre-Naboo; faster than Gian; unstable rough terrain. RSF underground resistance (Amidala/Panaka); some later Rebel use.
