# NEGVV 2003 — notes154: AT-TE → Chiss Clawcraft (printed 14–23 / scan 036–045)

## Printed 14–15 / S036–037 — AT-TE (All Terrain Tactical Enforcer)
- **Readout:** **5.02 m** tall, **12.4 m** long; **60 kph**; Rothana Heavy Engineering (KDY); Old Republic.
- Debut Geonosis; six-legged low-CG precursor to AT-AT; multipurpose assault/troop/escort.
- **Weapons:** Roof mass-driver projectile turret (bunker busters / heat-seekers / sonic charges) + 6 antipersonnel lasers (4 fwd / 2 aft).
- **Crew 7:** pilot, spotter, 4 support laser gunners, projectile gunner (clone specialist); typically IM-6 medical droid; ≤**20** clone troopers (fore/aft compartments). Deployed via barges or LAAT/c.
- Decommissioned post-Clone Wars by Palpatine; some Outer Rim GCW use then abandoned (hard to transport).
- **Quote (Palpatine):** “These weapons of war would be awe-inspiring, if they were not so terrifying.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Projectile cannon; 2 Antipersonnel lasers; 3 Primary drive motor; 4 Periscope/rangefinder; 5 Gunner’s station (exposed seat).

## Printed 16–17 / S038–039 — A-Wing (Alliance RZ-1)
- **Readout:** **9.6 m**; **5,100 G / Class 1 hyperdrive / 1,300 kph**; Alliance Underground Engineering; **Affiliation printed on readout: “GALACTIC EMPIRE”** (visual+OCR verified — likely print error vs body text “Rebel Alliance / New Republic”).
- Dodonna + Walex Blissex (Delta-7 originator) pre-Endor vs TIEs. Fastest GCW fighter; Novaldex J-77 Event Horizon + thrust-vector; hit-and-run/recon/surgical. Trio destroyed *Executor*.
- Wing blasters pivot **60°**; concussion missiles; some rear cannons; power-jamming. Weak armor/small shield; exposed cockpit; **no astromech** (weight). High maintenance; hard to fly at top speed.
- **Quote (Han Solo):** “Any pilot who volunteers to fly an A-wing better be brave or crazy. Probably helps to be a little of both.”
- **Front wedge callout:** Arvel Crynyd collision tactic vs *Executor* (explosive fuel + wedge).
- **Schematics:** 1 Laser; 2 Concussion launcher; 3 Targeting sensor; 4 Forward sensor array; 5 Sublight engine; 6 Deflector shield gen; 7 Front wedge.

## Printed 18–19 / S040–041 — Bongo (Tribubble Bongo Sub)
- **Readout:** **15 m**; **85 kph**; Otoh Gunga Bongameken Cooperative; Gungans.
- Organic coral skeleton grown then fitted; electromotive field + rotating aft tentacles; hydrostatic bubble canopies; oil-filled hydrostatic chambers for dive/surface; trade Naboo for electronics/metal. Variants: cargo, Grand Army (≥5 armed), monobubble racers; tech → Ohma-D’un colonization. Hull strong but vulnerable to deep-sea monsters.
- **Quote (Boss Nass):** “Wesa give yousa una bongo. Da speedest way tooda Naboo tis goen through the planet’s core. Now go.”
- **Cockpit bubble callout:** access panel under bubble for steering repairs; Obi-Wan jump-start.
- **Schematics:** 1 Drive fins; 2 Engine; 3 Nav lights; 4 Cockpit bubble; 5 Diving plane; 6 Cargo bubbles.

## Printed 20–21 / S042–043 — B-Wing (Slayn & Korpil)
- **Readout:** **16.9 m**; **2,390 G / Class 2 / 950 kph**; Slayn & Korpil; Rebel Alliance. (OCR misread size as 76.9 — visual confirms 16.9.)
- Rebellion’s most formidable assault fighter; Endor + NR staple. Ackbar + Verpine. Long wing + folding airfoils; 8 modular weapon mounts; gyro cockpit keeps pilot stationary while craft rotates — extremely difficult; damaged gyros → spin out of control.
- Variant **B-wing/E2** elongated module for gunner: higher kill ratio, less maneuverable.
- **Quote (Ackbar):** “You’re among the best pilots in the galaxy. You’ll have to be if you want to control this starship.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Twin auto-blasters; 2 Proton torpedo launcher; 3 Modular weapons emplacement; 4 Shield generator; 5 Nav sensors; 6 Sensor array; 7 Gyroscopic cockpit.

## Printed 22–23 / S044–045 — Chiss Clawcraft (Nssis-class hybrid)
- **Readout:** **7.65 m**; **3,800 G / Class 1.5 / 1,000 kph**; Chiss; Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet.
- Thrawn-facilitated Chiss–Imperial hybrid: TIE ball cockpit + twin ion engines; four curving weapon arms with modified SFS L-s7.2 cannons doubling as control vanes. Has deflector shields + heavier armor vs standard TIE; slightly slower sublight. Hyperspace via **anchor-point/beacon network** (not internal nav computer).
- **Quote:** “You have to admire the Chiss. They don’t just steal your technology—they make it better.” —Anonymous Imperial engineer
- **Schematics:** 1 Hyperspace beacon; 2 Laser cannons; 3 Control jets; 4 Deflector shield generator; 5 Ion engine.
