# NEGVV 2003 — notes154: Havoc → Howlrunner (printed 54–59 / scan 076–081)

## Printed 54–55 / S076–077 — Havoc (Modified Nubian Scurrg H-6 Prototype Bomber)
- **Readout:** **22 m**; **2,730 G / Class 1.5 / 1,000 kph**; Nubian Design Collective; Lok Revenants.
- Feeorin pirate **Nym**; Naboo rejected as excessively armed; Bith **Jinkins** stole prototype with Nym. Naboo + Geonosis battles. 6 fwd fire-linked lasers (3/wing); rotating top turret; **bomblet generator** (energy bombs replacing protons); pre-Geonosis: missiles, proximity mines, ionized cluster missiles. Automated targeting + 2 astromechs → solo crew (was 5). Logo = Nym’s Blood Pits tattoo / Lok Revenants flag.
- **Quote (Nym):** “Sometimes, I get the feelin’ the Havoc is alive. She sometimes fires before I pull the trigger, an’ when we’re in hyperspace, I swear her engines sing me to sleep.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Pirate symbol; 2 Laser cannons; 3 Laser turret; 4 Bomb chute; 5 Deflector shield gen.

## Printed 56–57 / S078–079 — Hound’s Tooth (Modified CEC YV-666 Light Freighter)
- **Readout:** **23 m**; **2,360 G / Class 1.5 / 1,190 kph**; CEC; Bossk.
- Trandoshan bounty hunter. 3 decks: main (quarters/training/armory/medical/prison with mag cages, force-field, skinning table, interrogation); command (cockpit/monitors; scanners/motion/voice-recog); lower (power/weapons/life support). Dock bay for scout *Nashtah Pup* (top hatch). Weapons: retractable quad laser, ion, fwd concussion (mag 6); shields + armor. **X-10D** droid brain verbal commands.
- **Boarding ramp callout:** voice security, antipersonnel blasters, electrocution array, durasteel net.
- **Quote (Jan Ors):** “The stench onboard was horrible. Between drying Wookiee pelts, a rotting corpse on the skinning table, and Bossk’s own repellent scent, it was worse than a slaughterhouse on a sewage planet.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Quad laser bay; 2 Concussion launcher; 3 Deflector-shield gen; 4 Engine systems; 5 Drive nozzles; 6 Sensor mask projector; 7 Boarding ramp.

## Printed 58–59 / S080–081 — Howlrunner (Incom I-7)
- **Readout:** **11.4 m**; **4,100 G / 1,300 kph** (no hyperdrive class on readout — short-range); Incom; Galactic Empire / Wild Knights.
- Imperial Civil War / Palpatine return; later Saba Sebatyne’s Wild Knights. Designer **Jo Ewsli** (Kamar howlrunner predators). Fixed-wing + 2 fins; faster than old X/Y, slower than E-wing/TIE/I; has dedicated shields (unusual for Imperial short-range). Swarm tactics. First post-nationalization Incom ship to overshadow X-wing; Remnant staple. Exposed power generator = ace target.
- **Quote (Jo Ewsli anthropology):** “As I sat watching the howlrunners, the walls of the safari dome seemed to melt away. For a brief moment, I was part of the pack, hunting and howling with these magnificent predators. I have spent the rest of my life trying to recapture that feeling, from the cockpit of a starfighter.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Laser; 2 Deflector-shield gen; 3 Power generator (exposed); 4 Laser cannon power gen; 5 Sensor grid.
