# NEGVV 2003 — notes154: Millennium Falcon → MTT (printed 88–93 / scan 110–115)

## Printed 88–89 / S110–111 — Millennium Falcon (Modified CEC YT-1300 Transport)
- **Readout:** **27 m**; **3,000 G / Class 0.5 / 1,050 kph**; Corellian Engineering Corporation; Han Solo.
- Battered freighter; fastest. Mods: military rectenna; 2 quad laser turrets (+ later autotracking by Jowdrrl); Star Destroyer armor macrofused by Shug Ninx; augmented deflectors; Class 0.5 HD with ~3-min start-up; 3 droid brains; concealed smuggling compartments. Lando won ~4 yrs pre-Yavin → lost to Han sabacc. Yavin, Endor, Thrawn, YV (Chewie death); Leia current copilot.
- **Quote (Han):** “It's the ship that made the Kessel Run in less than twelve parsecs! I've outrun Imperial starships, not the local bulk cruisers, mind you. I'm talking about the big Corellian ships now.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Quad laser; 2 Concussion-missile tubes; 3 Armor plating; 4 Deflector shield gen; 5 Sensor dish; 6 Drive units; 7 Smuggling compartments (Death Star boarding hide).

## Printed 90–91 / S112–113 — Mon Cal Cruiser (*Home One*) (Mon Calamari MC80 Star Cruiser)
- **Readout:** **1,200 m**; **1,550 G / Class 1**; Mon Calamari; Rebel Alliance / New Republic.
- Modified civilian/colony ships donated to Alliance. Std: 20 ion / 48 turbolasers / 6 tractor; bay ≤36 fighters; thick hull + overlapping deflectors from bulbous pods. *Home One*: 36 ion / 29 TL / 120 fighters (10 sqns). Mon Cal ergonomics (body motion + holodisplays distorted to other spp). Variants: MC80B (*Mon Remonda*, +1 yr Endor); MC90 (*Galactic Voyager*, first dedicated battle cruiser: 75 TL / 35 ion / 6 proton tubes); Viscount-class Star Defender; light cruisers (*Poesy*: 14 TL / 18 ion / 6 heavy tractor).
- **Quote (Mon Mothma memoirs):** “Make no mistake, the Mon Calamari saved the galaxy. Their cruisers protected our fleet at the Battle of Endor, allowing the starfighters to penetrate the second Death Star’s core. Without Admiral Ackbar and his people, we would all be the Emperor’s slaves.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Tractor-beam projector; 2 Turbolaser pod; 3 Sensor array; 4 Drive system; 5 Main reactor; 6 Modular pod (shields/TL/sensors/ion).

## Printed 92–93 / S114–115 — MTT (Trade Federation Multitroop Transport / Baktoid)
- **Readout:** **31 m** long / **13 m** tall; **35 kph**; Baktoid Armor Workshop; Trade Federation.
- Delivers mass troops; Naboo. Extendable rack ≤**112** folded battle droids (rack removable for unarmored carriers). Heavy frontal armor + alloy studs; 4 independent antipersonnel blasters. Battering-ram into structures → unload inside. Battle-droid pilot + gunner; preprogrammed / DCS updates. Kuat Premion Mk. II power generator (print note says exhaust under “AAT” — likely MTT typo).
- **Quote (Hexler Pend, Naboo RSF Keren):** “We were hiding in the armory and then *bam!* The whole wall just fell down. Three tons of duracrete reduced to rubble by the business end of an MTT… I’m not ashamed to admit that we surrendered.”
- **Schematics:** 1 Twin blaster cannons; 2 Power generator housing; 3 Control center; 4 Repulsor thrusters; 5 Armored hull; 6 Exhaust vents; 7 Droid hatch.
