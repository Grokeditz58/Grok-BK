Unique access (panels/phrases); Bodo Baas Holocron auto-activates when Force-attuned grasps. Interactive hosts = personalities of past Masters; restrict advanced Force until student proves responsibility. Palpatine destroyed many in Purge. Luke first ~decade after Obi-Wan tutorials: Bodo Baas Holocron liberated by Leia from reborn Emperor; later destroyed.
- **Quote crop target:** interactive hosts / Purge / Bodo Baas / Leia liberate.

## Printed 208–209 / scan 119 — ORGANIC GILL (Mon Calamari)
- Engineered biotech symbiont; transparent gelatinous blob over nose/mouth filters ocean water → breathable air. Accepts chin protector / goggle clamps / comlink (mic+earphones) permanently. Many O2 species (human/Sullustan); lasts weeks; **200** cr. Leia + Cilghal used to find Ackbar at Mkbuto Seatree Preserve.

Max range **50 m**; cells drain **1 min**; transmitter receptors/matter transmitters burnout ~**3** uses. Beyond NR tech; theory: matter→energy or hyperspace sheath. Magwit befriended then stole from alien owner. Boba Fett recruited Magwit → trick pirate **Bar-Kooda** through hoop into *Bloodstar* cargo hold.
- **Quote crop target:** real teleport / Fett / Bar-Kooda.
