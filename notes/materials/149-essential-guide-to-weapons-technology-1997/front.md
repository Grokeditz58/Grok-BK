## Introduction (paraphrase; 1996–1997 edition-specific)
- Opens with Yoda ESB quote (Adventure/Excitement/Jedi craves not).
- Scope: handbook of **100 devices** (thermal detonators, bowcasters, Lobot implant, Imperial cloaking, etc.).
- Six sections: Ranged; Melee; Starship/planetary weapons & defenses; Communications & sensors; Security; General equipment.
- Theme: reliance on technology limits us (Luke vs DS1 without targeting computer); still “never hurts to have the right tool handy.” — Bill Smith, June 16, 1997.

## Blaster firing process (from Blasters entry; paraphrase / technical)
Gas → **XCiter (gas conversion enabler)** → actuating blaster module → particle+light beam → **prismatic crystal housing** → **galven** focusing down barrel. Power pack (small arms) or generator (heavy).
