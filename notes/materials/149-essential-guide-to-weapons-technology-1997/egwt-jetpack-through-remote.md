## Printed 188–189 / scan 109 — JET, ROCKET, AND REPULSOR PACKS (Mitrinomon Z-6)
- Outdated transport but romanticized (Old Republic Rocket-jumpers ~4,000 BBY). Boba Fett Z-6: intake→turbines; ≤**100 m** horiz / **70 m** vert; directional nozzles. **30 kg**; fuel **20** bursts; **300** cr; lift user +**100 kg**. Forearm panel + helmet verbal. Repulsor: slow **40 km/h** / **10 min** / no zero-G. Hybrids for hover/stability. Diagram includes **homing missile** atop Z-6.
