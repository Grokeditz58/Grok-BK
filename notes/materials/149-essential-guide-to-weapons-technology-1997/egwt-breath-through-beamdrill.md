# EGWT 1997 — notes149: Breath Mask → Beamdrill (printed 172–179 / scan 101–104)

## Printed 172–173 / scan 101 — BREATH MASK (Gandorthral Roamer-6)
- Portable air purifier; Solo/Chewie/Leia vs mynocks. Near-vacuum → poisonous; 6 scrubber layers; compress thin / dilute thick; heat/chill; can convert compounds (CO2→O2). Filters **1 h** replaceable; compressed tank **10 min** emergency; coupler to backpack or microfine scrubbers. Built-in comlink. Not full vacuum or skin-penetrating corrosives → need enviro suit. Boushh-like filters for Ubese trace gases on human worlds.

## Printed 174–175 / scan 102 — VAPORATOR (Pretormin GX-8)
- Arid worlds (Tatooine). Low-power ionized field + refrigerated cylinders/chiller bars; condenses at **1.35%** humidity → underground tanks. Computer adjusts vs wind/temp; solar-charged cells. Tatooine: spaced **250 m** → ~**1.5 L/day**. Binary programming (Owen bought C-3PO for binary load lifters). Sand + Tusken threats; locks/voice recognition. Portable versions in survival kits/escape pods.

## Printed 176–177 / scan 103 — ELECTROBINOCULARS (Neuro-Saav TD2.3)
- Stereoscopic + computer enhance; ~**100** cr; plastic casing; cells ≤**6 months**. Instant focus; distance/elevation/azimuth readouts; ≤**500×** mag; day/near-dark chips; flash dampeners. 8 preprogrammed zooms; secondary non-electronic **100×** backup. Data ports for holo/datapad/recording rod; TD2.3 can sync blasters (fire vector). **TT-4** internal recorder ≤3 h / >**2,000** cr. Luke Tatooine watched *Tantive IV* vs *Devastator* / searched R2. Macrobinoculars cheaper, no enhance chips → truer but less detailed/dark-weak, more rugged.

## Printed 178–179 / scan 104 — BEAMDRILL (Karflo G2-GE)
- Intense plasma pulses cut stone/rock/duracrete/durasteel/armor. Mining/construction/yards. Single-worker on repulsor sled **15 km/h**. Beam **1 cm–1.5 m**; low melts plastics; full vaporizes waste. Rotate **180°**; sensor strips; 3 fuel slugs ≤**10 h**; cooling + auto shutoff if operator leaves sled. Variants G2-GS (life-support yards); **TS-Titan** 10 m / 10 m beam. Solo Dellalt: Maltorran cut war-robot armor with beamdrill → power-core explosion killed him (DL-44 couldn’t penetrate).
