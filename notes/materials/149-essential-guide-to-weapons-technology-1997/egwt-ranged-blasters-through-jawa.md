# EGWT 1997 — notes149: Ranged Weapons intro → Jawa Ionization Blaster (printed 2–17 / scan 016–023)
1997 paraphrase; quotes only when crop verified full-passage.

## Printed 2–3 / scan 016 — RANGED WEAPONS (section intro)
- Sociology: Wookiees hunting→troopers; Mon Cal cast out enslavers; Rodians clan wars→bounty hunting.
- Evolution: spears/darts/bows → slug throwers/missiles/flame/mag-accel/sonic → portable power cells + high-energy gases → beam tubes → blasters dominate.
- Unique: bowcaster (strength/recoil; Kashyyyk); Jawa ionization (disable droids); Stokhli spray sticks (Manress game; mil secondary).
- Terminology: personal / light artillery (T-21, E-Web; crews) / artillery (fortified) / planetary (orbit).
- Technical: range, ammo/fire rate (actuator cooling; power-cell discharge), LOS vs guided vs unguided.

## Printed 4–5 / scan 017 — BLASTERS (BlasTech DH-17)
- Most common sidearm; BlasTech / Merr-Sonn / SoroSuub. Hold-out → E-Web.
- **Firing process:** gas → XCiter → actuating module → particle+light beam → prismatic crystal → galven barrel.
- DH-17: Imp Navy + Rebel standard; opt **30 m** / max **120 m**; pierces trooper armor, not hull; stun ≤10 min human. Gas >500 shots; pack 100; reload ~5 s. Semi; full-auto mod risks melt/explosion ≤20 s.
- Diagrams: scope, power pack, cooling vents; cutaway XCiter/actuating module/gas chamber/emitter.

## Printed 6–7 / scan 018 — BLASTER RIFLE (BlasTech E-11)
- Stormtrooper standard; compact; one-hand accurate; opt **100 m** / max **300 m**; computer-enhanced scope (smoke/haze/low-light). Tubular gas chamber wraps barrel; slide-out rear module. Treetol liquid cooling → forward vent capacitor. Pack 100 shots; stun/full/semi/auto/pulse. Empire exclusive sales but Rebels via black market/theft. SoroSuub Stormtrooper One → Freedom One clone; also Heavy Tracker 16, Merr-Sonn G8, BlasTech A280.
- Diagrams: folded/extended stock; rotating coupler releases internals.

## Printed 8–9 / scan 019 — HEAVY BLASTER PISTOL (BlasTech DL-44 + Merr-Sonn Power 5)
- Han Solo fame; rifle punch in sidearm; close-quarters; punches trooper armor. Opt **25 m** / max **50 m**. XCiter drains **4×** standard pistol; pack only **25 shots**; grip **cautionary pulser** at ≤5 shots; quick-release. Solo custom: motion-sensor scope + enhanced low-light; galven mods ↑ damage no extra drain. Duels Greedo, Uul-Rha-Shan, Gallandro; still carries for family. Merr-Sonn Flash 4 knockoff; Power 5 more compact.
- **Stat-like ranges/pack counts = stats, not quotes.**

## Printed 10–11 / scan 020 — SPORTING BLASTER (Drearian Defender)
- Low-power; Leia Senate conceal; often legal; honor duels; 350 credits; opt **30** / max **60 m**. Disassembles 3 parts (code panel); reassemble <10 s. Competes Merr-Sonn Quick 6.

## Printed 12–13 / scan 021 — LIGHT REPEATING BLASTER (BlasTech T-21)
- Deadliest Imp blaster one soldier can carry/operate; Burning Sands sandtroopers Tatooine; Rebel/NR use. Belt tripod + **20 kg** backpack gen; setup <30 s. Tripod max **300** / opt **150 m**. Pack 25 shots; backpack continuous but heat → **≤1 shot/s** or barrel melts. Slices personal armor, force fields, light vehicle armor.

## Printed 14–15 / scan 022 — E-WEB REPEATING BLASTER
- Emplacement Weapon, Heavy Blaster; BlasTech+Merr-Sonn (EWHB-10). Opt **200** / max **500 m**; punches snowspeeder armor. Ideal 2-man (gunner + gen monitor); solo = greatly reduced efficiency. Eksoan Class-4T3 gen (overheats) + Gk3 Cryocooler; 3 m conduit. Cold setup **15 min**. F-Web +shield needs 3rd crew. EWeb(15) post-Emperor: autoconfigure <5 min; Gk7 Cryocooler ≤1 hr continuous.

## Printed 16–17 / scan 023 — JAWA IONIZATION BLASTER
- Cobbled: strip rifle internals except pack; add restraining bolt + accu-accelerator (often from capital ion drives). Ion stream = droid “halt.” Fragile; spectacular explosions on fail. Opt **8** / max **12 m**; deactivate droid ≤20 min no permanent damage; organics = painful sting only. Compare Merr-Sonn DEMP gun (pistol/carbine; stun→destroy battery).
