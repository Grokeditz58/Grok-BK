## Prologue: Jacen in the blood; Tsavong Lah's new foot

## Chapter 2: Anakin aboard the Errant Venture
> Somewhere between the Corellian Trade Spine and the Kathol sector, the Star Destroyer Errant Venture dropped out of hyperspace, reoriented its massive wedge-shaped frame, and resumed lightspeed. An uninformed observer would have had less than a minute to wonder what a Star Destroyer was doing in such an out-of-the-way part of space and why it was painted red. / Deep in the Destroyer’s belly, Anakin Solo hardly noticed the transition, so intent was he on what he was doing.
