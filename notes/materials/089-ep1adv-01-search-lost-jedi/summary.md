# EA1 Search for the Lost Jedi: summary

- Full read of the scan (OCR l.1–2087). The epub extract is truncated: the rest of Ch. 2 through Ch. 9 are missing (see README).

## Top feats
1. **Qui-Gon Force-shatters a giant hydraulic propeller underwater** by visualising its blades bending, which causes an explosion (p.41).
2. **Qui-Gon kills 6 security droids "within seconds"**, faster than their photoreceptors can track (p.33–34). He also redirects bolts to burst a water tower and wipe out 8 more (p.35).
3. **Obi-Wan, blindfolded, beats 3 armory droids in under 11 seconds**, with deflections and a blur-speed leap (p.7–8).
4. **Qui-Gon cuts one Bartokk in half and disarms another in a single sweep** (p.71). He also throws his saber to cut a droid and catches it (p.61), and reflects a double-barrelled guard droid's fire back at it (p.64).
5. **Telepathy with a tentacled moat creature** (p.39). Vel Ardox outdraws Obi-Wan (p.26).

## Anti-feats
- Obi-Wan doesn't sense Qui-Gon entering (p.8).
- Qui-Gon fails to Force-unlock the hatch under stress (p.45) and crushes his own comlink.
- The mind trick fails on a Kloodavian (species immunity, p.48).
- Qui-Gon's droid override fails (p.69).

## Lore and continuity
- Jorus C'baoth is Palpatine's adviser. The Council roster includes Yaddle and Yarael Poof. There are over 10,000 Jedi.
- The Xi Charrians hold the TF droid-fighter monopoly. The TF wants 50 hyperdrive-capable fighters (Sidious's "galactic expansion plan").
- Species: Kloodavians (limb regeneration, mind-trick immunity), Bartokks (hive mind, hives of 15, parts that keep fighting), Blubreen, Baxthrax.
- Rhinnal has a Jedi chapter house.
- Adi once saved Qui-Gon's life; the story is left untold here.
