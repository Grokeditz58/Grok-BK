# EA1 Search for the Lost Jedi: notes, whole book

- Source: the Scholastic scan OCR (OCR l.1–2087). Printed page numbers are cited as p.N.

## Front matter (OCR l.1–148)
- Cover; the series ad ("The galaxy is yours… #1 Search for the Lost Jedi, #2 The Bartokk Assassins… and more to come"); title page; copyright page: ISBN 0-439-10138-7, © 1999 Lucasfilm, first Scholastic printing September 1999.
- Introduction: the 12-member Council guides "over ten thousand Jedi Knights". Qui-Gon "might have been a member of the Council", but he chose the life of a Knight.

## Ch. 1: Temple training arena (p.7–10)
- Obi-Wan, **25**, is blindfolded against three armory droids. He deflects bolts back and kills two, shears the third's arm, blurs across the arena and cleaves it in half: **"just under eleven seconds"** (p.7–8).
- Anti-feat: he never sensed Qui-Gon enter. "You were focusing too much attention on the droids."
- Qui-Gon has friends in the lower levels of Coruscant.

## Ch. 2: The Council (p.13–20)
- Lists the Council roster, which includes Yaddle and Yarael Poof. **Jorus C'baoth** is "personal advisor to Senator Palpatine".
- Adi Gallia is missing on Esseles: Darpa Sector, on the Perlemian, capital Calamar, a hyperdrive R&D centre. She has missed two reports.
- An anonymous data card reports that Trinkatta Starships is building **50 experimental droid starfighters with hyperdrives**. Oppo Rancisis: they are "flying hired guns".
- The Council suspects a member world is plotting civil war, so the Senate is kept out.
- Qui-Gon: "I wouldn't be standing here now if she hadn't saved my life". The story is never told in this book.
- Team: **Vel Ardox** (female Blubreen, amphibious, Ploo Sector) and **Noro Zak** (winged Baxthrax).
- Mace orders Obi-Wan to stay. Qui-Gon tricks him aboard the *Radiant VII*, which has a crew of 8 (the "stuck hatch" ruse): "you're my responsibility".

## Ch. 3: Esseles, the checkpoint (p.23–27)
- The factory is an old fortress of about 2 km², with a moat, an observation tower and toxic chimneys. Noro scouts by air.
- The security droids say the plant is being "fumigated", and the chimneys shut. Qui-Gon senses Adi inside and injured.
- **Vel Ardox outdraws Obi-Wan** and cuts down both droids before they fire (p.26). Someone has reprogrammed the droids to kill.
- p.28: the Game Book choice interstitial, then the section title "Qui-Gon Jinn's Adventure: Search for the Lost Jedi".

## Ch. 4: The bridge and the moat (p.33–36)
- Qui-Gon goes in alone as the decoy. **He kills 6 security droids "within seconds"**, moving "faster than the droids' photoreceptors could follow" (p.33–34).
- He deflects 8 rooftop droids' fire into the water tower and bursts it ("thousands of liters"), sweeping them off the roof. He is "surprised by the magnitude" of the blast, then dives 10 m into the moat (p.35).
- He swims an underwater tunnel with a breather, sensing the walls through the Force.
- A tentacle seizes his ankle.

## Ch. 5: Tunnel and silo (p.39–42)
- Qui-Gon's empathy for all life-forms reaches the creature's mind, and it **telepathically apologises** ("I mistook you for a large fish") and warns him about the current. This is Force telepathy with a non-sentient-seeming creature (p.39).
- The current carries him into a silo above a giant hydraulic propeller. **He uses the Force to visualise the blades bending, and the propeller tears itself apart in an underwater explosion** (p.41).
- The rusted hatch wheel crumbles; he is trapped.

## Ch. 6: Boll Trinkatta (p.45–50)
- Qui-Gon fails to Force-open the unseen lock under stress ("found it difficult to remain calm"). Anti-feat (p.45).
- He cuts through the hatch with his saber and crushes his own comlink landing.
- **Boll Trinkatta** is a Kloodavian, a yellow-scaled reptilian with a beak. His droids cut off his arm, and Kloodavians regrow limbs in days.
- An eight-armed maintenance droid ambushes; Qui-Gon dodges and decapitates it. Trinkatta: "Only a Jedi moves that fast!"
- **"Kloodavians are immune to Jedi mind tricks"**: the mind trick fails (p.48).
- Intel:
  - The Trade Federation ordered the fighters, although the **Xi Charrians** hold the TF's exclusive droid-fighter contract.
  - When Trinkatta balked at fitting hyperdrives, his test pilot vanished.
  - All 50 fighters have been stolen.
  - The droid control room is on Level 19 of the observation tower.

## Ch. 7: Assembly room (p.53–56)
- He trips two operations droids with his grappling hook "to conserve the charge on his lightsaber" (p.53).
- His breather is nearly empty. The chimney controls are wrecked, so he fires an unfinished starfighter's lasers through the 9-storey roof to vent the smoke and dives into a grease pit as debris crushes the ship.
- A Force vision shows Adi "high above ground". An unknown two-seat skyhopper sits on the 20-storey tower's top landing deck.

## Ch. 8: Spaceport and tower (p.59–65)
- He topples a raised transport onto 3 astromechs (slash a block, kick).
- The sabotaged landspeeder won't slow, so he bails out. The lift stops between floors 18 and 19, slamming him into the ceiling.
- **Saber throw:** he slices a shaft droid off its treads, catches the saber and cuts through the Level 19 doors.
- Over 100 droids march on the tower.
- The detention block holds 10 cells, 9 of them Adi holograms. He kills a hidden double-barrelled guard droid by reflecting its bolts. Adi is "Barely alive."
- His override command fails (p.69: "The saboteurs must have tampered with the computer").

## Ch. 9: Auto-destruct and Bartokks (p.69–73)
- He triggers the tower's auto-destruct (3-minute timer) and carries Adi up to the deck.
- **Bartokks:** insectoid assassins with a distributed hive mind, so severed parts keep attacking. They have 4 arms (two manipulators and two hooked claws) and travel in hives of 15.
- He warns them, "This encounter doesn't have to end in your deaths." Then, **in one sweep, he cuts one in half and de-arms the other**, then hacks the crawling pieces apart (p.71).
- He escapes in the Bartokk skyhopper as the tower explodes, and every droid freezes.

## Ch. 10: Regroup and Sidious (p.77–79)
- Vel: Adi suffered "a massive shock to her system". Obi-Wan suggests the Jedi chapter house on **Rhinnal** (medicine, Darpa Sector), and Vel and Noro fly her there.
- Gunray waits near the Ringali Nebula at the edge of the Colonies region, with Rune Haako.
- Sidious: the fighters "are essential for our galactic expansion plan". He orders Gunray to find and "silence… permanently" whoever informed the Council.

## Ch. 11: Deduction (p.83–85)
- Trinkatta's arm is regrowing.
- Qui-Gon: Bartokks travel in hives of 15 and only 2 were here, so 13 remain, and "they haven't left Esseles!" He drafts Trinkatta.
- Teaser: "Next adventure: Fight the Bartokk Assassins". Back cover: "…fellow Jedi are her only hope. The rescue has begun."
