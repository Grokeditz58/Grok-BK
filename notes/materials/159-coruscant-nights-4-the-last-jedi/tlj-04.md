## Excerpts and era intros (l.8895–11158), not counted toward The Last Jedi feats

- **EXCERPT: Dawn of the Jedi: Into the Void (Tim Lebbon), "DARK MATTERS" (l.8895–9246).** Je'daii Ranger Lanoree Brock is recalled to Tython. Council briefing on a dark-matter device meant to open a rumored Old City hypergate (would create a mini black hole and swallow the system). The man is her supposedly dead brother Dalien Brock / the Stargazers. Private message pod from Master Dam-Powl. Ends with Lanoree distracted by memories of Dal (l.9246). NOT COUNTED.
