## Part One "Vaster Than Empires"; Ch. 1 (l.271–491)
- I-Five mimics Tuden Sal's voice to get *Far Ranger* (modified Corellian Action VI) clearance from Westport (l.273–297). Jax is at the yoke. Recap of the Patterns of Force climax: a Whiplash member betrayed them; another "self-immolated trying to assassinate Vader"; "In the thrall of a potent drug that enhanced Force abilities in unpredictable ways, Vader had lashed out wildly… The release of energy had been enough to vaporize the unfortunate Haninum Tyk Rhinann… Both of them had fallen a great distance. Rhinann had died." "Vader had vanished." (l.327–331). Jax knows Vader was Anakin: "He had sensed it through the Force, and in their last encounter Vader had confirmed it with a slip of the tongue that might well have been intentional." "The man who wouldn't die." (l.333–335). They are moving Thi Xon Yimmon off Coruscant after four near-deaths in three weeks; Pol Haus tipped two plots (l.351–353). Photonic-charge attack near a tavern leaves "photonic imprints of people on the walls" (l.365–367).
- SPECIAL WATCH (PRIMARY HIT), Jax spoken to Den, TLJ ch. 1 (l.339): "Nothing. But Den, if he'd died, I think I'd know that. There would have been a huge shift in the Force if a being of that much focused power was destroyed." Immediate context, Laranth at l.347: "Sorry, Den. I'm of the same opinion. He might be severely injured and in a bacta tank somewhere, but he's not dead. The most we can hope for is that he'll be out of commission long enough for us to get Yimmon to safety." Related context, Jax narration, l.277: he will not Force-nudge the dispatcher because "Even something as minor as that could alert Darth Vader to their movements … if Vader was, against all odds, still alive." Then l.279–281: "Jax believed that he was. Even though he hadn't sensed the Dark Lord's uniquely powerful indentation in the fabric of the Force lately, it was difficult to conceive of such power, such concentrated evil, being gone, being over, being done. And until he gazed upon Vader's corpse with his own eyes, until he could reach out and touch him with the tendrils that constituted his own connection with the living Force and sense no reciprocation … Well, until that came to pass, Jax knew he couldn't be too careful." (These are the only watch hits in the novel; later passages about Vader's death are political or personal, not Force-shift claims. See summary / COVERAGE.)
- LORE (Cephalons): time is "plastic"/Den: "squishy"; they see time as spatial; punctuated intelligence / sub-brains; linked through the Force; tenses lost on them (l.399–405). Jax, disguised as an Elomin diplomat, meets Aoloiloa ("the one before Lo and after Il") in a methane loft (l.405–411). Message: "Depart you (have/will)?"; "Crux" / "Nexus, … Locus. Dark crosses/has crossed/will cross light."; "At crux: Choice is/has been/will be loss. Indecision is/has been/will be all loss."; "Choice upon choice. … Indecision is/was/will be cumulative." (l.415–437).
- Recap: Vader used "a rare biological agent to enhance his own connection to the Force"; un-enhanced he might have succeeded; "the Dark Lord had overreached and defeated himself." (l.473). Yimmon: maybe the attacks are a method to make him leave Coruscant (l.481–485). Tuden Sal wants their best near Palpatine "and Vader, if he re-emerges"; Jax: not if, when (l.489–491). Jax has a new lightsaber from a crystal "from an unexpected source" and has stowed "the Sith blade he'd been using" (l.391–393).

## Ch. 2 (l.493–771)
- Route debate: Laranth and I-Five wanted a direct shot into Wild Space and Myto's Arrow (unstable corridor past "the Twins" binary; magnetic fields cloak attitude changes) (l.495–497). Laranth: "After Vader crushed the miners' revolt last year, the Emperor has kept a watchful eye on things" (Bandomeer) (l.499–503). Hydian Way: Bandomeer, Botajef, Celanon, Feriae Junction, Toprawa (Antarian Rangers) (l.507). Jax's "deep but fragile hope" he is not "the last one" Jedi (l.509). LORE: ionite cancels charge, useful vs shields/comms/weapons (l.513). Toprawa ten days after leaving Coruscant (l.517). Yimmon cites Ki-Adi-Mundi: "the Force flows in the sap of forests like these"; Ki-Adi died in Order 66 (l.533–537). FEAT (Jax/Laranth): they feel "a silken fabric of Force energy" in the forest; Jax thinks he feels "a flicker—the merest shiver—of something not of the forest" (l.541–551).
- Disguises: Jax as freighter captain "Corran Vigil"; Laranth as "Pala D'ukal," lekku mantles hiding a truncated left lekku (old firefight injury) (l.561–583). Meet Aren Folee at the Mossy Glen Inn. Folee's ionite antisurveillance medallion fabricates boring trade talk from real speech (l.597–599). Folee has heard Yimmon works with Force-talented operatives, "one … a Twi'lek" (l.647–655). Laranth seriously considers basing with the Rangers; Jax: "We can't just leave Coruscant, Laranth." (l.679–699). Laranth: Tuden Sal still wants to assassinate Palpatine; Pol Haus may balance him (l.701–707). "It's not about what we want… It needs to be free of darkness." (l.711).
- Jax meditates with the Code, "There is no death; there is the Force" (l.731–751). LORE: he usually sees the Force as a web / tendrils; on Toprawa it flowed up from the world through the trees (l.755–759). He briefly recalls touching "the hem of the Cosmic Force" months earlier (l.761). Then: "And touched the black heart of vacuum. / Vader!" (l.763–765). He and Laranth both sense it and run for the bridge (l.767–771).

## Ch. 3 (l.773–1087) — ambush at the Twins; Laranth dies; Yimmon taken
- Ship yanked from hyperspace; ~20 Imperial ships (Vader's Fist / 501st); a cruiser's gravity generators pulled them out (l.817–875). Plan: skim the Twins' matter stream, reorient toward Dathomir, not Dantooine (l.849–859). FEAT (Jax): Force as "a web of freezing energy"; currents between the stars "linked through him like reins" (l.891). Second contingent waits on the other side; Jax knows the center ship carries Vader without probing (l.907). FEAT/LORE: five ships generate a stasis field "spun among them like a spider's web," "flawless and exact," "likely attributable to the presence of Darth Vader—likely with a select group of his Inquisitors" (l.939–943). Jax's Jedi training helps him resist the field's slowing of neurons (l.941). Vader switches to a tractor beam — Jax calls it a mistake (l.947).
- Laranth stays in the dorsal bay, cut and bleeding, to "send Lord Vader one last message"; fires the turbolaser point-blank into the belly of Vader's ship (l.1023–1033). Backlash shatters her bones, nearly severs the left lekku, puts shrapnel in her neck (l.1035–1051). DEATH (Laranth Tarak): she whispers something into Jax's ear and "gave her soul back to the Force. It felt as if the entire universe paused to observe the moment" (l.1059). Last words later identified as "There is no death; there is the Force" (l.1099–1101). (This is the Jedi Code, not a special-watch claim about Vader's death.)
- Vader and four troopers; his saber "blood-red" vs Jax's "blue-green" (l.1063–1065). Vader: "Your dead comrade… disabled our stasis field—an act for which she has paid with her life… I have but one more thing to take from you." Jax: "My life?" Vader: "No. That would be too easy… I am well aware of the Emperor's orders. I execute them in my own fashion. What I take from you, Jax Pavan, is the very thing you have been so jealously guarding" — Yimmon (l.1069–1079). FEAT (Vader): Yimmon's eyes roll back; Vader Force-grasps to arrest the slide (l.1079). Jax and I-Five leap; troopers fire; Yimmon taken; I-Five's head blasted off (l.1081–1087).

## Ch. 4 (l.1089–1157)
- Jax leaves Laranth's body, takes the miisai and one of her blasters, escapes with Den and I-Five's head in a starboard pod as *Far Ranger* dives into the starstuff (l.1091–1147). Jax recites the Cephalon warning as fulfilled: "I choose Yimmon—I lose Laranth…" (l.1143). I-Five (optics flickering): "There is no death; there is the Force." (l.1145). Vader "almost casually blasted I-Five to bits" and "just as casually caused Jax's muscles to lock in titanic spasm," then left (l.1153). Ranger escort recovers them (l.1155–1157).

## Ch. 5 (l.1159–1367)
- Dart ships take them to Aren's ship, then Toprawa "Mountain Home": holographic cliff, cavern lake, waterfall (l.1161–1175). Ships include a scorched red Delta-7 Aethersprite found drifting empty after Geonosis, navcom wiped, no identifiable Jedi imprint (l.1177–1199). Jax still carries two sabers (Sith blade from "an anonymous someone" plus the new one he and Laranth made), Anakin's pyronium "for safekeeping," and his father's Sith holocron (l.1241). Degan Cor co-leads with Aren (l.1235–1261). I-Five: only Whiplash or a handful on Toprawa knew they'd leave the filed itinerary at the Twins — possible mole (l.1307–1319). Jax: at their last encounter Vader ingested a "powerful biotic agent" that "opened the floodgates on his Force perception"; overload might deaden or hypersensitize him (l.1303–1305). Degan offers Jax command of a fighter wing; Jax refuses until Yimmon is freed (l.1325–1337). FEAT/LORE (Yimmon): more mental discipline than some Jedi; Force-sensitive enough that maybe only Laranth knew how sensitive; when Vader reached out, Yimmon "seemed to… give [consciousness] up… disappear or—or shut off before Vader could control him," surprising Vader (l.1343–1347).

## Ch. 6 (l.1369–1663)
- Jax sends a terse encrypted message to Tuden Sal; no reply (l.1373). Meditation fails; he will not reach for Laranth in the Force and "could no longer pretend that Darth Vader was the reason for his reluctance" (l.1397). He weeps (l.1399). Workshop with Geri (Rodian orphan) and Candy (P2 + RX head hybrid). I-Five rejects a 5Y "garbage snipe" chassis. LORE: BB-4000 "Bobbie-Bot" HRD; LeisureMech failed; 3000-series melted (Kaird saw one in the Factory District); 4000s use neural-net processing, no melting; "Eerie Coulee Disorder" / pareidolia (l.1475–1509). I-Five takes a brainless DUM pit-droid chassis (Sacha's old "Ducky"); cortex in a removable mounting cage / sagittal crest with tri-clad durasteel (l.1521–1617). Sacha Swiftbird (ex-Podracer, cybernetic left eye) helps refit a Helix interceptor (l.1547–1555). Her scars remind Jax of Laranth's from Order 66 / Flame Night (l.1555). Ship named *Laranth*, then registry can't use that; later rechristened *Corsair* (l.1577–1901).

## Ch. 7 (l.1665–1773)
- Aren: Vader used "gravimetric distortions around the Twins to disguise his movements," then ion propulsion to position (l.1677). I-Five builds a holo-sim: Imperial ships came from toward the Core; extra "yellow" formations arrived with the 501st; a lone red contact (Jax infers Vader) altered course (l.1735–1763). Vague report of a brief Imperial presence on Mandalore; clearer reports of fighters in the Core (l.1767). They go to Coruscant as the most likely interrogation site (l.1769–1773).

## Ch. 8 (l.1775–1901)
- typography "tranquillity" (l.1799). Jax gropes for "the darkness that would be in Vader's wake"; flashback: "I have one more thing to take from you" — "Anakin Skywalker had said that" (l.1801–1811). Epiphany: this is Vader's choices, not Palpatine's; Vader "obeyed the Emperor in his own way" (l.1815). "Why do you hate me?" — answer: Jax survived Order 66; "When he looks at me… does he see what he might have been?" (l.1821–1825). LORE: pyronium from Anakin "alleged to be a source of unimaginable power," secret "revealed on the Sith holocron" from Rhinann / once sought by Lorn (l.1829). Sith holocrons "deeply disturbing to the Force and seductive"; it already "created a slight disturbance in the Force through its very presence" / "subtle pull"; Jax has never activated it (l.1831–1835). Memory: sparring with Anakin, sudden berserker assault; Anakin "radiating tendrils of blackest night"; "a black hole—sucking light and color"; Obi-Wan on the sidelines seemed not to notice (l.1837–1847). Jax reaches toward Vader's wake in the sim, then stops: "He'll sense you. He'll know you seek him." / "Leave it for now. Let him think you might be dead." (l.1851–1861). Night lift, no running lights.

## Ch. 9 (l.1903–2175)
- *Corsair* lands at a Westport satellite dock as "Corran Vigil." Pol Haus "arrests" them for show, then delivers them to a derelict maglev terminal; I-Five now has a weapons-grade laser by his optic (l.1909–1957). Hovertrain is the new Whiplash HQ (Haus's idea) (l.1981–2037). Captains: Sheel Mafeen, Fars Sil-at (Amanin, Sil's Place), Dyat Agni (Devaronian), Acer Ash (l.1999). They've swept for bugs; only six knew the plan on Coruscant. Sal suspects Folee (her last mission failed, accomplices caught, she wasn't) (l.2013–2019). Jax: neither he nor Laranth sensed anything off in Folee; Sacha "might bear watching" (l.2021–2027). Jax refuses to lead: "It's because of me that we're having to replace him" (l.2049). SCALING (Jax), TLJ ch. 9 (l.2059): "I've never known a Force-user as powerful as Vader." Haus: Jax is "the real prize" (l.2061). Sal is scrapping the whole network because Yimmon could jeopardize it; wants a "decisive and devastating" strike rather than a rescue (l.2069–2095).
- SCALING (Haus / Jax), TLJ ch. 9 (l.2153–2157): Haus: the Emperor "rules through secrecy and fear, while he has only one thing to fear himself … well, that is if he's smart enough to fear it." Jax: "Vader." (This is political fear, not a special-watch Force-shift claim.)

"Yimmon's separation destroys/has destroyed/will destroy us." "Us?" "All of us." Aoloiloa is afraid. Jax: betray the resistance? "Eclipse." (l.2355–2375). ## Ch. 11 (l.2377–2559)
- Jax interprets eclipse as the resistance blotted out, "only for a time" (l.2379–2385). Recap: Kaj Savaros compromised by Vader, "nearly destroyed," sent to Shili into the care of The Silent (l.2387–2389). LORE/FEAT: most beings have faded Force signatures; a trained user sees a pronounced one as "a bump or loop" (l.2391). He follows I-Five's Force signature to Honest Yarg's Droid Emporium — "You're not even supposed to have a Force signature." I-Five admits he experiences attachment and loss (l.2439–2455). I-Five finds large Imperial credits flowing to hidden Mandalore accounts — they infer bounty hunters, possibly to hunt Jedi (l.2469–2471). Haus: Vader seen at ISB and has met Palpatine, but no special-prisoner activity; "the cream" of the Inquisitors, including Tesla, shipped out yesterday on an unregistered itinerary; Vader's long-range shuttle is in preflight (l.2481–2499). Jax goes alone as Lt. Pel Kwinn (l.2517–2559).

## Ch. 12 (l.2561–2847)
- Palace "like a malignant coral reef." FEAT (Jax): Force-suggests a bored human guard ("I've been on this duty for months… You've seen me here before") (l.2581–2587). Worries Vader might sense even that "infinitesimal use" (l.2595). Garden of Imperial heroes: Palpatine, Phow Ji of the Drongaran occupation (l.2613). Jax dons an Inquisitor robe; guards avert their gazes (l.2633–2639). FEAT: can't Force a turbolift AI; pries doors, Force-jumps the shaft, hangs under a descending car, uses a gentle Force buffer, then Force-slides to Level Nine (l.2643–2671). He searches Vader's Lambda shuttle with Force tendrils for Yimmon; finds troopers and a taozin-blurred Inquisitor, not Yimmon (l.2685–2693). Vader appears; Jax almost leaps down (no saber; Laranth's lesson: a Jedi shouldn't limit to one weapon) (l.2703–2705). Vader pauses on the ramp and looks up; Jax bows and walks away. "If he'd known me, he would have challenged me… I would have felt it." (l.2711–2727). Back at Westport, a real Inquisitor had been sniffing the pad; I-Five fires a fingertip bolt as a "thieves" ruse (l.2829–2847).
