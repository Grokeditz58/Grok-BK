## Part Three "Journey's End"; Ch. 29–30 (l.5719–5899)
- Haus learns Vader has returned "without warning." He advances a sting on the maglev; Sal planted a decoy — one abandoned car **explodes**, injuring officers including Bothan Lt. Kalibar Droosh (l.5723–5757). The villa attack goes ahead anyway: jetty burns, villa intact, bodies in the courtyard including a Sakiyan (Sal *appears* dead at first glance) and most of the council; Vader steps into the courtyard (l.5771–5789). "Constable"/"Poet" message to Toprawa: Whiplash shattered; Vader was there (l.5867–5877). I-Five: Vader will squeeze Yimmon harder; Yimmon knows about Toprawa and Dantooine (l.5885–5887). Jax pretends to sleep, then takes the stripped silver **Delta-7** and lifts off alone (l.5895–5899).

## Ch. 31 (l.5901–6049)
- DEATH: Haus finds the hidden hovertrain; Tuden Sal is alive inside ("A general doesn't go into battle… I sent them on a suicide mission"). Sal admits he used Whiplash for a personal vendetta (l.5967–5997). Lt. Droosh (ISB mole since his transfer) interrupts to arrest them for reward. Sal charges, is shot in the ribs; his return shot kills Droosh between the eyes. Sal's last words: "Hide… the train. The data…" / "Stupid… so many mistakes." Dies (l.6023–6045). Haus will hide the train with Sheel.

## Ch. 32 (l.6051–6141)
- Four-day solo trip in the Aethersprite. Without the miisai, Jax uses the **Sith holocron** as a cloak/locus and finds Yimmon by triangulating Vader's "seemingly random" deflection fields — "randomness was a chimera. Patterns… emerge despite the most rigorous attempts to avoid them." (l.6065–6071). Then a Cephalon *network* (Aoloiloa "but not") cuts in: "Listen. Indecision is all loss. Yimmon's separation destroys us all." Message narrows: "Separation destroys us" / "Separation destroys." "Seek, communion. Seek sisters." (l.6079–6105). He Force-sets a course and later realizes it is already laid for **Dathomir**. LORE recap: Witches vs Nightsisters/Nightbrothers; Infinity Gate/Star Temple destroyed ~13 years earlier; planet "all but quarantined"; Allya's mantra "Never concede to evil." (l.6125–6137).

## Ch. 33 (l.6143–6181)
- Tesla orbits Yimmon, probing; Yimmon flinches then holds. Tesla almost tells him Whiplash is dead (Vader had so informed him) but stops, then *does* plant the coup/assassination/network-shattered suggestion. A "flash flood of Force energy… static rain" flays Tesla as he pretends to leave — gone as swiftly as it came. He wonders if Yimmon is a secret adept and is eager to put "physical separation of Yimmon's dual cortices" to Vader (l.6145–6181).

## Ch. 34 (l.6183–6323)
- Jax overflies the Infinity Plain ruins; the holocron resonates with residual power (l.6191). Lands at **Singing Mountain Clan**; met by Magash (Dathomiri Zabrak) and Duala (human) plus warriors with static lances and cortosis staffs (l.6195–6201). They "felt [the Jedi's] dying echoes in the Force." Scan: "there is darkness in you—a confusion of shadow and light." Jax: mate dead, clan leader taken by the Dark Lord; losses must be "redeemed," not avenged. He turns to face her and bows. "Not avenged, but redeemed." (l.6217–6279). (This is Jax about his own losses, *not* a claim that Vader was redeemed or found a power "stronger than the dark side.") Clan matriarch **Augwynne Djo** receives him; Magash objects: "he is a stranger. And a man." (l.6297–6323). Augwynne treats Jax as a Jedi first; Magash is distressed that a *man* can channel the Force (Dathomiri men are "gatherers… breeding stock") (l.6307–6323).

## Ch. 35 (l.6325–6415)
- On Toprawa, I-Five reports Jax has taken the starfighter. Sacha: everyone already knew a Jedi was among them (l.6333–6341). typography: "karking" (l.6351). Plan: Den, I-Five, and Sacha will pose as a Black Sun freighter and dock at Kantaros. I-Five sliced ident codes in Keldabe; they will fly as the *Raptor* out of Mandalore (l.6397–6413).

## Ch. 36 (l.6417–6565)
- Magash touches the Aethersprite and feels "a whisper of dark energy"; she chants a calming spell to the Unformed. Later she calls it **"the Dread Thing"** (l.6427–6445, 6547–6557). Jax tells Djo he was led by Laranth and the Cephalons' "Seek sisters"; he needs "a tool. A weapon. A strategy to use against the Dark Lord," possibly tied to residual Infinity Gate energy / "eddies in the Force… possibly in time, itself." He does not mention the holocron (l.6451–6481). Djo grants a visit if a Sister accompanies him. Magash volunteers. He veils the holocron with a miisai-memory Force cloak; Magash immediately notices the Dread Thing is *gone* from the ship (l.6541–6557).

## Ch. 37 (l.6567–6791)
- Sacha finds the hidden **Sith lightsaber** in the miisai planter. I-Five/Den explain: anonymous source before the Aurra Sing fight; Jax thought it might have been Sing's (she had a Jedi weapon); he used it until he and Laranth built a new saber; the plan was to recystal it for a future Padawan (l.6625–6651). Haus and Sheel hide the first three cars of the train at a deep, nearly cut-off level with a hidden back door and dead-man wipe on the core (l.6665). In Sal's private correspondence Sheel finds an encrypted text to ISB/Vader: **"Urgent. Att'n Lord Vader. Some reason to suspect movement of Pavan and 'persons of interest' through Myto's Arrow."** plus a date range covering the Yimmon relocation (l.6695–6701). **Tuden Sal was the traitor** — anonymous, minimal data (not the exact Toprawa arrival). Motive: scare Jax/Yimmon off-world so they could not stop the Palpatine plot (l.6713–6719). Tesla visits Yimmon at mealtime: "Laranth Tarak, Den Dhur, Jax Pavan—all dead." Yimmon's hope/certitude persists; Tesla reads flashes of Jax outwitting the Inquisitorius, snatching Kaj, forcing Tesla to flee (l.6751–6775). Tesla: "He felt something far stronger than mere hope. Certitude." (l.6771 — *not* "a power stronger than the dark side"). Vader, having "dealt the rebels on Imperial Center a mortal wound," is **returning to Kantaros**. Tesla meditates that "it was a shame Jax Pavan was already dead, because Tesla would have liked very much to be the one to kill him." (l.6785–6791).

## Ch. 38 (l.6793–7097)
- On the walk to the Infinity Plain, Jax on Gray Paladins: less rigid; "the warrior attuned to the weapon" vs Jedi "weapon attuned to the warrior"; "a Jedi—or any Force-user—is the weapon." Magash: incantations are a focus, "never to concede to evil." Jax challenges Dathomiri slavery of men as evil (l.6799–6881). At the center: residual Force displacement. A misty female figure among the spires — a touch / reflection: **Laranth**. A vision of two pasts: one in which he calls her aft and she comes to safety; one in which she dies on the deck. "Two paths. Two pasts." He stumbles, cuts his palm on obsidian, the vision breaks (l.6915–6977). He wonders if the place is a temporal nexus, whether he came to "cheat Death… cheat time." "There is no death; there is the Force" — he is no longer sure he accepts it (l.6979–6987). On the slab the holocron pulses and the Star Temple "teemed with energies… that had driven the Infinity Gate and that had been bent to the service of the dark side." (l.6991–6999). Jax opens the outer plates; I-Five had once watched Yanth the Hutt manipulate the outer electron-lattice shells and posited a catalyst for deeper layers (l.7009–7011). **Jax's blood** is the catalyst: a drop makes a corner turn (l.7013–7017). A rodent's blood does not react. Conclusion: Ramage required **the blood of a sentient, most likely a Force-user**, in an amount that would cripple the opener if it were his own — i.e., a dark-side sacrifice of another Force-user, to keep light-siders out (l.7081–7083). Magash returns after feeling his anguish (l.7045, 7095–7097).

## Ch. 39 (l.7099–7181)
- In Keldabe, Sacha becomes fascinated with the Sith saber — "no evil from it. Power, yes, but no darkness." She practices with it in engineering; I-Five almost hears it (l.7101–7145). Haus/Sheel holo: Sal died later, "rather heroically"; he was the tipster; Toprawa allies can be trusted (l.7157–7179). I-Five: Vader's intercept was only approximate until he sensed Jax and Laranth (l.7175–7177).

## Ch. 40 (l.7183–7225)
- Jax, Augwynne, Magash, and Duala **commingle their blood** and open the holocron cooperatively — something Ramage "could [not] have imagined" (l.7187–7207). A flood of knowledge knocks Jax unconscious (l.7197–7225). typography: Magash "Darshi" once at l.7457 vs earlier "Drashi."

## Ch. 41 (l.7227–7319)
- *Corsair* as *Raptor* follows a Toydarian freighter into Kantaros; docking accepted (l.7277–7287). I-Five is a spiked Nemesis/protocol hybrid with self-transfer between chassis (l.7299–7303).

## Ch. 42 (l.7321–7523)
- In a mental Jedi Temple library metaphor, Jax selects: Force projections; Ramage on **pyronium**; healing; Force cloaking (miisai-style); an ancient Master on the nature of the Force; Force communications; Ramage's **"tunneling"** (focus so tight he touches nothing but the target) (l.7329–7343). The red core is a treatise on **time**: time as an ocean of currents and "islands" (fixed nexuses vs floating points) (l.7347–7359). He realizes the knowledge was torn from Cephalons by isolating them from their network — "Separation destroys us" (l.7365–7369). He leaves the physical holocron with Djo and asks that, if he survives, they **purge the knowledge from his mind** so neither he nor Vader can misuse it (l.7435–7439). He will not try to undo Laranth's death: cascade effects on every downstream current; temptation (l.7455–7463). Insight: to move an island you must change its currents, which alters every downstream island — only a cooperative network of minds "not entirely in the time stream" (Cephalons) could do it; a Sith collective could not, because of fear/distrust/ambition; nor can a lone remaining Jedi (l.7483–7497). FEAT (Jax): he floats the **pyronium** on Force tendrils; it absorbs and re-emits directed kinetic energy and stays aloft after he withdraws (l.7513–7521).

## Ch. 43 (l.7525–7749)
- Stationmaster Cleben: stay out of the **Red Zone**; no glitterstim "especially when he's here" — Vader (l.7563–7573). R2 units pass the Red Zone portal unchallenged; I-Five goes in as **"R2-Five."** (l.7647–7697). Jax's plan: locate Yimmon, send a projection as misdirection, approach from the opposite side. Blast cage and sonics are lesser concerns given his new skills; much hinges on Vader still believing him dead. "He couldn't possibly be prepared for a Jedi who had opened Darth Ramage's holocron." (l.7597–7607). I-Five finds the Vader-restricted detention portal at the asteroid's center (l.7717–7721), then nearly rolls past **Tesla and Vader** returning; Vader's mask reflects the R2 (l.7747–7749).

## Ch. 44 (l.7751–7857)
- Tesla feels Vader's agitation as "a chaotic, eddying current"; Vader is "a being… of towering passion" with "a deep, hot molten core beneath the icy exterior" (l.7753–7755). FEAT/LORE (Vader), TLJ ch. 44 (l.7771–7779): Vader felt the holocron opening across the galaxy: "A door opened, and out of it poured light and darkness… twilight. Like the moment before dawn." Tesla did not feel the twilight door. Tesla proposes surgically disconnecting Yimmon's cortices; Vader agrees and will program the surgical droid himself (l.7819–7823). Vader then forces Tesla to confess he suggested to Yimmon that Whiplash had fallen and the resistance would be "as dead as Jax Pavan." "The compulsion was stronger than mere words, leaving Tesla with the impression that Vader held his will in one gloved hand." (l.7849). A flare of rage; Tesla wonders if it was the disobedience or **the mention of the dead Jedi** (l.7849–7857).

## Ch. 45 (l.7859–7987)
- FEAT (Jax): Force-projects a shuttle-sized ice/rock into a corvette's path, soft-docks under its keel, rides inside its shields, then projects the Delta-7 as a courier into a diplomatic bay (l.7861–7875). I-Five reports Yimmon is being moved to medbay for Vader-sealed neurosurgery (l.7903–7915). Sacha's ionite **sensor recursor** loops cams; they stun two troopers, drug them, Sacha wears the armor, Den is "prisoner," I-Five escort (l.7887–7977). I-Five: "Vader looked at me… No one notices droids." (l.7981–7987).

## Ch. 46 (l.7989–8165)
- Jax "tunnels" to Yimmon; they've moved and **drugged** him (l.7991–7995). He uses pyronium to power the Aethersprite's ongoing disguise (leaving it behind, so he cannot use it as a personal power source: "With it, perhaps he would be Darth Vader's equal") (l.8029). He knocks out Elomin apprentice Renefra Ren with a temple-touch, takes a spare robe (l.8041–8049). Touching Yimmon, he also feels Tesla; he understands the Cephalon riddle: Vader will surgically separate Yimmon's brain (l.8051–8065). Tesla briefly contemplates "when, at last, the Emperor died and Darth Vader moved to choose an apprentice—or if Vader were destroyed and the Emperor had to choose a new champion" (l.8069) — succession, not a Force-shift claim. Sacha (untested but "gets feelings") and I-Five both sense a "shift"; I-Five asks if she's been tested for Force sensitivity (l.8113–8125).

## Ch. 47 (l.8167–8255)
- Jax's decoy draws Vader's "thick, viscous cable of frigid malice" and spiraling Inquisitor hostility (l.8173–8177). Tesla, probing Yimmon's dreaming cortex, receives Vader's burst then a visual: **Jax Pavan**. He cannot sense a Jedi signature (l.8187–8193). In the corridor he meets "Renefra Ren" who says, in another voice, "Yes… He is [on the station]." The real team exits the lift. FEAT (Tesla): Force-wrenches Sacha's rifle and slams her into the lift wall; FEAT (Jax): a Force wave "blew him heels-over-head." Lightsaber duel; a second projected Jax with a saber appears; Tesla: "You're the last Jedi, and you have the last Jedi lightsaber." (l.8221–8249). **DEATH, Probus Tesla, l.8253–8259:** Sacha, in trooper armor, activates the **Sith lightsaber** and kills him; his last thought is "Impossible…"

## Ch. 48 (l.8257–8433)
- They wake Yimmon with stimulant; Jax blurs his signature, then seeks Vader and is immediately felt — Vader is on the same level, coming for them, while troopers/Inquisitors sweep upward (l.8303–8305). typography: "right long with him" (l.8341) is a book typo for "along." Jax peels off the robe, tells I-Five to get them out, and sprints away to keep Vader off the group and to retrieve the starfighter (l.8333–8339). The others disguise Yimmon as an Inquisitor (Sith saber in his hands) and reach the *Laranth*/*Raptor*. I-Five is missing from the ship; in the hold an empty **LeisureMech BB-4000** crate (Geri's "further modifications") and the I-Nemesis is missing its arm-mounted blaster (l.8423–8433). I-Five has gone back for Jax in an HRD body.

## Ch. 49 (l.8435–8783) — Jax vs Vader
- Jax must not let Vader get Yimmon, the starfighter, *or him*: the knowledge in his head would mean "destruction of another order of magnitude. What it would mean to the Cephalon species alone was terrifying." (l.8439–8443). He almost turns left to face Vader for vengeance ("I would be vengeance") then rejects it as dark-side and runs right, for the ship (l.8445–8465). Closest non-watch line, l.8451: "If he could destroy Darth Vader—even if he sacrificed his own life in doing it—it would be worth it. Vader's death would serve to inspire more people to resist the Emperor's will. More important, it would deprive Palpatine of his greatest weapon… and it would avenge the destruction of the Jedi Order, the deaths of so many brilliant Force-users." (Inspiration, depriving Palpatine of a weapon, vengeance — not a Force-shift claim.)
- FEAT (Jax): Deflects trooper fire; slices two rifles; Force-yanks an apprentice into his superior's stroke (lightfoil spun away, apprentice down, not clearly a death) (l.8475–8493). Projects **Darth Vader** stepping from a lift, buying an escape (l.8495–8501). Real Vader Force-chokes him in the lift core: "Impressive… This is dark knowledge… Just as I felt it when you acquired it." Jax lets him glimpse the Ramage holocron. Vader: Anakin "is dead. Burned to a cinder and blown away by the winds of betrayal." Jax: "You were the betrayer." Offer: share the knowledge and live; "I have you, now. What do I need with [Yimmon]?" (l.8507–8555). FEAT (Jax): projected Yimmon+Den in a lift break Vader's concentration; a Force blast "sent the Dark Lord staggering back, one, two, three steps" (l.8561); Jax hovers prone six centimeters up and hurls past him (l.8565). Vader sees through projections that lack life signs — but these *hold* a beat longer than he expects. He then "scents" the pyronium-powered ship cloak. Irony: Anakin gave Jax the pyronium "for safekeeping" (l.8575–8591). FEAT (Jax): flings shielding, then Force-tumbles an entire shuttle off its struts onto Vader; rips a deck hatch and drops a level (l.8595–8603). Galley: hail of pots/trays, then turns kitchen droids (knives, pestles) on Vader (l.8625–8627). Vader: "Leave him to me!"
- Jax's insight: Vader needs the last Jedi not merely to die but to be *broken*; if Jax makes Vader end him unbowed, Anakin/Vader would be "without purpose" (l.8647–8653). Closest non-watch line, l.8659: "If he died, would that put an end to Vader's purpose? If Vader died, would that put an end to Jax's?" (Purpose, not a Force-shift claim.) Lightsaber duel; Vader hurls a deckhand at him (Anakin "knew what he would and would not do"); Jax stirs local time currents so Vader's next barrage hits where he *was* (l.8679–8687). Vader flings the shuttle Jax rolled under aside. Jax refuses surrender: "I'll die before I let you have what's in my head, Anakin." Vader: "As you wish… Jax." (l.8701–8703). Jax prepares to trigger the pyronium/ship self-destruct and take Vader with him.
- A burst of light with a weak, steady Force signature; a voice: "Run!" Blasterfire; Vader Force-leaps over Jax and under the Aethersprite; fire melts the port struts. The figure is **I-Five in a BB-4000 HRD**, Nemesis blaster on his android arm: "I am not losing you the way I lost your father." (l.8707–8733). Explosion; I-Five's synthflesh-stripped lower leg in flames, but Jax finds the intact HRD *head* ("This… is getting old.") (l.8737–8747). They escape in a two-seat courier. The Aethersprite self-destructs; pyronium "devoured the energy of the blast" and is jettisoned as untrackable flotsam. Vader *survived*: "he could feel the waves of cold rage even now." (l.8761–8765). Soft-dock with the *Laranth*. Den: "Welcome back, Jax. If you are back." "For good, this time." Course: **Dathomir** (l.8781–8783).

## Epilogue (l.8785–8893)
- Augwynne Djo purges Ramage's knowledge: events remain clear; *how* he influenced time currents is a "fuzzy hollow"; the concepts are "mere vapors" (l.8787–8789). Next: take Yimmon to Toprawa, reconnect with Haus and Sheel. Jax lets Sacha **keep the red lightsaber** (l.8809–8811). Yimmon on Tesla: "two brains instead of one… he wore his desires—and his fears—too close to the surface… Tesla had quite correctly suspected that separating my cortices would rob me of that advantage." (l.8815). Meditation with the miisai: "There is no death; there is the Force" finally understood — no cause for grief, no need for revenge; reconnection after having "uprooted his own 'tree.'" (l.8829–8833). I-Five (still in HRD) thought Jax projected the burst of light that covered his approach; Jax did not — he "felt a Force signature." They look at the miisai: implied **Laranth** (l.8843–8853). Jax will message Magash: if he is the last Jedi, "someone's got to rebuild the Jedi Order." He wonders if he might teach a droid the ways of the Force (l.8885–8893). Novel body + epilogue complete.
