Continuity: 19 BBY, after Patterns of Force (timeline l.11272–11282). Jax, Laranth, Den, I-Five and Thi Xon Yimmon leave Coruscant aboard *Far Ranger* after four near-deaths in three weeks. Jax believes Vader survived the bota-storm fall: "There would have been a huge shift in the Force if a being of that much focused power was destroyed" (l.339). At the Twins, Vader's 501st pulls them from hyperspace and a five-ship stasis field holds them; Laranth dies firing a point-blank turbolaser into Vader's ship, and Vader takes Yimmon (l.939–1087). Tuden Sal is later revealed as the anonymous tipster who pointed Vader at Myto's Arrow so the Palpatine plot could go ahead (l.6695–6719). Sal's Western Sea villa strike fails with Vader in the courtyard; Sal dies after killing the ISB mole Droosh (l.5771–6045). Jax opens Darth Ramage's holocron on Dathomir with Augwynne Djo, Magash and Duala by commingling blood (l.7187–7207), uses the knowledge at Kantaros Station, and refuses to undo Laranth's death. Sacha Swiftbird kills Inquisitor Probus Tesla with the Sith lightsaber (l.8253–8259). Jax vs Vader: projections, a Force blast that staggers Vader three steps (l.8561), a tumbled shuttle, and a stirred time current; I-Five in a BB-4000 HRD pulls Jax out. Vader survives. Augwynne purges the time knowledge; the implied Force signature that covered the rescue is Laranth (l.8787–8853).

Character quick bios:
- **Jax Pavan:** Jedi Knight, now using a new blue-green saber (the Sith blade is stowed, later hidden in the miisai). Knows Vader is Anakin (l.333). Navigates the Twins' matter-stream currents (l.891). Opens Ramage's holocron with commingled blood (l.7187–7207). Force-projects, tunnels, and in the Kantaros duel staggers Vader three steps and stirs local time currents (l.8559–8687). Refuses to undo Laranth's death. Asks Augwynne to purge the time knowledge (l.7435–7439, 8787–8789). Will try to rebuild the Jedi Order (l.8885).
- **Darth Vader:** Survived the prior book's fall. Jax: a being of "that much focused power" would have caused "a huge shift in the Force" if destroyed (l.339). Directs a five-ship stasis field, takes Yimmon, casually wrecks I-Five and locks Jax's muscles (l.939–1153). "I've never known a Force-user as powerful as Vader" (Jax, l.2059). "Planet-sized dent in the space–time continuum" (l.2985). Felt the holocron open across the galaxy (l.7771). Survives the Kantaros duel and the pyronium/ship blast; "waves of cold rage" (l.8761–8765). Anakin "is dead. Burned to a cinder…" (l.8535) — he is *not* redeemed in this book.
- **Laranth Tarak:** Gray Paladin. Dies at the Twins firing a point-blank turbolaser into Vader's ship; last words are the Jedi Code (l.1023–1101). Implied Force signature covers I-Five's rescue of Jax (l.8843–8853).
- **Probus Tesla:** Vader's Inquisitor; practices Soresu with a lightfoil (l.4795–4819). Assigned only to observe Yimmon; plans to surgically separate the Cerean's dual cortices (l.5253–5259, 7819–7823). Killed by Sacha Swiftbird with the Sith lightsaber (l.8253–8259).
- **I-Five (I-5YQ):** Has a Force signature (l.2439–2455). Rebuilt through a DUM chassis, then a Nemesis hybrid, then a BB-4000 HRD. Rescues Jax: "I am not losing you the way I lost your father." (l.8733).
- **Thi Xon Yimmon:** Cerean Whiplash leader; more mental discipline than some Jedi; can "shut off" before Vader can control him (l.1343–1347). Cephalon warning "Yimmon's separation destroys us all" is about surgical separation of his dual cortices (l.8061–8065). Rescued at Kantaros; taken toward Toprawa.
- **Tuden Sal:** Sakiyan; authors the Palpatine assassination plot; anonymous tipster who pointed Vader at Myto's Arrow (l.6695–6719). Dies after a suicide villa strike and after killing ISB mole Lt. Droosh (l.6023–6045).
- **Prince Xizor:** Falleen Black Sun Vigo (Dramatis typo: "Faleen"). Confirms Jax is alive, takes a promissory note, creates a diversion that draws Vader back to Coruscant, then pulls the plug when an *actual* Palpatine plot is confirmed (l.3879–5545). "Just business."
- **Sacha Swiftbird:** Ex-Podracer, cybernetic left eye, possible latent Force sensitivity (l.8113–8125). Kills Tesla with the Sith saber; Jax lets her keep it (l.8253–8259, 8809–8811).
- **Den Dhur / Pol Haus / Aren Folee / Augwynne Djo / Magash Drashi:** Den stays with the rescue; Haus hides the surviving Whiplash train with Sheel Mafeen; Folee and Degan Cor run Toprawa Mountain Home; Augwynne helps open and then purge the holocron; typography Magash "Darshi" once at l.7457 vs "Drashi" in the Dramatis.
