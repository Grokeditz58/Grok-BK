## Part Two "Flight and Pursuit"; Ch. 13 (l.2853–3037)
- They flee legally toward the Hydian Way (l.2861–2863). I-Five: Vader's "overweening self-assurance" may make him believe they're dead (l.2877). They head for Mandalore for intel, under pirate/journalist cover (l.2907–2929). Jax doesn't sense Yimmon was ever on Coruscant (l.2901). Den wonders if the shuttle visit was a trap; I-Five: Vader would have made certain of Jax's destruction; question: "how well does Darth Vader know himself?" (l.2955–2973).
- Meditation: seeking Yimmon's "tiny ripples" vs Vader. LORE/SCALING, TLJ ch. 13 (l.2985): Vader is "a much more noticeable presence than Yimmon, like a planet-sized dent in the space–time continuum as opposed to a small asteroid." If he touches Vader with rage or fear, "He'll know you're alive… And he'll know he can reel you in." He must "armor himself" to sense Vader without being sensed (l.2989–2991). He secretly decides to use Sal's Black Sun contacts (l.3003–3037).

## Ch. 14 (l.3039–3147)
- Encrypted holo to Sal via Champala bounce. Sal won't commit resources (l.3049–3057). Sal: "What if you are the last Jedi… the only person alive who can transmit the knowledge of the Jedi to future Padawans." (l.3069). Jax asks for Black Sun contacts; Sal gives Tyno Fabris (Arkanian lieutenant), who thinks Sal is an arms dealer on Klatooine and must not learn Sal is on Coruscant (l.3079–3105). Haus's intel: all but four or five Inquisitors sent offworld, then Vader left; Sal seizes on Palpatine "missing most of his deadliest defenders"; rumors the Emperor may not be at the Palace (l.3133–3147).

## Ch. 15 (l.3149–3323)
- LORE: Mandalore divided after the Shadow Collective; Satine's government fell; Death Watch; puppet Prime Minister; Xizor ("Falleen Vigo") filled the vacuum (l.3151–3155). *Corsair* now has a Tatooine registry at Keldabe under MandalMotors tower. Jax's Corran Vigil disguise includes a fake cybernetic right eye — "Sacha Swiftbird's male twin" (l.3157–3163). Oyu'baat tapcaf. Balosar server Tlinetha (antenepalps / empathy) (l.3181–3185). At a droid-parts warehouse, a woman with filed teeth sold "a bunch of this junk to the Empire," including demand for **blast cages** she doesn't stock (l.3303–3315).

## Ch. 16 (l.3325–3595)
- Tlinetha lies that Imperials haven't been in Keldabe for "Months. Years. Decades." (l.3341–3351). Tyno Fabris: four-digit surgically altered hand, black lenses, displayed pointed ears (l.3357–3389). He had a hold-out blaster hidden by the fire; Jax felt it in the Force (l.3491). Jax's cover: Vader enlisting mercs for a "special project" and seeking "a very special substance intended to aid in the interrogation of particularly resistant minds," data on a Sith holocron (l.3427–3473). Fabris admits Imperials were there briefly; he suggested Concordia "might better meet their needs"; they wanted mercs and weapons (l.3441–3451). Tlinetha: the air around Vigil "shivers… charged"; felt only twice before: once with "that thing" among Vader's men (an Inquisitor), once with "A Jedi." (l.3501–3517). Fabris's IR-sensitive eyes also picked up a concentrated energy source on Vigil (the pyronium) (l.3519). I-Five obtains Trang Robotics N-101 Nemesis parts (laser cannon, repulsor, confounder, beetle-camouflage helm) (l.3527–3529). Parts vendor: Imperials sought sonic traps, sensor webbing, a photonic bender, and a blast cage (sent to Concordia) (l.3539). I-Five: those items plus a blast cage make a sensor-defeating trap; "I imagine [the Inquisitors would] make good Jedi traps nonetheless" (l.3563–3575). Jax still asserts "Vader thinks I'm dead" (l.3577).

## Ch. 17 (l.3597–3731)
- LORE: the Sith holocron holds writings/lab notes of Darth Ramage, plus later Imperial strategic additions; pyronium can increase the yield of a dose of bota (now "irrelevant"); rumors Ramage "had done experiments in the manipulation of time." (l.3607–3609). Cephalons "could see through time." Aoloiloa on the Force: "Force is sea. Force is drop. Force is all. Force is not all." (l.3613). Holocron tingles, glows oxidized-iron red; opening may require both a Force key and a crystal; Jax nearly opens it, red glow creeps up his wrist, then I-Five/Den interrupt (l.3603–3643). He admits the Black Sun contact; will go after Yimmon "whatever it takes" (l.3693–3697). Recalls Vader's damage to Kaj's mind; Yimmon is more disciplined (l.3699). Pol Haus learns the Emperor will go to a villa on the Western Sea, approachable by water; he withholds this from Sal because Sal would act (l.3725–3731).

## Ch. 18 (l.3733–3883)
- Fabris wants the holocron opened first; Jax claims he can't, that it "was taken from a Jedi… Had a name. He's dead now. His name was Jax Pavan." (l.3785–3807). Fabris detects a "white-hot source of energy": **pyronium**, "constantly absorbing electromagnetic energy… Storing it in virtually unlimited amounts in some sort of hyperspatial lattice." Getting it to *release* the energy is rumored to be in the holocron (l.3835–3849). Deal: Vader's whereabouts for the pyronium. After Jax leaves, Xizor enters: "It would seem the rumors of Jax Pavan's demise are somewhat exaggerated." "By all means, let's get him what he wants." (l.3879–3883). typography: Dramatis listed Xizor as "Faleen"; body consistently "Falleen."

## Ch. 19 (l.3885–4221)
- Sal sees smugglers avoiding Western Sea corridors and concludes the Emperor has relocated to the villa and "is reachable." He realizes Haus should have already reported this and did not (l.3895–3909). Stormtroopers sought mercs with "an extreme lack of sensitivity to the Force" so they wouldn't be hit by "psychic ricochets" when "powerful minds collided." Cerean dual brains may handle Force energy differently. Yimmon, tortured in youth: "I hid… [tapping his skull]." (l.4029–4039). Fabris: three Imperial waves; Vader aboard Lambda shuttle *Questor*; coded messages to Imperial Center and a destination in **Bothan space** (l.4119–4125). Xizor appears (pheromones); last time he tried to kill Jax with Jax's own lightsaber, "just business." He wants not the pyronium/holocron today but a "voucher" / "promissory note" — "a Jedi Knight indebted to me." Jax says "No" and walks out, then later heads back out: "it's just business." (l.4139–4199). typography: Sal's *yithrœl* (clan-pride) (l.4253, in ch. 20).

## Ch. 20 (l.4223–4351)
- Sal confronts Haus for withholding the Western Sea intel; Haus: "Stay away from the Emperor… Stop plotting to take his life." Failure *or* success "could cost us the entire network" (l.4269–4277). Sal expels Haus from Whiplash, dumps him on a deep service platform, then tells Dyat he *lied* about standing down (l.4299–4343). Haus wears energy-absorptive shielding; notes Sal won't kill a suspected-betrayer comrade, perhaps because of Lorn/I-Five (l.4347). "Sal really should have shot him" (l.4351).

## Ch. 21 (l.4353–4641)
- Jax grants Xizor the promissory note on condition it not harm the resistance or help the Empire (l.4365–4373). Xizor already deduced the Far Ranger story; "getting in and killing [Yimmon] would be simpler" — Jax's "Jedi sensibilities rule that out" (l.4391–4401). Intel: no landfall in the Bothan system; extraordinary activity around **Kantaros Station**, an ex-Republic depot/medical facility now a high-level POW dump; "the family heir apparent seems to be in residence." Data wafer with complement, armaments, schematics (l.4411–4419). I-Five as an R2 is caught snooping. Jax turns down ships/weapons but asks Xizor to create a diversion to draw Vader back to Coruscant "within hours"; "Better if you don't know." (l.4441–4451).
- Council (Acer, Dyat, Fars, Sheel) signs on to strike the Western Sea villa with explosives and "a couple of cars from this train" (l.4521–4533). Fars Sil-at: "I'd say the Empire has two heads." Sal: "Vader is the Emperor's lapdog. Without his master, he will be without direction or purpose." Fars worries that killing Palpatine would make Vader "even more driven to wipe out Jax Pavan" (l.4535–4539). (Political, not a Force-shift claim.) I-Five challenges Jax's death wish; Jax: "Right now, Five, I'm the most dangerous man in the galaxy—because I have nothing to lose." I-Five: "There is much still to be lost… it's not yours to lose." (l.4617–4633). They leave for Kantaros.

## Ch. 22 (l.4643–4791)
- Dream: Jedi Temple gallery in daylight (not Order 66/Flame Night timing). Laranth: "it isn't time." "Time is a spiral," twinned with a Cephalon voice: "Time is/was/will be a spiral." "Choice is loss." Then the dying *Far Ranger*; Laranth and a darker voice both say "I'm not there" — the dark voice is Vader (l.4645–4705). Kantaros is hidden in the Fervse'dra asteroid belt (Both system; between Taboth and Bothawui); no transponder; Black Sun ships are auto-pulled in (l.4775). Jax will hunt Force signatures, especially **Probus Tesla**, whose signature he "will never forget" (l.4775). Tesla's Force "does not weave; it boiled, surged, undulated," perhaps liquid like Kaj's (l.4783). Jax considers using the miisai's strong plant signature to obscure his own, as Inquisitors use taozin scales (l.4787).

## Ch. 23 (l.4793–4927)
- Tesla POV: practices Soresu with a lightfoil, reciting the Sith Code (l.4795–4819). Apprentice: Elomin Renefra Ren. Vader returns from a Bothawui rendezvous "disturbed," "an undercurrent of fury," "dark static" (l.4837–4869). SCALING (Tesla): Vader is "the most powerful Force-user Tesla had ever known," yet "inexplicably allow[ed] the Jedi Jax Pavan to provoke him to irrational acts"; Tesla thought Vader would rather wound Pavan than extinguish the resistance (l.4853). After (supposedly) destroying the Jedi, Vader took Yimmon and handles interrogation *alone*, relegating Inquisitors to guard duty; he forbade Tesla to assist (l.4855–4857). Private meditation chamber: rumored "only within that specialized structure was the Dark Lord able to exist outside of his enviro-suit" (l.4867). Vader must leave again: "Things are transpiring on Imperial Center" (Xizor's diversion). Tesla is to "attend [Yimmon] daily" but *not* interrogate or use the Force except "to read his moods and his passions. I want you merely to observe him." (l.4879–4893). Vader: Tesla is to ask only questions he is "moved to ask," then report. Tesla asks why Imperial Center; Vader: "someone appears to be plotting against the life of Emperor Palpatine." The tip is from **Black Sun**, which Vader trusts "far more" than the Imperial spy network ("motivated by fear and ideology" vs "simple greed and opportunism") (l.4911–4919). Jax, camouflaged in miisai energies, "brushed Tesla" while Tesla was with Vader; Tesla "didn't seem to notice" (l.4943–4947).

## Ch. 24 (l.4929–5017)
- Kantaros: asteroid-buried station, one Imperial frigate in a tractor/pressor web, little visible patrol. Jax: "There are no more Jedi." Den: "Then we'd better take good care of the one we've got." (l.4957–4959). Fighters swarm; they flee. Jax concludes infiltration needs Black Sun, secretly intends to go back to Xizor rather than Concordia (l.5001–5017).

## Ch. 25 (l.5019–5067)
- Sal at Hotel Sunspire: Nautolan/Mon Cal "repair" a water-skimmer bot and plant overrides/charges. Plan: ~70 charged cleaning bots to bring down the villa and landing pad; second wave in the water and streets if Palpatine flees. "Emperor Palpatine's senses were clouded by arrogance… about to find out how limited they were." (l.5031–5039). Sal views Yimmon's abduction as a "fitting reward" for timidity (l.5043). Yimmon's cell: cavernous, no furniture; a harsh light *moves with him* (sleep deprivation); blast cage is built into Tesla's hidden gallery (l.5047–5051). When Yimmon's heart rate rests, lights/sound barrage; he walks the perimeter eyes closed and stops *directly under Tesla* (l.5053–5063).

## Ch. 26 (l.5069–5259) / Tesla "attends" and Xizor's diversion works
- Jax vs I-Five/Den: "The last time I faced [Vader] I had help… it took Vader overreaching and Rhinann throwing his life away to even get us out… Right now, I've got Xizor." I-Five: a Jedi "in the service of a Black Sun Vigo" is a mistake. Jax leaves them behind (l.5109–5121). FEAT (Jax): Force-summons a fallen Dathomir warrior statuette into his palm, frightening Fabris (l.5187). Xizor: "My distraction worked quite well. Darth Vader has left Kantaros Station to return to Imperial Center." (l.5221). Tesla Force-touches a "serene pool" then a "fathomless unknown"; feels *he* is being watched (l.5229–5231). Yimmon opens amber eyes: **"Do you know yourself?"** then shuts mind and eyes (l.5243–5247). Tesla plans to "neutralize his dual cortex… divide and conquer," wondering if the record's silence on the dual brain is an oversight or a test (l.5253–5259).

## Ch. 27 (l.5261–5497)
- Haus (disguised as a Togruta with a rancor-tooth pendant) meets Sheel. Sal has pieced out the plan so only he has the whole picture; field ops in shore maintenance; Acer has huge explosives (l.5315–5323). Haus's patrols: Imperial security moving into the Golden Crescent at night; remaining Inquisitors no longer "drifting around" the Inquisitorius (l.5335–5343). He warns Sal by holo; Sal calls him a coward, refuses to stand down: "The Emperor is going to die." (l.5365–5423). Acer Ash bargains with Zeltron Captain Donari Caron (Black Sun) for palm-sized P-shields (2 m range, reflect fire). He keeps two for Sal, and leaks: "Palpatine isn't going to be a factor for much longer, and once he's out of the picture, Vader and his spooks… will be running around trying to figure out what happened." (l.5461–5465). Strike day: in three days, Senators collect at the villa; Sal discounts Haus's warning because his own ops report no extra forces (l.5477–5489). Collateral damage is accepted (l.5491).

## Ch. 28 (l.5499–5713)
- Fabris reports Caron's leak to Xizor: the rumor mill not only drew Vader off but "someone actually is plotting to kill Palpatine." (l.5511–5519). Consequently Xizor **pulls the plug** on the Kantaros run; *Raptor* stands down (l.5535–5545). FEAT (Jax): Force-opens the locked tapcaf, swipes a blaster over the banister, Force-crushes/twists the Zabrak's weapon "into an unrecognizable lump," Force-thrusts her four meters, rips Fabris's door from its hinges (l.5583–5595). Confronts Xizor with lit saber; threatens to drop the chandelier and "embed you in that wall" (l.5605–5617). Xizor explains: the rumor to draw Vader was that someone would assassinate Palpatine; Acer Ash then confirmed an *actual* plot; Xizor will not risk a Black Sun ship being linked to both the assassination and a Yimmon breakout (l.5637–5653). "Nothing personal… just business." FEAT (Jax): sunders remaining tapestries, Force-storm of flying objects, explodes the barred stained-glass window (wall and all), Force-lowers himself to the street, then wrenches the chandelier down in "crystal and flame" (l.5667–5685). No injured sensed. He tells Den/I-Five they're going to **Toprawa** (l.5693). In his cabin a "horrible, dark, quivering desire" explodes: a Force tantrum wrecks the room; the miisai is half-crushed, roots naked; the **Sith lightsaber** concealed in the planter falls onto the deck (l.5705–5711). He replants the tree and feeds it "energy from his own life force" (l.5713).
