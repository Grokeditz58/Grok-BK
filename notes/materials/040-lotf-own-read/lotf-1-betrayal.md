# Legacy of the Force 1: Betrayal (Aaron Allston, 2006) — own full read

Own read of the licensed novel text, every chapter in order (c01–c34, no prologue/epilogue in this book). Citation [cNN ¶L] = chapter NN, paragraph L of the chapter text (one paragraph per line, chapter title line = ¶1). All quotes checked verbatim against the text with a script (0 mismatches). Era: 40 ABY. Continuity: Legends.

## 1. Summary
- Jacen Solo and Ben Skywalker raid an Adumari factory (c01–c02), then Luke sends them to sabotage Centerpoint Station before Corellia can use it (c04–c05). Thrackan Sal-Solo traps them with anti-Jedi sonic weapons and a Force-sensing shock field (c10, c15). Jacen recovers, beats Thrackan, catches blaster bolts bare-handed, and decides to execute him; Thrackan escapes (c15). Ben's Anakin-shaped droid scrambles Centerpoint (c15).
- Jaina's Team Purella attacks a Corellian convoy (c09–c10). The GA fleet is ambushed and withdraws (c09, c11, c14).
- At the Toryaz Station summit, terminally ill Corellian assassins with anti-Jedi gear hit the Jedi and delegates. A hidden female Force-user (Lumiya) has mind-controlled the station security chief (c19–c20). Luke's best feats of the book come here.
- Mara infiltrates Thrackan's mansion (c22–c23). Han and Wedge fly a Shriek bomber raid on Rellidir. Syal Antilles, Wedge's daughter, fights for the GA; Han spares her; the GA headquarters is destroyed (c27, c30–c33).
- Jacen and Ben follow a Sith tassel to Lorrd and meet Nelani Dinn (c25–c27). Jacen deliberately sets off a bomb, killing one hostage to save the rest (c27). They follow Brisha Syo to an asteroid that was once Darth Vectivus's retreat (c28–c30).
- Force phantoms attack Ben, Nelani, Jacen, and even Luke and Mara on Coruscant (c30–c32). Brisha reveals she is Lumiya and pitches the Sith path; she claims Vergere was a Sith candidate (c32). Nelani attacks Lumiya. Jacen foresees that he kills Luke in every future where Nelani lives, so he kills Nelani (c33). He wipes Ben's memories of Brisha and hides Lumiya (c34). Luke feels the man who doesn't exist now exists (c34).

## 4. Duels and fights log (blow by blow, with conditions)
- **D1 Jacen + Ben vs Adumari troopers [c01 ¶89–118]:** deflection kills; Ben guards his back. Easy win.
- **D2 Jacen vs Thrackan's CorSec and sonic droids [c10 ¶42 → c11 ¶74–90]:** CONDITION: an anti-Jedi sonic weapon dropped him before he sensed any danger [c10 ¶26–27, ¶42]. He recovers by pain tolerance, TK-recalls his saber, and fights blind by Force-sense, killing the shooters and both sonic droids. Win after a debuff.
- **D3 Jacen vs CorSec squad, probots and ~20 agents [c13 ¶3–52]:** precogs a booby-trapped door; saber plus TK multitasking (F9); grazed twice under full-auto fire [c13 ¶37]; ceiling shield (F10); remote power-pack overloads (F11). Clean win.
- **D4 Jacen vs Thrackan [c15 ¶105–153]:** TK slam; tricked by a recording, and a shot knocks his saber away [c15 ¶109–110]; catches bolts bare-handed; disarms Thrackan; Thrackan escapes by launch tube. Jacen wins physically and loses on wits.
- **D5 Ben vs the anti-Jedi shock field [c15 ¶19–23]:** a brain-wave detector shocks him every time he uses the Force. Tech counter; no scaling.
- **D6 Jaina, Zekk, Kolir, Thann vs YVH droids and CorSec [c09–c10]:** Jaina deflects 4 YVHs' near-continuous fire, though it tires her [c10 ¶62]; big TK finishes (F7). Thann is kicked "like being hit by a pneumatic ram" [c09 ¶38].
- **D7 Toryaz raid: Luke, Mara, Jacen vs ~20 anti-Jedi commandos [c19 ¶60–81]:** CONDITION: the attackers are dying men with no aggression for Jedi to sense ("Not aggression, fatalism. Disease—" [c19 ¶62]); Jacen senses nothing [c19 ¶53–54]. Luke: door reversal, two-way push, then caught in a contracting net; Mara kills the netter; Luke drops the ceiling, tears the net and wins the spin. Rout by the Jedi. Leia gets two kills with a Force leap [c20 ¶6–10].
- **D8 Luke vs phantom Jacen, Coruscant [c31 ¶3–8, ¶118–125]:** the first blow is met "with little effort"; the phantom bounds wall to wall; Luke counters everything, singes its robe, and suffers a near-hit burn on his forearm [c31 ¶120]. No finish shown. Luke even or ahead.
- **D9 Mara vs phantom Ben [c31 ¶10–19]:** dodges the spear-thrust-into-sweep; a negligent wave throws it through the wall. Mara wins.
- **D10 Nelani vs phantom mynocks / Vectivus phantom [c31 ¶21–68]:** acrobatics in low gravity; refuses to kill the phantom she is told is linked to a real life. No duel.
- **D11 Ben vs phantom Mara [c30 ¶145–149, c31 ¶127–139]:** Ben slips the push and hides his presence, then hesitates at his mother's peaceful face and is kicked unconscious. Psychological loss.
- **D12 Jacen vs phantom Luke [c32 ¶2–12]:** Jacen's low-gravity bounding tactics are read; he switches to Force-braced flurries; the phantom shifts to soft defense "turning away each of Jacen’s all-out attacks" [c32 ¶10], then sidesteps the fifth blow and counters into his back. Lumiya cancels it; Jacen still feels pain and his robe is burning [c32 ¶12]. **Loss.** CONDITION: the phantom drew on the asteroid's dark-side energy; Jacen did not.
- **D13 Nelani vs Lumiya [c33 ¶2–26]:** Lumiya dodges behind a bust; her multi-tendril lightwhip cuts Nelani's cheek, chest and bicep; "baffled by the older woman’s superior technique" [c33 ¶10]. TK duel: bust shattered, then an even push-of-war, strained because Lumiya is talking the whole time; she loses it and is disarmed. Jacen intervenes. **Lumiya ahead on weapon, roughly even on TK.**
- **D14 Jacen vs Nelani [c33 ¶119–140]:** she ambushes him with a flying side kick in low gravity; he senses it with eyes closed and swats her thigh without looking or using strength; she cannot stand; a disarming bind, then a thrust through the sternum. **Total mismatch** (she was already wounded and was weaker in low gravity [c33 ¶119]).

## 5. Character analyses (characterization and scaling)
- **Jacen Solo:** the book's main feat-holder. Elite Knight-plus: bare-hand bolt catch (F12, called rare by S3); fine-control TK multitasking (F9, F11); vehicle-scale TK (F19); strong precog (F28, the c05 ¶65 sims). Characterization: ends-justify-means ([c01 ¶89]; the c15 ¶142 execution decision; F21; Nelani). His fall is his own choice (S12). Scaling: below Luke by his own word (S2) and by losing to a dark-side-boosted Luke phantom (D12).
- **Luke Skywalker:** best combat showing at Toryaz (F13–F15) while handicapped by enemies he cannot sense. Holds even or better against a phantom Jacen (D8). Wide Force vision range (Leia, across systems [c30 ¶74]). Han's and Luke's own lines frame him as the top Jedi.
- **Mara Jade Skywalker:** the Master-level stealth multitask (F18) is her standout, with a stated Master-only bar. Effortless against a phantom (D9), and decisive at Toryaz [c19 ¶71].
- **Ben Skywalker (13):** apprentice-level but creative: blast dampening (F20), push redirection (F25), stealth. Loses to the phantom Mara through emotional hesitation (D11).
- **Lumiya:** multi-site phantom puppeteering (F23), possibly from one asteroid to Coruscant, though it depends on the place's dark-side reservoir [c34 ¶63] (Jacen's lie, but he matches the setup); mind control of Tawaler [c19 ¶12–39]; whip superiority over a Knight; only even TK with Nelani (D13). Her claim that cyborgs can't become true Masters (S10) caps her by her own account.
- **Nelani Dinn:** competent young Knight (F22, low-gravity acrobatics); weaker than Lumiya in technique and far below Jacen.
- **Jaina Solo:** strong TK under fire (F5, F7); she dominates as a pilot over Rellidir [c33 ¶79–85] (Han's POV, and he can't tell whether she Force-detonated missiles [c33 ¶82]).
- **Leia:** Knight-level leap and kills [c20 ¶6–10]; Force-aided gunnery [c14 ¶94–95].
- **Zekk / Kolir / Thann:** Kolir can lift "tons" only with time to focus (F6); Jaina is faster and surer than Kolir (F5); Thann can TK-throw a YVH [c09 ¶39].
- **Not in this book:** Alema Rar, Tahiri, Kyp and Fett do not appear in Betrayal.

## 6. Scaling chains
- Luke > Jacen (40 ABY): S2 + D12 (a Luke-plus-dark-side phantom beats Jacen) + D8 (Luke even or ahead vs a Jacen phantom). Caveat: phantoms are Lumiya constructs, and the dialogue in D8 and D12 is mirrored word for word [c31 ¶121–125 = c32 ¶2–8], so they are staged fights.
- Jacen >> Nelani (D14) ≥ Lumiya on TK (D13) → Jacen > Lumiya in raw power, while Lumiya wins on whip technique vs Nelani.
- Mara ≥ Master tier: F18 + S-line [c22 ¶99].
- Jaina > Kolir on TK speed (F5). Corran TK < most Jedi (S5).

## 7. Honest scaling takeaway
Betrayal is a low-ceiling book. The biggest feats are precise rather than large: bolt catching, simultaneous TK plus blade work, power-pack overloads, Mara's three-way stealth. The largest raw TK is the Y-wing flip and Jaina's cargo-drone drop. Jacen gets the most feats, but every direct comparison still puts Luke above him. The phantom fights are useful but conditional: they are staged by Lumiya and powered by the Vectivus asteroid. Nelani's death shows the gap between Jacen and an ordinary Knight, not a peak. Use Betrayal for baselines (40 ABY Jacen ≈ elite Knight with Master-level versatility per S1; Luke above him) rather than for top-end feats.
