# Legacy of the Force 8: Revelation (Karen Traviss, 2008) — own full read

Own read of the licensed novel text, every section in order (prl, c01–c20, epl). Citation [cNN ¶L] = chapter NN, paragraph L of the chapter text (one paragraph per line, chapter title line = ¶1); [prl ¶L] / [epl ¶L] = prologue / epilogue. All quotes checked verbatim against the text with a script (0 mismatches). Era: 41 ABY, about twelve weeks after Mara's death. Continuity: Legends.

## 1. Summary
- **prl–c03:** Jaina decides to train with Boba Fett so she can stop Jacen. Ben doubts that Alema killed Mara and goes to Coruscant to work the case with GAG captain Lon Shevu. Niathal, shaken by Tebut's killing, starts feeding Luke information. Caedus sends Tahiri to court Pellaeon. Fett recovers his ex-wife Sintas Vel, blind and amnesiac, from carbonite.
- **c04–c08:** Caedus tests Tahiri at Fondor. Jaina reaches Keldabe and meets the Force-sensitive Mandalorians Gotab and Venku; Fett agrees to train her. On Kavan, Mara's Force ghost tugs her own hair for Ben [c05 ¶54–55]. Ben's forensic droid then finds that hair in Jacen's StealthX [c08 ¶125]. Jacen tells Niathal he is a Sith [c06 ¶122]. Pellaeon quietly calls in Daala.
- **c09–c12:** Beviin trains Jaina. Fondor's minelayers are destroyed because of the leak Luke passed on [c10 ¶49]. Luke's phantom fleet humiliates Caedus [c11 ¶188–217]. Luke then cripples Caedus's StealthX, but lets him go rather than endanger civilians [c12 ¶23–84].
- **c13–c16:** Second Battle of Fondor. The Jedi hide the Fondorian fleet. Caedus drives his commanders into a battle rage and uses the feedback to talk Fondor's shield crew into dropping the planetary shield. He then bombs Oridin [c13 ¶151–173]. Niathal accepts Fondor's surrender and relieves Caedus. Tahiri murders Pellaeon [c14 ¶122] and becomes Caedus's apprentice [c15 ¶40–43]. Daala's Maw Irregular Fleet routs Caedus, the GA splits, and Niathal forms a government in exile.
- **c17–c18:** Mandalorians storm *Bloodfin*. Mirta and Jaina bring down Tahiri, Jaina breaks Caedus's Force choke on Mirta, and Carid kneecaps Caedus, who still escapes with Tahiri. Back on Coruscant, Caedus tells Shevu how he killed Mara, and Shevu is secretly recording.
- **c19–c20, epl:** Ben plays the evidence for the family. Fett and Gotab (revealed as Bardan Jusik) bring Jaina to the decision that she must kill Jacen. Gotab restores Sintas's sight and memory. Mirta marries Orade. Mara's ghost comes to Luke [epl ¶14].

## 4. Duels and fights log (blow by blow, with conditions)
- **D1 Fett vs Jaina, barn spar [c07 ¶139–150]:** Fett, using his lightsaber hilt as a knuckleduster, lands a gut punch she "hadn’t sensed" [c07 ¶143]. Her reflex counter-slash only scorches his beskar paint [c07 ¶144]. CONDITION: training; Jaina relaxed and holding back; Fett in full beskar. Lesson, not a scale.
- **D2 Beviin vs Jaina, beskads and sticks [c09 ¶49–93]:** she is floored within two seconds in round 1 [c09 ¶49] and "couldn’t find a way to get inside his reach" [c09 ¶54]. She scores only with a Force-push and pin [c09 ¶54]. In the stick drill Beviin's berserk focus hammers her until she Force-throws him [c09 ¶93]. CONDITION: deliberately restricted to an unfamiliar weapon. Beviin rates her as lethal within a week (S23).
- **D3 Luke (+ a grappler Jedi) vs Caedus, StealthX duel [c12 ¶23–84]:** (1) Caedus can neither see nor feel his attacker, and Luke wingtip-rams him twice [c12 ¶28–39]. (2) Luke shears off both wing cannons [c12 ¶53–54] and tells him he could have killed him "several times over" (S5). (3) Luke's TK works in the drives; Caedus's block fails, his seat shears off its runners and a rib cracks [c12 ¶65]. (4) A second StealthX with grappling arms closes for a capture [c12 ¶65–66]. (5) Caedus aims at a civilian replenishment tunnel, Luke's horror costs him seconds, and Caedus escapes [c12 ¶67–77]. He Force-brakes into a crash landing and heals himself [c12 ¶79–84]. **Luke wins outright**; Caedus escapes only because of Luke's restraint. CONDITION: fighters, not sabers; Luke was trying to capture, not kill; Caedus didn't see it coming.
- **D4 Caedus vs Fondor's shield crew (mind-influence) [c13 ¶151–173]:** amplified by the battle link to 100+ commanders, at orbital range, against mundane minds. Win, then drained.
- **D5 Mirta + Jaina vs Tahiri, Bloodfin ducts [c17 ¶67–74]:** Tahiri deflects Jaina-side fire into Mirta's plates [c17 ¶67–68]. Mirta charges and beats her reaction time [c17 ¶69], and Tahiri's lightsaber fails on the beskar [c17 ¶70]. Jaina attacks in Beviin's "blind violence" mode [c17 ¶71–72]. Mirta's vibroblade opens Tahiri's femoral artery and Tahiri flees up the hatch [c17 ¶72–74]. CONDITION: 2v1, confined space, Tahiri trying to escape. Win for Jaina and Mirta.
- **D6 Caedus (choke) vs Jaina (break) [c17 ¶76]:** Caedus chokes Mirta from the docking tube to cover Tahiri, and Jaina tears the choke apart. CONDITION: Caedus is exhausted from F16 [c17 ¶100], cannot see the scene, and is juggling Tahiri, the cutters and the venting tube [c17 ¶97–100]. Low-medium weight for Jaina vs Caedus.
- **D7 Caedus vs Carid, docking tube [c17 ¶111 → c18 ¶15]:** Carid crushes Caedus's ankle and jams a blaster into his belly. Caedus's face-projection trick of Ailyn Vel makes Carid pause, but he isn't fooled ("Doesn’t suit you, dar’jetii" [c18 ¶13]). He shoots Caedus through the knee and lets go [c18 ¶14]. Caedus Force-punches the hatch open and escapes [c18 ¶15]. CONDITION: Caedus drained and holding a dying Tahiri; Fett had ordered the Mandalorians to leave Jacen to Jaina [c18 ¶24]. A non-Force professional who expects the trick beats it.
- **D8 Jaina vs Venku, evening spar [c20 ¶243–250]:** no result. Shows that Venku, trained by Gotab, wields two lightsabers.

## 5. Character analyses (characterization and scaling)
- **Darth Caedus (Jacen Solo):** the widest power set of the series to this point. Fleet battle meditation with a feedback loop (F15), mass mind-influence that drops a planetary shield (F16), long-range Force-listening (F4), blind micro-jumps (F9), self-healing (F13) and face projection (F22). He is also repeatedly beaten: by Luke (D3), by collective Jedi concealment (F14), by non-Force preparation (F8, D5–D7) and by his own exhaustion (S9, D6). Characterization: dissociating and self-justifying (c18 ¶140–145, "Darth Caedus" [c18 ¶203]). His blind spot is believing that Luke can't or won't kill him (S6).
- **Luke Skywalker:** the clear winner of the book's only Master-level clash (D3, S5). His toolset is concealment, illusion and raw TK. His restraint, not any lack of power, is what lets Caedus survive and bomb Oridin. He also carries guilt over the minelayers [c10 ¶49].
- **Jaina Solo:** says she matches Jacen in Force strength but not in training (S1, S2). Her concealment beats his senses (F5), she breaks a drained Caedus's choke (F21), and she now has the Mandalorian commitment and the intel on his face trick (S21, S22). Her arc ends in resolve: "this is as far as it goes. I have to stop him now." [c20 ¶211].
- **Mara Jade Skywalker (posthumous):** S11–S15 all say the same thing: she ambushed him, pulled down the ceiling, injured him with lightsaber, blaster and crush wounds, and forced the poison as a last resort. Fett's reading is "She nearly succeeded." [c19 ¶80] This is the strongest pro-Mara evidence for the *Sacrifice* duel.
- **Ben Skywalker:** an investigator, not an avenger. His feats are utility (F1, F6), and his Force-hiding is good enough that Caedus credits him with Mara-level concealment [c12 ¶36].
- **Tahiri Veila:** becomes Caedus's apprentice by murdering Pellaeon (F17). She is badly outclassed on *Bloodfin* (D5) and saved only by Caedus.
- **Boba Fett:** trains Jaina in thinking, not power (S22). His beskar stops both a lightsaber (D1) and a point-blank blaster [c17 ¶18]. He deliberately leaves Jacen to Jaina [c18 ¶24; c19 ¶84].
- **Mandalorians (Beviin, Mirta, Carid, Medrit):** Traviss's thesis that armour, attitude and preparation beat Force users plays out in D2, D5 and D7.
- **Gotab (Bardan Jusik) and Venku (Kad'ika):** an ex-Jedi Clone Wars general who left the Order, now a healer (F24), and his adopted son, who can use the Force but avoids it and carries two lightsabers. Gotab: "I’d kill him, out of love." [c20 ¶194].
- **Lumiya and Alema** are dead before this book and appear only in Caedus's memories [c18 ¶197]. **Kyp** does not appear.

## 6. Scaling chains
- Luke > Caedus (41 ABY): D3 + S5 + Caedus's own POV concession [c12 ¶65]. Caveats: a fighter duel, Luke had a wingman, and Luke was holding back.
- Caedus's mass influence (F16) depends on the battle link and a mundane target, and it drained him. It is a strategic feat, not a dueling one. Even so, he cannot override a fleet's morale (S10).
- Mara ≈ near-win over Caedus (*Sacrifice*), confirmed in-universe by S11–S14 and Shevu's forensics [c19 ¶2].
- Jaina ≈ Caedus in raw strength by her own word (S1, S2); Jaina > drained Caedus at a choke contest (D6); Jaina > Tahiri (D5).
- Beskar-armoured Mandalorians > lightsaber attacks in close quarters (D1, D5); a forewarned non-Force pro > Caedus's illusion (D7).

## 7. Honest scaling takeaway
*Revelation* is a framing book, not a dueling book. Its one big comparison, Luke vs Caedus in StealthXs (D3), is a clean Luke win: he out-hides, out-illusions and out-muscles Caedus in TK. Caedus's biggest feat, the planetary shield drop, is enormous in reach but conditional (battle-link boost, mundane minds) and leaves him drained. That drain is why Jaina can break his choke and Carid can kneecap him later the same day. The recorded confession is the most useful thing in the book for Mara scaling. Traviss consistently tilts the outcome toward preparation and will over raw power, so use this book for Luke > Caedus, Mara-nearly-won, and Caedus's weaknesses, not for peak power claims.
