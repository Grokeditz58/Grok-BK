# Legacy of the Force 9: Invincible (Troy Denning, 2008) — own full read

Own read of the licensed novel text, every section in order (prl, c01–c20, epl). Citation [cNN ¶L] = chapter NN, paragraph L of the chapter text (one paragraph per line, chapter title line = ¶1); [prl ¶L] / [epl ¶L] = prologue / epilogue. All quotes checked verbatim against the text with a script (0 mismatches). Era: 41 ABY, three weeks after Second Fondor. Continuity: Legends.

## 1. Summary
- **prl–c02:** Twin-bond flashback [prl ¶5]. On Nickel One (Roche), Jaina finishes Fett's training and fights alongside him against Remnant troops and a nanokiller cloud. At Shedu Maad the Jedi council sanctions killing Caedus. Luke refuses to do it himself because every future in which he goes after Caedus "ends in darkness" [c02 ¶105], so Jaina takes the job.
- **c03–c05:** Caedus bullies the Moffs into an alliance (c03). Tahiri ambushes Ben on Coruscant and captures him [c03 ¶109–122]. Jaina and Leia fail to free him and get gassed (c04). Luke reveals he foresaw the capture, and that he is jamming Caedus's foresight and hiding Jaina from it [c05 ¶32; c05 ¶54].
- **c06–c09 (Nickel One):** Jaina drops in by dropsuit. Mirta's Mandalorian team hits the Moffs' planning forum, and Caedus kills Roegr and Vatok, using shatterpoint on beskar [c08 ¶137–139]. Jaina, sniping, wounds his shoulder [c08 ¶143]. In the projection booth Caedus fights someone he sees as Luke. It is actually Jaina, flooded with the Force while Luke's body sits empty in the blastboat [c09 ¶57–59]. She cuts off his arm with Vatok's beskad [c09 ¶102–103], and his lightning breaks her ribs [c09 ¶106–107].
- **c10–c13:** Tahiri's lightning kills Shevu; Ben escapes [c10 ¶132–157]. Caedus's blood stains on Jaina won't wash off [c11 ¶4; c11 ¶16–19]. Ben shoves SD-XX into space; the *Anakin Solo* captures Isolder [c12 ¶138–149]. Caedus interrogates the paralysed Mirta, finds the Jedi base by a Nightsister blood trail [c13 ¶53–56] and decides to kill Luke [c13 ¶128].
- **c14–c16:** Jaina learns shatterpoint from Luke and crumbles raw beskar [c14 ¶30–35]. Tenel Ka arrives; the blood trail is identified [c14 ¶92]. The ambush in the Throat follows: Zekk goes missing [c15 ¶116], Luke's StealthX wing bombs the *Anakin Solo* [c15 ¶121–125], and Jaina crash-boards [c15 ¶130]. Caedus misreads a vision of Allana on a white throne as victory [c16 ¶9–19], tells Tahiri that flow-walking cannot change the past [c16 ¶44–55], and vetoes the Moffs' nanokiller plan.
- **c17–c18:** Jaina finds Mirta in the prison hold [c17 ¶69–100]. Caedus snaps Isolder's neck with the Force [c17 ¶136] and finds that the Moffs took Isolder's blood anyway [c18 ¶65–66]. **Final duel** in the Biodisposal Pit: Jaina kills Caedus while he reaches out to warn Tenel Ka [c18 ¶74–120].
- **c19–c20, epl:** Ben stops Tahiri's baradium warhead and she surrenders [c19 ¶107–152]. The Jedi take the *Anakin Solo*, and Luke puts Jag Fel over the Moffs [c20 ¶81–97]. Daala becomes Chief of State [epl ¶35]. Allana is alive as "Amelia" [epl ¶63–70].

Not a clean 1v1.

CONDITION: he let the strike happen because it killed his Moff rivals [c09 ¶64].

## 5. Character analyses (characterization and scaling)
- **Darth Caedus (Jacen Solo):** widest toolset in the series. Shatterpoint (F12), Aing-Tii fighting-sight (D3), the Nightsister blood trail (F23), fleet battle meditation (F30), ship-wide sensing (F31), Force-backed suggestion (F24), lightning and a lethal Force-grip (F18, F33). He remains frightening while crippled (D2, D7, S15). His limits: Jaina's concealment beats his senses (S22, F28), he cannot read Lecersen's thoughts clearly [c16 ¶88], and his foresight is jammed by Luke (F7). He misreads his own vision (S13). Characterization: his love for Allana survives to the end (c17 ¶109–112, S14). He breaks Tahiri deliberately: "That was kind of the point." [c16 ¶134].
- **Luke Skywalker:** the strongest figure in the book, by his own foresight (S1, S3) and by Caedus's fear (S4, S5, [c16 ¶8]). His feats are strategic and mystic: jamming foresight (F7), the Nickel One boost or projection (F15, S10, S11), and casual TK (F38, F39). He refuses to be the killer on moral grounds (S2).
- **Jaina Solo:** «magnitudes» below Caedus in raw Force strength by her own account (S6–S8). She wins through training (Mandalorian commitment, shatterpoint F26), concealment, Luke's help at Nickel One (D3), and a light-side surge plus a cripple-and-kill sequence in D7. She is the Sword of the Jedi [c20 ¶50–52].
- **Ben Skywalker:** a utility and investigation profile (F19, F21, F22). He wins D4, D5 and D8 and spares Tahiri.
- **Tahiri Veila:** a capable duelist (F36, D1, D8) who is psychologically broken by Caedus (c16 ¶41–56). She surrenders: "I’m done with all Solos." [c19 ¶137].
- **Boba Fett:** non-Force. "Your brother kills you." [c01 ¶217]. Beskar stops Jaina's reflex strike (F3). Exiled from Mandalore by the nanokiller [epl ¶22–27].
- **Mirta Gev, Roegr, Vatok:** outclassed by Caedus even in beskar once he uses shatterpoint (D2). Mirta survives paralysed and later heals [epl ¶21].
- **Kyp, Saba:** cameo TK (F38). Saba frames the strategy (S19, S23).
- **Lumiya and Alema** are dead; Lumiya is named only in Saba's taint remark [c02 ¶109–111]. **Mara** appears only as Luke's motive.
- **Zekk:** missing after the Throat [c15 ¶116]. The text does not show him dying [epl ¶14–17].

## 6. Scaling chains
- Luke > Caedus: S1, S3, S4, S5, plus Revelation D3. High.
- Jaina + Luke > wounded Caedus (D3), Jaina solo > crippled and distracted Caedus (D7). Neither is Jaina > healthy Caedus (S6, S7).
- Caedus > four beskar Mandalorians plus a non-Force sniper (D2).
- Jaina ≈ Caedus in shatterpoint access (F12 vs F26); Luke has mastered it (S17).
- Ben > Tahiri (D8, D4), with the caveat that Tahiri had stopped wanting to win.

## 7. Honest scaling takeaway
*Invincible* is built so Jaina can win without out-powering anyone. Every statement in the book, including her own, puts Caedus far above her in raw Force strength. The arm comes off while Luke is pouring the Force into her (S10, S11). The kill comes after a first-second gut stab, against a one-armed Caedus whose attention is on saving Allana and who stops defending at the end. Inside those conditions the duel is still hard-fought: he out-anticipates her in the dark and nearly incinerates her with TK. Use the book for Luke > Caedus, for Caedus's full toolset (shatterpoint, blood trail, fighting-sight), and for «Jaina wins the fight she prepared for». Do not use it for Jaina > peak Caedus.
