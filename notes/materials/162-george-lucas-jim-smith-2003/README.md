This is a career-guide / film-criticism volume in Virgin's Film series, not a primary interview book and not a conventional biography (Smith says so on p. 6). Lucas is quoted throughout, usually from named interviews, production notes, DVD/VHS featurettes, and the book's own **Index of Quotations** (printed pp. 278–285). Those quotations are transcribed literally below and flagged as Smith's report of Lucas, not as independently checked interview transcripts. Smith's own paraphrases, draft-history reconstructions, and pre-2005 speculation about *Episode III* are not treated as Lucas's words.
