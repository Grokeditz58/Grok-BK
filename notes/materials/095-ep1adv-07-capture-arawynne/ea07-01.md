# EA7 Capture Arawynne: notes

## Ch. 1 (l.33–134): the garden's hazards
- **Deneba bush** (a predatory tree that drops its leaves, grabs and swallows), cucul, swamp slugs, syren plants, the **eye-snatcher** bird from the Ottega system, the vesuvague tree (strangles), urchin flowers (fire needles).
- **Wisties** ("fire-folk") from the Endor moon are slaves used as night-lights. Oo Wen, a Ho'Din gardener, protects the children. Arawynne: "The same plants that eat you will eat anyone who tries to catch you."

## Ch. 2 (l.136–284)
- Jabba introduces his father **Zorba**, old and bearded. Dorn reads him perfectly: "You have set a trap for me…" Bothans "often fool probe droids".
- Jabba's test: betray Pala by sunset and go free working for Zorba, or die. "It will do you good to learn to betray a friend." Secretly, Sebulba will follow Dorn.

## Ch. 3–4 (l.286–407)
- Anakin's scrambled comlink. Dorn begs for a jammer.
- **Anakin dreams of "a tall man… tan poncho… lightsaber"** coming to take him off Tatooine: Force foresight of Qui-Gon.
- The Ghostlings' grotto: woven mats, trip-vines, punji sticks, a tree-top lookout. Ghostlings are frail but strong climbers with sharp senses. Tempter beasts.

## Ch. 5–6 (l.409–604)
- Sebulba tracks Dorn by his implant over breakfast (eye-snatcher eggs). Anakin taunts Sebulba to his face: "You're going to have to settle for second place from now on."
- Dorn's jammer locket kills the signal. **Racer's Edge Cantina shootout:** Brant Rumble (a cyborg Sullustan) and his crew against Sebulba's gang. Khiss and Djas Puhr drop them all; Rumble escapes. **Gondry, used as a shield, takes "a dozen hits" and recovers after two glasses of water.**
- Djas Puhr spots the boys from a vaporator and follows their scent to the fortress.

## Ch. 7 (l.606–787)
- Sebulba's gang carry blasters set to stun and Stokhli spray sticks ("goo guns") for the Ghostlings.
- **Djas Puhr stuns Dorn** out of a tree.
- **Anakin lures Gondry into Circarpous V quickclay**, pulls him out for a promise, and Gondry betrays him ("I had my toes crossed").
- Sebulba ambushes and catches **Kitster** (the "dark-headed boy").

## Ch. 8–10 (l.789–1021)
- Gondry gets urchin quills in him, then **an eye-snatcher tears out his only eye**. It "would take hours for it to grow back".
- **Anakin rigs a Myrkr SS-23 sewage spreader into a "sewage cannon"** and knocks Sebulba flat.
- Pala lures Khiss onto a cucul disguised as a log. He knocks it out with a stomp and shoots a swamp slug's antennae.

## Ch. 11–13 (l.1023–1288)
- Sebulba takes Oo Wen hostage, shoots plants and splits his mattock with a shot. Oo Wen gets away.
- Djas Puhr falls into a pit trap next to a **worrt**'s den and is bitten. He works out that the escape route is the air vent.
- **Pala fakes a surrender while Khiss charges the deneba bush, which swallows him whole.** Khiss dies (l.1273–1288). This is the only death in the book, and a protagonist causes it.

## Ch. 14 / "16" (l.1290–1439)
- Djas Puhr finds the hideout by watching the Wisties. Sebulba aims a Corellian freighter's exhaust down the vent. Arawynne is webbed and unconscious.
- Pala's backstory: the Toydarian baron **Begubb** demanded ryll tribute and took one child in ten from her village on Ryloth.
- Djas Puhr: "Life as a slave isn't so bad." Pala, Kitster, Dorn and all the Ghostlings are captured. **Only Anakin escapes**, through a vent.

## Ch. 17 (l.1441–1468)
- Gondry regrows his eye. Sebulba is glad he won't have to pay Khiss. NEXT: *Trouble on Tatooine*.
