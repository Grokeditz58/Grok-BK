# Yoda: Dark Rendezvous: summary of strongest findings

Source: Sean Stewart, *Star Wars: Yoda: Dark Rendezvous* (Del Rey 2004; ebook v3.1). Hand-read in full on 2026-11-03: all 12 chapters, front matter, dedication, author pages and Del Rey back matter. Citations are "YDR ch. N".

1. **Yoda's self-assessment and the cost of wisdom.**
   > "Loved more than you, have I, Padawan. Lost more. Hated more. Killed more." (YDR ch. 9)
   > "Think you I have never felt the touch of the dark? Know you what a soul so great as Yoda can make, in eight hundred years?" (YDR ch. 1)
2. **Dark-Yoda vision. Dooku judges a fallen Yoda greater than Sidious.**
   > "If Yoda ever turned that way, Sidious himself would be annihilated. The universe had yet to comprehend the kind of evil that a Jedi Knight of nearly nine hundred years could wield." (YDR ch. 11)
3. **Duel on Vjun. Yoda overpowers Dooku on dark-side-steeped ground, and Dooku has to flee with a missile gambit.**
   > "Among the other Jedi, perhaps only Mace Windu would have been his equal on neutral ground" (YDR ch. 11)
   > "Then their blades clashed together in a lace of fire, green and red: but the green burned hotter. Slowly, slowly, Dooku gave way" (YDR ch. 11)
   > "I know how to kill," Dooku hissed. ... "Yes—but Yoda knows how to live!" (YDR ch. 11)
   - Yoda then turns aside an orbital missile with the Force, "not to oppose the missile’s force with force, but to touch it gently on the side" (YDR ch. 11).
4. **Push-feather: Dooku's childhood.** Dooku never beat Yoda, and it set up the book's central promise.
   > "When one fighter his balance has lost, win his opponent has," Yoda said mildly. "I win." (YDR ch. 11)
   > "When you fall, apprentice … catch you I will!" (YDR ch. 11)
5. **Ventress is better than Jai Maruk.**
   > "She was better than he was." (YDR ch. 8)
   - She also states her creed: "The truth is, there is no good, and no evil, either," ... "There is only life … or not." (YDR ch. 9)
6. **Dooku's dark-side doctrine, and his end state.**
   > "To know the dark side is merely to stop lying. Stop pretending you don’t want what you want. Stop pretending you don’t fear what you fear." (YDR ch. 11)
   > "Alone, the shell whispered. Alone, alone, alone." (YDR ch. 12)

Other notes:
- **Vjun lore.** Count Malreaux's consortium was "genetically enhancing the natural midi-chlorian levels of Vjun’s populace" and the population went mad (YDR ch. 10).
- **Back matter.** It adds four excerpts not seen in 044: *Labyrinth of Evil*, *Darth Bane: Path of Destruction*, *Hard Contact* and *Shadows of Mindor* (see yda-05). The rest is the same as the 044 back matter.
