# Yoda: Dark Rendezvous: notes part 3, Chapters 7–8 (Phindar)

## Ch. 7: Jai and Whie; the Phindar ambush
- Public distrust of the Jedi: "baby-napper!" and "‘Palpatine’s Secret Police’ was a whisper he was hearing more and more often—even, painfully, from schismatic Jedi who had left the Order."
- Jai on Scout: "a girl like that was going to be dead within a year." He grieves for Politrix, "Killed in an ambush two months after Geonosis." Yoda's saying: "Too long sorrow makes a stone of the heart."
- Whie's Broken Gate form: the Force "called ... down like a thunderbolt" and bursts the floor mat.
> "It is the decision to dominate, to crush, to draw your strength from another being’s weakness that signals a turn to the dark side. Dark or light is not a feeling, but a choice." (Jai Maruk, ch. 7). Also: "Once the dark side has you, boy, it doesn’t let go." And on whether he would kill a fallen Jedi: "Yes." / "Because he was family".
- Whie: "I was a good boy ... I thought that was the same as virtue." Jai: "Amazing how easy it is to resist other people’s temptations, isn’t it?"
- Jai: "My death ... is somebody else’s responsibility."
- Solis's history: "My family was killed more than two hundred standard years ago." Later he says he has "worked and schemed and bullied and cheated for four hundred standard years" to keep his life. They are servant droids with hardwired loyalty: "if, in effect, one allows it to live—the property has a disconcerting habit of developing a personality".
- At Phindar Spaceport, the SPCB (Sentient Property Crime Bureau) confronts the droids. Whie and Scout mind-trick Major Quecks. Ventress then arrives with **18 Confederacy assassin droids** ("the full complement that Last Call could carry"). They are custom-fitted for Jedi-hunting with flechette launchers, hard-sound projectors, sonic grenades and "tactical tractor beam prototypes". Jai recognizes the type "from Anakin Skywalker’s report on his mission to Jabiim".
- The hard-sound guns burst Jai's eardrums. Maks Leem smashes one by Force-hurling a stanchion. They pin the R2 and grenade it, but Ventress carves it open to find it empty: "Yoda had carved an escape hatch through the floor." Droid A77's head "popped out of the hole".
- Maks Leem: "her hatred for the war had manifested itself in a dull subconscious resistance to the whole idea of fighting." She "lopped the arm off" a droid and fired its own blaster.
- Solis: "Not my fight." / "Animals die. It’s what you do." Fidelis: "Shocking lack of values. There’s one in every production run." Fidelis holds Whie back ("I didn’t wait ten years outside the Jedi Temple only to let you throw your life away"). Scout is hit.

## Ch. 8: Yoda and Chuff, Jai's choice, Ventress's sermon
- Yoda meets Chuff ("Worse for wear, I look!"). Chuff has set *Last Call*'s engines to fire. Yoda cuts its magnetic anchors, deflects a sonic grenade with "a swat of the Force", and plays decoy for four assassin droids "Without so much as a lightsaber". He hands Chuff his lightsaber ("Live your part, you can. Be a Jedi hero, you must!" … "It usually is!"). *Last Call* punches out through the station wall. Yoda holds an air pocket in the lift by will ("A pocket of air remained in the lift, held there by Yoda’s will"), and the droids are blown into space.
- Fidelis tears an assassin droid's head off with his fingers through the eyeholes, then crushes its innards "to a gleaming lump the size of a sugar cube": "Cheap thugs ... Terribly underbred."
- Maks Leem Force-crimps a droid's blaster barrel and fires it, blowing the droid up.
- **Jai Maruk vs Ventress:**
> "There was only one thing he had never fully admitted into his analysis. She was better than he was. / Just. / Better." … "Faster. More elegant. Better footwork. More precise with her blades. ... even her touch with the Force was better than his: more powerful, more subtle, more nuanced, and ... more deeply understood." (ch. 8)
- She is "younger than him by five years or more". The Temple nicknamed Jai "The Hawk-bat". Ventress cuts down Maks Leem ("Good ... That’s it, seventeen"), who seals the hull breach with the Force as she dies. Her silent "Don’t, Jai."
> "Nobody would ever know how close Jai had come to giving in to the dark side. ... To Jai Maruk, it meant everything. / For the next thirty seconds he fought more beautifully than he had in his life, and when Asajj finally cut him down, he was smiling." (ch. 8)
- Ventress kills the helpless Leem: "Eighteen". She Force-flings Whie into a counter. "My first Master was a Jedi. The Order abandoned him to torment and death."
- Whie: "I dreamed my own death. And this wasn’t it." … "I will die under a Jedi’s hand."
- **Ventress on the dark side:**
> "Let me tell you about the dark side. Typical Jedi propaganda to name it so. Let me give it another name. Let’s call it the truth." / "The truth is, there is no good, and no evil, either. There is only life … or not." / "‘Give in to your anger!’ It’s a simple trick, and an effective one, because it works. ... But that is not the end of the journey. It is the beginning." (Ventress, ch. 8)
- She calls Yoda "an eight-hundred-odd-year-old hypocrite who may live forever". She leaves after blowing a hull breach with a dart gun. Jai's dying words to Scout: "Still … winning … the hard way" / "Never … my Padawan."

## Ch. 9: Fallout, "Kill your Master", the candle speech
- Dooku thinks: "It was beginning to look very much as if Dooku could simply win: march his battle droids into Coruscant and claim the Republic outright." / "When one has sufficient force, there is no need for schemes." He calls Sidious "an expert in personal disintegration".
- *Last Call* is "the third one she’d lost, if one counted the craft Anakin and Kenobi had stolen from her".
- Sidious: "would you like to know what I do, when my servants show enough … initiative?" … "I crush them." Dooku touches the red button.
- **Palpatine maneuvers** Mace into sending Obi-Wan and Anakin to Vjun: "I think the world of Master Yoda, but he is very old, and perhaps not all that he once was."
- Dooku to Ventress: "If I have a prisoner, or indeed an ally ... that is costing too much in resources ... I eliminate that person." / "Right now, you are costing me more than you’re worth."
- **Ventress:** "Kill your Master." / "Sooner or later, every Sith apprentice tries to overthrow his Master." / "What happens when you are the most dangerous being left standing?" / "He’ll pick someone younger, weaker, easier to influence." Ventress knows Dooku has a Master and suspects his fate (an ROTS foreshadow).
- Dooku: "Master Yoda, perhaps, is the exception. His loyalty to his students runs deeper than theirs to him, I think." Dooku orders her back to Vjun.
- Ventress paid Solis only a third. Solis: "I may be a traitor, but I’m not a cut-rate one." / "We’ll see about that."
- Anakin almost blurts out his promise to Padmé. Obi-Wan covers: "The students in Master Iron Hand’s class". Obi-Wan: "I feel, with every nerve in my body, that someday you won’t thank me for it."
- Yoda haggles for the B-7 light freighter *Nighthawk* ("Fly," it doesn't). "So waste the people’s money I should?" / "Not interested in fair, am I. Interested in results". Yoda's saying: "Age more than a count of heartbeats is. Age is how many mistakes you have made."
- Whie fears he will fall: "I think I’m going to go over to the dark side ... That’s why a Jedi cuts me down." Scout: "Sometimes pretending is all there is."
- Fidelis: "House Malreaux, of course, has produced the finest adepts of its arts" and "Count Malreaux’s attempt to apply genetic manipulations to the midi-chlorian bodies was ... perhaps overly ambitious." This is the cause of Vjun's madness. Yoda: "Strong it is … in the dark side."
- Yoda: "Old are we, and strong; trees that have survived many frosts. But for these two, their Masters’ deaths a first winter are."
- **The memorial feast:** Yoda: "Honor life by living, Padawan. Killing honors only death: only the dark side." Yoda Force-catches the thrown platter, then:
> "Teach me about pain, think you can? ... Loved more than you, have I, Padawan. Lost more. Hated more. Killed more. ... Think wisdom comes at no cost? The dark side, yes—it is easier for them. The pain grows too great, and they eat the darkness to flee from it. Not Yoda. Yoda loves and suffers for it, loves and suffers." (Yoda, ch. 9)
> "If no plan there is, no fate, no destiny, no providence, no Force: then what is left? ... Nothing but our choices, hmm? / Asajj eats the darkness, and the darkness eats her back. ... To be Jedi is to face the truth, and choose. Give off light, or darkness, Padawan. ... Be a candle, or the night, Padawan: but choose!" (Yoda, ch. 9)
- Whie: "What if there is nothing but stars and the black space between them and the galaxy does not care if we live or die?" Yoda: "It’s true." / "Perhaps." / "Many days, feel certain of a greater hope, I do. Some days, not so."
