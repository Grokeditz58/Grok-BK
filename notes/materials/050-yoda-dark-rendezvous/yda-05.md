## New in this edition: four excerpts not in 044 (hand-read)
### bm03–bm04: *Labyrinth of Evil* (James Luceno), ch. 1 opening, Cato Neimoidia
- Obi-Wan and Anakin attack Gunray's redoubt through manax orchards. Anakin: "Sorry, Master, but your head was where my lightsaber needed to go." / "Your fears are only in your mind."
- **Anakin status details:** his braid was "ritually severed after his audacious actions at Praesitlyn". His face is "scarred from a contest with Dooku-trained Asajj Ventress". He has a mechanical right hand in an "elbow-length glove". He is now a Jedi Knight, and Obi-Wan sits on the Council.
- Task force commander Dodonna gives the briefing. Cody's campaign stripes: "Aagonar, Praesitlyn, Paracelus Minor, Antar 4, Tibrin, Skor II". The ARCs are named: Alpha (Rattatak) and Jangotat (Ord Cestus). "Cody wasn’t genetically an Advanced Recon Commando, he had ARC training".
- Jedi pushed for "second- and third-generation troopers be given names rather than numbers". Obi-Wan: "if Chancellor Palpatine had genuine concern for your welfare, he would have kept you closer to Coruscant."
- They run under harvester beetles through a minefield. Anakin Force-leaps "the Force guiding him to safe landings among the mines" and cuts off a beetle's pincers. More than 100 STAPs attack. Anakin uses "a Jedi healing technique to keep the clone from going into deep shock".
- **Army scale:** "only half the original army of 1.2 million in fighting shape". Droidekas roll in at "better than eighty kilometers per hour".

### bm07–bm08: *Darth Bane: Path of Destruction* (Drew Karpyshyn), ch. 1–2
- Dessel mines cortosis on Apatros for Outer Rim Oreworks (ORO). Cortosis is "Impervious to both heat and energy ... supposedly could withstand even the blade of a lightsaber". His jack weighs "more than thirty kilos".
- Fight with Gerd. Des's precognition:
> "sometimes he could just anticipate an opponent’s next move. ... It was more like a vision, a brief glimpse into the future. And whenever it happened, Des always knew what to do, as if something was guiding and directing his actions." (*Path of Destruction* ch. 1)
- Rage gives him strength: "Dark rage exploded inside Des ... his body felt strong and rejuvenated". He bites off Gerd's thumb. "Bane" was the name his abusive father Hurst used for him.
- **Ch. 2:** he is suspended. Republic cargo crews are recruiting against the "Brotherhood of Darkness". Groshik, the Neimoidian bartender, runs the cantina. Sabacc rules: modified Bespin Standard, 23, "pure sabacc". Des sits down against the Republic commander: "I don’t need luck tonight".

### bm011: *Republic Commando: Hard Contact* (Karen Traviss), ch. 1
- **Geonosis casualty figures:** "SERIOUS INJURIES, TWELVE THOUSAND ... WALKING WOUNDED EIGHT THOUSAND ... SEVENTY-TWO THOUSAND COMBAT-FIT TROOPS". RC-1136 Darman has lost his squad (Jay, Vin and Taler); the Duros loadmaster says "Nobody alive, anyway."
- Clone doctrine: "A clone didn’t need to. Clones knew. They had been perfected for their role". The DC-17 is "Ion pulse blaster, RPG anti-armor, and sniper", in "enhanced Katarn armor".
- Etain Tur-Mukan hides from a Weequay on Qiilura. Ghez Hokan is "the voice". Her Master Fulier was "unfortunate. And betrayed." She can't use the mind trick under panic and calls herself "a breath away from building permadomes in refugee camps".
- Niner (RC-1309) and Fi (RC-8015) are both sole survivors. Niner: "It was a waste ... Deploying us in an operation like Geonosis. It was an infantry job." Gear check takes "twenty-six minutes and twenty seconds". Mandalorian chant: "Gra’tua cuun hett su dralshy’a. Our vengeance burns brighter still."

### bm014: *Shadows of Mindor* (Matthew Stover), ch. 1
- Rogue Squadron uses a fake *Corellian Queen* to ambush "Shadowspawn"'s TIE Defender marauders with flechette torpedoes ("a very, very expensive cheese grater"). TIE Defender specs: "twin Novaldex deflector generators were nearly as powerful as those found on capital ships", but no particle shields.
- Hobbie loses another prosthetic left hand ("He hadn’t had a real left arm since sometime before Yavin") and is banter-towed by Janson ("Batravian gumplucker").
- Marauders self-destruct ("For Shadowspawn and the Empire! Forward the Restoration!") via a "deadman interlock". Passive transponders track them to Taspan / Mindor: "The Big Crush", the gravity-well projector testing site. Lando sends the Rapid Response Task Force under "General Skywalker".
