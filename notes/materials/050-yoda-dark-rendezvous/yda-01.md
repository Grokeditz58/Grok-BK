## Ch. 1: the shell and the candle
- Opening image: "The long day of the Republic had come to an end." Padmé: "Hope. It isn’t a luxury. It’s our duty."
- Jedi Master Jai Maruk returns with lightsaber burns. He tracked disguised Trade Federation transports ("Deep-space vectors") to Vjun. "In a nerf’s coat, this krayt dragon was." (Yoda)
> "Strong in the dark side, Vjun is." / "A trial of being old is this: remembering which thing one has said into which young ears." (Yoda, ch. 1)
- Present: Yoda, Ilena Xan ("Iron Hand", a joint-lock specialist who teaches hand-to-hand), and Mace Windu. Padawan Tallisibeth "Scout" (red hair, about 14) eavesdrops. Her Master was Chankar Kim, "fallen in the ring at Geonosis".
- Mace: "The Force is weak in that one." He suggests the Agricultural Corps. Yoda: "But if you make her stop, tell you it is ‘kind,’ she will not!" / "Nor do Jedi Masters [want what is best for them]".
> "Best of all, Dooku would be!" … "Our great student!" … "Our great failure." (Yoda, ch. 1)
- Maruk lost to Ventress ("We fought. I lost.") and was spared by Dooku as a messenger: "Make no mistake, I am here because Dooku wanted me here." Dooku says he wants peace. Ilena: "Bioweapons slaughter innocents by the millions on Honoghr and he wants peace!" The token is a hermit-crab shell.
- **Flashback, 63 years earlier:** Dooku, by the Temple pond the night before leaving, has Padawan of Thame Cerulian. He lifts Yoda's glow light with "the barest flick of his attention". "Every Jedi is a child his parents decided they could live without." Yoda's question, "What are we, think you, Dooku?", gets the answer "alone".
> "Think you I have never felt the touch of the dark? Know you what a soul so great as Yoda can make, in eight hundred years?" … "Many mistakes!" (Yoda, ch. 1)
> "Your Master, Thame Cerulian, says the most gifted Padawan he ever saw, you are." / "Fear the dark side, you should. In the mighty is it mightiest." / "A candle will I light, for you to find your way home." (Yoda, ch. 1)
- Mace: "Dooku should be dead. I should have killed him on Geonosis. I could have stopped the whole war then." He backs the gamble: "balance that chance, however small, against a million lives". Mace "lost the knack" of laughing "somewhere in the ring on Geonosis".
- Ventress brings Dooku the frozen severed hand and lightsaber of Jang Li-Li: "I make that sixteen [Jedi]" … "Seventeen, it should have been, if you had allowed me to kill that spy, Maruk." Her head has "twelve marks, one for each of the twelve warlords she had killed".
- Dooku refuses her apprenticeship with the sickle-back mantis parable:
> "You would betray me. ... It is the unhappy hazard of embracing the dark side. I am old, and I have learned the limits of my ambition. You are young, and strong, and those two things have always led to one place in the history of the Sith." (Dooku, ch. 1)
> "In a perfect world, one could feed an apprentice just enough to keep him growing ... Then if any of the Master’s plans went wrong, why, he could take the fall." (Dooku, ch. 1). This mirrors his own position under Sidious.
- Dooku tortures Ventress with one finger ("Like sharp stones in your throat and chest" / "the blood vessels ... stretch inside, like balloons about to pop"). His own memories surface: "By the pitiless stars, the things you’ve done …"
- Age and physicality: "he wore his eighty-three standard years better than most humans half his age". Before Sidious he feels his years.
- Sidious on Troxar: "The war has already damaged the planet too much to make it worth saving. Its only value now is to chew up more troops and resources." Dooku: "How casually we betray our creatures."
- Château Malreaux: the mad seventeenth Viscount's spy-eye system, Vjun's madness plague, and acid rain and carnivorous Vjun moss. Whie's "Momma" (the madwoman with the Vjun fox) sees the bloodstain: "The Baby’s coming home!"
- Sidious: "Do you still love him?" … "A good student always loves his teacher." Dooku plans "a nine-hundred-year-old head" as a gift.
> "Vividly his mind went back to their last meeting, on Geonosis: swords drawn at last, and finally equal. ... to see Yoda again, and be a match, or more than a match for him" (Dooku POV, ch. 1)
- Sidious calls Anakin "one strong in the Force. 'Just a little piece in a great game'", which makes Dooku jealous. Anakin "had recently killed a clone of Count Dooku of Serenno".
- Padmé's box, via the Troxan under-palatine, holds "a plain wax candle".

## Ch. 2: the lightsaber message and Whie's true dream
- Two assassin droids (the "cream with crimson" Malreaux-livery droid and his rusted gray twin, the HK-style "Waiting" pair) play dejarik on a skyrise and watch the Temple through optics that outclass "the legendary Tau/Zeiss telescopic sniperscope".
- Timeframe: "some thirty months after the Battle of Geonosis".
- Master Maks Leem, a Gran, sees the war turning Jedi "contemptibly, into soldiers". The empty Temple: "Forty places set for dinner in a hall made to hold a hundred." The children are "learning to feel with hearts made too old, too hard, too soon." It is "For the first time since the Sith War" that a generation grows up "surrounded by a Force clouded by the dark side".
- Her Padawan Whie: "The Force was strong in him." Leem: "Not so strong as in young Skywalker, but in the next level down: and between you and me, he carries it better than Anakin ever has." Yoda: "I think he wishes to please you very much."
- Yoda wants to move the Temple off Coruscant (half in jest): "Only on a planet such as Coruscant, with no forests left, no mountains unleveled, no streams left to run their own course, could the Force have become so clouded." / "Tired I am of all this … making. Where is the time for being, Maks Leem?"
- **The afterlife theme:** Yoda's pebble and wave image, "Speak of this once, Qui-Gon did." Leem's former Padawan Eremin Tarn left the Order to follow the dissident Jeisel.
> "But when the day comes that even old Yoda does not learn something from his students—then truly, he shall be a teacher no more." / "No greater gift there is, than a generous heart." (Yoda, ch. 2)
- Jang Li-Li's modified hilt plays a Dooku hologram:
> "I felt it growing in the Republic years ago. I fled it then, and tried to bring the Order with me. You wouldn’t come. ... Perhaps the Temple was the only lantern to keep the darkness at bay ... Or perhaps the darkness was inside me all the time." / "I feel it all the time, the cries of the dying in the Force, beating in me like a vein about to burst." / "you are betrayed more profoundly than you guess, Master." / "Bring none but Jedi with you." (Dooku hologram, ch. 2)
- **Qui-Gon speaks to Yoda in the Force:** "Become a wave he has, Yoda thought. A wave without a shore." Qui-Gon on Dooku: "He is a fencer ... Leverage, position, advantage." / "He thinks he is lying." Yoda's flashback calls Dooku "a boy of twenty" in "a cave on Geonosis" (memory blur).
- Whie's "true dreams" (precognition since age 8): he sees a Château room, Ventress ("Kiss her, Whie" / "Welcome home") and a bleeding Scout. "His death was coming. Soon."

## Ch. 3: Apprentice Tournament, rounds 1–2 (Scout)
- Tallisibeth Enwandung-Esterhazy ("Scout"), from the slums of Vorzyd V. "the Force was weak in Tallisibeth"; her one gift is anticipation. Chankar Kim chose her, then died three months later. Earlier events: "the devastation on Honoghr and the Rendili Fleet Crisis".
- The tournament has 32 entrants, sudden-death, with training sabers at the lowest setting (three burns or three taps to lose). Scout has been burning herself daily to get used to the pain.
- R1 vs Pax Chizzik (age 11): Pax unconsciously Force-persuades her ("I should let him win") until Yoda's snuffle breaks it. He weaves "the Mrlssi half-hitch ... all in light". Scout fakes a surrender bow, then wrist-locks him.
- R2 vs Hanna Ding (Arkanian, 14), fought in the dark. Hanna Force-drags Scout's sword arm and pulls her lightsaber away. Scout refuses to yield ("Doesn’t hurt enough yet") and grabs Hanna's blade bare-handed as her second burn, then chokes her out: "that was a real fight." Master Xan: "I guess it was, at that."
