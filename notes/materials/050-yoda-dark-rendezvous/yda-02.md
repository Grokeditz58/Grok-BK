# Yoda: Dark Rendezvous: notes part 2, Chapters 4–5

## Ch. 4: Round of Eight in the refectory
- Yoda's food: "hot, swampy stews that smelled like boiled mud". He fights a rectory droid over his bowl ("Ignorant machine! Not on menu, my food ever is.").
- **Whie's origin:** "Born on Vjun was he." / "Insane, the father went. And his mother … very strong she was. Very strong." / "‘Take him from the slaughterhouse.’ Her words those were. Drunk she was, and half out of her mind with grief, for there was murder in the house that day." As a child, Whie made a Rigelian iris bloom at his touch.
> "Sometimes wrong and right only have meaning in small time. In big time, in decades, in centuries … then we see that things are as they are. Each choice, the branch of a tree is ... Each act, you see, is like a fossil, preserved in the Force" (Yoda, ch. 4)
- Jai Maruk: "in the work we do, I think she would be dead in six months." Yoda's wager: if Scout finishes in the bottom four of the eight, she goes to the corps; if she wins, "your Padawan she will be."
- Master Xan: "The living Force you come to see here, under Master Yoda’s guidance, is the truest reality".
- Free-for-all: Scout chokes out Sisseri Deo (a 2.3 m Firrerreo) after a muja-juice feint. Whie leaps "five effortless meters" and backflips; seeing Scout, he recoils (the dream). Whie thumb-locks Lena. In the final, Scout drives into the lock, knowing "He was too nice" to snap her thumb, then shoulder-throws him and wins. Hanna: "until she runs out of thumbs."
- Pax Chizzik: "being a Jedi is about being resourceful, keeping your eyes open, and never, ever giving up."

## Ch. 5: Padawans, the decoy and the hunters
- Yoda to Scout: "Between the stars, so much darkness there is. Why would I throw away one who burns so bright?" / "Grand Master of Jedi Order am I! ... Won this job in a raffle I did, think you?"
- Whie in the Star Room has been studying the Honoghr disaster for Master Tycho. "situations, like people, could give in to the dark side: and once the dark side had one in its grip, it never, ever let one go." Also the Battle of Brentaal.
- Whie: "So long as they allow themselves to do things we will not, they will always have an advantage." Leem: "Ah, but the dark side eats its young." (déjà vu from a dream)
- Yoda: "Better things than fighting, should a Jedi Master be doing! Seeking wisdom. Finding balance. But these are the days given to us." Whie proposes the feint, and Scout's idea is to hide Yoda inside an R2 shell.
- **Sidious to Dooku:** "you were born to one of the wealthiest families in the galaxy ... Your understanding is deep; your will, adamant." / "a man who will not bow to the Jedi Council, or even Master Yoda … I wondered if perhaps loyalty was too mean". Dooku: "I have paid for your war with my time, my riches, my friends, and my honor." Sidious tells Dooku Yoda is coming: "see that you treat him as he deserves."
- Dooku's "small red button", installed after hearing Yoda intended to come: "A last card to play." Whirry (the madwoman) and her fox Miss Vix read spilled stimcaf: "Death from a high place" / "the Footman, which stands for the easy destruction of a faithful servant." Dooku: "How easily we betray our creatures."
- Decoy: the actor Palleus Chuff, "the greatest adult actor on Coruscant under one meter tall", author of *Jedi!*, plays Yoda flying to Ithor.
- Assassin droids **Fidelis** (in Malreaux livery, still loyal to the Family) and **Solis** (unpainted, with "Tau/Zeiss tacoptics") spot the Temple party: "Your target’s leaving the Temple." / "There’s something funny about that artoo". Solis: "Time to hunt."
- Ventress waits in deep space above Coruscant in her starfighter *Last Call* (a "Huppla Pasa Tisc fan-blade starfighter").
- Spaceport comedy at "Chancellor Palpatine Spaceport" ("Chance Palp"): Jai Maruk ("born on Druckenwell") gets a Deep Tissue Inspection. Whie mind-tricks a guard. The fan guard Charpp has seen *Jedi!* fifteen times ("People say the Jedi are only out for themselves now. They say they’re just the Chancellor’s secret police."). Yoda-in-R2 Force-levitates over a refresher stall and knots its bolt shut, with "a strange, hollow cackle".

## Ch. 6: Ventress ambush, Arkania, Palpatine and Mace, Reasonable Doubt
- **Ventress dogfight (feat):** The *Last Call* carries a Geonosian-built "gemcutter" that detects ships about to drop out of hyperspace (stolen Carbanti prototype). She kills three Tavya-class pickets, steering one into a proton torpedo. "On a good day, Asajj Ventress could walk between raindrops". She reaches out with the Force: "Last call, lover ... It’s closing time." She senses the decoy as "a weak and puny thing" and recognizes "factory-standard evasive maneuvers". She takes Chuff prisoner and scatters debris to fake Yoda's death. Dooku's maxim: "excellent wine, and confusion to the enemy."
- Obi-Wan and Anakin on Arkania. The Jedi Serifa Altunen resigns: "I follow the Force—not the law. I serve the people—not the Senate. I will make peace—not war." / "It is the Order that has strayed from the path."
- Obi-Wan knows about the secret messages ("You just guessed." / "I am a wise and powerful Jedi Knight, you know").
> "To follow your heart, to either love or hate, in the long run is the same mistake. Your judgment becomes clouded. Your motives, confused. If you are not very careful, Padawan, love will take you to the dark side. Slower than hate, yes, but no less surely for that." (Obi-Wan, ch. 6)
- Anakin: "I trust the living Force. I trust love. The ‘principle of nonattachment’ …? That’s an awfully abstract thing to pledge loyalty to."
- Padmé: "I’ll believe he’s gone when I see them bring his body back. Not before."
- **Palpatine and Mace (manipulation):** Palpatine refuses to deny Yoda's death ("The Chancellor serves the people and the Senate, not the Jedi Order"). He orders the surviving pilot court-martialed for cowardice.
> "But Master Yoda is a teacher at heart. You are a warrior. Regrettably, this sad age of the world may be your time more than his." (Palpatine to Mace, ch. 6). Mace: "Master Yoda is many things, and I am not his equal in peace or war." Palpatine: "We may need that, too."
- Mace quotes Yoda: "The best security, Master Yoda once said, lies in creating a society that nobody wishes to attack."
- Orn Free Taa proposes a security force "answerable directly to the Chancellor’s office", which Padmé distrusts (a proto-Imperial security apparatus).
- The Verpine liner *Reasonable Doubt* (formerly *Asymptotic Approach to Divinity* / *Stardust*). The Jedi travel as the "Pho family". Scout to Whie: "Jedi serenity is all very well, but in a thirteen-year-old boy it’s sort of creepy."
- **Solis** (who will betray them, "my treason tables are very precisely calibrated"): "Then Fate intervenes, strips us down to bare metal, and we see we are little more than debris, floating in darkness."
- **Fidelis reveals:** "You were born Whie Malreaux" ... "unlike your unfortunate brother". He is "programmed for absolute loyalty to the House Malreaux, which I have served through madness and war for twelve generations." His mother (Whirry) is "currently the head of House Malreaux" and is "daily insulted and degraded by the odious Count Dooku."
- Yoda-in-R2 steals a Fizzy-Bip with a mind trick on a child ("What drink?"). A fire alarm sets off chaos: "Keep a low profile."
- The news reports Ventress as "wanted on eight worlds in connection with the deaths of eleven Jedi Knights". Ventress: "Seventeen!"
- Solis sells Yoda's location to Ventress for "seven hundred and thirty-four thousand nine hundred ninety-five Republic credits". She looks him up in *Peterson’s Guide to Droids*: "THE LEGENDARY TAC-SPEC FOOTMAN DROID ... THE DEADLIEST PERSONAL SERVICE UNITS EVER CREATED".
