# Yoda: Dark Rendezvous: notes part 4, Chapters 10–12

## Ch. 10: Vjun
- **Setting:** Château Malreaux sits above the Bay of Tears and the River Weeping. "The first Count Malreaux had been a pirate". Vjun moss glows "a venomous green". Bitter End "had numbered some sixty thousand souls before plagues and madness had rendered it a ghost town a decade before." The acid rain rots Obi-Wan's robes ("I’d hate to be the minister for tourism here").
- Solis watches the B-7's faked crash through his "T/Z telescopic sniperscope". Dooku's "guard of honor" is 15 humans, 10 elite assassin droids and two platoons.
- Dooku tortures Miss Vix, pulling out fur ("would you like to read your future?"). He knows about Fidelis ("A Tac-Spec Footman who served the House Malreaux for twelve generations"). Whirry: "Which it was supposed to be a surprise, like."
- Yoda leaves the Padawans: "Vjun calls to the dark side in him." Whie dances skeletons with the Force ("The original owners aren’t using them."). Whie: "The dark side is here ... We carry it with us wherever we go."
- **Vjun's backstory (Fidelis):** "The Count your father was part of a consortium involved in genetically enhancing the natural midi-chlorian levels of Vjun’s populace. The experiment was, in fact, wildly successful." Scout: "You mean they created a whole planet of Forcesensitives with no mental training for handling it?" Whie: "Madness. They all went mad, didn’t they? You can hear the rocks screaming." Also: "In times past, Vjun has had some contact with the Sith".
- Yoda's teachings recalled: the Silent Meditation ("A child full of the Force like a cloud carrying lightning is"). "When you look at the dark side, careful you must be … for the dark side looks back."
- **Dooku remembers Yoda's first lightsaber class:** "Combat timing is, the Master used to say; and the job of the warrior, to destroy his opponent’s timing." / "the Force gathered at the tip of his wooden sword like caged lightning, and when he lifted his foot and stamped it back down into a wide ready stance, you could feel the whole Temple shake."
- Dooku: "Funny to think he had ever been so young that a single life seemed precious." Also "You must stand too much in his sun, Count." His monitors show Honoghr six months after the toxic catastrophe, "part of General Grievous’s proposal to step up use of bioweapons in the Outer Rim campaigns." His hand shakes. Yoda appears on the fifth-story window ledge, "grinning like a gargoyle".
- Anakin's Chryya-class courier makes a suicidal reentry and melts a pursuing picket. "It’s not just about winning, Master ... It’s about winning with style." / "I could walk on water". Blinded droids use echolocation ("in one of the latest updates"). Anakin's Force-guided grenade collapses the caves ("think a little bit longer before rearranging the landscape").
- Scout Force-pins Whie's hand to a rock in a flood ("you’re my arrogant show-off ... That’s what Jedi do for each other."). Fidelis leads them into the carpeted room from the dream, where Ventress waits with six droids: "Master Malreaux ... Welcome home." (Fidelis had frozen earlier; Whirry had been ordered to contact him.)

## Ch. 11: The rendezvous (key chapter)
- **Push-feather flashback:** Dooku "won the Twelve-and-Under Tournament when he was nine" by popping a pressure point. He never beat Yoda, who let weaker children win but "never lost to Dooku, never once." At twelve, Dooku decided to break his own arm to shame Yoda.
> "“I win,” Yoda said. / “What!” Dooku had yelped. “We haven’t even started!” / “When one fighter his balance has lost, win his opponent has,” Yoda said mildly. “I win.”" … "“When you fall, apprentice … catch you I will!”" (ch. 11)
- Dooku's rose memory from Serenno: "I was hiding in the rose garden and my finger was bleeding." / "My mother got angry if I hurt myself." / "The Counts of Serenno do not complain and cry."
- Whirry calls Yoda "one of they nasty cellar-goblins". Dooku: "small and old and shriveled up like an evil green potato". **Dooku's outburst projects his own childhood:**
> "The Jedi arrived like beggars on your doorstep and asked for your firstborn, your heir, your precious Baby … and you gave him up. ... Mother? Son? Love? ... You don’t know the meaning of the words." (Dooku to Whirry, ch. 11)
- Dooku: "the Jedi have become a tool in the hands of a corrupt Republic." Yoda cuts him off: "No lies for me, Dooku ... No Sora Bulq am I, to be caught in a web made of ideals." (Sora Bulq is a reference to the Clone Wars-era fallen Jedi.)
> "Turn me, Dooku. I beg you. Show me the greatness of the dark side." (Yoda, ch. 11)
- **Fidelis's death:** Fidelis had betrayed them on the orders of Whie's "lady mother" "in the long-term interests of House Malreaux". Ventress forces him to use the neural-net eraser on himself to save Whie: "Only remember, I served the House Malreaux." / "I didn’t think it would end like this." Ventress: "Loyalty ... It’ll get you every time." Solis: "hadn’t been programmed to hate, either, but he was a fast learner."
- **Dooku on the dark side:**
> "Yoda teaches like drunkards drink. Like killers kill." (Yoda) / "To know the dark side is merely to stop lying. Stop pretending you don’t want what you want. Stop pretending you don’t fear what you fear. Half the day is night, Master Yoda. To see truly, you have to learn to see in the dark." (Dooku, ch. 11)
- Dooku on Anakin: "Perhaps he reminds me too much of myself at the same age. Arrogant. Impulsive. Proud. ... if Fate is looking for an instrument to humble Skywalker, I confess myself willing to volunteer."
- Yoda: "Sent you the candle, did I". **The rose:** "I want a rose." / "Another rose, make for me!" Dooku: "The Force is not magic. I can’t create a flower out of thin air. Nobody can—not you, not the Lord of the Sith."
> "I will never be safe ... The universe is large and cold and very dark: that is the truth. What I love, taken from me will be, late or soon: and no power is there, dark or light, that can save me." (Yoda, ch. 11)
- **Dark Yoda vision:** "for one terrible instant he looked exactly like Darth Sidious ... a Yoda gone rotten" / "If Yoda ever turned that way, Sidious himself would be annihilated. The universe had yet to comprehend the kind of evil that a Jedi Knight of nearly nine hundred years could wield." Yoda: "Disappointment like I not, apprentice ... Give me my rose!"
- Ventress tempts Whie in the Crying Room: "In this world the only rule is power". She Force-pulls Scout's lips into a smile: "Kiss her, Whie." … "Welcome home ... Now choose." (The dream comes true.)
- Yoda: "Your hand is shaking." "Age." "Fear." Dooku: "This is a trap." Yoda: "A trap? Oh, yes it is."
> "Yoda a darkness carries with him … and Dooku bears a light. ... Even the blind seed grows to the light: should mighty Dooku be unable to achieve what even the rose can do?" / "Each instant the universe annihilates itself, and starts again. ... Choose, and start again!" (Yoda, ch. 11)
- Scout breaks the Force grip: "You’re going to make the right choice!" Whie: "I’m not a bad guy!" / "that scares me less than the idea I was going to … to turn into you ... No offense." / "None taken".
- **Solis returns** with a "Rika/Moab mini rail cannon" ("Upgrades.") and destroys all six droids. "I didn’t want you to die ... I never got very attached to the boy." Ventress erases him with the eraser, and he shoots her through the left leg as he dies. Ventress runs into Obi-Wan and Anakin ("True as you tell it ... But what are you going to do with us?") and flees.
- The red light: "Your new Master calls. Dooku, ask yourself: which of us loves you better?" "I serve only Darth Sidious." "Not my question, apprentice." "more forgiveness will you find from your old Master than from the new one."
- **Dooku decides** when he sees Anakin on the monitors ("Your new favorite son"). He Force-hurls Whirry out the window to distract Yoda, then attacks: "I intend to enjoy killing you." Yoda: "More than eight hundred years has Yoda survived" / "I know how to kill." / "Yes—but Yoda knows how to live!" "Among the other Jedi, perhaps only Mace Windu would have been his equal on neutral ground".
> "And yet, even here on Vjun, where the dark side whispers and whispers to me … love you enough to destroy you I do." (Yoda, ch. 11)
- The red button launches a missile from orbit: "what means more to you, Master Yoda? Saving their lives—or taking mine?" Yoda deflects the 200-kilo missile with a "push-feather game with all their lives on the line", and it plunges "a kilometer offshore". Dooku escapes.
- Anakin: "Did you almost kill Dooku? ... How frustrating!" Yoda gives him "an odd look—almost angry." Anakin: "This is the New Brutalism." Whie hugs Anakin: "I’m so glad you’re not coming to kill me!" (This is dramatic irony given Whie's vision of dying under a Jedi blade.) Ventress steals the Chryya ("the first time I met Asajj Ventress, I stole her spaceship ... On Queyta").

## Ch. 12: Homecoming
- They bought a secondhand Seltaya on the Hydian Way, trading in "two Trade Federation gunships they had hijacked to get off Vjun". Scout commandeered the ships at the spaceport.
- Obi-Wan: "Humility endless is" / "Did you ever hear Mace Windu’s translation? ... You’re never too old to make another big mistake."
- Anakin looks for "one lit window" on Coruscant, a hint at Padmé.
- Whie: "No, we’re awake now ... The Temple was the dream." / "You gave me permission to be good." He still believes his death vision. Scout misses Hanna Ding: "The Order is the only family we have." On Geonosis: "Even the ones who survived came back different people. Grimmer."
- Whie on his mother: "What she lost was her baby, and that baby is gone. When she saw me, she saw a stranger."
> "Never step in the same river twice can you. ... The Jedi travel to the stars: and wait: and hope, with a candle in the window. Some return; some are broken; some come back so different only their names remain. Some choose the dark side, and are lost until the last journey ... The dark side within you is: you know this." … "Not a pile of permacrete, home is ... Wherever we are, is home." (Yoda, ch. 12)
- **Dooku** walks a shore at his new HQ "deep behind Trade Federation lines", with Ventress "clamoring to be made his apprentice" and a meeting with Grievous ("a great deal less interesting as a dinner-table conversationalist"). He finds a shell that echoes his Serenno childhood:
> "In the end, what we are is: alone. / Alone, the shell whispered. Alone, alone, alone. / He crushed the shell in his hand" (ch. 12)
- **Whirry** sits by the smashed window in a bloody ball gown. "When she first read her future in the broken glass, she wept." "Full dark fell, but she did not move to put a light in the window." By morning the fox is gone, which implies her death.
- Dawn on Coruscant. Padmé: "Something I can wear outside, but it must make me look wonderful" … "They’re home."
