## Thesis

The author introduces it as a "mini blog/respect thread". The Six Gods of Iokath (Tyth, Aivela, Esne, Nahut, Scyva, Izax) are galaxy-level superweapons. Their key points:

- they killed over 10 trillion beings;
- they fed on death and destruction;
- they rank above the Eternal Fleet, the Gravestone and the Eternal Throne;
- Izax's fully charged Omnicannon would wipe out many planets.

Conclusion: "Vaylin < Tyth < Aivela&Esne << Nahut << Scyva <<< Izax".

## Arguments (numbered)

1. **Collective accolades.** Points in this section:
   - The Gods were built to wipe out civilisations. Theron: "Over ten trillion lives, gone." (B002)
   - The Six Gods codex calls them superweapons "likely designed to pacify, subjugate, and destroy entire civilizations" (B001, OCR).
   - They are the creators' "greatest achievements", which the author places above the Eternal Fleet/Throne. The Fleet and Throne were "the greatest threats our galaxy has ever faced" (Satele, B003) and "The most powerful fleet in history" (Valkorion, B006).
   - The Gods are also placed above the Gravestone (B007, B050), which could destroy "entire sections of the Fleet… with a single shot", and above Iokath, a Dyson sphere (B008).
2. **Nothing can stop them.** Acina: "I've awakened the gods. Nothing can stop them." (B009) Oramis: "No one--not even you--can extinguish their radiance, but you can tame it." (B010) He adds that the Outlander's fate "is clouded in darkness" (B011).
3. **Death-feeding.** Boss lines show the Gods feeding on sacrifice, death and destruction. Examples are TYTH "A SACRIFICE. SUPERIOR." and IZAX "DESTRUCTION DETECTED. I GROW STRONGER." The author infers they "more than likely fed on the "10 trillion" of deaths". This is an inference: FORUM OPINION.
4. **Telepathy and visions.** Acina sees "a radiant empire, infused with the Force, built by the molten gods" (B013). The Gods "showed me the end of all things" (B014).
5. **Tyth** is presented as:
   - "unimaginable power" (B016);
   - the "ultimate superweapon" (B017);
   - a source of "galactic devastation" (B018);
   - "more powerful than anything we've ever encountered", and "Quite possibly" more powerful than the Eternal Throne (B019–B021);
   - able to "usurp the Eternal Alliance" (B022);
   - the Outlander's "greatest challenge" (B023), which the author reads as **Tyth > Vaylin/Revan**;
   - able to "obliterate the entire planet" of Iokath in "minutes" (B024–B026).
   
   Producer Ben Irving compares the encounter to Revan's for "calibre of challenge". Tyth also projects the Outlander's essence, senses the traitor (Theron) (B028–B033), and destroys an Eternal warship.
6. **Aivela & Esne** are stronger than Tyth by operation progression. The marketing line is "to determine the fate of the galaxy" (B036).
7. **Nahut** is described as "a superweapon capable of galactic destruction" (B038). He breaks Scyva's containment: "I AM UNCHAINED."
8. **Scyva** is called:
   - "a nigh-indestructible threat" (B041);
   - "the biggest threat to the galaxy" (B042);
   - the "source" of her children, and the one who "guides" and controls the Gods (Boyd).
   
   Her line "YOU TOOK MY LAST CHILD FROM ME. NOW YOUR TRUE TEST BEGINS." is cited.
9. **Izax** is "the mightiest" of the Gods and "capable of destroying entire fleets of warships" (B044). Further points:
   - It is the toughest boss to date.
   - Designer Matt Pucevich says Izax's main attacks hit for 136,000 in Veteran mode to show "how this destroyed the Iokatian creator race".
   - Torpedoes and the Omnicannon one-shot Eternal warships.
10. **Galaxy destroyer (the Omnicannon).**
    - The Omnicannon hits multiple targets, and a Gauntlet-style cannon "can fire on ships traveling through hyperspace" (B051).
    - Scyva's lines: "Destroy Izax before he fully awakens his systems or all is lost." / "With high probability the destruction of my platform will result in the extinction of your species in this galaxy."
    - Mechanics: 10 stacks take 4 s and deal about 80% of team health. 100 stacks take a 48 s channel with "all remaining power" (B052–B054).
    
    The author concludes that the Omnicannon "would have wiped out many planets if fired". This is FORUM OPINION and an extrapolation from gameplay.

## Common rebuttals / weak points (ANALYSIS — not from the thread)

- **Operation boss order ≠ power order.** Saying later foes are stronger is a gameplay-pacing assumption.
- **Marketing copy** ("biggest threat to the galaxy", "toughest boss battles to-date") is routine hype.
- **Feeding on the 10 trillion test-world deaths is speculation.** The quoted lines show feeding on sacrifice and destruction in the fights, not on the test-world deaths.
- **The Omnicannon galaxy-buster is extrapolated** from a raid-wipe mechanic, Scyva's "extinction of your species" line, and Gravestone analogies. No source says Izax can destroy many planets.
- **Placing the Gods above the Eternal Fleet** relies on Lana's "Quite possibly", which is hedged.
- **"Greatest challenge" for Tyth** is said to the Outlander at that point in the story. It does not place Tyth above Vaylin's earlier fight in any explicit way.
- **The Gods are droid superweapons.** Scaling them against Force users (Vaylin < Tyth) mixes destructive output with combat ability.

## Characters touched and implied scaling (FORUM OPINION)

- Vaylin < Tyth < Aivela & Esne << Nahut << Scyva <<< Izax.
- The Gods > the Eternal Fleet/Throne > the Gravestone (pre-Zildrog).
- The Outlander beats them all, which implies the Outlander > the Six Gods.
- Revan's operation fight is compared via Ben Irving's "calibre of challenge" line.
