# Star Wars: The Phantom Menace — The Expanded Visual Dictionary (DK, 2012): hand-read notes (new and revised material only)

Written by David West Reynolds and Jason Fry. Published by Dorling Kindersley Limited, London. The copyright page reads: "First published in Great Britain in 1999 ... Revised edition published 2012"; "Copyright ©2012 Lucasfilm Ltd. and ™. All rights reserved. Used under authorization"; ISBN 978-1-40539-253-2; £14.99. Editor Hannah Dolan; Lucasfilm Executive Editor J. W. Rinzler; Keeper of the Holocron Leland Chee. This is an official licensed Lucas Books/DK title from before April 2014, so it counts as Legends. It expands the 1999 Episode I Visual Dictionary (see vd1-ep1.md). The back cover says: "a wealth of new and updated material ... including an exclusive cross-section view of Darth Maul's lightsaber."
Scans: the main copy (MEGA NCs0RBrI) has 100 image files covering the front cover, front matter and pp. 3–97. It is missing pp. 98–104 and the back cover. Those pages, plus the copyright page and back cover, were read from the alternate copy (MEGA Ifk01brC, 110 files, a library scan). Every page was viewed by hand.
Scope: these notes cover only what is NEW (a page or panel not in the 1999 book) or REVISED (rewritten or expanded text) compared with the 1999 VD1. Material repeated unchanged is not noted again. Citation convention: "VD1X p. NN" is the printed page number. Text in quotation marks is verbatim from the page. [KEY] marks power-scaling or lore-critical passages. Spelling quirks are kept as printed and marked [sic].
