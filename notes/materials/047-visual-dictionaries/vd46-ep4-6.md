# Star Wars: The Visual Dictionary (DK, 1998; Episodes IV–VI): hand-read notes

Written by David West Reynolds. Special fabrications by Don Bies & Nelson Hall; new photography by Alexander Ivanov. Published by DK Publishing, Inc. (First American Edition 1998; ISBN 0-7894-3481-4), ®, ™ and © 1998 Lucasfilm Ltd. Project Editor David Pickering; Project Art Editor Iain Morris; US Editor Jane Mason. This is an official licensed DK/Lucasfilm title covering the original trilogy.
Scan: 54 image files. They cover the front cover, front flap, half-title, title spread, contents, pp. 5–64, the author flap and the back cover. Spreads were scanned as single files (e.g. 44-45, 60-61). Every page was viewed by hand, and small text was checked with zoomed crops.
Citation convention: "VD4-6 p. NN" is the printed page number. Text in quotation marks is verbatim from the page. [KEY] marks power-scaling or lore-critical passages. Spelling quirks are kept as printed and marked [sic].

- **p04** contents: Special Technology 6 ... Acknowledgements 64. Props: Tarkin's and Motti's code cylinders, stormtrooper grappling hook, Rebel Hoth backpack, stormtrooper sergeant's/enlisted pauldrons.
- **p05** introduction (verbatim excerpt): "THE FANTASY WORLD of the Star Wars trilogy has come to take on a curious feeling of reality... Welcome to the world of Star Wars." Cut-away stormtrooper helmet.
- **p06-07** Special Technology [KEY]: "Traditional technology such as the Jedi lightsaber may remain constant for centuries." Lightsabers: "While the pure energy blade has no mass, the electromagnetically generated arc wave creates a strong gyroscopic effect that makes the lightsaber a distinct challenge to handle"; crystals "cannot be synthesized"; "A lightsaber must be assembled by hand ... The slightest misalignment will cause the weapon to detonate on activation." "Building a working lightsaber is one of the threshold tests for Jedi initiates: accomplishing the impossibly fine alignment task proves their Force sensitivity." Vader's saber "looks much like the one he used as a Jedi learner, only darker"; Luke's second saber "follows the type used by Luke's mentor Ben Kenobi". Ion blasters (Jawa ionization blasters stun droids). Blaster Weaponry: high-energy gas → plasma, "magnetic bottle effect"; "Plasma energy is dissipated as the bolt travels, limiting range". Droids: "the replication of true sentience has proven difficult and even dangerous"; droids escaping memory wipes "tend to develop identities and sentience of their own"; "In the Empire, no laws protect droids from abuse".
- **p08-09** Luke Skywalker: "A YOUNG FARMBOY living on the remote desert planet Tatooine"; homestead (garage roof, courtyard, moisture vaporator); Tosche power station in Anchorhead. Lightsaber [KEY]: "A gift from another era, Luke's lightsaber is the legacy of his father Anakin, a former Jedi Knight of the Old Republic and a warrior who fought in the Clone Wars"; "Luke has a natural ability with the saber and begins to learn rapidly from his mentor Ben Kenobi." DATA FILE: Biggs Darklighter (graduated with a commission on the freighter Rand Ecliptic; reunited at Yavin 4); sandstorms. Owns a T-16 skyhopper, "blasting womp rats" in Beggar's Canyon, Diablo Cut and the Stone Needle. X-34 landspeeder (three turbines, cracked windshield). Aunt Beru, Uncle Owen Lars.
- **p10** Skywalker: Pilot and Jedi: "Red Five"; "His Force abilities are awakened by Master Yoda". T-65 X-wing (astromech socket; small payload of proton torpedoes, "Luke goes into battle against the Death Star with only a single pair"). [KEY] "The target exhaust shaft leads straight to the hypermatter reactor deep within the core of the Death Star. Luke takes his critical shot using the Force, triumphantly destroying the entire gigantic battle station."
- **p11** Snowspeeder (Rogue Squadron vs. Imperial Blizzard Force AT-ATs); quad-laser turret: "Despite his inexperience, Luke adapts quickly and destroys two fighters, matching Han Solo's tally". Jedi Knight: "Returning to Dagobah, he learns from Yoda that he has almost achieved that noble level at last." "Although he spends only a short time with the wise Jedi Master, Luke learns much from Yoda, who awakens Luke's sleeping abilities and Force sensitivity." DATA FILE [KEY]: "Doubted by both his friends and enemies, Luke proves himself and his extraordinary abilities in battle against the Hutt's forces". The Shadow of Darth Vader: "Only in close combat with Vader does Luke learn the truth".
- **p12** Princess Leia Organa: Senate cover for diplomatic aid; Tantive IV ("Corellian Corvette ... sometimes called 'Blockade Runners'"); adoptive daughter of Viceroy Bail Organa; "highly educated in martial and political arts"; "She held firm against every torture."
- **p13** Leia cont.: Ice Princess (Echo Base); Jabba's Slave (lashaa silk, jerba leather boots); "Where dozens of professional assassins had failed, Leia succeeds in putting an end to the contemptible crime lord Jabba the Hutt." Rebel Leader: "still one of the best shots in the Alliance". Forest Diplomat. DATA FILE: "the youngest person ever to hold a seat in the Galactic Senate"; "excellent tactician and an expert shot with a blaster. She virtually never misses."
- **p14** Han Solo: "MERCENARY PIRATE, smuggler captain, and cocksure braggart"; worked up "through petty thievery to gain a commission in the Academy, from which he was later expelled"; won the Falcon "in the best game of sabacc he ever played"; "His reputation as a gunfighter matches his renown as captain of the Falcon." DL-44 pistol. Greedo scene ("The regulars could have warned Greedo that Han was the wrong man to threaten"). "Between Han's fast draw and Chewbacca's violent strength, the two are not to be trifled with." Jabba bounty. DATA FILE: "As a child Solo was raised by space gypsies"; medal of honor.
- **p15** Millennium Falcon: YT-1300; "Even at sublight speeds its velocity and maneuverability are extraordinary for a ship of its class"; Imperial military-grade armor, quad-laser cannons. Titan space slug. Solo, Rebel Leader (captain's commission; Hoth perimeter survey plan). Han in Carbonite ("Carbon-freezing is a way of bonding condensed Tibanna gas for transport").
- **p16-17** Chewbacca: "A MIGHTY WOOKIEE from the planet Kashyyyk, Chewbacca was recued [sic] from slavery by the daring Han Solo"; Wookiee life debt. [KEY] Bowcaster ammunition: "a magnetic accelerator to fire explosive quarrels, which are enveloped in a penetrating energy shell as they are fired. The bowcaster has a range of 50 meters and requires immense strength to cock." Spacecraft repair items. "Chewie's loyalty to Han shows as strong as ever and he nearly kills Lando in his vengeful rage." DATA FILE: family on Kashyyyk — wife Mala, son Lumpy, father Itchy; "Chewbacca is over 200 years old, in a Wookiee's prime"; loves strategy games but hates losing.
- **p18-19** C-3PO Protocol Droid: "fluent in over six million forms of communication"; TranLang III communication module; Myriad visual system Mk. 2; pelvic servomotor; power coupling. DATA FILE: "Luke Skywalker is his 43rd master"; a metal O-ring jarred loose from his left knee helped stormtroopers track the droids on Tatooine; component linkages designed to disengage rather than break.
- **p20** R2-D2 Astromech Droid: "stubborn determination and inventiveness that are extraordinary for a utility droid"; computer interface, grasping, power charge, lubricant application arms; hologram recording/projection a standard capability; Luke chooses R2 for the Death Star attack. DATA FILE: "R2-D2 has been around even longer than his counterpart C-3PO"; "R2-D2 resorts to innovative deceit when necessary". Fire extinguisher used to conceal friends.
- **p21** R2-D2 cont.: callouts (life-scan mesh scanner antenna, motivator housing, memory chip "Stored experiences", durasteel shell, motorized all-terrain treads); Jabba's personnel fit him to serve drinks on the sail barge.
- **p22** Lando Calrissian: Baron Administrator of Cloud City; "flew the Millennium Falcon for years before losing the ship to Han Solo in a sabacc match. The same game later won Lando control of the fabulous gas mining colony on Bespin." Cloud City (single giant column, processing reactor, directional vanes; "rare anti-gravitational tibanna gas"). Forced to betray Han; plots rescue with his aide Lobot.
- **p23** General Lando: "His penetrating judgment at the Battle of Tanaab won Lando promotion"; Lobot ("Chief Administrative Aide of Cloud City", cybernetic implants, direct contact with the city's central computer). Vibro-axe polearm (Jabba's skiff guard). DATA FILE: comlink security code addresses all of Cloud City; old underworld contact secures guard job at Jabba's palace.
- **p24** Obi-Wan Kenobi, Jedi Knight [KEY]: "In truth Kenobi is a Jedi Knight, a great warrior of the Old Republic who fought in the Clone Wars. One of Kenobi's students turned to the dark side of the Force, betraying the Jedi and assisting the rise of the Emperor." "Kenobi's powers make him a threat to the Empire even in his elder years." Training remote ("shock rays adjusted from harmless to painful"). "On board the Death Star, Kenobi uses his technical knowledge and Jedi mind powers to disable a crucial tractor beam without being noticed." Returns in spirit. DATA FILE: rescued Luke lost with his friend Windy; Owen forbade Kenobi from the farm; Luke builds his own lightsaber at Kenobi's home.
- **p25** Yoda, Jedi Master [KEY]: "the wise Jedi Master Yoda is very powerful with the Force. At almost 900, his years of contemplation and training have given him deep insight and profound abilities." "To his final student Yoda imparts the heart of the ancient Jedi traditions". Dagobah; gimer stick; healing earth-rock; Force spirits "restored and complete". DATA FILE: house handcrafted by Yoda; "In the days before the sinister Empire, Yoda held a seat within the Jedi high council on the Republic's capital world of Coruscant."
- **p26** Rebel Leaders: "A good Rebel leader can overcome the Empire's numeric advantage with inventive tactics"; DH-17 Rebel blaster ("not as powerful or as long-range as Imperial military-issue blasters"). Carlist Rieekan (seven hidden levels of Echo Base). Jan Dodonna [KEY]: "a dauntless master tactician, commanding the Rebel assault on the Death Star in the Battle of Yavin"; strategy "enabled a small fleet of 30 one-man fighters to annihilate a battle station over 160 kilometers wide." Massassi base tactical display.
- **p27** Admiral Ackbar: from Mon Calamari; "Once a slave to Grand Moff Tarkin, Ackbar was rescued by Rebels"; Mon Cal star cruisers "the largest ships in the Rebel fleet"; Headquarters Frigate. Mon Mothma ("highest leader of the Rebellion"; Chandrillian Freedom Medal). Rebel helmet of Red Leader Garven Dreis. DATA FILE: Bothan spynet ("Bothan teams will endure heavy losses to accomplish their objectives"); 300-meter Nebulon B escort as medical frigate.
- **p28** Tauntauns: snow lizards; slow metabolism at night; Hoth habitable only in a subarctic band around its equator; Echo Base on the northern edge; wampas killed tauntauns in the stables. DATA FILE: irritated by ultrasonic frequencies of droids such as a tactical 3PO unit.
- **p29** Medical Droids: FX-7 ("antiquated but still serviceable"); 2-1B ("An excellent surgeon and field medic ... seems actually to care about his patients"); bacta ("can heal grievous flesh wounds"). DATA FILE: 2-1B treated Luke on Hoth and again after he lost his hand.
- **p30-31** Darth Vader [KEY]: "Once regarded as mad human wreckage, with the increasing favor of the Emperor Vader has risen in power and influence to become a much-feared military commander. Grand Moff Tarkin was one of the few who recognized Vader's capabilities in spite of his bizarre appearance and eccentric conduct, and as Tarkin's right-hand man Vader attained a new level of respect"; "Unable to survive without the constant life support provided by his suit, Vader is nonetheless a powerful figure whose knowledge of the dark side of the Force makes him unnerving and dangerous." Rose through the ranks; isolation chamber with mechanical arms; chestplate controls; belt; helmet ("connecting with a flat backpack to cycle air in and out of Vader's broken lungs and keeping his hideously damaged skull in shape"). "Vader plotted with the Emperor to sway Luke to the dark side ... Where Vader's loyalties really stood at this time is lost in the darkness filling his soul." DATA FILE [KEY]: "Over time Vader has advanced in his ability to manipulate the dark side of the Force, and has used it to sustain his own damaged body as well as to persuade opponents of his will. Under the Emperor's tutelage, Vader learns to kill with mere suggestion." "Vader's injuries occurred in a battle against his former teacher, Obi-Wan Kenobi, and much of his body is now replaced by machine components." "Vader incurs the wrath of high officers by piloting his own fighter into combat." Anakin Skywalker: Luke "committed to finding redemption for his father".
- **p32** Imperial Leaders: "The price for failure can be death"; "many of the Empire's commanders are formidable military talents in a system that values ruthless efficiency." [KEY] The First Death Star: "contains a hypermatter reactor that can generate enough power to destroy an entire planet. Invulnerable to large-scale assault, the space station has a fatal weakness in a small thermal exhaust port". Death Star gunners ("titanic energies"). Grand Moff Tarkin ("Governor of the Imperial Outland Regions, Grand Moff Wilhuff Tarkin conceives the horrific Death Star superweapon as part of his doctrine of Rule by Fear"). General Maximillian Veers. Admiral Piett, Admiral Ozzel, Captain Needa, Moff Jerjerrod.
- **p33** Emperor Palpatine [KEY]: "Senator Palpatine used deception to become elected President of the Galactic Senate. Once in office he appointed himself Emperor"; "drew his powers of persuasion and control from the blackest depths of the dark side of the Force. While the Force has twisted his face, it has also sustained him beyond his years, and even in his old age the Emperor remains a figure of terrible power." Second Death Star "a colossal trap". Callout: "Emperor uses cane because he pretends to be weak, not because he needs it". DATA FILE [KEY]: Royal Guards "so highly trained in deadly arts that their chosen weapon is not a blaster but a vibro-active force pike, which they use with lightning swiftness to inflict precise and lethal wounds." Imperial Dignitaries ("society of twisted sycophants and back-stabbers").
- **p34-35** Imperial Stormtroopers [KEY]: "first-strike units ... Highly disciplined and completely loyal to the Emperor, stormtroopers carry out their orders without hesitation and without regard to their own lives"; "Shielded in white space armor worn over a body glove, stormtroopers are protected from harsh environments, projectile and impact weapons, and glancing blaster bolts"; "the most trusted and most effective troops in the Imperial military". E-11 BlasTech: "A standard power cell carries enough energy for 100 shots ... Plasma gas cartridges last for over 500 shots". Stormtrooper Officers (code cylinders; colored shoulder pauldrons). "In battle, stormtroopers are disciplined to ignore casualties within their own ranks." DATA FILE [KEY]: armor backplate allows survival "in the vacuum of space for limited periods"; "Stormtrooper armor is impervious to projectile weapons and blast shrapnel. It may be pierced by a direct blaster bolt, but will deflect glancing bolts and reduce damage from bolts absorbed." Armor "found half-buried at decades-old battle sites".
- **p36** Stormtrooper Equipment: "brutal training and intense conditioning"; "the galaxy's most dreaded soldiers"; Commander Praji; dewback search parties; comlink encryption; thermal detonator (controls unlabelled so enemies cannot use them); BlasTech DLT-20A laser rifle (longer range; "much prized on the black market"). DATA FILE: mortar launchers; "Squad leaders, who lead units of seven troopers, wear orange shoulder pauldrons."
- **p37** Stormtrooper Helmet cutaway: holographic images of terrain; vision through smoke, darkness, fire; anti-laser mesh; Comtech Series IV speaker; power cells.
- **p38-39** Specialist Stormtroopers: selected early for specialization; "their psychological conditioning to their particular identity is so strong that a trooper almost never wishes to change his division." Snowtrooper ("self-sufficient mobile combat elements"; "can survive for two weeks in deeply frozen environments on suit battery power alone"); E-Web heavy repeating blaster; Vader oversees the occupation of Echo Base with General Veers' Blizzard Force. Scout Trooper ("Trained to an unusual degree of independence for Imperial personnel"; armored only on head and upper body). Speeder bike (light repulsorlift Imperial speeder bike; forward-reaching repulsor fields not strong enough to deflect the bike from obstacles on their own). DATA FILE: other divisions include "flying airtroopers, liquid-borne seatroopers, tunneling underminers, and Magma troopers who crush revolts on volcanic mining worlds"; scout troopers' macrobinocular viewplates. Scout trooper blaster.
- **p40** Imperial Pilots [KEY]: "Only ten percent of those accepted into training graduate with commissions"; "entirely dedicated to target destruction"; "Each pilot knows he is expendable." TIE targeting systems "superior to anything available to Rebel fighters" (Seinar). TIE Fighter: no deflector shield or hyperdrive; "lethally agile"; "TIE pilots view shields as tools of cowards." Fuel is "a radioactive gas under high pressure"; twin ion engines have no moving parts. DATA FILE: TIEs contain no life support; no landing gear, launched from hangar racks.
- **p41** AT-AT Pilots: "conditioned to believe themselves invincible"; survival system power pack runs "for up to five days". AT-ST walker pilots (balance and dexterity; anti-personnel hunting). AT-AT Walker: "terror weapons. Their powerful walking controls can only be operated by pilots of great physical strength. Until the Battle of Hoth, AT-ATs were widely regarded as invincible in combat". DATA FILE: not climate-controlled; pilots "recruited from the strongest combat troops."
- **p42** Imperial Droids: Imperial droids "programmed with extremely harsh identity parameters"; "rarely develop anything approximating personality." Mouse droid (MSE; programmed to melt processors on capture; "paranoia and self-importance"). R4-19. Interrogator droid: "Illegal by the laws of the Republic ... surgically exploits every physical and mental point of weakness with flesh peelers, joint cripplers, bone fragmenters, electroshock nerve probes"; Leia "somehow maintained her resistance even near the point of death from the pain."
- **p43** Probe Droid (Probot): hyperdrive pods; HoloNet transceivers; self-destruct charges; probot from the Star Destroyer Avenger detects the Hoth base. Imperial Mark IV patrol droid IM4-099 (Mos Eisley). DATA FILE: utility droids with secret spy devices "to monitor military personnel, ensuring obedience."
- **p44-45** Jabba the Hutt: "Unlike many of his competitors, Jabba is highly intelligent"; gladiatorial games and creative executions. B'omarr monks (spider-droid brain carriers; oldest have four legs, newer six); palace originally a B'omarr monastery. Oola (kidnapped by majordomo Bib Fortuna). "Jabba Desilijic Tiure ... comes from the planet Nal Hutta ... raised (by his father, also a crime lord)"; "Hutts ... often exploit their physical power to control weaker species. Hutts run most of the galaxy's large criminal syndicates." Callouts: "Hutt skin secretes oil and mucus, making Hutts difficult to seize"; "Body has no skeleton". Sail barge Khetanna. Hermi Odle (Baragwin armorer); Ephant Mon ("Jabba's only real friend: the Hutt once saved his life"). Salacious Crumb (Kowakian monkey-lizard). DATA FILE: estate in Mos Eisley; eats nine meals a day; Buboicullaar (Bubo) is intelligent and "once ate a detonation link needed for a bomb, foiling an attempt to assassinate Jabba."
- **p46** Jabba's Entourage: "The Hutt regards the inevitable plots as amusement, pitting the different schemers against each other before compassing their destruction." Twi'lek dagger and poisons (rock wart sting juice, chall granules, krayt dragon venom). Bib Fortuna ("grew wealthy as a slave trader of his own people ... As Jabba's chief lieutenant, he plots to kill his boss"; "a powerful and dreaded, if cowardly, individual"); predecessor majordomo Sevan Domna killed in an assassination attempt decades ago. Tessek (Quarren accountant; embezzles and plans to assassinate Jabba).
- **p47** Skiff guard's blaster; skiff guards; Elom (extortion); J'Quille ("a brutal Whiphid from the cold planet of Toola ... actually a spy for a rival crime lord ... planning to kill the Hutt with a slow-acting poison"). Gamorrean Guards ("cannot be bribed or persuaded to betray"; prefer hand-to-hand weapons; vibro-lance, heavy-duty ax). DATA FILE: Jabba knows Tessek is plotting (revealed by a B'omarr monk); "Bib Fortuna and a now-murdered associate brought to Jabba his rancor monster, discovered in a mysterious crashed ship in the Tatooine desert"; Jabba doesn't like humans; Rodian hunters, sociopathic Snivvian, Weequay thugs.
- **p48-49** Jabba's Entertainers: bands are "slaves to debt, heavy spice users, or the singing dregs of galactic society"; Yarna d'al' Gargan (Askajian dancer); Doda Bodonawieedo (Rodian); Barquin D'an (brother of Figrin D'an); Ak-rev (drum master from a Sriluurian monastery to Am-Shak; assisted by Klatoonian Umpass-stay, secretly a bodyguard); Rystáll, Greeata; Bontormian klesplong; Lyn Me (Twi'lek from Ryloth's northern continent). DATA FILE: Jabba "feels entitled to feed bad musicians to his rancor"; Max Rebo's homeworld name "Rapotwanalantonee"; Shawda Ubb. Droopy McCool (Kitonak); Sy Snootles; Max Rebo ("blue Ortolan ... accepted a contract with Jabba that pays only in free meals"); red ball jet organ.
- **p50** Boba Fett [KEY]: "A MYSTERIOUS BOUNTY HUNTER with his own code of honor, Boba Fett wears a customized suit of Mandalorian battle armor from another era – battered, scarred, and still lethally effective"; "Fett takes only certain assignments, but devotes himself to those few with fanatical skill. His cool and calculating ways together with his manifold hidden capabilities have brought in many 'impossible' marks, and earned his reputation as the best bounty hunter in the galaxy." "Boba Fett has worked for Darth Vader on several occasions, enough to have been called Vader's right-hand man. Vader finds Fett an intelligent, ruthless, capable ally". Slave I ("aging, heavily-modified police craft"; stolen military sensor masking system). Jet backpack (missile or grappling hook). False touch, false eye, ion limpet homing beacon. DATA FILE: "Fett is notorious for completely disintegrating those whom he has been hired to track down and kill"; as a spy for Vader he first encountered Luke on a moon in the Panna system; "his honor cannot be bought."
- **p51** Boba Fett cont.: "Outsmarting all his bounty hunter rivals, Boba Fett tracks Solo to Bespin"; callouts: EE-3 blaster rifle, "Energized blast dissipation vest", "Braided Wookiee scalps attest past hunts", "Journeyman protector honor sash", spring-loaded boot spikes. Survival knife, sonic beam weapon, knee pad rocket darts; anti-security blades. Renewed assignment with Jabba for a bonus on the Han Solo bounty. [KEY] Multi-detonator: "less susceptible to damper shield effects than a conventional thermal detonator, and capable of tearing a starship engine into fragments."
- **p52** Bounty Hunters: "the profession as a whole is distinguished by outstanding slime." IG-88 [KEY]: "one of a set of five identical robots which massacred their constructors moments after activation"; "obsessed with hunting and killing"; assassin droids "illegal for good reason"; "Head sensors allow IG-88 to see in all directions at once"; trion gas dispenser, concussion discs, flamethrower, sonic stunner, pulse cannon, needle dart gun. "An IG-88 droid tracked Slave I to Cloud City, intending to kill Boba Fett. Fett ambushed it in the scrap processing levels. He paralyzed it with an ion cannon, then finished it off". DATA FILE: "IG-88 droids have attacked Boba Fett several times, badly damaging Slave I but never claiming Fett himself. Fett has now destroyed three of the assassin droids"; Rodians/Greedo. Dengar: Imperial assassin; brain surgery replaced his hypothalamus, "a nearly unfeeling killer"; 23 bounties; grudge against Han from swoop racing in the crystal swamps of Agrilat.
- **p53** 4-LOM (degraded protocol droid); Zuckuss (findsman of Gand; "tireless tracker and weirdly effective"); Bossk (Trandoshan; 12 captures; "Trandoshans can regenerate lost skin, fingers, and even limbs until they reach adulthood"; "as vile and mean as bounty hunters get"). Boushh (Leia's Ubese disguise; "Leia's military training served her well"); Boushh's thermal detonator; lance blade and stun attachments.
- **p54** Sand People / Tusken Raiders: "Masters of the desert, they survive where no one else can"; "Sand People almost never come into a house but they will slay a lone resister outside." Gaderffi stick, poisoned blade; Banthas (made members of their clans; ride in single file to hide numbers); bloodletting blade (sacrifice of animals, captives, or condemned Sand People); Gafsa wells sacred. DATA FILE: gaderffi (gaffi) stick from scavenged metal; clans' ceremonial caves.
- **p55** Jawas: "timid and acquisitive"; sandcrawlers "ancient vehicles from a mining era long ago"; restraining bolts; magnetic suction tube; ionization blaster; droid caller; Mark II reactor drone magfield sensor ball. DATA FILE: annual great salvage swap meet; "Things tend to disappear when Jawas are around."
- **p56-57** Ewoks: villages high in the oldest trees; "the forest floor ... by night ... becomes too dangerous for them." Wicket W. Warrick; Teebo ("has a mystical alignment with the forces of nature"); Chief Chirpa (leader for 42 seasons); Logray (tribal shaman; "uses his knowledge of ritual and magic"; suspicious of outsiders); shaman's kit ("A spirit staff helps summon dead ancestors for assistance"). DATA FILE: hang gliders and complex traps for Imperial occupation forces; wear teeth, horns, skulls as trophies.
- **p58-59** The Cantina Crowd: Mos Eisley Cantina "the haunt of the weirdest clientele in town"; "as long as foolish outsiders don't step in and get their heads blown off, everyone can have their own version of a good time"; bartender threatens to poison drinks; droid detector. Hrchek Kal Fas (Saurin droid trader), Duros, Devaronian "under the assumed name of Labria, on the run from a wicked past and one of the galaxy's highest bounties for his deadly crimes"; Muftak (Talz pickpocket). Figrin D'an and the Modal Nodes (Bith; Clak'dor VII; Figrin an expert gambler; asked to play at Jabba's palace); Tech Mor, Tedn D'hai, Nalan Cheel, Doikk Na'ts; kloo horn, fanfar, ommni box, bandfill, fizz.
- **p60-61** Creatures: sand creatures of Tatooine's deep deserts can grow to over 100 meters in length; "impossibly gigantic asteroid lurkers". Space Slug [KEY]: "Silicon-based space slugs survive in a vacuum, digesting minerals with a uranium-based metabolism. Recently a titan space slug was documented by an Imperial Star Destroyer on a pursuit mission in an asteroid field. The slug attacked and digested part of the Imperial vessel before being subdued." Mynocks (silicon-based; feed on starship energy emissions). Dianoga (up to ten meters; older specimens "very aggressive"). Wampa: "Standing three meters high"; "Cunning predators, wampas are normally solitary beasts, but they have been known to band together with uncanny intelligence in the face of threats like human settlements." Ronto; womp rat (Tatooine); worrt; rock wart ("painful neurotoxic venom ... to kill even large prey"). Rancor Monster [KEY]: "Standing five meters tall, this fearsome carnivore possesses an armored skin and colossal strength"; "little-known legends of rancor-like monsters on the remote planet of Dathomir"; "Tough, rigid hide can absorb blaster bolts". Malakili (keeper; gaffi stick gift from Tusken Raiders for killing a giant mutant womp rat). Sarlacc: "Lying in wait at the bottom of the Great Pit of Carkoon ... DATA FILE: dianogas change color to their last meal; Jabba's pet Hoover sucks blood of sleeping victims.

WED-15-77 Treadwell; CZ-1 (secretary droid, Stacchati); AV-6R7 (Imperial V-series supervisor building the second Death Star for Moff Jerjerrod; deprived of arms for failing to track a faulty power droid); EV-9D9 ("Her programming corrupted ... employing bizarre forms of droid torture"); 8D8 (Verpine-made, lobotomized and reprogrammed for Jabba's torture chamber); LIN-V8K (mining droid; refurbished by Jawas); WED 15 "Septoid 2" (Fixer's Treadwell, Toshi power station, Anchorhead). DATA FILE: most droids have memory banks periodically erased; R5-D4 "Red" blew his motivator so Owen Lars would take R2 instead.
