# 047 summary: Visual Dictionaries I–VI + Expanded Ep I

## Strongest findings (verbatim, cited)
1. **Yoda's scale** (VD2 p. 27): "Yoda's capabilities with the Force give him amazing strength and speed, as well as the ability to levitate. These special powers, combined with his knowledge of fighting tactics, allow him to overcome virtually any opponent, though times are few indeed that Yoda has actually used his lightsaber in combat." VD3 p. 35 adds: "In his contest with Sidious, Yoda realizes he is overmatched and deserts the fight, perhaps because his spirit has been broken by so many Jedi deaths."
2. **Jango vs Obi-Wan** (VD2 p. 40 and p. 15): Jango "instantly launches a volley of blasts that would annihilate an ordinary opponent"; "Fett is a professional at the top of his field, but he cannot overcome Obi-Wan." On Form III: "Invented ages ago when blaster weapons first became common ... it has been refined into an expression of nonaggressive Jedi philosophy."
3. **Dooku and Anakin** (VD3 p. 13): "Too late, Dooku realizes Anakin is more powerful than he could have imagined." On Mace (VD3 p. 36): "Mace's lightsaber technique synthesizes deadly Form VII with a newly created form known as vaapad."
4. **Maul** (VD1X pp. 70–73): "a master of physical combat, pairing Orsis Academy elite combat training with the fighting style of Teräs Käsi and lightsaber disciplines Niman, Jar'Kai, and Dun Möch" (p. 70); "one of the most highly trained Sith in the history of the order" (p. 71); "With his double-bladed lightsaber, Maul is equal to two Jedi who are unprepared for his powers" (p. 73).
5. **Anakin's precognition** (VD1X p. 77, NEW): "Anakin thinks he simply has much faster reflexes than normal humans, but the truth is stranger: He uses the Force to react to events before they happen." His midi-chlorian levels "are higher than those of any known Jedi" (p. 21).
6. **Original trilogy** (VD4-6): the Death Star strategy "enabled a small fleet of 30 one-man fighters to annihilate a battle station over 160 kilometers wide" (p. 26); Vader "learns to kill with mere suggestion" (pp. 30–31); "Fett has now destroyed three of the assassin droids" (IG-88, p. 52).

## Other notable points
- **Droidekas** (VD1X p. 37): their shields "completely repel pistol fire and substantially weaken high-energy rifle or artillery bolts. This makes them nearly invincible in combat".
- **Aurra Sing** (VD1X p. 95): "it is claimed she is an Anzati vampire, a failed Padawan, a Hutt-built cyborg, or a Clawdite shape-shifter."
- **Expanded Ep I** (2012, Jason Fry) brings in later Legends lore: Orsis, Nightbrothers, Sora Bulq co-creating Vaapad, Xim the Despot, Quinlan Vos at Mos Espa, and Pi-Lippa.
