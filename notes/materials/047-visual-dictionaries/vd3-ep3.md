# Star Wars Revenge of the Sith: The Visual Dictionary (DK, 2005): hand-read notes

Author James Luceno, with special fabrications by Robert E. Barnes and John Goodson and new photography by Alex Ivanov. Published by DK Publishing, Inc. (First American Edition 2005; ISBN 0-7566-1128-8), © 2005 Lucasfilm Ltd. Lucasfilm Senior Editor Jonathan W. Rinzler; Continuity Supervisor Leland Chee. This is an official licensed DK/Lucas Books title.
Scan: 57 image files. They cover the front cover, front flap, half-title, title spread, contents, introduction, pp. 6–64, the back flap and the back cover. Spreads were scanned as single files (e.g. 14-15, 22-23). Every page was viewed by hand, and small text was checked with zoomed crops.
Citation convention: "VD3 p. NN" is the printed page number. Text in quotation marks is verbatim from the page. [KEY] marks power-scaling or lore-critical passages. Spelling quirks are kept as printed and marked [sic].

- **p13** Count Dooku [KEY]: CIS "nothing more than a fabrication, conceived by Darth Sidious as part of his plan to topple the Jedi Order and reinstate the Sith"; "For more than a decade now" thought of himself as Darth Tyranus "destined to sit at his left hand as joint master of the galaxy"; abduction "an elaborate ruse, engineered to ensnare Anakin and test him"; "Too late, Dooku realizes Anakin is more powerful than he could have imagined."
