Author David West Reynolds. Published by DK Publishing, Inc. (First American Edition 2002; ISBN 0-7894-8588-5), © 2002 Lucasfilm Ltd. This is an official licensed DK/Lucas Books title.
Scan: 62 image files ("(bZc-DCP)" in some filenames is a scan-group tag, not content). They cover the front cover, front flap, endpaper, half-title, title spread, contents, pp. 5–65 and the back cover. Every page was viewed by hand.
Citation convention: "VD2 p. NN" is the printed page number. Text in quotation marks is verbatim from the page. [KEY] marks power-scaling or lore-critical passages.
