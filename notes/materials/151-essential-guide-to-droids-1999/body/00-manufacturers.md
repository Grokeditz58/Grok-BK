# A Guide to Major Manufacturers — printed 1–5 (scan 010–014)

## Framing (P1)
Droids = intelligent mechanical contraptions; mobility + some intelligence vs computers. Often chattel / antidroid prejudice; long-term owners treat as friends. Big Two: Cybot Galactica + Industrial Automaton. Also Arakyd, Genetech, MerenData, Veril Line Systems. Factory worlds Mechis III (Arakyd/Genetech/SoroSuub/Veril; recently Brakiss → Thul family) and Telti (IA + Cybot). Some ancient / inventor / alien-gov unique units.

## Accutronics (P1)
Former IA subsidiary; family/children market; HQ Eriadu + Outer Rim sats. Models: Model E; TDL Nanny.

## Arakyd Industries (P1)
Empire political rise → leading military supplier; also consumer; post-Empire NR work. Models: Death Star Droid; G-2RD Guard; Mark X Executioner; Seeker Messenger; Moon Moth; Tracker; Probot series.

## Balmorran Arms (P1–2)
Balmorra Core-edge factory world; AT-ST majority; SD-9/SD-10 for Palpatine; Gov. Beltane’s X-1 Viper (“Automadon”) vs resurrected Emperor. Models: SD-9/10; X-1 Viper.

## Corellian Engineering Corporation (P2)
Big-three shipbuilder; StarHauler drone barge; criminal robot-ramship conversions. Models: Drone Barge; Robot Ramship.

## Corporate Sector Authority (P2)
CSA aloof post-Empire; diverse manufacturing. Models: CD-2 Harvester; Firefighting Robo.

## Cybot Galactica (P2)
Big Two; 3PO famous; labor→security. Models: Binary Load Lifter; Guardian; Imp City Maint.; LE Repair; LIN Demolitionmech; M-3PO; PD Lurrian; Robo-bartenders; 3PO; WED Treadwell.

## Geentech (P2)
Small medisensor; sued into bankruptcy by Genetech over name; 2-1B bought by IA. Model: 2-1B.

## Genetech Corporation (P2)
Pharma→droids; pioneered droid manufacturing (sparked antidroid backlash). Models: Systems Control; 3D-4X Administrative.

## Go-Corp/Utilitech (P2)
Etti IV vehicle droids (Corp Sector); Utilitech brains. Model: Robo-hack.

## Holowan Mechanicals (P2)
Front for IG assassin program; prototypes escaped, murdered design staff. Model: IG Assassin.

## Imperial Department of Military Research (P3)
Palpatine genocidal R&D. Models: Dark Trooper; HRD; Imp Mark IV Sentry; IT-O; Shadow Droid.

## Industrial Automaton (P3)
Big Two (Automata Galactica + Industrial Intelligence merger); R-series. Models: Asp; Decon; Elegance Message Drone; MD; FLR Logger; LOM; Hound-W2; MN-2E; P2/R1; R2; R3/R4; R5; R6/R7; SE4; Q9-X2.

## LeisureMech Enterprises (P3)
Corp Sector recreational/luxury; security spin-off → Ulban Arms. Models: Automated Sabacc Dealers; C5 Robo-bartender.

## Les Tech (P3)
Core Worlds exploration; resisted Cybot takeover; Imperial frontier then Rebel sales. Models: M38 Explorer; MULE.

## Loronar Corporation (P3)
Corrupt conglomerate; Defense Industries Strike cruisers; Meridian Sector condemnation; Carosi moon plant. Models: Fly Eye; Synthdroid.

## Lovolán (P3)
Core Worlds luxury ornamented. Model: R-10 Household.

## Medtech Industries (P3–4)
Early FX surgical; bankrupt pre-Hoth after Deep Core HQ move. Model: FX Medical Assistant.

## MerenData (P4)
Imperial security/target/sinister; NR outreach disappointing. Models: EV Supervisor; Positronic Processor; RM-2020 Espionage.

## PowerPost (P4)
Office supply → SCM-22 success. Model: SCM-22 Stenographer.

## Publictechnic (P4)
Industrial-maintenance; Sennatt (Bothan space). Models: 850.AA; Tree Feeder; U2C1 Housekeeping.

## Rebaxan Columni (P4)
Chadra-Fan flop; MSE-6 “mouse”; company closed, product persists military. Model: MSE-6.

## Rebel Alliance / NR R&D (P4)
Scratch-built then NR Dept R&D. Models: FIDO; Scavenger; NR 1100 Slicer.

## Roche (P4)
Verpine full-name hive corp; limited Roche Belt + Ithorian herdship distro. Models: 8D8 Smelting; 11-17 Miner; J9 Worker Drone.

## Serv-O-Droid (P4)
Ancient construction/labor; bankrupt; remainder house Elshandruu Pica. Models: CZ Secretary; TT-8L Tattletale; Wrecker; ZZ-4Z ZeeZee.

## Sienar Intelligence Systems (P4)
Santhe/Sienar arm; commission-only Imperial. Models: E522 Assassin; Scarab.

## SoroSuub Corporation (P4–5)
Sullust conglomerate; Imperial ally → NR friend. Models: Memory; Scout Collector/Surveyor; 12-4C-41 Traffic; Unit Zed.

## Telbrintel (P5)
Core Worlds scientific accuracy; paramedic/info. Model: TT-40 Library.

## Trang Robotics (P5)
Drewwa (Almanian); Duelist Elite fencing trainer. Model: Duelist Elite.

## Ulban Arms (P5)
LeisureMech military/security spin-off; Class I allegedly stolen Imperial blueprints. Models: Red Terror; S-EP1 Security.

## Veril Line Systems (P5)
Coruscant; boxy Power Droids + Construction Droids. Models: Construction; EG-6 Power; Gyrowheel 1.42.08 Recycling.

## Historical Droids (P5)
Ancient (Arkanian / Xim ~25k ya); manufacturers gone. Models: Basilisk; Gorm the Dissolver; Krath War Droid; Xim’s War-Robot.

## Inventors (P5)
Simonelle the Ingoian (possible first HRD); Massad Thrumble / Imp Dept. Models: B’omarr Brain Walker; Em Teedee; Fromm Tower; HRD; Widget.

## Miscellaneous Alien Species (P5)
Limited marketing; Palpatine discrimination; sketchy Vuffi Raa / Ssi-ruuk data. Models: C2-R4; 8t88; Rodian Hunter Trainer; Ssi-ruuvi Security; Vuffi Raa; X10-D Draft.
