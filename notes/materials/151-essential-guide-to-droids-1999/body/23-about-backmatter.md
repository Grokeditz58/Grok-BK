# ABOUT THE AUTHOR / ARTISTS — P206 (S115)
**Daniel Wallace:** advertising degree Michigan State; new media/promotional communications at large ad agency; author/coauthor EG Planets and Moons, Anakin Skywalker: Story of Darth Vader, forthcoming Essential Chronology (with Kevin Anderson); Detroit area with wife + two sons.
**Bill Hughes:** designed robots/spaceships young; self-published comics HS; University of Houston TV SFX; ad agencies + toys; comics *The SheBuccaneer*™ / *Addam Omega*™; 1995 penciled much of Dark Horse *Star Wars Droids*; children’s books/comics; Houston with wife Heidi + two children.
**Troy Vigil:** drawing from age 8; SW Trilogy inspired art career; print production → freelance computer artist; work in EG Vehicles and Vessels, EG Weapons and Technology, trading cards, portfolios, posters; Venice Beach CA with partner Stacey + eight cats.
