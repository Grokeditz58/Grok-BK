# MUNICIPAL — P56–65 (S040–)

## Systems Control (PIP/2) — P56–57 (S040)
Genetech pivot medical→supervisory; replace human/alien switchboard operators to eliminate anxiety foul-ups. Marketed “glitch-proof” but brain easily overtaxed — e.g. cross-wired habitation-sphere sewage with water-flow → 1,013 severe intestinal-distress cases. Programming patches improved 8-limbed keypunch precision; discontinued after 2 years. Lore: PIP/2 at Holographic Zoo of Extinct Animals (Coruscant) encountered Chewbacca. Illos: hexagonal console isometric.
**Schematics:** Front/Side — Rotating Reception Antennae, Primary Optic Sensors, Sensor Feed Hookup, Multitasking Cognitive Processor, Adjustable Optic Sensors (360° board view), Vocabulator/Broadcast Speaker, Eight Articulated Control Limbs, Multijointed Keypad Digits, Variable-height Telescoping Legs.

## 12-4C-41 Traffic Controller — P58–59 (S041)
SoroSuub; spaceport/orbital dock traffic; automated-system failures (e.g. Tala 9 collisions) motivate use. Bipedal humanoid for existing equipment interface. Sophisticated optic/recorder: panoramic + macroscopic; sees through thick fog. Middle torso = landing beacon (omnidirectional floodlight). Cloud City primary user. Lore: “Wuntoo Forcee” (1-2-4-C) survived EV-9D9 massacre; tracked killer to Tatooine for revenge.
**Schematics:** Front — Internal Beacon Floodlight, Tinted Transparisteel Panes, Zero-G Maneuvering Jets, Scomp-link Input, Audio Sensors, EM Sensor, Advanced Cognitive Processor, Panoramic Optic/Recorder, Vocabulator, Data-probe (retracted finger), Servomotor. Rear — Rear-facing sensors, back plate vent, more Zero-G jets.

## 5T Tree Feeder — P60–61 (S042)
Publictechnic; “gigantic clattering metal spider” gardening. 6 pointed limbs on dorsal support rod; arachnid body packed with retractable cutting/grooming tools. Rim photoreceptors zoom for insect damage/root structures. Articulated hose + adjustable sprayer (fertilizer/pesticides/water); large abdomen water tank; twin manipulators; edger/trimmer; pruning shears; pH/moisture probes; fruit-plucker. Intelligent at gardening; indifferent otherwise; may “irritate” at flower-pickers/lawn-walkers. Common: Kuat, Hesperidium, Belsavis (Plawal rift). Lore: Irek Ismaren used them to test Force abilities.
**Schematics:** Front/Side — Access Hatch, Photoreceptor Turret, Edger/Trimmer, Water Tank, Sensor Array, Spotlights, Body Support Rod, Sprayer Hose, pH Probe, Manipulator Arm, Pruning Shears, Weeding Arm, Digging Tips.

## Inferno Firefighting Robo — P62–63 (S043)
CSA modular extreme-heat system: 1 host carrier + 4 firefighter units. Firefighters: heat-resistant alloy; ventral foam spray nozzle; small laser cutter; 2 heavy grippers; head w/ IR photoreceptor, echolocation scanner, searchlight, loudspeaker. Stack like blocks or work tandem. Illos: single hovering unit; stacked team; host carrying 4; human silhouette ~chest-height.
**Schematics:** Host — Warning Flashing Light, Firefighting Robo, Chemical/Foam Storage Basin, Refueling Hoses. Unit — Searchlight, Audio/Echolocation Scanner, Refueling Port, Internal Tanks (2), Locking Clip, Repulsor Engine, Photoreceptor Unit, Loudspeaker, Arms (2), Protective Armor Shell, Hose, Cutting Laser, Warning Lights.

## Robo-hack — P64–65 (S044)
Go-Corp/Utilitech automated taxi (Core→Outer Rim); droid intelligence module in passenger vehicle. Standard = Metrocab enclosed landspeeder ~6 m; spartan interior (1–4 passengers, music speakers, snuff-pot, often grime/smell/torn upholstery). Destination via front-wall auditory sensor; minimal-chitchat vocoder; pay credit voucher or local currency. Non-pay → lock doors → nearest police. CLC (Central Learning Chips) learn shortcuts; some “canny” droids defraud tourists via longer routes. Modern resilient cover immune to most small-arms (early vulnerable to street gangs).
**Schematics:** Front — “On Call” Indicator Light, Transparisteel Canopy, Situational Tracking Sensors, Forward Headlights. Side — Sealed Computer Compartment, Access Doors, Traffic-Control Antenna, Repulsorlift Engine, Panoramic Rear Scanner, Fueling Port, Repulsorlift Pads.
