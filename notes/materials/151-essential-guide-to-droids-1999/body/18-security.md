# SECURITY — P162–169 (S093–)

## Red Terror — P162–163 (S093)
Ulban Arms Warden 10-24 (gladiatorial→security/bodyguard) modified; 500-unit automated police on factory moon Telti under Force-sensitive Brakiss. Scarlet paint; cognitive circuitry removed from 490 for obedience; 10 troops of 50 with 1 “Lieutenant” retaining circuitry. Enforce Telti bylaws; noncompliant droids “circuit-purged, ripped to pieces, and randomly scattered.” Hunched stance; finger-blasters; 2 double laser cannons under swinging chest panels; broadband antennae coordinate via hypersonic signals. Lore: 13 yrs after Endor, R2-D2 + C-3PO investigating Senate hall bombing; C-3PO captured/nearly disassembled; R2 + army of astromechs overwhelmed Red Terror.
**Schematics:** Front/Rear — Armor Plating, Broadband Antennae, Hydraulic Limbs, Photoreceptor, Operator Maintenance Handle, Spotlights (chest cavity), Systems Access Unit, Vocabulator, Heavy Blaster Cannons (behind hinged chest), Blaster Barrels in fingers.

## G-2RD Guard — P164–165 (S094)
Arakyd; tenacious/stubborn. **Pull quote (Han Solo):** “You get a G-2RD droid going, and you’ll have to take its head off to stop it.” [q04]
Repulsorlift; flight ceiling >900 m (Byblos vertical prisons). Ebony exterior: optical sensors (IR/UV), directional audio, broadband antenna. 3 equidistant arms: short-range stun prod; blaster cannon (deadly force); heavy gripper (unlock doors / drag subjects). Demanding/abusive personality for hardened criminals → often revokes visiting privileges for innocents. Shrugs off ion + limited laser; immune to standard restraining bolts (homemade bolts sometimes work). Used by Empire, CSA, locals; NR captured units on Coruscant. Notable prisoners: Adm. Ackbar, Mara Jade (Thrawn campaign).
**Schematics:** Front/Rear — Blaster Cannon, Grasping Claw, Floodlight, Broadband Antenna/Receiver extended, Audio Pickup, Stun Prod, Vocoder, Repulsorlift Engine Housing, Sensor Wand Cluster extended, Optic Sensors. ~chest–waist height vs human.

## S-EP1 Security — P166–167 (S095)

Disarm assassins, deactivate bombs, emergency medical; non-lethal preferred but deadly force to save master. Arjan IV logic; spectral filters; microscopic imaging; blinding flash bursts; energy/minor-explosion resistant torso; high-imaging holocamera; twin-opposable-thumb hands. Lore: Leia Organa Solo permanent residence guard; trusted with her three children.
**Schematics:** Front/Side — Floodlight, Durasteel Cranial Collar, Thermal/Radionic Sensors, Modified Arjan IV Cognitive Module, Flash Photoreceptors, Auditory Sensor, Head Rotation Pivot Joint, Olfactory Sensor, Vocabulator, Security Holocamera, Stun-gas Release Port, Piston Arms, Pelvic Gyro-balance Unit, Twin Opposable Thumbs. Holds stun staff.

## Foreign Intruder Defense Organism (FIDO) — P168–169 (S096)
Ackbar brainstorm; Mon Cal scientists; first tested NR secret base Anoth — decimated half an MT-AT assault. Inspired by predatory “krakana”; central pod like “overfed krabbex.” Only weapons: 26 durasteel threaded cables ending in razor-toothed plasteel pincers; extend ≤100 m; punch through Juggernaut hull. 4 legs for anchoring only (no self-mobility; place strategically). Sophisticated cognitive module friend/foe + improvise tactics. Blueprints “Class Red” NR Intelligence.
**Schematics:** Tentacle/Pincer Side — Durasteel Threaded Cable, Razor Cutting Edge, Plasteel Claw, Sensor Cluster. Top — Plasteel-armored Hull, Tentacle Ports (26), Interior Cable Spools, Anchoring Legs, Access Panels, Motion Detectors and Long-range Sensors. Pod ~2× human height, much wider.

## 501-Z Police Droid (Zed) — P170–171 (S097)
SoroSuub; highly autonomous; nickname “Zed”; frontier outposts / high-traffic planets e.g. Teyr. Advanced personality module + cognitive matrix → detective work with organic officers; hunts fugitives; unflagging optimism (irritating to some). 1.9 m; crimson durasteel; shock-resistant not blaster-proof; piston-driven arms. Default non-lethal (electrical stun sticks); some carry stun blasters. Lore: Unit Zed at Kalarba’s Hosk Station — bravery vs wrecker droid; destroyed on Nar Shaddaa.
**Schematics:** Front/Rear — Optical Slot, Crown Reinforcement Collar, Vocabulator, Rear Movement Sensors, Recharge Coupling, Holographic Projector/Recorder, Upper Arm Pistons, Shock-resistant Durasteel Chassis, Heavy Servogrips. Human scale.

## Hound-W2 SPD — P172–173 (S098)

No Basic vocabulator — Droidspeak (needs protocol interpreter or scomp datapad). Checkpoints: glitterstim, sweetblossom, detonite, baradium. NR: Imperial Palace + Senate Hall Coruscant; Delta Source leak investigation (Leia).
**Schematics:** Front/Side — Fabritech Communications Array, Extension Cables for Arms (internal spools), Extendible Video Sensor, Internal Probability Projection Computer, Fabritech Sensor Array, Pulse Scan Emitter, Laser Scan Emitter, Fine Grasper Arm, Tank Tread, Audio Receptor, Thermal Imager. ~⅓ R2 height.
