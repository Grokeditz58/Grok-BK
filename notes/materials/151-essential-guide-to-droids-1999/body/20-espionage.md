# ESPIONAGE — P182–187 (S103–)

## Moon Moth — P182–183 (S103)
Dual definitions: brainless blue-winged insect pest / Arakyd Mechis III espionage gadget. Mechanical cousin; extreme miniaturization + ultra-lightweight; flies on wing power alone (no repulsor). Imp contract; programmers vs engineers feud → compromise finger-sized body + rudimentary AI mimicking erratic lepidoptera flight. Verbal commands: pause/launch/return/acquire target. Antennae = visual + EM sensors. Plants microdot homing beacon (“egg”); bean-sized detonite self-destruct. Post-Empire sold to NRI; Gen. Crix Madine used one on Nal Hutta → discovery of Durga’s secret base + Darksaber project collapse.
**Schematics:** Top — Ultralight Wing Support, Auditory Pickup, Finely machined Metallic Armature, Fabric Wings with Camouflage Coloration Pattern. Side — EM/Visual Sensor Antennae, Landing Legs with Miniaturized Gripping Hooks, Self-destruct Detonite Trigger in Forward Abdomen, Microdot Homing Beacons (6) in Abdomen Rear.

## Fly Eye — P184–185 (S104)
Loronar response to Cybot AC1 “Spy-Eye”; ≤3 human finger-widths; spherical + tiny repulsor; max 10 km/h; flight ceiling 9 m; 4 wide venting ports (engine heat). Interior: high-imaging holographic lens + coded transmission (no internal archive — real-time tightbeam). Remotely piloted (joystick) or basic search-and-spy AI. Passive mode: clawed limbs on walls/ceilings, repulsor off → nearly undetectable. Lore: tabloid NovaNetwork; TriNebulon News Fly Eye in Imperial Palace recorded Han kidnapping Leia → broadcast 4 yrs after Endor.
**Schematics:** Top — Clawed Gripper Legs, Magnetized Tips, Holographic Panoramic/Zoom Lens. Side — Tightbeam Transmission Antenna, Heat Dispersal Vents, Miniaturized Repulsorlift Engine.

## Espionage Droid (generic + RM-2020) — P186–187 (S105)
Covert programming can turn mundane servant into spy. “True” espionage droids look identical to standard models (Model E, binary load lifter) with undetectable surveillance modules. Common masquerades: 3POs + Asps (overlooked). Empire: many Arakyd RA-7 “Death Star Droid.” Thrawn: modified IA Decon III infiltrated Noghri homeworld. **MerenData RM-2020:** dedicated (no disguise); humanoid torso on repulsor; battlefield troop-movement data; 160,000 credits; info record/broadcast + UV/IR detectors.
**Schematics:** RM-2020 Front — Vocabulator, Tightbeam Transmission Antenna retracted, Visual/Audio Sensors, Information Storage Archive, Hardwired Espionage Module, UV/IR Detectors, Specialized Manipulator Arms, Wide-dispersal Ionizer, Defensive Energy Cannon, Repulsorlift Engine. Modified Protocol Front (cutaway) — Sensor Baffler, Removable Storage Module, Internal Scanning Buffers, 3PO-Series Body Shell, High-gain Audio Sensors, Data Encryption Unit, Holorecording Photoreceptors.

## Slicer Droid — P188–189 (S106)
Tech answer to encryption; NR 1100 by New Republic R&D to crack Imperial Information Center (climate-controlled chamber beneath Imperial Palace). “Spindly arms, a lumpy carapace, and a generous overbite”; weak temporary repulsorlift.
**Schematics:** Front/Side — Removable Coding Modules, Datacard Insert Slot, Internal Slicing Hardware Matrix, Visual/Audio Recorder, Data Scanners (lining groove), Holographic Projector, Data-probe retracted, Keyboard Operation Digits / Supplementary Data-probes, Attachment Assembly, Primary Scomp-link Port, Repulsorlift Engine Housing.

## Positronic Processor — P190–191 (S107)
MerenData B2-X tiny titanium cubes; experimental Imp projects with immense computing power. Imp Intelligence disappointed by “flighty natures” + doodling in networks when bored → canceled; entered fringe. Outlaw-techs coated one blue lacquer → **Blue Max**; nestled in chest of antique labor droid **Bollux** to hide from CSA. Second unit **Flirt** owned by Wookiee bounty hunter Chenlambec; radically modified; took over Trandoshan X10-D draft droid body.
**Schematics:** Bollux + Blue Max — Plastron Compartment (housing Blue Max). Blue Max Front — Articulated Hand extended, Computer Interface Probe extended, Photoreceptor, Vocoder, Peg Mounts, Plastron Connection Jack extended.
