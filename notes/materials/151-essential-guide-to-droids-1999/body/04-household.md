# HOUSEHOLD SERVICE — P34– (S029–)

## TDL Nanny — P34–35 (S029)
1.9 m silvery; torso + multiple arms in soft brown synthskin at human body temp. Four arms (hold baby + other tasks). Internal 2 L refrigerated milk/formula basin → warming unit → detachable tube/bottle. Limited AA-1 Verbobrain + TranLang (millions of languages); child-rearing from thousands of cultures. **XL-Lioness** security variant: 2 concealed blasters behind removable lower-arm hands (high-profile clients). Lore: Leia’s modified Lioness TDL on Anoth — unfolding laser screen; destroyed protecting Anakin Solo from Imperial abduction.
**Schematics:** Front — Photoreceptors, Olfactory Sensor, Modulated Vocabulator, Auditory Sensors, Heated Synthskin Arms, Internal Child-Care Database, Smooth Corners, Blaster Cannons in Lower Forearms, Detachable Lower Hands, Recharge Port (shin), Tube/Bottle Connection. Rear — System Access Panel.

## Model E — P36–37 (S030)
Accutronics; playmates/secondary caregivers for young children; out of production >1 century; vintage still used (schools, childcare, pediatric wards, wealthy homes). Humanoid <1 m; smooth rounded non-threatening durasteel; bright colors (yellow/red/green/blue/orange/pink). Friendship programming; reads organic emotions; sings multi-culture songs; games/stories databanks; simple feeding/diaper (less complex than TDL). Lore: Kalarba trio Q-E/2-E/U-E ran town nursery 4 generations; coerced into illegal blaster assembly; U-E body destroyed → cognitive module reinstalled in freight droid. Illos: two Model Es; QE head variation inset; R2 silhouette for scale (Model E slightly shorter).
**Schematics:** Front/Rear — Personality Matrix Housing, Olfactory Sensor, Oversized Photoreceptors, Auditory Sensors, Modulated Vocabulator, Restraining Bolt, System Ventilation Port, Safety Light, Plastic-Coated Durasteel Chassis.

## Guardian Droid (GV/3) — P38–39 (S031)
Cybot Galactica; “ultimate protector, companion, and friend”; modeled on four-legged domesticated guard animals. Cannot speak (growls/roars/yips); understands simple Basic. Heavyset/powerful; hydraulic legs outrun humans / pin intruders; can carry a human. Solar panels; retractable serrated razor incisors; concealed belly blaster (usually stun for household). Popular with wealthy landowners, military, families; holo-series *Revella’s Journey: A Story of a Girl and Her Droid* drove kids’ sales. SAR variant in development (enhanced sensors/survival gear). Illos: dog/reptilian mechanical beast; human silhouette ~chest-height at shoulder.
**Schematics:** Front — Infrared Photoreceptors, Olfactory Samplers, Limited Vocabulator, Heavy Plasteel Plating, Retractable Blaster Cannon (behind panels), Hydraulically-powered Servomotors, Durasteel Claws. Side — Retractable Signal Antenna, Restraining Bolt Mount, Solar Energy Panels, Audio Sensors, Triple-reinforced Jaw Assembly, Retractable Razor Teeth, 360° Head Pivot Joint, Standard Recharge Port.

## ZZ-4Z (“Zee Zee”) — P40–41 (S032)
Serv-O-Droid JV-Z1/D butler; aristocrat fad; short humanoid with intentional subservient slouch. Infrared lintscope; genteel vocabulator; highbrow etiquette DB (e.g. fold Oseoni greatcoat). Lore: willed to Military Academy of Carida dean; served 17 successive deans; Cadet Mako Spince took it to Nar Shaddaa after expulsion (destroyed Carida’s “mascot moon”); lost in sabacc to young Han Solo; Solo left it on Smuggler’s Moon; 6 yrs post-Endor Solo returned → Zee Zee announced guest **Boba Fett**; torn to pieces in blaster fight.
**Schematics:** Front/Rear — Auditory Sensor, Photoreceptor, Supplementary Sensor Package, Infrared Lintscope, Olfactory Sensor, Vocabulator, Chromite Body Shell, Servomotor, Recharge Port, Auxiliary Power Cable.

## U2C1 Housekeeping — P42–43 (S033)
Publictechnic; cheap efficient hotel/janitorial; vaguely sinister; no personality (cost). Hardened blue plastic; optical slot + audio + basic vocabulator in head; all behavioral/performance circuitry in head/neck; torso = waste-storage bin w/ ventral discharge hatch. Right flex-tube = upholstery vacuum; left = stiff wire brush. Security flaw: easily subverted (jewelry theft / bomb planting). Lore: Lady Valarian used one to spy on Jabba; communicated with hunter J’Quille. Illos: hunched top-heavy shell on spindly legs; human silhouette ~chest-high.
**Schematics:** Front/Side — Audio Sensors, Pelvic Servomotor, Optical Slot, Internal Performance Hardware, Vocabulator, Waste-storage Bin, Discharge Hatch, Flex-tube Sweeper Arms, Wire Brush, Plastic Frame, Upholstery Attachment.

## SE4 Servant — P44–45 (S034)

Humorous open: bruallki/suuri shells/plicto steak etiquette — SE4 might “switch off its central processor and clatter to the floor in stunned horror.” Shining humanoid 1.6 m; stiff joints; flawless posture. 2 photoreceptors, 2 auditory, vocabulator, sensitive olfactory; simulated taste buds inside left index finger. Hardwired: party planning, home economics, culinary arts; secondary skill modules (local cuisine/maintenance); basic handyman (complex repairs → WED Treadwell); basic translator. Market ~2,600 credits. Icons: Rebel + IA (not Empire).
**Schematics:** Front — Secondary Olfactory (cosmetic forehead), Photoreceptors, Auditory Sensors, Vocabulator, Primary Olfactory (chest), Skill-matrix Insertion Slot, Systems Access Port, Servomotor, Pelvic Servomotor, Simulated Tastebuds (fingers). Rear — Recharge Coupling.

Lore: Roganda Ismaren kept several; son Irek controlled via Force-capable cybernetic implant.
**Schematics:** Front/Side — Electronically-charged Serving Tray, Serving Hands/Arms, Brass Rail, Black Marble Panels, System Ventilation, Inlaid Ornamentation, Vacuum/Cleaning Arm, Photoreceptor, Droid Brain.
