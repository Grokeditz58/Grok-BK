# ASSASSINS — P148–155 (S086–)

## IG Assassin Droid — P148–149 (S086)
“Most coldly efficient killing machine ever invented”; Chief Tech Loruss under Imp Supervisor Gurdun at Holowan Mechanicals (Project Phlutdroid). 2 m; cylindrical head studded red sensors (360° vision); no olfactory; advanced auditory/radionic/temp. Weapons: repeating blaster cannons each forearm; concussion grenade launcher left hip; flamethrowers; sonic stunners; paralysis cords; throwing flechettes; poison gas canisters; right fingers = cutting lasers; left palm deflects blaster bolts. Lore: 5 IG-88 prototypes — experimental sentience → first self-replicated consciousness + killed creators; A/B/C/D escaped; IG-72 independent; IG-88 bounty hunting; shell found Mechis III years later.
**Schematics:** Front/Rear — Retractable Antenna, Heat/Motion Sensors, Optical Sensor Array, Sound Sensor, Vocabulator, Gas Canister Rack, Sonic Stunner, Flamethrower, Retractable Paralysis Cord, Computer Interface Port, Throwing Flechettes Port, Concussion Grenade Launcher, Built-in Blaster Cannon, Blaster Reflective Palm, Blast Armor, Cutting Laser (fingertips), Acid-proof Servo Wires. Stormtrooper silhouette for 2 m scale.

## E522 Assassin — P150–151 (S087)
Empire outlawed assassin droids after Caprioril massacre (rogue robot killed >20,000 spectators to assassinate Gov. Amel Bokli); secretly Sienar Intelligence Systems developed E522 to eliminate vocal New Order critics. “Cyber-augmented weightlifter”: hulking upper body, narrow vulnerable waist; heavy armor; internal power plant; flat head (minimal target). Tank treads top 30 km/h; head = residual-heat sensors + 2 blinding spotlights. Right arm: heavy repeating blaster + neurological needler + ion device; left: explosive hunter-seeker projectile cannon; belly cavity small manipulators. Lore: Jabba’s “Eefive-Tootoo” fed to rancor (spat out) → Jawas repaired → Lady Valarian courier/delivery boy.
**Schematics:** Front/Side — External Power Cells, Heavy Armor Plating, Blinding Floodlights, Infrared Photoreceptors, System Ventilation, Heat Tracking Sensor Wand, Hunter-seeker Projectile Cannon, Manipulator Arms extended, Tank Treads, Neurological Needler / Ion Cannon / Heavy Repeating Blaster.

## Scarab Droid — P152–153 (S088)
Sienar Black Ops; sold to Imp Intelligence assassination branch. Palm-sized insectile; 18 varieties (Mark VI most produced). Compact body, 6 jointed legs, hard head w/ poison-injector piercing fangs → nerve toxins or hallucinogenic stimulants reservoir. Sensors: heat detectors, pheromone sniffers, biorhythm analyzers. Lore: Coruscant critic driven to “scarab-induced dementia” → jumped to death; cloned Emperor sent horde vs Luke on New Alderaan (saved by Vima-Da-Boda).
**Schematics:** Front — Low-light Photoreceptors, Pheromone/Biorhythm Sensor Package, Servomotors, Silencer Pads. Top (in palm) — Internal Poison Reservoir, Injection Fangs, Plasteel Body Casing.

## DZ-70 Fugitive Tracker — P154–155 (S089)
**Pull quote:** “DO NOT ATTEMPT ESCAPE. MUTINEERS AND EVADERS WILL BE CONSIDERED IN VIOLATION OF THE CAPITAL POWERS ACT.” — tracker droids aboard Imp battlemoon *Eye of Palpatine*. [q03]
Arakyd; Imp military hunt mandatory-service rejectees; also corps + black market. Superior to smaller seeker balls; single-minded brain; glossy blaster-reflective silver; repulsorlift hover; equatorial photoreceptors 360°. Track by appearance/mass/body temp/heart rate/movement patterns. Maintenance-intensive (constant sensor cleaning; full diagnostic every 12–15 months). Lore: many stored *Eye of Palpatine* 30 yrs unrepaired → malfunction captured anyone (Gamorreans→Jawas).
**Schematics:** Arms folded Front / extended Side — Pheromone Sniffer, Infrared Detector, Reflective Silver Body Shell, Temperature Sensor, Antenna, Searchbeams (4), EM Sensor, Photoreceptors (iris expanded), Laser Cannon, Arms with Pincers, Repulsorlift Engine, Vocabulator, Radionic Sensor, Heavy Grasper, Biorhythm Analyzer, Sensor Antennae, Ultraviolet Sensor, Stun Blaster. Human torso-sized.
