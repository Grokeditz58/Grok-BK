# MILITARY — P128–147 (S076–)

## Basilisk War Droid — P128–129 (S076)
Mandalorian clans ~4 millennia pre-Empire (Great Sith War); semiorganic alien constructs as direct extensions of riders’ bodies; more intelligent than domesticated beasts; attuned to masters’ emotions. Giant beetles w/ lasers for mandibles; rear “wings” lift → high-boost rockets. Variants: 2-seater bomber, stealth, open-combat one-crew. Atmosphere + deep space; heavy dangling front claws (batter/slash + landing gear). Open-combat: nose shockwave generator rods (plasma bursts); pulse-wave cannons; shatter-missile tubes; sensor clusters; riders often axes/swords. Lore: Mandalore conquered Kuar + Empress Teta carbonite factories; after Ulic Qel-Droma defeated Mandalore → Coruscant, Ossus, Onderon.
**Schematics:** Front — Intake Jets, Armored Wings, Laser Cannons (2), Shatter-missile Launchers (2). Side — Battle Mount Rider, Weapons Compartments, Shockwave Generator Rods, Fore Sensor Cluster, Heavy Brawling Claws, High-boost Engines, Aft Sensor Cluster, Rear Landing Legs.

## Xim’s War-Robot — P130–131 (S077)
Third Battle of Vontor (pre-Republic; Xim vs Kossak the Hutt; Hutt-victor history obscures). Early mechanized combat perfection; resilient/lethal bulky bipeds tower over humans. Thick armor; reinforced stress points; mirrored reflective surface parries lasers; shielded arm/hand apertures for archaic heatbeams + particle dischargers. Limited reasoning; orders via transmission horn from Xim command podiums. Corps Commanders: golden death’s-head; rank-and-file white. Decimated at Vontor by Hutts + Nikto/Vodran/Klatooinian. Legacy: 1,000 survivors as *Queen of Ranroon* treasure-ship guardians → Dellalt under Survivors cult; Han + Chewie encountered them.
**Schematics:** Front/Rear — Optical Lenses, Speaker Grille, Xim the Despot’s Insignia (chest skull), Systems Power Generator, Battlefield Pulse Cannons removable, Heavy Armor Plating, Laser-reflective Kiirium Sheen, Particle Discharger Aperture (ankles), Heatbeam Aperture (palm).

## Krath War Droid — P132–133 (S078)
Krath secret society ~4,000 yrs pre-GCW; Empress Teta nobles; dark-side magic/Sith secrets → military takeover. Lithe lethal; Cinnagar foundries; limited cognitive matrices (cost + hit-and-run aggression). Short sword + antique pulse-wave firing assembly; attenuated induction spines (generate charges / skewer). Hunched animal sprint on 2 powerful legs. Lore: Battle of Deneba — Krath ambush of ~10,000 Jedi via reprogrammed servant droids + war-droid life pods; Arca Jeth fell; Jedi eventually prevailed.
**Schematics:** Front/Rear — Melee Combat Short Sword, Gyro-balance Assembly, Armor Plating, Limited Cognitive Matrix, Threat Prioritization Sensors, Auditory Sensors, Pulse-wave Induction Spines, Pulse-wave Firing Barrel, Flexible Limb Joints.

## Dark Trooper — P134–135 (S079)
Highly encrypted; few Rebels survived encounters. Primary intel: Kyle Katarn (single-handedly derailed). Gen. Rom Mohc creator — personal-combat obsessed; Death Star loss = opportunity for “super stormtroopers.” Built at *Arc Hammer* titanic spacefaring factory.
- **Phase I:** Metal skeleton; forearm shield; razor-edged carving blade (*phrik*).
- **Phase II:** Standard combat; phrik body shell; repulsorlift/flight jets; assault cannon (plasma shells + rockets).
- **Phase III:** Ultimate; massive; seeker missile tubes; independent or human-worn exosuit.
End: Katarn destroyed *Arc Hammer* → Mohc dead; Palpatine refused further funding for multi-billion project.
**Schematics:** Phase I — Blast Shield, Cutting Sword, Power Cables, Phrik Skeletal Frame, Photoreceptors. Phase II — Repulsorlift Engine, Maneuvering Jets, Reinforced Phrik Body Shell. Phase III — Connection Point for Firing Tubes, Seeker Missiles (in frame), Exosuit Access Hatch. Human silhouette for scale.

## SD-9 and SD-10 — P136–137 (S080)
Balmorra factories + Umak Leth intellect; Gov. Beltane green-lit Imperial project. Early SD-1–5 lacked tactics until Balmorran Arms illegally sliced Mohc Dark Trooper data. **SD-9** for NR + Thrawn campaign: laser-reflective armor, ionization shielding, tireless servomotors, pinpoint targeting; forearms = heavy repeating blaster + explosive plasma cannon; Beltane claimed 1 SD-9 ≡ 100 organic infantry. **SD-10** (Leth + Balmorran after Empire return): stronger/tougher/smarter; mini concussion missile launcher; improved sensors; fast-reaction servos; point-of-impact shielding; experimental self-healing metals; DBs = all SD-9 tactics + counters. Lore: Beltane’s SD-10s made “bantha hash” of Empire’s SD-9s when executor tried to seize factories; ground units fared poorly vs aerial Shadow Droids.
**Schematics:** SD9 Front — Plasma-burst Cannon, Heavy Repeating Blaster, Ionization Shield Generator, Laser-reflective Armor Plating, Targeting Sensor Array. SD10 Front — Extended-fire Repeating Blaster, Self-healing Alloy Chassis, Point-of-impact Shield Generator, Missile Launcher/Targeter, Combat Sensor Package, Plasma-burst Cannon. ~2.5–3 m vs human.

## Shadow Droid — P138–139 (S081)
Cyborgs not true droids: organic brains (wounded TIE pilots) surgically in starfighter-like shells. Palpatine order on Byss post-Endor; Umak Leth design as Ssi-ruuvi “entechment” substitute. Brains in nutrient cocoons hardwired to tactical computers. Used Battle of Balmorra + *Eclipse II* defense. Tech likely lost post-Imperial collapse. Glossy black alloy.
**Schematics:** Front — Ion Cannon, Repeating Blaster Cannon, Visual Slot/Combat Sensor Array, Dorsal Laser Cannons, Concussion Missile Rack, Missile Launcher/Targeter, Internal Tactical Computer, Internal Nutrient Cocoon for Brain, Proton Torpedo Rack, Torpedo Launcher/Targeter, Electromagnetic Pulse Gun. Side — Reflective Ebony Alloy Hull, Ion Engine Bank, Repulsorlift Engine. Human ~1.5–2× hull peak.

## X-1 Viper (“Automadon”) — P140–141 (S082)
Balmorran Arms giant beetle war machine; orig. for NR; cloned Palpatine forced Balmorra to Empire → Beltane secret sabotage allowed NR hijack. Full-body molecular shielding: absorb laser → shunt to own power cells. Lore: Lando + Wedge strike team commandeered Vipers to Imperial Citadel gates on Byss; defeated by Palpatine’s chrysalis beasts (molecular shields useless vs physical claws/teeth).
**Schematics:** Front — Internal Control Compartment, Power Cables and Weapons Conduits, Boosted Blaster Cannons, Viewports, Sensor Array, Standard Laser Cannons. Side — Heavy Brawling Pincers, Molecularly-shielded Armor Plating, Drive Impeller. Human ~⅓–¼ height.

## Fromm Tower Droid — P144–145 (S084)
Tig Fromm (son of Sise Fromm) on Ingo pre-Yavin; believed mechanicals superior (loyalty/durability/firepower). 4 m; 6 oversized puncture-resistant self-sealing pneumatic wheels; 100 kph flat. Squat armor-plated chassis (engine/drivetrain) + cylindrical tower + saucer head 360°. Head sensors: visual/auditory/IR/EM/seismic; broadband antenna → seeker drones. Offense: “obsidian repulsor balls” from chest panels — antigrav spheres >250 kph explode ≤1 m of target; remote-pilot ≤5 at once. Lore: R2-D2 + C-3PO encountered during prisoner rescue; 431 operational on Ingo when Fromms captured by Boba Fett.
**Schematics:** Front — Visual/Radionic/Infrared/Seismic Sensors, Repulsor Ball Release Hatches, Self-sealing Pneumatic Tires. Side — Auditory Sensor, Broadband Antenna/Receiver, Electromagnetic Sensor, Tower Assembly, Laser Cannons, Armored Chassis, Drivetrain. Human ~½ height.

## Robot Ramship — P146–147 (S085)
“Deceptive, dishonorable, and flagrantly destructive”; not mass-produced — Outer Rim outlaw-techs assemble from junked warship shells (often CEC) + suicidal pilot droid brains. Kamikaze: interior packed solid durasteel for mass → hard to maneuver, “rippling multimegaton blast” on impact. Painted portholes + underpowered lasers mimic ship of the line. Lore: Battle of Centerpoint Station (Corellian crisis) — Sacorrian Triad 4 ramships vs Bakuran flagship *Intruder*; Adm. Ossilege realized diversion too late; *Intruder* destroyed but self-destruct took final ramship + part of Triad armada.
**Schematics:** Top — High-acceleration Quad Engines, Engine-blast Boost Lines, False Observation Port, Structural Support Rods. Side — Solid Durasteel in Nose, Droid Brain in Vessel’s Computer Core, Diversionary Laser Cannons, Durasteel Ballast Beneath Hull, Low-power Turbolasers, Guidance Computer, Extendible Sensor Suite.
