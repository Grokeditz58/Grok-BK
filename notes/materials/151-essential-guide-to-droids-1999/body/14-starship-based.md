## Gyrowheel 1.42.08 Recycling — P114–115 (S069)
Veril Line; for self-sufficient deep-space ships (e.g. Star Destroyers Unknown Regions). One-wheeled vs EG-6 biped; gyro-stabilized speed/maneuverability on decks. Whiplike multi-jointed metal extensors; optical sensors + removable gripping pincers on “hands”; negligible intelligence → ship master computer guidance. Locates organic refuse, empties waste pipes → recycling chambers; enzymatic acid → “organic soup” → bland ration bars. Lore: several aboard *Eye of Palpatine*; per *Children of the Jedi*, computer ordered them to subdue Luke and fling into recycling acid.
**Schematics:** Side — Temporary Holding Bin, Carrying Handle, Reception Antenna retracted, Spotlight Lumas, Retractable Extensor Arms, Grasping Pincers removable, Peripheral Scanning Sensors, Diagnostic Display Screen, Fuel Rods, Gyroscopic Balancing Unit, Single Drive Wheel. Front — Primary Optic Sensor, Mapping Sensors, Arm Reinforcement Rod, Running Lights, Specialized Optic Sensor on Palm. R2 silhouette (Gyrowheel wider/taller).

## MSE-6 (“Mouse Droid”) — P124–125 (S074)
Rebaxan Columni; “tiny, ubiquitous, and annoying”; Imperial Navy general-purpose. Modeled on cute homeworld pet but customers saw disease-carrying vermin; company bankrupt → Empire bought entire run cut-rate for Palpatine expansion shortages. Single modular circuit matrix (one skill at a time: repair/security/janitorial/programming); “black box” hides 2 retractable manipulators. Top compartment for sealed orders/docs (voice-code only). Lore: attempted assassin of High Inquisitor Tremayne via detonite-packed mouse; guide troops through Death Star.
**Schematics:** Front unsealed — Fine Manipulator extended, Command/Order Tray extended, Rank Command Cylinder, Heavy Manipulator extended, Treaded wheels. Side sealed — Auditory Sensor, Drive Wheels, Miniature Holocam, Electro-photoreceptor, Side Panel. Human silhouette ~mid-shin.

**Schematics:** Front/Rear — Advanced Auditory Sensors, Servomotor, Hidden Surveillance System, Magnetic Sensor, Broadband Photoreceptors, Vocabulator with Sounding Box, Interface Connection Port, Intersystem Connection Wires, Intermotor Actuating Coupling, Black Gloss Body Coating, Magnetic-grip Foot Plates.
