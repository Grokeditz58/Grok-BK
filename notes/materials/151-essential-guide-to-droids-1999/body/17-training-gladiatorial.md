# TRAINING / GLADIATORIAL — P156–161 (S090–)

## RHTC-560 Hunter Trainer — P156–157 (S090)
D-Tec; Rodian bounty-hunter training system. Master control unit (humanoid Rodian/arachnid mix; wicked claws; high agility) + HT drones. Master commands ≤10 drones (often remote through jungle while stationary); scomp-link downloads/replays drone visuals for debrief. Drones: small compact; legs or repulsors; no independent brains; ~50 km range; retractable manipulators, searchlight, stun blaster. Lore: Grand Protector Navik the Red uses lethal (disruptor) version on prisoners for entertainment. Market: bounty guilds, militias, police academies; controller ~5,500 credits; drones ~2,000 each.
**Schematics:** Command Front — Sharpened Claws, Stun Blaster, Scomp-link Access Port (behind panel), Armor Plating, Wideband Transceiver, Vocabulator, Sensor Array. HT Drone Side — Maneuvering Jets, Searchlight, Manipulators extended, Repulsorlift Engine, Stun Blaster, Armor Plating.

## Duelist Elite — P158–159 (S091)
Trang Robotics; high-end patrician fencing trainer; endless precise technique reproduction. Torso modeled on 2-1B but more durable; swiveling socket joints; sensitive internal gyroscope; red scoring-target circle on torso (beeps / shuts down in low-difficulty). Hands: gripper fingers around connection ports for sword hilts; ships with 3 flexisteel rapiers (saber/foil/épée); modifiable for vibro-shivs, force pikes, lightsabers. High-speed optic visor; wires from auditory pickups → induction spur lower back (2 m EM field boost); backpack voltage generator. Cognitive matrix learns/exploits adversary style. Hand-crafted ≤4,000/yr; 275,000 credits. Lore: Vader owned several (destroyed in lightsaber drills → used modified Asps); space pirate Raskar fan of “no-holds-barred swordplay.”
**Schematics:** Front/Rear — High-speed Optic Visor, Auditory Sensors, Electromagnetic Field Generation Filaments, Vocabulator, Voltage Generator, Scoring Target, Induction Spur, Internal Gyroscope, Flexible Socket Joints, Blade Connection Socket, Flexisteel Dueling Rapier.

## Mark X Executioner — P160–161 (S092)
Arakyd; “reinforced durasteel destroyer”; 35,000 credits. Gladiatorial offshoot of military tech for “entertaining victors.” Weakness: small underside patch between treads (only unarmored; external cooling circuitry). Lore: psychotic warrior fraternities sparring; crime bosses e.g. Bwoh’l the Hutt executions; late Mirkovig Hirken (CSA viceprex) kept one at Stars’ End.
**Schematics:** Front — Combat Sensor Array, Flechette Canister, Neuronic Whip, Force Pike, Armored Chassis, Vibro-saw Blades, Flame Projector, Articulated Catch-cables (behind port), Cooling Circuitry, Heavy Tank Treads. Right Arm Radial Array Side — Spiked Club, Vibro-ax, Missile Launcher, Vibro-blade, Sonic Cannon & Blaster Rifle.
