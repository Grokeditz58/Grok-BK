# ONE OF A KIND — P196–204 (S110–)

## Vuffi Raa — P196–197 (S110)
Exotic alien construct from Unknown Regions; advanced extinct starfish-like species. Small information gatherer (vs creators’ massive sphere-droids). Pentagonal plate of polished chromite; 5 elongated tentacle limbs; single glowing red photoreceptor (UV + IR); limb tips = five-tentacled hands → near-microscopic subfingers; can shunt heat to extremities (illuminate rooms / light cigars); detach all 5 limbs + remote control. Companion to Lando Calrissian (pilot/mechanic); later taken back to home system by his kind.
**Schematics:** Top — Multi-spectrum Photoreceptor, Silver Chromite Body Shell, Fingers/Subfingers. Side — Vocabulator, Limb Release Point, Articulated Tentacles, Optic Sensor in Palm.

## Q9-X2 — P198–199 (S111)
IA experimental Q9 (based on R7); line disaster (systems failures + costly repairs under stress). Self-upgraded: low-power repulsor pads (hover); vocoder (Basic); detection package (molecular backtrack sniffer, residual heat-trend directionalizer, retractable sensor wands); powerful forward floodlight + 2 lamps; photography system w/ telephoto + physical print slot; standard grasper/arc welder/fire extinguisher. Owned by Ebrihim the Drall (1,250 Drallish crowns). Insolent/headstrong/egotistical; helped Han’s children escape imprisonment on Drall (Corellian crisis).
**Schematics:** Side/Front — Molecular Backtrack Sniffer, Heat Trend Directionalizer, Auditory Sensors, Floodlight, Vocabulater, Access Panels, Radar Eye, Retractable Sensor Wands (behind panel), Holographic Projector, Function Indicators, Head Rotation Point, Retractable Spotlight, Photography System (behind panel), System Ventilation, Repulsor Pads.

## 8t88 — P200–201 (S112)
Free self-reliant; contemptuous of everyone; top-notch interpreter + data broker. Modified 88-series bookkeeping from defunct Tiss’sharl co.; original head replaced with disproportionately small cranial unit; resents “disfigurement.” Genius uncovering secrets, cracking codes, archaic languages; exorbitant fees; amoral. Office on Nar Shaddaa. Employed by Dark Jedi Jerec; crossed Kyle Katarn — lost arm in shootout; decapitated by Katarn on Sulon (“decapitation won’t kill a droid, and 8t88 is unlikely to stay down for long”). Silver skeleton + hydraulic tubing; central scanner plate; “pocket” behind hinged torso panel.
**Schematics:** Front/Rear — Vocabulator, Infrared Sensor, Microscopic Imager, Scanner Plate, Holographic Projector/Recorder, Directionalized Audio Sensor, Secret Tightbeam Communicator, Broadband Communicator, Internal Wiring Harness, Recharge Socket, Storage Compartment (behind panel), Roto-actuators, Hydraulic Limbs, Tiss’sharl Talons.

## Gorm the Dissolver — P202–203 (S113)
Legendary terrifying cyborg bounty hunter; “walking jumble of organics and electronics.” Created by Arkanian Renegades (~50 yrs BBY) for mercenary cyborg army; few survivors. Parts from 6 alien species + 7 generations of droid electronics; 2.5 m. Battered Ithullian infantry helmet; left forearm Trandoshan. Originally recycling-droid appendage reducing matter to molecular paste (“The Dissolver”) — lost to Jedi; now custom repeater rifle, chest incendiary slugthrower, concealed molecular garrote. Once took 50,000-credit Jabba bounty for hunter Zardra.
**Schematics:** Front/Rear — Targeting Range Finder, Ithullian Infantry Helmet, Molecular Garrote retracted, Armor-plated Shoulder Joint (Bothan), Photoreceptors, Vocabulator, Arkanian Electric Dynamo, Calcified Tentacles (unidentifiable), Left Limb (Trandoshan), Incendiary Slugthrower Shells, Gunfighter’s Belt, Gladiator Droid Durasteel Plating, Organic Leg Components (Sijee).

## Widget — P204–205 (S114)
“Walking toolbox” pieced from spare parts by owner Mora on remote M’haeli; built early teens after failed cognitive-processor inits. Stumpy legs, wide base, splayed gait, low CoG; “scrunched” head under cymbal-shaped metal pan; 2 red photoreceptors; flawed vocabulator (stutter under stress); twin power cables/antennae → back generator. Arms: dozens of machinist tools — right: magnifier, spotlight luma, wire cutters; left: laser welder, gripper clamps, computer-access link. Post-Yavin: assisted Leia + local M’haeli insurgency vs Imp Gov. Grigor (rescue prisoners + retrieve coded data).
**Schematics:** Front — Protective Head-pan, Photoreceptors, Voice Slot, Magnifier/Luma/Wire Cutters (inside arm), Diagnostic Input Panel / Function Indicators, Armored Torso, Clamps/Scomp-link/Gripper/Laser Welder (inside arm), Insulated Sheathing. Rear — Power Cables/Signal Antennae, Power Generator. ~waist-high vs human.
