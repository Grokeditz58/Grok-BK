# ASTROMECH — P6–15 (scan 015–019 approx)

## P2 and R1 — P6–7 (S015)
**Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton. **Illos:** P2 perspective + R1 tall unipod; Front P2 / Side R1 schematics w/ human silhouette (R1 taller than human).
- **P2:** Massive/cumbersome/sluggish prototype for Old Republic merchant fleet; rotating head dome + retractable manipulators; tech-infringement lawsuit killed line; video screen or computer-link comms.
- **R1:** Evolution of P2; reused tall black Mark II Reactor Drone shell; Intellex III; first IA astromech for single-jump hyperspace calc; introduced Droidspeak; criticized treaded-unipod locomotion failures.
**Schematic labels (paraphrase/stats):** P2 — Heavy Grasper Arm, Function Indicators, Auditory Sensors, Radar Eye, Sensor Package, Third Tread retracted, Power Cells, Motorized Treads, Buzz Saw, Mainframe Connection Socket, Folding Work Arms. R1 — Mark II Reactor Drone Shell, Sensor Package, Access Panels, Comlink Antenna, Treaded Unipod, Binary Acoustic Signaler, Video Display Screen, Retractable Manipulator Repair Arms, Rotation Point.

## R2 — P8–9 (S016)
**Pull quote (ad):** “NO JOB IS OVER THIS LITTLE GUY’S HEAD.” — Ad slogan from the R2 product launch. [VERIFY CROP if quoting verbatim]
- Record-breaking IA phenomenon; waist-high fit for X-wing/Y-wing sockets vs clunky P2/R1; Intellex IV (>700 spacecraft configs); sensors: transceiver, EM, heat, motion, life-form.
- Standard kit: 2 manipulator arms, electric arc welder, circular saw, holo projector, internal cargo, fire extinguisher. Mods: underwater props, laser pointers, zero-gee jets, inflatable rafts.
- Personality: obliging/quick-witted/sincere; headstrong without memory wipes. IA avoids R2-D2 heroics in ads (NR politics).
**Schematics:** Side/Front with Grasping Arm Compartment, EM Field Sensor, Holo Projector, Auditory Sensors, Third Tread, Motorized Treads, Processor State Indicator, Radar Eye, Access Panels, Data Card Input, Infrared Receptor, Function Indicators, Spacecraft Linkage Data Slot, Computer Interface/Lubricant Arms, Interference Pulse Stabilizers, Spacecraft Linkage & Repair Arms, Charge Arm, Loudspeaker, System Ventilation, Recharge Coupling, Power Cells.

## R3 and R4 — P10–11 (S017)
- **R3:** High-tech gov/military; transparent plastex dome (superior sensor range); Intellex V + Old Republic naval DB; OR bought 125 million for capital ships; Empire used on Star Destroyers/Death Stars. Illos: clear-dome R3 + tapered-cone R4.
- **R4:** Budget Outer Rim/low-income; no video screen / mini fire extinguisher; Intellex VI for common repulsorlifts not starfighters; one hyperspace jump in active memory; rugged → Rebel bases/hangars.
**Schematics:** R3 Front — Plastex Head Dome, Function Indicators, Sensor Wands, Auditory Sensors, Intellex V, Radar Eye, EM Field Sensor, Capital Ship Linkage Arms, etc. R4 Front — Auditory Sensors, Radar Eye, Access Panels, Holographic Computer, Grasping/Repair Arms, Heat Exhaust (vs R3 Polarity Sink).

## R5 — P12–13 (S018)
IA’s “first true astromech flop”; no clear market; cheap craftsmanship; behavioral glitches → caustic personalities; overheating, jammed servos, blown motivators. Rebels bought bulk cheap; engineers modded for up to 10 nav-coordinate sets; tall “flower-pot” heads = easier targets. Lore: Wedge’s R5-D2 “Mynock” memory-wiped → R5-G8 “Gate” (screeching personality).
**Schematics:** Side/Front; Holo Projector, Broadband Antenna/Receiver, EM Field Sensor, Infrared Receptor, 3 Photoreceptors, Auditory Sensor, etc.; human silhouette ~hip height.

## R6 and R7 — P14–15 (S019)
Post-Endor IA reputation repair after R5 flop; NR contract for E-wing droid.
- **R6:** Gap-filler while R7 developed; perfected R2 (bugs fixed); pleasant personality; ≤12 hyperspace jumps; cheaper than R2, costlier than R4/R5. Illos: dome R6 + angular/faceted R7 w/ triangular primary eye. Icons: NR + IA logos (not Empire).
- **R7:** Most sophisticated astromech; IA + FreiTek for E-wing; 15 hyperspace coords; armored vs power surges/ion; augments E-wing flight; poor with other fighters. Lore: Luke briefly owned E-wing + R7-T1, never comfortable.
**Schematics (Front):** R6 — Charge Arm, Ventilation, Spacecraft Linkage & Repair Arms, Loudspeaker, Computer Interface/Lubricant Arms, Infrared Receptor, Holo Projector, Radar Eye, Auditory Sensors, Access Panels, Recharge Coupling, Power Cells, Motorized Treads. R7 — E-wing Linkage Arms behind panels, Primary E-wing Linkage Slot, Flight-performance Monitors, Grasping Arm, Function Indicators, triangular Radar Eye, Heat Exhaust, Systems Diagnostic Input Receptors.
