- Keldabe: a population of about four million; "A few Mandalorians are an army" (l.9488–9490). Udelen smells like Kamino sea spray (l.9536). "A war is coming" (l.9524).

- Aboard: they covertly collect organic samples (l.9726–9750). Amphistaff (l.9736); vonduun crab armor "Blasters won't penetrate" (l.9768). Fett bargains (l.9784–9800).
