## Ch. 1 (l.240–749)
- Epigraph: Lorrd Artifact knotted-tassel prophecies ("He will strengthen himself through sacrifice ... He will immortalize his love") (l.242–262).
- Ben in the Sith meditation sphere: no controls, thinks commands; ship speaks in his mind, judges him "idiot pilot" (l.266–284). He dropped Shaker and Kiara on Drewwa (l.280). Destroyed one pursuing ship (l.286). Can't track Jacen, who is permanently hidden in the Force (l.318).
- LORE/FEAT (sphere): drops out of hyperspace, finds Jacen/Lumiya on its own (l.322–332).
- Jacen on *Anakin Solo*: senses Ben (55 kg, 14) across a corridor around the Perlemian (l.338–346); senses Lumiya aboard "as a swirling eddy" (l.352); magnifies his Force presence so Ben can find him (l.352–364).
- Sphere: 10 m veined sphere with wing-vanes (l.392, 424). Ben hands over the Amulet of Kalara (l.434–436). Ben: Dyur set him up; "I blew up the ship that was targeting me" (l.446). "It seems to have whatever weapons you want" (l.450). Jacen had told the Skywalkers he didn't know where Ben was (l.458).
- Lumiya: "A Sith meditation sphere"; it "says it's found me" (l.474–484). LORE: Jacen is trackable by his "wake" — the Force disturbance at Gilatter and the impressions of those around him — even while concealed (l.490–496). Lumiya takes the sphere (l.500). She knows whether she meant Ben to die but not whom Jacen must kill; "soon, and close" (l.506–516).
- Keldabe, Mandalore clan meeting: Fett dying, wants a cure and to find Sintas Vel (l.532–540). Mandalore lost ~1.5 million to the Vong (l.592), a third of the population (l.556). Fett: Mandalore neutral; individuals may sell their services (l.616). Fett can't speak Mando'a (l.590). "Graad" in purple armor with a brown shoulder plate argues for consolidation; Fett asks two million to return (l.618–664). Beviin stands in for Fett, "a complete savage with a beskad" (l.678–680). Fett: Jacen's "time will come. Leave him to me" (l.694).
- Fett and Mirta: ~65 days hunting the clone "Skirata" (l.706–712). Vong poisoned Mandalore's soil (l.720–724). Beviin is Fett's chosen successor (l.744). To find a bounty hunter, "You hire him" (l.748).

"The three individuals who were running the war dared not turn their backs on one another" (l.960).

Sphere: Ben "Not like us"; Alema "less like us than the one who follows" (l.1000–1006). Lumiya: Ben lacked ruthlessness; sphere "instrumental in Jacen's future" (l.1010). LORE: Jacen must "immortalize his love — to kill it" (l.1024). She considers Jaina, parents, Ben, Luke, Mara, herself (l.1026–1042).

- Mara and Kyp at a juice bar: shadow Council forming (l.1226). Mara: "I'm going to kill Lumiya" (l.1210); Luke "favor[s] taking people alive" (l.1218). Ben lands at the civilian port (l.1246). Mara learned to kill the Emperor's enemies by Ben's age (l.1272).

## Ch. 4 (l.1958–2739)
- Niathal's memo authorizes Jacen "any means necessary" on procurement (l.1960–1970).
- HM-3 legal droid: drafts the Legislative and Regulations Statute Amendment, devolving order-making powers to "appropriate persons" without Senate approval; worded generically for any administrative procedure (l.1976–2034). Omas, Niathal and Jacen are "effectively a triumvirate" under emergency powers (l.2042). LORE: Palpatine took power by manipulation, not the dark side (l.2050). Jacen: "can I use the law that lets me change laws to change that law itself?" (l.2062). Procurement chief Tav Vello (l.2066). Jacen can co-opt civilian staff for research anywhere (l.2092–2100).
- Jacen reassigns purchasing officer Biris Te Gaf to the front-line ship with the most cannon misfires under Emergency Measures Act art. 5 sub. C-27 (l.2112–2128). FEAT (minor): reads the room by "close-range ripples in the Force" and Force-enhanced hearing (l.2116, 2122). Lekauf: "Your grandfather would have done the same" (l.2132). HM-3 confirms the amendment can change itself (l.2154–2158). "I can bypass the Senate" (l.2164).
- Kuat City, Tekshar Falls Casino (casino inside a 500 m waterfall; l.2204). Fett once saved the Kuat shipyards (l.2174). Hacks the Kuat police database from his HUD (l.2210). Owns shares in the place (l.2232–2234). FEAT (Fett): fibercord loop around Fraig, over the rail "in two seconds," dangles him 15 m and more (l.2352–2376). FEAT (Mirta): head-butts and stabs the Hamadryas bodyguard, jams the doors (l.2354). Lead: Twi'lek family Himar (l.2374). Jet escape; unseen helper silently drops pursuing speeders (l.2410–2456). The gray-armored Mandalorian (Verpine rifle, a strill pinning Mirta) is aboard *Slave I*: "It's little Bob'ika ... my brother was shoving your head down the 'freshers" (l.2462–2486).
- GAG briefing: shift to Confederation professionals; close protection of Omas and ministers; tapping Senators' comlinks under the Emergency Measures Act (l.2514–2532). Shevu unhappy (l.2530). 967 Commando has had no fatalities (l.2558). Jacen on leadership: officers must be "sufficiently separate" to spend lives (l.2556–2560).
- FEAT/LORE (Ben): Jacen teaches Force-hiding — "break the container," see yourself as atoms, "Force camouflage"; Ben dissolves on the first try, "full marks for technique," and has to tear himself out (l.2584–2616).
- Jacen shows Ben intel that Gejjen planned the Tenel Ka hit (l.2636–2656) and orders him to assassinate Gejjen, with Shevu and Lekauf as backup (l.2664–2688). Refuses to drop the Solos' warrant (l.2682). Alone, Jacen weeps: "I can't kill him" (l.2700–2738). LORE: the Sith "deal in anything but absolutes" (l.2718).

Fett: Jaing tracked Grievous; "Master assassin, sniper" (l.2906). FEAT (Jaing): ambushed Mirta unseen and silently downed pursuing speeders with a Verpine rifle (l.2484, 2888). Jacen plans to take down Omas "with a sheet of flimsi" (l.3296). "They deserved to be ruled by the Sith" (l.3304).

Nineteen frigates drop in; their heat-seekers ignore chaff (l.3390–3420). 12–0 in frigates in under 20 minutes (l.3428–3444). LORE: Mon Cals and Quarren perceive the whole battlefield in combat (l.3426). Niathal plans an ultimatum to Omas and needs Jacen's backing (l.3460).
- GAG monitoring center under Captain Heol Girdun; automated voice-recognition tapping of Senators and staff (l.3464–3486). Ben practices hiding in the Force whenever he can (l.3492). Intercept: Gejjen's voice (l.3494–3500).
- The intercept: Gejjen offers Omas a cease-fire if "Niathal has to go. Jacen Solo has to go," with a Corellian opt-out clause and Centerpoint made inoperative; "there's take out, and take out"; they agree to meet on Vulpter (l.3502–3536). The GAG is tapping the Chief of State's secure line (l.3504).
- Jacen and Niathal: Jacen proposes amending the Emergency Measures Act so the GAG can detain heads of state and politicians and seize assets via the Treasury Orders Act (l.3612). Niathal: "I put my allegiance to the GA before my allegiance to an individual" (l.3632). Omas, given an opening, doesn't mention Gejjen: "his fate [was] sealed" (l.3692–3696). Jacen proposes "Regime change," a duumvirate; Niathal: "Military coup" (l.3752–3764). LORE: Jacen senses bugs in a room (l.3748). Plan: Jacen takes care of Gejjen, Omas under house arrest (l.3770–3778). Jacen calls Lumiya (l.3810).
