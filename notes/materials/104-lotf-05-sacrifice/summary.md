# Legacy of the Force 5: Sacrifice: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-04 CST. Full hand-read, l.1–13386. Citation "SA ch. N (l.x)". Detail is in sa-01 to sa-07.
- Author: Karen Traviss (title page; About the Author, l.10519–10521). This matches the inventory.

## Plot
- **Jacen's thread (ch. 1–7, 12, 14–16, 18–24):**
  - Ben returns in the Sith meditation sphere and hands Jacen the Amulet of Kalara. Lumiya takes the sphere (l.338–500).
  - Lumiya says Jacen must "immortalize his love — to kill it." He refuses to kill Allana and decides "It has to be Ben" (l.1024, 1622–1642).
  - A legal droid drafts a statute amendment that lets Jacen and Niathal change administrative law without the Senate. It passes (l.1976–2164, 3288–3294).
  - He sends Ben to assassinate Gejjen, hoping it will endanger him (l.2664–2688, 3878). A GAG intercept shows that Omas is negotiating with Gejjen (l.3502–3536).
  - At 0001 he arrests Omas, a "bloodless ... military coup," and Niathal makes him joint Chief of State (l.6044–6156, 6252–6274).
  - Mara confronts him and gives him an ultimatum (l.7198–7244). He visits Tenel Ka and Allana on Hapes (l.7708–7752).
  - Mara ambushes him. They fight a StealthX dogfight and then a duel in Kavan's tunnels, and he kills her with a poison dart (ch. 19–21).
  - He reinterprets the prophecy: what he loved and killed was Ben's devotion to him. He takes the name **Darth Caedus** (l.9102–9164).
- **Ben (ch. 1–2, 7–11, 13–14, 17, 19, 22–23):**
  - He trains as a sniper (95–97%) and kills Gejjen on Vulpter (l.4110–4136, 4370, 4938–4996).
  - Lekauf takes the rifle, claims the kill and blows himself up to cover Ben (l.5324–5418).
  - Ben confesses to Mara (l.5958–5978). Hidden in the Force, he overhears Jacen and Lumiya (l.6406–6464). He teaches Mara to hide in the Force (l.6678–6680).
  - He hunts the sphere and talks it down from firing on him (l.7864–7928). He finds Mara's body, and Jacen finds him despite his hiding (l.8662–8688).
  - He tells Luke that it wasn't Lumiya: "I was right next to her at the moment Mom died" (l.8958–8964).
- **Mara (Prologue, ch. 2–5, 8, 10–11, 14–21):**
  - She tracks Lumiya's "wake," fights her at Hesperidium and stabs her through the chest, but the sphere saves Lumiya (l.5018–5218).
  - She decides Jacen must die (l.7170). She tails him to Hapes and tries to lure Lumiya's sphere into Hapan fire (l.7630–7676).
  - She cripples and buries Jacen on Kavan, then dies to his poison dart. "Ben was the last word she ever spoke" (l.8068–8210).
- **Luke (ch. 3, 8, 20, 22–23):**
  - He regrets taking Lumiya's hand (l.1760–1774) and finds her decoy boots (l.4474–4482).
  - He feels Mara die (l.8228). He lures Lumiya, forces her sphere down and beheads her, Lumiya's "suicide-by-Skywalker" (l.8566–8656, 8852).
  - He learns he "killed the wrong— Sith" (l.8974–8976).
- **Fett and Mandalore (ch. 1, 3–5, 7, 9, 11, 14–16, 22–24):**
  - Fett is dying. With Mirta he hunts the clone Jaing Skirata, who takes a blood sample (l.2866–3066).
  - A new beskar lode is found (l.4238–4250). Fett forms a Roche–Verpine pact (l.5478–5500), and the Bes'uliik fighters are built (l.4708–4780, 6866–6880).
  - Venku (Kad'ika) brings a marrow cure: "The cell degeneration's stopped" (l.6532–6536, 7340–7378).
  - Gotab's psychometry reads Mirta's heart-of-fire (l.8702–8790). Sintas Vel turns out to be alive in carbonite on Phaeda (l.9038–9080).
  - Fett sends Han beskar gear "With deepest sympathy" (l.8546–8554).

## Top feats
1. **Mara nearly kills Jacen on Kavan (SA ch. 20–21):**
   - Her Force-assisted kick ruins his right knee (l.8068). She weakens the tunnel arches by Force-shaking them, holds them up by Force pressure, then drops both sections and buries him (l.8078–8106).
   - When he breaks out, she keeps coming through his Force pushes: "She shouldn't have been able to get near him. He had total mastery" (l.8152).
   - She deflects all four poison darts "in a blur of blue light" (l.8154) and buries her shoto under his collarbone (l.8166).
   - She loses only to his Ben-face illusion and a hand-driven dart (l.8172–8176).
2. **Jacen survives the cave-in and becomes a Sith Lord (SA ch. 21, 23):**
   - With "one last surge of the Force" he deflects a point-blank blaster bolt and blasts the rubble off "like a detonation," knocking Mara flat (l.8136).
   - On the *Ocean* he shows Sith battle awareness. He reads the hidden corvette flotilla and the Destroyer's moves before the sensors do, which makes "battle meditation look like a finger painting" (l.8880–8898).
3. **Luke kills Lumiya (SA ch. 20, 22):**
   - He finds Mara in the Hapes Cluster by Force-reading the holocharts (l.7990–7994).
   - He tractor-forces Lumiya's sphere onto a mesa (l.8620–8622) and fights her lightwhip with his lightsaber alone, no shoto (l.8630).
   - He presses in "like a battering ram" to the mesa edge, catches her when she slips, and then beheads her in a single arc (l.8646–8656).

## Other feats
- **Ben:**
  - Kills Gejjen with a "textbook head shot" at about 100 m, with guards crossing the line of fire (l.4938–4996).
  - Hits the 5 cm zone 95% of the time after a couple of hours, then scores 97% (l.4118, 4370).
  - Dissolves into Force camouflage on his first try (l.2584–2616).
  - Hides from Jacen and Lumiya in the same room (l.6406–6464).
  - Counts six people in a closed room (l.4874–4876) and talks the Sith sphere out of firing (l.7864–7928).
- **Mara:**
  - At Hesperidium: a 5 m Force-leap, a Force-boosted tackle into Lumiya's durasteel legs and a lightsaber stab through Lumiya's chest (l.5154–5164).
  - Mind-tricks two joggers (l.5184–5188).
  - Learns to hide in the Force in hours (l.6680) and ambushes Jacen unsensed (l.7198–7200). Places Ben to within 3 m by transponder (l.2816–2822).
- **Jacen:**
  - Fires eight proton torpedoes at the sphere (l.7846–7856).
  - Senses Ben across a corridor and Lumiya as "a swirling eddy" (l.338–352). Senses bugs in a room (l.3748).
  - Turns CSF officers away with light Force influence (l.6930).
  - Finds a hidden Ben on Kavan (l.8676–8688).
- **Lumiya:**
  - Force-influences a speaker in the Senate gallery (l.3268–3272).
  - Survives a lightsaber through the chest (l.5164–5180).
  - Is "more machine than organic"; a vibroblade scrapes metal in her thigh (l.1096–1098).
- **Sith meditation sphere:**
  - Jumps with no inertia (l.982–992) and finds Jacen and Lumiya on its own (l.322–332).
  - Extrudes a cable to throw Mara (l.5168–5176, 6626–6628) and grows wings (l.5180).
  - Absorbs eight proton torpedoes with no explosion and no wreckage (l.7846–7856). Its magnetic accelerator breaches Mara's StealthX (l.7834–7840).
- **Fett:**
  - Loops fibercord around Fraig and dangles him off a 15 m rail in two seconds (l.2352–2376).
  - Takes a bone-marrow injection into the sternum without analgesics (l.6780–6818).
- **Jaing Skirata:** ambushes Mirta unseen and silently drops pursuing speeders with a Verpine rifle (l.2410–2486, 2888).
- **Beviin (recalled):** swung his beskad so hard into a Vong officer that he had to stand on him to pull it free (l.4228).

## Anti-feats and atrocities
- **Jacen:**
  - Kills Mara with an illusion of Ben's face and a poison dart, then tells her "No healing trance" (l.8172–8184).
  - Stages a military coup and arrests Omas (l.6044–6156). Plans to use Ben's assassination mission to get Ben killed (l.3878).
  - Mara ruins his knee and buries him (l.8068–8106). He fails to sense her in the Senate lobby (l.7198–7200).
  - His proton torpedoes don't hurt the sphere (l.7846–7856).
- **Ben:**
  - Assassinates Gejjen (l.4938–4996): "I did the right thing for the wrong reasons" (l.7426).
  - Fails to stay hidden from Jacen on Kavan (l.8688).
- **Luke:**
  - Kills the wrong Sith, as Lumiya's last manipulation "broke something deep" (l.8974–8992).
  - Can't feel Ben or Mara (l.7972–7988).
- **Mara:**
  - Fails to kill Lumiya and is thrown by an unsensed sphere cable (l.5168–5196).
  - Is caught by Ben's face and the dart (l.8172–8176).
- **Lumiya:** stages Mara's distraction and "suicide-by-Skywalker" (l.3884–3922, 8338–8346, 8852). Set up the Ziost attack on Ben (l.3258–3260).
- **Lekauf:** dies by suicide to cover Ben (l.5372–5418).

## Lore
- **Prophecy:** the knotted-tassel prophecies of the Lorrd Artifact say "He will immortalize his love" (l.242–262). Lumiya reads it as kill (l.1024). Jacen reinterprets it: he killed Ben's love for him (l.9102–9134).
- **Sith meditation sphere:** a Sith *training* vessel. "Once I fought. Now I educate and protect apprentices ... I used to be for Masters at war" (l.7892–7924). It treats Ben and Mara as "the two with my flame" (l.7498–7518). It must not be targeted (l.7864–7886).
- **Force-hiding:** "break the container," Force camouflage (l.2584–2616). The sphere can find Jacen even when he hides (l.7318). A hidden Jacen can still be tracked by his "wake" (l.490–496).
- **Sith Master:** only a full Sith Master has "the complete Sith ability to see all the pieces in the game" (l.1128). Jacen gains it after Mara's death (l.8880–8898).
- **Beskar:** "the toughest metal known to science. Even lightsabers had trouble with it" (l.4250). There is a new lode north of Enceri (l.4238–4248). Lumiya's lightwhip is studded with beskar fragments (l.5156).
- **Bes'uliik:** 15 m by 8 m, 0.4 hyperdrive, stealth hull, micronized beskar skin (l.4708–4780, 6866–6880).
- **Mandalorian culture:** manda is the "oversoul" (l.5600); the Resol'nare (l.8494); Venku/Kad'ika (l.6558–6562); Mandalorian population figures (l.556, 592, 5588–5594, 8482–8490).
- **Name "Caedus":** contributed by Tawnia Poland in the "Darth Who" contest (l.10515).
