## Ch. 13 (l.6166–6487)
- Market news: ISE suspended; temporary martial law; MandalMotors and Roche shares up (l.6170).
- Niathal and G'Sil: Niathal privately calls it a coup; "there's always Fett" as a check on Jacen (l.6198). Bursa-among-nuna analogy for Mandalorians (l.6204). Fewer than three million on Mandalore (l.6220). Senate speech: temporary, no curfew, no censorship; the Omas footage is shown; she asks Jacen to be joint Chief of State (l.6252–6274). Haruun Kal delegate: "You got there first" (l.6272). Alliance Intel folded under the GAG with Girdun in charge; Omas's office sealed (l.6284–6292). Niathal: "putting citizens in camps and killing prisoners is not the done thing" (l.6300).
- Council: Kyp names Jacen ("Jacen, Jacen, Jacen") (l.6342). Mara: "There's rights, and there's right" (l.6370). Luke will see Niathal (l.6374).
- FEAT (Ben): hiding in the Force while walking; overhears **Jacen and Lumiya in Jacen's office** — Lumiya was the decoy; the "daughter" story was a cover; Jacen "mind-rubbing Ben about Nelani"; Lumiya: Ben "can't ever be your apprentice. Get rid of him"; Omas to go to house arrest (l.6406–6464). Neither of them senses Ben (l.6424–6426). Ben goes to find Mom (l.6484).

## Ch. 14 (l.6488–6759)
- Fett is visited by **Venku**, in motley armor of dead and living relatives (l.6496–6522). He gives Fett a vial to be injected into the bone marrow (l.6532–6536). An old armored Mando: "Su'cuy, Mand'alor, gar shabuir" (l.6546). Venku = **Kad'ika**, "Uncle Boba," with a Fett face (l.6558–6562).
- Mara and Ben in the tapcaf: Ben has near-perfect recall (l.6578). Mara now sees Jacen as an **accomplice**, not a victim (l.6582). Ben tells her about Ziost and the sphere: "it spoke to me ... if you visualize what you want it to do, it does it. It sticks out parts of itself and forms them into cannons" (l.6598–6624). Mara realizes the "droid" was the living Sith ship extruding a cable (l.6626–6628). Ben gave the ship to Jacen (l.6640). Mara tells Ben to "behave like a coward," report sick and stay away from Jacen (l.6654). FEAT (Mara): learns to hide in the Force from Ben in a couple of hours, holding it for a minute or two (l.6678–6680). Mara: "you're my heart" (l.6690).
- Jacen at home: "HE WILL IMMORTALIZE HIS LOVE" written in steam (l.6714). Nusito-pup analogy (l.6726–6728). He touches Allana in the Force and she seems to reach back (l.6730–6734). Vision of Ben sobbing "It's too high a price" (l.6740). Jacen's armory: lightsaber, blaster, a box of poisons and pathogens for darts (l.6752). Muses that Niathal "demonstrated an excellent grasp of the rule of two" (l.6756).

## Ch. 15 (l.6760–7189)
- Jacen epigraph: "tough on the causes of chaos" (l.6762).
- FEAT/endurance (Fett): bone-marrow injection into the sternum by a veterinarian, no analgesics (l.6780–6818). Venku is a field medic: "they say I have a healing touch" (l.6834). He takes an interest in Mirta's heart-of-fire (l.6836–6844).
- Bes'uliik prototype with micronized beskar skin flies over Keldabe; a solid-beskar skin would make it "invulnerable, but heavy" (l.6866–6880). Mandalore sector informally controls Roche as a protectorate (l.6902).
- Jacen walks among FREE OMAS protesters and shows them the sealed office for the HNE droid (l.6910–6950). Light Force influence turns away CSF officers (l.6930). Omas is sent home (l.6928). Niathal: "slick and oily" (l.6960). Murkhana Senator Nav Ekhat: the "bugs-and-thugs show" (l.6988).
- Jacen floats sending a GA squadron and frigate to Roche to scout the Mandalorian fighters; Niathal: "you will not commit fleet resources like that without discussing the matter with me" (l.7020–7030). Jacen's view of Mandalorians as a destabilizing "third element" (l.7050). Jacen takes 48 hours off (l.7082). He means to handle the Mandalorians as Vader did (l.7088).
- Drall, Luminous Gardens Spa: Leia and Mara. Corellia is run by a triumvirate (l.7096). Leia: Lumiya "could never make him act totally against his will" (l.7156). **Mara decides she must kill Jacen** (l.7170).

## Ch. 16 (l.7190–7401)
- Epigraph: Bothawui offers 20 million credits a month for a Mandalorian fleet and Bes'uliik exclusivity (l.7192).
- FEAT (Mara): ambushes Jacen in the Senate lobby; he doesn't sense her because she is hiding in the Force (l.7198–7200). Mara's ultimatum: come to the Temple and be "deprogrammed," or "Drop this Sith garbage now, or take what's coming" (l.7232–7240). Jacen: "Good night, Aunt Mara" (l.7244). "Jacen was going to die" (l.7254).
- Lumiya waits at Jacen's apartment: "the more flesh you sacrifice, the less heir to its limits you become" (l.7262). LORE: Jacen "could meditate in the middle of a hurricane" (l.7292). His apartment is increasingly austere (l.7312). The sphere can find Jacen when he hides in the Force (l.7318). Lumiya follows him (l.7320).
- Fett's nightmare about Jango's bones (l.7326–7336). Cured: "The cell degeneration's stopped" (vet Hayca Mekket; the needle was for banthas) (l.7340–7378). Another thirty years, maybe more (l.7392). Fett's HUD can "see through solid walls and eavesdrop kilometers away" (l.7358). The only death that would make him feel good is Jacen's: "Better that you rot than die" (l.7352–7354).

## Ch. 17 (l.7402–7605)
- Jaing epigraph on Taun We (l.7404).
- Ben at Shevu's (girlfriend Shula); "I did the right thing for the wrong reasons" (l.7426). Ben can Force-dry dishes (l.7438). The sphere calls to Ben telepathically (l.7444–7448). Ben signs out an unmarked GAG long-range speeder/shuttle to hunt it, leaving the vibroblade (the transponder) at Shevu's (l.7454–7458).
- Mara packs for a mission and leaves a note: "Gone hunting for a few days. Don't be mad at me, farmboy" (l.7462–7490).
- In the sphere: it was buried on Ziost; Alema is "the broken one"; it calls Mara and Ben "The two with my flame" and says it "stopped her fighting" (l.7498–7518). Jacen goes to Hapes with an entry code (l.7532–7542). **Lumiya feels Jacen's emotional release; the ship says "loves ... Two"; the soft toy in his hands — "Oh, Jacen, you had a child with Tenel Ka"** (l.7552–7596). She concludes Allana must be the sacrifice and weeps (l.7598–7604).

## Ch. 18 (l.7606–7795)
- HNE: Roche gives Murkhana 24 hours (l.7608).
- Mara in a StealthX at Hapes. Lumiya's sphere sits in an asteroid cluster (l.7630). She bluffs Hapan Fleet Ops as close protection for the "Chief of State," reports the sphere, and authorizes them to "neutralize" it (l.7656–7676). Jacen landed at the Fountain Palace compound (l.7664).
- Ben in a GAG shuttle loops via Taanab and maps the sphere's position by its "silent voice" (l.7686–7700).
- Fountain Palace: Allana names the stuffed tauntaun "Jacen" (l.7708–7718). No sign at Hapes that he must kill them (l.7722). Tenel Ka: "lonely at the top" (l.7742). Jacen's belt: blaster, lightsaber, vibroblade, toxin darts (l.7742). He fears Luke and Mara learning of Allana more than the Hapan nobles (l.7752). In the StealthX he senses something dark following; the stars wink out; a landing light floods his cockpit: "Game over, Lumiya." Mara: "You bet it is" (l.7758–7794).
