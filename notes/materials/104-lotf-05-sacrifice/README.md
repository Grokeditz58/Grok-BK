Character quick bios:
- **Jacen Solo / Darth Caedus:**
  - GAG colonel and Lumiya's apprentice. Drafts the law that bypasses the Senate (l.1976–2164) and arrests Omas in a coup (l.6044–6156).
  - Sends Ben to kill Gejjen (l.2664–2688). Has a secret daughter, Allana (l.7552–7596).
  - Kills Mara on Kavan (l.8172–8210), gains Sith battle awareness (l.8880–8898) and takes the name Caedus (l.9164).
- **Ben Skywalker:**
  - 14, a GAG junior officer. Learns Force camouflage (l.2584–2616) and kills Gejjen with a sniper shot (l.4938–4996).
  - Overhears Jacen and Lumiya (l.6406–6464) and talks the sphere down (l.7864–7928). Knows Lumiya didn't kill Mara (l.8958–8964).
- **Mara Jade Skywalker:**
  - Hunts Lumiya and stabs her through the chest (l.5154–5164). Learns Force-hiding from Ben (l.6680).
  - Gives Jacen an ultimatum (l.7232–7240). Buries Jacen and nearly kills him, then dies (l.8068–8210).
- **Luke Skywalker:** Grand Master. Finds Mara via the holocharts (l.7990–7994). Beheads Lumiya (l.8646–8656) and learns he killed the wrong Sith (l.8974–8976).
- **Lumiya:**
  - Dark Lady of the Sith, mostly cybernetic (l.1096–1098). Takes the Sith sphere (l.500) and acts as Jacen's decoy (l.3884–3922).
  - Learns of Allana (l.7596). Engineers her own "suicide-by-Skywalker" (l.8338–8346, 8852).
- **Boba Fett:** Mandalore. Dying, then cured by Venku's marrow vial (l.6532–6536, 7340–7378). Backs the beskar rearmament (l.4258–4284). Learns Sintas Vel is alive in carbonite (l.9038–9080).
- **Mirta Gev:** Fett's granddaughter, part Kiffar (l.1448). Fights at the Tekshar Falls Casino (l.2354) and swears to kill Jacen (l.8540).
- **Jaing Skirata / Venku (Kad'ika) / Gotab:**
  - Jaing is a Null ARC sniper with the strill Mird (l.2866–2930).
  - Venku is a field medic with a "healing touch" (l.6834). Gotab is a psychometrist (l.8702–8812).
- **Goran Beviin / Medrit Vasur / Dinua / Novoc Vevut / Ghes Orade:** Fett's chosen successor and his clan (l.744, 4210–4230, 5624–5642).
- **Jori Lekauf / Lon Shevu:** GAG men on the Gejjen job. Lekauf blows himself up to cover Ben (l.5372–5418).
- **Cha Niathal / Cal Omas / Dur Gejjen:**
  - Niathal becomes co-Chief of State (l.6252–6274).
  - Omas is arrested (l.6140), and Gejjen is assassinated (l.4996).
- **Others:** Tenel Ka and Allana, Leia, Han, Jaina, Kyp, Corran, Cilghal and Alema Rar (Dramatis Personae, l.136–174).
