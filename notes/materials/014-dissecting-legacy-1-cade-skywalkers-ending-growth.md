## Thesis

Across *Monster* (Legacy #43–46), *Extremes* (#48–50) and *Legacy: War*, Cade resolves his inner conflicts one by one and moves toward the light side. Ant lists them:

- he ends the fear and lies with Deliah;
- he forgives his mother;
- he (probably) stops blaming himself;
- he accepts that "love burns brighter";
- he accepts his destiny;
- he forms a plan;
- he believes he will kill Krayt, "guaranteed".

That growth is said to explain Talon's warning that Cade is "STRONGER... THAN HE WAS" and the recap's "new mastery of the Force". Ant puts the source of the new strength in the light side. In his words: "But powerful enough to beat Krayt? No, because he's still refusing to fully embrace the light and accept that he is a Jedi. Once he does so, Krayt goes down, too."

## Arguments (numbered)

1. **Monster takeaway (A): the light side is a new and stronger source.** Maladi says the dark side unlocks Cade's "true power". Wolf calls that a "lie". Cade heals Deliah, saying "love burns brighter", and later tells Wolf he was "right". Ant ties this to George Lucas and the *Fact Files* on Vader being filled with the "super energy" of love. He cites no page or scan for that.
2. **Monster takeaway (B): resolving conflict strengthens the will.** Maladi said Cade's "will is weak". Now he "knows what he is" ("I am what the Force has made me").
3. **Extremes: from no plan to a destiny and a plan.** Luke's spirit says Cade has "no plan". After Krayt's call, Cade's vision ends in "black nothingness". He then accepts "one final round" and "a job to do". The recap says he devises a "plan to draw Krayt into single combat".
4. **War: forgiveness.** At Kol's urging ("ONE OF THE GREATEST LESSONS OF THE LIGHT SIDE IS FORGIVENESS…"), Cade forgives and heals Morrigan Corde (War p53–55).
5. **Taivas: why Cade nearly dies to two Sith Troopers** (War p88–90). Ant gives three reasons:
   - (A) fatigue, since the trooper says "YOU TIRE";
   - (B) dark-side emotion he has not fully embraced, which hurts focus; when he says "ENOUGH" and gives in, he wins at once;
   - (C) recklessness from a belief that he "can't die here".
6. **Grief and self-blame resolved.** Jariah tells Cade "IT AIN'T ABOUT YOU" (War p96). Ant says Cade "likely" accepts this, because it is never raised again.
7. **Destiny accepted.** "TIME I OWNED UP TO MINE". Sazen: "I'M PROUD OF YOU" (War p106).
8. **Talon's assessment.** Cade "telekinetically dominates" Talon (p112). She concludes "CADE IS... STRONGER... THAN HE WAS. MUST...WARN... LORD KRAYT!" (p114). The recap calls this "Cade's new mastery of the Force" (p115).
9. **Conclusion.** The seven releases weaken Cade's dark side and strengthen his light side. He "must be actively drawing on" the light to explain Talon's reaction. He would beat Krayt only by fully embracing the light.

## Notable replies / counterarguments

- **Master Azronger (post 2):** "Good read. Even though I don't think the fight itself supports it, I can see where you're coming from with your 'thematic' argument for Cade being in contention with Krayt." He concedes the theme and rejects the fight evidence.
- ILS's full rebuttal (Material 019) targets parts #2 and #3, not #1. Its Part 3 answers the Talon argument indirectly: Talon "cares about Krayt deeply", and fear of a rival's growth says little about how a fight would go.

## Common rebuttals / weak points (ANALYSIS — not from the thread)

- **The light-side source is an inference.** No page in War says Cade's new strength comes from the light side. Talon says he is "STRONGER"; she also says "I SENSE THE DARKNESS IN YOU. YOU NEVER WALKED IN THE LIGHT" (p112).
- **The Lucas / Fact Files "super energy" line is uncited** (no issue, page or scan).
- **Point 6 ("likely" stops blaming himself) is an argument from silence.**
- **Point 5(B) cuts both ways.** Cade's decisive Taivas win comes from *embracing* anger ("ENOUGH", eyes red-orange, Force lightning, p90). That shows the dark side working for him in the moment, against the claim that the light side is his stronger source.
- **Growth relative to Talon is not growth relative to Krayt.** Ant concedes this ("powerful enough to beat Krayt? No").

## Characters touched and implied scaling (FORUM OPINION)

- End-of-War Cade > Talon (clearly) and > his pre-War self.
- Cade < Krayt *unless* he fully embraces the light (Ant).
- Azronger: the theme supports contention, the fight does not.

Ant also mis-narrates p88: Cade does not "shoot another Sith Trooper dead"; a crewman on the ramp does ("THAT'S FROM BANTHA!"). |
| 6 | Self-blame resolved | **Disagree (unproven)** | It is only inferred from silence. |
| 7 | Destiny accepted | **Agree** | p106 verified. |
| 8 | Talon's warning shows a real power jump | **Agree** | p112–115 verified. Two pushes floor Krayt's Hand. |
