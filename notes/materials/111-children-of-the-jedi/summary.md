# Children of the Jedi (Barbara Hambly, 1995): summary

Source: owner's epub, FULL.txt l.1–9354, every line hand-read. Cites are line numbers in FULL.txt. Bundled excerpts from other books (l.6933–8954) are excluded from feats.

## Plot
- **Ithor (ch1–3):**
  - Mad smuggler Drub McKumb attacks Han and gasps "They hid the children in the well. Plett's Well… The children of the Jedi" (l.260–366).
  - Luke probes Nichos Marr's memories and finds Belsavis/Plawal and a dark wave searching for something (l.512–574). Han and Leia go to Belsavis (l.638).
  - Luke, Cray, Nichos and 3PO follow Nichos's Force-trance coordinates to the Moonflower Nebula (l.670–736). Their ship is shot down by an "asteroid" (l.806–848).
- **The Eye of Palpatine (ch4–7, 9, 11, 14, 17, 19–20, 22–24):**
  - A Clone-Wars-era automated battlemoon run by the Will. It was meant to pick up scattered stormtrooper companies and destroy Plett's Jedi refuge on Belsavis (l.1012, 1430–1450, 2976–3004).
  - It indoctrinates whatever it collects: Gamorreans, Talz, Tuskens, Jawas, Affytechans, Kitonaks and Pothman (l.1350–1486). Cray is tried for mutiny and sentenced to death by laser grid (l.2680–2756, 3496–3510).
  - The trapped spirit of the Jedi Callista helps Luke. She disabled the relays 30 years ago and died in the gun room, and she still sabotages the Will (l.2200–2230, 3386–3480).
  - Luke "reprograms" the Gamorreans (l.5016–5054) and lifts them up the lift shaft to save Cray (l.5144–5230). He drugs the Tuskens with sugar syrup (l.5898–5932) and stampedes the Kitonaks to the shuttles (l.6113–6125).
  - Cray stuns Luke (l.6461). She and Nichos stay to destroy the Eye, and it explodes (l.6569–6591).
- **Belsavis (ch5, 8, 10, 12–13, 15–16, 18, 21, 23):**
  - Han and Leia find the Jedi ruins and Plett's House (l.1180–1330) and mad ex-smugglers in the tunnels (l.2536–2660). R2 is possessed and tries to kill Leia (l.3118–3172).
  - Leia unmasks Roganda Ismaren (l.3254–3368, 3938–3944) and finds Senex/Juvex lords and Seinar/Mekuun arms gathering for a coup (l.3808–3816, 4086–4136).
  - Leia is captured by Irek (l.4256–4348). She finds Magrody's notes on Irek's implant (l.4814–4940), Force-projects into the conspirators' reception (l.5640–5842), and exposes Roganda's betrayal (l.6229–6307).
  - Han rescues her (l.6367–6379).
- **Ending (ch25–26):**
  - Callista lives in Cray's body (l.6667–6727) but has lost the Force (l.6765–6775). Luke still loves her (l.6837).
  - Roganda and Irek are at large (l.6863).

## Strongest feats
1. **Luke** Force-supports an antigrav sled loaded to twice its capacity with about a dozen Gamorreans. He lifts it up an 80 m+ lift shaft, trimming four lifters separately while they jump about, while injured (l.5144–5170).
2. **Luke** superheats the air at the fire-sprinkler sensors along a whole memorized route of corridors, gangway and elevator shaft between the lounge and the Deck 16 hangar. The rain sets off a Kitonak stampede that breaks down walls (l.6113–6125).
3. **Luke**, maimed and held over an enzyme vat, goes "a leaf on the wind" and levitates sideways to the far rim (l.4462–4464). Just before, he reflects tracker droids' bolts off a diagnostic mirror to destroy both (l.4444–4450).

Also of note:
- **Luke:**
  - Swats a shredder 10 m into pieces, rips away a carbine and an ax from meters off, and throws Krok, about 175 kg (l.1740–1742).
  - Smashes a magnetically locked panel (l.2388–2390). Uses a hurricane sweep of a room's furniture (l.3544).
  - Makes the laser grid misfire and fires a tracker decoy up the shaft (l.4430–4432). Breaks Pothman's indoctrination (l.5202–5220). Self-heals (l.858–870, 6623–6625).
- **Irek:**
  - Forces an R-10 to break its black-box rules (l.5746–5776) and fights Leia's projection with Force illusions and a mind-maze (l.5780–5830).
  - Reactivates the Eye's relay through the Force (l.6217). Detonates a flamethrower tank (l.4262).
  - Cuts Garonnin in half (l.6311). Snaps the pulleys, and Keldor dies in the fall (l.6373–6379).
- **Leia:**
  - Out-of-body projection (l.5640–5666). A Force "lance" snaps the latch (l.6303), and she Force-pulls a blaster (l.6313).
  - Opens a dimension-shifted stone (l.4788). Fights four mad beings with a forcepike and flamethrower (l.4172).
- **Callista** (as spirit):
  - Has fought the Will from inside for 30 years and fired the guns that downed Luke (l.3462–3466).
  - Holds a door shut against the Tuskens (l.2946–2952). Her grid-misfire technique (l.3622–3630).

## Anti-feats
Mostly from injury or exhaustion.
- Luke can barely stand: "forget about levitating anything" (l.1514). He is grazed and knocked out by a low-power bolt (l.1516–1520).
- He can't light "so much as a candle" after the Gamorrean fight (l.1762). A Force blast can't break magnetic dog-bolts (l.2376).
- His mind trick fails against the Will's programming (l.2280–2286, 3524–3530). He can't levitate himself after lowering 3PO (l.3562–3568).
- The Tredwell's arms resist his lightsaber (l.4460). He is hit 4–5 times through the grid (l.4534).
- He can't tear the carbine from Pothman (l.5198). Cray stuns him (l.6461).
- Earlier, he is knocked out when the Huntbird is hit (l.848) and stunned by the lander's autocannon (l.1150–1154).

## Lore highlights
- **The Eye:**
  - "Largest and most heavily armored battlemoon still in existence"; a prototype between the torpedo platforms and the Death Star. It has "no destructor beams, but almost the power capacity of the Death Star" (l.5714).
  - Elsewhere it is "more vast than even the biggest of the Super Star Destroyers… firepower to waste a planet" (l.1444–1446).
- **Force and machines:** the Force "can't affect droids" (l.3470–3480). Irek can, through Magrody's subelectronic converter (l.4814–4882). Callista's spirit lives beside the Will, which Luke compares to Exar Kun in Yavin's stones (l.3590–3592).
- **Jedi lore:**
  - Plett, a Ho'Din Master, founded the Belsavis Jedi refuge (l.1180, 1228). Djinn Altis kept a Jedi enclave on Bespin (l.3696) and taught "1080 mistakes" (l.5340–5344).
  - Jedi children's toys train the Force: a puzzle sphere, mind-mazes and a sand tank (l.4202–4214, 6741–6763).
- **The Emperor:** Palpatine and Vader made "waking nightmare" creatures through the dark side (l.3056). Palpatine had another Hand besides Mara (l.5582–5584). Concubines competed to bear his heir (l.4900).
- **Scaling reference:** Luke is called "the mightiest Jedi in the universe… defeated the recloned Emperor and the Sith Lord Exar Kun" (l.5260).

## Atrocities
- The Will tortures captives and implants false memories (l.1350–1438, 5204). It massacres Affytechans (l.2892) and sentences Cray to death by laser grid (l.3504).
- The Eye's standing order is to destroy all life on Belsavis (l.6219).
- Roganda held Magrody for 4 years, drugged (l.4814–4882). She betrays her allies (l.6269–6307).
- Stinna Draesinge Sha was assassinated (l.190–204).
