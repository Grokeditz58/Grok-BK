Character quick bios:
- **Luke Skywalker:**
  - Jedi Master, ~11 ABY. Captured aboard the Eye; his leg tendons are severed by an ax (l.1522, 1686), and he is wounded, infected and exhausted for most of the book (l.4956–5000).
  - Force-heals his own concussion (l.858–870). Lifts an overloaded sled up an 80 m+ shaft (l.5144–5168). Levitates over the enzyme vat (l.4462–4464). Bounces bolts off a mirror (l.4450). Triggers the sprinklers along a whole route by heating air (l.6121).
  - Falls in love with Callista (l.3772–3774).
- **Callista:** Jedi from Chad III, trained 5 years by Djinn Altis (l.3598, 3696, 5348). She died in the Eye's gun-room core 30 years ago and lives on as a spirit beside the Will (l.2200–2230, 3462–3466). She took over Cray's body at the end (l.6727) but has lost the Force (l.6775).
- **Cray Mingla:** 26-year-old AI genius and Luke's student (l.376). Indoctrinated and condemned by the Will (l.1474–1486, 3504). Dies destroying the Eye with Nichos and gives her body to Callista (l.6569–6591, 6719–6723).
- **Nichos Marr:** Cray's fiancé, moved into a droid body after Quannot's Syndrome. He lost the Force in the transfer (l.414, 480) and knows he is not the living Nichos (l.6581).
- **Leia Organa Solo:**
  - Has a Force-vision of the past (l.1320–1322). Lights candles by the Force (l.3134). Separates liquids in a Force-puzzle (l.4202–4214) and opens a dimension-shifted compartment (l.4788).
  - Drug-aided out-of-body projection (l.5640–5666). A Force "lance" snaps the latch on Roganda's box (l.6303), and she Force-pulls a blaster (l.6313).
- **Han Solo / Chewbacca:** explore Plett's Well and fight mad ex-smugglers (l.2452–2638). Han rescues Leia, and Keldor falls to his death (l.6367–6379).
- **Roganda Ismaren:** ex-concubine of Palpatine and the Emperor's other Hand (l.3254–3288, 5582–5584, 6539). Sister of Lagan Ismaren (l.3938–3944). Uses the Eye for a coup, then betrays her allies (l.6229–6307). Escapes (l.6863).
- **Irek Ismaren:** her son, about 15 (l.4756, 6149). Magrody's subelectronic implant (put in at 5) lets him use the Force on AIs (l.4874–4888).
  - Controls R2 (l.4266–4276), an R-10 (l.5746–5776) and the kretch (l.4320–4338). Reactivates the Eye's relay through the Force (l.6217).
  - Duels Leia with a lightsaber and Force-chokes her (l.4314–4316). Bisects Garonnin (l.6311).
- **The Will:** the Eye's AI. It indoctrinates captives (Gamorreans, Talz, Tuskens, Affytechans, Kitonaks, Pothman) and herds Luke with doors and traps (l.1350–1438, 2362–2430, 2902–2904).
- **Triv Pothman:** the last stormtrooper of the Eye's pickup force (l.980–986). Luke breaks his conditioning (l.5202–5220), and he stays on as a horticulturist (l.6815).
- **Ohran Keldor:** Death Star designer and Magrody student, paid to build the Eye's relays (l.2976–3004). Dies in a fall (l.6373–6379).
- **Others:** Drub McKumb (l.304); Mara Jade (l.2966, 5554–5594, 6595–6625); Ugbuz, Krok, Bullyak and Gakfedd/Klagg Gamorreans (l.1072–1086); Djinn Altis (l.3696, 5340–5344); Geith (l.4470–4564); Magrody (l.4814–4916); Lord Garonnin, Drost Elegin, Theala Vandron (l.4256–4264, 5672–5712); Plett (l.1180, 1228).
