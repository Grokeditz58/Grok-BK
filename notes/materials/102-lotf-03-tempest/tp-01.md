Bothan threat: Reh'mwa (l.398, 416). Jacen: "I'd know if I had been followed. Not even Bothan assassins are that good" (l.402) — ANTI-FEAT, he did not notice Alema.

- Lumiya Force-acrobatics past tentacles (l.448); orders Jacen to slow the poison; "You are a servant to your emotions, not a master to them" (l.468). Jacen: "It's about justice" (l.462). Jacen summons blowgun: "Tell Reh'mwa we're coming" (l.474–476). Alema: "Jacen Solo, apprenticing with a Sith" (l.480).
