## Ch.12 (l.3076–3241) — Falcon in the Transitory Mists with Nashtah
- The Mists absorb light and block long-range sensors (l.3082–3086). Nashtah carries a vibrodagger (l.3086); the Noghri hide aboard (l.3112).
- Nashtah eats four raw nerf steaks (half a kilo each) and squeezes the blood into a glass to drink (l.3122–3126, 3204). "The dark side can be catching" — hint that she was once Force-trained (l.3134–3138).
- Her mother was human; her father unknown but long-lived (l.3142). She saw Anakin win the Boonta Eve Classic: "he didn't cheat" (l.3208–3222) — LORE: she is over 60.
- Leia's cover story: agents of Corellia, against "another Empire" (l.3144–3150). Leia recalls her Tatooine vision — "Mine … mine" — and her grandmother's vid-diary (l.3166–3168); it echoes Jacen's vision. Leia: "I won't let him become another Darth Vader. Whatever it takes" (l.3182).
- "I specialize in Jedi ... That's why they recruited me for Tenel Ka" (l.3188). Leia Force-stops Han (l.3178). Next stop: Telkur Station (pirate station) (l.3236).

## Ch.13 (l.3242–3595) — Terephon, Villa Solis: the Ducha's trap
- Ducha Galney's fleet (~30 ships, Miy'til squadrons) mobilised too early; no HoloNet in the Mists (l.3244–3250). Zekk is "over" Jaina, and she feels a sense of loss (l.3256–3290).
- Villa Solis is in 200 km of bog (l.3294). Serv-O-Droid gatekeepers; Jaina and Zekk cut them down in perfect unison (l.3302–3320). Entora Zar, murg handler and holosim "rated instructor" (l.3342–3386, 3444). Household hid, then fled in a yacht; ~20 normally stay; fighters refitted all week (l.3464–3478). Zekk: Zar was left as a decoy (l.3486).
- Two YVH battle droids ("Why-Vees") kill Zar (l.3496–3508). "Even Jedi were a poor match for the accuracy and ferocious firepower of YVH battle droids" (l.3498) — power-scaling yardstick.
- FEAT (Jaina): Force-acrobatics to stay "one eye-blink ahead" of the droids' targeting; two close deflections (l.3508–3510). She baits the droid with a thrown rock, controls her heart rate to beat its sensors, takes its leg off at the knee, and it blows off its own arm (l.3532–3556). Shrapnel in her back and neck; a mini-missile flattens 5 m of wall (l.3558).
- Zekk nearly overwhelmed by his YVH; he cripples it with a grenade volley (l.3536, 3544, 3560). ANTI-FEAT: the escaped murgs saved them (l.3516).
- Miy'til missiles destroy both StealthXs — six craters (l.3570–3574). Zekk opens the gate to free the murgs; Jaina: "I'm kind of starting to like it" (l.3580–3594).

- Jagged Fel: bearded; marooned two years on Tenupe (l.3674–3682); the recovery team vanished (l.3686); rescued by his family's hired company (l.3690). "Jaina has caused the Fel family enough problems" (l.3698).

- Fel is exiled: his family was liable for Lowbacca's damage at Supply Depot Thrago, the attack Jacen tricked them into (l.3810–3822). He saw Bwua'tu's communiqué and followed the Hapan Security team (l.3842, 3856–3866).
- Han grabs drugged Nashtah's gun arm — "not on his best day thirty years earlier" (l.3742). Nashtah's Force trance burns off the drug (l.3890).
- Han stuns the team leader (l.3878–3880). Nashtah kills the bartender (l.3902). Leia's deflection is slowed by the Renatyl (l.3910). Han is shot in the shoulder (l.3914–3916).

- Contact is Lady Morwan [Lalu Morwan], who serves "the council" (l.3960–3964). Leia sows distrust: "Someone on your council is a spy" (l.3968). The Chume'da goes first, then the Queen Mother (l.3978–3986). Nashtah takes Morwan's Batag Skiff (l.3990–4000).

- Morwan: "If the Council didn't want the Queen Mother killed, it wouldn't have hired Aurra Sing" (l.4040). Han: "an eighty-year-old woman" (l.4070).
- The Heritage Council tasked Morwan; Sing found Morwan after she researched her — "hire or die" (l.4072–4074).
- Han pulls a blaster on Morwan to stay out of medbay (l.4086–4090). Han and Leia agree to keep protecting Tenel Ka and drop Gejjen: "If she can't do it without dragging Hapes ... she shouldn't do it at all" (l.4142–4166).
- Morwan, a field surgeon at Qoribu aboard the *Kendall*; Leia's bluff identifies Aleson Gray / House AlGray as the ringleader — "it's impossible to lie to a Jedi" (l.4214–4240).
