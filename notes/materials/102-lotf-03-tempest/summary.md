# Legacy of the Force 3: Tempest: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-04 CST. Full hand-read, l.1–10312. Citation "TP ch. N (l.x)". Detail is in tp-01 to tp-06.
- Author: Troy Denning (title page; About the Author, l.7249–7251). The request said Karen Traviss, who only appears in the acknowledgments (l.7247) and the *Hard Contact* excerpt (l.7896–8186).

## Plot
- **Hapes thread (ch. 1, 5, 7, 12, 14–15, 20, 22, 24):**
  - Gejjen sends Han and Leia to Hapes for a meeting that turns out to be a trap: they are bait to break Tenel Ka's security (l.1494–1532).
  - Assassins strike at the Queen's Pageant (l.1940–2030), led by "Nashtah", who is really Aurra Sing (l.2076, 4040).
  - The Solos learn the Heritage Council and Aleson Gray are behind it (l.4214–4240) and switch sides (l.4142–4166). Han sends Tenel Ka the Heritage Fleet's reversion point (l.5490–5526).
  - The Royal Navy ambushes the fleet at the Battle of Hapes. Bwua'tu's Alliance fleet arrives (l.6186–6196).
- **Jacen's thread (prologue, ch. 6, 11, 16–17, 19, 23):**
  - The World Brain is poisoned and dies (l.428–432, 1734). Jacen sets Lumiya on the Bothan kill list (l.1738–1764) and issues a warrant for his parents (l.3066).
  - Over Hapes he has the "Mine" vision (l.2766–2794). He sends Lumiya to Roqoo to fight Luke (l.4682–4694) and defeats Aurra Sing (ch. 19).
  - His long-range turbolasers break the Galney fleet (l.6414–6416). He fires on the Falcon (l.6684–6692).
- **Luke and Mara (ch. 3, 9–10, 16, 21):**
  - Lobi's murder (l.2364) leads them to Lumiya's flat, which is tied to the GAG (l.2678–2712).
  - At Roqoo Depot they fight Lumiya and Alema. Luke loses his hand but shoots Lumiya, and they escape her proton vest by about two minutes (l.5920–6056, 6586–6590).
- **Jaina, Zekk and Ben (ch. 2, 4, 8, 13, 18, 22, 24):**
  - Jaina and Zekk warn Tenel Ka, survive the Ducha's trap at Villa Solis (l.3496–3574) and are rescued by Ben's *Rover* (l.4876–4924).
  - The *Rover* is destroyed (l.6356). Aboard the Falcon, Ben draws his saber on Jaina and wounds Zekk (l.6862–6892).
- **Epilogue:**
  - Tenel Ka clears the Solos (l.7038–7042), but Jacen keeps the warrant: "one law" (l.7152–7166).
  - Lumiya may have survived (l.7178–7186). Jacen lies: "I've never even met the woman" (l.7188).
  - Ben is ordered home (l.7168–7218).

## Top feats
1. **Luke vs Lumiya and Alema at Roqoo Depot:**
   - He Force-throws a live grenade back up the corridor before it goes off (TP ch. 21, l.5850–5854).
   - He has lost his shoto hand and his torso is flayed (l.5924); his body is "literally boiling his own cells" (l.5990) and fails him, and he loses his saber (l.6002–6006).
   - Even so, he falls backward under the whip and, guided by the Force, shoots Lumiya three times, then twice more, with a dead spacer's blaster (l.6034–6036). Then he Force-summons both weapons (l.6044).
2. **Jacen vs Aurra Sing:**
   - He pushes into Sing's mind to "crush her own presence"; she "simply was not strong enough" (ch. 19, l.5270–5276).
   - He Force-summons her class-C thermal detonator mid-throw (l.5298–5300).
   - After she beats him in close combat (knee, saber-lock, kick-knife strikes, disarm; l.5302–5322), he cartwheels over her blade and dislocates her knee (l.5334–5338). Allana's sedative finishes her (l.5352–5364).
3. **Luke spars Ben blindfolded:**
   - With eyes closed, he tracks Ben through the Force and holds Ben's lit hilt motionless 2 m away (ch. 3, l.1034–1036).
   - He roots himself so a flying kick doesn't move him (l.1038–1040).
   - His own saber had no power cell all along: "A Jedi's greatest weapon is his mind" (l.1054–1056).

## Other feats
- **Tresina Lobi vs Alema plus Lumiya:**
  - She breaks through sustained Force lightning and Force-throws an urn Alema can't redirect (l.1804–1816).
  - She pulls Alema into the lightwhip's path and severs Lumiya's cybernetic weapon arm (l.1818–1824).
  - She dies only to a two-on-one, with Alema's Force-whisper doubt (l.1838–1850).
- **Aurra Sing:**
  - Fights through a concussion grenade with two heavy blasters (l.2022–2030).
  - Drugged with Renatyl, she burns it off in a trance and kills a whole security team with double headshots (l.3890, 3920–3926).
  - Cuts DeDe apart (l.5266, 5282).
- **Alema:**
  - Force-crushes a throat through a crippled arm (l.284–286).
  - Her slippery presence erases her from memory (l.330).
  - Hides a dart in the Force from Jacen (l.422–426).
- **Jacen** Force-lightnings Alema (l.438–440).
- **Jaina vs a YVH battle droid:** "Even Jedi were a poor match" for these droids (l.3498). She stays "one eye-blink ahead" of its targeting, controls her heart rate to beat its sensors and takes off its leg (l.3508–3556).
- **Ben:**
  - Force-tracks Jaina across 300 km of bog from residual frustration (l.4876–4898).
  - Knocks Luke off the beam with a wobble ball (l.960).
- **Leia:**
  - Senses Jaina's damped presence from the Falcon (l.816–818).
  - Kills three, deflects bolts and crushes two under armor (l.1992–1994).
  - Flies through a broken Nova's spine (l.5576–5604) and pulls a partial "Solo Slingshot" around Megos while dodging a turbolaser by danger sense (l.6120–6184).
- **Luke and Mara** share a danger sense that defuses a thermal-detonator keypad (l.2538–2550).
- **Mara** dodges a dart moving near blaster speed (l.5928–5932), scissor-takes Alema to knock her out and disarms the dead-man charge (l.5976–5988).

## Anti-feats and atrocities
- **Luke:** Alema's despair projection opens him to the lightwhip, which takes his hand (l.5920–5924). He then loses his saber (l.6002–6006).
- **Lumiya:** loses her arm to Lobi one-on-one (l.1824). Is gunned down by a wounded Luke (l.6034–6036).
- **Mara:** overconfident; Alema's talons pierce her throat (l.5970–5972).
- **Jacen:**
  - Fails to notice Alema following him: "Not even Bothan assassins are that good" (l.402).
  - Can't match Sing with the saber alone (l.5302–5322).
  - Force-chokes Espara (l.6470) and orders fire on the Falcon, killing Cakhmaim and Meewalh (l.6684–6692, 6764–6768).
  - Imprisons hundreds of thousands of Corellians (l.1246) and runs the Bothan kill list (l.1738–1764).
- **Hapan conspirators:** poison every witness in the Well (l.3002–3014).
- **Ben:** ignites on Jaina and slashes Zekk (l.6862–6892).
- **Zekk** is nearly overwhelmed by a YVH; the escaped murgs save them (l.3516, 3536).

## Notes
- Lumiya's survival is left open: her vest took about 2 minutes to go off (l.7178–7186). She has a plan "carried out whether or not I survive" and hints at more Sith (l.4786–4796).
- Aurra Sing lore: taken from the Jedi by pirates at 9, 75+ years ago; held at Oovo IV (l.4058–4064).
- The bundled excerpts are out-of-book material and are not counted as Tempest feats. Examples: in *Exile*, Kyp moves the *Jade Shadow* with the Force (l.7425) and Jacen sabotages Lavint's hyperdrive (l.7590–7598); in *The Joiner King*, Akanah says Jacen has Luke's power "without the light" (l.9284).
