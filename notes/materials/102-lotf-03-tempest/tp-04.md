## Ch.19 (l.5174–5371) — Relephon: Aurra Sing attacks Allana

AlGray's fleet is gone — leak through the pilots (l.5188, 5206). Jacen puts the Force behind his words (l.5198). Aide Orlopp (Jenet), Commander Twizzl (l.5210–5212).
- SD-XX reports someone at the girl's cabin (l.5228); Level Two lockdown (l.5236–5248). Sing kills the guards; she cleaves one guard (l.5250–5254).
- Allana touches Jacen through the Force, then Sing's cold presence intrudes (l.5256–5260). DeDe fires a concussion grenade and blaster cannon; Sing deflects with a crimson saber and cuts DeDe apart (l.5262–5266, 5282).
- FEAT (Jacen): Force domination — pushes into Sing's mind, "crush her own presence"; she "simply was not strong enough" (l.5270–5276). She breaks free when the thermal detonator starts blinking (l.5296). Jacen Force-summons the class-C thermal detonator mid-throw and blocks (l.5298–5300).
- FEAT (Sing): knee to Jacen's gut, saber-lock forcing the tip toward his eye; kick-knife boot strikes to the ribs and kidney (l.5302–5316). Jacen: "pain was an old friend" (l.5318); Force push; Sing disarms him on the way out (l.5320–5322).
- Jacen gathers Force lightning but stops because Allana is watching (l.5326–5330). He cartwheels over her blade and dislocates her knee with a kick (l.5334–5338). Allana injects Sing with her "safety stick" sedative (l.5352–5364). "Yedi never kill helpless people" (l.5368). (Sing ≈ evenly matched with Jacen in sabers; he beats her only with the Force plus Allana.)

- FEAT (Leia, piloting): flies between the halves of a broken Nova's spine and past a molten Battle Dragon (l.5576–5604).
- Morwan pulls a hold-out blaster on Han (l.5638). Han grabs it (palm burned) and smashes her nose with his elbow (l.5686–5692). Noghri lock her up (l.5712–5716).
- Leia sensed Tesar watching at the comet, so an Alliance trap is coming (l.5770–5780). Han chooses conscience over Corellia: "my reputation doesn't matter. My conscience does" (l.5752–5756); 63 worlds at stake (l.5754). "There really is no such thing as a Jedi flying trance" (l.5786). Han broadcasts to Tenel Ka in the clear (l.5788).

## Ch.21 (l.5790–6057) — Roqoo Depot: Luke and Mara vs Lumiya and Alema
- Blue giant Roqoo's aurora blocks comms (l.5792). Luke: "something wrong is coming" (l.5800); Jacen set them up (l.5808). Raynar Thul, captured by Luke, in the Temple basement with a prosthetic arm (l.5830–5836).
- FEAT (Luke): Force-throws a grenade back up the corridor before it detonates (l.5850–5854). Hull breach (l.5856).
- Lumiya's lightwhip (six strands: energy, leather, crystal-studded metal) kills patrons (l.5870–5886). She uses the Force to turn the crowd on Luke (l.5888). Luke wields two blades (shoto and long) (l.5880).
- Lumiya's lightwhip strikes "high, low, and in between all at once"; Luke cuts one strand (l.5904).
- Alema holds back her blowgun dart to make Luke watch Mara die (l.5906–5910). Luke's "unbelievable parry" (l.5912).
- Alema's Force projection of despair distracts Luke, and the lightwhip takes his shoto hand and flays his torso (l.5920–5924). ANTI-FEAT: Luke loses a hand to Lumiya through the distraction.
- Mara dodges the near-blaster-speed dart thanks to Luke's warning; it kills a Twi'lek (l.5928–5932). Alema's Force shadow (l.5902, 5938). Alema stabs through an Arcona; Mara's shoto grazes her lekku (l.5944–5948).
- ANTI-FEAT (Mara): overconfident; Alema's talons pierce her throat (l.5970–5972). Mara dodges lightning and scissor-takes Alema; her head hits the floor and she is out (l.5976–5980). Mara spares her (l.5980). Dead-man proton charge on Alema's heart; Mara disarms it in sequence (l.5986–5988).
- Luke "literally boiling his own cells" by drawing on the Force (l.5990). He refuses to kill a hostage (l.5994); Force-shoves Lumiya and pulls the serving girl free (l.5996–5998); wraps his blade in the whip strands (l.6000).
- ANTI-FEAT (Luke): his wrecked body fails and he loses his lightsaber trying to yank the whip away (l.6002–6006). Lumiya hints that she has used Jacen's codes and that Ben is being turned: "bringing your family legacy full circle" (l.6018–6026).
- FEAT (Luke): falls backward under the whip and, Force-guided, shoots Lumiya three times, then twice more, with a dead spacer's blaster (l.6034–6036). Her life-support girdle fails; Luke Force-summons both weapons (l.6044). Proton dead-man vest blinking — "we'd better run" (l.6056). (So Lumiya does NOT die by saber; the blaster plus the vest.)

- FEAT/ATROCITY (Jacen): Force-chokes Major Espara when she calls him a coward; freezes her aides with a glance (l.6470–6474). He will not risk Allana — "some things he would never sacrifice" (l.6470).

Lumiya's proton vest destroyed the whole cantina; the Skywalkers escaped by two minutes (l.6586–6590). Alema sabotaged the *Jade Shadow*'s containment line (l.6594). Ben never reached Roqoo (l.6608).
- Jacen senses Ben, Jaina, Zekk; Jaina shuts him out (l.6616–6618). "Longshot" = the Falcon (l.6620). Alema plants doubt about his parents (l.6628–6648). Jacen gives her the dinghy and threatens her (l.6654–6662).
