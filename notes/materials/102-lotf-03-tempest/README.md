Gejjen's Corellia (Wedge as Supreme Commander) works with Hapan nobles of the Heritage Council to kill Tenel Ka and Allana. Jacen, now commanding the GAG Star Destroyer *Anakin Solo*, wins the Battle of Hapes but fires on the *Millennium Falcon*. Lumiya ambushes Luke and Mara at Roqoo Depot. The Solos become fugitives and Ben is pulled home from the GAG.

Character quick bios:
- **Jacen Solo:**
  - GAG colonel commanding the *Anakin Solo*, in a black cape (l.4376–4378); Lumiya's apprentice.
  - Has a vision of faces saying "Mine" over Hapes and reads it as a Sith dynasty (l.2766–2836). Lumiya explains why she chose him (l.2854–2880).
  - Sets Lumiya up to fight Luke at Roqoo (l.4682–4694). Force-dominates and defeats Aurra Sing (l.5270–5352). Force-chokes Major Espara (l.6470).
  - Orders the *Anakin Solo* to fire on the Falcon; the Noghri die (l.6684–6692, 6764–6768). Won't lift the warrant: "There's one law" (l.7152–7166).
- **Allana (Chume'da):** age 4; her paternity (Jacen) is secret (l.2760). Very strong in the Force (l.4382). Sedates Sing with her "safety stick" (l.5352–5364).
- **Tenel Ka:** Queen Mother of Hapes and still a Force user who can sense lies (l.2190). The Heritage Council plot fails, and she ends as "uncontested monarch of sixty-three worlds" (l.6960). Clears the Solos (l.7038–7042) and spares Lady Galney: "I am not my grandmother" (l.7112).
- **Han and Leia Solo:** Sent by Gejjen as bait at Hapes (l.1494–1532), they turn to protect Tenel Ka (l.4142–4166). Han: "my reputation doesn't matter. My conscience does" (l.5752–5756). Leia was knighted four years earlier by Saba (l.530) and flies the Falcon through the battle (l.5576–5604, 6120–6184). Fugitives at the end.
- **Luke Skywalker:** Spars Ben blindfolded with an unpowered saber (l.1034–1056) and takes up Jar'Kai with a shoto (l.1136). At Roqoo he loses his hand to Lumiya's lightwhip but shoots her five times (l.5924, 6034–6036). Orders Ben home (l.7168).
- **Mara Jade Skywalker:** Suspects Jacen (l.4354). Beats Alema at Roqoo but takes talons in the throat (l.5970–5980).
- **Ben Skywalker:** 13, GAG. Tracks Jaina across 300 km of bog (l.4876–4898). Ignites on Jaina and slashes Zekk (l.6862–6892). Sent home (l.7168–7218).
- **Jaina Solo / Zekk:** Discharged for refusing Jacen's order (l.1150), they warn Tenel Ka. Fight YVH droids at Villa Solis (l.3496–3560). Their StealthXs are destroyed (l.3570–3574).
- **Lumiya:** Dark Lady with a lightwhip. Kills Tresina Lobi with Alema but loses her cybernetic arm to her (l.1824–1850). Wears a proton dead-man vest at Roqoo (l.4802–4806). Her death is doubtful (l.7178–7186).
- **Alema Rar:** last of the Gorog; uses wasber dart venom (l.244–254) and a slippery presence taught by Lomi Plo (l.330). Knows who Allana's father is and uses that as her safeguard (l.6542–6556).
- **Aurra Sing ("Nashtah"):** hired by the Heritage Council; Jedi-trained as a child, now over 80 (l.4040–4070). Saw Anakin win Boonta Eve (l.3208–3222). Captured and kept in a coma on the *Anakin Solo* (l.6372, 6422).
- **Dur Gejjen / Wedge Antilles / Lalu Morwan / Aleson Gray / Lady Galney / Ducha Galney / Nek Bwua'tu / Tresina Lobi / Jagged Fel:** supporting cast (Dramatis Personae, l.136–176; Fel exiled for Thrago, l.3810–3822).
