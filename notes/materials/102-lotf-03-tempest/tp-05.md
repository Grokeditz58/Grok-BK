## Epilogue (l.6958–7240)
- Tenel Ka is "uncontested monarch of sixty-three worlds" aboard the *Dragon Queen* (l.6960). Queen's Justice still to come (l.6964).
- Jacen is returning Allana on a GAG shuttle; Tenel Ka warns him through the Force (l.6966–6968). Zekk is on a stretcher with a lightsaber wound (l.6970, 6984); "Ben made a mistake" (l.6988). Luke is missing his cybernetic hand and bandaged; he has come out of a healing trance (l.6996–7000).
- Jacen is the hero of loyal Hapans after destroying the Galney fleet (l.7002). Jaina: "Does that give him an excuse to fire on his own parents?" (l.7008). Ben: "They're terrorists" (l.7022). Jacen claims his parents were involved (l.7026–7030). Tenel Ka publicly clears the Solos — they supplied the AlGray reversion coordinates (l.7038–7042). Jacen shows shame (l.7044).
- Luke: "You're very quick to believe the worst about someone you love" (l.7052). Tenel Ka abandons the plan to reveal Allana's paternity (l.7060).
- Lady Galney confesses that her consort reported to her sister; Tenel Ka knew and spares her: "I am not my grandmother" (l.7092–7112).
- In the *Shadow*'s salon, Ben admits they ignored the Roqoo order (l.7128–7136). Jacen refuses to cancel the detention warrant: "There's one law" (l.7152–7166).
- Luke orders Ben home and says "Jacen isn't a Master"; Ben: "No one's as strong in the Force—" (l.7168–7174). Lumiya's death is doubted — the bomb took ~2 minutes to go off (l.7178–7186). Jacen: "I've never even met the woman" — a lie (l.7188). Luke: Lumiya named Ben as her helper in GAG (l.7204). Jacen: "working together again much sooner than you expect" (l.7218). Tenel Ka kisses Jacen goodbye (l.7240).
