Aayla framing: start of Quin↔Obi-Wan friendship (Quin later forgets?).

2. **Adi → Coruscant (06–07 / 076–077).** Adi pilots Stark personal ship + co-pilot + Valorum + Gunray. Nav analysis hoped for virus counter. Near Coruscant: controls **booby-trapped** SCHRAKK — co-pilot killed; Adi: pods now. THOOM ship explodes; pods snared; Valorum IDs them. Adi reports Tyvokka† to Council. Valorum Senate hearing: Republic does **not** surrender — but Senate deems unauthorized Troiken troops (+Plo) **“expendable”**; nav rebuild prioritizes trade ships; **no relief**.

3. **Cave siege worsens (08–09 / 078–079).** Out of bacta/food/ammo; Stark finds main cavern (Billibango warned). Battle splash: Plo yellow-orange saber + Qui-Gon green. Jace wounded (Qui-Gon tends; no bacta). Plo from mind-probe: Adi got Valorum/Gunray back but ship self-destructed; Senate against relief. Tarkin: surrender. Plo: “moment of opportunity” — needs all Jedi.

4. **Sha Koon telepathic link (10 / 080).** Plo’s niece **Sha Koon** + Saldith wake Council. Saesee: related minds + other Jedi can bridge distance. Council circle joins Sha’s thoughts → Plo. Plo wants **Oppo Rancisis**’s thoughts especially. Plan: Qui-Gon obtains virus **patch**; Tholme broadcasts; Plo arranges ground.

5. **Trade Fed blackmail (11–12 / 081–082).** Mace+Adi to **Lott Dod**: Federation freighters for Jedi fighters. Dod refuses → Adi: truth about **manufactured bacta shortage**. Dod caves for silence (+ possible “hero”). Parallel: Combine fractures; Trodos chokes Stark; Stark: “ally on the **inside**!”

6. **Fleet prep / Raptor infil (13–14 / 083–084).** TF freighters + Jedi fighters; Oppo battle-meditates on bridge; Saesee leads squadrons — needs patch. Tholme+Quin on *Raptor* for comlink broadcast; Qui-Gon+Obi-Wan for patch. Patch locked in droid needing **code word**; scientist doesn’t know it.

7. **Stark turns (15–16 / 085–086).** Cave: Plo delays; Billibango guides fall-back. On *Raptor*: red-haired **Iaco Stark** (dialogue: “ally I spoke of was a Jedi… Plo Koon… amnesty… collective finished”) kills hardliners, cooperates. Qui-Gon/Obi press for password (sabers UMM). Concurrent: Combine bridge Cap’n (subordinate, **not** Stark) sees Republic exiting mountain rear while Combine enters front — “lock and load.”

8. **Relief arrives / shields sabotaged (17–18 / 087–088).** Combine: ships from hyperspace! Saesee: “Squadrons Red and Blue, follow my lead.” Patch reached. Tholme: why no shields? Quin switched shield controls (raise→lower). Fight off ship before destroy.

9. **Tarkin† / Challat (19–21 / 089–091).** Plo: blow entrances. Jace: last charge **missing** — so is Tarkin. Tarkin rant: stolen command/glory; will destroy Collective by trapping them with **Challat Eaters**. CHUDOOM — Tarkin dies in blast; bugs loose. Plo telepathy: seal exit; allow surrenderers; kill bugs. Jace: no charges left. Plo yellow saber into swarm.

10. **Endgame (22–23 / 092–093).** Tholme+Quin escape *Raptor* WHAKOOM; Saesee almost ready for surface rescue. Qui-Gon/Tholme: Stark + main complex secured; ask after Plo. Plo Force-collapses cave RUMMMBLE/SHPOM — seals mountain. Emerges: “The mountain is… sealed. … Bring down the ships. The conflict is over.”

11. **Coda / framing (24 / 094).** Plo: war over; Collective finished; honors Tyvokka — **joins Council**. Stark → **Commerce Guild** (hated Outer Rim; cunning businessman). Gunray’s freighter “vision” → **Viceroy** (denies seeing Jedi). Valorum rising → Chancellor path; Tarkin family price: Ranulph = **“Hero of Troiken.”** Plo: Jedi don’t seek fame. Elder Jace kneels: troops’ undying loyalty to Plo. **END.**
