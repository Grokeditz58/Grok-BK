## Plot

### Framing aboard *Intrepid* (03–06)
1. **Memory recover (03–04).** Aayla, guided by Plo’s telepathy, clears recovered memories (Xoman Free rescue with Quin). Aboard *Intrepid* to Coruscant with Tholme (her new Master; former Master of Quin), Mace, Adi Gallia. Plo: “As any of us would be without our memories.”
2. **Jace Dallin (05).** Scarred officer seeks Plo; met Tholme/Adi at Troiken siege. Plo introduces Aayla as his Padawan (framing). Scars = “battle flags.”
3. **Retelling opens (06).** Mace: Stark Hyperspace War = last major conflict before Naboo; began **fourteen years ago**. Outer Rim resources extracted; Core returns goods at inflated prices. **Iaco Stark** — smuggler/pirate; sold stolen goods cheaper than Trade Federation; “a rogue with vision.”

### Backstory / Senate (07–08)
4. **Stark Commercial Combine (07).** Coalition of smugglers/pirates/mercenaries/bounty hunters/assassins challenges Trade Federation. **Thyferra** Alzahi (bacta ingredient) plant explodes → bacta cut ~⅔; Core hoards; Outer Rim starved. Combine targets bacta ships → Outer Rim heroes; war threat.
5. **Senate (08).** Gunray: protect TF or allow **increased droid armies** / stop trade. Tarkin (Eriadu militarist): make Republic Army/Navy official; Jedi not answerable to chamber. Valorum: strong central military → abuses; solution = diplomacy + address bacta shortage causes.

### Ads break (09–10)
Hasbro Power of the Jedi deluxe (Leia/Amanaman/Maul/Luke); Tag & Bink Are Dead promo (Rubio/Marangon).

### Council assigns teams (11–14)
6. **Tyvokka (11–12).** Not all convinced of bacta shortage. Tyvokka: powerful Council Master; “sense the future.” Plo = his last Padawan/friend. Balcony: humility vs pride; Tyvokka names Plo aide for Valorum-arranged summit (Stark Combine / TF / Republic). Plo suggests Qui-Gon.
7. **Thyferra probe (13).** Summit site = **Troiken** (secret). Tyvokka suspects scarcity may be **profiteering** (TF + Thyferrans). No proof → send **Tholme + Padawan Quinlan Vos** covert to Thyferra. Yoda: “Much darkness is there in that one.” Adi: “So far he has served us well.” Yoda: “‘So far’ not ‘forever’ is.”
8. **Troiken team (14).** Mace backs Tyvokka. Team: Tyvokka, Plo, Qui-Gon + new apprentice (Obi-Wan). Tarkin requests Adi Gallia as personal adviser — she agrees. Yoda: “May the Force be with us all.” Parallel conference: Tarkin/Gunray.

### Tarkin blackmails Gunray (15)
9. Jace (narrating): aide to Tarkin; Gunray wavering. Tarkin styles himself **General**; has assembled prototype Republic Navy/Army from sympathetic worlds; wants conference date/location to smash Combine. Gunray fears exposure → Tarkin threatens to reveal Gunray’s secret deals → Gunray names **Troiken**. Tarkin: destroy pirate scum; Valorum’s objections swept aside; “The Republic will have a strong military… and a strong new leader.”

### Thyferra investigation (17–19)
10. Tholme + Quin infiltrate burned plant while Troiken meeting convenes. Armed security droids at “burned-out shell” — odd. Quin psychometry: fire/explosion images **faded** (old). Vines cut back to fake recency → “This did not happen recently.”
11. Hunter-killer droid: “ACCESS… DESTROYED ON SIGHT.” Green sabers; CHOOM fire; Tholme mid-air cut. Quin: “Someone is **serious** about protecting the secrets…” Tholme: battle may alert; lightsaber marks recognizable. “Come, Quinlan.” Leave.

### Troiken summit → trap (20–23)
12. **Stark arrives (20).** Plo narrates late dramatic entrance. Co-directors Trodos, Boor-Daa, Tam Gozon, Lom Portom (+ unnamed girls “eye candy”). Stark: pretend deadlock then he’ll show real resolution. Gunray: earplug translators for Wookiee; TEK button.
13. **Tarkin’s jump (21).** Signal from “Neimoidian General” → players in place. Tarkin: jump to lightspeed; turn conference into crater; wipe “several layers of scum.” Flagship ***Invincible*** leads first/last assemblage of so-called Republic Navy — “…towards **disaster.**”
14. **Guns drawn (22).** Tyvokka skips preliminaries; Stark: “Capital idea.” Mercenaries draw on Jedi/delegates. Qui-Gon steadies Obi-Wan: cue from Tyvokka.
15. **Trap turned (23).** Stark: trap was for him; tables turned — covert Republic Army/Navy under **Ranulph Tarkin** inbound (**fact**). Valorum (shocked, blue coat): Republic doesn’t *have* army/navy; “Tarkin! That damned fool!” — actions unauthorized. Stark: signal to Tarkin came **from this chamber**. Gunray: “However, the point is moot…”

### Navcomputer virus / fleet ruin (24–27)
16. Stark piggybacked **navcomputer virus** on the signal. Infected ships exit into planets / **suns** / black holes; survivors misplaced, unable to return; some arrive at Troiken **anticipated and outnumbered** by Combine fleet.
17. Space battle over Troiken. Stark: they’ll be annihilated. Ultimatum: Republic concedes all Combine demands — “But first? You’re going to surrender. Or would you rather **die?**” Caption: **to be continued…**
