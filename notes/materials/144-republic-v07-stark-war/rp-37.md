## Plot (read/03–24 = digital 029–050)

1. **Summit ultimatum (03–04 / 029–030).** Valorum: Republic knew nothing of fleet. Tyvokka: fleet is **excuse** not cause; virus long-planned; not about bacta scarcity — **power play**/trap. Stark admits; “surrender or die.” Tyvokka flips table: `<THERE IS ALWAYS ANOTHER CHOICE.>` Qui-Gon green saber; melee opens.

2. **Firefight / droid disaster (05–07 / 031–033).** Tyvokka assigns: Obi-Wan→Gunray; Adi→Valorum; Plo with him. Plo orange saber; Adi saber; Qui-Gon green; Obi-Wan blue. Force-push KRASH. Qui-Gon urges Gunray deploy droids → Gunray “YES… DROIDS, KILL THEM ALL!” → Qui-Gon **NO!** — droids fire indiscriminately. Stark flees (“with luck they’ll kill each other”). Tyvokka hit (THWAP); collapses. Gunray: “MY DROIDS! … What have you done…!”

3. **Evacuate (08 / 034).** Adi: Tyvokka needs help fast. Tyvokka: find defensible position; Stark returns or bombs. Qui-Gon forces walking Gunray: protect despite “duplicity and cowardice” or leave for Stark. Tyvokka: combine with Republic ships; check own ships still commandable.

4. **Fleet hammered (09–13 / 035–039).** Jace narrates: ⅓ scattered, ⅓ destroyed, remainder outnumbered; *Invincible* favored target. Valorum holo: Lycinder Plain / **Mount Avos** Hormick Range — abandoned spice mine; land then fortify caves. Tarkin: “Damn you, Valorum! This is **MY** command!” Bridge hit KHU-DOOM; shields dropping → Tarkin orders escape pods; nontroop ships protect pods. Tarkin’s personal shuttle destroyed → takes **life pod** (smaller; enemy focuses on evac). *Invincible* loses shields, slips into atmosphere — **“SHE’S GONE!”**

5. **Ground escape (14–15 / 040–041).** Plo: Valorum sent coords; Tarkin may not comply. Gunray insists on his ship/more droids → TF shuttle **FWADOOM** destroyed. Qui-Gon: shields; get to rendezvous. Diplomatic vessel unarmed; damaged; enemy fighter on tail.

6. **Tyvokka’s charges & death (16–17 / 042–043).** Limited bacta; no tank. Charges: (a) Adi — get delegates esp. Valorum safely away (peace key in Valorum’s hands); (b) Qui-Gon — how Stark corrupted navcomputers + counteract; (c) Plo — **rally the troops** (if they surrender, so will Senate); (d) Council seat opening — wish Plo in time. Fourth precept: “There is no death—there is only the Force.” Tyvokka dies: “You… make me proud… Plo Koon…” Obi-Wan summons Plo forward; Plo: focus on the moment.

7. **Force illusion (18–19 / 044–045).** Joint meditation (Plo telepathy + Qui-Gon + Adi) projects fighter swarm into pursuer’s mind → Blue 9 ejects; ship KZASH. Obi-Wan: “amazing.” Qui-Gon: possible because Plo’s telepathy + single pursuer. Framing grief: Aayla asks if grief wrong; Mace — feel but not ruled by emotion; Adi — good to mourn the little things. Plo to Aayla: accept sorrow; strange gifts; learn and go on.

8. **Thyferra conspiracy (20–21 / 046–047).** Tholme: disaster faked; secret TF–**Xucphra** meeting. **Adol Bel** (Neimoidian) to **Hask** (Xucphra): solely a **Neimoidian ploy**; Hask: sitting on tons of unmovable bacta; if faked shortage exposed, galaxy blames them for war. Adol Bel: HK droids immune to mind tricks; Jedi won’t leave alive. Probe droid finds Tholme (VZZZ).

9. **Chase cliff (22–24 / 048–050).** Tholme leaps; Quin speeder catch; green saber SCHRAAK vs chase droid. More droids linked. Quin into sewer; **Tunnel-Grinder**; WHHROOM crash; Force-leap clear. Tholme: “Are you **testing** my faith…?” Quin: “No, Master. This is just a **mistake**.” **Issue end → #38.**

## Feats / Force / limits
- Tyvokka Force-push; table flip; yellow saber on cover / combat; dies of blaster wounds despite bacta.
- Plo orange saber; telepathy enables joint mind-illusion (multi-Jedi meditation → phantom fighters).
- Qui-Gon/Obi-Wan/Adi lightsabers; deflection.
- Tholme/Quin: green saber; Force leap from exploding speeder; psychometry established prior.
- Limits: Gunray’s panicking droid order wounds Tyvokka; diplomatic ship unarmed; bacta scarce even for Jedi; Tarkin’s ego delays landing; nav virus still uncountered; HK/probe droids resist mind tricks.
