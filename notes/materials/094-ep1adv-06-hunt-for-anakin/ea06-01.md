# EA6 The Hunt for Anakin Skywalker: notes

## Ch. 1 (l.31–172)
- Sebulba shoots Gondry in the stomach, then rips out his moustache and bites him. The wound heals in minutes. Note: here Gondry is called a greenish-tan **Abyssin** (EA5 had a "cyclops from Byss"); this is a known naming quirk. He regenerates limbs and wounds.
- **Khiss kills a charging giant mora beetle** with a blaster rifle. A ghost spider's web is "stronger than steel".
- Djas Puhr smells "two humans, a Twi'lek, and something else". A flake of Twi'lek skin goes to the DNA scanner. Hutt bounty on anyone who helps slaves escape.

## Ch. 2 (l.174–341): Madam Vansitt
- She has teeth filed to points. Her maxim: "Punishment for a crime is a price that only the stupid are forced to pay." She hints that she knows it was Pala. Gardulla once made a rug out of a thief.
- Pala reasons that Vansitt's reputation is better served if Pala escapes than if she is caught. "Say good-bye to your friends." Pala's full name, given in Ch. 9: **Pala Kwi'teksa**.

## Ch. 3 (l.343–622)
- The seeker droid picks up Anakin's scent on the transmitter counter. Watto dates the cube to "one, two thousand years" and decides it's a die.
- **Jira** hides the kids. She has a contact who smuggles slaves off-world at about 10,000 wupiupi a head. Anakin designs a **signal jammer** that he says works so well "you won't even be able to find yourself".

## Ch. 4–5 (l.624–907)
- Djas Puhr and the seeker tail Anakin into the market. Anakin builds the jammer into a carved jinapoor necklace and gives up his savings, the money meant to free his mother.
- He shakes the tail through the cantina (Mars Guo and Teemto Pagalies are there) and a drainage pipe. Djas Puhr shoots one of Sebulba's rivals in passing.
- Dorn, disguised as a Jawa, grabs Sebulba's stun-set blaster, stuns a ronto and sets off a riot, then rides under Sebulba's speeder.

## Ch. 6 (l.909–975)
- Pala, disguised as a Jawa, throws buckets of ganno juice that blind the seeker's olfactory sensors and Djas Puhr's visor.

## Ch. 7–8 (l.977–1588): the Podracer joyride
- Anakin flies **Sebulba's own orange racer** (Collor Pondrat Plug-F Mammoths) with Kitster. He spears the seeker on the **energy binders**: 0 to 100 km/h, impact at 150, and the droid is fused and blown apart. Binder contact paralyses a human limb for hours.
- He bursts through the hangar wall at about 230 km/h. The reinforced ram-engines punch through, and part of the roof comes down. **Gondry takes the binders and is thrown 30 m; he survives** ("I guess"). Dorn steals Sebulba's speeder and runs down Djas Puhr.
- Khiss hangs onto a Split-X vane. Anakin finds the hidden **flame-vent weapon** and burns him off. He blasts a market with jet wash and **flame-vents Gardulla and her Gamorreans**.
- Sebulba chases in **Brant Rumble's** crimson racer and plays chicken while firing his blaster. **Anakin reads the bluff in slow time ("time seemed to slow")** and throws Sebulba's goggles and spanners into Rumble's intake. One engine explodes and the cockpit is dragged at 500 km/h. Sebulba survives.
- Insight: Sebulba's ram-reinforced engines are slow to accelerate, a weakness that sets up TPM. Anakin flies deliberately badly so no one guesses a human is driving.
- Sebulba's view: "Only one human in the galaxy had ever successfully piloted a Podracer… never won a race." He rules out Anakin as not that reckless.

## Ch. 9–10 (l.1590–1855)
- Gardulla confronts Vansitt. The tracker shows static because of the jammer. Gardulla has Vansitt **detonate Pala's implant, and the jammer blocks the command**. Pala is hidden with the Ghostlings, wearing perfume, and her clothes are scattered by Jawas.
- A ship arrives tomorrow night if the money is raised. Anakin masks his scent with hubba gourd. Shmi silently knows, and asks if he has money: "I gave it all away."
- **The cube dream:** "No one can help you… You must open the cube!" "From the inside." The cube is warm, and Anakin thinks "Live creatures give off heat." Set-up for the Sith holocron.

## Ch. 11 (l.1858–1919)
- Jabba tells Sebulba to stop relying on technology and use a spy, then sends for **Dorn**: "I'll kill him if he fails." NEXT: *Capture Arawynne*.
