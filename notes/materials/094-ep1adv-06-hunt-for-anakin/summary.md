- Full read (l.1–1919). No Jedi appear.

- **Anakin (about 9):** pilots Sebulba's unfamiliar racer through a hangar wall and town. Kills the seeker droid with the energy binders. Wins a game of chicken against Sebulba by Force-slowed perception, wrecking Brant Rumble's pod (Ch. 8). Builds a working implant jammer that blocks a detonation command (Ch. 9–10).

- **Sebulba:** dodges a heavy blaster at racing speed. Survives a 500 km/h one-engine crash.
- Khiss: one-shots a giant mora beetle.
