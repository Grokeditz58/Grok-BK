## Thesis

SWTOR scaling has been inflated. The six-bullet conclusion (Section 6) says:

- the Hero of Tython (HoT) and the Outlander should be scaled separately;
- the Outlander does not scale above Vaylin and "perhaps not even Arcann", because of Valkorion's aid;
- Valkorion is a top-tier rival to OT/DE Palpatine but "cannot easily consume planets without preparation", and his mindscape fights do not carry over to physical fights;
- Valkorion has roughly the same power-level from the Revan novel through EOO (Echoes of Oblivion), with "little growth";
- Revan is "genuinely a threat" to Valkorion;
- the HoT vs Vitiate on Dromund Kaas is a "true Grand Master Luke Skywalker-level champion" against Valkorion with a vast portion of his power.

## Arguments (numbered)

**Section 1 — Author intent**

1. Author and developer statements (Charles Boyd, Drew Karpyshyn, Hall Hood) are "as official as it's going to get" for reading what the writers intended. The supporting scans were Discord images, now UNCLEAR.

**Section 2 — The Outlander is not as powerful as we think**

2. **(2A) The Outlander and the HoT are not officially the same character.** The author cites Hall Hood: "…The Emperor was all but destroyed by the Hero of Tython on Dromund Kaas…". Hood and Karpyshyn write the HoT as by far the most powerful Jedi. Against that, Boyd, who wrote KOTFE/KOTET and favours class parity, says a Trooper fighting "on his own terms" should have "better-than-even odds". So KOTFE's Outlander is written for parity, not as the HoT.
3. **(2B) Valkorion deserves "at least partial credit" for the Outlander's wins.** Valkorion can intervene "AT ANY GIVEN MOMENT", and the mindscape is purely mental. Further points:
   - Arcann overpowers the Outlander, and Valkorion blocks with his hands.
   - Valkorion's shield and empowered bash appear in the gameplay.
   - Boyd's Vaylin statements are discussed.
4. **(2C) The Outlander's own showings are good but not top-tier.** The evidence offered:
   - the "best of your breed" interview line;
   - beating the Surro avatar on Ziost;
   - Boyd calling Valkorion the Outlander's "most powerful opponent";
   - the Marr and Satele bios.
   
   Conclusion: "the Outlander still starts off as a tier 7 or 8 combatant. But he's no Yoda."

**Section 3 — Valkorion is not infinitely more powerful than the main characters**

5. **(3A) Karpyshyn on Vitiate.** The author cites YouTube 13:57, that Vitiate has "only a certain level of power" short of "world destroying abilities", and a killermovies Q&A ("pretty solid… very insecure"). A Hall Hood tweet is reported lost (killermovies thread 640212, UNCLEAR). A feats list follows.
6. **(3B1) Ziost required preparation.** This comes from the Boyd passionatelycasual interview (2018-06-30, ep. 47): Valkorion needed "a little bit of prep work". The author also cites the "only Tuesday" remark. **(3B2)** Boyd says in-universe info can be "suspect" and "window dressing". **(3B3)** Revan novel: Revan says "He's stronger now… I don't think he'll go that far". The author reads this as the Emperor not casually consuming Dromund Kaas.
7. **(3B4) Mindscape battles are not literal.** Wins and losses in mindscapes do not translate to physical combat.
8. **(3C) No growth between the Revan novel and KOTFE.** Five reasons are given, built on Nathema quotes. They include Nyriss's line that Vitiate was "stronger than any Sith" before him, the chorus voice, and his mind-brush with Scourge. The author then answers four counters:
   - Scourge's resistance;
   - the Encyclopedia "amassed" wording;
   - Force drain is non-permanent (he cites his own "A Case for the Neo-Antediluvians" blog);
   - Ziost and the mindscape feats.

**Section 4 — Revisiting threats to Valkorion**

9. **(4A) Revan is a genuine threat.** The arguments:
   - The long Revan-novel fight passage, broken into five parts.
   - Comparisons with Karpyshyn's lightning and the Dark Empire Sourcebook on Force rage.
   - Scourge's vision of a "million possible futures".
   - Encyclopedia lines that Revan "nearly assassinated" Vitiate and "approached within striking distance".
   - The ERC's "undefeatable", reinterpreted.
   - HoT class-story Scourge lines, and Revan's "If I'd had you with me…".
   - Boyd's "legitimate threat" list: Revan, the Outlander, Vaylin, Dramath.
   - SoR dialogue options.
   - Backus calling Revan and Vitiate "two of the most powerful Force users, like ever".
   - Boyd: "it would've been bad".
   - The mental war, with Boyd saying this "may have also influenced Vitiate's transition to Valkorion".
   - Meetra's "small stream" analogy.
10. **(4B) The Hero of Tython is GM-Luke-level.** The evidence:
    - Accolades from Orgus Din, Satele, Braga and Scourge.
    - The mission log says Vitiate was only "temporarily" weakened.
    - The "final apocalyptic duel" framing.
    - Vitiate's "immense power but lacks the purity of will" assessment of the Hero.
11. **(4C) Vaylin.** Boyd calls her "probably the most powerful figure" short of Valkorion. The author links Azronger's Vaylin blog (Material 007, t4504).

**Section 5 — Preemptive counters**

12. HoT vs Yoda: two possible paths are discussed.
13. Valkorion vs RotS Palpatine is a quote war.
14. Who is the Outlander? The HoT was weakened after Act III, so the identity question is head-canon.
15. **Mindscapes and "Balloon Theory".** Valkorion converts tethered spirits into extra energy (xSupremeSkillz and DarthAnt66's theory). This explains his "sub-planetary-level offensive power but galactic-level defensive power": dark siders "cling to physical reality".

## Rebuttals raised in response (Menace blog — Material 004)

Point by point, using the numbering above. Labels are TheGalacticMenace's positions (FORUM OPINION). The new quotes he brings are listed in Material 004.

- **#1 author intent →** He argues source material outranks author statements. He cites a FFG Force and Destiny Core Rulebook entry on Nathema/Medriaas as G-adjacent lore.
- **#2 Outlander ≠ HoT →** He argues the Outlander *is* the HoT, for four reasons:
  - the prophesied champion and successor to Revan;
  - the male Knight in all promotional art;
  - the de facto main character;
  - the strongest class story.
- **#3 Valkorion's credit →** The Arcann concessions come from the dark-side route, where the Outlander refuses Valkorion's power. Only the Outlander can stop Vaylin, per Valkorion: "Only you are strong enough to stop this". Boyd concedes the Outlander's skill closes the gap.
- **#4 Outlander "tier 7 or 8" →** He replies that the Outlander outgrows the HoT, citing a scan that 003 itself used (now a dead link). He says it "caps the Outlander over the Hero of Tython". He adds the Six Gods as the "greatest test" (see Material 005), the "Demon Saviour" prophecy, and EOO lines.
- **#4 also: post-Act-3 class parity →** Other protagonists matching the Outlander is a budget/game abstraction. Only one protagonist canonically goes past Act 3. Operations are solo. Orgus Din's spirit lines show the HoT growing.
- **#5 Karpyshyn/Hood →** These opinions are "useless" against the game's text.
- **#6 Ziost prep →** The "prep" is a possession cycle (an unattributed quote he says Boyd agreed with). Full Valkorion could do it off the cuff, citing:
  - the HoT and Marr lines;
  - the Vitiate codex "not strong enough to usurp all life on Yavin 4";
  - Lana's "more immediate. More final.";
  - the Yavin vision image;
  - the original game plan scan.
- **#7 mindscapes →** Mindscapes would favour Valkorion. He uses a Beyond Shadows / Abeloth analogy and a D6 transfer-essence quote. He adds that timestop is real (video timestamps).
- **#8 no growth →** He calls this "completely inarguable" the other way. He cites:
  - Encyclopedia lines: "amassing insurmountable power", "unfathomable", "ever-increasing";
  - Revan-novel feeding;
  - Boyd's word "initial" for Nathema;
  - the claim that Valkorion was split for 1,000 years through the Voice, Children of the Emperor and the Knights. On that reading the HoT beat under 50% of Valkorion.
- **#9 Revan a threat →** He gives a six-step re-read of the novel fight: Revan was overwhelmed "nigh-instantly" once Vitiate tried. He cites the ERC "undefeatable", Scourge's betrayal, his own reading of Boyd's threat list, Foundry Revan not being weakened, SoR being a mess, and the mental war being overstated.
- **#10 HoT GM-Luke-level →** Accepts the HoT is strong but says he beat a fragment ("under 50%"). He adds that Terror from Beyond is stated above the Vitiate the Hero faces.
- **#11 Vaylin <=> Outlander →** He reverses the order: Outlander > Vaylin, via the "ultimate combat test" and "greatest test" marketing lines.
- **#15 Balloon Theory →** Valkorion's "deeper reserves" mean he is whole and at full power, not drawing on tethered spirits.
- **Net counter-chain:** "Voice << Terror from Beyond << Revan << Arcann <<<< Vaylin <<< Outlander <<< Valkorion".

## Common rebuttals / weak points (ANALYSIS — not from the thread)

- **Reliance on developer social-media statements** (Boyd tweets and interviews, Karpyshyn Q&As, Hood tweets). These are not canon text under the Lucasfilm hierarchy, and some cannot be verified now because the tweets and screenshots are lost.
- **The Outlander ≠ HoT split** cuts against the game's own presentation, where any class becomes the Outlander. Opponents argue this is a selective choice to lower the ceiling.
- **"Prep work" for Ziost** sits awkwardly with in-game presentation and with codex lines, as 004 shows.
- **Balloon Theory** is a fan construct (acknowledged as the author's own theory).
- **Most evidentiary scans are now dead links**, so much of the blog can no longer be checked.

## Characters touched and implied scaling (FORUM OPINION)

- **Valkorion/Vitiate:** top tier and ~ OT/DE Palpatine, with no large growth. Not able to "easily" consume planets.
- **Revan (novel/SoR):** a genuine threat to Vitiate, below him.
- **Hero of Tython:** ~ GM Luke; beat Vitiate with a "vast portion" of his power.
- **Outlander:** tier 7–8 on his own, below Vaylin, possibly below Arcann.
- **Vaylin:** second only to Valkorion (Boyd).
- **Arcann:** around or above the unaided Outlander.
- **Marr, Satele:** high-tier support (bios).
