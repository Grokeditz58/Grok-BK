## Plot
1. **Planet Talas, Kamdon system (029–031).** Teal arched ruins / "garden." Aurra stalks; Droidekas (Colicoid destroyers) unfold around her (VRRR/CHNK). Ignites red saber (VMMMM). Two Quarren greet: "Welcome." / "Yes. Welcome to our garden, Aurra Sing." / "Do be careful." / "It's a rather dangerous place."
2. **Droideka "test" combat (032–035).** Unshielded Droidekas open fire (VDOW); Aurra deflects with red saber, acrobatic leaps. **Force blast (033):** crackling blue-white energy on open palm → purple/white concentric shockwave (KAWHAM) shreds a Droideka. Continues: "Colicoid toys! They're not the only danger here!" / "You lied to me! You summoned me here to kill me!" Quarren: "NO! NO! IT WAS ONLY A TEST!" Aurra: "I DON'T LIKE TESTS!" Quarren: had to be sure she's as good as stories; **if they wanted her dead, Droidekas would have been shielded.**
3. **Hire pitch — Tallet & Lekket (036–042).** Aurra: expensive trial; with Droideka money could have bought a planet. Quarren: already have one — standing on it; exile, miss Mon Calamari / Heurkea. Aurra impatient for money/job. **Intro (036):** speaker: "I am **TALLET**, and this is my egg-mate **LEKKET**." Once honored citizens of floating city Heurkea; most prosperous Quarren in Calamari system. Partner **Tikkes** (now Senator) siphoned funds into campaign; when exposed, Tikkes moved to eliminate them; also turned over old Hutt-smuggling records → Republic Guard raid on their engine factory on Mon Calamari.
4. **Flashback — Dark Woman raid (037–040).** Scanline holo/memory: Republic Guard storm factory; **a Jedi with them — the Dark Woman** (hooded; **pink/magenta–purple saber**). Lekket & Tallet flee through factory; Dark Woman deflects bolts; a deflected bolt penetrates a **toxic fuel cell**; acid-like fuel sears flesh. Caption (039): "MY POOR TALLET WAS CAUGHT IN THE FLOW…" Escape cruiser flees Mon Calamari. Balmorra labs: outlaw **Colicoid** scientists rebuild ruined body with bacta + bionics (caption 040: "SHE PAID outlaw Colicoid scientists…" — exact lettering). Buy "horrid" planet Talas; hide; dream of revenge.
5. **Target named (041–042).** Found the Jedi who helped Tikkes ruin them — want her death; Aurra would enjoy it. Holo of short-haired woman: renounced own name; "**…The Dark Woman!**" Aurra recognition: "The Dark Woman." Datapad with coordinates: Jedi nearby on **unclaimed planet at edge of this system**. Planet arranged to be sold to **Tikkes** — he arrives **tomorrow** to inspect. Kill Jedi + Tikkes together. Aurra: return soon for discussed payment.
6. **Ship — trophies + obsession (043–044).** Aurra's patrol/cruiser in space. Opens red-lined trophy case: **multiple captured lightsaber hilts** (+ small data object). Captions: "WHO COULD BELIEVE IT? AFTER ALL THESE YEARS. ALL MY SEARCHING." / "NOW, YOU FALL RIGHT INTO MY LAP." Holo of younger Aurra beside Dark Woman: "THE DARK WOMAN." / "JEDI. TEACHER." Crushes projector: "**ENEMY.**" "TOMORROW, I WILL KNOW JOY… FOR THE FIRST TIME IN MY LIFE." / "TOMORROW… YOU DIE."
7. **Jedi hunt team intercept (045–050).** Consular-class cruiser pursues Aurra's craft toward asteroid belt near green world. Adi Gallia: scouts trailed her; "let's get this over with." Ki-Adi: take her before asteroid belt. Comm: "AURRA SING! THIS IS KI-ADI-MUNDI… BY ORDER OF SUPREME CHANCELLOR PALPATINE, YOU ARE UNDER ARREST!" Proton torpedoes locked. Adi: she's receiving but not slowing. Ki: get ready to fire. **A'Sharad Hett (Force):** "NO! WAIT! SOMETHING IS WRONG! CAN'T YOU FEEL IT?" Ki presses attack; Adi/Hett cut off. Aurra already ejected **fighter** from cruiser; asteroid belt confused sensors; attacks from rear while power on front shields. Ki: divert rear shields—too late. Systems failure; shields down; engines gone/inoperable. Escape pod scramble. Aurra: "JEDI SCUM." Strafing pass; **cruiser detonates** (050 splash — Aurra's fighter banks away; planet below). Cliffhanger into #30.

## Feats / Force / combat
- **Aurra — lightsaber deflection** vs Droideka blaster volleys (032–034); single red blade; acrobatic mobility (leaps, dive-slash).
- **Aurra — Force telekinetic blast (033):** open-palm crackling blue-white energy → expanding purple/white shockwave destroys unshielded Droideka (KAWHAM). **Not** classic continuous Sith lightning stream; kinetic blast with electrical FX. Limit: used vs unshielded droid; Quarren note shielded Droidekas would have killed her.
- **Aurra — piloting / fighter ambush (047–050):** stores fighter aboard cruiser; uses asteroid belt to mask ejection; rear attack vs forward-shielded Consular; disables engines/shields; destroys cruiser. Callsign hatred: "JEDI SCUM."
- **Dark Woman (flashback 038–039):** magenta/pink-purple saber; agile leap; bolt deflection; accidental (deflected) toxic-cell rupture — **limits:** collateral harm to non-targets; does not prevent Quarren escape.
- **A'Sharad Hett — Force danger sense (046):** feels "something wrong" before Masters act; warning overridden by Ki-Adi's arrest urgency. Limit: junior Padawan sense not heeded in time.
- **Limits (hunt team):** Consular vulnerable once rear exposed; sensor confusion in asteroids; Palpatine-ordered arrest (legal frame) doesn't prevent ambush.

## Lore
- **Talas**, Kamdon system — Quarren exile world / "garden" of rocky spires; bought after Balmorra rebuild.
- **Tikkes** — Quarren Senator; embezzlement + framing via Hutt-smuggling records; arranged as co-target with Dark Woman.
- **Heurkea** — floating city, Mon Calamari / Calamari system.
- **Dark Woman (An'ya Kuro)** — former teacher of Aurra; sanctuary on unclaimed planet edge of Kamdon system; prior Republic Guard assist vs Quarren smugglers.
- **Colicoids** — Droideka makers; also outlaw scientists on Balmorra (bacta/bionics).
- Aurra trophy culture: case of stolen lightsaber hilts; personal vendetta > pure contract.
- Hunt authority: arrest order from **Supreme Chancellor Palpatine**.
