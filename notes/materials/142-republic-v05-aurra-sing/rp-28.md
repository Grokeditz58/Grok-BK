## Plot
1. **Coruscant trail (007–009).** Splash: capital of the Republic. Ki-Adi-named hunt team in art is Peerce + J'Mikel + Xiaan tracking "her." Peerce finds warm blood; can feel her despite cloaked presence. Peace-officer squad (5) — only **four** bodies; one missing (gave chase or pursued). Xiaan: beings joined the Force; J'Mikel comforts—she will be a great Knight.
2. **Split pursuit (010–011).** J'Mikel: circle around; Force guidance to trap wounded she-beast between Peerce and himself. Peerce finds missing officer bound in cables; asks who did this; officer's eyes reflect figures behind Peerce; Peerce spins, yellow/orange saber ignites (VMMMM).
3. **Aurra vs Peerce (012).** Aurra leaps red saber vs Peerce's orange/yellow blade amid industrial cables — ambush strike.
4. **J'Mikel arrives / Peerce dead (013–014).** Ionized air from saber duel. J'Mikel (purple) + Xiaan approach hooded figure: "PEERCE! MY FRIEND!" Reveal: Peerce slain (visceral stab; blood); Aurra in doorway with red saber: "HELLO, JEDI." / "LOOKING FOR ME?"
5. **Duel J'Mikel vs Aurra (015–017).** Named: "AURRA SING! AWAY, MURDEROUS BEAST!" She: heard of me—good; "ONE SHOULD KNOW HIS KILLER'S NAME!" Extended clash (KVASH/ZZOK/VMMMM); she is cut on the cheek—"YOU CUT ME." / "IT WON'T HAPPEN AGAIN." Licks blood; orange eyes; "I'M GOING TO ENJOY KILLING YOU, JEDI."
6. **J'Mikel killed — dual-blade trick (018).** During saber-lock, Aurra activates a **second, shorter red blade** behind her back; thrusts through J'Mikel's torso (GUH!); both red sabers lit over fallen Master. Xiaan: "NO! MASTER!"
7. **Xiaan spared with threat (019–020).** Aurra: Padawan how nice; Master joined the Force—"It's a good thing, in its way. Is it not?" (echoes Xiaan's earlier comfort words). Paid well to slay Master and friend; would have done it for nothing. Xiaan hasn't built first lightsaber yet; go mourn; learn well; someday Knight—"THEN AURRA WILL KILL YOU, TOO." Takes fallen sabers.
8. **Silent aftermath (021–022).** Xiaan tears; Aurra snarl/leave; Xiaan alone over bodies; ships depart past moon.
9. **Jedi Council (023–028).** Morning, Temple. Two Jedi slain in Temple's shadows. Padawan in physicians' care—months to free mind of horror. Yoda: why hate Jedi so? Depa: how so strong—two Jedi + peace-officer squad? Eeth: Dark Side must be strong. Ki-Adi-Mundi (Council, Tatooine prior meeting): neither Light nor Dark disciple—assassin, not random. **Dark Woman** interrupts—will track Aurra; was Aurra's teacher; Mace denies mission (remorse/uncertainty would guide her weapon; Aurra a ghost haunting her). Dark Woman denied. Ki-Adi volunteers in first teacher's stead; **A'Sharad Hett** volunteers—Aurra killed his father Sharad Hett; seeks justice not vengeance (Yoda probes; Ki knows his heart). Mace warns Dark Side arms open if wrong; Ki won't allow—charge is his; cruiser prepared. **Adi Gallia** joins for diplomacy + saber. Team: Ki-Adi-Mundi, A'Sharad Hett, Adi Gallia. "May the Force be with you."

## Feats / Force / combat
- **Presence cloaking (Aurra)** — Peerce: she cloaks presence well but he can still feel her (007–008). Limit: cloak imperfect vs skilled Anzati tracker.
- **Blood/Force tracking (Peerce)** — warm blood + Force sense; third-person narration of certainty.
- **Lightsaber combat — Aurra vs Peerce** (012): aerial red vs orange/yellow; Peerce dies.
- **Lightsaber combat — Aurra vs J'Mikel** (015–018): matches Cerean Master; takes cheek cut; kills via **concealed second short red blade** during lock (ambush, not pure Form mastery). Limit: needs deception/off-hand surprise; single-blade exchange was contested.
- **Trophy sabers** — collects victims' hilts (020).
- **Psychological warfare** — mirrors Padawan's Force-death comfort back at her; delayed-kill promise.
- **Council assessment** — power enough for two Jedi + squad; debated Dark Side vs "in between"; Dark Woman emotionally compromised (Mace).
- **Limits:** Aurra flees Coruscant after hits; does not finish Xiaan; Council response is pursuit team, not Temple lockdown shown.
