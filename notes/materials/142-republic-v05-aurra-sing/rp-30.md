## Plot
1. **Cruiser kill believed complete (051–052).** Jedi Consular detonates (KA-BOOM); Aurra’s fighter banks away. Captions: "JEDI CRUISER. WONDER HOW MANY WERE ABOARD." / "A PITY. ALL THAT FIRE. WON'T BE ABLE TO COLLECT THEIR LIGHTSABERS." / "SOMETIMES, A GIRL HAS TO MAKE SACRIFICES."
2. **Survivors in escape craft (053).** Adi: she thinks we're dead. Ki: might be yet—hold on. After blast: "She's good. **VERY** good." Comlink + thrusters out; drift until air runs out unless distress beacon heard. A'Sharad: another ship approaching!
3. **Tikkes’s Mon Calamari ceremonial ship (054–058).** Grapples pull Jedi shuttle aboard. Adi: Quarren soldiers—lower weapons; official High Council + Judiciary business. Guard: "SENATOR! THEY ARE JEDI!" Tikkes: "BAH! RAGAMUFFIN MAGICIANS!" Then flips to hospitality on learning Council status + A'Sharad son of **Sharad Hett**. Tikkes claims "unclaimed investment properties" inspection. Offers nearest Jedi outpost; Ki insists they accompany Tikkes homeward instead. Adi cites Republic law duty to aid distress. **Force (058):** A'Sharad asks why not go to outpost to keep chasing Aurra; Ki: if one followed mind not Force impulses; Adi: "I feel it, too… keep our eyes on the good Senator… crossroads where we shall meet our quarry is somehow linked to his path!"
4. **Dark Woman’s haven (059–062).** Jungle refuge planet; Skyfall meteor season. Captions: years of haven from Jedi life; refugee from life not memory—failure; "the beast whom I unknowingly unleashed"; "The assassin… Aurra Sing." Purple saber ignited; Council denied her chase—why not let her make amends; "Why do I feel she is near me now?" Feels Aurra’s approach; seeks shelter as Consular/ship + meteors streak in; crash-landing silhouette.
5. **Aurra on-world (063–065).** Gas-mask entry into burning wreck/corridor: "THIS MUST BE THE PLACE." Binoculars confirm Quarren intel; "I SHOULD BE PAYING THEM. I’VE WAITED A LONG TIME FOR THIS." Swoop (skull-mounted handlebars). Sees ship penetrate atmosphere: "Right on time… I hate a late funeral." / "I have flowers to arrange."
6. **Landing party — Quayd Runium (066–067).** Impact trauma / Class B seasonal meteor storm nourishes jungle. Ki finds purple mineral: pure unrefined **Quayd Runium**. Tikkes aide Tracton nearly spills expeditionary findings—Tikkes muffles him. Tikkes claims no storm until tomorrow; well protected—flamethrower clears native lizard; orders meson diagnostic scanners path cleared.
7. **Force shadow + snare (068–070).** A'Sharad: "A dark shadow skirts the edges of the Force." Adi concurs; Ki acknowledges—stay close. Jungle Quarren scout finds gold coins → snare yanks him (AAHHIEE). Tikkes: cut him down. **Adi Gallia:** "NO! WAIT! SOMETHING IS WRONG!" Mines detonate (BABOOM)—most scout squad gone. Ki (purple saber): "MINES! SOMEONE PLANNED IT THAT WAY!"
8. **Dark Woman appears — shot (071).** From ridge, purple saber; calls "KI-ADI-MUNDI! IS THAT YOU?" Adi: "THE DARK WOMAN! HERE?" Quarren guard (translated): "<SHE'S THE ONE WHO PLANTED THE MINES!>" Ki: "NO!" Guard fires (BVOW); bolt hits Dark Woman in chest; purple saber flies; she falls (FWAP).
9. **Cliffhanger (072).** Ki: "WHAT HAVE YOU DONE?!" / unsure if she lives. Off-panel: "DEAD NOW, DEAD LATER. IT DOESN'T MATTER. YOU ARE **MINE!**" Reveal: Aurra on swoop with long rifle: "AURRA SING WILL KILL YOU **ALL**, KI-ADI-MUNDI!"

## Feats / Force / combat
- **Aurra — starfighter ambush payoff (051–052):** destroys Consular; forgoes saber trophies ("sacrifices"). Limit: believes kill complete; leaves survivors.
- **Aurra — trap craft (068–070):** gold-coin snare bait + mines; Force-sensitive Adi senses wrongness before second cut-down; still delayed enough for detonation. Conventional explosives, not Force attack.
- **Aurra — sniper/swoop threat (072):** rifle aimed at hunt team; verbal claim on all of them.
- **Adi Gallia — Force danger sense (069):** "SOMETHING IS WRONG" before mine blast; physical holdback of Tikkes.
- **A'Sharad — Force shadow sense (068):** dark shadow at Force’s edges; confirmed by Adi; Ki slower to weight it.
- **Ki-Adi + Adi — Force path-sense (058):** quarry meeting linked to Tikkes’s path; choose stay with Senator over outpost.
- **Dark Woman — Force proximity sense (060):** feels Aurra near despite distance; purple saber; emotional regret over unleashing Aurra. Limit: Council denied chase; mistaken for mine-planter → shot by Quarren before fighting Aurra.
- **Ki-Adi lightsaber:** purple blade (070).
- **Adi / A'Sharad:** crimson/red blades shown ignited (071–072); A'Sharad Tusken dual-heritage gear.
- **Limits:** Jedi diplomacy buys Tikkes berth but not control of Quarren guards; Dark Woman ally-fire casualty; minefield attrition of escorts.
