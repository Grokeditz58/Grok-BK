## Feats / Force / combat
- **Aurra — sniper/swoop (073–075):** long rifle from ridge; red-saber blaster deflection while mounted; survives Adi’s Force-boulder wreck of swoop by leap. Limit: loses mount; does not stay to duel Adi immediately.
- **Aurra — trophy case thermal trap (078–080):** case booby-trapped with thermal explosive Adi Force-senses as province-obliterating; Adi still opens → mine/proximity trigger **KH-WHOOM** downs Adi. Conventional explosives + bait case, not a Force attack. Limit: Adi survives (089).
- **Aurra — hull infiltration (082–083):** enters sealed ship via **air-filtration intakes** near turbines; rifle-kills guard; states kill order Tikkes→Padawan→outside Jedi.
- **Aurra — Jar'Kai dual red sabers (084–088):** standard + reverse-grip shoto; aerial assault; headbutt/kick CQC; psychological warfare (father’s murder, Ki as shared teacher). **Force Push (085):** palm-emitted kinetic rings slam A'Sharad into machinery. Limit: overcommits/leaves opening—ends unconscious and briefly chained (089–090) before meteor chaos frees her (093–094).
- **Aurra — escape (093–094):** exploits Skyfall bombardment scramble; ship shields priority; recovered rifle; leaves Jedi without clean capture.
- **Adi Gallia — Force TK boulder (074–075):** lifts/hurls massive rock destroying swoop. **Force sense trap (078):** detects thermal booby-trap magnitude before opening. Limit: still opens case and is caught by ground mine (079–080); wounded but alive.
- **Dark Woman — dual purple sabers (077); Force-glow wound management (077):** yellow energy at chest wound after Quarren bolt; prioritizes Aurra threat over treatment. Identity: Ki’s **First Teacher**; Aurra’s former **student** teacher. Offers to take A'Sharad as student (092). Limit: fails to secure Aurra during storm (093–094).
- **Ki-Adi-Mundi — purple saber; Force path/urgency (075–077):** finds First Teacher via blood + Force presence; senses darkness on A'Sharad’s heart (091). Leaves Padawan choice free (093).
- **A'Sharad Hett — dual blades / Jar'Kai (073+):** father’s lightsaber among them (Aurra ID 084); Padawan braid under Tusken wraps (088); refuses Senator’s Republic-oath framing—oath only to master (081); defeats/captures Aurra momentarily then confesses kill-urge (090–091). Limit: Force-pushed, braid ripped, darkness admitted; asks release of vows.
- **Limits (arc):** Jedi cannot hold Aurra through environmental chaos; Council hunt ends without prisoner; Dark Woman’s student-link unresolved in field.

## Lore
- Dark Woman = Ki’s First Teacher; Aurra’s former master/teacher; self-assigns A'Sharad tutoring if Ki permits.
- Aurra keeps lightsaber trophies; recognizes Sharad Hett’s blade; claims shared past training under Ki briefly.

- Tikkes: Quarren Senator (Mon Calamari polity); tries to commandeer Padawan via Judiciary framing; fails; ship sealed but air intakes = vulnerability.
