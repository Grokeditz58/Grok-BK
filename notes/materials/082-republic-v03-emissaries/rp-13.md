## Plot
1. **The Dark Woman tests A'Sharad (p.5–14).** At the Jedi Training Center on Coruscant, the Dark Woman spars with A'Sharad Hett. He wields his father Sharad Hett's saber plus one he built himself, both red-orange. She taunts him about whether he is Tusken or Jedi, and he answers each taunt with a line of the Jedi Code. He gets her saber against her throat, and she goads him: "Kill me now!" He refuses. When he relaxes, she kicks him flat. Ki-Adi-Mundi ends the session as Mace Windu and Even Piell watch. Then she knocks him down again to teach him that "anyone can be an enemy."
2. **Aurra Sing (p.15–19).** The Dark Woman offers condolences for Sharad and fades away in a purple shimmer. Later she reappears to Ki, whom she once taught ("first teacher"). Ki confirms that Aurra Sing, the Dark Woman's former student, killed Sharad Hett and other Jedi. The Dark Woman dissolves into purple particles again.
3. **The Council Gathering (p.19–24).** The Council opens with a formal invocation. Adi Gallia briefs them on the Lannik war against the Red Iaro terrorists. Years before, Even Piell stopped a seven-man suicide squad alone, saving the Lannik Prince and Adi's parents (Corellian emissaries). The Red Iaro now want peace talks on Malastare, hosted by the Gran. Six Councillors are to act as arbiters, at the Judicial Department's request and with Chancellor Palpatine's endorsement. Mace returns Eeth Koth's lightsaber, ending their saber exchange under the "Concordance of Fealty."
4. **The night before departure (p.25–26).** A'Sharad hates being indoors on Coruscant. A youngling who says he "used to be a podracer" tells them about the Phoebos Run on Malastare.

## Feats (#13)
- **A'Sharad Hett dual-wields** two lightsabers against the Dark Woman, pushes her back and gets his blade to her throat (p.9–11). She admits she "beat down your defenses so easily" early on, but he holds back on purpose: "Because you are not an enemy, Master" (p.13). Caveat: she later says she could feel him holding back, and she floors him twice with kicks or sweeps (p.12, p.14). Most likely this was a test and she was not fighting all-out.
- **A'Sharad built his own lightsaber** from parts his father brought to Tatooine (p.6).
- **The Dark Woman's Force vanishing.** She dissolves into purple particles and is gone (p.15, p.19). Even Piell: "her Force powers are strong!" (p.15). Mace "I'd heard she was dead" (p.15).
- **Even Piell's backstory feat.** "Fighting alone, he stopped all seven of the murderers," saving the Lannik Prince and the Corellian emissaries (Adi, p.21). He "paid a high price for his bravery" (his facial scar).
- Ki on Aurra Sing: "Seldom have I seen one so skilled! ... Sharad Hett wasn't the first Jedi she's slain!" (p.18).

## Lore
- The Council chamber roll call names 12 members with captions (p.20): Yoda, Mace Windu, Yaddle, Saesee Tiin, Ki-Adi-Mundi, Adi Gallia, Oppo Rancisis, Even Piell, Eeth Koth, Yarael Poof, Plo Koon and Depa Billaba.
- The Malastare delegation is Ki, Even Piell, Mace, Adi, Plo Koon and Yaddle. Yoda says the other six stay home (p.22–23). Saesee Tiin offers to go ("Also me!") but stays behind; Ki wishes Tiin were their pilot (p.24). Yaddle's membership is confirmed in #14, p.39/41.
- Jedi saber exchange tradition: "When I lent you that weapon, you lent me your own, as is our tradition." It is formalized as the "Concordance of Fealty" (p.23–24).
- The Dark Woman taught Ki-Adi-Mundi and Aurra Sing. The Council already knows "the Sith have returned" (p.14), which places this after Maul's appearance on Tatooine (TPM), in line with Outlander. Note that the title-spread caption says "shortly before the Battle of Naboo."
- The Phoebos Run is named "after some mechanic who started the sport." Malastare is "one of the only worlds beyond the Outer Rim that still allows the races" (p.26). This conflicts with p.21's "Middle Rim."
