# Emissaries to Malastare TPB — back matter (p.137–144)

- Pages read: **p.137–144**, hand-read 2026-11-03 CST, in order, one image at a time.

## Cover gallery (p.137–142)
- **p.137:** Header "COVER GALLERY", with "Featuring the original comic-book series cover art by Mark Schultz and Jan Duursema." Masked A'Sharad Hett (yellow tunic, red saber) leaps at the white-haired Dark Woman (red saber) in front of a round starfield viewport. This matches #13's training duel.
- **p.138:** Mace (blue), Even Piell and Adi Gallia (red), and a hooded Jedi with a green saber (unidentified) on a rock in front of Malastare towers, with a pink moon and a podracer-like craft. Probably #14.
- **p.139:** Mace (blue) and Adi (red) with Even Piell, hanging from Sebulba's orange podracer above a stadium crowd. This matches the #15–16 podrace fall.
- **p.140:** Yaddle (orange-yellow saber) leaps onto an akk dog, with Ki-Adi-Mundi (**purple** saber) and Adi (red) on the palace steps. This matches #16.
- **p.141:** Depa (yellow hooded robe, red saber) and Mace (blue) on the swoop among Vrblthers and industrial pipes. This matches #17.
- **p.142:** Mace (blue) leaps over a giant red akk dog, with Depa (red) below, a Hutt in the box and skulls. This matches #18; "JD" signature.
- Six covers for six issues, in issue order. None of them is captioned with its issue number.

## Endpaper and back cover (p.143–144)
- **p.143:** Inside back cover: a gold/sepia duotone circle crop of the p.142 cover on a starfield and purple band. There is no text.
- **p.144:** Back cover: "GRAPHIC NOVEL/SCIENCE FICTION", "STAR WARS: EMISSARIES TO MALASTARE." Blurb: "In a time after the events of Episode I — The Phantom Menace, half of the Jedi Council travel to the exotic world of Malastare to negotiate a peace treaty between two of that planet's warring factions. But the whole affair is being manipulated by a secret third party out to assassinate the Jedi! ... This story features many familiar characters from The Phantom Menace and continues the ongoing adventures that bridge the gap between Episodes I & II!" Dark Horse Books and Lucas Books logos, over a duotone of the p.137 art.

## Page-order / file notes
- No missing or duplicated pages. p.137–144 are about 1024×1557–1582, slightly taller than the story pages.
- The back cover says "after the events of Episode I," which agrees with the story (#15 p.54, 61) and **contradicts** the p.3 intro caption "shortly before the Battle of Naboo."
- The "Dark Horse Books" logo and circle-frame trade dress on p.143–144 probably come from a later printing than the August 2001 first-edition indicia on p.4. Same mixed-printing pattern as 081.
- Saber colors on the covers: Ki is purple (p.140) and Yaddle orange-yellow (p.140). Interior colors differ, so do not cite cover colors as canon.
- No letters page, reader names or addresses.
