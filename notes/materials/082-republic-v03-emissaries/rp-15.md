# Star Wars (1998) #15 — Emissaries to Malastare, Part 3 (EM p.49–70)

Pages hand-read: EM p.49–70 (22 pages). No issue-cover pages are reprinted between chapters. #16 opens with a full-page splash at p.71.

## Plot
- **p.49–53.** In the palace of the Gran Protectorate governor in Pixelito, the delegation waits. A Gamorrean guard shoves A'Sharad Hett, and he ignites his twin sabers. Ki-Adi rebukes him, and Yaddle defuses the guards by flattering them. Plo, Mace and Adi talk about walls, danger and the Gran "Protectorate's" oppression of Malastare's natives.
- **p.54–57.** Governor Gujdim Wiphshun opens the summit, which is moderated "as per our agreement with Supreme Chancellor Palpatine." Senator Aks Moe attends. Prince R'cardo of Lannik snubs Even Piell. Myk'chur Zug, leader of the Red Iaro, arrives. Piell reminds him that Piell gave him his scar in the throne-room fight, where "six died… Only one being walked out. The other ran."
- **p.58.** Mace opens the talks. A'Sharad, Plo and Ki all sense "impending disaster."
- **p.59–63.** Final race of the Vinta Harvest Classic, called by announcers Fode and Beed. Sebulba torches Teemto Pagalies' pit crew with his vents. The finalists are introduced as "survivors of the Boonta Eve Classic on Tatooine." Nugtosh starts the race: "And they're off!"
- **p.64–65.** Zug offers to "concede" the Palesia territory. The Prince explodes at advisor Hutar and calls the Jedi "freaks and mystics." Mace adjourns, and Hutar lends the Jedi his personal shuttles.
- **p.66–69.** On the shuttles, which are flying over the podrace's first-lap route, Hutar reveals a thermite bomb vest: "For once, the terrorists are right… At least I'll die with you." A guard fires, Piell deflects the shot, and the Jedi leap off before both shuttles explode (WHAKOOM / BLOOM / BA'WOOM). The Lannik guards fall to their deaths.
- **p.70.** The Jedi fall into the path of the podracers, and a startled Sebulba sees them. Cliffhanger.

## Feats (#15)
- **Every Jedi delegate plus A'Sharad survives a thermite suicide bombing.** They Force-leap clear of two moving shuttles at altitude as the bombs go off (p.68–69).
- **Mace Windu** catches and carries **both Even Piell and Adi Gallia** in mid-air free-fall after the blast, while holding his lit saber (p.69; also the #16 opening splash, p.71).
- **Even Piell** deflects a point-blank blaster bolt the instant it is fired: "Too late, old man!" / "Oh, no?" (p.68).
- **Precognition.** A'Sharad, Plo Koon and Ki-Adi sense the danger before it happens (p.58), and Yaddle questions the scheduling (p.66).
- **Yaddle** calms hostile Gamorrean guards with words alone (p.51–52): "Trouble also, could Yaddle give them. Better this way, it is."
- **A'Sharad** draws twin lightsabers at once (p.50).
- **Tech and hazard.** Sebulba's vent flamethrower scorches a pit crew (p.60). Thermite vest bombs destroy shuttles in the air (p.67–69).

## Lore
- **Timeline.** The racers are "survivors of the Boonta Eve Classic on Tatooine," Sebulba has "guild demerits" from Tatooine (p.61), and Palpatine is Supreme Chancellor (p.54). The story is therefore **after** TPM. This contradicts the TPB intro's "shortly before the Battle of Naboo" (p.3).
- **Vinta Harvest Classic finalists (p.61):** Sebulba, Aldar Beedo, Teemto Pagalies, Mawhonic (the local Gran favorite), Durundo, Wan Sandage, Elan Mak, Wasser Barer and Dankko Verrimuch.
- The Gran Protectorate rules Malastare. A'Sharad Hett is a Tusken-raised Padawan to Ki-Adi.
- Even Piell is Lannik, and he once served alongside the palace guard. Hutar is the old royal advisor and a Red Iaro sympathizer.

## Page and continuity notes
- **Naming (resolved).** The Red Iaro leader is called "Myk'chur Finux" / "friend Finux" on p.35 and "Zug" / "Myk'chur Zug" on p.56–57. p.80 (#16) gives his full name as **Myk'chur Finux Zug**, so the different names are not an error.
- The advisor is lettered "Hutar" on p.64–67. My raw notes for p.55 read "Mutar Zash" as his military advisor, so that is possibly a different person or a misreading.
- No page-order problems in p.49–70.
