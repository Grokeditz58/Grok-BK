# Star Wars (1998) #17 — Emissaries to Malastare, Part 5 (EM p.93–114)

Pages hand-read: EM p.93–114 (22 pages). No chapter cover reprinted. Ends on a cliffhanger; p.115 (wordless splash) opens #18.

## Plot
- **p.93.** Captions establish Nar Shaddaa (Y'Toub system, moon of Nal Hutta), the "Smuggler's Moon," its mass "increased... three times over" by docking towers.
- **p.94–95.** Thug boss Kydod Vurd (lettering ambiguous, "KYDOD" p.94/98) moves to kill the alewoman Aunuanna for selling ale on his street.
- **p.96–98.** Hooded Mace intervenes. A mind trick forces one thug to walk off ("I-I cannot help m-m-myself," p.97). Vurd complains the slaver guaranteed his "thuglings" were immune to Jedi mind tricks; Mace: "Some of them are immune, it seems." Vurd whistles up a mob.
- **p.99–102.** Depa Billaba, who has been shadowing Mace, drops in. The two cut through dozens of armed thugs (some colored as wielding lightsabers, p.101). Vurd flees on a Gamorrean's shoulders swearing revenge.
- **p.103–106.** Aunuanna says she saw a third Jedi that day. A hooded Jedi who hid his presence from both Council members reveals the beasts are in the lowest levels, where "games of death" are held, and gives them a datapad map. He is **Quinlan Vos** (p.106), supposedly on Ryloth; his Padawan waits for him. "What I've learned could shake the Galactic Senate!" He vanishes.
- **p.107–109.** Aunuanna's baby, "Angel," was taken by the Jedi long ago. She gives them her Angel's father's old swoop and warns of slavers, Kessel pirates, Gank killers and Vrblthers. Mace pays her.
- **p.110.** Swoop dive into the depths. Mace explains his people's honor pledge to akk dogs, "partners in the herding of the grassers."
- **p.111–112.** Below, the Circus Horrificus (with Gargonn the Hutt) crowns Glamaxon Tvurl, "the Blade of Barab" — the announcer raises his wounded arm and it tears off. Then: "Surprise double feature day!" "Release the akk dogs! Let the carnage begin!"
- **p.113–114.** Vrblthers swarm the swoop; Depa loses control and they crash through the Circus Horrificus banner toward the arena.

## Feats (#17)
- **Mace Windu:** mind-trick compulsion on a thug while others are trained/bred to be immune (p.97–98). Clears a ring of a dozen-plus armed thugs, including saber-armed ones, with Depa (p.100–102).
- **Depa Billaba:** shadows Mace undetected across Nar Shaddaa ("I knew someone was shadowing me!" p.101 — he sensed it but couldn't pin it); fights the mob alongside him (p.100–102); pilots an old swoop down Nar Shaddaa's shafts (p.110–114).
- **Quinlan Vos:** cloaks his presence from **two Jedi Council members** at close range (p.103–104, Mace: "Your powers are great... to cloak your presence even from us"); vanishes in steam (p.106).
- **Hazard scaling:** Nar Shaddaa's depths are "a place that can teach even a Jedi fear!" (p.105). Vrblther swarm downs a two-Jedi swoop (p.114).

## Lore
- Depa was rescued as a baby and raised by Mace; she is his former Padawan and now a Council member (p.101–102). She calls him "Master."
- Mace's people herd "grassers" with akk dogs (p.110). Homeworld still unnamed.
- Nonsentient Species Transportation Act (p.104). Vrblthers (p.109, 114). Gargonn the Hutt, Circus Horrificus (p.111).
- Vos crossover: supposed to be on Ryloth with his Padawan (p.106), tying into the Vos/Twilight arc.
- "With the return of the Sith" (p.101) again places this after TPM.
