# Star Wars (1998) #14 — Emissaries to Malastare, Part 2 (EM p.27–48)

22 files. The issue boundary is inferred: the splash on p.27 starts a new chapter, and p.48 closes on "every last one of the Jedi delegates will be dead!"

## Plot
1. **Phoebos Memorial Run elimination heat (p.27–34).** The two-headed Troig announcers Fode and Beed call the race over Malastare's methane geysers. Aldar Beedo and Wan Sandage plot to kill Sebulba under cover of the fumes. Elan Mak, a Fluggrian, tries to shoot Beedo to avenge his father, "lord of the Fluggrian crime families." By't Distombe crashes on a shortcut over the gas pool. Sebulba wins in his home city of Pixelito. He needs the win because he was suspended "for indiscretions during the Boonta race on Tatooine" (p.34).
2. **The conspiracy (p.34–37).** Gran Senator Aks Moe, a Ffib priest/inquisitor and Myk'chur Finux, the scarred Lannik Red Iaro leader, scheduled the summit during the race so the press is distracted. Aks Moe tells Finux: "Your Red Iaro terrorists have the full cooperation of the Malastare Protectorate." Finux is pleased that Even Piell is coming: "Only once [have we met]--although I'm sure that he'll remember me."
3. **Arrival (p.38–41).** The Gran senators Baskol Yeesrim and Aimlee Teem greet the Jedi and push Dug "workers" on them as porters. The Jedi refuse ("We can carry our own"). Piell and Yaddle sense they are being watched (telepathic balloons). The shadowers are a Red Iaro gunman and a Ffib sub-priest inquisitor.
4. **Ambush (p.42–45).** Piell and Adi confront the two shadowers and draw blaster fire, including a heavy repeater blast (THOOM). Ki and A'Sharad cut the gunmen off, and the attackers flee. Gran guards claim "we saw no one."
5. **Suspicion (p.46–48).** Mace: the blasters "were allowed" into the spaceport. Plo: "I sense great danger here!" Aks Moe orders the bunglers' deaths ("And be sure to hide the bodies"): "By the time that the moons rise tomorrow... every last one of the Jedi delegates will be dead!"

## Feats (#14)
- **Even Piell and Adi Gallia** leap clear of a heavy blaster explosion that blows apart a stone stair, then deflect bolts in mid-air (p.44).
- **Ki and A'Sharad** (twin red blades) force two armed assassins to flee just by showing up (p.44).
- **Telepathic sensing**: Piell, Yaddle and Plo exchange danger sense in thought balloons (p.41–42). Plo: "I sense great danger here!" (p.47).
- **Podracing tech**: Fode says the Malastare run is "not as fast as Tatooine's" but is "one of the most environmentally varied runs in professional podracing" (p.32). The racers take shortcuts over burning gas pools, and one pod explodes (p.31). The "eyewitness probe" camera droids can be blinded by the fumes (p.30).

## Lore
- Malastare: a Gran Protectorate with an indentured Dug underclass, jungle cities next to a desert (A'Sharad: "I smell the desert," p.40), methane geysers, and the city of Pixelito.
- Sponsors: Vinta Harvest Ale ("Tradium-brewed") and Hoar Chall Engineering's "Ring Drive Jell" lubricant (p.28, 33).
- Ffib: a fanatical priesthood/bureaucracy with "inquisitors" (p.37, 41).
- Malastare is only the third world A'Sharad has visited (p.40).
