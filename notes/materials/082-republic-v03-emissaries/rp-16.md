# Star Wars (1998) #16 — Emissaries to Malastare, Part 4 (EM p.71–92)

Pages hand-read: EM p.71–92 (22 pages). p.71 is a full-page splash with no logo; it is not a reprinted cover.

## Plot
- **p.71–77.** Free-falling over the podrace course, Mace, who is carrying Even Piell and Adi, grabs Sebulba's podracer. Sebulba tries to beat them off with a wrench, and Mace cuts it in half. Meanwhile Yaddle (with A'Sharad's help) flies the surviving shuttle, carrying Ki and Plo. Aldar Beedo ("ace assassin," p.75) is on a contract to kill Sebulba. Mace loses his grip, and Adi snags a podracer cable ("Got it!"). A blaster shot from Beedo or Sebulba cuts the cable, and the three fall again.
- **p.78–79.** Yaddle catches all three in the shuttle. The Jedi work it out: the guards were Red Iaro wearing House of Lannik uniforms, and they waited for the cam droids so the killing would look as if the Prince had ordered it. The goal is to topple the Prince and hand Lannik to the Red Iaro "and whatever power is backing those terrorists." Piell: Lannik's "riches unmined, its forests uncut."
- **p.80–82.** Red Iaro seize the Prince in his quarters. A Ffib priest is with them, and he names "Myk'chur Finux Zug." Zug sets akk dogs on the Prince.
- **p.83–87.** Ki-Adi and A'Sharad crash through the window, and the rest of the delegation arrives. Mace recognises the akk dogs as animals from his own homeworld, bought on Nar Shaddaa's black market. Yaddle stabs an akk in the eye. Mace calms another one with a touch: "What is done can sometimes be undone!" The Ffib priest is caught.
- **p.88–90.** Zug escapes by jetpack and is sucked into a passing podracer's engine (p.89). A'Sharad: "Unfortunate."
- **p.91–92.** Senator Aks Moe has the priest dragged off before he can talk. Adi points out that the akks needed inside help. Moe cancels the talks. Mace sends the others back to Coruscant and heads for Nar Shaddaa to trace the akk smuggling.

## Feats (#16)
- **Mace Windu** hangs one-handed from a racing podracer while carrying **Piell and Adi** at podrace speed, and he cuts Sebulba's swung wrench in half with his other hand (p.72–75). He holds on until he is exhausted ("Can't… hold on…", p.75). He also says outright that he *could* "levitate down," which is a Force levitation claim (p.72).
- **Adi Gallia** catches a podracer's cable in free fall while all three Jedi are hanging off her (p.76).
- **Yaddle** flies a shuttle through the podrace traffic and catches three falling Jedi (p.73, 78). She leaps onto an akk dog and stabs its eye (p.86).
- **Ki-Adi-Mundi and A'Sharad** burst through a palace window and fight trained Red Iaro. A'Sharad blocks a flail and strikes (p.83–84).
- **Mace** pacifies a trained killer akk dog by touch. It is calm and being petted by p.91 (p.87–91).
- **Plo Koon** stops the Ffib priest from escaping (p.87).
- **Tech/hazard.** A podracer engine intake shreds a jetpacking Lannik (p.89). Akk dogs are bred for "feral efficiency" and fight in pairs, one feinting while the other strikes (p.85).

## Lore
- **Akk dogs** come from Mace's homeworld, which the text does not name here; Haruun Kal per later canon. "Of all the worlds… akks can be bred on only one." The text also says Mace's people share a Force bond with them (p.86, 92).
- Nar Shaddaa's black market sells smuggled akks (p.85).
- **The Ffib priesthood** is involved with the Red Iaro, and Aks Moe silences the priest (p.87, 91).
- Lannik's untapped resources are the motive (p.79).
- **Saber colors as printed:** Mace blue, Ki-Adi violet/white, A'Sharad twin red, Adi red/pink, Yaddle orange, Plo yellow/orange. The coloring varies from page to page.
- Myk'chur Finux Zug dies (p.89).
