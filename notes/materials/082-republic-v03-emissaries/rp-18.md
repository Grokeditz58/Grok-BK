# Star Wars (1998) #18 — Emissaries to Malastare, Part 6 (EM p.115–136)

Pages hand-read: EM p.115–136 (22 pages). p.115 is a wordless full-page splash (cover-style art, no logo); not a reprinted cover. Story ends "END" on p.136.

## Plot
- **p.115–118.** Mace and Depa fight Vrblthers off the out-of-control swoop ("We are the intruders here... We must respect the local fauna!" p.116), then crash through the roof of the Circus Horrificus arena (p.118).
- **p.119–121.** Gargonn the Hutt (half his head missing from a wandrella on Circarpous V; Hutts regenerate, "in a hundred years it should be as good as new," p.120) demands their execution. Mace names himself and Depa as Jedi High Council members investigating violations of the "Non-Sentient Creatures Transportation Act" (p.120). Gargonn feigns cooperation. Malakili (the arena's beast handler) is present.
- **p.121–123, 130–132.** In the stands, Vilmarh "Villie" Grahrk bets that the Hutts will kill the Jedi "within 15 millicycles." "Khiss—investment advisor to the stars" raises him for a nameless client, who turns out to be **Sebulba** (p.131–132), winner of the Phoebos Classic (p.123). When the time runs out, the bettors riot.
- **p.124–125.** Gargonn has Malakili open the gates; cybernetically harnessed akk dogs flood the arena. "A thousand pardons... someone has accidentally released some crazed beasts."
- **p.126–129.** Mace explains his people's pledge ("traditions and home, family and herd") and turns his saber off to speak to the akks through their Force bond (p.127). It fails — the beasts have been mutated in body and mind, "their ties to the Force and my people forever broken" (p.129).
- **p.129–130.** Lightsabers barely hurt the akks. A twin strike ("As one, Depa! Strike!") wounds one and gives the pack pause.
- **p.132–135.** The riot draws off the guards. The Jedi vault off the akks into the sponsors' box, take the Hutts hostage, and Gargonn blames the Circus Horrificus. Depa calls in their orbiting cruisers and hopes the Chancellor can lean on Nal Hutta.
- **p.135–136.** Depa: "this game is definitely over." Mace: "You think so, friend Depa?... I hope so... I truly do." Last panel: Villie running from the angry mob. END.

## Feats (#18)
- **Mace & Depa** fight off a Vrblther swarm on a crashing swoop and survive the crash through the arena roof (p.116–118).
- **Mace** tries Force telepathy with the akks, "in the language... of the Force" (p.127). It fails because they were altered (p.129). This is a limit, not a feat.
- **Anti-feat / durability:** Depa: "The one I struck has barely slowed down! Lightsabers have little effect on those thick hides!" (p.129). A simultaneous two-saber strike only wounds one: "Hurt—but not down!" (p.130). Useful for akk/beast durability arguments; compare Mace calming a natural akk by touch in #16 p.87.
- **Mace & Depa** leap from the arena floor up the akks' backs into the elevated sponsors' box (p.133). A saber deflects or cuts a Hutt's blaster ("SPANG!", p.133). Mace holds a Hutt by a cable around its neck while keeping the guards back (p.134).

## Lore
- Mace's people: akk dogs herd "great grasser herds" and protect "from beasts and bandits"; a youth pledge; a Force bond with the akks (p.126). Homeworld still unnamed in the text.
- Hutts regenerate slowly (p.120). Nar Shaddaa is "a Hutt-governed sector" (p.121). Nar Shaddaa Sporting Commissioners (p.119).
- Cameos: Vilmarh Grahrk (Villie, Devaronian), Malakili, Sebulba, Gargonn. The Phoebos Classic was won by Sebulba (p.123).
- The act's name differs between pages: "Nonsentient Species Transportation Act" (p.104) vs "Non-Sentient Creatures Transportation Act" (p.120).

## Page notes
