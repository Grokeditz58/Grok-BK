# Republic Vol. 3 — Emissaries to Malastare: summary of findings

Sources: Star Wars (1998) #13–#18 (1999–2000), collected in the *Emissaries to Malastare* TPB (Dark Horse, August 2001). Hand-read page by page. Citations are "EM p.N" = the N-th TPB file. Issue mapping: #13 p.5–26, #14 p.27–48, #15 p.49–70, #16 p.71–92, #17 p.93–114, #18 p.115–136.

## Arc in one paragraph per issue
- **#13:** The Dark Woman tests A'Sharad Hett (twin red sabers) at the Jedi Training Center. He beats her to the throat and refuses to kill her. Ki-Adi confirms that Aurra Sing killed Sharad Hett. Adi briefs the Council on the Lannik/Red Iaro war, and six Councillors (Mace, Ki, Adi, Piell, Plo, Yaddle) plus A'Sharad are sent to arbitrate peace on Malastare.
- **#14:** The Phoebos Run heat (Sebulba wins). Gran Senator Aks Moe, a Ffib priest and Red Iaro leader Myk'chur Finux Zug plot to kill the Jedi. There is a spaceport ambush, and Piell and Adi survive a heavy repeater blast.
- **#15:** The summit, with the Vinta Harvest Classic final running at the same time. Royal advisor Hutar detonates a thermite vest on the Jedi shuttles. Everyone leaps clear, and Mace catches Piell and Adi in free fall.
- **#16:** Mace hangs off Sebulba's racing pod with both of them. Yaddle catches the falling Jedi with a shuttle. The Red Iaro seize the Prince with akk dogs. Mace calms an akk by touch. Zug dies in a podracer intake. Aks Moe silences the priest. Mace heads alone for Nar Shaddaa.
- **#17:** On Nar Shaddaa, Mace mind-tricks a thug. Depa Billaba, who has secretly followed him, joins a mob fight. Quinlan Vos, cloaked even from both of them, gives them a map to the depths. They take an alewoman's old swoop, Vrblthers swarm it, and they crash toward the Circus Horrificus.
- **#18:** The arena. Gargonn the Hutt releases cyborg-harnessed, mutated akk dogs. Mace's Force-bond appeal fails, and sabers barely cut the akks' hide. A betting riot (Villie, Khiss and Sebulba) gives the Jedi an opening to leap into the Hutt box and take the sponsors hostage. Republic cruisers are called in. END.

## Top feats
1. **Mace Windu: the free-fall and podracer sequence** (EM p.69–77). He catches **Even Piell and Adi Gallia** in midair after a shuttle bombing, then hangs one-handed from Sebulba's pod at race speed while carrying both. He cuts Sebulba's swung wrench in half with his other hand and says outright that he "could just levitate down" (p.72). He tires ("Can't… hold on…", p.75).
2. **Quinlan Vos cloaks his presence from two Council members** (Mace and Depa) at close range. Mace: "Your powers are great, brother Jedi, to cloak your presence even from us" (p.104). Vos then vanishes (p.106).
3. **The whole delegation survives a thermite suicide bombing** by Force-leaping off two shuttles at altitude (p.68–69). Even Piell deflects a point-blank bolt as it is fired (p.68).
4. **Mace calms a trained killer akk by touch**: "What is done can sometimes be undone!" (p.87). Compare #18, where his telepathic Force-bond appeal **fails** on akks whose minds were altered (p.127–129).
5. **Mace's mind trick works on a thug** whom the slaver had "guaranteed" to be immune; "some of them are immune, it seems" (p.97–98).
6. **Mace and Depa** clear a ring of dozens of armed thugs, some colored with sabers (p.100–102). Later they leap from the arena floor up the akks' backs into the elevated Hutt box and take Gargonn hostage (p.133–134).
7. **A'Sharad Hett** dual-wields against the Dark Woman and gets his blade to her throat (p.9–11). She was testing him, not fighting all-out, and she floors him twice.
8. **Even Piell's backstory feat:** fighting alone, he stopped seven assassins and saved the Lannik Prince and the Corellian emissaries (p.21). "Of the eight who fought… six died. Only one being walked out. The other ran" (p.57).
9. **Yaddle** flies a shuttle through podrace traffic to catch three falling Jedi (p.73, 78), and stabs an akk dog's eye (p.86).

## Limits / anti-feats
- **Lightsabers vs mutated akk dogs:** "The one I struck has barely slowed down! Lightsabers have little effect on those thick hides!" (Depa, p.129). A twin strike only wounds one: "Hurt—but not down!" (p.130).
- Mace's telepathic bond with akks fails against mind-altered beasts (p.127–129).
- Mace's grip on the podracer gives out (p.75). A Vrblther swarm knocks the swoop out of control (p.114).
- The Dark Woman beats A'Sharad's defenses "so easily" at first (p.10) and floors him twice.

## Character bios (from this book)
- **Mace Windu:** "senior councilor of the Jedi High Council and acting director for this mission" (p.56). His people herd grassers with akk dogs and share a Force bond with them; youths pledge to protect "traditions and home, family and herd" (p.126). The homeworld is not named in the text. Blue saber.
- **Depa Billaba:** Council member and Mace's former Padawan. Mace saved her life as a baby and raised her (p.102). Red/pink saber in this book. Dry humor ("To whom do we send the bill?", p.99).
- **Even Piell:** Lannik, with a facial scar from the seven-assassin fight. Gave Zug his scar. "My heart will ever belong to my homeworld" (p.55).
- **Adi Gallia:** Corellian emissaries' daughter; Piell saved her parents (p.21). Briefs the Council on Lannik.
- **Ki-Adi-Mundi:** A'Sharad's master; the Dark Woman was his first teacher.
- **Plo Koon:** danger sense ("All walls hold danger," p.52). Stops the Ffib priest (p.87).
- **Yaddle:** pilot, diplomat, akk-stabber.
- **A'Sharad Hett:** son of Sharad Hett, Tusken-raised, Ki's Padawan. Built his own saber and also carries his father's.
- **Quinlan Vos:** on Nar Shaddaa tracing a lead, "supposed to be on Ryloth" with his Padawan. "What I've learned could shake the Galactic Senate!" (p.106).
- **Villains:** Senator Aks Moe (Gran), Myk'chur Finux Zug (Red Iaro), a Ffib priest, Hutar (royal advisor turned bomber), Kydod Vurd (Nar Shaddaa thug boss), Gargonn the Hutt (half his head gone; Hutts regenerate, "in a hundred years… as good as new," p.120).
- **Tholme does not appear** anywhere in p.1–144.

## Lore
- Twelve-member Council roll call on p.20. The "Concordance of Fealty" saber-exchange tradition (p.23–24).
- "The Sith have returned" (p.14, 101). Palpatine is Supreme Chancellor (p.54). The racers are Boonta Eve survivors (p.61). The back cover says "after the events of Episode I" (p.144). **All of this contradicts the title-page caption** "shortly before the Battle of Naboo" (p.3).
- The akks can be bred on only one world, Mace's (p.86). They are smuggled via Nar Shaddaa's black market in violation of the "Nonsentient Species Transportation Act" (p.104), called the "Non-Sentient Creatures Transportation Act" on p.120.
- Nar Shaddaa: a moon of Nal Hutta in the Y'Toub system, the "Smuggler's Moon," a "Hutt-governed sector" (p.93, 121). Vrblthers live in its depths. The Circus Horrificus runs pit fights there.
- Cameos: Sebulba, Aldar Beedo, Teemto Pagalies, Malakili, Vilmarh Grahrk.

## Page integrity
- 144 of 144 files viewed in order. No missing or duplicate pages, and no order problems.
- p.3 is a 2048-wide title spread. On p.125 the art is printed reduced inside a wide margin, with nothing cut off.
- Lettering and continuity quirks are kept as printed. Malastare is "Middle Rim" (p.21) vs "beyond the Outer Rim" (p.26). The race is the "Phoebos Run" (p.26–27), the "Vinta Harvest Classic" (p.59) and the "Phoebos Classic" (p.123). "principals" [sic] (p.92). Vurd's first name lettering is uncertain (Kydod). Mace is colored with a red saber in p.129 panel 2. Thugs are colored with sabers on p.101.
