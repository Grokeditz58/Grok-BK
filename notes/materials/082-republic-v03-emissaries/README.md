Page-order notes:
- No missing pages, no duplicates and no order problems.
- p.3 is a single 2048-wide spread file. p.1–4 and p.137–144 are about 1024×1557–1582. All story pages are 1024×1536.
- On p.125 the art is printed reduced inside wide margins. The file is full size and nothing is cut off.
- Timeline conflict, kept as printed: p.3 says "shortly before the Battle of Naboo," but the story (Sith returned, Palpatine Chancellor, Boonta Eve survivors) and the p.144 back cover place it after TPM.
- Other text quirks: "Middle Rim" (p.21) vs "beyond the Outer Rim" (p.26); three names for the race; "principals" [sic] (p.92); two names for the transport act (p.104/120).

Only notes and short quotes are committed. No images or comic files. This TPB has no letters pages, so no reader names or addresses appear.
