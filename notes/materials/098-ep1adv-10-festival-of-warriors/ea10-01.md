# EA10 Festival of Warriors: notes

## Ch. 1–3
- Boss Nass comes back from inspecting the statues at the sacred place. That matters at the end. Private Tattogg reports that Jar Jar is working in an arena kitchen under **Brass Marshoo**, a retired Grand Army general. Jar Jar sneaks a faa fish; the recyclopper fish serves as the garbage disposal.
- **Boss Nass lifts Jar Jar one-handed by the collar.** Jar Jar is hiding from Fassa's life-debt devotion, though she has done his laundry. Tarpals has warned him that Nass is protective of his only niece. Jar Jar is smitten ("Mesa nobody"), then: "Mesa gonna enter da Big Nasty… un mesa gonna win!"

## Ch. 4–6
- The arena holds 20,000+ spectators. **The Big Nasty Free-For-All**: 436 entrants, 2 stun-only weapons each. The stages are the gulli-brawl (218 gullipuds), the gulli-throw, the vine climb to the portal, a swim, swamp, the watchtower, a cliff dive, and back.
- Boss Nass won 3 years running in his youth and routinely places in the top 50. **Tarpals** has been top 10 at the last six festivals.
- Rep Teers reports droid tracks on the beach. Nass offers extra points for disabled "mackineeks".
- **Tarpals:** springs off a rival's shoulders for a somersault catch, sprays a gullipud's spit at an attacker, and makes a rebound dunk.
- 86 fail the throw, leaving 131 finalists in the text (218−86 = 132, an arithmetic slip).
- **Jar Jar catapults himself off the flex pole** and ends up above Tarpals. Fassa takes the maintenance lift and shoves Tarpals: "Dis is da Big Nasty!"

## Ch. 7
- **Fassa** scares off 30 faynaa with a shout and passes Tarpals swimming. Tarpals rides a hohokum.
- **She defuses a mine.** Mines are banned by both Gungans and Naboo. She lassoes the finned aquatic droid with a plant and shoves it into a fissure as it auto-destructs, sheltering behind a boulder. She takes its propulsion cylinder, then gets out of a mudpit with no help from a hrumph.

## Ch. 8–9
- Tarpals: the Shore Patrol commander demands a wrestle ("Paonga style or Umberbool style?") and Tarpals flips him into the swamp, then takes the kaadu. Over the ravine he **hangs from a peko peko's leg** (6 m wingspan) after stumbling into her nest. He reaches checkpoint 2 first.
- Moppo Dop, a pro gulli-ball player, calls him a cheater. Jar Jar vouches for him and is laughed at. "It no gotta be like dis, friend." **Tarpals pins Moppo in a blink; 60+ witnesses can't say how he moved.** Moppo takes it back.

## Ch. 10–11
- Fassa: "Mesa was concern-ed dat yous was gonna paste Moppo Dop permanent-ly." Her cliff dive puts her in an undertow and into a cavern with an alien sub.
- **She picks the airlock with her belt buckle. Two droids shoot each other when she ducks.** The third fires a torpedo at the arena ("You should have shot me"). She tackles it, chops away its blaster and rams it into the ceiling. She flips override switches, and the torpedo climbs out of the lake and bursts high in the atmosphere.
- **Fassa wins the Big Nasty in the captured sub.** Tarpals is 2nd, **Jar Jar 13th**, Boss Nass 48th.

## Ch. 12
- **Captain Swagg**: blue skin, red eyes, red uniform, a pirate with a camouflage-netted ship 2 km from the Gungans' sacred temple ruins. The droid attack was a decoy, and he means to steal the statues. Sets up EA11.
