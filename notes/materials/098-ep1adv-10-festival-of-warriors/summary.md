# EA10 Festival of Warriors: summary

Full read (1330 lines). Gungans only, with Fassa as the star.

## Feats
- **Fassa:** mine defused; 4 battle droids taken down in all (1 by fissure, 2 by friendly fire, 1 by hand); a torpedo diverted; wins the Big Nasty.
- **Tarpals:** acrobatic catch; throws the Shore Patrol commander; rides a peko peko; blink-speed takedown of Moppo Dop; 2nd place.
- **Boss Nass:** one-handed lift; 48th place (3-time champion in his youth).
- **Jar Jar:** pole catapult; 13th of 436. A real feat for him.
