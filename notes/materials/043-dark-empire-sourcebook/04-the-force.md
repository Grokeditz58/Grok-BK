## New Force Powers (p. 72–76)
### Rage (Control)
> "When the Jedi leaves this state, she gets a +10 modifier to all Force skill rolls for a period of time equal to the time spent in rage." — p. 72
> "Example: While preparing to transfer his life to a new clone body, Palpatine expects an attack from Luke Skywalker. ... Palpatine’s roll is 44, so when Luke enters the chamber, Palpatine instantly makes from his rage and confronts Luke ..." — p. 72
> "When a raged person uses transfer life, their original body is instantly and totally consumed by the Dark Side, often bursting into unholy blue flames." — p. 72

### Create Force Storms (Control, Sense and Alter)
> "“The ability to destroy a planet is insignificant compared to the power of the Force. “ - Darth Vader" — p. 72
> "Control Difficulty: Heroic Sense Difficulty: Heroic Alter Difficulty: Heroic. ... +15 for a base of one kilometer, and +2 to difficulty for every kilometer diameter in size. Modified by damage: +5 per 1D of damage." — p. 72, 74
> "Effect: This is perhaps the single most destructive Force power known. This power allows the Jedi to twist the space-time continuum to create vast storms of force. ... Capable of creating annihilating vortices, the storms can swallow whole fleets of spaceships or tear the surfaces off worlds." — p. 74
> "When the Force user attempts to create a Force storm, the Jedi must determine the diameter and the amount of damage (the damage dice are capital scale). If the Jedi fails any of the rolls, the storm is summoned with the desired damage, but it attempts to consume the summoner." — p. 74

### Doppleganger
> "This power creates a doppleganger of the person using the power. The doppleganger is an illusion, but to those who interact with it, it will seem real. ... the doppleganger registers as normal on all droid audio and video sensors. ... The doppleganger acts with half the skill dice of the person using the power." — p. 74
- (ANALYSIS) Luke (p. 10) and the Emperor (p. 38) both list doppleganger.

### Force Harmony
> "It can act as a shield against the powers of the Dark Side, giving an extra 5D for each Force user involved to resist the effects of powers called upon by Dark Side servants." — p. 74
> "Example: Leia and Luke Skywalker try to disrupt Palpatine’s Force storm, which is destroying the Republic’s fleet. ... Leia spends a Force Point and links Luke and the child with the Light Side; her control roll is 47 and her sense roll is 36. ... If both of these rolls exceed Palpatine ‘s highest skill roll when he summoned the Force storm, then Palpatine ‘s control over the Force storm is severed." — p. 74–75

### Drain Life Essence
> "Sense Difficulty: ... Heroic 1 million to 10 million" (victims) — p. 75
> "1 million to 10 million—One Force Point per 6 hours and +4D+2 to all Force skills" (drained one week to one month) — p. 76

### Transfer Life
> "Specially Prepared Clone Host Body Easy Recently Dead Body Moderate Live Willing Host Very Difficult Live Unwilling Host Heroic" — p. 76
> "Effect: This power allows the character to transfer his or her life energy into another body. The key to immortality itself, this is one of the most difficult and evil of all Dark Side powers. ... Though theoretically possible, it is not yet known what the effect on an unborn fetus would be." — p. 76
