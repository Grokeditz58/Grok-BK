# Dark Empire Sourcebook (WEG 1993) — summary of power-scaling findings

Read in full 2026-11-03 CST: 135 PDF pages (printed pp. 1–133 + covers). Printed page citations.

## Top findings
1. **Emperor's D6 stats are the highest in the book.** "Type: Jedi Master"; lightsaber 13D; "Control: 15D+2 Sense: 17D Alter: 15D+1"; "Force Points: 40 Dark Side Points: 50 Character Points: 85" — pp. 32–34. For comparison, Luke (p. 11) has lightsaber 11D and Control 13D+1 / Sense 11D+1 / Alter 10D+2 with 6 FP.
2. **The Emperor's fleet-scale and long-range power.** He can use the Force to control whole fleets and destroy enemies from a distance (p. 34). He was disembodied for "over a year" (p. 33). The book says "Even Ulic Qel-Droma would be envious" (ch. 2).
3. **New dark side powers.** Create Force storms that can "swallow whole fleets ... tear the surfaces off worlds"; Force harmony gives +5D per extra user; drain life essence gives up to +4D+2; transfer life (pp. 76–80). Force storms can "shatter the fabric of space itself" (Ch. 4).
4. **The Eclipse superlaser.** It is "much more powerful than the units used on the Death Star ... shatter the most powerful planetary shields and sear whole continents in a flash" (p. 92). Stats: "Scale: Death Star", "build a charge of 1D per minute up to 8D. Current reactor can only generate 11D total per day" (p. 92). Planets are rated "10D-20D (Death Star scale)" (p. 130). (ANALYSIS) So by the rules a single Eclipse shot burns a continent but does not destroy a planet.
5. **The Universal Energy Cage** "blocks Force energies ... with an effectiveness of up to 15D" (p. 129). It was built by Umak Leth for Jedi captured in the Purge.
6. **World Devastators.** "Neither Umak Leth nor his technicians have any idea what the upper limit to a Devastator’s growth is" (p. 115). Silencer-7: 3,200 m long, hull 8D+1 (p. 117). They were beaten by the Master Control Signal, not by firepower (p. 115).

## Other notable items
- Boba Fett: blaster 9D, FP 5, armor "+4D/+3D", "most lethal set of personal battle armor", Sarlacc escape "I made an exit" (Ch. 3).
- Vima-Da-Boda: Control 12D / Sense 12D / Alter 11D, FP 8 (Ch. 4).
- Emperor's guards: Sovereign Protectors are C2D/S1D/A1D; Adepts Gwellib (lightsaber 9D+2, C7D+1/S9D+1/A8D) and Savuud (C8D/S5D+2/A8D+1) (Ch. 4).
- The Citadel garrison is "three legions of stormtroopers, 400 Sovereign Protectors and 600 Sentinels". The Emperor keeps "collection of lightsabers taken from Jedi he has killed" and "at least a dozen clones" (p. 82).
- Feat (Luke): "Only the skill and special abilities of Luke Skywalker prevented all hands from being killed in the crash landing" of the Liberator (p. 98).
- Thrawn led the mission to kill the six Jedi on Outbound Flight (Hextrophon lecture, p. 114).
