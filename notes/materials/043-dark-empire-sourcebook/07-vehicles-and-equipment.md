## Universal Energy Cage (p. 128–129)
> "During the Great Purge of the Jedi Knights, it was much easier to capture them than to keep them imprisoned. ... The Jedi often used their Force powers to break free. ... Some way had to be found to keep even the most powerful Jedi Master from escaping." — p. 128–129
> "Working from the little information the Emperor gave him about the nature of the Force, he came to realize that the Force was an energy field. Rather than block it, it would be more efficient to disrupt it with more energy, he reasoned." — p. 129 (Umak Leth)
> "Game Notes: Energy cage encloses prisoner In a force field with a Strength of up to 15D ... Somehow, the cage emanates a special type of energy that blocks Force energies and similar mental energies with an effectiveness of up to 15D." — p. 129
> "It is currently unknown what the specific limitations of the energy cage are, how strong a prisoner it could hold" — p. 129
- (ANALYSIS) 15D Force-blocking is above Luke's Alter 10D+2 and Control 13D+1 (Luke was held in one on Byss in the comic), but below the Emperor's Sense 17D.

## Planetary Shields / superlaser (p. 130)
> "Instead of weakening a shield, the superlaser is able to pierce through it by using a coupled neutrino charge. This neutrino charge not only plunges through the shield, but it penetrates the mantle and lower levels of the planet." — p. 130
> "Notes On Planets: A typical habitable planet has a structural rating of 10D-20D (Death Star scale). Alderaan had no shields of any kind, so it was utterly vaporized. A shielded planet that is overcome by a superlaser may “merely” have its entire surface burned off or split into several pieces." — p. 130
- DefenStar 5000: "6D Death Starscale protection", 100 km² per shield, 5D backup (p. 130).
- (ANALYSIS) Eclipse superlaser max 8D (p. 92) and Death Star scale vs planet 10D–20D. By the book's own rules, a single Eclipse shot does not reliably destroy a planet; it is a continent-searing/shield-breaking weapon, matching the p. 92 text.
