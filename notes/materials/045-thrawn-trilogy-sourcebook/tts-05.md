# Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook, notes part 5: PDF pp. 53–66 (book pp. 56–69), Ch. 3 powers (end); Ch. 4 Remnants of the Empire (start)

## Ch. 3 (cont.)
- **Farseeing:** past or present accuracy 50/75/90/100%, future 10/25/50/75% by margin.
> "the future is always fluid, always in motion, never set until it becomes the present" — p. 57
- **Sidebar "Jedi Twins"** (Na'al interviewing Leia):
> "I 'touched' their minds quite regularly during my pregnancy … And that's when one of the babies — I'm still not sure which — actually 'touched' back." — p. 57
> "They have a bond between them, a link in the Force … They are so strong, so rich in the Force. … They'll never be alone. Even … when they're apart." — p. 57
- **Force Lightning (rules, verbatim):**
> "These lightning bolts cannot be parried or dodged. … A Jedi armed with a lightsaber can use the lightsaber combat power to block force lightning … This power can be dissipated with the absorb/dissipate energy power" — p. 58
> "the damage is 1D for each 1D of alter the user has. The victim resists damage with their Strength; armor is useless against force lightning." — p. 58
  - (ANALYSIS: by these rules, C'baoth's Alter 11D to 12D gives 11D to 12D lightning.)
- **Inflict Pain**, and **Control Mind**: dark side 1 target Very Easy up to 9–15 targets Heroic; light side max 4–5 targets Heroic. "may not be used on droids or computers."
- **Enhanced Coordination** (verbatim table): 1–10 Very Easy; 11–100 Easy; 101–500 Moderate; 501–5,000 Difficult; 5,001–50,000 Very Difficult; "51,000-500,000" Heroic.
> "This power was often used by Emperor Palpatine to increase the fighting ability of his troops, mentally driving them on … Joruus C'baoth currently uses the power to increase the abilities of Grand Admiral Thrawn's troops." — p. 59
  - Gives +1D per 3D in three chosen skills; only works on willing targets.
- **Telekinetic Kill:** "collapse the trachea, stir the brain, squeeze the heart" (with Mara's attempt on Thrawn quoted).
- **Dim Other's Senses** table.
- **Force Scream (verbatim):**
> "a wave of hatred, amplified and fueled by the dark side, that is capable of smashing through mental and physical defenses with ease." — p. 60
> "The Force scream causes damage equal to the dark Jedi's alter skill to all beings within 50 meters, including the dark Jedi who unleashes this mental energy." — p. 60
- **Sidebar "The Teachings of Joruus C'baoth":**
> "A Jedi uses the Force for knowledge and defense, never for attack? A platitude for the simpleminded. … I am beyond such things." — p. 60

## Ch. 4: Remnants of the Empire (p. 61 on)
- "At the decisive Battle of Endor, the Jedi returned to aid the Alliance in the form of Luke Skywalker and his father Anakin."
- After Endor, stormtroopers are "scarce". The NR reduced the Empire "to a quarter of its original size".
- **Thrawn:** "Once, a dozen Grand Admirals served"; "the only alien to be admitted into the Grand Admiral brotherhood"; he won "the title of Warlord". He destroyed the species behind the real sculpture "Because of this". "the man was a genius."
> "The Emperor was feared. Thrawn was respected and trusted." — p. 63
> "Forging an uneasy alliance with the mad Jedi Master Joruus C'baoth, Thrawn could coordinate attacks with a precision virtually unheard of in the history of galactic warfare." — p. 63
- **Thrawn stats (verbatim):**
  - Sluis Van: tactics: fleets 12D+1, tactics: capital ships 11D, art 12D, command 12D+1, command: Imperial Navy officers 13D, bargain 12D+1, blaster 9D+1, dodge 9D. FP 6, DSP 20, CP 52.
  - Katana: FP 5, DSP 21.
  - Bilbringi: "tactics: fleets 13D … tactics: sieges 12D … Command: Chimaera crew 15D+2". FP 4, DSP 25.
- **Fiction "In the Unknown Regions":** the VSD *Iron Fist* (Captain Ferob, ~3 years under Thrawn) at UR41-284:
> "I want you to level everything within a seventy-kilometer radius of those coordinates without touching the coordinates themselves. … The coordinates bound the village cluster's museum district." — p. 64
  - (ANALYSIS: a VSD bombardment with precise area control over a 70 km radius.)
- **Pellaeon:** a Corellian who lied about his age to enter the Academy; as an ensign at Gavryn he hid in the planet's magnetic pole to beat pirates. Stats: tactics: capital ships 9D, ISD piloting 9D+2, DSP 11. At Bilbringi his willpower is 5D*, "reduced due to the effects of Joruus C'baoth's mind-tampering powers". Whereabouts unknown after Thrawn's death.
- **Captain Brandei** (*Judicator*): from Mantooine; believes the ISD is "the mightiest vessel"; doctrine "the Imperial will sacrifice any number of lesser beings to ensure victory".
- **Sidebar "Grand Admiral Thrawn's Armada":**
  - ISDs "measure sixteen hundred meters", each "capable of carrying six TIE fighter squadrons". The Chimaera (Imperial II) carries a full complement.
  - The text calls the centerpiece "five Imperial Star Destroyers" but names Chimaera, Death's Head (Harbid), Judicator (Brandei), Inexorable, Stormhawk, and Nemesis. (ANALYSIS: six named; the diagram shows Stormhawk, Nemesis, Chimaera, Death's Head, and Inexorable.)
> "For the major assault on Sluis Van, Thrawn added … twelve Strike-class cruisers, twenty-two Carrack-class light cruisers, and thirty full squadrons of TIE fighters." — p. 66
- **PDF p. 65 diagram (viewed, book p. 68):** a five-ISD main battle line with the A-class freighter at the point; two close-support columns of six Strike cruisers each; two picket lines of 11 Carracks each. One TIE icon = "one TIE fighter squadron (12 TIE fighters)".
- **Captain Dorja** (*Relentless*): no casualties at Endor (stayed back); six generations of naval officers; plotted to seize the Empire before Thrawn returned. Failed at New Cov.
- PDF pp. 59 and 64 are full-page art.
