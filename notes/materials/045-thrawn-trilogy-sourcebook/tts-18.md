## Droids
- **Espionage droid** (MerenData in a protocol shell): hidden programming "will survive standard memory wipes … the program modules must be physically pulled". Languages 8D.
- **Guard droid:** Arakyd G-2RD.
- **MSE-6 mouse droid:** a Chadra Fan design; the Empire bought the entire run.
- **IT-0:** "apply maximum pain with minimal damage"; laser scalpel 2D, power shears 5D.
- **MD-5:** medicine 8D.
- **MN-2E:** floats as high as 75 m; used in the Palace Grand Corridor.
- **Viper probot:** hyperdrive pods (Arakyd Predator I, x2) "can scan ships as they make the jump to lightspeed, calculate probable trajectories, and follow after its quarry", good for three jumps. Sensors search 500 m.
- **Servant droid** SE4.
- **SPD Hound-W2:** search for transmitting devices 8D+2; arms extend 15 m.
- **Sidebar "The Slave Circuit/Droid Debate":**
> "The Katana fleet of dreadnaught warships was the ultimate test of the slave circuit solution. It reduced each vessel's crew by fourteen thousand … When the fleet disappeared with the help of those same slave circuits, the last proponents of the master computer solution were silenced." — p. 200

## Ch. 12: Vehicles (framed as the journal of Pollo Tipn, "roughly nine years after the destruction of Mount Tantiss")
- **AT-AT:** move 21 (60 km/h), body 6D; heavy lasers 6D, range to 3 km. "If it cannot see over it, walkers can usually crash through it."
- **AT-ST:** 90 km/h, body 3D; "blaster fire barely annoys scout walkers".
- **AT-PT:** an Old Republic design, "a large number … installed in the Katana fleet". Body 2D, twin blaster 4D.
- **Chariot LAV** (Uulshos QH-7): 11.8 m; coordinates about a dozen units; uplink to orbit.
- **Talon I combat cloud car:** 1,500 km/h, ceiling 100 km; 4D+2.
- **Freerunner** (KAAC): Kelliak went bankrupt after losing the Imperial contract; used by Bel Iblis. Anti-vehicle lasers 5D.
- **Hoverscout** (Mekuun Swift Assault Five): Covell pairs them with AT-ATs; heavy blaster 6D; ceraglass windscreens.
- **Landspeeder** (SoroSuub OP-5).
- **Mole miners:** plasma jets; "Grand Admiral Thrawn stole over fifty of the units from Nkllon" and burrowed through emergency hatches near the bridges of Sluis Van ships (cont.).
