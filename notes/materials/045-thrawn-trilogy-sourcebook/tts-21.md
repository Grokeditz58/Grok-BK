## Appendix: Tactics and Battles
- **Cracken Twist** (Motto Shemson narrator; Xyquine; Mon Cal watch commander Gingal): offsets the second digit of the exit vectors to throw pursuers off "by a couple o' light years".
  - "I never saw a fighter wing take out a Victory-class Star Destroyer before".
- **Battle at Ukio** (key Force-coordination feat):
> "Thrawn was able to cloak four small Carrack-class cruisers and well before the attack place them in Ukio's atmosphere, underneath the boundary of the planet's shields (50 kilometers above the planet's surface)." — pp. 244–245
> "Using the Force powers of Joruus C'baoth, the Empire aligned the Carrack cruisers to precise coordinates directly underneath the Imperial Star Destroyer in upper orbit … C'baoth maintained constant mental contact with the vessels' crews and controlled their movements and firing patterns to create multiple simultaneous firings." — p. 245
  - (ANALYSIS: C'baoth coordinates crews of multiple ships from orbit down to inside the atmosphere, with precision timing. The "super weapon" was a hoax.)
- **Sidebar "News From Ukio":** TriNebulon News reports casualty rumors "from the thousands to the millions". A Ukian official describes the "X'Cal", a winged-fire demon-beast of Ukian myth.
- **Siege of Coruscant:**
  - Drayson fought too high for the Golans. Bel Iblis pulled back; the ISD tractor emplacements registered "hundreds" of launches, and an ion beam found a cloaked asteroid.
  - "over 270 such 'empty launches'"; lowering shields would risk "killing millions of civilians".
  - Sensor-reflective dust plus a negative ion beam was a limited countermeasure.
  - Karrde revealed "a mere 22 asteroids".
- **Screen formation** (Wedge): ≥6 fighters swap positions; optional rule gives +2D to attack difficulty.
- **A-wing slash** (Bel Iblis): optional rule gives −1D to enemy maneuver.
