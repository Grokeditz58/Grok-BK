# Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook, notes part 12: PDF pp. 126–137 (book pp. 129–140), Ch. 6 end (Poderis–Yaga Minor); Ch. 7 Aliens (start)

## Planets (cont.)
- **Poderis:** population 17 million; day 10 hours; year 77 days.
- **Qat Chrystac:** radioactive lava seas; volcanic-glass mesas "several kilometers high"; lava "has a severe scrambling effect on all manner of computers and sensors".
  - Rebels buried a captured shieldship as a base. Imperial Radiation Zone Troopers fought "almost years" (sic) from "an older model siege platform"; "one of the few truly elite stormtrooper divisions left since the death of the Emperor".
  - Population 2,000 NR troops and about 1,500 Imperial Radtroopers; gravity 1.4.
- **Rishi:** star Rish; planets Risha, Rishal, Rishos. H'kig colonists live in swamp vales; avian Rishii live in the peaks. Karrde's fallback base; population 2 billion.
  - Sidebar "The H'kig Religion": H'kig, a martyr on Galand, executed after insulting the Viceroy. Followers emigrated "Seventy years ago" in two colony ships.
- **Sarka:** Sarkan Nova Rubies; Salyrini cave-city; population 4 billion.
- **Sluis Van:** "about 100 medium-sized or larger vessels in transit".
> "There were 112 transient warships in the system when Grand Admiral Thrawn launched his attack on Sluis Van. … Sixty-five of the warships had been pressed into cargo carriers" — p. 133
  - Perimeter battle stations "aren't much good against a full-blown offensive".
  - PDF p. 129: Sluis Van Shipyards Orbit-Dock V-475 diagram.
  - **Orbit Dock V-475** (Rendili StarDrive Space Dock): length 4,846 m; crew 9,500; hull 7D; shields 5D.
- **Svivren:** weapons banned; "Any violent actions on Svivren result in immediate and public execution"; population 8.5 billion.
- **Tangrene:** Ubiqtorate base with "one of the few crystal gravfield trap arrays near the New Republic border".
  - Defended by "a minimum of two Imperial-class Star Destroyers, and anywhere from 10 to 50 smaller vessels"; fifteen garrisons.
  - "The Tangrene people were completely wiped out" by order of the sector Moff (genocide). Population 290,000 Imperial troops.
  - (ANALYSIS: Bel Iblis's raid blasted the Ubiqtorate center despite these defenses; the text gives two ISDs minimum here vs. "three Star Destroyers in orbit" in the Bel Iblis entry.)
- **Trogan:** three moons, huge tides. Whistler's Whirlpool Tapcafe (Ortolan owner; geyser six times a day). PDF p. 132: Tapcafe map (art).
- **Ukio:** Abrion sector; population 7.5 billion; Overliege Monarchy. **Tol dosLa:** the Overliege's spokesman and heir (son of Gol dosLa).
- **Vaathkree:** gravity 1.8; living "vnals" (life-towns) and "Stonesingers" (children); population 6 billion.
- **Woostri:** NeuroSaav Model 608 Mega'puters in Gopsthal. HoloScan Database complex "thousands of kilometers across and several hundred stories tall"; NR War College branch; population 18 billion.
  - (ANALYSIS: an extreme official scale figure.)
- **Xa Fel:** KDY produces "fully 20 percent of the corporation's inventory of starship hyperdrive engines" there; acid air; population 16 billion.
- **Yaga Minor:**
> "The Old Republic used Yaga Minor as a launch point for the 'Outbound Flight' project, a valiant attempt to learn more about what lies beyond the galaxy." — p. 138
  - "some of the early prototypes for the Empire's torpedo spheres were tested at the Yaga Minor weapons range". The Yagai subspecies was "genetically engineered … as a labor source".

## Ch. 7: Aliens (p. 139 on; Na'al journal)
- "countless thousands — possibly millions! — of intelligent life-forms" (Scout Service advertisement).
- **Barabels:** 1.75–2.5 m; "They have even been known to shrug off light blaster bolt hits"; armor +2D physical/+1D energy. "Jedi Respect" story factor; the Barabel War legend, where Jedi stopped a species-wide war.
- **Bimms:** "Luke Skywalker noticed that the Bimms' weapon detectors did not register his lightsaber as a weapon"; five-part-harmony language; touch procession.
- **Defel** (wraiths): seen only in UV; from Af'El, orbiting the UV supergiant Ka'Dedus (cont.).
