# Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook, notes part 11: PDF pp. 114–126 (book pp. 117–129), Ch. 6 Planets (Filve–Poderis)

- **Filve:** self-contained interlocked climate-controlled mega-cities; Sienar and BlasTech branch offices; population 6 billion. (The text layer interleaves its stat block with Generis's: Filve is Dry/Urban/6 billion; Generis is Hot, Moist, 1.1 g, jungle, population 2,000, comm center.)
- **Generis** (Atrivis): NR comm nexus taken by Thrawn; Pash Cracken saved the Atrivis Communications Group. Temple ruins of an unknown vanished species; "hiridiu crystals" boost comm signals "by almost 30 percent".
- **Hijarna:** a world apparently "destroyed by warfare"; the black-stone Fortress of Hijarna, used by Karrde for the smugglers' alliance meeting. Pop-media theories include "aliens from another galaxy", a super-virus, and a "huge temporal rift".
  - (ANALYSIS: an unexplained planet-scale devastation; no power figure given.)
- PDF pp. 116 and 118: full-page art (no text).
- **Jomark:** C'baoth ruled from the "High Castle" on a volcanic rim above Rim Lake ("four hundred meters below"); alien-built, purpose unknown. Colonists settled "a few thousand years ago"; population 2.8 million. C'baoth has left.
- **Kashyyyk:** a "layered death trap"; Wookiee slavery under Imperial law; population 45 million.
  - Rwookrrorro covers "more than a square kilometer". "all of the wroshyr trees in the Rwookrrorro grouping are a single giant plant". Kshyy vines are "stronger than composite cables and self-repairing".
- **Mrisst:** MTSA; spirepines "thousand-meter-tall"; Thrawn's staging area for the Coruscant assault; population 3.5 billion Mrissi plus 6 billion students.
- **Myrkr:** "populated for nearly three hundred years"; trees with "high metal content effectively blocks sensor sweeps". Thrawn was drawn there by "an obscure reference he found years ago".
  - Hyllyard City: "a free-standing archway rose ten meters high … Witnesses reported that Skywalker used a lightsaber to topple the massive structure." (feat, p. 124)
- **New Cov:** eight domed cities, with entry vents admitting up to "a W-class space barge". Sentient plants eat harvesters. Biomolecules "cannot be synthesized". Sanctioned Imperial "tribute" raids; population 90 million.
- **Nkllon** (Athega): normal ships' hulls are "quickly peeled away by the intense heat", so shieldships are required. Day 3,527 standard hours (about 90 days); year 5 local days.
  - **Nomad City:** "an old Dreadnaught-class cruiser … fitted … on top of 40 captured Imperial AT-AT Walkers". Based on Lord Ecclessis Figg's plans for Miser. 51 mole miners stolen; population 5,000.
- **Obroa-skai:** library "said to contain the gathered knowledge of the galaxy"; population 73 million.
- **Ord Mantell:** founded "by Corellian colonists 12,000 years ago"; "countless billions of tourists a year"; Ord Mantell Fighter Wing; population 4 billion.
- **Ord Pardron:** "Ord" stands for "Ordnance/Regional Depot".
> "Even without planetary shields, the underground base at Ord Pardron can withstand sustained orbital bombardment." — p. 126
  - Population 68,000 NR troops.
- **Ord Trasi:** Imperial ISD yard; "since the return of Thrawn, the facility has concentrated solely on building Imperial Star Destroyers"; Wookiee slaves.
- **Pantolomin** (with Toloran, with its ice dragons, and Atloran zero-g camps; Panto Prime station): the *Coral Vanda* has eight casinos (Tralla Room, Safkin Room). PDF p. 125 is a Coral Vanda cutaway diagram. Lomins; population 1 billion.
- **Poderis:** near-perpendicular axial tilt, hurricane winds, 10-hour days, mesa archipelagos with starship-metal windbreaks. Thrawn used it as a lure for Luke.
