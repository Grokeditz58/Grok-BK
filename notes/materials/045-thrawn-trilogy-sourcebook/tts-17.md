## Ch. 10 end
- **"His Finest Work" ending** (p. 185): "And if I ever need your services again, I will not hesitate to clone you." "the Emperor threw vicious bolts of lightning from his hands and ensured that the galaxy's finest architect was forever a part of his last, greatest work."
- Three shielded armories (PLEX-4s, E-Webs).
> "It is known that there were four massive fusion reactors powering Mount Tantiss … Each generator put out enough power to run an Imperial Star Destroyer" — p. 185
- **Labyrinth:** "These chambers may stretch for kilometers below the surface … each mapping scan returns a different picture of the maze". The GM note says the labyrinth and crypt were built secretly by Palpatine, "including 'silencing' the work crews".

## Ch. 11: Equipment and Droids
- **Cloaking shield:**
> "The prototypes which came closest to actually working required more energy than a ship could produce. The bigger the ship, the more energy that was heeded." — p. 187
  - The Emperor ordered success "by the time the second Death Star was completed — or else". A "null field cape" absorbs active sensor radiation; "Just before the Battle of Endor … a working, cost-effective cloak prototype".
  - "Nothing gets into a cloak field, and nothing gets out. No sensors, no communications". Sluis Van: the *Nartissteu* decoy; "the soldiers almost got away with close to fifty capital ships" (p. 188).
- **Delta Source:** ch'hala trees with implanted tubes, "a giant, organic microphone system that had been in place since the Emperor had taken control of the Imperial Palace". Winter has "absolute recall".
- **Electronic lock breaker:** OutlawTech, 25,000 credits; defeats the Palace gene-scan/random-flux locks.
- **Holorecording macrobinoculars:** Neuro-Saav TT4; +2D beyond 100 m.
- **HoloNet:**
> "Hundreds of thousands of non-mass transceivers were connected through a vast matrix of coordinated hyperspace s-threads, or simu-tunnels" — p. 190
  - Palpatine shut down large portions so that "knowledge of the Emperor's atrocities would be slow to reach the public".
- **Jammer pack:** 150 m radius, or a whole network (e.g., the Imperial Palace comm system).
- **Life-form analyzers:** with an ISD computer, "can even scan whole planets".
- **Cluster trap:** 6D within 2 units.
- **Conner net:** 50 m, 10D, +5D electrical penalty.
- **E-Web(15):** 8D, Gk7 Cryocooler, shields 1D/2D.
- **PLX-4:** savant missiles; 6D speeder scale; range to 10 km.
- **Sidebar "Gottu and His Vibro-ax"** (Page's Commandos, Jendorn/Bruzion): an activated vibro-ax buried in a turbolaser tower's armored wall "Twenty minutes later … had finally cut its way to the power core".
  - (ANALYSIS: a vibro-ax eventually cuts through turbolaser tower armor; a low-end armor durability reference.)
- **Stokhli spray stick:** 200 m; 6D stun; 1 charge makes up to 500 m of spraynet.
- **Thermal detonator** (scaling-relevant):
> "Generally, thermal detonators contain enough baradium for a five meter sphere. Recently, modified black market detonators that can allegedly create 100 meter blast spheres have been confiscated" — p. 195
  - "Anything outside the sphere is undamaged". Damage 10D/8D/5D.
- **Decon droid:** Decon III, 3 kg per hour.
- **Espionage droids** (start).
