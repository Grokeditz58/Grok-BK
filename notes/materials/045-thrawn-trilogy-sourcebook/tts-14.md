# Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook, notes part 14: PDF pp. 146–163 (book pp. 149–160), Ch. 7 end; Ch. 8 Creatures

**Scan note:** PDF pp. 158–163 are an exact duplicate of PDF pp. 152–157 (book pp. 155–160). This was verified by text comparison of all six page pairs. The pages were read once and are counted once.

## Aliens (cont.)
- **Svivreni:** 0.6–0.9 m; stamina and willpower +2D. They resisted Thrawn's occupation "until the death of Thrawn".
- **Ukians:** "fear of the impossible", which "Grand Admiral Thrawn exploited to capture Ukio with its shield generators intact". The Overliege is the best harvester of each 10-year period.
- **Vaathkree:** live 300–350 years; Stonesingers mature over two decades and pick a "permanent form"; armor STR+2D physical/+1D energy; shifting trade language (+15 to decipher).
- **Verpine** (Roche field): radio communication via antennae, 1 km alone but covering the whole Roche field as a hive. "a single Verpine in contact with the communal consciousness can speak with complete authority for the entire hive." Slayn & Korpil; Technical +2D.
- **Wookiees:** "their huge fists shattering whatever they hit"; claws dishonorable; life debt; honor family; STR 2D+2/6D; berserker +2D.
- **Woostoids:** computer +2D; the "Logical minds" story factor cites "Thrawn's 'super weapon' ploy" (−1D when confused).
- **Yagai:** tripeds with 9-fingered hands.
  - **Yaga Drone** (subspecies): 2.5–3 m, "genetically engineered to survive in harsh environments like deep space". "require no food, water or other sustenance, save the solar energy they absorb". Armor +2D energy/+3D physical. Classed as "non-citizens".
- **ZeHethbra:** "no less than 80 distinct cultural, racial and ethnic groups"; spray in a 3 m radius (5D stun, Difficult willpower).

## Ch. 8: Creatures (book pp. 155–160)
- **Clawbirds** (stana, Wayland): flocks of 5 to 100+; poison intensity 3D, +1D per hour up to 6D (stun, paralysis, mortal wound, death bands). A stuffed clawbird is a truce symbol.
- **Cracian Thumpers** (Jomark): claws 3D+1, tail 4D+2; carry 100 kg.
- **Divto** (Endor): three-headed 3 m snake; three attacks per round; poison stun 6D+2.
- **Garrals:** engineered by commander Luthos Garral from the Mantessan panthac; frenzy within 200 m of an active repulsorlift.
- **Katarn** (Kashyyyk): "silent, shadowy and deadly", the mascot of Page's commandos.
- **Mantessan Panthac.**
- **Preducor** (Endor): 4 m tall, 5 m long; STR 6D; teeth 7D.
- **Vine snakes** (Wayland): groups of 10–100; neurotoxin 3D per round, "usually fatal within an hour".
- **Vornskr:**
> "While Luke Skywalker was on the world, the vornskr demonstrated an unnatural hatred for the Jedi." — p. 160
  - Force Sense 3D: detects Force users at ≤10 m (Very Easy) up to >100 m (Heroic); "they confuse all Force-users with the ysalamiri". Karrde's tail-less pets are named Sturm and Drang.
- **Ysalamiri** (key anti-Force rules):
> "Single ysalamiri create 'bubbles' of Force-empty space up to 10 meters in radius, though two or more ysalamiri in close proximity create much larger bubbles." — p. 160
> "Within the bubble, characters cannot use Force skills, Force Points or Character Points. Force attacks directed at ysalamiri fade away as they enter the creature's 'Force bubble.'" — p. 160
  - Thrawn's nutrient-pipe frame makes a mobile defense.
  - (ANALYSIS: the WEG rule also negates Character Points, not just Force use; no upper limit is given for clustered bubbles.)
