# Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook, notes part 6: PDF pp. 66–76 (book pp. 69–79), Ch. 4 (cont.): Imperial forces, Intelligence, officers

## Imperial Forces
- **Stormtroopers:**
> "There has never been a documented account of a stormtrooper being bribed, seduced or blackmailed into betraying the ideals which they serve." — p. 69
> "At the Empire's height, the core stormtrooper forces were said to outnumber the regular army." — p. 69
  - Post-Endor survivors show "somewhat better skills and abilities than their pre-Endor counterparts". Only scouts and standard troopers have been seen since Endor.
  - Stats: blaster 5D, dodge 5D. Armor +2D physical, +1D energy, −1D DEX.
- **Zero-G Assault Troopers ("spacetroopers"):** "second only to the Imperial Royal Guard (who have disappeared from the galaxy) in training and destructive capability"; "over two meters tall and is twice as wide as an unarmored man".
  - Mark I armor: +4D physical/+3D energy; 4 hours of power, 10 hours of O2.
  - Weapons: grenade launcher (30 concussion grenades, 5D); mini-proton torpedo launcher 6D (ammo 6); blaster cannon 6D; "Laser Cutters: 0.3-meter range, 3D damage (starfighter scale)". At Sluis Van they carried extra back-mounted mini-proton launchers.
  - The recognition guide (PDF p. 69) labels the Mark II spacetrooper (forward lights, grenade launcher, laser cutter, blaster cannon, magnetic couplers, sensor units, comlink, torpedo hurler, RPU, torpedo backpack). The Stormtrooper callout reads "18-piece anti-blaster cocoon shell".
  - Technical Guide art: the Arakyd Mini Proton Torpedo Launch Pack.
- **Scout troopers:** Aratech 74-Z; helmet sensor array scans "a 180-degree area"; used in the Myrkr search.
- **Naval personnel:** gunners are increasingly conscripts ("fire accuracy is not as great as it was in the Empire's glory days"). Naval troopers now wear black, though the Grand Admiral's detail still wears Death Star uniforms. Standard pilot, TIE pilot (starfighter piloting 6D), gunner (capital ship gunnery 5D), and navy trooper stats.
- **Army:** lost prestige after Endor; Thrawn's revitalization had commanders "recruit whole battalions". Army trooper and assault trooper stats.
- **Imperial Intelligence:** four divisions:
  - Ubiqtorate
  - IntOrg, with IntSec and IntCon
  - Analysis Bureau (carrier-wave code sampling)
  - Bureau of Operations (assassination)
  - **Delta Source** "was not handled by Imperial Intelligence … operated within the New Republic's Inner Council".
- **Sidebar "Imperial Intelligence Agents":** solo agents are "super agents". "Often, solo Intel agents were recruited by the Emperor to serve as his 'hands.'" Teams of 3–16; specialists at 9D–10D. Skills are "artificially enhanced by specialized training and mnemiotic drugs".
- **Major Molo Himron:** "handpicked by Emperor Palpatine"; trained with "memory-enhancing drugs, subliminal re-education and physical conditioning". Blaster 9D+2, sneak 10D, security 10D*, survival 10D*.
- **Fingal (new):** aide to Governor Staffa of Berchest and a reactivated Intel agent watching for Luke and Karrde.
- **General Freja Covell:** learned AT-AT doctrine under Veers; walker operation: AT-AT 9D+1, tactics: ground assault 10D+1.
> "Jedi have often demonstrated the ability to plant 'suggestions' in a target's mind, but few Jedi have ever attempted to totally supplant a person's mind. C'baoth effectively addicted Covell to the touch of the dark Jedi's mind." — pp. 77–78
> "when their shuttle entered the influence of the ysalamiri at Mount Tantiss, the loss of mental contact with C'baoth wiped out the young General's mind completely. … the tissue of his brain, traumatized by the Jedi Master's influence, simply shut down" — p. 78
- **General Theol Drost:** Bilbringi security chief; a Karrde raid "cost the Empire a nearly-completed Star Destroyer". Tactics: space perimeter security 6D+1.
- **Colonel Kleyn Selid:** temporary Tantiss commander; "skeptical of Grand Admiral Thrawn's claims that the ysalamiri were necessary to the cloning process". Fiction sidebar "Not What He Expected …": Covell arrives under C'baoth's control and salutes "clumsily, as if he had never performed the motion before" (continues).
