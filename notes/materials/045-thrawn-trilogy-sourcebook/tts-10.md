# Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook, notes part 10: PDF pp. 106–114 (book pp. 109–117), Ch. 6 Planets (Berchest–Filve)

- **Berchest:** Imperial Governor; one of Thrawn's "false shipping routes" during the clone hunt.
  - Sidebar "Calius Saj Leeloo": the City of Glowing Crystal glows red at sunset. The Sanctum Arcti houses "over 2,000 scholars and artisans" and "stands almost a full kilometer tall and covers nearly three square kilometers".
- **Governor Raccyx Staffa:** ex-smuggler who blackmailed the prior governor into retiring. Imperial Intelligence tolerates him to use his fringe ties. Command 8D.
- **Bespin:** layers of clouds, liquid-metal rethen, and a metal core. The Life Zone is "roughly 150 kilometers below the outer border". Thrawn installed a new garrison and raided the Tibanna stores. Population 4 billion; year 14 standard years.
- **Bilbringi:** shipyards orbit Bilbringi VII. Mazzic and Ellor destroyed a near-complete ISD; the NR raid sought "a Crystal Gravfield Trap array". "The Golan II battle platforms and shield generators that protected Bilbringi were damaged beyond repair"; abandoned and scavenged by the NR. Drost removed. Population 12 million.
- **Bimmisaari:** asaari moving trees; Tower of Law; Noghri ambush on Leia.
- **Bpfassh:**
> "The Imperials attacked with a five-ship task force — five Imperial Star Destroyers under the command of Grand Admiral Thrawn. The mission was a simple hit-and-fade … a tactic Thrawn had practiced a few months past in Draukyze system." — p. 111
> "During the Clone Wars, a group of Bpfasshi dark Jedi wreaked havoc before being stopped. … One such Jedi got as far as Dagobah before being put down." — p. 112
  - Bpfasshi petition the NR for protection from the new dark Jedi (C'baoth). A double planet; "surprisingly few deaths".
- **Chazwa:** garrison upgraded with "some cloned stormtroopers"; the Interdictor *Rampart*.
- **Coruscant:** "An entire continent on the planet has been set aside" as the federal zone; Galactic City became Imperial City. Population "5 billion prior to New Republic takeover".
  - (ANALYSIS: an early-1990s WEG figure that later sources supersede with trillions; note as an official but outdated stat.)
  - Sidebar "Imperial City": "During Palpatine's reign, aliens were off-limits in the city unless they were with their masters."
- **Crondre:** NR medical depot in glaciers; Fabritech shields, ion cannon, Golan II. Population 2,000 on the surface, 800 on the platform.
- **Fiction "Scouting Report SS-176.01, Imperial Year One":** scout Keog Boorn traces a beckon signal to the Dagobah cave, hears "the loud, terrifying howl", and logs "Dagobah's empty … completely empty". (ANALYSIS: implies Yoda, or the cave, scared him off; a canon-adjacent Year One date.)
- **Dagobah** (verbatim, Force-relevant):
> "Since hearing the stories of the dark Jedi of Bpfassh, he has learned that Dagobah has been credited as the last stop in the most powerful dark Jedi's reign of terror through the Sluis systems. … Luke has speculated that Yoda confronted the dark Jedi. He shudders trying to imagine an all-out confrontation between masters of both sides of the Force." — p. 115
> "The cave shielded him, Luke believes. Like a pair of positive and negative charges, Yoda's good Force was cancelled by the cave's evil. They were so close together that from a distance they looked like no charge at all." — p. 115
  - "In ages past, he had trained more Jedi than he could remember." The beckon call has "five triangular keys".
- **Dolomar:** underground colony with planetary shields. "The initial Imperial invasion fleet was ambushed and wiped out." Diflu complex in the L'tiri Peaks; population 1.5 billion.
- **Elom:** lommite; massive armament program.
- **Endor:** trees "over three hundred meters"; Bright Tree Village (~200 Ewoks, 50 m up); Salfur's Trading Post. Population 10 million.
> "A curiosity over Endor is a 'psychic bloodstain' of sorts. … Here is where the Emperor died, and those who are sensitive to the Force can feel his presence when they pass through the spot. It is a malign, hateful presence, chilling to all Force-wielders who pass through it." — p. 116
- **Farrfin** (Farghul; steam storms; Jahhnu betting houses; resists Imperial occupation; population 6 billion) and **Filve** (begins).
