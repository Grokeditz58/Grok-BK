# Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook, notes part 20: PDF pp. 225–237 (book pp. 222–234), Ch. 13 Starships (cont.)

Image pages:
- PDF p. 227: "Starfighter Recognition Guide" (X-wing, B-wing, TIE, Mole Miner, TIE Interceptor).
- PDF p. 231: art.
- PDF p. 236: *Lady Luck* deck plans (side, interior, main deck, observation deck; secret compartment, escape pods, retractable laser).

- **MC80a weapons:** 48 turbolaser batteries 4D (atmosphere range to 150 km); 20 ion batteries 3D; 6 tractors.
- **Strike-class** (Loronar): modular and prefabricated; "Well-placed hits during combat can knock out whole systems". Hull 6D, shields 2D+2; 20 turbolasers 5D, 10 turbolaser batteries 7D. Part of Thrawn's Sluis Van armada.
- **IPV-1:** hull 3D+1, shields 3D.
- **Star Galleon:** 300 troopers; the cargo-hold pod can be jettisoned and jumps to hyperspace on its own drive; hull 5D+2.
- **Victory SD:**
> "The Clone Wars saw a host of technological advances sweep the galaxy. Perhaps the most impressive by-product of those violent years was the Victory-class Star Destroyer. These ships formed the core of the Old Republic Navy." — p. 223
  - Can enter the upper atmosphere; hyperspace travel "twice as fast as the newer Imperials". Hull 4D, shields 3D+1; 10 quad turbolasers 5D; 80 concussion launchers 9D.
- **A-wing:** space 12; jammer gives −2D to enemy fire control, but it "only works against starfighter class craft".
- **B-wing:** Shantipole Project with the Verpine; laser 7D, torpedoes 9D.
- **Raptor/Skyclaw** (Mazzic, modified SoroSuub Preybirds): 200,000 credits; rumored Shada involvement; concussion missiles 8D.
- **Scimitar assault bomber:** "the first product of Thrawn's demands"; hull 5D; 16 concussion missiles 9D.
- **TIE/ln:** TIEs "have lost their 'throw-away' designation".
- **TIE Interceptor:** "Captain Pellaeon ordered his technicians to install shield generators in all TIE interceptors". Shields 2D; 4 lasers 6D.
- **X-wing T-65B:** hull 4D, shields 1D, lasers 6D, torpedoes 9D.
- **Y-wing BTL-S3.**
- **Z-95:** "spawned more specialized and dedicated variants than any other starfighter".
- **W-23 space barge:** 15,000 t.
- **Stock YT-1300 vs. *Millennium Falcon*:** the *Falcon* has x1/2 hyperdrive, space 8, hull 6D, shields 3D, quad lasers 6D, concussion missiles 9D, and a retractable blaster.
- **Action V bulk freighter:** 80,500 t.
- ***Wild Karrde*** (Action VI): x1; hull 4D, shields 3D; 3 turbolasers 5D; sensor mask +4D+2; "the tightest sensor stealth mode this side of an Imperial spy ship"; vornskr kennel.
- ***Lady Luck*** (SoroSuub Luxury 3000): previously owned by an Orthellin royal mistress from the Binarran Cloud; slave-circuit remote start.
- ***Distant Rainbow*** (Mazzic; modified KDY Starwind): 1,610,000 credits; x1 (cont.).
