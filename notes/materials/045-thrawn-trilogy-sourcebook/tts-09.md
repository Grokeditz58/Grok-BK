# Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook, notes part 9: PDF pp. 96–106 (book pp. 99–109), Ch. 5 end; Ch. 6 Planets (start)

## Fringe (cont.)
- **Sena Midanyl** stats: command: Peregrine's Nest 8D, languages 8D.
- **Irenez:** Bel Iblis's security chief; trained at the "Old Republic Military Academy on Corellia" and lost her commission under the Empire. Ran a merc platoon; played a "pivotal part in the raids on Tangrene and Gestron". Blaster pistol 8D+2, starfighter piloting 8D+2.
- **Samuel Tomas Gillespee:** retired to Ukio; *Kern's Pride*; 20+ ships.
> "I don't happen to think people ought to come off an assembly line, thanks. And if they did, I sure as mynocks wouldn't put the Empire in charge of the factory." — p. 101
- **Shirlee Faughn** (2IC, communications 8D+2); **Rappapor the Bith**; **Ellor** (Duro, met a young Karrde; outran an Interdictor with Gy'lan spice).
- **Billey:** smuggler "for nearly 60 years"; refused a cyber-spine after the crash (Goshyn Detention Center). Uses a wheeled chair.
- **Dravis:** Academy graduate, stole a Lambda shuttle (the "DL-44 nuptials" rumor). Starfighter piloting: TIE 9D+1. His father wanted him in "the infamous Scimitar Wing assault group".
- **Mazzic:** the smugglers' Bilbringi raid "managed to destroy an Imperial Star Destroyer that was under construction"; "a poor strategist". His murdered "friend" may have been his wife. Ships: *Distant Rainbow*, *Raptor*, *Skyclaw*.
- **Shada D'ukal** (as "Shada"): "Mistryl shadow guards" from an unnamed planet "stamped into submission". She put a tech in "a neural reconstruction facility for nearly a month".
> "in combat Shada can hurl these [zenji needles] with deadly accuracy, and with sufficient force to crack even some forms of battle armor." — p. 104
  - Thrown weapons: zenji needles 9D+2 (STR+3D+1), martial arts 7D+2, Move 12.
- **Clyngunn the ZeHethbra:** Stratis Games champion on Hallrin IV ("Multi-Sentient Unarmed Combat Rounds"); "still holds the record for most sentients defeated in a single round". Brawling 9D, brawling parry 10D+2; 5D stun spray; *Lady Sunfire*.
- **Balig** (gunner, starship gunnery 8D+1); **Griv** (Rodian ex-cop); **Fein** (rifle 9D, ex-swoop; Whistler's Whirlpool on Trogan); **Lishma the Gotal** (killed at Trogan; "did not think opposing the Empire was a wise idea").

## Ch. 6: Planets (p. 107 on)
> "At the height of its power, the Old Republic governed a million worlds, yet millions of systems remained unclaimed, unmapped and unknown." — p. 107
- "During Thrawn's campaign, three-quarters of the known systems were aligned … with the New Republic. The Empire controlled barely one-quarter of its original territory."
- A sector was originally ~50 settled systems.
> "Wild Space had been opened to exploration as one of Emperor Palpatine's last acts. Grand Admiral Thrawn was charged with taming the wilderness." — p. 107
- The Borderland Regions are an unofficial militarized zone.
- **Abregado-rae:** the LoBue bar; the hill-clan embargo; Torve's *Etherway* impounded. Population 40 million, "Repressive Bureaucracy (New Republic Allied)".
- **Berchest:** Anthos sector, where an ancient planetary collision left sensor-fouling routes. The Leefari Sea; crystal deposits "sometimes several kilometers in diameter" carved into cities ("citysculpting"); moon Terhaari. Population 3 billion.
