# Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook, notes part 2: PDF pp. 25–32 (book pp. 28–35), Ch. 2 The New Republic (cont.)

- **Commander Sesfan:** a Mon Cal sensor officer and Ackbar's aide. May have **Mon Calamari Nerveshock**, a virus that is "dormant and undetectable … for years" with no cure for adult strains; "one in three cases of the disease are fatal". Willpower 7D+2.
- **Tav Breil'lya:** Fey'lya's aide, with a family crest neckpiece; liaison to Bel Iblis at New Cov. Bothan story factors: "Opportunist" and fur "Communication".
- **Han Solo:**
> "That medal I got at Yavin? It's in the Falcon somewhere ... I think … We probably melted it down to patch together some circuitry." — p. 29
  - Stats (Sluis Van): blaster 8D+1, heavy blaster pistol 10D+1, dodge: energy weapons 8D+2, space transports: YT-1300 12D, starship gunnery 9D, astrogation 9D, streetwise 9D. FP 3, CP 31. His DL-44 does 5D+1.
  - Katana: dodge: energy weapons 9D; FP 5. Tantiss: FP 6, CP 47.
- **Chewbacca:** born "over 200 years ago"; "some feel he may actually be getting stronger"; "killed five of the slavers with his bare hands" before capture.
  - Stats: bowcaster 10D, brawling 11D, lifting 11D, stamina 10D, intimidation 11D, STR 5D; Berserker Rage +2D STR; Climbing Claws; Move 13.
  - Tantiss: learned "Noghri martial arts" (+2D, STR+1D+2 damage).
- **Sidebar "Chewbacca and the Life-Debt"** (Na'al interview via 3PO): the legend of Urothko and Elder Stalpaac vs. a **Graaa'shad**, a "fire-spirit" species that drains strength with flame and "vanished in a pillar of flame". It is the origin of the life-debt ("Or so the legend claims").
- **Artoo:** keeps a "counterpart" relationship with Luke's X-wing computer (not memory-wiped), which "increased the vessel's response time and efficiency". Astrogation 12D. Full equipment list: welder 3D, saw 4D, sensor +3D to 100 m, compressed-air launcher "used for Luke's lightsaber". 0.96 m tall.
- **Threepio:** languages 13D; "TranLang III Communication module with over six million languages"; impersonated Leia. 1.67 m.
- **Admiral Drayson:** Chandrilan barge-fleet businessman, then Chandrila Defense Fleet admiral; ran Mon Mothma's command-ship pickets ("no less than a dozen smaller ships"); now commands the Coruscant system fleet ("at least half … in orbit or system space at all times").
- **General Crix Madine:** former commander of the Imperial Storm Commandos; defected mid-mission from a criminal order "which came directly from the Emperor". Rieekan vouched for him; he formed the Alliance special forces and planned the Endor shuttle and bunker raid. Tactics: squads 13D+2, military history 11D+1, command 10D.
- **General Carlist Rieekan:** witnessed Alderaan's destruction from a satellite station; "bleeds when they bleed, and dies when they die"; a prime planner of the "Core Campaign" that captured Coruscant. Tactics: planetary defenses 9D+2, sieges 9D+2.
- **Colonel Bren Derlin:** son of Old Republic Senator Galen Derlin (friend of Bail Organa); "Battle of Nentan" (continues).
