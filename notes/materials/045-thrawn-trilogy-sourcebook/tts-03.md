# Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook, notes part 3: PDF pp. 32–41 (book pp. 35–44), Ch. 2 (cont.): NR military

- **Bren Derlin** (cont.): his father Galen Derlin was "murdered by an Imperial assassin" on Tiisheraan; Bren is in his early fifties. Tactics: squads 10D+1; carries "Klawthra's Military Tactics Journal".
- **Colonel Jak Bremen (new):** from Shiffrin, where an hfredium ore find led an Imperial Moff to execute protesters, including Bremen's father Kallin. On Coveway, a COMPNOR garrison killed his wife and infant daughter. Security chief of the cruiser *Starveil*, now Director of Security for the NR Council. "A bit of a martinet"; hostile to Mara. Law enforcement 9D, security 9D.
- **Captain Afyon:** Alderaanian Clone Wars veteran. The escort frigate *Larkhess* has a "nine hundred and twenty-crew" complement but is run "with just 15 men" as a cargo hauler. He was ready to scuttle at Sluis Van.
- **Captain Sarin Virgilio:** his Corellian gunship picked up the Bothan spies; "only the six standing on Virgilio's bridge remained", carrying the DS2 plans and "the Emperor's personal schedule". Captain of the escort frigate *Quenfis* in Drayson's Home Guard Fleet.
- **Wing Commander Diblen Harleys (new):** Coruscant-born son of a bureaucrat, who told him the Survey Corps' real job was "finding primitive, exploitable worlds for the Emperor to conquer". His starfighter tactics helped at Endor. Tactics: starfighters 9D+2; blue gemstone earring.
- **Wedge Antilles:** pirate fuel-depot explosion backstory; "survived three prior engagements" before Yavin. Older pilots "spoke of a Skywalker who had fought during the Clone Wars. He was a pilot". Wedge refused A- and B-wings ("Rogue Squadron will stick with the X-wings"); turns down "promotion after promotion". Starfighter piloting: X-wing 7D, gunnery 7D+1.
- **Sidebar, Rogue Squadron insignia:** a red twelve-pointed star, "one for each member of Rogue Squadron".
- **Hobbie Klivian:** "just shy of 2 meters"; "I don't know if we can hit a target that small …". Starfighter piloting 8D+2.
- **Wes Janson:** a "True Gunner"; tail gunner for Wedge on Hoth; Tierfon base. Starship gunnery 9D+2.
- **Pash Cracken:** son of Airen Cracken; attended the Imperial Vensenor Academy under a false identity. "Pash and his entire TIE wing (nearly 72 pilots and ships) defected", and Vader reportedly "personally placed a new commander aboard the Star Destroyer". The "Cracken Twist" evacuation code at Xyquine II. Generis (Atrivis) comm center, "the communication nexus to and from the Outer Rim", fell to Thrawn. Starfighter piloting 9D+2, starfighter repair 10D+2.
- **Lieutenant Page:** son of a corrupt Corulag Senator; served under Veers; deserted after hearing Leia speak to the "Council of Galactic Rights". Refused promotion like Han and Luke. "the perfect commando". Blaster 7D+2.
- **Page's Katarn Commandos:** 12 members, named by Frorral after the katarn of Kashyyyk ("invisible until they wanted to be seen"). Roster roles: pathfinder Lilla Dade; urban specialists Gottu and Idow; Wookiee wilderness fighter Frorral (continues).
