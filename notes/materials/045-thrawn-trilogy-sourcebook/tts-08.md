# Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook, notes part 8: PDF pp. 87–96 (book pp. 90–99), Ch. 5 The Fringe (cont.)

- **Locate and Detain List** (fiction, end): bounty hunter Gunner Groth, using a fake "Dengar" ID, tails Mara Jade on Rishi. "It was several hours before anybody noticed Gunner Groth's body."
- **Fynn Torve** stats; gambling 7D.
- **Ghent:** computer programming/repair 8D, security 9D; at the Siege of Coruscant, computer programming/repair 9D+1, security 9D+1.
- **Brasck:** a Brubb from Baros, ex-Jabba merc, ship *Green Palace*. Deals in slaves and kidnapping.
> "He remembers stories of the man from when he was a boy on Baros. Then, the planet was considered part of the Unknown Regions instead of the Outer Rim Territories. Thrawn found his world, and taught it to respect and fear the might of the Empire." — p. 91
  - (ANALYSIS: evidence of Thrawn's pre-exile Unknown Regions campaigns, consistent with the Outbound-era framing.) Natural armor +2D; color change.
- **Par'tah:** a Ho'Din from Moltok, "almost three meters tall"; mourning the Gotal Lishma.
- **Niles Ferrier:** could "palm a Corellian Gunship while the Corellians are still aboard". Thrawn's bounty was "twenty percent above market value for any ship over one hundred thousand tons that could fight". His gang is five humans, a Verpine, and a Defel ("wraith").
  - He gave the Empire three Sienar patrol ships stolen from the Cavrilhu pirates. Thrawn fitted "an unbreakable doomsday mechanism set to detonate in exactly three months time" to his freighter.
  - Two homing beacons on the *Lady Luck*; Hoffner was locked up at the Coral Vanda. He orchestrated the Trogan ambush (Lishma killed) and was killed evading the "smugglers' Alliance".
  - Stats: value: starships 10D+2; computer programming/repair: spaceport security systems 10D.
- **Sidebar "Populating the Fringe":** crime lords are rare ("Jabba the Hutt was perhaps the most well known and powerful crime lord to ever carve out a territory"); also smugglers, mercs, pirates, bounty hunters.
- **Sidebar "The Cavrilhu Pirates"** (fiction): Captain Zothip of the gunship *Void Cutter*, "scourge of the Amorris star system" for almost ten years; Togorian first mate Keta; base on Morr Three; Fudot. Zothip stats: command: Cavrilhu Pirates 8D, intimidation 8D, brawling 8D+2.
- **Garm Bel Iblis:** Han calls him "sir". He met Han when Han was 11. Palpatine had "his wife, and his two children" executed in front of him; "commonly believed that Bel Iblis died on Anchoron".
> "At Tangrene, the warriors of Peregrine's Nest blasted a major Ubiqtorate center into fine dust, even though three Star Destroyers were in orbit guarding the facility." — p. 96
  - Mon Mothma "is terrified of trusting the lives under her command to anyone else". His leadership saved the Siege of Coruscant from "a complete rout".
  - Stats: tactics: ground assault 9D, capital ship 8D+2, command 9D, bargain 9D. Siege of Coruscant: command: Peregrine's Nest troops 11D, tactics: capital ship 9D.
- **Sidebar "Differences of Opinion"** (fiction): the Bel Iblis and Mon Mothma split over the attack on Milvayne (Gyrica system). He says it's "an Ubiqtorate base"; Mothma: "The Rebel Alliance will no longer require your services." Bail is dead (post-Alderaan).
- **Peregrine's Nest base key:** sensor mask +2D; 12–24 soldiers per cabin; anti-orbit cannons ("capital ship defenders and anti-starfighter turbolasers"); medical building treats 100 at once with twelve bacta tanks; mess hall holds a quarter of personnel.
- **Sena Leikvold Midanyl:** Bel Iblis's chief aide since his Senate days. "A part of him died when attention turned from him to the charismatic woman from Chandrila" (the Corellian Treaty). It was her idea to have Han invite Bel Iblis back.
