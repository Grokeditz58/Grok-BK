# Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook, notes part 16: PDF pp. 176–188 (book pp. 173–185), Ch. 10 Mount Tantiss

PDF pp. 178 (Mount Tantiss cutaway schematic, labels only: Emperor's private turbolift, Royal Chambers, emergency shuttle hangar, air intake vent, labs, medical bays, hangar bay, main computer core, command wing; "NOT TO SCALE") and 180 (art) are image pages. PDF p. 182 is the Spaarti cylinder diagram (top view and control readout).

- **"Age of Mount Tantiss":** the cloning facility "was considerably older than the technology present in other areas of the mountain". Lando: "like it was relocated entirely … pretty vintage stuff".
- Tantiss "could have supported a full Imperial battlegroup"; Thrawn left "a mere battalion". The blast door would need "an aerial bombardment of incredible power" to force it.
- **Throne room:** a galaxy hologram with "a single blazing point of light … for each of the galaxy's billions of stars", tied to HoloNet. Luke: "dank and primitive".
- **Field Report sidebar** (Garv Debble):
> "We have identified the remains of over 200 lightsabers of various styles, power ratings and ages, though only fragments survived the blast." — p. 176
  - Pre-Republic warheads; Alderaanian "sleeper pod"; the AB-1 "floatcoach"; "Is the Mount Tantiss base simply one of a network of treasure vaults?"
- **Sidebar "Mirror Image"** (Luke's journal):
> "The clone that Joruus C'baoth created to test me with was the fulfillment of my vision in the cave on Dagobah. … The numbing, buzzing pressure on my mind, the exhausting struggle to conquer an enemy that shared my thought processes, all under the oppressive gaze of a dark Jedi." — p. 178
  - "I lured the clone Luuke into striking the power conduit, which exploded in its face … Mara nearly cut my double in half".
- **Royal chambers:** "He was known to keep his repository of information on the Force in a separate library at this level." The second turbolift is retinal-keyed and hidden, reaching the crypts and labyrinth.
- **Emergency shuttle:** a modified Lambda with an advanced hyperdrive "(supposedly capable of reaching the x1/2 hyperdrive barrier)". The cloak rumor is deemed unlikely: "Mount Tantiss itself only contained plans for a cloaking device".
- **Upper vaults:** "up to three hundred meters square". Intruders get nerve gas plus force and repulsor fields "in less than 10 seconds". The surviving Farnican chime-painting is *Idyll*.
- **Fiction "Clone B-2332-54"** (p. 180), key Vader and Palpatine feats:
> "With a flick of Vader's gauntleted wrist, the severed hand, still clutching the deactivated lightsaber in its grasp, floated to the Dark Lord of the Sith." … "The impertinent engineer was dead before they ever reached him." — p. 180
> "Suddenly, Vader was struck to his knees by the horribly powerful voice that rolled like fiery thunder through his brain. … 'Come to Mount Tantiss, immediately.' … His Master had detected his rebellious thoughts." — p. 180
  - (ANALYSIS: Palpatine reads Vader's private thought and telepathically drives him to his knees aboard a shuttle leaving Bespin, over interplanetary or interstellar range. The B-2332-54 clone template is Luke's hand.)
- **Cloning chamber:**
> "The Spaarti cylinders present in Mount Tantiss numbered in the thousands (over 20,000 cylinders were estimated to be in the facility …)" — pp. 179–180
  - "well over 10 stories tall"; cylinders about 4 m; "flash-pumping" training.
> "clones needed at least a year of growth to remain even marginally mentally stable, and the recommended time period was three to five years. This was because the presence of multiple clones creates a 'disturbance' in the Force. … Thrawn produced mentally stable clones in 15 to 20 days." — p. 181
- **Tech vaults** ("nearly 30"): a cloaked-ship guidance system; an advanced stasis chamber (beyond Stars' End); a "time-sensitive hive-virus … with genocidal results"; bio-tech of the "Charon"; "a massive automated weapons construction facility".
  - (ANALYSIS: a likely nod to World Devastators.)
- **Medical bays:** about 30 operating theaters, about 200 diagnostic booths, bacta tanks that take up to five patients. Hangar with TIE roof racks and Skipray blastboats.
- **Corporal Terkuss** (sidebar; clone template): conscripted under threat that the Empire would "destroy his homeworld". Blaster rifle 9D; FP 2, DSP 6.
- **Lt. Creb** (Black Wing Squadron; template): 16 engagements in under two years; starfighter piloting 8D; DSP 5.
- **Main computer core:** larger than the cloning chamber; duplicated "virtually every scrap of information that was moved through the Imperial data network".
- Detention block with an interrogation "operating theater"; security section with an ISB office; barracks for a full battlegroup.
- **Fiction "His Finest Work"** (pp. 184–185): architect Garrbo V'Droz built the labyrinth with "trac-reflective material … No sensor map of the labyrinth will be the same twice".
> "The main chamber is over a kilometer down" — p. 184
  - The crypt holds "ancient energy weapons, amulets, crystals, alien trinkets and religious idols, mummified bodies", including "a powerfully built human dressed in brown robes, clutching a metal cylinder" (a Jedi corpse with lightsaber) and the murdered construction foreman. Palpatine "raised his hands, preparing to strike" (continues).
