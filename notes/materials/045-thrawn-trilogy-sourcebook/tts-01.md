# The Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook (WEG 40131, 1996), notes part 1: PDF pp. 1–24 (book pp. 4–27)

Front matter, Ch. 1 "Thrawn's Campaign", the Declaration of a New Republic, and Ch. 2 "The New Republic" (start). Collects the Heir/DFR/TLC sourcebooks, updated to the 2nd Edition rules.

## Front matter
- PDF p. 1 (book p. 4): Contents page (image), read visually: Foreword (Zahn) p. 5; Introduction p. 6; Ch. 1 Thrawn's Campaign p. 8; Ch. 2 The New Republic p. 16; Ch. 3 The Force p. 49; Ch. 4 Remnants of the Empire p. 61; Ch. 5 The Fringe p. 82; Ch. 6 Planets p. 107; Ch. 7 Aliens p. 139; Ch. 8 Creatures p. 155; Ch. 9 The Noghri p. 161; Ch. 10 Mount Tantiss p. 170; Ch. 11 Equipment and Droids p. 187; Ch. 12 Vehicles p. 201; Ch. 13 Starships p. 211; Appendix: Tactics and Battles p. 243.
- **Foreword by Timothy Zahn** (dated December 13, 1995):
> "It was just after four o'clock on November 6, 1989 … when the phone rang." — p. 5
> "in early July, I got a box from West End Games. It seemed that Lucasfilm had decided I ought to coordinate with their Star Wars role-playing game background stuff. … Here I was, eleven chapters into the book" — p. 5
  - Zahn says he has since been "vetting the sourcebooks they've created from my books". (ANALYSIS: author-vetted sourcebook.)
- **Introduction "Field Work"** (fiction): Polio Tipn, a Devaronian student, and Professor Voren Na'al (former head of NR Council Research; wife Rivoche) are asked by Mon Mothma to investigate "unusual reports" near the Minos Cluster, at Exocron ("wasn't even on the official star charts"). The frame for the book is Tipn reading Na'al's files.
> "This information was originally printed in Heir to the Empire Sourcebook, Dark Force Rising Sourcebook and The Last Command Sourcebook. These three books have been collected in one volume … updated to be compatible with Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition." — p. 7

## Ch. 1: Thrawn's Campaign (pp. 8–15; report by Polio Tipn, "nearly 10 years after the destruction of Mount Tantiss")
> "Grand Admiral Thrawn was undeniably one of the most capable, cunning soldiers the Emperor commanded. … a non-human given high rank in an intolerant, human-dominated regime" — p. 8
- The campaign begins "roughly five years after the Battle of Endor", opening with the Elomin task force destroyed at Obroa-skai.
- A recap of Heir follows. Points beyond the novels:
  - "51 mole miners" were stolen from Nkllon (p. 9).
  - Mara is "apparently Force-sensitive, though not as well-trained as Skywalker".
> "C'baoth was able to coordinate distant attacks through the Force, allowing greater accuracy and efficiency." — p. 9
- Sluis Van: mole miners with "zero-g stormtroopers"; "every ship damaged by the activation of the mole miners was now inoperable" (p. 10).
- The DFR recap: the Katana fleet is "an early attempt at crew reduction; the ships were 'slave-rigged' together"; Han rams a Dreadnaught into the ISD Peremptory by remote (p. 12).
> "Thrawn had apparently found a way to produce viable clone soldiers in a fraction of the time … poised to unleash a new round of Clone Wars" — p. 12
- **The planetary shield doctrine** (TLC recap):
> "Since planetary shields can handle all but the most determined aerial bombardments, standard military doctrine is to dispatch a fast-moving ground force to destroy the shield generators. The subsequent orbital assault bludgeons the planet into submission. … The fact that Ukio fell with its shields intact was at the time considered impossible" — p. 12
- C'baoth "sent the entire task force after" Leia at Filve and gives Thrawn an ultimatum (p. 13).
- The "Covert Shroud Gambit" at Poderis.
- Cloaked asteroids at Coruscant force the planetary shield up; a Crystal Gravfield Trap is the only detection method.
- Ysalamiri were "necessary to create stable clones in the time frame Thrawn required" and Tantiss was "dark" to the Force.
- C'baoth uses "Force lightning" on Mara; Han's shots fail and he is "severely burned in C'baoth's counter-attack"; C'baoth "telekinetically brought the roof … crashing down" (p. 14).
  - (ANALYSIS: the sourcebook says C'baoth caused the roof collapse; in the novel it was the Noghri's explosive.)
- C'baoth used "a remote detonator" to kill the ysalamiri at the vats. Rukh assassinates Thrawn on the bridge (p. 15).
- PDF p. 8: full-page art (Luke with a lightsaber and Mara). p. 12: tail text only.

## Ch. 2: The New Republic (pp. 18–27, Na'al datapad "shortly after the destruction of Mount Tantiss")
- The NR holds "nearly three-quarters of the former Galactic Empire" (p. 18). Under the Corellian Treaty the Chief of State was "an elected dictatorship"; the Provisional Council has run for "nearly five years". The Inner Council: Mon Mothma, Ackbar, Fey'lya, Leia.
- **Mon Mothma:** the youngest Senator until Leia; "the last member of either the Old Republic or the Empire to earn" the title Senior Senator. Palpatine was elected "President". Stats: Bargain 10D, command 10D, bureaucracy 11D; Force Points 5. Some skills are lowered for "aging".
- **Ackbar:** designed the B-wing (built by the Verpine). Tactics: fleets 9D, capital ship piloting 8D+1; Mon Calamari moist/dry environment rules; "can withstand extreme pressures found in ocean depths".
- **Leia:** "Born 28 years ago". "So far, she has mastered only the basics of both the lightsaber and the Force."
  - Stats (Kashyyyk): Control 2D, sense 2D, alter 1D; powers absorb/dissipate energy, control pain, resist stun, danger sense, life detection, life sense, magnify senses, receptive telepathy, telekinesis; Blaster 8D+2.
  - Honoghr: Control 3D, sense 3D, alter 1D; FP 8. Tantiss: Control 3D+1, sense 3D+1; lightsaber 3D+2; FP 10.
- **Sidebar "Aptitude Test":** Luke gauges the infant twins:
> "I had no idea, he thought with awe. Such ... potential. The next generation of Jedi has truly arrived." — p. 22
- **Sidebar "Leia's Lightsaber":** Leia built her saber with Ben's tools in eight days. "it often took Jedi nearly a month to build a lightsaber. She clearly shared the same extraordinary natural abilities he had." (p. 23)
- **Fey'lya:** grew up on Kothlis; secretly supported Bel Iblis and "even helped him defend his base against a Victory-class Star Destroyer". His Bothans found the second Death Star. Con 10D.
- **Councilor Sian Tew (new):** a Sullustan friend of Nien Nunb who got Sullust to join the Alliance; the Endor fleet assembled at Sullust at his insistence. Fiction sidebar "Council Debate" (Tew vs. Ackbar on smugglers).
- **Winter:** perfect "holographic memory"; Alliance Procurement code-name "Targeter"; "After eight years, every memory remains fresh". Stats p. 27.
- **Commander Sesfan (new):** Mon Calamari, Ackbar's representative (continues).
