# 045 Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook: summary of strongest findings

All 251 PDF pages hand-read. Note that PDF pp. 158–163 duplicate pp. 152–157 (a scan defect; nothing is missing).

## Force and feats
- **C'baoth Enhanced Coordination** (tts-05): the difficulty table goes up to "51,000-500,000" beings (Heroic). At Ukio, "C'baoth maintained constant mental contact with the vessels' crews and controlled their movements and firing patterns to create multiple simultaneous firings" (p. 245; tts-21).
- **Luke at Mount Tantiss** (tts-04): "Lightsaber 11D … Force Skills: Control 13D+1, sense 11D+1, alter 10D+2 / Force Points: 33 / Character Points: 70".
- **C'baoth at Tantiss** (tts-04): "Alter 12D / Force Points: 20 / Dark Side Points: 35"; KNOWLEDGE and PERCEPTION decay. Force lightning "cannot be parried or dodged" without the lightsaber combat power (p. 58).
- **Dagobah** (p. 115; tts-10): a Bpfasshi dark Jedi was "put down" on Dagobah, likely by Yoda. "Yoda's good Force was cancelled by the cave's evil … from a distance they looked like no charge at all."
- **Endor** (p. 116): the Emperor's "psychic bloodstain … a malign, hateful presence, chilling to all Force-wielders".
- **Palpatine and Vader** (fiction "Clone B-2332-54", p. 180; tts-16): Palpatine's telepathic voice strikes Vader to his knees after sensing his rebellious thoughts. In "His Finest Work" (p. 185), Palpatine kills the architect V'Droz with "vicious bolts of lightning".
- **Ysalamiri** (p. 160; tts-14): a 10 m bubble, larger for groups; no Force skills, FP, or CP inside.
- **Vornskr:** Force Sense 3D; hunts Force-users.
- **Duinuogwuin** (tts-13): deep-space survival "without apparent protection from vacuum and void"; "almost genetic predisposition toward the Force"; legends of 100+ m.

## Tech and scale
- **ISD** (p. 219; tts-19): "enough fire power to reduce a civilized world to slag or take on a fleet of lesser enemy vessels". Its resources and energy are "often greater than what a system can produce in a year".
- **Tantiss reactors** (p. 185): "Each generator put out enough power to run an Imperial Star Destroyer" (four reactors, three operational).
- **Clones** (p. 181): a 1-year minimum and 3–5 years recommended; Thrawn ran 15–20 days using ysalamiri. Over 20,000 Spaarti cylinders.
- **Cloaking shield** (p. 187): early prototypes "required more energy than a ship could produce"; "Nothing gets into a cloak field, and nothing gets out."
- **Thermal detonator** (p. 195): a 5 m sphere normally; black-market versions "can allegedly create 100 meter blast spheres".
- **Thrawn's 70 km leveling order** (p. 64; tts-05): "level everything within a seventy-kilometer radius … without touching the coordinates themselves".
- **Katana fleet** (p. 216): 200 Dreadnaughts lost to a hive virus. Slave rigging cut each crew by 14,000.
- **Planetary shield boundary at Ukio:** "50 kilometers above the planet's surface".
- **Siege of Coruscant:** 270+ "empty launches", only 22 real cloaked asteroids.
