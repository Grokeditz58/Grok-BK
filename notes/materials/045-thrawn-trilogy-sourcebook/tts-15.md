# Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook, notes part 15: PDF pp. 164–176 (book pp. 161–173), Ch. 9 The Noghri; Ch. 10 Mount Tantiss (start)

PDF pp. 165 and 174 are full-page art with no text.

## Ch. 9: The Noghri
- Vader convinced Noghri leadership "that only the Emperor's forces could restore their homeworld". The Emperor "had all traces of Honoghr removed from Imperial records". Noghri killed "a good number" of his rivals and dissidents.
- The disaster happened "48 Noghri years ago … the same as 44 Imperial years … perhaps even during the Clone Wars"; the Empire was "slowly poisoning the soil of Honoghr for years".
- At Tantiss, the Noghri "subdued all natural predators and the planet Wayland's less-than-friendly natives" and "reportedly neutralized a large number of Imperial troops".
- **Honoghr:** seven planets, from Logru (lava) to Kuthul. The Clean Land; Nystao with the Grand Dukha and High Seat; population 10 million.
- **Hidden Valley** ("the future"): cliffs "almost a kilometer in height each" jam sensors; built by Ovkhevam "in less than a month". Luke quote on the vegetation (p. 163).
- **Noghri template:** Attribute Dice 16D; DEX 2D+1/5D+2; STR 2D+2/5D+2.
  - Brawling: martial arts gives +2D and +1D+2 damage vs. non-practitioners. Claws +1D, teeth +2D; stealth +2D; search +2D; 1.3 m.
  - Extended loyalty to Luke, Leia, and the Solo children as Vader's descendants.
- **Dukha:** 20 m across, 4 m high plus 4 m roof; no internal pillars, held by chains to a hanging dish.
- **Rukh** (clan Baikh'vair): "one of Grand Admiral Thrawn's earliest recruits when he discovered the homeworld of the Emperor's dreaded Death Commandos".
  - Stats "as of the assassination of Thrawn": brawling parry 9D, dodge 9D, knife 8D+1, Noghri martial arts 9D, hide/sneak 9D; FP 1, CP 16; Move 14.
> "Rukh believed that Thrawn's death should only come after the Grand Admiral learned that the Noghri had betrayed him … what better time to slay Thrawn, a master of deception, than when he realized that he was being deceived himself?" — p. 165
- **Fiction "Setting Things Right"** (p. 166): Akh'laht (clan Kihm'bar) tells Rukh of the Mal'ary'ush; Rukh volunteers for a suicide mission. "Tell the dynasts that things will be set right."
- **Khabarakh:** "has withstood repeated attacks from the mighty Chewbacca, and even earned the massive Wookiee's respect". Stats: martial arts 6D+2; later FP 1.
- **Cakhmaim** (clan Eikh'mir): Mal'ary'ush honor guard leader; Rukh's childhood rival. Brawling parry vs. martial arts 9D; sneak 9D.
- **Ovkhevam** (clan Bakh'tor): agriculture 7D+2; FP 2. (The scan prints the heading "Ekhrikhor" above the Noghri Elder block, but that block is Ovkhevam's: agriculture tool kit.)
- **Ekhrikhor** (clan Bakh'tor): cleared the path to Tantiss unseen, and "It was only the Force skills of Luke Skywalker that allowed the New Republic warriors to learn that the Noghri were present". Search: tracking 9D; martial arts 9D+2; DSP 2.
- **Mobvekhar:** Cakhmaim's apprentice.

## Ch. 10: Mount Tantiss (p. 170 on)
- The Noghri "have sworn to destroy every remnant of the Emperor". A Wayland filing error erased the colony.
> "the dark Jedi Joruus C'baoth arrived on Wayland and confronted the Guardian. C'baoth emerged the victor, but then subjugated the people of Wayland … he used his mental powers to instill obedience." — p. 170 (native myths, unconfirmed)
  - The **Wayland** entry contradicts this, calling the Guardian himself "a dark Jedi named Joruus C'baoth" (p. 172).
  - (ANALYSIS: the book carries both versions: the Guardian clone or the defeated Guardian. Later canon says C'baoth killed the Guardian.)
> "The Guardian grew lonely, bored, and eventually ventured out of the mountain. He built his own kingdom, forging the three separate species into a single society through the power of his mind." — p. 172
- "His last additions were locked within the depths of Mount Tantiss just before the Battle of Endor." Thrawn, from Unknown Regions reports, "believed the Emperor's technicians had finally developed a practical cloaking shield."
- Wayland: population 28 million; government "Dictatorship (Joruus C'baoth)".
- **Myneyrsh:** four-armed, blue crystal skin (+1 armor). Their crossbows "lack the strength to pierce Imperial battle armor".
- **Psadan:** rocky shell, +2 armor.
- Sidebar "Unhealed Wounds, Unforgotten Prejudices": Na'al's family died in the Clone Wars. "Factory-assembled 'people' like the soldiers Thrawn tried to create were responsible for countless deaths".
