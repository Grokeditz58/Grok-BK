## Ch. 13: Starships (report by Bel Iblis, Ackbar, and Drayson after the Siege of Coruscant)
- **NR assault frigate:** converted Dreadnaught, crew cut "from 16,000 … to 5,000"; 20 umbilical docks; hull 5D, shields 3D; 15 turbolaser batteries 7D.
- **Bulk cruiser** (*Battle Horn*): 600 m; 30 quad lasers, 2 tractors.
- **Carrack:** "among the fastest cruisers in either fleet" (space 8); 10 heavy turbolasers 7D; hull 5D.
- **Corellian corvette:** hull 4D, 6 double turbolasers 4D+2.
- **Corellian Gunship:** concussion missiles 9D.
- **Dreadnaught:** "Before the Clone Wars shook the galaxy, the Dreadnaught-class heavy cruiser was the largest warship to patrol the space lanes."
- **Sidebar "The Katana Fleet":**
> "the fleet was made up of two hundred Dreadnaught-class heavy cruisers, led by the flagship, Katana. The entire fleet was mysteriously lost years before the start of the Clone Wars." — p. 216
> "A crewer picked up a hive virus … back then such viruses were known to level entire planetary populations … the hive virus drove its victims insane before it killed them. The dying crewers slaved their ships together, and the whole fleet jumped to lightspeed and disappeared." — p. 216
  - **Katana Dreadnaught stats:** 600 m; crew 2,204 with slave rigging; 3,000 troops; hull 5D+2, shields 2D+1. Weapons: 10 turbolaser cannons 2D, 20 quad turbolasers 4D, 10 turbolaser batteries 7D.
- **Nebulon-B:** hull 3D+2, 12 turbolaser batteries 4D.
- **Golan stations:**
  - I: 1,231 m, hull 4D, shields 2D, 28 turbolasers 4D+2, 5 torpedoes 7D.
  - II: 2,158 m, hull 4D, shields 2D+2, 35 turbolasers 5D, 10 torpedoes 9D, 8 tractors 6D.
  - III NovaGun: 2,600 m, hull 5D+2, shields 4D, 50 turbolasers 5D, 24 torpedoes 9D, 15 tractors.
  - "The unique 'crossbeam' structure of the hull makes it capable of absorbing a great deal of punishment."
- **ISD** (key scaling; the column text is interleaved in the scan and was reassembled):
> "The Imperial Star Destroyer has enough fire power to reduce a civilized world to slag or take on a fleet of lesser enemy vessels." — p. 219
> "The resources and energy used up by such a vessel is often greater than what a system can produce in a year — no wonder whole planets quaked in fear at the thought of a Star Destroyer entering their system." — p. 219
  - Full complement: stormtrooper division, 20 AT-ATs, 30 AT-STs, 6 TIE squadrons. "No new Star Destroyers have been constructed since the death of the Emperor."
  - Imperial I: hull 7D, shields 3D; 60 turbolaser batteries 5D (atmosphere range to 150 km); 60 ion cannons 3D; 10 tractors 6D.
- **Interdictor:** gravity well "simulating the presence of a large stellar body"; projectors' range to 150 space units; used on Luke's X-wing.
- **Lancer:** 20 quad lasers, fire control 4D.
- **MC80a:** 1,200 m; crew 5,156; x1; hull 6D, shields 3D plus "6D of back-up shields". Six cruisers always guard Coruscant.
