# Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook, notes part 7: PDF pp. 76–87 (book pp. 79–90), Ch. 4 end; Ch. 5 The Fringe (start)

## Ch. 4 end
- "Not What He Expected …" (cont.): Covell speaks in a "monotone, mechanical" voice while C'baoth whispers in his ear; the troops came aboard the *Draklor*. Selid hears "Joruus C'baoth's mocking laughter".
- **Sidebar "The Covert Shroud Gambit"** (Lt. Rejlii Mithel's communique to Thrawn): acquisition, shroud, and escape phases.
  - "Trac-reflective particles are the most effective". Grain was once dumped against a CSA Dreadnaught: "Since the tractor beam attempts to lock onto thousands — even millions — of particles at one time, the hold on the larger mass is lost."
  - The "charged particle missile" counter failed. Imperial doctrine is "blanket firing of turbolasers … 'lucky shot'".
  - The "shear-plane mode" ("datapage 87,126 of the Imperial Tractor Beam Primer") causes a "loop snarl"; Mithel requests a level-three technician.
- **Lt. Reynol Kosk:** Trogan garrison officer bribed by Niles Ferrier to attack Karrde's smuggler meeting; killed.
- **Lt. Rejlii Mithel:** "son of the infamous 'Mauler' Mithel one of Vader's wingmen, killed at the Battle of Yavin". Promoted by Thrawn after the Ghtroc-freighter shroud incident. Tractor beams 7D+2.

## Ch. 5: The Fringe (p. 82 on; Tipn report "roughly nine years after the destruction of Mount Tantiss")
- **Lando:** Nkllon (Athega) mining was financed by NR "low-interest loans"; "fifty-one of Lando's mole miners" were stolen. He once searched for the Dark Force himself, "couldn't find so much as a seat cushion".
> "I just hate to see brutal clods like the Imperials pick up too many winning hands … These clowns just have no finesse." — p. 83
  - Stats: business: mining 11D+2, bargain: tibanna gas 11D+2, gambling 10D+1, starfighter piloting 9D, blaster 8D+2. Tantiss: blaster 9D, bureaucracy: Imperial Navy procedures 9D. FP 6.
- **Sidebar "A Game of Sabacc, Anyone?":** 76 card-chips; four suits (sabers, staves, flasks, coins) of 1–11 plus Commander, Mistress, Master, Ace (12–15).
  - Face cards: Idiot (0), Queen of Air and Darkness (−2), Endurance (−8), Balance (−11), Demise (−13), Moderation (−14), the Evil One (−15), the Star (−17).
  - Pure sabacc is 23. An idiot's array (0, 2, 3) "beats a pure sabacc" at most tables. Skifter cheating.
- **Talon Karrde:** keeps his top position secret. His data proved the Ukio "super weapon" "was a cleverly constructed hoax"; Bilbringi data showed "22 otherwise ordinary asteroids" being modified. He went to Wayland "for a mere 70,000 credits, paid by agitated Bothan diplomats". He refuses to deal in slavery or kidnapping. Command 9D. Rumored NR Intelligence training, which he denies.
- **Mara Jade** (Na'al confidential journal):
> "If she had been allowed to go on the sail barge with the rest of Jabba's court, Luke's vision would have been history instead of one possible past." — p. 86
> "Her one great talent was that she could hear the Emperor's call no matter where she was, and she could report back to him in the same way." — p. 87
  - "rudimentary Force abilities, taught to her by the Emperor".
  - Recognition code: "Hapspir, Barrini, Corbolan, Triaxis; the Emperor's Hand has returned". At Thrawn's secret promotion she was introduced as "the Emperor's favorite dancer, Lianna".
  - Her Force strike on Thrawn failed: "her powers were not that great". She knows "the private back door the Emperor had installed in the main computers of every Star Destroyer".
  - After the Katana battle she spent "nearly a month unconscious". She foresaw the Palace attack and "mentally project[ed]" to Leia; Himron falsely named her as the mole. She received Anakin's lightsaber afterward.
  - **Stats:** "Force skills: Control 2D, sense 2D+2, alter 1D+1"; blaster 9D+1, hold-out blaster 10D+2, starfighter piloting 9D+2. Powers include telekinetic kill and projective telepathy. DSP 5. Tantiss: lightsaber 4D+2; Control 2D+2, sense 2D+2, alter 1D+2.
- **Karrde's organization:**
  - Aves (dispatcher)
  - Chin (from Myrkr, vornskr handler, ysalamiri removal)
  - Dankin, Fynn Torve, Wadewarn (pilots)
  - Ghent: "probably one of the best 'slicers' in the galaxy"; found Delta Source; about 1.5 m tall.
  - Ships: Etherway, Starry Ice, Wild Karrde. Abregado relief shipments.
- PDF p. 86: full-page "Talon Karrde's Myrkr Base" map/illustration (art).
- **Sidebar "The Empire's Locate and Detain List"** (fiction): bounty hunter Gunner Groth tails a red-gold-haired woman on Rishi. The List was a Palpatine "temporary security measure" to keep COMPNOR busy (continues).
