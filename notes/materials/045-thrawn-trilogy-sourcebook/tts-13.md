# Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook, notes part 13: PDF pp. 137–146 (book pp. 140–149), Ch. 7 Aliens (Defel–Svivreni)

- **Defel:** absorb non-UV light, so they appear as shadows; invisibility gives +3D to sneak; claws STR+2D. "Defels are considered to be a myth by most of the galaxy"; often taken for supernatural beings.
- **Duinuogwuin (Star Dragons)**, the key scaling entry:
> "Star Dragons average about ten meters in length, but legends persist that a few ancient Duinuogwuin have reached sizes of over one hundred meters." — p. 141
> "They have been encountered on diverse worlds and even in deep space without apparent protection from vacuum and void, thus earning the name Star Dragons." — p. 141
> "They have a deep-rooted sense of morality, and an almost genetic predisposition toward the Force. … Tales that Duinuogwuin once served as Jedi Knights remain nothing more than stories and rumors" — pp. 141–142
  - Each generation can show new traits ("seem to evolve with each succeeding generation"). The legendary Dragon Graveyards; they "never die in public places".
  - Some offspring are "ravaging monsters which the parents are forced to destroy".
  - Stats: Attribute Dice 18D; STR 3D/6D; space survival (stamina roll monthly); flight in space and atmosphere, Move space 5, atmosphere 210 (600 km/h); size "10-100+ meters long".
  - Story factor "Evil Cunning": "In truth, they are among the most dangerous creatures in the galaxy."
  - Gotal entry: "the Duinuogwuin-Gotal conflict … only the intervention of Jedi mediators were able to stave off full-scale war."
- **Elomin:** order-loving; lommite is "a major component in the manufacturing of transparisteel".
- **Ewoks:** catapults deal "2D speeder scale damage"; smell +1D.
- **Farghul:** teeth +2D, claws +1D; con +2D. "The Jedi Knights once attempted to clean out the smuggling and piracy bases" on Farrfin. NR citizens "are so easy to liberate from their credit vouchers."
- **Filvians:** "capable of going as long as 30 standard days without food or water"; tech aptitude.
- **Gotals:** "Gotal hunters on their homeworld have learned to sense prey as far away as ten kilometers". Search +3D within 10 km; mood detection table (−3D to +3D); fast initiative +1D; droids within 3 m impose −1D. Homeworld is Antar IV's moon.
- **Herglics** (Giju): submitted to the Empire after "endless slaughter"; blubber +1D physical; gambling frenzy; can be found on the *Coral Vanda* and *Kuari Princess*.
- **Humans: Xa Fel:** a lost colony; lifespan dropped "from 120 standard years to less than 50"; Attribute Dice 9D.
- **Mrissi:** 0.3–0.5 m avians; freed two years ago, then retaken by Thrawn.
- **Rishii:** perfect mimics; slings (STR+1D); flight 13/18 (37/52 km/h).
- **Sarkans:** greetings "must last at least an hour", and formal ones "often last more than eight hours"; three-hour dances; tail STR+3D; STR 3D/6D+1; can die within 20 minutes of freezing.
- **Sluissi:** Technical +4D, but tasks take twice as long; unexcited even by the Sluis Van raid.
- **Svivreni:** stocky; hair length signals status (cont.).
