# Owner Material 037 — Star Wars D20 Drive folder (Core, Revised Core and Saga Edition): Sourcebooks/Star Wars Gamemaster Screen.pdf [Star Wars Roleplaying Game Saga Edition (2007)]

- **Source:** Wizards of the Coast Star Wars Roleplaying Game books, web enhancements, Living Force campaign modules and articles (2000–2010), from the owner's "Star Wars D20" Drive folder (only the three edition subfolders; "General" was not read, as instructed). Secondary source (licensed Legends reference). Scan OCR'd on the box; the scan stays box-only and is **not** published.

Pages with nothing power-relevant are still listed with what is on them.
- **Citation:** "*Star Wars Gamemaster Screen* (SWRPG Saga Edition), p.X" by printed page ("scan p.X" where no printed number applies). **Weight:** Licensed RPG material: a secondary source. Narrative text sums up Legends lore; game statistics are rules for players, not canon power levels, and rank lowest.
