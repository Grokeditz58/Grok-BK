Pages with nothing power-relevant are still listed with what is on them.
- **Citation:** "*Living Force Campaign - Year* (SWRPG d20 Revised Core), p.X" by printed page ("scan p.X" where no printed number applies). **Weight:** Licensed RPG material: a secondary source. Narrative text sums up Legends lore; game statistics are rules for players, not canon power levels, and rank lowest.
