## scan p.2 (scan page 2)

Page contents: Credits and legal. Intro: a complete story arc—liberate worlds, delve into the secrets of the extinct Jedi, "feel the bitter taste of betrayal at the hands of a trusted ally." The Traitor's Gambit (Thompson): Sel Zonn Station over Brentaal; former Imperial Admiral Gilder Varth funneled information to Bail Organa, then imprisoned on Felucia; heroes rescue him. A Wretched Hive (Thompson): the "Sarlacc Project"; Darga the Hutt on Cato Neimoidia; heroes free Jedi Master Denia, comatose in his dungeons; the Force-sensitive majordomo. (hand-read)


### Stat block notes (game mechanics, not feats)

- Gilder Varth (campaign primer): A former Imperial Admiral who "has been funneling information about the Empire to the Senator for some months now, though he has gone silent since being arrested and imprisoned on the planet Felucia"; rescued in The Traitor's Gambit; reveals he funneled money and resources to the "Sarlacc Project." (Source: *Gamemaster Primer* (SWRPG Saga Edition), scan p.2)
- Denia found in Darga's palace: "Darga has been keeping a Jedi Master and veteran of the Clone Wars in a coma in the dungeons of his palace. After the heroes free Master Denia, she helps them deal with the Hutt's majordomo, a Force-sensitive criminal who has been using the Force to rise in power within Darga's organization." (Source: *Gamemaster Primer* (SWRPG Saga Edition), scan p.2)

### Stat block notes (game mechanics, not feats)

- Varth's betrayal (primer): "They receive a transmission from the Admiral aboard the Imperial Star Destroyer, who reveals that he has merely been trying to uncover Organa's treachery all along." (Source: *Gamemaster Primer* (SWRPG Saga Edition), scan p.4)
- Denia's sacrifice (primer): "He has clearly grown in power since their last confrontation and seems unbeatable in open conflict. But Master Denia sacrifices her own life to weaken Draco, which makes the battle more of a fair fight. Rather than fall to the heroes, Draco flees the Citadel in his personal shuttle." (Source: *Gamemaster Primer* (SWRPG Saga Edition), scan p.4)
- Lady Alya Aldrete (primer): "Lady Alya reveals that her connections in the Deep Core go quite deep, and she has been marshaling an entire fleet of smugglers and pirates right under the Empire's nose." (Source: *Gamemaster Primer* (SWRPG Saga Edition), scan p.4)
