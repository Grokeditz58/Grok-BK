Secondary source (licensed Legends reference). Scan OCR'd on the box; the scan stays box-only and is **not** published.
- **Method:** every scanned page was OCR'd and machine-screened sentence by sentence for power-relevant statements (fights, kills, defeats, Force feats, rankings, superweapons) that name a character on our roster; each kept sentence is quoted verbatim from the OCR text and has a crop of the printed passage directly under it. Pages with nothing power-relevant are still listed with what is on them.
- **Citation:** "*Campaign Standards v1-2* (SWRPG Saga Edition), p.X" by printed page ("scan p.X" where no printed number applies). **Weight:** Licensed RPG material: a secondary source. Narrative text sums up Legends lore; game statistics are rules for players, not canon power levels, and rank lowest.


## scan p.1 (scan page 1)

Page contents: RPGA Campaign Standards v1.2, 1st/3rd Level, December 2007, by Rodney Thompson and Chris Tulach. What is Dawn of Defiance (10 linked adventures between Episodes III and IV, levels 1–20); How Do I Participate (downloads, 12–16 hours each, Herald-level GM, Rewards Points/Star Wars Rewards cards); What Do I Need (SWSE core, 4–6 players plus GM, play in order). Character Creation: start 1st level with Traitor's Gambit or 3rd with A Wretched Hive; 28-point planned ability generation (table 9=1 … 18=16); any core species except Ewoks; Nautolan via the Battle of Glee Anselm Rewards card; Devaronian via web enhancement; droids use Option 1. (hand-read)


## scan p.2 (scan page 2)

Page contents: Custom droids only, 23 ability points; all heroic classes (Force-users should be careful given the Empire's policy toward Jedi); maximum starting credits; allowed options (core incl. Jensaarai Defender and Dathomiri Witch talent trees, Tech Specialist and Devaronian web enhancements, Rewards cards); Sith Apprentice and Sith Master unavailable. Equipment: no military/illegal at creation except heavy and hold-out blaster pistols; "(unlicensed)" found items; black market items count as legit. Vehicles: no military/illegal, droid starfighter or tri-fighter. Destiny: destruction, discovery or rescue only; a Force Spirit ties to another Force-sensitive PC. The Force: Dark Side Score can't be reduced; falling to the dark side counts as death. (hand-read)


## scan p.3 (scan page 3)

Page contents: Character Death (sit out the adventure, return at the next adventure's minimum level with 1st-level credits). Playing A Wretched Hive First (new 3rd-level character; can't go back to Traitor's Gambit). The Golden Rule (GM is final authority). After the Adventure: logsheets; hit points per level (noble/scoundrel 4, scout 5, Jedi/soldier 7, + Con modifier). (hand-read)


## scan p.4 (scan page 4)

Page contents: Blank Dawn of Defiance logsheet (adventure name, XP, Force/Destiny points, destiny choice, Dark Side points, death, destiny fulfilled). (hand-read)
