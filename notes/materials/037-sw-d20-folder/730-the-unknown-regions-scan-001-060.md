# Owner Material 037 — Star Wars D20 Drive folder (Core, Revised Core and Saga Edition): Sourcebooks/The Unknown Regions.pdf [Star Wars Roleplaying Game Saga Edition (2007)] (scan pages 1–60)

- **Source:** Wizards of the Coast Star Wars Roleplaying Game books, web enhancements, Living Force campaign modules and articles (2000–2010), from the owner's "Star Wars D20" Drive folder (only the three edition subfolders; "General" was not read, as instructed). Secondary source (licensed Legends reference). Scan OCR'd on the box; the scan stays box-only and is **not** published.
- **Method:** every scanned page was OCR'd and machine-screened sentence by sentence for power-relevant statements (fights, kills, defeats, Force feats, rankings, superweapons) that name a character on our roster; each kept sentence is quoted verbatim from the OCR text and has a crop of the printed passage directly under it. Pages with nothing power-relevant are still listed with what is on them.
- **Citation:** "*The Unknown Regions* (SWRPG Saga Edition), p.X" by printed page ("scan p.X" where no printed number applies). **Weight:** Licensed RPG material: a secondary source. Narrative text sums up Legends lore; game statistics are rules for players, not canon power levels, and rank lowest.

### Stat block notes (game mechanics, not feats)

- The Unknown Regions: The Republic's Outbound Flight expedition is a well-known example of a large-scale, officially sanctioned expedition that proves to be a complete failure. Less well known, but of greater galactic impact, is the Emperor's decree that Grand Admiral Thrawn establish an Empire of the Hand to extend Imperial control into a region known as Wild Space. (Source: *The Unknown Regions* (SWRPG Saga Edition), scan p.7)
- The Empire: Under the Emperor's orders, Grand Admiral Thrawn enters the unknown region called Wild Space to establish the Empire of the Hand. The Emperor also orders greater exploration of the Deep Core, where he seeks out isolated worlds for his own purposes. (Source: *The Unknown Regions* (SWRPG Saga Edition), scan p.7)
- The New Republic and the Galactic Alliance: After the establishment of the New Republic, the Unknown Regions become a greater source of threats to the stability of the galaxy, including the return of Grand Admiral Thrawn and the Killik-instigated Swarm War. (Source: *The Unknown Regions* (SWRPG Saga Edition), scan p.7)
