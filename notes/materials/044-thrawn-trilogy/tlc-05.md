# The Last Command: notes part 5, ch. 11 (cont.) to ch. 13

## Ch. 11 (cont.): Mara reveals Wayland
- Mara's origins: "I remember the first time I met the Emperor, and the ride here in his private ship. But I don't have any memories of where I started from." Of her parents: "Not much more than shadows."
- **Mount Tantiss cloning cavern:**
> "One of the artificial caverns held a complete cloning facility he'd apparently appropriated from one of the clonemasters. … It had a full nutrient delivery system in place, plus a flash-teaching setup for personality imprinting and tech training on the clones while they developed. … It was arranged in concentric tiers, sort of like a sport arena, and it filled the whole cavern. … I'd say at least twenty thousand. Maybe more." — Mara, ch. 11
> "Twenty thousand … And he can turn out a clone from each one every twenty days." — Leia, ch. 11
  (ANALYSIS: ≈1,000 clones/day at capacity.)
- Mara was at Wayland "only once, and the Emperor flew the ship himself."
- Leia senses Han and Luke approaching from a distance ("cocking her head slightly … as if listening").
- Luke senses "turmoil in the Wookiee's emotions". He also does a Force sweep around the office: "No beings or droids anywhere nearby."
- Leia's reasoning: "I don't think Thrawn would have tried so hard to convince us that Mara was a party to that kidnapping attempt unless he wanted us to disbelieve anything she might have told us about Wayland." Luke: "somehow his destiny and Mara's were joined together at this place in time."
- Team: Luke, Han, Lando, Mara, Chewie (later), and the droids. Leia's idea is a Noghri guard, so Chewie can go.

## Ch. 12: Bilbringi
- Bilbringi: "a massive Golan II battle station bristling with armament". Major yards are also at "Ord Trasi or Vaga Minor".
- "Twenty-two" asteroids "maybe forty meters across each" are being prepared (ANALYSIS: they become the cloaked-asteroid siege of Coruscant).
- Mazzic's saboteurs blow up a nearly finished ISD: "the starboard flank of the Star Destroyer blew up … a section of the forward hull followed". Ellor's two Corellian Gunships wipe out a TIE formation.
- Karrde: "We're going to have to organize. All of us."
- **C'baoth takes over the Chimaera:**
> "GRAND ADMIRAL THRAWN!" The shout roared through the bridge like a violent thunderclap … An angry radiance seemed to burn the air around him; behind him, the two stormtroopers guarding the entrance to the bridge were sprawled on the floor, unconscious or dead." — ch. 12
- C'baoth Force-reads "hundreds of officers and techs" around the asteroids to piece together Thrawn's plan: "trying to draw out those speculations and compile them into a complete picture".
> "He raised his hands above his head, an eerie blue-white coronal sheen playing about them. … The crewers were sitting stiffly in their chairs … the officers were equally stiff … the displays had all gone static. … C'baoth had taken command of the Chimaera." — ch. 12
- **Thrawn's scale objection (key scaling quote):**
> "It's a minimum of five days to Coruscant from here … Five days during which you'll have to maintain your control of the Chimaera's thirty-seven thousand crewers. Longer, of course, if you intend for them to actually fight at the end of that voyage. And if you intend for us to arrive with any support craft, that figure of thirty-seven thousand will increase rather steeply." — Thrawn, ch. 12
- C'baoth: "I see many possible futures … In not all of them do you survive." "Never assume you are indispensable to my Empire, Grand Admiral Thrawn. Only I am that."
- Aftermath: crew "collapsed limply in their seats … lying trembling on the deck". Thrawn needs "a five-hundred-man caretaker crew for the next six hours". Pellaeon later reads "the medical reports for those who'd had to recover in sick bay".
- Thrawn: "History is on the move, Captain. Those who cannot keep up will be left behind … And those who stand in our way … will not watch at all."
- Thrawn lets General Drost off with a warning: "His chief weakness is a tendency to become complacent."

## Ch. 13: Noghri guard; jailbreak; C'baoth to Wayland
- The Noghri honor guard is led by Cakhmaim clan Eikh'mir, with Mobvekhar clan Hakh'khar and Khabarakh. Han is "consort of the Lady Vader". The Noghri are silent: "He would have sworn none of the half-sized aliens were anywhere near him."
- **Luke's TK catch:** "The figure simply held up a hand, and the data pad skidded to a halt in midair." Mara confirms it's Luke by "stretching out with her mind".
- A G-2RD guard droid is disabled with a restraining bolt: "You get a G-2RD droid going, and you'll have to take its head off to stop it."
- Route: Obroa-skai is "the last stop before Wayland on that trip".
- **C'baoth leaves for Wayland,** sensing something ("Has something happened on Wayland?"). Thrawn sends him on the Star Galleon Draklor with General Covell, "a company of the general's best troops", and "a list of troops and crewers assigned as cloning templets". Thrawn: "this is more likely to be a solution than a problem."
- Delta Source: "Someday I'll turn Delta Source over to you. But not yet."
- Pellaeon on Endor: "Vader's Super Star Destroyer Executor and far too many of the best and brightest".

## Ch. 14 (opening): Coruscant siege begins
- **Leia's vision-dream:** "Mara and Luke, waiting as the dark hooded shadow moved toward them, a lightsaber glittering in its hand. Back behind the figure an old man stood, craziness in his eyes and blue lightning in his hands." Leia: "Luke would be able to tell from space if C'baoth or another Dark Jedi was there."
- "eight Imperial Interdictor Cruisers" plus "two more Interdictors, plus an escort of eight Katana-fleet Dreadnaughts" arrive at Coruscant's Sector Four.
