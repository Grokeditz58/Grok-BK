# Dark Force Rising — notes part 6: ch. 15–17

## Ch. 15 — Bel Iblis's history; blue-gold upholstery
- **Tangrene**: "We blasted a major Ubiqtorate center into fine powder ... And then waltzed out right under the collective nose of the three Star Destroyers that were supposed to be guarding the place." All five of his warships survived, one "out of commission for nearly seven months". He now has six Dreadnaughts.
- Peregrine's Nest is the fourteenth site in seven years. The buildings are "bi-state memory plastic". At Lelmra, lightning triggered a fold-up with "nearly fifty people still inside".
- Fey'lya's favors: a supply line through New Cov, summoning Star Cruisers, and shifting funds.
- Bel Iblis knows the Palace leak is called **Delta Source** and that it reports "personally to the Grand Admiral".
- Lando's skepticism: the camp "has been here a lot longer than half a year". The worst case he raises is "a giant Imperial scam".
- **The Katana clue:** under the gray chair upholstery the fabric is blue-gold. "The Fleet doesn’t do the interiors of military ships in blue-gold ... Except one time ... The Katana fleet." Lando "once spent two whole months researching the Dark Force". The ships run "only two thousand crewers instead of the normal sixteen".

## Ch. 16 — The Corellian Treaty; the Coral Vanda; Thrawn's misread
- Lando checks the bar's repeater panel for slave-circuit input slots and serial numbers.
- Sena: "It was Senator Bel Iblis, not Mon Mothma, who convinced those three resistance groups to agree to a meeting" (the Corellian Treaty). After Bail Organa's death, Bel Iblis suspected Mon Mothma would "overthrow the Emperor only to set herself up in his place." He moved to Peregrine's Nest "just under three years ago", ran only materiel raids, and waited.
- Bothans "think in terms of political and persuasive influence, not military power". When a Bothan leader stumbles, he is attacked: "In the distant past the attacks were literal—knives and usually death." "Bothans don’t stick their necks out far enough to concoct plots on their own."
- Their Dreadnaught source is a thin, white-haired, "weasely" gambler who meets on the **Coral Vanda**, a subocean casino on Pantolomin. (ANALYSIS: the survivor of Karrde's old crew, named later in the book.)
- Bel Iblis: "I won’t go to Mon Mothma like a beggar". "what we have here barely qualifies as a strike force." Sena: "if you betray the Senator ... you will die."
- Thrawn's informant smuggler: "fifty-two reports over the last ten years, forty-eight of which proved to be accurate."
- **Thrawn's misread:** he thinks Khabarakh's return was a deliberate Rebel distraction. "Doubtless when we break Khabarakh, we’ll learn a great many things from him that will cost us countless man-hours to finally prove wrong." He leaves Khabarakh for "the full seven days of public shame". He guesses Leia went to Endor for "The location of the Mount Tantiss storehouse."

## Ch. 17 — Abregado; Mara turns herself in; "Emperor's Hand"
- Wedge, posing as a broker, releases the *Etherway*. He passes on Han's request for information on "our friend with the eyes".
- Mara's danger sense ("the back of her neck began to tingle") warns her before the VSD *Adamant* appears.
- Recognition code: "Hapspir, Barrini, Corbolan, Triaxis". "Tell him ... that he knew me as the Emperor’s Hand."
- **Hyperdrive speed figure (scaling):**
> "pushing uncomfortably far past a Victory Star Destroyer’s normal flank speed of Point Four Five. Possibly even as high as Point Five, which would mean they were covering a hundred twenty-seven light-years per hour." — DFR ch. 17
- (ANALYSIS: Point Five is about 127 ly/h, so roughly 3,050 ly/day. The Abregado–Endor transit took 22 hours.)
- At Endor, Mara can "feel the evidence of his death. It’s not pleasant."
- Proof of identity: at the dedication of the Imperial Palace's Assemblage wing, the Emperor introduced Mara "as Lianna, one of his favorite dancers". In the private ceremony afterward came "Your secret promotion to the rank of Grand Admiral". She picks out her "Xyquine" shoulder-sculp from holos.
- The Emperor's compulsion surfaces: "YOU WILL KILL LUKE SKYWALKER." It is "the Emperor’s wizened face glaring at her".
- Mara's Force abilities: "teaching her how to use her rare sensitivity to the Force to serve him". She was "hearing his commands more clearly than the rest of his Hands".
> "Your memory serves you poorly, Emperor’s Hand. When all is said and done, you were little more than a highly specialized courier." / "What do you mean, the rest of his Hands? ... I was the only—" — DFR ch. 17
- Mara demands a 3 million credit line and the bounty lifted in exchange for the Katana fleet. Thrawn gives her "exactly eight days" and plants a homing device on her ship.
- She says of Thrawn's voice: "The voice was rich with the overtones of command, and Mara found herself walking toward him before she realized". The second time she resists.
- Thrawn displays the art that decorated the Rendili StarDrive and Fleet planning offices during the Katana design. He says the Bel Iblis Dreadnaughts are slave-rigged: "subtle, but visible enough when you know to look". The fleet has been lost "for nearly fifty-five years".
- Ferrier is re-tasked to follow Solo and Calrissian. Thrawn: "She’s not part of the Empire anymore".
