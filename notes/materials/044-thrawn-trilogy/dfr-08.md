# Dark Force Rising — notes part 8: ch. 22–24

## Ch. 22 — Infiltrating the Chimaera
- Luke cuts through the shuttle's floor and the ISD hangar deck with his lightsaber, beveling the plug, and works the winch switch by Force. He drops to the deck below: "his Jedi-enhanced muscles handled the impact without trouble."
- **Mass mind suppression:** in a ready room with three men, "Reaching out through the Force, he gently touched their minds, shunting the curiosity away." He takes three flight suits and keeps holding the suppression afterward "until they’ve forgotten I was ever in there". He contrasts this with Nkllon's grabbing and twisting of minds, which brought him "perilously close to the edge of the dark side". He doubts it would work on "too alert" detention guards.
- Garbage compactor: Luke makes a Jedi-enhanced jump that reaches "only about halfway to the top", then chimney-climbs. He cuts the grating and frees Karrde, who is in the "softening-up phase. Lack of food and sleep".
- Thrawn deduces the intrusion from an ysalamir found in a crashed Skipray. He infers hard-wired Emperor codes and **shuts down the Chimaera's main computer** ("blind and cripple the craft").
- *Falcon* is moved to vehicle deep storage. The Endor patrol is waiting for Leia's return.

## Ch. 23 — The escape
- Mara drops two gunners with hand-edge neck strikes. Turbolift cars track passenger counts.
- Mara kills two stormtroopers with "two shots per stormtrooper". "A single sweep of the lightsaber caught them both" (two naval troopers).
- Thrawn reads lightsaber wounds: "near-microscopic cuts across the bodies with partial cauterization ... one of the intruders is the Jedi Luke Skywalker." He has the ysalamiri from Engineering and Systems Control moved to the hangar bays and herds the intruders through turbolift nexus control. Then: "The Millennium Falcon ... Where is it? ... We have them, Captain."
- Mara: "If there was one thing Thrawn had over all the other Grand Admirals, it was a knack for predicting his enemies’ strategy."
- **Telekinesis feats:** "reaching out through the Force, he yanked the blaster out of the trooper’s hand ... The blaster, heading straight toward Luke, suddenly reversed direction. The trooper caught the butt end full in the stomach".
- Luke sends a telepathic message: "Mara, he sent silently ... Wait until I give the word". She reacts to it.
- **vs. 8 stormtroopers:** Luke's "first sweep took off the barrel of the lead stormtrooper’s blaster rifle; reaching out with the Force, he gave the man a hard shove, pushing him into his companions and sending the whole bunch of them tumbling". He deflects "a half dozen more shots". "The lightsaber slashed four times and it was over."
- **Force catch:** Mara jumps from 5+ m. "Reaching out with the Force, Luke caught her in an invisible grip, slowing her descent".
- Karrde flies the *Falcon* through the gap of a descending lift plate and skims above the ISD's main drive exhaust to shield the ship's underside from TIEs. Luke on the quad guns: "focused his mind and let the Force flow into him".
- With the main computer down, the "weapons and tractor beam systems were useless against a ship that small, that fast, and that distant."
- Karrde: "approximately two hundred pre-Clone Wars vintage Dreadnaught-class heavy cruisers". Thrawn: "We’ve lost a round, Captain ... No more." Intelligence has identified Karrde's former crewman, and Thrawn sets course for Pantolomin: "Now it’s a race."

## Ch. 24 — Nystao: Leia wins over the Noghri
- Leia recalls "Her mother’s face, taut and sad, lifting her from the darkness of the trunk ... Crying again when her mother died". (ANALYSIS: Legends-era Leia memories of her mother, pre-prequel. Later retconned.)
- The maitrakh lost four sons to the Emperor's battles. Khabarakh is "A son of the son of my firstson".
- **Timeline:** "Forty-eight Noghri years ... In years of the Emperor, forty-four." Leia: "It didn’t happen during the Rebellion ... It happened during the Clone Wars."
- A convocation forms "close to a thousand Noghri". There are 13 dynasts, with Vor'corkh leading.
- **Leia's Force feats:** "she reached out through the Force with all the power and control she could manage— And her lightsaber leaped from her belt ... jumping up in front of her. Her eyes and mind found the switch, and with a snap-hiss the brilliant green-white blade flashed into existence". She holds it floating while speaking, "wondering how long she could hold the weapon steady in midair".
> "I am not merely the daughter of the Lord Vader ... I am the Mal’ary’ush: heir to his authority and his power." — Leia, DFR ch. 24
- Noghri hunters catch the fleeing espionage droid (a probe droid motivator/recorder with no antenna) and carry it back "like the prize from a hunt".
- **The kholm-grass truth:** "The Emperor’s scientists took your kholm-grass and changed it ... A chemical which has one purpose only: to inhibit the growth of all other plant life. The machines ... are in fact doing nothing but destroying this special kholm-grass". A drop of the decon droids' catalytic agent makes the plant "turn black and wither ... disintegrated into a fine powder."
- Nine of the thirteen dynasts side with Ir'khaim to hear her. Vor'corkh: "True warriors understand the value of patience." The cover story: Khabarakh "escaped", with three commando teams in "pursuit". Skipping Endor to go straight to Coruscant saves "Approximately four days".
