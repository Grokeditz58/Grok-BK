# Dark Force Rising — notes part 3: ch. 6–8

## Ch. 6 — Sarka convoy hit; Delta Source; C'baoth at Taanab
- A Bulk Cruiser converted to a carrier launches "three full squadrons of A-wing starfighters". Thrawn: the idea "was first proposed over twenty years ago." A-wings are suited to "hit-and-fade", and escort duty "largely neutralizes their speed advantage".
- Thrawn on Fey'lya: "I’ve spent many hours studying Bothan art ... Councilor Fey’lya will play his part beautifully. As beautifully as if we were pulling his strings directly."
> "Ackbar himself is replaceable, Captain. The delicate political balance the Rebellion has created for itself is not." — Thrawn, DFR ch. 6
- The *Death's Head* is sent to Palanhi to trap Solo. **Delta Source** reports Skywalker is going to Jomark; it feeds information "from the very heart of the Imperial Palace" and eludes "hourly counterintelligence sweeps".
- Thrawn reads a cluster trap on a Frigate blister and loses one TIE interceptor "half a heartbeat too late".
- **The ysalamiri leash:** "Each of them creating a bubble in the Force where none of C’baoth’s Jedi tricks would work."
> "a man with C’baoth’s mental and emotional instabilities can never be a threat to us. Yes, he has a great deal of power, and at any given moment he could certainly do considerable damage to our people and equipment. But by his very nature he’s unable to use that power for any length of time. Concentration, focus, long-term thinking—those are the qualities that separate a warrior from a mere flailing fighter." — Thrawn, DFR ch. 6
- Thrawn takes a course to Honoghr: "The entire Noghri populace ... needs to be reminded of who it is they serve."
- "Economics and psychology, Captain." He lets the convoy go to spread the tale. Ship hunt: "five more capital ships ... Nothing larger than an old Star Galleon".
- **C'baoth's POV — the Taanab feat (KEY):**
> "Yes, it had been difficult, that business of taking command of the Bellicose’s turbolaser crews for the few seconds it had required to annihilate that Rebel ship. It had gone beyond any previous stretch of concentration and control, and the mental aches he was feeling now were the payment for that stretch." — DFR ch. 6
> "They made him fight their battles for them—drove him by their disbelief to do things he hadn’t attempted for years and years. And then, when he’d succeeded in doing the impossible ..." — DFR ch. 6
- Captain Aban was "shouting and blaspheming at him ... as C’baoth calmly inflicted his punishment on the Rebel ship". C'baoth disobeyed a direct order; Pellaeon calls it "mutiny".
- He tracks a rider on a Cracian Thumper from bed at a distance through the Force ("hard to concentrate—so very hard"). He calls his robe to him telekinetically.
- On Wayland he ruled a city-state of humans, Psadans, and Myneyrshi. Plan: "to bend and twist another Jedi to his will without the other even being aware". "And when he’d succeeded, there would be two of them … and who could tell what might be possible then?"

## Ch. 7 — Endor; Ilic, New Cov; Luke as judge
- **Leia passes through the spot where the second Death Star exploded:** "a black curtain seemed to drop across her vision … The menace, the rage; the hatred, the despair." Chewie and 3PO feel nothing. "anger and bitterness; but at the same time, there was something almost sad". (ANALYSIS: a Force echo of the Emperor's death site five years later.)
- Ilic is a domed jungle city. Its biomolecule exports pay a "covert form of taxation" to the Empire through raids.
- Barabel vs. Rodian: the Barabel shoots a droid "In the flick of an eye". Luke separates the blasters with the ignited blade. Luke uses "sensory enhancement techniques" and smells Ferrier's tabac in the crowd.
- Imperial scrip; exchange rate "five to four Empire/Republic" (Ferrier gives 5/3). Barabels "always had a soft spot for Jedi."
- Luke: "Ben Kenobi had introduced him to the Force; Yoda had taught him how to use the Force for self-control and self-defense. Neither had ever taught him anything about mediating arguments."

## Ch. 8 — Sena, Irenez; escape from Ilic; the Dreadnaughts
- Breil'lya calls Han an "impostor" and fails Sena's test.
- Imperial force estimate: "three drop ships ... one or two on the ground ... a hundred sixty to two hundred men".
- **Luke's mind trick on a stormtrooper:** "Luke’s hand making some sort of gesture; and suddenly the Imperial spun around in the opposite direction ... ‘He thinks he heard a noise.’"
- Luke makes "a single leap" to the top of the X-wing and cuts the restraining bolt with his lightsaber.
- The beckon-call decoy trick. Out of four TIEs over the city, "Luke got three of them on the fly, and Han took out the fourth."
- Luke declines to repeat the Nkllon pilot-scrambling: "I—don’t think it’s good for me to do that sort of thing."
- Luke flies his X-wing through two TIE waves: "the X-wing managed somehow to thread its way unscathed through the melee ... the whole squadron hot on his tail."
- Three Dreadnaughts drop in "in perfect unison". The ships are "six hundred meters long each ... even three of them working together would be hard pressed to take out an Imperial Star Destroyer." They use ion barrages and then escape.
- The Commander "seemed extremely interested in meeting again with Captain Solo." Luke heads for Jomark.
