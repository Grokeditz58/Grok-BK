# Dark Force Rising — notes part 1: front matter, ch. 1–2

## Front matter
- The praise page quotes reviews of *Heir to the Empire* (Chicago Sun-Times, Hartford Courant, etc.).
- Copyright: Bantam hardcover June 1992, paperback April 1993; text © 1992 Lucasfilm; eISBN 978-0-307-79618-9.
- Contents: 29 chapters, About the Author, Also By, the EU intro, the **TLC excerpt** (bm3), six era intros, and the Legends timeline.

## Ch. 1 — Myrkr raid; Wild Karrde; C'baoth on Jomark
> "Never forget, Captain, that our goal is no longer merely the pitiful rear-guard harassment of the past five years. With Mount Tantiss and our late Emperor’s collection of Spaarti cylinders in our hands, the initiative is once again ours." — Thrawn, DFR ch. 1
- Thrawn's twelve-Grand-Admiral context: "one of his twelve Grand Admirals ... given Thrawn’s not-entirely-human heritage and the Emperor’s well-known prejudices". He has commanded the *Chimaera* for "the year".
- Thrawn plans the raid three ways: the base, the Hyllyard City contacts, and field experience. It is masked by a timed transmission summoning the Interdictor *Constrainer*.
> "it’s the only sizable cover available for nearly ten million kilometers around Myrkr." — Thrawn, DFR ch. 1
> "You forget, Captain ... that I’ve met the man. More important, I’ve seen the sort of artwork he collects." — Thrawn, DFR ch. 1
- Covell's force: 4 AT-ATs (one per side), AT-STs, and hoverscouts carrying 8-man squads. Covell "had directed literally hundreds of assault campaigns". Myrkr's "metal-rich flora" dampens comms.
- Vornskrs stare at Mara the way the wild ones stared at Luke "just before they attacked him". (ANALYSIS: this marks her Force-sensitivity.)
- **Mara's precognitive danger sense**: she powers up *before* the Interdictor arrives. "They’re coming." The Interdictor drops in "not twenty kilometers away".
> "With their four huge gravity-wave generators capable of simulating planet-sized masses, Interdictor Cruisers were the Empire’s weapon of choice ... coming in fresh out of lightspeed itself, the Interdictor would need another minute before it could power up those generators." — DFR ch. 1
> "It was all coming back, as it had so many times before in the past five years. The hunches and sensory flickers, the urges and the compulsions. Which meant that, very soon now, the dreams would probably be starting again, too." — Mara, DFR ch. 1
- Mara's post-Endor jobs: serving girl in a Phorliss cantina, "come-up flector for a swoop gang on Caprioril", hyperdrive mechanic in the Ison Corridor. Karrde is "the most powerful smuggler in the galaxy".
- Karrde: "Mara is second in command."
- Thrawn: "Or else he was somehow warned off." He starts working the Mara puzzle.
- The warship invitation: "Eight of the fifteen groups ... expressed interest". Thrawn: "we'll make the invitation a bit more compulsory."
- C'baoth: the Taanab operation will be his last for now. Thrawn takes "a personal hand" in the Leia matter. "The Noghri ... are loyal to me personally."
- Each assault yields "the three best infantry troopers and two best mechanized operators" for transfer to Mount Tantiss and Wayland. (ANALYSIS: these are clone templates.)
- **C'baoth's POV**: Jomark's High Castle sits on a volcanic rim, with Ring Lake "four hundred meters almost straight down". His memory is failing ("only glimpses of memory").
> "He could call upon the Force when he wanted to, and that was what was important. As long as he could do that, no one could ever hurt him" — DFR ch. 1
> "This wasn’t Wayland, which he’d wrested from the Dark Jedi whom the Emperor had set to guard his Mount Tantiss storehouse ... On Wayland he hadn’t had to do anything like that, not since he’d fought against the Emperor’s Guardian. ... It had been a grand battle, that fight against the Guardian." — DFR ch. 1
- He has sensed Force "flickers" "three times before in the past few seasons ... Like someone who had learned how to utilize the Force for a time, but then somehow forgotten." (ANALYSIS: this is Mara.)
- He senses the ISD "entering high orbit, far above the clouds". Battle coordination costs him "effort and pain"; it is "hard ... to hold his thoughts and feelings closely in line, and for long periods at a time."

## Ch. 2 — Sluis Van aftermath; Niles Ferrier
- From Sluis Van to Coruscant is "a good six-day flight" for Luke's X-wing.
- Luke uses Force **short-term memory enhancement** ("one of the Jedi skills he’d learned from Yoda") to replay and freeze a passer-by from a moment earlier.
- Luke senses "Six humans and two aliens, the nearest about two hundred meters straight ahead."
> "And moved almost of its own accord to neatly block the blaster bolt ... Guided by the Force, his hands again shifted the lightsaber blade into the path of the attack." It produces "an odd sort of tunnel vision effect" — DFR ch. 2
- Two blasters fire continuously; Luke blocks everything while advancing.
- Defel ("wraith"): "Their bodies absorb all visible light."
- Lando: Luke is "the guy who took down Darth Vader". The narration: "Luke had, after all, defeated Vader in their last lightsaber duel".
- Ferrier: "The mystic Jedi powers have always been exaggerated." Lando: "We’ve also pretty well proved that Luke can take all of you without any trouble." Ferrier's reply: "The Jedi might survive ... But you wouldn’t."
- The Empire's call for ships pays "twenty percent above market value for anything over a hundred thousand tons that can fight."
- Verpine: they reportedly helped Ackbar design the B-wing.
