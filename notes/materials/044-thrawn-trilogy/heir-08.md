# Heir to the Empire — notes part 8: Chapters 24–25

## Ch. 24
- Han and Lando find Luke's micrel power supply ("Sibha Habadeet", bioelectronic, from an artificial hand). Karrde admits holding Luke (ch. 24).
- Thrawn's deduction: radio silence plus Karrde's refusal plus "the search was over" add up to Skywalker in the Skipray. He deploys "three squads of stormtroopers ... half a biker scout unit, too, and three Chariot light assault vehicles" to Hyllyard City (ch. 24).
> "Sluissi art clearly indicates a biannual cyclic pattern, and I want to hit them at their most sluggish point." — Heir, ch. 24 (Thrawn)
- Mara's training: "Know your territory was the first rule that had been drilled into her ... had sought out and killed several vornskrs and other predators to learn the fastest ways of taking them down" (ch. 24).
- Mara knows Thrawn personally: "Because she knew Thrawn—knew the Grand Admiral’s tenacity and the limits of his patience both." (ch. 24)
- The vornskr ignores Mara and goes for Luke (hunts Force users). Mara kills it with one head shot in the dark (ch. 24).
- Mara: "it hadn’t been his Jedi tricks that had saved him. It had been something else." (ch. 24, about the Emperor's death)
- Karrde: if they can't free Skywalker from the Imperials, "we can’t let them have him alive." (ch. 24)

## Ch. 25 — Leia vs. Noghri (feat); "Mal'ary'ush"
- Leia's Alderaan self-defense reflexes: a kick into body armor, then a pillow-hidden lightsaber "sliced him almost in half" (Feat: Leia kills a Noghri) (ch. 25).
- The second Noghri knocks her saber away and pins her fully; Leia was helpless until he smelled her (ch. 25).
- Noghri attack: 5+ commandos; Salporin killed; Chewie wounded; a stun weapon "set too low for Wookiees" against Ralrra (ch. 25).
- Feat: Leia drops her lit saber on a rope as a pendulum and slices an airspeeder's repulsorlift (ch. 25).
> "You are the Mal’ary’ush ... The daughter and heir of the Lord Darth Vader. ... He who was our master." — Heir, ch. 25 (Khabarakh)
> "Two great starships met in the space over our world ... They fought all the day and much of the night . . . and when the battle was over, our land was devastated." — Heir, ch. 25 (Honoghr backstory)
> "The Lord Vader could read the souls of the Noghri ... You are indeed his Mal’ary’ush." — Heir, ch. 25
- Meeting set: "in orbit above the world of Endor in one month’s time" (ch. 25).
