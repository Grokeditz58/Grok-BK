# Heir to the Empire — notes part 2: Chapters 4–7

## Ch. 4 — C'baoth vs. ysalamiri; Thrawn's plan
- Wayland: "three hundred fifty light-years between Myrkr and Wayland" took "nearly five days at its Point Four cruising speed" (ch. 4).
- Feat (Rukh): he flattens a two-storey building with a blaster in about 20+ shots (ch. 4).
> "Without warning, blue lightning bolts flashed from his fingertips— / And vanished without a trace a meter away from each of them." — Heir, ch. 4 (C'baoth's Force lightning vs. ysalamiri, three salvos)
> "A single ysalamir can occasionally create a bubble as large as ten meters across; a whole group of them reinforcing one another can create much larger ones." — Heir, ch. 4 (Thrawn)
> "And three meters from the Imperials the bolt came to an abrupt halt in midair. ... With a crackle of splintering wood, the crossbow bolt shattered" — Heir, ch. 4 (C'baoth TK)
> "in a blur of motion almost too fast to see, a flat stone detached itself from the ground and hurled itself directly into the path of the shot, shattering spectacularly as the blast hit it." — Heir, ch. 4 (C'baoth blocks Rukh's blaster bolt with TK)
- C'baoth claims: "I am a Jedi Master" and "I killed them, of course. Just as I killed the Guardian." The candles mark offworlders he killed (ch. 4).
> "It was the Emperor’s will that drove you; the Emperor’s mind that provided you with strength and resolve and efficiency. You were as dependent on that presence as if you were all borg-implanted into a combat computer." — Heir, ch. 4 (Thrawn on Endor and Emperor's battle meditation)
> "Then you must have wondered how a few dozen Rebel ships could possibly rout an Imperial force that outgunned it by at least ten to one." — Heir, ch. 4
- Thrawn asks C'baoth only to "enhance the coordination between ships and task forces—and then only at critical times" (ch. 4).
> "Jorus C’baoth is dead ... He was one of the six Jedi Masters aboard the Old Republic’s Outbound Flight project. ... It was intercepted by a task force outside Old Republic space and destroyed. ... Because I was the force’s commander." — Heir, ch. 4
> "Joruus C’baoth—note the telltale mispronunciation of the name Jorus—is a clone." — Heir, ch. 4
> "Would you rather we have brought back a full-fledged Dark Jedi? ... A second Darth Vader, perhaps" — Heir, ch. 4
- Mount Tantiss yields "a genuinely practical cloaking shield" and "that other small, almost trivial, bit of technology" (the Spaarti cylinders), found in "seven hours of searching" (ch. 4).

## Ch. 6–7 — Bimmisaari ambush (Noghri)
- Stokhli sticks: "shoot a spraynet mist two hundred meters, with enough shockstun juice to take down a good-sized Gundark" (ch. 6, Han).
- Luke reads the Bimms: "I don’t sense any real duplicity in them." He misses the trap (ch. 6).
- Leia senses Luke being cornered at range: "They’ve got him, too." (ch. 6)
> "And reaching out through the Force, Luke triggered the thumb switch." — Heir, ch. 7 (TK on the enemy's trigger)
> "It was a trick that only a Jedi could have pulled off, and it was a trick that, by all rights, ought to have worked. All seven of the aliens were in the room ... But by the time it landed ... all seven had somehow managed to back completely out of its way." — Heir, ch. 7 (Noghri reflexes vs. Luke's TK tapestry drop)
> "And, reaching back over his left shoulder, Luke hurled his lightsaber with all his strength. ... Guided by the Force, the spinning lightsaber cut through their ranks in a twisting curve, striking each of them in turn. / A heartbeat later, it was all over." — Heir, ch. 7 (Feat: Luke kills 7 Noghri with one saber throw)
> "five floors was too far for even a Jedi to safely leap." — Heir, ch. 7 (Statement, limit)
- Luke Force-pulls a stokhli stick, swings down a spraynet line, "leaped over a half dozen bystanding Bimms, cutting both attackers down before they even knew he was there." (ch. 7)
- Leia's TK pulls a jewelry box; it is a strain ("strained to establish a grip"). On disarming all 11: "You’ll never get all eleven" (ch. 7).
- Luke offers to build Leia a lightsaber. "nearly all the Jedi of the Old Republic carried lightsabers, even those who were primarily healers or teachers." (ch. 7)
