# Heir to the Empire — notes part 4: Chapters 11–14 (to line ~1661 of ch. 14)

## Ch. 11 — Dagobah cave
- Luke wonders whether Yoda suppressed his sensors on his first approach. He lifts Artoo out of the X-wing with the Force (ch. 11).
> "It was, clearly, the source of the low-level pressure he’d felt ever since his arrival on Dagobah, and for a moment he wondered why the effect had never been this strong before. / Perhaps because Yoda had always been here before, his presence shielding Luke from the true strength of the cave’s power." — Heir, ch. 11
- Vision (Mara foreshadow): at the Carkoon skiff, his lightsaber is pulled away from him in mid-air: "Frantically, Luke reached out for it with the Force; but to no avail ... And came to rest in the hand of a slender woman ... She had the power of the Force" (ch. 11).
- He finds the Bpfasshi Dark Jedi's beckon call (ch. 11).

## Ch. 12 — Nkllon
- Shieldship: "half as big across as an Imperial Star Destroyer"; the crossing takes ten hours without a slave circuit (ch. 12).
- Nkllon sunlight "strong enough to fry every sensor we have in a few seconds and take the Falcon’s hull off a couple of minutes later" (Han, ch. 12).
- Nomad City is "the old Dreadnaught Cruiser on top, the forty captured Imperial AT-ATs underneath" (ch. 12).
- Leia confirms that Luke is really in the X-wing; Luke's sleep is troubled (ch. 12).

## Ch. 13 — Luke's Force fighting; C'baoth's long-range contact
> "Half closing his eyes, flying entirely on reflex, Luke reached out with the Force. ... to touch that same mind with the intent of deliberately distorting its perception was something else entirely. ... Perhaps this sort of action—even done purely in self-defense—was dangerously close to the edge of the dark areas where Jedi were forbidden to go." — Heir, ch. 13
> "You will come to me, Luke ... You will find me . . . and the Jedi shall rise again." — Heir, ch. 13 (C'baoth's voice in Luke's mind from the Chimaera, far out in the Athega system)
- Luke "had somehow lost over half an hour. Half an hour that his internal time sense had no recollection of whatsoever." During the blackout the TIE squadron was routed; "We got two of the TIE fighters" (ch. 13).
- C'baoth guided the *Judicator* raid in and out blind: "They obtained fifty-one of the mole miner machines" (ch. 13). He senses Luke "in the very center of the battle" from the far system edge (ch. 13).
> "The Jedi had ways of influencing people, Captain, even over considerable distances. It’s possible that even out here he was close enough to Skywalker to plant a suggestion or compulsion." — Heir, ch. 13 (Thrawn)
- Thrawn: "Skywalker has proved himself to be highly dangerous, and by all accounts has already withstood at least one attempt to turn him." He plans an ambush "at least twenty light-years away" on the Nkllon–Jomark line (ch. 13).

## Ch. 14 (first half)
- Lando loses "fifty-one of my best reconditioned mole miners ... almost half my work force" (ch. 14).
- Beckon call: "Pre-Clone Wars vintage ... the ship would come straight to you ... Some of them would even fight their way through opposing ships". Han: "Tell that to the Katana fleet." (ch. 14, first mention)
- Luke: "The cave where I found it absolutely tingles with the dark side. I think it must have been the place where he died." (ch. 14)
- Nkllon rotation "Just over ninety standard days". The plan: Chewie takes Leia to Kashyyyk (ch. 14).
