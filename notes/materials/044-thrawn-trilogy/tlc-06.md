# The Last Command: notes part 6, ch. 14–16 (opening)

## Ch. 14: Battle of Coruscant (cloaked asteroids)
- Ghent sliced the ILKO encrypt "back when I was twelve … took me almost two months". The colonel says ILKO "was one of the master encrypt codes the Empire used for data transfer between Coruscant and the original Death Star construction facility at Horuz. It took us nearly a month to crack it."
- **Order of battle:** "Six Imperial Star Destroyers" split into groups of three against "the two massive midorbit Golan III battle stations", with TIEs, Dreadnaughts, and support ships forming the perimeter. Coruscant raises its "energy shield". An ion cannon blast from the surface is "way out of range". "Ion cannon weren't exactly known for tight-beam accuracy."
- The Interdictor-edge micro-jump: "He uses an Interdictor Cruiser to define a hyperspace edge, then brings a ship in along an intersecting vector to drop out at a precisely chosen point." Ships come in "blind, with deflectors down and no targeting references". Two VSDs drop in at point-blank range and deliver "massive broadsides". The station shield "flickered wildly before settling down again."
- **Golan durability (Thrawn):** "Golan defense platforms can take considerably more punishment than their former ground commander apparently realized." Leia agrees: they "were proving themselves more capable of absorbing damage than Leia had realized they could."
- Bel Iblis takes command at Mon Mothma's request. His insight on Mon Mothma: "with everything she does there are lives hanging in the balance … she's terrified of trusting anyone else with those lives." The Dreadnaught Harrier carries Sena's sons Peter and Dayvid.
- **Cloaked asteroids:** the Chimaera's tractor launchers fire "pulling far too much power" with "nothing at all left the bay". The Escort Frigate Evanrue is hit by "physical impact". The Harrier uses ion cannon only to collapse one cloak: "the stars in that empty region vanished. For a couple of heartbeats there was complete blackness as the cloaking shield collapsed in on itself". Thrawn then destroys that asteroid with turbolasers.
- Numbers: 22 real asteroids, 287 total firings, the rest "dry firings". The Chimaera's "power feedback shunt's starting to glow white". Drayson explains the fake: "run a feedback shunt from the tractor beam projector, either to a flash capacitor or a power dissipator".
- Effect: "Until we've cleared them out, we can't risk lowering the planetary shield." Thrawn: "For the moment, at least, Coruscant is effectively out of the war." Bel Iblis: cloaks are too expensive for Thrawn to squander "three hundred of them".
- Ghent hands Leia the sliced Delta Source pulse-transmitter encrypt code ("cross-frequency split-phase").

## Ch. 15: Hijarna; Mazzic released
- Hijarna fortress is "A vast expanse of incredibly hard black stone … a plain that still bore the deep scars of massive destruction". It was found by "the Fifth Alderaanian Expedition", crumbling for "perhaps a thousand years".
- The coalition frays. Par'tah is offered an Ord Trasi sublight-engine contract. Karrde plans to have Ghent slice the smugglers onto a New Republic sector fleet privateer payroll.
- **Vornskrs hunt via the Force (key lore):**
> "possibly the only animals in the galaxy who hunted via the Force. … Force sensitivity itself wasn't unheard of, certainly—the Gotal had a fairly useless form of it, and there were persistent rumors about the Duinuogwuin as well … the existence of the bizarre Force-empty bubbles created by Myrkr's ysalamiri could finally be explained if they were simply a form of defense or camouflage against predators." — Karrde, ch. 15
  - Supporting evidence: vornskr reactions to Luke and to Mara, which came right before her Interdictor hunch.
- Thrawn frees Mazzic and blames Lt. Kosk's "bribery". Ferrier's Defel will plant a forged Karrde–Kosk data card aboard the Wild Karrde. "Some people automatically distrust free information."

## Ch. 16 (opening): Wayland landing
- Mara: the cooling system draws "water from a river flowing down the northeastern slope". "The only entrance" is on the southwest.
- Han suspects they were followed: "Could be something with a really good sensor stealth mode."
- **Garrals:** two-meter predators used as Imperial watchdogs. "There's something in the ultrasonic signature of a repulsorlift that's supposed to sound like one of their prey animals … They can hear a ship-sized repulsorlift coming in from kilometers away." No speeder bikes can be used. The trek is estimated at "twelve or thirteen days".
- Luke cuts down two leaping garrals in a "blurred line of brilliant green".
- Natives: Psadans and Myneyrshi.
