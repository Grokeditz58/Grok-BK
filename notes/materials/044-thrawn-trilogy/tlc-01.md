## Ch. 1: Ukio / Filve
- Opening distances: the Chimaera is "three thousandths of a light-year" from the Ukio star. It takes "seventy-six seconds it would take to reach the Ukio system from their position" (a hyperspace micro-jump).
- Feint task forces each pair an ISD with Katana Dreadnaughts: "an Imperial Star Destroyer and eight Katana fleet Dreadnaughts". The Ukio force is the Chimaera, the Stormhawk, and "the ten Katana fleet Dreadnaughts".
- **Planetary shields (doctrine):**
> "With planetary shields able to hold off all but the most massive turbolaser and proton torpedo bombardment, conventional wisdom held that the only way to subdue a modern world was to put a fast-moving ground force down at the edges and send them overland to destroy the shield generators. … To capture a planet relatively undamaged but with shield generators still intact was considered an impossibility." — TLC ch. 1
- The Ukio shield is "a hazy blue shell surrounding the planet about fifty kilometers above the surface". It has "thirty overlapping shield generators".
- **The cloaked-cruiser trick:** Chimaera turbolaser bolts "struck the hazy blue of the planetary shield, splashed slightly as their energy was defocused and reflected back into space". Meanwhile, cloaked cruisers on repulsorlifts beneath the shield fire with perfect timing "into two of Ukio's major air defense bases". The Ukians see "two devastating shots cleanly through an impenetrable planetary shield." (ANALYSIS: an ISD battery can't penetrate a full planetary shield. The whole operation depends on that.)
- **C'baoth's long-range fleet control has matured.** He coordinates four cloaked cruisers, whose sensors are blind under cloak, by locating "the minds he was touching":
> "I can see the men inside your precious cloaking shields … I can see their thoughts and guide their wills. What does the metal itself matter?" — ch. 1
> "What doesn't matter to a Jedi Master does not matter to the universe." — ch. 1
> "Barely two months ago, Thrawn had confidently said that C'baoth would never be a threat to the Empire because he lacked the ability to focus and concentrate his Jedi power on a long-term basis. Somehow, between that time and now, C'baoth had obviously succeeded in learning the necessary control." — ch. 1
- The cruiser crews are "thousand-man crews … composed of variants on just twenty or so different minds". Pellaeon wonders whether C'baoth's control is easier because he is himself a clone. (ANALYSIS: in-universe speculation only.)
- **Mind control of Pellaeon:** C'baoth makes him agree to order secret clone "sample B-2332-54" (later revealed to be Luuke Skyywalker). "Once you have obeyed, you will forget it even happened." Pellaeon later can't recall it. C'baoth also reads his thoughts casually: "I am the Empire, Captain Pellaeon … Your thoughts are a part of your service to me."
- **C'baoth hijacks the fleet:** sensing Leia at Filve ("My Jedi are at Filve"), he cuts off Thrawn's intercom and turns "the whole Imperial force" at Filve onto the Falcon. Leia feels "a distant malevolence pressing against the edges of her mind" from the system's edge.
- Leia's Endor note: "For a few seconds I could feel something like the Emperor's presence around me", at the spot where the Death Star blew up.
- Leia: "The Emperor, it had long been rumored, had had the ability to use the Force to exercise direct control over his military forces."
- Threepio: "fluent in over six million forms of communication". The Cracken Twist: add 2 to the second digit of each jump coordinate, which puts a chase force "a couple of light-years off target".
- **Clone madness rule (Pellaeon):**
> "a permanent mental and emotional instability, inversely scaled to the length of the duplicate's growth cycle. … no clone grown to maturity in less than a year would be stable enough to survive outside of a totally controlled environment." — ch. 1
- Thrawn lists Imperial support for the Jomark setup: Intelligence planted the rumor, plus Transport, Supply, and Engineering ("camouflaged island landing site").
- C'baoth gives his ultimatum: "if you cannot accomplish even so small a task, perhaps I will deem you unworthy to lead the military forces of my Empire."
- Spelling: the TLC epub reads "Spaart cloning cylinders" (typo), while the DFR excerpt has "Spaarti". Covell: "three of the companies … composed entirely of clones". Top commanders were told about the Tantiss project only "two weeks" earlier.
