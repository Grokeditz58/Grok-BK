# The Last Command: notes part 4, ch. 9–10 (and ch. 11 opening)

## Ch. 9: the Palace raid
- Drayson refuses the Assault Frigate: "At the rate he's going, Grand Admiral Thrawn could take three more sectors in ten days." "when you go up against someone like Thrawn, all the usual rules get tossed out the lock."
- The commandos jam comlinks ("a soft squeal of static").
- **Mara's Force sending to Leia:** Leia is woken by a voice in her head, "Leia Organa Solo. Wake up. You're in danger." It is revealed later as Mara: "stretching out through the Force as she had earlier when Calrissian had gone for his blaster. No more certain now than she had been then that Organa Solo could even hear her." A second sending, "Surrender. Surrender. Surrender.", makes Leia call a surrender, which draws the Imperials into Mara's line of fire.
- Leia senses "Five or six of them at least" outside the door. She uses the "Jedi sensory enhancement routine" to pick up the "faint hum" of an electronic lock-breaker.
- Palace doors: the wood is "one of the hardest and strongest known in the galaxy". Blaster shots "probably didn't gouge their way more than a quarter of the way through", which triggers the metal security door.
- Mara's danger sense: "It was the sudden tingle of danger in the back of her mind that saved her life."
- The Emperor's private passages open with a hand-warmth sensor panel ("Two minutes and three staircases later").
- Mara kills two rear guards, then "had two of them down before the others even woke" (4 total at the door). Five seconds. Sole survivor: Major Himron.
- **Thrawn's poison pill:** Himron frames Mara: "Back door. Put in … same time … private passage system. Locked from inside. She let us in." "Special agent of … Empire. Once called … Emperor's Hand."
- Lando places Mara as a dancer at Jabba's who "begging" to come on the Sail Barge.
- The guards bring "an E-Web repeating blaster". Bel Iblis has the look of "a leader preparing to send men to their deaths."
- The plan was a hostage screen, since the turbolift was blocked and fighters were closing the airspace above the Palace.

## Ch. 10: Trogan smugglers' summit
- Attendees: Brasck (Brubb), Par'tah (Ho'Din), Ellor (Duro), Dravis (for Billey), Mazzic (with Gotal Lishma and the needle-throwing bodyguard), Clyngunn (ZeHethbra), Ferrier, Gillespee, Karrde.
- Ferrier's fake ambush: "about thirty Imperial army troops … two pair of white-armored stormtroopers … two Chariot command speeders". Ferrier's Defel throws a stormtrooper "bodily off his feet". Aves and Fein hang in web harnesses inside the Drinking Cup with A280s.
- Brasck: "It was he who forced my world under the Imperial shroud." Brasck refuses to join but won't interfere.
- **Thrawn on Vader (contrast):**
> "You were directly responsible for the deaths of four stormtroopers and thirty-two Imperial army troops … I am not the Lord Darth Vader, Ferrier—I do not spend my men recklessly. Nor do I take their deaths lightly." — ch. 10
- Thrawn: "I've made it a point to deal personally with each of them at least once over the past year." The ambush united the smugglers, "precisely the turn of events I'd gone to great lengths to avoid." He plans to frame Karrde for the attack.
- Thrawn on Himron: "Major Himron's fingering of Mara Jade should succeed in neutralizing her as a threat to us."

## Ch. 11 (opening)
- Colonel Bremen (Security) keeps Mara under house arrest: "records search, background search, acquaintance correlation, and a couple of question/answer sessions with our interrogators."
- Ch'hala trees change color when struck: "an angry red … ripples" (ANALYSIS: Delta Source setup).
- Luke senses Leia's surprise across the Palace: "I just felt a disturbance in the Force … More like surprise. Or shock … coming from Leia."
- Mara senses her visitor through the Force before the door opens: "Reaching out with the Force, she tried to identify her visitor".
