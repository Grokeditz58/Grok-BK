# The Last Command: notes part 10, ch. 24 (cont.) to ch. 27 (opening)

## Ch. 24 (cont.): Thrawn's gamble; entry into Tantiss
- Thrawn: the Rebel ships "pulled out of those sectors between forty and seventy hours ago, leaving behind a few men with the proper uniforms and insignia to confuse our spies."
> "All war is risk, Captain … If I'm wrong, we lose one Ubiqtorate base … But if I'm right, we stand a good chance of destroying two entire Rebel sector fleets." … "You don't have to believe. But be prepared to be proved wrong." — Thrawn, ch. 24
- Inside the ysalamir bubble: "it was like being blind." Luke hears distant blaster fire but can't use "his Jedi enhancement techniques" to place it.
- **Mara:** "Whatever it costs, don't let me go over to C'baoth's side … Even if you have to kill me." Luke, asked if C'baoth can force her: "There was so much he didn't know yet about the Force. 'No.'" Luke: "That voice you hear is just a memory he left behind inside you … Your destiny is in your hands".
- Psadans attack the main gate "armed with blasters, portable missile launchers, and thermal detonators" and demand C'baoth's release (ANALYSIS: arms and data pad came via the Covell company).
- Luke's saber cuts the air-intake grating ("Four careful slices"). Two Noghri escort the Myneyrshi.
- Leia can't sense Luke at range, though "Back at Bespin … she'd been able to sense Luke's call from almost this far away."
- Noghri landing-light recognition signal. The Wild Karrde shoots a landing gap with turbolasers.
- Karrde's vornskrs: "On Myrkr they use the Force to hunt their prey. Here, they're going to use it to find Mara."

## Ch. 25: cloning cavern; Bilbringi trap; C'baoth on the throne
- Luke's saber slices "horizontally through the heavy metal of the blast door". Lando: "the Emperor must have just picked up the cloning complex and dropped it in here whole."
- **Cavern scale:** "at least ten stories" of balconies, "Thousands of little circles. Each one the top cover plate of a Spaarti cloning cylinder."
- Mara: "The Emperor wasn't one for leaving things behind for other people to use", so she suspects a self-destruct in the throne room.
- Bilbringi: Thrawn's fleet arrives ("Star Destroyers … Dreadnaughts … Lancer Frigates … Strike Cruisers … TIE squadrons"). Interdictors are in an "entrapment configuration" for the Republic fleet, not the smugglers.
- **Emperor's galaxy hologram:**
> "a blaze of light twenty meters across, was the galaxy. … sculpted in exquisite and absolutely unique detail, with a single accurately positioned spot of light for each of the galaxy's hundred billion stars." — ch. 25
- Luke's thrown saber misses without the Force ("without the Force, Skywalker wasn't that good a shot"). C'baoth waits on the throne: "Together we will teach the galaxy what it means to serve the Jedi."

## Ch. 26: C'baoth's claim; ysalamiri killed; Luuke
- C'baoth staged the Psadan riot as a "distraction": "What better purpose could they have than the honor of dying in the service of the Jedi?"
- Luke tells him he's a clone (Jorus died on Outbound Flight per Katana records). C'baoth laughs it off.
- **C'baoth's power philosophy and mind-rebuilding:**
> "The true power of the Jedi is not in these simple tricks of matter and energy. The true might of the Jedi is that we alone of all those in the galaxy have the power to grow beyond ourselves. To extend ourselves into all the reaches of the universe." … "With General Covell. What even the Emperor never did. I took his mind in my hands and altered it. Re-formed it and rebuilt it into my own image." … "the army he thinks he is creating for himself he is instead creating for me." — C'baoth, ch. 26
  - (ANALYSIS: Luke reasons that because clone minds are near-identical, breaking one clone breaks the whole template batch.)
- A remote activator triggers charges planted by Covell's men. "something distant and very alien seemed to shriek in agony", and the Force returns. In the cavern this is seen as "many simultaneous small" explosions in the clusters of cylinders.
- **C'baoth's lightning versus Luke:** He TK-tears Mara's blaster away and hits her square in the chest with lightning. Luke blocks "most of it on his lightsaber blade" as "a third burst, and a fourth, and a fifth" follow. Mara survives, "still pretty much on her feet", burns "not too bad".
- **Luuke:** "created from the hand you left behind in the Cloud City on Bespin. Wielding the lightsaber you lost there." Luke feels a "buzzing pressure", possibly the "close presence of an exact duplicate that was itself drawing on the Force". This echoes the Dagobah cave vision.
- **Duel feats:**
  - The first blade clash "nearly tore the lightsaber from his grip."
  - Luke Force-backflips over the guardrail onto a guard platform.
  - Luuke's thrown saber cuts "only half of the base" of the platform.
  - Luke's backflip to a catwalk 5 m away falls short, "too distracted by the buzzing".
- C'baoth: "Join me … Together we can save the galaxy from the lesser peoples".
- Chewie plans a "power core arhythmic resonance" overload: a negative flow coupler run into a positive flow regulator.
- Bilbringi: Rogue Squadron is pulled out of hyperspace by Interdictors, too far out. Ackbar: "It appears to be a trap."
- Han meets Leia, Karrde, and the vornskrs. Leia: "The Force is back."

## Ch. 27 (opening): duel; C'baoth's Force scream
- Mara on C'baoth: "His would be a more personal form of empire: minds re-formed and rebuilt into his own conception of what a mind should be." C'baoth replies to her unspoken thought (ANALYSIS: he reads her mind).
- **Emperor's mental-shield pattern:** "a pattern for those times when he'd wanted his instructions hidden even from Vader". C'baoth punishes her first attempt with "a sudden jolt of pain", and her second attempt holds. C'baoth: "Do you truly believe I need to hear your thoughts in order to read your heart?"
- C'baoth will claim "his sister and her children".
- **TK tug-of-war:** C'baoth stops Luke's recalled saber dead in midair. When Luke tries to cut the other saber's handle, C'baoth hurls Mara's blaster into Luke's shoulder and TK-returns the saber to Luuke. "This duel must be to the death … weapon against weapon, mind against mind, soul against soul."
- C'baoth uses TK to block 3 of Han's blaster shots with Mara's blaster (the third shot hits the power pack, "a spectacular fireball"), then rips Han's blaster away.
- **C'baoth's berserk Force scream (key):**
> "It was like nothing she'd ever experienced before; not from Vader, not from the Emperor himself. The utter, animal ferocity—the total loss of every shred of self-control … Wave after wave of fury swept over her, ripping through the mental barrier she'd created and battering her mind with a numbing combination of hatred and pain." — Mara POV, ch. 27
- Han takes lightning and is thrown into a guardrail. Leia blocks the third blast on her blade. C'baoth's lightning splits the catwalk. Leia cuts it and is struck on the head and shoulder.
- C'baoth: "I could crush you like a small insect beneath my heel." Luke deactivates his saber and offers: "Let them go, and I'll stay."
