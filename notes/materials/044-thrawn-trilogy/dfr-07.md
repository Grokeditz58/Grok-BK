# Dark Force Rising — notes part 7: ch. 18–21

## Ch. 18 — Planning on Honoghr; Ferrier's wraith
- Decon droids each scoop "a quarter cubic meter of topsoil with each bite". Chewie estimates "Eight years" for the existing droid count to clean the land. (ANALYSIS: this sets up the proof that the Empire is stalling.)
- The maitrakh sneaks up on Leia unnoticed: "These people must have been fantastic hunters before the Emperor turned them into his private killing machines."
- Leia: "The Empire once destroyed an entire world because of me."
- The maitrakh joins the rescue: "There is honor in you, Lady Vader."
- Ferrier's lure message to Han ("money-changing center"). Ferrier leaks Thrawn's name: "With Grand Admiral Thrawn in charge over there? He’s involved in everything." Han: "At last they had a name to go with the uniform."
- Ferrier plants two beacons. The second is placed by his **Defel** "wraith": "Humans need movement to see. Not-moving shadows are of no concern." Solo "looked straight back at you once" and missed it.

## Ch. 19 — Karrde captured; Mara's Force choke on Thrawn
- The capture at Karrde's base: TIE sonic boom, landing pit paving turned to "steaming blocks of half-molten ceramic", and stormtroopers on droplines.
- **Rukh vs. Mara:** "Thrawn’s Noghri bodyguard sidled in from two meters away, threw his arm across her neck and shoulder, and spun her around and halfway to the deck ... His forearm was pressing solidly against her carotid artery".
- **Mara's TK choke on Thrawn (scaling):**
> "Mara glared at him and lashed out again, this time with the Force. Thrawn frowned slightly, fingers moving across his neck as if trying to brush away an intangible cobweb ... Thrawn gazed unblinkingly back at her, his throat muscles moving as he fought against the grip ... a minute later, gasping for breath, Mara had to concede defeat." — DFR ch. 19
> "I trust you’ve learned the limits of your small powers ... A little trick the Emperor taught you?" — Thrawn, DFR ch. 19
- (ANALYSIS: Mara's post-Palpatine Force strength is weak. A sustained full-effort choke for about a minute only bothers Thrawn's throat muscles. Note there was no ysalamir right next to her, though there was one on the ship; it's a hangar bay scene.)
- Thrawn: "I rule the Empire now ... The only treason is defiance of my orders." He offers Mara first officer of a Katana Dreadnaught, or else she'd be "given instead to Master C’baoth as a little bonus gift".
- "Joruus C’baoth is a mad Jedi Master ... in exchange for Jedi to mold into whatever twisted image he chooses."
- **The Emperor's back door:** "the Emperor had had a private back door installed into the main computer of every Star Destroyer ... no commander could ever lock him out of his own ships. Not him, and not his top agents." Mara uses it to pull the prisoner list, detention block diagram, duty roster, and the *Chimaera*'s 6-day course.
- Aves gives her a boosted Skipray blastboat (from Vagran) and an ysalamir on a portable frame.

## Ch. 20 — Jomark lessons; Mara's dream; C'baoth's remote attacks
- C'baoth expects Luke to read minds: "Everything you needed to know about the damage was right there in their minds."
- C'baoth's voice "echoed grandly" (a possible Force technique).
> "For a thousand generations we stood tall among the lesser beings of the galaxy ... The Senate could debate and pass laws; but it was the Jedi who turned those laws into reality ... Do you truly believe that even the Emperor could have succeeded in such a task without the consent of the entire galaxy?" — C'baoth, DFR ch. 20
- C'baoth's mind is "as always ... closed to him". Luke finds himself "two paces back from C'baoth without any recollection of having taken the steps" (Force compulsion).
> "I will protect her from the Empire; and will teach her such power as you can’t imagine." — C'baoth, DFR ch. 20
- Mara's recurring dream: Vader and Luke turn on the Emperor, who "raised his hands, sending cascades of jagged blue-white lightning at his enemies. Both men staggered". "YOU WILL KILL LUKE SKYWALKER!" She has had "The same dream ... for five years now."
- **C'baoth's long-range fatigue attack:** the Skipray drops into the atmosphere while Mara is asleep. One step from the ysalamir, "One second she felt as if she had just finished a four-day battle; half a step later ... the fatigue abruptly vanished." (ANALYSIS: Luke's weeks of fatigue are now explained as C'baoth's influence.)
- **Telekinetic rock barrage on a starship:** "small, fast-moving rocks, undetectable by most of the Skipray’s sophisticated sensors. The fourth impact knocked out the repulsorlifts, sending the Skipray falling out of the sky." The impacts kick the craft "a few centimeters upward" and yaw it "hard to starboard".

## Ch. 21 — Showdown at the High Castle
- Mara "airstilts" the Skipray on its drive to a ledge about 50 m below the rim. The drive trail ignites the rock.
- C'baoth: "I saw it all in your mind as you approached." Mara: "It was like hearing the Emperor speak again."
> "Do you truly believe that simply because you stand in the middle of an empty space in the Force that I am powerless against you?" — C'baoth, DFR ch. 21
> "I could destroy you, you know ... Right there where you stand, before you even knew the attack was coming ... I’ve felt your presence over the years, Mara Jade; the rising and falling of your power after the Emperor’s death took most of your strength away ... Someday you will kneel before me. I have foreseen it." — C'baoth, DFR ch. 21
- Luke: "I felt a disturbance in the Force ... As if a battle were taking place nearby." He touches the ysalamir bubble and his fatigue lifts.
> "A Jedi uses the Force for knowledge and defense, never for attack." — Luke, DFR ch. 21
- **Fight:** a TK-thrown rock "struck her gun hand with paralyzing force". Luke "took a long step forward to put himself outside the ysalamir’s influence" and cut "two more incoming rocks".
> "Still laughing, C’baoth raised his hand and sent a flash of blue lightning toward them. Skywalker caught the bolt on his lightsaber, and for an instant the green of the blade was surrounded by a blue-white coronal discharge. A second bolt shot past him to vanish at the edge of the empty zone around Mara; a third again wrapped itself around the lightsaber blade." — DFR ch. 21
- (ANALYSIS: Force lightning is negated at the ysalamir bubble's edge, and Luke blocks it with his blade.)
- R2 fires the X-wing lasers. C'baoth is knocked out ("Not even singed", "the sonic shock"). Luke refuses to kill him: "He’s ill. Maybe he can be cured." Luke now judges: "there was no doubt in Luke’s mind that he was now moving along the path of the dark side."
- Mara: "Vader nearly died for that blunder [Yavin] ... That was when he lost his right hand, in fact." (ANALYSIS: Legends canon at the time. Later sources revised this.)
- The plan is to board the *Chimaera* at the Wistril system supply stop in 4 days.
