# The Last Command: notes part 11, ch. 27 (end) to ch. 29, plus front of back matter

## Ch. 27 (end): Mara kills Luuke
- Lando: "C'baoth's at least as dangerous as the Emperor was. Maybe even more so." Covell's men killed all the ysalamiri with "blasting disk explosions".
- C'baoth TK-twitches Leia's dropped blaster "another half meter" away from Han while also supporting the duel: "Whatever effort C'baoth was expending on the lightsaber duel, he clearly still had enough concentration left to toy with his prisoners."
- Luuke's leaping strike drives Luke "backward to the edge of the stairway". Luke backflips up from the lower floor.
- C'baoth TK-pulls Karrde's blaster, Leia's lightsaber, and Han's target blaster all at once: "As will their insignificant weapons."
- **Mara breaks C'baoth's TK grip:**
> "Reaching through the chaos surrounding her mind, she stretched out to the Force, focusing her eyes and mind on the weapons flying across the room toward C'baoth's hand. She felt his inattentive control snap— And Organa Solo's lightsaber arced away from the blasters to land firmly in her hand." — ch. 27
  - C'baoth's counter-tug is "twisted with confusion and dread". Note that his control was "inattentive".
- Luke lures Luuke into slashing a viewscreen, which blows into Luuke's face. Mara kills Luuke: "YOU WILL KILL LUKE SKYWALKER … she let herself give in … and cut him down." "the voice in the back of her mind fell silent … And she was finally free."

## Ch. 28: Bilbringi; C'baoth's death; Thrawn's death
- Thrawn plans to let Ackbar's cruiser survive as a messenger, so the Republic will suspect betrayal.
- At least four Golan II stations at Bilbringi. Aves's smugglers grab the CGT and punch out past the Golans. Rogue Squadron escorts them in: "With or without support, we're going in." Wedge on trust: "a farm kid … a former high-stakes gambler … a smuggler".
- Luke: "the buzzing pressure in Luke's mind was gone" when Luuke dies.
- **C'baoth's TK rockfall (key):**
> "with a thunderous explosion, the sections of throne room ceiling directly above him and Mara suddenly collapsed in a downpour of gravel-sized rocks. … the center of the rockfall moved with him. … Too numerous and too small for him to get a grip on through the Force, they kept coming, pummeling against him with bruising impact." — ch. 28
  - "standing untouched by the destructive rock storms he'd unleashed": "I am the Jedi Master C'baoth! … The Empire—the universe—is mine."
  - Luke is buried "to above his waist". C'baoth's lightning on Luke's blade knocks him to his knees. Lightning "throwing coronal fire all through the stone pile". Luke's saber is TK-plucked away.
  - C'baoth: "You cannot die yet. Not until I have taken you down to the Grand Admiral's cloning chamber … One Mara Jade … or another."
  - Simultaneous rockfall covers Han, Leia, and Karrde under the catwalk, plus stones piled in front of the turbolift.
- Lando and Chewie collapse the bridge onto the balcony three levels down. The central column blows ("the column crashed to the cloning cavern floor"), and the power core arrhythmic resonance starts.
- **Final fight:** Mara slices gashes in the floor to drain the stones. She blocks repeated lightning on her blade ("C'baoth fired again and again"). C'baoth hurls stones curving into her face.
  - **Leia guides Mara by telepathy:** "Keep your eyes closed, Mara, and listen to my voice. I can see; I'll guide you."
  - Luke TK-ignites his buried saber from range, flies it in, and cuts the vornskrs' leashes. C'baoth's lightning downs one vornskr.
  - Mara stabs upward from her knees: "With a last, mournful scream, C'baoth crumpled—"
- **Dark-side death explosion (key):**
> "And as it had with the Emperor aboard the Death Star, the dark side energy within him burst out in a violent explosion of blue fire. Luke was ready. Throwing every last bit of strength into the effort, he caught Mara in a solid Force grip, pulling her back away from that burst of energy … felt the slight easing of stress as Leia's strength joined his effort." — ch. 28
> "The walls and ceiling were blackened and cratered; the metal of the floor where C'baoth had stood was buckled and half melted; the throne itself had been ripped away … through a jagged crack in the rear wall, he could see the bright twinkle of a single star." — ch. 28
- C'baoth's position: "They will accept my rule, or they will die." Luke: "You have a vast store of experience and power".
- **Thrawn's death:** Rukh delivers a throat strike that paralyzes Pellaeon. "For the treachery of the Empire against the Noghri people … We were betrayed. We have been revenged." The knife goes through Thrawn's chest.
> "'But,' he whispered, 'it was so artistically done.' … and Thrawn, the last Grand Admiral, was gone." — ch. 28
  - Pellaeon: "Thrawn could still have pulled an Imperial victory out of it. But he, Pellaeon, was not Thrawn." "Prepare to retreat."

## Ch. 29: epilogue
- Mara is offered the role of smuggler-coalition liaison. The Noghri are being transplanted to a new world (Chewbacca's team).
- Luke gives Mara his old (Anakin's) lightsaber: "Because you earned it. Because you're on your way to becoming a Jedi". Mara: "Her last link with the past had been broken in the Mount Tantiss throne room."
- Mara on the Emperor: "I don't think he ever saw the Imperial City as people and lights—to him it was just power and opportunities."
- **End of TLC main text: 29 chapters read in full.**

## Back matter (start)
- About the Author: Zahn has "nearly seventy short stories", a Hugo for best novella, and "more than four million copies in print". The bibliography lists Choices of One through Heir.
- bm1 Legends note (Del Rey, May 2014): same text as Heir and DFR.
- **bm3 = Choices of One excerpt** ("CHAPTER ONE"). This differs from the DFR bm3, which was a TLC ch1 excerpt.
  - Commander Gilad Pellaeon is third bridge officer of the Chimaera under Captain Calo Drusan. Grand Admiral Zaarin is aboard the ISD Predominant.
  - "Lord Odo" (masked, with "fifty different species biomarkers … at least eight of which the computer can't even identify") flies a Kazellis-class freighter, the Salaban's Hope, with pilot "Sorro" (= Jorj Car'das).
  - Odo's tractor/debris trick destroys raiders.
  - Pellaeon on the ISD: "a kilometer and a half of heavy armor and awesome weaponry, the very symbol and expression of Imperial power".
  - **Thrawn and Palpatine at the Endor second Death Star site:**
    - Thrawn: "the multifrequency force field you have set up should be more than adequate to protect the construction site".
    - Thrawn's recommendation: "burning off the forest for a hundred kilometers around the generator and putting a small mechanized force of AT-ATs and juggernaut heavy assault vehicles under the umbrella shield" plus hoverscouts.
    - Thrawn: "Unless, of course, you're setting a trap." Palpatine: "You of all my officers should understand the usefulness of a well-laid trap." Thrawn: "don't dismiss too quickly those natives … Even primitives can sometimes be used to deadly effect."
    - **Palpatine reads Thrawn's mind:** "'And now, I sense you have a request to make. Speak.' … If he was surprised or discomfited by the Emperor's casual reading of his mind, it didn't show".
    - Warlord Nusoesva in the Unknown Regions has "an unusually strong spacegoing navy, along with many slave and tributary worlds". Thrawn needs "a minimum of six more Star Destroyers". Thrawn: "The Rebellion is a threat, but hardly the most serious one facing the Empire."
  - Car'das background: he once ran "a network of smugglers and information brokers who had serviced everyone from the Hutts to the highest levels of the Imperial court". He was with Thrawn "to the edge of Chiss space … back before the Clone Wars". Vader since Yavin has "grown a whole lot darker".
- Choices of One excerpt (end): Car'das had a "nearly fatal encounter long ago with that Dark Jedi", an "illness and weakness and impending death", and abandoned his organization. Odo is given "ultimate command of the Chimaera". First officer is Senior Commander Grondarle. First stop is Wroona for "specialized equipment". Pellaeon: the only alien in senior position he knew of was "Senior Captain Thrawn … frequently sent away into the Unknown Regions".
