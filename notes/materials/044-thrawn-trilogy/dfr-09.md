# Dark Force Rising — notes part 9: ch. 25–27

## Ch. 25 — Coral Vanda raid
- The *Coral Vanda* is a subocean casino with eight rooms like the Tralla Room.
- Ferrier's Defel wraith grabs Han: "strong furry arms wrapped around his chest and face". Han's blaster hovers "right in the middle of a thick and strangely solid shadow". Han had "always thought wraiths were just space legend."
- **ISD turbolaser fire into the sea (scaling):**
> "a short red line dug briefly into the sea near the tapered black cylinder that marked the Coral Vanda’s position. For an instant the red line was sheathed in the pale green of seawater suddenly flashed into superheated steam; and then the pale green spread outward in all directions, and the Coral Vanda rocked visibly as the shock wave passed it." — DFR ch. 25
- The shots are deliberate near misses, used to force the ship to surface. "Main propulsion’s been knocked out ... microfractures in their hull seams." Thrawn admires the coral reefs as art "created by living yet nonsentient beings".
- Han escapes the wraith by toppling a crate, then sets spilled Menkooro whiskey alight with his blaster ("alcohol fires weren’t all that hot"). Fire-suppression foam blinds the wraith.
- The ship supplier is named: **Captain Hoffner**. Ferrier says Thrawn "supplied me with the name". Ferrier's team takes Hoffner. Stormtroopers board, and a "Subsonic grenade" is used. Han and Lando escape by pod.
- Lando: Dreadnaughts need "two thousand crewers apiece ... I doubt they have four hundred thousand of them."

## Ch. 26 — Coruscant council; Fey'lya; Karrde's moves
- Karrde: "Captain Hoffner was a very astute man ... I worked with Hoffner for over two years".
- Mon Mothma orders an Escort Frigate and two X-wing squadrons (via Admiral Drayson). Karrde holds back the coordinates until morning.
- Fey'lya: "I do not intend to fall ... My supporters among the New Republic military will see to that. Ackbar will fall, and I will rise in his place."
- Mara at the Imperial Palace: "YOU WILL KILL LUKE SKYWALKER." "I’m trying." She has "no excuses left."
- Karrde sends Mara to the Trogan system to call in Aves and "everything we have that can both fly and fight". Ghent is sent to Coruscant to slice Ackbar's bank account. Leia gets Mara a reconnaissance Y-wing.
- Karrde: "my organization intends to remain completely neutral."
- Luke wakes Wedge for Rogue Squadron: a dozen X-wings in an old transport. Fey'lya demands that Solo and Skywalker be arrested and takes "personal charge of this mission".

## Ch. 27 — The Katana fleet battle opens; C'baoth's hijack
- The Katana fleet sits "Dead center in the middle of nowhere".
- **Luke's Force intuition** picks the one Dreadnaught that turns out to be the *Katana* itself ("It was just intuition through the Force").
- The ships are maintained by droids. "Six of the eight main power converters have been knocked out of alignment".
- The Dreadnaught is "the backbone of the Old Republic’s fleet ... its massive turbolaser batteries still packed an awesome punch."
- *Quenfis* (Escort Frigate, Captain Virgilio) is crewed by Fey'lya loyalists. The ISD *Judicator* (Captain Brandei) launches 12 drop ships and two TIE squadrons.
- **Single Katana broadside:** "The laser bolts hit the drop ship formation dead center ... took out five of the drop ships ... A few of the TIE fighters, too" (follow-up report: "Five drop ships and three TIE fighters destroyed"). The capacitor bank disintegrates, so it is a one-shot weapon.
- Luke: "maybe forty-to-one odds". Han: "Never tell me the odds."
- Fey'lya draws a blaster on Leia. **Leia's TK:** "Her rudimentary abilities with the Force weren’t nearly strong enough for her to grab or deflect the blaster". However, "reached out with the Force ... and without even a click the intercom was on. Another effort and the comm was, too." (ANALYSIS: this sets the low end of Leia's telekinesis at this point.)
- Karrde baits Fey'lya into saying "What other use are soldiers to a politician? ... all those who are not on my side are my enemies" over the open comm. His X-wings desert him, and Virgilio relieves him: "some of us don’t consider loyalty to be all that outmoded".
- Leia resists using the dark side: "the almost overwhelming urge to snatch her lightsaber ... Violent hatred was the path of the dark side."
- Thrawn's "Depot" region, and ISD *Peremptory* sent to assist.
- **C'baoth's remote mind control (KEY):** a Lancer frigate arrives at the system's sentry line. Meanwhile the *Chimaera*'s nav officer is frozen ("unnaturally stiff") until C'baoth releases him ("the nav officer gasped and slumped ... now-shivering").
> "He most likely hijacked it ... He’s been sending messages for us over distances of several light-years, and he certainly knows how to take control of people. Apparently, he’s learned how to meld the two abilities." — Thrawn, DFR ch. 27
- (ANALYSIS: C'baoth's Force communication works across several light-years. He controls a person on another ship from at least sentry-line distance, in-system. He also hijacked a Lancer and its crew.)
- Thrawn: "It may be time to reconsider our arrangement with Master C’baoth."
