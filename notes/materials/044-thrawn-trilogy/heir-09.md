# Heir to the Empire — notes part 9: Chapters 26–29 (to line 3618)

## Ch. 26 — Mara Jade = Emperor's Hand (key lore)
- Mara's endurance: "Once, long ago, she’d gone six days without sleep. Now, after only two, she was already starting to fall apart." (ch. 26)
- With no Force, Luke uses the krayt-dragon call "Ben Kenobi had used to scare the sand people". He takes his saber back from Mara's belt and cuts the vornskr in half. The vornskr tail-whips Luke and tears off Artoo's welder and grasping arm "almost casually" (ch. 26).
> "I was a dancer at Jabba the Hutt’s palace the day you came for Solo." — Heir, ch. 26
> "Vader was a fool, and skating on the edge of treason along with it. My master sent me to Jabba’s to kill you, not recruit you." — Heir, ch. 26
> "I was his hand, Skywalker ... That’s how I was known to his inner court: as the Emperor’s Hand. I served him all over the galaxy, doing jobs the Imperial Fleet and stormtroopers couldn’t handle. That was my one great talent, you see—I could hear his call from anywhere in the Empire, and report back to him the same way. I exposed traitors for him, brought down his enemies" — Heir, ch. 26
> "The Emperor did ... He knew. And what he knew, I knew." — Heir, ch. 26 (the Emperor knew of Vader's Cloud City offer)
> "Jabba was highly resistant to the mind-controlling aspects of the Force." — Heir, ch. 26 (Luke)
- Luke's reading of the Dagobah vision: "The first time, years ago, the cave had spun him an image of a possible future. This time ... it had shown him a possible past. ‘You would have succeeded,’" (ch. 26)
- "There were no records kept of my activities"; after Endor, "four and a half years sloshing around the rotten underfringes" (ch. 26).

## Ch. 27 — Cloaking shield test; C'baoth policy
- Sluis Van count: "A hundred twelve transient warships in all. Sixty-five being used as cargo carriers" (ch. 27).
> "That’s how a cloaking shield works—nothing gets out, nothing gets in." — Heir, ch. 27 (Thrawn)
> "All men want power. And the more they have, the more they want." — Heir, ch. 27 (Thrawn on C'baoth)
- Thrawn limits C'baoth's use: "we can’t afford to use C’baoth too much or too often ... we don’t give Master C’baoth too much of a taste for this kind of power." (ch. 27)

## Ch. 28 — Hyllyard
- Mara: "The ysalamiri effect is supposed to extend several kilometers past the edge of the forest—none of those little attack-anticipation tricks will work anywhere near Hyllyard City." (ch. 28)
- Luke's saber was hidden in Artoo's dome at Jabba's, confirmed here (ch. 28).
- Escort: "no fewer than ten biker scouts and twenty stormtroopers" (ch. 28).
- Mara knows "the standard stormtrooper format for a web" (ch. 28).

## Ch. 29 — Arch collapse
- Without the Force: "With the Force to guide his hand, he could have held out against them indefinitely, blocking their blaster shots with the lightsaber. ... the Force was still silent." (ch. 29)
- Feat: Luke kills four stormtroopers "in a single sweep, the blazing green lightsaber blade slicing through the glistening stormtrooper armor with hardly a tug". He cuts the 10 m stone arch's pillars and throws the locked-on saber to cut the far pillar; the arch crushes the stormtroopers and the Chariot (ch. 29).
- Lando holds Aves at blaster point to wait for Luke's signal (ch. 29).
