# Dark Force Rising — notes part 5: ch. 12–14

## Ch. 12 — The maitrakh's account of Honoghr
- Leia senses Chewie's position by "a little concentration through the Force" and runs "Jedi calming exercises".
- Dukha: about 20 m across and 4 m high. Chains hang a concave star dish "four meters off the ground".
- **The space battle over Honoghr:**
> "It was like a war between gods ... Their lightning flashed across the sky, through the night and into the next day ... Only once was there a distant crash like thunder. It was much later before we learned that one of our higher mountains had lost its uppermost peak." — the maitrakh, DFR ch. 12
> "The groundshake was their war hammer. Whole cities vanished as the ground opened up beneath them. Fire-mountains that had been long quiet sent out flame and smoke that darkened the sky over all the land." — DFR ch. 12
- Leia's inference: a warship crashed, triggered massive quakes, and released a toxic chemical carried "to every part of the planet". She believes it was an older Alliance ship ("Our ship. Our fault.").
- **Noghri vs. Vader's troops (KEY):**
> "nearly two tens of warriors chose to attack ... Only three of the two tens died in the battle. In turn, they killed many of the Lord Vader’s attendants, despite their lightning-weapons and rock-garments. It was only when the Lord Vader himself intervened that the warriors were defeated." — DFR ch. 12
- (ANALYSIS: about 20 "effectively unarmed primitives" Noghri beat armored troops, presumably stormtroopers, with only 3 losses. Vader alone ended it.)
- Vader brought food, medicine, tools, and decon droids, and ordered all clans to share the Clean Land. Only kholm-grass grows outside it.
- Chewie is learning Noghri wrist and arm locks from Khabarakh. "Wookiees have great strength, but no knowledge of the subtlety of combat."

## Ch. 13 — Thrawn in the dukha; the orbital demonstration
- Chewie leaps at a pillar "from two meters out", climbs with his claws, and swings hand over hand along the chain carrying 3PO.
- Leia uses "the Jedi sensory enhancement techniques Luke had taught her". This backfires when the turbolasers strike.
- Thrawn's authority "surpassed even the smug condescension she’d faced from Governor Tarkin".
- **Orbital turbolaser "reminder":** a comlink signal brings "a blinding burst of green light ... a turbolaser blast" from the orbiting ISD, then "several more turbolaser blasts ... as the orbiting Star Destroyer fired again and again into the hills surrounding the village." The thunderclaps nearly knock out Leia inside the dukha.
> "I am the law on Honoghr now, maitrakh ... If I choose to follow the ancient laws, I will follow them. If I choose to ignore them, they will be ignored." — Thrawn, DFR ch. 13
- Khabarakh is arrested for "three days of public shaming" in Nystao before interrogation on the *Chimaera*.

## Ch. 14 — Luke meets "C'baoth" on Jomark
- Luke senses the other Jedi "since first dropping out of hyperspace" but can't make direct contact. Jomark has about 300,000 km² of dry land.
- C'baoth: "Then you should be in control ... In control of yourself; in control of the people and events around you. Always."
> "I called you through the Force; and when the Force calls, you must obey." — C'baoth, DFR ch. 14
> "Dark Jedi like Vader. I fought the last of them on—" — C'baoth, DFR ch. 14
> "We are the true justice of this galaxy. We two, and the new legacy of Jedi that we will forge to follow us." — C'baoth, DFR ch. 14
- "Droids are an abomination—creations that reason, but yet are not genuinely part of the Force."
- Luke's theory: Yoda stayed hidden only because "the dark side cave near his home had somehow shielded his own influence on the Force".
- C'baoth on the Emperor: "during most of his reign I was beyond his reach." He says of Outbound Flight: "All of them died. I alone survived to return. ... It changed me."
- C'baoth reacts strongly to Mara's name: "his eyes glistening".
- **C'baoth's Force lightning:**
> "even as Luke’s green-white blade snapped into existence, C’baoth raised his hand; and from his fingertips flashed a sizzling volley of all-too-well-remembered blue lightning bolts. Svan took the blast full in the head and chest ... a second blast ... The slugthrower flew from his hand, its metal surrounded for an instant by a blue-white coronal discharge." — DFR ch. 14
- The injuries are "angry red burns", not serious, and C'baoth was "faster" than Luke. Luke uses the Force to "alleviate the other’s pain".
- "pain is the one teacher no one will ignore."
- Luke is unusually weary ("wearier than he’d been ... since ... the Emperor") with "a dull ache in his muscles". (ANALYSIS: this foreshadows C'baoth's influence.)
- Luke's Force-ghost theory: a dying Jedi can "anchor himself to another Jedi". He wonders whether the five other Outbound Flight Masters anchored to C'baoth. Luke judges C'baoth insane rather than dark: "he doesn’t have the same sort of evil aura".
- Luke hears C'baoth's mental call: "Luke?"
