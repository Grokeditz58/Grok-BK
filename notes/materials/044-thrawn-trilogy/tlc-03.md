# The Last Command: notes part 3, ch. 5 (end) to ch. 8

## Ch. 5 (cont.)
- Jacen is born second. Bel Iblis's battle: Rogue Squadron vs. "Two more Victory-class Star Destroyers" that drop in at "point-blank range" next to the Calamari cruiser Orthavan. Bel Iblis's force is "down to barely two-thirds of the fifteen capital ships he'd started with … Surrounding it … were over twenty Star Destroyers and Dreadnaughts."
- Wedge's realization: the precise in-battle jumps came from Interdictor-supplied vectors ("wait until the edge of the gravity well cone yanked them back into normal space"), not "half-mystical coordination". (ANALYSIS: at least some of the Imperial coordination is technical, not C'baoth.)
- The Orthavan's turbolasers and ion cannons produce "a geyser of superheated gas" from a VSD midsection and send it "rotating ponderously away".

## Ch. 6: Chazwa; Thrawn's commando briefing
- Aves doubts Luke's growth figures. Karrde: "I don't think any information on the cloning techniques of that era has survived."
- Gillespee (Kern's Pride) retired to Ukio: "I had about as good a front-row seat to the bombardment as you could get." He has a macrobinocular data card of it.
- **Lancer frigate:** "the Lancer's twenty quad laser batteries" … "Standard Lancer crew was 850". Two Lancers are judged "better than an even match" for Karrde's group. Karrde's 15 freighters plus Gillespee's ~20 ships swarm them. The smugglers' meeting is set for Trogan in eight days.
- Major Himron's 14-man Intelligence commando team needs "No sooner than six days" to penetrate the Palace.
- Thrawn on Mriss art: "not a single one of the dozens of Mriss cultures had ever developed any form of three-dimensional artwork". Mrisst is a lure for the Coruscant sector fleet.
- Bilbringi is low on "Tibanna gas, as well as hfredium and kammris". The Bespin garrison is ordered to increase output. The earlier Nkllon raid "cost the Empire over a million man-hours" readying and repairing the Judicator. This time: "Three of our new Dreadnaughts" hide behind a captured shieldship. Shieldships are "little more than shielding, coolant systems, and a small container ship's worth of power and crew. Six fully loaded assault shuttles should make quick work of it."

## Ch. 7: Honoghr; Nomad City lost
- Honoghr's landmass is "a flat, uniform brown". The Empire bought "four decades of Noghri service".
- Luke tries to sense the Noghri pilot: "Even at this range he should have been able to sense a human pilot". He also wishes "he were skilled enough to get some reading—any reading—on this species." (ANALYSIS: Noghri are hard for Luke to read through the Force.)
- The hidden green gorge was built in "barely a month".
- **Force vision of the future (Coruscant attack):**
> "Leia was crouching in the darkness, her blaster and lightsaber in her hands … Behind her was Winter, holding tightly to two small lives … A faceless person, moving toward his sister and her children from behind the shadowy evil. A person edged with the power of the Force …" — ch. 7
- Luke: "Even Jedi have limitations, Khabarakh. Distant danger is very hard to detect." "I don't think Honoghr can be saved." "The Jedi, indeed, had their limitations."
- Ovkhevam offers to have the commandos seize the Imperial Strike Cruiser. Luke refuses and waits two days.
- **Nomad City:** "Twenty days … before Nkllon's slow rotation took them from the center of the night side across into full sunlight." Everything mined was taken: "Hfredium, kammris, dolovite … About four months' worth. A little over three million at current market prices." "Just under five thousand" people. Bel Iblis comes from Qat Chrystac. An Assault Frigate plus heavy lifters would be needed to move the city.

## Ch. 8: twins, Winter's suspicion, Mara's confession, commandos
- Winter notes Mara knew the code name "Targeter", which Winter used "only … for a few weeks on Averam. Before Imperial Intelligence broke the cell there."
- Mara on Thrawn: "He has no patterns; no favorite strategies; no discernible weaknesses. He studies his enemies and tailors his attacks against psychological blind spots."
- Ysalamiri trade: Karrde "once figured they'd hauled five or six thousand of them out of there".
- **The Emperor's Hand, key statement:**
> "I was a personal agent of the Emperor himself. He brought me here to Coruscant and the Imperial Palace and trained me to be an extension of his will across the galaxy. I could hear his voice from anywhere in the Empire, and knew how to give his orders to anyone from a stormtrooper brigade all the way up to a Grand Moff. … They knew me as the Emperor's Hand" — Mara, ch. 8
- Mara's dream of the Emperor: "sending cascades of jagged blue-white lightning at his enemies. Both men staggered". (ANALYSIS: dream imagery, not an event.)
- Endor: "Like an emotional bloodstain … Marking the spot where he died." Leia suggests the "YOU WILL KILL LUKE SKYWALKER" compulsion is the Emperor's lingering command, not Mara's own will.
- Mara's danger sense ("the quiet alarm bell") detects the commandos. They move in a "bent diamond formation" dressed as Palace security. Long-range comm is down (sabotage). Mara is captured at blasterpoint at the end of the chapter.
- Han on the twins: "They eat like starving Wookiees."
