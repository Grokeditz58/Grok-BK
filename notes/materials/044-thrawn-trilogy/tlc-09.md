# The Last Command: notes part 9, ch. 21 (end) to ch. 24 (opening)

## Ch. 21 (end): C'baoth cut off; Mara learns who Luke is
- Covell after the ysalamir cut-off: "I can hear you … He looked past C'baoth's face, to the interesting pattern of lines on the shuttle bulkhead." C'baoth: "I no longer touch your mind, but I am still your Master … I will give you things to say, and you will repeat every word."
- Noghri plan: "When the time is right the Noghri will turn upon their enslavers … five groups will come here." The Noghri goal is "eliminating every last reminder of the Emperor's presence on Wayland." They passed the Wayland coordinates to Coruscant.
- **Mara learns that Luke is Vader's son:** "The Emperor didn't want her to kill Skywalker for his own sake. It was, instead, one final act of vengeance against his father."
- **C'baoth's long-range telepathic assault on Mara (from inside the mountain, before the ysalamiri cut him off):**
> "I have seen you in my meditations, the voice continued placidly. Have seen you coming to me and kneeling at my feet. … the words and laughter disappeared beneath a distant but steady pressure against her mind. Setting her teeth, she pushed back against it. … And then, with a suddenness that made her catch her breath, the pressure was gone." — ch. 21
  - Luke: "I didn't hear any words. But I felt the pressure." They are "only a couple of days from the mountain".

## Ch. 22: Covell's death; C'baoth arrested; clone-C'baoth plan
- Selid reports Covell died in his sleep: "large portions of the General's brain had simply shut down." C'baoth had kept Covell at his side "Muttering, sort of, the whole time". Through Covell, C'baoth sent the Draklor company out of the mountain with explosives under "secret orders".
- **Thrawn's diagnosis (key Force-damage quote):**
> "My guess is that our beloved Jedi Master was trying to take over Covell's mind, perhaps even replacing entire sections of it with his own. When they hit the ysalamir bubble and he lost that direct contact, there wasn't enough of Covell left to keep him alive for long." — Thrawn, ch. 22
- Ysalamir bubble: "the entire mountain and some of the surrounding area should be within the Force-empty bubble."
- C'baoth: "The Force is not so easily betrayed as you think … With my destruction will come your own. I have foreseen it." "General Covell was mine to do with as I pleased … As is everything in my Empire."
- C'baoth is arrested in the royal chambers. The Covell company is barred from the mountain as "unwitting accomplices to treason".
- **Thrawn's C'baoth-clone plan:**
> "C'baoth is insane … But such insanity is in his mind. Not in his body. … I envision bringing such a clone to childhood and then allowing it to grow to maturity at a normal pace for its last ten or fifteen years. … Possibly on one of the worlds in the Unknown Regions where I once served the Emperor." — Thrawn, ch. 22
  - Tantiss fast-growth "is all well and good for techs and TIE fighter pilots, but not a project of this delicacy."
- Aves catches an Aqualish spy tailing Rogue Squadron. Wedge feeds him the "twenty-eight hours" Tangrene timing hint on purpose.

## Ch. 23: asteroids gone; Leia solves the clone-speed secret
- Leia had expected at least "seventy cloaked asteroids … could easily be twice that many". Twenty-one were found.
- Karrde's Bilbringi sensor log shows exactly 22, so the siege is over. Bel Iblis: Thrawn won't spread cloaking tech; "The last thing he can afford would be for us to get our hands on a working model."
- **Key lore: why fast clones go mad, and the ysalamiri fix:**
> "When you make an exact duplicate of a sentient being, there's a natural resonance or something set up through the Force between that duplicate and the original. That's what warps the mind of a clone that's been grown too fast—there's not enough time for the mind to adapt to the pressure on it. It can't adjust; so it breaks." … "He's using ysalamiri to block the Force away from the cloning tanks." — Leia, ch. 23
  - Evidence: Mara's "five or six thousand ysalamiri" from Myrkr weren't on warships, since Luke used the Force freely aboard the Chimaera.
- Leia: "What she'd seen that night hadn't been a dream, but a Jedi vision."
- Fey'lya pays Karrde "seventy thousand" to take Leia to Wayland: "the Emperor's storehouse may not be destroyed before the Grand Admiral can remove some of its contents … a disaster. To both the Bothan people and to the galaxy."
- Mobvekhar, disguised as a Jawa, senses and catches Fey'lya eavesdropping.
- Old Senate Library research on Clone Wars cloning techniques turns up nothing.

## Ch. 24 (opening): at the mountain; Bilbringi raid begins
- **Luke senses the ysalamir dead zone from a distance:** "It's the mountain … It's dark. All of it. … Like Myrkr was."
- Luke: "There are Jedi techniques for enhancing vision. Maybe they'll work on a macrobinocular image." With them he reads a data pad in Psadan hands at range.
- Mara keeps "a set of five stones she'd gotten to hover in the air" (ANALYSIS: Luke's training has raised her multi-object TK).
- Bilbringi: smuggler ships (Etherway, Kai Mir, Klivering, Starry Ice, Dawn Beat, Lastri's Ort, Amanda Fallow) are placed for Mazzic's CGT grab. Security fields now limit traffic to narrow corridors ("Deviate more than about fifteen meters").
