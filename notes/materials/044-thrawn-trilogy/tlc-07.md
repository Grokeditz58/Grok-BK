# The Last Command: notes part 7, ch. 16 (cont.) to ch. 18 (most)

## Ch. 16 (cont.): the trek begins
- Luke's sensing range: "I don't sense anyone else nearby … But they could just be out of range."
- Luke's lightsaber cuts a brown-scaled animal off Threepio's arm. The party covers "nearly ten kilometers" on day one.
- Luke uses TK to lift and place cut thornbushes into a barricade ("Reaching out with the Force, he lifted the two bushes off the ground and lowered them into the creek bed").
- **Mara's view of the Emperor's death, corrected:**
> "Both of you moved in against him with your lightsabers. I saw you do it." … "I didn't move against him, Mara … He was about to kill me when Vader picked him up and threw him down an open shaft. I couldn't have done anything even if I'd wanted to—I was still half paralyzed from the lightning bolts he'd hit me with." — ch. 16
  (ANALYSIS: Mara "had been in contact with her master at the moment of his death, and had seen it all. Except that, somehow, she'd gotten it wrong". Her Force link to the Emperor carried a distorted image across the galaxy.)
- Mara stares at Luke's lightsaber with a "haunted expression" (ANALYSIS: foreshadowing, since it is Luke's original blade, found in Tantiss).

## Ch. 17: Delta Source found
- Winter's perfect memory: she recalls "every single detail of fifty separate one-minute conversations" (for example, "Only forty-seven" listeners to a given rumor).
- Stardust plan: dust clouds are dropped and dispersed with negative ion beams. Dust reflectance is "point nine-three and nine-eight". One asteroid is found by its turbulence trail and uncloaked by Harrier turbolasers, then self-destructs: "a mesh of thin lines … flared brilliantly". A second asteroid hits a transport, and a full-pattern bombardment of a "probability cone" by the fleet and battle stations fails to find it.
- The crippled transport burns up on the planetary shield: "the sharp blue-white edge of shattered atomic bonds".
- CGT arrays ("Crystal gravfield traps, originally designed to zoom in on the mass of sensor-stealthed ships from thousands of kilometers away") were lost at Svivren.
- **Delta Source:** ch'hala trees in the Grand Corridor, with modules dug from "nearly eight meters down" on the taproot.
> "Any pressure on the ch'hala tree's trunk—including the pressure created by sound waves—sets off small chemical changes in the inner layers of bark. … The module takes the chemical data, turns it back into pressure data, and from there back into speech." — Bremen, ch. 17
- Mon Mothma: "so very like the Emperor … Even after his death, it seems, his hand can move against us." Bel Iblis: "An organic microphone … With no electronics anywhere in sight for a counterintelligence sweep to pick up."
- Leia and Bel Iblis both sense that the source is in the Grand Corridor: "It's here, though, somewhere close by. I can feel it." Leia notes she lacks "Bel Iblis's fine-honed warrior's intuition" and "Luke's far deeper Jedi skill".

## Ch. 18: Hijarna confrontation
- Mazzic arrives with the Distant Rainbow, the Skyclaw, and the Raptor, plus 12 enforcers. His bodyguard is named here as Shada.
- Ferrier's Defel planted the forged card. Karrde: the vornskrs are good at tracking "Only if you're hunting ysalamiri or Jedi."
- Ferrier gives himself away by naming "Kosk", which no one else had said. Clyngunn: "Thrawn is not like Vader. He would not waste troops in a deliberately failed attack."
- Ferrier pulls a "thermal detonator" and forces Karrde to fly him to his ship. Karrde signals with running lights and threatens to self-destruct the ship with a knee-panel switch. Clyngunn knocks out the Defel. Ferrier shoots up the fire-control board and flees.
