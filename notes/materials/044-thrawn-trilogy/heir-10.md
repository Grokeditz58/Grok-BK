# Heir to the Empire — notes part 10: Chapters 30–32, About the Author, Also By

## Ch. 30
> "One man ... And without the Force, too," — Heir, ch. 30 (Karrde on the Hyllyard arch collapse)
> "And abruptly, right in the middle of Han’s last word, Luke felt a surge of awareness and strength fill him. ... The Force was again with him. ... Just over twelve kilometers. Karrde had been right—those ysalamiri did, indeed, reinforce one another." — Heir, ch. 30 (Key stat: the Myrkr ysalamiri Force-null extends to about 12 km altitude)
- Luke: Mara "wants to kill me". He feels "a strangely compelling reluctance" to tell Han her past (ch. 30).
- Sluis Van task force: "five Imperial Star Destroyers, twelve Strike-class cruisers, twenty-two of the old Carrack-class light cruisers, and thirty full squadrons of TIE fighters". Staging point "three-thousandths of a light-year out" (ch. 30).
- Thrawn: "We’re here to gain ships, not lose them." He infers Myrkr's fall because the garrison is two hours late reporting ("I left orders for them to report every twelve") (ch. 30).

## Ch. 31 — Battle of Sluis Van
- The Larkhess is an Escort Frigate run with "just fifteen men" instead of a 920 crew (ch. 31).
- The cloaked freighter carries "at least forty TIE fighters and fifty truncated-cone-shaped things" (mole miners with stormtroopers and spacetroopers) (ch. 31).
- Luke counts the Imperial force by sight: "I make it five Star Destroyers and something over twenty smaller ships." Wedge's sense: "the tingles refused to go away." (ch. 31)
- Luke: "the Imperials can’t possibly have packed more than four or five stormtroopers in each one of them ... Yes, but I could take them" (ch. 31).
- Spacetrooper zero-g armor fires "miniature proton torpedoes or something" from a backpack (ch. 31).
- Thrawn's precision fire: "Starboard turbolasers: focus all fire on the Assault Frigate ... Concentrate on the starboard side of the ship only". He then tractors it in as a shield (ch. 31).
- Status: "Forty-three of the mole miners have successfully attached ... thirty-nine are secure" (ch. 31).
- Thrawn on spacetroopers: "that sort of deep-space combat is not what spacetrooper suits were designed for." (ch. 31)
- Lando restarts the radio-remote mole miners and cripples all the stolen ships (ch. 31).
> "We haven’t been defeated, Captain ... Merely slowed down a bit. We have Wayland, and we have the treasures of the Emperor’s storehouse. ... I will not waste ships and men trying to change that which cannot be changed." — Heir, ch. 31
> "Thrawn was not merely a soldier ... He was, instead, a true warrior, with his eye set on the final goal and not on his own personal glory." — Heir, ch. 31 (Pellaeon)

## Ch. 32
- Eight remaining spacetroopers: "Six were hunted down and destroyed; the other two eventually self-destructed, one managing to cripple a Corvette" (ch. 32).
- Cliffhanger: "Admiral Ackbar has been arrested and removed from command. On charges of treason." / "To Be Continued . . ." (ch. 32)
