# Heir to the Empire — notes part 7: Chapters 22–23

## Ch. 22
- Karrde turns down Han's offer to avoid "taking sides". Ghent is "possibly the best in the business" as a slicer (ch. 22).
- Thrawn comes to Myrkr for more ysalamiri ("None of them has died, Captain. We simply need more of them."). He wants warships, which is Karrde's guilty reaction and an early Katana hint (ch. 22).
> "Skywalker is a Jedi; and among a Jedi’s bag of tricks is the ability to go into a sort of comatose state." — Heir, ch. 22 (Thrawn)
- Karrde's hospitality code: "they’re our guests. They’ve sat at our table and eaten our food . . . and like it or not, that means they’re under our protection." (ch. 22)
- Lando: "I’ve seen her before ... and I don’t think she was a smuggler at the time." (ch. 22, about Mara)
- Mara, on killing Luke without the Force: "he would be an easy target, even for someone like her who hadn’t picked up a lightsaber more than a handful of times in her life." (ch. 22). Compare her skilled cutting in ch. 23.
- Luke escapes in a Skipray blastboat ("twice the length of Luke’s X-wing", heavy armor). Mara pursues (ch. 22).

## Ch. 23
- Thrawn arrives on the rightmost shuttle, not the center one: "So much ... for him doing things the obvious way." (ch. 23, Karrde)
- Thrawn saw the Skipray crash from orbit: "We had a higher observation angle" (ch. 23).
- Han identifies a Noghri in Thrawn's entourage as one of the Bimmisaari species (ch. 23).
- Mara senses the incoming Imperial shuttle before it can be heard: "her eyes going strangely unfocused" (ch. 23).
> "I’ve wanted to kill you for a long time. I dreamed about your death every night for most of that first year. ... You destroyed my life ... It’s only fair that I destroy yours." — Heir, ch. 23 (Mara)
- Artoo and X-wing counterpart interface: "something like thirty percent faster than a baseline astromech/X-wing interface. Maybe thirty-five." From "close to three thousand hours of flight time" (ch. 23).
> "She raised the weapon, ignited it— / And in a handful of quick, sure swipes trimmed, shortened, and cut the branches from the tree. ... ‘You’ve used a lightsaber before.’" — Heir, ch. 23 (Mara's saber competence)
- Mara knows "standard stormtrooper procedure" (sensors left behind) (ch. 23).
