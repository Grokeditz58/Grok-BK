# Dark Force Rising — notes part 2: ch. 3–5

## Ch. 3 — Council hearing; Ackbar under house arrest
- Han tells the Council the Imperial strike force was "approximately forty TIE fighters and fifty stolen mole miners". Han and Lando "had to fry the nerve centers of over forty capital ships".
- Han reveals the Grand Admiral ("light blue skin ... eyes that glowed red ... white uniform"). Mon Mothma: "We’ve accounted for all the Grand Admirals." Fey'lya calls it "a self-granted promotion".
- Ackbar: the Imperial records held only "a list of the Grand Admirals’ names and a little about their assignments". Leia: the Grand Admirals were "part of his plan to bring the Imperial military more personally under his control." Ackbar: "As was the Death Star project itself".
- **Cloaking shield (Ackbar's reasoning):**
> "By its very nature a cloaking shield would be more danger to the user than it was worth. A cloaked warship’s own sensor beams would be as useless as those of its enemies, leaving it to flail about totally blind. Worse, if it were under power, the enemy could locate it by simply tracking its drive emissions." — DFR ch. 3
> "For all his ambition and self-confidence, Councilor Fey’lya hasn’t the tactical skills we need against a threat of this magnitude." — Ackbar, DFR ch. 3
- The Mon Cal Star Cruisers "were originally passenger liners".
- Leia's Force use: "stretching out with all her rudimentary Jedi skills to try to sense his purpose and feelings. It didn’t get her very far." She senses the twins: "So potentially strong in the Force … and yet so utterly helpless."

  - Ando Demilitarization Observation Group; Senate Interspecies Advisory Committee; "PERSONAL JEDI ADVISER TO SENATOR PALPATINE 6\79 TO 5\77" (details "UNAVAILABLE").
  - "MEMBER OF JEDI FORCE ASSEMBLED TO OPPOSE THE DARK JEDI INSURRECTION ON BPFASSH 7\77 TO 1\74 PE." He helped resolve the Alderaan ascendancy contention and the Duinuogwuin–Gotal mediation (with "TRA'S M'INS"). Ambassador at large to the Xappyh sector.
  - "HIGHLY INSTRUMENTAL IN CONVINCING SENATE TO AUTHORIZE AND FUND OUTBOUND FLIGHT PROJECT. ONE OF SIX JEDI MASTERS ATTACHED TO PROJECT ... NO RECORD EXISTS AFTER PROJECT DEPARTURE FROM YAGA MINOR".
- Luke: one of the Bpfassh Dark Jedi survived "long enough to face Master Yoda on Dagobah".
- Winter has a "perfect and indelible memory". She describes C'baoth as about 40 in the Alderaan holo, "Fairly muscular ... rather odd for a Jedi". Luke: "control of the body was linked to control of the mind."
- Luke: if Jomark's Jedi is C'baoth, "he has to be over a hundred by now". Yoda lived "a good nine hundred years".
- The Nkllon contact "there for a short time, and then gone without a trace. Was C'baoth perhaps no longer fully in control of his powers?"
- Ch'hala trees ripple red when touched (sets up the TLC plot).
- The Bothans held off a *Victory*-class SD at New Cov with four ships "until a Star Cruiser could come".
- Leia promised Khabarakh she would come alone to Honoghr. Chewie insists on coming. Han: "Khabarakh knows I’m Darth Vader’s daughter".

## Ch. 5 — Rishi; the Katana fleet; Mara's telekinesis
- **Katana fleet / "Dark Force"**: "two hundred Dreadnaught-class Heavy Cruisers ... lost about ten years before the Clone Wars". Full-rig slave circuitry cut the crew "upwards of sixteen thousand men each ... down to around two thousand". A hive virus drove the crews insane; "the entire fleet went with them". This was the origin of the shift to decentralized droids.
- Karrde found it "just over fifteen years ago" while a navigator/sensor specialist. The ships were still "fully functional, with both interior and running lights". Their own ship hit "the mass shadow of a large comet": 8 dead, 6 left. He memorized the coordinates and erased the records.
> "We don’t give up the Dreadnaughts under duress ... Not to Thrawn; not to anyone else." — Karrde, DFR ch. 5
- **Mara's training and powers (key):**
> "But the Emperor’s intensive training had included direct manipulation of the Force as well as the long-range communication abilities that had been her primary value to his regime. Those skills had vanished at the moment of his death, reappearing only briefly and erratically in the years since then." — DFR ch. 5
> "Setting her teeth, clearing her thoughts as best she could, Mara reached her mind out toward the tubing. ... The length of tubing twitched, rolled a couple of millimeters— ... She took a deep breath, straining with every bit of strength she possessed ... And behind her captor, the tubing fell" — DFR ch. 5
- (ANALYSIS: post-Endor, Mara's TK is a low-end baseline. It takes maximum strain to tip a short length of tubing two meters away.) She then kills the bounty hunter "Dengar Roth" (a forged ID) with a forearm-sheathed hold-out blaster: "shot him neatly in the head". He was a "good" pro who dropped and spun within one second.
- "I can’t turn it on and off like a sensor pack ... Not anymore." Karrde asks: "Is this a remnant of some previous Jedi training?"
- Bounty: Karrde 20,000. They leave Rishi in about 2 hours. "the dreams started again."
- Rishi city-vale: about 150 km E–W, a few km N–S.
