# The Last Command: notes part 8, ch. 18 (end) to ch. 21 (Covell)

## Ch. 18 (end)
- Ferrier escapes under a "Conner net" ("a violently expanding tangle of metal mesh … throwing off sparks"). Mazzic's fighters Griv and Amber destroy his Gunship ("a brief flare of light"). Mazzic apologizes. The coalition holds.

## Ch. 19: Wayland trek; Bilbringi briefing
- Unseen helpers are clearing the way. A clawbird was "killed in flight. By a single stab." Myneyrshi are "supposed to have made an art of that kind of close-blade combat."
- **Luke teaches Mara to resolve minds:**
> "'Four of them,' Skywalker said quietly. 'No. Five.' … 'Try looking for deviations … The ways the minds are different from each other. That's the best way to resolve them.'" — ch. 19
  - Luke: "I'd know if there were any other humans nearby." Mara: "Bet that comes in handy." Luke: "It's just a matter of training."
- Mara on the Emperor's training: "Enough … All the basics." Luke offers a "refresher course" after seeing "A few images and pictures that didn't make any sense."
- On teaching the twins: "They're going to be strong in the Force, and with that strength comes responsibility. How do I teach them … how not to abuse their power?"
- **Bilbringi plan (Ackbar/Madine):** fake Tangrene (Ubiqtorate base, CGT) and actually hit Bilbringi. Pash Cracken: Generis fell "three days ago". Clones are "same rabid dedication, same cold-blooded machine-precision fighting … they're everywhere now". Wedge: clone TIE pilots "were pulling stunts I didn't think TIEs were capable of." Kryll believes Thrawn picks "his best people for his cloning templets."
- Leia uses "short-term memory enhancement techniques Luke had taught her." Mobvekhar senses her waking from her breathing.

## Ch. 20: Thrawn reads the feint
- Kanchen sector falls after a "thirty-hour battle". Xa Fel surrenders under the threat of bombardment.
- **The Ukio "superweapon" is exposed** by Gillespee's macrobinocular record: "The Star Destroyer's blast is not, in fact, penetrating Ukio's planetary shield. What appears to be that same blast is actually a second shot, fired from a cloaked vessel inside the shield … most likely a Carrack-class light cruiser". The counter is "saturation fire at the spot where the turbolaser blasts appear to penetrate the shield." Bel Iblis attributes the timing to C'baoth: "The position and timing were exquisitely handled."
- Wayland coordinates reach Coruscant from a "reliable" source that Winter won't name (ANALYSIS: the Noghri).
- **Fey'lya on Mount Tantiss:** "The Bothan people knew the Emperor … There are things in that storehouse which must never again see light. Weapons and devices which Thrawn will some day find and use against us".
- Mon Mothma: "The Empire rules by force and threat; we rule instead by inspiration and leadership."
- **Thrawn deduces Bilbringi from art:** Ackbar's own sculptures and Bel Iblis's chosen art (from the "Obroa-skai information raid"). Intelligence gave Tangrene "a 94 percent probability". Thrawn: "they attempt to use my own tactical skill and insight against me. They pretend to walk into my trap … it makes no difference whether we crush them at Tangrene or at Bilbringi." He notes "Delta Source has been silenced."
- Karrde's smugglers plan to steal the Bilbringi CGT during the battle. Ghent will write them onto the payroll.

## Ch. 21: Mara's training; Noghri escort; natives; C'baoth and Covell
- **Mara's lightsaber training:** Luke moves a seed pod with TK, and Mara immobilizes it with her own TK while stabbing it ("It's difficult to split your attention between two separate mental and physical activities"). Luke recalls the saber into his hand with TK. Mara: "The Emperor's philosophy was to use everything you had available." Obi-Wan "never taught me anything about using the tip of the lightsaber blade."
- The compulsion "YOU WILL KILL LUKE SKYWALKER" recurs. Mara: "Not yet … I still need him."
- Luke senses groups of 5–6 and two species but "I don't have any basis for figuring out what kind." Mara senses that they have stopped.
- Han's blaster "blew a sizable pit in one side of the trunk".
- Ekhrikhor clan Bakh'tor and 8 Noghri covertly escort the party ("Two each have traveled before, after, and on either side of you"). Han becomes "Han clan Solo".
- **Myneyrshi and Psadans; Wayland history:** first human colonists, then the Empire building "into the forbidden mountain" with slaves, then "the Guardian", then "the one who called himself the Jedi Master came, and in a battle that lit up the sky he defeated the Guardian". The Wookiee with a "lightning bow" proves they don't keep slaves. Safe conduct is given by satna-chakka.
- **C'baoth's control of Covell (key):**
> "'Yes, I heard,' C'baoth said, his amusement echoing through his voice. And through Covell's mind." … "This—this—is true power. Holding another's life in the palm of your hand. Having the power to choose his path, and his thoughts, and his feelings. To rule his life, and decree his death. … To command his soul." — C'baoth, ch. 21
> "He, like the Grand Admiral, saw power only as how far outside himself he could reach. And it destroyed him … For if he'd truly commanded Vader …" — C'baoth on the Emperor, ch. 21
- C'baoth senses Luke and Mara on Wayland from orbit/approach: "They are here. Skywalker and Jade both." "the key to his soul is even now waiting for me in the mountain below" (ANALYSIS: the Luuke clone). "I have seen her in my meditations—have seen her coming to me and kneeling at my feet."
- Covell experiences sudden "emptiness" when C'baoth's link is cut (ysalamiri, continued in the next slice).
