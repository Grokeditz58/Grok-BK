# Heir to the Empire — notes part 5: Chapters 14 (end)–18

## Ch. 14 (end) — Yoda/Dagobah logic
> "Did it ever occur to you to wonder why Master Yoda was able to stay hidden from the Emperor and Vader all those years? ... They knew I existed by my effect on the Force. Why not Yoda?" — Heir, ch. 14 (Luke)
> "His own skirmishes with Darth Vader had been bad enough; a full-scale Force war between Jedi Masters would be terrifying. ‘And he probably didn’t stop him with a lot of time to spare.’" — Heir, ch. 14 (Yoda vs. the Bpfasshi Dark Jedi, as inferred by Luke)
> "The cave shielded him ... Just like a pair of positive and negative electric charges close enough together—to a distant observer they look almost like no charge at all." — Heir, ch. 14 (Leia)
- Luke thinks Han and Lando "don’t have the resistance to interrogation that you do" (to Leia). Jomark: "Less than three million people" (ch. 14).
- Threepio gets voiceprint-doctored to sound like Leia (ch. 14).

## Ch. 15 — Thrawn's deduction feats
- From probe footage of the Falcon/Lady Luck docking: "Four minutes fifty-three seconds ... Three people transferred". Thrawn deduces that Leia and Chewie went on the Lady Luck and that the Falcon's "Leia" is a "voiceprint-doctored droid"; therefore "Kashyyyk" (ch. 15).
> "He’ll make no stopovers ... Jedi use a hibernation state for trips of such length." — Heir, ch. 15 (Thrawn)
- Pellaeon: "Kashyyyk’s ecology can best be described as a layered deathtrap." (ch. 15)
> "It was my one failure, out on the Fringes. The one time when understanding a race’s art gave me no insight at all into its psyche. ... I wound up destroying their world." — Heir, ch. 15 (Thrawn)

## Ch. 16 — Luke escapes the Chimaera + Interdictor
- The Interdictor pulls Luke out of hyperspace "nearly twenty light-years short of Jomark" (ch. 16).
- Feat: Luke gives a cold-launched proton torpedo "a slight realignment tap" with the Force and destroys the decoy freighter as a screen (ch. 16).
> "And with a scream of horribly stressed electronics, the X-wing came to a sudden dead stop." — Heir, ch. 16 (reverse-triggered compensator; the tractor lock jumps to his torpedoes, which destroy "one of the Chimaera’s ten projectors")
> "Forcing himself to relax, he let the Force flow through him, allowing it to guide his hands on the controls the way it did his lightsaber. The ship jumped once as a shot got through" — Heir, ch. 16 (Luke dodging a full ISD barrage; he loses a laser-cannon tip and the canopy is scorched)
- Thrawn has Rukh execute tractor operator Pieterson: "Anyone can make an error, Ensign. But that error doesn’t become a mistake until you refuse to correct it." Rukh's speed: "Pellaeon never even saw Rukh move." (ch. 16)
- Thrawn predicts the hyperdrive failure ("A light-year away, no farther") and offers smugglers "a bounty of thirty thousand" (ch. 16).

## Ch. 17 — stranded; Kashyyyk
- The hyperdrive fails after ten minutes at Point Four, about half a light-year. The subspace antenna is "crystallized"; Artoo rewinds "eight kilometers" of jammer wire (ch. 17).
- Luke's Force call to Leia across hundreds of light-years: "Leia, he called silently, putting all the power of his new Jedi skill into the effort." Leia then wakes on the Lady Luck: "She could have sworn she’d heard his voice." (ch. 17)
- Hibernation: "As long as I come up every few days for food and water, hibernation is perfectly safe." (ch. 17)
- Kashyyyk: kshyy vine "stronger than composite cable material, and cannot even by blasters be cut ... self-repairing". Ralrra pledges a life debt on behalf of the Wookiees. Wookiee claws are shown (ch. 17).
- Leia sees a Noghri face in a window but senses nothing: "reaching out with all the Jedi sense she could muster. Nothing." (ch. 17)

## Ch. 18 — Karrde captures Luke
- The Wild Karrde's aft holds feel "oddly dark" to Luke (ysalamiri, not yet named) (ch. 18).
> "With a lightsaber attached to your belt? ... You were either Luke Skywalker, Jedi, or else someone with a taste for antiques and an insufferably high opinion of his swordsmanship." — Heir, ch. 18 (Karrde)
> "It was one of my associates, Mara Jade, who led us here." — Heir, ch. 18 (Mara sensed Luke from light-years away)
> "Never before, not even from the Emperor, had he ever felt such a black and bitter hatred." — Heir, ch. 18
> "There were Jedi methods for fighting off unconsciousness. But they all took at least a split second of preparation—a split second that Luke did not have." — Heir, ch. 18 (stunned from behind)
