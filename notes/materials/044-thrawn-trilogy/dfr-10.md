# Dark Force Rising — notes part 10: ch. 28–29 + end matter

## Ch. 28 — Battle of the Katana fleet
- Wedge estimates "three hundred troops aboard those drop ships". His trick: he ducks under a TIE fighter so the pursuing interceptor has to swerve, then kills it with "A single point-blank blast". Gold Three and Four take out six of seven drop ships.
- Luke senses the boarders' minds as "Something not quite right about them" (they turn out to be clones). He pre-cuts booby-trapped ceiling and wall sections with his lightsaber.
- **Luke can't lift a wrecked mini scout walker:** "Master Yoda had lifted his X-wing out of a Dagobah swamp once … but Master Yoda had been far stronger in the Force than Luke was ... The walker didn’t even quiver ... Either the machine was wedged too tightly ... or Luke simply didn’t have the strength to lift it." — DFR ch. 28 (ANALYSIS: a lift-capacity data point, possibly confounded by the wedging.)
- Han uses the walker's blaster cannon on a squad. Luke deflects three shots, then kills the climbing point man.
- Mara's Z-95 blinds the *Judicator*'s lower-aft-central sensor package by firing at the shielding beneath the central drive nozzle. Karrde's group is 18 craft.
- **Bel Iblis arrives with six Dreadnaughts**: "smothering it with a flood of ion cannon fire and ignoring the increasingly sporadic turbolaser blasts". Han had secretly invited him.
- **Only 15 of the original 200 Katana ships remain.** Thrawn had already moved the rest after getting the location from Hoffner. Karrde: "the Grand Admiral simply applied a large infusion of cash."
- ISD *Peremptory* arrives. Three Dreadnaughts can't "hold down a Star Destroyer for long".
- Mara's danger sense fires too late. She ejects into the ion bombardment and loses all suit electronics.
- **Luke senses Mara in trouble across the battlefield:** "It’s Mara ... She’s in trouble ... Hit and … and lost ... She must have run into one of the ion beams."
- **Luke's lightsaber throw breaches the hull:** "the kid took a step back and threw his lightsaber to the side. It spiraled across the anteroom, spun into the wall— And with a crack like thunder, sliced the anteroom open to space." The blast doors seal and the boarders die in the decompression.
- **Slave-rig ram:** Han flies a slaved Dreadnaught by remote from the *Katana*'s helm into the *Peremptory*, whose systems were "still being scrambled by Bel Iblis’s ion attack".
> "Even from the Katana’s distance, the impact and explosion were pretty spectacular." — DFR ch. 28
- (ANALYSIS: a Dreadnaught ramming an ISD destroys it outright, "the Peremptory died its fiery death". The *Judicator* withdraws.)

## Ch. 29 — Aftermath; clones
- "Locating a single inert ejection seat among all the debris of battle had been a nearly hopeless task for Karrde’s people. For a Jedi, it was no trick at all." Mara is rescued (air nearly gone, mild concussion).
- **C'baoth's power claims:**
> "I have grown in power and ability since I left Wayland ... I am C’baoth; Jedi Master. The Force which binds the galaxy together is my servant." — DFR ch. 29 (his "voice boomed and echoed throughout the room")
> "The circle has closed. The Jedi will rule again." ... "As of now, I am all that is not indispensable to the Empire." — DFR ch. 29
- C'baoth will take over the Mount Tantiss project on Wayland. Pellaeon steps "into the protective sphere of the ysalamir".
- **Clones:** the dead boarders share "a single face". Luke: "the Empire’s found a set of Spaarti cloning cylinders. And has gotten them working." Han: crews in "Maybe only a few months." The book closes with "To Be Concluded …"
