# Heir to the Empire — notes part 11: Back matter (Del Rey Legends edition, lines 4023–5560)

Read in full. Most of this back matter (Legends intro, five era intros, the excerpts, and the timeline) is boilerplate shared by the Del Rey 2014 "Legends" ebook editions. Only the bm03 excerpt is specific to this book. It is DFR ch. 1, so it gets full notes under DFR (dfr-01.md). Quick power-relevant notes on the shared excerpts are below. Their own source novels are not part of this item, so these are "as excerpted" only.

## bm03 — Dark Force Rising ch. 1 excerpt (summary, details in dfr-01.md)
- Thrawn attacks the abandoned Myrkr base with General Covell's AT-ATs as "field experience". He deduces Karrde is hiding behind the only asteroid "for nearly ten million kilometers" and calls in the Interdictor *Constrainer*.
> "I could hear his call from anywhere in the Empire" (Heir ch. 26) is echoed here: "The hunches and sensory flickers, the urges and the compulsions" return. Mara senses the Interdictor coming before any sensor does. — DFR ch. 1
- Vornskrs now stare at Mara, which marks her as Force-sensitive.
- Interdictor: "four huge gravity-wave generators capable of simulating planet-sized masses".
- C'baoth on Jomark: Wayland "wrested from the Dark Jedi whom the Emperor had set to guard his Mount Tantiss storehouse ... since he’d fought against the Emperor’s Guardian." He senses a Force "flicker" three times before (Mara). Battle coordination is "hard ... the effort and pain."

## Excerpts (shared set)
- *Deceived* (Kemp): Zeerid on Ord Mantell. Malgus heads for the Jedi Temple, saying "The Force is conflict". Aryn Leneer on Alderaan. Note: Malgus was "near death from that Jedi witch" after the Battle of Alderaan.
- *Dark Lord: Rise of Darth Vader* (Luceno), Murkhana: Shryne's Force-guided jump from about 10 m; "droid buster" flattens droids "within a radius of fifty meters".
- *Allegiance* (Zahn), ch. 1: ISD *Reprisal* (Ozzel); "a two-by-two group of brilliant green turbolaser bolts ... blasting four of the [pirate] ships into rubble"; the LaRone and Brightwater POVs.
- *X-Wing: Rogue Squadron* (Stackpole), ch. 1: Corran's simulator run. Shield stat: an X-wing aft shield "demisphere approximately twenty meters behind"; a TIE bomber impact against it is "roughly equivalent to a vehicle hitting a ferrocrete wall at sixty kilometers per hour".
- *Vector Prime* (Salvatore), ch. 1: Nom Anor "crushing the stone in his grasp" with "tremendous power". Mara: "Even when I tried to use the Force to gain a better perspective on him, I drew . . . A blank". Mara's illness requires "hours and tremendous Force energy merely holding it in check."
- *Betrayal* (Allston), ch. 1: Jacen deflects blaster bolts from 20+ Adumari troops back into them; Ben cuts a wall panel; a Force-eased drop of "a dozen meters".
- *Outcast* (Allston), ch. 1: Valin Horn's "Not-Mirax" delusion opening.
