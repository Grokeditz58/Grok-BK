## Ch. 1 — Thrawn's debut (tactics/intellect)
> "Here, under the command of possibly the greatest military mind the Empire had ever seen." — Heir, ch. 1 (Pellaeon POV)
> "His brilliant successes had won him the title of Warlord and the right to wear the white uniform of Grand Admiral—the only nonhuman ever granted that honor by the Emperor." — Heir, ch. 1
> "Pellaeon had often wondered how the Battle of Endor would have ended if Thrawn, not Vader, had been commanding the Executor." — Heir, ch. 1
- Feat (Thrawn): from a three-sentry-ship probe he works out that the attacking force is Elomin, then uses a Marg Sabl that they are "psychologically" unable to counter. Four Assault Frigates and three X-wing wings are wiped out: "An hour later, it was all over." (ch. 1). One Star Destroyer with a green crew.
> "Learn about art, Captain ... When you understand a species’ art, you understand that species." — Heir, ch. 1
- Lore: Executor loss at Endor ("five years") gutted the officer corps. Thrawn has been searching for the Wayland location "for over a year". Myrkr was left alone by "the Old Republic and the Jedi of that time" (ch. 1).
- Rukh (Noghri) ambushes Pellaeon unseen in the anteroom; "slender assassin’s knife" (ch. 1).

## Ch. 2 — Luke/Leia Force state at story start
> "You don’t need me, Luke. You are a Jedi, strong in the Force. ... You are strong in the Force, Luke, and with perseverance and discipline you will grow stronger still." — Heir, ch. 2 (Ben's farewell)
> "Not the last of the old Jedi, Luke. The first of the new." — Heir, ch. 2
- Luke senses C-3PO coming through a door "twenty meters behind him" before he turns (ch. 2).
> "Halfway across the palace’s upper section he could feel Leia’s drowsy presence. Her presence, and that of the twins she carried within her. ... the fact that he could sense them at all implied they must be tremendously strong in the Force." — Heir, ch. 2
- Leia's fine control, a low feat: "The lamp didn’t even flicker. ... Still not enough fine control over the Force, obviously, for something as small as a light switch." (ch. 2). She senses Luke's mood across the Palace at night (ch. 2).
- Luke remembers that the Dagobah cave showed "the sheer power and presence of the dark side". He senses no residual evil in the Palace: "But just because he couldn’t sense it didn’t necessarily mean it wasn’t there." (ch. 2)
- Winter has verbatim recall: "Anyone who could remember whole conversations verbatim" (ch. 2).
- Han and Dravis: Talon Karrde is named as the likely new top smuggler (ch. 2).

## Ch. 3 — Karrde / Mara / ysalamiri
> "An ysalamir in this stage is sessile—its claws have elongated to the point where they’ve essentially grown directly into the core of the branch it inhabits." — Heir, ch. 3
> "Coming all the way out here to collect ysalamiri is a bit of an overkill to use against a single Jedi." — Heir, ch. 3 (Karrde)
- Mara Jade: "five and a half" months with Karrde. She reacts sharply on the Emperor and Vader ("He died there"). Karrde: "what it was Luke Skywalker had done to make her so desperately hate him." (ch. 3)
- Myrkr vegetation: "high metal content ... makes sensor readings unreliable" (ch. 3).
