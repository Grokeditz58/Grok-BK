# Dark Force Rising — notes part 4: ch. 9–11

## Ch. 9 — To Honoghr; Khabarakh vs. Chewbacca
- Khabarakh greets Leia as "Lady Vader". The trip takes "Approximately four days". He accepts the Wookiee life debt: "The Wookiee code of honor is similar to our own."
- 3PO knows "six million languages".
- Honoghr from orbit is "a uniform brown ... the entire planet might have been dead", with a pale green patch (the Clean Land).
- **Noghri vs. Wookiee:**
> "Just in time to see Khabarakh, seated at the pilot’s station, throw up his right arm and somehow send Chewbacca spinning past him to crash with a roar into the underside of the control board." — DFR ch. 9
> "The Noghri took the charge head-on this time, grabbing Chewbacca’s huge wrists in his wiry hands and holding on for all he was worth. It wasn’t enough. Slowly but steadily, Khabarakh’s arms were bent steadily backwards" — DFR ch. 9
- (ANALYSIS: the Noghri redirects the charge with technique, but Chewie wins on raw strength. Khabarakh ends it with a built-in cockpit stun weapon.)
- Khabarakh: "I have promised to defend you, and I will. To my own death, if need be."
- **Vader introduced Thrawn to the Noghri**: "the Lord Darth Vader first brought him to us ... his duties against the Emperor’s enemies would now be taking his full attention. That the Grand Admiral would henceforth be our lord and commander." "The Lord Vader had been the only one save the Emperor who cared for Noghri well-being."
- Thrawn notices the static-damped ship of "Khabarakh, clan Kihm'bar ... once part of commando team twenty-two ... of which team only Khabarakh still survives."

## Ch. 10 — The village; the maitrakh; Thrawn's visit
- Leia can't read Noghri minds: "as always, this particular alien mind seemed totally closed to her." She can, however, track Chewie approaching with her thoughts extended.
- The maitrakh verifies Leia by scent: "I greet you, Lady Vader. But I do not welcome you."
- Leia hides from life-form sensors using the bake house as a heat mask: "the chief long-range parameter was ... a heat source".
- Thrawn questions Khabarakh. The tech team's report gives a plausible malfunction explanation. Thrawn: "He is indeed [young] ... One reason, I presume, why he’s such a bad liar." Rukh: "He was unsettled ... That much I saw in his hands and his face."
- Thrawn has a decon droid rigged with a Viper motivator and an optical/audio recorder to spy on the village. Khabarakh is reassigned to the Valrar base, Glythe sector.

## Ch. 11 — Peregrine's Nest; Bel Iblis; Ferrier drafted
- Dreadnaughts "can pull ... about Point Four". The trip took 47 hours, which Han reckons as no more than "a hundred fifty light-years from New Cov".
- Peregrine's Nest defenses: Golan Arms anti-infantry batteries, Speizoc anti-vehicle weapons, KAAC Freerunners, a "crystal grav-field trap receptor", and the ranging control for "a KDY v-150 Planet Defender ion cannon".
- **Garm Bel Iblis**: "The Emperor wasn’t quite able to kill me at Anchoron ... He took everything I had except my life". He met Han at age 11. He says of Han's Academy dismissal: "you’d have been quietly eliminated in the Emperor’s purge of those officers he hadn’t been able to seduce to his side."
- Sena Leikvold Midanyl is his chief adviser.
- Thrawn displays holograms of Corellian flame miniatures. The *Relentless* recorded the three Dreadnaughts. Thrawn: "That was a Corellian leading that task force ... I’m sure of that now." He remembers "that scare we gave them at Linuri".
- Ferrier is given "three standard months" to deliver the Dreadnaughts, on an Intelligence freighter carrying "an impressive and totally unbreakable doomsday mechanism". Ferrier delivered "three Sienar patrol ships" to the Empire (so the Amorris tip paid off).
- The scan of Khabarakh's ship finds "Wookiee hairs ... All over the ship." Thrawn deduces capture and interrogation, then calls up a troop shuttle, a double squad of stormtroopers, and two flights of Scimitar assault bombers: "Let them all pray that I don’t change my mind."
