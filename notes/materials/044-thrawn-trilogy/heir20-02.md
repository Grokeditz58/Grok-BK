# Heir 20th Anniversary: "CRISIS OF FAITH", an original Star Wars novella by Timothy Zahn (bm1)

Official, licensed, Del Rey 2011; Legends. Read in full (about 19k words). It is set just before Heir: Thrawn's last campaign in the Unknown Regions before he returns to the Empire.

## Setting and cast
- **Quethold:** the insectoid Quesoth have castes (Queen, Circlings, Midlis, Workers, Soldiers) and three cities (Red, White, Black). Queens die when the next arises: "In two years the Queen of the White would arise, the air would change, and the Queen of the Red would die … Eighteen years later, the Queen of the Black would arise".
- **Nuso Esva** ("Storm-hairs": yellow multifaceted eyes, black hair) has ~30 "Chosen" left. This is the same warlord Thrawn names in Choices of One ("Nusoesva", see tlc-11).
- **Empire of the Hand forces:** ISD *Admonitor* under Senior Captain Voss Parck. Baron Soontir Fel commands the TIE squadrons ("Gray Squadron"). Stormtrooper Commander Balkin and the **501st** ("consist of humans and nonhumans … Including a number of your own people"). General Tasse. Line Lt. Dramos Sanjin. Stromma stormtrooper Lhagva. The Chiss pilot Stent.
- The Stromma (grass-furred allies) have Council Liaison Nyama.

## Scale of Nuso Esva's war
> "Ever since the alien and his people—the warriors he proudly called his 'Chosen'—had emerged from a still-unidentified planet in the Unknown Regions, they'd been cutting a deadly swath through peoples, worlds, and even small federations. Of all those attacked, only Thrawn had shown the skill and resolve necessary to block Nuso Esva's expansion" — Crisis of Faith
> "Millions had died in Nuso Esva's conquests, and millions more in the aftermath of his retreats." — Crisis of Faith
- His fleet remnants are "scattered across probably a million cubic light-years of space". "After ten years of sporadic combat … across the Unknown Regions". The Braccio campaign "nearly destroying half a dozen species". He made one incursion into Imperial space (Candoras sector), where Thrawn worked with Pellaeon. The Chaos Triangle is named.
- Thrawn's coalition is "the Empire of the Hand". Parck: "I can name at least ten such threats right now".

## Thrawn's art-reading as a counter-intel weapon (key)
- Thrawn deliberately asks to see the art in Nuso Esva's chambers. Nuso Esva arranges fake art "to lead our oh-so-clever Grand Admiral to exactly the wrong conclusions" and has the Queen fake a dissident cell to plant the holocam.
- Nuso Esva confirms the ability: "rest assured, Thrawn is able to perform such magic."
- **Thrawn anticipated the double-bluff:**
> "Why else did you think I let him arrange the art in the Dwelling of Guests and then make it sound like I needed to see it? I wanted him to think that he'd manipulated our operation and had it under his control." — Crisis of Faith
- Thrawn: "More important, he understands how I work."
- Narration: "Thrawn's unique ability to read a species' deepest psychological core by studying its artwork … enabling him to anticipate his opponents' moves right down to their likely battlefield tactics."

## Battle of the Red City
- **Quesoth defenses:** umbrella shields over the city center; the Admonitor's turbolasers are "incapable of penetrating the shields". Twin-barreled laser cannons have "enough power to take out one of your vaunted TIE fighters with a single shot". There are ~4,000 Soldiers with maces and swords whose "natural chitinous hides were tough enough to shrug off even a blaster bolt or two unless they were hit squarely in a vital organ".
- **Imperial gear:** juggernauts at "twenty-two meters' worth of weapons and heavy armor … on its ten wheels". The A-rack carrier has a top-mounted E-Web/M and carries a 10-trooper squad. Stormtroopers carry E-11s.
- **Thrawn's minimal force:** 3 of 6 TIE squadrons and 3 stormtrooper squads (36 troopers vs. hundreds) out of ">three thousand troopers available". Parck wonders if "this become personal?" Thrawn: "Patience, Captain."
- **The deception:** the juggernauts are droid-driven with no weapons ("The juggernauts have no crews … nothing but droids … there are no weapons"). Thrawn uses them as bait to pull 3,500 Soldiers away from the palace.
- **Fel's wing-eject TIE:** a fake-fire gimmicked wing, then explosive bolts eject both wings and he "slid the cockpit section of his TIE sideways through the flytrap opening". He destroys the cannon, then the shield generators and 13 of the 14 Soldier-Speak loudspeakers. "Impossible is Gray Squadron's specialty."
- **Queen's voice as a weapon:** Thrawn replays a recording of the Queen's own order captured by the holocam ("Go through the Dwelling doors. Surround and protect the Guests"). All 4,000 Soldiers march on Nuso Esva. Nuso Esva shoots the Queen and his Chosen are overwhelmed.
- **Thrawn's ethics:** "We do not make war on civilians, Liaison Nyama"; "our job as civilized beings is to minimize that suffering".
- **Ending (ties into Heir):** Thrawn turns to "the restoration of the Empire". The Empire has "shrunk to barely a quarter of the size it had attained under Palpatine's rule". He will contact "Captain Gilad Pellaeon … ISD Chimaera" and arranges "a bodyguard" (foreshadowing the Noghri and Rukh). Nuso Esva's dying threat: "with my death, his own will not be far off. My followers … will find him."
  - Thrawn: "It's also the beginning. The beginning of victory."
