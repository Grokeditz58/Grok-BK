# The Last Command: notes part 2, ch. 2–4 (and ch. 5 opening)

## Ch. 2 (Calius saj Leeloo, Berchest) and ch. 3 (Mara wakes; Thrawn/C'baoth plan)
- Calius is "a single gigantic crystal" city grown from salt spray.
- **Luke's clone-sensing:** he scans crowds for "the strange aura he'd detected in the boarding party of clones". He picks Karrde's "coolly calculating mind" out of a "torrent of human and alien thoughts". He locates the clones at "Half a kilometer away" and estimates "twenty to thirty of them" inside a closed cargo carrier.
- **Clone growth-time scaling:**
> (ch. 2) "Clones can only be grown so quickly if you want them mentally stable enough to trust with your warships. One year minimum per clone, as I recall the old rule of thumb." … "The pre-Clone Wars documents I've seen suggest three to five years would be a more appropriate period." — Karrde, ch. 2
> "The full cycle was fifteen to twenty days." — Luke, ch. 2, on the Katana clones
- Ukio is "one of the top five producers of foodstuffs in the entire New Republic". Karrde: "A knack for doing the impossible was one of the things Grand Admirals were selected for."
- The Empire holds "most of the two hundred Dreadnaughts". Winter later puts it at "nearly a hundred and eighty".
- The bounty on Karrde is "nearly fifty thousand". Fingal is an Intelligence watchdog on Governor Staffa. Thrawn's directive is to let Luke leave unhindered, since Calius is a decoy conduit.
- **Ch. 3, Mara:** she wakes after "most of a month" of neural reconstruction plus bacta, and recalls "YOU WILL KILL LUKE SKYWALKER." "Had watched through his mind as Luke Skywalker cut him down" (ANALYSIS: Mara on Coruscant experienced the Emperor's death across the galaxy through their link).
- Ghent cleared Ackbar "two days after I got here": the bank deposit was an Imperial Intelligence plant. Delta Source is "sending them stuff right out of the Palace".
- Winter = "Targeter. The one with the perfect memory". She grew up with Leia on Alderaan.
- War status: "In the past five days we've effectively lost control of four sectors, and thirteen more are on the edge." Rumor of a superweapon "able to fire straight through the Ukians' planetary shield".
- Mara's hidden blaster is in the fake "Complete History of Corvis Minor" card box. Her forearm holster is noted.
- **Thrawn vs. the Emperor:**
> "On no fewer than four occasions I told the Emperor that I would not waste his troops and ships attacking an enemy which I was not yet prepared to defeat." … "The first time I refused he called me a traitor and gave my attack force to someone else. After its destruction, he knew better than to ignore my recommendations." — Thrawn, ch. 3
- No frontal assault on Coruscant: "We don't have the numbers for a frontal assault on Coruscant; neither have we the supply lines and bases necessary for a traditional siege."
- C'baoth: "I rule the Empire! … You will obey me, or you will die!" Pellaeon: the clone-madness bursts are "becoming more frequent and more vicious. Like a system in a positive feedback loop".
- Thrawn suspects the Noghri: "There's something wrong with the Noghri … I can sense it with every communication I have with the dynasts". The Palace has an Emperor's "private way in and out". The plan is an Intelligence team to kidnap Leia and neutralize Mara, who might know about Wayland: "I knew many of the Emperor's secrets, and it still took me a great deal of effort to find Wayland."
- Thrawn's plan for C'baoth: "we'll have come to a suitable arrangement with our Jedi Master over the sharing of power … " (Pellaeon reads this as: or he would have the Jedi Master killed).

(Chapter numbering: epub SEC c02 = ch. 2, Calius; SEC c03 = ch. 3, Mara/Winter/Thrawn-C'baoth. The bullets above cover both.)

## Ch. 4: the Poderis trap
- Poderis has a "ten-hour rotational cycle" and wind shield-barriers "Built to withstand two-hundred-kilometer winds".
- Luke senses the ysalamiri as "small pockets of darkness where his Jedi senses could read absolutely nothing".
- **Feats:**
  - A Force-assisted jump to a two-story parapet ("He almost didn't make it").
  - A TK blaster pluck and toss, then firing it remotely: "Focusing the Force with all the control he could manage, Luke turned the blaster back toward its former owner and fired. Fired safely over the crowd … there was no possible way for him to aim accurately enough to hit the Imperial."
  - A lightsaber-groove slide down a ~100 m wind barrier, cutting a path for "Over a hundred meters".
- Thrawn: "I've warned Intelligence repeatedly about underestimating the range of Skywalker's sensing abilities."
- Luke senses the ISD below the horizon before Artoo does: "He got it an instant before Artoo's startled warning shrill". He "sensed the locking of the tractor beam a half second before it actually occurred."
- The covert shroud gambit is a freighter self-destructing into "trac-reflective particles" plus an X-wing launched by a deck-mounted blast-booster. Ensign Mithel is promoted rather than executed. Pellaeon: "the old Empire was gone. The new Empire, with Grand Admiral Thrawn at its head, had been born."
- "The late Lord Vader would have summarily strangled the man."
- Luke diverts to Honoghr for fuel.

## Ch. 5 (opening): Council, birth of the twins
- Ackbar on shield penetration: "focusing nonvisible energy against a shield … weaken a section long enough to fire a turbolaser blast through the opening. But such a thing would take a tremendous power output."
- Bel Iblis came to the Katana battle with "six Dreadnaughts". He is now posted to the outer sectors.
- Leia uses Luke's technique to block labor pain with the Force over 10+ hours. **Twins bond in the Force:** "The twins, growing together in the Force even as they'd grown together within her, had somehow become attuned to each other". One twin's calming spread to the other. Jaina is born first.
