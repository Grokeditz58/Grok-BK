## Context

This blog (September 1st) follows Master Azronger's addendum in Material 019 (August 23rd). That addendum claimed Krayt "was maintaining telepathic contact with 'millions' of his Sith Troopers… more of an active than passive effort". It added that before War his mind was open only to his "closest disciples", and that Cade and Stryfe felt him leave their heads after Had Abaddon. The "long-standing proposals" Ant rebuts here are those claims, so 018 is effectively Ant's answer to Azronger.

## Thesis

"A very short blog reviewing my alternate take on the Sith Troopers."
- Krayt took Force-strong children, enhanced them cybernetically and conditioned them to unquestionable loyalty.
- They are Force-linked to him, but the link "(demonstrably) isn't strong enough for Krayt to commune with them directly".
- Their suicidal madness after his death is conditioned devotion.
- The popular idea that Krayt is "pouring vast amounts of his power into controlling everything the Sith Troopers do" is "a fan-fiction theory… never said and never implied".

## Arguments (numbered)

1. **Origins:** "Krayt took the most potentially powerful One Sith as children, cybernetically enhanced them, and conditioned them to have unquestionable loyalty to him." (Scan dead.)
2. **Self-description:** the troopers say they are "linked through the dark side" and that Krayt's will is their will. (Scans dead.)
3. **Post-death behaviour:** after Krayt dies they move to suicide and attack Jedi and Sith alike. K'Kruhk: "appear to have gone mad!" (Scan dead.)
4. **Counter-evidence to telepathic command:** Krayt orders Nihl to order the attack instead of commanding the troopers directly. (Scan dead.)
5. **Against "telepathic domination":** loyalty culled "from birth" means they would willingly embrace a Force link; domination isn't needed.
6. **Against "Krayt linked to them only later"** (based on the Legacy #18 line, scan dead):
   - (1) the troopers could be Force-linked without being "open to his mind";
   - (2) the line comes while Krayt tells his backstory with other One Sith present, and the troopers are a **private** project;
   - (3) the troopers could count among his "closest servants";
   - (4) under Lucasfilm Licensing rules, a statement cannot cover something introduced after its publication.
7. **Against "at least a telepathic link":** this is called "unfounded speculation".
   - The Nihl relay is counter-evidence ("perhaps Krayt was very lazy that day, etc. But it's still a point against").
   - Force bonds vary in strength, and only the strongest show the effects claimed.
   - Yoda is "linked through the Force with all his former pupils" but never talks to or empowers them across the galaxy.
8. **Against "madness proves a deep link":** real-world devotion explains it without "Force hijinks".
9. **Alternative proposal:** troopers taken from birth; a link that isn't strong enough for direct communion; conditioned loyalty that drives them to join Krayt in death.

## Common rebuttals / weak points (ANALYSIS — not from the thread)

- **Point 6(4), the Licensing rule, is the weakest.** It is a meta argument about publication order, and it cuts against Ant's own use of later material elsewhere. The in-universe reading (2) is enough on its own.
- **Point 6(2) is plausible but untestable.** Who is present at the #18 telling is not established in our read of 013.
- **Nihl's relay at p75 is a weak counter.** Krayt is a hologram on Nihl's bridge, and Nihl is the fleet commander; chain of command explains it. Ant concedes the "lazy" or ceremonial readings himself.
- **A minimal link is undercut by later material Azronger posted in 019 (B070/B071, unread issues, scan-sighted):**
  - Darth Stryfe: "I CANNOT FEEL THE DARK LORD IN MY MIND ANYMORE -- AND KRAYT IS ALWAYS PRESENT IN MY MIND!"
  - Cade: "DIDN'T FEEL HIM ITCHING IN MY MIND ANYMORE."
  - So Krayt's mental presence in other One Sith is persistent. That weakens "never said and never implied" as a general claim, though neither line is about troopers.
- **Ant's strongest point:** neither War nor anything we have says Krayt *pilots* or *powers* the troopers. "Linked through the Dark Side" plus conditioned loyalty explains every on-page behaviour, including suicide runs (K'Kruhk: "MAY ATTEMPT TO FOLLOW HIM INTO THE ABYSS").

## New knowledge

- **Legacy #50 (unread) is the origin source for the troopers.** Per Wookieepedia: "culling those strongest in the Force shortly after their births", plus a trooper killing himself on Krayt's order. Adding #50 to the reading list would settle Ant's point 1 and the private-project argument (6(2)).
- **The #18 wording is "closest disciples", not "servants"** (013 p105). This matters for 6(3).
- Nihl ordering "DESTROY ALL SITH TROOPERS!" (p127) shows the One Sith themselves treated the post-death troopers as uncontrolled.
