# Young Jedi Knights 13: Trouble on Cloud City: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-20 CST. Every line of READ l.1–1449 was read in order: the front matter, the whole story (ch. 1–21) and the back matter. Notes are limited to scaling-relevant material (owner's rulings, 2026-11-20). The quotes are in tc-01 and tc-02; this summary is in our own words.
- Authors: Kevin J. Anderson & Rebecca Moesta. Thirteenth Young Jedi Knights book (Boulevard Books, 1998), second of the 'Under a Black Sun' arc. Cites are TC ch. N, p.X (printed pages).

## Plot
- **Yavin 4 (ch. 1–3):** Anja Gallandro has been at the academy for weeks, coached with her lightsaber by Jacen and Zekk; Luke senses no Jedi potential in her. Lando invites the young Jedi Knights (and Anja) to test SkyCenter Galleria, the amusement complex he is building on Cloud City with his old friend Cojahn. On arrival they learn Cojahn fell from a balcony, ruled a suicide.
- **Investigation (ch. 4–9):** Lando refuses to believe it. Lando spots Anja's andris-spice addiction; confronted, she storms out and reports the investigation to Czethros. Czethros, running his rebuilt arm of Black Sun from Ord Mantell behind a respectable shipping business, orders the young Jedi killed. Lando, Jaina and Zekk go to Clak'dor VII after the fled Bith band Figrin D'an and the Modal Nodes.
- **Port Town and the fall (ch. 10–14):** Jacen and Tenel Ka save Anja from a fall in the Climateria tree. Jacen, Tenel Ka and Lowie trail the fired Ugnaught foreman into a hired-killer ambush, flee through Port Town, and are dumped out of a garbage chute. Lowie catches an antenna and Tenel Ka, but Jacen falls into Bespin's clouds. Lowie claws Tenel Ka's arm to hold her and hauls them up. Anja, believing Jacen dead, realizes she caused it. The thranta rider M'kim catches the falling Jacen.
- **The truth (ch. 12, 15–16):** Figrin tells how Black Sun pressured Cojahn and the band. M'kim tells Jacen he saw Czethros throw Cojahn off the balcony. Lowie slices Cloud City's doctored records and finds Czethros on a casino holocam. Czethros tells Anja his coup will soon begin and his killers will finish the job.
- **Ambushes and escape (ch. 17–20):** Turncoat Wing Guards and chameleon creatures ambush Lando's group; Jaina brings down the hovercoaster on the chameleons and Lando traps the guards in the vortex chute. Jacen, Tenel Ka and Lowie flee in cloud cars; the thranta riders capture Lowie's pursuers, and Jacen turns a velker pack onto theirs while Tenel Ka cuts down two velkers. They watch M'kim join the sky rodeo. Czethros vanishes.
- **Ending (ch. 21):** On Yavin 4 Anja's spice is almost gone and Czethros is unreachable; she begins to doubt that Han killed her father.

## Strongest scaling findings
- **Lowbacca:** the book's main strength showing. He hauls himself across a chain hand over hand, catches an antenna one-handed while falling and snatches Tenel Ka 'With lightning reflexes', digs in his claws to hold her, then pulls them both up 'as if sitting up and lifting weights simultaneously'. Later he makes the grappling throw one-handed while holding her, and slices doctored city records.
- **Tenel Ka:** scales the Great Temple single-armed, then runs ten kilometers. She deflects blaster fire on foot and kneeling on a speeding cloud car, and slashes open two velkers. She steadies herself with the Force while hanging by one arm. Limitations: her arm shakes from climbing, she blacks out from blood loss, and she does not sense through the Force that Jacen is alive.
- **Jacen:** his Force cannot stop his fall: he can lighten himself but not rise, and his mental call does not reach anyone. Velkers resist his animal empathy, so he redirects them onto his pursuers instead. Statement: 'never underestimate a Jedi'.
- **Jaina:** lets the Force guide a jungle run. Her lightsaber slices a durasteel support pillar 'as easily as if it were a hot knife', bringing down the hovercoaster on the massed chameleons. She tackles Lando out of a blaster volley and deflects a bolt.
- **Zekk:** parries Anja's wild attacks easily; moves fast enough to drop out of the opening volley.
- **Anja Gallandro:** a muscular, fast, reckless fighter with no Force potential (Luke). Andris spice drives her energy and is her clear limitation: shaking hands, blurred vision, lost minutes. She fumbles her lightsaber under fire and falls from a branch. She claims her wits, intuition and reflexes make her as good as a Jedi.
- **Czethros:** Black Sun lieutenant and former hired killer and bounty hunter. He picks Cojahn up by the collar and throws him off a balcony. His infrared visor reads hidden text. He plans a simultaneous coup across Bespin, Kessel, Mon Calamari, Ord Mantell, Borgo Prime and Coruscant. He vanishes without a trace.
