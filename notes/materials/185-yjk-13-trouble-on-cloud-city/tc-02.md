- Read 2026-11-20 CST, every line in order. This part covers READ l.751–1449.
- Scope (owner's rulings, 2026-11-20): every line was read, but only scaling-relevant material is noted. That means feats of every kind (Force, lightsaber, strength, durability, speed, reflexes, agility, stamina, pain tolerance, marksmanship, hand-to-hand, tech and tactics, senses), skills, statements about characters, narrative portrayal, and anti-feats and limitations. Ordinary non-Force piloting is not noted (the cloud-car escapes and chases in ch. 17–19, Lando's flying). Each chapter keeps a short Plot bullet (our words) so the story can be followed.
- Citation: "TC ch. N, p.X" uses the printed page numbers of the 1998 Boulevard paperback (the epub's page anchors match the printed pages; checked against the scan). ## Ch. 12 (READ l.751–805) — Figrin's story
- Plot: Figrin D'an tells how Black Sun, gone underground with respectable-looking lieutenants, wanted to run SkyCenter Galleria; Cojahn refused, reported the threats (the complaints were lost), and fired his Ugnaught crew boss for being in with Black Sun, then 'took his little dive'. The band fled when Black Sun demanded triple Guild dues and put stinger eels in their instruments.
- BLACK SUN STATEMENT (Figrin: Black Sun lieutenants act respectable but run weapons, illegal spice, and now gambling; 'Those bullies could stick your head in a carbon-freezing tube, or drop you out an exhaust shaft'): ""It's all about Black Sun," Figrin said. "They've gone underground for many years, but they've got a cover story now. Black Sun lieutenants act respectable, but when nobody's looking, they set up their old criminal connections, just like Prince Xizor used to do, and Durga the Hutt, and all the other deposed kingpins. Black Sun has its clutches on weapons runners, illegal spice trade, and now the gambling and entertainment industries.''" (TC ch. 12, p.102) ""Cojahn did his best, man, but Black Sun infiltrators popped up everywhere. You never knew who they were, or when they might come after you in a dark corridor down in Port Town. Got so you had to have a Wing Guard escort to take you to the gambling tables and back again. Those bullies could stick your head in a carbon-freezing tube, or drop you out an exhaust shaft. They meant business."" (TC ch. 12, p.104)
- Plot (cont.): Lando vows someone in Black Sun will pay. The band holds its nightly jam with the swamp's own creatures before Lando, Jaina and Zekk leave.
- JAINA SENSE (senses that Figrin's companions have little confidence in his gambling): "The band's melody turned sour and skeptical at these words, and Jaina sensed that Figrin's companions didn't have much confidence in their leader's gambling prowess." (TC ch. 12, p.107)

## Ch. 14 (READ l.869–921) — Jacen falls; M'kim
- Plot: Jacen falls from Cloud City into the gas giant, where the pressure would crush him long before any surface. (TC ch. 14, p.117) "The clouds swirled below, streaming in spirals like a whirlpool far, far beneath him. With each instant he fell closer and closer to oblivion." (TC ch. 14, p.118) "In his mind he tried to call out to his sister Jaina or to Tenel Ka, but he couldn't seem to make contact. In any case, there was nothing they could do ... at least, not in time."
