Line refs ("l.") point to the extracted text kept outside the repo (`FULL.txt`). Legends continuity. ## Ch. THREE (l.1275): ground assault; Ahsoka's best feats

  - She "leapt to meet the droid on its STAP and slashed both machines in half with a single swiping blow" (l.1512).
