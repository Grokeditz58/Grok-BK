# Clone Wars Gambit: Stealth: summary of strongest findings

1. **Temple leap records (explicit ranking), ch. SIXTEEN.** Anakin's record "had smashed Mace Windu's leap by nearly fifteen meters" (l.9720). Obi-Wan's leap "was only a meter and a half behind Master Windu's" (l.9781). So Anakin > Mace > Obi-Wan in Force-jumping; Anakin set the record "just over a year ago".
2. **Obi-Wan's lethal-telekinesis statement**, ch. SIXTEEN: "Without laying a finger on her I could crush her throat or her skull or burst her heart in her chest. I could kill her ten times over without breaking a sweat." (l.9368)
3. **The breakout vs. a droid army**, ch. TWENTY. Anakin "sank deeper into the Force than he'd ever gone before" (l.12624). "Even the Chosen One had his limits" (l.12667). His final push makes "the first two rows of clankers" tumble "like leaves in a storm" (l.12726–12729). He flies a hotwired groundcar over the fence.
4. **Ahsoka's feats and presence rankings**, ch. THREE. She pulls "half a high stone wall down" on four shielded droidekas (l.1521) and halves two machines with one blow (l.1512). She says Obi-Wan lights up the Force "like a bonfire… Almost as bright as… Master Skywalker" (l.1791).

5. **Palpatine knows about Padmé**, ch. SIX. "Senator Amidala. Padmé… You miss her, Anakin… I can feel it." "You can hide from the Jedi—but you can't hide from me." "There is nothing you could tell me that would change my feelings for you." (l.3639–3661)
6. **Durd's anti-detection device from Dooku**, ch. NINETEEN. "I have protection from Count Dooku, a guarantee that those…" / "I took it off… It hurts my skin." (l.11778–11782). This explains the earlier "strange slipperiness" around Durd that is gone once he stops wearing it: "No more strange slipperiness about him." (l.8468)
7. **The Samsam murder and Bant'ena's betrayal**, ch. NINETEEN. Durd has Bant'ena's friend Samsam shot on holo over Lake Radu (l.11699–11730), and she gives the Jedi up: "So either they lied or they failed… I will save who I can." (l.11856–11858)
8. **The Anakin/Obi-Wan argument about Palpatine and the Senate**, ch. NINETEEN: "If he had more control over them he could fix what's wrong." Obi-Wan: "I have compassion. I have empathy. What I don't have are scars." (l.12034–12068)
9. **Yoda orders a lie**, ch. TWENTY: "Then lie to her you must, Obi-Wan. Tell her everyone is safe." (l.12278). Anakin: "We are Jedi, and Jedi don't lie." (l.12323) and then thinks "My whole life is a lie." (l.12375–12376)
10. **Taria Damsin's illness is named**, ch. TWENTY: "Borotavi syndrome. It's not contagious but it is terminal." (l.12135–12141). Earlier, in the Mata rescue with Ahsoka, she bleeds from her nose and eyes (l.11599–11605).

Also notable:
- Anakin Force-extracts eight transparisteel splinters (l.1994–2013).
- The two Jedi fuse wrist microchips via the Force (l.7227) and hide in the Force (l.6174, l.7206, l.7312).
- Lore: Anakin's machine "quirk" explains why his prosthetic doesn't weaken his Force connection (l.7907).
- Yoda: "not in nine hundred years, had his abilities been so compromised" (l.10489); "Burn in the Force's light always you have" to Obi-Wan (l.6139).
- Taria Damsin (Master, terminally ill) and Ahsoka defeat an untrained Force-sensitive Anzati (l.11564–11585).
- Zigoola's lasting damage to Obi-Wan's foresight continues from 068 (l.1130).
