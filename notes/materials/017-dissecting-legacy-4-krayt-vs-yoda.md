## Thesis

Midi-chlorians set Force potential, which is "almost 1:1 linked" and "deeply George Lucas-endorsed". Yoda's count ("approx. 20,000") is second only to Anakin's. A'Sharad Hett would certainly have been tested, so his count must be below Yoda's; otherwise he would have been a Chosen One candidate. Yoda had 800+ years to realise his potential; Krayt is "a fourth-rate Jedi prodigy". So "Darth Krayt should be **bound **beneath Yoda -- **especially **the Krayt that fights Abeloth in Fate of the Jedi: Apocalypse." (emphasis as posted)

1. **Anakin > Yoda in midi-chlorians.** Sources cited:

"No one" exceeding 20,000 shows that "all Jedi are tested". Hett's career was "relatively unexceptional", and he was not on the Council by 38.

## Common rebuttals / weak points (ANALYSIS — not from the thread)

- **The core inference is an argument from absence.** No source gives Hett's count or says he was tested. "Must be less than Yoda's" relies on (a) universal testing and (b) the Order reacting in a certain way. Neither is shown for Hett.
- **Midi-chlorians as a 1:1 ceiling is contested even in Legends.** Ant concedes "exceptions exist"; potential ≠ realised power. Krayt's later growth (Vong shaping, the Embrace, death and rebirth) is exactly the kind of non-standard route a count would not capture.
- **The "fourth-rate Jedi prodigy" label is rhetoric.**
- **Ant's best point is the simplest:** Dark Transfer does not visibly reverse aging (War Krayt is not younger), so the "power burning out his body" theory is unnecessary.
- **Ant is right on the arm.** 013 p101: "MY CYBERNETIC HAND HAD BEEN REPLACED WITH SOMETHING ORGANIC". The Vong replacement was already organic, so War's human-looking arm need not be a regrowth.
- **"Undying" is Azronger's word, not page text.** The nearest is Cade's report, "HIS BODY WILL HEAL AND LIFE WILL RETURN" (p128), which is Krayt's voice in Cade's head. Azronger's "it's a fact that he could regrow an entire missing limb" and "was going to… resurrect it" overstate a claim into a fact.
- **"Anorexic and shriveled" body restored:** this matches the p4 montage (Krayt's body in the stasis column, captions "I REMEMBER HEALING MYSELF WITH THE FORCE, GRADUALLY BECOMING STRONGER…"). The words are Azronger's description of the art.

## New knowledge

- **Arm point settled from our own material.** Krayt's hand had already been replaced with "something organic" by the Vong (013 p101). Arm-regrowth claims for Dark Transfer should be dropped.
- **War p130 names *both* Andeddu and Karness Muur** as Krayt's teachers of transfer essence. That is worth stating precisely in debates.
- **Proposal worth considering:** "Dark Transfer heals injury but not age" (Ant). It is simple and consistent with War's art.
