**Redeems-his-father attribution conflict (do not collapse).** The line “when Luke saves the galaxy and redeems his father, that’s where that story ends” appears in Norwich (2013) as a report of a January 2012 *New York Times* interview (notes 165, pp. 107–108; certified crops `q04-p107-retiring-after-jedi.jpg` and `q05-p108-redeems-his-father.jpg`). The same wording is cited by Brian Jay Jones (2016) to Geoff Boucher, “George Lucas: ‘Star Wars’ Won’t Go beyond Darth Vader,” *Los Angeles Times,* May 7, 2008 (notes 166, Jones p. 438; Jones Notes, ch. 13 n.11). This addendum records both attributions and does not decide which is correct. Lucas may have reused the line. Check the originals before collapsing them.

A related production-history conflict, also not collapsed: Smith (162, pp. 102–103) says the *Empire* shooting draft had “Obi-Wan is your father.” Jones (166) says Prowse, on set, delivered “Obi-Wan killed your father.” Those are different claims (draft line vs. on-set delivery).
