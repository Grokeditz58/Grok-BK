Continuity: 19 BBY, several months after Jedi Twilight (l.275). It opens with Padmé's funeral, where the autopsy says she was choked through the Force (l.193–201). Captain Typho comes to Coruscant to find Anakin. Jax, Laranth, I-Five and Den take a murder case from the Zeltron Dejah Duare after her partner, the Caamasi light sculptor Ves Volette, is stabbed. Vader sends Aurra Sing after Jax. Jax learns that a Sith (the Zabrak with the red double blade) killed his father (l.2699–2707). I-Five hands him the last bota extract (l.2761–2779). The killer turns out to be the Umbers' protocol droid (l.3321–3347). Typho, wrongly believing Vader killed Anakin and Padmé, sells Jax to Vader and sends Jax Sing's stolen lightsaber. Vader kills Typho ("Yes, I killed Anakin Skywalker", l.3427). Jax beats Sing at Spaceport Nine by dropping her into a sand hopper (l.3611–3625). Laranth is badly wounded and leaves the group; Dejah joins the resistance; Rhinann plots to use the bota against the Emperor (l.3723–3731).

Character quick bios:
- **Jax Pavan:**
  - Former Jedi Knight with no lightsaber at the start; he uses a cortosis vibrosword, then a Velmorian energy sword (l.251, 325–351).
  - Reflex-blocks I-Five's surprise laser (l.453–455). Feels Caamas die (l.475–481) and senses that Anakin is alive (l.493–495). Beats the Cathar Sele (l.2611–2625).
  - Learns a Sith killed his father (l.2707). Beats Aurra Sing with a crimson saber (l.3529–3625).
- **Darth Vader:** burns out Sing's saber crystal through the Force (l.907–913). Swats Sing aside unarmed (l.1773–1787). His Force probe is blocked by a taozin nodule (l.3281). Kills Typho (l.3393–3427).
- **Aurra Sing:** ex-Padawan bounty hunter with a biocomputer implant (l.519, 535). Vader commutes her sentence to hunt Jax (l.561). Fastest fighter Jax has met (l.3557). Falls into a sand hopper (l.3623); her fate is left open.
- **Captain Typho:** Padmé's one-eyed security chief (l.161, 177, 1123). Takes Sing's saber (l.1107–1109). Tricks Vader with taozin (l.3149–3167, 3281). Choked by Vader (l.3421–3435).
- **Laranth Tarak:** Gray Paladin. Blocks bolt on bolt (l.253, 2869), with aim "on the same skill level as that of someone taught in the Temple" (l.2937). Senses Sing over a klick away (l.2973). Nearly loses her arm and leaves the group (l.3649–3655).
- **I-Five:** self-aware droid of Lorn Pavan. Melts his restraint plug (l.1235–1245) and carried the last bota (l.2761–2779).
- **Den Dhur:** Sullustan ex-reporter. Builds a lightsaber kit (l.1841–1857) and finds the fence Spa Fon (l.2481–2517, 2633–2641).
- **Dejah Duare:** Zeltron, Volette's partner. Her pheromones strain Jax's Force shield (l.2249, 3051). Joins the resistance (l.3633–3641).
- **Pol Haus:** Zabrak sector police prefect (l.159, 783–785): "Round up the usual species" (l.3369).
- **Haninum Tyk Rhinann:** Elomin defector. Researches Lorn's death and plots with the bota (l.1041–1051, 2661–2707, 3723–3731).
- **Baron Vlaçan and Baroness Kirma Umber:** Vindalian patrons. Their 3PO droid is the killer (l.1429–1437, 3249–3347).
