## Plot

**Padmé's funeral and Typho (Prologue, ch. 9, 13, 16).** Captain Typho blames himself for Padmé's death. The autopsy says she was choked through the Force (l.193–201), and he resolves to go to Coruscant, find Anakin and learn the truth (l.203–211). He comes to Coruscant and captures Aurra Sing using a flash trick, taking her lightsaber (l.1107–1131). Imperial records list Jax Pavan "possibly on Imperial Center" (l.1957–1967). Records also put Sidious on Mustafar with Padmé and Anakin (l.2393). From this Typho wrongly concludes that Vader killed both of them, so Vader must die (l.2407–2411).

**The Volette murder (ch. 1–11, 15, 17–18, 23, 25–26).** Jax, Laranth, I-Five and Den are low on money. They take a case from the Zeltron Dejah Duare. Her partner, the Caamasi light sculptor Ves Volette, has been stabbed in his studio, and Prefect Pol Haus is investigating (l.735–965). Caamas was destroyed recently and the official story blames actinium bombs (l.371–401). The leads are:
- the Cephalon's cryptic warning (l.969–1001, 1159–1177)
- the patrons Baron and Baroness Umber, and the compressed energy crystals (CECs) at the heart of the Volette sculptures (l.1409–1688)
- the fence Spa Fon, reached after Jax duels the Cathar Sele (l.2529–2656)
- a Drall witness and Sele's tip that an Umber skimmer was seen near the studio (l.3097–3119)

When Jax confronts her, Kirma admits she argued with Ves but says "I left him alive" (l.3267). Their protocol droid confesses: "I did" (l.3271). It stabbed him with its data spike out of loyalty to the family, then wipes its own neural net (l.3321–3361). The clue was that "Mechanicals were notoriously hard to read through the Force" (l.3355).

**Sing hunts Jax (ch. 5–6, 8, 12, 14, 22, 28–29).** Vader commutes Sing's sentence and sends her after Jax (l.497–573). He burns out her lightsaber crystal through the Force and puts her down unarmed (l.903–913, 1773–1787). Sing:
- butchers three drunks in a bar (l.2107–2119)
- fights Laranth at a Holo House and follows her (l.2893–2957)
- ambushes the group at Spaceport Nine (l.3501–3525)

**Jax's past (ch. 19–20).** Rhinann's research shows Jax's parents resisted giving him to the Temple (l.2669–2675). Lorn Pavan was killed by a Zabrak with a red double-bladed lightsaber (l.2687–2707). I-Five gives Jax the last bota extract, which came from Barriss Offee; it can link a user "to all beings, all places, throughout all times" (l.2761–2793).

**Typho vs Vader (ch. 21, 24, 26–27).** Typho meets Jax at a Whiplash meeting (l.2809–2865). He hides his mind from Vader with a taozin nodule (l.3149–3167, 3281). He offers Jax for 5 million credits and sends Jax Sing's red saber (l.3299–3313, 3443–3453). At a condemned hangar Vader hurls him aside and chokes him: "Yes, I killed Anakin Skywalker… He was weak" (l.3389–3435).

**Climax (ch. 29–30).** Jax duels Sing with the crimson blade. He tires, then baits her into a sand hopper (l.3527–3625). Laranth is maimed and leaves the group, after a Force-link reveals her feelings (l.3649–3675). Dejah stays to back the resistance (l.3633–3641). Rhinann: "The droid was the key" (l.3723–3731).

## Strongest feats (power-scaling)

1. **Vader kills Typho (l.3389–3427).** An invisible hand throws Typho "with bone-breaking force" against the far wall. Vader levitates up through a 6 m hole (l.3393). "The flare in the Force… was enough to shake the foundations of the building" (l.3413). Then a Force choke (l.3421–3427).
2. **Jax beats Aurra Sing (l.3529–3625).** He blocks her strike to save Laranth (l.3535). She is "faster than anyone he had ever encountered", and only the Force let him anticipate her (l.3557). He survives a long duel, then Force-leaps, senses where she lands on the safety field, and shuts it off, dropping her into the sand hopper (l.3611–3625).
3. **Vader vs Sing, unarmed (l.1773–1787).** He blocks her Force-aided punch and throws her across the room (l.1775). He ducks her double kick and taps her spine (l.1779–1781). Her sweep is like "trying to cut down a bronzewood tree", and a Force surge pins her while guards in the hall are nearly knocked off their feet (l.1783–1787). He never draws his saber (l.1777). He also burns out her saber crystal through the Force (l.907–913).

Other feats:
- **Sing:** three kills in "two blurs" without spilling her drink (l.2107–2119). Force-leaps up through two floors (l.2953). Her acceleration would be hard for a droid to match (l.1783).
- **Laranth:**
  - Four bolt-on-bolt blocks against a training droid, with the fifth shot hitting its photoreceptors (l.2869).
  - Gets a bolt past Sing's saber with Temple-level aim, and lands a punch (l.2937–2945).
  - Senses Sing from more than a klick away (l.2973).
- **Jax:**
  - Force-hurls three troopers into a wall (l.251–255).
  - Reflex-blocks I-Five's surprise laser (l.453–455) and feels Caamas die (l.475–481).
  - Wears down the Cathar champion Sele with a flamesword (l.2611–2625).
  - With Laranth, senses Vader inside a ten-square-kilometre radius (l.3463–3479).
  - Gets advance warning of Sing's ambush through the Force (l.3515–3517).

## Anti-feats

- Jax:
  - Needs Force help to shield himself from Dejah's pheromones (l.2249, 3051).
  - Fails to probe the Baroness (l.1665).
  - Sing cuts through his boot and heel (l.3559).
  - He is nearly spent, "the Force was all that was holding him up" (l.3573).
- Sing:
  - Falls to Typho's flash trick (l.1107–1131).
  - Is hit by Laranth (l.2937–2945).
  - Is reckless, since her passion is hunting Jedi, not fighting them (l.3571), and her stamina fades fast (l.3593).
  - Vader: "you will never master [the Force]" (l.1795).
- Vader's probe is blocked by the taozin nodule (l.3281, 3301). He is wounded emotionally by Padmé's name (l.3429–3435).

## Lore highlights

- The Caamas destruction and the cover story about actinium bombs (l.371–401).
- Padmé was choked through the Force (l.193–201).
- Lorn Pavan was killed by the red double-bladed Zabrak (l.2687–2707). Sith blades have been red since Bane (l.2705).
- Bota is an adaptogen and touches the cosmic Force (l.2767–2791).
- Taozin ("Force dragon") skin hides a mind from the Force (l.3149–3167).
- Whiplash is linking with groups on other worlds, the "first stirrings" of organized rebellion (l.2821–2829).
- Volette sculptures run on compressed energy crystals (l.1565–1581).
- Jax senses Anakin is alive (l.493–495).

## Atrocities

- Sing butchers three drunks in a bar (l.2107–2119).
- The Umbers' droid murders Volette (l.3321–3347).
- Vader kills Typho (l.3421–3435).
