## Ch. 1 (l.204–414)
- Kashyyyk is burning; the Solos plant firebreak beacons for a Confederation cruiser, the *Lillibanca* (l.209–259).
- FEAT (Alema): Force-pulls Han's blaster with a left arm that was ruined years ago and is now "fine" (l.271–273). Han kills the inertial compensator and stands the *Falcon* on her tail (l.283–287).
- Leia draws on the Force to stand under several g (l.297). Waroo's strength (l.295, 307). Alema walks on the ceiling "as though gravity didn't matter" (l.331, 375, 411). Her half-missing foot is whole and her face ageless: "We are simply ageless and eternal now" (l.377–381).
- ANTI-FEAT (Leia): her Force wave only rocks Alema (l.389). Leia slows Waroo's fall with the Force (l.393). Alema's first strike is clumsy, then "as swift and ferocious as any Jedi's" (l.395, 401). Leia fights defensively (l.407). Alema fades and vanishes from a sealed ship (l.409–413). Leia felt only Waroo where Alema was (l.325, 658).

## Ch. 2 (l.415–586)
- Caedus is briefed: the Hapan withdrawal costs 20% of naval strength (l.428). He recalls the duel with Luke, "brilliant demonstrations ... of raw power within the Force"; Ben's vibroblade went through a shoulder blade and nearly reached his heart (l.444–450). His wounds: kidney, scalp (l.452). He "battered his way out" of the Kashyyyk encirclement (l.454). Says he has an operation to bring the Hapans back (l.462–474). ATROCITY: orders Seyah arrested and executed for "desertion" (l.486–490). Allana: "He would cut down anyone" (l.518).
- Luke on Endor, paralyzed by memories (l.525–551). LORE: Yoda said electric shocks block the Force (l.525). Luke on Jacen: "commanded Master-level abilities in the Force" though never a Master (l.531). Luke's comlink was switched off (l.559), likely Alema. Jedi base on Endor; StealthXs; younglings (l.561). Luke defers to Kyp (l.577).

## Ch. 3 (l.587–700)
- Kashyyyk, Maitell Base. Jaina repaired the hyperdrive (l.610). Alema left no fingerprints, threads or skin cells (l.642). Jaina's theory that it was a phantom like the evil Mara phantom (l.674–682). Jag doubts Brisha Syo existed (l.686). Jag wants Jacen's shuttle (l.700).
- Han's plan: reconstruct the asteroid's location from the fuel and hyperspace-time records in Jacen's shuttle (l.704–708). Jag thinks Brisha Syo was a fiction, an alternate identity of Lumiya (l.734). Jag's bleakness: "one purpose left" (l.742).
