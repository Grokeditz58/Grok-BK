## Ch. 20 (l.3271–3407)
- Centerpoint's imprinting on Anakin "punished" them, with overloads; days or weeks before it can refire (l.3294–3308). Delpin's distaste for Koyan (l.3322).
- The *Anakin Solo* limps home (l.3334). Caedus: "They knew he was the most significant individual in the galaxy" (l.3340). ATROCITY (intent): everyone who supported Corellia "would die ... or be fed to rancors" (l.3342). Nevil's son Turl is dead; Caedus can't feel sorry (l.3356–3358).
- Second Fleet shambles; Blue Diver and Limpan lost (l.3364–3368). Caedus lies to Niathal (l.3374). Plan: take Centerpoint and "point it at anyone we choose" (l.3394–3400).

## Ch. 21 (l.3408–3500)
- Syal and Tycho. Her fiancé Tiom Rordan died (l.3437). Both sides use scorched-earth (l.3467). "Everyone's afraid of him ... Nobody talks about him" (l.3471). Tycho is recruiting her and asks her to contact Wedge (l.3485–3497).
- Phennir rebukes Koyan: Jacen is alive, the secret is given away (l.3514–3530). Koyan cuts Phennir off (l.3536).

## Ch. 22 (l.3542–3769)
- Jag's anti-Jedi kit: helmet, beskar breastplate, crushgaunts, a low-g thruster pack, a vacuum-rated oversized blaster with a secret feature, and a flight suit laced with cortosis alloy so a saber graze does minor or no damage (l.3554–3588). Karrde yields the coordinates (l.3594).
- Allana wakes and pulls her injector pen; "I hate you" (l.3608–3630). Caedus reveals "I'm your daddy," pouring love into the Force with "nothing of Sith training" (l.3642–3652).
- Korriban: White Eyes and Dician (a tattooed human woman) (l.3660–3662). LORE: Alema's phantom projection "match[es] the lost technique of Darth Vectivus"; "Vectivus ... was a fraud" (l.3676–3680). White Eyes sends the frigate *Poison Moon* with asteroid-busting explosives to destroy the dark-side source and capture or kill Alema (l.3686–3690). Dician poses as a Confederation Intelligence operative (l.3674).
- Endor: Tenel Ka and Luke (l.3696–3768). Tenel Ka: "I don't see visions of the future anymore" (l.3748). She can feel Allana's movements, which match the *Anakin Solo*'s (l.3756). Luke realizes the girl in the Blur was Allana (l.3758). The Jedi Order offers a rescue (l.3766).

## Ch. 23 (l.3770–3927)
- Star System MZX32905 near Bimmiel. The Falcon hunts for the asteroid (l.3774–3786). Alema feels a "ripple" and knows she is hunted; she makes "all the rules" here (l.3790–3806). The dark power "drown[s] out all lesser noises" (l.3800).
- *Poison Moon*, 150 m frigate; crew Wayniss and Ithila (l.3812–3826). Dician's "Perfect" mantra (l.3904–3908).
- Jag flies up into the habitat with his thruster (l.3848–3864). Improvised bombs (l.3870–3874). Zekk tracks Ship's trail down into the asteroid (l.3882). Leia feels Alema's pulse (l.3920).

## Ch. 24 (l.3928–4073)
- Mynock phantoms: one carries Han's Force signature (l.3956–3958), and among the cave mynocks one is Jag's (l.3994). *Poison Moon* attacks the Falcon (l.4018–4022). Dician feels the Force brush (l.3976).
- Protocol droids with durasteel rail clubs; Zekk's ribs cracked (l.4032–4044).
- LORE: phantoms can't project damage at range; they are confined to their simulated bodies but can wield lightsabers (l.4048).
- Beskar stops a mynock tail (l.4054).
- Alema is tired by maintaining many phantoms; gravity is half Coruscant's; Darth Vectivus's mansion cavern (l.4068–4070).

## Ch. 25 (l.4074–4200)
- **Ship fires on Jaina; Zekk Force-pushes her clear and dodges the shots** (l.4078–4090).
- **FEAT/LORE (Zekk): taps his Shadow Academy dark-side training, overpowers Alema's commands in Ship and "frees" it to seek a better master** (l.4110–4148). ANTI-FEAT: he is flooded by the dark side and fantasizes about killing Jag and possessing Jaina (l.4152–4162).
- FEAT (Jaina): sees through Alema disguised as a towed Jag, using Jag's "assumption" lesson (l.4176–4188). Alema's blue-black saber (l.4188).

## Ch. 26 (l.4206–4333)
- **FEAT (Leia): duels the Alema phantom in the Falcon's doorway with an economical defense; she rides Han's mind to anticipate each maneuver, so Alema is thrown off balance and hits the doorjamb** (l.4212–4218). "Across forty years now, the same speech" (l.4222).
- Zekk is "eaten" by the dark energy and drops out of Jaina's Force sense (l.4228–4238). Ship leaves; Alema panics and her phantoms vanish (l.4242–4256).
- Jag is dragged through tunnels; his elbow is hurt, then his left leg cracks against a wall (l.4268–4276).
- Alema is exhausted; Ship is gone (l.4280–4292). Dician sees the sphere is empty, follows it to Ziost, and triggers the bombs (l.4296–4332). Ziost is the "original homeworld of the Sith" (l.4322).

## Ch. 27 (l.4334–4599)
- Jaina cuts the track and the railcar crashes (l.4360–4364). Jonex mine alarm (l.4382). Alema's sitting telekinetic dark-side blast bends the track and hurts Jaina's chest (l.4390–4392). Alema climbs the vertical track "using only her feet and the Force" (l.4394). Jag shoots her and knocks her 50 m down (l.4396).
- Jag: suit good for "an hour or more" in vacuum vs five minutes with a mask (l.4416). Alema Force-throws his explosive pouch away (l.4430).
- **FEAT (Alema): Force-tears a 4 m section of track and clubs Jag twice; the beskar holds** (l.4434–4446). She Force-pulls his blaster in mid-draw (l.4460).
- **The booby-trapped blaster detonates once out of range and blows off Alema's right forearm. Jag snaps her neck with a crushgaunt; "Remember"** (l.4464–4504). Alema's last lines: "We cannot feel droid intent" (l.4466–4474). LORE (weakness).
- Jaina rescues the dazed Zekk ("It's in me. The evil of this place") (l.4510–4534). A 2 m magnetic space mine rolls past (l.4538–4546). The habitat is blown free and tumbles (l.4554–4562). Jaina blasts the hangar open with a shadow bomb (l.4572–4588). "Both dead, I think" (l.4598).

## Ch. 28 (l.4600–4844)
- The asteroid is destroyed with fission bombs, "since near the start of the Yuuzhan Vong War" (l.4606). Jag survived (l.4616–4628).
- Luke is told Alema is dead (l.4639–4667). LORE: Luke feels the outpost and moon "as though it were a skin," but the Force gives "no guidance, no visions" (l.4689–4697).
- **Ben's insight (FEAT, emotional/Force-reading): Luke "want[s] to be dead. At peace. With her"** (l.4727–4777). Luke sets down his grief and takes up his duty again (l.4791–4811). Ben's graceful whirl (l.4763).
- **Luke tells Ben to build his lightsaber: no formal trials; "pulling yourself back from the brink that Jacen pushed you to ... and then pulling the Grand Master back from his own brink"** (l.4835–4843).
