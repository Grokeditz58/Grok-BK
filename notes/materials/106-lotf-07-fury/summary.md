# Legacy of the Force 7: Fury: summary, plot and strongest findings

- Read 2026-11-04 CST. Full hand-read, l.1–9523. Citation "FU ch. N (l.x)". Detail is in fu-01 to fu-06.
- Author: Aaron Allston (About the Author, l.6509–6511; the books list includes Fury, l.6540). This matches the inventory.

## Plot
- **Kashyyyk and Alema's phantom (ch. 1, 3):** while the Solos fight the Kashyyyk fires, an "ageless" Alema appears on the Falcon. She Force-pulls Han's blaster, walks on the ceiling under high g and vanishes from the sealed ship (l.209–413). Jaina guesses she is a phantom (l.674–682).
- **Caedus takes Allana (ch. 2, 5–6, 8):**
  - Disguised, he finds Allana on Hapes through the Force (l.932–960). He kills on his way out and flees in a StealthX (l.1076–1132).
  - He blackmails Tenel Ka: put the Hapan forces under him "or your daughter will die" (l.1154).
  - He orders Seyah executed (l.486–490).
- **Luke's grief (ch. 2, 4, 11):**
  - On Endor the Force is "silent" for Luke (l.894–898), though he still dominates the Masters' sparring (l.756–802).
  - Ben has worked out that Jacen killed Mara and isolated Ben the way Palpatine isolated Anakin (l.1819, 1835–1843). Luke: strike "only if you can do it without hate" (l.1897).
- **Senate ambush (ch. 10–11):** Katarn's team attacks Caedus, a mission meant to fail so it can plant a tracer (l.1917–1933).
  - Caedus impales Katarn with a speeder (l.1682–1692) and beheads Mithric (l.1748–1752).
  - Valin's deflected bolt drops him to one knee (l.1722).
- **Commenor (ch. 9):** a cloaked asteroid is shattered over the planet (l.1463–1503).
- **Jag's training of Jaina (ch. 7, 12):**
  - Fett sends beskar and crushgaunts (l.1197–1221). With them, Jag beats Jaina (l.2076–2088).
  - His lesson: she is "training yourself to lose" (l.2108–2126).
- **The Love Commander and Centerpoint (ch. 14–20):**
  - Leia negotiates aboard the *Anakin Solo* while her team downloads Jacen's shuttle telemetry and plants gear (l.2498–2600).
  - The Valorum talks are a trap. Caedus flees in the Blur with Allana (l.2902–2926).
  - Luke hits him by feel, then aborts when he senses Allana (l.3103–3111, 3197–3209).
  - Centerpoint wipes out both task forces (l.3231–3257).
- **Allana and Caedus (ch. 22):** "I'm your daddy" (l.3642–3652).
- **The One Sith (ch. 22):** White Eyes sends Dician and the *Poison Moon* after Alema. Lore: the phantom technique is Darth Vectivus's (l.3660–3690).
- **The asteroid hunt (ch. 23–28):**
  - In Lumiya's asteroid there are phantom mynocks and droids (l.3956–4054).
  - Zekk uses Shadow Academy technique to free Ship and is corrupted (l.4110–4162, 4228–4238).
  - Leia beats the phantom by reading Han (l.4210–4224).
  - **Jag kills Alema**: the booby-trapped blaster takes her forearm, then he crushes her neck (l.4464–4504). Dician blows up the asteroid (l.4296–4332, 4604–4606).
- **Ben and Luke (ch. 28):** Ben names Luke's death wish, and Luke steps back into his duty. He tells Ben to build his lightsaber (l.4727–4843).
- **Planning and Corellian politics (ch. 29–32):**
  - The Jedi plan to rescue Allana and destroy Centerpoint while Jacen tries to capture it (l.4928–5036).
  - Teppler and Phennir plot to remove Koyan (l.5136–5208), and Koyan is shot (l.5835–5845).
- **Assault (ch. 31–37):**
  - Tycho does his duty and exposes the infiltrators (l.5506–5522).
  - Luke, Ben and Saba cut through the YVHs (l.5751–5815) and storm the bridge, where Caedus blasts Saba (l.5961–6115).
  - Han and Leia kill a YVH and recover Allana (l.6099–6175). Caedus feels her joy and abandons the duel, but is too late (l.6201–6305).
  - Seyah's coordinate trick makes Vibro's shot at Coruscant collapse Centerpoint and every nearby ship (l.5901–5913, 6310–6334).
- **Aftermath (ch. 37–38):**
  - Caedus Force-chokes Tebut and breaks her neck (l.6386–6404).
  - Leia recognizes Jacen in Allana: "I'm your grandmother" (l.6422–6442).
  - Jaina goes to "her teacher" (l.6465–6493).

## Strongest feats (power-scaling)
1. **Caedus vs Katarn's team (l.1606–1752):**
   - He Force-turns the grenades back (l.1606–1608) and yanks a GAG speeder through Katarn (l.1682–1692).
   - He catches Mithric's blow, redirects a bolt into his chest and beheads him (l.1748–1752), and is "still the best lightsaber swordsman around—excepting possibly Luke" (l.1746).
   - This is while he "wasn't yet recovered from his duel with Luke" (l.1740–1744).
2. **Caedus on the bridge:**
   - His self-assessment: he could beat Leia or Ben "without trouble; Saba, with difficulty. Saba plus Luke would be impossible odds" (l.5576).
   - He telekinetically slides Saba into a grenade blast (l.6111–6115).
   - Luke's assessment: "Jacen was Luke's match" (l.6197).
   - His battle meditation streams probabilities across the whole task force and holds through a hyperspace jump (l.5344–5348). In ch. 13 it "helped his forces inflict heavy losses" (l.2208).
   - His rage wave hits Leia across space (l.3131–3139).
3. **Luke:**
   - Hits the Blur "by feel," without targeting computers (l.3103, 3111).
   - Sparring: "calculated which objects had a chance of hitting him and incinerated only them," and takes Cilghal's saber mid-strike (l.784, 790).
   - Against the YVHs: jams a minirocket in its vent (l.5801), rolls a YVH with the Force and stabs it through laminanium (l.5809), and hurls a YVH through the viewport (l.6191–6193). He charges through heavy squad-weapon fire (l.5765).
4. **Ben:**
   - Force-wrenches a laminanium YVH's cannon arm to turn its fire on another YVH (l.5795–5799).
   - Force-plants grenades that blow YVHs through bulkheads (l.6103–6107).
   - Reads Luke's death wish (l.4727–4777), and his trials are deemed passed (l.4835–4843).
5. **Jag Fel (non-Force):** beats Jaina in practice (l.2076–2088) and kills Alema (l.4464–4504).
6. **Saba:** holds a grenade under the plug with the Force (l.5757), parries cannon fire, catches a lightning arc and beheads a YVH (l.5777, 5805–5807), and stands up charred to keep deflecting (l.6177).
7. **Alema:** a dark-side wave bends the track (l.4390–4392). She Force-tears a 4 m section of track to club Jag (l.4434–4446). Her phantom fights "as swift and ferocious as any Jedi" (l.401).
8. **Han and Leia:** Han shoots down a YVH's barrel at 3 m (l.6125–6131), and Leia finishes it (l.6135–6141).

## Anti-feats and limits
- Caedus: his concentration and strength are slipping, so Valin's bolt drops him (l.1722, 1740–1744). He misreads the dazzle grenades (l.1610–1612). He never detects the tracer until Tebut finds it (l.5233–5245). He loses Allana (l.6303–6305).
- Luke: the Force gives him no guidance (l.894–898, 4695). Syal grazes him (l.3203). He is herded by the YVHs (l.5777).
- Ben: stopping cannon bolts would exhaust him "within seconds" (l.5781). Caedus judges he could beat him "without trouble" (l.5576).
- Jaina: Jag beats her (l.2076–2088). She admits Jacen has "had training I haven't" (l.6465).
- Leia: her Force wave only rocks Alema (l.389).
- Zekk: corrupted by the dark side (l.4152–4162, 4228–4238).
- Phantoms: can't do ranged damage (l.4048). Alema "cannot feel droid intent" (l.4466–4474).

## Lore
- Darth Vectivus's phantom technique; "Vectivus ... was a fraud" (l.3676–3680). Ziost is the original Sith homeworld (l.4322).
- Beskar and crushgaunts (l.1207). A YVH is an even match for a Jedi Knight (l.1570). A cortosis-laced flight suit (l.3554–3588).
- Centerpoint: 350 km long (l.5359). Its beam can be retargeted; in the end it targets itself (l.5901–5913, 6328–6334).
- Electric shocks block the Force, per Yoda (l.525).
