- The *Errant Venture* is the staging base (l.5260–5262). Jag will fly the Falcon (l.5266–5272). Rakehell Squadron roster (l.5316–5326). Kyp's fake GAG team: "Let the enemy do the work!" (l.5330–5338).
- **FEAT/LORE (Caedus's Sith battle meditation): feels the specks of life across the task force; "probabilities and eventualities began streaming"; can focus on anyone's next few minutes; maintains the meditation through a hyperspace jump** (l.5344–5348).
- Centerpoint: 350 km long, 100 km bulge (l.5359). Rogue Squadron is on the GA side (l.5363).

## Ch. 32 (l.5379–5523)
- FEAT (Caedus): pushes the Panther Star's commander to greater speed and confidence through meditation; detects the Rakehells by their possibility streams (l.5385–5389).
- Delpin and Teppler (l.5394–5432). LORE: Centerpoint is operational again; the techs think a planet- or star-killing beam is possible (l.5424).
- The Reveille's run; Tycho escorts it in and betrays the team out of duty, and is shot in the chest by a jittery guard (l.5434–5522).

## Ch. 33 (l.5524–5600)
- Teppler and Phennir (l.5538–5546).
- Caedus withdraws from the meditation and senses Luke, Ben, Saba and Leia (l.5554–5560). Plan Bastion; eight YVH droids crumple deck plates (l.5566–5572).
- **SCALING (Caedus's self-assessment): "Jacen could defeat his mother or Ben without trouble; Saba, with difficulty. Saba plus Luke would be impossible odds"** (l.5576).

- Caedus watches the Jedi cut through blast doors. Leia avoids him in the Force, so her presence "brushed over him" (l.5624–5628). Vibro proposes destroying Coruscant (l.5641–5645). Teppler lures Koyan to an airlock (l.5663–5691).
- Rogue Squadron attacks the Rakehells. Syal kills a Rogue (l.5719–5721). Wedge kills Rogue Leader Lensi (l.5857).

## Ch. 34 (l.5729–5868)
- The Broadside docks hot and the fake GAG team storms in (l.5731–5749).
- **FEAT (Ben): Force-pulls the hot plug he cut out of the blast door** (l.5751). **FEAT (Saba): flips the plug onto a grenade and holds it down with the Force** (l.5757–5759). Ben leaps 12 m (l.5759).
- **YVH fight: Luke charges through the hole into heavy squad-weapon fire, batting bolts** (l.5765). Saba parries full-autofire streams of blaster cannon (l.5777). Luke dodges "like a dancer" but is herded (l.5777). ANTI-FEAT (Ben): staggered by cannon bolts; he could stop them all but would be exhausted "within seconds" (l.5781). He angles his blade to ricochet them (l.5785).
- **FEAT (Ben): Force-wrenches a YVH's cannon arm, laminanium and all, spins the droid and makes it kill another YVH ("Let the enemy do the work")** (l.5795–5799).
- **FEAT (Luke): telekinetically jams a YVH's chest minirocket so it explodes in the vent** (l.5801). He Force-rolls another YVH facedown and stabs it through the armor (l.5809).
- **FEAT (Saba): pushes up a cannon stream, catches its lightning arc, rolls under and beheads the YVH; "greater-than-human strength"** (l.5805–5807). "Good tacticz" (l.5811).
- Chev pilot Chinnith shoots Koyan, unsure whether fatally ("Forgot to set it to stun") (l.5835–5845).

- Leia, Iella and Han crawl through ducts. Leia finds Allana by the presence Luke gave her and avoids touching Jacen (l.5939–5945). Iella finds blank spots in the plans (l.5949).
- Caedus opens the bridge doors: "Care for some caf?" The Ben taunt draws "not one flicker of anger" from either of them (l.5969–5981). "Kill." Eight YVHs fire (l.5983–5985).
- Commenori reinforcements arrive (l.6004). Vibro, a widower full of hatred, targets Coruscant (l.6019–6039). Kyp and Seyah abort plan three (l.6041–6073).

## Ch. 36 (l.6075–6220)
- Leia finds Jacen's lightsaber workshop and the nearly finished saber (l.6083–6085).
- **FEAT (Ben): Force-throws plundered grenades onto the YVHs' chests and blows them through the bulkhead** (l.6103–6107). Luke and Saba kick the flanking droids into the pits (l.6109).
- **FEAT (Caedus): telekinetically slides Saba into the pit just before the grenade goes off; she is blasted 5 m up a wall, aflame. "One down"** (l.6111–6115).
- **FEAT (Han): at 3 m he shoots down a YVH's arm-cannon barrel and blows its forearm off** (l.6125–6131). **FEAT (Leia): deflects the YVH's fire, knocks its electric arm aside, severs the arm and stabs up into the skull** (l.6121, 6135–6141).
- Allana: the code phrase "bantha excess glow rod" (l.6161–6175). Leia: "so familiar-looking" (l.6151).
- **FEAT (Saba): stands up charred and bleeding and keeps deflecting** (l.6177). Luke orders her out (l.6183–6187).
- **FEAT (Luke): Force-hurls a YVH through the weakened viewport into space** (l.6191–6193). Luke's assessment: "Jacen was Luke's match" (l.6197).
- Caedus feels Allana's joy, breaks off, crosses his own droids' fire and leaps across the bridge to the aft door (l.6201–6205). Luke shields Ben; Ben welds the door shut (l.6209–6217).

- YVH-908 is wrecked (l.6229). Jag flies the Falcon in close to the hull ("That boy can fly") (l.6241–6269). Kyle Katarn is at the docking ring (l.6249–6283). Allana is aboard (l.6289).

- **ATROCITY: Vibro fires Centerpoint at Coruscant's coordinates, but Seyah's substituted data makes the beam hit the station itself: a sphere of light about 500 km across contracts to nothing, taking Centerpoint and every Corellian, GA and Commenori ship inside it** (l.6310–6334). Kyle feels "a cessation in the Force" (l.6338).
- Caedus is dazed on the bridge (l.6348–6364). **ATROCITY/FEAT (Caedus): Force-chokes Lt. Tebut in midair, Force-throws Captain Nevil across the walkway one-handed, and breaks Tebut's neck** (l.6386–6404). "He didn't like them anymore" (l.6410).
