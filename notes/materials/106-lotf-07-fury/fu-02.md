## Ch. 10 (l.1521–1693)
- Ambush at the Senate Building. HoloNet: Third Fleet asteroids hit Commenor population centers; the task-force commander acted "on his own initiative" and faces charges (l.1542–1550).
- LORE: a YVH droid is an even match for a Jedi Knight (l.1570). Allana is in YVH-908's crate (l.1596).
- **FEAT (Caedus vs Katarn + 3 Knights):**
  - Force-turns a dozen rolling gas grenades back (l.1606–1608). ANTI-FEAT: he misreads the dazzle grenades and loses his troops (l.1610–1612).
  - "Separately or collectively, these Jedi Knights were no match for him" (l.1620).
  - Force-throws Kolir into Mithric; side-kicks Valin (l.1624–1628). Two seconds (l.1630).
  - Swats aside Katarn's thrown saber "negligently" (l.1664). Telekinetically yanks Kolir between himself and the Knights to cut off their Force push (l.1676).
  - **Force-yanks a GAG speeder into Katarn's back and impales him to the hilt** (l.1682–1692).
- Seha Force-sends the 5 cm tracer patch to his cloak (l.1638–1646, 1708).

- **Jag beats Jaina in a practice bout using the crushgaunts and beskar: his arm ignores the shock, he crushes her training-saber hilt, and her kick goes "konk"** (l.2076–2088). Jaina Force-hurls Jag 5 m into a tree (l.2090–2092). Typo "swhat" (l.2090).

## Ch. 16 (l.2678–2800)
- Leia drops off the walkway, uses an isolation helmet, and notices no bridge noise in the enemy transmission: a skeleton crew or automated ship (l.2692–2730). Hoclaw asks Jacen to step down (l.2734). Caedus gives a 10-minute ultimatum (l.2738).
- Centerpoint Station fire control: Admiral Delpin, Koyan, Teppler. Sim firings risk catastrophic failure (l.2753–2771).
- LORE (Alema): a goddess complex. She projects herself via the dark power pooled in Lumiya's asteroid (l.2777–2787). The Kashyyyk projection left her sleeping for days; "with practice came stamina" (l.2783). She anchors her projection on a pure, bright child presence, which is Allana (l.2793–2799). She plans to exterminate the Korriban Sith (l.2781).
- Caedus finds the guard knocked out (l.2813). He fears a bomb in his hangar (l.2863). Allana: "I dreamed there was a lady here" (Alema) (l.2847). Centerpoint is ready; Koyan authorizes Delpin to fire (l.2872–2878).
