He nearly kills Kyle Katarn's team in an ambush that was staged to plant a tracer on him. Centerpoint fires on the Second Fleet. Jag kills Alema Rar. Ben helps Luke step out of his grief. The Jedi rescue Allana from the *Anakin Solo* while Seyah's sabotage makes Centerpoint destroy itself. Caedus murders Lt. Tebut. Character quick bios:
- **Jacen Solo / Darth Caedus:**
  - GAG colonel and joint Chief of State (l.174). Kidnaps Allana from Hapes, killing along the way, and blackmails Tenel Ka (l.932–1154).
  - Impales Katarn with a speeder and beheads Mithric (l.1682–1692, 1748–1752). Hit by Luke's shot from range and floods the Force with rage (l.3111, 3131).
  - Tells Allana he is her father (l.3642–3652). Runs his battle meditation (l.5344–5348). Loses Allana, then kills Tebut (l.6386–6404).
- **Luke Skywalker:**
  - Grand Master, paralyzed by grief, and "the Force was silent" (l.525–551, 894–898). Beats the Masters in sparring (l.756–802). Hits the Blur by feel (l.3103–3111), then aborts when he senses Allana (l.3197–3209).
  - Ben pulls him back from his death wish (l.4727–4811). Leads the bridge assault: fights the YVHs, and "Jacen was Luke's match" (l.5765–6197).
- **Ben Skywalker:** knows Jacen killed Mara (l.1819). Reads his father's grief, and Luke tells him to build his lightsaber (l.4727–4843). Leads the YVH fight with Force-wrenches and grenades (l.5795–5799, 6103–6107).
- **Jagged Fel:** hunts Alema with Fett's beskar and crushgaunts (l.1197–1253, 3554–3588). Beats Jaina in practice (l.2076–2088). Kills Alema with his booby-trapped blaster and a crushgaunt (l.4464–4504). Flies the Falcon in the extraction (l.6241–6269).
- **Jaina Solo:** trains for Jacen; "I'm his twin" (l.6465). Sees through Alema's disguise (l.4176–4188). Goes to "her teacher" (l.6493).
- **Zekk:** uses his Shadow Academy training to free Ship, and the dark side "eats" him (l.4110–4162, 4228–4238).
- **Alema Rar:** a phantom projection in the Vectivus technique (l.271–413, 3676–3680, 4048). Killed by Jag (l.4504).
- **Han and Leia Solo:** fight the Alema phantom on the Falcon (l.389–413, 4210–4224). Leia leads the Allana rescue and kills a YVH with Han (l.5931–6175, 6119–6141). Learn they are Allana's grandparents (l.6422–6442).
- **Tenel Ka / Allana:** Allana is kidnapped and held, then rescued with the code phrase "bantha excess glow rod" (l.6161).
- **Kyle Katarn / Kyp Durron / Saba Sebatyne:**
