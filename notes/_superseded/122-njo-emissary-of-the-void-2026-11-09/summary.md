# Emissary of the Void — Summary

Greg Keyes’s six-part Hyperspace serial follows rescue pilot Uldir Lochett and crew as they pick up Jedi Klin-Fa Gi on Bonadan, chase her to Wayland’s Tantiss dig, recover a genetic key to a Shaper qahsa, and board a slave transport to free Jedi Bey Gandan. The real prize is a Yuuzhan Vong bioweapon that would turn bacta into a delayed-kill plague. On Thyferra, Bey is revealed as the willing Shaper agent; Uldir stops him; hunter Tsaa Qalu dies; Leaft survives. Placed whole after Conquest / before Rebirth.
