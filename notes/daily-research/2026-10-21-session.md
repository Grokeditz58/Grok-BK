## 2. Feats (with primary sources and how much weight each gets)

1. **Katarr.** He drained the Miraluka homeworld during the Jedi conclave, killing the Jedi there and everything else touched by the Force except Visas Marr. Sources: KOTOR II (Kavar, Atris and the Enclave Masters all say it), *Star Wars Tales* 24 "Unseen, Unheard". Weight: high. It's told, not shown, in the game, but it's consistent across the game, the comic and the encyclopedias. Named victims include Masters Zhar Lestin, Dorak and Vandar Tokare (*Conversations with the Jedi Masters*, via Wookieepedia).
2. **The Ravager and the fleet.** Verified in the LP transcript, Tobin: "This ship... is it his weakness? It should not exist, yet it cruises the darkness between the stars. He tore it from the mass shadows of Malachor, along with his fleet... that is a measure of his power." He also says "He holds it together" (that line is in the reference library's StrategyWiki pass, and Wookieepedia carries the same wording). Weight: high for raw power, but see the caveats in section 4: the orbit-or-surface question is unresolved and a dark side nexus amp is very likely.
3. **Overthrowing Traya, together with Sion.** Kreia (StrategyWiki transcript, verified this session): "To be united by hatred is a fragile alliance at best. But my will was not law. There were disagreements, ambition. And hunger for power. There are techniques within the Force against which there is no defense. I was cast down. Stripped of my power. Exiled." Weight: medium. It was two on one and a betrayal, and the line never names which apprentice did what.
4. **Stunning the Exile's strike team on sight.** The Prima Guide says he "effortlessly stuns you with a wave of his hand" (quoted in t3365). The Encyclopedia says "Darth Nihilus managed to stun the Exile". Weight: medium. It's a real final-game event.
5. **Armor-bound existence.** He keeps his spirit in his mask and armor. Primary confirmation from our own *Legacy* #5 read (p83), Krayt: "LORD NIHILUS -- YOU ESCAPED DEATH BY CONTAINING YOUR CONSCIOUSNESS WITHIN YOUR ARMOR. HOW?!" Krayt, at his own weakest, asks Nihilus's holocron for help. The holocron calls him a pretender along with Bane and Andeddu (p81–84). Weight: it's a hax and durability point, not a power ranking.
6. **Passive killing presence.** Tobin (LP transcript): "This ship... it is a graveyard of a terrible battle. Everything on it slowly dies as long as he hungers." Kreia: "his mere presence kills all around him, slowly, feeding him."
7. **Sensing range.** Campaign Guide and Saga rules (via Wookieepedia): he could reach through the Force "as far as a star system". Visas: "As he is a seam in the fabric of the Force, he senses fractures and weaknesses around him."

**Excluded.** The cut scene where Sion is "rebuffed by a surge of the Force" is cut content. The bridge Force Wave that knocks the party "across the bridge" is also cut: LP Archive Part 54 says "Nihilus was suppose to do a Force Wave and send everyone sprawling... But it didn't get animated." Neither one counts.

- Tobin, on his link to Malachor: "I think he is of that place. If so, then his power may be great indeed... greater than I had thought." (LP transcript) This one is **new**: it wasn't in the owed Nihilus list from 2026-10-19 or in the library extracts.

He is already dead, it is simply a question of how many he kills before he falls." (KOTOR II, via Wookieepedia and the library's StrategyWiki pass)

## 4. Author intent and framing (Chris Avellone, KOTOR II lead writer)

- 2007 interview (StarWarsKnights.com, verified): Nihilus "was created when Malachor V was torn apart, and he was intended as the Exile's other half." He also says: "As powerful as Nihilus' ability is in the short term, the drawback is that it robs the user of almost [all] identity but hunger, which is why is never employed by the Sith Lords of old." Avellone flags this himself: "what I say here is not canon."
- 2010 AV Club interview (verified): "he'd given himself over to the Dark Side so completely he had no personality left... He was more of a force of nature."
- **Bot's read.** The writer built him as the Exile's mirror. His loss to the Exile is a designed thematic counter, the black hole beating the vampire, not proof that anyone at the Exile's power level beats him. It also means his drain "never [being] employed by the Sith Lords of old" is about the cost of the power, not a sign that older Sith couldn't match it.
- Forum posters say Avellone tweeted (2018) that Vader can beat Nihilus and that Revan beats both (t2106). **Unverified.** The tweet wasn't opened, and an off-the-cuff tweet is low weight anyway.

## 5. Forum arguments, critically questioned

### t3365 blog, "Did Malachor V amp Nihilus to perform his TK feat?" (user "Darth Nihilus", 2020)
- **Claim 1: the Ravager feat was pre-prime, done before he drained any planet.** Is it valid? Mostly yes. The Campaign Guide says his power "grew dramatically" as he fed, and Tobin's lines tie his strength to feeding. The blog's key quote ("He used it to escape imprisonment on Malachor V") is credited to the game, but we found no game line for it. **Source unverified.**
- **Claim 2: if the nexus were strong enough to explain the feat, Meetra would have been crippled at the Trayus Core, so her beating Traya there means the nexus can't be that strong.** This is the blog's main argument, and I don't buy it. It assumes Malachor hinders Meetra, but the blog itself quotes the line "You're like Malachor... it's in you, it's what you are now" about her. The Exile is a Malachor survivor and a wound in the Force herself, so there's no basis for saying the nexus hurt her. Azronger's reply holds up better. The Prima Guide says Malachor's energies eroded land formations and mutated wildlife, and the lore treats a nexus as a passive amp whether or not a scene mentions it. The blogger then accepted that he was amped (post 7).
- **Claim 3: the "Telos-starved" Nihilus dominated Meetra plus companions with a gesture, so prime Nihilus must be far stronger.** Half right. The starving is verified (Tobin). But the evidence for domination is the opening stun and a Force Wave. The opening stun is real (Prima Guide). The **Force Wave the blog quotes is labeled "(Cut Content)" in its own citation**, and the LP confirms it never made the final game. The bridge-wide TK point is therefore invalid.
- **Claim 4: Meetra's team had to kill him before detonating the bombs, because he would hold the ship together.** Plausible and consistent with "He holds it together", but the exact line wasn't found in a transcript this session. Unverified.
- **New point worth keeping.** Azronger notes the Prima Guide says the Ravager's broken hull "remained in orbit", which conflicts with "ripped from the gravity well". The blogger's fix is that he pulled it from orbit and then climbed out of the gravity well on board. That's reasonable, but it's the blogger's own reconstruction. My take: the feat is real and huge, its exact size is uncertain, and it was nexus-boosted.

### t893 Nihilus vs DoE Bane (Round 1 no drain, Round 2 all out)
- HellfireUnit says Nihilus is "trash" without drain. That's weak. Azronger answers that, with no drain, he still holds a 1,200 m capital ship together and stunned the strike team with a gesture. That rebuttal holds.
- **Best argument in the thread (Azronger, post 31).** Quoting "Power Beyond Belief" (Saga Edition web supplement), the planetary drain is "rarely used directly against the heroes", only "lesser aspects of his terrible hunger" are, and targets can "make attacks, or use other abilities to escape or mitigate its effects." It's sourced, though the scan wasn't opened, so the wording is as quoted. It cuts against "drain = instant win" posters on both sides, and I buy it as the right model: his duel drain is strong but not a one-shot.
- Deronn_Solo's argument (Bane is far above Kaan, so he's close to Nihilus) is scaling by chain, not a link to Nihilus. It's weak on its own.
- Tally (opinion): mostly Nihilus in R2, split in R1.

### t3372 Nihilus vs Obi-Wan (106 posts)
- **Pro-Nihilus.** DarthAnt66: Nihilus's drain is "stated to be generally indefensible by Kreia, the KOTOR 2 loading screen, and Chronicles of the Old Republic." We verified the Kreia line, but **it doesn't name Nihilus or drain**. It's her account of being overthrown by both apprentices. The loading screen and *Chronicles* claims are unverified. So "indefensible drain" is an inference, a fair one but not a fact.
- **Pro-Kenobi.** HellfireUnit: "Just because Kreia cannot defend herself from it, doesn't mean it is indefensible." That's a fair point. LOTL's case is about telekinesis, not Nihilus: PT-era characters only get thrown when caught off guard. That's mostly irrelevant to drain.
- **Dark Reaper side-argument.** One side says only Anakin, taught by Ulic's spirit, resisted the Reaper, so a technique is needed. The other says Anakin just drew on the Force harder. The Dark Reaper is a different power (Force Harvester, closer to Drain Life), so neither side's analogy proves much.
- ReaperAce's split: if Nihilus keeps distance he drains Kenobi; if Kenobi closes, Kenobi wins. That's the most honest summary of the matchup.

### t852 gauntlet, t439 Arcann, t3945 Anakin, t2749 Caedus, t4239 Malak (opinion summary)
- Gauntlet: posters had him dying anywhere from #4 Ulic to #12 RotJ Sidious. The Ellimist, one of the more careful posters, puts it at Revan's later versions or Vitiate. Consensus: **he loses to SWTOR Vitiate and the Sidious tier.**
- Arcann: mostly Nihilus. MasterCilghal's point that it depends on whether Arcann can resist drain is the real question.
- Anakin and Caedus: hostile, mostly joke posts. "Anakin has drain resistance" (Vaelias) leans on the Dark Reaper analogy above, so it's weak. The God Emperor's "If Caedus can resist force drain, he wins. Otherwise, Nihilus wins" sums up the debate.
- Malak: one-line votes, with the most chain-based ranking (SF Malak > Exar Kun > Bane > Nihilus). There's no argument to assess.

**Pattern across all threads.** Almost every Nihilus debate comes down to one question: does the drain work on the opponent? The second most common is distance. Nobody cites a duel feat for him, because he has none apart from a loss.

## 6. New or revised verdicts (our own calls)

### Darth Nihilus vs Darth Sion (both KOTOR II)
- **Verdict: Nihilus, mid diff. Confidence: moderate.**
- Fights: no canon fight between them (the Sion scene is cut content, excluded). Common opponent, the Exile: Sion lost to her four times on Malachor and gave up. Nihilus needed Visas to break their bond and his drain to backfire on the Exile.
- Statements: Atris calls Nihilus the greatest of Traya's apprentices. Kreia gives Nihilus world-ending framing and Sion durability framing ("he is not a beast of flesh and blood").
- Key reasoning: Sion's whole edge is dark-side-powered regeneration, and Nihilus's power feeds on exactly that kind of energy. That last link is our inference, not a statement.
- Could flip it: a source saying Sion's pain-based undeath resists drain, or treating the cut scene as canon.
- Date: 2026-10-21.

### Darth Nihilus vs Darth Traya (KOTOR II, pre-exile Traya and Telos Nihilus)
- **Verdict: lean Nihilus, high diff. Confidence: low.**
- Fights: he and Sion beat her together, as a betrayal (Kreia: "There are techniques within the Force against which there is no defense").
- Why it's only a lean: it was two on one, a surprise, and the line doesn't say Nihilus alone did it. The chain "Traya beats Exile, Exile beats Nihilus" **doesn't carry over**, because the Exile is a designed hard counter to Nihilus specifically (Avellone: "the Exile's other half").
- Could flip it: a source saying Nihilus alone did the severing, which would raise confidence, or that Sion's beating did most of it, which would flip toward Traya.
- Date: 2026-10-21.

### Darth Nihilus vs Obi-Wan Kenobi (RotS, Legends)
- **Verdict: lean Nihilus, high diff. Confidence: low-moderate.**
- For Nihilus: a massive raw-power gap (Katarr, the Ravager), Force reach, and a drain that nothing in Obi-Wan's resume shows a defense against.
- For Obi-Wan: Nihilus has no duel resume (one-handed style, one loss), and the duel drain is "lesser aspects" that can be mitigated ("Power Beyond Belief", as quoted). Kenobi's precognition and Soresu make closing the distance realistic.
- Could flip it: verifying that the duel drain is weak and slow would push it to Kenobi. Verifying the loading screen or *Chronicles* "indefensible" lines would push it to Nihilus at mid diff.
- Date: 2026-10-21.

### Darth Nihilus vs Darth Bane (Dynasty of Evil)
- **Verdict: lean Nihilus, high diff. Confidence: low.**
- Reasoning: Nihilus has much bigger raw feats, and Bane has nothing like a fleet-scale TK feat. Bane's case is skill and closing the gap, plus whatever drain knowledge he has from his holocron studies (forum claim, unverified).
- Could flip it: Bane-favoring rules (drain off), which give Bane Round 1 at mid diff by our read, since without drain it's Nihilus's TK against Bane's dueling.
- Date: 2026-10-21.
